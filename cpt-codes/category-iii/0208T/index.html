0208T Audiometry air only

Category III

Also known as: air audiometry, air conduction threshold testing

Comprehensive audiometry evaluation including air conduction thresholds only, without bone conduction. This hearing test measures air conduction hearing levels across multiple frequencies.

In Plain Language

Hearing test through air; Sound detection test

Clinical Context

Used for initial hearing screening and monitoring in patients suspected of conductive or mixed hearing loss. Common in audiology clinics and primary care settings.

RVU Information

CPT 0208T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
