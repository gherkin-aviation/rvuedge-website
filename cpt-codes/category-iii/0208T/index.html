0208T Audiometry air only

Category III

Also known as: air audiometry, air conduction threshold testing

Comprehensive audiometry evaluation including air conduction thresholds only, without bone conduction. This hearing test measures air conduction hearing levels across multiple frequencies.

Clinical Context

Used for initial hearing screening and monitoring in patients suspected of conductive or mixed hearing loss. Common in audiology clinics and primary care settings.
