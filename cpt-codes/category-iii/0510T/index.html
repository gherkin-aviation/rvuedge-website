0510T removal sinus tarsi implant

Category III

Also known as: Sinus tarsi implant removal, Subtalar implant removal

Removal of sinus tarsi implant placed for subtalar joint stabilization.

In Plain Language

Removal of ankle spacer; Subtalar joint implant removal

Clinical Context

Used for extraction of sinus tarsi implants previously placed for subtalar joint stabilization in cases of flat feet or posterior tibial tendon dysfunction when implant fails or causes complications.

RVU Information

CPT 0510T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
