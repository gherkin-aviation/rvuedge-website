0480T Fxjl abl lsr each additional 100sqcm

Category III +Add-on

Also known as: additional fractional laser, additional laser resurfacing

Fractional laser ablation of skin lesions, each additional 100 square centimeter area. Laser destruction of additional skin areas beyond initial treatment.

In Plain Language

Laser treatment for more skin area; Additional fractional resurfacing

Clinical Context

Used when treatment area exceeds 100 square centimeters. Allows coding for extensive laser treatment.

RVU Information

CPT 0480T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
