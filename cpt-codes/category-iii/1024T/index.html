1024T Percutaneous tissue dsplmt soft tissue

Category III

Also known as: Soft tissue displacement, Tissue plane separation

Percutaneous tissue displacement in soft tissue. Minimally invasive technique creating surgical planes and separation of tissue planes for improved access.

In Plain Language

Soft tissue separation for surgery; Surgical plane creation

Clinical Context

Category III investigational code for percutaneous soft tissue displacement techniques in plastic and reconstructive surgery.

RVU Information

CPT 1024T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
