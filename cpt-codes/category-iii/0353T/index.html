0353T Intraop oct breast cavity

Category III

Also known as: intraoperative cavity assessment, margin assessment OCT

Intraoperative optical coherence tomography with cavity assessment of breast surgical site. Real-time imaging of breast tissue bed after tumor removal to assess completeness.

Clinical Context

Used during breast-conserving surgery to assess adequacy of tumor removal and margin status. Reduces re-excision rates.
