0728T Diagnosis alys vestibular implant uni first

Category III

Also known as: Vestibular device analysis, Device interrogation, Implant testing

Diagnostic analysis of implanted vestibular device, unilateral, first evaluation.

In Plain Language

Testing balance implant to ensure it works; Checking balance device function

Clinical Context

Initial diagnostic evaluation of vestibular implant function including device interrogation, telemetry, and functional assessment.

RVU Information

CPT 0728T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
