0348T Rsa spine examination

Category III

Also known as: RSA spine analysis, 3D spine measurement

Radiostereometric analysis with three-dimensional measurement and report of the spine. Precise three-dimensional imaging analysis of spinal mechanics and motion.

In Plain Language

3D measurement of spine motion; Detailed spine motion analysis

Clinical Context

Used to assess spinal fusion healing, implant position, and micromotion. Research tool for evaluating spine devices and procedures.

RVU Information

CPT 0348T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
