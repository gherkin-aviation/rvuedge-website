0678T Laps reposition lead implantable subcutaneous defibrillator sensing system each add

Category III +Add-on

Also known as: Laparoscopic device lead repositioning each additional

Laparoscopic repositioning of device lead for pacemaker or implantable cardioverter-defibrillator for each additional lead. This Category III code represents additional lead repositioning.

In Plain Language

Adjustment of additional device wires

Clinical Context

Used for laparoscopic repositioning of multiple device leads for pacemakers or defibrillators.

RVU Information

CPT 0678T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
