0678T Laps reposition lead implantable subcutaneous defibrillator sensing system each add

Category III +Add-on

Also known as: Laparoscopic device lead repositioning each additional

Laparoscopic repositioning of device lead for pacemaker or implantable cardioverter-defibrillator for each additional lead. This Category III code represents additional lead repositioning.

Clinical Context

Used for laparoscopic repositioning of multiple device leads for pacemakers or defibrillators.
