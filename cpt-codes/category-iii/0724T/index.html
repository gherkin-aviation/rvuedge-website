0724T Qmrcp with diagnosis MRI same anatomy

Category III

Also known as: Quantitative MRCP with diagnostic MRI, Enhanced QMRCP, MRCP diagnostic

Quantitative magnetic resonance cholangiopancreatography with diagnostic magnetic resonance imaging of same anatomical region.

In Plain Language

MRI scan measuring and visualizing bile and pancreatic ducts; Detailed imaging of liver and pancreas system

Clinical Context

Quantitative MRCP combined with diagnostic MRI for comprehensive evaluation of biliary and pancreatic pathology in same session.

RVU Information

CPT 0724T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
