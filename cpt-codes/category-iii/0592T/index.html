0592T Hlth & wb coaching indiv f-up

Category III

Also known as: Health coaching follow-up

Health and wellness coaching provided to an individual patient for follow-up visits. This Category III code includes ongoing lifestyle modification support and progress assessment.

In Plain Language

Follow-up appointment with health coach

Clinical Context

Used for continued behavioral health coaching to monitor progress on lifestyle modifications, reinforcement of healthy behaviors, and adjustment of wellness plans.

RVU Information

CPT 0592T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
