0985T Intravenous img xtrc cere vsl oct each

Category III

Also known as: repeat OCT extraction, additional vessel

Intravenous optical coherence tomography imaging for extraction of cerebral vessel occlusion device. Each additional vessel.

In Plain Language

Additional clot removal; Multiple vessel treatment

Clinical Context

OCT-guided extraction of thrombotic occlusions in additional cerebral vessels.

RVU Information

CPT 0985T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
