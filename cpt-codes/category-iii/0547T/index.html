0547T Bone matrl qualitative tst mcrind tib

Category III

Also known as: Bone quality test, Microindentation testing

Bone material quality testing using microindentation of tibial cortex.

Clinical Context

Used to assess bone quality and mechanical properties in osteoporosis or metabolic bone disease patients by measuring cortical bone resistance to indentation.
