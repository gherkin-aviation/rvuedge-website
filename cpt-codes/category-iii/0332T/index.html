0332T Heart symp image plnr spect

Category III

Also known as: cardiac sympathetic SPECT, tomographic cardiac imaging

Cardiac sympathetic imaging using single photon emission computed tomography in tomographic format with image interpretation and report. Nuclear imaging of cardiac sympathetic function using tomographic (3D) imaging.

Clinical Context

Used for detailed assessment of cardiac innervation and autonomic dysfunction. Better anatomic detail than planar imaging.
