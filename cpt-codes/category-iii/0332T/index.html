0332T Heart symp image plnr spect

Category III

Also known as: cardiac sympathetic SPECT, tomographic cardiac imaging

Cardiac sympathetic imaging using single photon emission computed tomography in tomographic format with image interpretation and report. Nuclear imaging of cardiac sympathetic function using tomographic (3D) imaging.

In Plain Language

3D nuclear scan of heart nerves; Advanced heart nerve imaging

Clinical Context

Used for detailed assessment of cardiac innervation and autonomic dysfunction. Better anatomic detail than planar imaging.

RVU Information

CPT 0332T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
