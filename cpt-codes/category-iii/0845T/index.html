0845T Dgtz gls mcrscp imfluor first

Category III

Also known as: digital immunofluor first, digital fluorescence analysis

Digital glass microscopy immunofluorescence analysis for first specimen. Computer-assisted interpretation of fluorescent antibody staining for first tissue sample.

In Plain Language

Computer-aided review of fluorescent staining under digital microscope of first sample; Digital immunofluorescence first

Clinical Context

Used for initial immunofluorescence analysis of tissue specimens for detection of specific antigens in autoimmune and infectious diseases.

RVU Information

CPT 0845T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
