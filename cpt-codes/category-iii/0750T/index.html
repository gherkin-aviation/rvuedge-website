Category III

Also known as: Single view DXA, DXA single projection, Limited bone density scan

Bone strength and fracture risk assessment using dual-energy X-ray absorptiometry with single view.

In Plain Language

Limited bone density scan; One-view bone strength testing

Clinical Context

Bone mineral density assessment using DXA with single radiographic projection, potentially for follow-up or limited assessment.

RVU Information

CPT 0750T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
