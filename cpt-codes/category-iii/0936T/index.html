0936T Photobiomodulation therapy rta

Category III

Also known as: light therapy, phototherapy, PBM

Photobiomodulation therapeutic procedure using light energy for tissue healing and regeneration. Non-invasive light therapy treatment.

In Plain Language

Healing light treatment; Tissue repair therapy

Clinical Context

Non-invasive therapeutic use of near-infrared or red light for wound healing, pain management, or tissue regeneration.

RVU Information

CPT 0936T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
