0483T Tmvi percutaneous approach

Category III Global 0d

Also known as: transcatheter mitral valve replacement, TMVR, percutaneous mitral replacement

Transcatheter mitral valve implantation via percutaneous approach. Catheter-based implantation of replacement mitral valve without surgery.

In Plain Language

Replacing heart valve without surgery; Catheter-based valve replacement

Clinical Context

Used for severe mitral valve disease in high-risk surgical patients. Provides hemodynamic improvement with lower morbidity.

RVU Information

CPT 0483T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
