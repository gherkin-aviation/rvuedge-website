0545T Transcatheter tv annulus rcnstj

Category III

Also known as: Transapical tricuspid annuloplasty, TA tricuspid repair

Transapical transcatheter tricuspid valve annulus reconstruction.

In Plain Language

Beating heart right valve repair; Tricuspid annulus reconstruction

Clinical Context

Used for tricuspid regurgitation repair via transapical access using transcatheter annulus reconstruction to restore valve competence.

RVU Information

CPT 0545T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
