0604T Rem oct rta device setup & educaj

Category III

Also known as: Remote refraction device setup

Remote ocular refraction technology device setup and patient education. This Category III code includes initial configuration and instructional counseling for remote vision assessment.

In Plain Language

Setting up remote eye testing device

Clinical Context

Used for setup of remote refraction technology allowing patients to obtain accurate eye measurements at home for eyeglass or contact lens prescriptions.

RVU Information

CPT 0604T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
