0450T Insj aqueous drainage device each

Category III +Add-on

Also known as: aqueous shunt bilateral, second eye drainage implant

Insertion of an aqueous drainage device in the anterior chamber of the eye, each additional eye. Placement of drainage implant in second eye or additional devices.

Clinical Context

Used when bilateral glaucoma requires drainage devices in both eyes.
