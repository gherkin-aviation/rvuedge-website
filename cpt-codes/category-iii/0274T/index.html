0274T Prq lamot/lam dcmpr crv/thrc

Category III

Also known as: percutaneous laminectomy, minimally invasive decompression

Percutaneous laminotomy and laminectomy with decompression of nerve root in cervical or thoracic region. Minimally invasive removal of lamina and disc material to relieve nerve compression.

In Plain Language

Tiny surgery to relieve nerve pinching in neck/upper back; Keyhole surgery for spine

Clinical Context

Used for cervical or thoracic radiculopathy, stenosis refractory to conservative treatment. Less invasive than open decompression.

RVU Information

CPT 0274T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
