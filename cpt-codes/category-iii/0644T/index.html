0644T Transcatheter removal/deblock intracardiac mass percutaneous

Category III

Also known as: Percutaneous intracardiac mass removal

Transcatheter removal or dislodgement of intracardiac mass via percutaneous approach. This Category III code represents catheter-based extraction of cardiac masses.

Clinical Context

Used for treatment of intracardiac thrombi, vegetations, or other masses obstructing cardiac flow via percutaneous transcatheter approach.
