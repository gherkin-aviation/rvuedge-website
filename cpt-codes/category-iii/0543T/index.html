0543T Ta mv repair with artif chord tendon

Category III

Also known as: Transapical mitral repair, Chordal replacement repair

Transapical mitral valve repair with artificial chords and tendon implantation.

Clinical Context

Used for mitral regurgitation repair via transapical access, particularly for patients with leaflet prolapse or chordae tendineae rupture, using artificial chordal reconstruction.
