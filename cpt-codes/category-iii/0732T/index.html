0732T Immntx admn electroporatn intramuscular

Category III

Also known as: Electroporation immunotherapy, EP-based therapy, Intramuscular immunotherapy

Immunotherapy administration via electroporation for intramuscular injection.

Clinical Context

Used for enhancing delivery and effectiveness of immunotherapy agents through electroporation, a technique that uses electrical pulses to increase cell membrane permeability.
