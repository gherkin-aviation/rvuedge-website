0351T Intraop oct brst/node specimen

Category III

Also known as: intraoperative OCT, surgical OCT, real-time tissue imaging

Intraoperative optical coherence tomography with specimen tissue examination of breast or breast nodes. Real-time microscopic imaging during breast surgery to assess margins and tissue quality.

Clinical Context

Used during breast cancer surgery to assess tumor margins, lymph node quality, and tissue viability. Improves surgical precision.
