0072T Fcsd ultrasound ablation leiomyom>=200

Category III

Also known as: MRgFUS large fibroid treatment, focused ultrasound large fibroid ablation

Focused ultrasound ablation treatment of uterine leiomyomata (fibroids), total treatment volume 200 cubic centimeters of tissue or greater. Category III code for MRI-guided focused ultrasound surgery for larger-volume fibroid treatment.

In Plain Language

ultrasound treatment for large fibroids; non-surgical large fibroid shrinking procedure

Clinical Context

Used for MRI-guided focused ultrasound ablation of uterine fibroids with total treatment volume of 200cc or more. Longer procedure than 0071T due to larger treatment volume. Requires more extensive cooling periods between sonications to prevent thermal injury to adjacent structures.

RVU Information

CPT 0072T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
