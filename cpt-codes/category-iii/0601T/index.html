0601T Ablation ire 1+tumors open

Category III

Also known as: Open prostate IRE ablation

Ablation of one or more malignant tumors of the prostate using irreversible electroporation via open surgical approach. This Category III code represents surgical ablation with electroporation technology.

In Plain Language

Surgical electrical destruction of prostate cancer

Clinical Context

Used for open surgical ablation of prostate tumors using irreversible electroporation when percutaneous approach is not feasible or multiple lesions require treatment.

RVU Information

CPT 0601T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
