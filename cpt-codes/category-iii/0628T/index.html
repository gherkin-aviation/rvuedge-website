0628T Percutaneous njx algc fluoroscopy lumbar each

Category III +Add-on

Also known as: Percutaneous lumbar analgesic injection each additional

Percutaneous injection of analgesic with fluoroscopic guidance of lumbar spine for each additional injection. This Category III code represents additional pain medication deliveries.

Clinical Context

Used for multiple-level lumbar spine injection procedures or serial analgesic delivery for persistent lumbar radiculopathy.
