0164T Remove lumbar artif disc additional

Category III +Add-on

Also known as: additional lumbar disc removal, lumbar ADR removal add-on

Removal of total disc arthroplasty (artificial disc), each additional interspace in the lumbar spine. Category III code for removal of artificial lumbar disc at additional levels beyond the primary level.

In Plain Language

removal of additional artificial lower back disc; taking out extra artificial disc from lower back

Clinical Context

Used when removing a total disc arthroplasty device at additional lumbar levels beyond the first. Reported for each additional interspace. Lumbar disc replacement removal may be necessary for device failure, subsidence, or persistent symptoms requiring conversion to fusion.

RVU Information

CPT 0164T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
