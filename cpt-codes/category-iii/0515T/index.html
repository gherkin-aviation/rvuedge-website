0515T Insj wcs lv complicated sys

Category III

Also known as: LVAD insertion complete, Artificial heart pump implant

Insertion of windowed-closure left ventricular assist device, complete system.

In Plain Language

Heart pump surgery; Artificial heart system placement

Clinical Context

Used for advanced heart failure patients requiring mechanical circulatory support. Windowed-closure system provides percutaneous access for device management and hemodynamic monitoring.

RVU Information

CPT 0515T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
