0345T Transcath mtral vlve repair

Category III

Also known as: mitral clip, percutaneous mitral repair, transcatheter mitral repair

Transcatheter mitral valve repair via percutaneous approach. Catheter-based repair of mitral regurgitation without surgical intervention.

Clinical Context

Used for functional or degenerative mitral regurgitation unfit for surgery or high-risk surgical candidates. Improves symptoms and reduces heart failure progression.
