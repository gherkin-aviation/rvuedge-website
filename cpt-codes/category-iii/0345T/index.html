0345T Transcath mtral vlve repair

Category III

Also known as: mitral clip, percutaneous mitral repair, transcatheter mitral repair

Transcatheter mitral valve repair via percutaneous approach. Catheter-based repair of mitral regurgitation without surgical intervention.

In Plain Language

Fixing leaky heart valve without surgery; Catheter-based valve repair

Clinical Context

Used for functional or degenerative mitral regurgitation unfit for surgery or high-risk surgical candidates. Improves symptoms and reduces heart failure progression.

RVU Information

CPT 0345T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
