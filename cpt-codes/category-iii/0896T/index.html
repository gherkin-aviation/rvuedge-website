0896T Connj lvr algrft prfu device each

Category III +Add-on

Also known as: liver perfusion additional connection, additional device attachment

Connection of liver allograft perfusion device each additional connection. Attachment of perfusion apparatus at additional sites.

In Plain Language

Connecting the perfusion pump to additional sites on transplanted liver; Additional perfusion connections

Clinical Context

Used for connection of perfusion device at additional sites in liver allograft during complex procurement or transplantation.

RVU Information

CPT 0896T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
