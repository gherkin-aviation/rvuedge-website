0330T Tear film img uni/bi with i & r

Category III

Also known as: tear film analysis, ocular surface imaging

Tear film imaging with interpretation and report, unilateral or bilateral with interferometry and refractometry. Advanced imaging of tear film and ocular surface to diagnose dry eye disease.

Clinical Context

Used for dry eye evaluation, meibomian gland assessment, and tear film analysis. Helps guide treatment for ocular surface disease.
