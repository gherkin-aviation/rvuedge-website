0237T Trluml perip athrc brchiocph

Category III

Also known as: brachiocephalic atherectomy, subclavian revascularization

Transcatheter revascularization of peripheral artery, brachiocephalic artery using transluminal atherectomy. Minimally invasive removal of atherosclerotic plaque from brachiocephalic arteries via catheter.

Clinical Context

Used for subclavian artery stenosis and brachiocephalic disease. Preserves arm perfusion and reduces stroke risk.
