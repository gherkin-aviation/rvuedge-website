0098T Revision artific disc additional

Category III +Add-on

Also known as: additional cervical disc revision, cervical ADR revision add-on

Revision including replacement of total disc arthroplasty (artificial disc), each additional interspace in the cervical spine. Category III code for revision of cervical disc replacement at additional levels.

Clinical Context

Used when revising or replacing a total disc arthroplasty at additional cervical levels beyond the first. Cervical disc replacement revision may involve exchange of the device, repositioning, or conversion to fusion for device failure or malpositioning.
