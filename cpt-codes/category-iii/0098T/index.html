0098T Revision artific disc additional

Category III +Add-on

Also known as: additional cervical disc revision, cervical ADR revision add-on

Revision including replacement of total disc arthroplasty (artificial disc), each additional interspace in the cervical spine. Category III code for revision of cervical disc replacement at additional levels.

In Plain Language

revision of additional artificial neck disc; fixing extra artificial disc in neck

Clinical Context

Used when revising or replacing a total disc arthroplasty at additional cervical levels beyond the first. Cervical disc replacement revision may involve exchange of the device, repositioning, or conversion to fusion for device failure or malpositioning.

RVU Information

CPT 0098T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
