0495T Mntr cdvr don lng first 2 hours

Category III

Also known as: ECMO lung monitoring initial, Normothermic lung monitoring first hours

Monitoring of extracorporeal donor lung, first two hours of preservation and perfusion.

Clinical Context

Critical period during organ preservation where parameters such as perfusion pressure, oxygen consumption, and lactate clearance are monitored. Used to assess lung viability before transplantation.
