0211T Speech audiom thresh & recog

Category III

Also known as: speech recognition and threshold, SRT and word recognition

Speech audiometry threshold and recognition testing measuring both the quietest speech signals understood and word recognition ability at comfortable levels. Comprehensive assessment of speech perception.

In Plain Language

Speech understanding and recognition test; Complete speech hearing test

Clinical Context

Used for comprehensive hearing assessment including both threshold and discrimination. Helps guide treatment decisions and hearing aid fitting.

RVU Information

CPT 0211T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
