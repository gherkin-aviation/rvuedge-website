1013T Laparoscopic srg imp/rpl eso neuroelectrode array & pulse generator/r

Category III

Also known as: Laparoscopic esophageal neuromodulation, Laparoscopic esophageal stimulator

Laparoscopic surgical implantation or replacement of esophageal neuromodulation system near the gastroesophageal junction. Minimally invasive placement of electrical stimulation device for reflux or motility disorders.

In Plain Language

Laparoscopic esophageal stimulator placement; Keyhole esophageal pacemaker insertion

Clinical Context

Category III investigational code for minimally invasive placement of esophageal electrical stimulation device for gastroesophageal reflux or esophageal dysmotility.

RVU Information

CPT 1013T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
