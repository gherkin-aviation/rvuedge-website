0824T Transcatheter removal 1chmbr ldls pm ra

Category III

Also known as: leadless PM removal, transcatheter pacemaker extraction

Transcatheter removal of one-chamber leadless pacemaker from right atrial position. Percutaneous extraction of wireless single-chamber pacemaker from atrial location.

In Plain Language

Removing a wireless single-chamber heart device through a catheter; Leadless pacemaker removal

Clinical Context

Used for percutaneous removal of single-chamber leadless pacemakers due to malfunction, infection, or need for upgrade to different system.

RVU Information

CPT 0824T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
