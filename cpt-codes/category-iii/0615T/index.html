0615T Auto alys binocular eye mvmt

Category III

Also known as: Automated binocular eye movement analysis

Automated analysis of binocular eye movement patterns with comprehensive assessment. This Category III code includes computerized evaluation of ocular motility.

Clinical Context

Used for objective assessment of binocular vision coordination to diagnose strabismus, vergence disorders, or neurologic eye movement abnormalities.
