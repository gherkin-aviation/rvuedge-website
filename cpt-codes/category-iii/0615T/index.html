0615T Auto alys binocular eye mvmt

Category III

Also known as: Automated binocular eye movement analysis

Automated analysis of binocular eye movement patterns with comprehensive assessment. This Category III code includes computerized evaluation of ocular motility.

In Plain Language

Computer analysis of eye movement coordination

Clinical Context

Used for objective assessment of binocular vision coordination to diagnose strabismus, vergence disorders, or neurologic eye movement abnormalities.

RVU Information

CPT 0615T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
