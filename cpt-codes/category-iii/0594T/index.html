0594T Osteot humerus external lngth device

Category III

Also known as: Humerus lengthening, Arm lengthening with external fixator

Osteotomy of the humerus (upper arm bone) using external fixation and lengthening device to correct deformity or achieve limb lengthening. This Category III code represents surgical repositioning and gradual lengthening.

Clinical Context

Used for limb length discrepancy, bone deformity correction, or limb lengthening in the humerus via surgical osteotomy and external fixation with distraction techniques.
