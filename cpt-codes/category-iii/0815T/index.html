0815T Ultrasound rems bone dns hips plvs/spinal

Category III

Also known as: remote bone density US, ultrasound bone assessment

Ultrasound remote monitoring with bone density assessment of hips and pelvis/spine. Real-time imaging assessment of bone mineral density in skeletal regions.

Clinical Context

Used for monitoring bone density changes in patients with osteoporosis or metabolic bone disease using advanced ultrasound technology.
