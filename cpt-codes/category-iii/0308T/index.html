0308T Insj ocular telescope prosthesis

Category III

Also known as: intraocular telescope, ocular prosthesis, retinal implant

Insertion of an ocular telescopic prosthesis surgically into the eye to improve central vision in macular degeneration. Surgical implantation of magnifying device for vision enhancement.

Clinical Context

Used for end-stage age-related macular degeneration with significant central vision loss. Provides magnification for remaining peripheral vision.
