1000T Autol muscle cell therapy admn

Category III

Also known as: Autologous myoblast therapy, Muscle cell therapy

Administration of autologous muscle cell therapy. This emerging therapy involves injecting the patient's own cultured muscle cells to repair or regenerate damaged muscle tissue.

In Plain Language

Injection of patient's own muscle cells; Cell therapy for muscle damage

Clinical Context

Category III temporary code for investigational treatment of cardiac or skeletal muscle dysfunction. Research indicates potential use in heart failure and muscular dystrophy management.

RVU Information

CPT 1000T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
