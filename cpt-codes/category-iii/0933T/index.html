0933T Transcatheter implant wrls l atr prs snr

Category III

Also known as: LAA monitor implant, wireless sensor implant

Transcatheter atrial left appendage monitoring system implantation with wireless technology and left atrial pressure sensor. Minimally invasive insertion.

In Plain Language

Heart monitor wire implant; Pressure sensor placement

Clinical Context

Percutaneous implantation of wireless left atrial pressure sensor for heart failure monitoring.

RVU Information

CPT 0933T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
