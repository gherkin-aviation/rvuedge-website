0076T supervision and interpretation stent/chest vertebral artery

Category III

Also known as: S&I great vessel stent, carotid stent imaging

Supervision and interpretation for stent placement of intrathoracic common carotid artery or innominate (brachiocephalic) artery including angioplasty, when performed. Category III code for imaging guidance during great vessel stenting.

Clinical Context

Used for the radiological supervision and interpretation component of intrathoracic great vessel stenting. Reported separately from the surgical component (0075T) to capture the imaging professional services.
