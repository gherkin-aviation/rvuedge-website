0352T Oct brst/node i & r per specimen

Category III

Also known as: OCT tissue analysis, breast OCT interpretation

Optical coherence tomography with specimen tissue examination and interpretation per specimen of breast or breast nodes. Detailed post-procedure microscopic analysis of removed breast tissue.

Clinical Context

Used for comprehensive assessment of surgical specimens to assess margins, grade disease, and identify additional pathology.
