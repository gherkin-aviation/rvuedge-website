0667T Don hysterectomy rcp uterine

Category III

Also known as: Robotic donor hysterectomy with reconstruction

Donor hysterectomy with recipient uterine allograft reconstruction via robotic-assisted approach. This Category III code represents robotic procurement and preparation.

In Plain Language

Robot-assisted uterus procurement and preparation

Clinical Context

Used for robotic-assisted donor hysterectomy with simultaneous recipient site preparation for uterine transplantation.

RVU Information

CPT 0667T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
