1003T Arthrp first crp/mtcrpl prostc

Category III

Also known as: Joint injection, Prosthetic joint injection

Arthrocentesis with first carpometacarpal or metacarpophalangeal prosthetic joint injection. Diagnostic and therapeutic injection into artificial hand joint prostheses.

In Plain Language

Injection into artificial hand joint; Prosthetic joint fluid injection

Clinical Context

Category III investigational code for management of prosthetic hand joint dysfunction. Used for aspiration and injection of therapeutic agents into artificial hand joints.

RVU Information

CPT 1003T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
