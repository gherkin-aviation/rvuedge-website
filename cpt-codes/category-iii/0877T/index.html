Category III

Also known as: augmented CT analysis, AI CT interpretation

Augmented analysis of chest computed tomography in child without computed tomography. Advanced analysis of CT findings using artificial intelligence without acquisition.

In Plain Language

Computer analysis of existing chest CT scan in child; Enhanced CT interpretation

Clinical Context

Used for advanced artificial intelligence-assisted interpretation of pediatric chest CT scans for improved diagnostic accuracy.

RVU Information

CPT 0877T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
