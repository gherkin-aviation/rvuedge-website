0198T Ocular blood flow measure

Category III

Also known as: ocular blood flow measurement, OBF analysis, ocular hemodynamics testing

Measurement of ocular blood flow by repetitive intraocular pressure sampling, with interpretation and report. Category III code for non-invasive assessment of ocular hemodynamics through IOP fluctuation analysis.

Clinical Context

Used for non-invasive measurement of ocular blood flow through repetitive intraocular pressure sampling. Evaluates pulsatile ocular blood flow which may be relevant in glaucoma, ocular ischemic syndrome, and assessment of optic nerve head perfusion.
