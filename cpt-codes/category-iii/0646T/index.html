0646T Ttvi/replacement with prstc vlv percutaneous

Category III

Also known as: Percutaneous tricuspid valve replacement

Transcatheter tricuspid valve replacement with prosthetic valve via percutaneous approach. This Category III code represents catheter-based valve replacement.

In Plain Language

Catheter replacement of tricuspid heart valve

Clinical Context

Used for severe tricuspid regurgitation or stenosis in high-risk surgical candidates via transcatheter implantation of prosthetic valve.

RVU Information

CPT 0646T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
