0683T Prgrmg device evaluation implantable subcutaneous defibrillator sensing system inpatient

Category III

Also known as: ISDSS device programming evaluation

Programming device evaluation of insulin-dependent diabetes management system with in-person assessment. This Category III code represents device programming and adjustment.

In Plain Language

Adjustment of diabetes device settings

Clinical Context

Used for in-person programming and evaluation of implantable insulin delivery systems to optimize glucose management.

RVU Information

CPT 0683T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
