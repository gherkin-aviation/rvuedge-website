0494T Prep & cannulj cdvr don lung

Category III

Also known as: Lung donor prep ECMO, Normothermic lung perfusion setup

Preparation and cannulation of donor lung for transplantation using extracorporeal membrane oxygenation technology.

In Plain Language

Getting donor lung ready for transplant; Setting up lung preservation machine

Clinical Context

Used in advanced lung transplant programs to optimize donor lung quality, particularly for donation after circulatory death or marginal lungs. Extends preservation time and improves transplant outcomes.

RVU Information

CPT 0494T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
