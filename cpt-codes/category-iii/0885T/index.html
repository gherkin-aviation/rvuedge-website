0885T Colsc flx first tndsc dilation

Category III Global 0d

Also known as: colonoscopic dilation, colon scope dilation

Colonoscopy flexible first time using tendinoscopic dilatation. Initial endoscopic examination of colon with tissue-sparing dilation technique.

In Plain Language

Camera examination of colon with gentle widening; Colonic widening procedure

Clinical Context

Used for treatment of colonic strictures using tendinoscopic dilation during flexible colonoscopy.

RVU Information

CPT 0885T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
