0488T Diabetes preventive online/elec

Category III

Also known as: Online diabetes prevention, E-health diabetes program

Diabetes prevention program delivered through online or electronic means, including coaching and monitoring.

Clinical Context

Used for patients at risk of developing type 2 diabetes mellitus. Provides lifestyle modification coaching, nutrition guidance, and physical activity recommendations through digital platforms.
