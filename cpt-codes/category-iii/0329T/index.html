0329T Mntr io press 24hrs/> uni/bi

Category III

Also known as: 24-hour IOP monitoring, continuous IOP monitoring

Monitoring of intraocular pressure for 24 hours or longer with unilateral or bilateral measurement. Continuous or intermittent intraocular pressure monitoring to diagnose glaucoma.

Clinical Context

Used for suspected glaucoma with normal office pressures, ocular hypertension, and fluctuating pressures. Helps diagnosis and treatment decisions.
