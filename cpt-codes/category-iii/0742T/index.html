0742T Aqmbf spect xers/strs & rest

Category III

Also known as: Quantitative SPECT, Myocardial blood flow SPECT, Stress/rest imaging

Quantitative myocardial blood flow single photon emission computed tomography with exercise and rest.

In Plain Language

Heart imaging during exercise and rest; Special camera imaging of heart blood flow

Clinical Context

Used for assessment of coronary artery disease through quantification of myocardial blood flow at rest and with stress/exercise.

RVU Information

CPT 0742T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
