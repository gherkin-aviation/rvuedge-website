0442T Ablation percutaneous plex/trncl nerve

Category III

Also known as: plexus ablation, trunk nerve ablation

Ablation of plexus or truncal nerve using percutaneous approach. Destruction of major nerve plexus or trunk for pain management.

Clinical Context

Used for cancer pain, complex regional pain, or pain from plexus injuries.
