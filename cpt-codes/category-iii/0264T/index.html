0264T Intramuscular bone mrw cel therapy xcl hrvst

Category III

Also known as: bone marrow cell injection, pre-harvested BMC injection

Intra-articular injection of bone marrow cell therapy for joint regeneration, excluding harvest, with concentration and injection. Cellular therapy using externally harvested and concentrated bone marrow cells.

Clinical Context

Used when bone marrow has been previously harvested or obtained from outside source. Reduces procedure time and morbidity.
