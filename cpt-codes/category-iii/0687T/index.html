0687T Treatment amblyopia device setup first

Category III

Also known as: Amblyopia digital treatment setup

Treatment of amblyopia using digital device with initial setup. This Category III code represents vision therapy setup and calibration.

Clinical Context

Used for setup of digital/video-based amblyopia therapy devices for home-based vision training in patients with lazy eye.
