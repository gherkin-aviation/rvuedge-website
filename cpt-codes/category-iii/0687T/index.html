0687T Treatment amblyopia device setup first

Category III

Also known as: Amblyopia digital treatment setup

Treatment of amblyopia using digital device with initial setup. This Category III code represents vision therapy setup and calibration.

In Plain Language

Starting computer-based lazy eye treatment

Clinical Context

Used for setup of digital/video-based amblyopia therapy devices for home-based vision training in patients with lazy eye.

RVU Information

CPT 0687T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
