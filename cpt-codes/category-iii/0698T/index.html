0698T Quan mr tissue with MRI mlt orgn

Category III

Also known as: Quantitative MRI multi-organ, Comprehensive qMRI, Multi-organ tissue characterization

Quantitative magnetic resonance imaging of tissue with magnetic resonance imaging of multiple organs using a multicompartment model.

Clinical Context

Used for evaluating tissue characteristics across multiple organ systems simultaneously, particularly in systemic diseases like cirrhosis, amyloidosis, or iron overload affecting multiple organs.
