0395T Hdr elctr ntrst/ntrcv brchtx

Category III

Also known as: HDR brachytherapy, electronic brachytherapy

High-dose-rate electronic brachytherapy for interstial or intracavitary treatment with dosimetry. Radiation therapy using high-dose-rate electronic sources for cancer treatment.

Clinical Context

Used for gynecologic, head and neck, and other cancers. Delivers high doses to tumors while sparing normal tissue.
