0902T Qtc ntrvl augmnt alg aly ecg

Category III

Also known as: QT interval analysis, Torsade risk assessment

Quantitative Torsade de Pointes interval augmented algorithm analysis of electrocardiogram. Specialized analysis of QT interval prolongation risk using advanced algorithms.

In Plain Language

Special heart rhythm test to check for dangerous prolonged QT interval; QT interval risk analysis

Clinical Context

Used for assessment of QT interval prolongation and risk of Torsade de Pointes arrhythmia in patients on pro-arrhythmic medications.

RVU Information

CPT 0902T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
