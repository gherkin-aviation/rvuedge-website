0633T CT breast with 3d uni c-

Category III

Also known as: Breast CT 3D without contrast

Computed tomography of the breast with three-dimensional imaging without intravenous contrast. This Category III code represents advanced breast imaging without contrast material.

Clinical Context

Used for breast cancer screening and diagnosis with three-dimensional computed tomography offering improved sensitivity without contrast administration.
