0397T Ercp with optical endomicroscpy

Category III

Also known as: ERCP with optical microscopy, confocal ERCP

Endoscopic retrograde cholangiopancreatography with optical endomicroscopy for tissue examination. ERCP with real-time microscopic tissue assessment during procedure.

Clinical Context

Used for suspected cholangiocarcinoma, pancreatic cancer surveillance, and Barrett's esophagus evaluation. Improves tissue diagnosis.
