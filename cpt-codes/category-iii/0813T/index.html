0813T Egd vol adjmt bariatric balo

Category III

Also known as: gastric balloon adjustment, balloon volume adjustment

Endoscopic gastrointestinal volume adjustment of bariatric balloon. Endoscopic revision of gastric balloon size for weight management.

In Plain Language

Adjusting the size of a stomach balloon for weight loss; Balloon downsizing procedure

Clinical Context

Used to adjust the volume of a previously placed intragastric balloon to enhance weight loss or improve tolerability in obese patients undergoing bariatric therapy.

RVU Information

CPT 0813T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
