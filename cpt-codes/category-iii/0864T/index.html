0864T Low ntsty eswt corpus cvrnsm

Category III

Also known as: shockwave therapy ED, low intensity ESWT

Low intensity extracorporeal shock wave therapy for corpus cavernosum. Non-invasive acoustic energy treatment for erectile dysfunction.

In Plain Language

Sound wave therapy to improve blood flow for erectile dysfunction; Shockwave erectile dysfunction treatment

Clinical Context

Used for treatment of erectile dysfunction due to vasculogenic causes using low-intensity extracorporeal shock wave therapy.

RVU Information

CPT 0864T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
