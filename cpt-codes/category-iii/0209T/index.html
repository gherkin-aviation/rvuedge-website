0209T Audiometry air & bone

Category III

Also known as: air and bone audiometry, comprehensive audiometry, bone conduction testing

Comprehensive audiometry evaluation including air and bone conduction thresholds. This hearing test measures both air conduction and bone conduction hearing levels to differentiate conductive from sensorineural loss.

In Plain Language

Complete hearing test; Air and vibration hearing test

Clinical Context

Used to differentiate conductive hearing loss from sensorineural hearing loss. Standard in comprehensive audiometric evaluation. Common for suspected middle ear pathology.

RVU Information

CPT 0209T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
