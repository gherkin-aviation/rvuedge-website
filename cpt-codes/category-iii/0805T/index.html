0805T Transcatheter s & ivc prstc vl implant prq

Category III

Also known as: transcatheter valve implant, percutaneous valve placement

Transcatheter system and intracardiac venous prosthetic valve implantation via percutaneous approach. Catheter-based placement of synthetic valve into venous system.

In Plain Language

Inserting an artificial heart valve through a catheter in the groin; Nonsurgical valve implant

Clinical Context

Used for percutaneous implantation of prosthetic valves in venous systems to treat valve regurgitation, particularly in patients who are poor surgical candidates.

RVU Information

CPT 0805T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
