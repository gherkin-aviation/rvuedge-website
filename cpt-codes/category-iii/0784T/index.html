0784T Ins/rplmt eltrd ra spinal neurostimulator

Category III

Also known as: Spinal cord stimulator placement, SCS electrode placement, Spinal electrode implant

Insertion or replacement of electrode array for spinal cord neurostimulation.

Clinical Context

Used for chronic pain management through implanted electrodes delivering electrical pulses to spinal cord.
