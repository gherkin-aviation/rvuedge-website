0184T excision rectal tumor endoscopic

Category III

Also known as: TEM, transanal endoscopic microsurgery, TAMIS

Excision of rectal tumor by transanal endoscopic microsurgery (TEM) approach. Category III code for minimally invasive surgical excision of rectal tumors using an endoscopic operating platform inserted through the anus.

In Plain Language

minimally invasive rectal tumor removal through the anus; endoscopic rectal tumor surgery

Clinical Context

Used for transanal endoscopic microsurgery for excision of rectal adenomas, early rectal cancers (T1), or rectal carcinoids. Provides superior visualization and precision compared to traditional transanal excision. Alternative to radical proctectomy for selected early rectal cancers.

RVU Information

CPT 0184T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
