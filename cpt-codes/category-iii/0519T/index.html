All Codes / Category III / 0519T

0519T removal & replacement pulse generator wcs lv both

Category III

Also known as: LVAD pump exchange, Artificial heart replacement

Removal and replacement of windowed-closure left ventricular assist device, both pump and controller.

In Plain Language

Heart pump replacement surgery; Artificial heart system exchange

Clinical Context

Used for LVAD exchange in patients with device malfunction, thrombosis, infection, or upgrade to newer generation system. Requires removal and implantation of complete device.

RVU Information

CPT 0519T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
