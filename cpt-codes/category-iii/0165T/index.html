0165T Revise lumbar artif disc additional

Category III +Add-on

Also known as: additional lumbar disc revision, lumbar ADR revision add-on

Revision including replacement of total disc arthroplasty (artificial disc), each additional interspace in the lumbar spine. Category III code for revision of lumbar disc replacement at additional levels.

Clinical Context

Used when revising or replacing a total disc arthroplasty at additional lumbar levels beyond the first. Lumbar disc replacement revision may involve exchange of the device, repositioning, or conversion to fusion for device failure or malpositioning.
