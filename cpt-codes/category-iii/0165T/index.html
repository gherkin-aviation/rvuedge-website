0165T Revise lumbar artif disc additional

Category III +Add-on

Also known as: additional lumbar disc revision, lumbar ADR revision add-on

Revision including replacement of total disc arthroplasty (artificial disc), each additional interspace in the lumbar spine. Category III code for revision of lumbar disc replacement at additional levels.

In Plain Language

revision of additional artificial lower back disc; fixing extra artificial disc in lower back

Clinical Context

Used when revising or replacing a total disc arthroplasty at additional lumbar levels beyond the first. Lumbar disc replacement revision may involve exchange of the device, repositioning, or conversion to fusion for device failure or malpositioning.

RVU Information

CPT 0165T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
