0263T Intramuscular bone mrw cel therapy cmpl

Category III

Also known as: bone marrow cell therapy, stem cell injection, BMC injection

Intra-articular injection of bone marrow cell therapy for joint regeneration, complete procedure including harvest, concentration, and injection. Full cellular regenerative therapy for joint disease.

In Plain Language

Bone marrow cells injected into joint

Clinical Context

Used for osteoarthritis and joint cartilage defects. Promotes tissue regeneration through stem cell properties.

RVU Information

CPT 0263T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
