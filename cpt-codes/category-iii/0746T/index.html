0746T Car ablt radiation arr cnv loc map

Category III

Also known as: Conventional mapping, Catheter mapping, Electrogram mapping

Cardiac ablation of arrhythmia with conventional localization and mapping.

In Plain Language

Finding heart rhythm problem using catheters; Mapping arrhythmia with electrodes

Clinical Context

Intra-procedural mapping and localization of cardiac arrhythmia substrate using conventional electrophysiologic mapping techniques.

RVU Information

CPT 0746T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
