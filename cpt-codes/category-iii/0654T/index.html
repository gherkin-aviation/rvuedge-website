0654T Egd flx transnasal tube/catheter

Category III

Also known as: Transnasal endoscopy tube placement

Esophagogastroduodenoscopy with flexible transnasal approach for tube or catheter placement. This Category III code represents therapeutic placement via transnasal endoscopy.

Clinical Context

Used for percutaneous placement of feeding or drainage tubes into the upper gastrointestinal tract via transnasal endoscopic technique.
