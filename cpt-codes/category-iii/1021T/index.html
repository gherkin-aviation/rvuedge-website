1021T Active thrc irrigation spx

Category III Global 0d

Also known as: Thoracic irrigation system, Active chest irrigation

Active thoracic irrigation with sphincter activation. Continuous irrigation system with dynamic closure mechanism for managing thoracic injuries or infections.

In Plain Language

Chest wound irrigation; Active infection control system

Clinical Context

Category III investigational procedure for management of complex thoracic injuries, empyema, or persistent infections requiring continuous irrigation.

RVU Information

CPT 1021T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
