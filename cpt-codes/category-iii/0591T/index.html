0591T Hlth & wb coaching indiv first

Category III

Also known as: Health coaching first visit, Wellness coaching initial

Health and wellness coaching provided to an individual patient for the initial visit. This Category III code includes personalized lifestyle counseling and goal-setting.

In Plain Language

First appointment with health coach

Clinical Context

Used for structured behavioral health coaching to address lifestyle modifications, preventive health, weight management, or disease prevention in individual sessions.

RVU Information

CPT 0591T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
