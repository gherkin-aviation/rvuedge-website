0525T Insj/replacement complicated iims

Category III

Also known as: Hemodynamic device insert, IHM system implant

Insertion or replacement of implantable hemodynamic monitoring device, complete system.

In Plain Language

Heart pressure monitor implant; Hemodynamic sensor placement

Clinical Context

Used for implantation of devices that continuously monitor cardiac hemodynamics in heart failure patients to guide therapy and reduce hospitalizations.

RVU Information

CPT 0525T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
