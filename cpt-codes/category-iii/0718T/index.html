Category III +Add-on

In Plain Language

Injecting your own cells into torn shoulder muscle; Cellular treatment injection for rotator cuff

Clinical Context

Injection component of autologous dermal cell therapy for partial rotator cuff tears, including localization and delivery of cellular material.

RVU Information

CPT 0718T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
