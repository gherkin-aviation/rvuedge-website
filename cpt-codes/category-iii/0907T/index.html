0907T Coms therapy each additional<=50 square centimeters

Category III +Add-on

Also known as: compression wrap add-on, additional compression

Compression therapy, each additional area of 50 square centimeters or less. Used as add-on to initial compression therapy application.

Clinical Context

Add-on code for additional treatment areas when compression therapy is applied to multiple body regions.
