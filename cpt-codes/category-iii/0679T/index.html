0679T Laps removal lead implantable subcutaneous defibrillator sensing system

Category III

Also known as: Laparoscopic device lead removal

Laparoscopic removal of device lead for pacemaker or implantable cardioverter-defibrillator system. This Category III code represents minimally invasive lead extraction.

In Plain Language

Minimally invasive removal of device wire

Clinical Context

Used for laparoscopic removal of infected, fractured, or abandoned pacemaker or defibrillator leads.

RVU Information

CPT 0679T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
