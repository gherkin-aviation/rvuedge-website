0777T R-t prs sensing edrl gdn sys

Category III

Also known as: Epidural pressure sensing, Epidural guidance system, Pressure-guided placement

Real-time pressure sensing epidural guidance system.

In Plain Language

Smart guidance system for epidural injection; Pressure-sensing technology for spine procedures

Clinical Context

Used for improved accuracy in epidural steroid or other injections through real-time pressure monitoring during needle placement.

RVU Information

CPT 0777T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
