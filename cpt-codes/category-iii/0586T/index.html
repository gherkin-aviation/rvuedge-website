0586T Open islet cell transplant

Category III

Also known as: Open islet transplant

Open surgical islet cell transplantation performed through a traditional large abdominal incision. This Category III code represents the conventional approach to islet cell transplantation.

In Plain Language

Major surgery to transplant insulin-producing cells

Clinical Context

Used for Type 1 diabetic patients undergoing islet cell transplantation via open surgical approach when percutaneous or laparoscopic approaches are not feasible.

RVU Information

CPT 0586T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
