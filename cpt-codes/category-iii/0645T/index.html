0645T Transcatheter impltj c sinus rdctj device

Category III

Also known as: Cardiac sinus reduction device

Transcatheter implantation of cardiac sinus reduction device with septal reduction. This Category III code represents catheter-based geometry modification for heart failure.

Clinical Context

Used for heart failure management via transcatheter implantation of devices that modify cardiac geometry and reduce ventricular dimensions.
