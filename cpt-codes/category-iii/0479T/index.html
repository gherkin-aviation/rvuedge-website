0479T Fxjl abl lsr first 100 square centimeters

Category III Global 0d

Also known as: fractional laser, laser resurfacing

Fractional laser ablation of skin lesions first 100 square centimeters. Laser destruction of skin targets in defined area.

In Plain Language

Laser treatment for skin lesions; Fractional skin resurfacing

Clinical Context

Used for solar lentigines, actinic keratosis, scars, and skin rejuvenation. Promotes controlled tissue renewal.

RVU Information

CPT 0479T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
