0358T Bia whole body

Category III

Also known as: BIA, body composition analysis, impedance analysis

Bioelectrical impedance analysis of whole body composition. Non-invasive measurement of body water, fat mass, and lean mass composition.

In Plain Language

Body fat and muscle measurement; Composition analysis

Clinical Context

Used for assessing nutritional status, obesity evaluation, and monitoring body composition changes in disease or treatment.

RVU Information

CPT 0358T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
