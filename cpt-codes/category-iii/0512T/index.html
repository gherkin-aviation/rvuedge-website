0512T Esw integ wnd hlg first wnd

Category III

Also known as: Shockwave wound therapy, ESWT wound healing

Extracorporeal shockwave therapy for integumentary wound healing, first wound site.

Clinical Context

Used for chronic, non-healing wounds such as diabetic foot ulcers or pressure injuries. Shockwaves promote neovascularization and cellular healing response.
