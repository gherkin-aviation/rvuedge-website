0894T Cannulation liver allograft

Category III

Also known as: liver cannulation, allograft vascular access

Cannulation of liver allograft vessels. Surgical vascular access establishment in transplanted liver.

In Plain Language

Creating blood vessel access in transplanted liver; Liver vessel cannulation

Clinical Context

Used for establishment of vascular cannulation in liver allograft during transplant surgery or perfusion techniques.

RVU Information

CPT 0894T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
