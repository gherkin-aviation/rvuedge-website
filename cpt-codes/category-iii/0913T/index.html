Category III Global 0d

Percutaneous transcatheter therapeutic intervention with intra-arterial balloon catheter. First session treatment for vascular occlusive disease.

Clinical Context

Minimally invasive vascular intervention using balloon catheter to treat arterial stenosis or occlusion.
