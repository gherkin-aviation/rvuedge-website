0908T Opn imp int nstm sys vgs nerve

Category III

Also known as: vagus nerve stimulator implant, VNS surgery, vagus nerve surgery

Open implantation of an implantable neurostimulation system for vagus nerve stimulation. Surgical placement of neurostimulator pulse generator and electrode leads.

Clinical Context

Treatment for drug-resistant epilepsy or chronic depression via surgical implantation of vagus nerve stimulation system.
