0988T Opn ins/rplc ins pattern sbq & sbf

Category III

Also known as: pain pump implant, drug pump

Open insertion or replacement of implantable pain medication pump in subcutaneous and subfascial tissues. Implant placement.

In Plain Language

Pain medication pump; Implanted pain relief

Clinical Context

Surgical implantation or replacement of intrathecal pain medication delivery system.

RVU Information

CPT 0988T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
