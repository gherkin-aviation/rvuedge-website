0513T Esw integ wnd hlg each additional

Category III +Add-on

Also known as: Shockwave additional wounds, ESWT multi-site therapy

Extracorporeal shockwave therapy for integumentary wound healing, each additional wound site.

Clinical Context

Used when treating multiple wound sites in same session, such as bilateral diabetic foot ulcers. Each wound receives targeted shockwave application to promote healing.
