0489T Regn cell treatment scldr hands

Category III Global 0d

Also known as: Stem cell hand injection, Regenerative cell therapy scleroderma

Regenerative cell therapy injection for the treatment of scleroderma in the hands, initial injection.

In Plain Language

Stem cell treatment for hardened hand skin; Cell therapy for hand scleroderma

Clinical Context

Used for systemic sclerosis affecting the hands with skin fibrosis and functional limitation. Regenerative cell therapy aims to improve skin elasticity, reduce fibrosis, and restore hand function.

RVU Information

CPT 0489T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
