0509T Pattern erg with i & r

Category III

Also known as: Pattern ERG, Steady-state pattern ERG

Pattern electroretinography with interpretation and report.

Clinical Context

Used to assess function of retinal ganglion cells in conditions such as glaucoma, optic neuropathy, or retinal pathology. Provides objective measure of macular and optic nerve function.
