0419T destruction neurofibroma xtnsv

Category III

Also known as: neurofibroma excision, neurofibroma removal

Destruction of neurofibroma, extensive, single lesion. Surgical removal or ablation of large neurofibromas.

In Plain Language

Removing large nerve tumor; Neurofibroma removal surgery

Clinical Context

Used for neurofibromatosis type 1 with symptomatic neurofibromas. Addresses pain, dysfunction, or cosmetic concerns.

RVU Information

CPT 0419T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
