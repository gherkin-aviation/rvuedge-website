0580T removal subtalar sinus implant defibrillator pulse generator only

Category III

Also known as: S-ICD generator removal, Subcutaneous ICD device removal

Removal of subcutaneous implantable defibrillator, pulse generator only.

Clinical Context

Used for removal of subcutaneous ICD pulse generator without removing electrode when replacing device or due to infection.
