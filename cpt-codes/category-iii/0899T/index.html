0899T N-invas deter aqmbf aug cmr

Category III

Also known as: aqueous humor flow assessment, ocular perfusion imaging

Non-invasive determination of aqueous humor blood flow augmented cardiac magnetic resonance. Assessment of aqueous humor dynamics using advanced cardiac imaging.

Clinical Context

Used for assessment of aqueous humor production and outflow in patients with glaucoma using augmented cardiac magnetic resonance imaging.
