0481T Njx autol wbc concentrate

Category III Global 0d

Also known as: white blood cell injection, autologous leukocyte injection

Injection of autologous white blood cell concentrate obtained through apheresis for joint disease treatment. Cellular therapy using patient's own white blood cells.

In Plain Language

White blood cell injection for joint pain; Immune cell therapy

Clinical Context

Used for osteoarthritis and chronic joint inflammation. Promotes anti-inflammatory and regenerative effects.

RVU Information

CPT 0481T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
