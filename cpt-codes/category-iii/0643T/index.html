0643T Transcatheter l ventr rstrj device implant

Category III

Also known as: Left ventricular pressure reduction device

Transcatheter implantation of left ventricular pressure reduction device with septal shunt. This Category III code represents catheter-based hemodynamic modification.

In Plain Language

Catheter-based device to reduce heart pressure

Clinical Context

Used for symptomatic heart failure with reduced ejection fraction to improve hemodynamics via catheter-based pressure reduction.

RVU Information

CPT 0643T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
