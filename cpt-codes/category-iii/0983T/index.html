Category III

Also known as: sensor interrogation, remote review

Remote monitoring interrogation by physician of wireless inferior vena cava sensor system. Physician data review.

In Plain Language

Device data review; Remote monitoring check

Clinical Context

Physician review and interpretation of remotely transmitted IVC sensor hemodynamic data.

RVU Information

CPT 0983T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
