0786T Insj/replacement prq ra sacral neurostimulator

Category III

Also known as: Perineural sheath placement, Nerve sheath implant, Sheath insertion

Insertion or replacement of perineural sheath with spinal cord neurostimulation.

In Plain Language

Placing protective sheath around spinal nerves; Spinal nerve sheath implant

Clinical Context

Used to improve positioning and protection of spinal cord stimulator electrodes through perineural sheath placement.

RVU Information

CPT 0786T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
