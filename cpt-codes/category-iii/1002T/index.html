1002T Air displacmnt plethysmograp

Category III

Also known as: Body plethysmography, Air displacement plethysmogram

Air displacement plethysmography testing. Non-invasive measurement of total lung capacity and functional residual capacity using whole-body plethysmograph.

Clinical Context

Category III diagnostic procedure for comprehensive pulmonary function testing. Used in diagnosis of obstructive and restrictive lung diseases when standard spirometry is insufficient.
