0704T Rem treatment amblyopia setup & edu

Category III

Also known as: Remote vision therapy, Amblyopia treatment, Vision rehabilitation setup

Remote therapeutic intervention for amblyopia with setup and patient education.

In Plain Language

At-home vision exercise program for lazy eye; Setting up remote therapy for weak eye

Clinical Context

Used for treating amblyopia (lazy eye) through remote digital therapy. This initial code includes equipment setup and patient/parent education.

RVU Information

CPT 0704T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
