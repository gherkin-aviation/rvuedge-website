0563T Evac meibomian glnd heat bi

Category III

Also known as: Meibomian gland heat therapy, Thermal meibomian evacuation

Evacuation of meibomian gland secretions using thermal energy, bilateral.

Clinical Context

Used for meibomian gland dysfunction and dry eye disease by applying thermal energy to liquefy and express blocked meibomian secretions from both eyelids.
