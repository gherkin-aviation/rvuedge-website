0927T Interrog device evaluation ccm-d inpatient

Category III

Also known as: CCM interrogation, device check

Interrogation and device evaluation of cardiac contractility modulation implantable pulse generator via in-person assessment. Evaluation without programming changes.

In Plain Language

Check device function; Device status review

Clinical Context

In-office assessment of cardiac contractility modulation system parameters and function.

RVU Information

CPT 0927T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
