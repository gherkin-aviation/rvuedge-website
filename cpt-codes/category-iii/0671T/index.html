All Codes / Category III / 0671T

0671T Insj anterior segment aq drainage device 1+

Category III

Also known as: Anterior segment aqueous drug device insertion

Insertion of anterior segment sustained-release aqueous drug delivery device for one or more locations. This Category III code represents implantable glaucoma medication delivery.

In Plain Language

Eye implant for medication release

Clinical Context

Used for implantation of sustained-release drug delivery devices in anterior eye segment for glaucoma and anterior segment disease management.

RVU Information

CPT 0671T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
