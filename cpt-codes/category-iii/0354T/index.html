0354T Oct breast surgical cavity i & r

Category III

Also known as: OCT margin analysis, breast cavity OCT

Optical coherence tomography with interpretation and report of breast surgical cavity margins. Detailed post-operative analysis of surgical bed margins for completeness assessment.

In Plain Language

Detailed imaging of surgery area borders; Margin quality assessment

Clinical Context

Used to verify adequate tumor removal and margin clearance. Helps identify areas requiring additional resection.

RVU Information

CPT 0354T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
