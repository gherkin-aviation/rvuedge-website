0331T Heart symp image plnr

Category III

Also known as: cardiac sympathetic imaging, SPECT imaging, MIBG imaging

Cardiac sympathetic imaging using single photon emission computed tomography in planar format with image interpretation and report. Nuclear imaging of cardiac sympathetic nervous system for heart failure assessment.

In Plain Language

Nuclear scan of heart nerve supply; Heart nerve function imaging

Clinical Context

Used for risk stratification in heart failure and assessment of autonomic dysfunction. Prognostic tool for arrhythmia risk.

RVU Information

CPT 0331T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
