0496T Mntr cdvr don lng each additional hour

Category III +Add-on

Also known as: ECMO lung monitoring additional hours, Extended lung perfusion monitoring

Monitoring of extracorporeal donor lung, each additional hour beyond the first two hours of preservation.

Clinical Context

Used for prolonged organ preservation beyond initial two-hour period. Continued monitoring of physiological parameters ensures lung remains viable for successful transplantation.
