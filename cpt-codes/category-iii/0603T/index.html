0603T Transdermal gfr monitoring

Category III

Also known as: Transdermal GFR monitoring

Transdermal monitoring of glomerular filtration rate with continuous assessment of renal function. This Category III code represents continuous non-invasive kidney function tracking.

Clinical Context

Used for continuous or serial monitoring of renal function in patients with acute or chronic kidney disease to guide medication management and treatment decisions.
