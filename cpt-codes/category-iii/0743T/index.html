0743T Bone str & fracture rsk vrt fracture assmt

Category III

Also known as: VFA, Vertebral fracture assessment, Bone health screening

Bone strength and fracture risk assessment by vertebral fracture assessment.

In Plain Language

Imaging scan to check for spine fractures and bone strength; Vertebral fracture risk assessment

Clinical Context

Used for screening asymptomatic patients for osteoporosis and vertebral fractures, particularly in at-risk populations.

RVU Information

CPT 0743T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
