0556T Bone str & fracture rsk assessment

Category III

Also known as: Bone risk assessment, Fracture risk calculation

Bone strength and fracture risk assessment using validated risk algorithm.

In Plain Language

Bone fragility evaluation; Break risk assessment

Clinical Context

Used to calculate fracture risk in patients with or at risk for osteoporosis using validated algorithms based on bone strength and clinical factors.

RVU Information

CPT 0556T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
