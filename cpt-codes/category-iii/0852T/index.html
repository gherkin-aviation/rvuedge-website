0852T Dgtz gls mcrscp mphmtrc each 1

Category III +Add-on

Also known as: digital morphometry single, single parameter measurement

Digital glass microscopy morphometric analysis for each additional single measurement. Computer-assisted quantification of single tissue parameter.

In Plain Language

Computer-aided measurement of one tissue feature under digital microscope; Single parameter morphometry

Clinical Context

Used for quantitative analysis of individual tissue parameters in specimens for focused morphologic assessment.

RVU Information

CPT 0852T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
