0856T Dgtz gls mcrscp electron mic

Category III

Also known as: digital electron microscopy, ultrastructural analysis

Digital glass microscopy electron microscopy interpretation. Computer-assisted analysis of ultrastructural tissue features.

In Plain Language

Computer-aided review of very tiny tissue structures under electron microscope; Digital electron microscopy

Clinical Context

Used for detailed ultrastructural analysis of tissues using electron microscopy for diagnosis of cellular and subcellular pathology.

RVU Information

CPT 0856T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
