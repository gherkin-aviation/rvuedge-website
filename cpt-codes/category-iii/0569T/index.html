0569T Ttvr percutaneous appr first prosthesis

Category III

Also known as: Percutaneous tricuspid valve replacement, TTVR first valve

Transcatheter tricuspid valve replacement, percutaneous approach, first prosthesis.

Clinical Context

Used for severe tricuspid regurgitation in high-risk patients using percutaneous transcatheter approach to implant replacement valve without surgery.
