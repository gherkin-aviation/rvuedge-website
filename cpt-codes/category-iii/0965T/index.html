0965T I&cyst prp jw xpn dl arch non

Category III

Also known as: dual arch expansion, bilateral expander

Implant and custom preparation of jaw expansion system for dual dental arches using non-fixed approach. Bilateral jaw expansion.

In Plain Language

Both-jaw expansion; Bilateral widening

Clinical Context

Surgical implantation of jaw expansion system affecting both upper and lower dental arches.

RVU Information

CPT 0965T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
