0174T Cad cxr with interpretation

Category III

Also known as: CAD chest X-ray, AI chest X-ray analysis, computer-aided chest film

Computer-aided detection (CAD) of chest radiographs with physician interpretation and report. Category III code for AI-assisted analysis of chest X-rays to identify abnormalities such as nodules, masses, and other pathology.

In Plain Language

computer-assisted chest X-ray reading; AI-helped chest X-ray interpretation

Clinical Context

Used for computer-aided detection applied to chest radiography with physician interpretation. CAD software assists radiologists by flagging potential abnormalities (lung nodules, pneumothorax, fractures) for review. Includes the physician interpretation and report.

RVU Information

CPT 0174T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
