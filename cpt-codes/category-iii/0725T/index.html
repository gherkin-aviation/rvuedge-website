0725T Vestibular device impltj uni

Category III

Also known as: Vestibular implant, Balance implant, Inner ear implant

Implantation of vestibular device, unilateral.

Clinical Context

Used for patients with bilateral vestibular loss causing severe balance impairment. Implant provides sensory feedback to help restore balance function.
