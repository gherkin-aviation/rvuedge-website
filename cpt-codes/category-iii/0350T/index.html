0350T Rsa lower extr examination

Category III

Also known as: RSA lower extremity, leg RSA, hip RSA

Radiostereometric analysis with three-dimensional measurement and report of the lower extremity. Precise three-dimensional imaging of lower extremity mechanics and implant function.

Clinical Context

Used to assess hip and knee implant integration, migration, and longevity. Gold standard for evaluating joint replacement performance.
