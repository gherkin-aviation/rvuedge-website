0071T Ultrasound leiomyomata ablate <200

Category III

Also known as: MRgFUS fibroid treatment, focused ultrasound fibroid ablation, ExAblate treatment

Focused ultrasound ablation treatment of uterine leiomyomata (fibroids), total treatment volume less than 200 cubic centimeters of tissue. Category III code for MRI-guided focused ultrasound surgery (MRgFUS) for small-volume fibroid treatment.

In Plain Language

ultrasound treatment for small fibroids; non-surgical fibroid shrinking procedure

Clinical Context

Used for MRI-guided focused ultrasound ablation of uterine fibroids with total treatment volume under 200cc. Non-invasive alternative to myomectomy or hysterectomy for symptomatic fibroids. Performed with the patient in an MRI scanner while focused ultrasound energy heats and destroys fibroid tissue.

RVU Information

CPT 0071T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
