0373T Adapt bhv treatment each 15 minutes

Category III

Also known as: behavioral therapy, adaptive behavior treatment

Adaptive behavioral treatment per 15-minute time unit for behavioral modification in patients with autism spectrum disorder or intellectual disability. Therapeutic intervention using behavioral modification techniques.

In Plain Language

Behavior change therapy; Coping skills training

Clinical Context

Used for autism, intellectual disability, and behavioral disorders. Teaches adaptive behaviors and reduces maladaptive patterns.

RVU Information

CPT 0373T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
