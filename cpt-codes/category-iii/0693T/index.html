0693T Compre ful bdy 3d mtn alys

Category III

Also known as: Full-body 3D motion analysis

Comprehensive full-body three-dimensional motion analysis. This Category III code represents advanced biomechanical assessment.

In Plain Language

Full-body movement analysis with 3D imaging

Clinical Context

Used for comprehensive biomechanical assessment of whole-body movement patterns in gait analysis, orthopedic evaluation, or neuromuscular disorders.

RVU Information

CPT 0693T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
