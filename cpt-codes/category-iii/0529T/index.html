0529T Interrog device evaluation iims inpatient

Category III

Also known as: Hemodynamic device check, IHM interrogation

Interrogation and evaluation of implantable hemodynamic monitoring device, in-office procedure.

In Plain Language

Heart monitor reading check; Device function assessment

Clinical Context

Used to assess function, battery status, and hemodynamic data from implantable monitoring systems in heart failure and cardiac patients.

RVU Information

CPT 0529T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
