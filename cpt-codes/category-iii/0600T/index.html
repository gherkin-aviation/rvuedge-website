0600T Ablt ire 1+ o/t lv/prst8 prq

Category III

Also known as: Prostate irreversible electroporation, IRE prostate ablation

Ablation of one or more malignant lesions of the left or right prostate using irreversible electroporation via percutaneous approach. This Category III code represents cellular destruction through electrical field exposure.

In Plain Language

Needle-based electrical destruction of prostate cancer

Clinical Context

Used for percutaneous ablation of localized prostate cancer using irreversible electroporation technology for tissue destruction with preservation of surrounding structures.

RVU Information

CPT 0600T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
