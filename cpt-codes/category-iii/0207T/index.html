0207T Clear eyelid gland with heat

Category III

Also known as: meibomian gland treatment, dry eye treatment, gland expression

Clearing or expression of meibomian gland dysfunction using heat and pressure, with or without gland evacuation. This procedure removes inspissated meibum from blocked oil glands in the eyelid margin.

Clinical Context

Used for meibomian gland dysfunction and evaporative dry eye disease. Applied when conservative measures fail. Improves tear film stability and ocular surface health.
