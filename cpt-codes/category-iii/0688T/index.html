0688T Treatment amblyopia assmt with report

Category III

Also known as: Amblyopia digital treatment assessment

Treatment of amblyopia using digital device with assessment and report. This Category III code represents ongoing therapy monitoring and analysis.

In Plain Language

Progress check on computer vision therapy

Clinical Context

Used for assessment and monitoring of digital amblyopia therapy effectiveness with clinical reporting of vision improvements.

RVU Information

CPT 0688T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
