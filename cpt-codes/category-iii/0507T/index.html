0507T Near ifr 2img mibmn glnd i & r

Category III

Also known as: Breast multispectral imaging, Near-infrared breast imaging

Near-infrared two-image multispectral imaging and interpretation of bilateral breast tissue.

In Plain Language

Thermal breast imaging; Special light breast scan

Clinical Context

Used for breast tissue characterization and evaluation of lesions using spectral signature analysis. May assist in differentiating benign from malignant breast tissue and assessing vascular changes.

RVU Information

CPT 0507T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
