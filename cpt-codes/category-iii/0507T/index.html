Category III

Near-infrared two-image multispectral imaging and interpretation of bilateral breast tissue.

Clinical Context

Used for breast tissue characterization and evaluation of lesions using spectral signature analysis. May assist in differentiating benign from malignant breast tissue and assessing vascular changes.
