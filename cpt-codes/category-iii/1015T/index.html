1015T revision/removal lw esophageal sphnc npg/r

Category III

Also known as: Esophageal sphincter revision, Endoscopic device revision

Revision or removal of lower esophageal sphincter neuromodulation system using endoscopic guidance without fluoroscopy. Endoscopic modification or extraction of sphincter electrical stimulation device.

In Plain Language

Endoscopic esophageal device revision; Scope-guided device removal

Clinical Context

Category III investigational code for endoscopic management of implanted lower esophageal sphincter neuromodulation systems with modification or removal.

RVU Information

CPT 1015T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
