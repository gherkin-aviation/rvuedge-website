0055T Bone srgry cmptr CT/MRI imag

Category III +Add-on

Also known as: computer-assisted navigation CT/MRI, surgical navigation CT-based

Computer-assisted musculoskeletal surgical navigational tracking procedure using CT or MRI images for bone surgery. This is a Category III code for emerging technology involving preoperative cross-sectional imaging-based computer navigation.

In Plain Language

computer-guided bone surgery with CT or MRI images; GPS-guided bone surgery using scan images

Clinical Context

Used for computer-assisted surgical navigation that uses preoperative CT or MRI images registered to the patient for intraoperative guidance during bone surgery. Provides 3D navigation without intraoperative radiation exposure from fluoroscopy.

RVU Information

CPT 0055T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
