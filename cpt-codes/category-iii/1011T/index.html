1011T Pbm therapy of oral cavity

Category III

Also known as: Photodynamic therapy, PDT of mouth

Photodynamic therapy of oral cavity lesions. Light-activated treatment using photosensitizing agents to treat dysplasia or malignancy in mouth tissues.

In Plain Language

Light therapy for mouth lesions; Photochemical mouth treatment

Clinical Context

Category III investigational procedure for treatment of oral dysplasia, leukoplakia, or early-stage oral cancer using systemic or topical photosensitizers.

RVU Information

CPT 1011T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
