0629T Percutaneous njx algc CT lumbar first

Category III

Also known as: Percutaneous lumbar spine analgesic CT injection

Percutaneous injection of analgesic with computed tomographic guidance of lumbar spine for initial injection. This Category III code represents computed tomography-guided pain medication delivery.

In Plain Language

Pain relief injection in lower back with CT guidance

Clinical Context

Used for computed tomography-guided delivery of analgesic agents to lumbar spine structures for precise targeting of pain generators.

RVU Information

CPT 0629T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
