0102T Esw phy anesthesia lateral hmrl epcndl

Category III

Also known as: ESWT tennis elbow, shockwave for lateral epicondylitis

Extracorporeal shock wave therapy with anesthesia involving the lateral humeral epicondyle (tennis elbow). Category III code for focused shock wave treatment of lateral epicondylitis under anesthesia.

Clinical Context

Used for extracorporeal shock wave therapy specifically targeting the lateral humeral epicondyle for chronic lateral epicondylitis (tennis elbow) refractory to conservative treatment. Performed under local or regional anesthesia due to high energy levels required.
