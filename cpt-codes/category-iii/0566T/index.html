0566T Autol cell implant adps njx

Category III

Also known as: Autologous cell injection, Adipose cell injection therapy

Autologous cellular implant using adipose-derived regenerative cells, injection procedure.

In Plain Language

Healing cell injection; Stem cell injection treatment

Clinical Context

Used to inject autologous adipose-derived regenerative cells into target tissues for treatment of joint disease, tendon pathology, or other regenerative indications.

RVU Information

CPT 0566T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
