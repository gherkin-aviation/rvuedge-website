0338T Trnscth renal symp denrv unl

Category III

Also known as: renal denervation, RDN, renal artery ablation

Transcatheter renal sympathetic denervation via catheter approach for hypertension treatment, unilateral renal artery. Catheter-based ablation of renal sympathetic nerves to reduce blood pressure.

In Plain Language

Disabling nerve signals in kidney blood vessel; Blood pressure control through nerve ablation

Clinical Context

Used for resistant hypertension uncontrolled by multiple medications. Reduces sympathetic tone and blood pressure.

RVU Information

CPT 0338T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
