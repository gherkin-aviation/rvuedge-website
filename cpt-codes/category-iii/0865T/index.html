0865T Quan MRI alys brn without diagnosis MRI

Category III

Also known as: quantitative brain MRI, qMRI analysis

Quantitative magnetic resonance imaging analysis of brain without diagnostic magnetic resonance imaging. Advanced quantitative analysis of brain tissue without concurrent diagnostic imaging.

In Plain Language

Detailed computer analysis of brain MRI without full diagnostic scan; Quantitative brain imaging

Clinical Context

Used for quantitative tissue characterization of brain using advanced MRI algorithms for research or specialized clinical assessment.

RVU Information

CPT 0865T does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Category III codes for emerging technology do not receive RVU assignments. Reimbursement is negotiated with individual payers.

Billing & Documentation

Category III codes are temporary codes for emerging technology, services, and procedures. They are not assigned RVU values by CMS. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer — check with individual insurers before billing. These codes sunset after 5 years if not converted to Category I.
