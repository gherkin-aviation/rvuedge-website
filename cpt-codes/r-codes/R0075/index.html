R0075 Transport port x-ray multipl

R Codes

Also known as: portable X-ray transport, multiple views, comprehensive bedside imaging

Transportation of portable X-ray equipment to patient location for multiple radiographic exposures or procedures.

In Plain Language

bedside X-ray series; multi-view imaging; bedside radiology service

Clinical Context

Used when multiple radiographic projections or procedures are required at patient's bedside. Transport includes equipment setup for comprehensive imaging evaluation.

RVU Information

CPT R0075 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

R-codes cover transportation of portable diagnostic equipment. Documentation must include the origin and destination, medical necessity for portable service, and the number of patients served during the transport.
