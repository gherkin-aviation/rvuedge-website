R0070 Transport portable x-ray

R Codes

Also known as: portable X-ray transport, bedside X-ray service, mobile radiography

Transportation of portable X-ray equipment to patient location, including transport and setup.

In Plain Language

bedside X-ray; mobile X-ray service; in-room imaging

Clinical Context

Used for bedridden patients unable to transport to radiology department. Technician transports portable X-ray equipment to patient's location for imaging.

RVU Information

CPT R0070 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

R-codes cover transportation of portable diagnostic equipment. Documentation must include the origin and destination, medical necessity for portable service, and the number of patients served during the transport.
