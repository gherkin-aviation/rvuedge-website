00474 Anesthesia prtl rib rescj radiation procedures

Anesthesia

Also known as: pectus repair anesthesia, radical chest wall anesthesia

Anesthesia for partial rib resection involving radical procedures, such as pectus excavatum repair. Covers general anesthesia for extensive chest wall reconstruction or radical rib procedures.

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for radical chest wall procedures including pectus excavatum repair (Nuss or Ravitch procedure), pectus carinatum correction, or radical rib resection for tumor. Often performed in pediatric/adolescent patients.
