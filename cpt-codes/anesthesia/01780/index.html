01780 Anesthesia procedures vn upper arm & elbow not otherwise specified

Anesthesia

Also known as: upper arm vein surgery anesthesia

Anesthesia for procedures on the veins of the upper arm and elbow not otherwise specified. Covers anesthesia for venous surgical procedures of the upper arm and antecubital fossa region.

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for venous procedures on the upper arm and elbow not covered by 01782. Includes venous access procedures, venous reconstruction, and treatment of upper arm venous pathology.
