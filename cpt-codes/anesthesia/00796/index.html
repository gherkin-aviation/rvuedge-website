00796 Anesthesia iper upper abdominal lvr transplant

Anesthesia

Also known as: liver transplant anesthesia, OLT anesthesia

Anesthesia for intraperitoneal procedures in the upper abdomen involving liver transplant. Covers general anesthesia for orthotopic or living-donor liver transplantation, one of the most complex anesthetic procedures.

In Plain Language

anesthesia for liver transplant surgery; being put to sleep for receiving a new liver

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for liver transplantation, both donor and recipient procedures. Requires management of end-stage liver disease, coagulopathy, hemodynamic instability, metabolic derangements during anhepatic phase, and reperfusion syndrome. Typically 8-12+ hour procedure.

RVU Information

CPT 00796 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Anesthesia codes use a base unit + time unit system rather than standard RVUs. Contact your payer for the anesthesia conversion factor.

Billing & Documentation

Anesthesia codes are billed using base units plus time units. One time unit typically equals 15 minutes of anesthesia time. Document start and stop times, patient status (P1-P6), and any qualifying circumstances. Modifiers AA, QK, QX, or QY indicate the provider arrangement.
