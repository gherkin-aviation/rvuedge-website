01941 Anesthesia neuromd/ntrvrt crv/thrc

Anesthesia

Also known as: spinal cord stimulator anesthesia cervical, SCS implant anesthesia, intrathecal pump anesthesia

Anesthesia for percutaneous placement of neurostimulator or intrathecal drug delivery device in the cervical or thoracic spine. Covers anesthesia for spinal cord stimulator or intrathecal pump placement in the upper spine.

In Plain Language

anesthesia for pain device placement in upper spine; being put to sleep for spinal stimulator or pain pump in neck/mid-back

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for placement of neuromodulation devices (spinal cord stimulators) or intrathecal drug delivery systems in the cervical or thoracic spine. Requires patient cooperation for intraoperative testing during SCS placement, often using MAC or light sedation.

RVU Information

CPT 01941 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Anesthesia codes use a base unit + time unit system rather than standard RVUs. Contact your payer for the anesthesia conversion factor.

Billing & Documentation

Anesthesia codes are billed using base units plus time units. One time unit typically equals 15 minutes of anesthesia time. Document start and stop times, patient status (P1-P6), and any qualifying circumstances. Modifiers AA, QK, QX, or QY indicate the provider arrangement.
