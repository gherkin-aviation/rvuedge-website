00190 Anesthesia procedures facial bone/skull not otherwise specified

Anesthesia

Also known as: facial bone surgery anesthesia, skull surgery anesthesia

Anesthesia for procedures on the facial bones or skull not otherwise specified. Covers general anesthesia for surgical procedures involving the facial skeleton or calvarium that lack a more specific anesthesia code.

In Plain Language

anesthesia for facial bone surgery; being put to sleep for face or skull surgery

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for procedures on the facial bones or skull not covered by more specific codes. Includes open reduction internal fixation of facial fractures, orthognathic surgery, and other craniofacial procedures.

RVU Information

CPT 00190 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Anesthesia codes use a base unit + time unit system rather than standard RVUs. Contact your payer for the anesthesia conversion factor.

Billing & Documentation

Anesthesia codes are billed using base units plus time units. One time unit typically equals 15 minutes of anesthesia time. Document start and stop times, patient status (P1-P6), and any qualifying circumstances. Modifiers AA, QK, QX, or QY indicate the provider arrangement.
