01650 Anesthesia artery sho & ax not otherwise specified

Anesthesia

Also known as: axillary artery surgery anesthesia, shoulder artery anesthesia

Anesthesia for procedures on the arteries of the shoulder and axilla not otherwise specified. Covers anesthesia for surgical procedures on the axillary and subclavian arteries.

In Plain Language

anesthesia for shoulder artery surgery; being put to sleep for armpit artery procedure

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for arterial procedures on the shoulder and axilla not covered by more specific codes (01652-01656). Includes axillary artery repair, subclavian artery procedures, and thoracic outlet decompression with arterial repair.

RVU Information

CPT 01650 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Anesthesia codes use a base unit + time unit system rather than standard RVUs. Contact your payer for the anesthesia conversion factor.

Billing & Documentation

Anesthesia codes are billed using base units plus time units. One time unit typically equals 15 minutes of anesthesia time. Document start and stop times, patient status (P1-P6), and any qualifying circumstances. Modifiers AA, QK, QX, or QY indicate the provider arrangement.
