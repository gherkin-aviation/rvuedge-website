00918 Anesthesia trurl procedures urtrl cal removal

Anesthesia

Also known as: urethral stone removal anesthesia, urethral calculus anesthesia

Anesthesia for transurethral procedures involving urethral calculus removal. Covers anesthesia for endoscopic extraction or lithotripsy of stones lodged in the urethra.

In Plain Language

anesthesia for removing a stone from the urine tube; being put to sleep for urethral stone extraction

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for transurethral removal of urethral calculi, which may have migrated from the bladder or kidney. Often a brief procedure but may require sedation or regional anesthesia for patient comfort and cooperation.

RVU Information

CPT 00918 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Anesthesia codes use a base unit + time unit system rather than standard RVUs. Contact your payer for the anesthesia conversion factor.

Billing & Documentation

Anesthesia codes are billed using base units plus time units. One time unit typically equals 15 minutes of anesthesia time. Document start and stop times, patient status (P1-P6), and any qualifying circumstances. Modifiers AA, QK, QX, or QY indicate the provider arrangement.
