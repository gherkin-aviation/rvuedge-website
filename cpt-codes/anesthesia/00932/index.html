00932 Anesthesia male gent complicated amp pns

Anesthesia

Also known as: total penectomy anesthesia, complete penile amputation anesthesia

Anesthesia for procedures on the male genitalia involving complete amputation of the penis. Covers general anesthesia for total penectomy, typically performed for advanced penile carcinoma.

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for complete penile amputation (total penectomy) for advanced penile squamous cell carcinoma. Requires creation of a perineal urethrostomy. May be combined with inguinal and/or iliac lymph node dissection.
