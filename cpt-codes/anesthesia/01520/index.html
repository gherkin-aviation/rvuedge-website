01520 Anesthesia procedures veins lower leg not otherwise specified

Anesthesia

Also known as: lower leg vein surgery anesthesia

Anesthesia for procedures on the veins of the lower leg not otherwise specified. Covers anesthesia for venous surgical procedures below the knee.

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for venous procedures on the lower leg not covered by 01522. Includes varicose vein procedures, venous stripping, and venous reconstruction below the knee level.
