01210 Anesthesia open procedures hip joint not otherwise specified

Anesthesia

Also known as: open hip surgery anesthesia, hip joint surgery anesthesia

Anesthesia for open procedures involving the hip joint not otherwise specified. Covers general or regional anesthesia for open surgical procedures on the hip joint that lack a more specific code.

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for open hip procedures not covered by 01212-01215. Includes open hip biopsy, synovectomy, removal of loose bodies, and hip debridement. May use neuraxial, peripheral nerve block, or general anesthesia depending on procedure scope.
