01482 Anesthesia opn lower l/a/f radiation rescj

Anesthesia

Also known as: lower leg bone tumor anesthesia, radical ankle/foot bone surgery anesthesia

Anesthesia for open procedures on the bones of the lower leg, ankle, and foot involving radical resection. Covers general anesthesia for radical tumor excision of bones below the knee.

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for radical resection of bone tumors of the lower leg, ankle, or foot. May include wide excision of primary bone tumors (osteosarcoma, Ewing sarcoma) with endoprosthetic or allograft reconstruction, or below-knee amputation for malignancy.
