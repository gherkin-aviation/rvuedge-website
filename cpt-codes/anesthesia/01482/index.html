01482 Anesthesia opn lower l/a/f radiation rescj

Anesthesia

Also known as: lower leg bone tumor anesthesia, radical ankle/foot bone surgery anesthesia

Anesthesia for open procedures on the bones of the lower leg, ankle, and foot involving radical resection. Covers general anesthesia for radical tumor excision of bones below the knee.

In Plain Language

anesthesia for bone tumor removal in lower leg or foot; being put to sleep for cancer surgery on lower leg bone

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for radical resection of bone tumors of the lower leg, ankle, or foot. May include wide excision of primary bone tumors (osteosarcoma, Ewing sarcoma) with endoprosthetic or allograft reconstruction, or below-knee amputation for malignancy.

RVU Information

CPT 01482 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Anesthesia codes use a base unit + time unit system rather than standard RVUs. Contact your payer for the anesthesia conversion factor.

Billing & Documentation

Anesthesia codes are billed using base units plus time units. One time unit typically equals 15 minutes of anesthesia time. Document start and stop times, patient status (P1-P6), and any qualifying circumstances. Modifiers AA, QK, QX, or QY indicate the provider arrangement.
