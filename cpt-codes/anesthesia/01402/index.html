01402 Anesthesia opn/arth tot knee arthrp

Anesthesia

Also known as: total knee replacement anesthesia, TKA anesthesia, knee replacement anesthesia

Anesthesia for open or arthroscopic procedures on the knee joint involving total knee arthroplasty. Covers general or regional anesthesia for primary total knee replacement surgery.

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for primary total knee arthroplasty for osteoarthritis, rheumatoid arthritis, or post-traumatic arthritis. Neuraxial anesthesia (spinal) with peripheral nerve blocks (adductor canal/femoral) is preferred at many centers for improved outcomes and reduced opioid use.
