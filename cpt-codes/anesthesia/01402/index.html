01402 Anesthesia opn/arth tot knee arthrp

Anesthesia

Also known as: total knee replacement anesthesia, TKA anesthesia, knee replacement anesthesia

Anesthesia for open or arthroscopic procedures on the knee joint involving total knee arthroplasty. Covers general or regional anesthesia for primary total knee replacement surgery.

In Plain Language

anesthesia for knee replacement surgery; being put to sleep for new knee joint

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for primary total knee arthroplasty for osteoarthritis, rheumatoid arthritis, or post-traumatic arthritis. Neuraxial anesthesia (spinal) with peripheral nerve blocks (adductor canal/femoral) is preferred at many centers for improved outcomes and reduced opioid use.

RVU Information

CPT 01402 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Anesthesia codes use a base unit + time unit system rather than standard RVUs. Contact your payer for the anesthesia conversion factor.

Billing & Documentation

Anesthesia codes are billed using base units plus time units. One time unit typically equals 15 minutes of anesthesia time. Document start and stop times, patient status (P1-P6), and any qualifying circumstances. Modifiers AA, QK, QX, or QY indicate the provider arrangement.
