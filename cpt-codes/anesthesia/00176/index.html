00176 Anesthesia intraoral procedures radiation surgical

Anesthesia

Also known as: radical oral surgery anesthesia, oral cancer surgery anesthesia

Anesthesia for intraoral procedures involving radical surgery. Covers general anesthesia for extensive intraoral surgical procedures including radical excision of oral tumors, composite resections, and mandibulectomy.

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for radical intraoral surgery, typically for oral cavity malignancies. May include glossectomy, mandibulectomy, composite resection, or floor of mouth excision. Often combined with neck dissection and reconstruction requiring prolonged anesthesia.
