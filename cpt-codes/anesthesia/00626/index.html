00626 Anesthesia thrc spine with 1 lng vent

Anesthesia

Also known as: thoracic spine anterior approach anesthesia, thoracic spine OLV anesthesia

Anesthesia for procedures on the thoracic spine with one-lung ventilation. Covers general anesthesia for thoracic spine surgery requiring lung isolation for an anterior or lateral surgical approach via thoracotomy or VATS.

In Plain Language

anesthesia for mid-back surgery requiring lung collapse; being put to sleep for spine surgery through the chest

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for thoracic spine procedures requiring one-lung ventilation, typically for anterior or lateral approaches via thoracotomy or thoracoscopy. Includes anterior thoracic corpectomy, disc replacement, or deformity correction from an anterior approach.

RVU Information

CPT 00626 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Anesthesia codes use a base unit + time unit system rather than standard RVUs. Contact your payer for the anesthesia conversion factor.

Billing & Documentation

Anesthesia codes are billed using base units plus time units. One time unit typically equals 15 minutes of anesthesia time. Document start and stop times, patient status (P1-P6), and any qualifying circumstances. Modifiers AA, QK, QX, or QY indicate the provider arrangement.
