01716 Anesthesia nerve msc upper a & e tndsis

Anesthesia

Also known as: biceps tenodesis anesthesia, elbow tenodesis anesthesia

Anesthesia for procedures on nerves, muscles, tendons of the upper arm and elbow involving tenodesis. Covers anesthesia for surgical fixation of a tendon to bone at the upper arm or elbow.

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for tenodesis procedures at the upper arm or elbow, most commonly proximal biceps tenodesis. May be performed as a standalone procedure or combined with other shoulder/elbow procedures.
