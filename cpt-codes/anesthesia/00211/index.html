00211 Anesthesia intracranial procedures craniectomy/craniotomy hematoma

Anesthesia

Also known as: hematoma evacuation anesthesia, crani for bleed anesthesia

Anesthesia for intracranial procedures involving craniotomy or craniectomy for evacuation of hematoma. Covers general anesthesia for surgical evacuation of epidural or subdural hematomas via craniotomy.

In Plain Language

anesthesia for brain bleed surgery; being put to sleep for removing blood clot from the brain

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for emergency or urgent craniotomy/craniectomy to evacuate intracranial hematomas. Common in traumatic brain injury with epidural or acute subdural hematomas. Often an emergent procedure requiring rapid induction and careful ICP management.

RVU Information

CPT 00211 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Anesthesia codes use a base unit + time unit system rather than standard RVUs. Contact your payer for the anesthesia conversion factor.

Billing & Documentation

Anesthesia codes are billed using base units plus time units. One time unit typically equals 15 minutes of anesthesia time. Document start and stop times, patient status (P1-P6), and any qualifying circumstances. Modifiers AA, QK, QX, or QY indicate the provider arrangement.
