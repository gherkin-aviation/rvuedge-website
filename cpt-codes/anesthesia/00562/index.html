00562 Anesthesia procedures hrt with pump age 1yr+

Anesthesia

Also known as: cardiac bypass anesthesia, open heart surgery anesthesia, on-pump cardiac anesthesia

Anesthesia for procedures on the heart, pericardial sac, and great vessels of the chest with cardiopulmonary bypass, in patients aged one year or older. Covers general anesthesia for open heart surgery in children and adults.

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for cardiac surgery with cardiopulmonary bypass in patients 1 year and older. Includes valve replacement/repair, ASD/VSD closure, aortic root surgery, and other open heart procedures requiring the heart-lung machine.
