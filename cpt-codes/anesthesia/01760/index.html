01760 Anesthesia opn/arthr elbow tot rplct

Anesthesia

Also known as: total elbow replacement anesthesia, TEA anesthesia, elbow replacement anesthesia

Anesthesia for open or arthroscopic procedures on the elbow involving total elbow replacement. Covers general or regional anesthesia for total elbow arthroplasty.

In Plain Language

anesthesia for elbow replacement surgery; being put to sleep for new elbow joint

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for total elbow arthroplasty for rheumatoid arthritis, post-traumatic arthritis, or distal humerus fractures in elderly patients not amenable to ORIF. Brachial plexus block provides excellent anesthesia and postoperative pain control.

RVU Information

CPT 01760 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Anesthesia codes use a base unit + time unit system rather than standard RVUs. Contact your payer for the anesthesia conversion factor.

Billing & Documentation

Anesthesia codes are billed using base units plus time units. One time unit typically equals 15 minutes of anesthesia time. Document start and stop times, patient status (P1-P6), and any qualifying circumstances. Modifiers AA, QK, QX, or QY indicate the provider arrangement.
