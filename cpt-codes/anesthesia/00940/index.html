00940 Anesthesia vaginal procedures not otherwise specified

Anesthesia

Also known as: vaginal surgery anesthesia, vaginal procedure anesthesia

Anesthesia for vaginal procedures not otherwise specified. Covers general, spinal, or monitored anesthesia care for surgical procedures performed through the vaginal approach.

In Plain Language

anesthesia for vaginal surgery; being put to sleep for vaginal procedure

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for vaginal procedures not covered by more specific codes (00942-00952). Includes vaginal biopsies, excision of vaginal lesions, pelvic floor repair, and other transvaginal surgical procedures.

RVU Information

CPT 00940 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Anesthesia codes use a base unit + time unit system rather than standard RVUs. Contact your payer for the anesthesia conversion factor.

Billing & Documentation

Anesthesia codes are billed using base units plus time units. One time unit typically equals 15 minutes of anesthesia time. Document start and stop times, patient status (P1-P6), and any qualifying circumstances. Modifiers AA, QK, QX, or QY indicate the provider arrangement.
