00539 Anesthesia tracheobronchial rcnstj

Anesthesia

Also known as: tracheal reconstruction anesthesia, airway reconstruction anesthesia

Anesthesia for tracheobronchial reconstruction. Covers general anesthesia for surgical reconstruction of the trachea or bronchi, including tracheal resection and reanastomosis or bronchoplasty.

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for tracheobronchial reconstruction, typically for tracheal stenosis, tracheomalacia, or tracheal tumors. Complex airway management requiring intermittent ventilation, jet ventilation, or cross-field intubation during surgical reconstruction of the airway.
