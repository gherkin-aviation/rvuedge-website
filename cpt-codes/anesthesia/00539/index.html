00539 Anesthesia tracheobronchial rcnstj

Anesthesia

Also known as: tracheal reconstruction anesthesia, airway reconstruction anesthesia

Anesthesia for tracheobronchial reconstruction. Covers general anesthesia for surgical reconstruction of the trachea or bronchi, including tracheal resection and reanastomosis or bronchoplasty.

In Plain Language

anesthesia for windpipe reconstruction surgery; being put to sleep for airway repair

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for tracheobronchial reconstruction, typically for tracheal stenosis, tracheomalacia, or tracheal tumors. Complex airway management requiring intermittent ventilation, jet ventilation, or cross-field intubation during surgical reconstruction of the airway.

RVU Information

CPT 00539 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Anesthesia codes use a base unit + time unit system rather than standard RVUs. Contact your payer for the anesthesia conversion factor.

Billing & Documentation

Anesthesia codes are billed using base units plus time units. One time unit typically equals 15 minutes of anesthesia time. Document start and stop times, patient status (P1-P6), and any qualifying circumstances. Modifiers AA, QK, QX, or QY indicate the provider arrangement.
