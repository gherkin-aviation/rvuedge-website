00732 Anesthesia upper gi ndsc procedures ercp

Anesthesia

Also known as: ERCP anesthesia, bile duct scope anesthesia

Anesthesia for upper gastrointestinal endoscopic procedures involving endoscopic retrograde cholangiopancreatography (ERCP). Covers general anesthesia or deep sedation for this specialized biliary and pancreatic endoscopic procedure.

In Plain Language

anesthesia for bile duct scope procedure; being put to sleep for pancreas and bile duct examination

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for ERCP, a specialized endoscopic procedure for diagnosis and treatment of biliary and pancreatic disorders. Typically performed in prone or left lateral position. Indicated for choledocholithiasis, biliary strictures, and pancreatic duct pathology.

RVU Information

CPT 00732 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Anesthesia codes use a base unit + time unit system rather than standard RVUs. Contact your payer for the anesthesia conversion factor.

Billing & Documentation

Anesthesia codes are billed using base units plus time units. One time unit typically equals 15 minutes of anesthesia time. Document start and stop times, patient status (P1-P6), and any qualifying circumstances. Modifiers AA, QK, QX, or QY indicate the provider arrangement.
