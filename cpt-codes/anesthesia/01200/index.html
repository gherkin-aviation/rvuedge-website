01200 Anesthesia all closed procedures hip joint

Anesthesia

Also known as: closed hip procedure anesthesia, hip reduction anesthesia

Anesthesia for all closed procedures involving the hip joint. Covers anesthesia for non-operative management of hip pathology including closed reduction of hip dislocations and application of traction.

In Plain Language

anesthesia for hip joint manipulation without cutting; being put to sleep for hip joint procedure without surgery

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for closed hip joint procedures including closed reduction of hip dislocations, application of skeletal traction, and manipulation under anesthesia. Hip dislocations are orthopedic emergencies requiring prompt reduction within 6 hours.

RVU Information

CPT 01200 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Anesthesia codes use a base unit + time unit system rather than standard RVUs. Contact your payer for the anesthesia conversion factor.

Billing & Documentation

Anesthesia codes are billed using base units plus time units. One time unit typically equals 15 minutes of anesthesia time. Document start and stop times, patient status (P1-P6), and any qualifying circumstances. Modifiers AA, QK, QX, or QY indicate the provider arrangement.
