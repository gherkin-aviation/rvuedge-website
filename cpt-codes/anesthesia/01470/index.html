01470 Anesthesia procedures nerve msc lw l/a/f not otherwise specified

Anesthesia

Also known as: lower leg soft tissue anesthesia, ankle tendon surgery anesthesia

Anesthesia for procedures on nerves, muscles, tendons, and fascia of the lower leg, ankle, and foot not otherwise specified. Covers anesthesia for soft tissue surgical procedures of the lower extremity below the knee.

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for soft tissue procedures of the lower leg, ankle, and foot not covered by 01472 or 01474. Includes fasciotomy for compartment syndrome, tendon repair, and nerve procedures. Regional anesthesia with popliteal and saphenous blocks is commonly used.
