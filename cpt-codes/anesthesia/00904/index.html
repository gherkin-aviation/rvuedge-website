00904 Anesthesia radical perineal procedures

Anesthesia

Also known as: radical perineal surgery anesthesia, perineal cancer surgery anesthesia

Anesthesia for radical perineal procedures. Covers general anesthesia for extensive perineal surgical procedures including radical perineal prostatectomy and wide local excision of perineal malignancies.

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for radical perineal procedures, typically for perineal malignancies or radical perineal prostatectomy. These procedures are more extensive than routine anorectal surgery and may require lithotomy positioning with associated hemodynamic and positioning considerations.
