00865 Anesthesia xtrprt lower abdominal prst8ect

Anesthesia

Also known as: radical prostatectomy anesthesia, prostate cancer surgery anesthesia

Anesthesia for extraperitoneal procedures in the lower abdomen involving radical prostatectomy. Covers general anesthesia for surgical removal of the prostate gland and surrounding tissue for prostate cancer, via retropubic or perineal approach.

In Plain Language

anesthesia for prostate cancer removal; being put to sleep for prostate removal surgery

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for radical prostatectomy including open retropubic, perineal, laparoscopic, or robot-assisted approaches. Nerve-sparing techniques are common. Requires steep Trendelenburg positioning for robotic approach with attention to ocular and airway complications.

RVU Information

CPT 00865 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Anesthesia codes use a base unit + time unit system rather than standard RVUs. Contact your payer for the anesthesia conversion factor.

Billing & Documentation

Anesthesia codes are billed using base units plus time units. One time unit typically equals 15 minutes of anesthesia time. Document start and stop times, patient status (P1-P6), and any qualifying circumstances. Modifiers AA, QK, QX, or QY indicate the provider arrangement.
