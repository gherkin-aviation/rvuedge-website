00140 Anesthesia procedures on eye not otherwise specified

Anesthesia

Also known as: eye surgery anesthesia, ophthalmic anesthesia

Anesthesia for procedures on the eye not otherwise specified. Covers general anesthesia or monitored anesthesia care for ophthalmic procedures that do not have a more specific anesthesia code assigned.

In Plain Language

anesthesia for eye surgery; being put to sleep for eye procedure

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for ophthalmic procedures that are not covered by more specific anesthesia codes (00142-00148). Includes strabismus surgery, enucleation, evisceration, orbital procedures, and other miscellaneous eye surgeries.

RVU Information

CPT 00140 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Anesthesia codes use a base unit + time unit system rather than standard RVUs. Contact your payer for the anesthesia conversion factor.

Billing & Documentation

Anesthesia codes are billed using base units plus time units. One time unit typically equals 15 minutes of anesthesia time. Document start and stop times, patient status (P1-P6), and any qualifying circumstances. Modifiers AA, QK, QX, or QY indicate the provider arrangement.
