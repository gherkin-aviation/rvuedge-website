00140 Anesthesia procedures on eye not otherwise specified

Anesthesia

Also known as: eye surgery anesthesia, ophthalmic anesthesia

Anesthesia for procedures on the eye not otherwise specified. Covers general anesthesia or monitored anesthesia care for ophthalmic procedures that do not have a more specific anesthesia code assigned.

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for ophthalmic procedures that are not covered by more specific anesthesia codes (00142-00148). Includes strabismus surgery, enucleation, evisceration, orbital procedures, and other miscellaneous eye surgeries.
