00540 Anesthesia thoracotomy procedures not otherwise specified

Anesthesia

Also known as: thoracotomy anesthesia, open chest surgery anesthesia

Anesthesia for thoracotomy procedures not otherwise specified. Covers general anesthesia for open surgical procedures through the chest wall that do not have a more specific thoracotomy anesthesia code.

In Plain Language

anesthesia for open chest surgery; being put to sleep for surgery through the chest wall

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for thoracotomy procedures not covered by more specific codes (00541-00548). Includes exploratory thoracotomy, chest wall tumor resection, and other open chest procedures. May require one-lung ventilation and thoracic epidural for pain management.

RVU Information

CPT 00540 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Anesthesia codes use a base unit + time unit system rather than standard RVUs. Contact your payer for the anesthesia conversion factor.

Billing & Documentation

Anesthesia codes are billed using base units plus time units. One time unit typically equals 15 minutes of anesthesia time. Document start and stop times, patient status (P1-P6), and any qualifying circumstances. Modifiers AA, QK, QX, or QY indicate the provider arrangement.
