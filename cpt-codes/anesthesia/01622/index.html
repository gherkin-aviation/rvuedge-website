01622 Anesthesia diagnosis arthr procedures shoulder joint

Anesthesia

Also known as: shoulder scope anesthesia, diagnostic shoulder arthroscopy anesthesia

Anesthesia for diagnostic arthroscopic procedures of the shoulder joint. Covers anesthesia for diagnostic shoulder arthroscopy to evaluate intra-articular shoulder pathology.

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for diagnostic shoulder arthroscopy. Often the arthroscopy becomes therapeutic (converted to 01630) once pathology is confirmed. Interscalene nerve block provides excellent surgical anesthesia and postoperative pain control. Beach chair or lateral decubitus positioning.
