01622 Anesthesia diagnosis arthr procedures shoulder joint

Anesthesia

Also known as: shoulder scope anesthesia, diagnostic shoulder arthroscopy anesthesia

Anesthesia for diagnostic arthroscopic procedures of the shoulder joint. Covers anesthesia for diagnostic shoulder arthroscopy to evaluate intra-articular shoulder pathology.

In Plain Language

anesthesia for shoulder camera procedure; being put to sleep for shoulder scope

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for diagnostic shoulder arthroscopy. Often the arthroscopy becomes therapeutic (converted to 01630) once pathology is confirmed. Interscalene nerve block provides excellent surgical anesthesia and postoperative pain control. Beach chair or lateral decubitus positioning.

RVU Information

CPT 01622 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Anesthesia codes use a base unit + time unit system rather than standard RVUs. Contact your payer for the anesthesia conversion factor.

Billing & Documentation

Anesthesia codes are billed using base units plus time units. One time unit typically equals 15 minutes of anesthesia time. Document start and stop times, patient status (P1-P6), and any qualifying circumstances. Modifiers AA, QK, QX, or QY indicate the provider arrangement.
