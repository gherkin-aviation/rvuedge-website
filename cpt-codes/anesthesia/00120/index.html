00120 Anesthesia procedures ear with biopsy not otherwise specified

Anesthesia

Also known as: ear surgery anesthesia, ear biopsy anesthesia

Anesthesia for procedures on the external, middle, or inner ear not otherwise specified, including biopsy. Covers general anesthesia for ear procedures that do not have a more specific anesthesia code.

In Plain Language

anesthesia for ear surgery; being put to sleep for ear procedure

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for ear procedures including biopsies of the ear that are not covered by more specific codes (00124, 00126). Common indications include excision of ear lesions, ear reconstruction, or diagnostic biopsies.

RVU Information

CPT 00120 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Anesthesia codes use a base unit + time unit system rather than standard RVUs. Contact your payer for the anesthesia conversion factor.

Billing & Documentation

Anesthesia codes are billed using base units plus time units. One time unit typically equals 15 minutes of anesthesia time. Document start and stop times, patient status (P1-P6), and any qualifying circumstances. Modifiers AA, QK, QX, or QY indicate the provider arrangement.

Specialties

anesthesiology
