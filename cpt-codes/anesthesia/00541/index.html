00541 Anesthesia thrcm procedures 1 lung vntj

Anesthesia

Also known as: thoracotomy OLV anesthesia, one-lung ventilation thoracotomy

Anesthesia for thoracotomy procedures utilizing one-lung ventilation. Covers general anesthesia requiring lung isolation with a double-lumen endotracheal tube or bronchial blocker for open chest surgery.

In Plain Language

anesthesia for open chest surgery with one lung breathing; being put to sleep for chest surgery requiring lung collapse

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for thoracotomy procedures requiring one-lung ventilation for surgical exposure. Requires expertise in lung isolation techniques, management of hypoxemia during OLV, and protective ventilation strategies for the ventilated lung.

RVU Information

CPT 00541 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Anesthesia codes use a base unit + time unit system rather than standard RVUs. Contact your payer for the anesthesia conversion factor.

Billing & Documentation

Anesthesia codes are billed using base units plus time units. One time unit typically equals 15 minutes of anesthesia time. Document start and stop times, patient status (P1-P6), and any qualifying circumstances. Modifiers AA, QK, QX, or QY indicate the provider arrangement.
