01380 Anesthesia all clsd procedures knee joint

Anesthesia

Also known as: closed knee procedure anesthesia, knee manipulation anesthesia, knee MUA anesthesia

Anesthesia for all closed procedures on the knee joint. Covers anesthesia for non-operative knee joint procedures including closed reduction of patellar dislocations, knee manipulation under anesthesia, and aspiration.

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for closed knee joint procedures including manipulation under anesthesia for stiff knee after arthroplasty or ACL reconstruction, closed reduction of knee dislocation, and joint aspiration under anesthesia. May use regional or general anesthesia.
