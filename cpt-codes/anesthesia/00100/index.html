00100 Anesthesia procedures salivary gland with biopsy

Anesthesia

Also known as: salivary gland anesthesia, parotid surgery anesthesia

Anesthesia for procedures on the salivary glands, including excision of salivary gland with or without biopsy. Covers general anesthesia administration and monitoring for surgical procedures involving the parotid, submandibular, or sublingual glands.

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for excision or biopsy of salivary glands, most commonly the parotid gland. Indicated for conditions such as salivary gland tumors (pleomorphic adenomas, Warthin tumors), sialolithiasis, or chronic sialadenitis.
