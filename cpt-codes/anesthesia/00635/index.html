00635 Anesthesia diagnostic/therapeutic lumbar puncture

Anesthesia

Also known as: lumbar puncture anesthesia, spinal tap anesthesia, LP anesthesia

Anesthesia for diagnostic or therapeutic lumbar puncture. Covers anesthesia or sedation for spinal tap performed for diagnostic cerebrospinal fluid analysis or therapeutic CSF drainage.

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for lumbar puncture, particularly in pediatric patients, anxious adults, or patients requiring sedation for cooperation. Diagnostic LPs evaluate for meningitis, subarachnoid hemorrhage, or CSF abnormalities. Therapeutic LPs treat idiopathic intracranial hypertension.
