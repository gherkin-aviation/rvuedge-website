00802 Anesthesia procedures lower abdl wal pannic

Anesthesia

Also known as: panniculectomy anesthesia, tummy tuck anesthesia, abdominoplasty anesthesia

Anesthesia for procedures on the lower anterior abdominal wall involving panniculectomy. Covers general anesthesia for surgical removal of the pannus (hanging abdominal skin and fat apron), often after massive weight loss.

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for panniculectomy, removal of excess abdominal skin and subcutaneous tissue. Commonly performed after massive weight loss following bariatric surgery, or for functional panniculectomy due to recurrent skin infections, limitations in hygiene, or mobility restriction.
