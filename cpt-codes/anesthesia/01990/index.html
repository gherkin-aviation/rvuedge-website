All Codes / Anesthesia / 01990

01990 Physiol support hrvg organs

Anesthesia

Also known as: organ harvest anesthesia, donor management anesthesia, organ procurement anesthesia

Physiological support for harvesting of organs from a brain-dead donor. Covers anesthesia services for maintaining hemodynamic stability, ventilation, and organ perfusion during organ procurement surgery from a deceased donor.

In Plain Language

anesthesia support for organ donation surgery; managing a donor during organ removal for transplant

Clinical Context

Used for providing physiological support during organ procurement from brain-dead donors. Requires management of hemodynamic instability, diabetes insipidus, hypothermia, coagulopathy, and hormonal derangements common in brain death. Critical role in optimizing organ quality for transplantation.

RVU Information

CPT 01990 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Anesthesia codes use a base unit + time unit system rather than standard RVUs. Contact your payer for the anesthesia conversion factor.

Billing & Documentation

Anesthesia codes are billed using base units plus time units. One time unit typically equals 15 minutes of anesthesia time. Document start and stop times, patient status (P1-P6), and any qualifying circumstances. Modifiers AA, QK, QX, or QY indicate the provider arrangement.
