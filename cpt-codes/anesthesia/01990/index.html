01990 Physiol support hrvg organs

Anesthesia

Also known as: organ harvest anesthesia, donor management anesthesia, organ procurement anesthesia

Physiological support for harvesting of organs from a brain-dead donor. Covers anesthesia services for maintaining hemodynamic stability, ventilation, and organ perfusion during organ procurement surgery from a deceased donor.

Clinical Context

Used for providing physiological support during organ procurement from brain-dead donors. Requires management of hemodynamic instability, diabetes insipidus, hypothermia, coagulopathy, and hormonal derangements common in brain death. Critical role in optimizing organ quality for transplantation.
