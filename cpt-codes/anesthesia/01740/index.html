01740 Anesthesia opn/arthrs procedures elbow not otherwise specified

Anesthesia

Also known as: elbow surgery anesthesia, open elbow anesthesia

Anesthesia for open or arthroscopic procedures on the elbow not otherwise specified. Covers general or regional anesthesia for therapeutic elbow surgery.

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for open or arthroscopic elbow procedures not covered by more specific codes (01742-01760). Includes arthroscopic debridement, loose body removal, lateral epicondyle release, and open elbow procedures. Brachial plexus block is the primary regional technique.
