00812 Anesthesia lower intst scr colsc

Anesthesia

Also known as: screening colonoscopy anesthesia, screening scope anesthesia

Anesthesia for lower intestinal endoscopic procedures involving screening colonoscopy. Covers anesthesia specifically for screening colonoscopies performed for colorectal cancer prevention in average-risk or high-risk patients.

In Plain Language

anesthesia for cancer screening colonoscopy; being put to sleep for routine colon cancer screening

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia specifically for screening colonoscopy, distinguished from diagnostic colonoscopy (00811). Screening colonoscopy is typically performed starting at age 45 for average-risk patients and earlier for high-risk populations. Important for coding and reimbursement differences.

RVU Information

CPT 00812 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Anesthesia codes use a base unit + time unit system rather than standard RVUs. Contact your payer for the anesthesia conversion factor.

Billing & Documentation

Anesthesia codes are billed using base units plus time units. One time unit typically equals 15 minutes of anesthesia time. Document start and stop times, patient status (P1-P6), and any qualifying circumstances. Modifiers AA, QK, QX, or QY indicate the provider arrangement.
