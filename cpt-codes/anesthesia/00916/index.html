00916 Anesthesia trurl procedures rescj bleeding

Anesthesia

Also known as: post-TURP bleeding anesthesia, TUR hemorrhage anesthesia

Anesthesia for transurethral procedures involving post-transurethral resection bleeding. Covers anesthesia for emergency or urgent endoscopic management of hemorrhage following a previous transurethral procedure.

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for management of post-transurethral resection bleeding, a surgical emergency. Patients may present with hemodynamic instability, bladder clot retention, and potential TUR syndrome. Requires emergent anesthesia with full stomach precautions and blood product availability.
