01404 Anesthesia opn/arthrs disrtcj knee

Anesthesia

Also known as: knee disarticulation anesthesia, through-knee amputation anesthesia

Anesthesia for open or arthroscopic procedures on the knee joint involving disarticulation at the knee. Covers general anesthesia for amputation through the knee joint (through-knee amputation).

In Plain Language

anesthesia for amputation at the knee; being put to sleep for knee-level leg removal

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for knee disarticulation (through-knee amputation), an alternative to above- or below-knee amputation. Indications include peripheral vascular disease, trauma, and malignancy. Provides a longer lever arm than BKA for prosthetic fitting.

RVU Information

CPT 01404 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Anesthesia codes use a base unit + time unit system rather than standard RVUs. Contact your payer for the anesthesia conversion factor.

Billing & Documentation

Anesthesia codes are billed using base units plus time units. One time unit typically equals 15 minutes of anesthesia time. Document start and stop times, patient status (P1-P6), and any qualifying circumstances. Modifiers AA, QK, QX, or QY indicate the provider arrangement.
