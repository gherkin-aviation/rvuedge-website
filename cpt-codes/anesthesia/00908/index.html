00908 Anesthesia perineal prostatectomy

Anesthesia

Also known as: perineal prostatectomy anesthesia, perineal approach prostate anesthesia

Anesthesia for perineal prostatectomy. Covers general or regional anesthesia for surgical removal of the prostate gland through a perineal approach, typically in the exaggerated lithotomy position.

In Plain Language

anesthesia for prostate removal through the perineum; being put to sleep for prostate surgery from below

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for perineal prostatectomy, either radical for prostate cancer or simple for benign prostatic hyperplasia. Performed in exaggerated lithotomy position with associated risks of compartment syndrome, neuropathy, and hemodynamic changes from positioning.

RVU Information

CPT 00908 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Anesthesia codes use a base unit + time unit system rather than standard RVUs. Contact your payer for the anesthesia conversion factor.

Billing & Documentation

Anesthesia codes are billed using base units plus time units. One time unit typically equals 15 minutes of anesthesia time. Document start and stop times, patient status (P1-P6), and any qualifying circumstances. Modifiers AA, QK, QX, or QY indicate the provider arrangement.
