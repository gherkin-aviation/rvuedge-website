01472 Anesthesia repair report achilles tendon

Anesthesia

Also known as: Achilles repair anesthesia, Achilles tendon surgery anesthesia

Anesthesia for repair of ruptured Achilles tendon with or without graft. Covers general or regional anesthesia for surgical repair of a complete Achilles tendon rupture.

In Plain Language

anesthesia for torn Achilles tendon repair; being put to sleep for heel tendon surgery

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for open or percutaneous repair of a ruptured Achilles tendon. Common sports injury in adults aged 30-50. Prone positioning required for posterior approach. Popliteal sciatic nerve block provides excellent surgical anesthesia and postoperative pain control.

RVU Information

CPT 01472 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Anesthesia codes use a base unit + time unit system rather than standard RVUs. Contact your payer for the anesthesia conversion factor.

Billing & Documentation

Anesthesia codes are billed using base units plus time units. One time unit typically equals 15 minutes of anesthesia time. Document start and stop times, patient status (P1-P6), and any qualifying circumstances. Modifiers AA, QK, QX, or QY indicate the provider arrangement.
