01942 Anesthesia neuromd/ntrvrt lumbar/sacral

Anesthesia

Also known as: spinal cord stimulator anesthesia lumbar, SCS implant anesthesia lumbar, pain pump anesthesia

Anesthesia for percutaneous placement of neurostimulator or intrathecal drug delivery device in the lumbar or sacral spine. Covers anesthesia for spinal cord stimulator or intrathecal pump placement in the lower spine.

In Plain Language

anesthesia for pain device placement in lower back; being put to sleep for spinal stimulator or pain pump in lower back

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for placement of neuromodulation devices or intrathecal drug delivery systems in the lumbar or sacral spine. Most common location for SCS lead placement. Requires intraoperative paresthesia mapping during trial or permanent lead placement.

RVU Information

CPT 01942 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Anesthesia codes use a base unit + time unit system rather than standard RVUs. Contact your payer for the anesthesia conversion factor.

Billing & Documentation

Anesthesia codes are billed using base units plus time units. One time unit typically equals 15 minutes of anesthesia time. Document start and stop times, patient status (P1-P6), and any qualifying circumstances. Modifiers AA, QK, QX, or QY indicate the provider arrangement.
