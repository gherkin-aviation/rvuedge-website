01966 Anesthesia induced abortion procedures

Anesthesia

Also known as: induced abortion anesthesia, elective termination anesthesia

Anesthesia for induced abortion procedures. Covers anesthesia for elective termination of pregnancy by surgical or pharmacologic methods requiring anesthetic support.

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for induced abortion procedures including surgical termination by suction curettage, dilation and evacuation (D&E), and labor induction termination. Anesthetic approach depends on gestational age and surgical technique.
