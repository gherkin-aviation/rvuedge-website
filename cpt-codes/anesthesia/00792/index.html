00792 Anesthesia iper upper abdominal prtl hptc

Anesthesia

Also known as: liver resection anesthesia, hepatectomy anesthesia, liver surgery anesthesia

Anesthesia for intraperitoneal procedures in the upper abdomen involving partial hepatectomy or management of liver hemorrhage. Covers general anesthesia for liver resection or control of hepatic bleeding.

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for partial hepatectomy (lobectomy, segmentectomy) or management of liver hemorrhage from trauma. Requires preparation for significant blood loss, coagulopathy management, and hemodynamic monitoring. Low CVP anesthesia technique often employed.
