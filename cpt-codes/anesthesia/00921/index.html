00921 Anesthesia procedures male genitalia vasec

Anesthesia

Also known as: vasectomy anesthesia, male sterilization anesthesia

Anesthesia for procedures on the male genitalia involving vasectomy. Covers anesthesia or sedation for surgical division or occlusion of the vas deferens for male sterilization.

In Plain Language

anesthesia for vasectomy; sedation for male sterilization

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for vasectomy. Most vasectomies are performed under local anesthesia in an office setting, but some patients require additional sedation or general anesthesia due to anxiety, failed local anesthesia, or complex anatomy.

RVU Information

CPT 00921 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Anesthesia codes use a base unit + time unit system rather than standard RVUs. Contact your payer for the anesthesia conversion factor.

Billing & Documentation

Anesthesia codes are billed using base units plus time units. One time unit typically equals 15 minutes of anesthesia time. Document start and stop times, patient status (P1-P6), and any qualifying circumstances. Modifiers AA, QK, QX, or QY indicate the provider arrangement.
