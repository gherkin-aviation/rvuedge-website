00882 Anesthesia maj lower adb vsl ivc ligament

Anesthesia

Also known as: IVC ligation anesthesia, IVC filter surgery anesthesia

Anesthesia for procedures on major lower abdominal vessels involving inferior vena cava ligation. Covers general anesthesia for surgical ligation or clip placement on the inferior vena cava.

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for inferior vena cava ligation, plication, or clip application. Historically performed for recurrent pulmonary embolism when anticoagulation is contraindicated. Now largely replaced by percutaneous IVC filter placement, but still indicated in select cases.
