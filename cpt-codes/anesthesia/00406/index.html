00406 Anesthesia integ sys radiation brst dsj

Anesthesia

Also known as: extended radical mastectomy anesthesia, Urban mastectomy anesthesia

Anesthesia for procedures on the integumentary system involving radical mastectomy with internal mammary node dissection. Covers general anesthesia for the most extensive form of breast cancer surgery including parasternal lymph node sampling.

In Plain Language

anesthesia for extensive breast cancer surgery; being put to sleep for major breast and lymph node removal

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for radical mastectomy with internal mammary node dissection (extended radical or Urban mastectomy). This is the most extensive breast cancer operation with highest anesthetic complexity, potential for pneumothorax, and significant blood loss risk.

RVU Information

CPT 00406 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Anesthesia codes use a base unit + time unit system rather than standard RVUs. Contact your payer for the anesthesia conversion factor.

Billing & Documentation

Anesthesia codes are billed using base units plus time units. One time unit typically equals 15 minutes of anesthesia time. Document start and stop times, patient status (P1-P6), and any qualifying circumstances. Modifiers AA, QK, QX, or QY indicate the provider arrangement.
