00567 Anesthesia direct cabg with pump

Anesthesia

Also known as: on-pump CABG anesthesia, CABG anesthesia, coronary bypass anesthesia

Anesthesia for direct coronary artery bypass grafting with cardiopulmonary bypass (on-pump CABG). Covers general anesthesia for traditional coronary bypass surgery using the heart-lung machine.

In Plain Language

anesthesia for heart bypass surgery; being put to sleep for coronary bypass surgery with heart-lung machine

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for coronary artery bypass grafting with cardiopulmonary bypass. The most common cardiac surgical procedure. Requires invasive monitoring, TEE, management of cardiopulmonary bypass, and careful post-bypass hemodynamic management.

RVU Information

CPT 00567 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Anesthesia codes use a base unit + time unit system rather than standard RVUs. Contact your payer for the anesthesia conversion factor.

Billing & Documentation

Anesthesia codes are billed using base units plus time units. One time unit typically equals 15 minutes of anesthesia time. Document start and stop times, patient status (P1-P6), and any qualifying circumstances. Modifiers AA, QK, QX, or QY indicate the provider arrangement.
