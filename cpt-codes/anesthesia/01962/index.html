01962 Anesthesia urgent hysterectomy

Anesthesia

Also known as: emergency postpartum hysterectomy anesthesia, peripartum hysterectomy anesthesia

Anesthesia for urgent hysterectomy following delivery. Covers anesthesia for emergency postpartum hysterectomy performed for life-threatening hemorrhage, uterine rupture, or other obstetric emergencies.

In Plain Language

anesthesia for emergency uterus removal after delivery; being put to sleep for emergency hysterectomy after childbirth

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for urgent hysterectomy following delivery for obstetric emergencies including placenta accreta/percreta, uterine atony unresponsive to medical management, uterine rupture, or DIC. One of the most critical obstetric anesthesia emergencies with massive hemorrhage potential.

RVU Information

CPT 01962 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Anesthesia codes use a base unit + time unit system rather than standard RVUs. Contact your payer for the anesthesia conversion factor.

Billing & Documentation

Anesthesia codes are billed using base units plus time units. One time unit typically equals 15 minutes of anesthesia time. Document start and stop times, patient status (P1-P6), and any qualifying circumstances. Modifiers AA, QK, QX, or QY indicate the provider arrangement.
