00800 Anesthesia procedures lower anterior abdl wal not otherwise specified

Anesthesia

Also known as: lower abdominal anesthesia, lower belly surgery anesthesia

Anesthesia for procedures on the lower anterior abdominal wall not otherwise specified. Covers general anesthesia for surgical procedures involving the lower anterior abdominal wall below the umbilicus.

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for lower anterior abdominal wall procedures not covered by 00802. Includes lower midline laparotomy for gynecologic, urologic, or colorectal procedures, appendectomy, and lower abdominal wall exploration.
