01270 Anesthesia procedures arteries upper leg not otherwise specified

Anesthesia

Also known as: femoral artery surgery anesthesia, upper leg arterial anesthesia

Anesthesia for procedures on the arteries of the upper leg not otherwise specified. Covers general or regional anesthesia for arterial surgical procedures of the thigh, including bypass and repair.

In Plain Language

anesthesia for thigh artery surgery; being put to sleep for upper leg artery procedure

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for arterial procedures on the upper leg not covered by 01272 or 01274. Includes femoral-popliteal bypass, femoral artery patch angioplasty, and arterial reconstruction for peripheral vascular disease.

RVU Information

CPT 01270 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Anesthesia codes use a base unit + time unit system rather than standard RVUs. Contact your payer for the anesthesia conversion factor.

Billing & Documentation

Anesthesia codes are billed using base units plus time units. One time unit typically equals 15 minutes of anesthesia time. Document start and stop times, patient status (P1-P6), and any qualifying circumstances. Modifiers AA, QK, QX, or QY indicate the provider arrangement.
