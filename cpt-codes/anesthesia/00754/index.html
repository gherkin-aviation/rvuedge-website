00754 Anesthesia hernia repair omphalocele

Anesthesia

Also known as: omphalocele repair anesthesia, abdominal wall defect anesthesia

Anesthesia for hernia repair involving omphalocele. Covers general anesthesia for surgical repair of omphalocele, a congenital abdominal wall defect where intestinal contents protrude through the umbilical ring covered by a membrane.

In Plain Language

anesthesia for belly button birth defect repair; putting a baby under for belly wall repair

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for omphalocele repair in neonates. Requires specialized neonatal anesthetic management with attention to temperature regulation, fluid management, and potential for increased abdominal pressure after closure. May be associated with other congenital anomalies (Beckwith-Wiedemann, pentalogy of Cantrell).

RVU Information

CPT 00754 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Anesthesia codes use a base unit + time unit system rather than standard RVUs. Contact your payer for the anesthesia conversion factor.

Billing & Documentation

Anesthesia codes are billed using base units plus time units. One time unit typically equals 15 minutes of anesthesia time. Document start and stop times, patient status (P1-P6), and any qualifying circumstances. Modifiers AA, QK, QX, or QY indicate the provider arrangement.
