00500 Anesthesia all procedures on esophagus

Anesthesia

Also known as: esophageal surgery anesthesia, esophagectomy anesthesia

Anesthesia for all procedures on the esophagus. Covers general anesthesia for surgical procedures involving the esophagus, including esophagectomy, esophagomyotomy, and esophageal repair.

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for esophageal procedures including esophagectomy, Heller myotomy, esophageal diverticulectomy, repair of esophageal perforation, and antireflux procedures. Often requires one-lung ventilation and has significant hemodynamic and fluid management implications.
