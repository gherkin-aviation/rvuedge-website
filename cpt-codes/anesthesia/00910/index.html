00910 Anesthesia transurethral procedures not otherwise specified

Anesthesia

Also known as: transurethral surgery anesthesia, cystoscopy anesthesia, endoscopic urinary anesthesia

Anesthesia for transurethral procedures not otherwise specified. Covers general, spinal, or monitored anesthesia care for endoscopic procedures performed through the urethra.

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for transurethral procedures not covered by more specific codes (00912-00918). Includes diagnostic cystoscopy, ureteroscopy, and other endoscopic urologic procedures. May be performed under general, spinal, or sedation depending on complexity.
