00750 Anesthesia hernia repair upper abdominal not otherwise specified

Anesthesia

Also known as: hernia repair anesthesia upper, upper abdominal hernia anesthesia

Anesthesia for hernia repair in the upper abdomen not otherwise specified. Covers general anesthesia for surgical repair of upper abdominal hernias that lack a more specific anesthesia code.

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for upper abdominal hernia repairs not covered by more specific codes (00752-00756). Includes epigastric hernia repair, paraesophageal hernia repair, and hiatal hernia repair. May involve laparoscopic or open approach.
