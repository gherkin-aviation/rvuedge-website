00103 Anesthesia reconstructive procedures eyelid

Anesthesia

Also known as: eyelid surgery anesthesia, blepharoplasty anesthesia

Anesthesia for reconstructive procedures of the eyelid, including blepharoplasty, ptosis repair, and other eyelid reconstructive surgeries. Covers general anesthesia or monitored anesthesia care for these procedures.

In Plain Language

anesthesia for eyelid repair surgery; being put to sleep for eyelid surgery

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for reconstructive eyelid procedures such as blepharoplasty, ptosis repair, ectropion/entropion repair, or eyelid tumor excision with reconstruction. Requires consideration of oculocardiac reflex.

RVU Information

CPT 00103 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Anesthesia codes use a base unit + time unit system rather than standard RVUs. Contact your payer for the anesthesia conversion factor.

Billing & Documentation

Anesthesia codes are billed using base units plus time units. One time unit typically equals 15 minutes of anesthesia time. Document start and stop times, patient status (P1-P6), and any qualifying circumstances. Modifiers AA, QK, QX, or QY indicate the provider arrangement.

Specialties
