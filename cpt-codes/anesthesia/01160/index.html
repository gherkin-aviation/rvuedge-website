01160 Anesthesia clsd procedures symph pub/si joint

Anesthesia

Also known as: pelvic ring closed procedure anesthesia, SI joint closed anesthesia

Anesthesia for closed procedures on the symphysis pubis or sacroiliac joint. Covers anesthesia for non-operative management of pelvic ring injuries including closed reduction and external fixation.

In Plain Language

anesthesia for closed pelvic joint procedure; being put to sleep for pelvic manipulation without surgery

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for closed procedures on the pubic symphysis or sacroiliac joint, including closed reduction of pelvic ring disruptions and application of external fixation for pelvic fractures. Often emergent in trauma settings with associated hemorrhage.

RVU Information

CPT 01160 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Anesthesia codes use a base unit + time unit system rather than standard RVUs. Contact your payer for the anesthesia conversion factor.

Billing & Documentation

Anesthesia codes are billed using base units plus time units. One time unit typically equals 15 minutes of anesthesia time. Document start and stop times, patient status (P1-P6), and any qualifying circumstances. Modifiers AA, QK, QX, or QY indicate the provider arrangement.
