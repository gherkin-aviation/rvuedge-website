00148 Anesthesia procedures eye ophthalmoscopy

Anesthesia

Also known as: EUA eye anesthesia, retina exam under anesthesia

Anesthesia for procedures on the eye involving ophthalmoscopy. Covers anesthesia for examination of the retina and posterior segment under anesthesia using an ophthalmoscope, typically in pediatric patients.

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for ophthalmoscopic examination, most commonly examination under anesthesia (EUA) in pediatric patients for retinoblastoma screening, retinopathy of prematurity assessment, or evaluation of pediatric glaucoma.
