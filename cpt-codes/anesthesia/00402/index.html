00402 Anesthesia integ sys reconstructive breast

Anesthesia

Also known as: breast reconstruction anesthesia, breast augmentation anesthesia, breast reduction anesthesia

Anesthesia for procedures on the integumentary system involving reconstructive procedures on the breast, including reduction mammoplasty and breast augmentation.

In Plain Language

anesthesia for breast reconstructive surgery; being put to sleep for breast surgery

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for reconstructive breast procedures including breast augmentation, reduction mammoplasty, mastopexy, and post-mastectomy reconstruction with implants or tissue expanders. Distinct from radical/modified radical mastectomy codes.

RVU Information

CPT 00402 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Anesthesia codes use a base unit + time unit system rather than standard RVUs. Contact your payer for the anesthesia conversion factor.

Billing & Documentation

Anesthesia codes are billed using base units plus time units. One time unit typically equals 15 minutes of anesthesia time. Document start and stop times, patient status (P1-P6), and any qualifying circumstances. Modifiers AA, QK, QX, or QY indicate the provider arrangement.
