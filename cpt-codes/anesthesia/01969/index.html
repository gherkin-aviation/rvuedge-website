01969 Anesthesia c hyst flwg neuraxial

Anesthesia +Add-on

Also known as: labor to cesarean hysterectomy conversion anesthesia

Anesthesia for cesarean hysterectomy following neuraxial labor analgesia/anesthesia. Covers anesthesia when a labor epidural is extended to provide anesthesia for both cesarean delivery and subsequent hysterectomy.

Clinical Context

Used when neuraxial analgesia for labor is converted to anesthesia for cesarean hysterectomy. Typically an emergency situation such as placenta accreta discovered during cesarean, or uncontrolled postpartum hemorrhage requiring hysterectomy. May need conversion to general anesthesia.
