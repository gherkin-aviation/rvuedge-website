01486 Anesthesia open total ankle replacement

Anesthesia

Also known as: total ankle replacement anesthesia, TAR anesthesia, ankle replacement anesthesia

Anesthesia for open procedures on the bones of the lower leg, ankle, and foot involving total ankle replacement. Covers general or regional anesthesia for total ankle arthroplasty.

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for total ankle arthroplasty for end-stage ankle arthritis. Alternative to ankle arthrodesis (fusion). Requires careful patient selection. Popliteal sciatic and saphenous nerve blocks provide excellent anesthesia and postoperative analgesia.
