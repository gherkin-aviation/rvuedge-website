00160 Anesthesia procedures nose & sinus not otherwise specified

Anesthesia

Also known as: sinus surgery anesthesia, nasal surgery anesthesia

Anesthesia for procedures on the nose and accessory sinuses not otherwise specified. Covers general anesthesia for nasal and sinus surgeries that do not have a more specific anesthesia code.

In Plain Language

anesthesia for nose or sinus surgery; being put to sleep for nose surgery

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for nasal and sinus procedures not covered by more specific codes. Includes septoplasty, turbinate reduction, nasal polypectomy, and functional endoscopic sinus surgery (FESS). Common outpatient anesthesia procedure.

RVU Information

CPT 00160 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Anesthesia codes use a base unit + time unit system rather than standard RVUs. Contact your payer for the anesthesia conversion factor.

Billing & Documentation

Anesthesia codes are billed using base units plus time units. One time unit typically equals 15 minutes of anesthesia time. Document start and stop times, patient status (P1-P6), and any qualifying circumstances. Modifiers AA, QK, QX, or QY indicate the provider arrangement.
