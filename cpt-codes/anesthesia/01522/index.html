01522 Anesthesia procedures vn lower leg venous thrmb

Anesthesia

Also known as: lower leg DVT thrombectomy anesthesia, venous thrombectomy anesthesia

Anesthesia for procedures on the veins of the lower leg involving venous thrombectomy. Covers anesthesia for surgical removal of thrombus from lower leg veins for deep venous thrombosis.

In Plain Language

anesthesia for removing blood clot from lower leg vein; being put to sleep for deep vein clot removal

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for venous thrombectomy of the lower leg for acute deep venous thrombosis. Rarely performed as most DVTs are managed medically; surgical thrombectomy reserved for phlegmasia cerulea dolens or cases refractory to anticoagulation and catheter-directed therapy.

RVU Information

CPT 01522 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Anesthesia codes use a base unit + time unit system rather than standard RVUs. Contact your payer for the anesthesia conversion factor.

Billing & Documentation

Anesthesia codes are billed using base units plus time units. One time unit typically equals 15 minutes of anesthesia time. Document start and stop times, patient status (P1-P6), and any qualifying circumstances. Modifiers AA, QK, QX, or QY indicate the provider arrangement.
