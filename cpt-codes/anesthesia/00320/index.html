00320 Anesthesia all procedures neck not otherwise specified 1yr/>

Anesthesia

Also known as: neck surgery anesthesia, cervical procedure anesthesia

Anesthesia for all procedures on the neck not otherwise specified, for patients aged one year or older. Covers general anesthesia for cervical procedures not covered by more specific codes in patients beyond infancy.

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for neck procedures in patients aged 1 year and older that are not covered by more specific codes (00322, 00326, 00350, 00352). Includes thyroidectomy, parathyroidectomy, neck dissection, and branchial cleft excision.
