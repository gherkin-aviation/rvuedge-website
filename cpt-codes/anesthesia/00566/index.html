00566 Anesthesia direct cabg without pump

Anesthesia

Also known as: off-pump CABG anesthesia, OPCAB anesthesia, beating heart bypass anesthesia

Anesthesia for direct coronary artery bypass grafting without cardiopulmonary bypass (off-pump CABG). Covers general anesthesia for beating-heart coronary bypass surgery performed without the heart-lung machine.

In Plain Language

anesthesia for heart bypass surgery without stopping the heart; being put to sleep for beating-heart bypass surgery

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for off-pump coronary artery bypass grafting where anastomoses are performed on the beating heart. Requires expertise in hemodynamic management during cardiac displacement, use of stabilizers, and potential for emergent conversion to on-pump bypass.

RVU Information

CPT 00566 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Anesthesia codes use a base unit + time unit system rather than standard RVUs. Contact your payer for the anesthesia conversion factor.

Billing & Documentation

Anesthesia codes are billed using base units plus time units. One time unit typically equals 15 minutes of anesthesia time. Document start and stop times, patient status (P1-P6), and any qualifying circumstances. Modifiers AA, QK, QX, or QY indicate the provider arrangement.
