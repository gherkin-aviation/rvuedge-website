01782 Anesthesia vn upper arm & elbow phlbrpy

Anesthesia

Also known as: upper arm vein repair anesthesia, brachial vein repair anesthesia

Anesthesia for procedures on the veins of the upper arm and elbow involving phleborrhaphy (vein repair). Covers anesthesia for surgical repair of injured veins in the upper arm and antecubital region.

In Plain Language

anesthesia for arm vein repair; being put to sleep for upper arm vein sewing repair

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for phleborrhaphy (venous repair) of the upper arm and elbow, typically for traumatic venous injuries. Includes repair of the brachial, basilic, or cephalic veins following penetrating or blunt trauma.

RVU Information

CPT 01782 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Anesthesia codes use a base unit + time unit system rather than standard RVUs. Contact your payer for the anesthesia conversion factor.

Billing & Documentation

Anesthesia codes are billed using base units plus time units. One time unit typically equals 15 minutes of anesthesia time. Document start and stop times, patient status (P1-P6), and any qualifying circumstances. Modifiers AA, QK, QX, or QY indicate the provider arrangement.
