00561 Anesthesia procedures heart with pump<1 yr

Anesthesia

Also known as: infant cardiac bypass anesthesia, neonatal open heart anesthesia, baby heart surgery anesthesia

Anesthesia for procedures on the heart, pericardial sac, and great vessels of the chest with cardiopulmonary bypass, in patients younger than one year of age. Covers general anesthesia for open heart surgery in neonates and infants.

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for congenital heart surgery with cardiopulmonary bypass in patients under 1 year. Includes repair of tetralogy of Fallot, transposition of great arteries, AV canal defects, and other congenital heart lesions. Highest complexity anesthesia code.
