00813 Anesthesia upper lower gi ndsc procedures

Anesthesia

Also known as: combined endo anesthesia, EGD plus colonoscopy anesthesia, bidirectional endoscopy anesthesia

Anesthesia for combined upper and lower gastrointestinal endoscopic procedures. Covers anesthesia when both EGD and colonoscopy are performed during the same anesthetic session.

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for combined upper and lower GI endoscopy in the same session. Common when patients require both EGD and colonoscopy, allowing a single anesthetic rather than two separate procedures. Typically the EGD is performed first.
