00144 Anesthesia procedures eye corneal transplant

Anesthesia

Also known as: corneal transplant anesthesia, keratoplasty anesthesia

Anesthesia for procedures on the eye involving corneal transplant. Covers general anesthesia or monitored anesthesia care for penetrating keratoplasty, endothelial keratoplasty (DSAEK, DMEK), or other corneal transplant procedures.

In Plain Language

anesthesia for cornea transplant surgery; being put to sleep for cornea replacement

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for corneal transplant procedures including penetrating keratoplasty, deep anterior lamellar keratoplasty, or endothelial keratoplasty. Indicated for keratoconus, Fuchs dystrophy, corneal scarring, or corneal edema.

RVU Information

CPT 00144 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Anesthesia codes use a base unit + time unit system rather than standard RVUs. Contact your payer for the anesthesia conversion factor.

Billing & Documentation

Anesthesia codes are billed using base units plus time units. One time unit typically equals 15 minutes of anesthesia time. Document start and stop times, patient status (P1-P6), and any qualifying circumstances. Modifiers AA, QK, QX, or QY indicate the provider arrangement.
