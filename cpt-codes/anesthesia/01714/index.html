01714 Anesthesia nerve msc upper a & e tnplsty

Anesthesia

Also known as: elbow tendon repair anesthesia, tenoplasty upper arm anesthesia

Anesthesia for procedures on nerves, muscles, tendons of the upper arm and elbow involving tenoplasty. Covers anesthesia for surgical repair or reconstruction of tendons at the upper arm or elbow.

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for tenoplasty (tendon repair or reconstruction) at the upper arm or elbow. Includes distal biceps tendon repair, triceps tendon repair, and tendon transfer procedures around the elbow.
