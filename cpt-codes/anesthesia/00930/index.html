00930 Anesthesia procedures male gent orchiopexy

Anesthesia

Also known as: orchiopexy anesthesia, testicular fixation anesthesia, torsion repair anesthesia

Anesthesia for procedures on the male genitalia involving orchiopexy. Covers general anesthesia for surgical fixation of the testis within the scrotum, performed for testicular torsion or undescended testis.

In Plain Language

anesthesia for testicle fixation surgery; being put to sleep for testicle positioning surgery

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for orchiopexy, either for correction of undescended testis or for fixation following testicular torsion/detorsion. Testicular torsion is a surgical emergency requiring emergent anesthesia within 6 hours to preserve testicular viability.

RVU Information

CPT 00930 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Anesthesia codes use a base unit + time unit system rather than standard RVUs. Contact your payer for the anesthesia conversion factor.

Billing & Documentation

Anesthesia codes are billed using base units plus time units. One time unit typically equals 15 minutes of anesthesia time. Document start and stop times, patient status (P1-P6), and any qualifying circumstances. Modifiers AA, QK, QX, or QY indicate the provider arrangement.
