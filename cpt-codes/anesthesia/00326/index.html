00326 Anesthesia all procedures larynx & trach<1yr

Anesthesia

Also known as: infant airway surgery anesthesia, neonatal laryngeal anesthesia

Anesthesia for all procedures on the larynx and trachea in a child younger than one year of age. Covers general anesthesia for airway procedures in neonates and infants, requiring specialized pediatric airway management.

In Plain Language

anesthesia for airway surgery in a baby; putting a baby under for throat surgery

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for laryngeal and tracheal procedures in patients under 1 year of age. Includes laryngoscopy, bronchoscopy, laryngeal web excision, and subglottic stenosis repair in neonates and infants. Requires specialized neonatal/pediatric airway expertise.

RVU Information

CPT 00326 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Anesthesia codes use a base unit + time unit system rather than standard RVUs. Contact your payer for the anesthesia conversion factor.

Billing & Documentation

Anesthesia codes are billed using base units plus time units. One time unit typically equals 15 minutes of anesthesia time. Document start and stop times, patient status (P1-P6), and any qualifying circumstances. Modifiers AA, QK, QX, or QY indicate the provider arrangement.
