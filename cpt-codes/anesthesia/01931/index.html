01931 Anesthesia ivnt radiation ntrhpt/prt crc

Anesthesia

Also known as: TIPS anesthesia, portal vein intervention anesthesia

Anesthesia for therapeutic interventional radiological procedures involving the intrahepatic or portal venous circulation. Covers anesthesia for TIPS procedures and other hepatic/portal venous interventions.

In Plain Language

anesthesia for liver vein shunt placement; being put to sleep for TIPS procedure

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for transjugular intrahepatic portosystemic shunt (TIPS) placement and other intrahepatic/portal venous interventions. TIPS is performed for portal hypertension with variceal bleeding or refractory ascites. Patients typically have advanced liver disease with associated coagulopathy.

RVU Information

CPT 01931 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Anesthesia codes use a base unit + time unit system rather than standard RVUs. Contact your payer for the anesthesia conversion factor.

Billing & Documentation

Anesthesia codes are billed using base units plus time units. One time unit typically equals 15 minutes of anesthesia time. Document start and stop times, patient status (P1-P6), and any qualifying circumstances. Modifiers AA, QK, QX, or QY indicate the provider arrangement.
