01340 Anesthesia clsd procedures lower 1/3 femur

Anesthesia

Also known as: distal femur closed procedure anesthesia

Anesthesia for all closed procedures on the lower one-third of the femur. Covers anesthesia for non-operative management of distal femoral fractures including closed reduction and splinting or casting.

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for closed procedures on the distal femur, including closed reduction of supracondylar or intercondylar femur fractures and application of traction or splints. May use regional or general anesthesia.
