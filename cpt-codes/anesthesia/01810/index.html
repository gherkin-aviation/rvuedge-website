01810 Anesthesia procedures nerve muscle f/arm wrist

Anesthesia

Also known as: hand surgery anesthesia, wrist tendon surgery anesthesia, carpal tunnel anesthesia

Anesthesia for all procedures on nerves, muscles, tendons, fascia, and bursae of the forearm, wrist, and hand. Covers anesthesia for soft tissue surgical procedures of the distal upper extremity.

In Plain Language

anesthesia for hand or wrist tendon/nerve surgery; being put to sleep for hand surgery

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for soft tissue procedures of the forearm, wrist, and hand including carpal tunnel release, trigger finger release, tendon repair/transfer, Dupuytren's fasciectomy, and peripheral nerve repair. Brachial plexus or peripheral nerve blocks commonly used.

RVU Information

CPT 01810 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Anesthesia codes use a base unit + time unit system rather than standard RVUs. Contact your payer for the anesthesia conversion factor.

Billing & Documentation

Anesthesia codes are billed using base units plus time units. One time unit typically equals 15 minutes of anesthesia time. Document start and stop times, patient status (P1-P6), and any qualifying circumstances. Modifiers AA, QK, QX, or QY indicate the provider arrangement.
