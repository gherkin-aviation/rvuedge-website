00145 Anesthesia procedures eye vitreoretinal surgical

Anesthesia

Also known as: vitrectomy anesthesia, retina surgery anesthesia

Anesthesia for procedures on the eye involving vitreoretinal surgery. Covers general anesthesia or monitored anesthesia care for posterior segment procedures including vitrectomy, retinal detachment repair, and membrane peeling.

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for vitreoretinal procedures including pars plana vitrectomy, scleral buckle, retinal detachment repair, epiretinal membrane peeling, and macular hole repair. These are often longer procedures requiring stable anesthesia.
