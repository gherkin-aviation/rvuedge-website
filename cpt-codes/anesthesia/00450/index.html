00450 Anesthesia procedures clav & scapula not otherwise specified

Anesthesia

Also known as: clavicle surgery anesthesia, collarbone surgery anesthesia

Anesthesia for procedures on the clavicle and scapula not otherwise specified. Covers general anesthesia or regional anesthesia for surgical procedures involving the clavicle or scapula.

In Plain Language

anesthesia for collarbone surgery; being put to sleep for shoulder blade surgery

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for clavicle or scapula procedures not covered by 00454. Includes open reduction internal fixation of clavicle fractures, scapular fracture repair, and excision of bone lesions. May use interscalene or supraclavicular nerve block.

RVU Information

CPT 00450 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Anesthesia codes use a base unit + time unit system rather than standard RVUs. Contact your payer for the anesthesia conversion factor.

Billing & Documentation

Anesthesia codes are billed using base units plus time units. One time unit typically equals 15 minutes of anesthesia time. Document start and stop times, patient status (P1-P6), and any qualifying circumstances. Modifiers AA, QK, QX, or QY indicate the provider arrangement.
