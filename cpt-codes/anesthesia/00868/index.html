00868 Anesthesia xtrprtl procedures rnl transpl

Anesthesia

Also known as: kidney transplant anesthesia, renal transplant anesthesia

Anesthesia for extraperitoneal procedures involving renal transplant. Covers general anesthesia for kidney transplantation, both donor nephrectomy and recipient transplant procedures.

In Plain Language

anesthesia for kidney transplant surgery; being put to sleep for receiving a new kidney

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for renal transplantation (recipient procedure) or living donor nephrectomy. Recipients typically have end-stage renal disease with associated comorbidities including hyperkalemia, fluid overload, anemia, and uremic coagulopathy requiring careful anesthetic management.

RVU Information

CPT 00868 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Anesthesia codes use a base unit + time unit system rather than standard RVUs. Contact your payer for the anesthesia conversion factor.

Billing & Documentation

Anesthesia codes are billed using base units plus time units. One time unit typically equals 15 minutes of anesthesia time. Document start and stop times, patient status (P1-P6), and any qualifying circumstances. Modifiers AA, QK, QX, or QY indicate the provider arrangement.
