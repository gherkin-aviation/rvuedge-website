01500 Anesthesia procedures artery lower leg not otherwise specified

Anesthesia

Also known as: tibial artery surgery anesthesia, lower leg arterial anesthesia

Anesthesia for procedures on the arteries of the lower leg not otherwise specified, including anterior tibial, posterior tibial, and peroneal arteries.

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for arterial procedures on the lower leg not covered by 01502. Includes tibial artery bypass, arterial reconstruction for peripheral vascular disease, and tibial artery repair. Often performed in patients with critical limb ischemia.
