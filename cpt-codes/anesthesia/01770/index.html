01770 Anesthesia procedures artery upper arm & elbow not otherwise specified

Anesthesia

Also known as: brachial artery surgery anesthesia, upper arm artery anesthesia

Anesthesia for procedures on the arteries of the upper arm and elbow not otherwise specified. Covers anesthesia for arterial surgical procedures of the brachial artery region.

In Plain Language

anesthesia for upper arm artery surgery; being put to sleep for arm artery procedure

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for arterial procedures of the upper arm and elbow not covered by 01772. Includes brachial artery repair, arterial bypass, and management of brachial artery injuries from trauma or catheterization.

RVU Information

CPT 01770 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Anesthesia codes use a base unit + time unit system rather than standard RVUs. Contact your payer for the anesthesia conversion factor.

Billing & Documentation

Anesthesia codes are billed using base units plus time units. One time unit typically equals 15 minutes of anesthesia time. Document start and stop times, patient status (P1-P6), and any qualifying circumstances. Modifiers AA, QK, QX, or QY indicate the provider arrangement.
