00770 Anesthesia procedures maj abdominal blood vessel

Anesthesia

Also known as: aortic surgery anesthesia, AAA repair anesthesia, abdominal vascular anesthesia

Anesthesia for all procedures on major abdominal blood vessels. Covers general anesthesia for surgical procedures on the abdominal aorta, inferior vena cava, or other major intra-abdominal vascular structures.

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for major abdominal vascular procedures including abdominal aortic aneurysm repair, aorto-bifemoral bypass, and mesenteric vascular surgery. Requires invasive arterial and central venous monitoring, cross-clamp management, and blood product availability.
