01951 Anesthesia 2&3 burn less 4 % tbsa

Anesthesia

Also known as: burn surgery anesthesia small, burn debridement anesthesia

Anesthesia for second and third degree burn excision or debridement with or without skin grafting, total body surface area treated less than 4 percent. Covers anesthesia for small burn wound management.

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for surgical management of second or third degree burns covering less than 4% TBSA. Includes tangential excision, escharectomy, and skin grafting. Even small burn excisions may require general anesthesia due to pain and need for precise surgical technique.
