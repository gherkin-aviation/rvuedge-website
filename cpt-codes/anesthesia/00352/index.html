00352 Anesthesia procedures maj vsl nck simple ligament

Anesthesia

Also known as: neck vessel ligation anesthesia, jugular ligation anesthesia

Anesthesia for procedures on the major vessels of the neck involving simple ligation. Covers general anesthesia or monitored anesthesia care for ligation of major neck vessels such as the external carotid or jugular vein.

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for simple ligation of major neck vessels, commonly the external carotid artery or internal jugular vein. May be performed for hemorrhage control, pre-operative devascularization, or as part of trauma management.
