01432 Anesthesia procedures vn knee & popliteal av fstl

Anesthesia

Also known as: popliteal AV fistula anesthesia, knee AV fistula anesthesia

Anesthesia for procedures on the veins of the knee and popliteal area involving arteriovenous fistula. Covers anesthesia for creation, repair, or ligation of AV fistulas in the popliteal region.

In Plain Language

anesthesia for arteriovenous connection surgery behind the knee; being put to sleep for AV fistula surgery at the knee

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for arteriovenous fistula procedures in the popliteal region, including creation for dialysis access (uncommon location), repair of traumatic AV fistula, or ligation of congenital AV malformations.

RVU Information

CPT 01432 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Anesthesia codes use a base unit + time unit system rather than standard RVUs. Contact your payer for the anesthesia conversion factor.

Billing & Documentation

Anesthesia codes are billed using base units plus time units. One time unit typically equals 15 minutes of anesthesia time. Document start and stop times, patient status (P1-P6), and any qualifying circumstances. Modifiers AA, QK, QX, or QY indicate the provider arrangement.
