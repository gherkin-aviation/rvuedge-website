00210 Anesthesia intracranial procedures not otherwise specified

Anesthesia

Also known as: craniotomy anesthesia, brain surgery anesthesia

Anesthesia for intracranial procedures not otherwise specified. Covers general anesthesia for surgical procedures within the cranial cavity that lack a more specific intracranial anesthesia code.

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for intracranial procedures not covered by more specific codes (00211-00222). Includes craniotomy for tumor resection, brain biopsy, and other intracranial procedures. Requires specialized neuroanesthesia monitoring and management.
