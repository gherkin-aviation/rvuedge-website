01670 Anesthesia all procedures vn shoulder & ax

Anesthesia

Also known as: axillary vein surgery anesthesia, shoulder vein anesthesia

Anesthesia for all procedures on the veins of the shoulder and axilla. Covers anesthesia for venous surgical procedures in the shoulder and axillary region.

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for venous procedures on the shoulder and axilla including axillary vein thrombectomy, venous reconstruction, and treatment of Paget-Schroetter syndrome (effort thrombosis of the subclavian/axillary vein).
