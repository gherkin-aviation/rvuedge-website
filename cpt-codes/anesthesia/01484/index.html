01484 Anesthesia opn lw l/a/f oste/ostpl

Anesthesia

Also known as: ankle osteotomy anesthesia, foot osteotomy anesthesia, bunionectomy anesthesia

Anesthesia for open procedures on the bones of the lower leg, ankle, and foot involving osteotomy or osteoplasty. Covers anesthesia for surgical bone cutting or reshaping procedures below the knee.

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for osteotomy or osteoplasty of the lower leg, ankle, or foot. Common procedures include tibial osteotomy for malalignment, calcaneal osteotomy, metatarsal osteotomy for bunion correction, and tarsal coalition resection.
