00600 Anesthesia procedures crv spine & cord not otherwise specified

Anesthesia

Also known as: cervical spine anesthesia, C-spine surgery anesthesia, neck spine anesthesia

Anesthesia for procedures on the cervical spine and spinal cord not otherwise specified. Covers general anesthesia for surgical procedures involving the cervical vertebrae or spinal cord that lack a more specific code.

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for cervical spine procedures not covered by 00604. Includes anterior cervical discectomy and fusion (ACDF), posterior cervical laminectomy, cervical arthroplasty, and other cervical spine procedures. Requires careful positioning and airway management.
