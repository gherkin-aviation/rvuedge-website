00404 Anesthesia integ sys radiation/modf brst

Anesthesia

Also known as: mastectomy anesthesia, radical mastectomy anesthesia

Anesthesia for procedures on the integumentary system involving radical or modified radical mastectomy. Covers general anesthesia for surgical removal of the breast including underlying tissue, typically for breast cancer treatment.

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for radical or modified radical mastectomy performed for breast cancer. Not for simple mastectomy. Requires consideration of axillary lymph node dissection, potential for significant blood loss, and positioning challenges.
