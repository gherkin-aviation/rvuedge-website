01756 Anesthesia opn/arthrs elbow radiation procedures

Anesthesia

Also known as: radical elbow surgery anesthesia, elbow tumor resection anesthesia

Anesthesia for open or arthroscopic procedures on the elbow involving radical procedures. Covers general anesthesia for radical surgical procedures of the elbow including radical resection of tumors.

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for radical procedures at the elbow including wide excision of bone or soft tissue tumors, radical synovectomy, and other extensive elbow procedures. May require reconstruction with prosthesis or allograft.
