01756 Anesthesia opn/arthrs elbow radiation procedures

Anesthesia

Also known as: radical elbow surgery anesthesia, elbow tumor resection anesthesia

Anesthesia for open or arthroscopic procedures on the elbow involving radical procedures. Covers general anesthesia for radical surgical procedures of the elbow including radical resection of tumors.

In Plain Language

anesthesia for major elbow surgery; being put to sleep for elbow cancer surgery

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for radical procedures at the elbow including wide excision of bone or soft tissue tumors, radical synovectomy, and other extensive elbow procedures. May require reconstruction with prosthesis or allograft.

RVU Information

CPT 01756 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Anesthesia codes use a base unit + time unit system rather than standard RVUs. Contact your payer for the anesthesia conversion factor.

Billing & Documentation

Anesthesia codes are billed using base units plus time units. One time unit typically equals 15 minutes of anesthesia time. Document start and stop times, patient status (P1-P6), and any qualifying circumstances. Modifiers AA, QK, QX, or QY indicate the provider arrangement.
