00902 Anesthesia anorectal procedures

Anesthesia

Also known as: anorectal surgery anesthesia, hemorrhoid surgery anesthesia, anal surgery anesthesia

Anesthesia for anorectal procedures. Covers general, regional, or local anesthesia with sedation for surgical procedures performed on the anus and rectum.

In Plain Language

anesthesia for rectal surgery; being put to sleep for hemorrhoid or anal surgery

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for anorectal procedures including hemorrhoidectomy, anal fistulotomy, anal sphincteroplasty, excision of anal lesions, and rectal prolapse repair. May use general, spinal, caudal, or local anesthesia with sedation depending on procedure and patient factors.

RVU Information

CPT 00902 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Anesthesia codes use a base unit + time unit system rather than standard RVUs. Contact your payer for the anesthesia conversion factor.

Billing & Documentation

Anesthesia codes are billed using base units plus time units. One time unit typically equals 15 minutes of anesthesia time. Document start and stop times, patient status (P1-P6), and any qualifying circumstances. Modifiers AA, QK, QX, or QY indicate the provider arrangement.
