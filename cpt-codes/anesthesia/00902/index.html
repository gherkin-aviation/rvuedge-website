00902 Anesthesia anorectal procedures

Anesthesia

Also known as: anorectal surgery anesthesia, hemorrhoid surgery anesthesia, anal surgery anesthesia

Anesthesia for anorectal procedures. Covers general, regional, or local anesthesia with sedation for surgical procedures performed on the anus and rectum.

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for anorectal procedures including hemorrhoidectomy, anal fistulotomy, anal sphincteroplasty, excision of anal lesions, and rectal prolapse repair. May use general, spinal, caudal, or local anesthesia with sedation depending on procedure and patient factors.
