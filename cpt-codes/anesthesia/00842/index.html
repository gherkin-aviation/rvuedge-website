00842 Anesthesia iper procedures amniocentesis

Anesthesia

Also known as: amniocentesis anesthesia, amnio sedation

Anesthesia for intraperitoneal procedures in the lower abdomen involving amniocentesis. Covers anesthesia or sedation for transabdominal amniocentesis performed for prenatal diagnosis or therapeutic amnioreduction.

In Plain Language

anesthesia for amniotic fluid sampling; sedation for pregnancy fluid testing

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for amniocentesis, typically in patients who require sedation for the procedure due to anxiety or for therapeutic amnioreduction. Most amniocenteses are performed with local anesthesia only, but some patients require additional sedation or anesthesia.

RVU Information

CPT 00842 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Anesthesia codes use a base unit + time unit system rather than standard RVUs. Contact your payer for the anesthesia conversion factor.

Billing & Documentation

Anesthesia codes are billed using base units plus time units. One time unit typically equals 15 minutes of anesthesia time. Document start and stop times, patient status (P1-P6), and any qualifying circumstances. Modifiers AA, QK, QX, or QY indicate the provider arrangement.
