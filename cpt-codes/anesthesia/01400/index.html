01400 Anesthesia opn/arthrs knee joint not otherwise specified

Anesthesia

Also known as: knee surgery anesthesia, open knee anesthesia

Anesthesia for open or arthroscopic procedures on the knee joint not otherwise specified. Covers general or regional anesthesia for surgical knee procedures that lack a more specific code.

In Plain Language

anesthesia for knee surgery; being put to sleep for knee operation

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for open or arthroscopic knee procedures not covered by 01402 or 01404. Includes therapeutic knee arthroscopy (meniscectomy, ACL reconstruction, chondroplasty), open knee procedures, and synovectomy. Peripheral nerve blocks commonly used.

RVU Information

CPT 01400 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Anesthesia codes use a base unit + time unit system rather than standard RVUs. Contact your payer for the anesthesia conversion factor.

Billing & Documentation

Anesthesia codes are billed using base units plus time units. One time unit typically equals 15 minutes of anesthesia time. Document start and stop times, patient status (P1-P6), and any qualifying circumstances. Modifiers AA, QK, QX, or QY indicate the provider arrangement.
