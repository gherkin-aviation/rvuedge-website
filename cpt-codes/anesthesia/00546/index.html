00546 Anesthesia thrcm procedures pulmonary rescj

Anesthesia

Also known as: lung resection anesthesia, lobectomy anesthesia, pneumonectomy anesthesia

Anesthesia for thoracotomy procedures involving pulmonary resection, including pneumonectomy, lobectomy, or segmentectomy. Covers general anesthesia for open surgical removal of lung tissue.

In Plain Language

anesthesia for lung removal surgery; being put to sleep for removing part or all of a lung

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for pulmonary resection procedures including pneumonectomy, lobectomy, bilobectomy, and segmentectomy via thoracotomy. Most commonly for lung cancer. Requires one-lung ventilation, careful fluid management (especially for pneumonectomy), and postoperative pain control.

RVU Information

CPT 00546 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Anesthesia codes use a base unit + time unit system rather than standard RVUs. Contact your payer for the anesthesia conversion factor.

Billing & Documentation

Anesthesia codes are billed using base units plus time units. One time unit typically equals 15 minutes of anesthesia time. Document start and stop times, patient status (P1-P6), and any qualifying circumstances. Modifiers AA, QK, QX, or QY indicate the provider arrangement.
