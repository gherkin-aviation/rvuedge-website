01170 Anesthesia opn procedures symph pub/si joint

Anesthesia

Also known as: pelvic ring ORIF anesthesia, SI joint surgery anesthesia, open pelvic fixation anesthesia

Anesthesia for open procedures on the symphysis pubis or sacroiliac joint. Covers general anesthesia for surgical procedures on the pelvic ring including open reduction and fixation of pelvic disruptions.

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for open surgical procedures on the pubic symphysis or sacroiliac joint, including open reduction internal fixation of pelvic ring injuries and SI joint fusion. May involve significant positioning challenges and blood loss.
