01230 Anesthesia opn upper 2/3 femur not otherwise specified

Anesthesia

Also known as: femur surgery anesthesia, hip fracture ORIF anesthesia, femoral nailing anesthesia

Anesthesia for open procedures involving the upper two-thirds of the femur not otherwise specified. Covers general or regional anesthesia for open surgical procedures on the proximal and mid-shaft femur.

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for open procedures on the upper two-thirds of the femur not covered by 01232 or 01234. Includes ORIF of hip fractures, intramedullary nailing of femoral shaft fractures, and DHS/sliding hip screw fixation. Common in geriatric trauma patients.
