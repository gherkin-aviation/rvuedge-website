01440 Anesthesia procedures artery knee & popliteal not otherwise specified

Anesthesia

Also known as: popliteal artery surgery anesthesia, knee artery anesthesia

Anesthesia for procedures on the arteries of the knee and popliteal area not otherwise specified. Covers general or regional anesthesia for arterial surgical procedures in the popliteal region.

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for arterial procedures in the knee and popliteal area not covered by 01442 or 01444. Includes popliteal artery repair, aneurysm repair, and arterial reconstruction in this region.
