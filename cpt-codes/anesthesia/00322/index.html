00322 Anesthesia all procedures nck needle biopsy thyroid

Anesthesia

Also known as: thyroid biopsy anesthesia, thyroid FNA anesthesia

Anesthesia for all procedures on the neck involving needle biopsy of the thyroid gland. Covers anesthesia for fine needle aspiration or core biopsy of thyroid nodules requiring sedation or general anesthesia.

In Plain Language

anesthesia for thyroid needle biopsy; being put to sleep for thyroid tissue sampling

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for needle biopsy of the thyroid. Most thyroid FNAs are performed with local anesthesia, but some patients require sedation or general anesthesia due to anxiety, difficult anatomy, or need for image guidance requiring patient immobility.

RVU Information

CPT 00322 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Anesthesia codes use a base unit + time unit system rather than standard RVUs. Contact your payer for the anesthesia conversion factor.

Billing & Documentation

Anesthesia codes are billed using base units plus time units. One time unit typically equals 15 minutes of anesthesia time. Document start and stop times, patient status (P1-P6), and any qualifying circumstances. Modifiers AA, QK, QX, or QY indicate the provider arrangement.
