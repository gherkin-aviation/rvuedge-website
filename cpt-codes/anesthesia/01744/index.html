01744 Anesthesia opn/arthrs elbow repair humerus

Anesthesia

Also known as: humeral nonunion repair anesthesia, elbow malunion surgery anesthesia

Anesthesia for open or arthroscopic procedures on the elbow involving repair of nonunion or malunion of the humerus. Covers anesthesia for surgical treatment of humeral fracture nonunion or malunion with bone grafting.

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for repair of humeral nonunion or malunion near the elbow. Often requires bone grafting, revision fixation, and may involve significant operative time. Nonunion of the distal humerus is a challenging surgical problem.
