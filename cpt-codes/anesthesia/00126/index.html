00126 Anesthesia procedures ear tympanotomy

Anesthesia

Also known as: tube placement anesthesia, myringotomy anesthesia, ear tubes anesthesia

Anesthesia for procedures on the ear involving tympanotomy. Covers general anesthesia for surgical incision into the tympanic membrane, including myringotomy with or without tube placement.

In Plain Language

anesthesia for ear tube surgery; being put to sleep for ear drum surgery

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for tympanotomy procedures including myringotomy with or without ventilation tube (PE tube) insertion. One of the most common pediatric anesthesia procedures, typically brief mask anesthesia with sevoflurane.

RVU Information

CPT 00126 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Anesthesia codes use a base unit + time unit system rather than standard RVUs. Contact your payer for the anesthesia conversion factor.

Billing & Documentation

Anesthesia codes are billed using base units plus time units. One time unit typically equals 15 minutes of anesthesia time. Document start and stop times, patient status (P1-P6), and any qualifying circumstances. Modifiers AA, QK, QX, or QY indicate the provider arrangement.
