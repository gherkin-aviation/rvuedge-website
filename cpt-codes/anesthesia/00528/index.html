00528 Anesthesia clsd ch mediast & thrc wo

Anesthesia

Also known as: mediastinoscopy anesthesia, VATS anesthesia without lung isolation

Anesthesia for closed chest procedures including mediastinoscopy and diagnostic thoracoscopy without one-lung ventilation. Covers general anesthesia for mediastinal exploration or VATS procedures not requiring lung isolation.

In Plain Language

anesthesia for chest scope without collapsing a lung; being put to sleep for chest exploration

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for mediastinoscopy or diagnostic thoracoscopy where one-lung ventilation is not required. Mediastinoscopy is commonly performed for staging of lung cancer by sampling mediastinal lymph nodes.

RVU Information

CPT 00528 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Anesthesia codes use a base unit + time unit system rather than standard RVUs. Contact your payer for the anesthesia conversion factor.

Billing & Documentation

Anesthesia codes are billed using base units plus time units. One time unit typically equals 15 minutes of anesthesia time. Document start and stop times, patient status (P1-P6), and any qualifying circumstances. Modifiers AA, QK, QX, or QY indicate the provider arrangement.
