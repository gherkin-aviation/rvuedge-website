00730 Anesthesia procedures upper pst abdl wall

Anesthesia

Also known as: retroperitoneal surgery anesthesia, kidney surgery anesthesia, flank surgery anesthesia

Anesthesia for procedures on the upper posterior abdominal wall. Covers general anesthesia for surgical procedures on the retroperitoneal region and upper posterior abdominal wall, including kidney surgery.

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for upper posterior abdominal wall and retroperitoneal procedures including nephrectomy, adrenalectomy, and retroperitoneal lymph node dissection. Often requires lateral decubitus or prone positioning.
