00124 Anesthesia procedures ear otoscopy

Anesthesia

Also known as: otoscopy anesthesia, ear exam under anesthesia

Anesthesia for procedures on the ear involving otoscopy. Covers anesthesia for diagnostic or therapeutic otoscopic examination procedures, including examination under anesthesia of the ear canal and tympanic membrane.

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for otoscopic procedures, particularly in pediatric patients who cannot tolerate awake ear examinations. Also used for detailed assessment of tympanic membrane pathology requiring magnification under general anesthesia.
