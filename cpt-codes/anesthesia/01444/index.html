01444 Anesthesia procedures artery knee & popliteal excision & grf

Anesthesia

Also known as: popliteal bypass anesthesia, popliteal aneurysm repair anesthesia

Anesthesia for procedures on the arteries of the knee and popliteal area involving popliteal artery excision and bypass graft. Covers anesthesia for popliteal artery aneurysm repair or bypass for occlusive disease.

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for popliteal artery excision with interposition graft or bypass graft. Most commonly for popliteal artery aneurysm repair or severe popliteal artery occlusive disease. Requires autologous vein harvest (saphenous vein) in many cases.
