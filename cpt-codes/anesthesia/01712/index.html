01712 Anesthesia procedures nerve msc upper a & e tnot

Anesthesia

Also known as: elbow tenotomy anesthesia, upper arm tenotomy anesthesia

Anesthesia for procedures on nerves, muscles, tendons of the upper arm and elbow involving tenotomy. Covers anesthesia for surgical division of a tendon at the upper arm or elbow.

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for tenotomy procedures at the upper arm or elbow, including biceps tenotomy, triceps tenotomy, or lateral epicondyle release. These are typically shorter procedures that may use regional block alone.
