01442 Anesthesia procedures artery knee & popliteal teaec

Anesthesia

Also known as: popliteal endarterectomy anesthesia, popliteal TEA anesthesia

Anesthesia for procedures on the arteries of the knee and popliteal area involving popliteal thromboendarterectomy. Covers anesthesia for surgical removal of atherosclerotic plaque and thrombus from the popliteal artery.

In Plain Language

anesthesia for clearing blockage from knee artery; being put to sleep for cleaning out the artery behind the knee

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for popliteal artery thromboendarterectomy, a procedure to remove atherosclerotic plaque from the popliteal artery for peripheral vascular disease. Less common than bypass procedures; regional or general anesthesia may be used.

RVU Information

CPT 01442 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Anesthesia codes use a base unit + time unit system rather than standard RVUs. Contact your payer for the anesthesia conversion factor.

Billing & Documentation

Anesthesia codes are billed using base units plus time units. One time unit typically equals 15 minutes of anesthesia time. Document start and stop times, patient status (P1-P6), and any qualifying circumstances. Modifiers AA, QK, QX, or QY indicate the provider arrangement.
