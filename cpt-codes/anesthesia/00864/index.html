00864 Anesthesia xtrprt lower abdominal tot cstc

Anesthesia

Also known as: cystectomy anesthesia, bladder removal anesthesia, radical cystectomy anesthesia

Anesthesia for extraperitoneal procedures in the lower abdomen involving total cystectomy. Covers general anesthesia for surgical removal of the entire urinary bladder, typically for bladder cancer, with urinary diversion.

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for radical cystectomy with urinary diversion (ileal conduit, neobladder, or continent diversion). Major oncologic procedure for muscle-invasive bladder cancer. Prolonged surgery (4-8 hours) with significant fluid shifts and blood loss potential.
