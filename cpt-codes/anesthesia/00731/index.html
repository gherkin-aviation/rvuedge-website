00731 Anesthesia upper gi ndsc procedures not otherwise specified

Anesthesia

Also known as: EGD anesthesia, upper endoscopy anesthesia, upper GI sedation

Anesthesia for upper gastrointestinal endoscopic procedures not otherwise specified. Covers anesthesia or deep sedation for esophagogastroduodenoscopy (EGD) and related upper GI endoscopic procedures.

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for upper GI endoscopic procedures not covered by 00732. Includes EGD with biopsy, dilation, stent placement, and hemostasis. Increasingly provided by anesthesiologists rather than nurse-administered sedation for complex patients.
