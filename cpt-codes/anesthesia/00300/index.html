00300 Anesthesia all procedures integ h/n/ptrunk

Anesthesia

Also known as: skin surgery anesthesia head/neck, soft tissue surgery anesthesia

Anesthesia for all procedures on the integumentary system, muscles and nerves of the head, neck, and posterior trunk not otherwise specified. Covers general anesthesia or monitored anesthesia care for skin and soft tissue procedures in these regions.

In Plain Language

anesthesia for skin surgery on head, neck, or back; being put to sleep for skin or muscle procedure

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for integumentary, muscle, or nerve procedures of the head, neck, or posterior trunk not covered by more specific codes. Includes skin lesion excisions, muscle biopsies, and nerve procedures in these regions.

RVU Information

CPT 00300 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Anesthesia codes use a base unit + time unit system rather than standard RVUs. Contact your payer for the anesthesia conversion factor.

Billing & Documentation

Anesthesia codes are billed using base units plus time units. One time unit typically equals 15 minutes of anesthesia time. Document start and stop times, patient status (P1-P6), and any qualifying circumstances. Modifiers AA, QK, QX, or QY indicate the provider arrangement.
