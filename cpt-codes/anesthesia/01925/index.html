01925 Anesthesia ivntl radiation artl crtd/c

Anesthesia

Also known as: carotid stent anesthesia, PCI anesthesia, coronary intervention anesthesia

Anesthesia for therapeutic interventional radiological procedures involving the arterial system of the carotid or coronary arteries. Covers anesthesia for carotid stenting and coronary interventions.

In Plain Language

anesthesia for neck or heart artery stent placement; being put to sleep for carotid or coronary artery procedure

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for interventional procedures on the carotid arteries (carotid artery stenting) or coronary arteries (PCI, rotational atherectomy). Carotid stenting requires neuromonitoring, while complex PCI may need hemodynamic support.

RVU Information

CPT 01925 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Anesthesia codes use a base unit + time unit system rather than standard RVUs. Contact your payer for the anesthesia conversion factor.

Billing & Documentation

Anesthesia codes are billed using base units plus time units. One time unit typically equals 15 minutes of anesthesia time. Document start and stop times, patient status (P1-P6), and any qualifying circumstances. Modifiers AA, QK, QX, or QY indicate the provider arrangement.
