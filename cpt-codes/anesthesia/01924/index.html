01924 Anesthesia therapy ivntl radiation artl not otherwise specified

Anesthesia

Also known as: interventional radiology anesthesia arterial, endovascular procedure anesthesia

Anesthesia for therapeutic interventional radiological procedures involving the arterial system not otherwise specified. Covers anesthesia for catheter-based arterial interventions.

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for therapeutic interventional radiological arterial procedures not covered by 01925 or 01926. Includes peripheral artery angioplasty/stenting, embolization for hemorrhage, and other catheter-based arterial interventions.
