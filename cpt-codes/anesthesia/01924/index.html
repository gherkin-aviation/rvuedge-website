01924 Anesthesia therapy ivntl radiation artl not otherwise specified

Anesthesia

Also known as: interventional radiology anesthesia arterial, endovascular procedure anesthesia

Anesthesia for therapeutic interventional radiological procedures involving the arterial system not otherwise specified. Covers anesthesia for catheter-based arterial interventions.

In Plain Language

anesthesia for artery treatment through a catheter; being put to sleep for artery procedure with imaging

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for therapeutic interventional radiological arterial procedures not covered by 01925 or 01926. Includes peripheral artery angioplasty/stenting, embolization for hemorrhage, and other catheter-based arterial interventions.

RVU Information

CPT 01924 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Anesthesia codes use a base unit + time unit system rather than standard RVUs. Contact your payer for the anesthesia conversion factor.

Billing & Documentation

Anesthesia codes are billed using base units plus time units. One time unit typically equals 15 minutes of anesthesia time. Document start and stop times, patient status (P1-P6), and any qualifying circumstances. Modifiers AA, QK, QX, or QY indicate the provider arrangement.
