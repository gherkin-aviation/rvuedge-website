01250 Anesthesia all procedures nerve muscle upper leg

Anesthesia

Also known as: thigh soft tissue surgery anesthesia, upper leg nerve/muscle anesthesia

Anesthesia for all procedures on nerves, muscles, tendons, fascia, and bursae of the upper leg. Covers anesthesia for soft tissue surgical procedures of the thigh region.

In Plain Language

anesthesia for thigh muscle or nerve surgery; being put to sleep for soft tissue surgery on the upper leg

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for soft tissue procedures of the upper leg including muscle biopsies, fasciotomy for compartment syndrome, nerve repair, tendon repair, and soft tissue tumor excision in the thigh. May use peripheral nerve blocks as primary or adjunct anesthesia.

RVU Information

CPT 01250 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Anesthesia codes use a base unit + time unit system rather than standard RVUs. Contact your payer for the anesthesia conversion factor.

Billing & Documentation

Anesthesia codes are billed using base units plus time units. One time unit typically equals 15 minutes of anesthesia time. Document start and stop times, patient status (P1-P6), and any qualifying circumstances. Modifiers AA, QK, QX, or QY indicate the provider arrangement.
