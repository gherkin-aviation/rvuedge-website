00632 Anesthesia procedures lumbar sympathectomy

Anesthesia

Also known as: lumbar sympathectomy anesthesia, sympathetic chain surgery anesthesia

Anesthesia for procedures on the lumbar region involving lumbar sympathectomy. Covers general anesthesia for surgical interruption of the lumbar sympathetic chain for treatment of vascular insufficiency or pain syndromes.

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for lumbar sympathectomy, which involves surgical resection or ablation of the lumbar sympathetic chain. Indicated for peripheral vascular disease, complex regional pain syndrome (CRPS), or hyperhidrosis of the lower extremities.
