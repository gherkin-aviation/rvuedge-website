01968 anesthesia/analg cs dlvr neuraxial

Anesthesia +Add-on

Also known as: epidural top-up for C-section, labor to cesarean conversion anesthesia

Anesthesia for cesarean delivery following neuraxial labor analgesia/anesthesia. Covers the additional anesthesia service when a labor epidural is dosed up for an unplanned cesarean delivery.

In Plain Language

using the labor epidural for a C-section; converting epidural to surgical anesthesia for cesarean

Clinical Context

Used when neuraxial analgesia provided for labor (01967) is converted to surgical anesthesia for cesarean delivery. The existing epidural is bolused with higher concentration local anesthetic to achieve surgical anesthesia. Reported in addition to 01967.

RVU Information

CPT 01968 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Anesthesia codes use a base unit + time unit system rather than standard RVUs. Contact your payer for the anesthesia conversion factor.

Billing & Documentation

Anesthesia codes are billed using base units plus time units. One time unit typically equals 15 minutes of anesthesia time. Document start and stop times, patient status (P1-P6), and any qualifying circumstances. Modifiers AA, QK, QX, or QY indicate the provider arrangement.
