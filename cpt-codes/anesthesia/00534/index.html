00534 Anesthesia insertion/replacement cvdfb

Anesthesia

Also known as: ICD implant anesthesia, defibrillator surgery anesthesia, AICD anesthesia

Anesthesia for insertion or replacement of a cardioverter-defibrillator (AICD/ICD), including transvenous placement of leads and generator implantation or replacement.

In Plain Language

anesthesia for defibrillator implant surgery; being put to sleep for heart shock device placement

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for implantable cardioverter-defibrillator (ICD) placement or generator change. Indicated for patients at risk of sudden cardiac death from ventricular tachycardia or fibrillation. Requires defibrillation testing capability in the procedure room.

RVU Information

CPT 00534 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Anesthesia codes use a base unit + time unit system rather than standard RVUs. Contact your payer for the anesthesia conversion factor.

Billing & Documentation

Anesthesia codes are billed using base units plus time units. One time unit typically equals 15 minutes of anesthesia time. Document start and stop times, patient status (P1-P6), and any qualifying circumstances. Modifiers AA, QK, QX, or QY indicate the provider arrangement.
