00640 Anesthesia spine manipulation

Anesthesia

Also known as: MUA spine anesthesia, spine manipulation anesthesia

Anesthesia for manipulation of the spine or for closed procedures on the cervical, thoracic, or lumbar spine. Covers general anesthesia for spinal manipulation under anesthesia or closed reduction of spinal fractures/dislocations.

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for manipulation under anesthesia (MUA) of the spine or closed reduction of spinal deformities or fractures. May be performed for adhesive conditions, closed reduction of spondylolisthesis, or cervical spine reduction.
