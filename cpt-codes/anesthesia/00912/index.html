00912 Anesthesia trurl procedures rescj bldr tumor

Anesthesia

Also known as: TURBT anesthesia, bladder tumor resection anesthesia

Anesthesia for transurethral procedures involving resection of bladder tumor(s). Covers general or spinal anesthesia for transurethral resection of bladder tumors (TURBT) for diagnosis and treatment of bladder cancer.

In Plain Language

anesthesia for bladder tumor removal through a scope; being put to sleep for bladder cancer scope surgery

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for transurethral resection of bladder tumors (TURBT), the primary diagnostic and therapeutic procedure for bladder cancer. Spinal anesthesia preferred to detect bladder perforation (patient reports shoulder pain). Risk of TUR syndrome with prolonged resection.

RVU Information

CPT 00912 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Anesthesia codes use a base unit + time unit system rather than standard RVUs. Contact your payer for the anesthesia conversion factor.

Billing & Documentation

Anesthesia codes are billed using base units plus time units. One time unit typically equals 15 minutes of anesthesia time. Document start and stop times, patient status (P1-P6), and any qualifying circumstances. Modifiers AA, QK, QX, or QY indicate the provider arrangement.
