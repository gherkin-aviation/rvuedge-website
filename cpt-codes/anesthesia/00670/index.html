00670 Anesthesia xtnsv sp & spinal cord procedures

Anesthesia

Also known as: complex spine surgery anesthesia, spinal fusion anesthesia, scoliosis surgery anesthesia

Anesthesia for extensive spine and spinal cord procedures, including instrumentation. Covers general anesthesia for complex, multi-level spinal reconstruction with instrumented fusion, typically involving significant operative time and blood loss.

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for extensive spinal surgery including multi-level spinal fusion, scoliosis correction, kyphosis correction, and complex spinal reconstruction. Requires neuromonitoring (SSEPs/MEPs), cell saver, large-bore IV access, and careful hemodynamic and transfusion management.
