01202 Anesthesia arthroscopic procedures hip joint

Anesthesia

Also known as: hip arthroscopy anesthesia, hip scope anesthesia

Anesthesia for arthroscopic procedures of the hip joint. Covers general or regional anesthesia for hip arthroscopy, a minimally invasive procedure for diagnosis and treatment of intra-articular hip pathology.

In Plain Language

anesthesia for hip scope surgery; being put to sleep for hip camera surgery

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for hip arthroscopy, increasingly performed for femoroacetabular impingement (FAI), labral tears, loose bodies, and cartilage lesions. Requires traction table, specialized positioning, and attention to pudendal and peroneal nerve compression from traction.

RVU Information

CPT 01202 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Anesthesia codes use a base unit + time unit system rather than standard RVUs. Contact your payer for the anesthesia conversion factor.

Billing & Documentation

Anesthesia codes are billed using base units plus time units. One time unit typically equals 15 minutes of anesthesia time. Document start and stop times, patient status (P1-P6), and any qualifying circumstances. Modifiers AA, QK, QX, or QY indicate the provider arrangement.
