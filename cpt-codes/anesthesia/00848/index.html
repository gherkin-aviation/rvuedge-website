00848 Anesthesia iper procedures pel exentration

Anesthesia

Also known as: pelvic exenteration anesthesia, total exenteration anesthesia

Anesthesia for intraperitoneal procedures in the lower abdomen involving pelvic exenteration. Covers general anesthesia for total pelvic exenteration, the most radical pelvic cancer surgery involving removal of all pelvic organs.

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for total pelvic exenteration involving removal of the bladder, rectum, and internal reproductive organs en bloc. Performed for recurrent or advanced pelvic malignancies. One of the longest and most complex surgical procedures with massive fluid shifts and blood loss.
