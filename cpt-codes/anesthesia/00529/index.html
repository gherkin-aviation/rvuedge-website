00529 Anesthesia clsd ch mediast & thrc with

Anesthesia

Also known as: VATS anesthesia with lung isolation, one-lung ventilation anesthesia

Anesthesia for closed chest procedures including mediastinoscopy and diagnostic thoracoscopy with one-lung ventilation. Covers general anesthesia requiring lung isolation via double-lumen tube or bronchial blocker.

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for thoracoscopic or mediastinoscopic procedures requiring one-lung ventilation. Requires double-lumen endotracheal tube or bronchial blocker placement. More complex anesthetic management than 00528 with attention to hypoxemia during OLV.
