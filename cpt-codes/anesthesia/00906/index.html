00906 Anesthesia vulvectomy

Anesthesia

Also known as: vulvectomy anesthesia, vulvar cancer surgery anesthesia

Anesthesia for vulvectomy. Covers general or regional anesthesia for surgical excision of the vulva, ranging from simple to radical vulvectomy, typically for vulvar carcinoma or severe vulvar intraepithelial neoplasia.

In Plain Language

anesthesia for vulvar surgery; being put to sleep for vulvar removal surgery

Clinical Context

Used when providing anesthesia for vulvectomy procedures including simple vulvectomy, radical vulvectomy with inguinal lymph node dissection, and skinning vulvectomy. Performed for vulvar squamous cell carcinoma, melanoma, or extensive VIN. May require lithotomy or modified lithotomy positioning.

RVU Information

CPT 00906 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Anesthesia codes use a base unit + time unit system rather than standard RVUs. Contact your payer for the anesthesia conversion factor.

Billing & Documentation

Anesthesia codes are billed using base units plus time units. One time unit typically equals 15 minutes of anesthesia time. Document start and stop times, patient status (P1-P6), and any qualifying circumstances. Modifiers AA, QK, QX, or QY indicate the provider arrangement.
