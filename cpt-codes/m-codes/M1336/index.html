M1336 Pts evaluation ini xm 2 wks

M Codes

Also known as: eye exam within 2 weeks, urgent retinal exam done, acute retinal evaluation

Patients with initial eye examination within 2 weeks. Documents appropriate urgent completion of retinal evaluation.

In Plain Language

eye doctor visit quickly; urgent retinal checkup completed

Clinical Context

Ophthalmology quality measure documenting timely completion of urgent retinal examination within 2-week window for acute retinal conditions.

RVU Information

CPT M1336 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

M-codes cover quality measures and performance reporting. They are used in value-based care programs to track whether specific clinical actions were taken. Documentation must support that the measured action was performed or a valid exclusion applies.
