M1043 Fs no odi 9-15mo

M Codes

Also known as: no disk impairment, ODI normal

Functional status shows no objective signs of diskogenic impairment 9 to 15 months after treatment.

In Plain Language

Spine disk fully recovered; No signs of disk problem

Clinical Context

Quality measure using Oswestry Disability Index (ODI) at 9-15 month follow-up showing successful treatment outcome for diskogenic back pain.

RVU Information

CPT M1043 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

M-codes cover quality measures and performance reporting. They are used in value-based care programs to track whether specific clinical actions were taken. Documentation must support that the measured action was performed or a valid exclusion applies.
