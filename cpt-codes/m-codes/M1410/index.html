M1410 No gmln brca1/brca2 couns

M Codes

Also known as: no BRCA counseling, missed genetic counseling, no gmln brca couns

Performance measure documenting that patient did not receive germline BRCA1 or BRCA2 genetic counseling. Identifies gap in hereditary cancer risk assessment.

In Plain Language

Genetic counseling not provided; Hereditary cancer risk not discussed

Clinical Context

Identifies patients not receiving appropriate genetic counseling for BRCA testing. Signals care gap in hereditary cancer risk assessment.

RVU Information

CPT M1410 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

M-codes cover quality measures and performance reporting. They are used in value-based care programs to track whether specific clinical actions were taken. Documentation must support that the measured action was performed or a valid exclusion applies.
