M1313 No tob scr/cess int

M Codes

Also known as: no tobacco care, tobacco assessment and cessation gap, incomplete tobacco management

No tobacco screening or cessation intervention provided. Documents failure to perform tobacco assessment or provide counseling.

In Plain Language

smoking not addressed; no smoking help provided

Clinical Context

Quality measure documenting comprehensive gaps in tobacco management where neither screening nor intervention was provided.

RVU Information

CPT M1313 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

M-codes cover quality measures and performance reporting. They are used in value-based care programs to track whether specific clinical actions were taken. Documentation must support that the measured action was performed or a valid exclusion applies.
