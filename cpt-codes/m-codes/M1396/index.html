M1396 Pt therapy clin trial

M Codes

Also known as: clinical trial participation, research protocol treatment, pt ther clin trial

Performance measure documenting that the patient is enrolled in or receiving therapeutic clinical trial for breast cancer treatment. Captures clinical research participation.

In Plain Language

Enrolled in cancer research study; Participating in experimental cancer treatment

Clinical Context

Identifies patients receiving treatment through clinical trial protocols. Appropriate denominator exclusion from standard treatment measures.

RVU Information

CPT M1396 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

M-codes cover quality measures and performance reporting. They are used in value-based care programs to track whether specific clinical actions were taken. Documentation must support that the measured action was performed or a valid exclusion applies.
