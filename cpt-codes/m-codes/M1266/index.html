M1266 Pts admit snf

M Codes

Also known as: SNF admission, skilled nursing care

In Plain Language

Admitted to nursing home; Skilled nursing facility stay

Clinical Context

Quality measure stratifier documenting skilled nursing facility admission for care coordination and outcomes tracking.

RVU Information

CPT M1266 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

M-codes cover quality measures and performance reporting. They are used in value-based care programs to track whether specific clinical actions were taken. Documentation must support that the measured action was performed or a valid exclusion applies.
