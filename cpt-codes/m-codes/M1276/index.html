M1276 Calc bmi out nrm param nof/u

M-Codes

Also known as: BMI normal, weight documented normal range, normal weight status

Body Mass Index (BMI) calculated and documented as within normal parameters. Confirms appropriate weight assessment and documentation during the measurement period.

Clinical Context

Used in preventive care measures to document that BMI screening and documentation were completed and patient weight is within healthy range (typically 18.5-24.9).
