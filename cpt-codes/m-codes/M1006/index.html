M1006 Dz not ases, no rsn

M Codes

Also known as: disease assessment not done, baseline assessment missing

Patient disease status not assessed and no reason documented for patient starting biologic disease-modifying antirheumatic drug.

In Plain Language

Disease severity evaluation not recorded; Baseline assessment gap

Clinical Context

Quality measure indicating failure to establish baseline disease activity before initiating biologic therapy. Essential for monitoring treatment response.

RVU Information

CPT M1006 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

M-codes cover quality measures and performance reporting. They are used in value-based care programs to track whether specific clinical actions were taken. Documentation must support that the measured action was performed or a valid exclusion applies.
