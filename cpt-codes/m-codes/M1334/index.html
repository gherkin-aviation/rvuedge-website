M1334 Pts act pvd 2 wks 2 wks

M Codes

Also known as: active PVD, acute proliferative disease, neovascularization acute

Patients with active proliferative retinopathy disease in the 2 weeks to 2 weeks timeframe. Documents acute phase of severe retinal disease.

In Plain Language

severe eye disease newly found; acute diabetic eye disease

Clinical Context

Retinal disease quality measure documenting patients with acute active proliferative changes requiring intensive urgent ophthalmology intervention.

RVU Information

CPT M1334 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

M-codes cover quality measures and performance reporting. They are used in value-based care programs to track whether specific clinical actions were taken. Documentation must support that the measured action was performed or a valid exclusion applies.
