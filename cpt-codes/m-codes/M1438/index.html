M1438 Lst knwn well <=210 minutes

M-Codes

Also known as: last known well <210 minutes, acute stroke window, lst knwn well <=210 min

Performance measure documenting last known well status with survival less than or equal to 210 minutes in acute stroke patients. Captures severe acute presentations.

Clinical Context

Documents acute ischemic stroke patients where last known well time was within 210 minutes, potentially eligible for thrombolytic or thrombectomy intervention.
