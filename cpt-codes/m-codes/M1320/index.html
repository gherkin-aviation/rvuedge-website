M1320 Pts screening + hrsn

M-Codes

Also known as: glaucoma screening normal, eye pressure normal, IOP within limits

Patient screened for glaucoma with normal test results and intraocular pressure. Documents appropriate eye pressure screening with normal findings.

Clinical Context

Ophthalmology quality measure documenting glaucoma screening with normal intraocular pressure (typically IOP ≤25 mmHg).
