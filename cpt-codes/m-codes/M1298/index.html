M1298 Doc pt preg dur msrmt pd

M Codes

Also known as: pregnant patient, pregnancy documented, maternity exclusion

Documented that patient is pregnant during the measurement period. Records pregnancy status as exclusion criterion for certain quality measures.

In Plain Language

patient is pregnant; expecting

Clinical Context

Quality measure exclusion identifying pregnant patients who may be ineligible for certain medications or screening measures due to pregnancy.

RVU Information

CPT M1298 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

M-codes cover quality measures and performance reporting. They are used in value-based care programs to track whether specific clinical actions were taken. Documentation must support that the measured action was performed or a valid exclusion applies.
