M1298 Doc pt preg dur msrmt pd

M-Codes

Also known as: pregnant patient, pregnancy documented, maternity exclusion

Documented that patient is pregnant during the measurement period. Records pregnancy status as exclusion criterion for certain quality measures.

Clinical Context

Quality measure exclusion identifying pregnant patients who may be ineligible for certain medications or screening measures due to pregnancy.
