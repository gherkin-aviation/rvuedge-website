M1188 Pt with ckd stg 5

M Codes

Also known as: CKD Stage 5, severe chronic kidney disease

Patient has chronic kidney disease Stage 5 without end-stage renal disease diagnosis during measurement period.

In Plain Language

Severe kidney damage; Severely reduced kidney function

Clinical Context

Quality measure stratifier for patients with severely reduced glomerular filtration rate (eGFR < 15 mL/min/1.73m2) but not yet on dialysis or transplanted.

RVU Information

CPT M1188 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

M-codes cover quality measures and performance reporting. They are used in value-based care programs to track whether specific clinical actions were taken. Documentation must support that the measured action was performed or a valid exclusion applies.
