M1277 Colorectal ca screening doc revision

M Codes

Also known as: colorectal screening, CRC screening, colon cancer screening

Colorectal cancer screening documented with documented review. Confirms completion and documentation of colorectal cancer screening with results review during measurement period.

In Plain Language

colon cancer screening; bowel cancer test

Clinical Context

Preventive health quality measure tracking completion of recommended colorectal cancer screening (colonoscopy, FIT, or FOBT) in adults age 50-75.

RVU Information

CPT M1277 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

M-codes cover quality measures and performance reporting. They are used in value-based care programs to track whether specific clinical actions were taken. Documentation must support that the measured action was performed or a valid exclusion applies.
