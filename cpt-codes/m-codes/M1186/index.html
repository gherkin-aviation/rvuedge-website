M1186 Pt with prescription for hspc/plltv care

M Codes

Also known as: hospice/palliative care enrollment, end-of-life services

Patient receives hospice or palliative care services during measurement period.

In Plain Language

Comfort-focused care services; End-of-life care enrollment

Clinical Context

Quality measure exclusion documenting enrollment in hospice or formal palliative care services, which may exempt patients from certain performance measures focused on curative treatment.

RVU Information

CPT M1186 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

M-codes cover quality measures and performance reporting. They are used in value-based care programs to track whether specific clinical actions were taken. Documentation must support that the measured action was performed or a valid exclusion applies.
