M1215 Medial rsn for no doc spiro

M Codes

Also known as: spirometry contraindication, medical exemption from pulmonary testing

Documented medical reason for not ordering or performing spirometry.

In Plain Language

Medical reason lung test not done; Valid reason for skipping spirometry

Clinical Context

Quality measure exclusion documenting medical contraindication to spirometry such as severe acute illness or inability to cooperate with testing.

RVU Information

CPT M1215 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

M-codes cover quality measures and performance reporting. They are used in value-based care programs to track whether specific clinical actions were taken. Documentation must support that the measured action was performed or a valid exclusion applies.
