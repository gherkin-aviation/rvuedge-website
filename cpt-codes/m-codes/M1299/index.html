M1299 Flu immunize order/administration

M Codes

Also known as: flu shot given, influenza vaccine, flu immunization

Influenza immunization ordered or administered. Documents completion of seasonal influenza vaccination.

In Plain Language

got flu shot; received flu vaccine

Clinical Context

Preventive care quality measure documenting completion of annual influenza vaccination in eligible patients (typically all ages, especially 6 months and older).

RVU Information

CPT M1299 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

M-codes cover quality measures and performance reporting. They are used in value-based care programs to track whether specific clinical actions were taken. Documentation must support that the measured action was performed or a valid exclusion applies.
