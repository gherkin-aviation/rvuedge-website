M1299 Flu immunize order/administration

M-Codes

Also known as: flu shot given, influenza vaccine, flu immunization

Influenza immunization ordered or administered. Documents completion of seasonal influenza vaccination.

Clinical Context

Preventive care quality measure documenting completion of annual influenza vaccination in eligible patients (typically all ages, especially 6 months and older).
