M Codes

Also known as: hospice care provided, end-of-life services

Hospice patient provided during specific time measurement per protocol.

In Plain Language

Hospice services given; End-of-life care delivery

Clinical Context

Documents provision of hospice services during measurement period. Indicates formalized comfort-focused end-of-life care.

RVU Information

CPT M1067 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

M-codes cover quality measures and performance reporting. They are used in value-based care programs to track whether specific clinical actions were taken. Documentation must support that the measured action was performed or a valid exclusion applies.
