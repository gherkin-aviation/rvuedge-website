M1381 Pt sec strk wthin 5 days

M Codes

Also known as: acute stroke event, 5-day stroke occurrence, cerebrovascular event

Performance measure documenting patient suffered a stroke within 5 days of measurement period. Captures acute cerebrovascular events occurring during monitoring timeframe.

In Plain Language

Recent stroke or brain attack; Acute blood vessel blockage in brain

Clinical Context

Documents occurrence of acute ischemic or hemorrhagic stroke within 5 days of measurement period. Important for tracking secondary prevention and rehabilitation needs.

RVU Information

CPT M1381 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

M-codes cover quality measures and performance reporting. They are used in value-based care programs to track whether specific clinical actions were taken. Documentation must support that the measured action was performed or a valid exclusion applies.
