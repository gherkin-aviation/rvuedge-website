M1370 Rehabilitation support msk care mvp

Also known as: rehabilitation MVP, musculoskeletal care MVP, rehab support MSK

Performance measure documenting rehabilitation support and musculoskeletal care coordination. This code represents the Measures Valued by Practices (MVP) category for rehabilitation and orthopedic care management.

Clinical Context

Tracks coordination of rehabilitation services and musculoskeletal care for patients with orthopedic conditions, injuries, or postoperative recovery needs.
