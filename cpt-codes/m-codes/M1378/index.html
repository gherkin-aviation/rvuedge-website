M1378 Medial rsn no 10 yr fu colscope

M-Codes

Also known as: no colonoscopy medical reason, colonoscopy contraindication, screening exception documentation

Performance measure documenting medical reason why 10-year follow-up colonoscopy was not performed. Captures exceptions to screening guidelines due to clinical contraindications.

Clinical Context

Documents medically appropriate exceptions to colorectal cancer screening, such as life expectancy <10 years, advanced age, or significant comorbidities precluding screening.
