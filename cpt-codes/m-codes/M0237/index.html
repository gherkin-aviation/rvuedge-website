M0237 Intrav inferior, toci, first dose

M-Codes

Also known as: toci infusion, first tocilizumab dose

Intravenous infusion of tocilizumab, first dose. Tocilizumab is an interleukin-6 receptor antagonist used primarily for rheumatoid arthritis and inflammatory conditions.

Clinical Context

Used in rheumatoid arthritis, giant cell arteritis, and polyarticular juvenile idiopathic arthritis when first administered intravenously. Represents the initial dose of this biologic therapy.
