M1392 Pt rsn no exm or lst to fu

M Codes

Also known as: melanoma surveillance barrier, pt no show reason, lost to follow-up documentation

Performance measure documenting patient reason for not receiving examination or lost to follow-up in melanoma surveillance. Captures care adherence barriers.

In Plain Language

Reason patient missed cancer check; Patient couldn't come to visit

Clinical Context

Documents appropriate reasons patients did not complete melanoma surveillance, including loss to follow-up or patient refusal. Important for care coordination efforts.

RVU Information

CPT M1392 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

M-codes cover quality measures and performance reporting. They are used in value-based care programs to track whether specific clinical actions were taken. Documentation must support that the measured action was performed or a valid exclusion applies.
