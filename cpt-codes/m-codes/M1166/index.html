M1166 Path report tissue specimen wle/reexc

M-Codes

Also known as: tissue pathology report, wide local excision pathology, WLE pathology documentation

Pathology report on tissue specimen received with wide local excision or re-excision documentation during measurement period.

Clinical Context

Used in quality measure tracking for surgical oncology cases where pathological examination confirms the extent of tumor removal with negative margins during wide local excision procedures.
