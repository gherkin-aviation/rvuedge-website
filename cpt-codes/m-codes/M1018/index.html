M1018 Pt diagnosis hst cr pt sk lg cr scr

M Codes

Also known as: cancer history screening, large cancer screening

Patient diagnosed with history of or current colorectal, breast, ovarian, or prostate cancer requiring large colorectal cancer screening.

In Plain Language

Cancer test with important history; Comprehensive cancer screening

Clinical Context

Documents screening approach in patients with personal history of malignancy. Requires more intensive surveillance protocols.

RVU Information

CPT M1018 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
