M1477 Diagnosis delirium

M-Codes

Also known as: Delirium diagnosis, Acute confusion

Patient has a diagnosis of delirium documented in the medical record.

Clinical Context

Documents acute confusional states in hospitalized or post-acute settings. Used to identify patients with temporary mental status changes requiring modified care approaches.
