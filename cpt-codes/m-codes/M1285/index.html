M1285 Screening mam perf rslts not doc

M Codes

Also known as: mammogram no documentation, mammography results missing, incomplete mammography record

Screening mammography performed with results not documented. Documents mammography completion without documented results in patient record.

In Plain Language

breast cancer screening test done; mammogram test not recorded

Clinical Context

Quality measure identifying gaps in cancer screening documentation where test was performed but results were not properly documented in the medical record.

RVU Information

CPT M1285 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

M-codes cover quality measures and performance reporting. They are used in value-based care programs to track whether specific clinical actions were taken. Documentation must support that the measured action was performed or a valid exclusion applies.
