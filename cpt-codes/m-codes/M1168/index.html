M1168 Pt recd flu vax 7/1-6/30

M Codes

Also known as: flu shot documentation, influenza vaccine administration, flu vaccination

Patient received influenza vaccination between July 1 and June 30 of the measurement year.

In Plain Language

Flu shot received; Annual influenza prevention

Clinical Context

Quality measure indicator documenting receipt of annual influenza vaccine during the standard flu season (July 1 to June 30) for preventive health maintenance.

RVU Information

CPT M1168 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

M-codes cover quality measures and performance reporting. They are used in value-based care programs to track whether specific clinical actions were taken. Documentation must support that the measured action was performed or a valid exclusion applies.
