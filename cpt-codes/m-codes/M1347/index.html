M1347 Pt pam incr 3 pt 4-12 mo

M Codes

Also known as: PAM improved 3+ points, patient activation increased, activation improvement

Patient with Patient Activation Measure increase of 3 points or greater in 4-12 month period. Documents modest improvement in patient activation.

In Plain Language

patient engagement improved somewhat

Clinical Context

Care management quality measure documenting modest improvement in Patient Activation Measure indicating increased patient engagement in self-management.

RVU Information

CPT M1347 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

M-codes cover quality measures and performance reporting. They are used in value-based care programs to track whether specific clinical actions were taken. Documentation must support that the measured action was performed or a valid exclusion applies.
