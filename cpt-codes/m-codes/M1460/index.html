M1460 Diagnosis copd, cf, or arf

M-Codes

Also known as: COPD/CF/ARF diagnosis, chronic lung disease documented, dx copd, cf, or arf

Performance measure documenting diagnosis of Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease (COPD), cystic fibrosis (CF), or acute respiratory failure (ARF). Captures significant respiratory disease.

Clinical Context

Documents diagnosis of COPD, cystic fibrosis, or acute respiratory failure. Important denominator exclusion for asthma-specific measures.
