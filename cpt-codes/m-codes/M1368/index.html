M1368 Preventive trt inferior d/o hiv/hep mvp

M Codes

Also known as: preventive infectious disease MVP, HIV/Hep prevention measure, infectious disease prevention tracking

Performance measure indicating whether preventive treatment for infectious disease was documented, including Human Immunodeficiency Virus and Hepatitis. This code captures the Measures Valued by Practices (MVP) category for preventive infectious disease care.

In Plain Language

Preventive care for blood-borne diseases; Tracking prevention of HIV and hepatitis

Clinical Context

Used in quality reporting to document whether preventive interventions for Human Immunodeficiency Virus and Hepatitis were discussed or implemented. Commonly tracked in primary care and infectious disease specialties.

RVU Information

CPT M1368 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

M-codes cover quality measures and performance reporting. They are used in value-based care programs to track whether specific clinical actions were taken. Documentation must support that the measured action was performed or a valid exclusion applies.
