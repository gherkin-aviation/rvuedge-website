M1197 Isa reduced >=3 fr ixv

M Codes

Also known as: symptom improvement documented, clinically significant symptom reduction

Symptom intensity or quality or affect score reduced by 3 or more points from initial or previous symptom assessment.

In Plain Language

Significant improvement in symptom control; Effective symptom management

Clinical Context

Palliative and oncology quality measure numerator documenting clinically meaningful improvement in symptom severity indicating effective symptom management intervention.

RVU Information

CPT M1197 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

M-codes cover quality measures and performance reporting. They are used in value-based care programs to track whether specific clinical actions were taken. Documentation must support that the measured action was performed or a valid exclusion applies.
