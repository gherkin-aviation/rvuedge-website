M1441 initial diagnosis slp apn = first enc

M Codes

Also known as: sleep apnea initial diagnosis, sleep study at first visit, init dx slp apn

Performance measure documenting initial diagnosis of sleep apnea where the initial diagnostic study equals the first encounter. Tracks sleep apnea diagnosis.

In Plain Language

Sleep breathing problem diagnosed at first visit; Initial sleep disorder diagnosis

Clinical Context

Documents initial diagnosis of obstructive sleep apnea where diagnostic sleep study was performed at first encounter. Tracks diagnosis timeliness.

RVU Information

CPT M1441 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

M-codes cover quality measures and performance reporting. They are used in value-based care programs to track whether specific clinical actions were taken. Documentation must support that the measured action was performed or a valid exclusion applies.
