M1165 Pt use hspc dur meas pd

M Codes

Also known as: hospice use, end-of-life services

Patient used hospice services at any time during the measurement period.

In Plain Language

Used comfort care services during period; Hospice care utilization

Clinical Context

Quality measure documenting any hospice utilization during measurement period. May indicate terminal diagnosis or advanced palliative care engagement.

RVU Information

CPT M1165 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

M-codes cover quality measures and performance reporting. They are used in value-based care programs to track whether specific clinical actions were taken. Documentation must support that the measured action was performed or a valid exclusion applies.
