M1449 Pt diagnosis scz/pys bef end msr pd

M Codes

Also known as: schizophrenia diagnosis, psychotic disorder documented, pt dx scz/pys bef end

Performance measure documenting diagnosis of schizophrenia or psychotic disorder before the end of the measurement period. Captures serious mental illness.

In Plain Language

Schizophrenia or severe psychosis diagnosed; Serious mental illness documented

Clinical Context

Documents diagnosis of schizophrenia spectrum or psychotic disorder. Often represents denominator exclusion from depression and behavioral health measures.

RVU Information

CPT M1449 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

M-codes cover quality measures and performance reporting. They are used in value-based care programs to track whether specific clinical actions were taken. Documentation must support that the measured action was performed or a valid exclusion applies.
