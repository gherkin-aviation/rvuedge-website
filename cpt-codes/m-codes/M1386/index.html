M1386 excision surgery melmn or mlnm is

M-Codes

Also known as: melanoma excision exclusion, skin malignancy treatment prior, exc sx melanoma

Performance measure documenting exclusion from melanoma screening due to excision or surgical treatment of skin lesion or malignancy. Indicates prior definitive treatment.

Clinical Context

Identifies patients appropriately excluded from melanoma screening measures because they received prior surgical treatment for melanoma or significant skin lesions.
