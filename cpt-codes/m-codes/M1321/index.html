M1321 Pts no 7wk inj,no iop,iop>25

M-Codes

Also known as: no glaucoma screening, high eye pressure, IOP >25 or not checked

Patient with no eye injection in prior 7 weeks, no intraocular pressure documented, or intraocular pressure greater than 25 mmHg. Documents glaucoma screening gap or elevated pressure.

Clinical Context

Ophthalmology quality measure identifying patients with inadequate glaucoma monitoring or elevated intraocular pressure requiring intervention.
