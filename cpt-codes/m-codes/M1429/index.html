M1429 Retinopathy in od, os, ou

M-Codes

Also known as: retinopathy documented, retinal disease present, diabetic retinopathy

Performance measure documenting presence of retinopathy in one eye (oculus dexter), the other eye (oculus sinister), or both eyes (oculus uterque). Captures diabetic and other retinal disease.

Clinical Context

Documents presence of diabetic retinopathy, hypertensive retinopathy, or other retinal disease in either or both eyes. Important for diabetes care quality measures.
