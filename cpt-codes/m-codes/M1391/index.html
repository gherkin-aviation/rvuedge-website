M1391 All pt diagnosis with rec mlnm

M Codes

Also known as: recurrent melanoma diagnosis, melanoma recurrence, all pt dx rec mlnm

Performance measure documenting that all patients diagnosed with recurrent melanoma were identified during the measurement period. Captures melanoma recurrence incidence.

In Plain Language

Melanoma cancer came back; Return of skin cancer

Clinical Context

Identifies patients with documented recurrent or metastatic melanoma during measurement period. Important for tracking disease progression and treatment outcomes.

RVU Information

CPT M1391 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

M-codes cover quality measures and performance reporting. They are used in value-based care programs to track whether specific clinical actions were taken. Documentation must support that the measured action was performed or a valid exclusion applies.
