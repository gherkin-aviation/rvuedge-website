M1385 Pt rsn not seen second pam

M Codes

Also known as: PAM second visit not completed, physical activity measure exclusion, pt not seen PAM

Performance measure documenting that the patient had a reason not to be seen for a second Physical Activity Measure (PAM). Documents denominator exclusion for second assessment.

In Plain Language

Patient missed follow-up physical activity assessment; Reason for skipping repeat activity screening

Clinical Context

Documents appropriate exclusion reasons for patients not returning for repeat physical activity measures. Important for quality reporting accuracy.

RVU Information

CPT M1385 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

M-codes cover quality measures and performance reporting. They are used in value-based care programs to track whether specific clinical actions were taken. Documentation must support that the measured action was performed or a valid exclusion applies.
