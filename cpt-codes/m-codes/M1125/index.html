M1125 Oc ni pt selfdc 1-2 vis

M-Codes

Also known as: OT brief self-discharge, early patient-directed discharge

Occupational therapy intervention not indicated as patient is providing self-discharge within 1 to 2 visits.

Clinical Context

Documents that occupational therapy is not continued because patient self-discharged after minimal interventions.
