M0076 Prolotherapy

M Codes

Also known as: prolotherapy, proliferation therapy

Prolotherapy Services. Reporting code for proliferative soft tissue injection therapies.

In Plain Language

injection therapy for ligaments; tissue regeneration treatment

Clinical Context

Used for reporting proliferation therapy and chronic pain treatment involving therapeutic substance injection into ligaments/tendons.

RVU Information

CPT M0076 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

M-codes cover quality measures and performance reporting. They are used in value-based care programs to track whether specific clinical actions were taken. Documentation must support that the measured action was performed or a valid exclusion applies.
