M1497 No doc fall medial rsn

M Codes

Also known as: Fall assessment contraindicated, No assessment with reason

No documented assessment of falls is present and there is a documented medical reason for not assessing fall risk.

In Plain Language

Fall risk not assessed for medical reason

Clinical Context

Documents appropriate exclusions from fall risk assessment due to medical contraindications or patient factors.

RVU Information

CPT M1497 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

M-codes cover quality measures and performance reporting. They are used in value-based care programs to track whether specific clinical actions were taken. Documentation must support that the measured action was performed or a valid exclusion applies.
