M1497 No doc fall medial rsn

M-Codes

Also known as: Fall assessment contraindicated, No assessment with reason

No documented assessment of falls is present and there is a documented medical reason for not assessing fall risk.

Clinical Context

Documents appropriate exclusions from fall risk assessment due to medical contraindications or patient factors.
