M1325 Doc medial rsn not seen

M Codes

Also known as: documented medical reason, medical excuse for visit miss, justified absence

Documented medical reason not seen at visit. Records valid clinical explanation for missing scheduled appointment or assessment.

In Plain Language

good reason not seen; medical reason for missing visit

Clinical Context

Quality measure exception documenting medical reasons (hospitalization, emergency care, acute illness) explaining missed follow-up visits.

RVU Information

CPT M1325 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

M-codes cover quality measures and performance reporting. They are used in value-based care programs to track whether specific clinical actions were taken. Documentation must support that the measured action was performed or a valid exclusion applies.
