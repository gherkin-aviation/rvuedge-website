M1382 Enc dur perf pd pos 11

M-Codes

Also known as: PHQ-11 depression screening, depression symptom encounter, mental health screening positive

Performance measure documenting an encounter that occurred during the measurement period with a documented Patient Health Questionnaire-11 (PHQ-11) score of 11 or greater.

Clinical Context

Identifies patients with elevated depression symptoms during encounters using PHQ-11 screening tool. Score ≥11 indicates depression requiring intervention.
