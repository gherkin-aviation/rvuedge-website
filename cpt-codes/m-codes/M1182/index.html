M1182 Not elg pre ex ibd/uc/crohn

M-Codes

Also known as: IBD/UC/Crohn's exclusion from ICI screening, pre-existing colitis exclusion

Patient is not eligible for pre-existing immune-related adverse event assessment due to inflammatory bowel disease, ulcerative colitis, or Crohn's disease diagnosis.

Clinical Context

Quality measure exclusion for patients with pre-existing inflammatory bowel disease, as they have increased risk for severe immune-related colitis with checkpoint inhibitor therapy.
