M1220 Dre wth interpretation rtnopthy

M Codes

Also known as: diabetic retinopathy screening positive, dilated eye exam with retinopathy

Dilated retinal eye examination with interpretation showing retinopathy.

In Plain Language

Eye exam shows diabetic eye damage; Retinal damage from diabetes detected

Clinical Context

Quality measure documenting dilated retinal examination with documented interpretation confirming diabetic or other retinopathy.

RVU Information

CPT M1220 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

M-codes cover quality measures and performance reporting. They are used in value-based care programs to track whether specific clinical actions were taken. Documentation must support that the measured action was performed or a valid exclusion applies.
