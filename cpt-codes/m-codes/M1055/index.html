M1055 Aspirin used

M Codes

Also known as: aspirin use documented, antiplatelet therapy

Patient is using aspirin therapy during the measurement period.

In Plain Language

Daily aspirin for heart protection; Blood thinner medication

Clinical Context

Quality measure documenting use of aspirin for cardiovascular prevention in patients with indicated conditions such as prior MI, stroke, or coronary disease.

RVU Information

CPT M1055 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

M-codes cover quality measures and performance reporting. They are used in value-based care programs to track whether specific clinical actions were taken. Documentation must support that the measured action was performed or a valid exclusion applies.
