M0249 Adm tocilizu covid-19 first

M Codes

Also known as: COVID tocilizumab first, toci COVID first dose

Administration of tocilizumab for coronavirus disease 2019 (COVID-19), first dose. Tocilizumab is used to reduce immune hyperactivation in severe COVID-19.

In Plain Language

Immune-controlling therapy for severe COVID; First anti-inflammatory infusion

Clinical Context

Administered in hospitalized patients with severe COVID-19 showing evidence of systemic inflammatory response. Used as an initial dose for immune modulation.

RVU Information

CPT M0249 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
