M1039 Pt diagnosis lum sp reg inferior

M Codes

Also known as: spinal infection, osteomyelitis lumbar

Patient diagnosed with infection of the lumbar spine region.

In Plain Language

Lower spine infection; Bacterial spine infection

Clinical Context

Documents infectious process affecting lumbar vertebrae, intervertebral discs, or paraspinal tissues. Requires antimicrobial and sometimes surgical management.

RVU Information

CPT M1039 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

M-codes cover quality measures and performance reporting. They are used in value-based care programs to track whether specific clinical actions were taken. Documentation must support that the measured action was performed or a valid exclusion applies.
