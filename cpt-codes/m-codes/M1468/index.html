M1468 Hep b vaccine received

M Codes

Also known as: Hepatitis B vaccination documented, HBV vaccine received, hep b vaccine received

Performance measure documenting that Hepatitis B vaccine was received. Tracks immunization compliance.

In Plain Language

Hepatitis B shot given; Liver disease vaccine received

Clinical Context

Documents completion of Hepatitis B vaccination series. Important preventive measure for high-risk populations and universal immunization.

RVU Information

CPT M1468 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

M-codes cover quality measures and performance reporting. They are used in value-based care programs to track whether specific clinical actions were taken. Documentation must support that the measured action was performed or a valid exclusion applies.
