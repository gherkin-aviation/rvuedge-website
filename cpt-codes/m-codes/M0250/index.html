M0250 Adm tocilizu covid-19 second

M-Codes

Also known as: COVID tocilizumab second, toci COVID second dose

Administration of tocilizumab for coronavirus disease 2019 (COVID-19), second dose. Continuation of immune modulation therapy for severe COVID-19.

Clinical Context

Administered as subsequent dose in hospitalized COVID-19 patients requiring continued immune suppression to prevent acute respiratory distress syndrome.
