M1274 Pts snf exl mo

M-Codes

Also known as: SNF prior stay, nursing home history, facility residence

Patient in skilled nursing facility (SNF) excluding the current measurement month. Documents patients transferred to or already residing in long-term care facilities prior to measurement period.

Clinical Context

Used to identify patients with SNF residency history for stratification in ESRD quality measures and to adjust comparisons for facility-level care quality.
