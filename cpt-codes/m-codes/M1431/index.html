M1431 Telehealth enc

M Codes

Also known as: telehealth visit, virtual appointment, remote consultation

Performance measure documenting an encounter conducted via telehealth or remote communication technology. Tracks virtual visit utilization.

In Plain Language

Doctor visit by video or phone; Online healthcare appointment

Clinical Context

Documents encounters performed using telehealth technology including video visit, phone consultation, or virtual care platforms.

RVU Information

CPT M1431 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

M-codes cover quality measures and performance reporting. They are used in value-based care programs to track whether specific clinical actions were taken. Documentation must support that the measured action was performed or a valid exclusion applies.
