Also known as: CRC screening counseled opt out, colorectal screening patient decline, documented screening refusal

Patients counseled on colorectal cancer screening with documented patient choice to opt out. Documents shared decision-making with patient refusal.

Quality measure exception documenting that patient was counseled on colorectal cancer screening options but made informed decision to decline.
