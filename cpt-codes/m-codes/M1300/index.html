M1300 Flu imm no administration doc rea

M-Codes

Also known as: flu vaccine not given, flu vaccination exception, documented flu vaccine refusal

Influenza immunization not administered with documented reason. Records valid exceptions for not administering flu vaccine.

Clinical Context

Quality measure exception documenting valid reasons for not administering flu vaccine (medical contraindication, prior severe reaction, patient refusal).
