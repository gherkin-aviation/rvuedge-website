M1335 Doc pts rsn no follow-up xm

M Codes

Also known as: documented acute exam exception, urgent follow-up documented excuse

Documented reason for no follow-up eye examination within 2-week window. Records exceptions for acute retinal condition monitoring.

In Plain Language

good reason for not seeing eye doctor urgently

Clinical Context

Ophthalmology quality measure exception documenting valid reasons for not completing urgent follow-up eye examination within critical 2-week window.

RVU Information

CPT M1335 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

M-codes cover quality measures and performance reporting. They are used in value-based care programs to track whether specific clinical actions were taken. Documentation must support that the measured action was performed or a valid exclusion applies.
