M1329 Pts act pvd 2 wks 8 wks

M Codes

Also known as: active proliferative retinopathy, PVD active, neovascularization

Patients with active proliferative retinopathy in the 2 weeks to 8 weeks timeframe. Documents patients with advanced retinal disease requiring intensive monitoring.

In Plain Language

severe diabetic eye disease; aggressive eye disease

Clinical Context

Retinal disease quality measure tracking patients with active neovascularization and proliferative changes requiring frequent monitoring and treatment.

RVU Information

CPT M1329 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation
