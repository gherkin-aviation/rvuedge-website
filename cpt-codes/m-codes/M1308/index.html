M1308 Flu immunize no administration

M Codes

Also known as: flu vaccine not given, flu immunization missed, influenza vaccine not documented

Influenza immunization not administered. Documents patients not receiving flu vaccine during measurement period.

In Plain Language

didn't get flu shot; no flu vaccine

Clinical Context

Quality measure documenting gaps in influenza vaccination where eligible patients did not receive annual flu vaccine.

RVU Information

CPT M1308 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

M-codes cover quality measures and performance reporting. They are used in value-based care programs to track whether specific clinical actions were taken. Documentation must support that the measured action was performed or a valid exclusion applies.
