M1414 Medial rsn no pdl1 bef first therapy

M Codes

Also known as: no PD-L1 testing reason, medical exception PD-L1, med rsn no pdl1

Performance measure documenting medical reason why PD-L1 testing was not performed before first-line immunotherapy initiation. Captures appropriate exceptions.

In Plain Language

Doctor's reason for skipping cancer protein test; Medical exception to tumor testing

Clinical Context

Documents medical contraindications to PD-L1 testing such as limited tissue availability or rapidly progressive disease requiring immediate treatment.

RVU Information

CPT M1414 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

M-codes cover quality measures and performance reporting. They are used in value-based care programs to track whether specific clinical actions were taken. Documentation must support that the measured action was performed or a valid exclusion applies.
