M1304 No pneum vax administration 19+

M-Codes

Also known as: pneumococcal vaccine not given, no pneumonia vaccine, pneumococcal vaccination missed

Patient age 19 and older did not receive pneumococcal vaccination. Documents patients who are due for pneumococcal vaccine but did not receive it.

Clinical Context

Quality measure documenting gaps in pneumococcal vaccination where eligible patients (especially those 65+ or with chronic conditions) did not receive appropriate vaccine.
