M1373 Mst rec gsa >=8 and <=9

M Codes

Also known as: GSA 8 to 9, geriatric good function, GSA score 8-9

Performance measure documenting most recent Global Subjective Assessment (GSA) score greater than or equal to 8 and less than or equal to 9 in geriatric care. Indicates relatively preserved function.

In Plain Language

Good functional ability in older adults; Mostly independent functioning

Clinical Context

Measures preserved functional status in geriatric populations. GSA score between 8 and 9 represents better functional independence with minimal support needs.

RVU Information

CPT M1373 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

M-codes cover quality measures and performance reporting. They are used in value-based care programs to track whether specific clinical actions were taken. Documentation must support that the measured action was performed or a valid exclusion applies.
