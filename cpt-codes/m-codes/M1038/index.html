M1038 Pt diagnosis lum sp reg fracture

M Codes

Also known as: lumbar fracture diagnosis, spinal compression fracture

Patient diagnosed with fracture of the lumbar spine region.

In Plain Language

Lower spine broken bone; Lumbar vertebra fracture

Clinical Context

Documents traumatic or pathologic fracture of lumbar vertebrae. Common in osteoporosis, trauma, or metastatic disease.

RVU Information

CPT M1038 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

M-codes cover quality measures and performance reporting. They are used in value-based care programs to track whether specific clinical actions were taken. Documentation must support that the measured action was performed or a valid exclusion applies.
