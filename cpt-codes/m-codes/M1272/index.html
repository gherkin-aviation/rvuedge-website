M1272 Pts kid transplt wtlst

M-Codes

Also known as: transplant recipient, kidney transplant patient, post-transplant

Patient with kidney transplant on waiting list. Documents ESRD patients who have a functioning kidney transplant and are considered for transplant candidacy.

Clinical Context

Used in ESRD quality measures to identify and track patients who have received kidney transplantation and are no longer on dialysis.
