M1377 Fu colscop 10 yr doc with disc

M-Codes

Also known as: 10-year colonoscopy follow-up, colon cancer screening interval, fu colscop discussion doc

Performance measure documenting 10-year follow-up colonoscopy with documentation of discussion. Tracks adherence to colorectal cancer screening guidelines in previously screened patients.

Clinical Context

Ensures patients who previously had normal colonoscopies receive appropriate 10-year follow-up screening per national guidelines. Critical for colorectal cancer prevention.
