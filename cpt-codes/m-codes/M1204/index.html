M Codes

In Plain Language

Moderate to severe baseline symptoms; High initial symptom score

Clinical Context

Palliative care quality measure documenting baseline symptom severity for treatment monitoring and efficacy tracking.

RVU Information

CPT M1204 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation
