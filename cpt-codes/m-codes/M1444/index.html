M1444 Del < 39 weeks gest

M Codes

Also known as: preterm delivery, early delivery documented, del < 39 weeks gest

Performance measure documenting delivery occurring before 39 weeks gestation. Captures preterm or early term deliveries.

In Plain Language

Baby born before due date; Early delivery documented

Clinical Context

Documents deliveries occurring before 39 weeks gestation. Important for tracking obstetric outcomes and potential complications of prematurity.

RVU Information

CPT M1444 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

M-codes cover quality measures and performance reporting. They are used in value-based care programs to track whether specific clinical actions were taken. Documentation must support that the measured action was performed or a valid exclusion applies.
