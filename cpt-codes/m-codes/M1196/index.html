M Codes

Also known as: baseline symptom score assessment, high symptom burden documented

In Plain Language

Moderate to severe initial symptom score; High baseline distress measured

Clinical Context

Palliative and oncology quality measure documenting baseline symptom severity using numeric rating scales to track treatment response and symptom improvement.

RVU Information
