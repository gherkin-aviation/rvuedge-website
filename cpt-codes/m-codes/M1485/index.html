M1485 Neg viro resp hep c/qualitative rna

M-Codes

Also known as: Negative virologic response, Treatment failure

Patient has a negative virologic response to hepatitis C treatment based on qualitative hepatitis C virus ribonucleic acid (RNA) testing.

Clinical Context

Documents patients who did not achieve virologic cure despite antiviral treatment, indicating treatment failure or need for alternative therapy.
