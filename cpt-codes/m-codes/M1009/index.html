M1009 Dc eoc doc medial rec

M Codes

Also known as: discharge documentation, end of care note

Discharge or end of care documented in patient medical record at the conclusion of episode.

In Plain Language

End of treatment record; Discharge summary

Clinical Context

Required documentation marking formal conclusion of care episode. Includes discharge summary with findings, treatment provided, and disposition.

RVU Information

CPT M1009 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

M-codes cover quality measures and performance reporting. They are used in value-based care programs to track whether specific clinical actions were taken. Documentation must support that the measured action was performed or a valid exclusion applies.
