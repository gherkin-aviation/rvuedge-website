M1273 Pts snf 1 yr dialysis

M Codes

Also known as: SNF dialysis patient, nursing home dialysis, facility-based renal care

Patient admitted to skilled nursing facility (SNF) for one year of dialysis treatment. Documents nursing home placement for ESRD patients requiring intensive long-term renal replacement therapy.

In Plain Language

nursing home kidney care; assisted living dialysis

Clinical Context

Identifies ESRD patients in facility settings receiving dialysis, important for quality assessment of facility-based nephrology care and appropriate management protocols.

RVU Information

CPT M1273 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

M-codes cover quality measures and performance reporting. They are used in value-based care programs to track whether specific clinical actions were taken. Documentation must support that the measured action was performed or a valid exclusion applies.
