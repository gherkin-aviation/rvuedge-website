M1213 No history spiro prs spiro>=70%

M Codes

Also known as: normal spirometry in COPD screening, no obstructive disease on testing

Patient has no history of spirometry performed and spirometry result shows forced expiratory volume in 1 second of 70 percent or greater.

In Plain Language

Lung function test normal; No airway obstruction detected

Clinical Context

COPD quality measure tracking spirometry results in patients with symptoms, identifying normal lung function to rule out obstructive disease.

RVU Information

CPT M1213 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

M-codes cover quality measures and performance reporting. They are used in value-based care programs to track whether specific clinical actions were taken. Documentation must support that the measured action was performed or a valid exclusion applies.
