M1179 No pcv recd

M Codes

Also known as: no PCV vaccination, incomplete pneumococcal protection

Patient did not receive pneumococcal conjugate vaccine during measurement period.

In Plain Language

Did not receive pneumonia vaccine; Missed pneumococcal protection

Clinical Context

Quality measure denominator tracking patients without appropriate pneumococcal conjugate vaccination, indicating gap in preventive immunization.

RVU Information

CPT M1179 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

M-codes cover quality measures and performance reporting. They are used in value-based care programs to track whether specific clinical actions were taken. Documentation must support that the measured action was performed or a valid exclusion applies.
