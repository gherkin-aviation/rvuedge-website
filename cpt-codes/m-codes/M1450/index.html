M1450 Pt hos/pal care dur id/pf pd

M Codes

Also known as: hospice/palliative care received, end-of-life care documented, pt hos/pal care dur

Performance measure documenting patient received hospice or palliative care during the initiation or performance period. Captures end-of-life care.

In Plain Language

Comfort and end-of-life care received; Hospice or palliative care services

Clinical Context

Documents patient receiving hospice or palliative care services. Often appropriate denominator exclusion from disease-management measures.

RVU Information

CPT M1450 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

M-codes cover quality measures and performance reporting. They are used in value-based care programs to track whether specific clinical actions were taken. Documentation must support that the measured action was performed or a valid exclusion applies.
