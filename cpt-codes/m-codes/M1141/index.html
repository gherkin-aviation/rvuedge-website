M1141 Fs no oks

M Codes

Also known as: no functional improvement, poor ODI outcome

Functional status shows no objective signs of improvement using Oswestry Disability Index outcomes scoring.

In Plain Language

Disability scores not improving; No progress on disability test

Clinical Context

Quality measure documenting lack of functional improvement on validated disability scale. May indicate need for treatment modification.

RVU Information

CPT M1141 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

M-codes cover quality measures and performance reporting. They are used in value-based care programs to track whether specific clinical actions were taken. Documentation must support that the measured action was performed or a valid exclusion applies.
