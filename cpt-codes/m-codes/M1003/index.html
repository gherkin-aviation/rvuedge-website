M1003 Tb scr 12 mo pri fst bio dz

M-Codes

Also known as: TB screening pre-biologic, tuberculosis baseline test

Tuberculosis screening within 12 months prior to initiation of first biologic disease-modifying antirheumatic drug. Assessment for active or latent tuberculosis infection.

Clinical Context

Required before starting TNF inhibitors or other biologic therapies due to increased risk of TB reactivation. Includes skin testing, chest X-ray, or interferon-gamma release assays.
