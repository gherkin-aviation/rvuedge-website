M1375 Ra diagnosis enc 90 days dur per pd

M Codes

Also known as: RA diagnostic encounter, rheumatoid arthritis visit, RA 90-day encounter

Performance measure documenting a rheumatoid arthritis diagnostic encounter within a 90-day measurement period. Tracks continuity of rheumatology or primary care follow-up for disease management.

In Plain Language

Rheumatoid arthritis checkup; Joint disease follow-up visit

Clinical Context

Monitors whether patients with rheumatoid arthritis receive appropriate diagnostic and management encounters. Essential for disease control and preventing complications.

RVU Information

CPT M1375 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

M-codes cover quality measures and performance reporting. They are used in value-based care programs to track whether specific clinical actions were taken. Documentation must support that the measured action was performed or a valid exclusion applies.
