M1148 Pt self dschg

M Codes

Also known as: self-discharge, AMA discharge

Patient self-discharged from care episode against medical advice or recommendation.

In Plain Language

Patient left treatment early; Patient signed out against advice

Clinical Context

Documents that patient chose to discontinue care despite clinician recommendation. May indicate non-compliance or dissatisfaction.

RVU Information

CPT M1148 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

M-codes cover quality measures and performance reporting. They are used in value-based care programs to track whether specific clinical actions were taken. Documentation must support that the measured action was performed or a valid exclusion applies.
