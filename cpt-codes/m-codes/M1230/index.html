M1230 Pt hcv rctv aby no follow-up tst

M Codes

Also known as: HCV antibody positive, no confirmatory test, incomplete hepatitis C testing

Patient with reactive hepatitis C virus antibody with no follow-up testing to confirm infection status.

In Plain Language

Hepatitis C screening positive, no follow-up; Initial positive test not confirmed

Clinical Context

Hepatitis C quality measure identifying patients requiring confirmation testing with hepatitis C viral RNA or recombinant immunoblot assay.

RVU Information

CPT M1230 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

M-codes cover quality measures and performance reporting. They are used in value-based care programs to track whether specific clinical actions were taken. Documentation must support that the measured action was performed or a valid exclusion applies.
