M1268 Pt acute stat kid trnsplt wtlst

M-Codes

Also known as: kidney transplant wait list, renal transplant candidate, transplant list status

Patient on active kidney transplant waiting list. Documents patients who are currently listed for kidney transplant and awaiting a donor organ.

Clinical Context

Used in end-stage renal disease (ESRD) quality measures to identify dialysis patients who are actively listed for kidney transplantation. This helps track patients pursuing curative treatment options.
