M1262 Pts transplt bef dialysis

M Codes

Also known as: pre-emptive kidney transplantation, transplant before dialysis

Patient received kidney transplant before initiating dialysis.

In Plain Language

Kidney transplant before dialysis needed; Pre-emptive kidney transplant

Clinical Context

Renal quality measure documenting successful pre-emptive kidney transplantation avoiding dialysis requirement.

RVU Information

CPT M1262 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

M-codes cover quality measures and performance reporting. They are used in value-based care programs to track whether specific clinical actions were taken. Documentation must support that the measured action was performed or a valid exclusion applies.
