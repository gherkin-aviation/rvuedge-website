M1040 Pt diagnosis lum idi or cong scol

M-Codes

Also known as: lumbar scoliosis, congenital spine curvature

Patient diagnosed with idiopathic or congenital scoliosis of the lumbar region.

Clinical Context

Documents abnormal lateral curvature of lumbar spine, either developmental or of unknown etiology. May be associated with pain or progressive deformity.
