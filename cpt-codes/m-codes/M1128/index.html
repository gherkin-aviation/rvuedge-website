M1128 Oc ni pt home prog

M Codes

Also known as: OT not indicated home prog, therapy contraindication

Occupational therapy intervention not indicated as patient is in home program.

In Plain Language

Physical therapy type not needed; Home exercise program instead

Clinical Context

Documents clinical determination that formal occupational therapy intervention is not indicated in favor of home-based independent program.

RVU Information

CPT M1128 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

M-codes cover quality measures and performance reporting. They are used in value-based care programs to track whether specific clinical actions were taken. Documentation must support that the measured action was performed or a valid exclusion applies.
