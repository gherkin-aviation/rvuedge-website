1183F Neuropsychiatric symp absent

Other

Neuropsychiatric symptoms are documented as absent with no behavioral, cognitive, or psychiatric symptoms noted in the patient's medical record.

In Plain Language

Mental health symptoms not detected; No behavioral problems identified

Clinical Context

Quality measure indicating the absence of problematic neuropsychiatric symptoms in patients with neurological disorders, representing optimal symptom control.

RVU Information

CPT 1183F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
