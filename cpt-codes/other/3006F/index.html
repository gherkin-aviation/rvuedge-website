3006F Cxr doc revision

Other

Also known as: CXR documented, chest film review, chest imaging review

Documentation of chest X-ray review during a patient encounter, indicating that imaging was examined and findings were incorporated into clinical assessment.

In Plain Language

chest X-ray review noted

Clinical Context

Performance measure code used to document physician review of chest imaging as part of quality reporting and care documentation; supports imaging appropriateness.

RVU Information

CPT 3006F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation
