1180F Thromboemb risk assessed

Other

Thromboembolic risk assessment is documented as having been performed and recorded in the patient's medical record.

In Plain Language

Blood clot risk evaluation; Assessment for risk of stroke or clot

Clinical Context

Quality measure used to track whether providers assess patients for venous thromboembolism and stroke risk, particularly important in acute care and surgical settings.

RVU Information

CPT 1180F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
