American Joint Committee on Cancer breast cancer stage 3 documented; quality measure indicating that American Joint Committee on Cancer staging has been recorded for breast cancer patients with stage 3 disease.

In Plain Language

Breast cancer stage 3 recorded; Advanced breast cancer stage documented

Clinical Context

Quality/performance measure used in oncology practices to ensure proper documentation of American Joint Committee on Cancer staging for breast cancer. AJCC stage 3 represents locally advanced breast cancer without distant metastasis.

RVU Information
