3270F No bone scn b/4 txmnt/aftrdx

Other

Also known as: no bone scan documentation, bone imaging non-performance, imaging workup decision

Performance measure documenting that bone scan imaging was not performed before treatment or after diagnosis of cancer when clinically indicated. Quality metric for appropriate test ordering decisions.

In Plain Language

documentation that bone scan was not needed

Clinical Context

Quality measure documenting appropriate clinical judgment when bone scanning is not indicated based on cancer type or stage, reducing unnecessary imaging and healthcare costs.

RVU Information

CPT 3270F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
