4305F Pt ed re ft care inspct rcvd

Other

Also known as: foot care education documentation, diabetic foot screening measure

Performance measure documenting that patient received education and inspection regarding proper foot care techniques and prevention of foot complications.

In Plain Language

documentation of foot care education and inspection

Clinical Context

Quality measure for diabetes care ensuring patients receive preventive education on foot inspection and care. Important for preventing diabetic foot complications and ulceration.
