6080F Pt/caregiver queried falls

Other

Also known as: falls risk assessment documentation, fall history documentation

Quality measure documenting that patient or caregiver was asked and queried regarding history of falls.

In Plain Language

documentation of fall risk assessment; documentation that patient was asked about falls

Clinical Context

Quality metric for geriatric and neurologic care, identifying patients at risk for falls to implement preventive measures, especially in elderly patients with cognitive impairment, movement disorders, or polypharmacy.

RVU Information

CPT 6080F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
