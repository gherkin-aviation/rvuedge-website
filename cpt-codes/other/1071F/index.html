1071F Alarm symp assessed-1+ prsnt

Other

Performance measure documenting assessment of alarm symptoms for gastrointestinal disease, with documentation that one or more alarm symptoms are present.

In Plain Language

Warning signs of serious GI disease present; Red flag GI symptoms

Clinical Context

Quality measure documenting presence of concerning gastrointestinal symptoms requiring workup and specialist evaluation.
