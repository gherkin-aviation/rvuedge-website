0012F Cap bacterial assess

Other

Also known as: CAP assessment measure, pneumonia quality measure

Community-acquired bacterial pneumonia assessment with documentation of vital signs, mental status assessment, and empiric antibiotic selection based on severity stratification and local resistance patterns.

In Plain Language

pneumonia care checklist; lung infection quality check

Clinical Context

Quality reporting measure for patients with community-acquired pneumonia. Ensures appropriate assessment including severity scoring (CURB-65 or PSI), blood cultures before antibiotics, and guideline-concordant empiric antibiotic therapy.

RVU Information

CPT 0012F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
