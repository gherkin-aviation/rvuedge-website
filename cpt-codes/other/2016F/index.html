2016F Asthma risk assessed

Other

Also known as: asthma risk assessed, asthma risk evaluated

Performance measure for documentation of asthma risk assessment including evaluation of risk factors for exacerbation or poor control. This metric tracks risk stratification.

In Plain Language

checking for factors that make asthma worse

Clinical Context

Used as quality measure for asthma management to identify patients at risk for exacerbations or poor control based on medication use, recent visits, or other risk factors.

RVU Information

CPT 2016F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
