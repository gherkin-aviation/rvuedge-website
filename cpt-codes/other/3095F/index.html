3095F Central dexa results docd

Other

Also known as: DEXA results documented, bone density documented, DEXA report

Documentation that central dual-energy X-ray absorptiometry (DEXA) scan results have been documented in the medical record for bone density evaluation.

In Plain Language

bone density test results recorded

Clinical Context

Quality measure documenting receipt and inclusion of DEXA results in patient record. Used for osteoporosis management and fracture risk assessment in appropriate populations.

RVU Information

CPT 3095F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
