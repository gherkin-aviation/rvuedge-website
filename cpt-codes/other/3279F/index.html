3279F Hgb lvl >= 13 g/dl

Other

Also known as: adequate hemoglobin documentation, target hemoglobin achievement, anemia control verification

Performance measure documenting that hemoglobin level is 13 grams per deciliter or greater. Quality metric for anemia management in renal disease patients.

In Plain Language

blood count adequate for kidney disease patient

Clinical Context

Quality measure ensuring anemia management targets are achieved in renal disease, supporting oxygen delivery and reducing cardiovascular complications.

RVU Information

CPT 3279F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
