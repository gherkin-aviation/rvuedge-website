0503F Postpartum care visit

Other

Also known as: Postpartum visit, Post-delivery checkup

Postpartum care visit including postoperative or postnatal assessment and documentation.

In Plain Language

After-birth checkup; Postpartum follow-up

Clinical Context

Used for postpartum patient encounters, typically within 6-12 weeks after delivery. Assesses maternal recovery, wound healing, emotional status, contraception needs, and infant bonding.

RVU Information

CPT 0503F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
