2028F Foot examination performed

Other

Also known as: foot exam performed, comprehensive foot exam

Performance measure for documentation that comprehensive foot examination was performed and findings documented. This quality metric tracks diabetes foot screening.

In Plain Language

checking feet for problems related to diabetes

Clinical Context

Used as quality measure for diabetic care to ensure annual foot examination screening for neuropathy, ulceration risk, and vascular insufficiency.

RVU Information

CPT 2028F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
