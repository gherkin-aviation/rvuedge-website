3142F Barium swallow test ordered

Other

Also known as: barium swallow order documented, upper GI series ordered

Performance measure indicating that a barium swallow test (also called upper gastrointestinal series) has been ordered for evaluation of swallowing dysfunction or esophageal pathology.

Clinical Context

Used as a quality metric to track when providers order appropriate diagnostic imaging for patients with dysphagia, suspected esophageal obstruction, or gastroesophageal reflux disease.
