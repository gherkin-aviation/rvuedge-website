4010F Ace/arb therapy rxd/taken

Other

Also known as: ACE-I/ARB therapy, RAAS inhibitor

Performance measure indicating documentation that ACE-inhibitor or Angiotensin Receptor Blocker therapy was prescribed or patient is currently taking these medications. Tracks cardioprotective therapy.

Clinical Context

Essential quality measure for heart failure, hypertension, and chronic kidney disease. ACE inhibitors and ARBs provide renal and cardiac protection.
