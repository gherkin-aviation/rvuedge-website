6100F Verify pt site pxd docd

Other

Also known as: surgical site verification, time-out documentation, wrong-site surgery prevention

Quality measure documenting that surgical site and procedure were verified and documented as correct prior to incision.

In Plain Language

documentation that correct surgery site was confirmed; safety check before surgery

Clinical Context

Critical quality measure for surgical safety, part of Universal Protocol to prevent wrong-site, wrong-patient, wrong-procedure surgery. Requires participation of entire surgical team including patient verification when possible.

RVU Information

CPT 6100F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
