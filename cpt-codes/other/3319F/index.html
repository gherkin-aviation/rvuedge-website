3319F X-ray/CT/ultrsnd et al ord

Other

Also known as: imaging ordered, diagnostic imaging documentation, imaging workup completion

Performance measure documenting that X-ray, computed tomography, ultrasound, or other imaging was ordered. Quality metric for appropriate diagnostic imaging.

In Plain Language

Clinical Context

Quality measure ensuring appropriate diagnostic imaging is obtained as part of cancer staging or symptom evaluation.
