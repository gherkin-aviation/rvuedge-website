1022F Pneumo imm status assess

Other

Performance measure documenting assessment of pneumococcal immunization status in a patient.

In Plain Language

Pneumonia vaccine status check; Pneumococcal vaccination review

Clinical Context

Quality measure to ensure appropriate pneumococcal vaccination status documentation, particularly important in elderly, asplenic, or immunocompromised patients.

RVU Information

CPT 1022F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
