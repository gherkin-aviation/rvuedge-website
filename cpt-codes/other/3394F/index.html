3394F Quantitative her2 ihc evaluation brst cx

Other

Also known as: HER2 testing, HER2 IHC, quantitative HER2

Quantitative human epidermal growth factor receptor 2 immunohistochemistry evaluation of breast cancer tissue; pathologic assessment and quantification of HER2/neu protein expression by immunohistochemistry.

In Plain Language

Breast cancer cell protein test; Growth factor receptor testing

Clinical Context

Quality measure for testing of HER2 status in breast cancer. HER2 positive tumors are associated with more aggressive behavior and benefit from targeted therapy (trastuzumab).

RVU Information
