3281F Hgb lvl <11 g/dl

Other

Also known as: severe anemia documentation, low hemoglobin status, significant anemia

Performance measure documenting hemoglobin level less than 11 grams per deciliter. Quality metric for severe anemia status in renal disease patients.

In Plain Language

blood count significantly low for kidney disease patient

Clinical Context

Quality measure documenting severe anemia requiring immediate intervention with erythropoiesis-stimulating agents, iron supplementation, and investigation of causes to prevent cardiovascular complications.

RVU Information

CPT 3281F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
