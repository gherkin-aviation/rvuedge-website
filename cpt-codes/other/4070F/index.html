4070F Dvt prophylx recvd day 2

Other

Also known as: DVT prophylaxis day 2, VTE prophylaxis delayed

Performance measure indicating documentation that deep vein thrombosis prophylaxis was received on day 2 or later of hospitalization. Tracks VTE prevention timing.

In Plain Language

blood clot prevention starting day 2 of hospital stay

Clinical Context

Quality measure tracking appropriate timing of VTE prophylaxis initiation. Some patients may have delayed prophylaxis due to bleeding risk.

RVU Information

CPT 4070F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
