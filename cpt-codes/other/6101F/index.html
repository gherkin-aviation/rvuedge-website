6101F Safety counseling dementia

Other

Also known as: dementia safety counseling, cognitive impairment safety education

Quality measure documenting that safety counseling regarding care environment and hazard prevention was provided to patient with dementia or cognitive impairment.

In Plain Language

safety discussion for dementia patient; home safety planning for cognitive impairment

Clinical Context

Quality metric for dementia care ensuring providers address environmental safety, medication management, caregiver support, and wandering prevention. Improves safety outcomes and reduces preventable injuries and hospitalizations.

RVU Information
