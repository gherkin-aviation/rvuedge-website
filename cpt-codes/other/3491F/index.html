3491F Hiv unsure baby of hiv+moms

Other

Also known as: HIV uncertain infant, maternal HIV exposure infant

Human immunodeficiency virus (HIV) status uncertain in infants of HIV-positive mothers. Performance measure code for HIV status in exposed newborns.

In Plain Language

baby born to HIV-positive mother with unknown status

Clinical Context

Quality measure for documentation of perinatally exposed infants whose HIV status is not yet determined. Guides prophylaxis.
