1008F Gi/renal risk assess

Other

Performance measure documenting assessment of gastrointestinal and renal risk factors for adverse medication effects in patients using non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs.

Clinical Context

Quality measure to ensure appropriate screening for contraindications before prescribing NSAIDs, particularly important in patients with renal or gastrointestinal disease.
