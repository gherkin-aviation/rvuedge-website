4481F Pt rcvng ace/arb blker <3mos

Other

Also known as: ACE/ARB and beta-blocker less than 3 months

Quality measure code documenting that patient is receiving ACE inhibitor or angiotensin II receptor blocker and beta-blocker therapy for less than 3 months duration.

Clinical Context

Quality reporting measure tracking patients recently started on dual therapy, indicating recent cardiac event or new diagnosis requiring optimization.

RVU Information

CPT 4481F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
