4169F No pt care icu/vent in 24hrs

Other

Also known as: no ICU ventilator care measure, ventilator bundle not implemented, ICU care gap measure

Performance measure code indicating that patient care measures for intensive care unit and mechanical ventilation were not implemented within 24 hours.

In Plain Language

breathing machine care measures not given; ICU care quality measures missed; ventilator bundle gap

Clinical Context

Quality measure documenting that evidence-based ICU or ventilator bundle protocols were not implemented within 24 hours, with or without documented reasons.

RVU Information

CPT 4169F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
