4175F Vis 20/40/> with in 90 days

Other

Also known as: visual acuity measure, vision screening measure, eye care quality measure

Performance measure code indicating that vision was 20/40 or better within 90 days of examination.

In Plain Language

good vision documented; vision testing within 90 days; sight quality measure

Clinical Context

Quality measure for ophthalmologic care documenting that patients have achieved or maintained adequate visual acuity.
