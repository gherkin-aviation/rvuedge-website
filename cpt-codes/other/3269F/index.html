3269F Bone scn b/4 txmnt/aftr diagnosis

Other

Also known as: bone imaging documentation, skeletal scintigraphy recording, bone metastasis workup

Performance measure documenting that bone scan imaging was performed before treatment initiation or after diagnosis of cancer. Quality metric for appropriate staging workup documentation.

In Plain Language

bone scan completion for cancer evaluation

Clinical Context

Quality metric ensuring appropriate staging imaging is obtained for cancer patients at risk of skeletal metastases, supporting treatment planning and prognostic assessment.

RVU Information
