3078F Diast bp <80 millimeters hg

Other

Also known as: DBP <80 documented, good diastolic BP documented, normal DBP documented

Documentation of diastolic blood pressure measurement less than 80 mmHg, indicating good diastolic blood pressure control.

In Plain Language

good lower blood pressure reading noted

Clinical Context

Quality measure code documenting adequate diastolic blood pressure; <80 mmHg is goal for most patients.

RVU Information

CPT 3078F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
