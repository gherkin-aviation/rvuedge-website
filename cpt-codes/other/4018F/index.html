4018F Therapy exercise joint prescription

Other

Also known as: therapy exercise, physical therapy, PT

Performance measure indicating documentation that therapeutic exercise or physical therapy was prescribed for joint or musculoskeletal condition. Tracks non-pharmacologic intervention.

In Plain Language

exercise or physical therapy for joints

Clinical Context

Quality measure for osteoarthritis, rheumatoid arthritis, and musculoskeletal conditions. Exercise and PT are foundational non-pharmacologic treatments.

RVU Information

CPT 4018F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
