4058F Pediatric gastric ed given caregvr

Other

Also known as: pediatric GI education, caregiver education

Performance measure indicating documentation that pediatric gastroenterology education was provided to caregiver. Tracks caregiver education in pediatric GI disease.

In Plain Language

teaching caregivers about children's stomach/bowel disease

Clinical Context

Quality measure for pediatric gastroenterology care. Education of parents improves management of conditions like GERD, inflammatory bowel disease, and constipation.
