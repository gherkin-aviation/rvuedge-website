4000F Tobacco use txmnt counseling

Other

Also known as: tobacco counseling, smoking cessation counseling measure

Performance measure indicating documentation that the patient received or was offered counseling for tobacco use treatment. This is a quality reporting code related to smoking cessation.

Clinical Context

Part of quality measure sets for chronic disease management (COPD, cardiovascular disease). Demonstrates performance on preventive counseling for tobacco-dependent patients.
