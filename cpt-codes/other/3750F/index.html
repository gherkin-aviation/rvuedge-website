3750F Ptnotrcvngsteroid>=10mg/day

Other

Also known as: high-dose steroid documentation, prednisone 10mg documentation

Documentation that the patient is receiving corticosteroid therapy at a dose of 10 milligrams per day or greater of prednisone or equivalent.

In Plain Language

patient on high-dose steroids

Clinical Context

Quality measure for patients with autoimmune or inflammatory conditions. High-dose steroids increase infection risk and require monitoring.

RVU Information

CPT 3750F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
