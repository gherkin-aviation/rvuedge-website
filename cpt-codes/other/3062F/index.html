3062F Pos macroalbuminuria revision

Other

Also known as: macroalbuminuria positive documented, advanced nephropathy documented, proteinuria documented

Documentation of positive macroalbuminuria result (urinary albumin excretion >=300 mg/day) in diabetic patient, indicating advanced diabetic nephropathy.

Clinical Context

Quality measure code identifying patients with advanced diabetic kidney disease; indicates urgent need for blood pressure control and renal protective therapy.
