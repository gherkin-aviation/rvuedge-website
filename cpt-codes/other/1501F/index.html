1501F Not initial evaluation for cond

Other

Also known as: initial eval indicator, functional status measure

Functional status performance measure indicating patient was not seen for initial evaluation of the documented condition during the reporting period.

In Plain Language

patient not seen for first visit; no initial appointment during period

Clinical Context

Used as quality measure reporting code for conditions where initial evaluation tracking is clinically important. Helps identify new versus established patient encounters.

RVU Information

CPT 1501F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
