3021F Lvef mod/sever deprs syst

Other

Also known as: LVEF depressed documented, systolic dysfunction documented, reduced EF documented

Documentation of left ventricular ejection fraction showing moderate or severe depression (typically less than 40%) in heart failure patients.

In Plain Language

weak heart pumping documented

Clinical Context

Quality measure code identifying patients with reduced ejection fraction heart failure requiring specific therapeutic interventions (ACE-I, beta-blockers, aldosterone antagonists).

RVU Information

CPT 3021F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation
