6102F Safety counseling dem order

Other

Also known as: antiepileptic safety counseling, seizure medication safety education

Quality measure documenting that safety counseling regarding care environment and fall risk reduction was provided to patient receiving antiepileptic medication.

In Plain Language

safety discussion for seizure patients; medication safety education

Clinical Context

Quality metric ensuring providers counsel patients on safety precautions while taking antiepileptic drugs, including driving restrictions, fall prevention, and importance of medication adherence.

RVU Information
