3762F Patient is dysarthric

Other

Quality measure documenting that the patient exhibits dysarthria, a disorder of articulation affecting speech clarity.

In Plain Language

Difficulty speaking clearly; Speech problem noted

Clinical Context

Used to track identification of patients with speech disorders, often related to neurologic conditions like stroke, Parkinson's, or cerebellar ataxia.

RVU Information

CPT 3762F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
