3498F Cd4+ cell >=15% (hiv)

Other

Also known as: CD4 percentage >= 15%, CD4 percent adequate

CD4+ cell percentage greater than or equal to 15% of total lymphocytes in HIV patients. Performance measure code for adequate relative immunosuppression.

In Plain Language

immune cells at least 15% of white blood cells

Clinical Context

Quality measure indicating adequate percentage-based CD4 recovery, particularly important in pediatric HIV management.

RVU Information

CPT 3498F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
