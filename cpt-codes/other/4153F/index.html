4153F Combo pegintf/rib prescription

Other

Also known as: peginterferon ribavirin measure, HCV combination therapy measure, Hepatitis C combination measure

Performance measure code indicating that combination pegylated interferon and ribavirin therapy was prescribed or recommended.

Clinical Context

Quality measure tracking use of combination pegylated interferon and ribavirin therapy for Hepatitis C. Now largely replaced by direct-acting antivirals.
