Other

Also known as: radiation dose documentation, fluoroscopy exposure documentation, quality reporting

Quality measure documenting that radiation exposure during fluoroscopy was documented in the patient's medical record, including timing and dose information.

Clinical Context

Quality metric ensuring radiation exposure is tracked and communicated to patients. Important for patients requiring multiple fluoroscopic procedures to monitor cumulative exposure.
