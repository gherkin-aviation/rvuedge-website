0015F Melan follow-up complete

Other

Also known as: melanoma follow-up measure, skin cancer surveillance measure

Melanoma follow-up care completed with documentation of comprehensive skin examination, assessment for recurrence, and appropriate surveillance imaging or laboratory studies as clinically indicated.

Clinical Context

Quality reporting measure for patients with a history of melanoma. Ensures appropriate follow-up care including full-body skin examination, regional lymph node assessment, and surveillance for recurrence based on stage and risk factors.
