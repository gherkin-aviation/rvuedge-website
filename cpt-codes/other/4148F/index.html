Other

Also known as: Hepatitis A vaccine measure, Hep A immunization measure, Hepatitis A vaccination

Performance measure code indicating that Hepatitis A vaccine injection was administered or received.

In Plain Language

Hepatitis A shot given; Hep A vaccine administered; Hepatitis A immunization

Clinical Context

Quality measure tracking Hepatitis A vaccination in at-risk populations including healthcare workers, men who have sex with men, chronic liver disease, and Hepatitis C patients.
