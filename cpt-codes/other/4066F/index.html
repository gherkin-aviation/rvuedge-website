4066F Ect provided

Other

Also known as: ECT, electroshock therapy

Performance measure indicating documentation that electroconvulsive therapy (Electroconvulsive Therapy) was provided. Tracks provision of this psychiatric treatment.

In Plain Language

brain stimulation treatment for severe depression

Clinical Context

Quality measure for treatment of severe depression, catatonia, or psychosis refractory to medications. ECT is highly effective but resource-intensive.

RVU Information

CPT 4066F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
