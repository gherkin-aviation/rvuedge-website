4131F Syst antimicrobial thx prescription

Other

Also known as: systemic antimicrobial measure, antibiotic therapy measure, systemic infection treatment

Performance measure code indicating that systemic antimicrobial therapy was prescribed or recommended.

In Plain Language

whole-body infection medication given; systemic antibiotic prescribed; oral or IV infection medicine

Clinical Context

Quality measure tracking appropriate systemic antimicrobial therapy in various conditions. Used in pressure ulcer care, acute wound treatment, and infection management.

RVU Information
