3048F Ldl-c <100 milligrams/dl

Other

Also known as: LDL <100 documented, good LDL documented, optimal cholesterol documented

Documentation of low-density lipoprotein cholesterol (LDL-C) level less than 100 mg/dL, indicating optimal or near-optimal lipid control.

In Plain Language

good cholesterol level noted

Clinical Context

Quality measure code documenting good lipid control; <100 mg/dL is optimal target for most patients, <70 mg/dL for high-risk patients.

RVU Information

CPT 3048F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation
