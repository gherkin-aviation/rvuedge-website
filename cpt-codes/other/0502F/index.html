0502F Subsequent prenatal care

Other

Also known as: Follow-up prenatal visit, Routine prenatal visit

Subsequent prenatal care visit including assessment, examination, and documentation.

In Plain Language

Regular pregnancy checkup; Ongoing maternity visit

Clinical Context

Used for routine follow-up prenatal visits after the initial visit. Includes assessment of maternal health, fetal development, vital signs, lab results, and ongoing care management.

RVU Information

CPT 0502F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
