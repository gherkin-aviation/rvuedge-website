5050F Plan 2 main dr by 1 month

Other

Also known as: retinopathy care plan, treatment planning, diabetic eye care planning

Documentation of a plan for a patient with diabetic retinopathy to receive treatment from a main ophthalmologist or retinal specialist within one month.

In Plain Language

documentation of diabetes eye care plan; eye doctor referral documentation

Clinical Context

Used for quality measurement to ensure proper care coordination and timely specialist evaluation for diabetic retinopathy.

RVU Information

CPT 5050F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
