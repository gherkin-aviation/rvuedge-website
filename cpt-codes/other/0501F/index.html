0501F Prenatal flow sheet

Other

Also known as: Prenatal flow sheet, OB flow sheet documentation

Prenatal care flow sheet documented with current status, findings, and plan.

In Plain Language

Pregnancy tracking form; Prenatal progress record

Clinical Context

Quality measure documenting standardized prenatal assessment parameters including vital signs, fundal height, fetal heart rate, labs, and clinical findings throughout pregnancy.

RVU Information

CPT 0501F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
