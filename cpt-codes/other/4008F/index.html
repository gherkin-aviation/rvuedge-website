4008F Beta-blocker therapy rxd/tkn

Other

Also known as: beta-blocker therapy, BB prescription

Performance measure indicating documentation that beta-blocker therapy was prescribed or patient is currently taking beta-blockers. Tracks cardiovascular medication use.

In Plain Language

heart medication to slow heart rate and lower blood pressure

Clinical Context

Quality measure for heart failure, hypertension, or coronary artery disease. Beta-blockers are foundational therapy in heart failure and post-MI management.

RVU Information

CPT 4008F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
