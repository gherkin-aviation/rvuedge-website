3250F Nonprim loc anat biopsy site tumor

Other

Also known as: cancer site documentation, tumor site documented

Performance measure documenting that the anatomic site of the primary tumor and site of metastatic disease have been documented for a cancer patient.

In Plain Language

cancer location documented

Clinical Context

Used as a quality measure to ensure complete staging and documentation of primary cancer location and metastatic sites for appropriate treatment planning.

RVU Information

CPT 3250F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
