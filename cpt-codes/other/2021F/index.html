2021F Dilation macular examination done

Other

Also known as: dilated macular exam done, macula exam documented

Performance measure for documentation that dilated macular examination was specifically performed and documented. This quality metric ensures detailed evaluation of macula.

In Plain Language

close-up detailed exam of center part of vision

Clinical Context

Used as quality measure for diabetic retinopathy screening to ensure specific documentation of macular examination for detection of diabetic macular edema.
