3027F Spirom fev/fvc>=70%/with ocopd

Other

Also known as: spirometry FEV/FVC >=70% without COPD documented, normal spirometry documented, no obstruction documented

Documentation of spirometry showing forced expiratory volume in 1 second to forced vital capacity ratio (FEV1/FVC) of 70% or greater without COPD diagnosis.

In Plain Language

normal lung function noted

Clinical Context

Quality measure code documenting normal or preserved spirometry in patient without COPD; supports reassurance or alternative diagnosis exploration.

RVU Information

CPT 3027F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation
