6040F Appro radiation ds dvcs techs docd

Other

Also known as: radiation dose reduction documentation, fluoroscopy safety measure, ALARA principle documentation

Quality measure documenting that appropriate radiation dose-reduction devices or techniques were documented during a radiologic procedure using fluoroscopy.

In Plain Language

documentation of efforts to reduce radiation exposure; safety documentation during X-ray procedure

Clinical Context

Quality metric for procedural safety in fluoroscopy-guided interventions. Demonstrates adherence to As Low As Reasonably Achievable (ALARA) principle for radiation protection.

RVU Information

CPT 6040F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
