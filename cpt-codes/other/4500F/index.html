4500F Ref to outpt card rehabilitation prog

Other

Also known as: cardiac rehab referral, outpatient cardiac rehabilitation

Quality measure code documenting that patient with qualifying cardiac condition was referred to outpatient cardiac rehabilitation program.

Clinical Context

Quality reporting measure tracking appropriate referral to cardiac rehabilitation for patients post-myocardial infarction or with heart failure.
