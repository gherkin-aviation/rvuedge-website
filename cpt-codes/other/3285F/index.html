3285F Iop down <15% of pre-svc lvl

Other

Also known as: IOP minimal reduction, IOP not adequately reduced, glaucoma control inadequate

Performance measure documenting intraocular pressure reduction to less than 15 percent below pre-intervention baseline. Quality metric for glaucoma management.

Clinical Context

Quality measure documenting suboptimal intraocular pressure control requiring intervention escalation, medication adjustment, or consideration of surgical glaucoma therapy.
