1182F Neuropsychi sympt 1+present

Other

Neuropsychiatric symptoms are documented as present with one or more symptoms noted in the patient's medical record.

In Plain Language

Mental health symptoms detected; Behavioral or emotional problems identified

Clinical Context

Quality measure indicating the presence of behavioral, cognitive, or psychiatric symptoms in patients with neurological conditions, flagging need for symptom management.

RVU Information

CPT 1182F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
