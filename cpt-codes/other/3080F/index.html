3080F Diast bp >= 90 millimeters hg

Other

Also known as: high diastolic pressure, elevated diastolic BP, hypertension flag

Documentation of diastolic blood pressure measurement of 90 millimeters of mercury or greater, indicating elevated or Stage 2 Hypertension.

In Plain Language

high bottom blood pressure number

Clinical Context

This performance measure documents the presence of elevated diastolic blood pressure in patient records, used for quality reporting and hypertension management tracking in primary care and specialty settings.

RVU Information

CPT 3080F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
