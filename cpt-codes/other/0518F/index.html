0518F Fall plan of care docd

Other

Also known as: Fall risk plan, Fall prevention strategy

Fall prevention plan of care documented.

In Plain Language

Injury prevention plan for falls; Safety plan for seniors

Clinical Context

Quality measure for at-risk patients documenting fall risk assessment and preventive interventions such as physical therapy, home modifications, medication review, and vision correction.

RVU Information

CPT 0518F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
