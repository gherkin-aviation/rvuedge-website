4525F Neuropsychia interven order

Other

Also known as: neuropsychiatric intervention documentation, behavioral intervention order tracking

Measure of physician quality: documentation that neuropsychiatric intervention (psychological counseling, behavioral therapy) was ordered for the patient.

Clinical Context

Used in quality measures for patients with conditions requiring neuropsychiatric support such as Parkinson's disease, multiple sclerosis, or other neurological disorders.
