9003F Aortic anrysm5.5-5.9cm diam

Other

Also known as: large AAA, aorta 5.5-5.9cm

Performance measure indicating abdominal aortic aneurysm of 5.5 to 5.9 centimeters in diameter documented on imaging.

In Plain Language

large aorta bulge; aorta 2.2-2.3 inches; large abdominal aneurysm

Clinical Context

Quality measure tracking large aneurysms near or at intervention threshold. Closely monitored for imminent surgical repair necessity.

RVU Information
