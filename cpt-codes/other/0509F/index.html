0509F Urine incon plan docd

Other

Also known as: Incontinence care plan, UI management plan

Urinary incontinence management plan of care documented.

In Plain Language

Bladder control treatment plan; Incontinence management strategy

Clinical Context

Quality measure documenting assessment and treatment plan for patients with stress, urge, or mixed urinary incontinence. Includes conservative management, pharmacotherapy, or referral recommendations.

RVU Information

CPT 0509F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
