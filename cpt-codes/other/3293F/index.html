Other

Also known as: ABO typing documentation, Rh typing completion, blood type documented

Performance measure documenting that Adenine Boron and Rh blood typing was completed and documented. Quality metric for blood type identification.

Clinical Context

Quality measure ensuring blood typing is completed for all pregnant patients to identify potential complications from Rh incompatibility and guide transfusion management.
