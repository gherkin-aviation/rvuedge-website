2031F H2o stat docd dehydrated

Other

Also known as: hydration dehydrated, dehydration documented

Performance measure for documentation that hydration status was assessed and findings documented as dehydrated. This quality metric tracks dehydration identification.

In Plain Language

patient is dehydrated

Clinical Context

Used as quality measure to document identification of dehydration on assessment, triggering need for intervention and fluid replacement.
