3055F Lvef less than/equal to 35%

Other

Also known as: LVEF <=35% documented, severely reduced EF documented, advanced systolic dysfunction documented

Documentation of left ventricular ejection fraction (LVEF) of 35% or less, indicating significantly reduced systolic function in heart failure.

In Plain Language

severely weak heart pumping documented

Clinical Context

Quality measure code identifying patients with advanced reduced ejection fraction heart failure; indicates indication for device therapy (pacemaker, defibrillator).

RVU Information

CPT 3055F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation
