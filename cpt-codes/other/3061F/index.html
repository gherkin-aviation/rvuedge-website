3061F Neg microalbuminuria revision

Other

Also known as: microalbuminuria negative documented, no early nephropathy documented, normal urine documented

Documentation of negative microalbuminuria result (urinary albumin excretion <30 mg/day) in diabetic patient, indicating absence of early diabetic nephropathy.

Clinical Context

Quality measure code indicating favorable renal status in diabetic patient; suggests good glycemic and blood pressure control.
