3513F Hep b scrng docd as done

Other

Also known as: hepatitis B screening, HBsAg screening, HBV serology

Hepatitis B surface antigen (HBsAg) or hepatitis B surface antibody (anti-HBs) screening documented as performed and test results interpreted.

In Plain Language

Hepatitis B blood test; Liver infection screening

Clinical Context

Essential preventive health measure for at-risk populations, pregnant women, healthcare workers, and routine health maintenance. Documentation of serologic interpretation supports assessment of immunity status and infection risk.

RVU Information

CPT 3513F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
