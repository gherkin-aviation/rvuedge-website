3216F Pt immunity to hep b docd

Other

Also known as: Hepatitis B immunity documented, HBV immunity

Performance measure documenting that patient immunity to Hepatitis B has been verified and documented, used as a quality measure for infection prevention in healthcare settings.

In Plain Language

hep B vaccine status noted

Clinical Context

Used to track compliance with immunization protocols and document assessment of Hepatitis B immunity status in healthcare workers and appropriate patients.

RVU Information

CPT 3216F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
