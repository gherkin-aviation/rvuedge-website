1503F Pt queried symp resp insuff

Other

Also known as: respiratory assessment measure, dyspnea evaluation

Functional status performance measure indicating patient was questioned regarding symptoms of respiratory insufficiency or dyspnea during the encounter.

In Plain Language

patient asked about breathing problems; assessment of shortness of breath

Clinical Context

Used for quality measure reporting in patients with chronic respiratory conditions, cardiac disease, or conditions affecting breathing capacity.

RVU Information

CPT 1503F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
