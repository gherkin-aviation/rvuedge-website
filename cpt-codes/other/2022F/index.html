2022F Dilation rta xm evc rtnopthy

Other

Also known as: dilated exam with retinopathy, retinopathy documented

Performance measure for documentation that dilated retinal examination was performed and documented with findings of retinopathy. This quality metric tracks diabetic retinopathy documentation.

In Plain Language

dilated eye exam showing retinopathy

Clinical Context

Used as quality measure for diabetic care to ensure documentation of retinopathy findings when present on dilated examination.
