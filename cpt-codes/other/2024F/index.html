2024F 7 fld rta photo evc rtnopthy

Other

Also known as: 7-field retinal photo with retinopathy, retinal photography

Performance measure for documentation that seven-field retinal photography was performed with evidence of retinopathy documented. This quality metric tracks fundus imaging documentation.

In Plain Language

photos of eye showing retinopathy

Clinical Context

Used as quality measure for diabetic retinopathy screening to document standardized retinal photographs when retinopathy is present.
