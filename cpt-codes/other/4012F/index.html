4012F Warfarin therapy prescription

Other

Also known as: warfarin therapy, anticoagulation

Performance measure indicating documentation that warfarin therapy was prescribed or patient is currently taking warfarin. Tracks anticoagulation for atrial fibrillation and thromboembolism.

Clinical Context

Quality measure for atrial fibrillation, venous thromboembolism prophylaxis, and mechanical heart valves. Essential for stroke prevention.
