1002F Assess anginal symptom/level

Other

Performance measure documenting assessment of anginal symptoms and severity level, including grading of symptom frequency and triggers.

In Plain Language

Chest pain evaluation; Angina symptom assessment

Clinical Context

Quality measure for cardiac patients to document symptom severity classification and functional impact. Essential for guiding treatment intensity and monitoring therapeutic response.

RVU Information

CPT 1002F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
