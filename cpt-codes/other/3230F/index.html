3230F Note hring tst with in 6 mon

Other

Also known as: hearing test documented, audiogram documented

Performance measure documenting that a hearing test (audiogram) has been documented within the last 6 months, used to track hearing assessment protocols.

Clinical Context

Used as a quality measure to document appropriate hearing assessment in appropriate patient populations, including occupational health or drug toxicity monitoring.
