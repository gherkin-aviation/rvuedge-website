4559F 1 bodytemp >=35.5cw/in 30min

Other

Also known as: normothermia documentation, intraoperative temperature management

Quality measure documenting that patient maintained core body temperature greater than or equal to 35.5 degrees Celsius within 30 minutes post-op.

In Plain Language

documentation of normal body temperature maintained after surgery

Clinical Context

Quality metric for intraoperative temperature management to prevent postoperative hypothermia complications.
