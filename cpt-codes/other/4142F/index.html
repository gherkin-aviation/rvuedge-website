4142F Corticoster sparng thrpy rxd

Other

Also known as: corticosteroid-sparing therapy measure, alternative asthma therapy measure, adjunctive asthma medication

Performance measure code indicating that corticosteroid-sparing therapy was prescribed or recommended.

In Plain Language

alternative asthma medication; steroid-reducing treatment; additional asthma control medicine

Clinical Context

Quality measure tracking use of alternative long-term asthma control medications like leukotriene modifiers or long-acting beta agonists to reduce steroid burden.

RVU Information

CPT 4142F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
