4132F No syst antimicrobial thx prescription

Other

Also known as: no systemic antimicrobial measure, systemic antibiotic not given, antimicrobial restraint

Performance measure code indicating that systemic antimicrobial therapy was not prescribed or was appropriately withheld.

In Plain Language

no systemic infection medication; antibiotic not needed or given; no systemic therapy

Clinical Context

Quality measure tracking appropriate antibiotic stewardship. Documents cases where systemic antimicrobial therapy is not indicated or appropriately withheld.

RVU Information

CPT 4132F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
