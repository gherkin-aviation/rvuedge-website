4145F 2+ anti-hyprtnsv agents tkn

Other

Also known as: multiple antihypertensive measure, blood pressure medication measure, hypertension therapy measure

Performance measure code indicating that two or more antihypertensive agents were prescribed or are being taken.

In Plain Language

multiple blood pressure medications; two or more high blood pressure drugs; hypertension treatment

Clinical Context

Quality measure for hypertension control. Tracks appropriate use of combination antihypertensive therapy to achieve blood pressure targets in high-risk patients.

RVU Information

CPT 4145F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
