Other

Performance measure documenting assessment of fall risk factors with documentation of two or more falls in a one-year period.

In Plain Language

Multiple falls documented; High fall risk assessment

Clinical Context

Quality measure for geriatric or high-risk patients to identify significant fall history requiring intervention and safety modifications.
