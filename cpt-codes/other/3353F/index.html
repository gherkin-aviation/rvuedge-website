3353F Mild-mod dep symp by deptool

Other

Also known as: mild-moderate depression screening, depression screening positive, depression identified

Clinical performance measure documenting that depression screening identified mild to moderate depressive symptoms using a validated depression assessment tool.

In Plain Language

mental health screening shows some depression

Clinical Context

Quality metric used to identify patients with mild-to-moderate depression requiring therapeutic intervention; documented as part of comprehensive behavioral health assessment in primary care.

RVU Information
