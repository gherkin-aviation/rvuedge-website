1460F Qualitative card diagnostic prior 12 mons

Other

Qualified cardiac diagnostic imaging performed prior to or within the past twelve months documented in the medical record.

In Plain Language

Heart imaging test on file; Recent cardiac diagnostic imaging

Clinical Context

Quality measure for documentation of cardiac imaging such as echocardiography, cardiac catheterization, or other qualified imaging.
