4045F Empiric antibiotic prescription

Other

Also known as: empiric antibiotics, empiric coverage

Performance measure indicating documentation that empiric antibiotic therapy was prescribed. Tracks appropriate empiric coverage initiation.

In Plain Language

antibiotics started without waiting for culture results

Clinical Context

Quality measure for infection management. Documents initiation of broad-spectrum empiric antibiotics for serious infections while awaiting culture/sensitivity results.

RVU Information

CPT 4045F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
