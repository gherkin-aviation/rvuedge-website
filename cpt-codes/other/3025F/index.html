3025F Spirom fev/fvc <70% with copd

Other

Also known as: spirometry FEV/FVC <70% with COPD documented, obstruction documented, COPD confirmed

Documentation of spirometry showing forced expiratory volume in 1 second to forced vital capacity ratio (FEV1/FVC) less than 70% in patient with COPD diagnosis.

Clinical Context

Quality measure code documenting diagnostic confirmation of COPD through spirometry; FEV1/FVC <70% is diagnostic of obstruction.
