4005F Pharmaceutical thx for outpatient rxd

Other

Also known as: osteoporosis therapy, bone protective medication

Performance measure indicating documentation that pharmacological therapy was prescribed for osteoporosis. Records prescription of bone-protective medications.

In Plain Language

medication to strengthen bones

Clinical Context

Quality measure for osteoporosis management in patients with fracture risk or diagnosed osteoporosis. Includes bisphosphonates, hormonal agents, or other bone-protective therapies.

RVU Information

CPT 4005F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
