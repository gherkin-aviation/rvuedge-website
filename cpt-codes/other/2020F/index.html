2020F Dilated fundus evaluation done

Other

Also known as: dilated fundus exam done, dilated eye exam

Performance measure for documentation that dilated fundus examination or dilated retinal examination was performed. This quality metric tracks diabetic retinopathy and other retinal disease screening.

In Plain Language

dilated eye exam to look at back of eye

Clinical Context

Used as quality measure for diabetic care to ensure screening for diabetic retinopathy, age-related macular degeneration, and other retinal pathology.

RVU Information

CPT 2020F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
