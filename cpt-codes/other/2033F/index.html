2033F Eye img valid without rtnopthy

Other

Also known as: eye imaging without retinopathy, normal eye imaging

Performance measure for documentation that valid eye imaging or photography was performed without evidence of retinopathy. This quality metric tracks retinal imaging in normal eyes.

In Plain Language

eye imaging showing normal retinas

Clinical Context

Used as quality measure for diabetic screening to document valid retinal imaging when retina is normal.
