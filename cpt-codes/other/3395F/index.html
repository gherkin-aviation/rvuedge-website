3395F Quantitative nonher2 ihc brst cx

Other

Also known as: Non-HER2 IHC, hormone receptor testing, immunohistochemistry

Quantitative non-human epidermal growth factor receptor 2 immunohistochemistry evaluation of breast cancer tissue; pathologic assessment and quantification of non-HER2 protein expression by immunohistochemistry.

Clinical Context

Quality measure for testing of non-HER2 tumor markers in breast cancer. May include estrogen receptor, progesterone receptor, or other relevant markers for treatment planning.
