3085F Suicide risk assessed

Other

Also known as: suicide screening, suicide assessment, SI assessment

Documentation that suicide risk assessment has been performed and documented in the medical record for psychiatric evaluation purposes.

In Plain Language

suicide safety check

Clinical Context

Quality measure documenting assessment for suicidal ideation in psychiatric visits. Required for depression, mood disorders, and behavioral health visits to ensure patient safety and appropriate care planning.

RVU Information

CPT 3085F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
