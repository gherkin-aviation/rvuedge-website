3091F Mdd severe with psychiatric

Other

Also known as: severe psychotic depression, severe MDD with psychosis, psychotic depression

Documentation of diagnosis of Major Depressive Disorder with severe severity including psychotic features such as delusions or hallucinations.

In Plain Language

severe mood disorder with hallucinations

Clinical Context

Performance measure for severe depression with psychotic features. Indicates highest severity requiring psychiatric hospitalization, antipsychotic medications, and intensive monitoring.
