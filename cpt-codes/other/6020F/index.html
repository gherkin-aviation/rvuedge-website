6020F Npo (nothing-mouth) ordered

Other

Also known as: NPO order documentation

Documentation that Nothing Per Oral (NPO) status was ordered appropriately for patients requiring fasting before procedures.

Clinical Context

Performance measure code; documents appropriate preoperative fasting orders.
