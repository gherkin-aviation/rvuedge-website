4165F 3d-crt/imrt received

Other

Also known as: 3D-CRT or IMRT measure, radiation therapy quality measure, advanced radiation therapy measure

Performance measure code indicating that 3D conformal radiation therapy or intensity-modulated radiation therapy was received.

Clinical Context

Quality measure tracking use of advanced radiation techniques in cancer treatment. 3D-CRT and IMRT provide more conformal dose distribution than conventional therapy.
