2025F 7 fld rta photo without rtnopthy

Other

Also known as: 7-field retinal photo without retinopathy, normal retinal photos

Performance measure for documentation that seven-field retinal photography was performed without evidence of retinopathy documented. This quality metric tracks imaging of normal retina.

In Plain Language

photos of eye showing no retinopathy

Clinical Context

Used as quality measure for diabetic care to document standardized retinal photographs when retina is normal or without diabetic changes.

RVU Information

CPT 2025F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
