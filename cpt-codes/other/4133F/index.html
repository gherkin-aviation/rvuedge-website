4133F Antihist/decong prescription/recom

Other

Also known as: antihistamine measure, decongestant measure, allergy symptom medication

Performance measure code indicating that antihistamine or decongestant medication was prescribed or recommended.

In Plain Language

allergy medication recommended; nasal decongestant prescribed; cold symptom medication

Clinical Context

Quality measure for upper respiratory conditions. Tracks appropriate use of antihistamine or decongestant therapy in acute cough and other respiratory conditions.

RVU Information

CPT 4133F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
