2019F Dilated macul examination done

Other

Also known as: dilated macula exam done, macular examination performed

Performance measure for documentation that dilated macular examination was performed to evaluate for diabetic macular edema or macular disease. This quality metric tracks diabetic retinopathy screening.

Clinical Context

Used as quality measure for diabetic care to ensure screening for macular edema and other macular pathology, critical for preserving vision.
