4015F Persist asthma medicine ctrl

Other

Also known as: asthma control medication, maintenance therapy

Performance measure indicating documentation that the patient is on persistent asthma control medication. Tracks appropriate management of persistent asthma.

Clinical Context

Quality measure for persistent asthma management. Includes inhaled corticosteroids and other controller medications for prevention of exacerbations.
