4144F Alt long-term cntrl medial rxd

Other

Also known as: alternative long-term control measure, long-term asthma control measure, controller medication measure

Performance measure code indicating that alternative long-term control medication was prescribed or recommended.

In Plain Language

daily asthma control medication; long-term asthma medication; asthma prevention drug

Clinical Context

Quality measure for asthma management tracking prescription of alternative long-term control medications including beta agonists, theophylline, or leukotriene modifiers.
