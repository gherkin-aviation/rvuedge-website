1003F Level of activity assess

Other

Performance measure documenting assessment of the patient's level of physical activity, exercise capacity, or activity tolerance.

In Plain Language

Activity level evaluation; Exercise capacity assessment

Clinical Context

Quality measure used in cardiac and pulmonary disease management to establish baseline functional status and guide rehabilitation recommendations.

RVU Information

CPT 1003F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
