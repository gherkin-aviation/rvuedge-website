1061F Doc lack perm & cont & parox fib

Other

Performance measure documenting that a patient lacks permanent, persistent, and paroxysmal atrial fibrillation, indicating no current atrial fibrillation.

In Plain Language

No atrial fibrillation; Regular heart rhythm confirmed

Clinical Context

Quality measure documenting absence of atrial fibrillation in patients with history or high risk, important for stroke risk assessment.

RVU Information

CPT 1061F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
