6150F Pt notrcvng1st antitnf txmnt

Other

Also known as: anti-TNF therapy documentation, rheumatoid arthritis quality measure

Quality measure documenting that patient is not receiving first-line anti-tumor necrosis factor (anti-TNF) therapy at current visit.

In Plain Language

documentation of immunosuppressive therapy for arthritis

Clinical Context

Quality metric for rheumatoid arthritis management tracking anti-TNF therapy use for disease monitoring and treatment optimization. Negative measure documenting when therapy is not being used.

RVU Information
