3380F Ajcc brstcncr stage 4 docd

Other

Also known as: AJCC breast stage 4, metastatic breast cancer staging

American Joint Committee on Cancer breast cancer stage 4 documented; quality measure indicating that American Joint Committee on Cancer staging has been recorded for breast cancer patients with stage 4 disease (metastatic breast cancer).

In Plain Language

Advanced breast cancer with spread documented; Metastatic breast cancer stage recorded

Clinical Context

Quality measure ensuring proper documentation of AJCC staging for patients with stage 4 (metastatic) breast cancer. This advanced stage indicates cancer has spread to distant organs.

RVU Information
