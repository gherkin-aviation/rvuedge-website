0507F Periton dialysis plan docd

Other

Also known as: PD care plan, Peritoneal dialysis treatment plan

Peritoneal dialysis plan of care documented for renal failure patients receiving treatment.

In Plain Language

Belly dialysis treatment plan; Peritoneal kidney cleaning plan

Clinical Context

Quality measure for end-stage renal disease patients on peritoneal dialysis. Documents comprehensive care plan including dialysis prescriptions, exchange schedules, medications, and comorbidity management.

RVU Information

CPT 0507F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
