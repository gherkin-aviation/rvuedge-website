3316F Er- or pr- breast cancer

Other

Also known as: ER negative breast cancer, PR negative status, hormone receptor negative

Performance measure documenting estrogen receptor negative or progesterone receptor negative breast cancer. Quality metric for hormone receptor status determination.

In Plain Language

breast cancer that does not respond to hormone therapy

Clinical Context

Quality measure documenting hormone receptor negative status indicating need for chemotherapy or other systemic therapy rather than endocrine therapy alone.

RVU Information

CPT 3316F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
