3089F Mdd moderate

Other

Also known as: moderate depression, moderate MDD, depressive episode moderate

Documentation of diagnosis of Major Depressive Disorder with moderate severity as assessed and documented in the clinical record.

In Plain Language

moderate mood disorder

Clinical Context

Performance measure documenting moderate depression severity with significant functional impairment. Indicates need for active treatment with medications and/or psychotherapy.

RVU Information
