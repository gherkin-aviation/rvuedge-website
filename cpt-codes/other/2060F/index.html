2060F Pt intrvwd on/before diagnosis mdd

Other

Also known as: patient interviewed on dx discussion, discussion documented

Performance measure for documentation that patient was interviewed on or before the visit on which diagnosis was discussed. This quality metric tracks patient engagement in care.

In Plain Language

patient talked to about diagnosis

Clinical Context

Used as quality measure for disease management to ensure patient involvement in diagnostic discussions and care planning.

RVU Information

CPT 2060F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
