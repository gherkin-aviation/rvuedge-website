9004F Aortic anrysm 6/> centimeters diam

Other

Also known as: giant AAA, aorta >=6cm, large AAA

Performance measure indicating abdominal aortic aneurysm of 6 centimeters or greater in diameter documented on imaging.

In Plain Language

giant aorta bulge; very large aorta; large abdominal aneurysm needing surgery

Clinical Context

Quality measure tracking very large abdominal aortic aneurysms typically warranting urgent surgical or endovascular intervention due to high rupture risk.

RVU Information

CPT 9004F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
