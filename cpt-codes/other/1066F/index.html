1066F Ischm stroke surgery onset>=3hr

Other

Performance measure documenting that a patient with acute ischemic stroke symptoms had symptom onset greater than or equal to three hours prior to evaluation.

In Plain Language

Stroke over 3 hours ago; Later presentation

Clinical Context

Quality measure documenting symptom onset timing beyond thrombolytic window, important for determining alternative acute interventions.

RVU Information

CPT 1066F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation
