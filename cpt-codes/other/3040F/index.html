3040F Fev <40% predicted value

Other

Also known as: FEV1 <40% documented, severe airflow obstruction documented, advanced COPD documented

Documentation of forced expiratory volume in 1 second (FEV1) measured at less than 40% of predicted value in pulmonary disease patient.

Clinical Context

Quality measure code documenting severe airflow obstruction requiring intensive management; indicates Global Initiative for Chronic Obstructive Lung Disease stage 3-4 disease.
