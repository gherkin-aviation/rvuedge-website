American Joint Committee on Cancer colon cancer stage 2 documented; quality measure indicating that American Joint Committee on Cancer staging has been recorded for colon cancer patients with stage 2 disease.

In Plain Language

Colon cancer stage 2 recorded; Intermediate colon cancer stage documented

Clinical Context

Quality measure ensuring proper documentation of AJCC staging for colon cancer. Stage 2 represents cancer that has invaded through the muscularis into the submucosa or beyond without regional lymph node involvement.
