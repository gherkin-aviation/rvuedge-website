4100F Biphos thxpy vein ord/recvd

Other

Also known as: bisphosphonate therapy measure, bone therapy measure, osteoporosis treatment tracking

Performance measure code indicating that bisphosphonate therapy was ordered or received by a patient.

In Plain Language

bone strengthening medication given; osteoporosis medication prescribed; bone treatment recorded

Clinical Context

Quality measure for patients at risk for bone loss or with osteoporosis. Tracks initiation or continuation of bisphosphonate therapy like alendronate, risedronate, or ibandronate.

RVU Information

CPT 4100F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
