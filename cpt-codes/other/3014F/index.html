3014F Screening mammo doc revision

Other

Also known as: screening mammogram documented, mammo reviewed, breast imaging documented

Documentation of a screening mammogram performed or reviewed, indicating breast cancer screening imaging was conducted and findings were assessed.

Clinical Context
