0500F Initial prenatal care visit

Other

Also known as: First prenatal visit, Initial OB visit

Initial prenatal care visit including history, physical examination, and care plan documentation.

In Plain Language

First pregnancy checkup; Initial maternity visit

Clinical Context

Used at the first prenatal encounter to establish baseline health status, confirm viability, establish due date, identify risk factors, and initiate prenatal care plan for pregnant patients.

RVU Information

CPT 0500F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
