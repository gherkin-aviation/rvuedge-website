Other

Also known as: LDL >=130 documented, high LDL documented, poor cholesterol control documented

Documentation of low-density lipoprotein cholesterol (LDL-C) level of 130 mg/dL or greater, indicating suboptimal lipid control.

Clinical Context

Quality measure code documenting elevated LDL requiring lipid-lowering therapy; indicates increased cardiovascular risk.
