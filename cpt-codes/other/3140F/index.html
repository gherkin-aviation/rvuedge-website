3140F Upper gi endoscopic shows barrtts

Other

Also known as: Barrett's esophagus documented, intestinal metaplasia found, Barrett's findings

Documentation that upper gastrointestinal endoscopy findings show Barrett's esophagus (intestinal metaplasia of distal esophagus).

In Plain Language

abnormal stomach entrance lining found

Clinical Context

Documentation of Barrett's esophagus diagnosis on endoscopy. Indicates increased cancer risk and requires surveillance per clinical guidelines.
