3051F Hg a1c>equal 7.0%<8.0%

Other

Also known as: A1c 7.0-8.0% documented, fair glucose control documented, acceptable A1c documented

Documentation of hemoglobin A1c laboratory value between 7.0% and 8.0% (greater than or equal to 7.0% but less than 8.0%), indicating fair glycemic control.

In Plain Language

fair diabetes control noted

Clinical Context

Quality measure code documenting acceptable but improvable glycemic control; indicates diabetes management is on track but fine-tuning may be needed.

RVU Information
