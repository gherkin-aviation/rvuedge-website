3111F CT/MRI brain done with in 24hrs

Other

Also known as: brain imaging within 24 hours, CT/MRI ordered timely, neuroimaging protocol

Documentation that computed tomography or magnetic resonance imaging of the brain has been performed within 24 hours of symptom onset or hospital admission.

In Plain Language

brain scan done within one day

Clinical Context

Quality measure ensuring timely neuroimaging for acute stroke evaluation. Compliance with guidelines requiring brain imaging within 24 hours of symptom onset.
