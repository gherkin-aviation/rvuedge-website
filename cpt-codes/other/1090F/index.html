1090F Pres/absn urine incon assess

Other

Performance measure documenting assessment of presence or absence of urinary incontinence in a patient.

In Plain Language

Bladder control assessment; Incontinence screening

Clinical Context

Quality measure to ensure screening for urinary incontinence and identification of appropriate intervention strategies.
