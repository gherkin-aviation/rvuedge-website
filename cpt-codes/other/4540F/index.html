4540F Disease modif pharmacothxpy

Other

Also known as: DMARD prescription documentation, disease-modifying therapy

Quality measure documenting prescription of disease-modifying antirheumatic medication (DMARD) for patients with inflammatory arthritis.

In Plain Language

documentation of medication to slow arthritis progression

Clinical Context

Performance metric used in quality reporting for rheumatoid arthritis and other inflammatory arthridites to ensure appropriate disease-modifying therapy.

RVU Information
