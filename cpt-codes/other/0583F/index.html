0583F Transfer care checklist used

Other

Also known as: Care transition checklist, Hand-off communication tool

Transfer care checklist used for transition of patient care.

In Plain Language

Care handoff checklist; Patient safety transfer form

Clinical Context

Quality measure documenting use of standardized transfer/handoff checklist when transitioning patient care between units or providers to improve safety.

RVU Information
