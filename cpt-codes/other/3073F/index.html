3073F Pre-surg eye measures docd

Other

Also known as: preop eye exam documented, preop ophthalmology documented, preop visual assessment documented

Documentation of preoperative eye examination, measurements, or other ophthalmologic assessment performed prior to elective ocular or systemic surgery.

In Plain Language

eye exam before surgery noted

Clinical Context

Quality measure code documenting baseline ocular status prior to surgery; supports identification of risk factors and optimization of perioperative care.

RVU Information

CPT 3073F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
