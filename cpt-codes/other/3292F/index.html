3292F Hiv tstng asked/docd/revwd

Other

Also known as: HIV screening documentation, prenatal HIV testing, HIV status documented

Performance measure documenting that Human Immunodeficiency Virus testing was offered, documented, or reviewed as part of prenatal care. Quality metric for infectious disease screening.

In Plain Language

documentation of HIV test in pregnancy

Clinical Context

Quality measure ensuring all pregnant patients receive HIV screening to identify infection early and prevent mother-to-child transmission through appropriate interventions.

RVU Information
