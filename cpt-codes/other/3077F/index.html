3077F Syst bp >= 140 millimeters hg

Other

Also known as: SBP >=140 documented, hypertension documented, elevated BP documented

Documentation of systolic blood pressure measurement of 140 mmHg or greater, indicating elevated blood pressure requiring intervention.

Clinical Context

Quality measure code documenting hypertension; indicates need for medication adjustment and cardiovascular risk management.
