4541F Pt offered treatment for pseudobulb

Other

Also known as: pseudobulbar affect treatment offer documentation

Quality measure documenting that patient was offered treatment for pseudobulbar affect or emotional lability.

In Plain Language

documentation of uncontrolled emotional outburst treatment offered

Clinical Context

Performance metric for neurological conditions (ALS, MS, Parkinson's disease) where pseudobulbar affect is a symptom requiring management.

RVU Information

CPT 4541F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
