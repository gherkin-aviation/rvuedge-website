4135F Systemic corticosteroids prescription

Other

Also known as: systemic corticosteroid measure, steroid therapy measure, oral steroid measure

Performance measure code indicating that systemic corticosteroid medication was prescribed or recommended.

Clinical Context

Quality measure tracking appropriate use of systemic corticosteroids in acute conditions such as acute cough, asthma exacerbations, or other inflammatory conditions.
