1034F Current tobacco smoker

Other

Performance measure documenting that the patient is a current active tobacco smoker.

In Plain Language

Current smoker; Active smoking

Clinical Context

Quality measure for documenting active tobacco use, essential for initiating smoking cessation interventions and assessing cardiovascular risk.
