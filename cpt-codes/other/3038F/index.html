3038F Pulmonary fracture with in 12 mon b/4 surgical

Other

Also known as: preop PFT documented, preop spirometry documented, preop lung function documented

Documentation of pulmonary function testing completed within 12 months prior to elective surgery, indicating preoperative pulmonary assessment.

In Plain Language

lung function test before surgery noted

Clinical Context
