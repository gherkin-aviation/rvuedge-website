Other

Also known as: pneumococcal vaccine, PCV, PPSV

Performance measure indicating documentation that pneumococcal vaccination was administered or patient received the vaccine. Tracks preventive immunization.

Clinical Context

Quality measure for infection prevention. Includes pneumococcal conjugate vaccine (PCV) and pneumococcal polysaccharide vaccine (PPSV) based on age and risk.
