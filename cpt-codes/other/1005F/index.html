1005F Asthma symptoms evaluate

Other

Performance measure documenting that asthma symptoms were evaluated and assessed, including symptom frequency and control level.

In Plain Language

Asthma symptom evaluation; Asthma control assessment

Clinical Context

Quality measure to ensure systematic evaluation of asthma control status, guiding medication adjustments and identifying patients needing step-up therapy.

RVU Information

CPT 1005F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
