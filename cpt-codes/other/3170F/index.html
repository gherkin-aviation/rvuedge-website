3170F Baselin flo cytometry b/4 treatment

Other

Also known as: baseline flow cytometry documented, pre-treatment flow cytometry assessment

Performance measure documenting baseline flow cytometry testing performed prior to cancer treatment initiation in patients with hematologic malignancy.

In Plain Language

blood test to check cancer cell types before treatment

Clinical Context

Quality metric tracking appropriate baseline diagnostic testing in hematologic malignancy patients before chemotherapy or other cancer-directed therapy.
