3301F Cancer stage docd metast

Other

Also known as: metastatic cancer documentation, stage documentation with metastases, M-stage documented

Performance measure documenting that cancer was staged with documentation of metastatic disease status. Quality metric for complete cancer staging.

In Plain Language

documentation of cancer spread to other parts of body

Clinical Context

Quality measure ensuring complete staging documentation including presence or absence of distant metastases, critical for prognosis prediction and treatment planning.
