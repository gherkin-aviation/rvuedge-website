3758F Pt ref pulmonary fracture test/peakflow

Other

Also known as: pulm function test, PFT documented, peak flow measurement

Documentation that the patient has been referred for or completed pulmonary function testing or peak expiratory flow measurement.

In Plain Language

breathing tests documented

Clinical Context

Quality measure for patients with respiratory disease or amyotrophic lateral sclerosis. Documents evaluation of respiratory function and progression.
