3700F Psychiatric disorders assessed

Other

Also known as: psych assessment, mental health screening

Documentation that the patient's psychiatric or psychological disorders have been assessed using validated screening tools or clinical evaluation.

Clinical Context

Used in primary care and specialty settings to document screening for depression, anxiety, PTSD, and other psychiatric conditions. Quality measure for comprehensive care.
