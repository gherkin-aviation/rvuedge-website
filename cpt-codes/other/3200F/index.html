3200F Barium swallow test not req

Other

Also known as: barium swallow not required, barium exclusion documentation

Performance measure documented that a barium swallow test was not performed or was not required, used as a quality/performance metric in clinical documentation.

In Plain Language

swallowing test not needed

Clinical Context

Used as a performance measure to document that swallowing studies were appropriately not ordered or performed when not clinically indicated.
