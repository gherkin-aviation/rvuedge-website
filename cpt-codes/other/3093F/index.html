3093F Doc new diagnostic first/additional mdd

Other

Also known as: new MDD diagnosis, first depression diagnosis, new depression

Documentation of new diagnosis of Major Depressive Disorder, either initial or additional diagnosis, during the patient encounter.

In Plain Language

newly diagnosed mood disorder

Clinical Context

Performance measure documenting first or new depression diagnosis. Important for initiating treatment and establishing baseline for ongoing depression management.

RVU Information

CPT 3093F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
