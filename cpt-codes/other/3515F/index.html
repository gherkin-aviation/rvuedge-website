3515F Pt has docd immun to hep c

Other

Also known as: hepatitis C immunity documentation, HCV immunity confirmation

Patient has documented immunity to hepatitis C virus (HCV) based on serologic confirmation of prior exposure and seroconversion.

In Plain Language

Protected from hepatitis C; Prior hepatitis C exposure with recovery

Clinical Context

Quality measure documenting successful resolution of HCV infection with immune response, indicating patient is not infectious and does not require antiviral therapy. Rarely encountered as most acute HCV infections become chronic; document for tracking immune status.

RVU Information

CPT 3515F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
