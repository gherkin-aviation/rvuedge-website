5020F Txmnts 2 phys/qhp by 1 mon

Other

Also known as: treatment documentation, management plan, monthly documentation

Documentation of treatment plans and interventions performed by physician or qualified health care professional within one month for diabetic management.

In Plain Language

diabetes treatment plan documentation; monthly eye care documentation

Clinical Context

Used for quality measurement and continuity of care documentation in diabetic patients with ocular disease to ensure timely management.

RVU Information

CPT 5020F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
