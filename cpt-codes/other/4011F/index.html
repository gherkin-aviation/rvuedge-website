4011F Oral antiplatelet therapy prescription

Other

Also known as: antiplatelet therapy, aspirin therapy

Performance measure indicating documentation that oral antiplatelet therapy was prescribed or patient is currently receiving it. Includes aspirin or other antiplatelet agents.

In Plain Language

blood-thinning medication to prevent clots

Clinical Context

Quality measure for coronary artery disease, peripheral vascular disease, and stroke prevention. Aspirin and other antiplatelets reduce thrombotic events.

RVU Information

CPT 4011F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
