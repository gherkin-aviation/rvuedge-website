3725F Screening depression performed

Other

Also known as: depression screening, depression screening performed

Documentation that depression screening has been performed and documented in the patient's medical record during the current encounter.

In Plain Language

depression screening done; mental health assessment completed

Clinical Context

Quality measure for comprehensive mental health assessment. Screening for depression is important in primary care, particularly in high-risk populations.

RVU Information

CPT 3725F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
