3100F Image test ref carot diam

Other

Also known as: carotid imaging documented, carotid diameter measured, carotid test documented

Documentation that imaging test results (computed tomography or magnetic resonance imaging) show carotid artery diameter measurements or evaluation.

Clinical Context

Quality measure documenting carotid artery evaluation for atherosclerotic disease, stenosis, or vascular risk stratification in appropriate patient populations.
