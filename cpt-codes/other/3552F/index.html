3552F Hgh risk for thromboembolism

Other

Also known as: high thromboembolism risk, VTE risk assessment

Patient assessed for risk of thromboembolic events and documented to be at high risk based on clinical evaluation.

Clinical Context

Quality measure for thromboembolism risk stratification. High-risk patients require aggressive thromboprophylaxis, frequent monitoring, and close clinical follow-up.
