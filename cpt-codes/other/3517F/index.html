3517F Hbv assess & results interpretation 1yr

Other

Also known as: HBV assessment and interpretation, hepatitis B panel interpretation

Hepatitis B virus (HBV) assessment and virologic and serologic test results interpreted within the past 12 months.

Clinical Context

Quality measure for chronic hepatitis B patients requiring regular monitoring of viral load and serologic markers. Assessment includes HBsAg, anti-HBc, HBeAg/anti-HBe, and HBV DNA quantification to guide treatment decisions.
