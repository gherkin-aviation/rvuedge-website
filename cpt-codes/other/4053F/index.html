4053F Hemodialysis via av graft

Other

Also known as: AVG hemodialysis, graft dialysis

Performance measure indicating documentation that patient received or is receiving hemodialysis via an arteriovenous graft. Tracks vascular access method.

Clinical Context

Quality measure for vascular access tracking. AV grafts are used when fistulas are not feasible but are inferior to fistulas for durability.
