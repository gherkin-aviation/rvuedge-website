2014F Mental status assess

Other

Also known as: mental status exam documented, mental status assessed

Performance measure for documentation of mental status assessment or screening completed at visit. This metric tracks cognitive and psychiatric evaluation in clinical practice.

In Plain Language

mental status checked and recorded

Clinical Context

Used as quality measure for primary care and specialist encounters to ensure screening for cognitive changes, mood disorders, and psychiatric conditions.

RVU Information

CPT 2014F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
