4260F Wound srfc culturetech used

Other

Performance measure documenting wound surface culture or culture technique was used for diagnosis of wound infection.

Clinical Context

Quality measure for wound care confirming diagnostic culture obtained to identify infection organisms and guide antibiotic therapy.
