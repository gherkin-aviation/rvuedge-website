3022F Lvef >=40% systolic

Other

Also known as: LVEF normal/mildly reduced documented, preserved EF documented, normal systolic function documented

Documentation of left ventricular ejection fraction of 40% or greater (normal or mildly reduced systolic function) in cardiac assessment.

Clinical Context

Quality measure code documenting preserved or mildly reduced systolic function; guides therapeutic decisions and prognosis assessment.
