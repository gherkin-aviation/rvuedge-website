Other

Also known as: chronic back pain code

Documentation of episode of back pain for greater than 6 weeks. This performance measure code indicates chronic or subacute back pain presentation.

Clinical Context

Used to document that back pain has persisted for more than 6 weeks, indicating chronic presentation.
