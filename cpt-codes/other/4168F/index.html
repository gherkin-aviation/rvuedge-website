4168F Pt care icu & vent with in 24hrs

Other

Also known as: ICU ventilator care measure, ventilator bundle measure, ICU quality measure

Performance measure code indicating that patient care measures for intensive care unit and mechanically ventilated patients were implemented within 24 hours.

In Plain Language

breathing machine care measures given; ICU patient care quality tracked; ventilator care protocols followed

Clinical Context

Quality measure tracking implementation of evidence-based ICU and ventilator bundle protocols within 24 hours of admission or intubation.

RVU Information

CPT 4168F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
