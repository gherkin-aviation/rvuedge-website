3215F Pt immunity to hep a docd

Other

Also known as: Hepatitis A immunity documented, HAV immunity

Performance measure documenting that patient immunity to Hepatitis A has been verified and documented, used as a quality measure for infection prevention.

In Plain Language

hep A vaccine status noted

Clinical Context

Used to track compliance with immunization protocols and document assessment of immunity status to Hepatitis A in appropriate patient populations.
