1004F Clin symp vol ovrld assess

Other

Performance measure documenting clinical assessment of symptoms related to volume overload or fluid retention, with evaluation of signs present.

In Plain Language

Fluid buildup symptom check; Swelling assessment

Clinical Context

Quality measure for heart failure patients to ensure systematic evaluation of signs and symptoms of congestion, guiding diuretic and medication management.

RVU Information

CPT 1004F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
