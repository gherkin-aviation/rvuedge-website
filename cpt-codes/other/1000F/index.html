1000F Tobacco use assessed

Other

Performance measure documenting that tobacco use status was assessed and documented during the clinical encounter.

In Plain Language

Smoking status checked

Clinical Context

Quality measure used across multiple medical specialties to ensure preventive screening. Required as part of cardiovascular, pulmonary, and general health assessments.
