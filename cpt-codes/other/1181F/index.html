1181F Neuropsychia sympts assessed

Other

Neuropsychiatric symptoms are documented as having been assessed and recorded in the patient's medical record.

In Plain Language

Mental health and behavior check; Assessment of psychological symptoms

Clinical Context

Quality measure for documenting assessment of neuropsychiatric symptoms in patients with dementia, Parkinson's disease, or other neurological conditions.
