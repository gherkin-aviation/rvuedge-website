3088F Mdd mild

Other

Also known as: mild depression, mild MDD, depressive episode mild

Documentation of diagnosis of Major Depressive Disorder with mild severity as assessed and documented in the clinical record.

In Plain Language

mild mood disorder

Clinical Context

Performance measure documenting severity of depression at clinical encounter. Mild MDD indicates depression meeting diagnostic criteria with minimal functional impairment.

RVU Information
