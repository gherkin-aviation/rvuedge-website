2030F H2o stat docd normal

Other

Also known as: hydration normal, hydration status normal

Performance measure for documentation that hydration status was assessed and findings documented as normal. This quality metric tracks normal fluid status assessment.

In Plain Language

patient has normal water intake and hydration

Clinical Context

Used as quality measure to document that hydration assessment revealed normal fluid balance without evidence of dehydration or overhydration.

RVU Information

CPT 2030F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
