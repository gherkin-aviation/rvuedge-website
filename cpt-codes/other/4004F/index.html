4004F Pt tobacco screening rcvd tlk

Other

Also known as: tobacco screening, smoking status documentation

Performance measure indicating documentation that patient tobacco use status was screened and discussed. This is documentation of counseling received.

In Plain Language

asking and documenting about smoking habits

Clinical Context

Foundational quality measure. Demonstrates clinician screening for tobacco use and offering counseling to all patients, regardless of current use.

RVU Information
