4044F Doc order given vte prophylx

Other

Also known as: VTE prophylaxis, thromboprophylaxis

Performance measure indicating documentation that physician ordered venous thromboembolism prophylaxis. Tracks appropriate VTE prevention measures.

In Plain Language

blood clot prevention

Clinical Context

Quality measure for surgical and hospitalized patients at risk for deep vein thrombosis and pulmonary embolism. Includes mechanical and pharmacologic prophylaxis.
