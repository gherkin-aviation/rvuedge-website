1036F Tobacco non-user

Other

Performance measure documenting that the patient is a non-user of tobacco products, including no current or past tobacco use.

Clinical Context

Quality measure documenting complete absence of tobacco use history, important baseline for cardiovascular and pulmonary disease risk assessment.
