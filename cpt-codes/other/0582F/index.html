0582F No trnsfr from anesthesia to cc

Other

Also known as: No transfer anesthesia to ICU doc, Standard recovery documented

Patient not transferred from anesthesia to critical care setting documented.

In Plain Language

No move to intensive care after surgery; Regular recovery after operation

Clinical Context

Quality measure documenting patients who did not require transfer to critical care after surgery, indicating successful perioperative course.

RVU Information

CPT 0582F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
