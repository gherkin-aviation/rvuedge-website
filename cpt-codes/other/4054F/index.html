4054F Hemodialysis via catheter

Other

Also known as: catheter dialysis, dialysis catheter

Performance measure indicating documentation that patient received or is receiving hemodialysis via a central venous catheter. Tracks alternative vascular access.

In Plain Language

dialysis using a tube in a large vein

Clinical Context

Quality measure tracking temporary or emergency dialysis access. Catheters are last-line access due to higher infection and thrombosis risk.

RVU Information

CPT 4054F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation
