0520F Radiation dos limts b/4 3d radiation

Other

Also known as: Rad dose limits, 3D-CRT planning

Radiation dose limits established before three-dimensional conformal radiation therapy.

In Plain Language

Radiation treatment safety limits; Cancer radiation therapy planning

Clinical Context

Quality measure documenting establishment of tumor dose and normal tissue tolerance limits before initiating conformal or intensity-modulated radiation therapy in cancer patients.

RVU Information

CPT 0520F does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
