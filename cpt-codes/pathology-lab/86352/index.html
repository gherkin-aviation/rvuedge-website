86352 Cell function assay with stim

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: Lymphocyte stimulation test, cell proliferation assay

Cell function assay with stimulation measures leukocyte functional responses to stimulation (mitogen, antigen, or other stimulants), assessing cellular immune competence.

Clinical Context

Used to assess lymphocyte response to stimulation in immunodeficiency disorders and severe infections. Measures cellular immune competence.
