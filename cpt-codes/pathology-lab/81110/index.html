81110 Hpa-6 genotyping

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: HPA-6 typing, human platelet antigen 6

Genotyping of the human platelet antigen 6 (HPA-6) system to identify allelic variants associated with platelet immunization.

In Plain Language

blood platelet type test; HPA-6 antigen detection

Clinical Context

Employed to assess platelet alloimmunization in transfused patients and to predict transfusion refractoriness due to HLA or HPA immunization.

RVU Information
