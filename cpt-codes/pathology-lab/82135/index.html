82135 Assay aminolevulinic acid

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: ALA level, aminolevulinic acid, porphyria screening

Enzymatic or chromatographic assay measuring aminolevulinic acid in serum or urine to screen for acute intermittent porphyria.

In Plain Language

porphyria test; rare blood disease screening; nerve pain disorder test

Clinical Context

Markedly elevated during acute porphyria attacks; diagnostic for acute intermittent porphyria and other porphyrias.
