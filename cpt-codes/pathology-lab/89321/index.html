89321 Semen anal sperm detection

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: semen analysis sperm detection, sperm presence/absence detection

Semen analysis with detection of sperm in specimen to confirm presence or absence of spermatozoa.

In Plain Language

sperm presence test; azoospermia screening

Clinical Context

Used to confirm azoospermia (no sperm) or detect sperm in suspected cases of vasectomy failure.

RVU Information
