82175 Assay of arsenic

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: arsenic level, heavy metal test, arsenic toxicity

Atomic absorption spectroscopy or inductively coupled plasma mass spectrometry measurement of arsenic in serum or urine.

In Plain Language

poison test; arsenic exposure check; toxic metal screening

Clinical Context

Monitors occupational or intentional arsenic exposure; elevated levels cause gastrointestinal, neurological, and cutaneous manifestations.

RVU Information
