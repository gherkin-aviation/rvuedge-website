84590 Assay of vitamin a

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: serum vitamin A, retinol level

Quantitative assay of vitamin A (retinol) concentration in serum or plasma, measured by liquid chromatography or fluorescence methods to assess nutritional status and fat-soluble vitamin absorption.

Clinical Context

Evaluates malabsorption, liver disease, or vitamin A toxicity/deficiency; low levels associated with night blindness, xerophthalmia, and immune dysfunction.
