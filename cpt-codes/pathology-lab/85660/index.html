85660 Rbc sickle cell test

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: Sickle cell test, hemoglobin S test

Red blood cell sickle cell test detects the presence of sickle hemoglobin (HbS) through solubility testing or electrophoresis, screening for sickle cell disease or trait.

Clinical Context

Initial screening for sickle cell disease and sickle trait, particularly important in African American and Mediterranean populations. Positive results require confirmatory hemoglobin electrophoresis.
