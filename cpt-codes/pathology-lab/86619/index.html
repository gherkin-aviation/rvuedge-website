Pathology & Lab

Borrelia species antibody detection measuring immune response to various Borrelia organisms beyond burgdorferi.

In Plain Language

tick-borne bacteria antibody; borrelia test

Clinical Context

Detects antibodies to Borrelia species including those causing relapsing fever. Allows identification of Borrelia infections other than Lyme disease.
