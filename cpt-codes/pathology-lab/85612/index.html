85612 Viper venom prothrombin time

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: RVVT, Russell viper venom test, viper PT

Viper venom prothrombin time test uses Russell viper venom to specifically activate Factor X, bypassing Factors V, VII, VIII, and IX to assess common pathway coagulation function.

In Plain Language

Specialized blood clotting test; Lupus anticoagulant screening

Clinical Context

Used to diagnose lupus anticoagulant and other inhibitors affecting the common coagulation pathway. More sensitive than standard PT for certain coagulation disorders.

RVU Information
