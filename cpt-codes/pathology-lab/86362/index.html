Pathology & Lab

Also known as: MOG-IgG1 CBA, myelin oligodendrocyte antibody CBA

Myelin oligodendrocyte glycoprotein Immunoglobulin G1 antibody detection via cytometric bead array, identifying immune response against this myelin antigen.

In Plain Language

myelin antibody test; immune protein test

Clinical Context

Detects pathogenic antibodies associated with neuromyelitis optica spectrum disorder and myelin oligodendrocyte glycoprotein-associated disease. Useful for diagnosing autoimmune demyelinating conditions affecting the central nervous system.
