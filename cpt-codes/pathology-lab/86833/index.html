86833 Hla class ii high defin qualitative

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: HLA class II high-resolution, HLA DR/DQ high-res molecular, HLA DNA sequencing

Human leukocyte antigen class II high-resolution typing using molecular methodology to determine specific HLA-DR and HLA-DQ alleles with maximal resolution.

Clinical Context

Provides allelic-level resolution for HLA-DR and HLA-DQ. Critical for optimal transplant matching and identification of disease-susceptibility loci in autoimmune conditions.
