82951 Glucose tolerance test (gtt)

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: glucose tolerance test, GTT

Laboratory test measuring blood glucose levels at multiple time points after glucose load (oral or intravenous) for assessment of glucose tolerance and diabetes diagnosis.

Clinical Context

Used for diagnosis of diabetes mellitus, gestational diabetes, and assessment of impaired glucose tolerance in non-diabetic populations.
