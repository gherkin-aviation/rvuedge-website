84110 Assay of porphobilinogen

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: PBG quantitative, porphobilinogen assay, 24-hour porphobilinogen

Quantitative assay of urine porphobilinogen concentration, measuring the specific amount of this porphyrin precursor.

In Plain Language

urine porphobilinogen level test

Clinical Context

Quantifies porphobilinogen to confirm acute intermittent porphyria and monitor disease severity; elevated levels indicate acute porphyria attacks.

RVU Information
