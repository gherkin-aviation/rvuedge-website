89352 Thawing cryopresrved embryo

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: thawing cryopreserved embryo, embryo thaw

Thawing and preparation of cryopreserved embryo(s) for potential transfer or further assessment.

In Plain Language

thawing frozen embryo; unfreezing stored embryo

Clinical Context

Thawing procedure for stored embryos when patient is ready for embryo transfer in subsequent fertility cycles.

RVU Information
