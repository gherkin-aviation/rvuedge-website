83012 Assay of haptoglobins

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: haptoglobin phenotyping, haptoglobin characterization

Assay of haptoglobins using phenotyping or other characterization methods to identify haptoglobin variants.

In Plain Language

blood test to identify haptoglobin type

Clinical Context

Identifies haptoglobin phenotypes (1-1, 2-1, 2-2) which may affect cardiovascular risk and immune response.
