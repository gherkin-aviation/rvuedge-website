87880 Strep a assay with optic

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: strep A test, strep throat test, GAS test

Infectious agent antigen detection by immunoassay with optical observation for Group A Streptococcus identification.

Clinical Context

Used for rapid detection of Group A Streptococcus in throat specimens for diagnosis of pharyngitis and guidance on antibiotic therapy.
