81596 Nfct ds chrnc hcv 6 assays

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: HCV confirmatory panel, hepatitis C test battery, 6-assay HCV panel

Infectious disease chronic hepatitis C virus screening utilizing 6 separate assays to detect current infection and assess treatment response.

In Plain Language

hepatitis C virus blood test; HCV confirmation test; liver disease screening

Clinical Context

Confirms hepatitis C diagnosis in patients with positive screening and monitors for successful treatment response after direct-acting antivirals.
