83030 Hemoglobin f fetal chemical

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: fetal hemoglobin test, HbF quantitation

Quantitative or qualitative chemical measurement of fetal hemoglobin using standard laboratory assay methods.

In Plain Language

fetal hemoglobin level; baby's red blood cell protein

Clinical Context

Used in obstetrics and hematology to assess fetal lung maturity, evaluate hemolytic disease in newborns, and diagnose hemoglobin disorders. Common indication is determining fetal status during pregnancy complications or hemolytic disease assessment.

RVU Information
