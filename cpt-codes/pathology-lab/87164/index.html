87164 Dark field examination specimen collj

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: dark field microscopy, darkfield exam, spirochete exam, DF microscopy

Microscopic examination of specimens using dark field illumination microscopy with collection of sample using direct darkfield visualizations of spirochetes and motile organisms.

Clinical Context

Primary method for detecting Treponema pallidum in primary syphilis lesions and Borrelia burgdorferi in Lyme disease. Also used for leptospirosis diagnosis.
