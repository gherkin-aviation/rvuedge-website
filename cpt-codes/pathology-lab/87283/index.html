87283 Rubeola ag if

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: measles antigen, rubeola IF, measles virus

Detection of measles (rubeola) virus antigens directly from respiratory or body fluid specimens using immunofluorescence microscopy.

Clinical Context

Rapid confirmation of measles infection during acute illness. Important for public health reporting and infection control measures.
