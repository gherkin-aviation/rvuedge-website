83861 Microfluid analy tears

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: tear fluid analysis, ocular biomarkers

Microfluidic or miniaturized analysis of tear fluid composition including electrolytes, proteins, and other analytes.

In Plain Language

tear chemistry test; eye fluid analysis

Clinical Context

Emerging technology for analyzing tears for systemic diseases, particularly diabetes and autoimmune conditions. Research applications for rapid point-of-care testing.
