87539 Hiv-2 quantitative & revrse trnscripj

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: HIV-2 quantitative test, HIV-2 viral load, HIV-2 RNA quantitation

Human immunodeficiency virus type 2 ribonucleic acid quantification using reverse transcription and amplification. Measures HIV-2 viral load.

In Plain Language

HIV-2 count test; AIDS virus type 2 level

Clinical Context

Quantifies HIV-2 ribonucleic acid to assess disease severity and monitor response to antiretroviral therapy in HIV-2 positive patients.
