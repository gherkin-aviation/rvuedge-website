In Plain Language

Respiratory chlamydia test; Atypical pneumonia detection

Clinical Context

Detects Chlamydia pneumoniae in respiratory specimens including sputum and lower respiratory secretions. Associated with community-acquired pneumonia and chronic respiratory conditions.
