86294 Immunoassay tumor qualitative

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: Tumor marker qualitative, tumor antigen screen

Immunoassay tumor antigen qualitative test detects the presence of tumor-associated antigens using immunoassay methodology without quantitative measurement.

In Plain Language

Cancer marker detection; Tumor protein screening

Clinical Context

Screens for tumor-associated antigens useful in cancer diagnosis, prognosis, and monitoring. Positive results require quantitative follow-up and clinical correlation.
