82127 Amino acid single qualitative

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: single amino acid screen, amino acid qualitative, metabolic screening

Qualitative identification of a single amino acid using chromatographic methods (gas, liquid, or thin-layer) without quantification.

In Plain Language

amino acid presence test; metabolic disorder screening; genetic disease test

Clinical Context

Part of newborn screening and metabolic evaluation; detects specific amino acid accumulation in inherited metabolic disorders.

RVU Information
