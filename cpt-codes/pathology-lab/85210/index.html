85210 Clot factor ii prothrom specimen

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: Factor II assay, prothrombin assay

Assay of coagulation factor II (prothrombin) using one-stage prothrombin time correction study or direct factor measurement, assessing Factor II functional activity in coagulation cascade.

Clinical Context

Evaluates prothrombin deficiency in liver disease, vitamin K deficiency, disseminated intravascular coagulation, or direct Factor II inhibitor therapy.
