83670 Assay of laparoscopic enzyme

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: LAP enzyme, leucine aminopeptidase

Quantitative measurement of leucine aminopeptidase enzyme activity in serum or plasma.

In Plain Language

liver enzyme test

Clinical Context

Assessment of hepatic and pancreatic function. Elevated LAP indicates biliary obstruction or acute pancreatitis. Less commonly used with availability of more specific enzymes.

RVU Information
