84181 Western blot test

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: western blot, protein detection, antibody detection

Western blot technique for protein detection and identification using antibody-based separation and recognition.

Clinical Context

Confirmatory test for various conditions; common uses include HIV confirmation, prion disease diagnosis, and protein expression analysis.
