80199 Drug screening quantitative tiagabine

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: tiagabine level, Gabitril level

Laboratory quantitative drug screen for tiagabine to assess serum level for therapeutic drug monitoring in seizure management.

In Plain Language

tiagabine blood test; seizure medication level

Clinical Context

Monitors tiagabine levels in epilepsy; gamma-aminobutyric acid reuptake inhibitor with varied kinetics.
