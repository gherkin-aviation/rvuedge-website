87552 Mycobacteria dna quantitative

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: Mycobacterium quantitative test, mycobacterial load

Mycobacterium species nucleic acid quantification using real-time polymerase chain reaction or similar quantitative technique. Measures bacterial load.

In Plain Language

Mycobacterium count test; Bacteria level measurement

Clinical Context

Quantifies mycobacterial burden to assess disease severity and monitor response to antituberculous therapy.
