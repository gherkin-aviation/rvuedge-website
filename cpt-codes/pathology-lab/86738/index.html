86738 Mycoplasma antibody

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: Mycoplasma antibody, mycoplasma serology

Mycoplasma species antibody detection measuring immune response to atypical bacterium causing respiratory infection.

In Plain Language

atypical pneumonia test; respiratory infection test

Clinical Context

Detects Mycoplasma pneumoniae antibodies in atypical pneumonia. Important for diagnosis when standard bacterial cultures negative.
