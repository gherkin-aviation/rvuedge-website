85445 Heinz bodies induced

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: Heinz bodies induced, unstable hemoglobin test

Visualization of Heinz bodies in red blood cells after chemical induction with oxidative reagent (typically methylene blue or brilliant cresyl blue) to precipitate unstable hemoglobin.

Clinical Context

Detects unstable hemoglobins and glucose-6-phosphate dehydrogenase deficiency; more sensitive than direct Heinz bodies for identifying oxidative hemoglobinopathy.
