81272 Kit gene targeted seq analys

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: KIT gene sequencing, GIST mutation analysis

KIT proto-oncogene receptor tyrosine kinase (KIT) gene targeted sequencing analysis to identify mutations in gastrointestinal stromal tumors.

In Plain Language

Stomach tumor genetic test; Treatment response prediction test

Clinical Context

Identifies KIT mutations in gastrointestinal stromal tumors (GIST) to guide imatinib and targeted therapy selection and predict treatment response.
