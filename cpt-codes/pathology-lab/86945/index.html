86945 Blood product/irradiation

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: Blood irradiation, Gamma irradiation, Transfusion-related GvHD prevention

Blood product irradiation to prevent transfusion-associated graft-versus-host disease in immunocompromised recipients.

Clinical Context

Used in immunocompromised patients (chemotherapy, bone marrow transplant, congenital immunodeficiency) to prevent transfusion-associated graft-versus-host disease.
