85732 Thromboplastin time partial

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: aPTT variant, modified PTT

Activated partial thromboplastin time test measures plasma clotting time using alternative methodology or specific reagent modifications from standard activated partial thromboplastin testing.

In Plain Language

Alternative clotting test; Modified screening test

Clinical Context

Alternative or specialized activated partial thromboplastin time measurement for specific clinical situations or laboratory preferences. Used in coagulation pathway screening.
