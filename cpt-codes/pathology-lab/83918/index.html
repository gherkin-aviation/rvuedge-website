83918 Organic acids total quantitative

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: organic acids total, urine organic acids quant

Quantitative measurement of total organic acids in urine using chromatographic methodology.

Clinical Context

Screening for organic acidemia disorders in evaluation of developmental delay, metabolic crisis, or failure to thrive. Total measurement followed by fractionation if abnormal.
