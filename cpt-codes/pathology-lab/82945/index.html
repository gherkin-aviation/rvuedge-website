82945 Glucose other fluid

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: glucose other fluids, CSF glucose

Laboratory assay measuring glucose concentration in cerebrospinal fluid, other body fluids, or tissues for assessment of glucose levels outside blood.

Clinical Context

Used in evaluation of meningitis, hypoglycemia in body cavity fluids, and assessment of glucose metabolism in non-blood compartments.
