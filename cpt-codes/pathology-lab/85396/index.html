85396 Clotting assay whole blood

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: whole blood clotting assay, POC clotting test

Whole blood clotting assay using in vitro or point-of-care methodology to assess global clotting function in undiluted whole blood rather than plasma.

In Plain Language

whole blood clotting test; point-of-care clotting

Clinical Context

Point-of-care assessment of coagulation status in perioperative setting or when rapid turnaround critical; includes thromboelastography and rotational thromboelastometry.
