81263 Igh vari regional mutation

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: IGH somatic hypermutation analysis, B-cell maturation assessment

Immunoglobulin Heavy Chain (IGH) variable region mutation analysis to detect hypervariable region mutations and assess B-cell lymphoma characteristics.

In Plain Language

B-cell lymphoma mutation testing; Immune cell clonality assessment

Clinical Context

Evaluates somatic hypermutation patterns in IGH variable regions for prognostic information in chronic lymphocytic leukemia and other B-cell malignancies.
