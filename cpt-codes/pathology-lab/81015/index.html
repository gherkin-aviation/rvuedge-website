81015 Microscopic examination of urine

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: urine microscopy, urine sediment exam

Urine microscopic examination. Detailed microscopic analysis of urine sediment including cells, casts, crystals, and organisms.

In Plain Language

detailed urine test under microscope

Clinical Context

Used to identify urinary tract pathology, infections, kidney disease, and evaluate abnormal urinalysis results.
