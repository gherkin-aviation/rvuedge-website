81560 Trnsplj pd lvr & bwl cd154+cll

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: multi-organ transplant rejection, pancreas-liver-bowel panel, CD154+ rejection test

Transplant rejection pancreatic-liver-bowel comprehensive molecular panel assessing cluster of differentiation 154 positive cells and circulating lymphoid cells for allograft surveillance.

Clinical Context

Used in pancreas, liver, and bowel allograft recipients to detect acute rejection and chronic allograft dysfunction before clinical manifestations appear.
