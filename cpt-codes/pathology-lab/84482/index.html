84482 T3 reverse

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: reverse T3, rT3, inactive T3

Quantitative assay of reverse triiodothyronine (reverse T3) concentration, measuring the inactive T3 metabolite.

In Plain Language

thyroid hormone test

Clinical Context

Measures inactive T3; elevated in illness, stress, and certain conditions; some use to guide thyroid hormone replacement.
