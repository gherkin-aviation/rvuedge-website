87551 Mycobacteria dna amp probe

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: Mycobacterium amplified test, acid-fast bacteria amplified

Mycobacterium species nucleic acid detection using amplified probe technique. Detects mycobacterial infection with enhanced sensitivity.

In Plain Language

Sensitive mycobacterium test; Amplified bacteria detection

Clinical Context

Provides enhanced detection of mycobacterial species for diagnosis of tuberculosis and non-tuberculous mycobacterial infections.
