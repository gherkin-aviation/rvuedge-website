82150 Assay of amylase

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: serum amylase, amylase level, pancreatitis enzyme

Enzymatic assay measuring amylase activity in serum to evaluate pancreatic inflammation and salivary gland disorders.

In Plain Language

pancreas enzyme test; pancreatitis test; digestive enzyme check

Clinical Context

Elevated in acute pancreatitis, salivary gland disease, and ruptured ectopic pregnancy; returns to normal within 3-7 days.

RVU Information
