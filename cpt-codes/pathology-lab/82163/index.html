82163 Assay of angiotensin ii

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: angiotensin II level, ANG II test, hypertension hormone test

Immunoassay measurement of angiotensin II in serum or plasma to assess renin-angiotensin system activity.

In Plain Language

blood pressure hormone test; kidney hormone check; hypertension marker

Clinical Context

Elevated in primary aldosteronism and renovascular hypertension; used with plasma renin activity to assess RAAS.

RVU Information
