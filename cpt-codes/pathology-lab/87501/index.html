87501 Influenza dna amp prob 1+

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: Influenza test, flu test, influenza screen

Influenza virus nucleic acid detection using amplified probe technique, detecting one or more subtypes/strains of influenza.

Clinical Context

Detects influenza A and/or B viruses in respiratory specimens during respiratory illness with suspicion for influenza. Used for diagnosis and public health surveillance.
