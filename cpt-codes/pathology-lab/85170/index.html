85170 Blood clot retraction

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: clot retraction test, retraction assay

Measurement of blood clot retraction rate, where freshly formed clot contracts and expresses serum over time, assessing platelet function and fibrin quality in hemostasis.

In Plain Language

clot shrinking test; blood clot contraction

Clinical Context

Evaluates platelet function and fibrin integrity; abnormal retraction may indicate platelet dysfunction, fibrinogen abnormalities, or polycythemia.

RVU Information
