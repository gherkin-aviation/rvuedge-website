83661 L/s ratio fetal lung

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: L/S ratio, lecithin/sphingomyelin ratio

Determination of lecithin-to-sphingomyelin (L/S) ratio in amniotic fluid by thin-layer chromatography for assessment of fetal lung maturity.

In Plain Language

lung maturity test; baby's breathing readiness

Clinical Context

Assessment of fetal lung maturity in high-risk obstetric conditions requiring preterm delivery. Ratio greater than 2:1 indicates low risk of respiratory distress syndrome.

RVU Information
