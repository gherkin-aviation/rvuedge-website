83701 Lipoprotein bld hour fraction

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: HDL cholesterol, good cholesterol

Quantitation of high-density lipoprotein (HDL) cholesterol fraction in serum or plasma using immunochemical or enzymatic methods.

In Plain Language

protective cholesterol level; good fat measurement

Clinical Context

Key component of lipid panel for cardiovascular risk assessment. Inverse relationship with atherosclerosis; higher levels are cardioprotective.

RVU Information
