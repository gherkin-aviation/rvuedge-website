86003 Allg specimen ige crude xtrc each

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: Crude extract IgE, broad allergen screening

Allergen-specific Immunoglobulin E (IgE) crude extract test measures IgE antibodies to crude allergen extracts, used for broad allergen screening without purified molecular components.

In Plain Language

Allergy sensitivity test; Immune reaction detection

Clinical Context

Used for initial allergen screening in allergy evaluation. Less specific than molecular testing but provides broad allergen sensitivity assessment for common environmental allergens.

RVU Information
