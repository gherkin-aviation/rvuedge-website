82136 Amino acids quantitative 2-5

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: amino acid panel 2-5, multiple amino acids quantitative, amino acid profile

Quantitative measurement of 2 to 5 amino acids using chromatography with specific concentration values for each amino acid.

In Plain Language

amino acid level numbers; metabolism test results; genetic disease assessment

Clinical Context

Quantifies multiple amino acids to diagnose and monitor amino acid disorders; used in newborn follow-up and metabolic disease management.
