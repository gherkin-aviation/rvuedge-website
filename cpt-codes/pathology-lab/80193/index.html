80193 Drug assay leflunomide

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: leflunomide level, Arava level

Laboratory quantitative drug assay for leflunomide to assess serum level for therapeutic drug monitoring in rheumatologic disease treatment.

In Plain Language

leflunomide blood test; arthritis medication level

Clinical Context

Monitors leflunomide (disease-modifying antirheumatic drug) levels in rheumatoid arthritis; helps optimize immunosuppressive effect.
