82805 Blood gases with o2 saturation

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: blood gas without O2 sat, basic blood gases

Laboratory measurement of blood gases including pH, pCO2, and pO2 but excluding oxygen saturation, providing core acid-base and oxygenation data.

Clinical Context

Used in assessment of respiratory and metabolic status when saturation measurement is not required or obtained separately.
