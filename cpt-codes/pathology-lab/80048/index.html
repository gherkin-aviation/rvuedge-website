80048 Basic metabolic pnl total ca

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: basic metabolic panel, BMP with total calcium

Laboratory test panel measuring basic metabolic parameters including electrolytes, kidney function, glucose, and total calcium for metabolic assessment.

In Plain Language

basic blood chemistry test; kidney and metabolism panel

Clinical Context

Standard screening metabolic panel for electrolyte, renal, and glucose status; most commonly ordered metabolic panel for general health assessment.
