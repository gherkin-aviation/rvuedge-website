82128 Amino acids mult qualitative

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: amino acid profile screen, amino acid qualitative panel, newborn screening

Qualitative identification of multiple amino acids using chromatographic methods without numerical quantification.

In Plain Language

amino acid level test; metabolic panel screening; genetic disease screening

Clinical Context

Expanded newborn screening detects amino acid disorders including phenylketonuria, maple syrup urine disease, and other aminoacidopathies.

RVU Information
