85290 Clot factor xiii fibrin stab

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: Factor XIII assay, fibrin-stabilizing factor assay

One-stage assay of coagulation factor XIII (fibrin-stabilizing factor) measuring enzymatic activity to assess cross-linking of fibrin and clot stability.

In Plain Language

blood clot stability test; fibrin cross-linking test

Clinical Context

Evaluates factor XIII deficiency presenting with delayed bleeding, poor wound healing, and recurrent miscarriages; identifies patients needing clotting factor replacement.

RVU Information
