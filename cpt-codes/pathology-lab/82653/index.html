82653 El-1 fecal quantitative

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: fecal elastase quantitative, E-1 quantitative

Laboratory assay measuring elastase-1 (pancreatic elastase-1) in fecal specimens with quantitative methodology for assessment of pancreatic exocrine function.

In Plain Language

pancreas enzyme stool test

Clinical Context

Used in diagnosis of pancreatic insufficiency and exocrine pancreatic dysfunction through non-invasive fecal analysis.
