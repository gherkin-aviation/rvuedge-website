87481 Candida dna amp probe

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: Candida DNA amplified test, fungal amplification assay

Candida species nucleic acid detection using amplified probe technique. Detects candidal nucleic acids with increased sensitivity through nucleic acid amplification.

In Plain Language

Sensitive yeast test; Candida amplified detection

Clinical Context

Used for sensitive detection of Candida in blood cultures, respiratory samples, and other body fluids. Recommended for critical diagnoses of disseminated candidiasis in hospitalized and immunocompromised patients.
