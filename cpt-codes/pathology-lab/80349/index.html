80349 Cannabinoids natural

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: cannabis screen, THC test

Drug screen for natural cannabinoids, primarily tetrahydrocannabinol and related naturally occurring cannabis compounds.

Clinical Context

Widely used in workplace drug testing, substance abuse screening, and law enforcement. Detects natural cannabis products.
