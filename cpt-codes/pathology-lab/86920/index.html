86920 Compatibility test spinal

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: Spin crossmatch, Saline crossmatch

Compatibility testing (cross-matching) using spin tube methodology to detect major incompatibilities at room temperature and 37 degrees Celsius.

In Plain Language

Blood compatibility test; Transfusion safety check

Clinical Context

Initial blood compatibility testing to detect major incompatibilities between patient serum and donor cells before transfusion.
