87483 Cns dna amp probe type 12-25

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: CNS pathogen multiplex test, meningitis panel amplified

Central nervous system pathogen nucleic acid detection using amplified probe technique for targets 12-25 organisms or strains. Detects multiple CNS pathogens in cerebrospinal fluid.

In Plain Language

Meningitis detection panel; Brain/spinal cord infection test

Clinical Context

Used for rapid diagnosis of bacterial meningitis, viral encephalitis, and other CNS infections. Critical in emergency settings for patients presenting with meningeal signs.
