82523 Collagen crosslinks

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: collagen crosslinks assay, bone collagen markers

Laboratory assay measuring collagen crosslinks, including lysine and hydroxylysine crosslinks, used as biomarkers for bone metabolism and collagen turnover assessment.

Clinical Context

Used in evaluation of bone turnover, osteoporosis monitoring, and assessment of collagen-related disorders affecting skeletal and soft tissue integrity.
