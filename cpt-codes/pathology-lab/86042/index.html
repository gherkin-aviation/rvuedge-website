86042 Acetylcholn rcptr blckg antb

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: Anti-AChR blocking, acetylcholine receptor blocking antibody

Acetylcholine receptor blocking antibody test measures antibodies that block acetylcholine binding to its receptor, alternative pathogenic mechanism in myasthenia gravis.

In Plain Language

Myasthenia gravis test; Nerve blockade detection

Clinical Context

Identifies blocking type autoimmune response in myasthenia gravis. May be present without binding antibodies in some seronegative myasthenia gravis patients.
