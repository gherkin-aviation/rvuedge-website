82670 Assay of total estradiol

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: total estradiol, E2 level

Laboratory assay measuring total estradiol concentration in serum or plasma, the predominant estrogen hormone in reproductive-age women.

Clinical Context

Used in evaluation of menstrual disorders, infertility, menopause, estrogen-producing tumors, and assessment of hormone replacement therapy.
