83930 Assay of blood osmolality

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: serum osmolality, plasma osmolality

Quantitative measurement of serum osmolality (concentration of dissolved particles) using freezing point depression osmometry.

In Plain Language

blood concentration test; particle count measurement

Clinical Context

Assessment of fluid status and electrolyte balance in critical illness, hypernatremia, hyponatremia, and toxin ingestion. Key in differentiating diabetes insipidus from SIADH.

RVU Information
