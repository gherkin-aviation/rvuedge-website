87168 Macroscopic examination arthropod

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: arthropod exam, parasite identification, bug identification, insect exam

Macroscopic and microscopic examination of clinical specimens for arthropod parasites including insects, arachnids, and their larvae.

In Plain Language

insect and mite testing

Clinical Context

Used for identification of scabies mites, lice, bed bugs, and other arthropod infestations. Important for dermatologic and public health assessment.
