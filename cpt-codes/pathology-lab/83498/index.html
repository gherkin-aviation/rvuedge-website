83498 Asy hydroxyprogesterone 17-d

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: 17-hydroxyprogesterone, 17-OHP assay

Quantitative measurement of 17-hydroxyprogesterone in plasma or serum using immunoassay or chromatographic methods.

In Plain Language

adrenal hormone test; congenital adrenal hyperplasia screening

Clinical Context

Neonatal screening and diagnostic test for 21-hydroxylase deficiency in congenital adrenal hyperplasia. Elevated 17-OHP indicates enzyme deficiency.

RVU Information
