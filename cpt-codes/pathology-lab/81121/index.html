81121 Idh2 common variants

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: IDH2 testing, isocitrate dehydrogenase 2

Molecular analysis to identify common pathogenic variants in the Isocitrate Dehydrogenase 2 (IDH2) gene, frequently mutated in acute myeloid leukemia.

In Plain Language

leukemia mutation test; blood cancer gene analysis

Clinical Context

Employed to detect IDH2 mutations in acute myeloid leukemia and other hematologic malignancies to guide treatment selection and predict response to targeted therapies.
