84143 Assay of 17-hydroxypregneno

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: 17-OH pregnenolone, hydroxypregnenolone, adrenal steroid intermediate

Quantitative assay of serum 17-hydroxypregnenolone, an intermediate in adrenal steroid hormone synthesis.

In Plain Language

adrenal hormone test

Clinical Context

Elevated in congenital adrenal hyperplasia (21-hydroxylase deficiency); used for newborn screening and diagnosis of adrenal disorders.
