86707 Hepatitis be antibody

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: Anti-HBe, hepatitis B e antibody

Hepatitis B e antibody detection indicating immune response to hepatitis B e antigen.

In Plain Language

hepatitis B disease marker test; viral activity test

Clinical Context

Detects anti-hepatitis B e antibodies indicating seroconversion from active viral replication. Positive result suggests lower viral load and infectivity.
