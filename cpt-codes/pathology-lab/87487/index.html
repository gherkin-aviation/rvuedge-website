87487 Chlmyd pneum dna quantitative

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: Chlamydia pneumoniae quantitative test, respiratory chlamydia load

Chlamydia pneumoniae nucleic acid quantification using real-time polymerase chain reaction or similar quantitative technique. Measures respiratory chlamydial load.

In Plain Language

Chlamydia count test; Respiratory infection level test

Clinical Context

Quantifies Chlamydia pneumoniae burden to assess infection severity and monitor treatment response in respiratory infections.
