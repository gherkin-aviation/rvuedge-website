80305 Drug test prsmv dir opt obs

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: presumptive drug test, direct optical drug screen

Drug test, presumptive, single or initial screen using direct optical observation. This method visually detects drug presence or metabolites in biological samples.

In Plain Language

quick visual drug test; direct observation drug check

Clinical Context

Used as a preliminary drug screening method where visual observation is sufficient for identification. Often employed in point-of-care or rapid screening scenarios.
