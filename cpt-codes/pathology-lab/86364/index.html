86364 Tissue trnsgltmnase each ig clas

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: tTG antibody, tissue transglutaminase test

Tissue transglutaminase antibody detection for each immunoglobulin class, measuring immune response to this enzyme implicated in celiac disease pathogenesis.

Clinical Context

Primary serologic test for celiac disease screening and diagnosis. Tissue transglutaminase antibodies are highly specific and sensitive for celiac disease. Used in initial screening and monitoring of patients on gluten-free diets.
