85999 Unlisted hematology & coagj procedures

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: Unlisted hematology, unlisted coagulation

Unlisted hematology and coagulation procedure code used for reporting laboratory tests related to blood and clotting studies that do not have a specific existing CPT code.

Clinical Context

Used when a hematology or coagulation test is performed that does not fit into any existing CPT code category. Requires detailed documentation and explanation of the procedure.
