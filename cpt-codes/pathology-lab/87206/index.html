87206 Smear fluorescent/acid stai

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: acid-fast stain, AFB stain, fluorescent stain, Ziehl-Neelsen stain

Microscopic examination of stained smears using fluorescent stains and acid-fast stains to detect acid-fast bacteria, particularly Mycobacterium species.

In Plain Language

tuberculosis stain test; special bacteria stain

Clinical Context

Critical for rapid detection of Mycobacterium tuberculosis in sputum and other specimens. AFB staining is the first-line screening for tuberculosis.

RVU Information
