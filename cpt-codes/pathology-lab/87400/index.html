87400 Influenza a/b each ag ia

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: influenza A/B antigen, flu A/B IA, multiplex flu test

Detection of influenza A and B virus antigens from respiratory specimens using immunoassay methodology with identification of each virus type.

Clinical Context

Rapid simultaneous detection of both influenza A and B in respiratory specimens. Useful during flu season for quick diagnosis and treatment initiation.
