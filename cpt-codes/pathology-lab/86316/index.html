Pathology & Lab

Also known as: Other tumor marker, miscellaneous tumor antigen

Immunoassay other tumor-associated antigens quantitatively measures tumor markers not classified under specific category codes for cancer diagnosis and monitoring.

Clinical Context

Used for detecting various tumor-associated antigens not specified by other codes, including PSA variants, novel tumor markers, and less common cancer antigens.
