85175 Blood clot lysis time

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: clot lysis time, fibrinolysis assay

Measurement of blood clot lysis time, where fibrinolytic activity is assessed by measuring the time required for formed clot to dissolve in vitro, indicating fibrinolytic pathway function.

Clinical Context

Assesses fibrinolytic activity; prolonged lysis time may indicate hypofibrinolysis, while shortened time suggests excessive fibrinolysis or disseminated intravascular coagulation.
