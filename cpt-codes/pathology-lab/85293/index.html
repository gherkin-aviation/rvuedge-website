85293 Clot factor wght kininogen

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: high-molecular-weight kininogen, HMWK assay

Assay of high-molecular-weight kininogen (HMWK), a contact activation factor supporting factor XII and prekallikrein function, using plasma correction methodology.

Clinical Context

Evaluates HMWK deficiency causing prolonged activated partial thromboplastin time; deficiency does not cause clinical bleeding.
