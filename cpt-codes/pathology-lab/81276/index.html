81276 Kras gene additional variants

Pathology & Lab +Add-on

Also known as: Extended KRAS analysis, KRAS full gene mutation testing

KRAS proto-oncogene GTPase (KRAS) gene analysis for additional variants beyond exon 2, including exons 3 and 4 mutations.

In Plain Language

Additional KRAS mutation detection; Comprehensive cancer mutation test

Clinical Context

Detects less common KRAS mutations in exons 3 and 4 that may have therapeutic implications not detected by exon 2 alone analysis.
