85347 Coagulation time activated

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: activated clotting time, ACT

Measurement of activated clotting time (ACT) using whole blood incubated with contact activator and measuring time to clot formation, useful for heparin monitoring.

In Plain Language

activated blood clotting time; heparin monitoring test

Clinical Context

Point-of-care test for heparin monitoring in operating room, catheterization laboratory, and extracorporeal circulation; therapeutic ACT 400-600 seconds in cardiac surgery.

RVU Information
