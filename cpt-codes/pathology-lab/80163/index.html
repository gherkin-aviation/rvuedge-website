80163 Assay of digoxin free

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: digoxin free level, free digoxin level

Laboratory quantitative drug assay for digoxin free (unbound) serum fraction for therapeutic drug monitoring in cardiac therapy.

Clinical Context

Measures bioactive unbound digoxin; particularly relevant in renal failure or when protein binding is altered.
