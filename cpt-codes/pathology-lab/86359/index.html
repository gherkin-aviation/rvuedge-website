86359 T cells total count

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: T cell count, total T cells

Total count of T lymphocytes, measured via flow cytometry to determine overall T cell population in peripheral blood.

In Plain Language

immune cell count; T lymphocyte test

Clinical Context

Essential for evaluating cell-mediated immunity, monitoring HIV/AIDS progression, and assessing response to immunosuppressive therapies. Commonly ordered with CD4 and CD8 counts to characterize immune status.

RVU Information
