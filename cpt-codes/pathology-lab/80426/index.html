80426 Gonadotropin hormone panel

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: gonadotropin panel, FSH/LH test

Gonadotropin hormone panel measuring follicle-stimulating hormone and luteinizing hormone levels to assess reproductive function.

In Plain Language

reproductive hormone test

Clinical Context

Used to evaluate infertility, hypogonadism, and reproductive dysfunction in both males and females.

RVU Information
