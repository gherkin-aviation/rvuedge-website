82542 Col chromotography qualitative/quan

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: column chromatography, col chromatography qual/quan

Laboratory technique using column chromatography for both qualitative identification and quantitative measurement of compounds in biological specimens.

In Plain Language

chemical separation and measurement test

Clinical Context

Applied in separation and identification of various compounds including amino acids, catecholamines, drugs, and metabolites in biochemical and toxicological analysis.
