87084 Culture of specimen by kit

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: Culture kit, Rapid culture system, Kit culture

Bacterial culture of specimen using commercial culture kit or rapid methodology. Standardized culture system.

In Plain Language

Rapid bacterial test kit

Clinical Context

Culture using commercial systems or kits that provide standardized growth conditions and rapid identification, particularly for fastidious organisms.
