82247 Bilirubin total

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: total bilirubin, serum bilirubin, jaundice test

Spectrophotometric measurement of total serum bilirubin (conjugated plus unconjugated) to evaluate jaundice and liver-biliary function.

In Plain Language

liver disease test; jaundice blood test; bile buildup check

Clinical Context

Elevated in hemolysis, hepatic disease, and biliary obstruction; used with direct bilirubin to determine etiology.

RVU Information
