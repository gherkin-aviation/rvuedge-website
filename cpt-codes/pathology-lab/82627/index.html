82627 Dehydroepiandrosterone

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: DHEA-S, DHEAS level, androstenediol sulfate

Laboratory assay measuring dehydroepiandrosterone sulfate (DHEA-S) concentration in serum, the sulfated form of DHEA representing adrenal androgen production.

In Plain Language

adrenal hormone sulfate test

Clinical Context

Used in evaluation of adrenocortical function, differentiation of PCOS from other causes of hyperandrogenism, and assessment of adrenal tumors.

RVU Information
