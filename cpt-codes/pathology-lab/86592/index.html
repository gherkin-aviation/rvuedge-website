86592 Syphilis test non-trep qualitative

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: RPR qualitative, VDRL qualitative, syphilis screen

Syphilis non-treponemal antibody test qualitative detection (rapid plasma reagin or venereal disease research laboratory) identifying serologic response to Treponema pallidum infection.

In Plain Language

syphilis test; sexually transmitted infection test

Clinical Context

Initial screening for syphilis infection using non-treponemal antibodies. Positive results require confirmation with treponemal-specific tests. Used for routine screening and monitoring treatment response.
