86803 Hepatitis c ab test

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: Hepatitis C serology, HCV antibody, Anti-HCV

Serologic testing to detect antibodies against hepatitis C virus (HCV), the causative agent of hepatitis C infection. Initial screening test for HCV exposure.

Clinical Context

Used for initial screening of hepatitis C in patients with risk factors, abnormal liver function, or suspected acute hepatitis. Positive results require confirmation with HCV RNA testing.
