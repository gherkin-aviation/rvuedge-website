82105 Alpha-fetoprotein serum

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: serum AFP, alpha-fetoprotein, cancer tumor marker

Immunoassay measurement of alpha-fetoprotein in serum used for prenatal screening and hepatocellular carcinoma surveillance.

Clinical Context

Elevated in hepatocellular carcinoma, germ cell tumors, and neural tube defects; serial measurements track tumor burden and treatment response.
