86384 Nitroblue tetrazolium dye

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: NBT test, nitroblue tetrazolium dye

Nitroblue tetrazolium dye reduction test measuring neutrophil oxidative burst capacity, assessing functional ability of phagocytes to generate reactive oxygen species.

In Plain Language

white blood cell function test; infection-fighting cell test

Clinical Context

Diagnoses chronic granulomatous disease by assessing neutrophil respiratory burst function. Measures ability of phagocytes to produce superoxide radicals essential for bacterial killing.

RVU Information
