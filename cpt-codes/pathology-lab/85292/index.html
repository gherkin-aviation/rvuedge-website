85292 Clot factor fletcher fact

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: Fletcher factor assay, prekallikrein assay

Assay of Fletcher factor (prekallikrein), a contact activation factor in the intrinsic coagulation pathway, using plasma correction methodology to measure functional activity.

In Plain Language

prekallikrein test; contact factor level

Clinical Context

Evaluates prekallikrein deficiency causing prolonged activated partial thromboplastin time; deficiency does not cause clinical bleeding.

RVU Information
