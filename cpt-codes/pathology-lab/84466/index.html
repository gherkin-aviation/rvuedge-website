84466 Assay of transferrin

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: transferrin, iron-binding capacity, iron transport

Quantitative assay of serum transferrin concentration, the iron-transport protein in blood.

In Plain Language

iron transport protein test

Clinical Context

Reflects iron status and liver function; elevated in iron deficiency, decreased in iron overload.
