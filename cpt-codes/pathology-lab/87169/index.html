87169 Macroscopic examination parasite

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: parasite exam, ova and parasites, O&P, parasite identification

Macroscopic and microscopic examination of clinical specimens for parasitic organisms including protozoa and helminths.

Clinical Context

Standard examination for intestinal parasites, tissue parasites, and bloodborne parasites. Commonly ordered for gastrointestinal symptoms, travel history, or immunocompromise.
