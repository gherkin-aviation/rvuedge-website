85530 Heparin-protamine tolerance

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: heparin-protamine tolerance

Tolerance test assessing heparin-protamine interaction by measuring heparin-protamine complex formation and assessing stability of heparin anticoagulation in presence of protamine.

In Plain Language

blood thinner and drug interaction test

Clinical Context

Evaluates protamine tolerance and heparin rebound risk in cardiac surgery and extracorporeal circulation procedures.

RVU Information
