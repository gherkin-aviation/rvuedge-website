87182 Sternoclavicular std carbapenemase nzm det

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: carbapenemase detection, carb resistance test, enzyme detection

Molecular detection and identification of carbapenemase enzyme production using non-enzymatic method such as biochemical assay to detect carbapenem resistance mechanisms.

Clinical Context

Critical for identifying multidrug-resistant gram-negative bacteria with carbapenemase production. Essential for infection control and treatment decisions in serious infections.
