89331 Retrograde ejaculation anal

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: retrograde ejaculation analysis, retrograde ejaculation test

Analysis of retrograde ejaculation specimen for presence of sperm and semen components in urine.

Clinical Context

Evaluation of retrograde ejaculation (sperm into bladder) in cases of absent or low ejaculate volume.
