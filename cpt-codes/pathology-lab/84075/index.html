84075 Assay alkaline phosphatase

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: alkaline phosphatase, ALP, alk phos

Quantitative measurement of alkaline phosphatase enzyme activity in serum using kinetic colorimetric or enzymatic assay methods.

In Plain Language

liver enzyme test; bone enzyme measurement

Clinical Context

Nonspecific marker of liver disease, biliary obstruction, or bone metabolism. Elevated in cholestasis, viral hepatitis, cirrhosis, Paget disease, and hyperparathyroidism.

RVU Information
