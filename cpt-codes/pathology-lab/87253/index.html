87253 Virus inoculate tissue additional

Pathology & Lab +Add-on

Also known as: additional culture, supplemental inoculation, additional TC

Additional inoculation of clinical specimen into supplemental cell culture systems as an add-on to initial tissue culture viral isolation.

In Plain Language

extra virus culture test

Clinical Context

Used when initial culture is negative or when testing multiple cell lines increases isolation sensitivity for certain viruses.
