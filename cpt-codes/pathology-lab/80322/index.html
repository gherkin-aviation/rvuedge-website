80322 Alcohols biomarkers 3/more

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: comprehensive alcohol biomarker panel, multiple alcohol markers

Alcohol biomarkers, three or more markers measured, for comprehensive assessment of chronic alcohol use and consumption patterns.

Clinical Context

Used in comprehensive evaluation of chronic alcohol use disorder, especially in clinical and legal settings. Provides more complete picture than single or dual marker testing.
