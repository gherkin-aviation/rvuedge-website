88302 Tissue examination by pathologist

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: tissue exam, microscopic pathology, histology exam

Microscopic examination of tissue sections (1 to 4 blocks) by a pathologist including slide preparation and diagnostic interpretation.

In Plain Language

microscopic tissue examination; tissue analysis under microscope

Clinical Context

Initial diagnostic examination of surgical specimens or biopsies; blocks are tissue samples preserved in paraffin for sectioning and staining.
