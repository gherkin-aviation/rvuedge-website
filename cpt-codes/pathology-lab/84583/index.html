84583 Assay of urine urobilinogen

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: urine urobilinogen alt method, Ehrlich urobilinogen test

Quantitative assay of urobilinogen in urine using an alternative methodology such as Ehrlich reaction or other chemical analysis technique distinct from standard automated methods.

In Plain Language

special urine urobilinogen test; alternative bilirubin test

Clinical Context

Provides alternative quantification of urinary urobilinogen when standard methods are unavailable; clinically equivalent to 84580 but using different laboratory methodology.

RVU Information
