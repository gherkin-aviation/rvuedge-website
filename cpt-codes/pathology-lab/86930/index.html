86930 Frozen blood prep

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: Frozen blood preparation, Cryopreservation, Blood freezing

Preparation and freezing of blood products for long-term storage. Enables extended preservation.

Clinical Context

Used to preserve blood components and products for extended storage. Often used with cryoprotectants like glycerol.
