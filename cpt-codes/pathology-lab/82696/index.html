82696 Assay of etiocholanolone

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: etiocholanolone assay, etiolanolone level

Laboratory assay measuring etiocholanolone concentration in serum or urine, a steroid metabolite with androgenic and estrogenic properties.

In Plain Language

steroid metabolite hormone test

Clinical Context

Measured in evaluation of steroid metabolism, assessment of adrenocortical function, and diagnosis of steroid-producing tumors.
