84403 Assay of total testosterone

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: total testosterone, serum testosterone, testosterone level

Quantitative assay of total serum testosterone concentration, measuring both free and bound hormone.

Clinical Context

Standard androgen assessment; evaluates hypogonadism, infertility, and virilization; must interpret with SHBG for clinical significance.
