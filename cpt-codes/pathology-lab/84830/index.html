84830 Ovulation tests

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: ovulation test, LH surge test, fertility predictor

Qualitative and/or quantitative assays of ovulation markers including luteinizing hormone (LH) surge or progesterone in urine or serum to predict ovulation timing in fertility management.

Clinical Context

Used by patients seeking pregnancy to identify fertile window; detects LH surge (occurs 24-36 hours before ovulation) or increased progesterone indicating ovulation occurred.
