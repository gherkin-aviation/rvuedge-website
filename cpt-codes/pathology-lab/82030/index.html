82030 Assay of adp & amp

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: ATP/ADP/AMP panel, adenine nucleotide assay, energy substrate test

Enzymatic assay measuring adenosine diphosphate and adenosine monophosphate in blood or other specimens to assess cellular energy metabolism.

In Plain Language

cell energy test; metabolism energy level; cellular exhaustion test

Clinical Context

Used in evaluating metabolic disorders, mitochondrial dysfunction, and cellular energy depletion states in severely ill patients.

RVU Information
