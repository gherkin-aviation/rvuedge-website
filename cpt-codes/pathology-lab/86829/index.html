86829 Hla class i/ii antibody qualitative

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: HLA class I/II antibody qualitative, HLA antibody detection qual

Serologic testing to detect human leukocyte antigen class I and class II antibodies using qualitative methodology. Identifies specific HLA antibody patterns.

In Plain Language

HLA antibody detection test; Transplant risk assessment

Clinical Context

Qualitative HLA antibody testing in transplant evaluation. Presence and pattern of antibodies help predict graft outcome and guide immunosuppression strategy.
