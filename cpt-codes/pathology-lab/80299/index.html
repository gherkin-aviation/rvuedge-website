80299 Quantitative assay drug

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: drug quantification, quantitative drug screen

Quantitative assay for drug or drug metabolite measured in blood, serum, plasma, or urine. This is an unlisted or miscellaneous drug assay not specified by other CPT codes.

Clinical Context

Used when a specific quantitative drug level needs to be measured but does not fit into a more specific CPT code category. Common in therapeutic drug monitoring and substance abuse assessment.
