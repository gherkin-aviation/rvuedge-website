85730 Thromboplastin time partial

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: aPTT, APTT, PTT

Activated partial thromboplastin time test measures plasma clotting time after addition of a contact activator and phospholipid reagent, screening for intrinsic and common pathway defects.

In Plain Language

Blood clotting screening test; Heparin monitoring test

Clinical Context

Primary screening test for bleeding disorders affecting intrinsic and common coagulation pathways. Essential for monitoring unfractionated heparin therapy and evaluating prolonged bleeding.

RVU Information
