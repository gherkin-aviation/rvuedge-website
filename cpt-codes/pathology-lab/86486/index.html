86486 Skin test unlisted antigen each

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: unlisted skin test, custom antigen test

Unlisted antigen skin test intradermal injection measuring delayed-type hypersensitivity response to non-standard or specialty antigens each test performed.

In Plain Language

specialty skin test; custom antigen test

Clinical Context

Used for intradermal testing with antigens not otherwise specified in standard testing panels. Allows flexibility for evaluating hypersensitivity to specialty organisms or environmental antigens.
