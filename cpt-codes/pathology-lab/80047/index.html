80047 Basic metablc pnl ionized ca

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: basic metabolic panel, BMP with ionized calcium

Laboratory test panel measuring basic metabolic parameters including electrolytes, kidney function, glucose, and calcium (ionized form) for metabolic assessment.

Clinical Context

Comprehensive screening test for electrolyte imbalances, renal function, and glucose control; used in acute illness, medication monitoring, and routine health maintenance.
