84432 Assay of thyroglobulin

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: thyroglobulin, Tg, thyroid cancer marker

Quantitative assay of serum thyroglobulin concentration, measuring this thyroid peroxidase product and thyroid marker.

In Plain Language

thyroid cancer test

Clinical Context

Tumor marker for thyroid cancer follow-up; elevated in differentiated thyroid cancer and thyroiditis.
