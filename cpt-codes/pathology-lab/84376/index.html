84376 Sugars single qualitative

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: single sugar screen, reducing sugar test, sugar detection

Qualitative test for a single sugar type in body fluids using chemical reactions for sugar detection.

Clinical Context

Screens for presence of sugars (glucose, fructose, galactose) in urine or other fluids; positive results warrant further testing.
