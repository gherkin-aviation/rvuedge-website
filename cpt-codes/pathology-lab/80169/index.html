80169 Drug assay everolimus

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: everolimus level, Afinitor level

Laboratory quantitative drug assay for everolimus to assess serum level for therapeutic drug monitoring in transplantation and immunosuppression.

In Plain Language

everolimus blood test; transplant medication level

Clinical Context

Monitors everolimus (mammalian target of rapamycin inhibitor) levels in transplant recipients and cancer patients on targeted therapy.
