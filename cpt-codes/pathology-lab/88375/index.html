88375 Optical endomicroscpy interpretation

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: optical endomicroscopy interpretation, confocal microscopy interpretation

Interpretation and report of optical endomicroscopy findings for intraepithelial diagnosis or real-time tissue imaging.

Clinical Context

Real-time optical imaging during endoscopy for immediate cellular-level assessment to guide biopsy or treatment decisions.
