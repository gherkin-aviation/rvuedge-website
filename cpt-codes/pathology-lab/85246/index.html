85246 Clot factor viii vw antigen

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: vWF:Ag, von Willebrand factor antigen

Measurement of von Willebrand factor antigen (vWF:Ag) in plasma using immunoassay to quantify von Willebrand factor protein concentration and assess factor VIII carrier protein levels.

In Plain Language

von Willebrand protein level; blood clotting protein test

Clinical Context

Evaluates von Willebrand disease, hemophilia A, and elevated vWF in inflammatory or thrombotic states; typically ordered as first-line testing for bleeding disorders.
