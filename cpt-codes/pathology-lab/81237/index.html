81237 Ezh2 gene common variants

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: EZH2 common variant panel, EZH2 mutation testing

Molecular analysis of the EZH2 gene to identify common mutations associated with B-cell lymphomas.

In Plain Language

lymphoma gene test; blood cancer mutation screening

Clinical Context

Used to detect EZH2 mutations in lymphomas for prognostic assessment and prediction of response to EZH2 inhibitor therapy.
