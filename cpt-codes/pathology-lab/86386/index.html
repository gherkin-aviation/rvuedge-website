86386 Nuclear matrix protein 22

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: NMP22, nuclear matrix protein 22

Nuclear matrix protein 22 detection measuring tumor-associated antigen presence in urine, used as a tumor marker for bladder cancer screening.

In Plain Language

bladder cancer marker test; urine cancer screening

Clinical Context

Detects nuclear matrix protein 22 in urine as marker for bladder cancer. Useful for surveillance of patients with history of transitional cell carcinoma and screening of patients with hematuria.
