82731 Assay of fetal fibronectin

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: fFN test, fetal fibronectin

Laboratory assay measuring fetal fibronectin in cervical/vaginal specimens, a biomarker for risk of preterm delivery when detected in second/third trimester.

In Plain Language

preterm labor risk test

Clinical Context

Used in pregnant patients with signs of preterm labor to assess risk of delivery within 2 weeks and guide management decisions.

RVU Information
