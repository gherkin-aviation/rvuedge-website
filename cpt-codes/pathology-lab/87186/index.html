87186 Sternoclavicular std microdil/agar dil

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: microdilution, broth microdilution, MIC testing, microbial broth

Susceptibility testing using microdilution broth method with agar dilution supplementation to determine minimal inhibitory concentration of antimicrobial agents.

In Plain Language

antibiotic strength testing

Clinical Context

Accurate and standardized method for determining antimicrobial susceptibility. Used for fastidious organisms and those requiring specific media.
