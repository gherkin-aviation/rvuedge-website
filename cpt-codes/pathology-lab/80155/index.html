80155 Drug assay caffeine

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: caffeine level, theophylline-like stimulant level

Laboratory quantitative drug assay for caffeine to assess serum level for therapeutic drug monitoring in certain conditions or toxicity evaluation.

Clinical Context

Rarely ordered; used for caffeine toxicity evaluation or monitoring in specific therapeutic contexts, primarily in pediatric or toxicology settings.
