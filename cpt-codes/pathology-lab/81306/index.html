Pathology & Lab

Also known as: NUDT15 pharmacogenomic test, thiopurine sensitivity testing

Nudix Hydrolase 15 (NUDT15) gene analysis for common variants affecting thiopurine metabolism and chemotherapy response.

In Plain Language

Chemotherapy response prediction test; Drug toxicity risk assessment

Clinical Context

Identifies NUDT15 variants that predict thiopurine toxicity in acute lymphoblastic leukemia and other malignancies treated with 6-mercaptopurine.
