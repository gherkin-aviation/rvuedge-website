81275 Kras gene variants exon 2

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: KRAS exon 2 mutation analysis, codon 12/13 KRAS testing

KRAS proto-oncogene GTPase (KRAS) gene variant analysis focusing on exon 2 mutations commonly associated with pancreatic and colorectal cancers.

In Plain Language

Pancreatic cancer mutation test; Colorectal cancer genetic marker

Clinical Context

Detects common KRAS exon 2 mutations (G12, G13) in pancreatic, colorectal, and lung cancers to predict response to targeted therapies and anti-EGFR agents.
