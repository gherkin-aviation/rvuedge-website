83727 Assay of lrh hormone

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: LHRH assay, gonadotropin-releasing hormone

Quantitative measurement of luteinizing hormone-releasing hormone in plasma or serum using immunoassay methods.

Clinical Context

Assessment of hypothalamic-pituitary-gonadal axis function in infertility evaluation and hypogonadism investigation. Less frequently used with availability of LH and FSH assays.
