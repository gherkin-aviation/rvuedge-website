88299 Unlisted cytogenetic study

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: unlisted cytogenetic study, cytogenetic unlisted procedure

Reporting code for unlisted or miscellaneous cytogenetic and molecular diagnostic studies not elsewhere classified.

In Plain Language

genetic test not listed; special genetic testing

Clinical Context

Use when performing cytogenetic or molecular analysis that does not fit standard CPT descriptors; requires detailed operative notes.
