87045 Feces culture aerobic bact

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: Stool culture, Fecal culture, GI bacterial culture

Bacterial culture of feces (stool) specimen for aerobic organisms. Screens for gastrointestinal bacterial pathogens.

In Plain Language

Bowel infection test; Diarrhea bacteria test

Clinical Context

Used in acute diarrhea evaluation to identify bacterial pathogens like Salmonella, Shigella, Campylobacter, and enteroinvasive Escherichia coli.

RVU Information
