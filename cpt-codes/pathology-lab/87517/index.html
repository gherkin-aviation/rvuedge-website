87517 Hepatitis b dna quantitative

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: Hepatitis B quantitative test, HBV viral load, HBV quantitation

Hepatitis B virus nucleic acid quantification using real-time polymerase chain reaction or similar quantitative technique. Measures hepatitis B viral load.

Clinical Context

Quantifies hepatitis B viral load to assess disease severity, treatment response, and need for antiviral therapy in chronic hepatitis B.
