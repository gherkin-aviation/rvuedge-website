81112 Hpa-15 genotyping

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: HPA-15 typing, human platelet antigen 15

Genotyping of the human platelet antigen 15 (HPA-15) system to identify allelic variants associated with platelet immunogenicity.

Clinical Context

Ordered to determine platelet alloimmunization patterns and manage transfusion support in patients with recurrent transfusion complications.
