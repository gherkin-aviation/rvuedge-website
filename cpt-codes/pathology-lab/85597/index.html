85597 Phospholipid pltlt neutraliz

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: Platelet neutralization test, phospholipid neutralization

Phospholipid platelet neutralization test assesses the effect of platelet phospholipids on prolonged activated partial thromboplastin time, helping identify lupus anticoagulant presence.

In Plain Language

Lupus anticoagulant confirmation; Blood clotting protein test

Clinical Context

Essential test for confirming lupus anticoagulant in patients with clinical suspicion. Used in thrombosis evaluation and antiphospholipid syndrome diagnosis.
