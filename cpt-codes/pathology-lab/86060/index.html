86060 Antistreptolysin o titer

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: ASO titer, antistreptolysin titer

Antistreptolysin O (ASO) titer test quantitatively measures antibodies against streptolysin O toxin, indicating recent or past Group A Streptococcal infection.

Clinical Context

Used to confirm recent Group A Streptococcal infection in patients with suspected acute rheumatic fever or post-streptococcal glomerulonephritis. Elevated titers indicate recent infection.
