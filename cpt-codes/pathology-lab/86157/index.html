86157 Cold agglutinin titer

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: Cold agglutinin titer, CA titer

Cold agglutinin titer test quantitatively measures the level of cold-reactive autoantibodies, determining disease severity and prognosis in cold agglutinin disease.

In Plain Language

Cold antibody strength test; Severity indicator

Clinical Context

Quantifies cold agglutinin levels to assess disease severity. Titers above 1:256 usually indicate significant hemolytic disease from cold exposure.

RVU Information
