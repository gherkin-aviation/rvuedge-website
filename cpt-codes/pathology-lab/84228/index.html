84228 Assay of quinine

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: quinine level, drug assay, therapeutic drug monitoring

Quantitative assay of serum quinine concentration, measuring this antimalarial and antiarrhythmic drug level.

In Plain Language

antimalarial drug level test

Clinical Context

Therapeutic drug monitoring for toxicity; historically used for leg cramps but concerns for cinchonism and thrombocytopenia limit use.

RVU Information
