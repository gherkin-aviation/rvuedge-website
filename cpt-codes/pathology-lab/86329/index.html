86329 Immunodiffusion nes

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: Immunodiffusion NES, gel diffusion test

Immunodiffusion not elsewhere specified (NES) test uses gel diffusion methodology for antigen-antibody reactions without specification of particular organism or technique.

In Plain Language

Antigen-antibody test; Gel diffusion serology

Clinical Context

Used for detecting various antigens and antibodies through double gel diffusion. Less common now due to replacement by more specific immunoassays.

RVU Information
