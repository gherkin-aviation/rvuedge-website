85046 Reticyte/hgb concentrate

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: reticulocyte hemoglobin, CHr, reticulocyte Hgb

Quantitative measurement of hemoglobin content in reticulocytes (immature red cells), providing assessment of iron incorporation into young RBCs and iron status evaluation.

Clinical Context

Assesses iron availability for erythropoiesis; low reticulocyte hemoglobin indicates iron deficiency in early stages before hemoglobin decline.
