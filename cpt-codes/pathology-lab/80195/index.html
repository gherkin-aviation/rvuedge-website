80195 Assay of sirolimus

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: sirolimus level, rapamycin level

Laboratory quantitative drug assay for sirolimus to assess serum level for therapeutic drug monitoring in transplantation and immunosuppression.

In Plain Language

sirolimus blood test; transplant medication level

Clinical Context

Monitors sirolimus (mammalian target of rapamycin inhibitor) levels in kidney and heart transplant patients for optimal immunosuppression.

RVU Information
