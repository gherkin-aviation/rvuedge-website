85240 Clot factor viii ahg 1 stage

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: Factor VIII assay, AHG assay, hemophilia assay

One-stage assay of coagulation factor VIII (antihemophilic globulin/AHG) measuring functional activity by patient plasma correction of factor VIII-deficient plasma.

Clinical Context

Diagnoses and monitors hemophilia A, evaluates acquired factor VIII inhibitors, and guides therapeutic factor VIII replacement dosing in hemophilia A patients.
