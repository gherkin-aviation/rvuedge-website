86950 Leukacyte transfusion

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: WBC transfusion, Granulocyte transfusion, Leukocyte transfusion

Leukocyte transfusion (white blood cell transfusion) for immunocompromised patients. Provides functional granulocytes or lymphocytes.

In Plain Language

White blood cell transfusion; Infection-fighting cell transfusion

Clinical Context

Used in severe infections in granulocytopenic patients when conventional treatment failing. Limited clinical utility due to alloimmunization risk.

RVU Information
