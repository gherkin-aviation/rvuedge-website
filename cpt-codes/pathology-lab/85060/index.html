85060 Blood smear interpretation

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: blood smear interpretation, physician review, pathologist review

Microscopic interpretation of peripheral blood smear by pathologist or technologist, assessing cell morphology, identifying abnormal cells, parasites, and providing clinical correlation with laboratory results.

In Plain Language

expert blood smear review; microscopic cell analysis

Clinical Context

Provides expert morphologic diagnosis when automated results abnormal or clinical suspicion high; identifies hematologic malignancies, hemolytic anemia, and parasitic infections.
