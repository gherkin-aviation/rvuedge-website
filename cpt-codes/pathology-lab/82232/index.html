82232 Assay of beta-2 protein

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: beta-2 microglobulin, B2M level, kidney disease marker

Immunoassay measurement of beta-2-microglobulin in serum to assess kidney function and screen for plasma cell dyscrasias.

Clinical Context

Elevated in chronic kidney disease and multiple myeloma; filtered by glomeruli and reabsorbed by tubules.
