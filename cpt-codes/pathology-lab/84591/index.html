84591 Assay of not otherwise specified vitamin

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: vitamin NOS assay, other vitamin measurement

Quantitative assay of not otherwise specified (NOS) vitamins in serum or urine, a catch-all code for vitamin measurement not specifically coded elsewhere.

In Plain Language

vitamin level test; other vitamin measurement

Clinical Context

Used when measuring vitamin levels not covered by specific codes (84590, 84597); includes various vitamin assays performed by specialty laboratories.

RVU Information
