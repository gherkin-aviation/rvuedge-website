89346 Storage/year oocyte(s)

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: storage oocyte yearly, oocyte storage per year

Storage and maintenance of cryopreserved oocyte (unfertilized egg) specimen(s) for one year.

In Plain Language

keeping frozen eggs for a year; annual egg storage fee

Clinical Context

Annual storage fee for cryopreserved oocytes for patients using fertility preservation or delayed childbearing strategies.

RVU Information

CPT 89346 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
