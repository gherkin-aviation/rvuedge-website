89268 Insemination of oocytes

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: insemination of oocytes, egg fertilization, IVF insemination

Insemination of oocytes with sperm for in vitro fertilization using standard or intracytoplasmic injection techniques.

Clinical Context

Core procedure in in vitro fertilization involving incubation of oocytes with prepared sperm for fertilization.
