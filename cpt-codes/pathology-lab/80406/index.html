80406 Acth stimulation panel

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: continued ACTH stimulation, repeated ACTH test

Adrenocorticotropic hormone stimulation panel for continued measurement or repeat stimulation with multiple collection points.

In Plain Language

repeat adrenal test

Clinical Context

Used when initial ACTH stimulation results are inconclusive or require confirmation.
