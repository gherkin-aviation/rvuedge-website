82017 Acylcarnitines quantitative

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: acylcarnitine quantitative, carnitine profile panel, fatty acid oxidation panel

Quantitative measurement of acylcarnitine profiles using tandem mass spectrometry or chromatography with specific concentration values.

Clinical Context

Diagnoses fatty acid oxidation disorders, organic acidemias, and mitochondrial disorders in newborns and children with metabolic decompensation.
