81551 Onc prostate 3 genes

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: prostate mRNA panel, 3-gene prostate test, prostate risk stratification

Oncology prostate cancer molecular panel utilizing 3-gene assessment to predict aggressive behavior and therapeutic response.

In Plain Language

genetic test for prostate cancer; prostate aggressiveness test; prostate cancer gene panel

Clinical Context

Identifies men with high-risk prostate cancer to guide treatment intensity and monitor for castration resistance. Used in newly diagnosed or recurrent disease.
