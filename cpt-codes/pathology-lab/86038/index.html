86038 Antinuclear antibodies

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: ANA, antinuclear antibody screen

Antinuclear antibody (ANA) test detects autoantibodies against nuclear antigens using immunofluorescence microscopy, screening for systemic autoimmune diseases.

In Plain Language

Lupus screening test; Autoimmune disease marker

Clinical Context

Primary screening test for systemic lupus erythematosus and other connective tissue diseases. Positive results require pattern recognition and reflex testing for specific antibodies.
