86807 Cytotoxic antibody screening

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: Cytotoxic antibody screen, HLA antibody screen, Panel reactive antibody

Serologic screening to detect cytotoxic antibodies against human leukocyte antigen and other antigens on lymphocytes. Initial screen before detailed antibody identification.

Clinical Context

Performed in transplant candidates to identify presence of cytotoxic antibodies that could cause hyperacute rejection. Positive results prompt identification of specific antigen targets.
