81204 Ar gene charac alleles

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: AR allele characterization, AR variant analysis

Allele characterization of the Androgen Receptor (AR) gene to identify androgen insensitivity syndrome variants in individuals with abnormal sexual development.

Clinical Context

Used to characterize AR variants in individuals with ambiguous genitalia or androgen insensitivity for diagnosis and genetic counseling.
