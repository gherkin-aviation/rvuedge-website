80186 Assay of phenytoin free

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: phenytoin free level, free Dilantin level

Laboratory quantitative drug assay for phenytoin free serum fraction for therapeutic drug monitoring in seizure management.

In Plain Language

free phenytoin blood test; active seizure medication level

Clinical Context

Measures unbound phenytoin; particularly valuable in patients with altered protein binding or renal/hepatic impairment.
