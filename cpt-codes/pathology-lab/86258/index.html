86258 Dgp antibody each ig class

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: Anti-DGP, deamidated gliadin antibody

Deamidated gliadin peptide (DGP) antibody test measures antibodies against modified gliadin peptides by immunoglobulin class, useful in celiac disease diagnosis.

Clinical Context

Used for celiac disease screening and diagnosis, particularly IgA anti-DGP. More sensitive in children than tissue transglutaminase antibody testing.
