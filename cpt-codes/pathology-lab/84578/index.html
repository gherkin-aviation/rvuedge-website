84578 Test urine urobilinogen

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: urine dipstick urobilinogen, qualitative urobilinogen

Qualitative or semiquantitative screening test for urobilinogen in urine samples using dipstick methodology to detect elevated urinary urobilinogen.

Clinical Context

Quick screening test for liver disease, hemolysis, or biliary obstruction; abnormal results typically prompt quantitative testing with code 84580 or 84583.
