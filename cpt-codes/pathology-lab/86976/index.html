86976 Rbc serum pretx id dilution

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: RBC-serum dilution, Dilution pretreatment

Red blood cell and serum pretreatment using dilution methodology to aid antibody identification. Dilutes interfering substances.

Clinical Context

Serial dilution of patient serum or treatment cells to enhance antibody specificity and aid identification in complex serologic cases.
