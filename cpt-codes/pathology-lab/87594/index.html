Pathology & Lab

In Plain Language

Pneumocystis pneumonia test; PCP infection detection

Clinical Context

Detects Pneumocystis jirovecii in bronchoalveolar lavage or respiratory specimens for diagnosis of Pneumocystis pneumonia in immunocompromised patients.
