85420 Fibrinolytic plasminogen

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: plasminogen activity, functional plasminogen

Measurement of plasminogen activity in plasma using chromogenic substrate or amidolytic methodology distinct from immunologic antigen measurement.

In Plain Language

fibrinolytic enzyme precursor activity; plasminogen function test

Clinical Context

Provides functional assessment of plasminogen versus antigen measurement; may detect dysfunctional plasminogen variants.
