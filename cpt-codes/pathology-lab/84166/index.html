84166 Protein e-phoresis/urine/csf

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: urine protein electrophoresis, CSF protein electrophoresis, UPEP

Electrophoresis of proteins in urine and/or cerebrospinal fluid separating and identifying protein fractions.

Clinical Context

Identifies abnormal proteins in urine (Bence Jones proteins in myeloma) or CNS fluid; detects monoclonal components and inflammatory patterns.
