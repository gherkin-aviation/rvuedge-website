82938 Gastrin test

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: gastrin stimulation test, gastrin with secretin

Laboratory assay measuring gastrin concentration in serum after standardized stimulation (secretin) for evaluation of gastrin-producing neuroendocrine tumors.

In Plain Language

stomach hormone test

Clinical Context

Used in diagnosis of Zollinger-Ellison syndrome and assessment of gastrin-secreting gastrinomas.

RVU Information
