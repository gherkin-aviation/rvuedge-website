88358 Analysis tumor

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: morphometric analysis tumor, tumor quantitative analysis

Morphometric and photometric analysis of tumor tissue for quantitative assessment of cellular features and diagnostic characteristics.

Clinical Context

Used for quantitative assessment of tumor cellularity, necrosis, and other morphologic features relevant to prognosis or classification.
