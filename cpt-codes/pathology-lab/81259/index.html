81259 Hba1/hba2 full gene sequence

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: HBA alpha gene sequencing, alpha globin full sequencing

Hemoglobin Alpha 1 and Alpha 2 genes full gene sequence analysis including all exons and introns to identify structural variants and mutations associated with hemoglobinopathies.

In Plain Language

Complete red blood cell protein gene test; Alpha globin chain genetic analysis

Clinical Context

Used to diagnose alpha thalassemia and other hemoglobinopathies where alpha globin chain synthesis is affected. Essential for carrier screening and prenatal diagnosis.

RVU Information
