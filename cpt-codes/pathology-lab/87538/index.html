87538 Hiv-2 probe & revrse trnscripj

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: HIV-2 probe and reverse transcription test, HIV-2 RNA test

Human immunodeficiency virus type 2 nucleic acid detection and quantification using probe technique combined with reverse transcriptase amplification.

In Plain Language

HIV-2 detection and measurement; AIDS virus type 2 RNA test

Clinical Context

Detects and quantifies HIV-2 ribonucleic acid for diagnosis and viral load assessment in HIV-2 infected patients.
