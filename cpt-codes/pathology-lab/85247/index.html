85247 Clot factor viii multimetric

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: vWF multimers, multimer analysis

Measurement of von Willebrand factor multimer structure and distribution by gel electrophoresis or similar methodology to characterize von Willebrand factor subtypes.

In Plain Language

von Willebrand structure test; vWF multimer pattern

Clinical Context

Identifies von Willebrand disease types (type 1 normal pattern, type 2 abnormal pattern, type 3 absent) and thrombotic thrombocytopenic purpura (TTP) with characteristic pattern.
