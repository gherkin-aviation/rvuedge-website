86382 Neutralization test viral

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: neutralization test, viral neutralizing antibody

Viral neutralization test measuring serum antibody capacity to neutralize infectious viral particles, providing functional assessment of antiviral immunity.

Clinical Context

Used to measure functional antiviral antibodies, particularly post-vaccination or infection. Determines extent of immune protection against specific viral pathogens. Increasingly used to assess coronavirus disease 2019 vaccine effectiveness.
