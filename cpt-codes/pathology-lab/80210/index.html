80210 Drug assay rufinamide

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: rufinamide level, Banzel level

Laboratory quantitative drug assay for rufinamide to assess serum level for therapeutic drug monitoring in seizure management.

In Plain Language

rufinamide blood test; seizure medication level

Clinical Context

Monitors rufinamide levels in Lennox-Gastaut syndrome and other seizure disorders; newer antiepileptic with emerging therapeutic drug monitoring data.
