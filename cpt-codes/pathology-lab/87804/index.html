87804 Influenza assay with optic

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: flu test, influenza antigen

Infectious agent antigen detection by immunoassay with optical observation for influenza virus identification.

In Plain Language

rapid flu test; quick influenza check

Clinical Context

Used for rapid detection of influenza in symptomatic patients presenting with respiratory symptoms during flu season to guide antiviral therapy decisions.
