89280 Assist oocyte fertilization

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: assist oocyte fertilization, assisted fertilization

Assisted reproductive technique to facilitate sperm penetration of oocyte zona pellucida including micromanipulation.

Clinical Context

Procedure for male factor infertility or previous fertilization failure requiring direct sperm injection into oocyte.
