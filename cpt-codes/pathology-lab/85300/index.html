85300 Antithrombin iii activity

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: antithrombin activity, AT III activity

Assay of antithrombin III (antithrombin) functional activity using chromogenic substrate methodology to measure inhibition of thrombin and factor X activity.

Clinical Context

Evaluates antithrombin III deficiency in thrombophilia, liver disease, disseminated intravascular coagulation, and response to heparin therapy.
