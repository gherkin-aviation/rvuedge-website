86336 Inhibin a

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: Inhibin A, maternal inhibin

Inhibin A measurement quantifies this hormone-like protein produced by ovarian follicles and placenta, used in maternal screening for Down syndrome risk.

Clinical Context

Component of second-trimester maternal screening (quad screen). Elevated inhibin A levels increase Down syndrome risk in combination with other markers.
