80420 Dexamethasone panel

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: dexamethasone suppression test, DST

Dexamethasone suppression panel measuring cortisol response to low-dose or high-dose dexamethasone administration.

In Plain Language

cortisol suppression test

Clinical Context

Used to diagnose Cushing syndrome and evaluate hypothalamic-pituitary-adrenal axis function. Important in endocrinology evaluation.
