82157 Assay of androstenedione

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: androstenedione level, A4 test, adrenal androgen test

Immunoassay measurement of androstenedione in serum to assess adrenal and ovarian androgen production.

In Plain Language

male hormone test; ovarian hormone test; adrenal function check

Clinical Context

Evaluates hirsutism, amenorrhea, and polycystic ovary syndrome; elevated in congenital adrenal hyperplasia and virilizing tumors.

RVU Information
