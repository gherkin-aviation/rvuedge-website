86756 Respiratory virus antibody

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: Respiratory virus serology, Viral resp serology

Serologic testing to detect antibodies against respiratory viruses, including influenza, respiratory syncytial virus, parainfluenza, and adenovirus. Used to identify recent or past viral respiratory infections.

Clinical Context

Used to confirm suspected viral respiratory tract infections including influenza, respiratory syncytial virus, and parainfluenza. Commonly ordered during respiratory illness workup to guide clinical management and infection control measures.
