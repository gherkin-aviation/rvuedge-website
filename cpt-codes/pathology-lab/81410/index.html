81410 Aortic dysfunction/dilation

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: aortic genetics test, familial thoracic aortic aneurysm testing

Genetic testing for aortic dysfunction and dilation conditions including thoracic aortic aneurysm syndrome genes.

In Plain Language

genetic test for aortic problems; inherited aorta enlargement test

Clinical Context

Identifies genetic mutations associated with aortic dysfunction and dilation; used for patients with aortic aneurysm or aortic dissection.
