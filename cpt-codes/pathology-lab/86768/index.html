86768 Salmonella antibody

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: Salmonella serology, Salmonella antibody

Serologic testing to detect antibodies against Salmonella species, gram-negative enterobacteria that cause gastroenteritis and systemic infections. Identifies salmonellosis exposure or infection.

In Plain Language

Salmonella food poisoning test; Bacterial infection antibody test

Clinical Context

Indicated in suspected Salmonella infection presenting with acute diarrhea, fever, and abdominal pain. May be used to confirm diagnosis or assess antibody response in chronic carriage.
