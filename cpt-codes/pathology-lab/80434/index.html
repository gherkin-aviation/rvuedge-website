80434 Insulin tolerance panel

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: insulin tolerance test, ITT

Insulin tolerance panel measuring growth hormone and cortisol response to insulin-induced hypoglycemia for assessment of pituitary function.

Clinical Context

Used to evaluate growth hormone and cortisol secretion in hypopituitarism and assessment of pituitary reserve.
