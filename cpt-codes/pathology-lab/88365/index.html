88365 Insitu hybridization (fish)

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: in situ hybridization FISH dual probe, FISH two-color

Fluorescence in situ hybridization (FISH) analysis using two different color probes or multiple sequential probes for detection.

Clinical Context

FISH using two different fluorescent probes for detection of two chromosomal regions or genes simultaneously.
