81291 Mthfr gene

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: MTHFR polymorphism test, MTHFR C677T testing

Methylenetetrahydrofolate Reductase (MTHFR) gene polymorphism analysis to assess variants affecting folate metabolism and thrombotic risk.

In Plain Language

Folate metabolism genetic test; Blood clot risk genetic marker

Clinical Context

Identifies MTHFR variants that may affect folate metabolism and thrombotic risk. Clinical utility remains controversial but used in some specialty practices.
