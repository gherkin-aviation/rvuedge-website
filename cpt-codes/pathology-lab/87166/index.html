87166 Dark field examination without collj

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: dark field exam, darkfield microscopy, wet mount dark field, motile organism exam

Microscopic examination of specimens using dark field illumination microscopy without collection collimation for detecting motile organisms and spirochetes.

In Plain Language

microscope test for moving organisms

Clinical Context

Used for detecting motile organisms including spirochetes, particularly in genital or body fluid specimens. Less specific than collimated dark field but more readily available.
