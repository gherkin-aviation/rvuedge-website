82384 Assay three catecholamines

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: three catecholamines, comprehensive catecholamine panel, full catecholamine panel

Simultaneous measurement of three catecholamines (epinephrine, norepinephrine, dopamine) in plasma or urine to fully assess sympathetic function.

Clinical Context

Complete catecholamine panel including dopamine assesses all sympathomimetic hormones for diagnosis and localization.
