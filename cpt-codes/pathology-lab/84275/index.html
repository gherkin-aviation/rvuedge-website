84275 Assay of sialic acid

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: sialic acid, serum sialic acid, glycoprotein marker

Quantitative assay of serum sialic acid concentration, measuring this component of glycoproteins and lipoproteins.

In Plain Language

cancer marker test

Clinical Context

Used as a tumor marker in some cancers; elevated in inflammation and malignancy; less specific than modern tumor markers.
