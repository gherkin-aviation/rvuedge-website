81163 Brca1&2 gene full seq alys

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: BRCA1/2 full sequencing, BRCA mutation testing

Complete sequence analysis of both BRCA1 and BRCA2 genes to identify point mutations and small indels associated with hereditary breast and ovarian cancer syndrome.

In Plain Language

breast cancer gene test; hereditary cancer genetic analysis

Clinical Context

Performed to comprehensively screen for pathogenic variants in BRCA1/2 in individuals with increased cancer risk for early detection, surveillance, and preventive management strategies.
