81193 Ntrk3 translocation analysis

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: NTRK3 translocation testing, NTRK3 fusion detection

Molecular analysis to detect NTRK3 gene rearrangements and translocations associated with secretory breast carcinoma and other malignancies.

In Plain Language

breast cancer gene test; cancer translocation detection

Clinical Context

Used to identify NTRK3 fusions in breast carcinomas and other cancers for therapeutic decision-making and treatment eligibility assessment.
