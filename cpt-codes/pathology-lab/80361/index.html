80361 Opiates 1 or more

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: opiate screen, morphine test

Drug screen for opiates, one or more agents. Detects natural opiates including morphine and codeine derived from poppy.

Clinical Context

Widely used in pain management monitoring, substance abuse screening, and emergency overdose assessment.
