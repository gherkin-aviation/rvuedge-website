84153 Assay of psa total

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: total PSA, serum PSA, PSA level

Quantitative assay of total serum prostate-specific antigen, including both free and complexed forms.

In Plain Language

prostate cancer screening test

Clinical Context

Standard screening tool for prostate cancer; elevated levels warrant further evaluation with free PSA ratio and digital rectal exam.
