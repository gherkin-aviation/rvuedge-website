88333 Path consltj surgical cyto xm 1

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: surgical consult cytology 1 specimen, intraoperative cytology consult

Pathology consultation during surgery for cytologic examination (Pap smear or other cytology) for one specimen, including rapid assessment.

Clinical Context

Intraoperative consultation for cytologic analysis (such as cervical cytology) to provide rapid diagnostic assessment during surgery.
