82331 Calcium infusion test

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: calcium infusion test, calcium stimulation test, PTH suppression test

Intravenous calcium infusion with serum calcium monitoring to assess parathyroid hormone suppression and function.

In Plain Language

parathyroid gland function test; calcium challenge test; hormone response test

Clinical Context

Specialized test to assess parathyroid reserve and response to calcium loading in patients with suspected parathyroid dysfunction.

RVU Information
