84085 Assay of rbc pg6d enzyme

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: RBC PGI assay, phosphoglucose isomerase, red cell enzyme assay

Quantitative assay of red blood cell phosphoglucose isomerase enzyme activity (formerly called red blood cell phosphohexose isomerase).

Clinical Context

Measured to assess red blood cell enzyme function and identify hemolytic anemias caused by enzyme deficiencies or to evaluate certain metabolic disorders.
