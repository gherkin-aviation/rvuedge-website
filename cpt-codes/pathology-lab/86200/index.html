86200 Ccp antibody

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: Anti-CCP, CCP antibody

Cyclic citrullinated peptide (CCP) antibody test measures autoantibodies against citrullinated proteins, highly specific for rheumatoid arthritis diagnosis.

In Plain Language

Rheumatoid arthritis test; Inflammatory joint disease marker

Clinical Context

More specific for rheumatoid arthritis than rheumatoid factor. Present in 70% of rheumatoid arthritis and may appear before clinical symptoms. Predicts disease severity.
