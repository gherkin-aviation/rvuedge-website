85652 Rbc sed rate automated

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: Automated ESR, automated sed rate

Red blood cell sedimentation rate automated test measures the distance red blood cells fall using automated analyzer technology, providing faster and more reproducible inflammation assessment.

In Plain Language

Computer-measured inflammation test; Automated swelling indicator

Clinical Context

Modern replacement for manual ESR with improved reproducibility and faster turnaround time. Used for inflammation screening and monitoring in various medical conditions.
