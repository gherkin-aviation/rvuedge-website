87081 Culture screening only

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: Culture screening only, Screen only, No identification

Bacterial culture screening only without organism identification. Presence/absence determination.

In Plain Language

Simple infection presence test

Clinical Context

Limited screening culture reporting only growth or no growth without identification. Used when full identification not clinically necessary.
