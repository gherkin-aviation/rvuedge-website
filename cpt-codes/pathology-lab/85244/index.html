85244 Clot factor viii reltd antgn

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: Factor VIII antigen, vWF antigen, vWF:Ag

Measurement of factor VIII-related antigen (von Willebrand factor antigen) using immunoassay methodology to quantify von Willebrand factor protein concentration independent of function.

Clinical Context

Evaluates von Willebrand disease and factor VIII deficiency; reduced levels indicate von Willebrand disease or hemophilia, while elevated levels suggest inflammation.
