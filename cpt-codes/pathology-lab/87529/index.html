87529 Hsv dna amp probe

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: HSV amplified test, herpes simplex amplified detection

Herpes simplex virus nucleic acid detection using amplified probe technique. Detects herpes simplex viral infection with higher sensitivity.

In Plain Language

Sensitive herpes test; Amplified herpes simplex detection

Clinical Context

Provides enhanced detection of herpes simplex virus for accurate diagnosis in neonatal herpes, meningitis, and disseminated disease.
