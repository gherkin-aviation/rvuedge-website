83037 Hb glycosylated a1c home device

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: HbA1c home device, at-home A1c test, portable HbA1c

Quantitative measurement of glycosylated hemoglobin using home-based or point-of-care testing device methodology.

Clinical Context

Point-of-care or home-based hemoglobin A1c testing enabling patient self-monitoring of diabetes control. Used for convenient patient education and real-time assessment during office visits or home monitoring programs.
