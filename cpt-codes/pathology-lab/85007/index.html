85007 Bl smear with diff wbc count

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: blood smear with diff, manual differential, slide review

Blood smear examination with manual differential white blood cell count, including morphologic assessment of cells and identification of abnormal forms and inclusions.

In Plain Language

blood cell under microscope; detailed white blood cell count

Clinical Context

Provides morphologic review for abnormal cells, blast forms, parasites, and inclusions; ordered when automated results are abnormal or suspected hematologic disorder.
