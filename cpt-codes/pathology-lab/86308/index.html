86308 Heterophile antibody screening

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: Monospot screen, heterophile antibody

Heterophile antibody screen qualitatively detects heterophile (sheep red blood cell reactive) antibodies characteristic of infectious mononucleosis.

In Plain Language

Infectious mononucleosis test; Monospot test

Clinical Context

Rapid screening test for infectious mononucleosis caused by Epstein-Barr virus. Heterophile antibodies agglutinate sheep red blood cells.

RVU Information
