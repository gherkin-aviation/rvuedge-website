86152 Cell enumeration & id

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: Cell count and differential, cell enumeration

Cell enumeration and identification procedure counts and identifies specific cell populations in various body fluids, assessing cellular composition without physician interpretation.

Clinical Context

Technical procedure for quantifying and identifying cells in blood, cerebrospinal fluid, synovial fluid, or other body specimens. Foundation for many hematologic diagnoses.
