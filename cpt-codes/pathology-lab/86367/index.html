86367 Stem cells total count

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: stem cell count, progenitor cell count

Total count of stem cells in blood or bone marrow, measuring progenitor cells capable of differentiation into multiple cell lineages.

Clinical Context

Used to quantify hematopoietic stem cells prior to stem cell mobilization or collection for transplantation. Guides dosing of mobilization agents and timing of apheresis procedures in patients undergoing autologous or allogeneic stem cell transplantation.
