87107 Fungi identification mold

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: fungal culture, mold ID, fungal identification

Culture-based identification of fungal and mold organisms from clinical specimens using standard microbiological techniques and biochemical testing.

Clinical Context

Used to identify fungal infections in patients with suspected mycotic infections, respiratory symptoms, or immunocompromised states. Commonly ordered for dermatophyte, Candida, or Aspergillus identification.
