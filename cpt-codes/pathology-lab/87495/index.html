Pathology & Lab

Also known as: CMV test, cytomegalovirus detection

Cytomegalovirus nucleic acid detection using direct probe technique without amplification. Identifies cytomegaloviral infection in clinical specimens.

In Plain Language

Cytomegalovirus test; CMV infection detection

Clinical Context

Detects cytomegalovirus (CMV) in blood, cerebrospinal fluid, or tissue specimens. Critical in immunocompromised patients, transplant recipients, and congenital infection evaluation.
