82757 Assay of semen fructose

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: semen fructose test, seminal fluid fructose

Laboratory assay measuring fructose concentration in semen specimens for assessment of seminal vesicle function and male fertility evaluation.

In Plain Language

semen sugar test

Clinical Context

Used in male infertility evaluation to assess seminal vesicle function and determine presence of functional ejaculate.
