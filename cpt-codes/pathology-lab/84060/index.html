84060 Assay acid phosphatase

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: acid phosphatase, acid phos

Quantitative measurement of acid phosphatase enzyme activity in serum using colorimetric or kinetic enzymatic assay methods.

In Plain Language

prostate enzyme test; tissue enzyme measurement

Clinical Context

Assessment of enzyme from prostate and other tissues. Elevated acid phosphatase historically used for prostate cancer detection; now largely replaced by PSA.

RVU Information
