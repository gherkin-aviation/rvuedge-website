83615 Lactate (ld) (ldh) enzyme

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: lactate dehydrogenase, LDH enzyme, LD assay

Quantitative measurement of lactate dehydrogenase (LD/LDH) enzyme activity in serum using kinetic or colorimetric methods.

In Plain Language

tissue damage enzyme test; heart and muscle injury marker

Clinical Context

Nonspecific marker of tissue injury used in myocardial infarction, hemolysis, hepatitis, and malignancy evaluation. Must be interpreted with LDH isoenzymes for specificity.

RVU Information
