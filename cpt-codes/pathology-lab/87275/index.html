87275 Influenza b ag if

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: flu B antigen, influenza B IF, flu B

Detection of influenza B virus antigens directly from respiratory specimens using immunofluorescence microscopy.

In Plain Language

type B flu test

Clinical Context

Rapid detection of influenza B virus during respiratory infection. Seasonally variable with peak incidence in winter months.
