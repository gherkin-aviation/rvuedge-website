86431 Rheumatoid factor quantitative

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: RF quantitative, rheumatoid factor titer

Rheumatoid factor quantitative measurement determining precise concentration level of rheumatoid factor in patient serum by titration.

In Plain Language

arthritis antibody level; rheumatoid antibody strength

Clinical Context

Quantifies rheumatoid factor levels in patients with positive screening. Higher titers may correlate with disease severity and poor prognosis. Used to monitor treatment response in rheumatoid arthritis.

RVU Information
