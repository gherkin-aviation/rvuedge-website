89264 Identify sperm tissue

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: identify sperm tissue, sperm identification tissue

Identification of sperm in tissue specimens or samples to assess azoospermia or confirm sperm presence.

Clinical Context

Used to identify sperm in tissues or fluids when azoospermia is questioned or to confirm viable sperm.
