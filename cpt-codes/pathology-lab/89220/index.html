89220 Sputum specimen collection

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: sputum specimen collection, sputum collection

Collection and handling of sputum specimen from patient for subsequent microbiologic or pathologic analysis.

In Plain Language

coughed mucus collection; sputum sample gathering

Clinical Context
