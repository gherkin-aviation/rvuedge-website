80176 Assay of lidocaine

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: lidocaine level, antiarrhythmic level

Laboratory quantitative drug assay for lidocaine to assess serum level for therapeutic drug monitoring and toxicity evaluation in cardiac therapy.

Clinical Context

Monitors lidocaine levels for intravenous antiarrhythmic therapy; narrow therapeutic window requires frequent monitoring to avoid toxicity.
