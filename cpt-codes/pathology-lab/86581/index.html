86581 Strptcs pneum antb serot ia

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: pneumococcal antibody, streptococcus pneumoniae serology

Streptococcus pneumoniae antibody serotype immunoassay measuring immune response to specific pneumococcal polysaccharide capsular antigens.

In Plain Language

pneumonia antibody test; lung infection immunity test

Clinical Context

Evaluates pneumococcal vaccine response and documents post-vaccine antibody development. Used to assess immune adequacy following pneumococcal conjugate or polysaccharide vaccination.
