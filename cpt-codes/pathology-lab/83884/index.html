83884 Assay neurflmnt light chain

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: neurofilament light chain, NfL assay

Quantitative measurement of neurofilament light chain protein in serum or cerebrospinal fluid using immunoassay methodology.

In Plain Language

nerve damage marker; neurodegeneration indicator

Clinical Context

Biomarker for neuronal injury in neurodegenerative diseases including multiple sclerosis, Alzheimer disease, and traumatic brain injury. Emerging as prognostic tool.
