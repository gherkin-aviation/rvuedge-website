86794 Zika virus igm antibody

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: Zika IgM, Zika acute antibody

Serologic testing to detect immunoglobulin M (IgM) antibodies against Zika virus, indicating acute or recent infection. Distinguishes current from past exposure.

Clinical Context

Critical in pregnant women or those planning pregnancy due to teratogenic risk causing congenital Zika syndrome. IgM positivity indicates recent infection within past 1-2 months.
