84135 Assay of pregnanediol

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: pregnanediol assay, progesterone metabolite, ovulation marker

Quantitative assay of serum pregnanediol, a metabolite of progesterone indicating ovulation and luteal phase function.

In Plain Language

ovulation test

Clinical Context

Assesses ovulatory function and luteal phase adequacy in fertility evaluations; elevated levels confirm successful ovulation.
