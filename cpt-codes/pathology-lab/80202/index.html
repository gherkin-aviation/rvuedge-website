80202 Assay of vancomycin

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: vancomycin level, glycopeptide level

Laboratory quantitative drug assay for vancomycin to assess serum level for therapeutic drug monitoring in serious gram-positive infections.

In Plain Language

vancomycin blood test; antibiotic level test

Clinical Context

Therapeutic drug monitoring for vancomycin to optimize efficacy in methicillin-resistant Staphylococcus aureus and serious gram-positive infections while avoiding nephrotoxicity.
