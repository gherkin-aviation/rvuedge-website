85384 Fibrinogen activity

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: fibrinogen activity, functional fibrinogen

Measurement of fibrinogen functional activity using clotting methodology to assess fibrinogen ability to be converted to fibrin and participate in clot formation.

In Plain Language

functional fibrinogen test; working fibrinogen level

Clinical Context

Evaluates dysfibrinogenemia and disseminated intravascular coagulation where fibrinogen activity may be discordant with antigen levels.

RVU Information
