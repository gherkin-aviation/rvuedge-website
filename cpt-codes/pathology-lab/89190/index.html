89190 Nasal smear for eosinophils

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: nasal smear for eosinophils, nasal eosinophil test

Microscopic examination of nasal mucus smear for identification and quantification of eosinophils.

In Plain Language

allergy cell count in nasal mucus; allergy nose test

Clinical Context

Used to assess allergic rhinitis and differentiate from infectious or other causes of nasal symptoms.
