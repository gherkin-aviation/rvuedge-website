83704 Lipoprotein bld quan part

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: lipoprotein quantitation, lipid particle measurement

Quantitative measurement of lipoprotein (a) and other lipoprotein fractions in blood using standardized methodologies.

In Plain Language

cholesterol particle size and count test

Clinical Context

Detailed lipoprotein particle analysis for advanced cardiovascular risk stratification. Includes patterns such as Pattern A (large particles) versus Pattern B (small dense particles).
