86804 Hep c ab test confirm

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: Hepatitis C confirmatory test, HCV confirmation, HCV Western blot

Serologic testing to detect and confirm antibodies against hepatitis C virus through supplemental or confirmatory methodology. Verifies positive HCV screening results.

In Plain Language

Confirmed hepatitis C test; Hepatitis C verification

Clinical Context

Performed to confirm positive hepatitis C serology results. Confirmatory test provides greater specificity than initial screening antibody test.
