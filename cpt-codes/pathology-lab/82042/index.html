82042 Other source albumin quan each

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: urine albumin, CSF albumin, other fluid albumin

Albumin quantitative measurement from non-serum sources (urine, cerebrospinal fluid, other body fluids) for protein characterization.

In Plain Language

protein in urine test; kidney protein leak test; spinal fluid protein test

Clinical Context

Quantifies proteinuria in diabetic nephropathy and other kidney diseases; elevated cerebrospinal fluid albumin suggests bloodbrain barrier disruption.

RVU Information
