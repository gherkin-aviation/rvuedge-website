86340 Intrinsic factor antibody

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: Anti-intrinsic factor, intrinsic factor antibody

Intrinsic factor antibody test measures autoantibodies against intrinsic factor, diagnostic for autoimmune gastritis and pernicious anemia.

Clinical Context

Highly specific for autoimmune gastritis and pernicious anemia. Antibodies prevent vitamin B12 absorption causing megaloblastic anemia.
