87627 Joint spc pthgn & prescription rsist gen26+

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: Synovial fluid pathogen panel, joint infection multiplex

Joint space pathogen nucleic acid detection and resistance gene identification for 26 or more organisms. Multiplex assay for arthrocentesis specimens.

Clinical Context

Detects bacterial, fungal, and viral pathogens in synovial fluid for diagnosis of septic arthritis and infectious joint disease.
