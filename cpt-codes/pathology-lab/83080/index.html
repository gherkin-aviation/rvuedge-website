83080 Assay of b hexosaminidase each

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: beta-hexosaminidase assay, hex-A/hex-B ratio

Quantitative assay of beta-hexosaminidase enzyme activity in serum, plasma, or tissue samples.

Clinical Context

Screening for hexosaminidase deficiency associated with Tay-Sachs disease and Sandhoff disease. Critical in neonatal screening programs and carrier identification in Jewish populations.
