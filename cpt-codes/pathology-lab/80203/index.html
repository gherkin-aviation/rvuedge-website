80203 Drug screening quantitative zonisamide

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: zonisamide level, Zonegran level

Laboratory quantitative drug screen for zonisamide to assess serum level for therapeutic drug monitoring in seizure management.

In Plain Language

zonisamide blood test; seizure medication level

Clinical Context

Monitors zonisamide levels in epilepsy management; primarily used in partial seizures and selected generalizedSeizures.
