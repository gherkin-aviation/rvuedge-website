80329 Analgesics non-opioid 1 or 2

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: non-opioid analgesic screen, acetaminophen test

Drug screen, analgesics non-opioid, one or two agents. Detects non-opioid pain medication including acetaminophen, nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drugs, and other analgesics.

In Plain Language

pain medicine test

Clinical Context

Used in pain management monitoring, toxicology screening, and substance abuse evaluation. Common in emergency and addiction medicine settings.

RVU Information
