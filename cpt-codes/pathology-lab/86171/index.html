86171 Complement fixation each

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: Complement fixation, CF test

Complement fixation test detects antibodies to specific antigens by their ability to fix complement, used for infectious disease diagnosis and syphilis serology.

Clinical Context

Traditional method for diagnosing fungal infections, certain viral infections, and syphilis. Detects antibodies indirectly through complement consumption.
