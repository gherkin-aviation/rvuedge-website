86658 Enterovirus antibody

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: enterovirus antibody, coxsackievirus serology

Enterovirus antibody detection measuring immune response to members of Enterovirus genus including coxsackievirus and echovirus.

In Plain Language

viral infection antibody test; enterovirus test

Clinical Context

Detects enterovirus antibodies in patients with respiratory, gastrointestinal, or neurologic disease. Useful for diagnosis when viral isolation unsuccessful.
