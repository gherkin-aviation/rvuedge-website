86941 Hemolysins/agglutinins

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: Hemolysin/agglutinin alloimmune, Alloimmune antibody test

Serologic testing to detect hemolysins and agglutinins with alloimmune specificity. Identifies alloimmune hemolytic complications.

In Plain Language

Transfusion reaction antibody test

Clinical Context

Detects alloimmune antibodies causing hemolysis or agglutination in hemolytic transfusion reactions and post-transfusion hemolytic anemia.
