89354 Thaw cryoprsvrd reprod tissue

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: thaw cryo tissue, tissue thaw

Thawing of cryopreserved reproductive tissue for fertility preservation and assisted reproduction procedures.

Clinical Context

Used in fertility clinics when patients use previously cryopreserved ovarian, testicular, or embryonic tissue for pregnancy planning. Essential step in utilizing frozen reproductive material.
