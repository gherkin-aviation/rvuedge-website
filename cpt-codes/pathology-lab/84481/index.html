84481 Free assay (ft-3)

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: free T3, FT3, free triiodothyronine

Quantitative assay of free triiodothyronine (free T3) concentration, measuring unbound physiologically active T3.

Clinical Context

More specific than total T3; evaluates thyroid function in complex cases and thyroiditis.
