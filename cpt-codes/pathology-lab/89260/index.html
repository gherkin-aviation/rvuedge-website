89260 Sperm isolation simple

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: sperm isolation simple, sperm wash, simple sperm preparation

Simple sperm separation technique to isolate motile sperm from seminal fluid for assisted reproductive procedures.

Clinical Context

Standard procedure for preparing sperm for intrauterine insemination or in vitro fertilization.
