Pathology & Lab

Also known as: VGCC antibody, calcium channel antibody

Voltage-gated calcium channel antibody detection identifying autoimmune response against neural calcium channels associated with neuromuscular disorders.

In Plain Language

nerve-muscle antibody test; neurological autoimmune test

Clinical Context

Detects antibodies to voltage-gated calcium channels in Lambert-Eaton myasthenic syndrome and other autoimmune neuromuscular disorders. Positive results confirm autoimmune etiology.
