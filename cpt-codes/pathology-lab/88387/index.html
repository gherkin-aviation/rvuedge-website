Pathology & Lab

Also known as: macroscopic exam dissection tissue preparation, tissue macroscopic prep

Macroscopic examination, dissection, and tissue preparation for specialized molecular, immunologic, or other advanced analyses.

Specialized gross examination and preparation of tissue specimens when molecular or advanced testing is required.
