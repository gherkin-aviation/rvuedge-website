87521 Hepatitis c probe & rvrs trnsc

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: Hepatitis C probe and quantitation, HCV reverse transcription test

Hepatitis C virus nucleic acid detection and quantification using probe technique combined with reverse transcriptase amplification. Detects and measures HCV RNA.

In Plain Language

Hepatitis C detection and measurement; Liver virus RNA quantitation

Clinical Context

Detects and quantifies hepatitis C viral ribonucleic acid for diagnosis and treatment monitoring in chronic hepatitis C.
