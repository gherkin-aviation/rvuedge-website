86927 Plasma fresh frozen

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: Fresh frozen plasma, FFP, Plasma transfusion

Fresh frozen plasma preparation and processing for transfusion. Contains clotting factors and fibrinogen.

In Plain Language

Clotting factor replacement; Plasma transfusion

Clinical Context

Used to replace multiple clotting factors in coagulopathy, massive transfusion, and specific factor deficiencies when concentrated factors unavailable.

RVU Information
