89337 Cryopreservation oocyte(s)

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: cryopreservation oocyte, oocyte freezing, egg freezing

Cryopreservation (freezing) of oocytes (unfertilized eggs) for future use and fertility preservation.

In Plain Language

egg freezing for future; female fertility preservation

Clinical Context

Fertility preservation for women facing cancer treatment or those delaying childbearing.

RVU Information
