84377 Sugars multiple qualitative

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: multiple sugar screen, sugar panel, carbohydrate screening

Qualitative test for multiple sugar types in body fluids using various chemical reactions and separation.

In Plain Language

multiple sugar screening test

Clinical Context

Identifies presence of multiple sugars (glucose, fructose, galactose) in urine; useful for diagnosing inborn errors of metabolism.
