88356 Analysis nerve

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: morphometric analysis nerve, nerve fiber analysis

Morphometric and photometric analysis of nerve tissue including axon count, fiber size distribution, and myelin thickness measurement.

In Plain Language

nerve fiber measurement and analysis; detailed nerve tissue examination

Clinical Context

Used in diagnosis of peripheral neuropathies, demyelinating diseases, and other neurologic conditions requiring quantitative nerve analysis.
