83013 H pylori (c-13) breath

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: H. pylori breath test, C-13 urea breath test, UBT

Helicobacter pylori breath test analysis using carbon-13 labeled urea to detect active H. pylori infection in the stomach.

In Plain Language

breath test for stomach bacteria; blow test for H. pylori

Clinical Context

Non-invasive test for active H. pylori infection; used for initial diagnosis and confirmation of eradication after treatment.
