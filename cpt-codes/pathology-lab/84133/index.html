84133 Assay of urine potassium

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: urine potassium, 24-hour potassium, urinary K+

Quantitative assay of urine potassium concentration, reflecting potassium excretion and renal function.

Clinical Context

Evaluates potassium balance, renal function, and electrolyte homeostasis; used to diagnose causes of hypokalemia or hyperkalemia.
