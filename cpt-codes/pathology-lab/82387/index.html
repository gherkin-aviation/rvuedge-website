82387 Assay of cathepsin-d

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: cathepsin-D assay, protease measurement, tumor progression marker

Immunoassay or enzymatic measurement of cathepsin-D protease activity in tissue extracts or body fluids.

In Plain Language

protein-breaking enzyme test; cancer aggressiveness marker; protease activity test

Clinical Context

Elevated cathepsin-D associated with breast cancer aggressiveness and prognosis; less commonly used in current practice.
