86932 Frozen blood freeze/thaw

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: Freeze-thaw cycle, Cryopreservation cycle, Complete preservation

Combined freezing and thawing (cryopreservation cycle) of blood products. Complete preservation and recovery cycle.

Clinical Context

Used in processing autologous blood and special blood products requiring both freezing for storage and thawing for transfusion.
