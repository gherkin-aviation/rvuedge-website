82777 Galectin-3

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: galectin-3 level, gal-3 assay

Laboratory assay measuring galectin-3 concentration in serum, a biomarker associated with inflammation, fibrosis, and cardiac remodeling.

In Plain Language

Clinical Context
