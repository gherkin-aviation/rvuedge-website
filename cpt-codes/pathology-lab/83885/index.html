83885 Assay of nickel

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: nickel assay, blood nickel

Quantitative measurement of nickel concentration in blood or urine using atomic absorption spectrophotometry or mass spectrometry.

Clinical Context

Assessment of occupational nickel exposure in industries including stainless steel, electroplating, and welding. Elevated nickel associated with respiratory cancer risk.
