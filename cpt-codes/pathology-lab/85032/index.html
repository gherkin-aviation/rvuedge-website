85032 Manual cell count each

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: manual cell count, hemocytometer count, parasitic count

Manual count of specific blood cell populations (e.g., red cells, white cells, parasites) performed using hemocytometer and microscopy, with cost per individual cell type counted.

Clinical Context

Used for cell counts in cerebrospinal fluid, body cavity fluids, and parasite identification when automated analysis unavailable or inappropriate.
