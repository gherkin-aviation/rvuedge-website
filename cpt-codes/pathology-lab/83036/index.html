83036 Hemoglobin glycosylated a1c

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: hemoglobin A1c, glycated hemoglobin, HbA1c test

Quantitative measurement of glycosylated hemoglobin (hemoglobin A1c) using chromatographic, immunological, or electrochemical methods.

In Plain Language

blood sugar control test; three-month average blood glucose; long-term diabetes marker

Clinical Context

Primary test for diabetes screening and monitoring, reflecting average blood glucose over 2-3 months. Used by endocrinologists and primary care physicians for diagnosis and treatment adjustment in diabetic patients.

RVU Information
