84510 Assay of tyrosine

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: tyrosine, serum tyrosine, amino acid

Quantitative assay of serum tyrosine concentration, measuring this amino acid involved in protein synthesis.

In Plain Language

amino acid test

Clinical Context

Evaluates amino acid metabolism disorders; elevated in certain inborn errors of metabolism and liver disease.
