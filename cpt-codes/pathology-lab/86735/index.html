86735 Mumps antibody

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: mumps antibody, mumps serology

Mumps virus antibody detection measuring immune response to paramyxovirus causing mumps.

In Plain Language

viral infection test; parotitis antibody test

Clinical Context

Detects mumps antibodies indicating immunity from vaccination or past infection. Used to assess vaccine response and confirm diagnosis.
