83735 Assay of magnesium

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: serum magnesium, magnesium assay

Quantitative measurement of magnesium concentration in serum, plasma, or urine using atomic absorption spectrophotometry or ion-selective electrode methods.

In Plain Language

magnesium level test; mineral measurement

Clinical Context

Assessment of magnesium status in evaluation of muscle cramps, cardiac arrhythmias, and neuromuscular disorders. Important electrolyte in critical illness monitoring.

RVU Information
