83045 Hgb methemoglobin qualitative

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: methemoglobin qualitative, MetHb screen

Qualitative assessment of methemoglobin presence in blood using spectrophotometric or co-oximetry methods.

In Plain Language

abnormal blood protein screen

Clinical Context

Screening for methemoglobinemia when toxin exposure is suspected, particularly from medications like dapsone or local anesthetics. Critical in emergency and critical care settings for unexplained cyanosis or hypoxia.
