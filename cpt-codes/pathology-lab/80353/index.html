80353 Drug screening cocaine

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: cocaine screen, benzoylecgonine assay

Drug screen for cocaine and its metabolites including benzoylecgonine, a metabolite of cocaine.

Clinical Context

Commonly used in emergency departments, workplace drug testing, and substance abuse programs. Cocaine metabolites persist longer than parent drug.
