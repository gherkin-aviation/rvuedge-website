85013 Spun microhematocrit

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: spun hematocrit, micro hematocrit, packed cell volume

Microhematocrit determination using centrifugation of capillary tube to measure percentage of blood volume composed of red blood cells, indicating anemia or polycythemia severity.

In Plain Language

red blood cell percentage test; packed cell volume

Clinical Context

Rapid point-of-care hematocrit measurement by capillary centrifugation; more accurate than automated hematocrit and useful in clinical settings without laboratory infrastructure.
