81120 Idh1 common variants

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: IDH1 testing, isocitrate dehydrogenase 1

Molecular analysis to identify common pathogenic variants in the Isocitrate Dehydrogenase 1 (IDH1) gene, often found in glioblastoma and acute myeloid leukemia.

In Plain Language

brain cancer gene test; leukemia mutation screening

Clinical Context

Used to identify IDH1 mutations in glioblastomas and acute myeloid leukemias for prognostic stratification and targeted therapy eligibility assessment.
