82286 Assay of bradykinin

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: bradykinin level, kinin test, kallikrein-kinin assessment

Immunoassay measurement of bradykinin in plasma to evaluate plasma kallikrein-kinin system activity.

Clinical Context

Elevated in bradykinin-mediated conditions including hereditary angioedema and certain inflammatory states.
