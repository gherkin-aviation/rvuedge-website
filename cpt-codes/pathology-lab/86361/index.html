86361 T cell absolute count

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: T cell absolute count, total T count

Absolute count of T lymphocytes in peripheral blood, quantifying total number of T cells per microliter of blood.

Clinical Context

Evaluates immune competence in immunocompromised states, monitors disease progression in HIV infection, and assesses adequacy of immune reconstitution after transplantation or vaccination.
