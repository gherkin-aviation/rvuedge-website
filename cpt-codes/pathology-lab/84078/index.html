84078 Assay alkaline phosphatase

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: alkaline phosphatase isoenzymes, ALP fractionation

Quantitative measurement of alkaline phosphatase isoenzymes in serum, identifying tissue sources (liver, bone, intestine, placenta).

Clinical Context

Determination of alkaline phosphatase origin to evaluate hepatic versus skeletal source of enzyme elevation. Aids in differentiation of various liver and bone diseases.
