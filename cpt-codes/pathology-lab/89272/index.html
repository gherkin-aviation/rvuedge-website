89272 Extended culture of oocytes

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: extended culture of oocytes, blastocyst culture

Extended culture of oocytes or embryos beyond 4 days for advanced developmental assessment in assisted reproduction.

In Plain Language

longer egg/embryo growing time; extended embryo development

Clinical Context

Prolonged culture to allow embryos to develop to blastocyst stage for improved selection and implantation potential.

RVU Information

CPT 89272 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
