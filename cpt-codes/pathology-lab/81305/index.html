Pathology & Lab

Also known as: MYD88 L265P testing, lymphoplasmacytic lymphoma genetic test

Myeloid Differentiation Primary Response gene (MYD88) gene analysis for the pathogenic p.Leu265Pro variant associated with lymphoplasmacytic lymphoma.

In Plain Language

Lymphoma mutation test; Blood cancer genetic marker

Clinical Context

Detects MYD88 L265P variant present in majority of lymphoplasmacytic lymphomas and Waldenstrom macroglobulinemia cases.
