86341 Islet cell antibody

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: ICA, islet cell antibody

Islet cell antibody test detects autoantibodies against pancreatic islet cell antigens, marker for autoimmune diabetes mellitus and type 1 diabetes risk.

In Plain Language

Type 1 diabetes marker; Pancreas autoimmunity test

Clinical Context

Detects autoimmunity against pancreatic beta cells predicting type 1 diabetes development. Present in most new-onset type 1 diabetics.
