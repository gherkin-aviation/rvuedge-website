84704 Hcg free betachain test

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: free beta hCG, free hCG, beta subunit

Quantitative assay of free beta subunit of human chorionic gonadotropin (free beta hCG) in serum or urine, measured by immunoassay to provide pregnancy risk assessment and prenatal screening.

In Plain Language

free hCG test; pregnancy risk marker

Clinical Context

Prenatal screening (trimester 1 and 2) for chromosomal abnormalities, Down syndrome, and neural tube defects; more specific than total hCG for pregnancy characterization.
