80323 Alkaloids not otherwise specified

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: alkaloid screen, alkaloid assay

Drug test for alkaloids not otherwise specified, including natural and synthetic alkaloid compounds not covered by specific drug codes.

Clinical Context

Used to detect various alkaloid-containing substances including some plant-derived drugs and novel psychoactive compounds. Less common than specific drug screens.
