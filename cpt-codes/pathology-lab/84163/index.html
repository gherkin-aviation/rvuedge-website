84163 Pappa serum

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: PAPPA, pregnancy protein A, first-trimester screening

Quantitative assay of serum pregnancy-associated plasma protein A (PAPPA), a marker for first-trimester fetal abnormalities.

In Plain Language

pregnancy screening test

Clinical Context

Part of first-trimester combined screening (11-14 weeks) for Down syndrome and other chromosomal abnormalities; decreased levels increase risk.

RVU Information
