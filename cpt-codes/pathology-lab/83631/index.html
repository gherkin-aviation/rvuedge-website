83631 Lactoferrin fecal (quant)

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: fecal lactoferrin quantitative, lactoferrin stool level

Quantitative measurement of lactoferrin concentration in fecal specimen using immunoassay methods.

Clinical Context

Quantification of intestinal inflammation for assessment of inflammatory bowel disease activity and response to therapy. Also used in evaluation of infectious colitis.
