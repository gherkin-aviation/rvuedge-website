82239 Bile acids total

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: total bile acids, serum bile acids, bile acid test

Enzymatic assay measuring total bile acid concentration in serum to assess enterohepatic circulation and liver-gut function.

In Plain Language

liver and gut function test; bile flow test; cholestasis screening

Clinical Context

Elevated in cholestasis, biliary obstruction, and cirrhosis; reflects hepatic synthesis and excretion function.
