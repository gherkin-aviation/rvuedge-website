86580 Tb intradermal test

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: TB skin test, Mantoux test, PPD test

Tuberculosis intradermal test (Mantoux test) measuring delayed-type hypersensitivity response to purified protein derivative antigen at 48-72 hours.

Clinical Context

Traditional screening for tuberculosis infection. Induration measured at 48-72 hours with interpretation based on patient risk factors. Limitations include bacille Calmette-Guérin vaccination false positives and variable sensitivity.
