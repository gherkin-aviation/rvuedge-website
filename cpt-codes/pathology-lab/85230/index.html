85230 Clot factor vii proconvertin

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: Factor VII assay, proconvertin assay

Assay of coagulation factor VII (proconvertin) using one-stage correction methodology to measure factor VII functional activity in the extrinsic coagulation pathway.

In Plain Language

clotting factor VII test; proconvertin measurement

Clinical Context

Evaluates factor VII deficiency in liver disease, vitamin K deficiency, disseminated intravascular coagulation, or warfarin therapy.
