86360 T cell absolute count/ratio

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: T cell absolute count, T cell ratio

Absolute T cell count and ratio determination via flow cytometry, providing both enumeration and percentage of T lymphocytes in circulation.

In Plain Language

T cell count and percentage; T lymphocyte levels

Clinical Context

Identifies T cell deficiency or expansion; used in evaluating immunodeficiencies, monitoring antiretroviral therapy effectiveness, and assessing post-transplantation recovery. Provides both absolute numbers and relative proportions.
