89254 Oocyte identification

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: oocyte identification, egg cell evaluation, oocyte assessment

Identification and evaluation of oocytes (egg cells) to assess maturity and morphologic quality for fertilization.

In Plain Language

egg cell examination; egg maturity check

Clinical Context

Standard procedure in in vitro fertilization to identify mature oocytes capable of fertilization.

RVU Information
