83003 Assay growth hormone (hgh)

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: growth hormone level, HGH, somatotropin

Laboratory assay measuring human growth hormone concentration in serum for assessment of growth, metabolism, and growth hormone secretion disorders.

In Plain Language

growth hormone test

Clinical Context

Used in evaluation of growth disorders in children, diagnosis of growth hormone deficiency or excess, and assessment of pituitary function.
