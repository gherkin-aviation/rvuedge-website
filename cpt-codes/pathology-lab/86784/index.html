86784 Trichinella antibody

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: Trichinella serology, Trichinella antibody

Serologic testing to detect antibodies against Trichinella spiralis, the nematode causing trichinellosis from consumption of undercooked infected meat. Confirms infection.

In Plain Language

Undercooked meat parasite test; Muscle parasite antibody test

Clinical Context

Indicated in suspected trichinellosis presenting with myalgia, fever, and eosinophilia following consumption of undercooked pork or wild game. Antibodies appear 3 weeks after infection.
