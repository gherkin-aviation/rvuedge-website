80436 Metyrapone panel

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: metyrapone test, 11-DOC test

Metyrapone panel measuring 11-deoxycortisol and cortisol response to metyrapone administration for assessment of hypothalamic-pituitary-adrenal axis.

In Plain Language

cortisol pathway test

Clinical Context

Used to assess pituitary-adrenal reserve and differentiate causes of hypercortisolism or adrenal insufficiency.
