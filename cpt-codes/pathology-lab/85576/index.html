85576 Blood platelet aggregation

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: Platelet aggregation, agg study

Blood platelet aggregation test measures the ability of platelets to clump together in response to aggregating agents, assessing platelet function and hemostatic competency.

Clinical Context

Used to diagnose platelet function disorders, assess aspirin effectiveness, evaluate bleeding diathesis, and screen for von Willebrand disease or other coagulation abnormalities.
