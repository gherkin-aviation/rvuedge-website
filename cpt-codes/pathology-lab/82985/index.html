82985 Assay of glycated protein

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: fructosamine test, glycated serum protein

Laboratory assay measuring glycated protein concentration in serum, reflecting average glucose control over preceding 2-3 weeks of amino acid metabolism.

In Plain Language

intermediate glucose control test

Clinical Context

Used for intermediate glucose control assessment, particularly in pregnancy and short-term monitoring of diabetes management.

RVU Information
