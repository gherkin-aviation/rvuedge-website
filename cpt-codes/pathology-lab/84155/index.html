84155 Assay of protein serum

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: serum protein, total protein, TP

Quantitative assay of serum protein concentration, measuring total protein including albumin and globulins.

In Plain Language

blood protein level test

Clinical Context

Basic chemistry panel component; abnormal levels indicate malnutrition, liver disease, kidney disease, or immunologic disorders.

RVU Information
