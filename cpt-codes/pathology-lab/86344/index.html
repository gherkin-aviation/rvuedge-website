86344 Leukocyte phagocytosis

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: Phagocytic function test, neutrophil function test

Leukocyte phagocytosis test measures the ability of neutrophils and other leukocytes to engulf and destroy bacteria, assessing critical immune function.

In Plain Language

Immune cell bacteria-fighting test; White blood cell function

Clinical Context

Used to diagnose neutrophil dysfunction and phagocytic defects in immunocompromised patients. Tests actual bacterial killing ability of leukocytes.
