80204 Drug assay methotrexate

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: methotrexate level, MTX level

Laboratory quantitative drug assay for methotrexate to assess serum level for therapeutic drug monitoring in cancer and immunosuppressive therapy.

Clinical Context

Monitors methotrexate levels after high-dose therapy to assess drug exposure and guide leucovorin rescue timing.
