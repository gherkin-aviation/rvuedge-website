80280 Drug assay vedolizumab

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: vedolizumab level, Entyvio level

Laboratory quantitative drug assay for vedolizumab to assess serum level for therapeutic drug monitoring in inflammatory bowel disease treatment.

In Plain Language

vedolizumab blood test; biological medication level

Clinical Context

Monitors integrin antagonist vedolizumab levels in ulcerative colitis and Crohn's disease for optimization of therapeutic efficacy and antibody detection.
