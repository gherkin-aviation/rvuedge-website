82554 Creatine isoforms

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: CK isoforms, CK isoenzymes, CK fractionation

Laboratory assay measuring creatine kinase isoforms (CK-BB, CK-MB, CK-MM) to identify tissue source of enzyme elevation and improve diagnostic specificity.

Clinical Context

Utilized to differentiate sources of elevated CK between cardiac, skeletal, and smooth muscle pathology.
