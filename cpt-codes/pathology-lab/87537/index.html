87537 Hiv-2 dna dir probe

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: HIV-2 test, HIV-2 direct probe

Human immunodeficiency virus type 2 nucleic acid detection using direct probe technique without amplification. Identifies HIV-2 infection.

Clinical Context

Detects human immunodeficiency virus type 2 in blood. HIV-2 is less common than HIV-1, primarily found in West Africa.
