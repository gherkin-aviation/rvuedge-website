83593 Fractionation ketosteroids

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: ketosteroid fractionation, 17-KS fractionation

Separation and quantitation of individual 17-ketosteroids in urine using chromatographic methods.

Clinical Context

Detailed analysis of androgen metabolism to identify specific steroid elevations in adrenal or gonadal disorders. Used when initial 17-KS is abnormal.
