84233 Assay of estrogen

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: estrogen, serum estrogen, estradiol

Quantitative assay of serum estrogen (estradiol, estrone, estriol) concentrations in blood.

In Plain Language

female hormone test

Clinical Context

Evaluates menstrual disorders, perimenopause, hypogonadism, and hormone replacement therapy; fluctuates with menstrual cycle.
