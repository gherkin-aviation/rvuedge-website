84307 Assay of somatostatin

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: somatostatin, somatostatin level, SRIF

Quantitative assay of serum somatostatin concentration, measuring this inhibitory neuroendocrine hormone.

In Plain Language

hormone regulation test

Clinical Context

Elevated in somatostatinomas and pancreatic neuroendocrine tumors; suppresses growth hormone, thyroid hormone, and insulin secretion.

RVU Information
