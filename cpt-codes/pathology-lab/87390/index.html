87390 Hiv-1 ag ia

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: HIV-1 antigen, HIV-1 Ag IA, p24 antigen

Detection of human immunodeficiency virus type 1 antigen from serum using immunoassay methodology.

Clinical Context

Detects HIV-1 p24 antigen during acute infection window. Earlier detection than antibody testing alone. Used for confirmation of acute HIV-1 infection.
