85004 Automated diff wbc count

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: automated diff, auto diff WBC, differential WBC

Automated differential white blood cell (WBC) count performed by hematology analyzer, quantifying percentages and absolute numbers of neutrophils, lymphocytes, monocytes, eosinophils, and basophils.

Clinical Context

Component of complete blood count (CBC) used to identify infections, leukemia, immune disorders, and acute/chronic inflammatory conditions based on WBC proportions.
