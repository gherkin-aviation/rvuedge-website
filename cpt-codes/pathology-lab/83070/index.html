83070 Assay of hemosiderin qualitative

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: hemosiderin qualitative, hemosiderin screen

Qualitative assay for hemosiderin, an iron storage compound, in biological specimens using Prussian blue stain or immunological methods.

Clinical Context

Assessment of tissue iron overload or chronic hemolysis indicated by hemosiderin accumulation. Used in evaluation of hemochromatosis, multiple transfusions, or chronic hemolytic anemias.
