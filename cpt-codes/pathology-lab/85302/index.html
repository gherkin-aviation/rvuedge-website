85302 Clot inhibit prot c antigen

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: protein C antigen, PC antigen

Measurement of protein C antigen concentration using immunoassay to quantify vitamin K-dependent anticoagulant protein C levels independent of functional activity.

Clinical Context

Evaluates protein C deficiency contributing to thrombophilia and warfarin-induced skin necrosis; identifies quantitative versus qualitative deficiency.
