89342 Storage/year embryo(s)

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: storage embryo yearly, embryo storage per year

Storage and maintenance of cryopreserved embryo(s) for one year including periodic monitoring.

In Plain Language

keeping frozen embryos for a year; annual embryo storage fee

Clinical Context

Annual storage fee for cryopreserved embryos for patients using in vitro fertilization.
