86318 Ia infectious agent antibody

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: Infectious disease serology, pathogen antibody

Immunoassay infectious agent antibody test detects antibodies against infectious agents, indicating exposure or infection with bacteria, viruses, fungi, or parasites.

In Plain Language

Infection antibody test; Disease exposure detection

Clinical Context

Identifies past or present infection with specific pathogens through antibody detection. Used for syphilis, HIV, hepatitis, toxoplasmosis, and many other infections.
