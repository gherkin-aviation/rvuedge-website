81226 Cyp2d6 gene com variants

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: CYP2D6 pharmacogenetic panel, CYP2D6 variant testing

Molecular analysis of the CYP2D6 gene to identify common pharmacogenetic variants affecting drug metabolism of numerous medications.

In Plain Language

medicine metabolism gene test; drug response genetic testing

Clinical Context

Used to guide dosing and selection of medications metabolized by CYP2D6 (e.g., antipsychotics, antidepressants, codeine) for therapeutic optimization.
