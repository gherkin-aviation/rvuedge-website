86353 Lymphocyte transformation

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: Lymphocyte blastogenesis, lymphocyte transformation

Lymphocyte transformation test measures lymphocyte proliferation and differentiation in response to mitogens or antigens, assessing T cell and B cell function.

In Plain Language

T cell function test; Immune response measurement

Clinical Context

Used to assess cellular immune function in severe immunodeficiency, chronic infections, and drug hypersensitivity reactions. Measures antigen-driven lymphocyte activation.
