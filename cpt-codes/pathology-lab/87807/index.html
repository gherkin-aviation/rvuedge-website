87807 Rsv assay with optic

Pathology & Lab

Infectious agent antigen detection by immunoassay with optical observation for respiratory syncytial virus identification.

Clinical Context

Used for rapid detection of respiratory syncytial virus in pediatric and adult patients with respiratory symptoms, important for infection control and antiviral consideration.
