84112 Evaluation amniotic fluid protein

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: amniotic fluid protein, AFP screening, fetal protein assessment

Evaluation and quantitative measurement of protein concentration in amniotic fluid, used to assess fetal development and maturity.

Clinical Context

Evaluated to assess fetal health, neural tube defects, and developmental abnormalities; elevated levels may indicate chromosomal abnormalities or other fetal conditions.
