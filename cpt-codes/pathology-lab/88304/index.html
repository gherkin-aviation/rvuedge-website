88304 Tissue examination by pathologist

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: tissue exam, histologic examination

Microscopic examination of tissue sections (5 to 8 blocks) by a pathologist with increased complexity from larger specimen volume.

Clinical Context

Diagnostic examination of larger surgical specimens or multiple biopsies requiring examination of 5-8 tissue blocks.
