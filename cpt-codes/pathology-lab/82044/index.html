82044 Ur albumin semiquantitative

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: urine dipstick albumin, urine protein screening, quick albumin test

Semiquantitative detection of albumin in urine using dipstick or other non-quantitative methods providing trace to 4+ results.

Clinical Context

Routine screening test for proteinuria in primary care; abnormal results warrant quantitative confirmation with 82043.
