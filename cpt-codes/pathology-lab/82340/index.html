82340 Assay of calcium in urine

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: urine calcium, 24-hour calcium, urinary calcium test

Spectrophotometric or ion-selective electrode measurement of calcium concentration in 24-hour urine or random urine specimen.

Clinical Context

Evaluates hypercalciuria in kidney stone disease and bone metabolism; low levels suggest hypoparathyroidism.
