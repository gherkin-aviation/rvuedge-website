82370 X-ray assay calculus

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: stone X-ray analysis, X-ray diffraction analysis, radiographic stone identification

X-ray diffraction or radiographic analysis of calculi to identify crystalline composition and mineral phases.

Clinical Context

Radiographic identification of stone composition without invasive testing; particularly useful for radiopaque stones.
