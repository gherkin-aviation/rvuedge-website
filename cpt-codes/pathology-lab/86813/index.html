86813 Hla typing a b or c

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: HLA class I molecular typing, HLA high-res A/B/C typing

Human leukocyte antigen class I typing for HLA-A, HLA-B, or HLA-C alleles using molecular or high-resolution methodology. Enhanced resolution typing for transplant evaluation.

Clinical Context

Used for enhanced HLA matching in organ transplant candidates, providing greater allelic resolution than serologic typing. Improves immunologic compatibility prediction.
