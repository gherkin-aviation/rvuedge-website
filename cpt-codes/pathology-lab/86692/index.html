86692 Hepatitis delta agent antibody

Pathology & Lab

Also known as: HDV antibody, hepatitis delta serology

Hepatitis delta agent antibody detection identifying infection with hepatitis delta virus requiring hepatitis B virus coinfection.

Clinical Context

Detects hepatitis delta virus antibodies in hepatitis B surface antigen positive patients. Indicates severe hepatitis with rapid progression to cirrhosis.
