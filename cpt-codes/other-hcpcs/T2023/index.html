T2023 Targeted case management per month

Other HCPCS

Also known as: Targeted case management, TCM services

Targeted case management services under waiver program, billed per month. Specialized intensive case management providing focused advocacy and service coordination for specific populations with complex needs.

In Plain Language

Specialized care coordination; Focused support services

Clinical Context

Monthly specialized case management for individuals with specific health or social conditions requiring intensive service coordination, advocacy, and care planning tailored to complex needs.

RVU Information

CPT T2023 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation
