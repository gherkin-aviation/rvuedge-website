T2044 Hospice respite care

Other HCPCS

Also known as: Hospice respite, Respite hospice

Hospice respite care. Temporary inpatient hospice care providing respite for family caregivers while maintaining comfort and symptom management for the patient.

Clinical Context

Hospice respite care providing temporary facility-based care allowing primary family caregivers essential rest while maintaining continuity of comfort-focused care.
