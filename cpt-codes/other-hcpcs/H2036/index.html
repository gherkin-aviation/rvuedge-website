H2036 A/d treatment program, per diem

Other HCPCS

Also known as: addiction treatment, substance abuse program, A/D treatment

Alcohol and drug treatment program service provided on a per diem basis, including daily comprehensive addiction treatment.

Clinical Context

Day treatment or intensive outpatient program for substance use disorders. Includes counseling, group therapy, and supportive services. Billed per calendar day.
