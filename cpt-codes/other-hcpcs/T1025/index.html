T1025 Pediatric compr care pkg, per diem

Other HCPCS

Also known as: pediatric care package, child comprehensive care, pediatric bundle

Pediatric comprehensive care package providing bundled health services on a per-diem basis for children.

In Plain Language

children's health package; comprehensive child care

Clinical Context

Comprehensive care services for children with complex or chronic conditions including primary, specialty, and supportive services.

RVU Information

CPT T1025 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

This HCPCS code requires documentation of medical necessity and a valid physician order. Coverage and reimbursement policies vary by payer. Check with the specific insurer for prior authorization requirements.
