H1010 Nonmed family planning ed

Other HCPCS

Also known as: family planning education, contraception counseling, reproductive health

Non-medical family planning education and counseling services addressing contraception and reproductive health.

In Plain Language

birth control information; family planning help

Clinical Context

Used for educational and counseling services providing information about contraceptive options, reproductive health, and family planning decisions.

RVU Information

CPT H1010 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

This HCPCS code requires documentation of medical necessity and a valid physician order. Coverage and reimbursement policies vary by payer. Check with the specific insurer for prior authorization requirements.
