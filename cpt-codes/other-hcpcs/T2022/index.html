T2022 Case management, per month

Other HCPCS

Also known as: Case management, Care coordination

Case management services under waiver program, billed per month. Coordination and advocacy services to assess needs, develop care plans, and facilitate access to health and social services.

Clinical Context

Monthly case management providing individualized assessment, service coordination, and advocacy for vulnerable populations including individuals with developmental disabilities or chronic health conditions.
