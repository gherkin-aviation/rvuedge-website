H0002 Alcohol and/or drug screenin

Other HCPCS

Also known as: substance abuse screening, drug screening, CAGE screening

Screening for alcohol and/or drug abuse or dependence using standardized screening tools.

In Plain Language

quick drug test; alcohol screening question

Clinical Context

Used for brief screening procedures identifying patients at risk for substance abuse or dependence to determine need for full assessment.

RVU Information

CPT H0002 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

This HCPCS code requires documentation of medical necessity and a valid physician order. Coverage and reimbursement policies vary by payer. Check with the specific insurer for prior authorization requirements.
