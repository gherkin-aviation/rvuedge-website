T2046 Hospice long term care, r & b

Other HCPCS

Also known as: Hospice LTC, Inpatient hospice

Hospice long-term care inpatient services, room and board. Facility-based hospice care including room, board, and end-of-life services in long-term care or hospice facilities.

In Plain Language

Facility-based hospice care with room and meals; Inpatient hospice facility services

Clinical Context

Long-term inpatient hospice care with full facility services including room, board, nursing, and comfort-focused care for terminally ill patients requiring facility-level services.

RVU Information

CPT T2046 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

This HCPCS code requires documentation of medical necessity and a valid physician order. Coverage and reimbursement policies vary by payer. Check with the specific insurer for prior authorization requirements.
