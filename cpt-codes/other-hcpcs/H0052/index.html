H0052 Mmip mental health and care

Other HCPCS

Also known as: MMIP program, pregnant mental health, postpartum services

Mental health and integrated care services provided through the Medicaid Modernization Initiative for Pregnant women and Postpartum women.

Clinical Context

Used for comprehensive integrated mental health and medical care services for pregnant and postpartum women addressing behavioral health needs during reproductive years.
