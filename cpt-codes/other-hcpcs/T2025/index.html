T2025 Waiver service, not otherwise specified

Other HCPCS

Also known as: Waiver services NOS, Other waiver services

Miscellaneous waiver services not otherwise specified. Catch-all code for waiver-funded services not covered by specific service codes that meet individual needs within waiver authority.

In Plain Language

Other covered support services; Specialized service as needed

Clinical Context

Used for specialized, individualized services funded under waiver authority that do not fit standard service categories but meet documented needs and waiver scope.

RVU Information

CPT T2025 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

This HCPCS code requires documentation of medical necessity and a valid physician order. Coverage and reimbursement policies vary by payer. Check with the specific insurer for prior authorization requirements.
