T2019 Habil superior empl waiver 15min

Other HCPCS

Also known as: Supported employment, Hab supported employment

Habilitation supported employment services under waiver, billed per 15-minute interval. Time-based job coaching and workplace support for employment maintenance and skill development.

In Plain Language

Timed job coaching; Workplace support services

Clinical Context

Intensive 15-minute interval billing for supported employment services ensuring proportionate compensation for job coaching, accommodation support, and ongoing employment maintenance.

RVU Information

CPT T2019 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

This HCPCS code requires documentation of medical necessity and a valid physician order. Coverage and reimbursement policies vary by payer. Check with the specific insurer for prior authorization requirements.
