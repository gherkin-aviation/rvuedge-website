H0001 Alcohol and/or drug assess

Other HCPCS

Also known as: substance abuse assessment, addiction evaluation, ASAM assessment

Comprehensive assessment and evaluation of alcohol and/or drug abuse or dependence conducted by a qualified clinician.

Clinical Context

Used for initial comprehensive evaluation of patients with suspected alcohol or drug abuse/dependence to determine diagnosis, severity, and appropriate treatment planning.
