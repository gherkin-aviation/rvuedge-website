H2041 Coord special care encounter

Other HCPCS

Also known as: care coordination, specialty coordination

Specialty care coordination service covering coordination of medical and behavioral health services for a single encounter or visit.

In Plain Language

help organizing your care; coordination during a medical visit

Clinical Context

Coordination of multiple specialty providers and services during a specific encounter. Used when coordination occurs around a single visit or interaction with medical or behavioral health system.

RVU Information

CPT H2041 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

This HCPCS code requires documentation of medical necessity and a valid physician order. Coverage and reimbursement policies vary by payer. Check with the specific insurer for prior authorization requirements.
