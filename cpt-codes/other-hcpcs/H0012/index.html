H0012 Alcohol and/or drug services

Other HCPCS

Also known as: substance abuse counseling, addiction counseling, drug rehab services

Behavioral health services for alcohol and/or drug abuse treatment provided in individual or group settings.

In Plain Language

alcohol or drug counseling; addiction treatment

Clinical Context

Used for therapeutic services addressing alcohol and drug abuse treatment including counseling, behavioral interventions, and recovery support.

RVU Information

CPT H0012 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

This HCPCS code requires documentation of medical necessity and a valid physician order. Coverage and reimbursement policies vary by payer. Check with the specific insurer for prior authorization requirements.
