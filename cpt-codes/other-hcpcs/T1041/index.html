T1041 Comm bh clinic service per month

Other HCPCS

Also known as: community mental health monthly, CMHC monthly, mental health clinic monthly

Community mental health clinic services providing psychiatric treatment and counseling on a per-month basis.

In Plain Language

monthly mental health service; mental health clinic care

Clinical Context

Comprehensive monthly community mental health services including ongoing psychiatric care, therapy, and medication management.

RVU Information

CPT T1041 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

This HCPCS code requires documentation of medical necessity and a valid physician order. Coverage and reimbursement policies vary by payer. Check with the specific insurer for prior authorization requirements.
