T4524 Adult size brief/diaper xl

Other HCPCS

Also known as: Adult diaper XL, Adult brief XL

Adult size brief or diaper, extra-large size. Disposable incontinence brief or diaper designed for adult use in extra-large dimensions.

In Plain Language

Adult-sized incontinence product, extra-large; Disposable diaper for larger adults

Clinical Context

Medicaid-covered disposable incontinence brief in extra-large adult sizing for individuals with urinary or fecal incontinence requiring higher-capacity absorbent protection.

RVU Information

CPT T4524 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

This HCPCS code requires documentation of medical necessity and a valid physician order. Coverage and reimbursement policies vary by payer. Check with the specific insurer for prior authorization requirements.
