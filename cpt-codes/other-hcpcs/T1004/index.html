T1004 Nsg aide service up to 15min

Other HCPCS

Also known as: nursing aide visit, CNA visit, aide 15 minute visit

Nursing aide or certified nursing assistant services provided up to 15 minutes for patient care and assistance with activities of daily living.

In Plain Language

home health aide; nursing assistant; personal care helper

Clinical Context

Support services for assistance with activities of daily living, personal hygiene, grooming, and basic patient care under RN supervision.

RVU Information

CPT T1004 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

This HCPCS code requires documentation of medical necessity and a valid physician order. Coverage and reimbursement policies vary by payer. Check with the specific insurer for prior authorization requirements.
