T1009 Child sitting services

Other HCPCS

Also known as: child sitting services, childcare support, babysitting services

Child sitting or childcare services provided by trained caregiver to support family functioning while parents attend medical appointments.

In Plain Language

babysitter; child care; childcare help

Clinical Context

Support service facilitating parent participation in treatment by providing safe childcare during medical visits or therapy sessions.

RVU Information

CPT T1009 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

This HCPCS code requires documentation of medical necessity and a valid physician order. Coverage and reimbursement policies vary by payer. Check with the specific insurer for prior authorization requirements.
