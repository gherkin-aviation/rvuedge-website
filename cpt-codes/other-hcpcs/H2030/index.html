H2030 Mh clubhouse service, per 15 minutes

Other HCPCS

Also known as: clubhouse service, psychiatric clubhouse

Mental health clubhouse service provided for 15-minute increments, a psychosocial rehabilitation program for individuals with serious mental illness.

Clinical Context

Clubhouse model provides vocational training, social activities, and peer support for individuals with serious mental illness. Billed in 15-minute units.
