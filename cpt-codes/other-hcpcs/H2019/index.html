H2019 Therapy behav service, per 15 minutes

Other HCPCS

Also known as: behavioral services, behavior therapy, therapeutic services

Therapeutic behavioral services addressing maladaptive behaviors and promoting positive behavior change per 15-minute increment.

In Plain Language

behavior therapy; behavioral counseling

Clinical Context

Used for therapeutic services focusing on behavioral interventions to address maladaptive behaviors and support positive behavioral change.

RVU Information

CPT H2019 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation
