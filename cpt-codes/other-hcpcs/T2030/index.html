T2030 Assist living waiver/month

Other HCPCS

Also known as: Assisted living, AL services

Assisted living waiver services, billed per month. Support services in assisted living residential settings facilitating independence with appropriate care and supervision.

Clinical Context

Waiver-funded monthly assisted living services providing residential support enabling individuals to maintain independent living with available assistance for activities of daily living.
