T4538 Diaper serv reusable diaper

Other HCPCS

Also known as: Diaper service, Laundry service

Diaper service providing reusable diapers or briefs. Commercial laundry service for delivery, cleaning, and pickup of reusable incontinence briefs.

In Plain Language

Cloth diaper washing and delivery service; Reusable diaper cleaning service

Clinical Context

Medicaid-covered diaper service providing delivery, laundering, and pickup of reusable incontinence briefs offering convenience and hygiene for individuals with incontinence.

RVU Information

CPT T4538 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

This HCPCS code requires documentation of medical necessity and a valid physician order. Coverage and reimbursement policies vary by payer. Check with the specific insurer for prior authorization requirements.
