T1015 Clinic service

Other HCPCS

Also known as: clinic service, ambulatory clinic visit, outpatient clinic

Clinic services including diagnostic testing, evaluation, or treatment provided in a facility-based ambulatory setting.

In Plain Language

clinic visit; doctor's office visit

Clinical Context

Ambulatory health services provided in a clinic setting including preventive, diagnostic, therapeutic, and rehabilitative care.

RVU Information

CPT T1015 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

This HCPCS code requires documentation of medical necessity and a valid physician order. Coverage and reimbursement policies vary by payer. Check with the specific insurer for prior authorization requirements.
