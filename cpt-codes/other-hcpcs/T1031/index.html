T1031 Lpn home care per diem

Other HCPCS

Also known as: LPN home care per diem, LVN home care, licensed nurse home visit

Licensed practical nurse or licensed vocational nurse home care services provided on a per-diem basis.

In Plain Language

home nurse visit; licensed nurse home care

Clinical Context

Skilled nursing care provided in home by LPN/LVN including wound care, medication administration, and routine nursing assessments.

RVU Information

CPT T1031 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

This HCPCS code requires documentation of medical necessity and a valid physician order. Coverage and reimbursement policies vary by payer. Check with the specific insurer for prior authorization requirements.
