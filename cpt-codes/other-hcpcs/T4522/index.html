T4522 Adult size brief/diaper medial

Other HCPCS

Also known as: Adult diaper medium, Adult brief medium

Adult size brief or diaper, medium size. Disposable incontinence brief or diaper designed for adult use in medium dimensions.

In Plain Language

Adult-sized incontinence product, medium; Disposable diaper for adults

Clinical Context

Medicaid-covered disposable incontinence brief in medium adult sizing for individuals with urinary or fecal incontinence requiring absorbent protection.

RVU Information

CPT T4522 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

This HCPCS code requires documentation of medical necessity and a valid physician order. Coverage and reimbursement policies vary by payer. Check with the specific insurer for prior authorization requirements.
