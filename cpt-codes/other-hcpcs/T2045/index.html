T2045 Hospice general care

Other HCPCS

Also known as: Hospice services, General hospice

Hospice general care. Miscellaneous hospice services and supports not covered by other specific hospice codes, including administrative coordination and general end-of-life support.

In Plain Language

Various hospice care services; End-of-life support services

Clinical Context

General hospice services and coordination including case management, family support, and administrative services facilitating comprehensive end-of-life care.

RVU Information

CPT T2045 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

This HCPCS code requires documentation of medical necessity and a valid physician order. Coverage and reimbursement policies vary by payer. Check with the specific insurer for prior authorization requirements.
