H1000 Prenatal care atrisk assessm

Other HCPCS

Also known as: prenatal assessment, high-risk pregnancy, pregnancy evaluation

Prenatal care assessment and evaluation for at-risk pregnancies including medical and psychosocial evaluation.

In Plain Language

pregnancy health check; high-risk pregnancy evaluation

Clinical Context

Used for comprehensive assessment of pregnant women at medical or social risk to identify complications and develop appropriate prenatal management plans.

RVU Information

CPT H1000 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

This HCPCS code requires documentation of medical necessity and a valid physician order. Coverage and reimbursement policies vary by payer. Check with the specific insurer for prior authorization requirements.
