H0045 Respite not-in-home per diem

Other HCPCS

Also known as: respite care, respite services, caregiver relief

Respite care services provided outside the home on a per diem basis to provide temporary relief to primary caregivers.

In Plain Language

temporary care break; respite services for caregivers

Clinical Context

Used for temporary relief services provided outside the home giving primary caregivers a break from full-time care responsibilities for individuals with behavioral health conditions.

RVU Information

CPT H0045 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

This HCPCS code requires documentation of medical necessity and a valid physician order. Coverage and reimbursement policies vary by payer. Check with the specific insurer for prior authorization requirements.
