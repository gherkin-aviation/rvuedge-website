H2033 Multisys therapy/juvenile 15min

Other HCPCS

Also known as: multisystemic therapy, MST juvenile

Multisystemic therapy for juveniles provided for 15-minute increments, addressing multiple systems affecting youth behavior.

In Plain Language

family and community focused therapy for youth; intensive therapy for teenagers

Clinical Context

Multisystemic therapy addresses individual, family, peer, and community factors in juvenile behavioral or mental health problems. Evidence-based for serious juvenile delinquency and emotional disturbance.

RVU Information

CPT H2033 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

This HCPCS code requires documentation of medical necessity and a valid physician order. Coverage and reimbursement policies vary by payer. Check with the specific insurer for prior authorization requirements.
