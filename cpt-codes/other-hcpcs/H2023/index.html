H2023 Supported employ, per 15 minutes

Other HCPCS

Also known as: supported employment, job coaching, employment support

Supported employment services helping individuals with disabilities obtain and maintain competitive employment per 15-minute increment.

In Plain Language

job training and support; employment assistance

Clinical Context

Used for supported employment services providing individualized job development, placement, and ongoing support to help individuals with behavioral health conditions work.

RVU Information

CPT H2023 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

This HCPCS code requires documentation of medical necessity and a valid physician order. Coverage and reimbursement policies vary by payer. Check with the specific insurer for prior authorization requirements.
