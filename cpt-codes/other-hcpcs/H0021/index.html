H0021 Alcohol and/or drug training

Other HCPCS

Also known as: substance abuse training, addiction education, drug education

Training and educational services related to alcohol and/or drug abuse prevention and treatment provided by qualified professionals.

In Plain Language

drug and alcohol education class; substance abuse awareness training

Clinical Context

Used for educational and training programs designed to inform patients, families, or communities about alcohol and drug abuse prevention, treatment options, and recovery.

RVU Information

CPT H0021 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

This HCPCS code requires documentation of medical necessity and a valid physician order. Coverage and reimbursement policies vary by payer. Check with the specific insurer for prior authorization requirements.
