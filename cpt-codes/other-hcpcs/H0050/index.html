H0050 Alcohol/drug service 15 minutes

Other HCPCS

Also known as: substance abuse services, addiction counseling, A/D services

Alcohol and/or drug abuse services per 15-minute increment including counseling and therapeutic interventions.

In Plain Language

addiction counseling; substance abuse help

Clinical Context

Used for billable substance abuse treatment and counseling services including cognitive-behavioral therapy, motivational interviewing, and recovery planning.

RVU Information

CPT H0050 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

This HCPCS code requires documentation of medical necessity and a valid physician order. Coverage and reimbursement policies vary by payer. Check with the specific insurer for prior authorization requirements.
