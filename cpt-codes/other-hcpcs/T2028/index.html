T2028 Special supply, not otherwise specified waiver

Other HCPCS

Also known as: Special supplies, Medical supplies

Special supplies not otherwise specified under waiver program. Miscellaneous medical supplies and adaptive equipment needed for individuals with disabilities.

Clinical Context

Waiver-funded supplies and equipment for individuals with disabilities not covered by specific supply codes but necessary for health, safety, or functional independence.
