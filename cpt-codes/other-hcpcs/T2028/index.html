T2028 Special supply, not otherwise specified waiver

Other HCPCS

Also known as: Special supplies, Medical supplies

Special supplies not otherwise specified under waiver program. Miscellaneous medical supplies and adaptive equipment needed for individuals with disabilities.

In Plain Language

Medical equipment and supplies; Adaptive equipment as needed

Clinical Context

Waiver-funded supplies and equipment for individuals with disabilities not covered by specific supply codes but necessary for health, safety, or functional independence.

RVU Information

CPT T2028 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

This HCPCS code requires documentation of medical necessity and a valid physician order. Coverage and reimbursement policies vary by payer. Check with the specific insurer for prior authorization requirements.
