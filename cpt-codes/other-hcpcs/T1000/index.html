T1000 Private duty/independent nsg

Other HCPCS

Also known as: private duty nursing, independent nursing, home nursing services

Private duty nursing or independent nursing services provided by a registered nurse or licensed practical nurse on an hourly or per-visit basis.

In Plain Language

private nurse; home care nurse; nursing care at home

Clinical Context

Skilled nursing care provided in home or institutional settings for patients with complex medical needs requiring ongoing professional nursing assessment and intervention.

RVU Information

CPT T1000 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

This HCPCS code requires documentation of medical necessity and a valid physician order. Coverage and reimbursement policies vary by payer. Check with the specific insurer for prior authorization requirements.
