H0030 Alcohol and/or drug hotline

Other HCPCS

Also known as: substance abuse hotline, helpline, crisis line

Hotline and telephone support services for individuals seeking information, support, or crisis assistance regarding alcohol and/or drug abuse.

In Plain Language

drug abuse support hotline; addiction crisis line

Clinical Context

Used for telephone-based support services providing 24/7 access to information, crisis assistance, referral, and peer support for individuals with substance abuse issues.

RVU Information

CPT H0030 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

This HCPCS code requires documentation of medical necessity and a valid physician order. Coverage and reimbursement policies vary by payer. Check with the specific insurer for prior authorization requirements.
