T2037 Camp day waiver/session

Other HCPCS

Also known as: Camp services, Day camp

Day camping or day program recreational services under waiver, billed per session. Daytime recreation and community integration activities in structured camp settings.

In Plain Language

Day camp activities; Daytime recreational program

Clinical Context

Waiver-funded daytime recreational camping programs providing social participation, skill development, and community experiences for individuals with disabilities.

RVU Information

CPT T2037 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

This HCPCS code requires documentation of medical necessity and a valid physician order. Coverage and reimbursement policies vary by payer. Check with the specific insurer for prior authorization requirements.
