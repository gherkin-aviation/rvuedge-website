T2038 Comm transverse waiver/service

Other HCPCS

Also known as: Transportation services, Community transport

Community transportation services under waiver program, billed per service. Transportation and attendant services to facilitate community access and participation.

Clinical Context

Waiver-funded community transportation services enabling individuals with disabilities to access employment, healthcare, social activities, and community participation.
