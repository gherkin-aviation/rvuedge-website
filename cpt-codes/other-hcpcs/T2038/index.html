T2038 Comm transverse waiver/service

Other HCPCS

Also known as: Transportation services, Community transport

Community transportation services under waiver program, billed per service. Transportation and attendant services to facilitate community access and participation.

In Plain Language

Rides to community activities; Transportation with support

Clinical Context

Waiver-funded community transportation services enabling individuals with disabilities to access employment, healthcare, social activities, and community participation.

RVU Information

CPT T2038 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

This HCPCS code requires documentation of medical necessity and a valid physician order. Coverage and reimbursement policies vary by payer. Check with the specific insurer for prior authorization requirements.
