T2018 Habil superior empl waiver/diem

Other HCPCS

Also known as: Supported employment, Hab supported employment

Habilitation supported employment services under waiver, billed per diem. Ongoing support to facilitate and maintain employment for individuals with disabilities in competitive or customized job settings.

Clinical Context

Waiver-funded daily per diem supported employment services providing ongoing job coaching, employer liaison, and workplace accommodation support for individuals with disabilities.
