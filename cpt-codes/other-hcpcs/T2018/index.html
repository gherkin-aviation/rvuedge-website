T2018 Habil superior empl waiver/diem

Other HCPCS

Also known as: Supported employment, Hab supported employment

Habilitation supported employment services under waiver, billed per diem. Ongoing support to facilitate and maintain employment for individuals with disabilities in competitive or customized job settings.

In Plain Language

Daily job support services; Employment assistance program

Clinical Context

Waiver-funded daily per diem supported employment services providing ongoing job coaching, employer liaison, and workplace accommodation support for individuals with disabilities.

RVU Information

CPT T2018 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

This HCPCS code requires documentation of medical necessity and a valid physician order. Coverage and reimbursement policies vary by payer. Check with the specific insurer for prior authorization requirements.
