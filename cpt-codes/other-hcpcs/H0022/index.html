H0022 Alcohol and/or drug interven

Other HCPCS

Also known as: substance abuse intervention, addiction intervention, treatment linkage

Intervention services for alcohol and/or drug abuse including referral, linkage, and facilitated access to treatment services.

Clinical Context

Used for structured intervention programs addressing substance abuse including strategies for motivating individuals to seek treatment and facilitating connections to recovery resources.
