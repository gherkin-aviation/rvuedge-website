H0022 Alcohol and/or drug interven

Other HCPCS

Also known as: substance abuse intervention, addiction intervention, treatment linkage

Intervention services for alcohol and/or drug abuse including referral, linkage, and facilitated access to treatment services.

In Plain Language

addiction help and referral; drug intervention services

Clinical Context

Used for structured intervention programs addressing substance abuse including strategies for motivating individuals to seek treatment and facilitating connections to recovery resources.

RVU Information

CPT H0022 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

This HCPCS code requires documentation of medical necessity and a valid physician order. Coverage and reimbursement policies vary by payer. Check with the specific insurer for prior authorization requirements.
