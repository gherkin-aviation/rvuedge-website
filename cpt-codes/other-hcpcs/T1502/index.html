T1502 Medication administration visit

Other HCPCS

Also known as: medication administration visit, med admin visit, medication management

Medication administration or medication management visit by nurse or healthcare provider.

In Plain Language

medication help visit; medicine administration

Clinical Context

Nursing visit focused on administration of medications, observation of medication adherence, and monitoring of medication effectiveness.

RVU Information

CPT T1502 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

This HCPCS code requires documentation of medical necessity and a valid physician order. Coverage and reimbursement policies vary by payer. Check with the specific insurer for prior authorization requirements.
