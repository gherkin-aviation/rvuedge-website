H0046 Mental health service, not otherwise specified

Other HCPCS

Also known as: mental health services, MH services, behavioral health

Mental health services not elsewhere specified covering miscellaneous mental health interventions and support services.

In Plain Language

mental health help; counseling and support

Clinical Context

Used as a catch-all code for mental health services not otherwise categorized including various therapeutic interventions and mental health support.

RVU Information

CPT H0046 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

This HCPCS code requires documentation of medical necessity and a valid physician order. Coverage and reimbursement policies vary by payer. Check with the specific insurer for prior authorization requirements.
