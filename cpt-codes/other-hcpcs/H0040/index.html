H0040 Assert comm treatment pgm per diem

Other HCPCS

Also known as: assertive community treatment, ACT program, intensive services

Assertive community treatment program services on a per diem basis.

In Plain Language

intensive community support program; mental health team services

Clinical Context

Used for comprehensive daily assertive community treatment programs providing integrated intensive services for individuals with serious mental illness.

RVU Information

CPT H0040 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

This HCPCS code requires documentation of medical necessity and a valid physician order. Coverage and reimbursement policies vary by payer. Check with the specific insurer for prior authorization requirements.
