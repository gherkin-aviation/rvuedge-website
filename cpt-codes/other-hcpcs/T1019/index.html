T1019 Personal care ser per 15 minutes

Other HCPCS

Also known as: personal care service, ADL assistance, personal care aide

Personal care services including assistance with activities of daily living provided in 15-minute increments.

Clinical Context

Non-medical assistance with activities of daily living including bathing, grooming, dressing, and toileting for individuals unable to perform independently.
