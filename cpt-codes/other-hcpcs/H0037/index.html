H0037 Comm psy superior treatment pgm per diem

Other HCPCS

Also known as: community psych services, mental health program, day program

Community psychiatric support and treatment program services on a per diem basis.

Clinical Context

Used for comprehensive daily community psychiatric treatment programs providing integrated support services for individuals with serious mental illness.
