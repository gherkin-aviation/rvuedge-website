T5001 Position seat specimen orth need

Other HCPCS

Also known as: Positioning seat, Orthopedic seat

Positioning seat with special orthopedic support. Specialized seating device providing postural support and alignment for individuals with orthopedic needs or disabilities.

In Plain Language

Special positioning chair; Medical support seating

Clinical Context

Medicaid-covered specialized positioning seat with orthopedic support for individuals with postural deficits, spasticity, or musculoskeletal conditions requiring therapeutic seating.

RVU Information

CPT T5001 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

This HCPCS code requires documentation of medical necessity and a valid physician order. Coverage and reimbursement policies vary by payer. Check with the specific insurer for prior authorization requirements.
