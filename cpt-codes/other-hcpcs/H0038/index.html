H0038 Self-help/peer service per 15min

Other HCPCS

Also known as: peer support, self-help group, support group

Self-help and peer support services for individuals with behavioral health conditions per 15-minute increment.

Clinical Context

Used for peer-led and self-help support programs including group meetings, peer counseling, and mutual aid activities for individuals with mental health or substance abuse issues.
