H0038 Self-help/peer service per 15min

Other HCPCS

Also known as: peer support, self-help group, support group

Self-help and peer support services for individuals with behavioral health conditions per 15-minute increment.

In Plain Language

support group; peer counseling

Clinical Context

Used for peer-led and self-help support programs including group meetings, peer counseling, and mutual aid activities for individuals with mental health or substance abuse issues.

RVU Information

CPT H0038 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

This HCPCS code requires documentation of medical necessity and a valid physician order. Coverage and reimbursement policies vary by payer. Check with the specific insurer for prior authorization requirements.
