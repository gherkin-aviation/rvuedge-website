T1024 Team evaluation & management

Other HCPCS

Also known as: team evaluation, multidisciplinary assessment, team conference

Team evaluation and management including multidisciplinary assessment and care coordination by clinical team.

In Plain Language

team health assessment; group care planning

Clinical Context

Multidisciplinary team evaluation and planning involving physicians, nurses, therapists, and other specialists to develop comprehensive care plans.

RVU Information

CPT T1024 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

This HCPCS code requires documentation of medical necessity and a valid physician order. Coverage and reimbursement policies vary by payer. Check with the specific insurer for prior authorization requirements.
