H0032 Mh service plan device by non-md

Other HCPCS

Also known as: care plan development, treatment planning, service coordination

Mental health service plan development and coordination services provided by a non-physician qualified mental health professional.

In Plain Language

mental health treatment plan; counseling plan

Clinical Context

Used for development of individualized mental health service plans by qualified non-physician clinicians addressing psychiatric diagnosis, treatment goals, and coordination of care.

RVU Information

CPT H0032 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

This HCPCS code requires documentation of medical necessity and a valid physician order. Coverage and reimbursement policies vary by payer. Check with the specific insurer for prior authorization requirements.
