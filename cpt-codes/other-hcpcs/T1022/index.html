T1022 Contracted services per day

Other HCPCS

Also known as: contracted services, vendor services, external service per day

Contracted services provided to patient on a per-day basis by external vendor or specialized service provider.

In Plain Language

hired services; specialized daily service

Clinical Context

Services provided by contracted vendors or specialized service providers including rehabilitation, therapy, or other healthcare services.

RVU Information

CPT T1022 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

This HCPCS code requires documentation of medical necessity and a valid physician order. Coverage and reimbursement policies vary by payer. Check with the specific insurer for prior authorization requirements.
