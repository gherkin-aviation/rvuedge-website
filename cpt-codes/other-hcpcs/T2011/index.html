T2011 Pasrr level ii

Other HCPCS

Also known as: PASRR Level II, PASRR specialized evaluation

Preadmission Screening and Resident Review (PASRR) Level II specialized evaluation. In-depth diagnostic assessment for individuals flagged during Level I screening who may have serious mental illness or intellectual/developmental disabilities.

Clinical Context

Comprehensive diagnostic assessment performed by mental health professionals when Level I screening identifies potential serious mental illness or developmental disability. Provides detailed recommendations for appropriate care settings and treatment.
