T2101 Breast milk procedure/store/distal

Other HCPCS

Also known as: Breast milk services, Milk bank services

Breast milk processing, storage, and distribution services. Professional handling and distribution of expressed human breast milk for nutritional and immunological benefits.

In Plain Language

Breast milk collection and delivery services; Human milk processing services

Clinical Context

Processing and distribution of maternal breast milk for infants in hospital or home settings, ensuring safe handling, storage, and administration of expressed milk.

RVU Information

CPT T2101 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

This HCPCS code requires documentation of medical necessity and a valid physician order. Coverage and reimbursement policies vary by payer. Check with the specific insurer for prior authorization requirements.
