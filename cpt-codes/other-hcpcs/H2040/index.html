H2040 Coord specialty care, month

Other HCPCS

Also known as: care coordination, specialty coordination, case management

Specialty care coordination service covering coordination of medical and behavioral health services for one calendar month.

In Plain Language

help organizing your medical and mental health care; coordination of different doctors and services

Clinical Context

Comprehensive coordination of multiple specialty services and providers for individuals with complex medical and behavioral health needs. Monthly administrative fee per beneficiary.

RVU Information

CPT H2040 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

This HCPCS code requires documentation of medical necessity and a valid physician order. Coverage and reimbursement policies vary by payer. Check with the specific insurer for prior authorization requirements.
