T1005 Respite care service 15 minutes

Other HCPCS

Also known as: respite care visit, respite care services, caregiver relief

Respite care services provided on a per-visit or hourly basis allowing primary caregivers temporary relief from caregiving responsibilities.

Clinical Context

Temporary care services allowing informal caregivers (family members) brief periods of rest while maintaining patient safety and comfort.
