H1003 Prenatal at risk education

Other HCPCS

Also known as: prenatal education, health education, pregnancy training

Educational services for pregnant women at risk addressing health behaviors, self-management, and risk reduction.

In Plain Language

pregnancy health class; expectant mother education

Clinical Context

Used for structured educational programs for at-risk pregnant women addressing nutrition, behavioral health, labor preparation, and healthy lifestyle behaviors.

RVU Information

CPT H1003 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

This HCPCS code requires documentation of medical necessity and a valid physician order. Coverage and reimbursement policies vary by payer. Check with the specific insurer for prior authorization requirements.
