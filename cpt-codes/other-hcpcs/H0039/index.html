H0039 Asser com treatment face-face/15min

Other HCPCS

Also known as: assertive community treatment, ACT, mobile crisis

Assertive community treatment services provided face-to-face per 15-minute increment.

In Plain Language

intensive community support; mobile mental health team

Clinical Context

Used for intensive community-based services for individuals with serious mental illness including intensive case management, crisis intervention, and coordinated treatment.

RVU Information

CPT H0039 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

This HCPCS code requires documentation of medical necessity and a valid physician order. Coverage and reimbursement policies vary by payer. Check with the specific insurer for prior authorization requirements.
