H1004 Follow up home visit/prental

Other HCPCS

Also known as: home visit, postpartum visit, perinatal home care

Follow-up home visit services provided to pregnant women in the postpartum or perinatal period.

In Plain Language

home health visit; postpartum check-up

Clinical Context

Used for home-based follow-up care and assessment in the immediate postpartum period providing maternal and infant monitoring and support.

RVU Information

CPT H1004 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

This HCPCS code requires documentation of medical necessity and a valid physician order. Coverage and reimbursement policies vary by payer. Check with the specific insurer for prior authorization requirements.
