H0031 Mh health assess by non-md

Other HCPCS

Also known as: mental health assessment, psychological evaluation, MH eval

Mental health assessment and evaluation services provided by a non-physician qualified mental health professional.

In Plain Language

mental health check-up; emotional health assessment

Clinical Context

Used for comprehensive mental health evaluation and diagnosis conducted by licensed clinical social workers, psychologists, or counselors to assess psychiatric and psychological functioning.

RVU Information

CPT H0031 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

This HCPCS code requires documentation of medical necessity and a valid physician order. Coverage and reimbursement policies vary by payer. Check with the specific insurer for prior authorization requirements.
