H0031 Mh health assess by non-md

Other HCPCS

Also known as: mental health assessment, psychological evaluation, MH eval

Mental health assessment and evaluation services provided by a non-physician qualified mental health professional.

Clinical Context

Used for comprehensive mental health evaluation and diagnosis conducted by licensed clinical social workers, psychologists, or counselors to assess psychiatric and psychological functioning.
