H0036 Comm psy face-face per 15min

Other HCPCS

Also known as: community psych services, mobile treatment, assertive community treatment

Community psychiatric support and treatment services provided face-to-face per 15-minute increment.

In Plain Language

community mental health support; in-community counseling

Clinical Context

Used for community-based mental health services including counseling, behavioral interventions, and support provided in community settings for individuals with serious mental illness.

RVU Information

CPT H0036 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

This HCPCS code requires documentation of medical necessity and a valid physician order. Coverage and reimbursement policies vary by payer. Check with the specific insurer for prior authorization requirements.
