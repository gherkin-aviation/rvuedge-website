H0024 Alcohol and/or drug preventi

Other HCPCS

Also known as: substance abuse prevention, addiction prevention, family prevention

Prevention services for alcohol and/or drug abuse targeting individuals and families to reduce substance abuse risk.

In Plain Language

drug prevention program; substance abuse prevention class

Clinical Context

Used for prevention programs and services targeting at-risk populations including individuals and families to reduce incidence and prevalence of alcohol and drug abuse.

RVU Information

CPT H0024 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

This HCPCS code requires documentation of medical necessity and a valid physician order. Coverage and reimbursement policies vary by payer. Check with the specific insurer for prior authorization requirements.
