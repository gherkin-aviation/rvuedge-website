T2016 Habil res waiver per diem

Other HCPCS

Also known as: Hab residential services, Residential habilitation

Habilitation residential services under waiver, billed per diem. Support services in a residential setting to develop or maintain functional abilities and community integration skills.

Clinical Context

Daily per diem waiver funding for residential habilitation services enabling individuals with developmental disabilities to live in community settings with appropriate skill development and daily support.
