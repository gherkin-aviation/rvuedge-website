Other HCPCS

Supply, not otherwise specified. Miscellaneous medical supplies and equipment not covered by specific supply codes.

In Plain Language

General medical supplies; Equipment not listed elsewhere

Clinical Context

Medicaid-covered miscellaneous medical supplies and equipment for individuals with health needs not specifically categorized by other supply codes.

RVU Information

CPT T5999 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation
