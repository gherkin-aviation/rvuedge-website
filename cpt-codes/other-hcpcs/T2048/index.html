T2048 Bh ltc res r & b, per diem

Other HCPCS

Also known as: BH LTC residential, Psychiatric residential care

Behavioral health long-term care residential services, room and board, per diem. Daily facility-based behavioral health services including accommodation and therapeutic care.

In Plain Language

Psychiatric facility room and daily care; Mental health residential services

Clinical Context

Daily per diem behavioral health residential services in facility settings providing 24-hour psychiatric care, therapeutic services, and room/board for individuals with serious mental illness.

RVU Information

CPT T2048 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

This HCPCS code requires documentation of medical necessity and a valid physician order. Coverage and reimbursement policies vary by payer. Check with the specific insurer for prior authorization requirements.
