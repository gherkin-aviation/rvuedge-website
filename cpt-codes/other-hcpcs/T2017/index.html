T2017 Habil res waiver 15 minutes

Other HCPCS

Also known as: Hab residential services, Residential habilitation

Habilitation residential services under waiver, billed per 15-minute interval. Time-based support for functional skill development and independence in residential community settings.

In Plain Language

Timed daily living support; Residential skills coaching

Clinical Context

Intensive 15-minute interval billing for residential habilitation ensuring individuals with developmental disabilities receive support proportionate to actual service time needed.

RVU Information

CPT T2017 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

This HCPCS code requires documentation of medical necessity and a valid physician order. Coverage and reimbursement policies vary by payer. Check with the specific insurer for prior authorization requirements.
