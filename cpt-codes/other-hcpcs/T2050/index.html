T2050 Financial mgt waiver/diem

Other HCPCS

Also known as: Financial management, Money management

Financial management services under waiver program, billed per diem. Comprehensive daily financial assistance including budgeting, bill payment, and financial oversight.

Clinical Context

Waiver-funded daily financial management services enabling individuals with developmental or cognitive disabilities to maintain financial stability and independence.
