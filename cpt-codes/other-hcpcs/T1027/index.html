T1027 Family training & counseling

Other HCPCS

Also known as: family training, family education, caregiver training

Family training and counseling services including education and support for family members involved in patient care.

In Plain Language

family caregiver training; family education program

Clinical Context

Educational and training services for family members and caregivers to develop skills and knowledge for providing effective patient care and support.

RVU Information

CPT T1027 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

This HCPCS code requires documentation of medical necessity and a valid physician order. Coverage and reimbursement policies vary by payer. Check with the specific insurer for prior authorization requirements.
