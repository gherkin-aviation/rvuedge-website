T1999 Noc retail items andsupplies

Other HCPCS

Also known as: retail medical supplies NOC, miscellaneous supplies, supplies not classified

Retail items and supplies not otherwise classified including medical supplies, equipment, or consumables.

In Plain Language

medical supplies; health supplies

Clinical Context

Billing code for various medical supplies, retail items, and consumables not specifically categorized in other codes.

RVU Information

CPT T1999 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

This HCPCS code requires documentation of medical necessity and a valid physician order. Coverage and reimbursement policies vary by payer. Check with the specific insurer for prior authorization requirements.
