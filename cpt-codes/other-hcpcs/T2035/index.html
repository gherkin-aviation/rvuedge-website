T2035 Utility services waiver

Other HCPCS

Also known as: Utility services, Utility assistance

Utility services under waiver program. Waiver coverage for essential utilities (electricity, water, gas, sewage) to maintain safe residential environments for individuals with disabilities.

Clinical Context

Waiver-funded utility assistance ensuring safe, habitable residential environments for vulnerable individuals with disabilities who may lack resources for essential utilities.
