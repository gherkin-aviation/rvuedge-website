T2020 Day habil waiver per diem

Other HCPCS

Also known as: Day habilitation, Day hab services

Day habilitation services under waiver program, billed per diem. Structured daytime programs providing skill development, social participation, and community integration for individuals with disabilities.

In Plain Language

Daily community activities program; Daytime skills and social activities

Clinical Context

Waiver-funded daytime habilitation services in structured settings enabling individuals with developmental disabilities to develop functional skills, social connections, and community participation.

RVU Information

CPT T2020 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

This HCPCS code requires documentation of medical necessity and a valid physician order. Coverage and reimbursement policies vary by payer. Check with the specific insurer for prior authorization requirements.
