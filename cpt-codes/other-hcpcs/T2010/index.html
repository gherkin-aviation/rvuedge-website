T2010 Pasrr level i

Other HCPCS

Also known as: PASRR Level I, PASRR screening

Preadmission Screening and Resident Review (PASRR) Level I evaluation. Initial screening assessment required by federal regulation for individuals entering long-term care facilities to identify those with serious mental illness or intellectual/developmental disabilities.

In Plain Language

Initial mental health screening for nursing home admission; First-level evaluation for mental illness or developmental disability

Clinical Context

Required before admission to Medicaid-funded long-term care facilities to determine if further specialized evaluation (Level II) is necessary for proper care planning and service coordination.

RVU Information

CPT T2010 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

This HCPCS code requires documentation of medical necessity and a valid physician order. Coverage and reimbursement policies vary by payer. Check with the specific insurer for prior authorization requirements.
