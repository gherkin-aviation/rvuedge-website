T1007 Treatment plan development

Other HCPCS

Also known as: treatment plan, care plan development, individualized education program update

Treatment plan development, review, or update by a multidisciplinary team or licensed clinician.

In Plain Language

care plan; treatment planning; health plan

Clinical Context

Development and documentation of comprehensive individualized treatment or service plans addressing patient goals, interventions, and progress monitoring.

RVU Information

CPT T1007 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

This HCPCS code requires documentation of medical necessity and a valid physician order. Coverage and reimbursement policies vary by payer. Check with the specific insurer for prior authorization requirements.
