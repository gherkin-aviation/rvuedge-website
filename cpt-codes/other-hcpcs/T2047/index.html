T2047 Hab prevo waiver per 15

Other HCPCS

Also known as: Hab prevoc services, Prevocational training

Habilitation prevocational services under waiver program, billed per 15-minute interval. Time-based employment preparation services for skill development and job readiness.

In Plain Language

Timed job preparation services; Work readiness skills training

Clinical Context

Intensive 15-minute interval prevocational habilitation services supporting individuals with developmental disabilities toward employment readiness and skills development.

RVU Information

CPT T2047 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

This HCPCS code requires documentation of medical necessity and a valid physician order. Coverage and reimbursement policies vary by payer. Check with the specific insurer for prior authorization requirements.
