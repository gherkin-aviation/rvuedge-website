H0035 Mh partial hosp treatment under 24h

Other HCPCS

Also known as: partial hospitalization, day treatment, PHP

Mental health partial hospitalization program or day treatment service lasting less than 24 hours per session.

In Plain Language

day mental health program; part-time hospital treatment

Clinical Context

Used for structured daytime mental health treatment programs providing intensive therapeutic services to patients not requiring full inpatient hospitalization.

RVU Information

CPT H0035 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

This HCPCS code requires documentation of medical necessity and a valid physician order. Coverage and reimbursement policies vary by payer. Check with the specific insurer for prior authorization requirements.
