H2024 Supported employ, per diem

Other HCPCS

Also known as: supported employment, employment program, job support

Supported employment services helping individuals with disabilities obtain and maintain competitive employment on a per diem basis.

In Plain Language

daily job support; employment services

Clinical Context

Used for daily supported employment programs providing individualized job development, placement, and ongoing support.

RVU Information

CPT H2024 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

This HCPCS code requires documentation of medical necessity and a valid physician order. Coverage and reimbursement policies vary by payer. Check with the specific insurer for prior authorization requirements.
