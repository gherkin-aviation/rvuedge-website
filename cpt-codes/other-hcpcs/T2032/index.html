T2032 Res care, not otherwise specified waiver/month

Other HCPCS

Also known as: Residential care, Group home services

Residential care services not otherwise specified under waiver program, billed per month. Comprehensive residential support services for individuals requiring 24-hour supervision and care.

In Plain Language

Monthly residential supervision and care; Group living facility services

Clinical Context

Waiver-funded monthly residential care in facility or group settings providing 24-hour supervision, personal care assistance, and support services for individuals unable to live independently.

RVU Information

CPT T2032 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

This HCPCS code requires documentation of medical necessity and a valid physician order. Coverage and reimbursement policies vary by payer. Check with the specific insurer for prior authorization requirements.
