H2034 A/d halfway house, per diem

Other HCPCS

Also known as: halfway house, addiction recovery housing, A/D halfway house

Alcohol and drug halfway house service provided on a per diem basis, including residential treatment and recovery support.

In Plain Language

residential recovery house for substance abuse; transitional living for people in recovery

Clinical Context

Residential facility providing structured environment, peer support, and recovery services for individuals transitioning from inpatient addiction treatment. Billed per calendar day.

RVU Information

CPT H2034 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

This HCPCS code requires documentation of medical necessity and a valid physician order. Coverage and reimbursement policies vary by payer. Check with the specific insurer for prior authorization requirements.
