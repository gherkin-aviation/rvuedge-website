H2034 A/d halfway house, per diem

Other HCPCS

Also known as: halfway house, addiction recovery housing, A/D halfway house

Alcohol and drug halfway house service provided on a per diem basis, including residential treatment and recovery support.

Clinical Context

Residential facility providing structured environment, peer support, and recovery services for individuals transitioning from inpatient addiction treatment. Billed per calendar day.
