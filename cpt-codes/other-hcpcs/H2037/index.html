H2037 Device delay preventive dp ch, 15 minutes

Other HCPCS

Also known as: early intervention, developmental delay program, dev delay prevention

Developmental delay prevention day program for children provided for 15-minute increments, addressing early developmental concerns.

Clinical Context

Early intervention program for children at risk for developmental delays. Includes therapeutic activities, parent training, and developmental assessment. Billed in 15-minute units.
