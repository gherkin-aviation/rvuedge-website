H0033 Oral medial adm direct observe

Other HCPCS

Also known as: directly observed therapy, supervised medication, observed dosing

Oral medication administration with direct observation by a qualified clinician to ensure medication adherence and proper use.

Clinical Context

Used in substance abuse treatment and other conditions requiring verification of medication ingestion to maximize adherence and prevent diversion.
