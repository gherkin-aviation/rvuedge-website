H0033 Oral medial adm direct observe

Other HCPCS

Also known as: directly observed therapy, supervised medication, observed dosing

Oral medication administration with direct observation by a qualified clinician to ensure medication adherence and proper use.

In Plain Language

watched medication; supervised pill taking

Clinical Context

Used in substance abuse treatment and other conditions requiring verification of medication ingestion to maximize adherence and prevent diversion.

RVU Information

CPT H0033 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

This HCPCS code requires documentation of medical necessity and a valid physician order. Coverage and reimbursement policies vary by payer. Check with the specific insurer for prior authorization requirements.
