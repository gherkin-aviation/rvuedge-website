T1018 School-based iep ser bundled

Other HCPCS

Also known as: school-based IEP services, special education services, school health services

School-based individualized education program services provided as a bundled or comprehensive package.

In Plain Language

special education help; school health services

Clinical Context

Health and therapeutic services integrated into school-based individualized education programs for students with disabilities.

RVU Information

CPT T1018 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

This HCPCS code requires documentation of medical necessity and a valid physician order. Coverage and reimbursement policies vary by payer. Check with the specific insurer for prior authorization requirements.
