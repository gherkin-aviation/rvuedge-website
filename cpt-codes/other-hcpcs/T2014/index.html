T2014 Habil prevoc waiver, per d

Other HCPCS

Also known as: Hab prevoc services, Prevocational training

Habilitation prevocational services provided under waiver, billed per diem. Services to prepare individuals for employment through skill development, work experience, and job readiness training.

In Plain Language

Job preparation training; Work readiness skills development

Clinical Context

Waiver-funded prevocational preparation for individuals with developmental disabilities, including assessment of abilities, work readiness development, and transition toward competitive or supported employment.

RVU Information

CPT T2014 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

This HCPCS code requires documentation of medical necessity and a valid physician order. Coverage and reimbursement policies vary by payer. Check with the specific insurer for prior authorization requirements.
