H0042 Fos c chld non-ther per mon

Other HCPCS

Also known as: foster care, placement services, out-of-home care

Foster care services for children including non-therapeutic foster placement on a per month basis.

Clinical Context

Used for foster care placement and support services for children in the child welfare system providing safe out-of-home placement when family care is not available.
