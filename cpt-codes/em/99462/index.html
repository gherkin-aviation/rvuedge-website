99462 Sbsq nb em per day hosp

E&M

Also known as: subsequent newborn exam, daily newborn exam, newborn follow-up

Subsequent history and physical examination for newborn care performed by physician per calendar day during hospital admission.

Clinical Context

Used for daily subsequent newborn evaluations during hospital stay following initial assessment, monitoring for jaundice, feeding, and other issues.
