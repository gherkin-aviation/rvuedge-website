99236 Hosp inpatient/obs same date hi 85

E&M

Also known as: complex same-day, high complexity admit-discharge, observation complex same day

Hospital inpatient or observation care, including the admission and discharge of the patient on the same date, with high medical decision-making complexity.

Clinical Context

Used for same-day hospital or observation admission and discharge requiring high medical decision-making complexity, such as complex procedures or acute conditions needing intensive hospital-level care.
