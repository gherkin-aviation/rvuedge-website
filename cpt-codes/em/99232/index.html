99232 Sbsq hosp inpatient/obs moderate 35

E&M

Also known as: subsequent hospital moderate, daily hospital visit, inpatient follow-up, subsequent hospital visit level 2, hospital follow-up moderate

Subsequent hospital inpatient or observation care, per day, for the evaluation and management of a patient with moderate medical decision-making complexity.

Clinical Context

Used for subsequent days of hospital or observation care with moderate medical decision-making complexity, such as adjustment of treatment plans or management of new or worsening conditions.
