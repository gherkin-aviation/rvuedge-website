99349 Home/res vst established mod mdm 40

E&M

Also known as: home visit new moderate, house call new moderate, new home moderate

Home or residence visit for the evaluation and management of a new patient, with moderate medical decision-making complexity and an expected duration of 40 minutes.

Clinical Context

Used for initial home-based evaluation and management of new patients with moderate medical decision-making complexity, such as comprehensive initial assessment with multiple acute or chronic conditions.
