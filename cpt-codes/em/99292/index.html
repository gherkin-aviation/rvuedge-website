99292 Critical care additional 30 minutes

Evaluation & Management +Add-on

Also known as: critical care additional, CCU add-on, ICU additional time, critical care additional 30 min, ICU care additional time

Critical care of the critically ill or critically injured patient; each additional 15 minutes of physician or qualified health professional time spent directly at bedside and on the patient's hospital care (to report 99291 and 99292 together).

In Plain Language

Additional intensive care time; Continued critical care

Clinical Context

Used to report each additional 15-minute increment of critical care time beyond the initial 30-74 minutes for critically ill or injured patients requiring ongoing intensive evaluation and management.
