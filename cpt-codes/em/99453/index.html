99453 Rem mntr physiol param setup

Evaluation & Management

Also known as: remote monitoring setup, RPM setup, device setup

Initial setup and patient education for remote monitoring of physiological parameters using connected devices or patient-recorded measurements.

In Plain Language

Clinical Context
