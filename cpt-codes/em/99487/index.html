99487 Complex chrnc care first 60 minutes

E&M

Also known as: complex chronic care management, CCM complex

Complex chronic care management service for first 60 minutes of clinical staff time, including comprehensive assessment, care coordination, and communication.

Clinical Context

Used for patients with multiple complex chronic conditions (>2 chronic conditions anticipated to last 12+ months) requiring intensive care coordination and monitoring.
