99385 Preventive visit new age 18-39

E&M

Also known as: preventive exam adult, well adult visit, preventive care young adult

Initial comprehensive preventive medicine evaluation and management of an individual including age and gender appropriate history, examination, counseling, and risk factor reduction interventions, new patient; age 18 through 39 years.

Clinical Context

Used for comprehensive preventive care visit with an adult age 18-39 years, including risk assessment, counseling on disease prevention, and age-appropriate screenings.
