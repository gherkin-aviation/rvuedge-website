99366 Team conf with pat by hc prof

E&M

Also known as: team conference with patient, care conference, family meeting

Team conference with the patient and/or family present, including history, examination, and medical decision-making by a physician or other qualified health professional.

Clinical Context

Used when a care team including the patient and/or family participates in a face-to-face conference involving evaluation and medical decision-making regarding the patient's care plan.
