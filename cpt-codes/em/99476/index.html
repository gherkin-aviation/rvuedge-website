99476 Pediatric critical care age 2-5 subsq

E&M

Also known as: pediatric critical care age 2-5 daily, PICU preschool daily

Subsequent critical care evaluation and management of child age 2-5 years per calendar day in intensive care unit setting.

Clinical Context

Used for daily critical care management of children age 2-5 years in intensive care unit, including ongoing assessment, monitoring, and treatment.
