99316 Nf dschrg management 30 min+

E&M

Also known as: nursing home discharge complex, SNF discharge extended, complex facility discharge

Nursing facility care, discharge day management; per day for the evaluation and management activities on discharge from the facility of a resident with more than 30 minutes of physician or qualified health professional time spent on final discharge visit.

Clinical Context

Used for nursing facility discharge day management when the physician or qualified health professional spends more than 30 minutes on discharge-related evaluation and planning activities.
