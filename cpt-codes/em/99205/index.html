99205 Office outpatient new hi 60 minutes

E&M

Also known as: complex office visit, comprehensive follow-up, high complexity visit, new patient office visit level 5, high complexity new visit

Office or other outpatient visit for the evaluation and management of an established patient with high medical decision-making complexity and an expected duration of 60 minutes.

Clinical Context

Used for established patient visits involving high complexity medical decision-making, such as management of multiple acute and chronic conditions with complex diagnostic and therapeutic planning.
