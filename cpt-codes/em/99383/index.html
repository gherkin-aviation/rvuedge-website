99383 Preventive visit new age 5-11

Evaluation & Management

Also known as: preventive exam school age, well child visit 5-11, preventive care school age

Initial comprehensive preventive medicine evaluation and management of an individual including age and gender appropriate history, examination, counseling, and risk factor reduction interventions, new patient; age 5 through 11 years.

In Plain Language

School age health check-up; Child preventive visit

Clinical Context

Used for comprehensive preventive care visit with a child age 5-11 years, including immunizations, developmental assessment, and health risk counseling.
