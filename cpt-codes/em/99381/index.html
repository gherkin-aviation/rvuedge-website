99381 initial pm e/m new pat infant

Evaluation & Management

Also known as: preventive exam infant, well baby visit, preventive care newborn

Initial comprehensive preventive medicine evaluation and management of an individual including age and gender appropriate history, examination, counseling, and risk factor reduction interventions, established patient; infant (less than 1 year).

In Plain Language

Baby health check-up; Infant preventive visit

Clinical Context

Used for comprehensive preventive care visit with an infant less than 1 year of age, including developmental assessment, immunization review, and anticipatory guidance.
