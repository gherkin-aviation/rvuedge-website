99252 inpatient/obs consltj new/established sf 35

E&M

Also known as: hospital consultation, inpatient consult, hospital specialist visit

Inpatient or observation hospital consultations for a new or established patient with straightforward medical decision-making complexity and an expected time of 35 minutes.

Clinical Context

Used for consultations provided to hospitalized or observation patients by a physician who is requested to evaluate specific problems with straightforward medical decision-making complexity.
