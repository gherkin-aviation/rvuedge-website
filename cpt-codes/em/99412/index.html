99412 Preventive counseling group

E&M

Also known as: group preventive counseling extended, health education group extended, preventive group intensive

Preventive medicine; group counseling consisting of education and counseling of patients with similar health problems in an individual visit; approximately 60 minutes per individual.

Clinical Context

Used for extended group preventive medicine counseling approximately 60 minutes per participant, providing comprehensive education and intervention for patients with similar health concerns.
