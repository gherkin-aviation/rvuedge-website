99392 Preventive visit established age 1-4

Evaluation & Management

Also known as: preventive recheck toddler, well child revisit 1-4, preventive follow-up toddler

Periodic comprehensive preventive medicine re-evaluation and management of an individual including age and gender appropriate history, examination, counseling, and risk factor reduction interventions, established patient; age 1 through 4 years.

In Plain Language

Young child check-up follow-up; Toddler preventive follow-up

Clinical Context

Used for periodic preventive care revisit with a child age 1-4 years, including continued assessment of development, immunizations, and health maintenance.
