99315 Nf dschrg management 30 minutes/less

Evaluation & Management

Also known as: nursing home discharge, SNF discharge 30 min, facility discharge

Nursing facility care, discharge day management; per day for the evaluation and management activities on discharge from the facility of a resident with 30 minutes or less of physician or qualified health professional time spent on final discharge visit.

In Plain Language

Nursing home discharge visit; Leaving the care facility

Clinical Context

Used for nursing facility discharge day management when the physician or qualified health professional spends 30 minutes or less on discharge-related evaluation and activities.
