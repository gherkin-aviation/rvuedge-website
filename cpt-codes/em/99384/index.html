99384 Preventive visit new age 12-17

Evaluation & Management

Also known as: preventive exam adolescent, well teen visit, preventive care teenager

Initial comprehensive preventive medicine evaluation and management of an individual including age and gender appropriate history, examination, counseling, and risk factor reduction interventions, new patient; age 12 through 17 years.

In Plain Language

Teen health check-up; Adolescent preventive visit

Clinical Context

Used for comprehensive preventive care visit with an adolescent age 12-17 years, including sexual health, substance abuse counseling, and mental health screening.
