99470 Rpm treatment management first 10 minutes

E&M

Also known as: remote monitoring management brief, RPM 10 min

Remote patient monitoring treatment management service provided by clinical staff for first 10 minutes of active interaction, monitoring physiological parameters.

Clinical Context

Used for brief remote monitoring management interactions focused on review of physiological data and patient communication without extensive clinical decision-making.
