99374 Home health care supervision

E&M

Also known as: home health supervision, home care oversight, HHA supervision

Supervision of a patient under care of a home health agency in the home; physician oversight of care performed by home health staff; approximately one supervisory visit.

Clinical Context

Used when a physician provides medical oversight and supervision of a patient receiving services from a home health agency, including review of care provided by home health staff.
