99466 Pediatric critical care transport

E&M

Also known as: critical care transport pediatric, PICU transport

Pediatric critical care transport service with physician or qualified healthcare professional face-to-face supervision during interfacility transport of critically ill or injured child.

Clinical Context

Used when critically ill pediatric patient requires physician-supervised transport between facilities, including continuous monitoring and treatment during transfer.
