99416 Prolng clin staff service each add

Evaluation & Management +Add-on

Also known as: prolonged staff service add-on, clinical staff additional, nurse prolonged additional

Prolonged clinical staff service (the service time is performed entirely by clinical staff in the office or other outpatient setting) requiring direct patient contact; each additional 15 minutes of service after the first hour.

In Plain Language

Additional clinical staff time; Extended nursing care time

Clinical Context

Used to report each additional 15-minute increment of prolonged clinical staff direct patient contact in office settings beyond the first hour of prolonged service.

RVU Information

CPT 99416 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
