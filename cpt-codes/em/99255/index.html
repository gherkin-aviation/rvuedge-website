99255 inpatient/obs consltj new/established hi 80

E&M

Also known as: complex hospital consultation, high complexity consult, comprehensive hospital consult

Inpatient or observation hospital consultations for a new or established patient with high medical decision-making complexity and an expected time of 80 minutes.

Clinical Context

Used for hospital or observation consultations with high medical decision-making complexity, requiring comprehensive evaluation and recommendations for complex, multi-system acute problems in hospitalized patients.
