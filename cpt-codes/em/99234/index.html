99234 Hosp inpatient/obs sm dt sf/low 45

E&M

Also known as: same-day admission discharge, admit and discharge, observation same day

Hospital inpatient or observation care, including the admission and discharge of the patient on the same date, with straightforward or low medical decision-making complexity.

Clinical Context

Used when a patient is admitted to hospital or observation status and discharged on the same calendar date, typically for procedures, observation, or acute care resolution on a single day.
