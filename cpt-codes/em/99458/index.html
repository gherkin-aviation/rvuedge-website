99458 Rpm treatment management each additional 20 minutes

E&M +Add-on

Also known as: remote patient monitoring add-on, RPM additional time

Remote patient monitoring treatment management service for each additional 20 minutes of clinical staff time, including ongoing monitoring and patient management.

Clinical Context

Used for additional clinical staff time spent managing complex remote monitoring cases requiring extended care coordination beyond initial 20-minute period.
