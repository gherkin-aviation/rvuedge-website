99348 Home/res vst established low mdm 30

E&M

Also known as: home visit new low, house call new low complexity, new home low

Home or residence visit for the evaluation and management of a new patient, with low medical decision-making complexity and an expected duration of 30 minutes.

Clinical Context

Used for initial home-based evaluation and management of new patients with low medical decision-making complexity, such as initial home health assessment or single problem evaluation.
