99450 Basic life disability examination

Evaluation & Management

Also known as: disability exam, work comp exam, LND exam

Basic life and disability evaluation examination performed by treating physician for disability determination; includes history, examination, and medical decision-making.

In Plain Language

doctor's evaluation to determine disability status

Clinical Context

Used when physician performs comprehensive evaluation to establish or verify patient's disability status for life insurance, disability benefits, or work-related purposes.

RVU Information

CPT 99450 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
