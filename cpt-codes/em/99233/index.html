99233 Sbsq hosp inpatient/obs high 50

E&M

Also known as: subsequent hospital complex, complex inpatient follow-up, high complexity day, subsequent hospital visit level 3, hospital follow-up high

Subsequent hospital inpatient or observation care, per day, for the evaluation and management of a patient with high medical decision-making complexity.

Clinical Context

Used for subsequent days of hospital or observation care requiring high medical decision-making complexity, such as management of multiple acute conditions or significant clinical changes.
