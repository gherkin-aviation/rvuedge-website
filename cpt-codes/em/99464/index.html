99464 Attendance at delivery

E&M

Also known as: attendance at delivery, delivery attendance, obstetric attendance

Attendance at delivery and management of complications related to delivery process; includes initial care and resuscitation of newborn if needed.

Clinical Context

Used when physician or qualified practitioner is present at delivery to manage labor/delivery complications and provide immediate newborn assessment and initial care.
