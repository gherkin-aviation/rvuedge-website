99490 Chrnc care management staff first 20

E&M

Also known as: chronic care management staff, CCM staff

Chronic care management service provided by clinical staff for first 20 minutes of patient interaction, including assessment, coordination, and documentation.

Clinical Context

Used when nursing or clinical staff provides care coordination for patients with established chronic conditions, including care plan development and ongoing management.
