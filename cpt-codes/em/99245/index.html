99245 Off/outpatient consltj new/established hi 55

E&M

Also known as: complex consultation, high complexity consult, comprehensive specialist visit, office consult level 5

Office or other outpatient consultations for a new or established patient with high medical decision-making complexity and an expected time of 55 minutes.

Clinical Context

Used for office-based consultations with high medical decision-making complexity, such as evaluation of complex, multi-system problems requiring comprehensive diagnostic and therapeutic planning from a specialist.
