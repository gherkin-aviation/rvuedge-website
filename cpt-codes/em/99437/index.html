Evaluation & Management +Add-on

Chronic care management service provided by physician for each additional 30 minutes beyond the first hour of established chronic condition management.

In Plain Language

doctor's care coordination for long-term health conditions

Clinical Context

Used when a physician provides chronic care management coordination for patients with multiple chronic conditions requiring ongoing physician oversight beyond the initial management period.
