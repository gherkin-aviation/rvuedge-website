99242 Off/outpatient consltj new/established sf 20

E&M

Also known as: office consultation, specialist consult, brief consultation, office consult level 2

Office or other outpatient consultations for a new or established patient with straightforward medical decision-making complexity and an expected time of 20 minutes.

Clinical Context

Used for office-based consultation by a physician who is requested to evaluate and provide recommendations on specific problems or conditions for new or established patients with straightforward complexity.
