99498 Advncd care plan additional 30 minutes

E&M +Add-on

Also known as: advance care planning additional, end-of-life counseling continued

Advanced care plan development and counseling service for each additional 30 minutes of patient conversation regarding serious illness and end-of-life care.

Clinical Context

Used for extended advance care planning discussions addressing complex family dynamics, organ donation, or detailed documentation of end-of-life preferences.
