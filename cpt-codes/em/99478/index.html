99478 Sbsq ic vlbw inf<1,500 gm

E&M

Also known as: VLBW day hospital care, very low birth weight

Subsequent day hospital care of very low birth weight newborn (less than 1,500 grams) per calendar day with continuing assessment and interventions.

Clinical Context

Used for ongoing day hospital management of extremely premature infants weighing less than 1,500 grams requiring observation and frequent monitoring.
