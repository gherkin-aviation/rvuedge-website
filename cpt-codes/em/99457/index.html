99457 Rpm treatment management first 20 minutes

E&M

Also known as: remote patient monitoring, RPM management, telemonitoring

Remote patient monitoring treatment management service for first 20 minutes of clinical staff time, including device data review and patient communication.

Clinical Context

Used when clinical staff actively manages patient care based on remote-monitored physiological parameters, including interpretation, clinical decision-making, and patient communication.
