99375 Home health care supervision

Evaluation & Management

Also known as: home health supervision bimonthly, HHA supervision frequent, home care oversight

Supervision of a patient under care of a home health agency in the home; physician oversight of care performed by home health staff; approximately two supervisory visits per month.

In Plain Language

Regular home health supervision; Frequent home care monitoring

Clinical Context

Used when a physician provides medical oversight and supervision of a patient receiving home health services, with approximately two supervisory visits per month.
