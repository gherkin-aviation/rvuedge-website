99422 Ol dig e/m service 11-20 minutes

E&M

Also known as: online E/M moderate, digital visit 11-20 min, telemedicine moderate

Online digital evaluation and management service, for an established patient, for up to 7 days of communication in a 7-day period; 11-20 minutes of clinical staff time with supervising physician review.

Clinical Context

Used for asynchronous online digital evaluation and management of established patients involving 11-20 minutes of clinical staff time with supervising physician review.
