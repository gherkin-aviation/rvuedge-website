99283 Emergency dept visit low mdm

Evaluation & Management

Also known as: ED visit low complexity, emergency low, ER low severity, ED visit level 3, moderate complexity ER visit, ER visit moderate, emergency room level 3

Emergency department visit for the evaluation and management of a patient with low medical decision-making complexity.

In Plain Language

Emergency room visit with evaluation; Minor emergency care

Clinical Context

Used for emergency department visits with low medical decision-making complexity, such as single acute problems without significant systemic involvement or minor chronic disease exacerbations.
