99439 Chrnc care management staf each additional

E&M +Add-on

Also known as: chronic care management staff add-on, CCM staff add-on

Chronic care management service provided by clinical staff for each additional 30 minutes beyond the first hour of established chronic condition management.

Clinical Context

Used when nursing or clinical staff provides chronic care management services for complex patients with established chronic conditions requiring ongoing staff coordination beyond initial management.
