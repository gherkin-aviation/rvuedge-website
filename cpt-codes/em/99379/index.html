99379 Nursing fac care supervision

Evaluation & Management

Also known as: nursing facility supervision, SNF oversight, facility supervision

Supervision of a patient in a nursing facility; physician oversight of care provided by nursing facility staff; approximately one supervisory visit per month.

In Plain Language

Nursing home supervision; Care facility oversight

Clinical Context

Used when a physician provides medical oversight and supervision of a patient residing in a nursing facility, including review of facility-provided care and resident management.
