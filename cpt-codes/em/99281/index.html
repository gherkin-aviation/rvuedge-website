99281 Emr dpt vst mayx req phy/qhp

E&M

Also known as: ED visit minimal, ER minimum severity, emergency minimal, ED visit level 1, straightforward ER visit

Emergency department visit for the evaluation and management of a patient with minimal complexity, where the patient may not require the presence of a physician or qualified health professional.

Clinical Context

Used for emergency department visits involving minimal medical decision-making, such as simple lacerations requiring basic wound care or minor complaints with straightforward evaluation.
