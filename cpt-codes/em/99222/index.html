99222 first hosp inpatient/obs moderate 55

E&M

Also known as: initial hospital visit, hospital admission moderate, inpatient initial, initial hospital admit level 2

Initial hospital inpatient or observation care, per day, for the evaluation and management of a patient with moderate medical decision-making complexity.

Clinical Context

Used for initial hospital or observation admission requiring moderate medical decision-making complexity, such as significant acute illness or multiple comorbidities requiring hospital-level care.
