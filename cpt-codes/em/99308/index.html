99308 Sbsq nf care low mdm 20

E&M

Also known as: nursing home visit low, SNF low complexity, facility follow-up

Nursing facility care, subsequent visit for the evaluation and management of a resident, with low medical decision-making complexity.

Clinical Context

Used for subsequent nursing facility visits with low medical decision-making complexity, such as management of single stable chronic condition or routine monitoring with minimal acute changes.
