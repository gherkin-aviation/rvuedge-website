99446 Ntrprof ph1/ntrnet/ehr 5-10

E&M

Also known as: interprofessional consult, eConsult, care coordination consult

Non-face-to-face interprofessional consultation including review of electronic health record for up to 10 minutes of physician or other qualified healthcare professional time.

Clinical Context

Used when a physician or qualified healthcare professional provides asynchronous consultation via EHR for interprofessional care coordination; common in complex chronic disease management.
