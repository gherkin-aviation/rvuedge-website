99304 first nf care sf/low mdm 25

E&M

Also known as: nursing home admission, nursing facility initial, SNF admission

Nursing facility care, initial evaluation and management of a resident on admission to the facility, with straightforward or low medical decision-making complexity.

Clinical Context

Used for initial evaluation and management of patients admitted to nursing facilities or skilled nursing facilities, including assessment of functional status and development of initial care plans.
