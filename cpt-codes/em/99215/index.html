99215 Office outpatient established hi 40 minutes

E&M

Also known as: complex office visit, high complexity, comprehensive visit, established patient office visit level 5, high complexity visit, complex visit

Office or other outpatient visit for the evaluation and management of an established patient with high medical decision-making complexity and an expected duration of 40 minutes.

Clinical Context

Used for established patient office visits requiring high medical decision-making complexity, such as new diagnosis of significant condition, complex medication adjustments, or management of multiple acute and chronic conditions.
