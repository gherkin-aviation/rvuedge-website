99445 Rem mntr physiol param 2-15

Evaluation & Management

Also known as: remote monitoring, RPM non-physician, patient-recorded data review

Remote monitoring of physiological parameters (such as blood pressure, weight, glucose) for up to 15 days per month, evaluated by non-physician professional.

In Plain Language

nurse or coordinator reviews your home health readings

Clinical Context

Used for chronic disease management programs where patients self-monitor health parameters at home and non-physician professionals review data periodically without direct practitioner engagement.

RVU Information
