99254 inpatient/obs cnsltj new/established mod 60

E&M

Also known as: hospital consultation moderate, complex inpatient consult, hospital specialist

Inpatient or observation hospital consultations for a new or established patient with moderate medical decision-making complexity and an expected time of 60 minutes.

Clinical Context

Used for hospital or observation consultations requiring moderate medical decision-making complexity, such as evaluation of multiple acute problems or complicated single conditions affecting hospitalized patients.
