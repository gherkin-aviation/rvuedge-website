99452 Ntrprof ph1/ntrnet/ehr rfrl

E&M

Also known as: interprofessional referral consult, eConsult referral

Interprofessional consultation for initial referral, with review of electronic health record and communication with referring provider by physician or qualified healthcare professional.

Clinical Context

Used when a specialist provides initial consultation in response to referral via electronic health record system with communication back to referring provider.
