99463 Same day nb discharge

Evaluation & Management

Also known as: same-day newborn discharge, newborn admit-discharge

Initial and same-day discharge history and physical examination and resuscitation or stabilization services for newborn admission and discharge on the same calendar day.

In Plain Language

doctor's exam and discharge of newborn all on same day

Clinical Context

Used when newborn is admitted to hospital and discharged on same day (e.g., uncomplicated vaginal delivery with mother), with full initial evaluation.
