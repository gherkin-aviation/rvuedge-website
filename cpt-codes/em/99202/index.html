99202 Office outpatient new sf 15 minutes

Evaluation & Management

Also known as: established office visit, office E/M, established patient visit, new patient office visit level 2, straightforward new visit

Office or other outpatient visit for the evaluation and management of an established patient with a straightforward medical decision-making complexity and an expected duration of 15 minutes.

In Plain Language

Doctor's office visit for an established patient; Brief follow-up appointment

Clinical Context

Used for routine follow-up visits in office settings where the patient is already established with the provider and the medical complexity is straightforward.
