99449 Ntrprof ph1/ntrnet/ehr 31/>

E&M

Also known as: interprofessional consult high, eConsult >30 min

Non-face-to-face interprofessional consultation including review of electronic health record for more than 30 minutes of physician or other qualified healthcare professional time.

Clinical Context

Used for highly complex interprofessional consultations requiring extensive review and detailed specialist recommendations, delivered asynchronously through electronic systems.
