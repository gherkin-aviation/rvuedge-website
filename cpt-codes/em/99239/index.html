99239 Hosp inpatient/obs dschrg management >30

Evaluation & Management

Also known as: complex discharge, extended discharge management, discharge over 30 min, hospital discharge day management over 30 min, hospital discharge day 40+ minutes

Hospital inpatient or observation care, discharge day management for a patient with more than 30 minutes of physician or qualified health professional time spent on final hospital visit and discharge activities.

In Plain Language

Extended hospital discharge planning; Complex discharge from hospital

Clinical Context

Used for hospital or observation discharge management when the physician or qualified health professional spends more than 30 minutes on discharge-related activities, such as complex discharge planning or education for complex conditions.
