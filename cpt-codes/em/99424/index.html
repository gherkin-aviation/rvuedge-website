99424 Prin care management phys first 30

E&M

Also known as: principal care management, chronic care management, disease management

Principal care management services, for a single high-risk disease, chronic condition, or two or more chronic conditions lasting at least 30 days; care plan oversight and coordination of care by a physician or other qualified health professional; first 30 minutes of physician or other qualified health professional time in a calendar month.

Clinical Context

Used for comprehensive care management services for patients with high-risk, complex chronic conditions requiring intensive coordination and oversight by physician or qualified health professional.
