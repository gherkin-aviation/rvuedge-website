99483 Assmt & care pln pt cog imp

E&M

Also known as: cognitive assessment, dementia assessment, memory care visit

Assessment and care plan development for patient with cognitive impairment, including history and physical examination, cognitive assessment, and comprehensive care coordination.

Clinical Context

Used for comprehensive evaluation of cognitive impairment (dementia, mild cognitive impairment) with development of management plan addressing medical, behavioral, and social needs.
