Emergency department visit for the evaluation and management of a patient with high medical decision-making complexity.

In Plain Language

Complex emergency room visit; Serious emergency care

Clinical Context

Used for emergency department visits with high medical decision-making complexity, such as critically ill or injured patients, multiple acute significant conditions, or presentations requiring extensive diagnostic and therapeutic evaluation.
