99223 first hosp inpatient/obs high 75

E&M

Also known as: initial hospital complex, high acuity admission, complex inpatient, initial hospital admit level 3, hospital admission high complexity

Initial hospital inpatient or observation care, per day, for the evaluation and management of a patient with high medical decision-making complexity.

Clinical Context

Used for initial hospital or observation admission requiring high medical decision-making complexity, such as critically ill patients, multiple acute conditions, or complex diagnostic and therapeutic planning.
