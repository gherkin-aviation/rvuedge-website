99454 Rem mntr physiol param 16-30

E&M

Also known as: remote monitoring 16-30 days, RPM extended, home health monitoring

Remote monitoring of physiological parameters (such as blood pressure, weight, glucose) for 16-30 days per month, evaluated and documented by healthcare provider.

Clinical Context

Used for chronic disease management with frequent home health parameter monitoring over two-week to monthly periods; common in heart failure, diabetes, and hypertension management.
