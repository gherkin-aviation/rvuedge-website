99491 Chrnc care management phys first 30

E&M

Also known as: chronic care management physician, CCM physician

Chronic care management service provided by physician for first 30 minutes of patient interaction, including comprehensive assessment and care coordination.

Clinical Context

Used when physician provides care coordination and management for established chronic conditions in patients with multiple acute and chronic diseases.
