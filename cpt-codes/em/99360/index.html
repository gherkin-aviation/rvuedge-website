99360 Physician standby services

Evaluation & Management

Also known as: standby service, physician standby, on-call standby

Physician or qualified health professional standby service requiring prolonged physician or qualified health professional attendance without direct patient care; first 30 minutes of standby service.

In Plain Language

Doctor on standby; Reserved medical time

Clinical Context

Used when a physician or qualified health professional is requested to be immediately available for potential provision of medical services but does not provide direct patient care.
