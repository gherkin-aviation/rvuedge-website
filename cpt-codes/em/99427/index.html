99427 Prin care management staff each additional

Evaluation & Management +Add-on

Also known as: care management add-on, CCM staff add-on

Principal care management service provided by clinical staff for each additional 30 minutes beyond the first hour of chronic care management coordination.

In Plain Language

ongoing health care coordination by nurse or care coordinator

Clinical Context

Used when nursing or clinical staff provides chronic care management services for complex patients with multiple chronic conditions requiring ongoing coordination beyond initial management period.
