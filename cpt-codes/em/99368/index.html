99368 Team conf without pat by hc pro

E&M

Also known as: team conference staff, care conference staff, interprofessional team

Team conference without the patient present, including history review and medical decision-making by a qualified health professional other than a physician.

Clinical Context

Used when qualified health professionals (nurse, therapist, social worker) participate in a team conference without the patient present to discuss care coordination and management.
