99425 Prin care management phys each additional

E&M +Add-on

Also known as: principal care management add-on, CCM additional, chronic disease management add

Principal care management services, for a single high-risk disease, chronic condition, or two or more chronic conditions lasting at least 30 days; each additional 15 minutes of physician or other qualified health professional time in a calendar month.

Clinical Context

Used to report each additional 15-minute increment of principal care management services beyond the first 30 minutes in a calendar month.
