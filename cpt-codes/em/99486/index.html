99486 Suprv interfac trnsport additional

E&M +Add-on

Also known as: transport supervision additional, extended transfer

Supervision of interfacility transport for each additional 30 minutes of physician or qualified healthcare professional face-to-face contact during critically ill patient transport.

Clinical Context

Used for prolonged critical care transport requiring extended physician supervision beyond initial 30-minute period for complex or lengthy transfers.
