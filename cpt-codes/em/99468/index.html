99468 Neonate critical care initial

E&M

Also known as: neonatal critical care, NICU admission, neonatal ICU care

Initial critical care evaluation and management of acutely ill or injured neonate (birth to 28 days) requiring intensive monitoring and high-acuity intervention.

Clinical Context

Used for initial comprehensive critical care of neonates with serious conditions (prematurity, sepsis, respiratory distress, congenital anomalies) in intensive care unit.
