99309 Sbsq nf care moderate mdm 30

E&M

Also known as: nursing home moderate, SNF moderate complexity, facility moderate visit

Nursing facility care, subsequent visit for the evaluation and management of a resident, with moderate medical decision-making complexity.

Clinical Context

Used for subsequent nursing facility visits with moderate medical decision-making complexity, such as adjustment of treatment plans for existing conditions or new acute issues in residents.
