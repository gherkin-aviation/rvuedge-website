99477 initial day hosp neonate care

E&M

Also known as: neonatal day hospital, newborn day program

Initial day hospital (observation/outpatient) care and management of neonate on the day of admission including assessment and any necessary initial interventions.

Clinical Context

Used for initial evaluation and management of neonates in day hospital/observation setting for monitoring, phototherapy, and other non-overnight interventions.
