99456 Disability examination

E&M

Also known as: disability evaluation, disability exam, impairment rating

Disability examination evaluation by treating physician for determination of disability status, eligibility, and work limitations; includes comprehensive clinical assessment.

Clinical Context

Used when physician conducts formal evaluation to document patient's functional status and disability for social security, disability insurance, or employer purposes.
