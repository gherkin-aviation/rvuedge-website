99345 Home/res vst new high mdm 75

E&M

Also known as: home visit complex, house call high complexity, complex home care

Home or residence visit for the evaluation and management of an established patient, with high medical decision-making complexity and an expected duration of 75 minutes.

Clinical Context

Used for home-based evaluation and management of established patients with high medical decision-making complexity, such as management of multiple acute and chronic conditions or complex clinical situations.
