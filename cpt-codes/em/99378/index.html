99378 Hospice care supervision

E&M

Also known as: hospice supervision frequent, hospice oversight bimonthly, complex end-of-life supervision

Supervision of a patient receiving hospice care; physician oversight of care provided by hospice staff; approximately two supervisory visits per month.

Clinical Context

Used when a physician provides medical oversight and supervision of a patient receiving hospice services with approximately two visits per month for complex symptom management or care coordination.
