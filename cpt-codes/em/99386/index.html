99386 Preventive visit new age 40-64

Evaluation & Management

Also known as: preventive exam middle age, well adult visit 40-64, preventive care middle aged

Initial comprehensive preventive medicine evaluation and management of an individual including age and gender appropriate history, examination, counseling, and risk factor reduction interventions, new patient; age 40 through 64 years.

In Plain Language

Middle age health check-up; Adult preventive visit

Clinical Context

Used for comprehensive preventive care visit with a middle-aged adult age 40-64 years, including screening for chronic disease risk factors and age-appropriate preventive interventions.
