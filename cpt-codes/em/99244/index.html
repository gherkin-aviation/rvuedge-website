99244 Off/outpatient cnsltj new/established mod 40

E&M

Also known as: office consultation moderate, specialist consult, consultation, office consult level 4

Office or other outpatient consultations for a new or established patient with moderate medical decision-making complexity and an expected time of 40 minutes.

Clinical Context

Used for office-based consultations with moderate medical decision-making complexity, involving evaluation of multiple problems or a single complex problem requiring specialist evaluation and recommendations.
