99212 Office outpatient established sf 10 minutes

E&M

Also known as: brief office visit, simple follow-up, established patient, established patient office visit level 2, straightforward visit

Office or other outpatient visit for the evaluation and management of an established patient with straightforward medical decision-making complexity and an expected duration of 10 minutes.

Clinical Context

Used for straightforward established patient office visits involving minimal medical decision-making, such as routine medication refills or simple follow-up for stable conditions.
