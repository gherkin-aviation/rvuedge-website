99358 Prolong service without contact

E&M +Add-on

Also known as: prolonged service office, extended office visit, prolonged E/M

Prolonged evaluation and management service in the office or other outpatient setting requiring direct patient contact beyond the usual service; first hour of service.

Clinical Context

Used when physician or qualified health professional spends prolonged time in direct patient contact in outpatient settings beyond what is typical for the primary evaluation and management code.
