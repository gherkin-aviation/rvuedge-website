99203 Office outpatient new low 30 minutes

E&M

Also known as: office visit low complexity, established patient follow-up, new patient office visit level 3, low complexity new visit

Office or other outpatient visit for the evaluation and management of an established patient with low medical decision-making complexity and an expected duration of 30 minutes.

Clinical Context

Used for office visits involving established patients requiring low-complexity medical decision-making such as management of stable chronic conditions or minor acute problems.
