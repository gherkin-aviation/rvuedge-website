99415 Prolng clin staff service first hour

E&M

Also known as: prolonged staff service office, clinical staff extended, nurse prolonged service

Prolonged clinical staff service (the service time is performed entirely by clinical staff in the office or other outpatient setting) requiring direct patient contact; first hour of service.

Clinical Context

Used when clinical staff spends prolonged time in direct patient contact in office settings performing extended services, such as detailed patient education or complex care coordination.
