99214 Office outpatient established mod 30 minutes

Evaluation & Management

Also known as: moderate office visit, established patient, office E/M, established patient office visit level 4, moderate complexity visit, extended visit, detailed office visit, complex follow up

Office or other outpatient visit for the evaluation and management of an established patient with moderate medical decision-making complexity and an expected duration of 30 minutes.

In Plain Language

Standard detailed visit; Medical appointment with evaluation

Clinical Context

Used for established patient office visits with moderate medical decision-making complexity, involving evaluation of new or worsening conditions or management of established conditions with some complexity.
