99408 Audit/dast 15-30 minutes

E&M

Also known as: substance abuse screening 15-30, AUDIT/DAST screening, alcohol screening brief

Alcohol and substance abuse screening and brief intervention services (not including illicit drugs); 15-30 minutes of physician or qualified health professional time devoted to screening, intervention, and referral to treatment services.

Clinical Context

Used for substance abuse and alcohol screening and brief intervention services, using validated screening instruments such as Alcohol Use Disorders Identification Test (AUDIT) or Drug Abuse Screening Test (DAST), 15-30 minutes.
