99347 Home/res vst established sf mdm 20

E&M

Also known as: home visit new simple, house call new patient, new home visit

Home or residence visit for the evaluation and management of a new patient, with straightforward medical decision-making complexity and an expected duration of 20 minutes.

Used for initial home-based evaluation and management of new patients with straightforward medical decision-making complexity, such as initial assessment for homebound or home health patients.
