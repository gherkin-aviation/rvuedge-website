99231 Sbsq hosp inpatient/obs sf/low 25

E&M

Also known as: subsequent hospital visit, daily hospital follow-up, inpatient day 2+, subsequent hospital visit level 1, hospital follow-up low

Subsequent hospital inpatient or observation care, per day, for the evaluation and management of a patient with straightforward or low medical decision-making complexity.

Clinical Context

Used for subsequent days of hospital or observation care involving straightforward or low medical decision-making complexity, such as stable patient status or standard post-operative monitoring.
