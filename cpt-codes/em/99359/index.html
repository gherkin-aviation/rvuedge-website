99359 Prolong serv without contact add

Evaluation & Management +Add-on

Also known as: prolonged service additional office, extended service add-on, prolonged add-on

Prolonged evaluation and management service in the office or other outpatient setting requiring direct patient contact beyond the usual service; each additional 30 minutes of service after the first hour.

In Plain Language

Additional extended visit time; Extra office appointment time

Clinical Context

Used to report each additional 30-minute increment of prolonged direct patient contact in outpatient settings beyond the first hour of prolonged service.
