99447 Ntrprof ph1/ntrnet/ehr 11-20

E&M

Also known as: interprofessional consult medium, eConsult 11-20 min

Non-face-to-face interprofessional consultation including review of electronic health record for 11-20 minutes of physician or other qualified healthcare professional time.

Clinical Context

Used for moderate-complexity interprofessional consultations delivered asynchronously through electronic health records, providing specialist input without requiring patient office visit.
