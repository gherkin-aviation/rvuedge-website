99213 Office outpatient established low 20 minutes

E&M

Also known as: office visit, low complexity visit, established patient, established patient office visit level 3, standard office visit, follow-up visit, return visit

Office or other outpatient visit for the evaluation and management of an established patient with low medical decision-making complexity and an expected duration of 20 minutes.

Clinical Context

Used for established patient office visits with low medical decision-making complexity, such as routine visits for stable chronic disease management or follow-up of minor acute conditions.
