Also known as: initial hospital admission, hospital inpatient, first day hospital, initial hospital admit level 1, hospital admission straightforward

Initial hospital inpatient or observation care, per day, for the evaluation and management of a patient with straightforward or low medical decision-making complexity.

Used for the initial evaluation and management of a patient admitted to an inpatient hospital setting or observation status with straightforward or low medical complexity.
