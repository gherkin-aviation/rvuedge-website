99461 initial nb em per day non-fac

E&M

Also known as: initial newborn exam non-hospital, newborn office H&P

Initial history and physical examination for newborn care performed by physician per calendar day in non-hospital setting (office, birthing center).

Clinical Context

Used when physician performs initial comprehensive newborn evaluation in outpatient setting such as office or birthing center, typically within 24 hours of delivery.
