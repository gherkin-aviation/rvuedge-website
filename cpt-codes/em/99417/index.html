99417 Prolng outpatient e/m each 15 minutes

E&M +Add-on

Also known as: prolonged office E/M add-on, extended outpatient additional, prolonged outpatient add-on

Prolonged office or other outpatient evaluation and management service beyond the usual service; each additional 15 minutes of physician or qualified health professional time.

Clinical Context

Used to report each additional 15-minute increment of prolonged direct patient contact in office settings beyond the initial E&M code.
