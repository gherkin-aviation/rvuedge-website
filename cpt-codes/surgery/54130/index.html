54130 Remove penis & nodes

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: penectomy with inguinal node dissection, radical penectomy

Amputation of the penis with removal of pelvic lymph nodes for staging and treatment of penile malignancy.

In Plain Language

removing penis and lymph nodes due to cancer

Clinical Context

Indicated for penile cancer with evidence of nodal involvement or high-risk features. Lymph node dissection required for staging.
