21480 Cltx tmprmand dislc first/sbsq

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: TMJ dislocation reduction, jaw dislocation closed reduction

Closed treatment of temporomandibular joint dislocation, first or subsequent occurrence. Non-surgical reduction of temporomandibular joint dislocation with manual manipulation to restore normal joint alignment.

In Plain Language

jaw joint popping back into place; jaw dislocation realignment

Clinical Context

Used for temporomandibular joint dislocation amenable to manual reduction. First-time and recurrent dislocations can be managed non-operatively. Patient requires post-reduction jaw mobility restrictions and may need muscle relaxants.
