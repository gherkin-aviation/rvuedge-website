24361 Reconstruct elbow joint

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: complex elbow reconstruction, extensive elbow reconstruction

Reconstruction of the elbow joint with more extensive procedure. Complex reconstruction addressing significant structural defects.

In Plain Language

Extensive surgical reconstruction of a severely damaged elbow joint; Complex rebuilding of the elbow

Clinical Context

Used for more extensive elbow joint reconstruction requiring greater surgical complexity such as multiple soft tissue transfers or extensive bony reconstruction.

This code has a 90-day global period, which includes the day of the procedure, 1 day preoperative, and 90 days of postoperative care.
