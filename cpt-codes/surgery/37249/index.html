37249 Trluml balo angiop additional vein

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: venous angioplasty additional, additional venous PTA

Percutaneous or open transluminal balloon angioplasty of each additional venous vessel beyond the first to restore luminal patency.

Clinical Context

Each additional vein in multi-vessel venous disease. Improves overall hemodynamics in staged venous interventions.
