17360 Chemical exfoliation acne

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: chemical peel acne, chemical exfoliation

Chemical exfoliation or chemical peeling procedure for treatment of acne using topical chemical agents such as salicylic acid or glycolic acid.

Clinical Context

Performed for mild to moderate acne when oral or topical medications are insufficient. Promotes skin turnover and unclogs pores.
