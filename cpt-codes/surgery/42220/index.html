42220 Reconstruct cleft palate

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: cleft palate repair, palate reconstruction

Surgical reconstruction of a cleft palate with specific attention to restoring normal anatomic relationships and function.

Clinical Context

Primary or secondary cleft palate reconstruction in pediatric or adult patients to restore anatomic form and functional swallowing and speech.
