64734 Incision of cheek nerve

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: infraorbital nerve incision, cheek nerve decompression

Surgical incision and decompression of the infraorbital nerve in the cheek region. Treats compression or entrapment of this branch of the trigeminal nerve.

Clinical Context

Used for infraorbital neuralgia or compression neuropathy causing cheek pain and facial dysesthesia. Surgery decompresses the nerve at the infraorbital foramen.
