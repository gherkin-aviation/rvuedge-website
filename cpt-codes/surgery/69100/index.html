69100 Biopsy of external ear

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: external ear biopsy, auricular biopsy

Biopsy of external ear including auricle or external acoustic meatus using punch or excision technique.

Clinical Context

Used for diagnosis of external ear lesions. Common indications include suspected malignancy, inflammatory conditions, or dermatologic pathology.
