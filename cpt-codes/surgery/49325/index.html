49325 Laparoscopic revision perm inpatient catheter

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: laparoscopic IP catheter revision, lap IP cath revision, peritoneal catheter repair

Laparoscopic revision or relocation of permanent intraperitoneal catheter with repair of tunnel complications.

Clinical Context

Laparoscopic revision of failing or infected peritoneal dialysis catheters. Indications include catheter migration, obstruction, exit-site infection, or tunnel tract complications.
