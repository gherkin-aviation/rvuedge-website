25110 Remove wrist tendon lesion

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: tendon lesion removal, ganglion debulking from tendon

Removal of a lesion (pathologic growth or abnormality) from a wrist tendon, preserving tendon continuity and function.

Clinical Context

Used for tendon cysts, nodules, or other non-inflammatory lesions affecting wrist tendons. Less morbid than full tendon excision.
