42210 Reconstruct cleft palate

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: cleft palate repair, complex palate reconstruction

Surgical reconstruction of a cleft palate with significant anatomic involvement or complexity requiring extensive surgical techniques and reconstruction.

In Plain Language

surgery to fix large cleft in roof of mouth

Clinical Context

Definitive surgical repair of complex cleft palate with extensive defects, multiple tissue layers involved, or requiring vomer flap techniques.
