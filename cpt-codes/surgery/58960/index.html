58960 Exploration of abdomen

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: exploratory laparotomy, abdominal exploration, peritoneal exploration

Exploration of the abdominal cavity and peritoneal surfaces, including inspection for endometriosis, adhesions, malignancy, or other pathology.

Clinical Context

Diagnostic procedure when imaging is inconclusive; allows for inspection, biopsies, and therapeutic intervention (lysis of adhesions, biopsy).
