49435 Insert subcutaneous exten to inpatient catheter

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: IP catheter extension, subcutaneous extension, catheter extension insertion

Insertion of subcutaneous extension catheter or tubing connected to intraperitoneal catheter as add-on procedure.

In Plain Language

attachment of tubing extension to dialysis catheter; placement of catheter tunnel extension

Clinical Context

Addition of subcutaneous extension catheter or port extension to intraperitoneal dialysis catheter. Used to extend reach of existing catheter or improve access.
