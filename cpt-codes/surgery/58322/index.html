58322 Artificial insemination

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: artificial insemination with preparation, prepared insemination

Artificial insemination with more complex preparation or placement involves additional processing of sperm sample or specialized placement technique.

Clinical Context

Used when sperm requires additional processing such as capacitation, selection, or concentration. May involve specialized placement into fallopian tube.
