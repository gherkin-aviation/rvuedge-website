36013 Place catheter in artery

Surgery

Also known as: arterial line, A-line placement, radial artery catheter

Percutaneous placement of arterial catheter for blood pressure monitoring or blood sampling. Arterial access.

In Plain Language

putting a tube in an artery to check blood pressure; arterial access line

Clinical Context

Used for continuous hemodynamic monitoring in critical care, perioperative settings, or frequent blood draws.
