27767 Cltx posterior ankle fracture

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: closed reduction posterior ankle, conservative posterior malleolus

Closed treatment of posterior ankle malleolus (tibia) fracture with manipulation and immobilization.

Clinical Context

Used for non-displaced posterior malleolar fractures. Conservative approach with casting and non-weight bearing.
