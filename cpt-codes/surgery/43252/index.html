43252 Egd optical endomicroscopy

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: EGD endomicroscopy, confocal laser endomicroscopy, optical biopsy

Esophagogastroduodenoscopy with optical endomicroscopy examination of esophageal or gastric lesions for real-time histologic imaging. This diagnostic technique provides in-vivo tissue characterization.

In Plain Language

microscope view through camera; real-time tissue imaging

Clinical Context

Emerging technology for Barrett's esophagus surveillance and early cancer detection; improves diagnostic accuracy and guides therapeutic decisions.
