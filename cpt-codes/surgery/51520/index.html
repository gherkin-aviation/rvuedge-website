51520 Removal of bladder lesion

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: bladder lesion removal, lesion excision, cystotomy for lesion

Removal of bladder lesion via cystotomy, surgical excision of pathologic lesion from bladder mucosa or wall.

Clinical Context

Used for benign bladder lesions, small tumors, or lesions not amenable to endoscopic management. Open surgical approach.
