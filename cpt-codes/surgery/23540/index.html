23540 Cltx acromclav dislc wo mnpj

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: closed treatment AC dislocation, conservative AC treatment, non-operative AC dislocation

Closed treatment of acromioclavicular dislocation without manipulation. Conservative management with strapping or sling immobilization without reduction under anesthesia.

Clinical Context

Appropriate for most acromioclavicular dislocations (types I-II); achieves functional results through pain control and graduated mobilization.
