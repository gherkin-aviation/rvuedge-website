54416 Remv/repl penis contain pros

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: prosthesis replacement, penile implant exchange

Removal and replacement of a contained penile prosthesis with a new device.

Clinical Context

Performed when exchanging a malfunctioning penile prosthesis for a new one during the same operative session. Common in urology for prosthesis maintenance or upgrade.
