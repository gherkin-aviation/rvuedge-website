46922 Excision of anal lesion(s)

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: anal lesion excision, surgical removal, lesion resection

Surgical excision of anal canal lesions. Complete removal of anal lesions by scalpel or other cutting instrument.

Clinical Context

Used for suspicious lesions requiring complete excision with pathologic examination, or lesions not amenable to ablation.
