35585 Vein byp fem-tibial peroneal

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: vein graft fem-tib bypass, infrapopliteal vein bypass

Venous bypass graft from femoral to tibial or peroneal artery (reversed saphenous vein or similar).

Clinical Context

Infrainguinal revascularization for tibial vessel occlusion using autogenous vein. Superior patency to prosthetic grafts; mainstay for critical limb ischemia salvage.
