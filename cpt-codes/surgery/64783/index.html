64783 Limb nerve surgery add-on

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: additional limb nerve lesion removal

Surgical removal of additional nerve lesion(s) in the limb as an add-on procedure to the primary limb nerve lesion removal.

Clinical Context

Used when multiple neuromas or lesions are removed from different sites in the same extremity or limb in a single operative session.
