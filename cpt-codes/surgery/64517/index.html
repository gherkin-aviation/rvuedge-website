64517 N block injection hypogas plxs

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: hypogastric plexus block, superior hypogastric plexus block, pelvic pain block

Nerve block of the hypogastric plexus through injection of anesthetic and/or steroid medication. Treats pelvic and abdominal pain.

In Plain Language

pelvic nerve pain injection; lower abdomen pain relief shot

Clinical Context

Used for chronic pelvic pain, cancer pain, and visceral pain from pelvic organs. Blocks sympathetic innervation to pelvis.
