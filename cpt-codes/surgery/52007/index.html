52007 Cysto urtrl cathj brush biopsy

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: cysto with brush biopsy, cysto with upper tract biopsy

Cystourethroscopy with ureteral catheterization and brush biopsy of the upper urinary tract to obtain tissue samples for cytologic or histologic analysis.

Clinical Context

Used for diagnosis of upper urinary tract malignancy suspected on imaging or from positive urine cytology. Increases diagnostic yield over ureteral catheterization alone.
