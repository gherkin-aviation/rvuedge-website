27826 Treat lower leg fracture

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: open reduction lower leg, lower leg ORIF

Initial open treatment of lower leg fracture (tibia-fibula) with direct visualization and internal fixation.

Clinical Context

Indicated for displaced, comminuted, or open lower leg fractures requiring anatomic reduction and stable fixation.
