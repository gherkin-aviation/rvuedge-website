32225 Partial release of lung

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: partial decortication, limited pleural release

Partial surgical release or mobilization of the visceral pleura, performed to restore some lung expansion in less extensive restrictive disease without complete decortication.

In Plain Language

partial lung surface release

Clinical Context

Used for localized pleural thickening, partial pulmonary restriction, or incomplete fibrothorax not requiring extensive total decortication.
