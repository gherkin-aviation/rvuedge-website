54065 Destruction penis lesion(s)

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: penile lesion destruction, extensive destruction

Destruction of lesions of the penis, typically using extensive technique for larger areas or multiple lesions.

Clinical Context

Used for extensive penile warts, large lesions, or when multiple treatment modalities required. Typically requires general anesthesia.
