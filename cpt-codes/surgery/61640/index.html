61640 Dilate ic vasospasm initial

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: vasospasm angioplasty, cerebral vasospasm treatment

Percutaneous transluminal catheter-based dilation of intracranial arterial vasospasm with infusion of vasodilatory agent.

In Plain Language

Treating squeezed brain blood vessel

Clinical Context

Endovascular treatment for subarachnoid hemorrhage-induced vasospasm. May improve outcomes when medical management fails.
