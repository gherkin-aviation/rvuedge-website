47801 Placement bile duct support

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Stent placement during reconstruction, internal stent, hepatic duct stent

Placement of a supportive stent or tube within reconstructed bile ducts to maintain patency during healing and reduce stricture formation.

Clinical Context

Performed as adjunct to bile duct reconstruction to support healing, prevent leak, and maintain ductal diameter during tissue remodeling.
