28312 Revision of toe

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: toe revision, lesser toe revision, digit revision

Surgical revision or reoperation on lesser toes (second through fifth digits), including repair of complications from prior surgery such as malposition, stiffness, or chronic pain.

Clinical Context

Used for failed prior toe surgery on lesser toes, including malunion, recurrent deformity, contracture, or chronic pain requiring revision.
