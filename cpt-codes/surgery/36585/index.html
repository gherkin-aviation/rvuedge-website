36585 Replace picvad catheter

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: PICC port replacement, PICC-with-port replacement, peripheral port replacement

Replacement of peripherally inserted central catheter (PICC), with subcutaneous port; younger than 5 years of age.

Clinical Context

Used for replacement of non-functioning PICC-with-port systems in pediatric patients.
