46753 Reconstruction of anus

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: anal reconstruction, anoplasty with sphincter reconstruction, canal reconstruction

Reconstruction of anal canal and anal sphincter complex. Creates or recreates functional anal canal with sphincter restoration.

Clinical Context

Performed for severe anal canal or sphincter damage from trauma, cancer resection, or congenital defects.
