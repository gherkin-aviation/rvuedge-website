45900 Reduction of rectal prolapse

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: rectal prolapse reduction, manual prolapse reduction

Manual reduction of rectal prolapse without surgical intervention, performed in office or emergency setting.

Clinical Context

Conservative management of acute rectal prolapse via manual reduction, often effective in children or incomplete prolapse.
