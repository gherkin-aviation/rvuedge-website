46748 Repair of cloacal anomaly

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: complex cloacal repair, cloacal repair with urinary diversion, advanced cloacal reconstruction

Repair of cloacal anomaly with ureterosigmoidostomy or complex reconstruction. Manages complex cloacal anatomy including urinary diversion.

Clinical Context

Performed for very complex cloacal anomalies requiring urinary diversion or multiple visceral reconstructions.
