67343 Release eye tissue

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: adhesion release, scar tissue lysis, tenolysis

Release of adhesions or scar tissue in the extraocular muscles or orbital space to improve ocular motility and reduce contracture in restrictive strabismus.

Clinical Context

Performed for restricted eye movements due to surgery, trauma, or inflammation; releases constrictive tissue to restore normal motility.
