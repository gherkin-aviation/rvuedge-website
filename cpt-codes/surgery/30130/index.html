30130 Excise inferior turbinate

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: turbinate reduction, turbinectomy, turbinate ablation

Excision or ablation of the inferior turbinate, including partial or complete removal of turbinal tissue to enlarge the nasal airway.

Clinical Context

Indicated for hypertrophic inferior turbinates causing nasal obstruction unresponsive to medical management; improves nasal airflow and sleep quality.
