43830 Gstrst open wo constj tube

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: open gastrostomy, surgical G-tube placement, open PEG

Gastrostomy via open approach without concomitant jejunal tube placement for enteral nutrition access.

Clinical Context

Surgical creation of gastrocutaneous fistula for long-term enteral nutrition in patients unable to maintain oral intake. Used when endoscopic placement not possible.
