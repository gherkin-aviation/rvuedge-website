47382 Percut ablate liver rf

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: RF ablation, RFA, radiofrequency ablation

Percutaneous ablation of liver tumor using radiofrequency energy through the skin, typically performed for hepatocellular carcinoma or metastatic lesions.

In Plain Language

Burning out liver tumors with heat; Non-surgical heat treatment for liver cancer

Clinical Context

Used for patients with unresectable hepatocellular carcinoma, cirrhosis, or small hepatic metastases who are not candidates for surgical resection. Often performed under ultrasound or CT guidance.
