35526 Artery byp graft aor/carot/innom

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: aortic/carotid upper extremity bypass, proximal upper extremity bypass

Arterial bypass graft from aorta, carotid, or innominate artery with restoration of systemic upper extremity perfusion.

In Plain Language

Bypass from large vessel to arm artery; Major bypass for upper extremity

Clinical Context

Revascularization for complex upper extremity vascular disease from proximal sources. Used when more distal donor sites inadequate or when addressing multisegmental stenosis.
