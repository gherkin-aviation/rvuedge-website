21557 Resection neck thorax tumor<5cm

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: radical neck resection small, neck thorax tumor wide excision

Radical resection of neck and thorax tumor, less than 5 centimeters. Extensive surgical removal of neck and thorax mass with wide margins and regional node dissection, for lesion under 5 centimeters.

In Plain Language

extensive cancer surgery of neck and chest; wide removal of neck chest tumor

Clinical Context

Used for malignant neck and thoracic lesions requiring radical resection with adequate margins and lymph node clearance. Lesion size under 5 centimeters indicates earlier stage disease. Requires oncologic principles of wide margins.
