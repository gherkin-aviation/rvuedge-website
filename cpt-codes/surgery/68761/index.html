68761 Close tear duct opening

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: canalicular closure, canalicular obliteration

Closure or obliteration of lacrimal canaliculus using surgical technique to eliminate tear drainage opening.

In Plain Language

closing tear duct permanently; blocking tear drainage duct

Clinical Context

Used for permanent closure of tear drainage when needed. Common indications include severe dry eye unresponsive to punctal plugs, or permanent obliteration.
