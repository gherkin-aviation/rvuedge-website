22319 Treat odontoid fracture with graft

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: odontoid fracture ORIF with graft, dens fracture with bone reconstruction

Open treatment of odontoid process fracture with bone graft. Surgical reduction and fixation of odontoid fractures with supplemental bone graft material for biological healing support.

Clinical Context

Used for odontoid fractures where bone graft enhances fusion potential. Graft harvesting and placement increase operative complexity. Indicated for comminuted fractures or when additional biologics needed.
