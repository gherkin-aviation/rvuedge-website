31651 Bronchial valve additional insert

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: additional valve placement, supplemental valve insertion

Add-on code for each additional endobronchial valve placed beyond the initial valve during the same bronchoscopy procedure for multi-segment treatment.

Clinical Context

Used when multiple valves are placed in different lung segments during one procedure to optimize therapeutic benefit in emphysema.
