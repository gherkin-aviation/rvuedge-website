21725 Revision of neck muscle

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: extensive neck muscle revision, complex neck reconstruction revision

Revision of neck muscle procedure with extensive reconstruction. Comprehensive surgical revision of neck musculature with complex reconstruction requiring flaps, transfers, or advanced techniques.

Clinical Context

Used for complex revision of neck muscle procedures with significant deformity or functional deficit requiring extensive reconstruction with flaps or muscle transfers.
