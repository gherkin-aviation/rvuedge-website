49465 Fluoroscopy examination of g/colon tube

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: tube study, fluoroscopic tube exam, tube fluoroscopy

Fluoroscopic examination of gastrostomy or colostomy tube position, patency, and function.

Clinical Context

Fluoroscopic imaging to assess gastrostomy or colostomy tube position, function, and integrity. Used to evaluate for malposition, obstruction, or leakage.
