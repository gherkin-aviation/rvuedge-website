22318 Treat odontoid fracture without graft

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: odontoid fracture open reduction, dens fracture ORIF

Open treatment of odontoid process fracture without bone graft. Surgical reduction and fixation of odontoid (dens) fractures, which are fractures of the second cervical vertebra.

Clinical Context

Used for displaced odontoid fractures requiring surgical fixation for stability. Anterior or posterior screw fixation typical. Without bone graft indicates simple reduction achievable without supplemental graft material.
