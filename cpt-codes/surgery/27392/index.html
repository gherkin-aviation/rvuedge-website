27392 Incision of thigh tendons

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: complex polytenotomy, multiple tendon procedure

Incision of multiple thigh tendons with additional procedures such as lengthening and repositioning.

In Plain Language

Multiple tendon surgery; Complex tendon adjustment

Clinical Context

Indicated for severe contractures or spasticity requiring extensive tendon work beyond simple division.
