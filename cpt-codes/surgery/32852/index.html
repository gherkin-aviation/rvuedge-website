32852 Lung transplant with bypass

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: double lung transplant with bypass, bilateral lung transplant with CPB, DLTx with bypass

Double lung transplantation with cardiopulmonary bypass support. Surgical placement of two donor lungs into a recipient with end-stage pulmonary disease using extracorporeal circulation.

Clinical Context

Performed in critically ill transplant candidates requiring hemodynamic support during the procedure or in those with complex anatomy. Provides better long-term outcomes than single lung.
