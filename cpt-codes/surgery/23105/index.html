23105 Arthrotomy glenohumeral joint

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Shoulder arthrotomy with synovectomy, GH joint synovectomy

Arthrotomy, glenohumeral joint, including synovectomy, without biopsy.

Clinical Context

Used for inflammatory shoulder arthritis; synovectomy removes inflamed synovium and improves pain.
