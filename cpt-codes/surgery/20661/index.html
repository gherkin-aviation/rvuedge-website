20661 Application halo cranial

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: halo fixation, halo vest, cranial halo

Application of a halo device with cranial pins for cervical spine immobilization.

In Plain Language

rigid ring with pins around head to stabilize neck; halo brace with skull pins

Clinical Context

Used for cervical spine fractures, instability, and post-operative immobilization; provides external immobilization with moderate restriction of motion.
