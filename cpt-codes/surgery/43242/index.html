43242 Egd ultrasound fine needle biopsy/aspiration

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: EUS-guided FNA, endoscopic ultrasound biopsy

Esophagogastroduodenoscopy (EGD) with endoscopic ultrasound and fine needle aspiration/biopsy of perigastric or pancreatic tissue structures.

In Plain Language

camera examination with ultrasound and needle sampling of deep structures

Clinical Context

Diagnostic procedure for tissue sampling of perigastric and pancreatic lesions under ultrasound guidance. Important for pancreatic cancer diagnosis.
