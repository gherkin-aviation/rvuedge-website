21395 Opn treatment orbit periorbt with graft

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: orbital fracture with graft, orbital reconstruction with bone

Open treatment of orbital fracture with periorbital bone graft insertion for reconstruction and structural restoration of orbital walls.

Clinical Context

Used for orbital fractures with bone loss requiring bone graft reconstruction for restored bony anatomy.
