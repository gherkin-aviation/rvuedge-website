35879 Revise graft with vein

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: graft revision with vein, vein patch revision, interposition graft revision

Revision of lower extremity bypass graft with autogenous vein using interposition vein patch or segment replacement. Addresses graft stenosis or failure.

Clinical Context

Performed for femoral graft stenosis, intimal hyperplasia, or degeneration using saphenous vein.
