15879 Suction lipectomy lower extremity

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: leg lipo, thigh liposuction, lower extremity lipo

Suction-assisted lipectomy of the lower extremities including thighs, calves, and ankles to reduce excessive subcutaneous adipose tissue.

Clinical Context

Performed for cosmetic body contouring of hips, thighs, and calves. Commonly done in post-bariatric patients and for cosmetic leg enhancement.
