67916 Repair eyelid defect

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: eyelid repair with graft, eyelid flap repair

Repair of eyelid defect not involving the margin using flap or graft technique for reconstruction of larger defect.

Clinical Context

Used for eyelid defects away from the margin that are too large for primary closure. Common indications include defects from lesion removal, burn injuries, or large traumatic defects of the eyelid body.
