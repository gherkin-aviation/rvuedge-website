27650 Repair achilles tendon

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: achilles repair, achilles tendon repair

Surgical repair of achilles tendon rupture with primary end-to-end repair or simple augmentation.

Clinical Context

Indicated for acute achilles tendon rupture. Performed to restore plantarflexion strength. Higher work RVU vs. other tendon repairs due to functional importance.
