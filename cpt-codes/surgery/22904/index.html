22904 Radical resection abdominal tumor<5cm

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Radical tumor resection, Wide local excision

Radical resection of tumor (eg, fibrosarcoma), abdominal wall, less than 5 centimeters.

Clinical Context

Used for malignant tumors of abdominal wall requiring radical resection with wide margins; less than 5 cm tumors.
