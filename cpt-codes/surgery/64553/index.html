64553 Implant neuroelectrodes

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: spinal cord stimulator implant, peripheral nerve stimulator implant, neuropathic pain implant

Open surgical implantation of neuroelectrodes for spinal cord stimulation or peripheral nerve stimulation with full operative approach.

In Plain Language

nerve pain electrical device surgery; spinal cord stimulator placement

Clinical Context

Used for chronic pain, post-laminectomy syndrome, and peripheral neuropathy. Provides long-term electrical stimulation for pain relief.
