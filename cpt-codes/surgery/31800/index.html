31800 Repair of windpipe injury

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: tracheal injury repair, tracheal primary repair

Surgical repair of injury to the trachea (windpipe), performed to restore structural integrity and function following traumatic or iatrogenic damage with straightforward primary closure or simple repair techniques.

In Plain Language

windpipe injury repair; tracheal trauma repair

Clinical Context

Used for acute traumatic tracheal injuries, post-intubation perforations, or iatrogenic tracheal defects requiring emergent or urgent surgical intervention.
