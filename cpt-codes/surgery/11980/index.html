11980 Implant hormone pellet(s)

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: Hormone pellet implant, Pellet therapy

Implantation of hormone pellet(s) beneath the skin for delivery of hormone replacement therapy or other hormonal treatment.

Clinical Context

Used for hormone replacement therapy in menopausal women or for testosterone replacement therapy. Pellets release hormones gradually over several months.
