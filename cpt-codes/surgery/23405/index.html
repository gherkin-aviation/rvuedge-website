23405 Incision of tendon & muscle

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: shoulder incision and drainage, shoulder I and D, shoulder abscess drainage

Incision and drainage (exploration) of shoulder and arm, including deep structures (muscles and fascia). Surgical opening of shoulder region for drainage of abscess or hematoma.

Clinical Context

Performed for septic arthritis, shoulder abscess, hematoma, or post-operative infection requiring surgical drainage; allows irrigation and removal of purulent material.
