64910 Nerve repair with allograft

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: nerve repair allograft, processed nerve graft

Surgical repair of a nerve injury using processed nerve allograft (cadaveric nerve) to bridge a nerve gap where autografting not possible or practical.

Clinical Context

Used when autografting not feasible due to insufficient donor nerve, extensive injuries requiring large grafts, or surgeon preference. Reduces donor site morbidity.
