31552 Laryngoplasty laryngeal sten

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: laryngoplasty with approach, laryngeal reconstruction technique, stenosis repair variant

Laryngoplasty for treatment of laryngeal stenosis using a specific surgical approach or material technique for restoration of normal laryngeal anatomy and airway patency.

Clinical Context

Surgical reconstruction for laryngeal stenosis with specific technical variation distinct from 31551. May involve different grafting materials, approaches, or reconstruction methods.
