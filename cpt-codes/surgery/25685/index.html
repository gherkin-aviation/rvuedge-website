25685 Optx trns-scphprlnr fracture dislc

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ORIF perilunate injury, open reduction internal fixation trans-scaphoid

Open treatment (surgical repair) of trans-scaphoid and perilunate fracture-dislocation with internal fixation.

Clinical Context

Used for trans-scaphoid perilunate fracture-dislocations requiring operative intervention. Often involves reduction and fixation of both fracture and dislocation components.
