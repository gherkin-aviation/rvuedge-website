57513 Laser surgery of cervix

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: laser cervical ablation, CO2 laser cervix, laser vaporization

Laser surgery of cervix uses laser energy to vaporize or excise abnormal cervical tissue with precision and minimal bleeding.

Clinical Context

Used for cervical dysplasia, condyloma acuminatum, and cervical polyps. Provides excellent hemostasis and allows simultaneous treatment and visualization.
