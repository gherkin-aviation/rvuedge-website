21385 Opn treatment orbit fracture transantral

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: orbital fracture transantral, transantral orbitotomy

Open treatment of orbital fracture via transantral approach, accessing the orbit through the maxillary sinus for reduction and fixation.

Clinical Context

Used for orbital floor or medial wall fractures accessible via transantral approach. Preserves external facial incisions.
