64417 Njx aa and/or strd ax nerve img

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: axillary nerve block, axillary nerve injection

Injection of anesthetic and/or steroid into axillary nerve with imaging guidance. This procedure delivers medication to the axillary nerve under ultrasound or fluoroscopic visualization.

In Plain Language

pain relief injection for shoulder nerve

Clinical Context
