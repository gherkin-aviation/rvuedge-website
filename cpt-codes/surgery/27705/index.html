27705 Osteotomy tibia

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: tibial osteotomy, tibia realignment

Surgical cutting and realignment of tibial bone (osteotomy) for angular deformity correction or biomechanical improvement.

Clinical Context

Used for valgus/varus deformity, post-traumatic deformity, or to offload arthritic compartments. Restores mechanical alignment.
