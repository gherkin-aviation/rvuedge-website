32905 Revise & repair chest wall

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: chest wall revision, chest wall repair, chest wall reconstruction

Revision and repair of the chest wall, addressing deformity, instability, or tissue defects. Includes reconstruction of chest wall components.

In Plain Language

repair of chest wall deformity or damage

Clinical Context

Used to treat pectus deformities, previous surgical complications, trauma, or radiation changes affecting chest wall integrity and appearance.
