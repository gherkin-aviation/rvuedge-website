43279 Laparoscopic myotomy heller

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: lap Heller myotomy, laparoscopic myotomy, POEM alternative

Laparoscopic Heller myotomy with partial anterior fundoplication for treatment of esophageal achalasia. This minimally invasive surgical procedure divides the circular muscle of the lower esophageal sphincter.

In Plain Language

cutting muscle at bottom of esophagus; minimally invasive myotomy

Clinical Context

Gold standard surgical treatment for achalasia providing durable symptom relief; laparoscopic approach reduces morbidity compared to open surgery.
