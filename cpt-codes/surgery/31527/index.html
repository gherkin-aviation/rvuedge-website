31527 Laryngoscopy for treatment

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: therapeutic laryngoscopy, treatment laryngoscopy, laryngeal treatment

Laryngoscopic procedure with therapeutic intervention or treatment performed on the larynx and vocal cords beyond simple visualization, excluding specific modalities listed separately.

Clinical Context

Umbrella code for laryngeal treatment procedures including scar release, vocal cord mobilization, or other therapeutic interventions not specified by other codes.
