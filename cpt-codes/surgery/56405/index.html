56405 incision and drainage vulva/perineal abscess

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: Vulvar abscess drainage, Perineal abscess incision

Incision and drainage of an abscess on the vulva or perineal area. Provides immediate relief and source control of infection.

Clinical Context

Used for acute vulvar abscesses, often from blocked Bartholin glands, hair follicles, or epidermal inclusion cysts. Provides immediate symptom relief and allows culture and sensitivity testing.
