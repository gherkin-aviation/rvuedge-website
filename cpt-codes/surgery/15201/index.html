15201 Fth/gft fr trnk each additional

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: full-thickness graft additional, pedicle graft add-on

Full-thickness or pedicle skin graft or flap, from trunk, each additional 20 square centimeters. Add-on code for extended full-thickness coverage from torso.

In Plain Language

additional thick skin graft from body; extra full-thickness coverage

Clinical Context

Used when initial trunk-sourced full-thickness graft is insufficient for complete coverage of larger defects.
