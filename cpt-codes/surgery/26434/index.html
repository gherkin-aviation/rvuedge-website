26434 Repair/graft finger tendon

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Finger tendon repair with graft, Finger tendon graft, Grafted repair

Surgical repair and grafting of a finger tendon with graft material to augment repair, bridge defects, or restore adequate length for functional restoration.

Clinical Context

Indicated for finger tendon lacerations with tissue loss, delayed repair with infection, or when primary repair tension would compromise function.
