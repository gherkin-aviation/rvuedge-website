34846 Visc & infraren abdominal 2 prosthesis

Surgery

Also known as: visceral and infrarenal EVAR 2 grafts, combined EVAR dual graft

Endovascular repair of visceral and infrarenal abdominal aorta with 2 fenestrated prostheses. Combined repair with dual fenestration.

Clinical Context

Used for extensive aortic disease with two major branch vessel involvements spanning visceral and infrarenal levels.
