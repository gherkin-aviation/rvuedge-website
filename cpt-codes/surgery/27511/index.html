27511 Treatment of thigh fracture

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: intramedullary nailing, IMN, proximal femoral nail

Open reduction and internal fixation of an intertrochanteric femoral fracture with intramedullary nail or rod, providing load-sharing fixation.

Clinical Context

Used for intertrochanteric and subtrochanteric femoral fractures. Provides good fixation and allows early weight-bearing.
