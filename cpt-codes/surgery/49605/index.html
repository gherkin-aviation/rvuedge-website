49605 Repair umbilical lesion

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: umbilical hernia repair with mesh, large belly button hernia repair, umbilical hernia reconstruction

Repair of large umbilical hernia with mesh or fascia reinforcement.

Clinical Context

Surgical repair of large umbilical hernia requiring mesh or fascia graft for reinforcement. Used for wide defects or adult umbilical hernias.
