58660 Laparoscopy lysis

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: laparoscopic adhesiolysis, lap adhesiolysis

Laparoscopy with lysis of periadnexal adhesions involves endoscopic division of scarring around ovaries and fallopian tubes to restore normal anatomy.

In Plain Language

Camera scar removal around ovaries; Minimally invasive adhesion removal

Clinical Context

Treats adhesions from prior surgery or endometriosis causing infertility or pain. Laparoscopic approach allows careful adhesiolysis under visualization.
