32960 Therapeutic pneumothorax

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: artificial pneumothorax, therapeutic collapse, pneumothorax induction

Therapeutic pneumothorax, the surgical instillation of air or gas into the pleural space to collapse the lung. Used historically for tuberculosis treatment.

Clinical Context

Historically used for tuberculosis therapy; rarely performed now due to effective antimicrobial agents. May still be used in select chronic empyema or difficult-to-manage pleural infections.
