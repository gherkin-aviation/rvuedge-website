46746 Repair of cloacal anomaly

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: complex cloacal repair, cloacal reconstruction with vaginoplasty, revised cloacal repair

Repair of cloacal anomaly with revision/mobilization. Complex cloacal repair with vaginoplasty or ureteroplasty component.

In Plain Language

major surgery to fix cloaca with additional reconstruction; cloacal repair with vaginoplasty

Clinical Context

Performed for complex cloacal anomalies requiring additional vaginoplasty, ureteroplasty, or revision of previous repairs.
