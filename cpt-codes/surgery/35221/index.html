35221 repair bld vsl dir intra-abdl

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: intra-abdominal vessel repair, visceral vessel repair

Repair of blood vessel injury in the intra-abdominal region using direct anastomotic reconstruction.

Clinical Context

Surgical management of abdominal vascular injuries from penetrating or blunt trauma. Direct repair appropriate for clean wounds; complex reconstruction may be needed for major aortic or visceral vessel involvement.
