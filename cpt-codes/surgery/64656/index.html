64656 revision/replacement bat mod sys pulse generator

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: neurostimulation generator revision, battery-powered modulator generator replacement

Revision or replacement of the pulse generator (battery pack) component of an implanted battery-powered modulator system.

In Plain Language

neuromodulation battery replacement surgery

Clinical Context

Used when pulse generator battery depletes or malfunctions, usually after 5-7 years.
