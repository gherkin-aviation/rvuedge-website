35261 repair blvsl grf oth/thn vn nck

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: neck prosthetic graft repair, carotid synthetic graft

Repair of blood vessel injury in the neck region using other than vein graft (prosthetic or arterial graft) reconstruction.

Clinical Context

Reconstruction of neck vascular injuries when venous graft not available or appropriate. Prosthetic grafts have higher infection and restenosis rates but may be necessary in contaminated wounds or emergent scenarios.
