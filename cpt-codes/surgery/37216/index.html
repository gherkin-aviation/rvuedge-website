37216 Transcath stent cca without eps

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: carotid stent without EPS, carotid stent no protection, non-protected carotid stent

Transcatheter placement of a stent in the carotid artery (cervical or intracranial) without embolic protection system during the procedure.

Clinical Context

Used in lower-risk patients or when embolic protection contraindicated. Still effective for significant symptomatic or asymptomatic carotid stenosis.
