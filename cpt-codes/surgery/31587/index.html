31587 Laryngoplasty cricoid split

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: cricoid split, laryngeal cricoid split, subglottic dilation

Laryngoplasty for treatment of laryngeal stenosis using cricoid split technique, which involves dividing the cricoid cartilage and inserting graft material to increase subglottic airway diameter.

In Plain Language

splitting and widening the voice box

Clinical Context

Surgical procedure for congenital or acquired subglottic stenosis, particularly in pediatric patients. Often combined with tracheostomy as first-line treatment for moderate stenosis.
