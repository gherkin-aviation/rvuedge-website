35642 Bpg carotid-vertebral

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: carotid-vertebral bypass, carotid-to-vertebral bypass, vertebral artery revascularization

Arterial bypass graft from carotid artery to vertebral artery for vertebral artery stenosis or occlusion causing posterior circulation insufficiency. Restores vertebrobasilar flow.

Clinical Context

Used for symptomatic vertebral artery stenosis or occlusion causing vertebrobasilar insufficiency. Less common with endovascular options.
