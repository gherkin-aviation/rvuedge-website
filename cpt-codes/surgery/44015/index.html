44015 Insert needle catheter bowel

Surgery

Also known as: needle catheter enterostomy, catheter placement in bowel, tube enterostomy

Insertion of needle catheter or tube into small bowel via enterotomy for decompression, feeding, or therapeutic access.

In Plain Language

small tube placement in intestine; bowel access via catheter

Clinical Context

Placement of needle catheter or feeding tube into small bowel via surgical incision. Provides access for feeding or decompression in patients unable to tolerate standard feeding.
