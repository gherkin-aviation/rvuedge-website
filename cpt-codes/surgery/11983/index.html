11983 Remove/insert drug implant

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: Implant replacement, Implant exchange

Removal and reinsertion or replacement of a subcutaneous drug delivery implant device in a single procedure.

Clinical Context

Used for replacement of expired or malfunctioning drug delivery implants with new devices in a single procedure.
