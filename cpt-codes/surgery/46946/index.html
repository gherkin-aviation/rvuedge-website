Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: multiple hemorrhoid banding, RBL multiple groups, ligation multiple hemorrhoids

Ligation of internal hemorrhoids (2 or more groups) without imaging guidance. Rubber band ligation for multiple hemorrhoid sites.

Clinical Context

Performed for bleeding internal hemorrhoids in 2 or more anatomic locations. May require multiple sessions.
