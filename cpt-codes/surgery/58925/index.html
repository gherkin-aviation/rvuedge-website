58925 Removal of ovarian cyst(s)

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ovarian cystectomy, cyst removal, ovarian cyst excision

Removal of ovarian cyst(s) with en bloc removal of the cystic lesion and a rim of normal ovarian tissue, preserving residual ovarian parenchyma.

Clinical Context

Standard treatment for benign ovarian cysts (endometriomas, mature teratomas) that are symptomatic or large in reproductive-aged women.
