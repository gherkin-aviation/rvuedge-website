24357 Repair elbow percutaneous

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: percutaneous elbow repair, mini-open elbow repair

Repair of elbow, percutaneous approach. Minimally invasive repair of elbow injuries through small puncture wounds.

In Plain Language

Surgical repair of the elbow through small holes; Minimally invasive elbow repair

Clinical Context

Used for repair of select elbow injuries using percutaneous or mini-open technique, reducing soft tissue trauma compared to open approaches.
