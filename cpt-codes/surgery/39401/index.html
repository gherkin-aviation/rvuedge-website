39401 Mediastinoscpy with medstnl biopsy

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: mediastinoscopy, mediastinal scope

Mediastinoscopy with biopsy of mediastinal tissue or mass. This minimally invasive endoscopic procedure allows visualization and tissue sampling of mediastinal structures.

Clinical Context

Used for staging lung cancer, evaluating mediastinal masses, or assessing lymph node involvement. Less invasive than thoracotomy, allows for sampling without large incision.
