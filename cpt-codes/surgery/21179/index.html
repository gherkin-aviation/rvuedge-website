21179 Rcnstj forehead with grafts

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: forehead graft reconstruction, forehead grafting

Reconstruction of the forehead region using bone graft material (allograft or xenograft) for contouring and structural support.

Clinical Context

Used for forehead defects, significant bone loss, or contour irregularities. Provides structural support and aesthetic improvement.
