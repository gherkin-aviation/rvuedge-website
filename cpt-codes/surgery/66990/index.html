66990 Ophthalmic endoscope add-on

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: endoscope-assisted cataract surgery, endoscopic visualization

Add-on code for use of ophthalmic endoscope during anterior segment surgery to improve visualization and precision during intraocular procedures.

In Plain Language

camera-assisted eye surgery; improved visualization during surgery

Clinical Context

Added to any anterior segment procedure where endoscope improves visualization; particularly useful in dense cataracts, capsular compromise, or guidance of IOL placement.
