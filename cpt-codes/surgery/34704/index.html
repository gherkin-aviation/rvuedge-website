34704 Evasc repair a-unilac ndgft report

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: repeat unilateral iliac EVAR, aortouniiliac EVAR redo

Endovascular repair of abdominal aorta with unilateral iliac involvement without graft (repeat procedure). Percutaneous redo repair of aortouniiliac disease.

Clinical Context

Performed for complications or failure of previous aortouniiliac repair. May involve component exchange or extension.
