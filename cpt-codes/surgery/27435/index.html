27435 Incision of knee joint

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: knee arthrotomy, knee incision, open knee arthroscopy

Surgical incision or arthrotomy of the knee joint to expose joint structures for inspection, removal of loose bodies, debridement, or other intra-articular procedures.

Clinical Context

Used for loose body removal, intra-articular fractures, complex ligament repairs requiring open exposure, or synovial pathology.
