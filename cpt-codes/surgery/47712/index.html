47712 Excision of bile duct tumor

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Complex bile duct cancer resection, hilar cholangiocarcinoma resection

Surgical excision of a bile duct tumor with complex hepatic resection, multiple reconstructions, or en bloc removal of adjacent structures for advanced disease.

In Plain Language

Removing bile duct cancer with surrounding tissue

Clinical Context

Performed for hilar (Klatskin) tumors or locally advanced cholangiocarcinoma requiring hepatic resection and vascular reconstruction.
