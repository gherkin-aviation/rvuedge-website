37615 Ligation major artery neck

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Major neck artery ligation

Surgical ligation of a major artery in the neck region, used for hemorrhage control or management of vascular lesions.

In Plain Language

Tying main neck vessel; Neck artery surgery

Clinical Context

Performed for severe uncontrollable epistaxis, carotid blowout syndrome, or large neck vessel hemorrhage following trauma or surgery.
