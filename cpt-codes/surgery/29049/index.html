29049 application cast figure-of-eight

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: figure-of-eight cast, shoulder harness, clavicle support

Application of figure-of-eight cast or harness for shoulder and clavicle stabilization, immobilizing the shoulder complex and maintaining scapular positioning.

In Plain Language

Shoulder figure cast; Clavicle cast; Shoulder harness

Clinical Context

Used for clavicle fractures, acromioclavicular joint injury, or shoulder stabilization; figure-of-eight maintains proper shoulder alignment.
