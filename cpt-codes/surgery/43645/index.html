43645 Laparoscopic gastric bypass incl smll i

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: lap Roux-en-Y bypass, laparoscopic intestinal rerouting, revised bypass, lap gastric bypass complex

Laparoscopic gastric bypass with small intestine reconstruction, including revision of prior gastric bypass.

In Plain Language

laparoscopic weight loss surgery with bowel rearrangement; intestinal bypass revision

Clinical Context

Used for primary or revision bariatric cases with small intestinal involvement. May include revision of inadequate weight loss or complications from prior bypass.
