58290 Vag hyst complex

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: complex VH, difficult vaginal hysterectomy

Complex vaginal hysterectomy addresses challenging cases requiring additional time and skill to safely remove uterus vaginally with significant pathology.

Clinical Context

Used for dense adhesions, severe endometriosis, large fibroids accessible vaginally, or anatomic anomalies making vaginal hysterectomy technically challenging.
