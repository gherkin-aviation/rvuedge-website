29901 Mcp joint arthroscopy surgical

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: MCP arthroscopy with surgery, finger joint repair, digital joint surgery

Arthroscopic surgical treatment of the metacarpophalangeal joint with therapeutic intervention including debridement, loose body removal, or chondral repair.

Clinical Context

Indicated for osteoarthritis, loose bodies, synovitis, chondral lesions, or capsular injuries of the MCP joint causing pain or functional limitations.
