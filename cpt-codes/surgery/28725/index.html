28725 Arthrodesis subtalar

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: subtalar fusion, subtalar arthrodesis, talocalcaneal fusion

Surgical arthrodesis (fusion) of the subtalar joint, permanently fusing the talus to the calcaneus to treat subtalar joint arthritis, instability, or hindfoot varus deformity.

Clinical Context

Used for subtalar joint arthritis, chronic subtalar instability, or hindfoot varus deformity; provides pain relief and stability.
