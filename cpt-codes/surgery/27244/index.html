27244 Treat thigh fracture

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: IM nailing, intramedullary rod, femur nailing

Open treatment of femoral shaft fracture with intramedullary nailing for load-bearing stabilization and early mobilization.

Clinical Context

Preferred method for displaced femoral shaft fractures allowing early weight-bearing and mobilization with minimal hardware prominence.
