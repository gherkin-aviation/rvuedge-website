42335 Removal of salivary stone

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: sialolith removal, stone extraction

Surgical removal of a salivary stone with more complex extraction or repair of damaged ductal tissue from the salivary gland system.

Clinical Context

Extraction of salivary stone with associated duct dilation, duct repair, or more complex surgical access required.
