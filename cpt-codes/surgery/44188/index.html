44188 Laparoscopic colostomy

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: lap colostomy, laparoscopic stoma creation, lap fecal diversion

Laparoscopic creation of colostomy (colonic stoma) for fecal diversion.

Clinical Context

Laparoscopic creation of colonic stoma for fecal diversion in patients with colon cancer, inflammatory bowel disease, or bowel dysfunction.
