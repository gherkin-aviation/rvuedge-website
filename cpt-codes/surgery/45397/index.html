45397 Laparoscopic remove rectum with pouch

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: laparoscopic IPAA, lap total proctectomy with pouch, minimally invasive J-pouch

Laparoscopic proctectomy with construction of ileal pouch-anal anastomosis (IPAA) for sphincter preservation.

In Plain Language

camera-assisted rectum removal with pouch creation; minimally invasive pouch surgery

Clinical Context

Performed for ulcerative colitis or familial adenomatous polyposis using laparoscopic approach with ileoanal pouch reconstruction.
