51798 Ultrasound urine capacity measure

Surgery

Also known as: bladder ultrasound volume, post-void residual ultrasound, PVR ultrasound

Ultrasound assessment of urine capacity measurement, non-invasive ultrasound determination of bladder volume and capacity.

Clinical Context

Non-invasive assessment of post-void residual urine or bladder capacity using ultrasound, avoiding catheterization.
