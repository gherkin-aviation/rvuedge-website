43116 Partial removal of esophagus

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: partial esophagectomy, esophageal resection

Partial esophagectomy (removal of a portion of the esophagus) via abdominal approach without reconstruction.

In Plain Language

removal of part of the esophagus

Clinical Context

Performed for esophageal lesions or strictures that can be managed with partial rather than total resection. Less morbidity than total esophagectomy.

Billing & Documentation

As a surgical CPT code, proper documentation must include the operative report detailing the procedure performed, patient positioning, approach, findings, and any complications. This code has a 90-day global period, which includes the day of the procedure, 1 day preoperative, and 90 days of postoperative care.
