43116 Partial removal of esophagus

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: partial esophagectomy, esophageal resection

Partial esophagectomy (removal of a portion of the esophagus) via abdominal approach without reconstruction.

Clinical Context

Performed for esophageal lesions or strictures that can be managed with partial rather than total resection. Less morbidity than total esophagectomy.
