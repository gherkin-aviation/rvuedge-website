50520 Close kidney-skin fistula

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: kidney-skin fistula closure, fistula repair, fistula closure

Surgical closure of a fistula (abnormal tract) between the kidney and skin.

In Plain Language

surgical closure of abnormal kidney to skin opening; fistula closure

Clinical Context

Usually result of chronic pyelonephritis, renal infarction, or iatrogenic injury. Requires control of underlying obstruction.
