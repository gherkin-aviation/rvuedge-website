29914 Hip arthroscopic with femoroplasty

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: hip scope with femoroplasty, CAM impingement repair, femoral neck decompression

Arthroscopic femoroplasty of the hip, including arthroscopic reshaping of the femoral head and neck junction to address cam-type femoroacetabular impingement.

Clinical Context

Indicated for symptomatic cam-type femoroacetabular impingement causing hip pain, limited motion, and risk of labral damage or early osteoarthritis.
