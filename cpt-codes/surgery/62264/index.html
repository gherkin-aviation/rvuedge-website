62264 Epidural lysis on single day

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: single session epidural lysis, same-day adhesion lysis

Epidural lysis of adhesions in spinal canal performed on a single day. Includes administration of lytic agents.

Clinical Context

Used for treatment of epidural scar tissue causing spinal cord compression in one session.
