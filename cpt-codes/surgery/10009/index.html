10009 Fna biopsy with CT gdn first lesion

Surgery

Also known as: FNA with CT guidance, CT-guided FNA

Fine needle aspiration biopsy with computed tomography guidance of first lesion. This percutaneous sampling procedure uses imaging to ensure accurate needle placement into a target lesion.

In Plain Language

Needle biopsy with CT scan guidance; CT-guided tissue sample

Clinical Context

Used for sampling suspicious lesions in the lungs, liver, kidney, or other organs that require precise imaging guidance. Common indications include evaluation of nodules, masses, or focal abnormalities seen on computed tomography.
