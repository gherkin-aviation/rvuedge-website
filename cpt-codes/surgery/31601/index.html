31601 Planned tracheostomy<2 yrs

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: infant tracheostomy, pediatric tracheostomy, neonatal trache

Planned tracheostomy in an infant or young child under age 2 years, creating airway access using pediatric-specific techniques and equipment tailored to smaller anatomy.

Clinical Context

Performed electively in infants and young children requiring long-term airway access for congenital airway anomalies, ventilator dependency, or severe airway obstruction.
