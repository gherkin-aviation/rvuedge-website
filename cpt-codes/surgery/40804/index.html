40804 Removal foreign body mouth

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: foreign body removal, FB extraction

Removal of foreign body from the mouth. Extraction of lodged material from oral cavity.

Clinical Context

Used for removal of food impactions, dental work, or other objects lodged in the mouth. May require local anesthesia or minimal instrumentation.
