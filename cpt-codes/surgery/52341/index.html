52341 Cysto with ureter stricture treatment

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: cysto with ureteral stricture treatment, intramural stricture management

Cystourethroscopy with incision, resection, or dilation of ureteral stricture at the intramural segment, restoring normal ureteral caliber and function.

Clinical Context

Used for strictures from prior surgery, radiation, or endometriosis. Endoscopic treatment successful in selected patients.
