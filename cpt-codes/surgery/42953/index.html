42953 Repair throat esophagus

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: pharyngoesophageal fistula repair, throat-esophagus repair

Repair (anastomosis or primary closure) of a pharyngeal to esophageal fistula or disruption of the pharyngeal-esophageal junction.

In Plain Language

repair of abnormal connection between throat and esophagus; throat-esophagus fistula surgery

Clinical Context

Indicated for iatrogenic or traumatic pharyngeal-esophageal fistulae, often complication of cancer surgery or severe reflux. Requires careful reconstruction to restore integrity.
