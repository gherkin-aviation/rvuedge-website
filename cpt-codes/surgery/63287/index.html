63287 biopsy/excision idrl imed lesion thrlmb

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: thoracolumbar intramedullary tumor removal, thoracolumbar syrinx or tumor biopsy

Biopsy and/or excision of intradural intramedullary spinal lesion in the thoracolumbar region. This procedure involves sampling or removal of lesions within the spinal cord at the thoracolumbar junction.

Clinical Context

Used for intramedullary tumors at the thoracolumbar junction.
