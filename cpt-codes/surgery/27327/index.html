27327 excision thigh/knee lesion sternoclavicular < 3 centimeters

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: soft tissue excision small, lesion excision <3cm

Excision of benign soft tissue lesion of thigh or knee measuring less than 3 centimeters.

Clinical Context

Applied to small benign cysts, lipomas, or superficial lesions in thigh or knee region.
