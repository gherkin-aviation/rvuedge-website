43771 Laparoscopic revise gastric adj device

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: lap band revision, gastric band tightening, band adjustment

Laparoscopic revision or manipulation of previously placed gastric restriction device (gastric band) including repositioning or tightening.

Clinical Context

Operative revision of lap-band for inadequate weight loss or complications. May involve tightening, loosening, or repositioning of band around stomach.
