23800 Arthrodesis glenohumeral joint

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: shoulder arthrodesis, shoulder fusion, glenohumeral arthrodesis

Arthrodesis of glenohumeral joint (shoulder fusion). Surgical fusion of shoulder joint via direct arthrotomy without graft material.

Clinical Context

Applied in severe glenohumeral arthritis, failed arthroplasty, or chronic instability in selected patients (often laborers) where function is prioritized over motion.
