53850 Prostatic microwave thermotx

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: transurethral microwave thermotherapy, microwave thermotherapy

Transurethral destruction of prostate tissue using microwave energy to reduce obstructive symptoms.

Clinical Context

Used for benign prostatic hyperplasia causing obstructive lower urinary tract symptoms. Minimally invasive alternative to transurethral resection of prostate.
