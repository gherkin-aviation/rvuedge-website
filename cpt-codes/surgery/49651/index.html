49651 Laparoscopic ing hernia repair recur

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: laparoscopic recurrent hernia repair, minimally invasive hernia rerepair, lap hernia rerepair

Laparoscopic repair of recurrent inguinal hernia with mesh reinforcement.

Clinical Context

Laparoscopic repair of recurrent inguinal hernia. Good option when open repair failed or to avoid scar tissue.
