61889 Ins sk-mnt crnl nstm pulse generator/rcvr

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: skin-mounted pulse generator, transcutaneous receiver

Insertion or replacement of skin-mounted cranial neurostimulation pulse generator or receiver outside skull.

Clinical Context

Implantation of neurostimulation device in subcutaneous pocket in scalp or skull region.
