27734 Repair lower leg epiphyses

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: bilateral epiphysis repair, both bones physeal repair

Surgical repair of epiphyseal injuries in both tibia and fibula to restore growth plate alignment and function.

Clinical Context

Indicated for physeal injuries affecting both tibia and fibula. Prevention of angular and length deformities is critical.
