64581 Opn impltj neuroelectrode array sacral nerve

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: sacral nerve stimulator implant, sacral neuromodulation implant, sacral stimulator

Open surgical implantation of a neuroelectrode near the sacral nerve roots for long-term electrical stimulation.

Clinical Context

Used for overactive bladder, urge incontinence, urinary retention, and fecal incontinence.
