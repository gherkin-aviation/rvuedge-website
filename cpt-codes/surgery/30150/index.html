30150 Rhinectomy partial

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: partial rhinectomy, partial nose removal, partial nasal resection

Partial rhinectomy, including partial removal or resection of nasal bone and soft tissue, performed to remove tissue involvement in trauma, infection, or malignancy.

Clinical Context

Indicated for advanced infection, significant trauma, or malignancy requiring partial nasal structure resection; significant functional and cosmetic implications.
