26842 Thumb fusion with graft

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: thumb fusion with graft, thumb arthrodesis with graft, thumb joint fusion

Surgical fusion of the thumb interphalangeal or metacarpophalangeal joint with autogenous bone graft and internal fixation devices.

Clinical Context

Used for complex thumb joint fusion cases requiring both grafting material and internal fixation for achieving solid bony union.
