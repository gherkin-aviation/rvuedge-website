50391 Instll prescription agnt into rnal tub

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: renal instillation, intracavitary chemotherapy, sclerosant injection

Instillation of a therapeutic agent (medication, chemotherapy, or sclerosant) into a renal tube or catheter for treatment of intraluminal pathology.

Clinical Context

Used for treatment of renal tumors, tuberculosis, fungal infections, or sclerosis of cysts via percutaneous nephrostomy.
