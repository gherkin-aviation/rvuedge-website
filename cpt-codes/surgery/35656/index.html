35656 Bpg femoral-popliteal

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: femoropopliteal bypass, fem-pop bypass, femoral-popliteal

Arterial bypass graft from femoral artery to popliteal artery for superficial femoral artery occlusion with claudication or critical limb ischemia. Restores blood flow to the calf.

Clinical Context

Most common lower extremity bypass; indicated for symptomatic SFA stenosis/occlusion. Saphenous vein is graft of choice.
