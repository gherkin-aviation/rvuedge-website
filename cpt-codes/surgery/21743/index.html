21743 Repair sternum/nuss with scope

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Nuss procedure minimally invasive, endoscopic pectus repair

Repair of sternum and Nuss procedure with endoscopic visualization. Surgical correction of pectus deformity using minimally invasive Nuss technique with endoscopic guidance for bar placement.

Clinical Context

Used for correction of pectus excavatum with endoscopic assistance for submuscular bar placement. Provides improved visualization compared to open approach while maintaining minimally invasive advantages.
