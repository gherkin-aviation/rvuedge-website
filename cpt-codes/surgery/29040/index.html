29040 application bdy cyst sho to hip head

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: body cast with head, cervical-thoracic cast, full spine cast

Application of body cast from shoulders to hips including head piece, providing comprehensive thoracolumbar and cervical spine immobilization.

Clinical Context

Used for thoracolumbar spine fractures or instability with concurrent cervical involvement requiring combined immobilization.
