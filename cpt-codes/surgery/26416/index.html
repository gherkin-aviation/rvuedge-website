26416 Graft hand or finger tendon

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Tendon graft, Tendon grafting, Autograft placement

Surgical grafting of a tendon to the hand or finger using harvested autograft, allograft, or synthetic material to restore function after excision of damaged tendon or in primary reconstruction.

Clinical Context

Used when primary tendon repair is impossible and staged reconstruction with grafting is planned. May be performed as staged procedure after infection control or initial debridement.
