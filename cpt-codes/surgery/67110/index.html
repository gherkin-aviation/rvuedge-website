67110 Repair detached retina

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: subretinal fluid drainage, retinotomy, internal drainage, fluid drainage for RD

Repair of retinal detachment by aspiration or drainage of subretinal fluid (retinotomy approach) to allow retinal reattachment and promote retinal-pigment epithelium adhesion.

Clinical Context

Often used adjunctively with buckle or vitrectomy; drainage retinotomy improves retinal mobility and flattening; carries risk of retinal break extension.
