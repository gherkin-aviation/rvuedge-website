29836 Elbow arthroscopy/surgery

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: OCD elbow, osteochondritis dissecans repair, osteochondral lesion repair

Arthroscopy of the elbow with arthroscopic treatment of osteochondritis dissecans or osteochondral lesion including debridement and fixation.

Clinical Context

Used for osteochondritis dissecans of the capitellum and other osteochondral lesions in throwing athletes.
