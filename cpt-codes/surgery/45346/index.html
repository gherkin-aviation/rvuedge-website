45346 Sigmoidoscopy with ablation

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: sigmoidoscopy with ablation, flexible sig with laser/radiofreq ablation

Sigmoidoscopy with ablation of lesion using electrocautery, laser, cryotherapy, or radiofrequency energy.

Clinical Context

Used for palliation of obstructing lesions, destruction of residual adenomatous tissue, treatment of Barrett's-like changes, or recurrent polyps.
