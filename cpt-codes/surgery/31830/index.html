31830 Revise windpipe scar

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: tracheal scar revision, tracheal stenosis revision

Surgical revision of tracheal stenosis or stricture caused by scarring, typically performed to improve airway diameter and function by removing scar tissue and preventing re-stenosis.

Clinical Context

Used for post-intubation or post-tracheostomy stenosis, previous surgical complication scarring, or other conditions causing tracheal narrowing requiring scar management.
