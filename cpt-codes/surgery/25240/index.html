25240 Partial removal of ulna

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ulnar head excision, partial ulnar resection

Partial removal of the ulna bone through a limited excision, commonly at the head or styloid process.

In Plain Language

removing part of ulna

Clinical Context

Used for ulnar head arthritis, impingement syndrome (ulnar impaction), osteochondroma, or after distal radioulnar joint pathology.
