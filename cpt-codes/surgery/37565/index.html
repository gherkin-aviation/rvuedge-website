37565 Ligation int jugular vein

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: IJV ligation, jugular vein ligation, internal jugular vein ligation

Surgical ligation (tying off) of the internal jugular vein, typically performed for management of thrombosis, infection, or traumatic injury.

Clinical Context

Performed for internal jugular vein thrombosis, severe infection (jugular thrombophlebitis), or traumatic injury. Affects venous drainage from head.
