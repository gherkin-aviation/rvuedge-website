44403 Colonoscopy with resection

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: colonoscopy stoma mucosal resection, transcolostomy endoscopic resection

Colonoscopy performed through colostomy stoma with endoscopic mucosal resection for complete removal of lesion with tissue submission for pathology.

Clinical Context

Used for removal of precancerous lesions or early cancers in colon/rectum of patients with colostomy. Allows en bloc resection with margin assessment.
