51715 Endoscopic injection/implant

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: urinary incontinence injection, transurethral injection, bulking agent injection

Endoscopic injection or implantation of material for urinary incontinence, cystoscopic delivery of bulking agent for intrinsic sphincter deficiency.

Clinical Context

Minimally invasive treatment for stress urinary incontinence from intrinsic sphincter deficiency. Temporary or longer-lasting agents available.
