27687 Revision of calf tendon

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: achilles revision, achilles tendon revision

Surgical revision of achilles (heel) tendon repair or reconstruction for inadequate function or failed primary repair.

Clinical Context

Performed for re-rupture, functional loss, or inadequate healing after primary achilles repair. Specialized revision addressing specific complications.
