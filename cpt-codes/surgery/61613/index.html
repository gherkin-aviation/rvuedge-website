61613 Remove aneurysm sinus

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: aneurysm clipping, aneurysm repair

Resection or clipping of cerebral aneurysm with aneurysmal sac removal and arterial reconstruction as needed.

Clinical Context

Open surgical treatment for cerebral aneurysm. May be performed acutely for subarachnoid hemorrhage or prophylactically for unruptured aneurysms.
