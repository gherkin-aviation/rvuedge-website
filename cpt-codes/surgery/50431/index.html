50431 Njx procedures nfrosgrm and/or urtrgrm

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: repeat pyelography, follow-up imaging, contrast study

Percutaneous injection under fluoroscopic guidance for nephrography and ureterography (radiographic imaging) with repeat or additional contrast studies.

Clinical Context

Used for follow-up imaging of percutaneous access site patency or assessment of stone passage after treatment.
