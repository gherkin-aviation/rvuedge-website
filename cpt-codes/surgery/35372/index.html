35372 Rechanneling of artery

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: upper extremity graft declotting, neck bypass graft reoperation

Reoperation (thrombectomy and revision) of upper extremity or neck arterial bypass graft with patency restoration.

Clinical Context

Urgent intervention for upper extremity or cervical bypass graft thrombosis. Timing critical for limb/organ salvage; early thrombectomy with revision achieves better patency than delayed intervention.
