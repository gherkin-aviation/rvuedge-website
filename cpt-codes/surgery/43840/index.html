Surgery Global 90d

Gastrorrhaphy (suturing) for repair of gastric ulcer perforation or duodenal ulcer perforation.

In Plain Language

stomach ulcer repair; ulcer hole closure

Clinical Context

Emergency surgical repair of perforated duodenal or gastric ulcer. May be performed via omental patch (Graham patch) or primary closure technique.
