25316 Revise palsy hand tendon(s)

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: multiple tendon transfers for paralysis

Multiple tendon transfers and/or reconstructive procedures for hand paralysis to restore several lost functions.

Clinical Context

Used for comprehensive restoration in high-level spinal cord injury or brachial plexus injury requiring multiple functional improvements.
