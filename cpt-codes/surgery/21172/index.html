21172 Reconstruction supr-lat orb rm & lw fhd

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: orbital reconstruction, orbitofacial reconstruction, superolateral orbit repair

Reconstruction of the superior and lateral orbital walls and floor with removal and repositioning of orbital contents or implant placement.

Clinical Context

Used for orbital fractures, tumor resection, or deformity affecting eye position or function. Critical for preventing diplopia and enophthalmos.
