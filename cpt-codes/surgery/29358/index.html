29358 application long leg cast brace

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: long leg cast with brace, hinged knee cast, articulated cast

Application of a long leg cast with an articulated brace or hinge mechanism at the knee to allow controlled knee flexion and extension.

In Plain Language

bendable knee cast; leg cast with moving knee

Clinical Context

Indicated for distal femur and proximal tibia fractures allowing controlled knee motion during healing and rehabilitation.
