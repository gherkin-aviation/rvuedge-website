33863 Ascending aortic graft

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Ascending aortic graft, ascending aorta replacement, tubular graft

Ascending aortic graft; surgical replacement of the ascending thoracic aorta with a prosthetic graft for treatment of aneurysm, dissection, or other pathology requiring ascending aortic reconstruction.

Clinical Context

Standard procedure for ascending aortic disease including aneurysm, dissection, and degeneration. Utilizes woven Dacron or composite grafts with preservation of coronary ostia and aortic valve when feasible.
