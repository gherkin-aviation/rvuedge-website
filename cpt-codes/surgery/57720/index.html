57720 Revision of cervix

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: extensive cervical revision, cervical reconstruction

Revision of cervix (extensive) involves more complex surgical reconstruction of cervical anatomy requiring additional dissection or tissue grafting.

Clinical Context

Used for severe cervical stenosis, extensive scar tissue, or anatomic defects requiring more complex reconstruction. May include tissue grafting or extensive dissection.
