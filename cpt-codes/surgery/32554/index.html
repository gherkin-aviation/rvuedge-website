32554 Aspirate pleura without imaging

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: thoracentesis, pleural aspiration, pleural tap, chest tap, pleural fluid drainage

Percutaneous aspiration of the pleural space without concurrent imaging guidance, performed to obtain fluid samples or provide symptomatic relief from pleural effusion.

In Plain Language

chest fluid removal with needle

Clinical Context

Used for diagnostic thoracentesis or therapeutic drainage of pleural effusion when imaging-guided approach is not needed or when bedside procedure is appropriate.
