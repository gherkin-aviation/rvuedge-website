33340 Percutaneous closure transcatheter l atr apndge

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: percutaneous LAA closure, LAAC, transcatheter LAA occlusion

Percutaneous closure of left atrial appendage using transcatheter approach and approved closure device.

Clinical Context

Minimally invasive alternative to surgical LAA exclusion for stroke prevention in atrial fibrillation.
