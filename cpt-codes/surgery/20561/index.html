20561 Needle insj without njx 3+ muscle

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: multiple muscle needle insertion, needle placement multiple muscles

Needle insertion into three or more muscles without injection of therapeutic medication. This add-on code accounts for multiple muscle needle placements.

Clinical Context

Used as add-on component when multiple muscle needle placements (three or more) are performed without therapeutic injection.
