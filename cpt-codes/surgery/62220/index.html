62220 Establish brain cavity shunt

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: cisternal drain placement, subarachnoid drainage, cisternal CSF drainage

Establishment of cerebrospinal fluid drainage system via cisternal puncture. Includes placement of drain or catheter in the subarachnoid space.

Clinical Context

Used for cerebrospinal fluid drainage in cases of hydrocephalus or increased intracranial pressure via cisterna magna puncture.
