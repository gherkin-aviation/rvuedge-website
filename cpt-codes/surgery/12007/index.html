12007 repair s/n/ax/gen/trnk >30.0 centimeters

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: Simple laceration repair, Major wound closure

Repair of simple laceration or wound on the scalp, neck, axilla, genitalia, trunk, or extremities measuring greater than thirty centimeters.

Clinical Context

Used for simple repair of very large lacerations that do not require layered or complex closure technique.
