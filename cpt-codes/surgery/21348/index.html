21348 Opn treatment nasomax fracture with graft

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: nasomaxillary fracture with graft, nasomaxillary with augmentation

Open treatment of nasomaxillary fracture with the addition of bone graft material for reconstruction and augmentation.

In Plain Language

Surgery for broken nose and jaw with bone graft

Clinical Context

Used when nasomaxillary fractures have bone loss requiring graft augmentation for structural restoration.
