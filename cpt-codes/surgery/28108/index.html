28108 Removal of toe lesions

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: toe lesion excision, toe cyst removal, toe growth removal

Excision of lesion or cyst of the toe region. This procedure removes benign lesions from the toes with appropriate soft tissue management.

In Plain Language

removing a growth or cyst from a toe; toe lesion surgery

Clinical Context

Performed for ganglion cysts, lipomas, or other benign lesions on the toes causing pain or functional impairment. Often simple procedure with minimal morbidity.
