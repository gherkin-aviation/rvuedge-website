51535 Repair of ureter lesion

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ureteral lesion repair, ureteral diverticulectomy

Repair of ureter lesion (diverticulectomy or similar), surgical excision and repair of ureteral lesion or diverticulum.

Clinical Context

Indicated for ureteral lesion, diverticulum, or small defect requiring surgical repair through partial ureterectomy.
