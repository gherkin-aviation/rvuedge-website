19300 Mastectomy for gynecomastia

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: gyne removal, male breast reduction

Surgical removal of breast tissue for treatment of gynecomastia, which is abnormal enlargement of male breast tissue. This procedure removes the excess glandular and/or fatty tissue.

In Plain Language

removal of enlarged male breast tissue

Clinical Context

Used to treat gynecomastia in male patients due to hormonal imbalance, medications, or idiopathic causes. Improves cosmetic appearance and relieves breast tenderness or pain.
