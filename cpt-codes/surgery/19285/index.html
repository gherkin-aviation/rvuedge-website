19285 Percutaneous device breast first ultrasound imag

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: ultrasound-guided wire placement, ultrasound localization

Image-guided placement of a percutaneous breast lesion localization device for the first lesion using ultrasound imaging guidance. This technique employs real-time ultrasound to precisely identify and mark breast abnormalities.

Clinical Context

Used for preoperative localization of nonpalpable lesions when ultrasound provides optimal visualization. Particularly useful for lesions in superficial locations or cystic lesions.
