22862 Revision rplcm rthrp 1ntrspc lumbar

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Lumbar ADIS revision, Disc replacement revision

Revision or replacement of total disc arthroplasty (including endplate preparation, discectomy, and interbody device insertion), single interspace; lumbar.

Clinical Context

Used when lumbar artificial disc requires revision or replacement due to mechanical failure or inadequate pain relief.
