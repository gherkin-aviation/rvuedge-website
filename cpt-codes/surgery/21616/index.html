21616 excision first and/or crv rib with sympth

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: first rib resection with sympathectomy, cervical rib removal with sympathetic resection

Excision of first and/or cervical rib with sympathetic chain resection. Surgical removal of cervical rib or first rib combined with resection of cervical sympathetic chain for thoracic outlet syndrome with sympathetic involvement.

Clinical Context

Used for thoracic outlet syndrome with associated sympathetic dysfunction or stellate ganglion involvement. Combination procedure addresses both structural compression and sympathetic-mediated symptoms.
