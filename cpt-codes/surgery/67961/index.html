67961 Revision of eyelid

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: canthoplasty, canthal revision

Revision of eyelid malposition including canthoplasty or canthal support procedure for lid alignment correction.

Clinical Context

Used for canthal malpositioning or laxity requiring canthal support procedures. Common indications include lateral canthal displacement, medial canthal insufficiency, or horizontal canthal laxity.
