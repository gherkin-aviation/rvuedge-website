27500 Treatment of thigh fracture

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: femur fracture fixation, ORIF femur

Closed or open reduction and internal fixation of a femoral shaft fracture with minimal displacement or low complexity, treated with internal fixation devices.

Clinical Context

Used for femoral shaft or subtrochanteric fractures. Very common orthopedic trauma procedure with multiple fixation options.
