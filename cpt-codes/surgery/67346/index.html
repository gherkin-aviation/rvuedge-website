67346 Biopsy eye muscle

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: muscle biopsy, orbital muscle biopsy

Biopsy of extraocular muscle tissue to obtain pathologic diagnosis for suspected muscle disease, inflammation, or infiltration.

Clinical Context

Performed for myasthenia gravis, thyroid eye disease, inflammatory myositis, or infiltrative conditions affecting ocular motility.
