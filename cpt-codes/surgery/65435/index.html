65435 Curette/treat cornea

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: corneal scraping therapeutic, superficial debridement

Scraping of cornea, therapeutic; superficial.

In Plain Language

removing damaged outer layer of cornea; gentle scraping to help cornea heal

Clinical Context

Removal of loose or damaged corneal epithelium in corneal abrasion, erosion, or dystrophy to promote re-epithelialization and reduce pain.
