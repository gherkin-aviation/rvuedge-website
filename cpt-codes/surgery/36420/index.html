36420 Venipuncture cutdown < 1 yr

Surgery

Also known as: cutdown, venous cutdown, surgical venipuncture

Venipuncture by cutdown method, performed on patients under 1 year of age; first vein.

Clinical Context

Used in neonates and infants when peripheral or central venous access cannot be obtained by standard means. Requires surgical incision to expose vein.
