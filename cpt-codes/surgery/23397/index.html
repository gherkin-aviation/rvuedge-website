23397 Muscle transfers

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: multiple muscle transfers, bilateral muscle transfers, complex muscle reconstruction

Muscle transfers (multiple or bilateral). Transfer of multiple muscles or bilateral muscle transfers for shoulder reconstruction and functional restoration.

Clinical Context

Applied in extensive rotator cuff insufficiency, brachial plexus injuries, or when single muscle transfer is inadequate; requires careful planning of donor and recipient sites.
