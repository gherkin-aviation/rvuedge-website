54220 Irrg crpra cavrnosa priapism

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: cavernosal irrigation, priapism treatment

Irrigation and drainage of the corpora cavernosa for treatment of priapism (prolonged involuntary erection).

Clinical Context

Emergency treatment for ischemic priapism. Irrigation and drainage remove stagnant blood and restore circulation.
