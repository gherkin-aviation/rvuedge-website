32900 Removal of rib(s)

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: rib resection, rib removal, rib excision

Surgical removal of ribs, including rib resection for access to intrathoracic structures or removal of diseased rib segments. May involve single or multiple ribs.

Clinical Context

Performed for access to mediastinal or lung structures, removal of rib tumors, or management of chronic pain syndromes. Provides wide exposure for thoracic procedures.
