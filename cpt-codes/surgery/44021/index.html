44021 Decompress small bowel

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: small bowel decompression, bowel decompression, obstruction relief

Decompression of small bowel via open approach, including nasogastric tube placement or operative decompression.

Clinical Context

Operative decompression of distended small bowel in setting of obstruction, volvulus, or intussusception. Includes evacuation of intestinal contents.
