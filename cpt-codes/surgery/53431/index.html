53431 Reconstruct urethra/bladder

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: urethroplasty with bladder reconstruction, urethra-bladder repair

Surgical reconstruction of both the urethra and bladder in a single procedure to repair complex lower urinary tract defects.

Clinical Context

Indicated for complex cases involving concurrent urethral and bladder dysfunction, such as severe traumatic injuries, failed prior repairs, or cloaca repairs.
