53431 Reconstruct urethra/bladder

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: urethroplasty with bladder reconstruction, urethra-bladder repair

Surgical reconstruction of both the urethra and bladder in a single procedure to repair complex lower urinary tract defects.

In Plain Language

urethra and bladder repair; fixing both urethra and bladder

Clinical Context

Indicated for complex cases involving concurrent urethral and bladder dysfunction, such as severe traumatic injuries, failed prior repairs, or cloaca repairs.

Billing & Documentation

As a surgical CPT code, proper documentation must include the operative report detailing the procedure performed, patient positioning, approach, findings, and any complications. This code has a 90-day global period, which includes the day of the procedure, 1 day preoperative, and 90 days of postoperative care.
