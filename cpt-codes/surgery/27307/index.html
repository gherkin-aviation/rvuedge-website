27307 Incision of thigh tendons

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: multiple tenotomy, polytenotomy thigh

Incision of multiple thigh tendons for division, lengthening, or release of contractures.

Clinical Context

Applied to complex contractures or spasticity involving multiple tendon structures requiring coordinated division.
