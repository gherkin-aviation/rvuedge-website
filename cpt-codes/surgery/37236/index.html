37236 Open/percutaneous place stent first

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: stent placement first, open stent placement

Open surgical or percutaneous placement of an intra-arterial stent in the first treated arterial segment or graft to maintain patency.

Clinical Context

Used in peripheral arterial disease when percutaneous approach unavailable or failed. First stent in multi-segment intervention.
