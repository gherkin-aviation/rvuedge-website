27386 Repair/graft of thigh muscle

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: thigh muscle repair with graft, muscle reconstruction

Repair of thigh muscle with bone or soft tissue graft for reconstruction of significant muscle defect.

In Plain Language

Muscle tear repair with graft; Thigh muscle reconstruction

Clinical Context

Applied to large muscle defects or losses requiring graft augmentation for functional restoration.
