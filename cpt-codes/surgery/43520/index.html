43520 Incision of pyloric muscle

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: pyloroplasty, pyloric sphincter incision, pylorus division

Pyloroplasty with incision and division of the pyloric sphincter muscle to improve gastric drainage. This procedure widens the pyloric opening.

In Plain Language

widening pylorus opening; cutting pylorus muscle

Clinical Context

Treatment for pyloric stenosis or severe gastroparesis; improves gastric emptying and prevents retention.
