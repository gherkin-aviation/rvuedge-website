33501 Repair heart vessel fistula

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: simple coronary fistula repair, CAF repair limited, single coronary fistula closure

Surgical repair of a coronary artery to cardiac chamber or great vessel fistula with simplified or less complex anatomic involvement compared to code 33500.

In Plain Language

fixing a leakage between heart artery and chamber

Clinical Context

Used for uncomplicated coronary artery fistulas with straightforward anatomy and single point of intervention, often managed with ligation or simple closure techniques.
