27720 Repair of tibia

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: tibial fracture repair, tibia osteosynthesis

Surgical repair of tibia fracture with or without internal fixation for union and alignment restoration.

In Plain Language

repair of broken shin bone

Clinical Context

Performed for tibial shaft, metaphyseal, or intra-articular fractures. May involve plates, screws, or intramedullary nailing.
