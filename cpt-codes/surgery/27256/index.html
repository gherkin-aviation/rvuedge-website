27256 Treat hip dislocation

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: hip reduction with bracing, immobilized hip dislocation

Treatment of hip dislocation through reduction with subsequent immobilization or bracing for stability and healing.

In Plain Language

Hip dislocation brace care; Immobilized hip recovery

Clinical Context

Applied to hip dislocations where reduction is achieved and maintenance of reduction is supported through external immobilization.
