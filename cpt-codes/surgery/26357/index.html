26357 Repair finger/hand tendon

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Flexor-extensor repair with graft, Combined tendon repair and graft, Dual system tendon graft

Surgical repair and grafting of both flexor and extensor tendons of the finger or hand, combining tendon repair with graft placement for two separate tendon systems that are damaged beyond primary repair capability.

In Plain Language

Fixing and grafting both bending and straightening tendons in a finger; Complex repair using graft material for multiple tendons

Clinical Context

Used in severe crushing or mangling injuries where both tendon systems cannot be primarily repaired due to tissue loss or contamination. Typically in Zone 2 injuries or combined laceration patterns.
