62290 Njx procedures discography lumbar

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: lumbar discography, disc injection diagnostic

Injection of contrast material via lumbar puncture for discography. Includes visualization of intervertebral disc structure.

Clinical Context

Used for diagnostic evaluation of lumbar intervertebral disc pathology through injection of contrast material.
