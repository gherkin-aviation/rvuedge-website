33025 Incision of heart sacral

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: pericardiotomy with drainage, surgical pericardial window, open pericardial drainage

Surgical incision of the pericardium with drainage, possibly with additional exploration or management of intrapericardial pathology. Modified open approach.

Clinical Context

Used for management of pericardial effusion, hemopericardium, or acute tamponade requiring open surgical drainage and potential exploration.
