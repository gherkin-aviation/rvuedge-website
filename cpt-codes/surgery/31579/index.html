31579 Laryngoscopy telescopic

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: rigid telescopic laryngoscopy, telescope laryngoscopic exam

Laryngoscopic examination and evaluation of the larynx using a rigid laryngoscope with telescopic magnification for comprehensive visualization of laryngeal anatomy.

Clinical Context

Diagnostic procedure using rigid laryngoscope with telescope for detailed visualization and assessment of laryngeal structures and pathology.
