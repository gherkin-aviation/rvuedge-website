50548 Laparoscopic remove with ureter

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: lap nephroureterectomy, laparoscopic upper tract removal, minimally invasive nephroureterectomy

Laparoscopic removal of the kidney and ureter (laparoscopic nephroureterectomy) for upper urinary tract malignancy.

In Plain Language

minimally invasive kidney and ureter removal; laparoscopic upper tract cancer surgery

Clinical Context

Used for upper tract urothelial carcinoma with excellent oncologic outcomes. Combined laparoscopic and cystoscopic approach usually employed.
