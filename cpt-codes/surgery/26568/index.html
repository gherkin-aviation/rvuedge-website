26568 Lengthen metacarpal/finger

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Metacarpal lengthening, Digit lengthening, Bone lengthening

Surgical lengthening of a metacarpal bone or finger through osteotomy, distraction, or bone graft placement to increase digit length and improve hand appearance or function.

Clinical Context

Indicated for short digit syndrome, metacarpal shortening, or when digit lengthening improves functional pinch or appearance.
