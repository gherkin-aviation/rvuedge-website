27514 Treatment of thigh fracture

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: fracture fixation with graft, ORIF with grafting

Open reduction and internal fixation of an intertrochanteric femoral fracture with application of bone graft to address comminution or poor bone quality.

In Plain Language

fixing a hip-level thigh bone break with bone graft

Clinical Context

Used for comminuted intertrochanteric fractures, bone loss, or osteoporotic bone. Bone graft aids healing.
