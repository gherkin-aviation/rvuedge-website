58321 Artificial insemination

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: intrauterine insemination, artificial insemination, IUI

Artificial insemination is placement of prepared sperm into the uterus or fallopian tubes to facilitate pregnancy in infertility treatment.

Clinical Context

Performed for male factor infertility, cervical factor, or unexplained infertility. Often combined with ovulation induction. Timing critical relative to ovulation.
