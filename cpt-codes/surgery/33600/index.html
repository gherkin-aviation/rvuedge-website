33600 Closure of valve

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: atrial septal defect closure, ASD repair, atrial septum closure

Surgical closure or resection of a congenital or acquired heart defect located between the atria (atrial septal defect).

Clinical Context

Indicated for hemodynamically significant Atrial Septal Defects with right ventricular volume overload and exercise intolerance; closure reduces long-term risk of arrhythmias and heart failure.
