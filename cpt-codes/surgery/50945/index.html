50945 Laparoscopy ureterolithotomy

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: lap ureterolithotomy, laparoscopic stone removal, lap ULT

Laparoscopic ureterolithotomy, minimally invasive removal of ureteral calculus (stone) via laparoscope with site-specific ureterotomy.

In Plain Language

minimally invasive removal of kidney stone from ureter

Clinical Context

Indicated for accessible ureteral stones not amenable to endoscopy or extracorporeal lithotripsy. Minimally invasive with faster recovery.
