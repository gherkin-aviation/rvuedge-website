27025 Fasciotomy hip/thigh any typ

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: hip fasciotomy, thigh fasciotomy, compartment release

Surgical fasciotomy (incision of fascia) of the hip and thigh region using any technique to relieve compartment pressure or contracture.

Clinical Context

Indicated for compartment syndrome, fascia contracture, or excessive compartment pressure causing pain and functional impairment in hip or thigh.
