36565 Insert tunneled cv catheter

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: implanted pump catheter, programmable pump, infusion pump catheter

Insertion of tunneled central venous catheter, with subcutaneous pump; 5 years of age or older.

In Plain Language

surgically implanting a central line with a programmable pump; implanted pump for continuous medication delivery

Clinical Context

Used for controlled medication delivery, including chemotherapy, pain management, and other therapies requiring precise dosing over time.
