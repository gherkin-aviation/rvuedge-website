67108 Repair detached retina

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: complex vitrectomy, extensive RD repair, combined PPV and lensectomy

Repair of complex rhegmatogenous retinal detachment with extensive vitrectomy, wide membrane dissection, and comprehensive lensectomy if needed for retinal visualization.

Clinical Context

Reserved for severe detachments with extensive proliferative changes, vitreous hemorrhage, cataracts limiting visualization, or anterior migration of break.
