45005 Drainage of rectal abscess

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: rectal abscess drainage, perirectal abscess drainage

Drainage of rectal abscess via open surgical approach for treatment of perirectal abscess or intramural rectal abscess.

Clinical Context

Used for rectal or perirectal abscess from anal fissure, hemorrhoid, or cryptoglandular source. Often requires incision under anesthesia.
