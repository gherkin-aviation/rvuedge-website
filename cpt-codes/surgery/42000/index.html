42000 Drainage mouth roof lesion

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: palatal abscess drainage, hard palate drainage

Surgical incision and drainage of a lesion or abscess located in the mucous membrane of the roof of the mouth (hard palate).

In Plain Language

drainage of infection in roof of mouth; pus drainage from hard palate

Clinical Context

Oral surgical procedure for infected lesions, abscesses, or cysts involving the hard palate requiring drainage to relieve symptoms and treat infection.
