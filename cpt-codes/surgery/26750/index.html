26750 Treat finger fracture each

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: DIP dislocation reduction, PIP dislocation reduction, finger joint reduction

Surgical treatment of interphalangeal joint dislocation with closed reduction or manipulation without internal fixation.

Clinical Context

Employed for simple interphalangeal joint dislocations managed with closed reduction and immobilization, typically without surgical fixation.
