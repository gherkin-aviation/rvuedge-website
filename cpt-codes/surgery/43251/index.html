43251 Egd remove lesion snare

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: EGD snare removal, polypectomy, snare polypectomy

Esophagogastroduodenoscopy with endoscopic removal of esophageal or gastric lesion using snare technique. This therapeutic procedure excises mucosal lesions with a wire loop.

Clinical Context

Standard treatment for benign polyps in esophagus and stomach; allows histologic examination and reduces risk of malignant transformation.
