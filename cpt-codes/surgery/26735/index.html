26735 Treat finger fracture each

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: phalanx ORIF, finger bone fixation, complex finger fracture repair

Surgical treatment of phalangeal (finger bone) fracture with open reduction and internal fixation using plates, screws, or wires with complex dissection.

Clinical Context

Used for complex phalangeal fractures including comminuted, intra-articular, or rotated fractures requiring open reduction and secure internal fixation.
