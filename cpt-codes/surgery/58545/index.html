58545 Laparoscopic myomectomy

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: laparoscopic myomectomy, lap myomectomy

Laparoscopic myomectomy is removal of uterine fibroids endoscopically while preserving the uterus for fertility.

In Plain Language

Laparoscopic fibroid removal; Minimally invasive fibroid removal

Clinical Context

Minimally invasive fibroid removal preserving uterus. Suitable for small to moderate subserosal and intramural fibroids. Higher recurrence rate than hysterectomy.
