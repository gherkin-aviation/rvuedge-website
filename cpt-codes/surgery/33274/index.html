33274 Transcatheter insj/rpl perm ldls pm

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: leadless pacemaker insertion, transcatheter pacemaker, Micra implant

Transcatheter insertion or replacement of permanent leadless pacemaker components or system, implanting the entire pacemaker without traditional transvenous leads.

Clinical Context

Used for patients requiring pacemaker therapy who wish to avoid traditional lead complications; implanted directly into right ventricle.
