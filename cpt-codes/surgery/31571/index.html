31571 Laryngoscop with vc injection + scope

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: microscopic vocal cord injection, operating scope vocal injection, micro augmentation, direct laryngoscopy

Laryngoscopic injection of therapeutic substance into the vocal cords using an operating microscope for magnified visualization and precise placement of augmentation material.

In Plain Language

Clinical Context
