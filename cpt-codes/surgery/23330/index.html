23330 Remove shoulder foreign body

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: superficial foreign body removal, splinter removal shoulder, FB removal superficial

Removal of foreign body from shoulder (superficial). Extraction of foreign material (splinter, metal fragment, etc.) from superficial soft tissues of shoulder region.

Clinical Context

Performed for foreign body in superficial tissues of shoulder (skin, subcutaneous tissues) causing irritation, infection, or functional concern; typically requires minimal incision.
