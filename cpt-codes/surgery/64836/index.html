64836 Repair of hand or foot nerve

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: extensive hand/foot nerve repair

Surgical repair of a nerve injury in the hand or foot with extensive grafting or reconstruction requirements, representing the most complex hand/foot nerve repair.

Clinical Context

Used for severe hand or foot nerve injuries requiring extensive grafting, multiple anastomoses, or significant reconstruction. Highest complexity level for hand/foot nerve repairs.
