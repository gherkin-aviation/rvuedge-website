58970 Retrieval of oocyte

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: egg retrieval, oocyte harvest, aspiration, ER

Retrieval of oocyte(s) for in vitro fertilization (Assisted Reproductive Technology), performed via needle aspiration of ovarian follicles under ultrasound guidance.

In Plain Language

retrieving eggs for fertility treatment

Clinical Context

Performed as part of in vitro fertilization cycle after ovarian stimulation; typically done 34-36 hours after human chorionic gonadotropin trigger.
