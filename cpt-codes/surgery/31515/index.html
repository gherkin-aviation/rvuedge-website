31515 Laryngoscopy for aspiration

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: laryngeal aspiration, airway clearance, laryngeal suction

Laryngoscopic aspiration or removal of fluid, secretions, or debris from the larynx and upper airway to clear obstruction and facilitate airway clearance or obtain diagnostic material.

Clinical Context

Performed in patients with thick secretions, mucus plugging, or fluid accumulation obstructing the larynx. May be therapeutic for aspiration issues or diagnostic for collecting specimens.
