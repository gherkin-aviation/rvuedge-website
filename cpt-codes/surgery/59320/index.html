59320 Revision of cervix

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: cervical repair, cerclage removal, cervical revision

Revision or reconstruction of cervix following delivery or prior procedures, including cervical cerclage removal or repair of cervical incompetence.

In Plain Language

surgical repair of the cervix

Clinical Context

Performed for cervical lacerations from delivery, cervical insufficiency management, or removal of cerclage sutures.
