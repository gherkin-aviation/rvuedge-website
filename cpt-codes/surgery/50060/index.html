50060 Nl removal calculus

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: nephrolithotomy, kidney stone removal, renal calculus removal

Nephrotomy (kidney incision) with removal of calculus (stone).

Clinical Context

Open surgical removal of kidney stone via nephrotomy. Used for large stones, anatomic anomalies, or failure of endoscopic management.
