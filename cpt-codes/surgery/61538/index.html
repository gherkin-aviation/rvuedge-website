61538 Removal of brain tissue

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: extratemporal lobectomy, cortical resection, epilepsy surgery

Craniotomy or craniectomy with removal or resection of brain tissue from supratentorial location, potentially extratemporal, for seizure control or other indications.

In Plain Language

surgical removal of brain tissue outside temporal lobe

Clinical Context

Performed for extratemporal focal epilepsy (frontal, parietal, occipital) with identified seizure focus. More technically demanding than temporal lobe resection due to eloquent cortex involvement.
