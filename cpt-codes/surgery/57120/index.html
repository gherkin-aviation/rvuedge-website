57120 Colpocleisis le fort type

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Le Fort colpocleisis, Vaginal closure

Colpocleisis using Le Fort technique. Partial closure of vaginal canal to correct severe prolapse.

In Plain Language

Closing vagina to correct severe sagging

Clinical Context

Used for complete uterovaginal prolapse in patients not candidates for vaginal reconstruction. Eliminates prolapse but eliminates coitus.
