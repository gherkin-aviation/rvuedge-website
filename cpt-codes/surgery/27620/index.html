27620 Explore/treat ankle joint

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ankle arthrotomy, ankle joint treatment

Surgical exploration and treatment of pathology within the ankle joint, including debridement, loose body removal, or synovial treatment.

Clinical Context

Performed for intra-articular loose bodies, osteoarthritis, synovitis, or traumatic injury. Less extensive than formal arthroscopy but allows therapeutic intervention.
