23585 Optx scapular fracture with int fixation

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ORIF scapular fracture, open scapula reduction, scapular ORIF

Open treatment of scapular fracture with internal fixation. Open reduction internal fixation of displaced scapular fracture via direct visualization and plate/screw fixation.

Clinical Context

Indicated for displaced glenoid or scapular body fractures with significant articular involvement or in polytrauma patients requiring early mobilization.
