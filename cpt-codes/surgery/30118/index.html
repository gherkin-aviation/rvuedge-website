30118 Removal of intranasal lesion

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: extensive intranasal lesion removal, complex nasal mass removal, intranasal tumor removal

Removal of an extensive intranasal lesion or mass using endoscopic approach with significant debridement and reconstruction of damaged nasal structures.

Clinical Context

Indicated for large, infiltrative, or complex intranasal lesions requiring extensive debridement or affecting nasal architecture; may include reconstruction.
