31634 Bronchial with balloon occlusion

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: bronchial occlusion balloon, bronchial blocker placement, selective intubation

Bronchoscopy with bronchial occlusion using an occlusion balloon to treat bronchial bleeding, secure airway during emergent situations, or selectively ventilate portions of lung.

In Plain Language

using balloon to block airway bleeding or control ventilation

Clinical Context

Performed for massive hemoptysis treatment, selective lung ventilation in ICU settings, or protective intubation strategies during surgery.
