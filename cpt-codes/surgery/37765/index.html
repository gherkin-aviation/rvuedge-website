37765 Stab phleb veins xtr 10-20

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: Stab phlebectomy, Micro-incision vein removal

Stab phlebectomy of veins in the extremity involving removal of 10-20 individual varicose vein segments through small incisions.

Clinical Context

Used for localized varicose veins as a complement to saphenous stripping or as primary treatment for small branch varicosities.
