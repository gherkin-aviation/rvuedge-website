35131 Repair defect of artery

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: renal artery repair, kidney artery repair

Repair of a defect or aneurysm of the renal artery with restoration of blood flow.

In Plain Language

Fixing a kidney blood vessel; Repairing kidney artery damage

Clinical Context

Performed for symptomatic renal artery stenosis, aneurysm, or dissection causing hypertension, renal insufficiency, or acute kidney infarction. Often required in fibromuscular dysplasia or atherosclerotic disease affecting renal perfusion.
