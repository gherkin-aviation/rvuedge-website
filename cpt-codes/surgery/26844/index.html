26844 Fusion/graft of hand joint

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: hand joint fusion with graft, finger joint fusion, metacarpal joint fusion

Surgical fusion of a hand joint (other than interphalangeal of thumb) with autogenous bone graft and internal fixation for enhanced union.

In Plain Language

fusing hand joint with bone graft; hand joint surgery with graft material

Clinical Context

Used for complex hand joint fusion cases requiring bone graft supplementation and internal fixation to achieve solid bony union in difficult cases.
