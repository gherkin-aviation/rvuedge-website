53040 Drainage of urethra abscess

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: urethral abscess drainage

Surgical drainage of urethral abscess with incision and exploration of surrounding tissue, allowing therapeutic decompression and control of infection.

Clinical Context

Treatment for urethral infection with abscess formation. Often associated with stricture disease.
