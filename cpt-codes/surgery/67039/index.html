67039 Laser treatment of retina

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: laser photocoagulation, scatter laser, focal laser, pan-retinal photocoagulation, PRP

Laser photocoagulation of retinal lesions, areas of neovascularization, or retinal breaks using thermal laser energy to create permanent retinal scarring and seal breaks.

Clinical Context

Used for proliferative diabetic retinopathy, retinal tears, retinal holes, branch vein occlusion with ischemia, wet macular degeneration.
