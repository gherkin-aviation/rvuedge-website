23333 Remove shoulder foreign body deep

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: deep foreign body removal, intramuscular FB removal, deep FB extraction shoulder

Removal of foreign body from shoulder (deep). Extraction of foreign material from deep tissues, muscles, or joint space of shoulder region, requiring more extensive dissection.

Clinical Context

Used for foreign bodies embedded in shoulder musculature, interstices, or joint spaces; requires careful tissue dissection to locate and remove with minimal additional trauma.
