63250 Revise spinal cord vsls crvl

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: cervical spinal AVM repair, spinal vascular malformation repair

Revision or repair of spinal cord vascular lesions in the cervical region. This procedure involves surgical management of arteriovenous malformations, fistulas, or other vascular abnormalities of the cervical spinal cord.

Clinical Context

Used for symptomatic spinal dural arteriovenous fistulas, spinal arteriovenous malformations, or cavernous malformations causing myelopathy or progressive neurological decline.
