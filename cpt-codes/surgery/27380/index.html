27380 Repair of kneecap tendon

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: patellar tendon repair, quadriceps tendon repair

Repair of patellar tendon through operative approach for partial tears or avulsion.

Clinical Context

Indicated for patellar tendon rupture or significant tears requiring surgical repair for functional restoration.
