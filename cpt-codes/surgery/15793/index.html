15793 Chemical peel nonfacial drm

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: non-facial deep peel, body dermal peel

Chemical peel of non-facial skin extending into dermal layer. Intermediate to deep chemical peel of trunk or extremity skin.

Clinical Context

Used for deeper scars and significant skin conditions on trunk or extremities requiring dermal penetration.
