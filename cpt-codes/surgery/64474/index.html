64474 Lower xtr fscl pln blk uni nfs

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: lower extremity fascial plane continuous infusion, lower extremity fascial plane catheter

Lower extremity fascial plane block performed with continuous infusion of anesthetic and/or steroid through a catheter on one side.

Clinical Context

Used for post-operative pain control in lower extremity surgery. Provides extended anesthesia (24-72 hours) via catheter.
