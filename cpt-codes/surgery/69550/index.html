69550 excision aurl glomus tumor trnscanl

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: glomus tumor removal, paraganglioma excision, jugular paraganglioma surgery

Excision of aural glomus tumor via transcranial approach, removing a highly vascular neuroendocrine tumor of the jugular bulb or temporal bone.

In Plain Language

removal of a vascular tumor in the ear; surgical removal of a blood vessel tumor

Clinical Context

Used for tinnitus, pulsatile otalgia, conductive hearing loss from mass effect, or when imaging reveals glomus jugulare or tympanicum. Requires careful vascular management.
