55801 Removal of prostate

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: retropubic prostatectomy, radical prostatectomy

Removal of prostate (prostatectomy) via retropubic approach without pelvic lymph node dissection.

Clinical Context

Open surgical removal of prostate for malignancy via retropubic (above pubic bone) approach.
