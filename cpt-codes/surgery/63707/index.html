63707 Repair spinal fluid leakage

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: CSF leak repair, pseudomeningocele repair, spinal fluid leak closure

Repair of spinal cord cerebrospinal fluid leak or pseudomeningocele. This procedure involves closure of persistent cerebrospinal fluid leaks or repair of postoperative fluid collections.

In Plain Language

repair of spinal fluid collection or leak

Clinical Context

Used for postoperative or post-traumatic cerebrospinal fluid leaks and pseudomeningoceles causing symptoms.
