68110 excision lesion conjunctiva <1 centimeters

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: conjunctival lesion excision, small conjunctival lesion removal

Excision of conjunctival lesion less than 1 centimeter using direct closure or primary repair technique.

In Plain Language

removing small growth from eye membrane; conjunctival lesion removal

Clinical Context

Used for removal of small benign or suspicious conjunctival lesions. Common indications include small nevi, cysts, benign tumors, or lesions less than 1 centimeter in diameter.
