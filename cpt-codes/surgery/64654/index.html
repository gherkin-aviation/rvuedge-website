64654 first opn implant bat modulj sys

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: neurostimulation system implant, battery-powered neuromodulation implant, spinal cord stimulator system

Open surgical implantation of a battery-powered modulator system for neurostimulation including implantation of pulse generator and electrodes.

Clinical Context

Used for chronic pain, neurological conditions requiring long-term electrical modulation therapy.
