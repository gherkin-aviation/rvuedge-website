26428 Repair/graft finger tendon

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Mid-palmar repair with graft, Palmar approach graft, Palmar tendon graft repair

Surgical repair and grafting of a finger tendon using palmar or mid-palmar approach with graft material to augment repair or bridge defects.

Clinical Context

Indicated for finger tendon injuries requiring palmar exposure with concurrent need for graft material due to tissue loss or complex injury patterns.
