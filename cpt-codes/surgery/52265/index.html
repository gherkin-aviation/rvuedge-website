52265 Cystoscopy and treatment

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: cysto with cold biopsy, cysto with cold snare resection

Cystourethroscopy with cold cup biopsy or cold snare procedure for removal of bladder lesions without thermal energy, minimizing mucosal trauma.

In Plain Language

tissue removal without burning

Clinical Context

Used for small benign lesions or diagnostic biopsies where thermal injury should be minimized. Reduced risk of bladder perforation.
