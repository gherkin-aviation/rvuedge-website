24615 Optx aqt/chrnc elbow dislc

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ORIF elbow dislocation, open reduction dislocation

Open treatment of acute or chronic elbow dislocation. Surgical management when closed reduction is not possible or unsuccessful.

Clinical Context

Used for elbow dislocations that cannot be reduced by closed methods or for chronic dislocations requiring surgical stabilization.
