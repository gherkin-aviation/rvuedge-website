37255 Revsc evasc ivt angio sf each

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: infrapopliteal angioplasty simple additional, additional tibial angioplasty

Percutaneous or open revascularization of the infrapopliteal artery (tibial or peroneal) using simple angioplasty in each additional vessel.

Clinical Context

Each additional infrapopliteal vessel in critical limb ischemia. Extends revascularization to improve foot perfusion.
