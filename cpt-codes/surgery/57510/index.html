57510 Cauterization of cervix

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: cervical cautery, cervix cauterization

Cauterization of cervix is a procedure in which heat or chemical agents are applied to destroy abnormal tissue on the cervical surface.

Clinical Context

Used to treat benign cervical lesions, polyps, or erosions causing bleeding or discharge. Can be used for hemostasis after cervical biopsy.
