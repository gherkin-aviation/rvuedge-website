26562 Repair of web finger

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Web space repair with graft, Syndactyly release with flap, Extensive web reconstruction

Surgical repair of webbing between fingers with extensive reconstruction including skin graft or local flap, performed for significant web space involvement or complex release.

Clinical Context

Indicated for extensive syndactyly requiring skin coverage, complex web space contracture, or when simple release would result in inadequate soft tissue coverage.
