41116 Excision of mouth lesion

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: oral mucosa excision, mouth lesion excision, mucosal lesion removal

Surgical excision of a lesion of the mouth including mucosa and submucosa with primary closure.

Clinical Context

Used for benign or malignant lesions of the oral cavity mucosa including lip mucosa, buccal mucosa, hard palate, or alveolar ridge.
