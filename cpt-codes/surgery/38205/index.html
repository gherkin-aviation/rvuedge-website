38205 Harvest allogeneic stem cell

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: Allogeneic stem cell harvest, Donor cell collection

Harvesting and collection of allogeneic hematopoietic cells or stem cells from a donor for transplantation to a recipient.

Clinical Context

Performed during stem cell or bone marrow transplant procedures where cells are collected from an unrelated or matched related donor.
