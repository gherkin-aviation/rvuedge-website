43212 Esophagoscop stent placement

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: endoscopic stent placement, esophageal stent insertion

Esophagogastroduodenoscopy (EGD) with placement of esophageal stent (tube) for stricture dilation or malignant obstruction.

In Plain Language

camera examination with placement of tube to open esophageal narrowing

Clinical Context

Therapeutic procedure for esophageal strictures or malignant obstruction. Stent maintains esophageal patency for swallowing.
