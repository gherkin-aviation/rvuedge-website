34502 Reconstruct vena cava

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: vena cava reconstruction, IVC reconstruction, SVC reconstruction

Reconstruction of the vena cava (superior or inferior). Surgical repair or replacement of vena cava for thrombosis, stenosis, or congenital abnormality.

Clinical Context

Used for vena cava stenosis, thrombosis, or reconstruction after trauma. Critical for restoring venous return to the heart.
