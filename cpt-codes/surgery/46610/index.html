46610 Anoscopy remove lesion

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: anoscopic lesion removal, anoscopic excision, lesion ablation

Anoscopy with removal of lesion(s). The anoscope is used to visualize and remove (excise) pathologic lesions from the anal canal.

In Plain Language

removing a growth from inside the anus using a scope; endoscopic removal of anal lesions

Clinical Context

Performed for angiomas, polyps, or small cancers accessible via anoscopy. May use cold, heat, or cutting techniques.
