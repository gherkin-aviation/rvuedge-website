53430 Reconstruction of urethra

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: urethroplasty, urethral reconstruction, urethral repair

Surgical reconstruction of the urethra, a procedure to repair or rebuild the urethral canal compromised by stricture, trauma, or congenital abnormality.

Clinical Context

Indicated for urethral strictures, traumatic injuries, or iatrogenic damage. Can be performed via various approaches (substitution, transection, or mobilization-based).
