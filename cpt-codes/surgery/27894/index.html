27894 Decompression of leg

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: four-compartment fasciotomy, total leg decompression, complete compartment release

Decompression of the lower leg with release of multiple compartments (all four compartments). This extensive fasciotomy releases all lower leg compartments for severe compartment syndrome.

In Plain Language

relieving pressure in all leg compartments; complete leg decompression

Clinical Context

Performed for severe compartment syndrome affecting multiple compartments, major crush injuries, or reperfusion injury. Most comprehensive decompression approach for life-threatening compartment syndrome.
