50436 Dilation xst trc ndurlgc procedures

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: tract dilation, ureteral dilation, percutaneous access dilation

Dilation of an existing percutaneous tract with new access established percutaneously using endoluminal guidance.

Clinical Context

Used to enlarge existing percutaneous tracts for better endoscopic access during stone removal or other therapeutic procedures.
