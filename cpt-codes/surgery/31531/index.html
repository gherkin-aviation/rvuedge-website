31531 Laryngoscopy with foreign body & outpatient scope

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: microscopic FB removal, operating scope FB removal, micro FB extraction

Laryngoscopic removal of foreign bodies from the larynx using an operating microscope for magnified visualization and precise extraction of difficult or deeply lodged objects.

Clinical Context

Performed for complex or difficult foreign body extractions requiring magnified visualization, such as deeply embedded objects or multiple foreign bodies requiring precise manipulation.
