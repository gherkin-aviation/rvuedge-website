22551 Arthrd anterior ntrbdy cervical

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Anterior cervical discectomy and fusion, ACDF, anterior cervical fusion

Arthrodesis, anterior/anterolateral approach, including discectomy and/or corpectomy to decompress spinal cord and/or nerve roots, cervical; single interspaces.

Clinical Context

Most common cervical fusion procedure; used for spondylosis, disc hernia, or myelopathy; anterior approach allows direct disc removal and decompression.
