53855 Insert prostate urethral stent

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: prostatic urethral stent insertion, UroLume stent, permanent stent

Transurethral insertion of a permanent or semi-permanent prostatic urethral stent to relieve obstruction from benign prostatic hyperplasia.

Clinical Context

Used for benign prostatic hyperplasia in patients unable or unwilling to undergo standard procedures. Self-expanding metal stent maintains patency.
