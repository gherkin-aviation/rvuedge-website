15931 excision sacral pr ulc prim suture

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: sacral ulcer exc, sacral sore removal

Excision of sacral (lower spine) pressure ulcer with primary closure by direct suturing without bone or muscle flap.

Clinical Context

Performed for Stage III-IV sacral pressure injuries managed by primary closure. Used when defect is relatively small without significant deep tissue involvement.
