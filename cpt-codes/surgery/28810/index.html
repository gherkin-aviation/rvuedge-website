28810 Amputation toe & metatarsal

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: toe and metatarsal amputation, digit amputation, partial forefoot amputation

Surgical amputation of a single toe (hallux or lesser digit) at the level including the metatarsal head, removing the entire digit and adjacent metatarsal bone.

In Plain Language

Toe removal with bone; Digit amputation; Partial foot removal

Clinical Context

Used for severe toe trauma, infection, vascular insufficiency, or malignancy when digit salvage is impossible.
