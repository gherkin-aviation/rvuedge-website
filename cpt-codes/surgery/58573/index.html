58573 Tlh with t/o uterus over 250 g

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: TLH-BSO large uterus, laparoscopic hysterectomy with ovaries enlarged uterus, lap total hysterectomy complex

Total laparoscopic hysterectomy with bilateral salpingo-oophorectomy and uterus greater than 250 grams involves endoscopic removal of enlarged uterus with ovaries.

In Plain Language

Laparoscopic removal of large uterus and ovaries; Minimally invasive complex removal

Clinical Context

Most complex TLH procedure combining large uterus removal with salpingo-oophorectomy. Requires advanced laparoscopic skills and careful morcellation/extraction.
