65860 Severing ads anterior segment laser

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: laser anterior synechiae lysis, posterior synechiae lysis

Severing adhesions of anterior segment by laser surgery (separate procedure).

Clinical Context

Laser lysis of anterior synechiae (iris-cornea adhesions) or posterior synechiae (iris-lens adhesions) in glaucoma or uveitis.
