43290 Egd flx trnsorl dplmnt balo

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: EGD intragastric balloon placement, gastric balloon insertion, Orbera balloon

Esophagogastroduodenoscopy with flexible transoral endoscopic placement of an intragastric balloon for weight loss. This bariatric procedure deploys the balloon device.

In Plain Language

placing balloon in stomach for weight loss; intragastric balloon placement

Clinical Context

Temporary bariatric treatment for obesity; balloon remains in place for 6 months providing early satiety and facilitating weight loss with lifestyle changes.
