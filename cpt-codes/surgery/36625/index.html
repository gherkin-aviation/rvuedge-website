36625 Insertion catheter artery

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: arterial cutdown, surgical arterial line, arterial cutdown cannulation

Arterial catheterization or cannulation; cutdown technique.

Clinical Context

Used when percutaneous approach unsuccessful or artery too small. Requires surgical exposure of vessel.
