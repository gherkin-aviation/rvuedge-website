42835 Removal of adenoids

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: revision adenoidectomy, complex adenoid removal

Removal of adenoids only (adenoidectomy) via transoral approach with moderate complexity or reoperation for previous adenoidectomy.

Clinical Context

Performed for recurrent adenoid overgrowth, incomplete previous adenoidectomy, or obstructive symptoms despite prior surgery. May involve extensive bleeding control or scar tissue management.
