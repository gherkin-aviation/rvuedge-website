37256 Revsc evasc ivt angio complex 1

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: infrapopliteal angioplasty complex first, tibial complex angioplasty

Percutaneous or open revascularization of the infrapopliteal artery (tibial or peroneal) using complex techniques in the first treated vessel.

Clinical Context

Complex lesions (calcified, occlusion, tortuous) requiring advanced techniques (laser, atherectomy). First infrapopliteal vessel.
