40520 Partial excision of lip

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: lip wedge excision, lip lesion excision

Partial excision of the lip for removal of lesion with primary closure. A variation of partial lip excision procedures.

Clinical Context

Used for smaller lesions or cosmetically sensitive areas where tissue conservation is important. Maintains lip structure and function.
