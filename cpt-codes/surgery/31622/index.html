31622 Diagnosis bronchoscope/wash

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: diagnostic bronchoscopy with lavage, BAL, bronchoalveolar lavage

Diagnostic bronchoscopy (visualization of lower airways) with bronchoalveolar lavage, instilling fluid to wash and collect cellular material from distal lung segments for diagnostic analysis.

Clinical Context

Performed for suspected infections (fungal, mycobacterial, viral), aspiration, or immunocompromised states where alveolar sampling is needed for diagnosis.
