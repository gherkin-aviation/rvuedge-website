69440 Exploration of middle ear

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: middle ear exploration, mastoid antrum exploration

Surgical exploration of the middle ear (mastoid antrum and middle ear cleft) to visualize and address middle ear pathology.

In Plain Language

opening the middle ear space; looking inside the ear drum area

Clinical Context

Used to evaluate and treat middle ear disease including infection, cholesteatoma, ossicular discontinuity, or to establish middle ear ventilation. Common in pediatric and adult patients with chronic or acute otitis media.
