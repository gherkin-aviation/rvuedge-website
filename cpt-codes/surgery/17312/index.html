17312 Mohs additional stage

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: Mohs additional stage, Mohs add-on

Mohs micrographic surgery additional stage beyond the first for head, neck, hands, feet, or genitals when additional layers require examination.

Clinical Context

Add-on code used when multiple tissue layers require examination and processing. Each additional layer adds complexity and ensures complete tumor removal.
