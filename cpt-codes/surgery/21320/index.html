21320 Clsd treatment nsl fracture with mnpj & stablj

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: closed nasal fracture with manipulation

Closed treatment of nasal fracture with manipulation of the jaw joint and/or dental alignment and stabilization.

Clinical Context

Used for nasal fractures involving associated dental or jaw joint involvement managed non-operatively.
