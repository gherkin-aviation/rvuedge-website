37191 Ins endovas vena cava filtr

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: IVC filter insertion, vena cava filter placement, IVC filter

Percutaneous or transvascular insertion of an inferior vena cava filter (IVC filter) via endovascular approach to prevent pulmonary embolism.

Clinical Context

Placed in patients with contraindications to anticoagulation, recurrent pulmonary embolism despite anticoagulation, or massive proximal deep vein thrombosis.
