53447 Remove/replace ur sphincter

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: AUS replacement, sphincter replacement, AUS revision

Surgical removal and replacement of a previously implanted artificial urinary sphincter with a new device.

In Plain Language

replacing bladder control implant; sphincter device replacement

Clinical Context

Performed for AUS malfunction, mechanical failure, or obsolescence. Allows transition to newer device technology or reimplantation at same location.
