61524 Removal of brain lesion

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: brain lesion resection, AVM resection, vascular lesion removal

Craniotomy or craniectomy with removal of brain lesion(s) from supratentorial location, potentially including arteriovenous malformations or other vascular lesions.

Clinical Context

Performed for various supratentorial brain lesions including cavernous malformations, venous anomalies, or other lesions requiring selective microsurgical resection.
