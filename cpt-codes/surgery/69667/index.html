69667 Repair middle ear structures

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ossicular repair with prosthesis, fistula repair with ORP

Repair of ossicular discontinuity or window fistula with ossicular replacement prosthesis placement.

Clinical Context

Used when ossicular repair requires prosthetic placement for sound transmission restoration.
