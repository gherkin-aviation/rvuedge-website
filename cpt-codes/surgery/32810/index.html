32810 Close chest after drainage

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: chest closure after drainage, thoracotomy closure, pleural cavity closure

Closure of chest after drainage of pleural fluid or air collection. Surgical closure of thoracotomy or chest tube sites following drainage procedures.

Clinical Context

Performed after drainage of pneumothorax, hemothorax, or pleural effusion to restore chest wall integrity and respiratory mechanics. Ensures complete healing of thoracotomy.
