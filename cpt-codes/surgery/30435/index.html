30435 Revision of nose

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: nasal revision complex, complex rhinoplasty revision, revision with reconstruction

Revision of prior nasal surgery with significant additional reconstruction or grafting to address major complications or prior inadequacies.

Clinical Context

Indicated for significant complications from prior nasal surgery requiring major reconstruction or additional grafting to restore form and function.
