55300 Prepare sperm duct x-ray

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: vasal exposure for imaging, vasography prep

Surgical exposure and preparation of vas deferens for radiographic imaging (vasography).

Clinical Context

Surgical exploration of vas deferens with exposure for radiographic imaging to evaluate patency and obstruction.
