42892 Revision of pharyngeal walls

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: pharyngeal wall revision, pharynx revision surgery

Revision (re-operation or reconstruction) of pharyngeal walls following previous surgery with moderate extent of reconstruction.

In Plain Language

surgery to revise or repair throat walls; redo throat surgery

Clinical Context

Performed to address complications of previous pharyngeal surgery including stenosis, fistula, or functional impairment. Requires reconstruction with possible myocutaneous flap.
