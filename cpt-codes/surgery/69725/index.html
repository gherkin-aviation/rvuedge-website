69725 Release facial nerve

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: facial nerve repair, facial nerve anastomosis, nerve repair reconstruction

Surgical repair of traumatic facial nerve injury or complete disruption of the facial nerve with complex reconstruction.

Clinical Context

Used for complete transection, major injury, or long-standing paralysis with significant loss of function.
