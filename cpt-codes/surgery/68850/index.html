68850 Injection for tear sacral x-ray

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: lacrimal imaging, dacryocystography contrast injection

Injection of contrast medium into lacrimal sac for lacrimal imaging (dacryocystography) for diagnostic evaluation.

Clinical Context

Used for diagnostic imaging of lacrimal system. Common indications include evaluation of nasolacrimal duct obstruction, fistula, or dacryocystitis.
