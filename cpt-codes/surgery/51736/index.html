51736 Urine flow measurement

Surgery

Also known as: uroflowmetry, flow study, urinary flow test

Urine flow rate measurement by uroflowmetry, non-invasive measurement of urinary stream flow rate and pattern.

In Plain Language

measurement of urine stream strength

Clinical Context

Simple screening test for voiding dysfunction, urinary obstruction, or poor detrusor function. Often first-line urodynamic test.
