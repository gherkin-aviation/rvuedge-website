27198 Clsd treatment pelvic ring fracture

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: closed pelvic reduction, pelvic manipulation

Closed treatment of pelvic ring fracture with manipulation, including reduction maneuvers and temporary stabilization for more complex fracture patterns.

In Plain Language

Hip fracture realignment; Pelvic bone setting

Clinical Context

Used for unstable pelvic ring fractures managed non-operatively through closed reduction and external immobilization such as pelvic binders.
