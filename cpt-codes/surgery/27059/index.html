Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: large hip tumor resection, large pelvic tumor resection, extensive hip tumor removal

Surgical resection of a hip or pelvic tumor of greater than 5 centimeters with complete excision and reconstruction.

In Plain Language

removing large hip tumor; extensive hip tumor surgery

Clinical Context
