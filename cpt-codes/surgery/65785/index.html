Implantation of intrastromal corneal ring segments.

In Plain Language

inserting rings into cornea to reshape it; implanting plastic rings to improve vision

Clinical Context

Minimally invasive procedure to implant intrastromal rings for keratoconus or post-refractive ectasia. Corrects irregular astigmatism and improves visual acuity.
