29907 Subtalar arthroscopic with fusion

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: subtalar arthrodesis, subtalar fusion scope, STJ fusion

Arthroscopic fusion of the subtalar joint, performed endoscopically to arthrodese the talus to calcaneus, typically using internal fixation to permanently immobilize the joint.

Clinical Context

Indicated for advanced subtalar arthritis, subtalar instability, or hindfoot deformity refractory to conservative treatment; provides permanent pain relief through joint fusion.
