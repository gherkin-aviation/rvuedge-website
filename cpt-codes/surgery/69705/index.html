69705 Nps surgical dilation eust tube uni

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: Eustachian tube dilation, ET dilation, balloon dilation ET unilateral

Endoscopic or non-powered surgical dilation of the Eustachian tube (auditory tube) unilaterally to improve middle ear ventilation.

In Plain Language

widening the tube that connects ear to throat

Clinical Context

Used for Eustachian tube dysfunction with conductive hearing loss, chronic otitis media with effusion, or ventilation failure.
