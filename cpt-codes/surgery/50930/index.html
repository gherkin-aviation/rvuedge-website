50930 Closure ureter/bowel fistula

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ureteral fistula repair, ureterovisceral fistula closure, ureter-bowel fistula closure

Closure of ureter to bowel fistula, surgical repair of abnormal communication between ureter and intestinal tract.

Clinical Context

Indicated for ureterovisceral fistula from stone erosion, prior surgery, or malignancy. Requires identification and layered closure of both structures.
