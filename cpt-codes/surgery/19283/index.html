19283 Percutaneous device breast first strtctc

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: structural imaging localization, 3D-guided wire placement

Image-guided placement of a percutaneous breast lesion localization device for the first lesion using structural guidance (CT, tomosynthesis). This technique employs three-dimensional imaging to precisely mark breast abnormalities.

Clinical Context

Used for preoperative localization of nonpalpable lesions when structural imaging (tomosynthesis or CT) provides superior visualization. Improves accuracy for lesions with complex anatomy.
