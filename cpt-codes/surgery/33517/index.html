33517 Cabg artery-vein single

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: single arterial-vein CABG, arterial-vein hybrid bypass, single mixed graft CABG

Surgical placement of one arterial and one venous coronary artery bypass graft, combining two conduit types for single combined revascularization.

Clinical Context

Used when combining arterial (typically internal mammary or radial) with venous conduit is beneficial; arterial grafts provide superior long-term patency.
