42961 Control throat bleeding

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: pharyngeal hemorrhage control with ligation, complex throat bleeding control

Control of hemorrhage (bleeding) from the pharynx (throat) with moderate complexity including vessel ligation or cauterization.

In Plain Language

stopping throat bleeding with vessel treatment; complex throat hemorrhage control

Clinical Context

Indicated for significant pharyngeal hemorrhage requiring vessel ligation, cauterization, or cautery with increased operative time. May require identification and direct vessel treatment.
