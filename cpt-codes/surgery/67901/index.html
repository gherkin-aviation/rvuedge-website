67901 Repair eyelid defect

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: eyelid defect repair, eyelid trauma repair, lid reconstruction

Repair of eyelid defect from trauma, burn, or excision using local tissue flap or primary closure with careful attention to lid function and alignment.

In Plain Language

repair of eyelid injury

Clinical Context

Reconstruction of partial-thickness eyelid defects from trauma, burn, or surgical excision; preserves function and appearance.
