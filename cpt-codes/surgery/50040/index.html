50040 Nfros nfrot with drainage

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: nephrotomy, kidney incision, renal incision

Nephrotomy (kidney incision) with or without drainage of renal abscess or infected material.

In Plain Language

surgical incision into kidney; opening of kidney for drainage

Clinical Context

Surgical incision into kidney for drainage of abscess or infected material. Used for intrarenal infection or calculus removal.
