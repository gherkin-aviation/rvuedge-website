36482 Endoven therapy chem adhes first

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: chemical adhesive ablation, cyanoacrylate closure, VenaSeal closure

Endovenous mechanical ablation with chemical adhesive (cyanoacrylate), first vein treated.

Clinical Context

Used for varicose vein closure with non-thermal chemical adhesive. Allows treatment without tumescent anesthesia.
