Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: fat grafting, lipofilling, fat transfer

Autologous fat injection or transplantation harvested by liposuction technique, first 50 cubic centimeters or less. Lipofilling or fat grafting procedure.

In Plain Language

injecting patient's own fat for volume; fat transfer surgery

Clinical Context
