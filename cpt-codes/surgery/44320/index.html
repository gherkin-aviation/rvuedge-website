44320 Colostomy

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: colostomy, open colostomy, end colostomy, loop colostomy

Open creation of colostomy (colon-to-skin opening) for permanent or temporary diversion of colonic contents to abdominal wall stoma.

In Plain Language

surgery to create opening from colon to skin; creation of bowel opening on abdomen

Clinical Context

Performed for colorectal cancer, inflammatory bowel disease, trauma, familial adenomatous polyposis, or pelvic dysfunction when restoration of continuity is not feasible or not desired.

This code has a 90-day global period, which includes the day of the procedure, 1 day preoperative, and 90 days of postoperative care.
