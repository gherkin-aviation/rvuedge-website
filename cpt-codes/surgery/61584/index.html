61584 Orbitocranial approach/skull

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: orbitocranial approach, orbital rim osteotomy

Orbitocranial approach with orbital rim resection and intracranial dissection for anterior cranial vault/orbital lesion treatment.

In Plain Language

Eye socket and skull surgery; Orbital tumor removal

Clinical Context

Performed for tumors involving orbit and anterior cranial fossa. Provides excellent access to suprasellar, intrasellar, and parasellar lesions.
