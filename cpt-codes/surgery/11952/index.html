11952 Subcutaneous njx fil matrl 5.1-10cc

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: Larger filler injection, Extended soft tissue augmentation

Subcutaneous injection of filling material or soft tissue augmentation substance in volume of five point one to ten cubic centimeters.

In Plain Language

Larger injection of filler material; More significant volume enhancement

Clinical Context

Used for more extensive soft tissue augmentation and volume restoration. Often requires multiple injection sites across the face or other areas.
