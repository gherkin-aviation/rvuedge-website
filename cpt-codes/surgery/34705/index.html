34705 Evac repair a-biiliac ndgft

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: aortobi-iliac EVAR, bilateral iliac EVAR, aortobi-iliac endovascular repair

Endovascular repair of abdominal aorta with bilateral iliac involvement without graft. Percutaneous repair of aortobi-iliac aneurysm or occlusion.

Clinical Context

Used for infrarenal abdominal aortic aneurysm with bilateral iliac artery involvement. Standard EVAR configuration.
