57530 Removal of cervix

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: cervicectomy, trachelectomy, cervical amputation

Removal of cervix (trachelectomy) involves surgical excision of the entire cervix, typically for benign pathology or early cervical malignancy.

Clinical Context

Performed for cervical cancer, persistent dysplasia, severe cervicitis, or cervical stenosis. May be fertility-sparing in young women with cervical cancer when combined with appropriate oncologic staging.
