27220 Treat hip socket fracture

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: closed acetabular treatment, conservative hip socket care

Closed treatment of acetabular fracture without significant reduction or with minimal manipulation.

In Plain Language

Non-surgical hip socket healing; Conservative pelvic care

Clinical Context

Used for simple, non-displaced acetabular fractures stable enough to heal without operative intervention.
