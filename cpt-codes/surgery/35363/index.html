35363 Rechanneling of artery

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: venous valve reconstruction, venous valvuloplasty

Rechanneling (thrombectomy with endarterectomy) of venous system with vein valve reconstruction or transposition.

Clinical Context

Specialized procedure for post-thrombotic venous disease with reflux causing chronic venous insufficiency and limb swelling. Restoration of valve competence improves venous hemodynamics and symptoms.
