49255 Removal of omentum

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: omentum removal, omentectomy, omental resection

Omentectomy; removal of the omentum (fatty abdominal membrane) with or without resection of related structures.

Clinical Context

Removal of omentum for various indications including tumor resection, cytoreductive surgery for peritoneal carcinomatosis, removal of inflamed tissue, or as part of larger abdominal surgery.
