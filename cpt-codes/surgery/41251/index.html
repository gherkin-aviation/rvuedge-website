41251 Repair tongue laceration

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: tongue laceration repair complex, extensive tongue wound closure, tongue wound with depth

Surgical repair of a laceration of the tongue with more extensive tissue involvement requiring complex closure.

In Plain Language

repairing larger tongue cut; tongue wound with deeper injury; complex tongue repair

Clinical Context

Used for more extensive tongue lacerations involving deeper structures or longer wounds. May require layer-by-layer closure.
