28545 Treat foot dislocation

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: foot dislocation reduction, closed reduction foot, manipulation treatment

Closed treatment of foot dislocation with manipulation to achieve anatomic reduction, used for displaced foot dislocations (tarsometatarsal or midfoot) amenable to closed reduction.

In Plain Language

Foot joint reduction; Foot dislocation reduction; Joint realignment

Clinical Context

Used for displaced foot dislocations; closed reduction restores normal joint anatomy and prevents chronic instability and arthritis.
