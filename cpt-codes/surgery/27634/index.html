27634 excision leg/ankle tumor dep 5 centimeters/>

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: large deep lesion excision, soft tissue excision deep >5 cm

Excision of deep soft tissue tumor or lesion 5 centimeters or larger in the lower leg or ankle with fascial plane dissection.

Clinical Context

Performed for larger deep neoplasms, sarcomas, or complex lesions requiring extensive dissection. Higher work RVU reflects greater complexity.
