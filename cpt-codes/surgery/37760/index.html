37760 Ligament prfratr vn radical 1 leg

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Perforator ligation radical stripping, Radical saphenous stripping

Surgical ligation of perforating veins and radical stripping of the saphenous system in a single leg for extensive venous disease.

Clinical Context

Performed for advanced chronic venous insufficiency with skin ulceration, lipodermatosclerosis, or extensive perforator incompetence.
