28666 Treat toe dislocation

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: percutaneous toe fixation, IP joint pinning, minimally invasive

Percutaneous skeletal fixation of toe dislocation with manipulation, using percutaneous pins to maintain reduction of displaced interphalangeal digit dislocations.

Clinical Context

Used for unstable interphalangeal joint dislocations requiring fixation; percutaneous pinning maintains reduction.
