35103 Repair artery rupture aorta

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ruptured aortic visceral valve repair, aortic branch rupture valve repair

Repair of a rupture of the aorta and its visceral branches with aortic valve involvement. Surgical repair of ruptured complex aortic pathology with branch and valve involvement.

Clinical Context

Rare, highly complex emergency for ruptured juxtarenal AAA with aortic dissection and valve insufficiency.
