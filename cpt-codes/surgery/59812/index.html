59812 Treatment of miscarriage

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: treatment of miscarriage, evacuation of retained products, D&E

Surgical treatment of spontaneous abortion (miscarriage) via dilation and evacuation or curettage for retained products of conception.

Clinical Context

Indicated when spontaneous abortion incomplete with significant retained tissue requiring operative management to prevent infection or hemorrhage.
