27070 Part remove hip bone super

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: partial hip bone resection, superficial pelvic bone removal, limited hip bone resection

Surgical partial removal of hip bone with superficial extent and limited soft tissue involvement.

Clinical Context

Used for partial hip bone resection in cases of tumor, infection, or structural abnormality with superficial involvement.
