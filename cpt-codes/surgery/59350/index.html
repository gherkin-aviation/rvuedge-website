59350 Repair of uterus

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: uterine repair, hysterotomy closure, uterine rupture repair

Surgical repair of uterine defect or rupture, including repair of uterine wound after cesarean delivery or treatment of uterine rupture.

Clinical Context

Performed at cesarean delivery to close hysterotomy, or emergently to treat uterine rupture with hemorrhage control.
