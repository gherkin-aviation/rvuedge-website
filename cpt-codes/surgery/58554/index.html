58554 Laparo-vag hyst with t/o complicated

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: complex LAVH with BSO, difficult laparoscopic-assisted hysterectomy with ovary removal

Complex laparoscopic-assisted vaginal hysterectomy with salpingo-oophorectomy combines complex hybrid hysterectomy with removal of fallopian tubes and ovaries.

Clinical Context

Most complex hybrid procedure combining challenging pathology, oophorectomy requirement, and hybrid approach. Requires significant surgical expertise and operative time.
