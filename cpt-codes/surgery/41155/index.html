41155 Tongue jaw & neck surgery

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: extensive composite resection, radical glossectomy with mandible, tongue mandible and neck resection

Surgical resection of tongue with floor of mouth, mandibular bone, and neck lymph node dissection for advanced cancer.

Clinical Context

Used for very advanced tongue cancer with mandibular invasion and lymph node involvement. Most extensive head and neck resection with significant functional implications.
