23184 Prtl excision bone proximal humerus

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: proximal humerus osteotomy, partial humerus excision, humeral reshaping

Partial excision of bone of proximal humerus (metaphyseal or diaphyseal resection). Selective bone removal from proximal humerus for decompression or structural alteration.

Clinical Context

Applied for proximal humeral hypertrophic changes, metaphyseal deformities, or to improve subacromial space and reduce mechanical impingement symptoms.
