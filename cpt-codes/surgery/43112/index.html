43112 Esphg tot with thrcm

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: esophagectomy with thoracotomy, complex esophageal resection

Esophagectomy (total removal of the esophagus) with thoracotomy (thoracic approach) and reconstruction via multiple procedures.

Clinical Context

Performed for complex esophageal pathology requiring thoracotomy and extensive reconstruction. Higher morbidity but necessary for certain tumors or strictures.
