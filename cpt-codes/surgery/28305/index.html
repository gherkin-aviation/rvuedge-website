28305 Incise/graft midfoot bones

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: midfoot incision with graft, navicular decompression with graft, midfoot graft

Surgical incision into the midfoot bones combined with bone graft placement for treatment of midfoot pathology such as avascular necrosis or structural defects requiring both decompression and bony augmentation.

Clinical Context

Used for midfoot avascular necrosis or significant bone loss requiring combined decompression and bone grafting to promote healing and prevent collapse.
