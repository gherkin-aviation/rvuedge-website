61548 Removal of pituitary gland

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: pituitary ablation, hypophysectomy

Hypophysectomy or pituitary gland ablation performed via open transfrontal or infratemporal approach for treatment of endocrine disorder.

In Plain Language

Surgical removal of the pituitary gland; Pituitary gland destruction for hormone control

Clinical Context

Performed for hormone-secreting pituitary adenomas refractory to medical management or when transsphenoidal approach not feasible. Less common in modern practice due to efficacy of transsphenoidal approach.
