58561 Hysteroscopy remove myoma

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: hysteroscopic myomectomy, submucosal fibroid removal

Hysteroscopy with removal of fibroid involves endoscopic resection of submucosal fibroid from uterine cavity.

Clinical Context

Treats submucosal fibroids causing heavy menstrual bleeding. Can be partial (leaving intramural component) or complete. Preserves uterus for fertility.
