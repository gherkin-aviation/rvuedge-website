64820 Sympathectomy digital artery

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: digital artery sympathectomy, digital sympathectomy

Surgical sympathectomy of the digital arteries in the hand. Performed to improve blood flow to the fingers in cases of severe vascular insufficiency.

Clinical Context

Indicated for severe Raynaud's phenomenon, critical ischemia, or gangrene of the fingers refractory to medical management. Aims to preserve finger tissue and prevent amputation.
