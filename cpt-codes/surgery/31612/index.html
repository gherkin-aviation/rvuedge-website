31612 Percutaneous trchl puncture ttrach aspiration

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: percutaneous tracheostomy, needle tracheostomy, transtracheal aspiration

Percutaneous (through skin) tracheostomy puncture or needle aspiration of the trachea for diagnostic specimen collection or therapeutic drainage of tracheal abscess or secretions.

Clinical Context

Performed for diagnostic culture collection from lower respiratory tract or therapeutic drainage of tracheal infection or abscess. Minimally invasive compared to open tracheostomy.
