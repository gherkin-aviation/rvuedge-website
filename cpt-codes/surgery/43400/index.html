43400 Ligate esophagus veins

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: esophageal variceal ligation, open esophageal ligation, variceal ligation

Ligation and division of esophageal varices via open approach for control of variceal bleeding. This procedure interrupts blood flow to bleeding varices.

Clinical Context

Surgical treatment for bleeding esophageal varices when endoscopic therapy fails or is contraindicated; high mortality procedure.
