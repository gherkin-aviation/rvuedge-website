27218 Treat pelvic ring fracture

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: complex pelvic ORIF, extensive pelvic fixation

Treatment of pelvic ring fracture through extensive operative open reduction and internal fixation, often including supplemental fixation or complex reconstruction.

Clinical Context

Applied to highly unstable or multi-part pelvic ring fractures requiring extensive reduction and fixation for stability in trauma situations.
