29435 application patllr tdn bearing cast

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: PTB cast, patellar bearing cast, patellofemoral weight-bearing cast

Application of a patellar tendon-bearing cast extending from below the knee to the foot, distributing weight through the patellar tendon rather than the tibial plateau.

Clinical Context

Used for tibial shaft fractures allowing weight-bearing while reducing stress on the fracture site through load sharing.
