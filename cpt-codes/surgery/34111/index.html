34111 Removal of arm artery clot

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: arm thrombectomy, brachial thrombectomy, arm embolectomy

Removal of an arterial thrombus or embolus from the arm artery. Surgical extraction of blood clot from brachial or upper extremity artery.

Clinical Context

Used for acute arm/brachial artery thrombosis or embolism. Presents with acute hand ischemia, pain, or paresthesias.
