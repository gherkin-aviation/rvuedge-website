54135 Remove penis & nodes

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: radical penectomy with extended node dissection, penectomy with pelvic lymphadenectomy

Amputation of the penis with extensive removal of pelvic and regional lymph nodes for advanced penile malignancy.

Clinical Context

Indicated for high-grade or advanced penile cancer with suspected regional metastases. Extended dissection includes pelvic nodes.
