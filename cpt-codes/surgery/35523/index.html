35523 Artery byp graft brchl-ulnr-rdl

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: brachial-radial bypass, forearm artery bypass

Arterial bypass graft from brachial to ulnar, radial, or digital artery with restoration of hand perfusion.

In Plain Language

Bypass from arm to wrist/hand arteries; Creating new blood supply to hand

Clinical Context

Revascularization for forearm/hand artery occlusion causing digital ischemia. Requires careful assessment of palmar arch patency and collateral circulation.
