36473 Endovenous mchnchem first vein

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: endovenous ablation, EVLA, mechanical ablation, EVT

Endovenous mechanical or chemical ablation (including radiofrequency, laser, cryotherapy, and sclerosing solution), first vein treated.

Clinical Context

Used for varicose vein treatment via catheter-directed endovenous methods. Minimally invasive alternative to surgical stripping with same-day recovery.
