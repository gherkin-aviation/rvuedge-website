64857 Repair arm/leg nerve

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: arm/leg nerve repair, extremity nerve repair

Surgical repair of a significant nerve injury in the arm or leg, often requiring graft reconstruction due to nerve gap or poor tissue quality.

In Plain Language

arm or leg nerve surgery; limb nerve repair

Clinical Context

Indicated for significant arm or leg nerve injuries requiring graft repair due to gap or complexity. Includes injuries from trauma, stretch, or iatrogenic causes.
