65273 Repair of eye wound

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: corneal perforation repair, keratoplasty

Repair of full thickness corneal wound or penetrating keratoplasty without foreign body removal.

In Plain Language

repairing hole through cornea; stitching full-thickness corneal cut

Clinical Context

Surgical repair of penetrating corneal wounds from sharp trauma without embedded foreign material. Requires precise wound apposition and suturing to restore structural integrity.
