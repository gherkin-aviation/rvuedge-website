15730 Mdfc flap with prsrv vascular pedcl

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: forehead flap, midline flap

Midline forehead flap or other flap with preserved vascular pedicle. Advanced reconstructive flap technique for nasal and facial reconstruction.

In Plain Language

forehead skin flap for nose repair; specialized facial reconstruction

Clinical Context

Used primarily for nasal reconstruction and large facial defects where forehead flap provides excellent blood supply and tissue characteristics.
