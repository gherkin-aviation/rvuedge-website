30020 Drainage of nose lesion

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: nasal lesion drainage, nose cyst I&D, nasal pus drainage

Incision and drainage of a nasal lesion, abscess, or cyst, performed with or without local anesthesia for therapeutic drainage and fluid evacuation.

In Plain Language

nose abscess drainage; nose swelling drainage

Clinical Context

Performed for infected or symptomatic nasal lesions, cysts, or fluctuant masses causing obstruction, pain, or infection.
