47460 Incise bile duct sphincter

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Sphincterotomy, sphincter of Oddi incision

Surgical incision of the sphincter of Oddi (the muscular valve at the junction of the pancreatic and common bile ducts entering the duodenum) to relieve obstruction.

Clinical Context

Performed for sphincter dysfunction, papillary stenosis, or when standard bile duct exploration is complicated. May be done as part of other biliary procedures.
