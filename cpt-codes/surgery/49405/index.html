49405 Image catheter fluid colxn visc

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: image-guided catheter placement, percutaneous drain, abdominal fluid drainage

Image-guided percutaneous catheter placement in intra-abdominal or visceral fluid collection for drainage.

Clinical Context

Percutaneous catheter placement under imaging guidance (ultrasound or computed tomography) for drainage of intra-abdominal or visceral fluid collections including seroma, abscess, or hematoma.
