44205 Laparoscopic colectomy part with ileum

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: lap ileocolic resection, lap right colectomy, laparoscopic ICR

Laparoscopic partial colectomy with anastomosis of colon to ileum (ileocolostomy).

Clinical Context

Laparoscopic resection of ileocecal region with anastomosis for ileocecal Crohn disease or right-sided malignancy. Extends into terminal ileum.
