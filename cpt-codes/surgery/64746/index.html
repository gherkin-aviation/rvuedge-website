64746 Incise diaphragm nerve

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: phrenic nerve incision, phrenic nerve sectioning

Surgical incision and sectioning of the phrenic nerve (Cranial Nerve XI) to treat intractable hiccoughs or provide diaphragmatic paralysis for therapeutic purposes.

In Plain Language

diaphragm nerve surgery; hiccough treatment

Clinical Context

Historically used for intractable hiccoughs unresponsive to medical management. Now rarely performed due to effective medical treatments. May be used therapeutically for specific pulmonary conditions.
