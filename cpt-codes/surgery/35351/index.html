35351 Rechanneling of artery

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: renal artery endarterectomy, renal artery rechanneling

Rechanneling (thrombectomy with endarterectomy) of renal artery with restoration of renal blood flow.

In Plain Language

Cleaning out kidney artery; Removing blockage from renal artery

Clinical Context

Surgical management of renovascular stenosis causing hypertension or progressive renal insufficiency. Direct endarterectomy appropriate for ostial or localized lesions with early atherosclerosis.
