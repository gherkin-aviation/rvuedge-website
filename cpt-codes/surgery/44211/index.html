44211 Laparoscopic colectomy with proctectomy

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: lap colectomy with proctectomy, lap left hemicolectomy with low anterior resection, lap sigmoid and rectal resection

Laparoscopic left colectomy with proctectomy and creation of colonic or colorectal anastomosis for removal of left colon and rectum with bowel continuity restoration.

In Plain Language

camera surgery to remove left colon and rectum; minimally invasive colon and rectum removal

Clinical Context

Used for rectal and sigmoid colon cancers, complex polyps, or inflammatory bowel disease affecting both left colon and rectum when laparoscopic approach is appropriate.
