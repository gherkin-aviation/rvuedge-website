32215 Treat chest lining

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: pleural treatment, pleurectomy

Surgical treatment of the pleura (chest lining), including procedures such as pleurectomy, decortication, or pleural stripping performed to treat pleural disease.

Clinical Context

Used for pleural thickening, restrictive pleurisy, recurrent pleural effusion, or symptomatic pleural disease requiring direct surgical intervention.
