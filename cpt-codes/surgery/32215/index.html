32215 Treat chest lining

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: pleural treatment, pleurectomy

Surgical treatment of the pleura (chest lining), including procedures such as pleurectomy, decortication, or pleural stripping performed to treat pleural disease.

In Plain Language

chest lining surgery

Clinical Context

Used for pleural thickening, restrictive pleurisy, recurrent pleural effusion, or symptomatic pleural disease requiring direct surgical intervention.

Billing & Documentation

As a surgical CPT code, proper documentation must include the operative report detailing the procedure performed, patient positioning, approach, findings, and any complications. This code has a 90-day global period, which includes the day of the procedure, 1 day preoperative, and 90 days of postoperative care.
