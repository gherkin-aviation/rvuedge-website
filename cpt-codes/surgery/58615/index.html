58615 Occlude fallopian tube(s)

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: tubal clipping, tubal occlusion, Filshie clip placement

Occlusion of fallopian tube(s) via occlusive device placement is placement of clip, ring, or other permanent occlusion device on tubes for sterilization.

In Plain Language

Fallopian tube clipping; Tube blockage with clip or device

Clinical Context

Mechanical sterilization using clips or bands. May be reversible with some devices. Used at time of other procedures or as interval sterilization.
