30100 Intranasal biopsy

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: nasal biopsy, intranasal biopsy, nose tissue biopsy

Endoscopic or direct tissue biopsy of the nasal cavity or intranasal structures, including removal of tissue for pathological examination.

Clinical Context

Indicated for suspicious nasal lesions, masses, polyps, or inflammatory conditions requiring histopathological diagnosis to rule out malignancy or determine pathology.
