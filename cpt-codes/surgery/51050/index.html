51050 Removal of bladder stone

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: cystolithotomy, bladder stone removal

Removal of bladder stone (cystolith) via cystotomy, surgical incision of bladder to extract calculus.

Clinical Context

Performed for bladder calculi too large for cystoscopic management or in cases with complex anatomy precluding endoscopic approach.
