35390 Reoperation carotid add-on

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: carotid bypass graft reoperation, carotid graft declotting

Reoperation of carotid artery bypass graft as adjunct to carotid procedures with declotting and revision.

Clinical Context

Add-on procedure for carotid artery bypass graft complications. Indicates urgent thrombectomy and revision to restore cerebral perfusion and prevent stroke.
