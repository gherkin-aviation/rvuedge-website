62230 Replace/revise brain shunt

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: shunt revision, shunt replacement, shunt valve revision

Replacement or revision of cerebrospinal fluid shunt system. Includes replacement of shunt valve and/or tubing.

In Plain Language

brain shunt replacement; hydrocephalus shunt revision

Clinical Context

Used for revision of malfunctioning cerebrospinal fluid shunts due to obstruction, malposition, infection, or mechanical failure.
