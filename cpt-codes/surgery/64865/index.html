64865 Repair of facial nerve

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: complex facial nerve repair

Surgical repair of the facial nerve with additional complexity such as longer gap, multiple level injury, or requiring graft repair.

Clinical Context

Represents more complex facial nerve repair requiring graft or extensive dissection. Higher value than simple facial nerve repair due to increased difficulty.
