36474 Endovenous mchnchem add-on

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: endovenous ablation, additional vein ablation, add-on ablation

Endovenous mechanical or chemical ablation (including radiofrequency, laser, cryotherapy, and sclerosing solution), each additional vein treated; add-on to primary procedure.

Clinical Context

Used when multiple varicose veins require endovenous treatment in same session. Must be reported with primary ablation code.
