43622 Removal of stomach

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: total gastrectomy with jejunal interposition, total gastrectomy with reconstruction, esophageal reconstruction

Total gastrectomy with jejunal interposition or other esophageal reconstruction for extensive gastric disease. This provides complex reconstruction.

Clinical Context

Performed for extensive gastric cancer with major tissue defects or when esophagojejunal anastomosis not feasible; highest complexity gastric surgery.
