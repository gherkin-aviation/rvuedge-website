25105 Remove wrist joint lining

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: wrist synovectomy, joint lining removal

Removal of the synovial lining (synovectomy) of the wrist joint to address chronic inflammation or infection.

Clinical Context

Indicated for chronic rheumatoid arthritis, hemophilic arthropathy, or persistent synovitis refractory to medical management.
