19081 Biopsy breast first lesion strtctc

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: stereotactic breast biopsy, stereo biopsy first

Percutaneous needle or core biopsy of breast lesion under stereotactic guidance for first lesion with tissue diagnosis.

Clinical Context

Performed for suspicious breast abnormalities detected on mammography. Stereotactic guidance provides precise lesion sampling. First lesion in session.
