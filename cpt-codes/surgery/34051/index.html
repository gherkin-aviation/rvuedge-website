34051 Removal of artery clot

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: iliac thrombectomy, iliac embolectomy, iliac clot removal

Removal of an arterial thrombus or embolus from the iliac artery. Surgical extraction of blood clot from the iliac vessels.

Clinical Context

Used for acute thrombosis or embolism of the iliac artery causing limb ischemia. May be combined with thrombolytic therapy.
