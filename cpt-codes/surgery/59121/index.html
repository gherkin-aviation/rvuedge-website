59121 Treat ectopic pregnancy

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: laparoscopic ectopic removal, lap ectopic surgery

Surgical treatment of ectopic pregnancy via laparoscopic approach with salpingostomy (tube-sparing) or salpingectomy, less invasive than laparotomy.

Clinical Context

Minimally invasive approach for hemodynamically stable patients with unruptured ectopic pregnancy; allows preservation of fallopian tube.
