33916 Surgery of great vessel

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Great vessel surgery, vascular reconstruction, major vessel repair

Cardiac surgery of great vessels including operations on aorta, pulmonary artery, superior/inferior vena cava, or their branches; complex great vessel reconstruction or repair.

Clinical Context

Umbrella code for complex great vessel operations not specifically defined by other codes. Includes atypical repairs, hybrid approaches, and combined procedures.
