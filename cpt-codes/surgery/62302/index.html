62302 Myelography lumbar injection

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: myelography lumbar, lumbar myelogram

Myelography with lumbar injection and spinal puncture. Includes injection of contrast material and imaging of spinal cord and nerve roots.

Clinical Context

Used for diagnostic imaging of lumbar spinal cord and nerve roots in evaluation of spinal stenosis and other pathology.
