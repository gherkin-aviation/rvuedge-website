12021 Treatment superficial wnd dehsn with packing

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: Dehiscence management with packing, Wound closure with packing

Treatment of superficial wound dehiscence with packing material placement for management of separation of wound edges.

In Plain Language

Packing of wound that has opened; Treatment of separated incision with gauze packing

Clinical Context

Used when superficial dehiscence requires packing material to manage wound separation and promote healing.
