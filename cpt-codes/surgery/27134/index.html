27134 Revise hip joint replacement

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: hip revision, revision total hip replacement, hip prosthesis revision, revision total hip

Surgical revision of hip joint replacement (revision arthroplasty) with removal and replacement of prosthetic components.

In Plain Language

replacing hip implant; hip revision surgery

Clinical Context

Indicated for prosthetic loosening, infection, wear, or failure requiring removal of failed components and insertion of new prosthetics.
