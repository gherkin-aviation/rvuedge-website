26489 Transplant/graft palm tendon

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Palmar tendon transfer with graft, Palm tendon transplant and graft, Complex intrinsic transfer

Surgical transplantation of a palm tendon with concurrent grafting to address functional deficit, tissue deficiency, or to optimize positioning for functional restoration.

In Plain Language

Moving a palm tendon with added tissue graft; Complex palmar tendon reconstruction

Clinical Context

Performed when palmar tendon transfer requires graft augmentation for length or tissue replacement, often in complex intrinsic deficit scenarios.
