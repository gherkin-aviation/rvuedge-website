47715 Excision of bile duct cyst

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Choledochal cyst excision, cyst resection

Surgical excision of a choledochal cyst (congenital bile duct dilation) with reconstruction of biliary anatomy through choledochoduodenostomy or hepaticojejunostomy.

Clinical Context

Performed for symptomatic or complicated choledochal cysts to prevent cholangitis, cancer risk, and recurrent pancreatitis.
