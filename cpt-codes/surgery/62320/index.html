Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: interlaminar injection cervical, interlaminar thoracic injection, interlaminar approach

Injection of therapeutic or diagnostic agent via interlaminar approach to cervical or thoracic spinal cord region.

Clinical Context

Used for delivery of therapeutic agents to cervical or thoracic spinal cord region via interlaminar needle approach.
