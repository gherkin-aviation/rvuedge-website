61606 resection/excise cranial lesion

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: complex lesion resection, lesion resection with reconstruction

Extended resection of complex intracranial lesion with vascular anastomosis, bypass, or additional reconstructive technique.

Clinical Context

Performed for lesions infiltrating or adherent to major vessels requiring vascular reconstruction. Common with parasagittal or tentorial meningiomas.
