61546 Removal of pituitary gland

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: pituitary tumor removal, transsphenoidal resection, transphenoidal hypophysectomy

Transsphenoidal hypophysectomy or pituitary tumor removal via endoscopic approach through the sphenoid sinus.

In Plain Language

Removal of pituitary gland through the nose; Pituitary tumor surgery

Clinical Context

Gold standard approach for pituitary adenomas including prolactinomas, growth hormone-secreting tumors, and non-functional adenomas. Used for both diagnostic and therapeutic resection.
