69915 Incise inner ear nerve

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: vestibular nerve section, CN VIII section, vestibular neurectomy

Surgical incision or transection of the vestibulocochlear nerve (cranial nerve VIII) or division of the vestibular nerve to treat vertigo.

Clinical Context

Used for intractable vertigo from Meniere's disease or BPPV when hearing preservation desired. Eliminates vestibular function.
