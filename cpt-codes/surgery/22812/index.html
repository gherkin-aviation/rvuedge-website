22812 Arthrd anterior dfrm 8+ vrt segment

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Anterior spinal fusion 8+ levels, Extensive anterior fusion

Arthrodesis, anterior technique, spinal deformity correction, with bone graft or nonvascularized bone graft; 8 or more vertebral segments.

Clinical Context

Used for extensive anterior column reconstruction in severe deformity; requires staged procedures when extensive anterior fusion needed.
