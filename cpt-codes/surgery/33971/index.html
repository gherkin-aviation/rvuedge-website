33971 Aortic circulation assist

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: LVAD insertion surgical, surgical VAD implant, open LVAD placement

Aortic circulation assist device insertion or replacement with surgical approach; surgical insertion or replacement of left ventricular assist device or other aortic circulation support system.

In Plain Language

Heart pump surgery insertion; Surgical mechanical heart support placement

Clinical Context

Surgical insertion of LVAD for destination therapy or bridge to transplantation in end-stage heart failure, or replacement of existing device.
