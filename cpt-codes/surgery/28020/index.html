28020 Exploration of foot joint

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ankle arthrotomy, ankle joint exploration, ankle open exploration

Arthrotomy (surgical opening) of the ankle joint or ankle region. This procedure provides direct visualization and access to the ankle joint for diagnosis or treatment of intra-articular pathology.

In Plain Language

opening the ankle joint; ankle joint surgery

Clinical Context

Performed for loose bodies, synovitis, early arthritis debridement, or assessment of joint damage. Often part of ankle fracture management or infection evaluation.
