12014 repair f/e/e/n/l/m 5.1-7.5 centimeters

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: Simple facial laceration repair, Facial wound closure

Repair of simple laceration or wound on the face, ear, eyelid, nose, lip, or mucous membrane measuring five point one to seven point five centimeters.

Clinical Context

Used for simple closure of medium-sized facial lacerations requiring careful technique for cosmetic outcome.
