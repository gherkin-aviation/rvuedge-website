34820 Opn iliac artery expos

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: open iliac exposure, iliac cutdown, iliac artery exposure

Open iliac artery exposure for vascular procedures. Surgical access and exposure of iliac artery.

Clinical Context

Used for surgical exposure of iliac artery when percutaneous access is not feasible or for endarterectomy/repair.
