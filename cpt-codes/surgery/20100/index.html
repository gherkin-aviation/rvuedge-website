20100 exploration pentrg wound neck

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: neck wound exploration, penetrating neck injury exam

Surgical exploration of a penetrating wound to the neck to identify and evaluate structure damage. This procedure examines the wound tract and assesses injury extent.

Clinical Context

Used for evaluation and management of penetrating neck trauma to assess for vascular, airway, esophageal, or neurovascular injury. Essential for appropriate triage and definitive treatment planning.
