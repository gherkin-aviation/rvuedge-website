63015 Remove spine lamina >2 crvcl

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: extensive cervical laminotomy, multilevel cervical decompression

Laminotomy with removal of lamina for greater than two levels in cervical spine. Extensive cervical decompression.

Clinical Context

Used for extensive cervical decompression when more than two levels require lamina removal.
