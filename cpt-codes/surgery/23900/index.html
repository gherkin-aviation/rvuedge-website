23900 Interthoracoscplr amputation

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: interthoracoscapular amputation, interscapulothoracic amputation, shoulder girdle amputation

Interthoracoscapular amputation (amputation through shoulder disarticulation and chest wall). Disarticulation of upper extremity at glenohumeral joint with chest wall reconstruction.

In Plain Language

removal of arm and shoulder blade; shoulder and chest wall amputation

Clinical Context

Rare procedure for massive shoulder girdle tumors, severe brachial plexus injuries, or large infected/necrotic shoulder girdle tissue; addresses extensive pathology.
