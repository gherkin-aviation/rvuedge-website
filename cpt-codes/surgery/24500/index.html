24500 Cltx humeral shft fracture without mnpj

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: closed treatment humerus shaft fracture, nonoperative fracture treatment

Closed treatment of humeral shaft fracture without manipulation. Conservative management of non-displaced humeral fracture.

Clinical Context

Used for stable, minimally displaced humeral shaft fractures treated conservatively with immobilization.
