27265 Treat hip dislocation

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: hip dislocation traction, closed traction reduction

Closed treatment of hip dislocation with application of traction and external immobilization devices for stabilization.

In Plain Language

Hip traction treatment; Non-surgical traction for hip

Clinical Context

Used for hip dislocations managed conservatively through traction and immobilization when closed reduction is successful.
