33272 removal of subcutaneous defibrillator

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: S-ICD removal, subcutaneous defibrillator removal

Removal of an implanted subcutaneous defibrillator device, extracting the entire subcutaneous system.

Clinical Context

Performed for device infection, malfunction, patient choice, or conversion to traditional transvenous ICD.
