62291 Njx procedures discography crv/thrc

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: cervical thoracic discography, disc injection diagnostic

Injection of contrast material via cervical or thoracic puncture for discography. Includes visualization of disc structure.

Clinical Context

Used for diagnostic evaluation of cervical or thoracic intervertebral disc pathology.
