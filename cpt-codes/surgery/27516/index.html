27516 Treat thigh fracture growth plate

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: physeal fracture treatment, epiphyseal fracture fixation

Treatment of a femoral fracture involving the growth plate (physeal or epiphyseal fracture) in pediatric patients with closed reduction or internal fixation.

Clinical Context

Used for femoral epiphyseal or physeal fractures in children. Risk of growth disturbance requires careful management.
