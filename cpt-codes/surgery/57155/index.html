57155 Insert uteri tandem/ovoids

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: Tandem and ovoid insertion, Intracavitary brachytherapy setup

Insertion of tandem (intrauterine) and ovoids (lateral vaginal applicators) for brachytherapy. Placement of intracavitary radiation applicators.

Clinical Context

Used for cervical or endometrial cancer brachytherapy. Provides high-dose radiation to primary tumor.
