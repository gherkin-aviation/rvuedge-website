28307 Incision of metatarsal

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: metatarsal incision with tissue removal, metatarsal access, metatarsal decompression

Surgical incision into a single metatarsal bone with additional complexity such as tissue removal, biopsy, or extended decompression for pathology such as stress fractures or bone marrow edema.

Clinical Context

Used for metatarsal stress fractures, bone marrow edema, or other pathology requiring surgical access with more extensive tissue involvement than basic incision.
