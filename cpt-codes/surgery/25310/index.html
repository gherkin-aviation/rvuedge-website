25310 Transplant forearm tendon

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: tendon transfer, tendon rerouting

Transplantation (transfer) of a forearm tendon from one location to another to restore lost function.

Clinical Context

Performed for nerve injuries, muscle paralysis, or loss of specific hand functions. Transferred tendons must have appropriate origin with adequate power.
