26433 Repair finger tendon

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Finger tendon repair variant, Alternative repair technique, Specialized finger repair

Surgical repair of a finger tendon in an alternative anatomic location or using a different repair technique than code 26432, allowing for reporting of variant repair scenarios.

Clinical Context

Used for primary repair of finger tendons when the specific anatomic pattern or technique differs from standard 26432 scenarios, allowing appropriate code selection.
