60512 Autotransplant parathyroid

Surgery

Also known as: parathyroid autotransplant, parathyroid banking, parathyroid tissue reimplantation

Autotransplantation of parathyroid tissue, typically removing a parathyroid gland and implanting a portion of it in the forearm muscle or sternocleidomastoid muscle for vascular access.

Clinical Context

Used when total parathyroidectomy is performed (such as in multiple endocrine neoplasia syndrome) to preserve parathyroid function by implanting tissue in easily accessible location. Allows future re-operation if hypoparathyroidism develops.
