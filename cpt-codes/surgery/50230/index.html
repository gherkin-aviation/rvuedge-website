50230 Removal kidney open radical

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: radical nephrectomy, open radical nephrectomy, cancer nephrectomy

Open surgical removal of the kidney (radical nephrectomy) including adjacent structures such as the adrenal gland and surrounding fat for malignancy.

Clinical Context

Used for renal cell carcinoma and other malignancies of the kidney. Standard approach includes removal of adrenal gland, perinephric fat, and Gerota's fascia.
