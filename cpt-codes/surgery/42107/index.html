42107 Excision lesion mouth roof

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: palatal lesion excision, extensive palate lesion removal

Surgical excision and removal of a large or extensive lesion from the palate, often requiring complex reconstruction and closure techniques.

Clinical Context

Complex surgical removal of large palatal lesions, malignancies, or extensive pathology requiring significant tissue removal and reconstructive techniques.
