33925 repair pul artery unifocal without cpb

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Unifocal PA repair, single PA branch repair, PA plasty without CPB

Repair of pulmonary artery unifocal branch stenosis without cardiopulmonary bypass; surgical reconstruction of a single pulmonary artery branch stenosis without extracorporeal support.

Clinical Context

Off-pump repair of single-site pulmonary artery branch stenosis. Simpler approach for lesions amenable to closed-heart technique without requiring cardiopulmonary bypass.
