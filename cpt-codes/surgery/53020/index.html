53020 Incision of urethra

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: membranous urethrotomy, perineal urethrotomy

Surgical incision of the membranous urethra or perineal body to relieve obstruction or manage post-traumatic strictures.

Clinical Context

Used for post-traumatic strictures of membranous urethra. Requires careful preservation of sphincter function.
