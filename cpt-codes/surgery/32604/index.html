32604 Thoracoscopy wbx sacral

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: VATS pleural biopsy, thoracoscopic pleural biopsy

Thoracoscopic biopsy of the pleural sac, obtaining tissue samples of parietal pleura for histological diagnosis of pleural pathology.

Clinical Context

Used for diagnosis of pleural disease including mesothelioma, tuberculosis, or malignancy when pleural involvement is suspected.
