33216 Insert 1 electrode pm-defib

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: single ICD lead insertion, lead for pacemaker-defibrillator, single defibrillator lead

Insertion of single transvenous electrode for combination pacemaker-defibrillator system. Addition of single lead for hybrid device.

Clinical Context

Used for patients with need for both pacing and defibrillation capability. Single lead system simplifies implantation.
