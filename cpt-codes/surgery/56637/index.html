56637 Vlvctmy radiation complicated bi lymphad

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Radical complete vulvectomy with bilateral node dissection

Radical vulvectomy with complete tissue removal and bilateral lymphadenectomy. Removes entire vulva with lymph node dissection on both sides.

In Plain Language

Complete deep vulva removal with both-sided lymph nodes

Clinical Context

Standard approach for most primary vulvar carcinomas. Provides comprehensive staging and treatment of regional nodes.
