31255 Nsl/sinus ndsc with tot ethmdct

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: endoscopic total ethmoidectomy, complete ethmoid ESS, total ethmoid removal

Nasal and sinus endoscopic surgery with total ethmoidectomy, completely removing all ethmoid air cells and mucosa.

Clinical Context

Comprehensive endoscopic removal of entire ethmoid sinus complex. For advanced ethmoid disease, mucoceles, or revision surgery.
