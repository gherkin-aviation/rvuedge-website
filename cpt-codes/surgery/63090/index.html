63090 Remove vertebral body dcmprn lumbar

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: lumbar vertebrectomy, lumbar corpectomy, lumbar vertebral body removal

Removal of vertebral body for decompression in the lumbar region. This extensive procedure involves lumbar vertebrectomy to decompress the lumbar spinal cord and cauda equina.

In Plain Language

lower back bone removal for spinal decompression

Clinical Context

Used for lumbar pathology including vertebral body tumors, trauma, severe stenosis, or substantial disc pathology causing myelopathy or cauda equina compression.
