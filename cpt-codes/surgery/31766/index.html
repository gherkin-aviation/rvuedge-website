31766 Carinal reconstruction

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: carinal reconstruction, carinal resection, carina repair

Carinal reconstruction (surgical reconstruction of the carina, the point where the trachea divides into mainstem bronchi) for stenosis, malacia, or malignancy affecting the carinal region.

Clinical Context

High-complexity procedure for carinal stenosis, malacia, or primary carinal malignancy. Requires cardiothoracic expertise and cardiopulmonary bypass capability.
