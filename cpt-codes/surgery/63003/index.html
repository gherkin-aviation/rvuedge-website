63003 Remove spine lamina 1/2 thrc

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: thoracic laminotomy, thoracic decompression

Laminotomy with removal of lamina and exploration in thoracic spine for one or two levels. Includes inspection of neural elements.

Clinical Context

Used for thoracic spinal decompression by removal of lamina in cases of myelopathy.
