33530 Coronary artery bypass/reop

Surgery

Also known as: redo CABG, repeat bypass, re-operative CABG

Surgical coronary artery bypass graft performed as a reoperation or revision surgery, addressing graft patency loss or new stenoses in previously revascularized vessels.

Clinical Context

Indicated when previous CABG grafts have failed or new stenoses develop; associated with increased operative risk, longer operative time, and requires careful conduit selection and planning.
