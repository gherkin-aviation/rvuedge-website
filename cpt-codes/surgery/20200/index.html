20200 Muscle biopsy superficial

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: superficial muscle biopsy, muscle biopsy superficial

Needle or open biopsy of superficial muscle tissue to obtain tissue sample for histologic diagnosis. This procedure evaluates inflammatory, dystrophic, or other muscular disorders.

In Plain Language

taking a small sample of muscle under the skin

Clinical Context

Used to diagnose myopathies, inflammatory muscle diseases, dystrophies, or metabolic muscle disorders when clinical features and testing suggest muscular pathology.
