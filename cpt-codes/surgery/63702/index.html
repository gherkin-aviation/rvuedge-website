63702 Repair of spinal herniation

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: meningocele repair, spinal meningocele closure

Repair of spinal cord meningocele with closure of associated skin defect. This procedure involves correction of cerebrospinal fluid protrusion through meningocele with skin repair.

In Plain Language

closure of spinal fluid bulge through skin

Clinical Context

Used for repair of meningoceles, which are dural herniations with cerebrospinal fluid but no nerve tissue involvement.
