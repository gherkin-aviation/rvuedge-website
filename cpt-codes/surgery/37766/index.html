37766 Phleb veins - extremity 20+

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: Stab phlebectomy, Multiple segment phlebectomy

Stab phlebectomy of veins in the extremity involving removal of 20 or more individual varicose vein segments through small incisions.

Clinical Context

Used for extensive branch varicosities throughout the lower extremity or comprehensive removal of all varicose tributaries.
