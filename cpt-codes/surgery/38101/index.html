38101 Removal of spleen partial

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Partial splenectomy, Splenic salvage

Surgical removal of a portion of the spleen (partial splenectomy) while preserving remaining splenic tissue and function.

Clinical Context

Performed for splenic trauma with preservation of immune function, splenic infarction, or certain benign splenic tumors.
