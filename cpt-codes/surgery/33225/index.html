33225 L ventric pacing lead add-on

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: LV lead insertion, left ventricular lead, biventricular add-on

Left ventricular transvenous pacing lead insertion. Add-on procedure placing lead in left ventricle for cardiac resynchronization therapy.

Clinical Context

Used to add left ventricular pacing to existing single or dual-chamber systems for cardiac resynchronization therapy in heart failure.
