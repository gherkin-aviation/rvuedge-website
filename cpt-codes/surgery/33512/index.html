33512 Cabg vein three

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: three vessel CABG, triple vein bypass, three vein CABG

Surgical placement of three coronary artery bypass grafts using saphenous vein conduits, revascularizing three coronary territories.

Clinical Context

Indicated for triple vessel coronary disease; associated with improved long-term outcomes and reduced mortality compared to medical therapy in patients with left main disease or impaired left ventricular function.
