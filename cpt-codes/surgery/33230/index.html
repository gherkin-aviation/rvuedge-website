Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: dual-chamber pacemaker insertion, dual-lead generator placement, DDD generator

Insertion of pulse generator for dual-chamber pacemaker system with complete device including dual transvenous leads. Comprehensive dual-chamber implantation.

In Plain Language

Clinical Context

Used for bradycardia with atrioventricular block or sinus node dysfunction. Provides physiologic dual-chamber pacing.
