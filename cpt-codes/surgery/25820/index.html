25820 Arthrd wrist lmtd without bone grf

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: limited wrist fusion, partial wrist arthrodesis

Arthrodesis (surgical fusion) of the wrist joint, limited fusion (partial) without bone graft.

In Plain Language

partially fusing wrist joint without bone transplant; selective wrist bone fusion

Clinical Context

Used for partial or limited wrist fusion (radiocarpal only or midcarpal only) when full wrist arthrodesis is not required. Preserves some wrist motion.
