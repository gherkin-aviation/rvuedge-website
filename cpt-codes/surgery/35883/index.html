35883 Revision fem anast nonautog grf

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: femoral anastomotic revision, prosthetic graft revision, anastomosis repair

Revision of femoral anastomosis of nonautologous (prosthetic) bypass graft. Addresses stenosis or repair at graft-artery junction.

In Plain Language

fixing the connection where a plastic bypass connects to the leg artery; repairing prosthetic graft problems

Clinical Context

Performed for anastomotic intimal hyperplasia causing graft stenosis or thrombosis.
