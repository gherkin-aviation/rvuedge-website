62141 Crnop skull defect>5 centimeters diam

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: large cranioplasty, major skull defect repair

Cranioplasty for repair of skull defect greater than 5 centimeters in diameter using bone graft, bone flap, or prosthetic material.

Clinical Context

Used for cranioplasty procedures addressing large skull defects exceeding 5 centimeters, often requiring bone grafting or alloplastic materials.
