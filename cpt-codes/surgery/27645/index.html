27645 Resection tibia tumor

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: tibia tumor resection, tibial osteosarcoma resection

Comprehensive surgical resection of tibial tumor including margins with oncologic principles and potential reconstruction planning.

Clinical Context

Performed for malignant bone tumors (osteosarcoma, Ewing sarcoma) with wide margin resection. Highest work RVU reflects complex oncologic procedure.
