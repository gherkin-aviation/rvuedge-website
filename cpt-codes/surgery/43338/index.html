43338 Esophageal lengthening

Surgery

Also known as: esophageal lengthening, Collis gastroplasty, esophagoplasty

Esophageal lengthening procedure performed alone or as an adjunctive procedure such as Collis gastroplasty via open approach. This creates functional esophageal length.

Clinical Context

Performed independently or as part of complex hernia repair; allows safe closure of large hernias without excessive tension.
