22103 Remove extra spine segment

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: additional vertebral segment ostectomy, per additional level ostectomy

Partial ostectomy of each additional vertebral segment. Each additional vertebra requiring partial ostectomy beyond the first segment in a staged or combined approach.

In Plain Language

additional vertebra bone removal

Clinical Context

Used as add-on code for each additional vertebra requiring ostectomy in procedures involving multiple levels. Typically appended to primary vertebral ostectomy codes when treating multilevel pathology.
