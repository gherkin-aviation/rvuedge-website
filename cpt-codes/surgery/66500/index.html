66500 Incision of iris

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: laser iridotomy, PI, peripheral iridotomy

Iridotomy/iridectomy by laser surgery (e.g., for glaucoma, prior to refractive surgery).

Clinical Context

Laser iridotomy for angle-closure glaucoma, pupillary block, or pre-refractive surgery. Safe, effective first-line therapy.
