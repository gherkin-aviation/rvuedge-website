61156 Burr hol aspiration hematoma/cyst icer

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: burr hole chronic subdural hematoma aspiration, subdural catheter placement

Burr hole and/or trephination with aspiration of chronic subdural hematoma and/or other fluid collection, with potential placement of subdural catheter.

Clinical Context

Used for chronic subdural hematoma (>20 days), often bilateral. Percutaneous needle aspiration or twist-drill craniostomy may be preferred for initial management; burr hole reserved for failures or complex cases.
