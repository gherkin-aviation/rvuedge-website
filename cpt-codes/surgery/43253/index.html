43253 Egd ultrasound transmural injxn/mark

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: EGD EUS injection, endoscopic ultrasound-guided injection, transmural marking

Esophagogastroduodenoscopy with endoscopic ultrasound-guided transmural injection or marking of esophageal or gastric lesions. This combined technique guides therapeutic or diagnostic intervention.

In Plain Language

ultrasound-guided treatment injection; marking tumor for surgery

Clinical Context

Used for submucosal tumor assessment, fiducial marker placement for radiation therapy, or therapeutic agent delivery; improves precision of interventions.
