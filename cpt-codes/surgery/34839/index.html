34839 Plnning pt specimen fenest graft

Surgery

Also known as: fenestrated graft planning, custom EVAR planning

Planning and preparation of patient-specific fenestrated graft. Preoperative planning, imaging analysis, and graft customization for fenestrated EVAR.

Clinical Context

Used for complex aortic aneurysms with branch vessel involvement. Requires specialized imaging and manufacturing.
