36823 Insertion of cannula(s)

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: AV graft, synthetic graft, internal graft, PTFE graft

Insertion of cannula for hemodialysis, arteriovenous; internal, nonautogenous (synthetic graft); external and internal approach.

Clinical Context

Used for complex synthetic graft placement requiring both external exposure and vascular access.
