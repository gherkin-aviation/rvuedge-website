25028 incision and drainage f/arm and/or wrist dp absc/hematoma

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: incision and drainage forearm abscess, I&D deep space infection

Incision and drainage of a deep abscess or hematoma in the forearm and/or wrist with removal of infected or blood-filled material.

In Plain Language

draining infection from forearm; opening and draining pus

Clinical Context

Performed for deep soft tissue infections, particularly those involving the forearm compartments or wrist space. Common in immunocompromised patients or following penetrating trauma.
