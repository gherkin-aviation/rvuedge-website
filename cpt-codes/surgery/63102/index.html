63102 Remove vertebral body dcmprn lumbar

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: lumbar anterior corpectomy with reconstruction, lumbar vertebrectomy with fusion

Removal of vertebral body with anterior decompression and reconstruction in the lumbar region. This comprehensive procedure includes lumbar vertebrectomy with anterior instrumented fusion.

Clinical Context

Used for lumbar vertebral body pathology requiring anterior decompression and stabilization, including trauma, tumor, or severe degenerative disease.
