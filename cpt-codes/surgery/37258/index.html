37258 Revsc evasc ivt stent sf first

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: infrapop stent simple first, tibial stent first

Percutaneous or open revascularization of the infrapopliteal artery (tibial or peroneal) using stent placement with simple technique in first vessel.

Clinical Context

Stenting of tibial/peroneal arteries in critical limb ischemia. Simple angioplasty with stent, first vessel treated.
