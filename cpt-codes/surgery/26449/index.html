26449 Release forearm/hand tendon

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Forearm and hand release, Extensive contracture release, Proximal release

Surgical release of contractured forearm and hand fascia and tendons, extending the scope of contracture release to include forearm structures to restore comprehensive hand function.

Clinical Context

Performed when contractures extend into the forearm, as may occur with complex post-traumatic cases, burn contractures, or extensive Dupuytren's disease.
