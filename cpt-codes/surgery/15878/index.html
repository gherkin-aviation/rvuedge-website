15878 Suction lipectomy upper extremity

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: arm lipo, upper arm liposuction

Suction-assisted lipectomy of the upper extremities including arms, forearms, and hands to reduce excess fatty tissue.

In Plain Language

Fat removal from arms; Arm contouring surgery

Clinical Context

Performed for cosmetic improvement of upper arm fullness or in post-bariatric patients with significant arm redundancy.

RVU Information
