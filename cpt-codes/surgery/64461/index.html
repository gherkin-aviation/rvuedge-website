64461 Pvb thoracic single injection site

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: thoracic paravertebral block, PVB thoracic single, thoracic nerve block

Paravertebral nerve block for thoracic region with medication injection at a single injection site for anesthetic and/or steroid administration.

In Plain Language

upper back nerve pain injection; thoracic pain relief shot

Clinical Context

Used for thoracic pain, chest wall pain, and anesthesia for thoracic and upper abdominal surgery. Single injection simplifies administration.
