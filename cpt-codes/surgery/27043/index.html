27043 excision hip pelvis lesion sternoclavicular 3 centimeters/>

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: hip lesion excision small, pelvic soft tissue removal, superficial lesion excision

Surgical excision of a hip or pelvic soft tissue lesion of superficial extent less than or equal to 3 centimeters.

Clinical Context

Indicated for small benign soft tissue lesions, lipomas, or cysts of the hip region requiring complete excision for diagnosis.
