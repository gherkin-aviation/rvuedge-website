12020 Treatment superficial wnd dehsn simple closure

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: Wound dehiscence repair, Reopening closure

Treatment of superficial wound dehiscence (separation of wound edges) with simple closure technique, typically performed as an outpatient procedure.

In Plain Language

Reclosure of wound that opened; Simple repair of separated surgical incision

Clinical Context

Used for management of superficial wound separation following surgery, typically minor separation not involving deep layers.
