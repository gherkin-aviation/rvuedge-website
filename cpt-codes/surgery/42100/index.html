42100 Biopsy roof of mouth

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: palate biopsy, palatal biopsy

Surgical procedure to obtain a tissue sample through biopsy from the mucous membrane of the roof of the mouth (hard palate or soft palate).

Clinical Context

Diagnostic procedure performed when suspicious lesions appear on palate to establish histologic diagnosis of ulcers, masses, or other pathology.
