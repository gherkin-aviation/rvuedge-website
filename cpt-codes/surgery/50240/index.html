50240 Nephrectomy partial

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: partial nephrectomy, nephron-sparing surgery, partial kidney removal

Partial nephrectomy (partial surgical removal of the kidney) preserving the remaining renal parenchyma for benign or malignant disease.

In Plain Language

surgical removal of part of kidney; kidney-sparing kidney surgery

Clinical Context

Used for renal cell carcinoma in solitary kidneys, bilateral disease, hereditary syndromes (Von Hippel-Lindau), chronic kidney disease, or when adequate renal function must be preserved.
