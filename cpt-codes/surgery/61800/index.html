61800 Apply srs headframe add-on

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: radiosurgery frame, stereotactic frame application

Application of stereotactic headframe or stereotactic head immobilization device for stereotactic radiosurgery or biopsy.

Clinical Context

Performed at start of stereotactic radiosurgery procedure. Includes frame placement, localization, and initial planning.
