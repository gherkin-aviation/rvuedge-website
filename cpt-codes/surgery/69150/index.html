69150 Extensive ear canal surgery

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: canal wall reconstruction, canal revision surgery

Extensive surgery of external auditory canal including canal wall reconstruction and resurfacing procedures.

Clinical Context

Used for extensive ear canal pathology. Common indications include canal cholesteatoma, canal wall down mastoidectomy, canal stricture, or reconstruction.
