62005 Treat skull fracture

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: skull fracture elevation, depressed skull fracture repair

Treatment of skull fracture with elevation of bone fragments. Includes opening of bone flap for fragment elevation and dural repair.

Clinical Context

Used for more complex skull fractures requiring surgical elevation of depressed bone fragments, often with intracranial inspection.
