61790 Treat trigeminal nerve

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: trigeminal nerve treatment, microvascular decompression

Surgical approach and treatment of trigeminal nerve including decompression, rhizotomy, or other therapeutic intervention.

Clinical Context

Performed for trigeminal neuralgia or glossopharyngeal neuralgia. Includes microvascular decompression or nerve section.
