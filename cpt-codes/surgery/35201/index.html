35201 Repair blood vessel dir neck

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: neck vessel repair, carotid/jugular repair

Repair of blood vessel injury in the neck region using direct suture technique with restoration of continuity.

Clinical Context

Surgical repair of penetrating or blunt neck vascular injuries involving carotid arteries, vertebral arteries, or jugular veins. Requires immediate vascular control and careful reconstruction to prevent stroke, thrombosis, or restenosis.
