32098 Open biopsy of lung pleura

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: open lung and pleural biopsy, combined lung-pleura biopsy

Open biopsy of the lung parenchyma and pleura performed together to evaluate pleural and parenchymal pathology, useful for diagnosis of diffuse lung disease with pleural involvement.

Clinical Context

Used when both pulmonary parenchymal and pleural disease require histological diagnosis, including suspected malignancy with pleural involvement or granulomatous disease.
