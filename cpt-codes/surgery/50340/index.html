50340 Recipient nephrectomy

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: recipient nephrectomy, native nephrectomy, pretransplant nephrectomy

Surgical removal of the native (patient's own) kidney in a renal transplant recipient to prepare for or facilitate the transplantation.

Clinical Context

Performed when chronic renal disease with uncontrolled hypertension, recurrent infections, or massive proteinuria warrants removal of native kidneys before transplant.
