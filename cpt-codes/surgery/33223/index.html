33223 Relocate pocket for defib

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ICD relocation, defibrillator pocket revision, device relocation

Relocation of implantable defibrillator pulse generator to a different site. Surgical movement of defibrillator device pocket.

Clinical Context

Used to address infection risks, patient comfort, or anatomic issues. Requires new pocket creation while preserving existing leads.
