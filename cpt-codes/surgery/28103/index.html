28103 Remove/graft foot lesion

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: foot lesion excision bone with graft, bone cyst removal with coverage, complex foot lesion excision

Excision of lesion or cyst of the foot including bone with skin graft or flap reconstruction. This procedure removes lesions involving bone and performs reconstruction.

Clinical Context

Performed for bone lesions (such as ganglion cysts, cystic lesions, or bony exostoses) requiring reconstruction. Addresses both bone and soft tissue defect.
