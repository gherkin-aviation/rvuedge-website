64859 Nerve surgery

Surgery

Also known as: additional nerve repair

Add-on code for repair of one or more additional nerve injuries at different levels or anatomical sites during the same operative session.

In Plain Language

Clinical Context

Used when multiple nerves at different locations require repair. Each additional nerve repair is coded with this add-on code.
