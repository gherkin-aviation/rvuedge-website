20610 drainage/injection joint/bursa without ultrasound

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: joint aspiration, bursa injection, large joint injection, knee injection, shoulder injection

Drainage or injection into a joint or bursa without ultrasound guidance.

In Plain Language

fluid removal from joint; injection into joint or fluid-filled sac

Clinical Context

Used for therapeutic or diagnostic drainage of synovial fluid from joints or bursae, including treatment of effusions, aspiration of infected fluid, or injection of corticosteroids or anesthetics.
