44121 Removal of small intestine

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: additional SB resection, second segment resection, multiple segmental resections

Add-on code for each additional segment of small intestine resected during the same operative session.

Clinical Context

Used when multiple non-contiguous segments of small bowel are resected in same operation, or when multiple sites within same segment require resection.
