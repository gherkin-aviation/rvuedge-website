64905 Nerve pedicle transfer

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: nerve pedicle transfer leg, vascularized nerve transfer

Surgical transfer of a nerve pedicle (nerve with attached vascular supply) to restore function to denervated muscle or tissue in the lower extremity.

Clinical Context

Used for lower extremity paralysis or severe denervation when direct repair impossible. Pedicle maintains blood supply to facilitate regeneration.
