58558 Hysteroscopy biopsy

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: hysteroscopic biopsy, hysterosc biopsy

Hysteroscopy with endometrial biopsy involves visualization of uterine cavity while obtaining endometrial tissue for histopathologic evaluation.

Clinical Context

Allows direct visualization while sampling endometrium. Used for abnormal bleeding, suspected endometrial pathology, or atypical cells on cytology.
