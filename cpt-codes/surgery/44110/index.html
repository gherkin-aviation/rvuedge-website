44110 Excise intestine lesion(s)

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: bowel lesion excision, intestinal lesion removal, segmental resection

Excision of one or more bowel lesions (benign or malignant) via open approach with primary anastomosis when appropriate.

Clinical Context

Surgical removal of benign (polyps, Meckel diverticulum) or malignant lesions of small or large bowel with restoration of continuity.
