21175 Reconstruction bfrnt sp orb rm & lw fhd

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: bifrontal orbital reconstruction, anterior orbit repair, superior orbit reconstruction

Reconstruction of the bifrontal and superior orbital areas with removal and repositioning of orbital contents for anterior orbital repair.

Clinical Context

Used for extensive frontal and superior orbital injuries, tumors, or deformities. More extensive than superolateral reconstruction.
