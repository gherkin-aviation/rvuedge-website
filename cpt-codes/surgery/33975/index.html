33975 Implant ventricular device

Surgery

Also known as: VAD implantation, LVAD implant, ventricular assist device

Implantation of ventricular assist device; surgical implantation of left or biventricular assist device for circulatory support as bridge to transplantation or destination therapy.

In Plain Language

Heart pump implantation; Mechanical heart support surgery

Clinical Context

Surgical implantation of ventricular assist devices for end-stage heart failure when conventional therapy has failed, as bridge to transplantation or as destination therapy.
