15733 Muscle myoq/fscq flp h & n pedcl

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: pedicled muscle flap, myocutaneous flap

Muscle and myocutaneous or fasciocutaneous flap transfer with preserved vascular pedicle for head and neck reconstruction. Muscular flap for complex defects.

In Plain Language

muscle and skin flap for head or neck; muscular tissue transfer

Clinical Context

Used for large head and neck defects, oral reconstruction, and pharyngeal repair where muscle flaps provide bulk and vascularization.
