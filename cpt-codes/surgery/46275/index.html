46275 Remove anal fist inter

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: intersphincteric fistula excision, intermediate fistula removal, fistulotomy

Surgical removal of an intersphincteric (intermediate) anal fistula. This fistula tract traverses between the internal and external anal sphincters.

Clinical Context

Performed for intersphincteric fistulas, which can be treated by fistulotomy or advancement flap repair depending on anatomy and risk to continence.
