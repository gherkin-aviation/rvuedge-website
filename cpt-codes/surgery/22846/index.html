Surgery

Also known as: Pedicle screw fixation extensive, Long construct instrumentation

Posterior segmental instrumentation (eg, pedicle screw, hook); 8 or more segments.

Clinical Context

Posterior segmental instrumentation for severe deformity or extensive instability; longest possible posterior construct.
