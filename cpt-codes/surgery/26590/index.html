26590 Repair finger deformity

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Finger deformity repair, Digital reconstruction, Congenital correction

Surgical repair of a finger deformity, including correction of congenital or acquired abnormalities affecting finger anatomy, alignment, or function.

Clinical Context

Indicated for various finger deformities including congenital anomalies, post-traumatic deformity, or other conditions requiring comprehensive finger reconstruction.
