26437 Realignment of tendons

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Tendon realignment, Tendon repositioning, Anatomic correction

Surgical realignment or repositioning of tendons in the hand or finger to restore proper anatomic relationships and improve functional outcomes, often performed after prior surgery or trauma.

Clinical Context

Performed for malpositioned tendons, poor tendon routes, or subluxation that impairs function. May follow prior repair, transfer, or occur after trauma.
