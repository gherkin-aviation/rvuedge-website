53453 Tprnl balo cntnc device removal each

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: balloon device removal, transurethral balloon removal

Transurethral removal of each balloon continence device previously inserted, performed as outpatient or office procedure.

Clinical Context

Used when balloon continence devices require removal due to patient preference, inadequate efficacy, or complications. Each device removal is separately reportable.
