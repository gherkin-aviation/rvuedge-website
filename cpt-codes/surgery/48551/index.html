48551 Prep donor pancreas

Surgery

Also known as: Pancreas donor preparation, bench work, graft preparation

Preparation and bench work on a donor pancreatic allograft including perfusion, flushing, and trimming of surrounding tissues prior to transplantation.

Clinical Context

Performed in transplant laboratory to preserve graft viability and prepare organ for implantation into recipient.
