46716 Rep perf anoper/vestib fistu

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: rectovestibular fistula repair, perineal-vestibular repair, fistula reconstruction

Repair of a perineal or vestibular anorectal fistula. Reconstructs complex communication between rectum and perineal or vestibular tissues.

In Plain Language

fixing a tunnel between rectum and perineal/vaginal area; complex fistula closure

Clinical Context

Performed for complex fistulas involving vestibular or extensive perineal involvement, often requiring flap reconstruction.
