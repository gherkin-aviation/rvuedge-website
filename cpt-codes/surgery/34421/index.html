34421 Removal of vein clot

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: femoral vein thrombectomy, unilateral femoral thrombectomy

Removal of a venous thrombus or embolus from the femoral vein (unilateral). Surgical extraction of blood clot from the femoral vein.

Clinical Context

Performed for acute femoral DVT with threatening limb ischemia or high risk of complications. May preserve venous function.
