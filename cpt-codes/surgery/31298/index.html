31298 Nsl/sinus ndsc surgical frnt & sphn

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: endoscopic frontal-sphenoid ESS, bilateral posterior ESS, frontal-sphenoid endoscopy

Nasal and sinus endoscopic surgery with combined management of both frontal and sphenoid sinuses for bilateral or complex disease.

Clinical Context

Endoscopic surgery addressing disease in both frontal and sphenoid sinuses. For pansinusitis or bilateral disease.
