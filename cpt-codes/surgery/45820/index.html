45820 Repair rectourethral fistula

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: rectourethral fistula repair, rectal-urethral fistula closure

Surgical repair of rectourethral fistula (abnormal connection between rectum and urethra) via abdominal approach.

Clinical Context

Surgical management of rectourethral fistula secondary to trauma, inflammatory bowel disease, or surgical complication.
