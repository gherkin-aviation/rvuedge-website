29876 Arthrs knee surgical synvct maj

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: major synovectomy, extensive synovectomy, complete synovectomy, knee arthroscopy with chondroplasty, knee scope cartilage

Arthroscopy of the knee with major synovectomy (removal of extensive inflamed synovial tissue).

Clinical Context

Used for advanced inflammatory arthropathies, extensive rheumatoid arthritis, and synovial osteochondromatosis.
