21011 excision face lesion sternoclavicular <2 centimeters

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: facial lesion excision, small facial tumor removal

Excision of facial soft tissue lesion less than two centimeters.

Clinical Context

Removal of benign soft tissue lesions of the face including lipomas, nevi, or cysts.
