15570 Skin pedicle flap trunk

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: pedicle flap trunk, local flap

Skin pedicle flap transfer applied to trunk as an intermediate step in a planned multi-stage flap procedure. Local tissue rearrangement with preserved blood supply.

Clinical Context

Used for complex trunk defects, scar revision, and reconstruction where local tissue provides better color and texture match than distant flaps.
