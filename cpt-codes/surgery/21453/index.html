21453 Cltx mndblr fracture ntrdntl fixation

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: interdental wiring mandible, closed reduction with arch bars

Closed treatment of mandibular fracture with interdental fixation. Non-surgical stabilization using interdental wiring or arch bars to maintain proper occlusal relationship during fracture healing.

In Plain Language

jaw fracture stabilization with wires; tooth-to-tooth wiring for jaw fracture

Clinical Context

Used for mandibular fractures requiring non-operative stabilization through interdental fixation. Relies on establishing and maintaining proper occlusion during healing. Common for simple uncomplicated fractures.
