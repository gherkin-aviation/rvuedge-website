21346 Opn treatment nasomax fracture with fixation

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: nasomaxillary fracture ORIF, open nasomaxillary reduction

Open treatment of nasomaxillary fracture with skeletal fixation devices to stabilize the nasal-maxillary complex.

Clinical Context

Used for nasomaxillary fractures requiring operative reduction and fixation for skeletal stability.
