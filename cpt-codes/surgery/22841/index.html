22841 Insert spine fixation device

Surgery

Also known as: Thoracic interspinous device, Thoracic spacer

Posterior non-segmental instrumentation (eg, spinous process fixation, interspinous process device); thoracic spine.

Clinical Context

Non-segmental instrumentation for thoracic spine to provide stability and neural decompression without requiring fusion.
