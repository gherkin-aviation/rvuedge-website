29848 Wrist endoscopy/surgery

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: wrist endoscopy, endoscopic wrist surgery, wrist endo

Arthroscopy of the wrist using endoscopic techniques for diagnosis or treatment of wrist pathology.

Clinical Context

Used for minimally invasive evaluation and treatment of wrist carpal tunnel or other endoscopic wrist procedures.
