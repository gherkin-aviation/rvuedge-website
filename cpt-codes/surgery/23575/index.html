23575 Cltx scapula fracture with mnpj +-tractj

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: closed reduction scapular fracture, scapula reduction with manipulation, manipulation scapular fracture

Closed treatment of scapular fracture with manipulation and/or traction. Conservative treatment including reduction of scapular fracture with manipulation under anesthesia.

In Plain Language

realignment of shoulder blade fracture without surgery; shoulder blade fracture reduction

Clinical Context

Performed when scapular fractures require manipulation for alignment, particularly glenoid neck or body fractures with significant displacement.
