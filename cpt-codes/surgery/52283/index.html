52283 Cystoscopy and treatment

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: cysto with stent removal, stent retrieval

Cystourethroscopy with removal of a previously placed ureteral stent (double-J catheter) after treatment completion or stent dwell time.

Clinical Context

Routine procedure following stent placement for temporary management of obstruction. Timing depends on indication and stent type.
