25645 Optx crpl fracture oth/thn scph each

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ORIF carpal bone, open reduction internal fixation carpal

Open treatment (surgical repair) of carpal bone fracture (other than scaphoid) with internal fixation.

Clinical Context

Used for displaced or unstable carpal bone fractures requiring operative intervention and internal fixation. May be used for non-unions or problematic fractures.
