21154 Rcnstj mdfc lefort iii without i

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Le Fort III, bipartition osteotomy, facial advancement

Reconstruction of the midface using Le Fort III osteotomy without maxillary involvement, advancing the entire facial skeleton above the Le Fort I level.

Clinical Context

Used for severe midface hypoplasia, Crouzon syndrome, Apert syndrome, or other craniofacial deficiency.
