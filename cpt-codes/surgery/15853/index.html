15853 Removal suture/stapl xreq anesthesia

Surgery

Also known as: suture removal, staple removal no anesthesia

Removal of sutures or staples that do not require anesthesia because they are superficial or the patient can tolerate removal without additional pain management.

In Plain Language

Simple stitch removal; Staple removal without numbing

Clinical Context

Performed in office or bedside settings for superficial closures. Typically done as routine wound care when anesthesia is unnecessary due to suture depth or patient tolerance.

RVU Information

CPT 15853 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
