61540 Removal of brain tissue

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: cortical incision, brain tissue incision

Craniotomy or craniectomy with surgical incision into brain tissue (cortical incision or opening) for access to deeper lesions or functional surgery.

Clinical Context

Performed as approach to deep brain lesions, vascular malformations, or during functional surgery for electrode placement, lesion resection, or therapeutic ablation.
