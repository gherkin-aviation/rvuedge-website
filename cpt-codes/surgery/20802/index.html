20802 Replantation arm complete

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: arm replantation, upper extremity reattachment

Replantation of a completely amputated arm with vascular anastomosis and nerve repair.

In Plain Language

sewing arm back on after amputation; surgical reattachment of entire arm

Clinical Context

Emergency surgical procedure for completely amputated arm; requires immediate vessel and nerve repair to restore function and circulation.
