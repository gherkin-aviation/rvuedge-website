31225 Removal of upper jaw

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: maxillectomy, partial maxillectomy, upper jaw removal

Surgical removal of maxillary bone (partial or total resection of upper jaw), typically for tumor, infection, or severe disease via external approach.

Clinical Context

Major surgical procedure for maxillary tumors, chronic infection, or trauma. Weber-Ferguson or other external incision provides comprehensive surgical access.
