42600 Closure of salivary fistula

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: fistula closure, fistula repair

Surgical closure of an oronasal fistula or salivary fistula by primary closure, flap reconstruction, or tissue rearrangement.

Clinical Context

Surgical repair of salivary fistula from trauma, post-operative complication, or infection causing drainage from oral cavity to skin.
