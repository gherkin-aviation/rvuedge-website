54385 Repair penis

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: complex penile repair, reconstructive repair

Surgical repair of the penis with complex reconstruction requiring tissue transfer or extensive rearrangement.

Clinical Context

Used for severe penile trauma with tissue loss. Complex reconstruction restores anatomy and function.
