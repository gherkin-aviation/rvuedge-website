15260 Fth/gft fr n/e/e/l 20 sq cm/<

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: facial feature graft, eyelid/lip graft

Full-thickness or pedicle skin graft or flap, from nose, ear, eyelid, or lips, first 20 square centimeters or less. Specialized full-thickness graft from delicate facial structures.

Clinical Context

Used for eyelid reconstruction, lip repairs, and nasal defects where grafts from anatomically similar donor sites provide optimal functional and cosmetic results.
