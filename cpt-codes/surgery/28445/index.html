28445 Optx talus fracture

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: talus ORIF, open reduction talus fracture, open fixation talus

Open surgical treatment of talus fracture with internal fixation, including open reduction and hardware placement to achieve anatomic alignment for displaced talus fractures.

In Plain Language

Talus surgery; Open ankle bone surgery; Talus fracture repair

Clinical Context

Used for displaced talus fractures requiring open reduction; internal fixation restores joint surface and prevents complications like osteonecrosis and arthritis.
