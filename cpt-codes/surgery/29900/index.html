29900 Mcp joint arthroscopy diagnosis

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: MCP scope, finger joint scope, digital arthroscopy

Arthroscopic diagnostic examination and visualization of the metacarpophalangeal joint, allowing assessment of cartilage, ligaments, and intra-articular pathology without therapeutic intervention.

Clinical Context

Performed for unexplained finger joint pain, swelling, or mechanical symptoms; useful when non-operative imaging is inconclusive or when internal derangement is suspected.
