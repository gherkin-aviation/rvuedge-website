32666 Thoracoscopy with wedge resection

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: VATS wedge resection, thoracoscopic wedge

Thoracoscopic wedge resection of the lung performed as the primary/initial procedure via minimally invasive approach, removing peripheral lung tissue.

In Plain Language

camera removal of small lung piece

Clinical Context

Used for small peripheral nodules, diagnostic biopsies, or benign lesions suitable for parenchyma-sparing resection via thoracoscopic approach.

Billing & Documentation

As a surgical CPT code, proper documentation must include the operative report detailing the procedure performed, patient positioning, approach, findings, and any complications.
