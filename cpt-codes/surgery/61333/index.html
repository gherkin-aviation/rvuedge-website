61333 exploration orbit with removal lesion

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: orbital exploration, orbital lesion removal via craniotomy

Craniotomy or craniectomy with exploration of the orbit (eye socket) and/or removal of orbital lesion (tumor, abscess, hematoma).

Clinical Context

Performed for orbital tumors, abscesses, or other lesions not amenable to transocular or transconjunctival approach. Provides excellent visualization and access to orbital apex and supraorbital pathology.
