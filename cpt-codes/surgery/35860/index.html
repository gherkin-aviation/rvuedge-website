35860 Explore limb vessels

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: limb vessel exploration, extremity vascular exploration, peripheral artery exploration

Surgical exploration of limb vessels (arteries and veins) for peripheral vascular disease evaluation, ischemia assessment, or trauma management. Provides access to extremity vasculature.

Clinical Context

Performed for claudication, critical limb ischemia, graft assessment, or traumatic vascular injury.
