62165 Remove pituit tumor with scope

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: endoscopic pituitary surgery, endoscopic pituitary tumor removal

Neuroendoscopic removal of pituitary tumor via endoscopic transsphenoidal or transventricular approach. Includes navigation and tumor resection.

Clinical Context

Used for endoscopic resection of pituitary adenomas and other sellar masses using transsphenoidal or transventricular approaches.
