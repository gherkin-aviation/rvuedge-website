69676 Remove middle ear nerve

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: facial nerve removal, facial nerve transposition, facial nerve decompression

Surgical removal or transposition of the facial nerve (cranial nerve VII) in the temporal bone to treat facial nerve paralysis or injury.

Clinical Context

Used for traumatic facial nerve injury, bell's palsy with complete paralysis, post-operative facial nerve injury, or facial nerve canal widening.
