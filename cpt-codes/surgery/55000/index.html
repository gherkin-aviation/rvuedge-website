55000 Drainage of hydrocele

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: hydrocele drainage, hydrocele aspiration

Drainage of hydrocele (fluid collection around testis) using needle aspiration or small incision.

Clinical Context

Percutaneous or minimal surgical drainage of hydrocele for symptomatic relief. Often temporary measure.
