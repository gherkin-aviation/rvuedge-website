21295 Revision of jaw muscle/bone

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: jaw muscle revision, minor jaw revision

Revision of jaw muscles and/or jaw bone for minor corrections, addressing muscular dysfunction or subtle skeletal malposition.

Clinical Context

Used for minor functional or aesthetic issues with jaw muscles or skeletal position.
