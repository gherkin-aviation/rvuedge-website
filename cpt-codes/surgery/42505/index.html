42505 Repair salivary duct

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: duct repair, complex duct reconstruction

Surgical repair of a salivary duct with more complex reconstruction, including duct rerouting or major ductal system reconstruction.

Clinical Context

Complex salivary duct repair with rerouting, anastomosis, or extensive reconstruction for obstruction or ductal damage.
