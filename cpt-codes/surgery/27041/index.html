27041 Biopsy of soft tissues

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: deep hip biopsy, open pelvic biopsy, hip muscle biopsy

Surgical biopsy of deep soft tissues around the hip and pelvis through an open approach with substantial tissue removal.

Clinical Context

Used for diagnosis of deep hip or pelvic soft tissue lesions including masses or infections requiring open biopsy with adequate tissue sampling.
