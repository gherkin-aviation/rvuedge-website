58262 Vag hyst including t/o

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: vaginal hysterectomy with BSO, vaginal TAH-BSO

Vaginal hysterectomy with removal of tubes and ovaries (salpingo-oophorectomy) removes uterus, cervix, fallopian tubes, and ovaries through vaginal approach.

In Plain Language

Uterus and ovary removal through vagina; Vaginal ovary and uterus removal

Clinical Context

Combines hysterectomy with oophorectomy when ovarian pathology present or prophylactic removal desired. Vaginal approach still feasible with adequate space and mobility.
