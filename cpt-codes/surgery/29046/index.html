29046 application bdy cyst sho to hip b th

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: body cast bilateral thighs, spica cast, bilateral hip-thigh cast

Application of body cast from shoulders to hips with bilateral thigh extensions, providing trunk and bilateral lower extremity immobilization for pelvis or bilateral hip pathology.

In Plain Language

Trunk and bilateral leg cast; Hip and both leg cast; Double-leg cast

Clinical Context

Used for pelvic fractures, bilateral hip pathology, or bilateral femur involvement requiring comprehensive trunk and bilateral lower extremity immobilization.
