47380 Open ablate liver tumor rf

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: open radiofrequency ablation, open RFA, hepatic radiofrequency ablation

Open (non-laparoscopic) ablation of liver tumor using radiofrequency energy. Direct surgical access with radiofrequency ablation.

In Plain Language

burning a liver tumor with radiofrequency through an open incision; open liver ablation

Clinical Context

Performed for hepatocellular carcinoma or metastases when laparoscopic approach not feasible or tumor location requires open access.
