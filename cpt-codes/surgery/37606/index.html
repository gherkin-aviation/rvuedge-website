37606 Ligament int/com carotid artery occl

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ICA ligation with occlusion, Carotid occlusion

Surgical ligation of the internal or common carotid artery with temporary or permanent intraluminal occlusion device placement.

Clinical Context

Performed for inoperable carotid tumors, large aneurysms, or skull base lesions where permanent vessel occlusion is therapeutic.
