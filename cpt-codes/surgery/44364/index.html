44364 Small bowel endoscopy

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: small bowel endoscopy therapeutic, enteroscopy treatment, surgical bowel dilation

Endoscopy of small bowel performed via enterotomy with therapeutic intervention including stricture dilation, volvulus reduction, or removal of foreign material.

Clinical Context

Used for therapeutic small bowel intervention including dilation of strictures (Crohn's disease, adhesions), reduction of volvulus, or removal of impacted foreign bodies.
