57500 Biopsy of cervix

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: Cervical biopsy

Biopsy of cervix. Tissue sampling from cervix for histologic diagnosis outside of colposcopy.

In Plain Language

Taking tissue sample from cervix

Clinical Context
