47610 Removal of gallbladder

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Open chole with choledochoduodenostomy, biliary-enteric anastomosis

Open removal of the gallbladder with exploration and management of the bile ducts including stone removal or choledochoduodenostomy for ductal obstruction.

Clinical Context

Performed for choledochal cysts, bile duct strictures, or choledocholithiasis with duct dilation. Creates permanent internal drainage.
