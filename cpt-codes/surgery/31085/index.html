31085 Removal of frontal sinus

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: extensive frontal obliteration, complex frontal surgery, advanced osteoplastic

Removal of frontal sinus mucosa with complete obliteration and extensive management including management of lateral or posterior wall complications.

In Plain Language

extensive forehead sinus closure

Clinical Context

Extensive frontal sinus surgery addressing multiple complications or revision of failed previous osteoplastic procedures with comprehensive cavity management.
