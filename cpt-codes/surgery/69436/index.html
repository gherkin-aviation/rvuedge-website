69436 Create eardrum opening

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: operative tube insertion, tympanostomy under anesthesia, tympanostomy tube insertion, ear tubes, PE tubes

Creation of opening in tympanum for drainage with ventilation tube or grommet insertion under operative conditions.

Clinical Context

Used for tympanoplasty with tube insertion under operative conditions. Common indications include otitis media with effusion requiring operative intervention.
