55041 Removal of hydroceles

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: bilateral hydrocele excision, bilateral hydrocele repair

Surgical removal of bilateral hydroceles (fluid around both testes) with separate incisions.

Clinical Context

Surgical treatment of hydroceles affecting both testes performed during single operative session.
