45563 Exploration/repair of rectum

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: rectal repair with colostomy, rectal exploration with diversion

Exploration of rectum via abdominal or perineal approach with repair and creation of colostomy or diverting ileostomy.

Clinical Context

Surgical management of complex rectal injuries or defects requiring both repair and fecal diversion via colostomy or ileostomy.
