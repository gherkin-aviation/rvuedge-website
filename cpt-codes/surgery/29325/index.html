29325 application hip spica cast1&1/2

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: one and one half leg spica, asymmetric spica, bilateral spica

Application of a hip spica cast encompassing one and one-half legs (typically one leg fully and the contralateral leg to knee), extending from torso to feet for bilateral support.

Clinical Context

Used for bilateral hip and femoral fractures, pelvis fractures, and conditions requiring asymmetric lower extremity immobilization.
