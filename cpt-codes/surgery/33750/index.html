33750 Shunt subclavian to pulmonary artery

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: BT shunt, subclavian-pulmonary shunt, Blalock-Taussig procedure

Surgical creation of a Blalock-Taussig shunt or similar subclavian-to-pulmonary artery connection for cyanotic heart disease palliation.

In Plain Language

connecting arm artery to lung artery

Clinical Context

Classic palliative procedure for cyanotic congenital heart disease (e.g., tetralogy of Fallot, tricuspid atresia); increases pulmonary blood flow until definitive repair.
