58291 Vag hyst incl t/o complex

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: complex VH with BSO, complicated vaginal hysterectomy with ovary removal

Complex vaginal hysterectomy with removal of tubes and ovaries adds salpingo-oophorectomy to complex vaginal hysterectomy addressing ovarian pathology.

Clinical Context

Complex vaginal case with concurrent ovarian pathology requiring removal. Increased operative time and difficulty due to both uterine and ovarian factors.
