47147 Prep donor liver/arterial

Surgery

Also known as: arterial reconstruction, hepatic artery preservation, donor liver arterial prep

Preparation of donor liver with arterial reconstruction or preservation of hepatic arterial supply.

In Plain Language

preparing a donor liver with special arterial care; liver preparation with artery reconstruction

Clinical Context

Performed for complex vascular anatomy requiring arterial reconstruction to maintain graft blood supply.
