36014 Place catheter in artery

Surgery

Also known as: brachial artery line, brachial catheter, BA-line

Percutaneous placement of arterial catheter via brachial artery approach for hemodynamic monitoring or sampling. Brachial artery access.

Clinical Context

Used for critical hemodynamic monitoring when radial access unavailable. Higher complication risk.
