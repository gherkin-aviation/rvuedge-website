43763 Rplc gtube revision gstrst trc

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: G-tube replacement with revision, revised gastrostomy tube placement, tract revision

Replacement of gastrostomy tube with revision or dilation of the gastrocutaneous tract and gastrostomy site.

Clinical Context

Used when gastrostomy tube replacement requires tract dilation due to stenosis or site revision for infection, granulation, or other complications.
