61343 Craniectomy sopl crv lam dcmprn

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: supraorbital craniotomy, lamina cribrosa decompression

Craniotomy or craniectomy with supraorbital cranial vault approach and lamina cribrosa decompression for skull base lesions.

Clinical Context

Used for skull base tumors, olfactory groove meningiomas, anterior cranial fossa lesions, and optic nerve decompression. Minimally invasive skull base approach with good visualization.
