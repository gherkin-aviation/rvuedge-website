29867 Allgrft implant knee with scope

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: allograft knee, osteochondral allograft, donor cartilage transplant

Arthroscopy of the knee with implantation of allograft cartilage (osteochondral allograft) for chondral defect repair from donor tissue.

Clinical Context

Used for large osteochondral defects, revision cases, and patients without suitable autograft donor sites.
