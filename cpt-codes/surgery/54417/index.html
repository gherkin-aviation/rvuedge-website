Surgery Global 90d

Removal and replacement of a penile prosthesis with management of associated complex anatomical complications.

Clinical Context

Used when prosthesis removal and replacement involve significant additional surgical work such as fibrosis, scarring, contracture release, or anatomical reconstruction.
