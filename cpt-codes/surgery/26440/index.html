26440 Release palm/finger tendon

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Dupuytren's release, Fascia release, Contracture release, Fasciotomy

Surgical release of contractured or tight palmar and finger fascia and tendons, performed to restore range of motion and function in hands with contracture, typically related to Dupuytren's disease.

Clinical Context

Indicated for Dupuytren's contracture or other causes of palmar fascial contracture limiting finger extension. Performed when conservative treatment has failed.
