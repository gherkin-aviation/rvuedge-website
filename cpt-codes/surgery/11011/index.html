11011 Debride skin muscle at fracture site

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: Muscle debridement at fracture, Deep fracture wound debridement

Debridement of skin, subcutaneous tissue, and muscle at fracture site. Removal of skin, soft tissue, and muscle contamination or necrosis at a fracture.

In Plain Language

Fracture area muscle cleanup; Deep tissue removal at injury site

Clinical Context

Used for open fractures with contamination of muscle and deeper tissues requiring removal prior to fixation.
