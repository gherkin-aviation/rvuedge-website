37282 Rvsc evsc tpvt angio complex 1

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: tibioperoneal angioplasty complex first, TPT complex angioplasty

Percutaneous or open revascularization of the tibioperoneal trunk or artery using complex angioplasty technique in the first treated vessel.

Clinical Context

Complex lesions (chronic occlusion, calcified) requiring advanced techniques. First tibioperoneal vessel.
