49185 Sclerotx fluid collection

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: sclerotx fluid collection, sclerotherapy, fluid collection sclerosing

Sclerotherapy or injection treatment of intra-abdominal fluid collection to promote fibrosis and prevent recurrence.

In Plain Language

chemical treatment to seal fluid pocket; injection to harden fluid collection

Clinical Context

Therapeutic procedure for persistent or recurrent intra-abdominal fluid collections (seroma, abscess, lymphocele). Used to obliterate cavity space and prevent complications.
