54520 Removal of testis

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: orchiectomy, testis removal

Removal of entire testis (orchiectomy) through inguinal approach with ligation of spermatic cord.

Clinical Context

Surgical removal of testicle for malignancy, severe infection, torsion with necrosis, trauma with loss of viability, or gender-affirming care.
