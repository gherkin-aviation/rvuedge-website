35533 Artery byp graft axill/fem/fem

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: bilateral femoral bypass, femoral-femoral bypass

Arterial bypass graft from axillary or femoral artery with bilateral femoral artery connection for lower extremity revascularization.

Clinical Context

Extraanatomic revascularization for bilateral iliac occlusive disease or unilateral iliac occlusion with contralateral involvement. Alternative to aortic reconstruction.
