64713 Revision of arm nerve(s)

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: arm nerve repair multiple, arm plexus nerve revision

Surgical revision or repair of one or more nerves in the arm region due to injury, compression, or dysfunction.

Clinical Context

Used for arm nerve injuries involving brachial plexus or major nerve trunks requiring surgical repair.
