23515 Optx clavicular fracture with int fix

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ORIF clavicle, open reduction clavicle, clavicle plating

Open treatment (open reduction internal fixation) of clavicular fracture with internal fixation. Surgical repair of clavicular fracture with plate and screw fixation.

Clinical Context

Performed for displaced clavicular fractures, particularly in active patients or those with associated injuries; provides rigid fixation allowing early mobilization.
