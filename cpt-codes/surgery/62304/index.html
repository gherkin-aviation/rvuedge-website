62304 Myelography lumbar injection

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: myelography with cerebrospinal fluid sampling, lumbar myelogram with tap

Myelography with lumbar injection and spinal puncture with cerebrospinal fluid sampling. Includes diagnostic imaging.

In Plain Language

spinal cord imaging with fluid sampling; lumbar imaging with fluid test

Clinical Context

Used for diagnostic imaging of lumbar spinal cord with simultaneous cerebrospinal fluid collection for analysis.
