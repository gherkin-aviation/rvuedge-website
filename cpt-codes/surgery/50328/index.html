50328 Prep renal graft/arterial

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: graft arterial prep, renal artery preparation, arterial anastomosis prep

Preparation of a renal allograft for transplantation with specific attention to arterial anastomosis preparation and evaluation.

Clinical Context

Used when specific technical preparation of the renal artery is required, such as extending the artery graft or managing multiple arteries.
