61322 craniectomy/craniotomy dcmprv without lobec

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: decompressive craniectomy, ICP reduction surgery

Craniotomy or craniectomy with decompression for reduction of intracranial pressure without lobectomy (partial brain removal).

Clinical Context

Performed for severe traumatic brain injury with refractory intracranial hypertension, massive brain swelling, or acute ischemic stroke with malignant edema. Removes bone but preserves brain tissue.
