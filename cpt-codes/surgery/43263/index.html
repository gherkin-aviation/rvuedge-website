43263 Ercp sphincter pressure meas

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: ERCP manometry, sphincter pressure measurement, manometry catheter

Endoscopic retrograde cholangiopancreatography with measurement of pancreatic or biliary sphincter pressure using a manometer catheter. This diagnostic measure assesses sphincter function.

Clinical Context

Used to diagnose sphincter of Oddi dysfunction in patients with recurrent pancreatitis or biliary-type pain; manometry findings guide sphincterotomy decisions.
