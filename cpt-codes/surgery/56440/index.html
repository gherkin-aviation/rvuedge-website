56440 Mrsplzatn brthlns glnd cyst

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: Bartholin marsupialization, Cyst marsup

Marsupialization of a Bartholin gland cyst. This procedure creates a permanent opening to prevent recurrent cyst formation.

In Plain Language

Creating permanent opening in cyst; Permanent drainage of Bartholin cyst

Clinical Context

Preferred definitive treatment for recurrent Bartholin gland cysts. The permanent opening prevents fluid accumulation and cyst reformation. Success rate exceeds 90% with low recurrence.
