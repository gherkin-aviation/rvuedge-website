24101 Arthrotomy elbow joint exploration biopsy removal

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: elbow arthrotomy with biopsy, elbow exploration and biopsy, joint exploration biopsy

Arthrotomy of elbow, joint exploration with biopsy and removal of loose body. Opening of elbow joint with exploration, diagnostic biopsy, and loose body removal.

Clinical Context

Used for diagnostic exploration of elbow with synovial biopsy and concurrent loose body removal for evaluation of joint pathology.
