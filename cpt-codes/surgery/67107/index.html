67107 Repair detached retina

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: vitrectomy for RD, PPV for detachment, vitreous surgery for RD, vitreoretinal repair

Repair of rhegmatogenous retinal detachment by vitrectomy with membrane dissection and internal tamponade (gas or silicone oil placement).

Clinical Context

Indicated for complex detachments with posterior breaks, proliferative changes, or failed buckle surgery; offers precise membrane control and visualization.
