27732 Repair of fibula epiphysis

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: fibular epiphysis repair, fibular physeal repair

Surgical repair of fibular epiphyseal injury or non-union to restore alignment in growing patients.

In Plain Language

repair of outer leg bone growth plate

Clinical Context

Used for fibular physeal fracture-separation with complications. Less common than tibial epiphyseal repair.
