14020 Tissue trnfr s/a/l 10 square centimeters/<

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Adjacent tissue transfer, Local tissue rearrangement, Local flap

Tissue transfer or graft from adjacent area to cover defect on the scalp, shoulder, or axilla measuring ten square centimeters or less.

Clinical Context

Used for coverage of small defects on the scalp, shoulder, or axilla using adjacent tissue.
