59430 Care after delivery

Surgery

Also known as: postpartum care, postpartum visit

Postpartum care and follow-up following vaginal or cesarean delivery, including office visits and management of postpartum complications.

Clinical Context

Represents management during postpartum period (first 6 weeks) when used separate from global obstetrical care codes.
