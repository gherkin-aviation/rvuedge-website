54120 Partial removal of penis

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: partial penectomy, penile amputation

Partial amputation or removal of the penis to address malignancy, trauma, or severe infection.

Clinical Context

Indicated for penile carcinoma with tissue preservation, severe traumatic injury, or necrotizing infection. Preserves urinary and sexual function when possible.
