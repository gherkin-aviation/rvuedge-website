68371 Harvest eye tissue alograft

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: allograft harvest, tissue harvest eye bank

Harvest of eye tissue allograft (cornea or conjunctival tissue) from donor eye for transplantation purposes.

In Plain Language

harvesting donated eye tissue; collecting donor eye tissue

Clinical Context

Used for procurement of donor tissue from deceased donor eyes. Common indications include corneal transplantation, eye bank operations, or tissue procurement.
