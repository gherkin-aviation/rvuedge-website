29902 Mcp joint arthroscopy surgical

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: MCP reconstruction, finger joint reconstruction, complex digital arthroscopy

Arthroscopic surgery of the metacarpophalangeal joint with reconstruction or extensive soft tissue repair, including collateral ligament repair or major capsular reconstruction.

Clinical Context

Indicated for significant ligamentous injuries, capsular disruption, or complex intra-articular pathology requiring reconstruction to restore joint stability and function.
