68040 Treatment of eyelid lesions

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: lesion destruction, eyelid lesion treatment, laser ablation

Treatment of eyelid lesion using destructive technique such as laser, electrocautery, or cryotherapy to destroy tissue.

Clinical Context

Used for treatment of benign eyelid lesions, precancerous lesions, or small eyelid tumors. Common indications include seborrheic keratosis, xanthelasma, molluscum contagiosum, or solar keratosis of the eyelid.
