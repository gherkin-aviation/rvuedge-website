50543 Laparoscopic partial nephrectomy

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: lap partial nephrectomy, laparoscopic nephron-sparing, lap tumor enucleation

Laparoscopic partial nephrectomy (removal of part of the kidney) for benign or malignant disease with nephron-sparing intent.

In Plain Language

minimally invasive kidney-sparing surgery; laparoscopic kidney tumor removal

Clinical Context

Used for renal cell carcinoma in patients with solitary kidneys, bilateral disease, or hereditary syndromes to preserve renal function.
