50360 Rnl altrnsplj without rcp nfrct

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: kidney transplantation, renal transplant, allograft implantation

Renal allograft transplantation (transplantation of a kidney graft into the recipient) without vascular anastomosis complications.

Clinical Context

Standard renal transplant performed in end-stage renal disease patients. Graft usually placed extraperitoneally in iliac fossa with vascular anastomosis to iliac vessels.
