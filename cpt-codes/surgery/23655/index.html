23655 Cltx sho dslc with mnpj with anesthesia

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: closed reduction with anesthesia, shoulder reduction under sedation, manipulation with anesthesia

Closed treatment of shoulder dislocation (anterior, posterior, or inferior) with manipulation with anesthesia. Reduction of shoulder dislocation via manipulation under anesthesia.

Clinical Context

Performed when shoulder dislocations require anesthesia for muscle relaxation to achieve reduction, particularly in chronic or irreducible dislocations.
