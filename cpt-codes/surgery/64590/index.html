64590 Ins/rpl prph sacral/gstr npg/r

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: peripheral pulse generator implant, peripheral implantable pulse generator

Insertion or replacement of a peripheral pulse generator or receiver for a peripheral nerve stimulation or gastric system.

Clinical Context

Used for neuromodulation therapy requiring an implantable pulse generator for sacral or gastric nerve systems.
