67973 Reconstruction of eyelid

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: complex eyelid reconstruction, free flap eyelid

Complex eyelid reconstruction using free flap or staged reconstruction for total or near-total eyelid defect.

Clinical Context

Used for extensive eyelid reconstruction requiring advanced techniques. Common indications include total or near-total eyelid defects, extensive cancer resection, severe burn injuries, or extensive traumatic defects.
