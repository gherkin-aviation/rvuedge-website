50385 Change stent via transureth

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: transurethral stent exchange, TUS exchange, cystoscopic stent change

Transurethral change (exchange) of an indwelling ureteral stent under endoscopic visualization through the urethra and bladder.

In Plain Language

replacement of kidney drainage tube through urine opening; stent replacement through bladder

Clinical Context

Used for routine stent maintenance via cystoscopy without need for percutaneous access. Preferred approach for patients with intact urinary tracts.
