39010 Exploration of chest

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: chest exploration with rib resection, extensive thoracotomy

Surgical exploration of the chest cavity including resection of one or more ribs, or extensive tissue removal for therapeutic intervention. This is a more complex exploration than 39000.

Clinical Context

Used for complex chest trauma requiring rib resection, repair of thoracic wall defects, or therapeutic debridement. More extensive than simple exploration, often therapeutic.
