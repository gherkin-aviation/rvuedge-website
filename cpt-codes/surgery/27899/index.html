27899 Unlisted procedures leg/ankle

Surgery

Also known as: unlisted procedure, unlisted ankle/leg surgery, not otherwise specified

Unlisted procedure on the lower leg or ankle. This code reports any procedure involving the lower leg or ankle that does not have a specific Current Procedural Terminology code and requires documentation and medical necessity explanation.

Clinical Context

Used when innovative techniques, combinations of procedures, or rarely performed ankle/leg surgeries are performed that do not fit into existing codes. Requires detailed operative report and medical necessity documentation.
