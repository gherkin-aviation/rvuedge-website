58285 Extensive hysterectomy

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: extensive VH with BSO, complex vaginal hysterectomy

Extensive vaginal hysterectomy with removal of tubes and ovaries involves complex removal of uterus, cervix, fallopian tubes, and ovaries via vaginal approach.

Clinical Context

Addresses complex gynecologic pathology (large fibroids, severe endometriosis, cancer) with vaginal approach when feasible. Requires significant operative skill.
