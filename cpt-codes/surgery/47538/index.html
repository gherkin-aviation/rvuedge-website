47538 Percutaneous plmt bile duct stent

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: Percutaneous biliary stent placement, PTBS

Percutaneous placement of a biliary stent into the bile duct through the skin to maintain ductal patency and allow internal drainage, for benign obstruction or narrowing.

In Plain Language

Placing a tube in the bile duct through the skin

Clinical Context

Used for benign biliary strictures, post-sphincterotomy papillary stenosis, or bridge therapy before definitive surgery. Allows internal drainage.
