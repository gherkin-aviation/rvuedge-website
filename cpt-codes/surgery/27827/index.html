27827 Treat lower leg fracture

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: intramedullary nailing lower leg, IM nailing tibia-fibula

Initial open treatment of lower leg fracture with intramedullary nailing for tibial and fibular fixation.

In Plain Language

surgical repair of leg with metal nail

Clinical Context

Used for tibia and fibula fractures where intramedullary approach provides optimal fixation and early mobilization.
