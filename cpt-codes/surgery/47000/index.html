47000 Needle biopsy of liver percutaneous

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: liver needle biopsy, percutaneous liver biopsy, liver aspiration biopsy

Needle biopsy of liver performed percutaneously. Small tissue sample obtained through needle puncture for diagnostic examination.

Clinical Context

Performed percutaneously under ultrasound or computed tomography guidance to diagnose liver disease including fibrosis, cirrhosis, and hepatitis.
