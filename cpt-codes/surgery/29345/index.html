29345 Application of long leg cast

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: long leg cast, full leg cast, hip to ankle cast

Application of a long leg plaster cast extending from the hip or groin to the ankle, providing complete immobilization of the hip, knee, and ankle.

Clinical Context

Indicated for femoral shaft fractures, distal femur fractures, knee injuries, and post-operative lower extremity stabilization.
