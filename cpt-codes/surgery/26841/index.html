26841 Fusion of thumb

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: thumb fusion, thumb arthrodesis, thumb joint fusion

Surgical fusion of the thumb interphalangeal or metacarpophalangeal joint without bone graft using direct bone-to-bone apposition.

Clinical Context

Indicated for thumb joint arthritis, chronic pain, or instability where bony union is desired without the need for additional graft material.
