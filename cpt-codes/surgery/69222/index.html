69222 Clean out mastoid cavity

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: mastoid cavity revision, mastoid cavity debridement

Cleaning of mastoid cavity with debridement and management of drainage or infection using operative approach.

Clinical Context

Used for symptomatic mastoid cavity disease. Common indications include chronic mastoid cavity drainage, infection, or granulation tissue.
