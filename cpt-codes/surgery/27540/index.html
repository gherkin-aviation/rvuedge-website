27540 Treat knee fracture

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: tibial articular fracture ORIF

Open reduction and internal fixation of a tibial articular fracture with complex internal or external fixation to restore knee joint anatomy.

Clinical Context

Used for complex articular tibial fractures requiring operative fixation to restore anatomic alignment.
