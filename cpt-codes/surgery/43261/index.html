43261 Endoscopic cholangiopancreatograph

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: ERCP diagnostic, cholangiography, pancreaticography

Endoscopic retrograde cholangiopancreatography with endoscopic visualization and diagnostic imaging of the pancreatic and biliary ductal system. This foundational diagnostic ERCP procedure.

Clinical Context

Primary diagnostic tool for evaluation of biliary and pancreatic disorders including obstruction, strictures, and suspected malignancy; essential for therapy guidance.
