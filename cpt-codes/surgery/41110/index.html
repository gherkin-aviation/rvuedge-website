41110 Excision of tongue lesion

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: tongue lesion excision, tongue tumor removal, glossectomy partial

Surgical excision of a lesion or tumor of the tongue with primary closure.

Clinical Context

Used for benign or malignant lesions of the tongue requiring excision. Common with squamous cell carcinoma, benign tumors, hemangiomas, or cystic lesions.
