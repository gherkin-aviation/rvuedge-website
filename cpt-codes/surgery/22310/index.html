22310 Closed treatment vertebral fracture without manj

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: vertebral fracture conservative treatment, spine fracture no reduction

Closed treatment of vertebral fracture without manipulation. Non-surgical management of vertebral fracture with maintained alignment, typically for stable fractures.

In Plain Language

spine fracture healing without adjustment; stable spine break treatment

Clinical Context

Used for uncomplicated vertebral fractures with minimal displacement or good inherent bony contact. Conservative management includes pain control, bracing, and activity restriction. Appropriate for stable fracture patterns.
