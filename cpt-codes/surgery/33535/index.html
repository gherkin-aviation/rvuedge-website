33535 Cabg arterial three

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: triple arterial CABG, three arterial graft CABG, extensive arterial bypass, CABG three arterial grafts, triple bypass, CABG x3

Surgical placement of three coronary artery bypass grafts using primarily internal mammary and other arterial conduits.

Clinical Context

Used for triple vessel disease with predominantly arterial conduits (left and right IMA with radial artery); achieves complete revascularization with optimal graft durability.
