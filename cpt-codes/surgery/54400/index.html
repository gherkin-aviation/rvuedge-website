Surgery Global 90d

Insertion of a semi-rigid penile prosthesis for treatment of erectile dysfunction.

In Plain Language

Clinical Context

Used for erectile dysfunction unresponsive to medical therapy. Semi-rigid prosthesis is always rigid, requiring positioning.
