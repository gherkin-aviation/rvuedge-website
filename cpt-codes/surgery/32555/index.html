32555 Aspirate pleura with imaging

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: image-guided thoracentesis, ultrasound-guided tap

Percutaneous aspiration of the pleural space with real-time ultrasound or imaging guidance, providing enhanced safety and accuracy for fluid sampling or drainage.

Clinical Context

Used for diagnostic or therapeutic thoracentesis when imaging guidance improves safety, particularly for loculated effusions or difficult anatomic situations.
