61516 Craniectomy treph excision cyst sttl

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: brain cyst resection, arachnoid cyst excision

Craniotomy or craniectomy with excision of brain cyst (arachnoid, porencephalic, or other) from supratentorial location.

Clinical Context

Performed for symptomatic brain cysts causing mass effect, headaches, or seizures. Most common are arachnoid cysts and porencephalic cysts. Drainage or marsupialization sometimes sufficient.
