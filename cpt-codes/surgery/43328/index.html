43328 Esophageal fundoplasty thoracic

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: thoracic fundoplication, transthoracic Belsey, thoracic anti-reflux surgery

Thoracic fundoplication with gastroplasty via thoracic approach for treatment of gastroesophageal reflux disease with esophageal dysmotility. This approach accesses the sphincter through the chest.

Clinical Context

Alternative approach to GERD surgery via thoracotomy; reserved for specific patient populations such as short esophagus or prior abdominal surgery.
