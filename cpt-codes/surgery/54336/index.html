54336 Revise penis/urethra

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: complex penile/urethral revision, salvage revision

Revision of penis or urethra with high complexity requiring extensive reconstruction or salvage procedure.

Clinical Context

Used for complex revision cases with extensive scarring or failed prior reconstruction. Highest complexity penile/urethral revision.
