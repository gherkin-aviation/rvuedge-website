63172 Drainage of spinal cyst

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: cervical syrinx drainage, cervical cyst drainage, cervical myelotomy

Drainage of spinal cord cyst in the cervical region. This procedure involves accessing and draining cerebrospinal fluid-containing cysts within or adjacent to the cervical spinal cord.

Clinical Context

Used for symptomatic post-traumatic or idiopathic cervical syrinx causing myelopathic symptoms, often following tethered cord release or for progressive syringomyelia.
