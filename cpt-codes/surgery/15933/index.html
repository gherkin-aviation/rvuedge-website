15933 excision sacral pr ulc prim str ostc

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: sacral ulcer osteo exc, sacral ulcer bone removal

Excision of sacral pressure ulcer with primary closure and removal of underlying bone to address osteomyelitis or bone involvement.

Clinical Context

Used when sacral pressure ulcers involve infected or damaged bone. Requires osteectomy or bone debridement in addition to soft tissue excision.
