51610 Injection for bladder x-ray

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: cystogram injection, contrast cystography

Injection of contrast for bladder imaging with gravity or catheter-assisted technique, introduction of dye for cystography.

Clinical Context

Diagnostic imaging preparation for cystography. Higher RVU suggests more involved technique or catheterization.
