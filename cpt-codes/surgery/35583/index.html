35583 Vein byp graft fem-popliteal

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: vein graft fem-pop bypass, saphenous vein fem-pop

Venous bypass graft from femoral to popliteal artery (reversed saphenous vein graft or similar).

Clinical Context

Infrainguinal revascularization using autogenous vein graft. Excellent long-term patency for femoropopliteal disease; preferred over prosthetic alternatives.
