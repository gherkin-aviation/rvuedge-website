28540 Treat foot dislocation

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: foot dislocation closed, joint dislocation non-operative, closed treatment

Closed treatment of foot dislocation (typically tarsometatarsal or midfoot joint) without manipulation, used for non-displaced dislocations managed with immobilization.

Clinical Context

Used for minimally displaced or spontaneously reduced foot dislocations suitable for conservative management with immobilization.
