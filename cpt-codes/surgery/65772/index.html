65772 Correction of astigmatism

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: limbal relaxing incisions, astigmatic keratotomy, corneal incisions for astigmatism

Corneal incision for correction of astigmatism (including use of topography-guided and wavefront-guided technology); initial treatment.

In Plain Language

making small cuts to correct astigmatism; corneal incisions to fix blurry vision

Clinical Context

Surgical correction of astigmatism using limbal or corneal incisions. Can be performed at time of cataract surgery or as standalone procedure.

Billing & Documentation

As a surgical CPT code, proper documentation must include the operative report detailing the procedure performed, patient positioning, approach, findings, and any complications. This code has a 90-day global period, which includes the day of the procedure, 1 day preoperative, and 90 days of postoperative care.
