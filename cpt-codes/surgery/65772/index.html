65772 Correction of astigmatism

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: limbal relaxing incisions, astigmatic keratotomy, corneal incisions for astigmatism

Corneal incision for correction of astigmatism (including use of topography-guided and wavefront-guided technology); initial treatment.

Clinical Context

Surgical correction of astigmatism using limbal or corneal incisions. Can be performed at time of cataract surgery or as standalone procedure.
