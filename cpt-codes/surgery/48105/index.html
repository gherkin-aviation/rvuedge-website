48105 resection/debride pancreas

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Pancreatic debridement, necrosectomy, tissue resection

Surgical resection or debridement of pancreatic tissue to remove necrotic, infected, or damaged pancreatic parenchyma, typically for acute or chronic pancreatitis.

In Plain Language

Removing dead or infected pancreas tissue

Clinical Context

Performed for severe acute pancreatitis with infected necrosis or extensive necrotic pancreas. High morbidity procedure requiring experienced surgeon.
