68810 Probe nasolacrimal duct

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: nasolacrimal duct probing, simple duct probing

Probing of nasolacrimal duct using probe to open obstruction and establish tear drainage patency.

Clinical Context

Used for nasolacrimal duct obstruction. Common indications include infantile nasolacrimal duct obstruction or adult duct obstruction.
