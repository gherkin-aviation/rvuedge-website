19328 removal intact breast implant

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: implant removal, breast implant removal

Surgical removal of an intact breast implant without capsulectomy. This procedure preserves the fibrous capsule around the implant.

Clinical Context

Used for implant removal due to patient request, size change, or complications not requiring capsule removal. May be followed by reinsertion of new implant or left unfilled.
