24147 Prtl excision bone olecrn process

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: olecranon diaphysectomy, olecranon segmental excision

Partial excision (diaphysectomy) of the olecranon process. Surgical removal of a segment of the olecranon.

Clinical Context

Used for chronic osteomyelitis, segmental defects, or pathologic lesions of the olecranon process requiring partial excision.
