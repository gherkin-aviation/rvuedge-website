28113 Part removal of metatarsal

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: metatarsal mid-shaft excision, metatarsal shaft removal, metatarsal partial amputation

Partial excision of metatarsal bone with mid-shaft removal. This procedure removes a middle portion of the metatarsal shaft.

In Plain Language

removing the middle part of a metatarsal bone; metatarsal bone reduction

Clinical Context

Performed for metatarsal stress fractures, nonunion, or malunion. Restores load-bearing mechanics and reduces stress on adjacent metatarsals.
