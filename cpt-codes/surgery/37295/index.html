37295 Rvsc evsc tpvt st ath cpx each

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: tibioperoneal complex stent after atherectomy add, additional TPT advanced atherectomy plus stent

Percutaneous or open revascularization of the tibioperoneal trunk or artery using complex stent placement following atherectomy in additional vessels.

Clinical Context

Each additional tibioperoneal vessel with complex atherectomy and stent. Greatly increases procedural complexity.
