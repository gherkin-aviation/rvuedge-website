33984 Ecmo/ecls removal prph cannula

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: ECMO peripheral removal, peripheral ECLS removal, cannula removal

Extracorporeal membrane oxygenation/extracorporeal life support removal of peripheral venous/arterial cannula; removal of peripherally placed ECMO cannulae.

Clinical Context

Removal of peripheral ECMO cannulae at time of ECMO weaning and discontinuation of extracorporeal support.
