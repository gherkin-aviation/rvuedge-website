50280 excision/unroofing cyst kidney

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: cyst unroofing, marsupialization, cyst excision

Surgical excision and unroofing of a simple renal cyst (marsupialization) with management of the cyst wall.

Clinical Context

Used for symptomatic simple renal cysts causing pain, infection, or obstruction. Unroofing allows drainage of cyst fluid and prevents recurrence.
