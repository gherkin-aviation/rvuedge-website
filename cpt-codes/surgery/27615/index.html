27615 Resection leg/ankle tumor < 5 centimeters

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: tumor resection lower leg, soft tissue tumor removal <5 cm

Surgical resection of soft tissue tumor or mass less than 5 centimeters in the lower leg or ankle with en bloc removal and closure.

Clinical Context

Performed for lipomas, cysts, hemangiomas, or other benign and malignant soft tissue neoplasms. Resection approach and size are primary differentiators.
