21235 Ear cartilage graft

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ear cartilage graft, auricular graft

Graft of auricular cartilage harvested from the ear for structural support or augmentation of facial features.

Clinical Context

Used for nasal tip support, small augmentations, or when limited cartilage is needed. Less morbidity than rib harvest.
