31647 Bronchial valve initial insert

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: endobronchial valve insertion, bronchial valve placement, emphysema treatment

Bronchoscopy with initial insertion of endobronchial valves (one-way valve devices) into diseased lung segments to redirect airflow and reduce hyperinflation in emphysema.

Clinical Context

Performed in severe emphysema patients to reduce air trapping and hyperinflation, improving breathing mechanics and exercise tolerance. FDA-approved therapeutic intervention.
