23195 Resection humeral head

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: humeral head resection, hemiartheoplasty preparation, head and neck resection

Resection of humeral head (arthroplasty type resection or hemiartheoplasty preparation). Removal of humeral head for reconstruction or arthroplasty.

Clinical Context

Used as preparation for hemiarteroplasty in complex proximal humeral fractures, severe osteoarthritis, or osteonecrosis of the humeral head when anatomic reconstruction is not possible.
