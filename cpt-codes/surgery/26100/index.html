26100 Biopsy hand joint lining

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: synovial biopsy hand, joint lining biopsy

Synovial biopsy (tissue biopsy) of hand joint synovium (joint lining).

Clinical Context

Used for diagnostic biopsy of hand joint synovium to evaluate arthritis, infection, or other joint pathology.
