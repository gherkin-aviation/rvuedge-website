27177 Treat slipped epiphysis

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: SCFE repair with fixation, femoral epiphysis repair

Treatment of slipped capital femoral epiphysis via open reduction with internal fixation, involving more extensive surgical dissection and realignment than simple pinning.

Clinical Context

Applied to chronic or severely displaced slipped capital femoral epiphysis requiring greater manipulation and more robust fixation than percutaneous pinning alone.
