46604 Anoscopy and dilation

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: anoscopy with dilation, anal dilation, anoscopic dilation

Anoscopy with dilation of the anal canal. The anoscope is used to visualize the canal, and dilation is performed to relieve stenosis or improve access.

In Plain Language

looking in the anus and stretching it to widen opening; examination and stretching of anal canal

Clinical Context

Performed for anal stenosis or stricture causing symptoms of difficult bowel movements. Can be therapeutic and diagnostic.
