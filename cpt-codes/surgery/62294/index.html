62294 Injection into spinal artery

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: intrathecal arterial injection, spinal artery injection

Injection of therapeutic or diagnostic agent into spinal artery via intrathecal approach. Includes vascular access and administration.

In Plain Language

spinal artery medication injection; therapeutic artery injection

Clinical Context

Used for injection of therapeutic agents directly into spinal arteries for treatment of vascular lesions or ischemic spinal cord disease.
