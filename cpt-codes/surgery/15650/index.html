15650 Transfer skin pedicle flap

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: flap transfer, pedicle flap transfer

Transfer of previously delayed skin pedicle flap from donor site to recipient site. Second stage of delayed flap procedure for reconstruction.

Clinical Context

Used as completion of multi-stage flap reconstruction after adequate delay to ensure vascular integrity and flap success.
