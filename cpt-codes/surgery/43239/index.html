43239 Egd biopsy single/multiple

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: EGD with biopsy, esophageal biopsy, upper endoscopy with biopsy, upper endoscopy biopsy, stomach scope biopsy

Esophagogastroduodenoscopy (EGD) with biopsy of esophageal or gastric lesion(s) using tissue forceps.

In Plain Language

camera examination with tissue sample from esophagus or stomach

Clinical Context

Standard diagnostic procedure for evaluation of esophageal and gastric lesions with tissue diagnosis. Most common biopsy approach.
