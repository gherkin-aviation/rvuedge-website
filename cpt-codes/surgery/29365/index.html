29365 application cylinder cast

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: cylinder cast, thigh cylinder, cylindrical leg cast

Application of a cylinder cast extending from the groin or hip to the ankle, providing circumferential immobilization without distinct knee articulation.

In Plain Language

straight leg cast; tube-shaped leg cast

Clinical Context

Used for isolated tibia and fibula fractures, and injuries requiring full length immobilization without weight-bearing.
