68500 Removal of tear gland

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: total dacryoadenectomy, lacrimal gland removal

Complete removal of lacrimal gland including the gland tissue and associated structures (total dacryoadenectomy).

In Plain Language

removing entire tear gland; complete tear gland removal

Clinical Context

Used for removal of lacrimal gland due to malignancy, severe infection, or trauma. Common indications include lacrimal gland carcinoma, lymphoma, or severe trauma.
