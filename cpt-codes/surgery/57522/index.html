57522 Conization of cervix

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: LEEP, loop conization, LLETZ

Conization of cervix performed via loop electrosurgical excision procedure technique uses a heated wire loop to excise a cone of cervical tissue in a single pass.

Clinical Context

Diagnostic and therapeutic procedure for cervical intraepithelial neoplasia and dysplasia. LEEP is more rapid than cold knife conization and provides good visualization and hemostasis.
