61050 Remove brain canal fluid

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: brain aspiration, parenchymal puncture, brain tissue aspiration

Percutaneous needle aspiration of the brain's cerebrospinal fluid through puncture of the brain parenchyma for diagnostic sampling or therapeutic drainage.

In Plain Language

needle sampling of brain fluid; direct brain drainage

Clinical Context

Rarely performed due to risk of brain injury; primarily reserved for diagnosis of diffuse CNS infection or suspected abscess when other routes unavailable. Requires image guidance for safety.
