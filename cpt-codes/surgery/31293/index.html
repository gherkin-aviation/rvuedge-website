31293 Nsl/sinus ndsc medial & inferior dcmprn

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: total orbital decompression, comprehensive orbital ESS, extended orbital decomp

Nasal and sinus endoscopic surgery with complete medial and inferior orbital wall decompression and management of superior/lateral orbital anatomy.

Clinical Context

Most extensive endoscopic orbital decompression addressing all orbital walls. For severe Graves ophthalmopathy with vision threat.
