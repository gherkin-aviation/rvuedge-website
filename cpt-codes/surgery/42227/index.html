42227 Lengthening of palate

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: palate lengthening, secondary palate surgery

Surgical lengthening of the soft palate using secondary or revision techniques to improve velopharyngeal function or address persistent insufficiency.

In Plain Language

revision surgery to lengthen soft palate

Clinical Context

Secondary lengthening procedure for persistent velopharyngeal insufficiency, hyponasal speech, or obstructive symptoms not resolved by initial surgery.
