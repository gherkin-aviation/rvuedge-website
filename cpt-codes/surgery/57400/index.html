57400 Dilation of vagina

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: Vaginal dilation

Dilation of vagina. Mechanical dilation of vaginal stenosis or stricture.

Clinical Context

Used for vaginal stenosis from prior surgery, radiation, or congenital narrowing. Office procedure.
