44346 Revision of colostomy

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: colostomy revision, complex colostomy revision, colostomy reconstruction

Revision of colostomy with major reconstruction including significant relocation, revision of stoma site, or restoration of substantial bowel continuity.

Clinical Context

Used for complex colostomy revisions requiring major reconstruction or significant relocation of stoma. Typically follows failed simpler revisions or involves restoration of continuity.
