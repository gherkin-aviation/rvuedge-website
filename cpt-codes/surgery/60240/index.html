60240 Removal of thyroid

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: total thyroidectomy, bilateral thyroidectomy

Total removal of the thyroid gland (total thyroidectomy) for benign or malignant pathology.

Clinical Context

Performed for thyroid cancer, Graves disease, multinodular goiter, or large bilateral nodules; requires lifelong thyroid replacement.
