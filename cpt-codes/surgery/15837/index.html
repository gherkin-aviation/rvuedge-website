15837 excision excsv skin forearm/hand

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: forearm exc, hand skin removal

Excision of excessive skin and subcutaneous tissue from the forearm and hand region, addressing redundant tissue in the distal upper extremity.

Clinical Context

Performed less frequently than proximal arm excision, but used when forearm and hand skin laxity is significant. Part of comprehensive body contouring.
