43100 Excision of esophagus lesion

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: esophageal lesion excision, esophageal polyp removal

Excision (removal) of a benign lesion of the esophagus with partial esophageal wall resection.

Clinical Context

Performed for benign esophageal lesions including polyps, cysts, lipomas, or duplications. Allows histologic diagnosis and definitive treatment.
