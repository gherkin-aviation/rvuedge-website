15822 Blepharoplasty upper eyelid

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: upper lid blepharoplasty, upper eyelid surgery

Blepharoplasty of upper eyelid including removal of excess skin. Eyelid surgery to improve upper eyelid appearance and function.

Clinical Context

Used for upper eyelid ptosis, dermatochalasis, and excess skin affecting vision or appearance.
