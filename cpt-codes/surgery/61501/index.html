61501 Craniectomy f/osteomyelitis

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: skull osteomyelitis debridement, infected bone removal

Craniotomy or craniectomy performed for removal of bone and/or debridement of infected bone (osteomyelitis) of the skull.

In Plain Language

surgical opening to remove infected bone

Clinical Context

Performed for acute or chronic osteomyelitis of skull bones with abscess formation, necrotic bone, or exposed dura. Requires adequate debridement and irrigation for infection control.
