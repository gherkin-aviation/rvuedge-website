43644 Laparoscopic gastric bypass/roux-en-y

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: lap gastric bypass, LRYGB, laparoscopic Roux-en-Y, lap RNY

Laparoscopic gastric bypass with Roux-en-Y jejunal interposition for morbid obesity.

Clinical Context

Primary bariatric procedure for morbid obesity (BMI >40 or >35 with comorbidities). Creates small gastric pouch with direct jejunal anastomosis bypassing duodenum.
