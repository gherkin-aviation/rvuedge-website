25628 Optx carpl scphd fracture int fixation

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ORIF scaphoid fracture, open reduction internal fixation scaphoid

Open treatment (surgical repair) of carpal scaphoid bone fracture with internal fixation.

In Plain Language

surgical repair of broken wrist bone; screw fixation of wrist fracture

Clinical Context

Used for displaced scaphoid fractures, non-unions, or fractures with poor healing potential requiring operative intervention and internal fixation.
