27438 Revise kneecap with implant

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: patellar arthroplasty, patellar resurfacing, patellar prosthesis

Surgical revision of the patella with prosthetic implant placement, typically patellar resurfacing or arthroplasty, to treat severe patellofemoral arthritis or failed prior surgery.

Clinical Context

Used for severe patellofemoral osteoarthritis, failed patellar procedures, or isolated patellofemoral arthritis in selected patients.
