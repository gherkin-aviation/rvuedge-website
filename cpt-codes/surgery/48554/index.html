48554 Transpl allograft pancreas

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Pancreatic transplant, pancreas transplantation, PT

Transplantation of a pancreatic allograft into a recipient with placement of vascular anastomoses and ductal management for restoration of insulin independence.

Clinical Context

Performed in transplant centers for type 1 diabetes, often combined with kidney transplant. Restores glucose homeostasis.
