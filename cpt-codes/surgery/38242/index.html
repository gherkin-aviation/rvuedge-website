38242 Transplt allo lymphocytes

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: Donor lymphocyte infusion, DLI

Transplantation of allogeneic lymphocytes (donor lymphocyte infusion) to promote graft-versus-leukemia effect in allogeneic transplant recipients.

Clinical Context

Used post-allogeneic transplant to treat relapse or minimal residual disease and enhance graft-versus-leukemia effect.
