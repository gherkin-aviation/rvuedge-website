28400 Cltx calcaneal fracture without mnpj

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: calcaneal fracture treatment, heel fracture non-operative, closed reduction

Closed treatment of calcaneal (heel bone) fracture without manipulation, typically for non-displaced or minimally displaced fractures managed with immobilization alone.

Clinical Context

Used for non-displaced or minimally displaced calcaneal fractures suitable for conservative management with casting and protected weight-bearing.
