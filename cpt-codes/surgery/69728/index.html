Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: OI implant removal large transcutaneous, large transcutaneous removal

Removal of an osseointegrated implant with skull transcutaneous approach, larger device (greater than 100 square centimeters contact area).

Clinical Context

Used for larger transcutaneous osseointegrated devices with more extensive skull contact.
