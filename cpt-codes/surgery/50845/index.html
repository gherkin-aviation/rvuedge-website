50845 Appendico-vesicostomy

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: appendicovesicostomy, Mitrofanoff stoma, appendix stoma

Appendicovesicostomy (creation of Mitrofanoff stoma), surgical creation of catheterizable urinary outlet using appendix implanted into bladder or pouch.

Clinical Context

Performed in neurogenic bladder or continent diversion cases where patient desires catheterizable outlet. Appendix provides continent mechanism.
