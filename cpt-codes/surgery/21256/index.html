21256 Reconstruction of orbit

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: orbital reconstruction, eye socket repair

Reconstruction of the orbital skeleton following defect or fracture to restore proper position and protection of ocular contents.

Clinical Context

Used for orbital fractures, tumor resection, or congenital deformities. Critical for preventing enophthalmos and diplopia.
