33496 Repair prosthesis valve clot

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: prosthetic valve clot removal, valve thrombectomy, clotted valve repair

Surgical repair or replacement of a prosthetic heart valve that has developed thrombosis (blood clot), restoring normal valvular function.

Clinical Context

Performed when a prosthetic heart valve becomes obstructed by thrombus, typically manifesting as acute hemodynamic compromise or echocardiographic evidence of reduced valve motion in patients at risk for thromboembolism.
