64575 Opn impltj neuroelectrode array perph nerve

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: peripheral nerve stimulator implant, peripheral neuroelectrode implantation

Open surgical implantation of a neuroelectrode near a peripheral nerve for long-term electrical stimulation.

Clinical Context

Used for peripheral neuropathic pain, phantom limb pain, and failed conventional treatments.
