23931 incision and drainage upper a/e bursa

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: bursa drainage, bursal I and D, olecranon bursa drainage

Incision and drainage (I&D) of upper arm/elbow, bursa (olecranon or other). Drainage of bursa around upper arm or elbow joint.

Clinical Context

Used for infected or significantly enlarged bursa of upper arm/elbow; less extensive than deep tissue drainage but addresses localized bursal pathology.
