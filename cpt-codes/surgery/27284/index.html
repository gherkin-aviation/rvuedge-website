27284 Arthrodesis hip joint

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: hip fusion, hip arthrodesis

Arthrodesis of hip joint through operative approach, eliminating joint motion to treat arthritis or instability.

Clinical Context

Indicated for hip arthritis, instability, or failure of arthroplasty where fusion provides pain relief and stability.
