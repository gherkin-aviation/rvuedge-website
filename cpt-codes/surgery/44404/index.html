44404 Colonoscopy with injection

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: colonoscopy stoma injection, transcolostomy injection therapy

Colonoscopy performed through colostomy stoma with injection therapy for endoscopic treatment of bleeding or delivery of therapeutic agents.

Clinical Context

Used for treatment of active bleeding or vascular lesions in colon/rectum of patients with colostomy via epinephrine injection or other hemostatic agents.
