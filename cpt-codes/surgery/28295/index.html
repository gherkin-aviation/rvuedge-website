28295 Cor hlx vlgs prx mtar osteot

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: proximal metatarsal osteotomy, bunion surgery, first metatarsal osteotomy

Correction of hallux valgus with osteotomy of the proximal metatarsal (base), realigning the first metatarsal through bone cutting to address the intermetatarsal angle deformity.

Clinical Context

Used for moderate to severe hallux valgus with elevated intermetatarsal angle; proximal osteotomy corrects the primary deformity at its source and provides lasting correction.
