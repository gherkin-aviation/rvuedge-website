24102 Arthrotomy elbow with synovectomy

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: elbow synovectomy, arthrotomy with synovectomy, inflamed synovium removal

Arthrotomy of elbow with synovectomy. Opening of elbow joint with removal of inflamed synovial membrane.

In Plain Language

removal of inflamed joint lining from elbow; joint lining removal

Clinical Context

Performed for chronic inflammatory arthropathy (rheumatoid arthritis), pigmented villonodular synovitis, or persistent synovitis unresponsive to conservative treatment.
