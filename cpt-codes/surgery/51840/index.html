51840 Attach bladder/urethra

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: bladder suspension, bladder neck suspension, Marshall-Marchetti-Krantz procedure

Attachment of bladder to urethra for support or suspension, surgical procedure for stress urinary incontinence.

In Plain Language

surgical support of bladder neck

Clinical Context

Indicated for stress incontinence from bladder neck hypermobility. Can be performed open or minimally invasive.

Billing & Documentation

As a surgical CPT code, proper documentation must include the operative report detailing the procedure performed, patient positioning, approach, findings, and any complications. This code has a 90-day global period, which includes the day of the procedure, 1 day preoperative, and 90 days of postoperative care.
