64488 Tap block bi injection

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: bilateral adductor canal block, bilateral tap block, bilateral saphenous nerve block

Tap block (adductor canal block) performed bilaterally with anesthetic injection on both sides without continuous infusion.

Clinical Context

Used for bilateral knee pain or surgery. Bilateral blocks require two separate injections.
