21407 Opn treatment orbit fracture with implant

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: orbital fracture with implant, open orbit reduction with plate

Open treatment of orbit fracture with implant placement. Direct surgical visualization and reduction of orbital fractures with insertion of prosthetic material for structural support.

Clinical Context

Used when orbital fractures require implant reconstruction for stability. Indicated for significant comminution or when bone loss necessitates prosthetic support for restoration of orbital volume and projection.
