43254 Egd endoscopic mucosal resection

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: EGD EMR, endoscopic mucosal resection, cap-assisted EMR

Esophagogastroduodenoscopy with endoscopic mucosal resection of esophageal or gastric lesion. This advanced therapeutic technique removes early neoplasia while preserving underlying muscle.

Clinical Context

Gold standard for early gastric cancer and Barrett's esophagus with high-grade dysplasia; allows complete histologic assessment and achieves curative resection.
