22326 Treat neck spine fracture

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: cervical vertebral fracture ORIF, cervical spine fracture surgery

Open treatment of vertebral fracture, cervical region. Surgical reduction and fixation of cervical vertebral fractures using internal fixation, typically for unstable injuries.

Clinical Context

Used for unstable cervical fractures with displacement or neurologic symptoms. Anterior or posterior approach selected based on fracture location and configuration. Critical for neurologic preservation.
