64774 Remove skin nerve lesion

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: neuroma excision, skin nerve lesion removal, cutaneous nerve tumor removal

Surgical removal of a lesion (tumor, neuroma, or other mass) affecting the cutaneous or sensory nerve of the skin. Preserves underlying nerve function when possible.

Clinical Context

Indicated for benign nerve tumors (neuromas, neurofibromas), painful neuromas, or other cutaneous nerve lesions causing pain or cosmetic concern.
