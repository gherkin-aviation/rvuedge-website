59610 Vbac delivery

Surgery

Also known as: VBAC global, trial of labor after cesarean global, VBAC delivery, vaginal birth after cesarean

Vaginal birth after previous cesarean section with comprehensive obstetrical care (antepartum through postpartum).

Clinical Context

Bundled payment for planned trial of labor after cesarean with successful vaginal delivery; requires appropriate candidacy and facility resources.
