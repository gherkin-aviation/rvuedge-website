29891 Arthr ank ostchn df tal and/or tib

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ankle scope, ankle arthroscopy, talar OCD repair

Arthroscopic treatment of ankle joint with osteochondral deficit involving the talus and/or tibia, performed endoscopically to visualize, debride, and repair cartilage defects.

In Plain Language

ankle cartilage repair; ankle joint cleaning

Clinical Context

Used for osteochondral lesions of the talus or tibia causing ankle pain, instability, or mechanical symptoms; indicated when conservative treatment fails and imaging confirms articular cartilage damage.
