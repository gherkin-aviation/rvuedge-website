64636 Destroy l/s facet jnt additional

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: lumbar/sacral facet ablation additional levels, multi-level lumbar medial branch ablation

Destruction of the lumbar or sacral facet joint nerves for each additional joint beyond the first.

Clinical Context

Used when multiple lumbar or sacral facet joints require ablation for comprehensive pain relief.
