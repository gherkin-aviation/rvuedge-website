25825 Arthrd wrist with autograft

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: limited wrist fusion with graft, partial wrist arthrodesis with autograft

Arthrodesis (surgical fusion) of the wrist joint, limited fusion (partial) with autogenous bone graft.

Clinical Context

Used for partial wrist fusion requiring bone graft material to achieve fusion or bridge gaps.
