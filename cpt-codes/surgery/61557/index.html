61557 Incise skull/sutures

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: dural incision, dural opening

Incision and opening of dural membrane with separation of bridging veins or meningeal structures for intracranial access.

In Plain Language

Opening the brain covering membrane; Incision of brain lining

Clinical Context

Performed as component of craniotomy for access to intracranial structures. Requires careful hemostasis to prevent subdural hematoma.
