52356 Cysto/uretero with lithotripsy

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: cysto with ureteral stone lithotripsy

Cystoureteroscopy with insertion of ureteroscope and use of lithotripsy technique to fragment and remove upper urinary tract stones of significant size.

Clinical Context

Alternative to 52353 code; same procedure. Reflects variations in documentation of complex stone treatment.
