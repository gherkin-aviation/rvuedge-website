44700 Suspend bowel with prosthesis

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: bowel suspension, bowel fixation, plication

Suspension or fixation of bowel to abdominal wall or peritoneum using suture or prosthetic material to prevent recurrent obstruction from volvulus or internal herniation.

Clinical Context

Used to prevent recurrent volvulus or internal hernia by anchoring mobile bowel loops to abdominal wall. Often combined with resection in volvulus management.
