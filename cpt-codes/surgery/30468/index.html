30468 repair nsl vlv collapse with implant

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: nasal valve repair with implant, valve collapse repair with implant, nasal valve support implant

Repair of nasal valve collapse with placement of internal or external implant to maintain airway patency and restore normal nasal physiology.

Clinical Context

Indicated for nasal valve collapse unresponsive to simple repair; implants provide structural support to maintain airway patency.
