36584 Complicated replacement picc rs & i

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: PICC replacement, image-guided PICC replacement, PICC exchange

Replacement of peripherally inserted central catheter (PICC), with imaging guidance.

Clinical Context

Used for PICC removal and reinsertion when line is infected, thrombosed, or malpositioned, with imaging confirmation.
