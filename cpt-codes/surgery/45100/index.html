45100 Biopsy of rectum

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: rectal biopsy, transanal biopsy

Biopsy of rectum via open or transanal approach for tissue sampling to diagnose rectal pathology including inflammation, dysplasia, or neoplasia.

In Plain Language

sampling of rectal tissue for examination

Clinical Context

Used for diagnostic evaluation of rectal lesions, ulceration, or inflammatory changes. Can be obtained via transanal, endoscopic, or open approach.
