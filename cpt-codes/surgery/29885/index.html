29885 Arthrs knee srg drlg ost diss

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: microfracture, arthroscopic drilling, OCD drilling, marrow stimulation

Arthroscopy of the knee with drilling or microfracture for osteochondritis dissecans and articular cartilage lesions.

Clinical Context

Indicated for osteochondritis dissecans and chondral lesions to stimulate fibrocartilage formation.
