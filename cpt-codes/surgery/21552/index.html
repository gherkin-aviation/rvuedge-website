21552 excision neck lesion sternoclavicular 3 centimeters/>

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: neck lesion excision simple, cyst or mass removal from neck

Excision of lesion of neck with closure, simple, greater than 3 centimeters. Surgical removal of small neck lesion with primary closure of operative defect, limited to lesions exceeding 3 centimeters in dimension.

In Plain Language

surgical removal of neck growth; lump removal from neck

Clinical Context

Used for benign superficial neck lesions including cysts, lipomas, or small masses greater than 3 centimeters. Simple closure indicates straightforward single-layer approximation without flaps or grafts.
