65855 Trabeculoplasty laser surgical

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: laser trabeculoplasty, argon laser trabeculoplasty (ALT), selective laser trabeculoplasty (SLT)

Trabeculoplasty, laser; for glaucoma (includes reading of gonioscopy results).

Clinical Context

Laser treatment of trabecular meshwork to improve aqueous outflow. First-line laser therapy for many glaucoma patients. Can be repeated.
