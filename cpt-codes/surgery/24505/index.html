24505 Cltx humeral shft fracture with mnpj

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: closed treatment with manipulation, reduction without surgery

Closed treatment of humeral shaft fracture with manipulation. Conservative management with reduction maneuver for displaced humeral fracture.

In Plain Language

Non-surgical treatment of arm fracture requiring realignment; Manipulation and casting of arm bone fracture

Clinical Context

Used for minimally displaced or moderately displaced humeral shaft fractures manageable with closed reduction and immobilization.
