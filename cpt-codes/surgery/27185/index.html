27185 Revision of femur epiphysis

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: epiphysis revision, revision SCFE, epiphyseal repair revision

Revision of femoral epiphysis or prior epiphyseal procedures, addressing persistent or recurrent slippage or complications of initial treatment.

Clinical Context

Indicated for bilateral slipped capital femoral epiphysis, persistent slippage despite initial fixation, or complications requiring repeat surgical intervention.
