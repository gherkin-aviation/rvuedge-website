44370 Small bowel endoscopy/stent

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: small bowel endoscopy with stent, enteroscopy stent placement, surgical stent placement

Endoscopy of small bowel performed via enterotomy with placement of endoscopic stent (metal or plastic tube) to relieve stricture or obstruction.

Clinical Context

Used for treatment of small bowel strictures from Crohn's disease, adhesions, or radiation enteritis. Stent allows passage of intestinal contents and may avoid resection.
