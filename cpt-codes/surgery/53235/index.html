53235 Removal of urethra lesion

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: complex urethral lesion removal

More extensive surgical excision or reconstruction of a urethral lesion with grafting, flap formation, or reconstruction for lesions requiring complex closure.

Clinical Context

Used for larger lesions or those requiring reconstruction to maintain urethral caliber.
