37214 Cessj therapy catheter removal

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: thrombolytic catheter removal, CDT termination, therapy catheter removal

Cessation of thrombolytic therapy catheter infusion and removal of the catheter following completion of intra-arterial or intra-venous thrombolysis.

Clinical Context

Endpoint of catheter-directed thrombolytic therapy course. Determines timing of anticoagulation transition and vascular intervention planning.
