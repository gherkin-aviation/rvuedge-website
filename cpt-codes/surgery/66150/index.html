66150 Glaucoma surgery

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: trabeculectomy, filtration surgery, glaucoma filtering surgery

Trabeculectomy ab externo; initial.

Clinical Context

Standard glaucoma filtration surgery creating fistula between anterior chamber and subconjunctival space. Effective but requires post-operative management.
