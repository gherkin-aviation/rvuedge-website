57170 Fitting of diaphragm/cap

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: Diaphragm fitting, Cervical cap fitting

Fitting of diaphragm or cervical cap. Sizing and instruction for contraceptive barrier device.

Clinical Context

Non-hormonal contraception. Requires instruction in proper insertion and use.
