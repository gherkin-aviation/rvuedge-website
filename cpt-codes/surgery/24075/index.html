24075 excision arm/elbow lesion sternoclavicular < 3 centimeters

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: excision superficial lesion <3cm, small lesion removal, minor lesion excision

Excision of arm/elbow lesion (benign, superficial, less than 3 centimeters). Removal of superficial benign lesion from arm or elbow, size less than 3 cm.

Clinical Context

Performed for small benign superficial soft tissue lesions of arm/elbow (lipoma, cyst, etc.) less than 3 cm.
