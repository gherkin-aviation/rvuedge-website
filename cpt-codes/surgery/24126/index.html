24126 excision/curettage bone cyst/tumor rds algr

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: radius cyst excision with allograft, radial bone tumor removal with donor graft

Excision or curettage of a benign cyst or tumor of the radius including allograft. Addresses pathologic lesions in the radius requiring removal and donor bone graft reconstruction.

In Plain Language

Removal of a benign lump from the forearm bone with donor bone graft; Surgical removal and reconstruction of arm bone growth with cadaver bone

Clinical Context

Used for benign bone lesions of the radius requiring excision with allograft bone reconstruction when autograft is unavailable or insufficient.
