21088 Impres & prep facial prosthesis

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: facial prosth prep, facial mold impression

Impression and preparation of a facial prosthesis, involving tissue molding and site preparation for prosthetic facial reconstruction or augmentation.

Clinical Context

Used for facial defects from tumor removal, severe trauma, or congenital conditions. Enables fabrication of custom facial prosthetics.
