28304 Incision of midfoot bones

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: midfoot incision, navicular surgery, midfoot bone access

Surgical incision into the midfoot bones (navicular, cuboid, or cuneiforms) for decompression, biopsy, or treatment of midfoot pathology such as avascular necrosis, stress fractures, or bone marrow edema.

Clinical Context

Used for midfoot bone pathology including avascular necrosis of the navicular, stress fractures, or other conditions requiring direct surgical decompression and evaluation.
