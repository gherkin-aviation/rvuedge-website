46948 Int hrhc tranal dartlzj 2+

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: transanal hemorrhoid artery ligation, THAL, dartolization

Transanal (through-the-anus) dartolization ligation of internal hemorrhoids in 2 or more groups. Denudation and artery ablation technique.

Clinical Context

Minimally invasive technique using ultrasound or Doppler guidance to identify and ligate hemorrhoidal arteries.
