35142 Repair artery rupture thigh

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: popliteal artery rupture repair, femoral rupture repair

Repair of traumatic rupture of the popliteal or femoral artery in the thigh region with hemostasis.

In Plain Language

Repairing a torn thigh artery; Fixing a bleeding knee/thigh artery

Clinical Context

Emergency surgical repair for penetrating or crush injuries to the lower extremity vasculature. Popliteal artery rupture carries high amputation risk; requires rapid intervention to restore distal perfusion and prevent limb loss.
