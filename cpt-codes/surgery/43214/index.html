43214 Esophagosc dilate balloon 30

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: EGD with large balloon dilation, esophageal balloon dilation >30mm

Esophagogastroduodenoscopy (EGD) with balloon dilation of esophageal stricture greater than 30 millimeters in diameter.

Clinical Context

Therapeutic procedure for severe esophageal strictures. Larger balloon needed for significantly narrowed esophagus.
