46060 incision and drainage ischiorectal/ntrmrl absc

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ischiorectal I&D, ischioanal abscess drainage, intramural abscess drainage

Incision and drainage of ischiorectal (ischioanal) or intramural anal abscess. These are deep abscesses located in the ischiorectal space or within the rectal wall, representing complex infections requiring surgical drainage.

Clinical Context

Used for ischiorectal abscesses causing severe perianal pain, fever, and systemic symptoms. Often associated with fistula formation and may require treatment of underlying fistula.
