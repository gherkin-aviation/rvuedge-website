36592 Collect blood from picc

Surgery

Also known as: PICC draw, PICC blood draw, blood collection from PICC

Collection of blood specimen from a peripherally inserted central catheter (PICC).

Clinical Context

Used for routine or urgent blood draws from PICCs, reducing need for additional venipunctures in patients with difficult venous access.
