42975 Dise evaluation slp do birth flx diagnosis

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: laryngeal videostroboscopy, videoscopy exam, endoscopic swallowing evaluation

Endoscopic evaluation and imaging of the larynx, pharynx, and associated structures to assess swallowing function, airway dynamics, or breathing patterns during specified tasks.

In Plain Language

camera examination of throat and voice box to check swallowing and breathing

Clinical Context

Diagnostic procedure used for dysphagia evaluation, vocal cord mobility assessment, aspiration risk, and laryngeal pathology. Important for swallowing studies and voice assessment.
