69740 Repair facial nerve

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: facial nerve repair, facial nerve anastomosis, facial reanimation surgery

Surgical repair of facial nerve injury or paralysis with nerve anastomosis, grafting, or reconstruction to restore function.

Clinical Context

Used for traumatic transection, delayed repair, or nerve gaps requiring graft for functional restoration.
