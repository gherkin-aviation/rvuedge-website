15786 Abrasion lesion single

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: lesion abrasion, single lesion removal

Abrasion of single skin lesion performed by mechanical, chemical, or laser technique. Removal or reduction of single lesion.

In Plain Language

removing or treating one skin lesion; single lesion resurfacing

Clinical Context

Used for individual warts, skin tags, benign tumors, or localized lesions requiring ablation or removal.
