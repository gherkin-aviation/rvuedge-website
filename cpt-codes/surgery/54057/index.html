54057 Laser surgical penis lesion(s)

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: laser destruction of penis, laser vaporization

Destruction of benign or malignant lesions of the penis using laser energy for precise vaporization.

Clinical Context

Used for warts, condyloma, or other lesions. Laser provides precise control with hemostasis and reduced scarring.
