17340 Cryotherapy for acne

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: cryo acne, cryotherapy acne

Cryotherapy or cryosurgical treatment of acne using liquid nitrogen or other cryogenic agents to treat active acne lesions.

Clinical Context

Performed for inflammatory acne when topical treatment has failed. Often used for cystic or severe acne. May require multiple sessions.
