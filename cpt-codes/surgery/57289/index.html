57289 Repair bladder & vagina

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Combined bladder and vaginal repair

Repair of bladder and vaginal defect. Combined surgical correction of bladder and vaginal injuries.

Clinical Context

Used for combined bladder and vaginal injuries or vesicovaginal fistula. Restores continence and anatomy.
