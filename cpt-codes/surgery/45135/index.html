45135 Excision of rectal prolapse

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: perineal proctosigmoidectomy, Altemeier procedure, perineal prolapse repair

Excision of rectal prolapse by perineal approach (Altemeier procedure), removing prolapsed rectal tissue via circumferential excision through the perineum.

In Plain Language

buttock area prolapse surgery; perineal rectum repair; external prolapse removal

Clinical Context

Preferred for elderly or infirm patients with full-thickness rectal prolapse, offering reduced morbidity compared to abdominal approach despite higher recurrence.
