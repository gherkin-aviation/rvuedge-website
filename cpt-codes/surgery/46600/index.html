46600 Diagnostic anoscopy spx

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: diagnostic anoscopy, anoscopic exam, anal canal inspection

Diagnostic anoscopy with spectroscopy for specialized visualization. Anoscope is inserted to visualize the anal canal with additional spectroscopic analysis.

In Plain Language

looking inside the anal canal with a scope; examination of the anus with a viewing tube

Clinical Context

Performed to diagnose anal canal pathology including fissures, hemorrhoids, polyps, and inflammatory conditions. Often done in office setting.
