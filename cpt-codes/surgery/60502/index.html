60502 Re-explore parathyroids

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: parathyroid re-exploration, reoperation parathyroid, second parathyroid surgery

Reoperation or re-exploration of parathyroid glands in a patient with prior parathyroid surgery, typically performed for persistent or recurrent hyperparathyroidism.

Clinical Context

Performed when initial parathyroidectomy fails to achieve eucalcemia or when hyperparathyroidism recurs. More technically challenging due to scar tissue, fibrosis, and altered anatomy from prior surgery.
