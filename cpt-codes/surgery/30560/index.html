30560 Lysis intranasal synechia

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: synechia lysis, nasal adhesion lysis, intranasal adhesion division

Lysis or division of intranasal synechia (adhesions between nasal or septal structures) to restore normal nasal function and prevent recurrence.

Clinical Context

Indicated for nasal adhesions following trauma, prior surgery, or chemical burns causing obstruction; requires careful lysis and often splinting to prevent reformation.
