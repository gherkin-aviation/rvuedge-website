28102 Remove/graft foot lesion

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: foot lesion excision with graft, foot cyst removal with coverage, lesion excision flap

Excision of lesion or cyst of the foot with skin graft or flap reconstruction. This procedure removes lesions and reconstructs the defect with skin graft or local flap.

Clinical Context

Performed for larger lesions requiring more complex reconstruction. Skin grafting prevents contracture and improves cosmetic and functional outcomes.
