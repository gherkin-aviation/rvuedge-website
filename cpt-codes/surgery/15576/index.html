15576 Pedicle e/n/e/l/intraoral

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: facial feature pedicle flap, orofacial flap

Pedicle flap transfer to eye, nose, ear, lip, or intraoral areas. Highly specialized pedicle flap for delicate facial features and oral structures.

Clinical Context

Used for eyelid reconstruction, nasal defect repair, lip reconstruction, and intraoral surgery where specialized flaps restore form and function.
