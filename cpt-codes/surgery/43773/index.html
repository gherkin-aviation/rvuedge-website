43773 Laparoscopic replace gastric adj device

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: lap band replacement, gastric band exchange, new band placement

Laparoscopic replacement of a previously placed adjustable gastric restriction device (gastric band) with a new band device.

In Plain Language

gastric band replacement; new weight loss device placement

Clinical Context

Replacement of gastric band in same anatomical position, typically due to band erosion, slippage, or other mechanical failure requiring device exchange.
