17106 destruction cutaneous vascular proliferative les<10sqcm

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: destroy vascular lesion small, hemangioma destruction

Destruction of cutaneous vascular lesion (hemangioma or port-wine stain) less than 10 square centimeters using laser or other modalities.

Clinical Context

Performed for cosmetic or symptomatic vascular lesions. Laser therapy most common. Appropriate for small, discrete lesions.
