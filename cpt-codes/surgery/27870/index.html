27870 Fusion of ankle joint open

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ankle fusion, ankle arthrodesis, tibiotalar fusion, TAF, ankle fuse

Ankle joint fusion performed via open arthrotomy approach. This procedure arthrodeses the ankle joint by removing articular cartilage and securing bone surfaces to eliminate motion and treat arthritis or severe instability.

Clinical Context

Performed for end-stage ankle arthritis, chronic instability, or severe fracture sequelae. Eliminates ankle motion but provides stability and pain relief for patients with disabling arthritis refractory to conservative treatment.
