59414 Deliver placenta

Surgery

Also known as: placental delivery, third stage management

Delivery of the placenta and membranes following delivery of the fetus, including management of the third stage of labor.

Clinical Context

Represents management of placental delivery with potential intervention (uterotonic agents, manual removal) for retained placenta.
