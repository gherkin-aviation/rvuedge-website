61867 Implant neuroelectrode

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: neurostimulation receiver, pulse generator implantation

Implantation of intracranial neurostimulation receiver or pulse generator beneath scalp or skull.

Clinical Context

Placement of receiver/pulse generator for intracranial stimulation systems (DBS, seizure management, pain).
