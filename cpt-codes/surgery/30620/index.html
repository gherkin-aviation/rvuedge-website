30620 Intranasal reconstruction

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: intranasal reconstruction, nasal interior reconstruction, nasal cavity reconstruction

Reconstruction of intranasal anatomy including reconstruction of nasal structures, defects, or restoration of normal anatomy following trauma or disease.

In Plain Language

inside nose repair and rebuilding

Clinical Context

Indicated for complex intranasal defects or structural loss from trauma, tumor resection, or infection requiring reconstruction.
