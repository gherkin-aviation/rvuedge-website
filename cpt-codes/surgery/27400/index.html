27400 Revise thigh muscles/tendons

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: thigh muscle revision, muscle repair, tendon repair

Surgical revision or repair of thigh muscles and tendons to address deformity, dysfunction, or complications from prior injury or disease affecting the quadriceps, hamstring, or adductor muscle groups.

In Plain Language

fixing thigh muscles or tendons; thigh muscle repair

Clinical Context

Used for muscle tears, contractures, fibrosis, or functional problems in thigh muscles. May be revision of prior surgical procedures or treatment of acute injury.
