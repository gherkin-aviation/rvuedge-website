42665 Ligation of salivary duct

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: duct ligation, salivary duct ligation

Surgical ligation and division of a salivary duct to divert or interrupt salivary flow, typically for Frey syndrome or chronic sialadenitis.

In Plain Language

tying off salivary duct

Clinical Context

Surgical interruption of salivary duct flow for management of gustatory sweating, fistula, or sialadenitis.
