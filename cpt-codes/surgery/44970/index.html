44970 Laparoscopy appendectomy

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: laparoscopic appendectomy, lap appy, minimal invasive appendectomy

Laparoscopic appendectomy for removal of inflamed or diseased appendix performed via minimally invasive approach with reduced postoperative morbidity.

Clinical Context

Currently preferred approach for acute uncomplicated appendicitis. Allows faster recovery and reduced pain compared to open approach.
