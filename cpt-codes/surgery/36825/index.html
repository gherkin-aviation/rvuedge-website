36825 Artery-vein autograft

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: autogenous fistula, vein transposition fistula, AV fistula creation

Creation of arteriovenous fistula by other than direct arteriovenous anastomosis (separate incision); autogenous (including vein transposition).

Clinical Context

Used for arteriovenous fistula creation with intervening tissue or using transposed vein, often for upper arm fistulas.
