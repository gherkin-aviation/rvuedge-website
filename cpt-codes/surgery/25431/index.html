25431 Repair nonunion carpal bone

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: carpal nonunion repair

Repair of nonunion of a carpal (wrist) bone with internal fixation and/or bone grafting.

Clinical Context

Indicated for symptomatic carpal nonunion, particularly scaphoid nonunion. Multiple techniques available.
