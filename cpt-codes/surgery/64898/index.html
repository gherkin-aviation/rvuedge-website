64898 Nerve grf mltst arm/leg >4 centimeters

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: multiple strand nerve graft arm/leg long, extended cable graft extremity

Surgical nerve graft using multiple strands of autologous nerve tissue 4 centimeters or greater for repair of arm or leg nerve injuries.

In Plain Language

large multi-strand nerve transplant arm/leg

Clinical Context

Indicated for extensive arm or leg nerve injuries requiring multiple nerve branch repair with significant gaps of 4 cm or more.
