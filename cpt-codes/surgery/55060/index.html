55060 Repair of hydrocele

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: hydrocele plication, tunica vaginalis plication

Repair of hydrocele with plication or inversion of tunica vaginalis to prevent recurrence.

Clinical Context

Surgical modification of tunica vaginalis to obliterate hydrocele cavity and prevent fluid reaccumulation.
