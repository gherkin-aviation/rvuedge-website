43201 Esophageal scope with submucous injection

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: flexible esophagoscopy with injection, esophageal variceal injection

Flexible esophagoscopy (examination of esophagus with flexible scope) via transoral approach with submucosal injection of medication or agent.

In Plain Language

camera examination with injection of medication to stop bleeding

Clinical Context

Therapeutic procedure for esophageal varices, bleeding lesions, or sclerotherapy. Critical for hemostasis in esophageal variceal bleeding.
