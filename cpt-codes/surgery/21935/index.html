21935 Resection back tumor < 5 centimeters

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: radical back tumor resection small, back tumor wide excision

Radical resection of back tumor, less than 5 centimeters. Extensive surgical removal of back malignancy with wide margins, for lesion under 5 centimeters.

Clinical Context

Used for malignant back lesions requiring radical resection with adequate margins. Lesion size under 5 centimeters indicates earlier stage disease. Requires oncologic principles of wide margins.
