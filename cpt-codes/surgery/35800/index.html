35800 Explore neck vessels

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: neck vascular exploration, cervical vessel exploration, carotid exploration

Surgical exploration of neck vessels (arteries and veins) for evaluation, diagnosis, or therapeutic intervention. Provides direct visualization of cervical vasculature.

Clinical Context

Performed for carotid atherosclerosis, aneurysm, trauma, or thrombosis diagnosis and management.
