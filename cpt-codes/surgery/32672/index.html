32672 Thoracoscopy for lvrs

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: VATS LVRS, thoracoscopic lung volume reduction

Thoracoscopic lung volume reduction surgery (LVRS) removing emphysematous lung tissue via minimally invasive approach to improve respiratory mechanics.

Clinical Context

Used for severe emphysema with upper-lobe predominance when VATS approach allows adequate tissue resection and lung volume reduction.
