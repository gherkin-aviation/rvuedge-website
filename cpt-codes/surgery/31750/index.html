31750 Tracheoplasty cervical

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: cervical tracheoplasty, tracheal reconstruction, cervical stenosis repair

Tracheoplasty (surgical reconstruction of the trachea) for management of tracheal stenosis, stricture, or malacia in the cervical (neck) portion of the trachea.

Clinical Context

Performed for cervical tracheal stenosis from intubation, trauma, external compression, or congenital abnormality. External approach allows direct visualization and repair.
