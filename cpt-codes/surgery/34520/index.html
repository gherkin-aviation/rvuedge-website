34520 Cross-over vein graft

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: venous crossover graft, femorofemoral graft

Cross-over venous graft procedure. Surgical placement of a graft connecting veins across the midline, typically for unilateral venous occlusion.

Clinical Context

Used for chronic venous obstruction or iliofemoral vein occlusion. Provides alternative venous drainage pathway from the occluded side.
