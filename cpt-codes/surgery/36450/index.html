36450 Bld exchange truj newborn

Surgery

Also known as: exchange transfusion, double volume exchange, newborn transfusion

Blood exchange transfusion, newborn; total volume transfusion (one or more repeated exchanges).

Clinical Context

Used in neonates with hemolytic disease, severe hyperbilirubinemia, or intrauterine transfusion complications. Involves removing infant blood and replacing with donor blood.
