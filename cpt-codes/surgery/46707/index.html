46707 Repair anorectal fist with plug

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: plug fistula repair, tissue plug closure, fistula plug insertion

Repair of anorectal fistula using a tissue plug. A biocompatible plug (usually acellular tissue) is placed to seal the fistula tract.

In Plain Language

closing an anal fistula with a plug of tissue; fistula treatment with bioengineered plug

Clinical Context

Minimally invasive approach for transsphincteric and other complex fistulas. High success rates with excellent continence preservation.
