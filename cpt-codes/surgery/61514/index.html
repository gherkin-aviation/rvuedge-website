61514 Craniectomy treph excision brn abs sttl

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: brain abscess drainage, supratentorial abscess excision

Craniotomy or craniectomy with excision of brain abscess from supratentorial location and debridement of infected tissue.

In Plain Language

surgical opening to remove infected brain tissue

Clinical Context

Performed for supratentorial brain abscesses with significant mass effect or failure of antibiotics. Drainage, debridement, and culture are critical for diagnosis and treatment.
