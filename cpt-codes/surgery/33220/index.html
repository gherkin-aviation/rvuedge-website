33220 Repair lead pace-defib dual

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: dual lead repair, two-lead repair, bilateral conductor repair

Repair of dual transvenous pacemaker or defibrillator leads, addressing insulation defects, fractures, or poor electrical function in both leads.

Clinical Context

Used to address failures in dual-chamber systems without requiring lead extraction. Preserves existing lead positioning.
