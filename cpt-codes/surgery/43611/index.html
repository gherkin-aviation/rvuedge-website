43611 Excision of stomach lesion

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: gastric lesion excision with bypass, ulcer excision with reconstruction, gastric lesion removal with anastomosis

Excision of gastric or duodenal lesion with creation of gastroduodenal or gastrojejunal anastomosis when primary closure inadequate. This removes lesions with reconstruction.

Clinical Context

Complex excision for large gastric lesions or ulcers requiring reconstruction; provides definitive therapy and improves gastric function.
