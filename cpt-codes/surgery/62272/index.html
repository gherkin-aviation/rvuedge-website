62272 Therapy spinal puncture drainage csf

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: therapeutic spinal puncture, therapeutic lumbar puncture, spinal drug injection

Therapeutic lumbar or lower spinal puncture including drug administration or cerebrospinal fluid drainage. Includes needle placement.

Clinical Context

Used for therapeutic cerebrospinal fluid withdrawal, drug administration, or pressure relief in intracranial hypertension.
