58400 Suspension of uterus

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: uterine suspension, uterosacral ligament shortening

Suspension of uterus is a surgical procedure to elevate and reposition the uterus to correct retroversion or retroflexion.

Clinical Context

Performed for infertility associated with severe retroversion, dyspareunia, or to prevent placental malposition. Less commonly performed with modern understanding of uterine position and infertility.
