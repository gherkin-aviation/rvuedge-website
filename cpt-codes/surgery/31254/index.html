31254 Nsl/sinus ndsc with prtl ethmdct

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: endoscopic partial ethmoidectomy, partial ESS with ethmoid, limited ethmoidectomy, endoscopic ethmoidectomy, FESS ethmoid

Nasal and sinus endoscopic surgery with partial ethmoidectomy, removing disease from some ethmoid air cells.

Clinical Context

Endoscopic removal of anterior or select ethmoid air cells while preserving posterior ethmoid cells. For localized ethmoid disease.
