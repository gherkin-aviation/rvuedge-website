58563 Hysteroscopy ablation

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: hysteroscopic ablation, endometrial ablation

Hysteroscopy with endometrial ablation involves endoscopic destruction of endometrial lining to treat abnormal uterine bleeding.

Clinical Context

Minimally invasive treatment for menorrhagia or abnormal uterine bleeding refractory to medical therapy. Various ablation modalities (electrical, thermal).
