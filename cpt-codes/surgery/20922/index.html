20922 Removal of fascia for graft

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: extensive fascia harvest, major fascia graft harvest

Removal of fascia for use as an autogenous graft, more extensive harvest.

Clinical Context

Performed when larger fascia grafts are needed for extensive reconstructions.
