24149 Radical resection of elbow

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: elbow radical resection, elbow tumor wide excision

Radical resection of tumor of the elbow joint area with en bloc removal of affected tissues. Wide excision of malignant or aggressive tumors with surrounding tissue and possibly bone.

Clinical Context

Used for primary malignant tumors of the elbow joint, soft tissue sarcomas, or other aggressive neoplasms requiring wide surgical margins and en bloc resection.
