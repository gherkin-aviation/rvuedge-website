61880 Revise/remove neuroelectrode

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: electrode revision, neuroelectrode removal

Surgical revision, repositioning, or removal of intracranial neuroelectrode or stimulation catheter.

In Plain Language

Removing or adjusting brain electrodes

Clinical Context

Performed for electrode malfunction, infection, or inadequate placement requiring replacement or repositioning.
