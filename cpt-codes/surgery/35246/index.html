35246 repair blvsl vn grf ntrthrc without

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: intrathoracic vein graft repair, thoracic vein reconstruction

Repair of blood vessel injury in the intrathoracic region using venous graft without bypass.

Clinical Context

Reconstruction of intrathoracic vascular injuries using venous interposition graft when direct repair not feasible. Appropriate for vessels where repair possible without requiring additional bypass conduit.
