44705 Prepare fecal microbiota

Surgery

Also known as: fecal microbiota transplant prep, FMT preparation, microbiota preparation

Preparation of fecal microbiota transplant material including processing, testing, and preparation for transfer to recipient colon for therapeutic reconstitution of microbiome.

Clinical Context

Used for Clostridioides difficile infection refractory to standard therapy. Restores normal microbiota and colonization resistance against pathogenic organisms.
