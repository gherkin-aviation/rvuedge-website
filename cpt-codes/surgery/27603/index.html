27603 Drainage lower leg lesion

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: lower leg lesion drainage, cyst aspiration, abscess drainage

Surgical drainage or incision of a soft tissue lesion of the lower leg including cyst, abscess, or hematoma drainage.

Clinical Context

Used for symptomatic soft tissue cysts, abscesses, hematomas, or other lesions in lower leg requiring drainage.
