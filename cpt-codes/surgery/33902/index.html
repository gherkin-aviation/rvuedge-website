33902 Percutaneous p-art revsc 1 abnor uni

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: Abnormal PA stent unilateral, anomalous PA intervention, abnormal artery stenting

Percutaneous pulmonary artery revascularization with placement of one abnormal artery stent in unilateral pulmonary circulation; minimally invasive stent placement in an anomalous or abnormal pulmonary artery branch.

Clinical Context

Treatment of stenotic anomalous pulmonary artery branches, aortopulmonary collaterals, or other abnormal vascular connections requiring revascularization.
