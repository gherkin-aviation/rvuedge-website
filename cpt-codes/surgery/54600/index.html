54600 Reduce testis torsion

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: testicular torsion reduction, testis fixation

Manual reduction or operative repair of testicular torsion with fixation to prevent recurrence.

Clinical Context

Emergency surgical treatment of testicular torsion causing rotational volvulus. Includes detorsion and bilateral orchiopexy for fixation.
