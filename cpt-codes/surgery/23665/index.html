23665 Cltx sho dslc fracture gr hmrl tbr

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: dislocation with GT fracture, dislocation fracture reduction, shoulder dislocation with tuberosity fracture

Closed treatment of shoulder dislocation with associated fracture of greater humeral tuberosity. Reduction of shoulder dislocation concurrent with greater tuberosity fracture management.

Clinical Context

Closed management when shoulder dislocation occurs with associated greater tuberosity fracture amenable to conservative treatment.
