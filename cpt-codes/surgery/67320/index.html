67320 Revise eye muscle(s) add-on

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: adjustable stitch, intraoperative adjustment, muscle adjustment add-on

Add-on code for intraoperative diagnostic measurement and fine-tuning of extraocular muscle position (adjustable sutures) during strabismus surgery.

Clinical Context

Allows surgeon to adjust muscle position with patient awake or during same session; improves accuracy of final alignment; typically used with muscle surgery codes.
