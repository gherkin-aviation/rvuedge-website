63085 Remove vertebral body dcmprn thrc

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: thoracic vertebrectomy, thoracic corpectomy, thoracic vertebral body removal

Removal of vertebral body for decompression in the thoracic region. This extensive procedure involves thoracic vertebrectomy for decompressing the thoracic spinal cord and nerves.

Clinical Context

Used for thoracic myelopathy from vertebral body pathology including OPLL, tumor, trauma, or severe degenerative disease.
