26373 Repair finger/hand tendon

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Flexor-extensor repair variant, Primary dual tendon repair, Alternative F-E repair

Surgical repair of flexor and extensor tendons of the finger without graft material in a different anatomic configuration or tendon combination pattern than code 26370, allowing for reporting of alternative repair scenarios.

Clinical Context

Used for primary repair of concurrent flexor and extensor lacerations using direct approximation, applicable in cases where the specific anatomic pattern differs from standard 26370 scenarios.
