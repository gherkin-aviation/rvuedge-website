53240 Surgery for urethra pouch

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: urethral diverticulum excision

Surgical treatment of urethral diverticulum through excision, drainage, or marsupialization to manage infection or improve urethral function.

In Plain Language

removing urethral pouch

Clinical Context

More common in females. Treatment prevents recurrent infections and resolves dysuria or dribbling.
