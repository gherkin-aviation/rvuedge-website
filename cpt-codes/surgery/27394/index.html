27394 Lengthening of thigh tendons

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: multiple tendon lengthening, polytendon plasty

Lengthening of multiple thigh tendons through operative approach using lengthening procedures.

In Plain Language

Multiple tendon stretching; Complex hamstring lengthening

Clinical Context

Indicated for multiple tendon contractures requiring simultaneous lengthening procedures.
