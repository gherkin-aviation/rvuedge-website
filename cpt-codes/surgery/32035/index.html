32035 Thoracostomy with rib resection

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: thoracostomy with rib resection, rib resection thoracostomy

Surgical opening of the chest cavity (thoracostomy) with removal of one or more ribs to provide access or drainage, commonly performed for empyema or extensive thoracic pathology.

Clinical Context

Used for drainage of pleural infection (empyema), chest wall reconstruction, or access to mediastinal pathology requiring rib resection for adequate exposure.
