27427 Reconstruction knee

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: knee reconstruction, knee stabilization, multi-tissue repair

Surgical reconstruction of the knee joint including soft tissue repair, ligament reconstruction, capsular plication, or multiple tissue procedures to restore knee stability and function.

Clinical Context

Used for complex knee injuries, chronic instability, or failed prior knee surgery. May involve ligament, cartilage, and capsular reconstruction.
