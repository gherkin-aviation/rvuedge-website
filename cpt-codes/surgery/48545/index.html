48545 Pancreatorrhaphy

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Pancreatic repair, pancreatorrhaphy

Surgical suturing and repair of the pancreas to restore pancreatic continuity after injury, preserve parenchyma, and prevent fistula formation.

In Plain Language

Stitching the pancreas back together

Clinical Context

Performed for pancreatic injury from trauma or iatrogenic damage. Primary repair preferred when feasible.
