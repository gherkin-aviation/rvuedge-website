33330 Insert major vessel graft

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: aortic graft insertion, vessel graft placement, aortic replacement

Insertion of an aortic or other major blood vessel graft for aortic disease or replacement.

In Plain Language

placing a tube graft in the large heart vessel

Clinical Context

Used for aortic aneurysm repair, aortic dissection, or irreparable vessel injury.
