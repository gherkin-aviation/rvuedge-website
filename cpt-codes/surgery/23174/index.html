23174 Sqstrctmy humeral head srg nck

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: humeral head sequestrectomy, surgical neck debridement, proximal humerus dead bone removal

Sequestrectomy of humeral head and surgical neck (removal of dead or devitalized bone fragment from the proximal humerus). Specialized approach for proximal humeral sequestrectomy.

In Plain Language

removal of dead bone from shoulder area; upper arm bone cleaning

Clinical Context

Applied in proximal humeral osteomyelitis or post-fracture sequestrum formation, where removal of necrotic bone at the humeral head and surgical neck is required.
