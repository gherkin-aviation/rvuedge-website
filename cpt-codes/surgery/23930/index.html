23930 incision and drainage upper a/e dp absc/hmtma

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: arm I and D, arm abscess drainage, upper arm incision and drainage

Incision and drainage (I&D) of upper arm/elbow, deep abscess or hematoma. Surgical drainage of deep soft tissue infection or blood collection in upper arm.

In Plain Language

surgical drainage of arm infection; opening arm to drain fluid

Clinical Context

Performed for deep arm abscess, hematoma, or septic bursitis requiring drainage and irrigation; allows removal of purulent material and prevention of spread.
