28405 Cltx calcaneal fracture with mnpj

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: calcaneal fracture reduction, closed reduction heel fracture, manipulation treatment

Closed treatment of calcaneal fracture with manipulation to restore anatomic alignment, used for displaced fractures that can be reduced through non-operative techniques.

In Plain Language

Heel fracture reduction; Heel bone fracture reduction; Manipulative fracture treatment

Clinical Context

Used for displaced calcaneal fractures amenable to closed reduction; manipulation realigns bone fragments and restoration of heel height improves functional outcomes.
