42845 Extensive surgery of throat

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: major pharyngeal reconstruction, free flap pharyngeal reconstruction

Extensive surgical reconstruction of the pharynx (throat) with maximum complexity including free tissue transfer, composite reconstruction, or multistage procedure.

Clinical Context

Performed for major pharyngeal defects including post-cancer reconstruction, severe stenosis, or complex fistulae requiring free tissue transfer. Highest complexity reconstruction.
