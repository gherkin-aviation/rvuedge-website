35905 Excision graft thorax

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: graft excision chest, infected graft removal thoracic, mediastinal graft extraction

Excision of infected or degenerated arterial graft in the thorax (chest) with or without reconstruction. Removes compromised vascular prosthesis from mediastinum.

Clinical Context

Performed for infected aortic graft, prosthetic endocarditis, or thoracic graft failure. Complex surgery.
