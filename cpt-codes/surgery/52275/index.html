52275 Cystoscopy & revise urethra

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: cysto with extensive urethral revision

Cystourethroscopy with more extensive revision of the urethra including dilation, incision, and reconstruction, typically for complex strictures or anatomic abnormalities.

Clinical Context

Used for more complex urethral pathology requiring combination of dilation and incision. Better outcomes than simple urethrotomy for selected patients.
