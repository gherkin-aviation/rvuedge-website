26530 Revise knuckle joint

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Knuckle arthroplasty, MCP arthroplasty, Knuckle reconstruction

Surgical arthroplasty or revision of the metacarpophalangeal knuckle joint to restore or improve joint function, typically performed for arthritis, trauma sequelae, or joint dysfunction.

Clinical Context

Indicated for metacarpophalangeal joint arthropathy, trauma sequelae, or dysfunction where joint reconstruction is preferable to fusion.
