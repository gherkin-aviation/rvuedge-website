64738 Incision of jaw nerve

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: mandibular nerve incision, jaw nerve decompression

Surgical incision and decompression of the buccal or lingual divisions of the mandibular nerve in the jaw region. Treats compression or sensory dysfunction.

Clinical Context

Indicated for mandibular nerve compression or neuropathy causing jaw pain, tooth numbness, or lip dysesthesia. May follow mandibular surgery or trauma.
