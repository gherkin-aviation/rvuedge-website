44207 L colectomy/coloproctostomy

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: lap left colectomy, laparoscopic colectomy, minimal invasive colectomy, lap low anterior resection, lap LAR

Laparoscopic left colectomy with anastomosis to rectum or colorectal junction for removal of portion of colon with restoration of bowel continuity.

In Plain Language

camera surgery to remove part of colon; minimally invasive colon removal

Clinical Context

Used for colorectal pathology (cancer, polyps, inflammatory bowel disease) in the left colon when laparoscopic approach is feasible. Allows for anastomosis to restore bowel continuity.
