50430 Njx procedures nfrosgrm and/or urtrgrm

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: percutaneous pyelography, antegrade pyelography, contrast injection study

Percutaneous injection under fluoroscopic guidance for nephrography and ureterography (radiographic imaging) with contrast injection.

Clinical Context

Used for diagnostic imaging when retrograde pyelography is impossible or to evaluate percutaneous access sites before stone removal.
