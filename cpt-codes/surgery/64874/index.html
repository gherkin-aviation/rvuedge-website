64874 Repair & revise nerve add-on

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: nerve repair and revision

Add-on code for repair and revision of a previously injured nerve that requires both repair of new injury and revision of prior repair.

Clinical Context

Used when a previously repaired nerve has recurrent injury requiring both repair of new injury and revision of original repair site.
