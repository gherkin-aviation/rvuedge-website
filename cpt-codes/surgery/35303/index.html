35303 Rechanneling of artery

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: iliac endarterectomy, iliac artery rechanneling

Rechanneling (thrombectomy with endarterectomy) of artery in the iliac region with restoration of distal perfusion.

Clinical Context

Surgical treatment of iliac artery stenosis or occlusion causing claudication or critical limb ischemia. Offers excellent long-term patency; may be combined with femorofemoral bypass for contralateral disease.
