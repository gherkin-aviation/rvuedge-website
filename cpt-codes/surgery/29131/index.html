29131 application finger splint dynamic

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: dynamic finger splint, active digit splint, articulated finger splint

Application of a dynamic finger splint with articulated components allowing controlled finger movement for early mobilization during recovery.

In Plain Language

movable finger splint; flexible finger brace

Clinical Context

Used for tendon repairs, extensor mechanism injuries, and conditions requiring early active or passive motion to maintain finger function.
