43832 Gstrst open with constj tube

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: open gastrojejunostomy, GJ tube placement, combined G-J tube

Gastrostomy via open approach with concomitant jejunal tube placement for combined gastric and small bowel access.

Clinical Context

Simultaneous placement of gastrostomy and jejunal feeding tubes. Allows gastric decompression with jejunal feeding in patients at risk for aspiration.
