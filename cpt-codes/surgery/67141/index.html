67141 Proph rta dtchmnt crtx dthrm

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: prophylactic laser, preventive laser, retinal break treatment, lattice treatment

Prophylactic treatment of rhegmatogenous retinal detachment risk by laser photocoagulation or cryotherapy around retinal breaks or areas of weak vitreoretinal adhesion.

Clinical Context

Applied to high-risk eyes with retinal breaks, lattice degeneration, or strong family history; reduces detachment risk in fellow eyes or after initial detachment.
