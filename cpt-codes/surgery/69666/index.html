69666 Repair middle ear structures

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ossicular repair, ossicular discontinuity repair, fistula repair

Repair of ossicular discontinuity or window fistula of the middle ear using ossicular reconstruction techniques.

Clinical Context

Used for ossicular chain disruption from trauma, infection, or prior surgery. May include window fistula repair.
