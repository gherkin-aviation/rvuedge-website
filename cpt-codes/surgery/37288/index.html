37288 Revsc evsc tpvt athrc sf first

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: tibioperoneal atherectomy simple first, TPT debulking first

Percutaneous or open revascularization of the tibioperoneal trunk or artery using atherectomy with simple technique in the first treated vessel.

Clinical Context

Atherectomy (plaque removal) as primary strategy. First tibioperoneal vessel with atherosclerotic disease.
