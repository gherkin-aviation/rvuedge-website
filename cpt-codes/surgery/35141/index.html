35141 Repair defect of artery

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: femoral artery repair, thigh artery repair

Repair of a defect or aneurysm of the femoral artery in the thigh region.

In Plain Language

Fixing a thigh artery; Repairing femoral artery damage

Clinical Context

Indicated for femoral artery aneurysm, pseudoaneurysm, or stenotic lesions causing claudication, tissue ischemia, or acute limb-threatening compromise. Commonly encountered in atherosclerotic disease and access site complications.
