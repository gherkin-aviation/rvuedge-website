27345 Removal of knee cyst

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Baker cyst removal, knee cyst excision

Removal of cyst of knee, typically Baker cyst, through operative approach.

In Plain Language

Knee cyst removal; Back of knee fluid sac removal

Clinical Context

Applied to symptomatic Baker cysts or other knee cysts causing pain or restricted motion.
