41870 Periodontal mucosal grafting

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: mucosal graft, soft tissue graft, periodontal graft

Surgical grafting of mucosal tissue to the periodontal area to improve gingival health, increase keratinized tissue, or correct gingival recession.

In Plain Language

gum graft; graft to fix gum recession; mucosal tissue transplant

Clinical Context

Performed by periodontists for root coverage in recession, increasing attached gingiva, or aesthetic improvement. Uses connective tissue or free gingival grafts.

RVU Information

CPT 41870 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
