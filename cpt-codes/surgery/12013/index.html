12013 repair f/e/e/n/l/m 2.6-5.0 centimeters

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: Simple facial laceration repair, Facial wound closure

Repair of simple laceration or wound on the face, ear, eyelid, nose, lip, or mucous membrane measuring two point six to five centimeters.

Clinical Context

Used for simple closure of small to medium facial lacerations with attention to cosmetic outcome.
