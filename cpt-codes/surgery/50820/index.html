50820 Construct bowel bladder

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ileal neobladder, ileal bladder, continent ileal reservoir

Construction of ileal bladder (ileal neobladder), surgical creation of a continent urinary reservoir from ileum with ureteral implantation.

Clinical Context

Performed after cystectomy for bladder cancer or severe bladder dysfunction. Provides continence and allows catheterizable pouch creation.
