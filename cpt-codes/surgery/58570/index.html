58570 Tlh uterus 250 g or less

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: TLH, total laparoscopic hysterectomy

Total laparoscopic hysterectomy with uterus 250 grams or less involves complete removal of uterus endoscopically without extension to vaginal removal.

Clinical Context

Minimally invasive hysterectomy approach with excellent outcomes for uncomplicated benign pathology. Standard approach when vaginal hysterectomy not feasible.
