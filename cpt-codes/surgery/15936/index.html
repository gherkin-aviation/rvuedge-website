15936 excision sacral pr ulc prep mus flap

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: sacral muscle flap, sacral myocutaneous flap

Excision of sacral pressure ulcer with preparation and coverage using muscle flap for maximum tissue vascularization and healing.

Clinical Context

Used for large or deep sacral pressure ulcers where muscle flap provides superior blood supply and reduces recurrence. Prepares the muscle for transposition.
