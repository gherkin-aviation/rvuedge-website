63012 Remove lamina/facets lumbar

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: lumbar laminofacetectomy, lumbar facet joint decompression

Laminotomy with removal of lamina and facet joint exploration in lumbar spine. Includes facetectomy and nerve root exposure.

In Plain Language

lumbar bone and joint removal for nerve relief; lumbar facet decompression

Clinical Context

Used for lumbar spinal decompression involving both lamina and facet joint removal.
