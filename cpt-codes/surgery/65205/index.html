65205 Remove foreign body from eye

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: anterior chamber foreign body removal, FB removal anterior

Removal of a foreign body from the anterior chamber of the eye without use of an operating microscope. Minor procedure typically performed in outpatient or emergency setting.

Clinical Context

Indicated for small or easily accessible foreign bodies in the anterior chamber (cornea or lens area). Performed with minimal anesthesia, minimal recovery time.
