26567 Correct finger deformity

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Finger deformity correction, Finger osteotomy, Digital alignment

Surgical correction of a finger bone or joint deformity to restore anatomic alignment and improve hand function, typically involving osteotomy or realignment.

Clinical Context

Indicated for finger deformity, malunion after fracture, rotational deformity, or angular deformity affecting function or appearance.
