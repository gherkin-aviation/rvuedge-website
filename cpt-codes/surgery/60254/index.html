60254 Extensive thyroid surgery

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: total thyroidectomy, complete thyroidectomy, thyroid resection with lymphadenectomy

Extensive or complete thyroidectomy with removal of the entire thyroid gland including isthmus, sometimes with management of cervical lymph nodes for thyroid cancer.

In Plain Language

complete thyroid removal; removal of entire thyroid gland

Clinical Context

Performed for thyroid cancer, diffuse Graves' disease, extensive multinodular goiter, or severe hyperthyroidism requiring complete ablation. Requires lifelong thyroid hormone replacement.
