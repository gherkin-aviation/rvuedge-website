31239 Nsl/sinus endoscopy surgical dcr

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: endoscopic DCR, lacrimal duct bypass, endoscopic tear duct surgery

Nasal and sinus endoscopic surgery with dacryocystorhinostomy, creating a new drainage pathway from the lacrimal sac to nasal cavity.

In Plain Language

tear duct opening surgery

Clinical Context

Endoscopic creation of drainage pathway for chronic nasolacrimal duct obstruction. Avoids external incision and offers good visualization and success rates.
