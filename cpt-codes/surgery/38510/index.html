38510 Biopsy/removal lymph nodes

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: Neck/axilla lymph node biopsy, Cervical node removal

Surgical biopsy or removal of lymph nodes from the supraclavicular, cervical, or axillary regions.

Clinical Context

Diagnostic procedure for evaluating supraclavicular, cervical, or axillary lymphadenopathy suggestive of malignancy or infection.
