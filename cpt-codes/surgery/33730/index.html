33730 Repair heart-vein defect(s)

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: complex venous defect repair, multiple vein repair, complex vein-heart repair

Surgical repair of heart-vein defect(s) with multiple venous anomalies or complex venous return abnormalities.

In Plain Language

fixing multiple vein connection problems

Clinical Context

Utilized for complex cases of anomalous venous return involving multiple pulmonary or systemic veins; achieves comprehensive venous rerouting.
