26746 Treat finger fracture each

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: complex phalanx fixation, intra-articular phalangeal repair, advanced finger fracture fixation

Surgical treatment of phalangeal (finger bone) fracture with open reduction and complex internal fixation including articular surface reconstruction.

Clinical Context

Used for complex intra-articular phalangeal fractures with joint surface involvement requiring precise reduction and fixation to prevent post-traumatic arthritis.
