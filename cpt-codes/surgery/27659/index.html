27659 Repair of leg tendon each

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: extensor tendon repair, anterior tibialis repair

Surgical repair of lower leg tendon in extensor compartment performed for each tendon repaired.

Clinical Context

Indicated for injury to tibialis anterior, extensor digitorum longus, or extensor hallucis longus. Restoration of dorsiflexion function.
