27691 Revise lower leg tendon

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: complex tendon revision, major tendon revision

Surgical revision of lower leg tendon with complex reconstruction including transfer, lengthening, or multiple tenodesis.

Clinical Context

Highest complexity tendon revision involving major reconstruction. Used for severe sequelae or failed complex repairs.
