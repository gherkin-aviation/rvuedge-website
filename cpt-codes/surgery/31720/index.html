31720 Clearance of airways

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: airway clearance, airway suctioning, secretion removal

Clearance of airways by suctioning, removal of secretions, or mechanical clearance of mucus plugs, blood, or other obstructive material from tracheal and bronchial passages.

Clinical Context

Performed in patients with impaired airway clearance, mucus plugging, or secretion accumulation causing hypoxemia or ventilation compromise.
