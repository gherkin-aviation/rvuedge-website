31370 Partial removal of larynx

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: supraglottic laryngectomy, supraglottic partial resection, epiglottectomy

Partial laryngeal resection via supraglottic approach, removing supraglottic structures (epiglottis, false cords) while preserving vocal cords.

Clinical Context

Supraglottic laryngectomy for cancer of epiglottis or false vocal cords. Preserves voice and requires modified swallowing rehabilitation.
