22844 Insert spine fixation device

Surgery

Also known as: Pedicle screw fixation multi-level, Segmental instrumentation 3-4 levels

Posterior segmental instrumentation (eg, pedicle screw, hook); 3-4 segments.

Clinical Context

Posterior segmental instrumentation for multi-level thoracolumbar instability; provides strong fixation across 3-4 vertebral segments.
