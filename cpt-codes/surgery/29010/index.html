29010 application risser jacket body only

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: Risser jacket, body cast, trunk cast

Application of Risser jacket body cast covering shoulders to hips on trunk only, used for thoracic and thoracolumbar spine immobilization without head inclusion.

Clinical Context

Used for thoracic and thoracolumbar spine fractures or post-surgical immobilization; Risser jacket provides trunk stability.
