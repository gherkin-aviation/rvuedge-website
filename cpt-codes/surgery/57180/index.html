57180 Treat vaginal bleeding

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: Vaginal hemorrhage control, Hemostasis of vaginal bleeding

Treatment of vaginal bleeding or hemorrhage. Management of excessive vaginal bleeding through cautery or other hemostatic technique.

Clinical Context

Used for anovulatory bleeding, atrophic vaginitis, or post-trauma hemorrhage. Provides immediate hemostasis.
