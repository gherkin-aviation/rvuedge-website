30450 Revision of nose

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: extensive nasal revision, major rhinoplasty revision, extensive nasal reconstruction revision

Revision of prior nasal surgery with extensive reconstruction including major grafting and structural repair for severe complications.

In Plain Language

extensive nose surgery redo

Clinical Context

Indicated for severe complications from prior nasal surgery (severe obstruction, collapse, defects) requiring major reconstruction and grafting.
