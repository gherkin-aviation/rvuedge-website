67900 Repair brow defect

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: eyebrow repair, brow defect repair, eyebrow reconstruction

Repair of eyebrow or supraorbital region defect from trauma, burn, or surgical loss using local tissue rearrangement, flap, or graft techniques.

Clinical Context

Reconstruction of eyebrow loss from trauma, burn, or surgical excision; uses local flaps, free grafts, or tissue expansion.
