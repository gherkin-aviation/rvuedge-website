61530 Removal of brain lesion

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: posterior fossa tumor resection, infratentorial tumor removal

Craniotomy or craniectomy with removal of intracranial mass lesion from infratentorial (posterior fossa) location.

In Plain Language

surgical opening to remove lower brain tumor

Clinical Context

Performed for infratentorial tumors (brainstem gliomas, cerebellar astrocytomas, fourth ventricle tumors, etc.). More technically challenging than supratentorial surgery due to proximity to vital structures.
