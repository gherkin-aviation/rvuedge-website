26862 Fusion/graft of finger joint

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: DIP fusion with graft, PIP fusion with graft, finger joint fusion

Surgical fusion of an interphalangeal joint of a finger with autogenous bone graft to enhance bony union and heal difficult cases.

In Plain Language

fusing finger joint with bone graft; finger joint surgery with bone material

Clinical Context

Indicated for interphalangeal joint fusion requiring bone graft supplementation in complex cases, revision surgery, or when primary fusion fails.
