23525 Cltx strnclav dislc with mnpj

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: closed reduction SC dislocation, SC dislocation reduction, manipulation SC dislocation

Closed treatment (manipulation) of sternoclavicular dislocation with manipulation. Conservative treatment including reduction of sternoclavicular dislocation under anesthesia.

Clinical Context

Used for anterior SC dislocations amenable to closed reduction; provides initial stabilization though recurrence is common.
