56605 Biopsy of vulva/perineum

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: Vulvar biopsy, Perineal biopsy

Biopsy of vulva or perineal tissue. This simple biopsy procedure provides tissue for histopathologic diagnosis.

Clinical Context

Used to diagnose vulvar pathology including dysplasia, carcinoma, dermatologic conditions, and infectious diseases. Office procedure under local anesthesia.
