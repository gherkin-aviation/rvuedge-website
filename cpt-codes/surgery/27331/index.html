27331 Explore/treat knee joint

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: knee arthrotomy exploration, loose body removal knee

Exploration and treatment of knee joint pathology including loose body removal, synovectomy, or cartilage treatment.

Clinical Context

Used for intra-articular loose bodies, synovitis, or cartilage flaps causing mechanical symptoms.
