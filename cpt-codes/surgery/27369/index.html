27369 Njx cntrst knee arthg/CT/MRI

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: knee injection contrast, knee arthrography, intra-articular injection

Injection of contrast medium into knee joint for arthrocentesis, arthropraphy, computed tomography, or magnetic resonance imaging studies.

Clinical Context

Used for diagnostic imaging of knee joint pathology before advanced imaging modalities.
