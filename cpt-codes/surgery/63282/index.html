63282 biopsy/excision idrl spine lesion lumbar

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: lumbar intradural extramedullary lesion, lumbar schwannoma or meningioma excision

Biopsy and/or excision of intradural extramedullary spinal lesion in the lumbar region. This procedure involves sampling or removal of lesions within the dura but outside the spinal cord at lumbar levels.

In Plain Language

removal of growth inside spinal sac but outside spinal cord in lower back

Clinical Context

Used for lumbar schwannomas, meningiomas, or other intradural extramedullary tumors causing cauda equina compression.
