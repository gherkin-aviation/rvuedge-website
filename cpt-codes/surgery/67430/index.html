67430 Explore/treat eye socket

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: superior orbital approach, superior orbitotomy, eyelid approach

Exploration and surgical management of orbit through superior approach with superior canthal opening or superior orbital access for lesions in superior orbit.

Clinical Context

Preferred approach for superior orbital lesions, lacrimal gland masses, or superior orbital compartment pathology.
