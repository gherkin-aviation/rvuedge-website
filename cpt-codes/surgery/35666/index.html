35666 Bpg fem-ant tib pst tib/prnl

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: femorotibial bypass, infrapopliteal bypass, fem-tibial

Arterial bypass graft from femoral artery to anterior tibial, posterior tibial, or peroneal artery for infrapopliteal disease. Restores blood flow below the knee to the calf.

Clinical Context

Indicated for critical limb ischemia with infrapopliteal disease suitable for intervention. Saphenous vein preferred; limb salvage procedure.
