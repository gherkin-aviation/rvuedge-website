33620 Apply right and left pulmonary artery bands

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: pulmonary artery banding, PA banding, bilateral PA banding

Surgical application of restrictive bands around the right and left pulmonary arteries to control pulmonary blood flow.

In Plain Language

putting bands around lung arteries

Clinical Context

Performed as a palliative procedure in neonates/infants with excessive pulmonary blood flow (e.g., truncus arteriosus, large VSD); reduces right ventricular volume overload pending definitive repair.
