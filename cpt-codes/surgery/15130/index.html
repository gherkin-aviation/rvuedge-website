15130 Drm agrft t/a/l first 100 sq cm

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: dermal graft trunk, full-thickness dermal graft

Dermal autograft applied to trunk, arms, or legs, first 100 square centimeters. Full-thickness dermal layer graft technique for deeper wound coverage.

Clinical Context

Used for deep burns, extensive wounds, and defects requiring dermal replacement to restore proper skin architecture and improve healing.
