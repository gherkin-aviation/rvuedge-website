47620 Removal of gallbladder

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Open chole with CBD reconstruction, open CBD repair

Open cholecystectomy with common bile duct exploration via choledochotomy, stone extraction, and primary closure or reconstruction of the bile duct.

Clinical Context

Performed for complex bile duct injuries, strictures, or large stone disease requiring reconstruction. Most technically demanding cholecystectomy variant.
