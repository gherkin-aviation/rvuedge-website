64856 Repair/transpose nerve

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: nerve repair and transposition, nerve decompression and relocation

Surgical repair and transposition of a nerve to relieve compression and restore function. Commonly performed for cubital tunnel syndrome or other compressive neuropathies.

In Plain Language

moving and repairing a nerve; nerve relocation surgery

Clinical Context

Indicated when a damaged nerve also requires relocation to prevent recurrent compression. Common procedure for ulnar nerve at elbow or tibial nerve at knee.
