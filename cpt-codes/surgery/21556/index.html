21556 excision neck tumor deep < 5 centimeters

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: deep neck lesion small excision, small deep neck tumor removal

Excision of deep lesion or tumor of neck, less than 5 centimeters. Surgical removal of deep neck lesion extending into muscle or deeper structures, with lesion under 5 centimeters in maximum dimension.

Clinical Context

Used for deep neck pathology of moderate size under 5 centimeters requiring removal through deeper tissue planes. Less extensive than larger deep lesions but still requiring careful dissection to avoid neurovascular structures.
