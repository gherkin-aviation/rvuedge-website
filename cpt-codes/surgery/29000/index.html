29000 application halo type body cast

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: halo body cast, cervical immobilization, halo cast

Application of halo-type body cast including head and trunk immobilization, used for cervical spine stabilization and immobilization following trauma or surgery.

Clinical Context

Used for cervical spine fractures, instability, or post-surgical immobilization; halo provides external stabilization and promotes healing.
