35304 Rechanneling of artery

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: femoral endarterectomy, femoral artery rechanneling

Rechanneling (thrombectomy with endarterectomy) of artery in the femoral region with restoration of vessel patency.

Clinical Context

Surgical management of femoropopliteal stenotic disease causing claudication or limb-threatening ischemia. Excellent results with good long-term patency; considered gold standard for suitable lesions.
