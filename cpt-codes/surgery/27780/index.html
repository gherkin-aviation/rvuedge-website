27780 Treatment of fibula fracture

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: closed reduction fibula, conservative fibular treatment

Initial closed treatment of fibular shaft fracture with manipulation and immobilization without operative intervention.

Clinical Context

Appropriate for uncomplicated closed fibular fractures without syndesmotic injury. Casting provides functional treatment.
