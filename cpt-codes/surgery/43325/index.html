43325 Revise esophagus & stomach

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: revision esophagogastrostomy, anastomotic revision, revisional surgery

Revision of esophagogastrostomy with reconstruction and rerouting of anastomosis for failed or compromised initial repair. This addresses complications of prior surgery.

Clinical Context

Performed for stricture formation, reflux, or poor function after initial esophagogastrostomy; higher morbidity than primary procedure.
