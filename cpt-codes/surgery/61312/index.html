61312 craniectomy/craniotomy sttl xdrl/sdrl

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: extradural decompression, epidural decompression, craniotomy evacuation

Craniotomy or craniectomy with exploration and/or decompression of extradural (outside dura mater) supratentorial space for management of mass lesions or elevated intracranial pressure.

In Plain Language

surgical opening for decompression of swelling outside brain lining

Clinical Context

Performed for epidural hematoma evacuation, decompression from brain swelling or mass effect, or management of increased intracranial pressure. Provides decompression while preserving dural integrity when possible.
