36522 Photopheresis

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: extracorporeal photochemotherapy, ECPT, photopheresis

Photopheresis, therapeutic (include one or more photochemotherapy cabins and related equipment).

In Plain Language

treating blood with light to fight cancer and autoimmune disease; light-activated blood treatment

Clinical Context

Used in cutaneous T-cell lymphoma (mycosis fungoides), GVHD, and certain autoimmune conditions. Combines apheresis with UV light exposure.
