20808 Replantation hand complete

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: hand replantation, hand reattachment

Replantation of a completely amputated hand with vascular and nerve repair.

Clinical Context

Emergency microsurgical procedure for complete hand amputation; requires intricate vessel, nerve, tendon, and bone repair for functional restoration.
