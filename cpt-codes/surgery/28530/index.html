28530 Treat sesamoid bone fracture

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: sesamoid fracture closed, sesamoid treatment non-operative, closed treatment

Closed treatment of sesamoid bone fracture without manipulation, used for non-displaced sesamoid fractures (typically medial or lateral sesamoids under first metatarsal head).

Clinical Context

Used for non-displaced sesamoid fractures managed conservatively with immobilization and protected weight-bearing.
