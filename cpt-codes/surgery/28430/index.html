28430 Cltx talus fracture without mnpj

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: talus fracture closed treatment, ankle bone fracture non-operative, closed reduction

Closed treatment of talus fracture without manipulation, used for non-displaced or minimally displaced talus fractures managed with immobilization alone.

Clinical Context

Used for non-displaced talus fractures suitable for conservative management with immobilization; avoids operative morbidity for uncomplicated fractures.
