25920 Amputate hand at wrist

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: wrist disarticulation, hand amputation at wrist

Amputation (removal) of the hand at the wrist level (disarticulation) or above metacarpal bases.

Clinical Context

Used for primary hand amputation at wrist level due to severe trauma, vascular insufficiency, infection, or malignancy.
