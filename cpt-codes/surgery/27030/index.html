27030 Arthrotomy hip with drainage

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: hip arthrotomy, hip joint drainage, hip joint exploration

Surgical opening of the hip joint (arthrotomy) with fluid drainage and exploration to treat infection or remove loose bodies.

Clinical Context

Indicated for septic hip arthritis, hemarthrosis, or loose bodies requiring open joint access for drainage and thorough exploration.
