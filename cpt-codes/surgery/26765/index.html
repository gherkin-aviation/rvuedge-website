26765 Treat finger fracture each

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: complex DIP repair, complex PIP repair, interphalangeal dislocation repair

Surgical treatment of interphalangeal joint dislocation with open reduction and complex fixation involving ligamentous repair or articular reconstruction.

Clinical Context

Indicated for complex interphalangeal dislocations with ligamentous injury, volar plate disruption, or fracture-dislocation requiring extensive surgical repair.
