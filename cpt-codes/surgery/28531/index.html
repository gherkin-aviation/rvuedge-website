28531 Treat sesamoid bone fracture

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: sesamoid fracture reduction, sesamoid manipulation, closed reduction

Closed treatment of sesamoid bone fracture with manipulation to achieve anatomic alignment, used for displaced sesamoid fractures amenable to closed reduction.

In Plain Language

Sesamoid fracture reduction; Sesamoid alignment; Ball of foot fracture reduction

Clinical Context

Used for displaced sesamoid fractures; closed reduction restores alignment and function of the first metatarsophalangeal joint.
