33900 Percutaneous p-art revsc 1 nm nt uni

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: PA stent unilateral, pulmonary artery revascularization, PA intervention

Percutaneous pulmonary artery revascularization with placement of one native artery stent in unilateral pulmonary circulation; minimally invasive stent placement in a single native pulmonary artery branch.

Clinical Context

Percutaneous intervention for pulmonary artery stenosis in patients with complex congenital heart disease, branch pulmonary artery obstruction, or chronic thromboembolic disease.
