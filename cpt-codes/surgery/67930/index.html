67930 Repair eyelid wound

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: eyelid laceration closure, eyelid wound repair

Repair of eyelid laceration or wound using primary closure with appropriate tissue-specific technique.

Clinical Context

Used for acute eyelid lacerations from trauma. Common indications include penetrating eye injuries, falls, motor vehicle accidents, or blunt eyelid trauma.
