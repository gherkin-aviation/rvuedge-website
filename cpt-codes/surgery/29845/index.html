29845 Wrist arthroscopy/surgery

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: wrist synovectomy, synovial cleanup, wrist joint cleanup

Arthroscopy of the wrist with synovectomy for removal of inflamed synovial tissue or treatment of inflammatory wrist disease.

Clinical Context

Used for rheumatoid arthritis, inflammatory synovitis, and chronic wrist inflammation.
