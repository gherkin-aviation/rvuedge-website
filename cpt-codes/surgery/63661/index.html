63661 Remove spine eltrd percutaneous aray

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: spinal electrode removal, SCS array removal, percutaneous electrode removal

Removal of spinal neuroelectrode array from percutaneous placement. This procedure involves removal of electrode arrays that were placed through a percutaneous approach.

Clinical Context

Used for removal of percutaneously placed spinal cord stimulation electrodes, typically due to malfunction, infection, or patient request.
