38530 Biopsy/removal lymph nodes

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Axillary lymph node dissection, ALND

Surgical removal of axillary (armpit) and supraclavicular lymph nodes for staging or treatment of upper extremity or thoracic malignancies.

Clinical Context

Commonly performed in staging and treatment of breast cancer, melanoma, and sarcomas of the upper extremity.
