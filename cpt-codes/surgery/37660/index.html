37660 Ligation common iliac vein

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Common iliac vein ligation

Surgical ligation of the common iliac vein, a major pelvic vessel returning blood from the lower extremity and pelvis.

Clinical Context

Performed for acute thrombosis with sepsis, massive pulmonary embolism when anticoagulation is contraindicated, or iliac vein obstruction.
