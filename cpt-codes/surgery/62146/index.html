62146 Crnop with autograft<5 centimeters diam

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: autograft cranioplasty small, bone graft skull repair

Cranioplasty with autologous bone graft for skull defect less than 5 centimeters in diameter. Uses patient's own bone.

In Plain Language

bone graft skull repair; natural bone skull repair

Clinical Context

Used for cranioplasty using the patient's own bone graft for defects smaller than 5 centimeters, providing biological incorporation.
