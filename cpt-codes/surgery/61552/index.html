61552 Release of skull seams

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: multiple suture release, compound craniectomy

Craniectomy with release and separation of multiple skull sutures for treatment of complex cranial vault abnormalities.

Clinical Context

Performed for multiple suture synostosis (bicoronal, biconal presentations) or complex skull deformities requiring more extensive vault expansion.
