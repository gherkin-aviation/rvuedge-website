33285 Insj subcutaneous car rhythm mntr

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: cardiac event recorder, subcutaneous rhythm monitor, CRM insertion

Insertion of a subcutaneous cardiac rhythm monitor, implanting a device under the skin to detect and record arrhythmias.

Clinical Context

Used for long-term monitoring of suspected arrhythmias, particularly for patients with syncope or palpitations.
