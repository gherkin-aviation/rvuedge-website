58270 Vag hyst with enterocele repair

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: VH with enterocele repair, vaginal hysterectomy with pelvic support

Vaginal hysterectomy with enterocele repair removes uterus via vaginal approach while addressing enterocele (hernia of small bowel into pelvis).

In Plain Language

Uterus removal with bowel hernia repair; Vaginal hysterectomy with enterocele fix

Clinical Context

Corrects pelvic prolapse and hernia of bowel during hysterectomy. May involve repair of cul-de-sac and pelvic fascia.
