33645 Revision of heart veins

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: vein revision, pulmonary vein rerouting, vein repositioning

Surgical revision of cardiac veins, typically repositioning or rerouting pulmonary veins or systemic veins to correct anomalous return or improve hemodynamics.

Clinical Context

Performed for partial anomalous pulmonary venous return or other abnormal vein drainage patterns; re-establishes normal venous drainage to appropriate chambers.
