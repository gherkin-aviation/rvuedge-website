66983 Cataract surgical with iol 1 stage

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: phacoemulsification with IOL, standard cataract surgery

Cataract surgery (e.g., phacoemulsification) with insertion of intraocular lens; one-stage procedure (combined with other modifying procedures for same eye, other than surgery of the cornea).

Clinical Context

Phacoemulsification with IOL implantation as standard surgical approach. Can include additional procedures like iris hooks, zonular support.
