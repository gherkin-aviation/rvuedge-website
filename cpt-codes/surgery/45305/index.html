45305 Proctosigmoidoscopy with biopsy

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: proctoscopy with biopsy, sigmoidoscopy with biopsy, rectal biopsy

Proctosigmoidoscopy with tissue biopsy of rectum or sigmoid colon for histopathological evaluation.

Clinical Context

Performed to evaluate suspicious lesions, inflammatory changes, persistent bleeding, chronic diarrhea, or abnormal findings requiring tissue diagnosis.
