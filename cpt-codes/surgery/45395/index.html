45395 Laparoscopic removal of rectum

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: laparoscopic rectal resection, lap proctectomy, minimally invasive rectal surgery

Laparoscopic total proctectomy (laparoscopic removal of rectum) with or without anastomosis or pouch construction.

In Plain Language

camera-assisted rectum removal; minimally invasive rectal surgery

Clinical Context

Performed for rectal cancer, inflammatory bowel disease, or familial polyposis using laparoscopic approach for reduced morbidity.
