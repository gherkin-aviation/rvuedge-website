Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: deep implant removal, deep hardware extraction, hardware removal, implant removal, plate removal, screw removal

Removal of a deep implant.

In Plain Language

removal of deep implant in bone or muscle; taking out buried bone hardware

Clinical Context

Used for removal of deeply buried hardware including plates, screws, intramedullary devices, or prosthetic components from bone.
