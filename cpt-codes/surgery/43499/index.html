43499 Unlisted procedure esophagus

Surgery

Also known as: unlisted esophageal procedure, other esophageal procedure

Unlisted procedure of the esophagus. This code is used for novel esophageal procedures not described by other CPT codes.

Clinical Context

Appropriate for experimental or novel esophageal procedures; requires detailed operative report for billing and medical necessity documentation.
