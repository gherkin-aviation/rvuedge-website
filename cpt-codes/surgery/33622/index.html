33622 Redo complicated cardiac anomaly

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: redo congenital repair, completion congenital repair, second stage congenital

Surgical redo operation for complete repair of a complex congenital cardiac anomaly, typically following initial staged procedure.

Clinical Context

Performed as definitive completion following initial palliation (e.g., after Fontan stage 2 or final VSD closure); higher operative risk due to adhesions and previous surgery.
