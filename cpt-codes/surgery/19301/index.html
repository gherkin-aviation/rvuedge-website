19301 Partial mastectomy

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: lumpectomy, partial mastectomy, segmental mastectomy, breast conservation surgery

Surgical removal of a portion of the breast tissue while preserving the remaining breast structure. This breast-conserving procedure maintains maximal breast tissue while removing the area of concern.

In Plain Language

removal of part of the breast; lump removal

Clinical Context

Used for treatment of early-stage breast cancer, benign lesions, or prophylactic removal in high-risk patients. Standard breast-conserving therapy often combined with radiation.

Billing & Documentation

As a surgical CPT code, proper documentation must include the operative report detailing the procedure performed, patient positioning, approach, findings, and any complications. This code has a 90-day global period, which includes the day of the procedure, 1 day preoperative, and 90 days of postoperative care. Ensure the diagnosis code (ICD-10) supports medical necessity for the procedure.
