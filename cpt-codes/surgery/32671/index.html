32671 Thoracoscopy pneumonectomy

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: VATS pneumonectomy, thoracoscopic total lung removal

Thoracoscopic pneumonectomy removing the entire lung via minimally invasive video-assisted approach, performed for advanced disease not suitable for partial resection.

Clinical Context

Used for advanced lung cancer, destroyed lung, or extensive disease requiring complete pneumonectomy when VATS approach is feasible.
