25350 Revision of radius

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: radial osteotomy, radius revision

Revision of the radius bone through osteotomy (cutting and realigning bone) or other procedures to correct alignment or malunion.

Clinical Context

Performed for radial malunion, angular deformity, or to correct pronation/supination loss.
