51575 Removal of bladder & nodes

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: radical cystectomy, cystectomy with PLND, cystoprostatectomy with lymphadenectomy

Removal of bladder with pelvic lymph node dissection, total cystectomy with en bloc removal of regional lymph nodes for cancer staging.

Clinical Context

Standard surgical treatment for muscle-invasive bladder cancer. Lymphadenectomy provides staging and oncologic control.
