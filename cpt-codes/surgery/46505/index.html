46505 Chemodenervation anal muscle

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: Botox injection, anal botox, botulinum toxin injection

Chemodenervation of anal muscles using botulinum toxin. Botox is injected into the anal sphincter to reduce spasticity and relieve anal fissure pain.

Clinical Context

Alternative or adjunct to surgery for chronic anal fissures. Reduces internal sphincter tone, improving blood flow and promoting healing.
