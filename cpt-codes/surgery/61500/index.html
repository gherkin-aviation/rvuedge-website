61500 Craniectomy excision tumor/bone lesion skull

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: skull tumor resection, bone lesion excision, cranial bone tumor removal

Craniotomy or craniectomy with excision of tumor or bone lesion of the skull, including external and internal bone removal.

Clinical Context

Performed for skull base tumors, bone tumors (osteoma, meningioma, etc.), traumatic bone defects, or congenital abnormalities requiring excision. Provides access for full-thickness skull resection.
