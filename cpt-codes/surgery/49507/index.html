49507 Prp i/hern initial block >5 yr

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: emergency hernia repair, incarcerated hernia older child, strangulated hernia repair, inguinal hernia repair incarcerated

Repair of inguinal hernia in patient age greater than five years with hernia incarcerated or strangulated.

Clinical Context

Urgent surgical repair of incarcerated or strangulated inguinal hernia in patients over five years old. Requires immediate intervention to prevent tissue necrosis.
