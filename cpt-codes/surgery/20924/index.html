20924 Removal of tendon for graft

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: tendon graft harvest, tendon autograft

Removal of tendon for use as an autogenous graft for tendon reconstruction.

Clinical Context

Used when autogenous tendon graft is needed for tendon reconstruction or repair; commonly uses palmaris longus or other expendable tendons.
