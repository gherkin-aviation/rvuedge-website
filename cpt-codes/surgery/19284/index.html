19284 Percutaneous device breast add strtctc

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: additional structural marking, extra 3D imaging localization

Image-guided placement of a percutaneous breast lesion localization device for each additional lesion using structural imaging guidance (CT, tomosynthesis). This represents add-on work for multiple lesion marking with 3D imaging.

In Plain Language

marking more breast lumps with 3D imaging

Clinical Context

Used for preoperative localization of multiple nonpalpable lesions when structural imaging is employed. Facilitates identification of all lesions requiring surgical intervention.
