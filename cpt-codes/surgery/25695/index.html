25695 Optx lunate dislocation

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ORIF lunate dislocation, open reduction lunate injury

Open treatment (surgical repair) of lunate dislocation with reduction and fixation.

Clinical Context

Used for lunate dislocations requiring operative intervention for reduction and stabilization. Often used when closed treatment has failed or for chronic dislocations.
