43217 Esophagoscopy snare lesion remv

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: EGD with snare polypectomy, endoscopic polypectomy

Esophagogastroduodenoscopy (EGD) with snare polypectomy (removal of esophageal polyp using snare wire loop).

Clinical Context

Therapeutic procedure for esophageal polyps. Snare technique allows complete removal with cauterization of base.
