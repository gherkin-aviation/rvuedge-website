28420 Optx calcaneal fracture with bone grf

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: calcaneal ORIF with graft, heel fracture graft, open reduction with bone graft

Open surgical treatment of calcaneal fracture with internal fixation and bone grafting, used for comminuted fractures with significant bone loss requiring structural augmentation.

Clinical Context

Used for comminuted intra-articular calcaneal fractures with bone loss; bone grafting provides structural support and enhances healing in severely fragmented fractures.
