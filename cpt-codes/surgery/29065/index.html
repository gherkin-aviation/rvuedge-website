29065 application cyst sho to hand lng arm

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: long arm cast, shoulder to hand cast, long arm plaster

Application of a short plaster cast extending from the shoulder to the hand and including the entire long arm to immobilize the upper arm and forearm for therapeutic or post-injury management.

In Plain Language

arm cast from shoulder to hand; full upper arm cast

Clinical Context

Used for proximal and mid-shaft humerus fractures, elbow injuries, and post-operative immobilization of shoulder and elbow procedures.
