26358 Repair/graft hand tendon

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Multi-tendon repair with graft, Complex tendon graft, Accessory tendon repair

Surgical repair and grafting of tendons in the hand with involvement of multiple tendon units, repair of more than one tendon strand with graft material. This includes repair and grafting of accessory structures or additional tendon components.

Clinical Context

Performed in severe hand injuries involving multiple tendon lacerations, extensive crush injuries, or when revision of prior failed tendon repairs is necessary with additional grafting.
