64568 Opn impltj crnl nerve neuroelectrode array & pulse generator

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: cranial nerve stimulator implantation, vagus nerve stimulator implant, VNS device implant

Open surgical implantation of a cranial nerve neuroelectrode for long-term electrical stimulation, such as vagus nerve stimulation.

In Plain Language

brain nerve stimulator surgery; vagus nerve pacemaker placement

Clinical Context

Used for epilepsy, depression, and chronic migraines. Vagus nerve stimulation provides long-term neurological treatment.
