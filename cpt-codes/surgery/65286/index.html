65286 Repair of eye wound

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: perforating injury repair, corneal-scleral flap repair

Repair of perforating eye injury; corneoscleral wound with conjunctival tissue.

Clinical Context

Perforating injuries involving both cornea and sclera requiring tissue coverage. Often performed urgently and may include additional procedures for anterior chamber reformation.
