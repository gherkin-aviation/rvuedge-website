58565 Hysteroscopy sterilization

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: hysteroscopic sterilization, Essure placement

Hysteroscopy with tubal sterilization involves endoscopic placement of contraceptive device or tissue destruction in fallopian tubes for permanent sterilization.

Clinical Context

Minimally invasive permanent contraception alternative to tubal ligation. Requires hysterosalpingography confirmation of tubal occlusion.
