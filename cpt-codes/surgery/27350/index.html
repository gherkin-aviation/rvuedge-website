27350 Removal of kneecap

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: patellectomy, kneecap removal

Removal of patella through operative approach, total patellectomy for severe patellofemoral arthritis or trauma.

In Plain Language

Kneecap removal surgery; Patella removal

Clinical Context

Indicated for severe patellofemoral arthritis, nonunion of patellar fracture, or anterior knee pain unresponsive to conservative care.

Billing & Documentation

As a surgical CPT code, proper documentation must include the operative report detailing the procedure performed, patient positioning, approach, findings, and any complications. This code has a 90-day global period, which includes the day of the procedure, 1 day preoperative, and 90 days of postoperative care.
