28052 Biopsy of foot joint lining

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: synovial biopsy midfoot, tarsal joint biopsy, Chopart joint biopsy

Biopsy of the synovial membrane (lining) of the midfoot or tarsal joints. This procedure obtains tissue from midfoot joint linings for histologic diagnosis.

Clinical Context

Used for diagnosis of midfoot arthropathy, infection, or inflammatory conditions. Lisfranc joint involvement commonly requires biopsy for diagnosis.
