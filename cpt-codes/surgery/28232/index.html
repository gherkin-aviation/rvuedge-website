28232 Incision of toe tendon

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: toe tendon tenotomy, toe tenotomy, toe tendon incision

Incision of a toe tendon without transfer or repair. This procedure divides a toe tendon for deformity correction.

Clinical Context

Used for toe contractures or deformities where simple tendon division improves position. Often combined with osseous procedures.
