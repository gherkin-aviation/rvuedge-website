40527 Reconstruct lip with flap

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: lip flap reconstruction, lip reconstruction

Reconstruction of the lip using a local flap, likely a more extensive flap procedure than 40525. Advanced lip reconstruction technique.

Clinical Context

Used for larger lip defects requiring more extensive flap reconstruction. May involve Bernard-Burrow-Hartill procedure or other complex reconstructive techniques.
