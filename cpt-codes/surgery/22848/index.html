22848 Insert pelvic fixation device

Surgery

Also known as: Pelvic fixation, Sacropelvic fixation

Pelvic fixation (attachment of caudal end of instrumentation to pelvis); any method.

In Plain Language

Attachment of spine hardware to pelvis; Spinal fixation to hip bone

Clinical Context

Used when spinal fusion extends to sacrum or requires additional stability by anchoring to pelvis; common in long constructs and deformity correction.
