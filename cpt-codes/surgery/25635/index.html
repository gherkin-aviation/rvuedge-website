25635 Cltx carpl fracture with mnpj each bone

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: closed reduction with manipulation carpal bone, percutaneous reduction carpal

Closed treatment (reduction) of carpal bone fracture (other than scaphoid) with manipulation, each bone.

Clinical Context

Used for displaced carpal bone fractures requiring manual reduction and immobilization. May be reported multiple times if multiple carpal bones are treated.
