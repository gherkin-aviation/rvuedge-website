49400 Air injection into abdomen

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: air insufflation, pneumoperitoneum, abdominal air injection

Pneumoperitoneum with injection of air into abdominal cavity for diagnostic imaging or therapeutic purposes.

Clinical Context

Intentional air injection into peritoneal cavity historically used for diagnostic imaging (pneumoperitoneum) or therapeutic indications. Rarely performed in modern practice.
