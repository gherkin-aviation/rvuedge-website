22804 Arthrd pst dfrm 13+ vrt segment

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Posterior spinal fusion 13+ levels, Extensive deformity correction

Arthrodesis, posterior technique, spinal deformity correction, with bone graft or nonvascularized bone graft; 13 or more vertebral segments.

Clinical Context

Used for severe scoliosis or kyphosis correction spanning 13 or more vertebral segments; most extensive fusion procedures.
