31375 Partial removal of larynx

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: true-false cord laryngectomy, extended partial laryngectomy, transoral partial resection

Partial laryngeal resection involving removal of both true and false vocal cord structures via transoral approach.

In Plain Language

multi-level voice box removal

Clinical Context

Partial laryngectomy involving both true and false vocal cord removal. More extensive than single-level approaches, requiring voice rehabilitation.
