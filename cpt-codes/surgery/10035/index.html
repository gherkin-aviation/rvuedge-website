10035 Plmt soft tissue loclzj device first

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: Localization wire placement, Bracketing, Tissue marker placement

Placement of soft tissue localization device, first lesion. Image-guided insertion of wire, clip, or other marker to identify and guide subsequent surgical excision of breast or soft tissue lesions.

Clinical Context

Performed prior to surgical excision to precisely locate impalpable lesions detected on imaging. Essential for breast-conserving surgery and accurate removal of small lesions.
