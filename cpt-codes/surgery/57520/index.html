57520 Conization of cervix

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: cone biopsy, cervical conization, LEEP, cold knife cone, CKC

Conization of cervix is a procedure in which a cone-shaped portion of cervical tissue is excised, typically using a scalpel, cold knife, or loop electrosurgical excision procedure technique.

In Plain Language

Cone removal from cervix; Cervix cone-shaped tissue removal

Clinical Context

Diagnostic and therapeutic procedure for cervical dysplasia. Provides tissue margins for histopathologic evaluation and removes dysplastic tissue. Indicated for cervical intraepithelial neoplasia grades 1-3.
