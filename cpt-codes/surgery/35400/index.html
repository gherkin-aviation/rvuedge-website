35400 Angioscopy

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: angioscopy, endoluminal visualization

Angioscopy (visualization of vessel interior) with imaging for diagnostic assessment or guidance of vascular procedures.

Clinical Context

Intraoperative imaging technique allowing direct visualization of vessel lumen to detect intimal flaps, thrombus, atherosclerotic disease, or assess anastomotic quality. Increasingly replaced by intraoperative duplex imaging.
