60210 Partial thyroid excision

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: partial thyroidectomy, thyroid lobectomy

Partial removal of thyroid gland (partial thyroidectomy) for benign pathology while preserving remaining thyroid tissue.

Clinical Context

Performed for unilateral thyroid pathology (nodules, goiter) to preserve contralateral gland function and avoid hypothyroidism.
