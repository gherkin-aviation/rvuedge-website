50948 Laparoscopic new ureter/bladder

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: lap ureteroneocystostomy, laparoscopic ureteral reimplant, lap UNC with flap

Laparoscopic creation of new ureter-to-bladder anastomosis with additional reconstruction or complex technique elements.

Clinical Context

Performed for complex ureteral obstruction or stricture requiring additional reconstruction. Laparoscopic approach with more extensive technique.
