24362 Reconstruct elbow joint

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: major elbow reconstruction, extensive elbow reconstruction

Reconstruction of the elbow joint with the most extensive procedure. Major reconstruction for severe or chronic instability.

Clinical Context

Used for extensive elbow joint reconstruction addressing severe instability, complex defects, or failed previous surgery.
