38207 Cryopreserve stem cells

Surgery

Also known as: Stem cell cryopreservation, Cell freezing

Cryopreservation (freezing) of harvested hematopoietic cells for long-term storage and later transplantation.

Clinical Context

Performed after harvesting stem cells to allow time for chemotherapy administration before reinfusion in transplant protocols.
