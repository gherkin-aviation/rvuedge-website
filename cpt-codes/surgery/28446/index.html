28446 Opn osteochondrl agrft talus

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: talar OCD surgery, osteochondral graft talus, talar dome grafting

Open surgical treatment of osteochondral fracture of the talus with graft placement, including reduction of the osteochondral fragment and bone graft augmentation to promote healing.

Clinical Context

Used for osteochondral lesions of the talus with bone loss; open reduction and bone grafting restores the articular surface and promotes healing.
