27447 Total knee arthroplasty

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: total knee arthroplasty, TKA, knee replacement, total knee replacement, knee arthroplasty

Surgical removal and replacement of all components of a primary total knee arthroplasty (femoral, tibial, and patellar if included) with new prosthetic components.

In Plain Language

knee replacement surgery

Clinical Context

Used for severe osteoarthritis, rheumatoid arthritis, post-traumatic arthritis, or severe knee dysfunction unresponsive to conservative treatment.
