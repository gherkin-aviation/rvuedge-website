27075 Resection hip tumor

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: hip tumor resection, pelvic tumor resection, hip sarcoma resection

Surgical resection of hip tumor with en bloc removal of the tumor and surrounding margin to ensure adequate oncologic clearance.

Clinical Context

Used for primary hip bone tumors or metastatic disease requiring wide surgical excision with appropriate margins for oncologic control.
