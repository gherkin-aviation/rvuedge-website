27788 Treatment of ankle fracture

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: percutaneous fixation lateral ankle, ankle fracture fixation

Initial closed treatment of ankle fracture (lateral malleolus) with manipulation and internal fixation.

Clinical Context

Performed for displaced lateral malleolar fractures requiring fixation through minimal approach or closed technique.
