33018 Prcrd drainage 0-5yr or with anomly

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: pericardial drain pediatric, pericardial drainage congenital, infant pericardiocentesis

Percutaneous pericardial drainage in patients age 0-5 years or patients with congenital cardiac anomaly. Modified approach for pediatric or complex cases.

Clinical Context

Used for pericardial effusion management in infants, young children, or those with congenital heart disease requiring special technical considerations.
