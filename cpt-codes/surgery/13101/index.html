13101 Cmplx repair trunk 2.6-7.5 centimeters

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: Complex laceration repair, Complex wound closure

Complex repair of laceration on the trunk measuring two point six to seven point five centimeters, involving layered closure with consideration of underlying structures.

In Plain Language

Complicated stitching of medium cut; Complex wound closure

Clinical Context

Used for medium-sized complex lacerations on the trunk requiring advanced reconstruction technique.
