67405 Explore/drainage eye socket

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: orbital drainage, abscess drainage, hematoma drainage

Exploration of orbit with drainage of abscess, fluid collection, or hematoma to relieve pressure and obtain culture material from infected or inflammatory orbital space.

In Plain Language

drainage of infection or fluid from eye socket

Clinical Context

Performed for orbital cellulitis with abscess, cavernous sinus thrombosis, or traumatic hematoma requiring urgent drainage.
