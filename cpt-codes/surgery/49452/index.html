49452 Replace g-j tube percutaneous

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: G-J tube replacement, gastrojejunal tube exchange, percutaneous G-J replacement

Replacement or repositioning of gastrojejunal feeding tube via percutaneous approach.

Clinical Context

Percutaneous exchange or replacement of gastrojejunal feeding tube due to dislodgement, clogging, or planned replacement.
