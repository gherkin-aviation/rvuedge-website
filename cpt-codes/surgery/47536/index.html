47536 Exchange biliary drainage catheter

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: Catheter exchange, tube exchange

Exchange of an existing percutaneous biliary drainage catheter for a new catheter, maintaining access while replacing the tube due to clogging, migration, or routine maintenance.

In Plain Language

Replacing the drainage tube while keeping the same opening

Clinical Context

Routine procedure for catheter maintenance in patients requiring long-term drainage. Prevents loss of access and maintains biliary decompression.
