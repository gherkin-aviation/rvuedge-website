44186 Laparoscopic jejunostomy

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: lap jejunostomy, laparoscopic feeding jejunostomy, lap J-tube

Laparoscopic creation of jejunal feeding access (jejunostomy) for enteral nutrition support.

Clinical Context

Laparoscopic creation of jejunal access for enteral feeding when gastric route unavailable or contraindicated (gastric outlet obstruction, aspiration risk).
