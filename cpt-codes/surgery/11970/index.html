11970 replacement tissue xpndr perm implant

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Tissue expander replacement, Implant replacement

Replacement of tissue expander with a permanent implant or reinsertion of expanded tissue, completing the reconstruction process.

Clinical Context

Following tissue expansion, the expander is removed and replaced with permanent reconstruction such as breast implant, autologous tissue transfer, or other reconstruction.
