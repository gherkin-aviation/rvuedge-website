43273 Endoscopic pancreatoscopy

Surgery

Also known as: pancreatoscopy, direct pancreatic visualization, pancreatic endoscopy

Endoscopic pancreatoscopy with direct visualization of the pancreatic duct and parenchyma using ultra-slim endoscope. This diagnostic procedure enables pancreatic assessment.

Clinical Context

Adjunctive ERCP procedure for evaluation of recurrent pancreatitis, ductal stones, strictures, or surveillance of cystic lesions; improves diagnostic accuracy.
