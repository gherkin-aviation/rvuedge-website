58920 Partial removal of ovary(s)

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: partial oophorectomy, ovarian wedge resection, partial ovarian removal

Partial removal of ovary or ovaries (partial oophorectomy), preserving remaining ovarian tissue to maintain hormonal and reproductive function.

Clinical Context

Used for ovarian cysts, benign tumors, or polycystic ovary disease in reproductive-aged women seeking to preserve fertility.
