36583 Replace tunneled cv catheter

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: IVC port replacement, port replacement, portacath replacement

Replacement of tunneled central venous catheter, with subcutaneous port; in inferior vena cava.

In Plain Language

replacing an implanted access port in the lower vein

Clinical Context

Used for replacement of IVC port catheters in patients with upper venous obstruction or thrombosis.
