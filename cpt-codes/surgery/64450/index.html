64450 Njx aa and/or strd other pn/branch

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: peripheral nerve block unspecified, other nerve block, miscellaneous nerve injection

Nerve injection for anesthetic and/or steroid administration to other peripheral nerves or branches not specifically listed. Single injection without imaging.

Clinical Context

Used for blocks of nerves not covered by specific codes, such as superficial cutaneous nerves or minor peripheral nerves. Flexible code for various pain management indications.
