26420 Repair/graft finger tendon

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Finger tendon repair with graft, Finger tendon graft, Single digit graft repair

Surgical repair and grafting of a finger tendon using graft material to augment repair, bridge defects, or restore adequate length in a single digit.

Clinical Context

Indicated for finger tendon lacerations with tissue loss, delayed presentation with infection, or tension-free repair that requires graft augmentation.
