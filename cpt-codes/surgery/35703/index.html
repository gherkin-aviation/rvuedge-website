35703 exploration n/flwd surgical lxtr artery

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: lower extremity exploration, leg vessel exploration, peripheral artery exploration

Exploration of lower extremity vessels with or without flowmetry for claudication, rest pain, or trauma evaluation. Assesses peripheral vascular disease.

Clinical Context

Performed for peripheral arterial disease assessment, graft surveillance, or trauma. Intraoperative imaging helps guide intervention.
