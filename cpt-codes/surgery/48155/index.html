48155 Removal of pancreas

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Pancreatectomy with splenectomy, distal or total resection

Distal or total pancreatectomy with splenectomy for distal or total pancreatic pathology including tumors, necrosis, or chronic inflammatory disease.

Clinical Context

Performed for distal or total pancreatic cancer, extensive necrosis, or severe chronic pancreatitis requiring both organs.
