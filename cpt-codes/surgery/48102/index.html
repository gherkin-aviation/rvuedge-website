48102 Needle biopsy pancreas

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: Percutaneous pancreatic biopsy, FNA, fine needle aspiration

Percutaneous needle biopsy of the pancreas using imaging guidance to obtain tissue samples for diagnosis of pancreatic disease.

In Plain Language

Taking small pancreas sample with needle through skin

Clinical Context

Minimally invasive approach to diagnose pancreatic cancer, cystic lesions, or focal inflammatory disease. Often ultrasound or CT-guided.
