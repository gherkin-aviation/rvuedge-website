23200 Radical rescj tumor clavicle

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: radical clavicular resection, en bloc clavicle excision, wide local excision clavicle

Radical resection of tumor (soft tissue and bone), clavicle. En bloc removal of malignant tumor involving clavicle and surrounding soft tissues with adequate margins.

In Plain Language

complete removal of cancer from collar bone; wide tumor removal collar bone

Clinical Context

Performed for primary bone tumors (sarcomas) or metastatic disease of the clavicle, requiring removal of tumor with adequate soft tissue margins to achieve local control.
