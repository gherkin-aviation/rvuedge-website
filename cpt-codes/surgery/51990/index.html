51990 Laparoscopic urethral suspension

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: laparoscopic Burch procedure, lap urethral suspension, laparoscopic continence procedure

Laparoscopic suspension of the urethra to correct stress urinary incontinence by elevating and stabilizing the urethra and urethrovesical angle using sutures placed through minimally invasive technique.

Clinical Context

Indicated for stress incontinence due to urethral hypermobility in women. Laparoscopic approach offers faster recovery than open Burch colposuspension with similar efficacy.
