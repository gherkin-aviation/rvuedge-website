23480 Revision of collar bone

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: clavicle revision, clavicular osteotomy revision, clavicle shortening revision

Revision of clavicle (osteotomy or bone shortening). Surgical revision or reshaping of clavicle for malunion, nonunion, or symptomatic hypertrophic changes.

Clinical Context

Performed for clavicular malunion with posterior displacement, shortening with neurologic symptoms, or previous fracture with poor healing requiring repositioning.
