65870 Incise inner eye adhesions

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: posterior synechiae laser lysis, iris-lens adhesion laser lysis

Incision of posterior synechiae; by laser surgery.

Clinical Context

Laser lysis of posterior synechiae in uveitis or following anterior uveitis. Prevents pupillary membrane complications.
