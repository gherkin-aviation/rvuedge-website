26725 Treat finger fracture each

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: metacarpal ORIF, hand bone fracture fixation, metacarpal plate fixation

Surgical treatment of metacarpal bone fracture of the hand with open reduction and internal fixation using plates, screws, or wires.

In Plain Language

repairing broken hand bone with hardware; fixing metacarpal with pins or plates

Clinical Context

Employed for displaced metacarpal fractures requiring operative intervention with internal fixation to maintain alignment and allow early mobilization.
