34707 Evasc repair ilio-iliac ndgft

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ilio-iliac EVAR, iliac-to-iliac endovascular repair

Endovascular repair of ilio-iliac vessels without graft. Percutaneous repair of iliac-to-iliac pathology without aortic involvement.

In Plain Language

connecting iliac arteries with stent across pelvis

Clinical Context

Used for isolated iliac artery disease or aneurysm. Provides iliac-to-iliac connection without aortic graft.
