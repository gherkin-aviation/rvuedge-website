28286 Repair of hammertoe

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: hammertoe repair simple, hammertoe correction simple, lesser hammertoe repair

Repair of hammertoe deformity with less extensive osseous component. This procedure corrects hammertoe with minimal bone work, primarily soft tissue.

Clinical Context

Performed for hammertoes requiring tendon release and joint release without significant osseous work. Often preserves joint motion.
