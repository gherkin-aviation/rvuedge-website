15782 Dermabrasion other than face

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: body dermabrasion, non-facial abrasion

Dermabrasion of body areas other than face such as trunk, arms, legs, or hands. Mechanical abrasion for non-facial skin resurfacing.

Clinical Context

Used for scars, surgical marks, or skin lesions on trunk and extremities requiring mechanical resurfacing.
