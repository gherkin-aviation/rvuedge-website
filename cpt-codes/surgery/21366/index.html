21366 Opn treatment complx malar with graft

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: complex malar with graft, malar fracture reconstruction

Open treatment of complex malar fracture with the addition of bone graft material for reconstruction and structural support.

Clinical Context

Used for complex malar fractures with bone loss requiring graft reconstruction.
