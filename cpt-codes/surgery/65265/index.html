65265 Remove foreign body from eye

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: intraocular FB removal, posterior segment FB removal, vitreous FB removal

Removal of foreign body from intraocular (posterior) segment of eye; all approaches.

In Plain Language

removing foreign object from inside back of eye; extracting debris from vitreous cavity

Clinical Context

Complex procedure for metallic or non-metallic foreign bodies in the vitreous or retinal area following penetrating eye trauma. Requires vitreoretinal expertise and sophisticated equipment.
