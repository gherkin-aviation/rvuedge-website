57556 Remove cervix repair bowel

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: cervicectomy with bowel repair, trachelectomy with intestinal repair

Removal of cervix with repair of adjacent bowel (intestines) involves cervicectomy combined with repair of small or large bowel injury or defect.

Clinical Context

Performed when cervical pathology coexists with bowel injury, often from endometriosis or prior surgery. Requires surgical expertise in both gynecology and general surgery.
