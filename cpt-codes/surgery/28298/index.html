28298 Cor hlx vlgs prx phlx osteot

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: double osteotomy hallux valgus, proximal-distal metatarsal osteotomy, double metatarsal cut bunion

Correction of hallux valgus with double osteotomy (typically proximal and distal metatarsal cuts), providing comprehensive realignment of the first metatarsal to address multiple components of the deformity.

In Plain Language

Bunion surgery with two bone cuts; Comprehensive bunion repair; Double level bunion correction

Clinical Context

Used for severe hallux valgus with both elevated intermetatarsal angle and metatarsophalangeal angle requiring correction at multiple levels for optimal functional outcome.
