45320 Proctosigmoidoscopy ablate

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: proctoscopy with laser ablation, sigmoidoscopy with electrofulg, rectal tumor destruction

Proctosigmoidoscopy with ablation of lesion using electrocautery, laser, cryotherapy, or radiofrequency technique.

Clinical Context

Used for palliation of obstructing rectal lesions, destruction of bleeding sources, treatment of recurrent polyps, or ablation of Barrett's-like changes.
