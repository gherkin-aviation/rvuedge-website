19355 Correct inverted nipple(s)

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: inverted nipple correction, nipple eversion

Surgical correction of inverted nipples by releasing fibrotic tissue and restoring nipple projection. This improves nipple appearance and function.

In Plain Language

fixing inverted or flat nipples

Clinical Context

Used for congenital inverted nipples or those becoming inverted with age. Improves breast appearance and may facilitate breastfeeding.
