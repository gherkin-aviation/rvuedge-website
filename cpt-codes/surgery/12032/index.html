12032 Intmd repair s/a/t/external 2.6-7.5

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: Intermediate laceration repair, Layered wound closure

Repair of intermediate complexity laceration on the scalp, axilla, trunk, or extremities measuring two point six to seven point five centimeters.

Clinical Context

Used for small to medium lacerations requiring layered closure with involvement of deeper tissue.
