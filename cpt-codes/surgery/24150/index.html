24150 Radiation rescj tumor dstl/shft humerus

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: humerus radical resection, humerus tumor wide excision

Radical resection of tumor of the distal or shaft humerus with en bloc removal of affected bone and surrounding structures. Wide excision for malignant bone tumors with adequate margins.

In Plain Language

Aggressive surgical removal of a tumor from the upper arm bone with surrounding tissue; Wide removal of bone cancer with safety margins

Clinical Context

Used for primary malignant bone tumors of the humerus such as osteosarcoma, Ewing's sarcoma, or chondrosarcoma with wide surgical margins.
