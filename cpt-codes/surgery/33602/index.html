33602 Closure of valve

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: complex ASD closure, modified ASD repair, ASD with additional procedure

Surgical closure of an atrial septal defect with modified approach or additional technical consideration for complex anatomy.

Clinical Context

Used for anatomically complex atrial septal defects or those with associated anomalies requiring modified surgical approach such as anomalous pulmonary vein connection.
