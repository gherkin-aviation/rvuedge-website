25290 Incise wrist/forearm tendon

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: tenotomy, tendon lengthening

Incision (tenotomy) of a wrist or forearm tendon to lengthen it, reduce tension, or improve function.

Clinical Context

Performed for contracture, spasticity, or to reduce tension in tight tendons. Often used in cerebral palsy or stroke patients.
