40845 Reconstruction of mouth

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: complex free flap reconstruction, maximal oral reconstruction, extensive flap surgery

Surgical reconstruction of the oral cavity and lips with the most extensive tissue transfer including free flaps with complex microvascular repairs.

In Plain Language

extensive mouth surgery with complex tissue transfer; major mouth rebuilding procedure; complex oral reconstruction

Clinical Context

Used for the most extensive oral defects involving multiple structures requiring complex free flap reconstruction with microvascular anastomosis. Common in massive cancer resection, extensive trauma, and severe burns.
