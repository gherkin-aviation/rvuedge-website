28310 Revision of big toe

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: hallux revision, first MTP revision, great toe revision

Surgical revision or reoperation on the first metatarsophalangeal joint or great toe, including repair of prior surgical complications or inadequate prior correction.

Clinical Context

Used for failed prior hallux surgery, including malunion, nonunion, recurrent deformity, or chronic pain requiring revision procedure.
