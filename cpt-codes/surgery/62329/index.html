62329 Therapy spinal puncture csf fluoroscopy/CT

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: CT-guided therapeutic spinal puncture, fluoroscopy-guided therapeutic puncture

Therapeutic lumbar or lower spinal puncture with imaging guidance via fluoroscopy or computed tomography. Includes drug or fluid administration.

In Plain Language

x-ray or CT guided spinal treatment; guided therapeutic spinal injection

Clinical Context

Used for therapeutic lumbar cerebrospinal fluid withdrawal or drug administration with fluoroscopic or computed tomography guidance.
