35531 Artery byp graft aorcel/aormesen

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: aortoceliac/aortomesenteric bypass, visceral bypass

Arterial bypass graft from aorta to celiac and superior mesenteric artery with restoration of visceral perfusion.

Clinical Context

Revascularization for chronic mesenteric ischemia from atherosclerotic disease. Indicated for patients with postprandial abdominal pain and weight loss refractory to medical management.
