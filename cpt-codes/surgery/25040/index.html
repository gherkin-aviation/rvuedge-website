25040 Arthrotomy rdcrpl/midcarpl joint

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: wrist arthrocentesis, wrist aspiration, radiocarpal tap

Arthrocentesis (surgical joint aspiration) of the radiocarpal joint (primary wrist joint) and/or midcarpal joint with removal of fluid and tissue for diagnostic or therapeutic purposes.

Clinical Context

Performed for diagnostic evaluation of joint effusions, infection assessment (septic arthritis), or therapeutic drainage of inflammatory fluid. Can be diagnostic (fluid analysis) or therapeutic.
