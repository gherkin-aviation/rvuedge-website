58575 Laps tot hyst resj mal

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: laparoscopic radical hysterectomy, laparoscopic oncologic hysterectomy

Laparoscopic total hysterectomy with radical dissection for malignancy involves endoscopic removal of uterus with extended parametrial and pelvic dissection.

Clinical Context

Oncologic hysterectomy via laparoscopy with parametrial dissection for gynecologic cancer. May include pelvic lymphadenectomy. Significant operative time and advanced skills required.
