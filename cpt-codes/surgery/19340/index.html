19340 Insj breast implant sm d mast

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: reconstruction at time of mastectomy, delayed-immediate reconstruction, breast implant insertion

Surgical insertion of a breast implant in conjunction with small (minor) primary mastectomy, such as subcutaneous or skin-sparing mastectomy. Reconstruction occurs at time of mastectomy.

In Plain Language

placing implant during breast removal surgery

Clinical Context

Used for immediate or delayed-immediate reconstruction following mastectomy for cancer or risk reduction. Improves cosmetic outcome and patient satisfaction.
