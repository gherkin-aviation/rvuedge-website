67911 Revise eyelid defect

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: complex eyelid revision, full-thickness eyelid repair

Revision of full-thickness eyelid defect involving margin repair with more complex reconstruction technique or larger defect.

In Plain Language

major eyelid repair fixing through entire lid; complex eyelid reconstruction

Clinical Context

Used for large or complex full-thickness eyelid defects involving the margin that require advanced reconstruction. Common indications include large traumatic full-thickness defects, complicated eyelid tumors, or burns affecting the eyelid margin.
