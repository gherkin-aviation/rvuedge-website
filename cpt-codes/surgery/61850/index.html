61850 Implant neuroelectrodes

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: DBS electrode implantation, neuroelectrode placement

Implantation of electrodes in brain for deep brain stimulation therapy for movement or neuropsychiatric disorder.

In Plain Language

Placing electrodes in brain for electrical stimulation

Clinical Context

Performed for Parkinson disease, tremor, dystonia, and other movement disorders; increasingly used for psychiatric conditions.
