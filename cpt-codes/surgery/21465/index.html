21465 Optx mndblr cndylr fracture

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: condyle fracture open reduction, TMJ condyle ORIF

Open treatment of mandibular condylar fracture. Surgical reduction and fixation of fractures involving the mandibular condyle, the articulating surface critical for temporomandibular joint function.

Clinical Context

Used for displaced or unstable mandibular condylar fractures affecting temporomandibular joint function. Requires careful technique to restore proper occlusion and joint mechanics. Often challenging due to anatomic complexity.
