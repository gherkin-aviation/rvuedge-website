33966 Ecmo/ecls removal prph cannula

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: ECMO peripheral removal second, additional cannula removal, multi-cannula removal

Extracorporeal membrane oxygenation/extracorporeal life support removal of peripheral venous/arterial cannula (second or greater removal); removal of additional peripheral cannulae.

Clinical Context

Removal of additional peripheral ECMO cannulae when multiple cannulae were placed and require sequential or staged removal.
