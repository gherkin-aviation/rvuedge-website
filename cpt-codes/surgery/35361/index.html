35361 Rechanneling of artery

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: venous thrombectomy, venous rechanneling

Rechanneling (thrombectomy with endarterectomy) of venous system with restoration of venous patency.

Clinical Context

Surgical intervention for acute venous thrombosis or chronic venous occlusion with hemodynamically significant obstruction. Primarily performed for lower extremity deep venous thrombosis with limb-threatening swelling.
