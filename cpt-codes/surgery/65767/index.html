65767 Epikeratoplasty

Surgery

Also known as: epikeratoplasty, surface keratoplasty

Epikeratoplasty.

In Plain Language

grafting tissue on cornea surface; early refractive surgery

Clinical Context

Historical refractive procedure placing corneal graft on epithelial surface. Largely superseded by LASIK and PRK.

RVU Information
