33864 Ascending aortic graft

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Composite graft, ascending aortic graft with AVR, Bentall procedure

Ascending aortic graft with composite graft including aortic valve replacement; surgical replacement of the ascending aorta and aortic valve with a composite conduit containing a mechanical or bioprosthetic valve.

Clinical Context

Used for patients with concurrent ascending aortic disease and aortic valve pathology, typically in connective tissue disorders (Marfan syndrome) or aortic root dilatation with regurgitation.
