26442 Release palm & finger tendon

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Extensive Dupuytren's release, Multiple digit release, Extensive fascia release

Surgical release of contractured palmar and finger fascia and tendons, performed for more extensive contractures involving multiple digits or more significant fascial involvement.

Clinical Context

Performed for advanced Dupuytren's contracture with involvement of multiple digits, or when previous release procedures have recurred.
