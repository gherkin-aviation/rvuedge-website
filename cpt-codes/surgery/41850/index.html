41850 Treatment of gum lesion

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: gum lesion treatment, gingival lesion excision

Surgical treatment or excision of a lesion located on the gingival tissue or gum. May include removal, cauterization, or other ablative procedures.

Clinical Context

Used when pathologic lesions appear on attached or free gingiva. May be biopsy for diagnosis or therapeutic removal of epulis, papilloma, or similar lesions.
