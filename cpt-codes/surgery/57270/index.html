57270 Repair of bowel pouch

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Uterine prolapse repair, Uterine suspension

Repair of uterine prolapse. Surgical correction of uterine descent into the vagina.

Clinical Context

Used for symptomatic uterine prolapse when uterus is preserved. Restores normal uterine position.
