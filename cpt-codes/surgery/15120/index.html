15120 Splt agrft f/s/n/h/f/g/m first

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: facial split graft, specialized STSG, full-thickness skin graft

Split-thickness skin autograft applied to face, scalp, neck, hands, feet, genitals, or mucosal surfaces, first increment. Specialized split-thickness graft for cosmetic areas.

In Plain Language

thin skin graft on face or sensitive area; specialized split-thickness transplant

Clinical Context

Used for facial burns, scalp injuries, hand injuries, and genital trauma requiring split-thickness grafts with specialized application for cosmetic outcomes.
