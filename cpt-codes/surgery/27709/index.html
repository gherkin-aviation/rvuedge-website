27709 Osteotomy tibia & fibula

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: tibial-fibular osteotomy, biplane osteotomy

Simultaneous surgical osteotomy of both tibia and fibula for complex deformity correction and leg realignment.

Clinical Context

Performed for complex varus/valgus deformities requiring correction at both bones. Increased work RVU vs. isolated procedures.
