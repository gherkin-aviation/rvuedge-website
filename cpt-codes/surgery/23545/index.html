23545 Cltx acromclav dislc with mnpj

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: closed reduction AC dislocation, AC reduction with anesthesia, manipulation AC dislocation

Closed treatment of acromioclavicular dislocation with manipulation. Conservative treatment including reduction of AC dislocation under anesthesia.

Clinical Context

Performed for AC dislocations requiring reduction to obtain anatomic alignment; helps control pain and improve early functional results.
