15845 Skin and muscle repair face

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: facial repair, face repair, skin muscle repair

Repair of facial skin and muscle defects using direct closure or local flaps to restore facial contour and function after trauma or surgery.

In Plain Language

Facial wound repair; Face skin and muscle reconstruction

Clinical Context

Used for traumatic facial injuries, post-tumor resection defects, or complications requiring simultaneous skin and muscle closure. Restores facial aesthetics and functional integrity.
