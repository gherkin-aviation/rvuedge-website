23600 Cltx proximal humeral fracture without mnpj

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: closed treatment proximal humerus, conservative shoulder fracture, non-operative shoulder fracture

Closed treatment of proximal humeral fracture (2-part, 3-part, or 4-part) without manipulation. Conservative management with sling immobilization and early passive motion.

Clinical Context

Appropriate for most minimally displaced proximal humeral fractures; achieves good functional outcomes with supportive care and guided rehabilitation.
