20838 Replantation foot complete

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: foot replantation, foot reattachment

Replantation of a completely amputated foot with vascular and nerve repair.

Clinical Context

Emergency procedure for complete foot amputation; complex reconstruction required for weight-bearing function restoration.
