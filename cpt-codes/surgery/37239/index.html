37239 Open/percutaneous place stent each add

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: additional segment stent placement, additional same-vessel stent

Open surgical or percutaneous placement of stent(s) in each additional segment within the same artery previously treated by endovascular intervention.

Clinical Context

Used for extensive single-vessel disease with multiple stenotic segments requiring stent coverage in same session.
