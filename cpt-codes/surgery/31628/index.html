31628 Bronchoscopy/lung biopsy each

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: transbronchial lung biopsy, bronchoscopic lung biopsy, TBLB

Bronchoscopy with transbronchial lung biopsy of lung parenchyma, using forceps or needle to obtain tissue from deeper lung structures for histopathologic diagnosis.

Clinical Context

Performed for suspected pulmonary infections (tuberculosis, fungal, viral), interstitial lung disease, or malignancy when tissue diagnosis from deeper lung is needed.
