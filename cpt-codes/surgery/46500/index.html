46500 Injection into hemorrhoid(s)

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: sclerotherapy, hemorrhoid injection, chemical ablation

Injection therapy into hemorrhoids. Chemical agents (sclerosants) are injected into or around hemorrhoid tissue to induce fibrosis and hemostasis.

In Plain Language

injecting medicine into hemorrhoids to shrink them; chemical treatment to stop hemorrhoid bleeding

Clinical Context

Minimally invasive treatment for internal hemorrhoids with bleeding. Causes fibrosis and hemostasis; recurrence common without additional treatment.
