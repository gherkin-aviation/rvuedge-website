69970 Remove inner ear lesion

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: labyrinthine lesion removal, inner ear tumor removal, labyrinth pathology excision

Surgical removal of inner ear lesion (tumor, cholesteatoma, or other pathology) from the labyrinth via translabyrinthine or other approach.

In Plain Language

removing a tumor or problem from the inner ear

Clinical Context

Used for vestibular schwannoma, hemangioma, cholesteatoma of labyrinth, or labyrinthine involvement of malignancy.
