28055 Neurectomy foot

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: foot neurectomy, nerve ablation foot, nerve resection

Neurectomy (nerve removal) of a nerve in the foot. This procedure removes or ablates a nerve to treat chronic neuropathic pain or neuroma.

Clinical Context

Indicated for symptomatic neuromas, chronic neuropathic pain, or chronic regional pain syndrome. Morton's neuroma is the most common indication.
