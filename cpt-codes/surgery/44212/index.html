44212 Laparoscopic total proctocolectomy

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: lap total proctocolectomy IPAA, lap ileoanal pouch, lap J-pouch

Laparoscopic total proctocolectomy with creation of internal pouch (ileal pouch anal anastomosis) without temporary ileostomy for complete removal of colon and rectum.

Clinical Context

Similar to 44210 but performed without need for temporary ileostomy, typically indicating surgeon confidence in immediate pouch function. Used for familial adenomatous polyposis and ulcerative colitis.
