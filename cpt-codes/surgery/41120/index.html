41120 Partial removal of tongue

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: partial glossectomy, hemiglossectomy, tongue partial removal

Partial surgical removal of the tongue (partial glossectomy) including necessary margins for malignancy.

Clinical Context

Used for tongue cancers requiring removal of a significant portion of the tongue. Common with T2-T3 squamous cell carcinoma with adequate margins.
