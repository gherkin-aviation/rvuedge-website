43107 Removal of esophagus

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: esophagectomy, total esophagus removal

Esophagectomy (total removal of the esophagus) via thoracic approach without reconstruction.

Clinical Context

Performed for esophageal malignancy, severe caustic injury, or end-stage achalasia. Requires reconstruction with stomach, colon, or small bowel for continuity.
