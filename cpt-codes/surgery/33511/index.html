33511 Cabg vein two

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: two vessel CABG, double SVG bypass, two vein bypass

Surgical placement of two coronary artery bypass grafts using saphenous vein conduits, revascularizing two distinct coronary territories.

Clinical Context

Performed for multivessel coronary disease affecting two territories; provides improved outcomes in patients with ejection fraction impairment or extensive ischemia burden.
