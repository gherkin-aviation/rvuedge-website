41017 Drainage of mouth lesion

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: salivary gland excision, glandular tissue removal, sialoadenectomy drainage

Surgical drainage with excision of salivary gland tissue or removal of involved gland.

In Plain Language

removing infected salivary gland; gland removal surgery; salivary gland excision

Clinical Context

Used for infected or diseased salivary glands requiring removal. Common with recurrent sialoadenitis, ductal obstruction, or malignancy of salivary gland.
