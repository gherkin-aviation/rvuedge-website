12056 Intmd repair face/millimeters 20.1-30.0

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: Intermediate facial laceration repair, Extended layered facial wound closure

Repair of intermediate complexity laceration on the face or mucous membrane measuring twenty point one to thirty centimeters.

In Plain Language

Multi-layer stitching of very large cut on face; Intermediate facial wound closure

Clinical Context

Used for very large facial lacerations requiring layered closure.
