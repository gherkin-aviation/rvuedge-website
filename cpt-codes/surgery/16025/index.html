16025 Dress/debrid p-thick burn m

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: partial thickness burn dressing medium, burn dress medium

Dressing and debridement of partial-thickness (second-degree) burn injury for medium treatment area.

Clinical Context

Performed for partial-thickness burns of moderate extent. Includes tissue debridement, cleansing, and dressing application.
