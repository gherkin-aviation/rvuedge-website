55400 Repair of sperm duct

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: vasovasostomy, vasal repair

Surgical repair or anastomosis of vas deferens (vasovasostomy) for obstruction or vasectomy reversal.

Clinical Context

Microsurgical reconstruction of vas deferens for vasectomy reversal or repair of vasal obstruction to restore fertility.
