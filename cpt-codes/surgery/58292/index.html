58292 Vag hyst t/o & repair complicated

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: complex VH with BSO and repair, complicated vaginal hysterectomy with repair

Complex vaginal hysterectomy with removal of tubes and ovaries plus repair of pelvic defects addresses multiple pelvic pathologies in single procedure.

Clinical Context

Most complex vaginal procedure combining challenging hysterectomy, oophorectomy, and pelvic floor reconstruction. Requires advanced surgical skill.
