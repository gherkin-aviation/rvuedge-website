27245 Treat thigh fracture

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: locked IM nailing, reamed IM nailing

Open treatment of femoral shaft fracture with intramedullary nailing including reaming and locked nailing for rotational control.

Clinical Context

Standard technique for femoral shaft fractures using reamed intramedullary nailing with proximal and distal locking for rotational and length control.
