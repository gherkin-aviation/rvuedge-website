63078 Spine disk surgery thorax

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: additional thoracic disc surgery, multi-level thoracic disc

Spine disc surgery in the thoracic region, add-on code for each additional segment. Used when thoracic disc procedures extend to multiple levels.

Clinical Context

Applied when multi-level thoracic discectomy is required for extensive disc pathology or compression affecting multiple spinal levels.
