28800 Amputation of midfoot

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: midfoot amputation, tarsal amputation, foot amputation

Surgical amputation through the midfoot (through the tarsal bones), removing the forefoot and toes while preserving the hindfoot and ankle for prosthetic fitting.

In Plain Language

Foot removal surgery; Midfoot amputation; Foot loss surgery

Clinical Context

Used for severe foot trauma, vascular disease, infection, or malignancy when foot salvage is impossible; provides functional level for prosthetic use.

Billing & Documentation
