41113 Excision of tongue lesion

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: tongue lesion with depth excision, deep tongue excision, muscle involvement excision

Surgical excision of a lesion of the tongue with removal of deep muscle tissue or structures.

Clinical Context

Used for tongue lesions with invasion into deep musculature or requiring removal of deeper structures. Common with invasive cancers or lesions with muscle involvement.
