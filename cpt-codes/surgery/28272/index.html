28272 Release of toe joint each

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: toe joint release, MTP joint release, toe deformity release

Release of a toe joint (metatarsophalangeal or interphalangeal joint) each. This procedure releases joint contracture on a per-toe basis.

Clinical Context

Performed for toe contractures (claw toes, hammertoes) limiting function. Per-toe coding allows documentation of bilateral or multiple toe involvement.
