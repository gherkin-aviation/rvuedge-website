53601 Dilate urethra stricture

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: urethral dilation, repeat dilation, serial dilation

Endoscopic dilation of a urethral stricture, typically requiring passage of multiple dilators over multiple sessions or using graduated dilation.

In Plain Language

widening narrow urethra multiple times

Clinical Context

Performed on separate occasion from initial dilation or with more graduated technique. Used for resistant strictures requiring multiple dilations.
