67030 Incise inner eye strands

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: vitreous membrane cutting, hyaloidotomy, membrane dissection, vitreous band lysis

Incision and cutting of vitreous bands or strands (posterior hyaloid membrane, vitreous membranes, or cyclitic membranes) to relieve traction on the retina.

Clinical Context

Performed to relieve vitreous traction in retinal detachment, proliferative diabetic retinopathy, posterior capsular opacification, or anterior vitreous opacification.
