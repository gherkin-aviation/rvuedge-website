31630 Bronchoscopy dilate/fracture repair

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: bronchial dilation, airway dilation, stenosis repair

Bronchoscopy with dilation and repair or fixation of airway stenosis or stricture to restore airway patency and improve ventilation.

In Plain Language

widening narrowed airways through scope

Clinical Context

Performed for airway stenosis from intubation, malignancy, external compression, or inflammatory conditions. May require repeated procedures or stent placement.
