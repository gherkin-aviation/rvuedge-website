51060 Removal of ureter stone

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ureterolithotomy, ureteral stone removal, open stone removal

Removal of ureteral stone via cystotomy and ureterotomy, surgical approach to access and remove ureteral calculus through bladder incision.

In Plain Language

surgical removal of kidney stone from ureter

Clinical Context

Indicated for ureteral stones not amenable to endoscopic or percutaneous management. Open surgical approach when minimal access contraindicated.
