21208 Augmentation of facial bones

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: facial bone augmentation, skeletal augmentation

Augmentation of facial bones to increase volume or projection, typically using implants or soft tissue approaches.

In Plain Language

Building up facial bone; Facial augmentation surgery

Clinical Context

Used for facial hypoplasia, post-traumatic deformity, or aesthetic enhancement of facial prominence.
