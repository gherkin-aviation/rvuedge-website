30545 repair choanal atresia trsnpltn

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: choanal atresia repair transpalatal, palatal approach choanal atresia repair, choanal atresia with stent

Repair of choanal atresia using transpalatal approach with creation of patent nasal airway, often requiring stent placement or follow-up procedures.

Clinical Context

Indicated for choanal atresia when intranasal approach is inadequate; transpalatal approach provides better visualization and space for wide opening.
