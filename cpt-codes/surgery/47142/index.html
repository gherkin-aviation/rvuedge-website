47142 Partial removal donor liver

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: extended hepatectomy, full-size donor graft procurement, major donor hepatectomy

Partial hepatectomy with extended hepatectomy or full-size graft procurement from donor.

Clinical Context

Performed for complex living or deceased donor liver transplantation requiring larger graft volume.
