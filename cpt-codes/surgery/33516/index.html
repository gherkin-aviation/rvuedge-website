33516 Cabg vein six or more

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: six or more vessel CABG, extensive CABG, multiple vein bypass

Surgical placement of six or more coronary artery bypass grafts using saphenous vein conduits, providing complete multivessel revascularization.

Clinical Context

Used for the most extensive coronary disease affecting six or more vessels/territories; achieves complete myocardial revascularization in patients with diffuse atherosclerotic disease.
