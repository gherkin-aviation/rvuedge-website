51703 Insert bladder catheter complex

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: complex catheterization, difficult catheterization, Foley catheter difficult, complicated catheterization

Insertion of bladder catheter with complex technique, challenging catheterization such as stricture dilation or suprapubic insertion.

In Plain Language

placement of catheter in difficult cases

Clinical Context

Used for urethral stricture, prior failed catheterization, or need for suprapubic approach. Higher RVU reflects complexity.
