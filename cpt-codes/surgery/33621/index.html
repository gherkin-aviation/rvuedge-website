33621 Transthor catheter for stent

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: transthoracic stent placement, surgical stent placement, catheter stent via surgery

Transthoracic catheter-based placement of a stent within the heart or pulmonary vasculature for palliation of stenosis.

Clinical Context

Utilized when percutaneous catheterization is not feasible; allows direct transthoracic access for stent deployment in complex congenital lesions or difficult anatomy.
