25075 excision forearm lesion sternoclavicular < 3 centimeters

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: excision small forearm lesion, removal small tumor

Excision of a subcutaneous lesion of the forearm measuring less than 3 centimeters, removing the mass with appropriate tissue margins.

Clinical Context

Used for benign lesions, small cysts, lipomas, or other superficial masses less than 3 centimeters. Less complex than larger lesion excisions.
