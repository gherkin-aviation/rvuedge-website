36581 Replace tunneled cv catheter

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: port replacement, portacath replacement, tunneled port replacement

Replacement of tunneled central venous catheter, with subcutaneous port; in jugular, innominate or subclavian vein.

Clinical Context

Used for replacement of non-functioning tunneled port catheters in upper torso venous systems.
