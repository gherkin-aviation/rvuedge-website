11055 Paring/cutting benign hyprker lesion 1

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: Callus removal, Corn removal, Hyperkeratosis treatment

Paring and cutting of benign hyperkeratotic lesion, single lesion. Non-invasive removal of callus or thickened skin on one lesion.

Clinical Context

Used for plantar calluses, corns, or other benign hyperkeratotic lesions causing discomfort.
