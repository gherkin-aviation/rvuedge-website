32601 Thoracoscopy diagnostic

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: diagnostic thoracoscopy, VATS diagnostic

Diagnostic thoracoscopy (video-assisted thoracoscopic surgery - VATS) performed to visualize intrathoracic structures and obtain diagnostic information without therapeutic intervention.

Clinical Context

Used for diagnosis of pleural effusion, suspected malignancy, or mediastinal disease when imaging is nondiagnostic and biopsy is needed.
