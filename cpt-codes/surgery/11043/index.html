11043 Debridement muscle and/or fsca first 20/<

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: Muscle and fascia debridement, Deep tissue cleanup, debridement to muscle, deep wound debridement, wound debridement muscle, deep debridement

Debridement of muscle and fascia (and subcutaneous tissue if necessary) for first twenty square centimeters or less. Removal of infected or necrotic muscle and fascial tissue.

In Plain Language

Muscle tissue removal; Fascial tissue cleanup

Clinical Context

Used for necrotizing fasciitis, deep soft tissue infections, or traumatic muscle injury with necrosis.
