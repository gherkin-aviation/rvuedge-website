27420 Revision of unstable kneecap

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: patellar realignment, patellar revision, medial reefing

Surgical revision of unstable patella (kneecap) through medial reefing, lateral release, patellar realignment, or soft tissue balancing to restore normal tracking and stability.

Clinical Context

Used for chronic patellar instability, lateral patellar tracking, recurrent patellar dislocation, and patellofemoral pain. Common in young patients.
