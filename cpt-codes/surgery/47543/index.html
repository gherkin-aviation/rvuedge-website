47543 Endoluminal biopsy biliary tree

Surgery

Also known as: Biliary biopsy, tissue diagnosis, cholangiobiopsy

Endoluminal biopsy of the biliary tree to obtain tissue samples from the bile ducts for diagnostic evaluation of strictures, masses, or suspicious lesions.

In Plain Language

Taking tissue samples from bile ducts

Clinical Context

Used to diagnose cholangiocarcinoma, primary sclerosing cholangitis, or differentiate benign from malignant strictures. Guides treatment decisions.
