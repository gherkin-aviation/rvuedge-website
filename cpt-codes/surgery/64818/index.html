64818 Sympathectomy lumbar

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: lumbar sympathectomy, lumbar sympathetic block surgical

Surgical sympathectomy of the lumbar sympathetic chain. Interrupts sympathetic innervation to the lower extremities to improve blood flow or reduce pain.

In Plain Language

lower back nerve surgery; leg nerve sympathetic surgery

Clinical Context

Indicated for lower extremity CRPS, severe Raynaud's phenomenon, intermittent claudication, or pain syndromes. May be performed open or endoscopically.
