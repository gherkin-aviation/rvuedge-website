35671 Bpg pop-tibl/-prnl artery

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: popliteal-tibial bypass, popopliteal-distal bypass, pop-tib

Arterial bypass graft from popliteal artery to tibial or peroneal artery for below-knee disease with critical limb ischemia. Distal limb salvage procedure.

In Plain Language

knee-area to calf artery bypass; extending bypass below the knee

Clinical Context

Performed for critical limb ischemia with isolated popliteal or distal disease. Usually requires saphenous vein graft.
