31570 Laryngoscope with vc injection

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: vocal cord injection, laryngeal injection, voice augmentation

Laryngoscopic injection of a therapeutic substance (filler material, augmentation agent, or medicament) into or adjacent to the vocal cords for voice restoration or laryngeal augmentation.

In Plain Language

injecting filler into voice box; voice augmentation injection

Clinical Context

Performed for vocal cord paralysis, presbylarynx (age-related voice changes), vocal cord atrophy, or dysphonia. Materials include calcium hydroxylapatite, hyaluronic acid, autologous fat, or collagen.
