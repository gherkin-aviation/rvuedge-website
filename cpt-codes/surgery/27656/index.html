27656 Repair leg fascia defect

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: fascia repair, leg fascia repair

Surgical repair of fascia defect in the lower leg with suture repair or fascia reconstruction.

Clinical Context

Indicated for compartment syndrome sequelae, traumatic fascia laceration, or fascial defects affecting tissue containment.
