41821 Excision of gum flap

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: gingival flap removal, gingival flap excision, periodontal flap surgery

Surgical excision of the gingival flap with removal of supporting tissue for periodontal disease treatment.

In Plain Language

removing gum flap; periodontal surgery; gum disease treatment surgery

Clinical Context

Used for periodontal disease requiring surgical removal of diseased gingival tissue and supporting structures. May include scaling and root planing.

RVU Information
