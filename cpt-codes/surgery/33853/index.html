33853 repair hypopl a-arch with byp

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Hypoplastic arch repair with CPB, aortic arch plasty with bypass, arch augmentation on pump

Repair of hypoplastic aortic arch with bypass; surgical reconstruction of an underdeveloped aortic arch to restore adequate luminal diameter, performed with cardiopulmonary bypass support.

Clinical Context

Used for complex hypoplastic aortic arch cases requiring cardiopulmonary bypass support to provide optimal hemodynamic and hypothermic conditions for reconstruction.
