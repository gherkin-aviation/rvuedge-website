46260 Remove in/ex hem groups 2+

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: extensive hemorrhoidectomy, multiple group hemorrhoidectomy, comprehensive hem removal

Surgical removal of internal and external hemorrhoid groups (2 or more groups). Extensive hemorrhoidectomy addressing multiple hemorrhoidal complexes.

Clinical Context

Indicated for extensive hemorrhoid disease with multiple symptomatic groups requiring surgical intervention. More complex than single-group procedures.
