24145 Prtl excision bone radial h/n

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: radial diaphysectomy, radial segmental excision

Partial excision (diaphysectomy) of the radial head or neck. Surgical removal of a segment of the proximal radius.

Clinical Context

Used for chronic osteomyelitis, segmental defects, or pathologic lesions of the radial head or neck requiring partial excision.
