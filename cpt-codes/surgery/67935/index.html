67935 Repair eyelid wound

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: complex eyelid laceration, eyelid wound with flap

Repair of complex eyelid laceration or wound involving multiple tissue layers or lid margin reconstruction.

In Plain Language

complex eyelid stitching; major eyelid laceration repair

Clinical Context

Used for complex eyelid wounds involving multiple layers or the eyelid margin. Common indications include penetrating eye injuries with eyelid involvement, complex crush injuries, or burn injuries affecting the eyelid.
