21139 Rdctj forehead cntrg & setback

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: forehead reduction with setback, complex forehead-jaw reconstruction

Reduction-contouring of the forehead combined with sagittal split and setback of the upper jaw and facial structures.

Clinical Context

Used for severe frontal bossing with maxillary protrusion. Requires coordinated orthognathic and contouring procedures.
