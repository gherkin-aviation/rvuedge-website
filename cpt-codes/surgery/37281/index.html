Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: tibioperoneal angioplasty simple additional, additional TPT angioplasty

Percutaneous or open revascularization of the tibioperoneal trunk or artery using simple angioplasty in each additional vessel.

Clinical Context

Each additional tibioperoneal vessel. Simple angioplasty extends revascularization in multilevel disease.
