42226 Lengthening of palate

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: palate lengthening, velopharyngeal surgery

Surgical lengthening of the soft palate to improve velopharyngeal closure and function, often for speech improvement or obstructive conditions.

Clinical Context

Performed for velopharyngeal insufficiency, hyponasal speech, or obstructive sleep apnea to extend soft palate length and improve pharyngeal mechanics.
