44140 Partial removal of colon

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: partial colectomy, segmental colectomy, right/left colectomy

Partial removal of colon with primary anastomosis (right colectomy, left colectomy, or segmental colectomy).

Clinical Context

Surgical removal of portion of colon for malignancy, inflammatory bowel disease, perforation, or other pathology with restoration of intestinal continuity.
