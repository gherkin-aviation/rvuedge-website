19105 Cryosurg ablate fa each

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: cryo fibroadenoma, cryoablation fibroadenoma

Cryosurgical ablation (destruction) of fibroadenoma or other benign breast lesion using cryogenic technology for each lesion.

In Plain Language

Freezing treatment for benign breast lump

Clinical Context

Minimally invasive treatment of fibroadenoma or benign breast lesion. Lesion destruction without open surgery. Each lesion coded separately.
