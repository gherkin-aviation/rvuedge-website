29821 Sho arthrs srg complicated synvct

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: complete synovectomy, full synovial removal, extensive synovectomy

Arthroscopy of the shoulder with complete synovectomy (removal of entire inflamed synovial lining) for extensive inflammatory disease.

Clinical Context

Used for advanced inflammatory arthropathies, extensive synovitis, and synovial osteochondromatosis.
