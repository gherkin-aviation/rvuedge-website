43245 Egd dilate stricture

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: EGD dilation, stricture dilation, bougie dilation

Esophagogastroduodenoscopy with dilation of esophageal or pyloric strictures. This therapeutic endoscopic procedure is performed to treat narrowing of the esophagus or pylorus.

In Plain Language

stretching tight area in food pipe; opening narrowed passage in stomach

Clinical Context

Performed for symptomatic strictures from caustic ingestion, radiation, surgery, or gastroesophageal reflux disease; provides symptomatic relief and improves swallowing.
