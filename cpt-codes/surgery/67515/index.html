67515 Inject/treat eye socket

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: sclerosing injection, orbital sclerosis, therapeutic drainage injection

Injection of therapeutic sclerosing or healing agent into orbital space to promote drainage, inflammation reduction, or tissue adhesion.

Clinical Context

Used for orbital cysts, lymphatic malformations, or inflammatory conditions; promotes internal drainage or tissue fibrosis.
