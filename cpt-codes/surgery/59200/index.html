59200 Insert cervical dilator

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: laminaria insertion, osmotic dilator placement, cervical ripening

Insertion of cervical dilator (such as laminaria or osmotic dilator) for gradual cervical dilation prior to operative procedures.

Clinical Context

Used before dilation and evacuation or labor induction to gradually dilate cervix, reducing risk of cervical trauma.
