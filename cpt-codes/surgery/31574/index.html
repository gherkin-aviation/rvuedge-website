31574 Largsc with njx augmentation

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: vocal cord augmentation injection, medializing injection, voice improvement injection

Laryngoscopic injection of augmentation material for vocal cord medializing or augmentation purposes, performed for therapeutic improvement of voice and airway function.

In Plain Language

injecting filler to improve voice

Clinical Context

Performed for vocal cord paralysis, atrophy, or dysphonia requiring augmentation to improve glottic closure and voice quality using materials like filler or autologous substances.
