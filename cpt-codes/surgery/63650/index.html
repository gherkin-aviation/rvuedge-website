63650 Implant neuroelectrodes

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: spinal electrode implant, neuroelectrode placement, SCS array implant

Implantation or replacement of spinal neuroelectrode array(s). This procedure involves placing electrodes for spinal cord stimulation or other neuromodulation purposes.

In Plain Language

placement of electrical stimulation leads in spinal cord area

Clinical Context

Used for spinal cord stimulation device implantation for chronic pain management or other neuromodulation indications.
