36222 Place catheter carotid/inom artery

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: carotid catheter, innominate catheterization, cerebral angiography

Percutaneous placement of catheter into carotid or innominate artery for selective angiography or intervention of cerebral circulation.

In Plain Language

putting a tube in the neck artery to the brain; carotid artery catheterization

Clinical Context

Used for carotid artery disease evaluation, cerebral angiography, or carotid intervention.
