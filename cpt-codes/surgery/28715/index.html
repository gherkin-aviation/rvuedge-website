28715 Arthrodesis triple

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: triple fusion, triple arthrodesis, subtalar-talonavicular fusion

Surgical arthrodesis (fusion) of the triple joint complex (talonavicular, subtalar, and calcaneocuboid joints), permanently fusing these three joints to correct hindfoot varus/valgus deformity.

Clinical Context

Used for severe hindfoot varus or valgus deformity, neuromuscular foot deformity, or post-traumatic arthritis affecting multiple hindfoot joints.
