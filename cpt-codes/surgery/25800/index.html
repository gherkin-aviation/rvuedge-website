25800 Arthrd wrist complete wo grf

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: wrist fusion without graft, wrist arthrodesis

Arthrodesis (surgical fusion) of the wrist joint performed completely without bone graft material.

Clinical Context

Used for advanced wrist arthritis, post-traumatic arthritis, or chronic instability where joint fusion is desired. Performed when adequate bone contact surfaces exist without requiring graft material.
