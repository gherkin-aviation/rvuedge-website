33986 Ecmo/ecls removal ctr cannula

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: ECMO central second removal, additional central cannula removal, second central removal

Extracorporeal membrane oxygenation/extracorporeal life support removal of central venous/arterial cannula (second removal); removal of additional centrally placed ECMO cannulae.

Clinical Context

Removal of additional central ECMO cannulae when multiple central cannulae were placed and require removal.
