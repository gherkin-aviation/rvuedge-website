33514 Cabg vein five

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: five vessel CABG, quintuple bypass, five vein CABG

Surgical placement of five coronary artery bypass grafts using saphenous vein conduits, achieving maximum standard revascularization.

In Plain Language

five vein bypasses for heart artery blockages

Clinical Context

Performed for extensive coronary disease involving five distinct territories; provides complete revascularization of territory-specific stenoses in patients with severe left ventricular impairment.
