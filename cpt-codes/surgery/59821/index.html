59821 Treatment of miscarriage

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: D&E for miscarriage, curettage for spontaneous abortion

Surgical treatment of spontaneous abortion including dilation, evacuation, and curettage for management of incomplete abortion with retained products.

In Plain Language

surgery to complete miscarriage

Clinical Context

Operative approach for incomplete spontaneous abortion with hemorrhage or infection risk; provides definitive removal of retained tissue.
