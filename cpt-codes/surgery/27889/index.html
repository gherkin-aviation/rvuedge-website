27889 Ankle disarticulation

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ankle disarticulation, foot disarticulation, Chopart amputation variant

Ankle disarticulation with complete removal of the foot. This procedure removes the entire foot by disarticulating at the ankle joint with appropriate soft tissue handling and bone shaping.

Clinical Context

Used for severe foot pathology including extensive trauma, necrotizing soft tissue infections, severe peripheral vascular disease, or malignancy when foot salvage is impossible.
