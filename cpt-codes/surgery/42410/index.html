42410 Excise parotid gland/lesion

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: parotidectomy, parotid gland removal

Surgical excision of the parotid gland and removal of associated lesions or tumors. May be partial or complete depending on pathology extent.

Clinical Context

Surgical treatment of parotid tumors (benign or malignant), chronic infections, or obstruction requiring gland removal and facial nerve preservation.
