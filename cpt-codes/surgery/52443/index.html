52443 Cysto first trurl prst8 comis

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: cysto with prostatic commissure incision

Cystoscopy with first endoscopic incision and treatment of urethral prostatic commissure stricture, relieving urinary obstruction at prostatic outlet.

Clinical Context

Used for post-prostatectomy or congenital commissure strictures. Can be combined with other endoscopic procedures.
