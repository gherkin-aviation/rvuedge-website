33788 Revision of pulmonary artery

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: PA revision, pulmonary artery revision, PA conduit revision

Revision of pulmonary artery; surgical correction of previously placed pulmonary artery conduits, patches, or reconstructions to address stenosis, insufficiency, or growth-related changes.

Clinical Context

Performed in patients with previous pulmonary artery repair or conduit placement who develop stenosis or other complications. Often required in growing children whose conduits become inadequate as the patient enlarges.
