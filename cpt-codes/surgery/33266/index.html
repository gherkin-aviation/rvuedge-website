33266 Ablate atria x10sv endoscopic

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: endoscopic extensive atrial ablation, minimally invasive 10+ site ablation

Ablation of extensive atrial region (10 or more lesions/sites) via endoscopic approach, minimally invasive multi-site treatment of complex arrhythmias.

Clinical Context

Advanced endoscopic technique for extensive atrial ablation with benefits of reduced trauma compared to open MAZE procedure.
