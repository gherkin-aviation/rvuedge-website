27664 Repair of leg tendon each

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: peroneal tendon repair, fibularis tendon repair

Surgical repair of peroneal tendon injury in lateral compartment of lower leg for each tendon repaired.

Clinical Context

Used for peroneus longus or brevis laceration or rupture. Restores eversion function and lateral ankle support.
