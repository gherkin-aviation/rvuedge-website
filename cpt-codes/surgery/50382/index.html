50382 Change ureter stent percut

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: percutaneous stent exchange, PCS exchange, stent change

Percutaneous change (exchange) of an indwelling ureteral stent under fluoroscopic guidance through the percutaneous nephrostomy tract.

Clinical Context

Used for routine stent maintenance, stent migration, or occlusion. Performed as outpatient procedure under fluoroscopy.
