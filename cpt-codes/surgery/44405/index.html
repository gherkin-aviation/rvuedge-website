44405 Colonoscopy with dilation

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: colonoscopy stoma dilation, transcolostomy stricture dilation

Colonoscopy performed through colostomy stoma with dilation of stricture using balloon or mechanical dilator for relief of obstruction.

Clinical Context

Used for treatment of strictures in colon/rectum of patients with colostomy. Balloon or mechanical dilation relieves obstruction and restores lumen patency.
