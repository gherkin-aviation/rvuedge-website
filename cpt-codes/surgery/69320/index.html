69320 Rebuild outer ear canal

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: complex canal reconstruction, canal revision with graft

Complex reconstruction of external auditory canal with multiple techniques including bone or cartilage grafting.

In Plain Language

major ear canal rebuild; complex ear canal reconstruction

Clinical Context

Used for complex external auditory canal defects. Common indications include severe canal stenosis, canal atresia repair, or complex reconstruction.
