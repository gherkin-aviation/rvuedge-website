30160 Rhinectomy total

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: total rhinectomy, complete nose removal, total nasal resection

Total rhinectomy, including complete resection of nasal structures (bone and soft tissue), typically for advanced infection, trauma, or malignancy.

In Plain Language

complete nose removal

Clinical Context

Reserved for severe or extensive disease (advanced malignancy, severe trauma, necrotizing fasciitis) requiring complete nasal resection; significant functional and cosmetic impact.
