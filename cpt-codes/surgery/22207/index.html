22207 Incis spine 3 column lumbar

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: lumbar spine arthroscopic incision drainage, minimally invasive lumbar I and D

Arthroscopic incision and drainage of lumbar vertebral column. Surgical drainage of infection or fluid collection of lumbar vertebral region using arthroscopic minimally invasive technique.

In Plain Language

minimally invasive lumbar spine drainage surgery; arthroscopic low back spine drainage

Clinical Context

Used for lumbar spine infections or fluid collections amenable to arthroscopic approach. Minimally invasive technique reduces soft tissue trauma while providing adequate visualization for drainage.
