63191 Incise spine accessory nerve

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: spinal accessory nerve section, CN XI rhizotomy

Incision of the spinal accessory nerve. This procedure involves surgical division of the spinal accessory nerve (cranial nerve XI) for functional restoration or pain management.

In Plain Language

neck nerve cutting for pain or function

Clinical Context

Less commonly used, may be applied in specific pain syndromes or functional disorders affecting the spinal accessory nerve distribution.
