35870 Repair vessel graft defect

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: graft defect repair, graft thrombectomy, anastomotic repair

Repair of vascular graft defect, including thrombectomy, patch angioplasty, or graft segment replacement. Restores graft integrity and patency.

Clinical Context

Performed for graft stenosis, thrombosis, rupture, or pseudoaneurysm at anastomosis.
