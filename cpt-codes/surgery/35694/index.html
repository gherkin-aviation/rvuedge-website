35694 Artery trnsposj subclav carotid

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: subclavian-to-carotid transposition, SC-C transposition, carotid-assisted subclavian

Arterial transposition of subclavian artery to carotid artery for subclavian artery origin disease with combined vertebral insufficiency. Restores both subclavian and vertebral flow.

Clinical Context

Performed when subclavian stenosis combined with carotid disease. Allows single intervention for both.
