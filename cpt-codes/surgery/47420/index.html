47420 Incision of bile duct

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Choledocholithotomy, CBD exploration, common bile duct exploration

Surgical incision into the common bile duct, typically to remove stones (choledocholithotomy) or address obstruction and biliary pathology.

Clinical Context

Performed for choledocholithiasis (bile duct stones), strictures, or obstruction. Open approach when endoscopic retrograde cholangiopancreatography (ERCP) is unsuccessful or contraindicated.
