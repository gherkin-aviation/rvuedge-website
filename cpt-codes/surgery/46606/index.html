46606 Anoscopy and biopsy

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: anoscopy with biopsy, anoscopic biopsy, anal biopsy

Anoscopy with biopsy of anal canal lesions. The anoscope is used to visualize and obtain tissue samples for pathologic examination.

Clinical Context

Performed to diagnose anal neoplasia, inflammatory disease, or infections. Biopsy results guide treatment decisions.
