43333 Transab esophageal hiat hern repair

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: transabdominal hernia repair with esophageal lengthening, complex hiatal hernia repair, Collis gastroplasty

Transabdominal hiatal hernia repair with esophageal lengthening or other esophageal reconstruction when required. This complex repair addresses large hernias.

In Plain Language

repairing large hernia and lengthening esophagus; complex hiatal repair

Clinical Context

Performed for large type III hernias where standard repair would cause excessive tension; esophageal lengthening allows safe closure.
