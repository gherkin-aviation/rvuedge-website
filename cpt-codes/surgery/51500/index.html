51500 Removal of bladder cyst

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: bladder cyst removal, cyst excision, bladder cystectomy

Removal of bladder cyst, surgical excision of cystic lesion of bladder wall or mucosa.

Clinical Context

Indicated for urachal cysts, mesodermal cysts, or other benign cystic lesions causing irritative symptoms or suspected malignancy.
