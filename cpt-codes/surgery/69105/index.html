69105 Biopsy of external ear canal

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: ear canal biopsy, external canal biopsy

Biopsy of external auditory canal using direct visualization and punch or excision technique.

Clinical Context

Used for diagnosis of external auditory canal lesions. Common indications include suspected malignancy, granulation tissue, or inflammatory lesions.
