65800 Drainage of eye

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: anterior chamber paracentesis, aqueous tap

Paracentesis of anterior chamber of eye; with removal of aqueous.

In Plain Language

needle drainage of fluid from front of eye; removing fluid from anterior chamber

Clinical Context

Diagnostic or therapeutic paracentesis to lower intraocular pressure or obtain fluid for analysis. Rarely used diagnostically in modern era.
