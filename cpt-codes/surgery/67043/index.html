67043 Vit for membrane dissect

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: membrane peel, proliferative tissue removal, retinal membrane dissection, posterior membrane surgery

Vitrectomy with dissection and removal of posterior retinal membranes or proliferative tissue from vitreoretinal interface.

Clinical Context

Used in proliferative diabetic retinopathy, retinal detachment repair, posterior capsular fibrosis, and removal of proliferative epiretinal membranes.
