32506 Wedge resection of lung add-on

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: additional wedge resection, wedge add-on

Add-on code for additional wedge resection(s) of the lung when performed in conjunction with another primary lung resection procedure.

Clinical Context

Used when multiple wedge resections are performed during the same operation, such as resection of satellite nodules during lobectomy.
