21243 Reconstruction of jaw joint

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: TMJ reconstruction with graft, costochondral TMJ reconstruction

Reconstruction of the temporomandibular joint with costochondral graft, using rib cartilage to restore joint structure and function.

In Plain Language

Jaw joint reconstruction with rib cartilage

Clinical Context

Used for TMJ ankylosis, severe condylar loss, or significant joint destruction. Provides growth potential in children.
