36680 Insert needle bone cavity

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: bone marrow aspiration, bone marrow biopsy, BMA, BMB

Insertion of needle into bone for bone marrow biopsy, aspiration, or sampling; each site.

Clinical Context

Used for evaluation of hematologic disorders, malignancies, infections, and other systemic conditions. Common sites include iliac crest and sternum.
