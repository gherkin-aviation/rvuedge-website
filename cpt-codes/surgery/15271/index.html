15271 Skin sub graft trnk/arm/leg

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: skin substitute graft, bioengineered skin

Skin substitute graft applied to trunk, arms, or legs, first application. Bioengineered or acellular skin substitute for wound coverage.

Clinical Context

Used for burns, traumatic wounds, and surgical defects when autologous graft is unavailable or insufficient. Reduces infection and promotes healing.
