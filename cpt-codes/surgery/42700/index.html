42700 incision and drainage abscess peritonsillar

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: abscess drainage, peritonsillar abscess drainage

Surgical incision and drainage of a peritonsillar abscess, a localized collection of pus adjacent to or within the tonsillar region.

In Plain Language

drainage of throat abscess; drainage of infection near tonsil

Clinical Context

Emergency or urgent drainage of peritonsillar abscess causing difficulty swallowing, breathing obstruction, or severe pharyngitis.
