21147 Rcnstj mdfc lefort i 3/>grf

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Le Fort I segmental with graft, complex Le Fort I with augmentation

Reconstruction of the midface using Le Fort I osteotomy as three or more pieces with the addition of bone graft material.

In Plain Language

Multi-piece upper face surgery with bone graft; Advanced midface reconstruction with augmentation

Clinical Context

Used for severe midface deformities or defects requiring both segmental repositioning and bony augmentation.
