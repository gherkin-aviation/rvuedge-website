63005 Remove spine lamina 1/2 lumbar

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: lumbar laminotomy, lumbar decompression

Laminotomy with removal of lamina and exploration in lumbar spine for one or two levels. Includes inspection of neural elements.

Clinical Context

Used for lumbar spinal decompression by removal of lamina in cases of stenosis or nerve compression.
