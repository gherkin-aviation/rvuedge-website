60500 Explore parathyroid glands

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: parathyroid exploration, parathyroid gland surgery, parathyroidectomy

Surgical exploration of the parathyroid glands, typically unilateral exploration with identification and palpation of glands to assess for hyperparathyroidism or locate abnormal glands.

Clinical Context

Performed for primary hyperparathyroidism to locate and remove adenomas or hyperplastic tissue. Can be unilateral (single-gland exploration) or bilateral (four-gland assessment) depending on imaging and intraoperative findings.
