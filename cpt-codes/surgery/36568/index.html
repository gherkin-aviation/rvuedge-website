36568 Insj picc <5 yr without imaging

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: PICC line, peripherally inserted central catheter, PICC

Insertion of peripherally inserted central catheter (PICC), younger than 5 years of age, without imaging guidance.

In Plain Language

inserting a long thin tube through an arm vein to the chest; arm central line for young children

Clinical Context

Used for intermediate to long-term access in pediatric patients. Can be placed at bedside without imaging.
