44381 Small bowel endoscopy br/wa

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: small bowel endoscopy washout with biopsy, enteroscopy irrigation biopsy

Endoscopy of small bowel performed via enterotomy for blood removal, washout, and biopsy to clear field while obtaining tissue samples for diagnosis.

Clinical Context

Used when both field clearing (removal of blood/debris) and tissue confirmation are needed during enteroscopy, common in setting of intraoperative hemorrhage with unclear source.
