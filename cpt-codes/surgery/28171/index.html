28171 Resection tarsal tumor

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: tarsal tumor resection malignant, tarsal bone cancer removal, radical tarsal resection

Radical resection of a malignant tarsal bone tumor. This comprehensive procedure removes cancer of the tarsal bones with wide margins.

Clinical Context

Performed for sarcoma, other malignancies of tarsal bones. Often requires amputation or major reconstruction due to wide margins required.
