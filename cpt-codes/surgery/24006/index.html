24006 Arthrotomy elbow capsl excision rls

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: elbow arthrotomy with capsular release, elbow capsulectomy, elbow capsular release

Arthrotomy of elbow with capsular excision and release. Arthrotomy combined with capsular release procedures for stiffness or contracture.

Clinical Context

Used for elbow stiffness, post-traumatic contracture, or inflammatory conditions requiring capsular release combined with arthrotomy.
