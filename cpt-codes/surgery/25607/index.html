25607 Optx dst rd xartc fracture/epi sep

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ORIF distal radius, open reduction internal fixation wrist, distal radius ORIF, wrist fracture ORIF

Open treatment (surgical repair) of distal radial fracture with internal fixation, including intra-articular fractures or physeal separations.

In Plain Language

surgical repair of wrist bone fracture; metal plate stabilization of wrist

Clinical Context

Used for intra-articular distal radial fractures or unstable physeal separations requiring surgical intervention and internal fixation with plates or screws.
