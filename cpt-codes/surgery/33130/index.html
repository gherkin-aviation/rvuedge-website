33130 Rescj external cardiac tumor

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: epicardial tumor removal, external cardiac tumor resection, surface cardiac lesion excision

Resection of external cardiac tumor. Surgical removal of tumor on the epicardial surface or cardiac wall.

In Plain Language

surgery to remove tumor from outside the heart

Clinical Context

Used for epicardial malignancies or benign tumors accessible from the pericardial surface. Provides complete resection with acceptable morbidity.
