36224 Place catheter carotd artery

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: internal carotid catheter, common carotid catheterization, carotid angiography

Percutaneous placement of catheter into carotid artery for selective catheterization and angiography of carotid distribution.

Clinical Context

Used for carotid atherosclerosis evaluation, carotid intervention, or cerebrovascular disease assessment.
