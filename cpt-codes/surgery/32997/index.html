32997 Total lung lavage

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: lung lavage, pulmonary lavage, whole lung irrigation

Total lung lavage (or pulmonary lavage), therapeutic washing out of the entire lung with saline or other solutions. Removes alveolar debris and inflammatory material.

Clinical Context

Used for pulmonary alveolar proteinosis, severe occupational lung disease, or acute respiratory failure from pulmonary edema. Provides temporary symptom relief and improved oxygenation.
