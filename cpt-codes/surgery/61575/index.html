61575 Skull base/brainstem surgery

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: skull base craniotomy, multifossa approach

Craniotomy using skull base approach (combined anterior, middle, or posterior fossa) for tumor or vascular lesion excision.

Clinical Context

Performed for complex skull base lesions requiring access to multiple compartments. Includes large meningiomas, chordomas, clival tumors, and vascular malformations.
