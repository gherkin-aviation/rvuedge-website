68505 Partial removal tear gland

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: partial dacryoadenectomy, partial lacrimal gland removal

Partial removal of lacrimal gland while preserving remaining gland function (partial dacryoadenectomy).

In Plain Language

removing part of tear gland; partial tear gland removal

Clinical Context

Used for removal of lacrimal gland lesions while preserving gland function. Common indications include benign lacrimal gland tumors, cysts, or localized pathology.
