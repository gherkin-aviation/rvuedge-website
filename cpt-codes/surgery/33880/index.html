33880 Evasc repair ta ndgft cov lsa

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: TEVAR with LSA coverage, endovascular aortic repair, stent graft with subclavian coverage, thoracic endovascular aortic repair

Endovascular repair of thoracic aorta with nondegenerative graft covering left subclavian artery; percutaneous or minimally invasive placement of an endovascular stent graft with coverage of the left subclavian artery origin.

In Plain Language

Minimally invasive aorta repair with covered graft; Stent graft placement in thoracic aorta

Clinical Context

Less invasive alternative to open surgery for descending thoracic aortic pathology. Left subclavian coverage may require revascularization via carotid-subclavian bypass to prevent arm ischemia.
