59025 Fetal non-stress test

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: non-stress test, NST, baseline fetal heart rate

Fetal non-stress test (Nonstress Test) involves monitoring fetal heart rate and its acceleration in response to fetal movement without external stress.

Clinical Context

Standard outpatient surveillance test in high-risk pregnancies; reactive pattern (accelerations with movement) indicates fetal well-being.
