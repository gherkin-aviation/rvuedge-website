29883 Arthrs knee srg mnisc repair m & l

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: bilateral meniscal repair, meniscus repair both, complex meniscal repair

Arthroscopy of the knee with repair of both medial and lateral menisci (bilateral meniscal repair).

Clinical Context

Indicated for bilateral meniscal tears in repairable zones where tissue preservation is possible.
