35281 repair blvsl gr ot/th vn ntr-ab

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: abdominal prosthetic graft, visceral synthetic reconstruction

Repair of blood vessel injury in the intra-abdominal region using prosthetic or arterial graft reconstruction.

Clinical Context

Abdominal vascular reconstruction with prosthetic graft when venous conduit unavailable or inappropriate. Infection risk significant in contaminated abdominal wounds; consider delayed reconstruction.
