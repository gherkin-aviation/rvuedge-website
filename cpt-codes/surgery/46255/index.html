46255 Remove int/external hem 1 group

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: combined hem removal, internal-external hemorrhoidectomy, mixed hemorrhoid excision, hemorrhoidectomy

Surgical removal of internal and external hemorrhoid groups in a single group. This combined approach addresses both internal and external hemorrhoid disease in one location.

In Plain Language

removal of both internal and external hemorrhoids in one area; treatment of mixed hemorrhoid disease

Clinical Context

Used when both internal and external hemorrhoids are present in the same anatomic group. Provides comprehensive treatment with single anesthesia episode.
