47146 Prep donor liver/venous

Surgery

Also known as: venous reconstruction, hepatic vein preservation, donor liver venous prep

Preparation of donor liver with venous reconstruction or preservation of hepatic venous outflow.

Clinical Context

Performed for complex splits or unusual vascular anatomy requiring venous reconstruction to maintain graft perfusion.
