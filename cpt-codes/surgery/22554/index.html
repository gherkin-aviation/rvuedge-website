22554 Arthrd anterior ntrbd minutes dsc crv

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Anterior cervical fusion with bone graft, ACDF with bone graft

Arthrodesis, anterior/anterolateral approach, including discectomy and/or corpectomy to decompress spinal cord and/or nerve roots, cervical; with mineralized bone void filler/bone matrix, single interspaces.

In Plain Language

Neck fusion with bone graft material; Spine fusion with bone spacer

Clinical Context

Similar to ACDF but specifically reports use of mineralized bone void filler or bone matrix; improves fusion rates and structural support.
