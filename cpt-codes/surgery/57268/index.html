57268 Repair of bowel bulge

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Enterocele repair, Bowel bulge repair

Repair of enterocele (bowel bulge/hernia). Surgical correction of small bowel herniation into pelvic floor.

Clinical Context

Used for symptomatic enterocele. Often combined with other prolapse repairs.
