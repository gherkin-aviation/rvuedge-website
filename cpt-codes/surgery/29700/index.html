29700 removal/bivlv gauntlet boot/cyst

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: gauntlet removal, boot removal, cast removal

Removal and splitting of a gauntlet-style boot or cast (circumferential covering of hand and foot region), typically to relieve pressure or inspect underlying structures.

Clinical Context

Performed for gauntlet cast removal, pressure relief, wound inspection, or cast adjustment.
