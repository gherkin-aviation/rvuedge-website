27236 Treat thigh fracture

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: femur ORIF, intramedullary nailing, femur plate fixation, hip ORIF, femoral neck ORIF

Open treatment of femoral shaft fracture with internal fixation using intramedullary nailing, plates, or screws for fracture stabilization.

In Plain Language

Thighbone metal repair; Internal rod fixation

Clinical Context

Standard treatment for displaced femoral shaft fractures requiring operative fixation to allow early mobilization and prevent complications.
