30600 Repair mouth/nose fistula

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: oral fistula repair, mouth-nose fistula repair, oromandibular fistula repair

Repair of oromandibular or oronasal fistula, including closure of abnormal communication between oral cavity and nasal or mandibular structures.

Clinical Context

Indicated for oral-nasal communication following trauma, infection, or prior surgery; prevents infection and maintains normal oral and nasal function.
