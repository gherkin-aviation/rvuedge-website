32855 Prepare donor lung single

Surgery

Also known as: single lung preservation, donor lung preparation, lung preservation

Donor lung preparation and preservation for single lung transplantation. Includes procurement, inspection, and preservation procedures to optimize graft quality.

Clinical Context

Performed on the donor lung to assess viability and prepare for transplantation. Involves flushing with preservation solution and management to prevent ischemic injury.
