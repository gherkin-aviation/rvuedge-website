27477 Surgery to stop leg growth

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: growth modulation, growth plate harnessing

Surgical guidance or modulation of skeletal growth through growth plate harnessing or temporary growth modulation techniques to correct limb length discrepancy.

Clinical Context

Used in growing children to gradually correct limb length discrepancy. Temporary intervention allowing continued growth with correction.
