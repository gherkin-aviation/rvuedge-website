35695 Artery trnsposj carotid subclav

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: carotid-to-subclavian transposition, C-SC transposition, carotid relocation

Arterial transposition of carotid artery to subclavian artery for carotid artery origin disease or innominate artery disease affecting carotid. Restores carotid perfusion.

Clinical Context

Used for innominate or carotid origin stenosis. Less common than other transpositions.
