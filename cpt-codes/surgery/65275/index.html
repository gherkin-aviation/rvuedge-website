65275 Repair of eye wound

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: corneoscleral wound repair, limbal wound repair

Repair of full thickness corneal wound involving conjunctiva; primary closure.

Clinical Context

Larger penetrating wounds extending beyond the cornea into the sclera and involving conjunctiva. Requires precise anatomic alignment and multi-layer closure.
