32482 Bilobectomy

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: bilobectomy, two-lobe resection

Surgical removal of two lobes of the lung (bilobectomy) performed for advanced lung cancer, infection, or disease involving multiple lobes requiring more extensive resection.

In Plain Language

removal of two lung sections

Clinical Context

Used for lung cancer involving two adjacent lobes, extensive bronchiectasis, or infection spanning multiple lobes when lobectomy is insufficient.
