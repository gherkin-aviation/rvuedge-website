67500 Inject/treat eye socket

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: retrobulbar injection, peribulbar injection, orbital injection

Injection of therapeutic agent into orbital space (retrobulbar, peribulbar, or sub-Tenon injection) for treatment of orbital inflammation, infection, or pain management.

Clinical Context

Administered for orbital inflammation, pain relief, antibiotic delivery, or corticosteroid treatment in orbital cellulitis.
