33264 removal & replacement defibrillator gen mlt ld

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ICD generator replacement multiple leads, defibrillator box change tri-lead

Removal and replacement of implantable cardioverter-defibrillator generator with multiple leads, managing complex multi-lead systems.

Clinical Context

Used for triple-lead or more complex ICD systems; common in cardiac resynchronization therapy ICDs (CRT-D).
