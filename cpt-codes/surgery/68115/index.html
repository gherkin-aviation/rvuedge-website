All Codes / Surgery / 68115

68115 excision lesion conjunctiva >1 centimeters

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: conjunctival lesion excision, large conjunctival lesion removal

Excision of conjunctival lesion greater than or equal to 1 centimeter using direct closure or reconstruction.

In Plain Language

removing larger growth from eye membrane; conjunctival lesion removal larger

Clinical Context

Used for removal of larger conjunctival lesions 1 centimeter or greater. Common indications include large nevi, pterygium, conjunctival papillomas, or larger suspicious lesions.
