46601 Diagnostic anoscopy

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: diagnostic anoscopy, anoscopic exam, anal examination

Diagnostic anoscopy without additional procedures. Simple visualization of the anal canal using an anoscope to identify pathology.

Clinical Context

Standard diagnostic procedure to visualize anal canal and assess for hemorrhoids, fissures, lesions, and other pathology.
