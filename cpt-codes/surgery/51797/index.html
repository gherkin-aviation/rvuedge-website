51797 Intraabdominal pressure test

Surgery

Also known as: abdominal pressure test, Valsalva study, leak point pressure

Intra-abdominal pressure measurement test, assessment of abdominal pressure during Valsalva or other maneuvers for stress incontinence evaluation.

Clinical Context

Urodynamic test for stress urinary incontinence assessment measuring abdominal leak point pressure.
