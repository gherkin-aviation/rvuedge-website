33141 Heart tmr with other procedure

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: TMR with other procedure, add-on TMR, concurrent TMR

Transmyocardial revascularization (TMR) performed in conjunction with other cardiac procedures. Add-on code for concurrent TMR and additional operations.

In Plain Language

laser blood flow treatment during other heart surgery

Clinical Context

Used when TMR is performed during bypass surgery or other cardiac procedures for comprehensive myocardial revascularization strategy.
