46700 Repair of anal stricture

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: stricture repair, anal strictureplasty, stricture reconstruction

Repair of anal stricture with primary reconstruction. Surgically reconstructs the anal canal in cases where stricture develops from scarring or trauma.

Clinical Context

Performed for anal strictures from trauma, surgery, inflammatory bowel disease, or caustic injury. May include anoplasty with skin flap.
