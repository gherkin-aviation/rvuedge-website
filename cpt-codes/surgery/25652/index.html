25652 Optx ulnar styloid fracture

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ORIF ulnar styloid, open reduction internal fixation styloid

Open treatment (surgical repair) of ulnar styloid bone fracture with internal fixation.

Clinical Context

Used for displaced ulnar styloid fractures requiring operative intervention, particularly when there is significant displacement or associated distal radioulnar joint instability.
