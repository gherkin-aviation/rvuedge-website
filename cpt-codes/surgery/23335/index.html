23335 Shoulder prosthesis removal

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: complex prosthesis removal, implant removal with reconstruction, complicated prosthesis takedown

Removal of implant (prosthesis) from shoulder with increased complexity; includes extensive soft tissue exposure and management of bone loss. Extraction of shoulder prosthesis with reconstructive considerations.

Clinical Context

Used for severe periprosthetic bone loss, massive cuff deficiency, or significant soft tissue compromise requiring extended exposure and reconstruction planning during implant removal.
