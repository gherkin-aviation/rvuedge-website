21931 excision back lesion sternoclavicular 3 centimeters/>

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: back lesion excision simple, back mass removal moderate size

Excision of lesion of back with closure, simple, greater than 3 centimeters. Surgical removal of superficial back lesion with primary closure, greater than 3 centimeters in dimension.

Clinical Context

Used for benign superficial back lesions including cysts, lipomas, or small masses exceeding 3 centimeters. Simple closure indicates straightforward single-layer approximation without flaps or grafts.
