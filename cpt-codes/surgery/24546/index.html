24546 Optx humerus fracture with ntrcndylr xtn

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ORIF complex humeral fracture, open reduction intraarticular

Open treatment of humeral fracture with intercondylar extension. Surgical management of complex intraarticular humeral fracture.

Clinical Context

Used for complex humeral fractures extending into the elbow joint (intercondylar fractures), requiring open reduction and fixation.
