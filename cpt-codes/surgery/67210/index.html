67210 Treatment of retinal lesion

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: pneumatic tamponade, gas bubble, gas injection for lesion, retinal laser photocoagulation

Treatment of intraocular lesion using inert gas injection for pneumatic tamponade or therapeutic management of retinal lesions or breaks.

In Plain Language

gas bubble treatment of retinal lesion; injection to support retinal repair

Clinical Context

Used for retinal breaks or shallow detachments manageable without vitrectomy; gas bubble provides internal tamponade promoting reattachment.
