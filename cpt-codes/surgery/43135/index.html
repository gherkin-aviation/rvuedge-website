43135 Removal of esophagus pouch

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: complex diverticulectomy, diverticulectomy with myotomy

Esophageal diverticulotomy with complex reconstruction or diverticulopexy with myotomy to address associated pharyngeal dysfunction.

In Plain Language

removal of esophageal pouch with additional muscle surgery

Clinical Context

Performed for complicated esophageal diverticula requiring associated cricopharyngeal myotomy or esophageal myotomy. Addresses both diverticulum and associated dysfunction.
