26455 Incision of finger tendon

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Finger fasciotomy, Finger incision, Limited finger release

Surgical incision of the fascia and tendons of a finger to address contracture, improve motion, or allow tissue manipulation in a single digit.

Clinical Context

Indicated for contracture of a single finger or when limited incision is sufficient for treating finger contracture or allowing manipulation.
