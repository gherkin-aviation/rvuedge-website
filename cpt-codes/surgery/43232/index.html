43232 Esophagoscopy with ultrasound needle biopsy

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: EUS fine needle aspiration, EUS-guided FNA

Esophagogastroduodenoscopy (EGD) with endoscopic ultrasound and fine needle aspiration/biopsy of esophageal or peripesophageal lesion.

In Plain Language

camera examination with ultrasound and needle sampling of tissue

Clinical Context

Diagnostic procedure for tissue sampling of esophageal and peripesophageal lesions. Allows histologic evaluation of lymph nodes and lesions.
