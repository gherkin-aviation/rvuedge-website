21336 Open treatment septal fracture with wo stabj

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: septal fracture repair, nasal septum reduction

Open treatment of septal fracture with or without stabilization, addressing fracture of the nasal septum specifically.

Clinical Context

Used for isolated septal fractures causing airway obstruction or deformity, with or without stabilization devices.
