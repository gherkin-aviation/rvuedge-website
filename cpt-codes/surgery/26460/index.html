26460 Incise hand/finger tendon

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Hand/finger fasciotomy, Limited fascial incision, Contracture incision

Surgical incision of the fascia and tendons of the hand or finger to address contracture or improve motion, typically a limited procedure for specific contracture management.

Clinical Context

Performed for contracture affecting hand or finger structures when limited incision approach is appropriate for addressing the contracture.
