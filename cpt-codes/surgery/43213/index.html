43213 Esophagoscopy retro balloon

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: retrograde dilation, retrograde balloon dilation

Esophagogastroduodenoscopy (EGD) with retrograde insertion of guidewire or balloon dilation through stricture.

Clinical Context

Therapeutic procedure for esophageal stricture with retrograde dilation technique. Used when antegrade approach unsuccessful.
