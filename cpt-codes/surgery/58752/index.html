58752 Revise ovarian tube(s)

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: tubal revision, hydrotubation, fallopian tube repair

Revision or reconstruction of the fallopian tube(s), including hydrotubation and/or diagnostic laparoscopy to improve tubal patency and function.

Clinical Context

Performed in infertility cases where tubal scarring or strictures compromise function, often combined with hysterosalpingography to confirm patency.
