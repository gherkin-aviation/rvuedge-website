21347 Opn treatment nasomax fracture multple

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: complex nasomaxillary fracture, comminuted nasomaxillary

Open treatment of multiple nasomaxillary fractures involving complex comminution or multiple fracture lines requiring comprehensive reduction.

Clinical Context

Used for comminuted nasomaxillary fractures with multiple fracture sites requiring complex reduction techniques.
