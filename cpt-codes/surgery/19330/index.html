All Codes / Surgery / 19330

19330 removal ruptured breast implant

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: implant removal for rupture, ruptured implant removal

Surgical removal of a ruptured or leaking breast implant along with surrounding fibrous capsule material. Removal of capsule is necessary to ensure complete removal of silicone or saline.

In Plain Language

removal of broken breast implant

Clinical Context

Used for management of implant rupture or significant leakage. Capsulectomy may be partial or complete depending on extent of contamination and patient preference.
