44407 Colonoscopy with needle aspiration/biopsy

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: colonoscopy stoma ultrasound biopsy, transcolostomy EUS biopsy

Colonoscopy performed through colostomy stoma with endoscopic ultrasound-guided needle aspiration and biopsy for tissue sampling under ultrasound guidance.

In Plain Language

camera ultrasound with needle sampling through colostomy; guided tissue sampling via stoma

Clinical Context

Used for tissue sampling of submucosal lesions or lymph nodes in colon/rectum of patients with colostomy under ultrasound guidance for accurate needle placement.
