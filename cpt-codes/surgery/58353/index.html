58353 Endometr ablate thermal

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: thermal endometrial ablation, endometrial thermal ablation

Endometrial ablation using thermal method involves destruction of endometrial tissue using heat energy to treat abnormal uterine bleeding.

Clinical Context

Minimally invasive procedure for heavy menstrual bleeding. Various thermal systems available (radiofrequency, hot fluid, microwave). Requires endometrial preparation.
