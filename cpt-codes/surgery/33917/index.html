33917 Repair pulmonary artery

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: PA repair, pulmonary artery reconstruction, PA plasty

Repair of pulmonary artery; surgical reconstruction or repair of a pulmonary artery branch to address stenosis, hypoplasia, or other obstruction.

Clinical Context

Performed for stenosis of pulmonary artery branches, often in patients with complex congenital heart disease, shunt procedures, or chronic thromboembolic disease.
