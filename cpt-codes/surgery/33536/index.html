33536 Cabg arterial four or more

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: quadruple arterial CABG, four or more arterial CABG, extensive arterial grafting

Surgical placement of four or more coronary artery bypass grafts using internal mammary and other arterial conduits.

Clinical Context

Employed for extensive multivessel disease with predominantly or all-arterial revascularization; represents the gold standard for coronary revascularization with lowest reintervention rates.
