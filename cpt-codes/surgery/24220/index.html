24220 Injection procedures for elbow arthg

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: elbow arthrocentesis, elbow joint injection

Injection of contrast or anesthetic agent for elbow joint arthrocentesis or arthrography. Diagnostic or therapeutic injection into the elbow joint.

Clinical Context

Used for diagnostic arthrography, therapeutic joint injection for pain relief, or aspiration of joint fluid for evaluation.
