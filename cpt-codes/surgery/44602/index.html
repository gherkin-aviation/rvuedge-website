44602 Suture small intestine

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: small bowel repair, small bowel suture, primary repair small intestine

Primary repair of small intestine via open surgical approach, including suturing of laceration or perforation to restore bowel continuity and integrity.

In Plain Language

surgical stitching of small intestine tear; repair of small bowel hole

Clinical Context

Used for traumatic injury to small bowel (perforation, laceration) or iatrogenic injury during surgery. Preserves bowel length and avoids resection when primary repair is feasible.
