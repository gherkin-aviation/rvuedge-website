46712 Repair per/vag pouch dbl procedure

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: two-stage pouch repair, staged reconstruction, cloacal repair

Repair of perineal or vaginal pouch as two-stage procedure. Initial stage establishes stoma, second stage reconstructs anatomy.

Clinical Context

Preferred approach for complex cloacal anomalies. First stage creates protective colostomy; second stage reconstructs anatomy.
