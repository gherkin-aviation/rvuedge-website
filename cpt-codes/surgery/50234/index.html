50234 Removal of kidney & ureter

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: nephroureterectomy, open nephroureterectomy, kidney and ureter removal

Open surgical removal of the kidney and ureter (nephroureterectomy) for a tumor or disease of the upper urinary tract.

Clinical Context

Used for upper urinary tract urothelial carcinoma, infected nonfunctioning kidney with ipsilateral ureteral pathology, and other upper urinary tract malignancies.
