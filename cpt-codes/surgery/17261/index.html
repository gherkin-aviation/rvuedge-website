17261 destruction mal lesion t/a/l .6-1.0cm

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: destroy malignant 0.6-1.0, skin cancer small-medium

Destruction of malignant skin lesion (trunk, arms, legs) with largest diameter between 0.6 and 1.0 centimeter.

Clinical Context

Used for malignant lesions on body that are slightly larger than 0.5 cm but less than 1 cm. Appropriate for early-stage lesions.
