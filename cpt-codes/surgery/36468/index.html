36468 Njx sclrsnt spider veins

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: spider vein sclerotherapy, telangiectasia treatment, small vein sclerosis

Injection of sclerosing solution, non-compound, into spider veins.

In Plain Language

treatment of small red spider veins; closing tiny veins near the skin surface

Clinical Context

Used for cosmetic and symptomatic treatment of telangiectasia and spider angiomas, often on face and legs. Requires delicate technique.
