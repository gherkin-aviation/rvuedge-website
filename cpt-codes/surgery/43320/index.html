43320 Fuse esophagus & stomach

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: esophagogastrostomy, esophageal bypass, esophageal-gastric anastomosis

Esophagogastrostomy with creation of surgical anastomosis between esophagus and stomach for esophageal obstruction or dysfunction. This bypasses obstructed esophagus.

In Plain Language

connecting esophagus to stomach surgically; esophageal bypass

Clinical Context

Palliation for unresectable esophageal cancer or treatment for severe esophageal dysfunction; provides symptom relief for dysphagia.
