15776 Hair transplant >15 punch grafts

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: hair transplant punch extensive, extensive micrografting

Hair transplantation by punch graft technique, more than 15 punch grafts. Micrografting procedure for extensive hair restoration.

Clinical Context

Used for moderate to extensive hair loss requiring larger numbers of punch grafts for comprehensive coverage.
