47100 Wedge biopsy of liver

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: wedge biopsy, open liver biopsy, hepatic wedge biopsy

Wedge biopsy of liver tissue. Surgical removal of liver wedge containing full thickness specimen.

Clinical Context

Performed during abdominal surgery to obtain full-thickness liver tissue for diagnosis of cirrhosis, fibrosis, or malignancy.
