12046 Intmd repair n-hf/genit20.1-30

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: Intermediate laceration repair, Extended layered wound closure

Repair of intermediate complexity laceration on the neck, non-head/non-foot genitalia measuring twenty point one to thirty centimeters.

Clinical Context

Used for large lacerations on the neck or genitalia requiring intermediate complexity layered closure.
