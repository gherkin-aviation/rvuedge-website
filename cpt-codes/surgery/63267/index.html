63267 Excise intrspinl lesion lumbar

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: lumbar intraspinal tumor removal, lumbar spinal lesion excision

Excision of intraspinal lesion in the lumbar region. This procedure involves removal of tumors, cysts, or pathological lesions within the lumbar spinal canal.

In Plain Language

removal of growth or abnormal tissue in lower back spinal canal

Clinical Context

Used for lumbar intraspinal lesions including tumors, cysts, or other compressive masses causing radiculopathy or cauda equina compression.
