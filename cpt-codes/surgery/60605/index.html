60605 Remove carotid body lesion

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: carotid body tumor excision with reconstruction, glomus tumor excision with vascular repair

Surgical removal of carotid body tumor with vascular reconstruction or repair of the carotid artery, performed when tumor involves or requires management of carotid vasculature.

Clinical Context

Performed for larger carotid body tumors, Shamblin class III lesions, or tumors with significant vascular involvement. Vascular reconstruction may include primary repair, patch angioplasty, or vein graft depending on extent of involvement.
