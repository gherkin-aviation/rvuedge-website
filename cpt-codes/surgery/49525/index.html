49525 Repair ing hernia sliding

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: sliding hernia repair, hernia repair with mobilization, complex inguinal hernia

Repair of inguinal hernia with sliding component (intestine or viscus herniating alongside spermatic cord).

Clinical Context

Repair of inguinal hernia with sliding component where bowel or organ protrudes alongside spermatic cord. Requires careful mobilization and reduction to prevent injury.
