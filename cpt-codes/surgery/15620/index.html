15620 Delay flap f/c/c/n/ax/g/h/f

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: facial flap delay, specialized flap delay

Delay procedure for skin pedicle flap on face, chin, cheek, mouth, neck, axilla, genitals, hands, or feet. Blood supply preparation for specialized flap regions.

In Plain Language

preparing facial area flap; improving specialized flap blood supply

Clinical Context

Used for facial reconstruction and specialized area repairs where delay procedure enhances flap survival and cosmetic outcomes.
