13102 Cmplx repair trunk additional 5cm/<

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: Complex laceration add-on, Additional complex wound closure

Complex repair of laceration on the trunk for each additional five centimeters or less beyond the primary wound.

Clinical Context

Add-on code used when complex trunk repair extends beyond the initial measurement.
