68520 Removal of tear sacral

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: dacryocystectomy, lacrimal sac removal

Complete removal of lacrimal sac including associated tissue (dacryocystectomy).

Clinical Context

Used for removal of lacrimal sac due to chronic infection, malignancy, or obstruction. Common indications include recurrent dacryocystitis, lacrimal sac carcinoma, or failed dacryocystorhinostomy.
