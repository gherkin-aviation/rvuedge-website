21209 Reduction of facial bones

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: facial bone reduction, bone reduction contouring

Reduction of facial bones through burring, grinding, or osteotomy to decrease projection or correct prominent features.

In Plain Language

Reducing prominent facial bones; Facial bone reshaping by removal

Clinical Context

Used for prominent facial features, post-traumatic deformity, or aesthetic refinement.
