63295 Repair laminectomy defect

Surgery

Also known as: laminectomy defect repair, spinal reconstruction, bone graft for laminectomy defect

Repair of laminectomy defect. This procedure involves closure or reconstruction of a defect created by previous laminectomy, often using bone graft or synthetic materials.

Clinical Context

Used for repair of symptomatic laminectomy defects causing instability, positional pain, or functional impairment after previous laminectomy.
