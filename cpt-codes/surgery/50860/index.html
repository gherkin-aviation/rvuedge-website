50860 Transplant ureter to skin

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ureterocutaneostomy, ureter to skin, cutaneous ureterostomy

Transplantation of ureter to skin, creating a ureterocutaneostomy to externalize urine drainage through a skin opening.

In Plain Language

bringing the ureter to the skin surface

Clinical Context

Rarely performed, used in select cases with limited life expectancy or where other diversions contraindicated. Simple conduit for urine drainage.
