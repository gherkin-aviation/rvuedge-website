59515 Cesarean delivery

Surgery

Also known as: cesarean with ante and post care, cesarean delivery including postpartum care, c-section with postpartum

Cesarean delivery with antepartum and postpartum obstetrical care, excluding global delivery attendance.

Clinical Context

Used when cesarean delivery planned and managed with antepartum monitoring and postpartum care.
