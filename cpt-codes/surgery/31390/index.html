31390 Removal of larynx & pharynx

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: laryngopharyngectomy, total laryngopharyngeal resection, combined larynx-pharynx removal

Total removal of the larynx and pharynx via combined external approach with reconstruction of continuity via staged procedures.

Clinical Context

Extensive surgical removal of larynx and pharynx for advanced cancer. Requires esophageal reconstruction to restore continuity and allow swallowing.
