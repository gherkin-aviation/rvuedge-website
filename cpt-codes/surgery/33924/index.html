33924 Remove pulmonary shunt

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: Shunt takedown, shunt removal, shunt closure

Removal of pulmonary shunt (prior systemic-pulmonary artery shunt procedure); surgical removal or takedown of a previously placed shunt connecting systemic circulation to pulmonary circulation.

Clinical Context

Performed when a prior Blalock-Taussig shunt, central shunt, or other systemic-to-pulmonary artery shunt is no longer needed, typically after definitive repair of the underlying cardiac defect.
