43425 Repair esophagus opening

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: esophageal repair with reconstruction, esophageal opening repair with anastomosis, cervicothoracic esophageal repair

Repair of esophageal opening with creation of esophagogastric or other anastomosis via cervical or thoracic approach for complex defects. This addresses complex esophageal defects.

Clinical Context

Complex repair for extensive esophageal defects requiring reconstruction; may require staged procedures or flap coverage.
