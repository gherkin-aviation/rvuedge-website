69700 Close mastoid fistula

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: mastoid fistula closure, fistula repair, mastoid fistula obliteration

Surgical closure or repair of a mastoid fistula (abnormal communication between mastoid and skin) to eliminate drainage and prevent infection.

Clinical Context

Used for post-mastoidectomy persistent fistula, inadequate mastoid obliteration, or granulation tissue-related fistulization.
