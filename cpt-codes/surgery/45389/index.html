45389 Colonoscopy with stent plcmt

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: colonoscopy with stent placement, colon stent insertion

Colonoscopy with placement of expandable metal stent for colonic obstruction relief or stricture palliation.

Clinical Context

Performed for malignant obstruction, benign strictures, or acute colonic obstruction as bridge to surgery or for palliative management.
