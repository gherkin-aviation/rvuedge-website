38204 Bl donor search management

Surgery

Also known as: Donor search, Registry management

Blood donor search and registry management services, including initial consultation and donor coordination for allogeneic bone marrow or stem cell transplantation.

Clinical Context

Used in hematopoietic cell transplantation programs to coordinate identification and evaluation of compatible allogeneic donors.
