33988 Insertion of left heart vent

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: LVAD insertion, left heart vent placement, VAD insertion

Insertion of a left ventricular assist device (LVAD) or left heart ventricular assist device. Surgical placement of mechanical support pump to augment cardiac output.

In Plain Language

putting in a heart pump; mechanical heart support; artificial pump to help the left heart

Clinical Context

Used for acute decompensated heart failure, cardiogenic shock, or bridge to transplantation. Critical for patients with severe left ventricular dysfunction.
