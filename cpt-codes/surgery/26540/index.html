26540 Repair hand joint

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Hand joint repair, Joint reconstruction, Ligament repair

Surgical repair of a hand joint, typically addressing ligamentous injury, cartilage damage, or joint instability through primary repair or reconstruction.

Clinical Context

Indicated for acute hand joint injuries with ligamentous damage, dislocation with instability, or other joint pathology requiring primary repair.
