61543 Removal of brain tissue

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: brain tissue resection, lobectomy, cortical resection

Craniotomy or craniectomy with removal or resection of brain tissue from supratentorial or infratentorial location for seizure control or other indications.

Clinical Context

Used for various epilepsy surgery procedures or therapeutic brain tissue removal. Code used when specific location or approach not captured by more specific codes.
