17313 Mohs 1 stage t/a/l

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: Mohs first stage trunk, Mohs body first

Mohs micrographic surgery for skin cancer with first stage histological analysis and mapping on trunk, arms, and legs.

Clinical Context

Performed for malignancies on trunk and extremities where tissue conservation is important. First stage analysis determines margin adequacy.
