43755 Diagnosis gastric intub with asp specs

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: guided gastric intubation, fluoroscopic gastric sampling, visualized diagnostic intubation

Diagnostic gastric intubation with aspiration and specimen collection under fluoroscopic guidance or with specialized visualization.

In Plain Language

X-ray guided stomach sampling; imaging-assisted stomach tube

Clinical Context

Used when direct visualization or radiographic guidance is necessary for proper tube placement or specimen collection. Aids in assessment of gastric outlet obstruction or gastric emptying.
