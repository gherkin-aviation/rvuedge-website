28238 Revision of foot tendon

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: foot tendon revision, tendon revision surgery, re-do tendon surgery

Revision of foot tendon procedure. This procedure revises or corrects a previous tendon operation or addresses complications.

Clinical Context

Performed for failed prior tendon surgery, recurrent deformity, or adhesions. Requires careful dissection and may be more complex than initial procedure.
