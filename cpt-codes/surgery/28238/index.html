28238 Revision of foot tendon

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: foot tendon revision, tendon revision surgery, re-do tendon surgery

Revision of foot tendon procedure. This procedure revises or corrects a previous tendon operation or addresses complications.

In Plain Language

redoing foot tendon surgery; foot tendon re-operation

Clinical Context

Performed for failed prior tendon surgery, recurrent deformity, or adhesions. Requires careful dissection and may be more complex than initial procedure.

Billing & Documentation

As a surgical CPT code, proper documentation must include the operative report detailing the procedure performed, patient positioning, approach, findings, and any complications. This code has a 90-day global period, which includes the day of the procedure, 1 day preoperative, and 90 days of postoperative care. Ensure the diagnosis code (ICD-10) supports medical necessity for the procedure.
