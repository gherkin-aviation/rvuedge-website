51702 Insert temp bladder catheter

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: temporary catheterization, acute catheter placement

Insertion of temporary bladder catheter with short-term dwell, placement for temporary drainage in acute situations.

Clinical Context

Used for acute urinary retention or post-operative drainage anticipated short-term.
