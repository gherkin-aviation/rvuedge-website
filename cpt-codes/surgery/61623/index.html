61623 Evasc temp balo artl occ h/n

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: balloon angioplasty, temporary balloon occlusion

Percutaneous transluminal balloon angioplasty or temporary balloon occlusion of arterial lesion in head and neck region.

In Plain Language

Balloon treatment of blood vessel; Temporary vessel blocking

Clinical Context

Endovascular technique for arteriovenous malformations, aneurysms, or vasospasm. Temporary occlusion used for surgical planning.
