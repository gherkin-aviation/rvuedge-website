35601 Bpg com crtd-ipsi int crtd

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: carotid bypass, carotid-to-carotid bypass, common to internal carotid bypass

Arterial bypass graft using composite prosthetic and autogenous vein material from common carotid to ipsilateral internal carotid artery. This procedure restores blood flow across carotid artery occlusions using a hybrid graft approach.

Clinical Context

Performed for symptomatic or asymptomatic carotid artery occlusion, stenosis, or aneurysm in high-risk surgical candidates. Used when standard carotid endarterectomy is not feasible.
