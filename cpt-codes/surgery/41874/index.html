41874 Alveoloplasty each quadrant

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: alveoloplasty, bone contouring, alveolar reshaping

Surgical reshaping of the alveolar bone and soft tissue in each quadrant to improve periodontal anatomy, reduce bony defects, or prepare for prosthetics.

Clinical Context

Performed per jaw quadrant to eliminate bony ledges, enhance ridge anatomy for denture retention, or improve periodontal defect contours.
