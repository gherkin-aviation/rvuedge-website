53442 Remove/revise male sling

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: sling removal, sling revision, male sling removal

Surgical removal or revision of a male urethral sling previously placed to address stress urinary incontinence.

In Plain Language

removing or fixing urethra support sling; sling adjustment surgery

Clinical Context

Performed when male sling causes complications (obstruction, erosion, inadequate continence) or requires repositioning. Also done when patient opts for alternative treatment.
