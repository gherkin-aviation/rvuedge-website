45347 Sigmoidoscopy with plcmt stent

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: sigmoidoscopy with stent placement, flexible sig with stent insertion

Sigmoidoscopy with placement of expandable metal or plastic stent for obstruction relief or stricture palliation.

In Plain Language

placement of metal tube to open blockage; stent insertion for stricture

Clinical Context

Performed for malignant obstruction, benign strictures, or perforation risk; often used as bridge to surgery or definitive therapy.
