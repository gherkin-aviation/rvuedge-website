52301 Cystoscopy and treatment

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: cysto with bladder neck and ureteral treatment

Cystourethroscopy with resection or dilation of bladder neck contracture with concurrent ureteral stricture treatment, addressing multiple obstructive lesions.

Clinical Context

Used when both bladder outlet and ureteral strictures coexist, allowing comprehensive endoscopic treatment in single procedure.
