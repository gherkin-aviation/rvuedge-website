64804 Sympathectomy cervicothorac

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: cervicothoracic sympathectomy, upper thoracic sympathectomy

Surgical sympathectomy involving both the cervical and upper thoracic sympathetic chain. Treats sympathetic dysfunction in the upper extremity and chest wall.

Clinical Context

Indicated for extensive upper extremity CRPS, Raynaud's phenomenon, or hyperhidrosis involving the upper body. Requires disruption of both cervical and first thoracic sympathetic ganglia.
