43286 Esphg tot with laps moblj

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: lap total esophagectomy, laparoscopic esophagectomy, total esophageal resection

Total laparoscopic esophagectomy with abdominal mobilization of the esophagus and esophagogastric anastomosis. This major surgical procedure removes the entire esophagus.

Clinical Context

Treatment for esophageal cancer, end-stage achalasia, or severe caustic injury; high morbidity procedure with significant postoperative recovery.
