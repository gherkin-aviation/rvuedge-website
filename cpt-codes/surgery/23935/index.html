23935 incision dp opn bone crtx humerus/elbow

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: cortical bone drainage, bone cortex drainage, humeral osteomyelitis drainage

Incision and drainage, with opening of cortex of humerus and/or elbow. Deep cortical drainage addressing bone involvement in upper arm infection.

Clinical Context

Performed for humeral or elbow osteomyelitis, cortical abscess, or when infection involves bone; requires cortical opening for adequate drainage and debridement.
