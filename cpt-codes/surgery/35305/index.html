35305 Rechanneling of artery

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: popliteal endarterectomy, popliteal artery rechanneling

Rechanneling (thrombectomy with endarterectomy) of artery in the popliteal region with antegrade flow restoration.

Clinical Context

Surgical management of popliteal artery stenosis or occlusion causing calf claudication or acute ischemia. Requires careful dissection to avoid injury to surrounding neural and muscular structures.
