27197 Clsd treatment pelvic ring fracture

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: closed pelvic fracture treatment, conservative pelvic fracture care

Closed treatment of pelvic ring fracture without reduction or with minimal manipulation, appropriate for stable fracture patterns.

In Plain Language

Non-surgical hip fracture care; Conservative pelvic healing

Clinical Context

Applied to stable pelvic ring fractures not requiring operative fixation, managed with rest, pelvic binder, and conservative support allowing fracture healing.
