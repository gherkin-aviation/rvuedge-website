44213 Laparoscopic mobil splenic fl add-on

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: lap splenic flexure mobilization, splenic flexure takedown

Laparoscopic mobilization of splenic flexure as an add-on procedure to facilitate completion of colectomy or proctocolectomy with adequate length and tension-free anastomosis.

Clinical Context

Add-on code used when splenic flexure must be mobilized during laparoscopic colectomy or proctocolectomy to achieve adequate length for tension-free anastomosis or to improve visualization.
