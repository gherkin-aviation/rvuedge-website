28130 Removal of ankle bone

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: talus removal, tarsal bone complete removal, ankle bone resection

Complete removal of the talus (ankle bone) or other tarsal bone. This comprehensive procedure removes an entire tarsal bone.

In Plain Language

removing an ankle bone; major ankle bone surgery

Clinical Context

Performed for avascular necrosis of the talus, severe crush injury, or infection. Significant surgery with major functional consequences; often leads to fusion or arthritis.

Billing & Documentation
