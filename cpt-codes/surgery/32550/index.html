32550 Insert pleural catheter

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: pleural catheter insertion, PleurX catheter, chest tube insertion

Percutaneous insertion of a pleural catheter under imaging guidance for drainage of pleural fluid, blood, or air without imaging directly guiding needle placement.

In Plain Language

chest fluid drain insertion

Clinical Context

Used for recurrent malignant pleural effusion or pneumothorax when repeated thoracentesis is necessary, allowing outpatient management.
