25248 Remove forearm foreign body

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: foreign body removal, FB extraction

Removal of a foreign body (embedded object) from the forearm using surgical exploration and dissection.

Clinical Context

Indicated for retained metal fragments, glass, wood, or other foreign bodies causing infection or functional impairment.
