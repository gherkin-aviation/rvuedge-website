64644 Chemodenerv 1 extremity 5/> mus

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: extremity chemodenervation extensive muscles, botulinum toxin multiple extremity muscles

Chemodenervation of one extremity with administration of chemical agent to five or more muscles.

Clinical Context

Used for severe spasticity in one extremity affecting five or more muscles.
