50045 Nephrotomy with exploration

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: nephrotomy with exploration, kidney exploration, renal exploration

Nephrotomy (kidney incision) with exploration of kidney parenchyma for calculus, mass, or other pathology.

Clinical Context

Surgical incision and exploration of kidney parenchyma to evaluate for stone, mass, or other pathology. Used when diagnostic imaging is inconclusive.
