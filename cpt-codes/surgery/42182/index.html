42182 Repair palate

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: palate repair, palatoplasty

Surgical repair of the soft palate, typically for lacerations, traumatic defects, or palatal insufficiency of varying sizes.

In Plain Language

stitching or repairing soft palate

Clinical Context

Repair of palatal defects from trauma, post-operative complications, congenital insufficiency, or other pathology affecting palatal function.
