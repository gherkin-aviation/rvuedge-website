27057 Buttock fasciotomy with debridement

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: buttock fasciotomy, buttock debridement, gluteal fasciotomy

Surgical fasciotomy and debridement of the buttock region with removal of necrotic or infected tissue.

Clinical Context

Indicated for buttock compartment syndrome, large abscess, or necrotizing soft tissue infection requiring emergency decompression and debridement.
