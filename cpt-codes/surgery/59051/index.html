59051 Fetal monitor/interpret only

Surgery

Also known as: fetal monitoring interpretation, strip interpretation

Fetal monitoring with interpretation and analysis only (without complete report), used when monitoring strip is reviewed for clinical decision-making.

Clinical Context

Used for periodic review and interpretation of fetal heart rate patterns during labor without generating formal comprehensive documentation.
