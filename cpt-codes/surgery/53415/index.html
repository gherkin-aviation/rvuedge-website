53415 Reconstruction of urethra

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: flap-based urethroplasty, pedicled flap urethroplasty

Complete one-stage urethroplasty with flap tissue (typically pedicled or rotational flap) for urethral stricture reconstruction, preserving vascular supply.

Clinical Context

Used when graft inadequate or for specific anatomic configurations. Maintains vascular pedicle for excellent tissue viability.
