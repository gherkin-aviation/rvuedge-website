21143 Rcnstj mdface lefort i 3/>pc

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Le Fort I segmental, Le Fort I 3+ piece, complex midface

Reconstruction of the midface using Le Fort I osteotomy as three or more pieces for maximum flexibility in repositioning severely deformed facial structures.

Clinical Context

Used for severe midface trauma, deformities, or asymmetries requiring independent positioning of multiple segments.
