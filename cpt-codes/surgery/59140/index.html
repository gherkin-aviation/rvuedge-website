59140 Treat ectopic pregnancy

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: needle aspiration of ectopic, percutaneous aspiration

Surgical treatment of ectopic pregnancy via needle aspiration (minimally invasive approach under ultrasound guidance), primarily for interstitial or cornual pregnancies.

Clinical Context

Minimally invasive option for select ectopic pregnancies (interstitial, cornual) where methotrexate is contraindicated or aspiration is definitive.
