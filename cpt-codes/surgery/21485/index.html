21485 Cltx tmprmand dislc complicated

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: complicated TMJ dislocation reduction, recurrent jaw dislocation treatment

Closed treatment of complicated temporomandibular joint dislocation. Non-surgical reduction of complex or recurrent temporomandibular joint dislocation with associated complications requiring extended management.

In Plain Language

complex jaw joint dislocation treatment; recurrent jaw popping treatment

Clinical Context

Used for complicated temporomandibular joint dislocations including those with significant delay in reduction, severe soft tissue trauma, or recurrent pattern. More extensive management needed compared to simple first-time dislocation.
