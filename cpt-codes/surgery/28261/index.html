28261 Revision of foot tendon

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: foot tendon revision with reconstruction, complex tendon revision, foot tendon re-do with graft

Revision of foot tendon procedures including reconstruction. This comprehensive procedure revises complex tendon pathology with possible graft or transfer.

Clinical Context

Performed for complex tendon problems including multiple tendon involvement, extensive adhesions, or functional restoration needs in revision setting.
