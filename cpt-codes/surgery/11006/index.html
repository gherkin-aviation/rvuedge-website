11006 Debridement skin external gent per

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: Extensive genital debridement, Genitoperineal cleanup

Debridement of skin and subcutaneous tissue of external genitalia, perineum, and adjacent areas. Extensive removal of infected or damaged tissue from external genitalia region.

Clinical Context

Applied for extensive Fournier's gangrene or large infected perineal wounds requiring aggressive debridement.
