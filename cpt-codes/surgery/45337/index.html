45337 Sigmoidoscopy & decompress

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: sigmoidoscopy with decompression, volvulus decompression

Sigmoidoscopy with decompression of sigmoid volvulus or colonic obstruction via needle aspiration or tube placement.

Clinical Context

Emergency procedure for acute sigmoid volvulus or acute colonic pseudo-obstruction (Ogilvie syndrome) presenting with abdominal distension and pain.
