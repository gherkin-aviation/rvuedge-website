44900 incision and drainage appendix abscess open

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: open appendiceal abscess drainage, I&D appendix abscess

Incision and drainage of appendiceal abscess via open surgical approach for treatment of acute appendicitis with abscess formation.

Clinical Context

Used for acute appendicitis with abscess formation when appendectomy is not performed acutely. Often followed by interval appendectomy after resolution.
