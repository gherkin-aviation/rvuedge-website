26105 Biopsy finger joint lining

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: synovial biopsy finger, joint lining biopsy finger

Synovial biopsy (tissue biopsy) of finger joint synovium (joint lining).

Clinical Context

Used for diagnostic biopsy of finger joint synovium to evaluate arthritis, infection, or other joint disease.
