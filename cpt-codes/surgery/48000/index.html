48000 Drainage of abdomen

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Pancreatic abscess drainage, open drainage

Surgical drainage of a pancreatic abscess or fluid collection through an abdominal incision, establishing a drainage tract for infection management.

Clinical Context

Performed for pancreatic abscess secondary to pancreatitis, trauma, or surgery. Open approach when percutaneous drainage fails or anatomy prevents it.
