49084 Peritoneal lavage

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: Peritoneal lavage, abdominal lavage, diagnostic lavage

Peritoneal lavage (washing) of the abdominal cavity with instillation of fluid to evaluate for peritonitis, perform diagnostic fluid analysis, or remove debris.

In Plain Language

Washing out the abdominal cavity

Clinical Context

Used in trauma to detect intra-abdominal hemorrhage or in peritonitis to irrigate and remove infected fluid.
