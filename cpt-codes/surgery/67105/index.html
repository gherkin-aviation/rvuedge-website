67105 Repair detached retina pc

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: posterior buckle, encirclement, scleral buckle repair, pars plana buckle

Repair of rhegmatogenous retinal detachment using scleral buckling technique through posterior (pars plana) approach with posterior imbrication or encirclement.

Clinical Context

Used for posterior retinal detachments or when anterior buckle inadequate; encircling band or buckle placed at equator or posterior to equator.
