35685 Bypass graft patency/patch

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: graft patency revision, graft patch angioplasty, bypass revision

Revision or supplementation of bypass graft patency assessment or patch angioplasty to correct intimal hyperplasia or anastomotic stenosis. Restores graft function.

In Plain Language

fixing a bypass graft that's narrowing; enlarging a graft with a patch

Clinical Context

Performed for early or late graft stenosis. May use interposition patch of vein or prosthetic material.
