27407 Repair of knee ligament

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: multi-ligament repair, ACL-MCL repair, complex knee reconstruction

Surgical repair or reconstruction of two knee ligaments, typically involving cruciate-collateral or cruciate-posterolateral combinations to address complex knee instability.

Clinical Context

Used for multi-ligament knee injuries from high-energy trauma. More complex than single ligament repair, affecting functional outcome and rehabilitation.
