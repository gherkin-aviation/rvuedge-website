68326 Revise/graft eyelid lining

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: amniotic membrane graft, conjunctival transplant

Revision and graft of conjunctival tissue including placement of conjunctival transplant or amniotic membrane graft.

In Plain Language

eye membrane repair with transplant; conjunctival tissue transplant

Clinical Context

Used for ocular surface reconstruction with grafting materials. Common indications include Stevens-Johnson syndrome, ocular cicatricial pemphigoid, severe dry eye, or chemical burns.
