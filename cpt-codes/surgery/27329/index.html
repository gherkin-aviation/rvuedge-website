27329 Resection thigh/knee tumor < 5 centimeters

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: soft tissue resection <5cm, tumor resection small

Resection of soft tissue tumor of thigh or knee measuring less than 5 centimeters with comprehensive removal.

Clinical Context

Indicated for soft tissue tumors or masses less than 5 centimeters requiring surgical resection.
