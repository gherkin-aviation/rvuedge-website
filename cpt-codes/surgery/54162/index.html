54162 Lysis penil circumic lesion

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: circumcision adhesion lysis, circumcision revision

Surgical lysis or freeing of penil circumcision adhesions or complications after circumcision.

In Plain Language

freeing stuck skin after circumcision

Clinical Context

Performed when circumcision develops adhesions between epithelial surfaces or skin bridges. Preserves function and appearance.
