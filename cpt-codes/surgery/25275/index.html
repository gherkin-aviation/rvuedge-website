25275 Repair forearm tendon sheath

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: tendon sheath repair, synovial repair

Repair of the tendon sheath (synovial membrane) surrounding forearm tendons to restore normal gliding mechanism.

Clinical Context

Performed when tendon sheath is damaged but the tendon itself is intact. Important for preserving tendon excursion.
