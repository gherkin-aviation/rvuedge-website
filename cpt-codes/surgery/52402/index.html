52402 Cysto trurl rescj ejacul dux

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: cysto with ejaculatory duct resection

Cystoscopy with transurethral resection of ejaculatory duct or ejaculated duct cyst to restore antegrade ejaculation in patients with azoospermia from ejaculatory duct obstruction.

Clinical Context

Treatment for obstructive azoospermia. Relatively uncommon procedure with variable success in semen quality restoration.
