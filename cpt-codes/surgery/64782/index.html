64782 Remove limb nerve lesion

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: limb nerve lesion removal, neurofibroma excision, limb neuroma excision

Surgical removal of a lesion affecting a nerve of the limb (arm or leg). Includes benign tumors such as neurofibromas, schwannomas, or painful neuromas.

Clinical Context

Indicated for benign nerve tumors or neuromas in the upper or lower extremity causing pain, functional impairment, or cosmetic concern.
