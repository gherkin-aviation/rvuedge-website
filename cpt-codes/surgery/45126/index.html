45126 Pelvic exenteration

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: pelvic exenteration with proctectomy, total pelvic clearance, anterior/posterior pelvic exenteration

Pelvic exenteration including proctectomy, performed to remove rectum and surrounding pelvic organs (bladder, prostate/uterus) en bloc for advanced malignancy.

Clinical Context

Reserved for locally advanced pelvic malignancies (rectal, bladder, or gynecologic cancers) that are locally invasive and potentially curable by complete en bloc resection.
