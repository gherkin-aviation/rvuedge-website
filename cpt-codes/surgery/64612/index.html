64612 Destroy nerve face muscle

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: facial muscle chemodenervation, botulinum toxin facial muscles, facial dystonia treatment

Chemodenervation of facial muscles with administration of chemical agent to treat muscle hyperactivity or facial movement disorders.

Clinical Context

Used for blepharospasm, hemifacial spasm, and facial dystonia.
