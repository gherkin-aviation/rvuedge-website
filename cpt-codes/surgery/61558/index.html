61558 Excision of skull/sutures

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: bone flap excision, skull lesion removal

Craniotomy with excision of bone flap for skull lesion removal or tumor treatment with complete osseous resection.

In Plain Language

Removal of a piece of skull bone; Excision of skull tumor

Clinical Context

Performed for skull vault tumors (osteosarcoma, meningioma), infection requiring debridement, or defect requiring reconstruction.
