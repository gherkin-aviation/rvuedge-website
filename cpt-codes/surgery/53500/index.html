53500 Urethrlys transvag with scope

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: transurethral urethrolysis, endoscopic urethral release

Transurethral urethrolysis with endoscopic visualization to lyse or release adhesions or scar tissue obstructing the urethra.

Clinical Context

Indicated for strictures amenable to endoscopic lysis. Used in scar tissue causing obstruction, post-traumatic or post-catheter changes.
