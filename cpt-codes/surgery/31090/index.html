31090 Exploration of sinuses

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: pansinusitis surgery, multiple sinus surgery, comprehensive sinusotomy

Comprehensive surgical exploration and management of multiple paranasal sinuses (sphenoid, ethmoid, and maxillary), often via multiple approaches or extended procedure.

Clinical Context

Extensive surgery for severe pansinusitis or complex disease involving multiple sinuses requiring combined approaches and comprehensive disease management.
