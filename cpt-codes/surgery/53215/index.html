53215 Removal of urethra

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: male urethrectomy

Radical surgical removal of male urethra with surrounding tissue and structures (sometimes including penis) for urethral carcinoma.

Clinical Context

Radical approach for anterior urethral malignancy. High morbidity but necessary for cure. May include partial penectomy.
