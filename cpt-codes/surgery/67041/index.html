67041 Vit for macular pucker

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: epiretinal membrane peel, ERM peeling, macular pucker repair, membrane dissection

Vitrectomy with dissection and removal of epiretinal membrane causing macular pucker or retinal contraction.

Clinical Context

Performed for symptomatic epiretinal membrane causing metamorphopsia, visual distortion, or decreased vision; improves retinal topography and vision recovery.
