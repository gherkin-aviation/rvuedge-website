65780 Ocular reconst transplant

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: cicatricial pemphigoid repair, ocular surface reconstruction

Ocular surface reconstruction for cicatricial pemphigoid; amniotic membrane; first 100 square millimeters or less.

Clinical Context

Surgical reconstruction of ocular surface in cicatricial pemphigoid using amniotic membrane. First stage of often multi-stage reconstruction for severe scarring.
