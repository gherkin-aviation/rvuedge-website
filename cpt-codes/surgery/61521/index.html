61521 Removal of brain lesion

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: supratentorial mass resection, cranial tumor removal

Craniotomy or craniectomy with removal of intracranial mass from supratentorial location, often with specific focus on extent or location.

Clinical Context

Performed for supratentorial brain lesions of varying types and complexity. Code used when extent or specific anatomy distinguishes from other tumor resection codes.
