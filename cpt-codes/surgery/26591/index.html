26591 Repair muscles of hand

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Intrinsic muscle repair, Hand muscle repair, Muscle reconstruction

Surgical repair of the intrinsic muscles of the hand to restore or improve function, typically performed for muscle injury, rupture, or functional deficit.

In Plain Language

Repairing hand muscles to improve grip; Surgery to fix damaged hand muscles

Clinical Context

Indicated for intrinsic hand muscle injury, rupture, or damage affecting hand function, grip, or fine motor control.
