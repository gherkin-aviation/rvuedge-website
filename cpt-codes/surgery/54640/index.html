54640 Orchiopexy ingun/scrot appr

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: orchiopexy inguinal, cryptorchid fixation

Surgical fixation of undescended testis (orchiopexy) using inguinal or scrotal approach with tissue mobilization.

Clinical Context

Standard operative treatment of cryptorchidism securing undescended testis in scrotum to preserve fertility and prevent malignancy risk.
