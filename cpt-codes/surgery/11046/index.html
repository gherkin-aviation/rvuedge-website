11046 Debridement muscle and/or fsca each additional

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: Additional muscle debridement, Extra deep tissue removal

Debridement of muscle and fascia (and subcutaneous tissue if necessary), each additional twenty square centimeters or less. Add-on code for additional muscular debridement.

Clinical Context

Applied for extensive necrotizing fasciitis or deep soft tissue infection involving multiple anatomic regions.
