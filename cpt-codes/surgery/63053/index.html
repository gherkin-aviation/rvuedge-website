63053 Lam factc/frmt arthrd lum each

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: additional lumbar laminectomy, lumbar fusion extension

Laminectomy with facetectomy or foraminotomy for arthrodesis, each additional lumbar segment. Add-on code for extending lumbar fusion decompression to additional levels beyond the first.

Clinical Context

Coded when lumbar laminectomy with fusion extends beyond L1, frequently used in multi-level degenerative disc disease or spondylolisthesis.
