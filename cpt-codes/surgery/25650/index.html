25650 Cltx ulnar styloid fracture

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: closed reduction ulnar styloid, conservative styloid fracture

Closed treatment (reduction) of ulnar styloid bone fracture, the small projection on the ulnar side of the wrist.

In Plain Language

non-surgical treatment of broken wrist bone tip; immobilized wrist bone fracture

Clinical Context

Used for isolated ulnar styloid fractures or those associated with distal radial fractures managed by closed reduction and immobilization.
