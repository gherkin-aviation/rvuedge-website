50389 Remove renal tube with fluoroscopy

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: percutaneous tube removal, nephrostomy removal, percutaneous drain removal

Percutaneous removal of a renal tube (nephrostomy catheter) under fluoroscopic guidance with visualization.

Clinical Context

Used for removal of percutaneous nephrostomy tubes after treatment is complete or when percutaneous access is no longer needed.
