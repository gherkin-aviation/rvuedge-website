43460 Pressure treatment esophagus

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: esophageal manometry, pressure study, motility study

Pressure measurement of esophageal lumen and sphincter using esophageal manometry for evaluation of motor function and achalasia. This assesses esophageal pressures.

Clinical Context

Diagnostic test for achalasia, diffuse esophageal spasm, and other motor disorders; essential for surgical decision-making in achalasia.
