47740 Fuse gallbladder & bowel

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Choledochoduodenostomy, bile duct-duodenal bypass

Surgical creation of an internal anastomosis between the common bile duct and duodenum (choledochoduodenostomy) to bypass distal bile duct obstruction.

In Plain Language

Connecting bile duct to small intestine

Clinical Context

Performed for distal bile duct obstruction from cancer, stricture, or benign disease. Allows internal drainage and normalizes biliary flow.
