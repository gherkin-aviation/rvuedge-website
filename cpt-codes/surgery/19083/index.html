19083 Biopsy breast first lesion ultrasound imag

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: ultrasound breast biopsy, US biopsy first

Percutaneous needle or core biopsy of breast lesion under ultrasound guidance for first lesion for tissue diagnosis.

Clinical Context

Performed for palpable or sonographically visible breast lesions. Ultrasound guidance allows real-time needle positioning.
