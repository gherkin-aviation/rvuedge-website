43754 Diagnosis gastric intub with asp specimen

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: diagnostic gastric intubation, gastric specimen collection, stomach sampling

Diagnostic gastric intubation with aspiration and specimen collection for gastric analysis or culture.

In Plain Language

stomach sampling; diagnostic stomach tube

Clinical Context

Used for diagnostic evaluation of gastric secretions, culture for infection, or assessment of gastric acid production in cases of suspected hypersecretion or achlorhydria.
