37218 Stent placemt ante carotid

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: antegrade carotid stent, direct carotid puncture stent

Transcatheter stent placement in the carotid artery via antegrade (direct ipsilateral puncture) approach to treat stenosis.

Clinical Context

Direct puncture approach used when transfemoral access unavailable. Addresses symptomatic and asymptomatic carotid stenosis.
