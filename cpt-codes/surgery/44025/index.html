44025 Incision of large bowel

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: colotomy, colon incision, large bowel incision

Incision of large intestine (colon) for exploration, decompression, or other therapeutic intervention via open approach.

In Plain Language

colon opening surgery; large intestine exploration

Clinical Context

Surgical opening of colon for management of obstruction, volvulus, foreign body removal, or decompression. May be combined with other colonic procedures.

Billing & Documentation

As a surgical CPT code, proper documentation must include the operative report detailing the procedure performed, patient positioning, approach, findings, and any complications. This code has a 90-day global period, which includes the day of the procedure, 1 day preoperative, and 90 days of postoperative care.
