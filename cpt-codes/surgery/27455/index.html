27455 Realignment of knee

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: tibial osteotomy, high tibial osteotomy, HTO

Surgical osteotomy of the knee joint area (typically proximal tibia) with realignment to correct angular deformity and improve lower extremity biomechanics.

Clinical Context

Used for genu varum or valgum, unicompartmental osteoarthritis, and to unload diseased knee compartment. Common joint-preserving procedure.
