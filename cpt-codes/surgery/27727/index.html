27727 Repair of lower leg

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: tibial non-union repair, lower leg union repair

Surgical repair of lower leg non-union or malunion involving tibia and fibula with osteosynthesis and reconstruction.

Clinical Context

Performed for hypertrophic or atrophic non-union of lower leg fractures. Requires assessment and fixation of both bones.
