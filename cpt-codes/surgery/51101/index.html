51101 Drainage bladder by trocar/catheter

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: percutaneous cystostomy, trocar drainage, suprapubic catheter

Drainage of bladder by trocar or catheter, percutaneous placement of drainage catheter via trocar technique for bladder decompression.

Clinical Context

Used for acute retention, inoperable cases, or when urethral catheterization contraindicated. Provides temporary or long-term drainage.
