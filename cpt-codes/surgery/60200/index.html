60200 Remove thyroid lesion

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: thyroid lesion removal, thyroidotomy

Removal of thyroid lesion or nodule via open surgical approach, including thyroidotomy or partial excision.

Clinical Context

Performed for benign thyroid pathology (nodules, cysts) requiring removal; preserves thyroid function.
