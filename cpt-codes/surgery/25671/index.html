25671 Percutaneous skel fix radiation/uln dislc

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: percutaneous fixation wrist dislocation, pin fixation carpal dislocation

Percutaneous skeletal fixation of radiocarpal or midcarpal dislocation without open incision.

Clinical Context

Used for wrist dislocations managed via percutaneous pinning, often as adjunct to or alternative to open surgery.
