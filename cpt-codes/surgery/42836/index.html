42836 Removal of adenoids

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: complex adenoidectomy, adenoidectomy with additional procedure

Removal of adenoids only (adenoidectomy) via transoral approach with advanced technique or significant additional procedure.

Clinical Context

Performed when adenoidectomy is combined with other procedures or requires advanced techniques. Includes cases with significant bleeding, extensive adhesions, or concurrent procedures.
