43496 Free jejunum flap microvasc

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: free jejunal graft, free jejunal transfer, microvascular esophageal reconstruction

Free jejunal graft with microvascular anastomosis for reconstruction of esophageal defects in extensive injuries or after esophagectomy. This provides tissue for reconstruction.

In Plain Language

using small intestine to reconstruct esophagus; free tissue transfer for esophagus

Clinical Context

Complex reconstruction for massive esophageal defects from trauma, caustic injury, or extensive malignancy; requires microsurgical expertise.

RVU Information

CPT 43496 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

As a surgical CPT code, proper documentation must include the operative report detailing the procedure performed, patient positioning, approach, findings, and any complications. This code has a 90-day global period, which includes the day of the procedure, 1 day preoperative, and 90 days of postoperative care.
