20660 Apply rem fixation device

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: external fixator, external fixation device

Application of a removable external fixation device for fracture stabilization or alignment.

In Plain Language

external metal frame to stabilize fracture; outside scaffold to hold bone in place

Clinical Context

Applied to complex fractures, open fractures, or when internal fixation is contraindicated; provides rigid stabilization while allowing soft tissue access.
