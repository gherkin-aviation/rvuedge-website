44340 Revision of colostomy

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: colostomy revision, colostomy repair, simple colostomy revision

Revision of colostomy with simple repositioning, resurfacing, or reconstruction without requiring major bowel resection or anastomosis.

Clinical Context

Performed for simple colostomy complications including retraction, prolapse, stenosis, parastomal hernia, or poor positioning affecting pouch fit and function.
