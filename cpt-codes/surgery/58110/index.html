58110 Biopsy done with colposcopy add-on

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: endometrial biopsy add-on, colposcopy with EMB

Endometrial biopsy performed as add-on procedure during colposcopy or other vaginal procedure uses tissue obtained for histologic evaluation without separate incision.

Clinical Context

Add-on code when endometrial tissue is sampled during colposcopy or similar procedure without additional operative time. Use only when primary procedure is separately reportable.
