59120 Treat ectopic pregnancy

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ectopic pregnancy treatment, salpingectomy, tubal pregnancy removal

Surgical treatment of ectopic pregnancy via primary abdominal approach (laparotomy), including salpingostomy or salpingectomy.

Clinical Context

Emergency surgical intervention when ectopic pregnancy is symptomatic with rupture or massive hemorrhage; alternative to medical management.
