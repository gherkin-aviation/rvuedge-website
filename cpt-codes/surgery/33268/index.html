33268 Excl laa opn oth procedures any meth

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: LAA exclusion add-on, LAA closure during other surgery

Exclusion of left atrial appendage performed as an additional procedure during another open surgical procedure, add-on LAA management.

Clinical Context

Performed in conjunction with other cardiac operations to provide stroke risk reduction for atrial fibrillation patients.
