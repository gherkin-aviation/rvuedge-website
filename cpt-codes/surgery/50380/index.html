50380 Rnl autotrnsplj reimplantation kdn

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: renal autotransplant, autotransplantation, auto-transplant

Renal autotransplantation (transplantation of the patient's own kidney back into the body) with reimplantation after initial removal for vascular repair or other purposes.

Clinical Context

Used for ex vivo repair of renal artery stenosis or thrombosis, complex nephrolithotomy, or other conditions requiring kidney removal and replacement.
