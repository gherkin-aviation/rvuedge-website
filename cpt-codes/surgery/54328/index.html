54328 Revise penis/urethra

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: penile/urethral revision, re-operation

Revision of penis or urethra for improvement of previous surgical outcome or correction of complication.

Clinical Context

Used for suboptimal outcomes from prior penile or urethral surgery. Requires assessment of anatomic changes and scar revision.
