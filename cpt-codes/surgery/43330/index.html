43330 Esophagomyotomy abdominal

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: abdominal myotomy, Heller myotomy open, esophagomyotomy

Esophagomyotomy with creation of esophageal opening via abdominal approach for treatment of achalasia or esophageal spasm. This divides the circular muscle layer.

Clinical Context

Open surgical approach to achalasia; provides durable relief of dysphagia and regurgitation; commonly combined with fundoplication.
