22810 Arthrd anterior dfrm 4-7 vrt segment

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Anterior spinal fusion 4-7 levels, Anterior multi-level fusion

Arthrodesis, anterior technique, spinal deformity correction, with bone graft or nonvascularized bone graft; 4-7 vertebral segments.

Clinical Context

Used for anterior column reconstruction in deformity correction when 4-7 vertebral segments require fusion via anterior approach.
