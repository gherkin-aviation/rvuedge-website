61458 Craniectomy sopl xpl/dcmpr crl nerve

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: cranial nerve decompression, microvascular decompression, MVD surgery

Craniotomy or craniectomy with exploration and/or decompression of cranial nerves in the skull base region, typically for microvascular compression.

In Plain Language

surgical opening to relieve nerve compression at skull base

Clinical Context

Performed for trigeminal neuralgia, hemifacial spasm, glossopharyngeal neuralgia, or other cranial nerve compression syndromes. Microvascular decompression separates offending vessel from nerve.
