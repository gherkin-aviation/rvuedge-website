27217 Treat pelvic ring fracture

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: open pelvic ring repair, pelvic ring ORIF

Treatment of pelvic ring fracture through operative open reduction with internal fixation, typically involving more extensive dissection than percutaneous techniques.

Clinical Context

Indicated for complex pelvic ring fractures with severe displacement, offering direct visualization and anatomic reduction.
