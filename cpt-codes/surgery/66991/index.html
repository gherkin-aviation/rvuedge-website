66991 Extracapsular cataract removal insj 1+

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ICCE with IOL, intracapsular cataract extraction with lens

Intracapsular cataract removal with implantation of one or more intraocular lens prostheses, where the entire lens including capsule is removed and replaced with artificial lens.

In Plain Language

full lens removal with artificial replacement; complete cataract surgery with lens implant

Clinical Context

Largely historical; used when zonules are compromised or in cases where intracapsular approach is beneficial; modern phacoemulsification has replaced most ICCE procedures.
