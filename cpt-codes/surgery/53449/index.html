53449 Repair uro sphincter

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: AUS repair, sphincter repair, AUS adjustment

Surgical repair or adjustment of a previously implanted artificial urinary sphincter to restore or improve continence mechanism function.

Clinical Context

Performed when artificial urinary sphincter develops leakage, insufficient closure, or mechanical dysfunction amenable to repair without complete replacement.
