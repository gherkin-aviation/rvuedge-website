67909 Revise eyelid defect

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: eyelid revision, eyelid revisionary surgery

Revision or secondary repair of previously surgically repaired eyelid defect to address complications or improve cosmetic outcome.

In Plain Language

fixing eyelid after earlier surgery; correcting eyelid repair

Clinical Context

Used to address complications from initial eyelid repair such as notching, entropion, ectropion, or poor cosmetic alignment. Common indications include eyelid margin notching, functional eyelid problems, or aesthetic concerns after primary repair.
