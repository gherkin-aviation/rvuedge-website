64708 Revise arm/leg nerve

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: arm/leg nerve repair, peripheral nerve revision extremity

Surgical revision or repair of a damaged peripheral nerve in the arm or leg region.

Clinical Context

Used for nerve injury, compression, or damage in arm or leg requiring surgical repair.
