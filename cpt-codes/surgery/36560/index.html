36560 Insert tunneled cv catheter

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: tunneled port catheter, portacath, port-a-cath, implanted port

Insertion of tunneled central venous catheter, with subcutaneous port; younger than 5 years of age.

In Plain Language

placing a central line with a port under the skin; implanted access port for young children

Clinical Context

Used for long-term access in pediatric patients, particularly with cancer requiring chemotherapy. Port provides durable, discreet access.
