25246 Injection for wrist x-ray

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: wrist arthrography, contrast injection wrist

Injection of contrast material into the wrist joint for radiographic imaging (arthrography) to visualize intra-articular structures.

Clinical Context

Performed for diagnostic imaging of wrist cartilage, ligaments, and other intra-articular structures. Increasingly replaced by MRI.
