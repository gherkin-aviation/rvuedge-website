67950 Revision of eyelid

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: eyelid revision, eyelid repositioning

Revision or correction of eyelid malposition using surgical technique to realign or reposition eyelid structure.

Clinical Context

Used for eyelid malposition or complications from previous eyelid surgery. Common indications include eyelid retraction, lagophthalmos, eyelid malpositioning after surgery, or cosmetic concerns.
