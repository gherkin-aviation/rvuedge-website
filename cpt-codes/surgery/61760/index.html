61760 Implant brain electrodes

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: electrode implantation, intracranial monitoring

Implantation of intracranial electrodes (grid, strip, depth) for seizure focus localization and functional mapping.

In Plain Language

Placing electrodes in brain for seizure mapping

Clinical Context

Performed in epilepsy surgery evaluation to localize seizure focus and identify eloquent cortex prior to resection.
