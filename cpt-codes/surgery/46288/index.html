46288 Repair anal fistula

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: fistula repair, advancement flap repair, fistula reconstruction

Repair of an anal fistula using reconstruction techniques. This typically involves closure of the internal opening with or without tissue flaps.

In Plain Language

repairing an anal fistula with tissue reconstruction; closing a fistula tunnel with surgical flap

Clinical Context

Used for primary or recurrent fistula repair using flap advancement or other tissue reconstruction. Preserves continence better than fistulotomy in high fistulas.
