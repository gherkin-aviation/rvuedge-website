28160 Partial removal of toe

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: distal toe amputation, toe with metatarsal head removal, toe partial ray excision

Partial removal of a toe including the metatarsal head. This procedure removes the distal portion of the toe with metatarsal head involvement.

Clinical Context

Performed for severe toe and metatarsal head pathology. More extensive than phalanx-only removal but less disabling than complete ray resection.
