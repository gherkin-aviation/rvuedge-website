65280 Repair of eye wound

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: corneoscleral wound repair staged, conjunctival advancement

Repair of full thickness corneal wound involving conjunctiva; without primary closure.

In Plain Language

repairing major corneal injury with staged healing; repairing large eye wound without immediate closure

Clinical Context

Complex full-thickness wounds with significant conjunctival involvement managed with delayed closure, tissue grafts, or healing by secondary intention. Often requires multiple procedures.
