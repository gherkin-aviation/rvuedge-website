22216 Incis additional spine segment

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: additional vertebral segment incision drainage, per additional level vertebral body drainage

Incision and drainage of vertebral body and epidural space, each additional segment. Each additional vertebra requiring incision and drainage of body and epidural space beyond the first.

Clinical Context

Used as add-on code for each additional vertebra requiring incision and drainage of body and epidural space in multilevel infections. Appended to primary vertebral body drainage codes.
