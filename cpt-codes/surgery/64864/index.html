64864 Repair of facial nerve

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: facial nerve repair, facial nerveraphy

Surgical repair of the facial nerve (Cranial Nerve VII) to restore facial motor function after injury or paralysis.

Clinical Context

Indicated for facial nerve injury from trauma, surgery, or Bell's palsy when spontaneous recovery unlikely. Repair should be performed acutely when possible for best outcome.
