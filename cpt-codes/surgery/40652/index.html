40652 repair lip fth<half ver height

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: lip repair less than half height, partial vermilion repair

Repair of laceration or defect of the lip involving less than half the vertical height of the vermilion border. Moderate lip involvement.

Clinical Context

Used for moderate lip lacerations or defects. More extensive than vermilion-only repair but less than complete lip height.
