23675 Cltx sho dislc neck fracture mnpj

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: dislocation with surgical neck fracture, dislocation neck fracture, shoulder dislocation with neck fracture

Closed treatment of shoulder dislocation with associated fracture of surgical neck. Reduction of shoulder dislocation concurrent with surgical neck fracture management.

Clinical Context

Closed management when anterior shoulder dislocation occurs with surgical neck fracture amenable to conservative treatment.
