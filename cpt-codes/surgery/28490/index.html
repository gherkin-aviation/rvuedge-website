28490 Treat big toe fracture

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: hallux fracture closed, big toe fracture non-operative, closed treatment

Closed treatment of hallux (great toe) fracture without manipulation, used for non-displaced or minimally displaced fractures managed with immobilization alone.

Clinical Context

Used for non-displaced great toe fractures suitable for conservative management with immobilization and protected ambulation.
