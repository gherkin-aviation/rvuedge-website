33820 Repair pda by ligation

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: PDA ligation, ductus ligation, PDA repair

Repair of patent ductus arteriosus by ligation; surgical closure of the patent ductus arteriosus (PDA) through simple ligation without division of the duct.

Clinical Context

Used to treat symptomatic patent ductus arteriosus, a common condition in premature infants where the fetal connection between the aorta and pulmonary artery fails to close, resulting in left-to-right shunting.
