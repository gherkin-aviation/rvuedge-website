43257 Egd with thrml txmnt gerd

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: EGD GERD treatment, radiofrequency ablation, RFA GERD

Esophagogastroduodenoscopy with thermal treatment of esophageal or gastric mucosa for gastroesophageal reflux disease. This therapeutic procedure applies radiofrequency energy.

Clinical Context

Minimally invasive treatment option for medically refractory GERD; provides symptom improvement in selected patients though durable benefit remains debated.
