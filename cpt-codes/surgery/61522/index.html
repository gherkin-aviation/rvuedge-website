61522 Removal of brain abscess

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: supratentorial brain abscess drainage, brain abscess removal

Craniotomy or craniectomy with drainage and/or removal of brain abscess from supratentorial location, with thorough debridement.

In Plain Language

surgical opening to treat brain infection

Clinical Context

Performed for supratentorial brain abscess with mass effect or failure of antibiotic therapy. Requires drainage, culture, and serial follow-up imaging to ensure adequate treatment.
