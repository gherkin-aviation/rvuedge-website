28046 Resection foot/toe tumor < 3 centimeters

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: foot tumor resection malignant, foot cancer removal, radical foot tumor excision

Radical resection (comprehensive removal) of a malignant tumor of the foot or toe with a diameter less than 3 centimeters. This procedure removes cancer with appropriate margins and depth.

Clinical Context

Performed for melanoma, sarcoma, carcinoma, or other malignancies of the foot. Requires wide margins and often full-thickness excision with possible skin grafting.
