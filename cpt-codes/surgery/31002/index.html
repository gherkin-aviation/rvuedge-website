31002 Irrigation sphenoid sinus

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: sphenoid irrigation, sphenoid sinus rinse, posterior sinus drainage

Irrigation of the sphenoid sinus through direct puncture or existing opening to remove secretions and treat sphenoid sinusitis.

In Plain Language

deep sinus washing procedure

Clinical Context

Used for sphenoid sinusitis with retained secretions. Sphenoid location makes this procedure technically challenging but necessary for posterior sinusitis management.
