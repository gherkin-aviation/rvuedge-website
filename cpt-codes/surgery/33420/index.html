33420 Revision of mitral valve

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: mitral valve revision, mitral valve repair open

Revision of mitral valve via open surgical approach, including repair or reconstruction.

Clinical Context

Used for mitral stenosis or regurgitation when repair can preserve valve function.
