38571 Laparoscopy lymphadenectomy

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: Laparoscopic lymphadenectomy

Laparoscopic lymph node dissection of abdominal and retroperitoneal lymph nodes via minimally invasive approach.

Clinical Context

Minimally invasive approach to lymph node removal for staging in gastric, colorectal, and other intra-abdominal malignancies.
