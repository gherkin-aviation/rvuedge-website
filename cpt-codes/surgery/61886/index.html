61886 Implant neurostim arrays

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: multi-array receiver, bilateral DBS receiver

Implantation or replacement of neurostimulation receiver with connections to multiple intracranial electrode arrays.

Clinical Context

Placement of receiver with capability to drive multiple electrode arrays (bilateral systems or complex configurations).
