23150 Removal of humerus lesion

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: humerus bone lesion excision, humeral cyst removal, bone tumor excision

Excision of benign lesion of humerus (proximal, shaft, or distal), bone. Distinct from 23140 by scope or depth of lesion removed. Basic removal of non-malignant humeral lesion.

Clinical Context

Standard approach for removal of benign bone lesions of the humerus causing pain, functional impairment, or fracture risk, including simple bone cysts and osteochondromas.
