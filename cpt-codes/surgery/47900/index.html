47900 Suture bile duct injury

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Bile duct injury repair, immediate duct repair, primary repair

Surgical repair of an acute bile duct injury by primary reanastomosis or reconstruction to restore biliary continuity and prevent stricture formation.

Clinical Context

Performed for intraoperative bile duct injury recognized during surgery. Primary repair at time of injury provides best functional outcome.
