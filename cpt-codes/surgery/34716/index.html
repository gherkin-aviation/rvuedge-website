34716 Opn ax/subcla artery expos cndt

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: open axillary/subclavian with conduit, axillary graft exposure

Open axillary or subclavian artery exposure with conduit insertion for catheter-based vascular procedures. Surgical exposure with graft placement for access.

Clinical Context

Used when percutaneous or simple open access to axillary/subclavian vessels requires graft material for safe closure.
