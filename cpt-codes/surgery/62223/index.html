62223 Establish brain cavity shunt

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: cervical drain placement, upper thoracic drain, cervical CSF drainage

Establishment of cerebrospinal fluid drainage system via cervical or upper thoracic subarachnoid space puncture. Alternative drainage location.

Clinical Context

Used for cerebrospinal fluid drainage when lumbar or cisternal approaches are contraindicated or inaccessible.
