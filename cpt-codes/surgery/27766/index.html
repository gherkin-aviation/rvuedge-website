27766 Optx medial ankle fracture

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: open reduction medial ankle, medial malleolus ORIF

Open treatment of medial ankle malleolus fracture with direct visualization and internal fixation.

Clinical Context

Performed for displaced medial malleolar fractures requiring open reduction and plate or screw fixation.
