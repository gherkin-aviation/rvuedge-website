28300 Incision of heel bone

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: calcaneal incision, heel bone surgery, calcaneal release

Surgical incision into the calcaneus (heel bone) for decompression, biopsy, or treatment of painful conditions such as calcaneal stress fractures, plantar fasciitis, or bone marrow edema.

Clinical Context

Used for calcaneal stress fractures, bone marrow edema, or other painful heel conditions not responsive to conservative treatment; provides pain relief through decompression.
