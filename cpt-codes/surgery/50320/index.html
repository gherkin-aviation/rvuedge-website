50320 Remove kidney living donor

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: living donor nephrectomy, donor nephrectomy, kidney donation surgery

Surgical removal and procurement of a kidney from a living donor for renal transplantation.

Clinical Context

Used for living related or unrelated kidney donation. Open approach versus laparoscopic approach depends on donor anatomy and surgeon preference.
