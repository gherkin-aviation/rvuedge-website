27170 Repair/graft femur head/neck

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: femoral head repair, head-neck graft, femoral head reconstruction

Repair or graft of the femur head and neck region, typically performed for avascular necrosis, large defects, or structural failure requiring reconstruction.

Clinical Context

Indicated for avascular necrosis of the femoral head, large osteochondral defects, or malunion following prior fracture, requiring graft incorporation for structural support.
