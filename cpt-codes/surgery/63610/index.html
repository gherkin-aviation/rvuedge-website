63610 Stimulation of spinal cord

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: spinal cord stimulator implant, SCS implant, neuromodulation electrode placement

Insertion or implantation of spinal cord stimulation electrode array. This procedure involves placing electrodes for neuromodulation to manage chronic pain through electrical stimulation.

Clinical Context

Used for chronic pain management in patients who have failed conservative treatment, including failed back surgery syndrome, complex regional pain syndrome, or neuropathic pain.
