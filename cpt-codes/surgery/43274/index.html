43274 Ercp duct stent placement

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: ERCP pancreatic stent, pancreatic duct stent placement, pancreatic ductal stenting

Endoscopic retrograde cholangiopancreatography with placement of a stent in the pancreatic duct. This therapeutic procedure maintains duct patency.

Clinical Context

Used for pancreatic duct strictures from chronic pancreatitis, traumatic injury, or post-sphincterotomy; improves ductal flow and reduces pain.
