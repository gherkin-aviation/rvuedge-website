25525 Optx rdl shft fracture & cltx radiation/ul

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ORIF radial shaft with reduction distal fracture, complex radial fracture repair

Open treatment (surgical repair) of radial shaft fracture combined with closed treatment (reduction) of associated distal radial and/or ulnar fracture.

Clinical Context

Used for complex fracture patterns involving radial shaft injury and associated distal radial or ulnar injury requiring surgical fixation of proximal component and closed management of distal component.
