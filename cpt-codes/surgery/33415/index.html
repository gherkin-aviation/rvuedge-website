33415 Revision subvalvular tissue

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: subvalvular tissue revision, myotomy, LV outflow tract widening

Revision of subvalvular tissue including ventricular myotomy or resection of hypertrophied muscle below aortic valve.

In Plain Language

removing or cutting heart muscle below aortic valve

Clinical Context

Used for hypertrophic cardiomyopathy or discrete subaortic stenosis.
