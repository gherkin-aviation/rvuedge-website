31529 Laryngoscopy and dilation

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: laryngeal dilation with fixation, dilation with spacer, laryngeal stenting

Laryngoscopic dilation and internal fixation or placement of spacer material within a narrowed larynx to maintain airway patency and prevent restenosis following dilation.

Clinical Context

Used for laryngeal stenosis with placement of temporary spacers or fixation devices to maintain airway patency while tissue remodeling occurs during healing.
