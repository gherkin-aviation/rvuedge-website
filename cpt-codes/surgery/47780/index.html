47780 Fuse bile ducts and bowel

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Hepaticoduodenostomy revision, redo hepatic anastomosis

Surgical creation of an internal anastomosis between the intrahepatic bile ducts and duodenum with revision of previous hepaticoenterostomy for failed or obstructed anastomosis.

In Plain Language

Fixing a previous bile duct connection to intestine

Clinical Context

Performed for failed or complicated hepaticoduodenostomy with recurrent cholangitis. Often technically demanding with altered anatomy.
