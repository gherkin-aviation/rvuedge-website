47300 Surgery for liver lesion

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: liver lesion excision, hepatic mass resection, lesion ablation

Surgery for liver lesion or mass. Excision or ablation of hepatic lesion with surrounding normal liver.

In Plain Language

surgical removal of a liver tumor or lesion; liver lesion treatment

Clinical Context

Performed for hepatocellular carcinoma, adenomas, metastases, or other hepatic lesions. Open or laparoscopic approach.
