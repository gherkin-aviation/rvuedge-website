15017 application skn cll ssp f/n/g/hf first

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: cell spray face/neck, specialized area spray graft

Application of autologous cultured skin cells via spray technique to face, neck, genitals, hands, or feet, first increment. Spray application to specialized body areas.

In Plain Language

spraying cells on face or sensitive areas; cell spray to specialized regions

Clinical Context

Used for facial burns, facial wounds, genital reconstruction, and hand/foot injuries where specialized cosmetic and functional outcomes are critical.

RVU Information
