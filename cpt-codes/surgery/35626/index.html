35626 Bpg aor-subcla/innom/carotid

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: aortic arch bypass, aortocervical bypass, aorta-to-branch bypass

Arterial bypass graft from thoracic aorta to subclavian, innominate, or carotid artery for repair of aortic arch disease or multiple branch vessel involvement. Restores cerebral and upper extremity perfusion.

In Plain Language

main heart artery to neck vessel bypass; rerouting blood from the main aorta to neck arteries

Clinical Context

Indicated for aortic arch aneurysm, aortic dissection with branch involvement, aortic atherosclerotic disease, or chronic aortic occlusion. Requires cardiopulmonary bypass consideration.
