21740 Reconstruction of sternum

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: sternal reconstruction, sternal repair reconstruction

Reconstruction of sternum. Surgical restoration of sternal anatomy and function using various techniques including osteoplastic flaps, prosthetic materials, or soft tissue flap coverage.

In Plain Language

breastbone reconstruction surgery; sternal repair with reconstruction

Clinical Context

Used for restoration of sternal defects from trauma, infection, or resection. Reconstruction restores chest wall integrity and provides soft tissue coverage over underlying mediastinal structures.
