27601 Decompression of lower leg

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: lower leg fasciotomy, selective decompression

Surgical decompression of the lower leg with limited fasciotomy of specific compartments for compartment syndrome or pressure relief.

In Plain Language

releasing pressure in specific areas of the lower leg

Clinical Context

Used for compartment syndrome affecting select compartments in the lower leg. Less extensive than full four-compartment fasciotomy.
