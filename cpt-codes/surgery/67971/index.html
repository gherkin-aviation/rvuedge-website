67971 Reconstruction of eyelid

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: eyelid flap reconstruction, eyelid reconstruction

Reconstruction of eyelid using free or rotational flap technique to reconstruct significant eyelid tissue loss.

Clinical Context

Used for extensive eyelid defects requiring flap reconstruction. Common indications include large post-mohs defects, cancer resection defects, burn injuries, or traumatic eyelid loss.
