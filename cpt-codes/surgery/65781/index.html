65781 Ocular reconst transplant

Surgery +Add-on Global 90d

Also known as: additional cicatricial pemphigoid repair, ocular surface extension

Ocular surface reconstruction for cicatricial pemphigoid; amniotic membrane; each additional 100 square millimeters or less.

Clinical Context

Additional amniotic membrane placement for larger areas of cicatricial pemphigoid. Staged approach managing severe ocular surface disease.
