15740 Island pedicle flap graft

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: island pedicle flap, island flap

Island pedicle flap graft with preserved vascular attachment. Flap technique where tissue maintains blood supply through narrow pedicle attachment.

Clinical Context

Used for complex reconstruction where island pedicle flaps provide mobility and vascular integrity for defects in hands, face, and trunk.
