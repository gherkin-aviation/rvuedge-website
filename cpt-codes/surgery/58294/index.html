58294 Vag hyst with enterocele complicated

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: complex VH with BSO and enterocele repair, complicated vaginal hysterectomy with enterocele

Complex vaginal hysterectomy with removal of tubes and ovaries plus enterocele repair addresses uterine pathology with significant pelvic floor defects.

Clinical Context

Combines complex vaginal hysterectomy with salpingo-oophorectomy while addressing severe enterocele and pelvic relaxation.
