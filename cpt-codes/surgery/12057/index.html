12057 Intmd repair face/millimeters >30.0 centimeters

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: Intermediate facial laceration repair, Major layered facial wound closure

Repair of intermediate complexity laceration on the face or mucous membrane measuring greater than thirty centimeters.

In Plain Language

Multi-layer stitching of extensive facial cut; Intermediate facial wound closure for large wounds

Clinical Context

Used for extensive facial lacerations requiring careful layered closure.
