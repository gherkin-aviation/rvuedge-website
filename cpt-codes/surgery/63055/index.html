63055 Decompress spinal cord thrc

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: thoracic decompression, thoracic laminectomy, spinal cord decompression

Decompression of the spinal cord in the thoracic region. This procedure relieves compression of the thoracic spinal cord from bone, disc, or soft tissue, typically addressing myelopathic symptoms.

Clinical Context

Used for thoracic myelopathy from various etiologies including stenosis, disc herniation, or ossification of posterior longitudinal ligament, common in Asian populations.
