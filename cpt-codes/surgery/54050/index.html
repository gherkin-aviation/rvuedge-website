54050 Destruction penis lesion(s)

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: penile lesion destruction, wart destruction

Destruction of benign or malignant lesions of the penis using chemical cautery or other destructive technique.

Clinical Context

Used for condyloma acuminata, molluscum contagiosum, or other lesions. Suitable for multiple lesions of varying types.
