27550 Treat knee dislocation

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: knee dislocation reduction, closed reduction

Closed reduction and treatment of a knee joint dislocation by manual reduction and immobilization to restore anatomic alignment.

Clinical Context

Used for acute knee dislocation requiring emergency reduction, often with imaging to assess for associated injuries.
