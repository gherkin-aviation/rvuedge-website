63744 Revision of spinal shunt

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: spinal shunt revision, shunt adjustment, shunt repositioning

Revision of spinal shunt system. This procedure involves modification, repositioning, or repair of an existing spinal shunt.

In Plain Language

adjustment or repair of spinal drainage tube

Clinical Context

Used for malfunction, obstruction, or inadequate function of an existing spinal shunt requiring revision.
