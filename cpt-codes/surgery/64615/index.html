64615 Chemodenerv muscle migraine

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: migraine chemodenervation, botulinum toxin migraine, chronic migraine injection

Chemodenervation of muscles involved in migraine pain pathways for treatment of chronic migraine.

Clinical Context

Used for chronic migraine prevention. Targets periosteal and meningeal nociceptors.
