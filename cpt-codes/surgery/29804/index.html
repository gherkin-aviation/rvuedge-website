29804 Jaw arthroscopy/surgery

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: TMJ surgery, jaw arthroscopic surgery, mandibular joint surgery

Arthroscopy of the temporomandibular joint with surgical intervention including procedures such as meniscal repair, discectomy, or lysis of adhesions.

In Plain Language

jaw joint surgery with camera; operating on jaw joint

Clinical Context

Used for surgical correction of temporomandibular joint meniscal tears, internal derangement, and intra-articular pathology.
