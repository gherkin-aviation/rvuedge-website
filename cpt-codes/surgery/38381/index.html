38381 Thoracic duct procedure

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Thoracic duct ligation extensive

Suture ligation of the thoracic duct with more extensive dissection and control of tributaries.

Clinical Context

Used for significant chyle leaks requiring identification and ligation of multiple thoracic duct branches.
