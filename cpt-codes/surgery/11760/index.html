11760 Repair of nail bed

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: nail bed repair, nail bed suture

Repair of nail bed. Surgical repair of damage to nail bed matrix following trauma, infection, or surgical removal.

Clinical Context

Used for restoration of nail bed following trauma, surgical excision, or infection to promote normal nail growth.
