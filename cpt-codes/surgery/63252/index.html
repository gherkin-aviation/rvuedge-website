63252 Revise spine cord vsl thrlmb

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: thoracolumbar spinal AVM repair, thoracolumbar vascular repair

Revision or repair of spinal cord vascular lesions in the thoracolumbar region. This procedure manages vascular abnormalities affecting the spinal cord at the thoracolumbar junction.

Clinical Context

Used for spinal dural arteriovenous fistulas or malformations at the thoracolumbar junction causing progressive myelopathy.
