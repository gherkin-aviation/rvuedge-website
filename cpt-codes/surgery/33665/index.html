Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: complex congenital repair, multiple procedure cardiac repair, comprehensive heart repair

Surgical repair of congenital heart defects with increased complexity or multiple component procedures.

Clinical Context

Utilized for more complex congenital lesions requiring multiple repair techniques or addressing associated anomalies simultaneously.
