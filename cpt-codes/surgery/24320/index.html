24320 Tenoplasty elbow to sho 1

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: tenoplasty, tendon repair, tendon reconstruction

Tenoplasty (repair or reconstruction of tendon) from elbow to shoulder, primary tendon. Surgical repair or reconstruction of damaged or degenerative tendon.

In Plain Language

Surgical repair of a damaged tendon in the arm; Reconstruction of torn or worn tendon

Clinical Context

Used for repair of chronic or degenerative tendon conditions of the arm, including tendon transfers or reconstruction after injury.
