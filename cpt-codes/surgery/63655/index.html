63655 Implant neuroelectrodes

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: spinal electrode implant with plate, neuroelectrode array with fixation

Implantation or replacement of spinal neuroelectrode array(s) with comprehensive lead management. This procedure involves more complex electrode placement with additional fixation or positioning measures.

Clinical Context

Used when spinal cord stimulation electrode placement requires more complex lead management or stabilization.
