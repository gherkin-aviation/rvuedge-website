67036 Removal of inner eye fluid

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: pars plana vitrectomy, PPV, core vitrectomy, complete vitrectomy

Complete removal of vitreous humor through three-port pars plana vitrectomy approach with wide-angle visualization and comprehensive removal of vitreous gel and membranes.

In Plain Language

complete removal of gel from back of eye; surgical removal of vitreous fluid

Clinical Context

Indicated for retinal detachment repair, severe vitreous hemorrhage, proliferative diabetic retinopathy, epiretinal membrane, macular hole, endophthalmitis.
