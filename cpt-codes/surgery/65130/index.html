65130 Insert ocular implant

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: orbital implant insertion primary

Surgical insertion of an orbital implant during a primary procedure for orbital volume maintenance and to support prosthetic eye fitting.

Clinical Context

Performed at time of initial enucleation or evisceration to maintain orbital volume. Improves esthetic and functional results with prosthetic eye.
