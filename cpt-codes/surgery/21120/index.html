21120 Genioplasty augmentation

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: genioplasty, chin augmentation, chin implant

Genioplasty augmentation involving bone or soft tissue augmentation to the chin to increase projection or improve facial contour.

In Plain Language

Chin enhancement surgery; Making your chin larger or more projecting; Chin reshaping with implant

Clinical Context

Used for microgenia, aesthetic improvement, or functional speech/swallowing issues. May involve implant placement or osteoplasty.
