15274 Skn sub graft t/a/l child add

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: pediatric skin substitute additional, child bioengineered add-on

Skin substitute graft applied to trunk, arms, or legs in pediatric patients, each additional application or area. Add-on code for extended pediatric synthetic graft coverage.

Clinical Context

Used for large pediatric burns requiring extended skin substitute coverage.
