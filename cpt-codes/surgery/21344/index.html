21344 Open treatment complicated front sinus fracture

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: complex frontal sinus fracture, comminuted sinus fracture

Open treatment of complex frontal sinus fracture involving significant comminution, posterior table involvement, or nasofrontal duct injury.

Clinical Context

Used for complicated frontal sinus fractures potentially involving dural laceration, posterior table, or nasofrontal duct.
