36571 Insert picvad catheter

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: PICC-with-port, portacath PICC, peripheral port catheter

Insertion of peripherally inserted central catheter (PICC), with subcutaneous port; 5 years of age or older.

Clinical Context

Used for extended vascular access in pediatric and adult patients, combining PICC convenience with port durability.
