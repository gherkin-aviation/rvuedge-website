58150 Total hysterectomy

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: total abdominal hysterectomy, TAH, abdominal hysterectomy

Total hysterectomy via abdominal approach involves removal of the uterus and cervix through an open abdominal incision without removing ovaries.

Clinical Context

Standard approach for hysterectomy when vaginal approach not feasible. Indicated for fibroids, cancer, prolapse, or dysfunctional uterine bleeding. Ovaries typically retained in premenopausal women.
