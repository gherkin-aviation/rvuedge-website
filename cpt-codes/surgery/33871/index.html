33871 Transverse a-arch grf hypthrm

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Arch graft HCA, transverse arch repair, full arch replacement

Transverse aortic arch graft with hypothermic circulatory arrest; surgical replacement of the transverse aortic arch with a prosthetic graft under conditions of hypothermia and circulatory arrest for operative visibility and neuroprotection.

In Plain Language

Full aorta arch replacement with cooling technique; Aorta arch surgery with circulatory arrest

Clinical Context

Used for extensive transverse and descending aortic arch pathology including aneurysm and dissection. Hypothermic circulatory arrest provides cerebral protection and excellent operative visualization for branch vessel anastomoses.
