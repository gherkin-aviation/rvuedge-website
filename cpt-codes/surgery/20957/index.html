20957 Mt bone graft microvasc

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: metatarsal free flap, metatarsal bone graft

Microvascular free bone graft using metatarsal bone with vascular anastomosis.

Clinical Context

Provides smaller corticocancellous bone grafts with excellent vascularity for specific reconstruction needs.
