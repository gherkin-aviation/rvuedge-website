44322 Colostomy with biopsies

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: colostomy with biopsy, colostomy and biopsy

Open creation of colostomy with concurrent tissue sampling via punch or excisional biopsy for histopathologic examination of intestinal tissue.

Clinical Context

Used when colostomy creation is performed concurrently with need for intestinal biopsy to confirm diagnosis of inflammatory bowel disease, dysplasia, or malignancy.
