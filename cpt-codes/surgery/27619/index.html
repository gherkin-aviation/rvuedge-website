27619 excision leg/ankle tumor deep <5 centimeters

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: deep lesion excision, soft tissue excision deep <5 cm

Excision of deep soft tissue lesion or tumor less than 5 centimeters in the lower leg or ankle, requiring deeper tissue plane dissection.

Clinical Context

Performed for deeper-seated lesions in muscle, fascia, or within compartments. Requires more extensive dissection than superficial excision.
