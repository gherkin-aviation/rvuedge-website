26502 Hand tendon reconstruction

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Complex hand reconstruction, Extensive hand reconstruction, Advanced reconstruction

Surgical reconstruction of hand tendons with more extensive or complex tissue rearrangement, potentially including multiple transfers, grafts, or advanced reconstructive techniques.

In Plain Language

Complex surgery to rebuild hand structures; Extensive reconstruction for severe hand dysfunction

Clinical Context

Indicated for severe hand dysfunction requiring multiple procedures, extensive tissue rearrangement, or advanced reconstructive techniques for functional restoration.

Billing & Documentation

As a surgical CPT code, proper documentation must include the operative report detailing the procedure performed, patient positioning, approach, findings, and any complications. This code has a 90-day global period, which includes the day of the procedure, 1 day preoperative, and 90 days of postoperative care.
