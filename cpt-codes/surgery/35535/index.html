35535 Artery byp graft hepatorenal

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: hepatorenal bypass, aorto-hepatorenal

Arterial bypass graft from aorta to hepatic and renal artery with restoration of liver and kidney blood flow.

Clinical Context

Complex visceral reconstruction for combined hepatic and renal artery stenosis causing mesenteric ischemia and renovascular hypertension. Specialized high-complexity procedure.
