61751 Brain biopsy with CT/mr guide

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: stereotactic biopsy, CT-guided biopsy

Stereotactic brain biopsy under computed tomography or magnetic resonance imaging guidance via needle or probe.

Clinical Context

Minimally invasive diagnostic procedure with localization accuracy for deep or difficult-to-access lesions.
