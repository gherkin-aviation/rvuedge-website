27605 Incision of achilles tendon

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: Achilles tenotomy, Achilles lengthening, Achilles incision

Surgical incision of the Achilles tendon including tenotomy or lengthening for correction of contracture or posterior ankle equinus deformity.

In Plain Language

cutting or lengthening the Achilles tendon

Clinical Context

Used for Achilles contracture, equinus deformity, toe-walking gait, or spasticity. Common in cerebral palsy and neurologic conditions.
