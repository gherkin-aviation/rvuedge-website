49446 Change g-tube to g-j percutaneous

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: G-tube to G-J conversion, gastrojejunal conversion, tube repositioning

Percutaneous conversion of gastrostomy tube to gastrojejunal tube by changing the position or configuration.

In Plain Language

changing stomach tube to combined stomach-bowel tube; conversion of feeding tube placement

Clinical Context

Percutaneous conversion of existing gastrostomy tube to gastrojejunal configuration. Used when gastric feeding becomes intolerant or aspiration risk is high.
