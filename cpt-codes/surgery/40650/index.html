40650 repair lip fth vermilion only

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: lip vermilion repair, lip margin repair

Repair of laceration or defect of the lip limited to the vermilion border only (the red visible lip margin), without full-thickness involvement.

Clinical Context

Used for minor lip injuries or partial-thickness defects affecting only the vermilion portion. Less complex than full-thickness repairs.
