22512 Vertebroplasty additional inject

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: Vertebroplasty additional injection, Cement augmentation add-on

Percutaneous injection of contrast and/or therapeutic substance for vertebroplasty (bone cement injection), each additional vertebral segment beyond the first.

Clinical Context

Used as an add-on code when vertebroplasty is performed on multiple vertebrae; increases reimbursement based on the number of additional vertebral bodies treated.
