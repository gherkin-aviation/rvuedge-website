32151 Remove lung foreign body

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: lung foreign body removal, FB removal from lung

Surgical removal of a foreign body from the lung parenchyma or pleural space, performed to extract aspirated or inhaled materials, bullets, or other extraneous objects.

Clinical Context

Used for aspiration of foreign objects, food particles, tooth fragments, prosthetic components, or traumatic penetrating injuries with embedded objects.
