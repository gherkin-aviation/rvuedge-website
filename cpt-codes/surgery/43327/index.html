43327 Esophageal fundoplasty laparoscopic

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: lap fundoplication with gastroplasty, hybrid fundoplication, reinforced fundoplication

Laparoscopic fundoplication with gastroplasty for treatment of gastroesophageal reflux disease and esophageal dysmotility. This minimally invasive procedure provides enhanced support.

Clinical Context

Modified approach combining fundoplication with gastroplasty for patients with baseline dysmotility; may improve outcomes in selected populations.
