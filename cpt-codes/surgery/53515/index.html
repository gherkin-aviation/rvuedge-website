53515 Repair of urethra injury

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: mobilization urethroplasty, augmented urethroplasty

Surgical repair of a urethral stricture using a tissue mobilization approach with or without graft material to widen the urethra.

Clinical Context

Indicated for strictures in areas where transection risks erectile dysfunction or loss of sensation. Allows tissue rearrangement without division.
