65410 Biopsy of cornea

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: corneal biopsy, keratobiopsy

Biopsy of cornea.

Clinical Context

Diagnostic biopsy for suspected corneal dystrophy, infection, or malignancy. Requires careful technique to avoid scarring of visual axis.
