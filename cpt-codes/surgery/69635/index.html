69635 Repair eardrum structures

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ossicular replacement prosthesis, ORP placement, prosthetic ossiculoplasty

Ossicular chain reconstruction with ossicular replacement prosthesis (ORP) that bridges from tympanic membrane to oval window or footplate.

In Plain Language

using a prosthetic device to rebuild ear bones

Clinical Context

Used when insufficient bone/cartilage autograft available or revision cases. Maintains sound transmission despite absent ossicles.
