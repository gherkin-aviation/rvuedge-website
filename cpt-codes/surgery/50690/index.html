50690 Injection for ureter x-ray

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: percutaneous ureterography, retrograde ureterography, antegrade study

Percutaneous injection of contrast under fluoroscopic guidance for ureterography (radiographic imaging of the ureter) with visualization.

Clinical Context

Used for diagnostic imaging of the ureter and characterization of obstruction.
