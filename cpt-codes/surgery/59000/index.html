59000 Amniocentesis diagnostic

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: diagnostic amniocentesis, genetic amniocentesis, amnio

Amniocentesis for diagnostic purposes, including amniotic fluid sampling for fetal karyotyping, genetic testing, or assessment of fetal lung maturity.

Clinical Context

Performed primarily for Down syndrome (Trisomy 21) and Neural Tube Defect screening when Quadruple Marker screening is abnormal or maternal age considerations apply.
