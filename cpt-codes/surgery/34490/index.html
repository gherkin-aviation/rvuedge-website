34490 Removal of vein clot

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: axillary vein thrombectomy, upper extremity thrombectomy

Removal of a venous thrombus or embolus from the axillary vein. Surgical extraction of blood clot from the axillary vein.

Clinical Context

Used for acute axillary vein thrombosis, often secondary to thoracic outlet syndrome or catheter placement. Prevents upper extremity complications.
