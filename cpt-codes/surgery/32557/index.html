32557 Insert catheter pleura with image

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: image-guided catheter insertion, ultrasound-guided catheter

Percutaneous insertion of a catheter into the pleural space with real-time ultrasound or imaging guidance, ensuring accurate placement and minimizing complications.

In Plain Language

ultrasound-guided drainage tube placement

Clinical Context

Used for pleural drainage requiring catheter placement when imaging guidance improves accuracy and safety, particularly for loculated effusions.
