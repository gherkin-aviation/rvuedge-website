15835 excision excessive skin buttock

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: buttock exc, gluteal skin removal

Excision of excessive skin and subcutaneous tissue from the buttock region, addressing redundant tissue commonly seen after massive weight loss.

Clinical Context

Common in post-bariatric body contouring when the buttocks have significant skin redundancy. Often combined with other lower body excisions.
