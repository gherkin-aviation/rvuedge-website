50727 Revise ureter

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ureteral revision, ureteral reconstruction, ureteroureterostomy

Revision of ureter, reconstructing the ureter to restore or improve urine flow, often performed as a definitive treatment for stricture or obstruction.

Clinical Context

Performed for symptomatic ureteral strictures, including those from stone passage, prior surgery, or radiation. Lower work RVU than open cases.
