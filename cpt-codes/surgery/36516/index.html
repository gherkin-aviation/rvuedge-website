36516 Apheresis immunoads slctv

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: immunoadsorption, selective apheresis, immunoabsorption

Apheresis, therapeutic, with selective adsorption and plasma reinfusion, immunoadsorption.

Clinical Context

Used in autoimmune conditions including anti-GBM disease, certain cases of transplant rejection, and immune thrombocytopenia.
