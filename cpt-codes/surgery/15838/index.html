15838 excision excsv submental fat pad

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: submental liposuction, submental exc, neck lift, double chin removal

Excision of excessive submental fat pad and skin, addressing excess tissue in the submandibular and neck region beneath the chin.

Clinical Context

Performed for cosmetic improvement of submental fullness or for patients with significant loose skin in the neck-chin area after weight loss. May be combined with liposuction.
