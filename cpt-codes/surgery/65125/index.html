65125 Revise ocular implant

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: orbital implant revision, prosthesis revision

Surgical revision of a previously placed ocular implant to correct positioning, size mismatch, or other complications affecting function or esthetics.

In Plain Language

fixing artificial eye implant

Clinical Context

Performed months or years after initial enucleation when implant complications develop. Lower relative value than initial implant insertion.
