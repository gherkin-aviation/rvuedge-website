21390 Opn treatment orbit periorbtl implant

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: orbital fracture with implant, orbitofacial reconstruction

Open treatment of orbital fracture with periorbital implant insertion to support and reconstruct the orbital wall.

Clinical Context

Used for orbital fractures with significant wall defects requiring implant placement for structural support.
