36819 Av fuse uppr arm basilic

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: basilic AVF, basilic transposition, upper arm fistula, basilic transposition

Arteriovenous fistula, direct, upper arm; basilic vein transposition.

Clinical Context

Used for dialysis access when cephalic vein inadequate. Basilic vein transposition provides good flow and cannulation.
