21127 Augmentation mndblr bone grf

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: mandibular bone graft, jaw bone graft, chin graft

Augmentation of the mandible using bone graft material placed as an onlay to enhance mandibular projection or volume.

Clinical Context

Used for mandibular deficiency, post-tumor resection, or aesthetic enhancement. Bone integrates with host mandible.
