35693 Artery trnsposj subclavian

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: subclavian transposition, subclavian artery relocation, SC transposition

Arterial transposition of subclavian artery (origin relocation) for proximal subclavian stenosis or occlusion with arm ischemia or vertebral steal. Restores subclavian perfusion.

In Plain Language

moving the subclavian artery to improve blood flow; relocating the shoulder artery origin

Clinical Context

Standard treatment for symptomatic subclavian artery origin disease. Avoids need for prosthetic graft.
