40831 Repair mouth laceration

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: lip laceration repair, lip wound closure, lip suturing

Repair of lacerations of the lip involving muscle and mucosa with primary suturing technique.

Clinical Context

Used for traumatic lacerations of the lip that extend through the mucosa and muscle layers requiring surgical closure. Common after blunt facial trauma, accidents, or interpersonal violence.
