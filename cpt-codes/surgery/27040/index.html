27040 Biopsy of soft tissues

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: hip soft tissue biopsy, pelvic soft tissue biopsy, needle biopsy hip

Surgical biopsy of soft tissues around the hip and pelvis through a small incision with minimal tissue removal.

In Plain Language

taking sample from hip muscle or tissue; hip tissue sample

Clinical Context

Indicated for diagnosis of soft tissue lesions, infections, or masses around the hip requiring tissue analysis for pathologic diagnosis.
