33615 Repair modified fontan

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Fontan revision, Fontan repair, TCPC revision

Surgical repair of a modified Fontan procedure, creating a direct connection between the inferior vena cava and the pulmonary artery.

Clinical Context

Performed for functional single ventricle physiology to improve pulmonary blood flow; modified Fontan includes fenestration or other modifications to optimize hemodynamics.
