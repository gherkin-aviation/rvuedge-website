26490 Revise thumb tendon

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Thumb tendon revision, Thumb tendon re-repair, Thumb tendon reconstruction

Surgical revision, re-repair, or reconstruction of thumb tendons, performed when prior tendon surgery has failed, or to optimize thumb function through improved tendon positioning or transfer.

In Plain Language

Fixing a thumb tendon that needs repair or improvement; Revision surgery to improve thumb tendon function

Clinical Context

Indicated for failed thumb tendon repairs, poor functional outcome from prior surgery, or when revision for improved opposition or pinch is needed.
