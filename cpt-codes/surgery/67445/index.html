67445 Explr/decompress eye socket

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: extensive orbital decompression, complex orbital management

Exploration, decompression, and tissue removal from orbit through extensive approach to address compartment syndrome, infection, or mass with significant anatomic disruption.

Clinical Context

Complex cases combining exploration, decompression, and removal of affected tissue; typically performed emergently for compartment syndrome.
