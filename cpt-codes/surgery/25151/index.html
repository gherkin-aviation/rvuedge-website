25151 Partial removal of radius

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: partial radial resection, radial reconstruction

Partial removal (segmental resection) of the radius bone with reconstruction to maintain forearm length and radioulnar joint mechanics.

In Plain Language

removing part of radius bone

Clinical Context

Indicated for radial head excision with intercalated defect, radial shaft pathology, or tumor requiring segmental resection.
