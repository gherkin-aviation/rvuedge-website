38200 Injection for spleen x-ray

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: Splenic arteriography, Splenic injection

Injection procedure for imaging the spleen with radiographic contrast material, typically used for diagnostic evaluation.

Clinical Context

Used to assess splenic vascularity, identify abnormalities, or evaluate for splenic infarction before intervention.
