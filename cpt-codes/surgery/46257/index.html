46257 Remove in/ex hem group & fiss

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: hem removal with fissure, hemorrhoidectomy with fissurectomy, combined treatment

Surgical removal of internal and external hemorrhoid groups with concurrent anal fissure treatment. Addresses both hemorrhoid disease and concurrent fissure pathology.

Clinical Context

Performed when hemorrhoids and anal fissures coexist, often with fissurectomy or sphincterotomy included. Common presentation in chronic hemorrhoid disease.
