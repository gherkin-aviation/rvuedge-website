34834 Opn brach artery expos

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: open brachial exposure, brachial cutdown, brachial artery exposure

Open brachial artery exposure for vascular procedures. Surgical access and exposure of brachial artery.

Clinical Context

Used for surgical access to brachial artery for AV fistula creation, repair, or other vascular interventions.
