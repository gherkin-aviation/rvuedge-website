19120 Removal of breast lesion

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: breast lesion removal, breast excision, lumpectomy, breast biopsy open, open breast biopsy, excisional breast biopsy

Excision of breast lesion or mass including local or circumscribed excision with clear margins for pathological evaluation.

Clinical Context

Performed for diagnosis or treatment of breast masses. Includes benign lesions and small malignancies. Complete excision with margins obtained.
