40819 Excise lip or cheek fold

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: lip fold excision, buccal fold excision

Excision of lip or cheek fold. Removal of redundant tissue creating folds or restricting function.

In Plain Language

removing extra tissue from lip or cheek

Clinical Context

Used for redundant tissue causing functional impairment (denture retention, eating) or cosmetic concerns.
