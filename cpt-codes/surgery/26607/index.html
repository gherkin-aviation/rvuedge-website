26607 Treat metacarpal fracture

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Metacarpal fracture open treatment, Open reduction, Surgical realignment

Open treatment of a metacarpal bone fracture without internal fixation, using operative intervention to realign the fracture with external immobilization or other non-fixation methods.

Clinical Context

Indicated for metacarpal fractures requiring surgical exposure and realignment but not requiring internal fixation devices.
