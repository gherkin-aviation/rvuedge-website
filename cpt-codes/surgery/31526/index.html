31526 Diagnosis laryngoscopy with oper scope

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: microscopic laryngoscopy, operating scope laryngoscopy, micro laryngoscopy

Diagnostic laryngoscopic examination using an operating microscope for enhanced visualization and magnification of the larynx and vocal cords, allowing detailed assessment of laryngeal pathology.

In Plain Language

examining voice box with magnifying microscope

Clinical Context

Preferred for evaluation of small lesions, vocal cord mobility assessment, and detailed visualization of laryngeal pathology. Commonly used in voice and laryngology subspecialty practice.
