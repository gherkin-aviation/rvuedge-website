27076 Resection hip tumor incl acetabul

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: hip tumor resection with acetabulum, acetabular tumor resection, extensive hip tumor removal

Surgical resection of hip tumor including acetabular involvement with extensive reconstruction.

Clinical Context

Indicated for hip tumors involving the acetabulum requiring wide resection including the hip socket with potential reconstruction.
