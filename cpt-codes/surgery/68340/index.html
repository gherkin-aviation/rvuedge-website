68340 Separate eyelid adhesions

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: symblepharon separation, adhesion lysis conjunctiva

Separation of conjunctival adhesions (symblepharon) using surgical separation and tissue prevention techniques.

Clinical Context

Used for treatment of symblepharon or conjunctival adhesions. Common indications include Stevens-Johnson syndrome, ocular cicatricial pemphigoid, chemical injuries, or post-surgical adhesions.
