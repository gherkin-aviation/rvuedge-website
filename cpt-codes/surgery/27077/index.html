27077 Resection hip tumor with innom bone

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: hip tumor resection with innominate bone, pelvic tumor resection, extensive pelvic tumor removal

Surgical resection of hip tumor including innominate bone with extensive pelvic reconstruction.

Clinical Context

Used for extensive hip tumors involving the innominate bone requiring major surgical resection and pelvic reconstruction.
