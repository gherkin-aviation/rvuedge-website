26706 Pin knuckle dislocation

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: knuckle dislocation with pinning, percutaneous pin fixation, finger joint pinning

Surgical reduction and treatment of metacarpophalangeal or proximal interphalangeal joint dislocation of the finger with percutaneous pinning for stabilization.

In Plain Language

fixing dislocated knuckle with pins; finger joint stabilization

Clinical Context

Employed for finger dislocations requiring percutaneous or minimal invasive fixation to maintain reduction and prevent redislocation during healing.
