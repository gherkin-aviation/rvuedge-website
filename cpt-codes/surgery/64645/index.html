64645 Chemodenerv 1 extremity 5/> each

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: extremity chemodenervation additional extensive groups

Chemodenervation of one extremity for each additional group of five or more muscles beyond the first.

Clinical Context

Used when additional muscle groups of five or more are treated in one extremity.
