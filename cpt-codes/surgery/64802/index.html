64802 Sympathectomy cervical

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: cervical sympathectomy, superior cervical ganglionectomy

Surgical sectioning of sympathetic nerve fibers in the cervical region to treat vascular insufficiency, pain, or other sympathetic dysfunction of the upper extremity.

Clinical Context

Indicated for severe Raynaud's phenomenon, complex regional pain syndrome (CRPS), or vascular insufficiency unresponsive to conservative treatment. Reduces sympathetic tone to improve blood flow.
