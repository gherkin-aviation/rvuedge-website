31536 Laryngoscopy with biopsy & outpatient scope

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: microscopic laryngeal biopsy, operating scope laryngeal biopsy, micro biopsy

Laryngoscopic removal of tissue (biopsy) from the larynx using an operating microscope for magnified visualization, allowing precise sampling of small or subtle laryngeal lesions.

In Plain Language

taking tissue sample with magnifying scope

Clinical Context

Preferred for small, subtle, or difficult-to-visualize lesions requiring magnified visualization for accurate tissue sampling and diagnosis.
