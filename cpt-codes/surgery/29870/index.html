29870 Arthrs knee diagnosis with wo syn biopsy

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: diagnostic knee arthroscopy, diagnostic knee scope, knee arthroscopy with biopsy

Arthroscopy of the knee with diagnostic evaluation, with or without synovial biopsy for tissue examination.

Clinical Context

Used for knee pain evaluation, inflammatory arthropathy assessment, and diagnostic tissue sampling.
