24802 Arthrd elbow joint autog graft

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: elbow arthrodesis with autograft, elbow fusion with bone graft

Arthrodesis of the elbow joint with autogenous graft. Surgical fusion of the elbow using patient's own bone graft.

Clinical Context

Used for elbow arthrodesis when additional bone graft augmentation is necessary for successful fusion.
