43265 Ercp lithotripsy calculi

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: ERCP lithotripsy, pancreatic lithotripsy, biliary stone fragmentation

Endoscopic retrograde cholangiopancreatography with extracorporeal shock wave lithotripsy of pancreatic or biliary ductal calculi for fragmentation. This therapeutic procedure fragments large stones.

Clinical Context

Used for large intrahepatic or choledochal stones that cannot be extracted intact; fragments enable subsequent extraction and reduce complications.
