23630 Optx gr hmrl tbrs fracture int fix

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ORIF greater tuberosity, open greater tuberosity repair, greater tuberosity ORIF

Open treatment of greater humeral tuberosity fracture with internal fixation. Open reduction internal fixation of displaced greater tuberosity fracture via screw or plate fixation.

Clinical Context

Indicated for displaced greater tuberosity fractures with articular involvement, subacromial impingement, or significant soft tissue attachments disruption.
