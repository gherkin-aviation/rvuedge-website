59030 Fetal scalp blood sampling

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: fetal scalp sampling, fetal blood pH, scalp blood gas

Fetal scalp blood sampling involves obtaining fetal blood from the fetal scalp during labor for assessment of fetal pH and acid-base status.

Clinical Context

Performed when fetal monitoring shows concerning patterns; normal pH (>7.25) reassures, while low pH (<7.20) indicates fetal acidemia requiring intervention.
