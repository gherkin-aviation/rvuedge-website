20101 exploration pentrg wound chest

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: chest wound exploration, thoracic wound exam

Surgical exploration of a penetrating wound to the chest wall to identify and evaluate injury to vital structures. This procedure examines the wound tract for cardiac, lung, or vessel involvement.

Clinical Context

Used for assessment of penetrating chest trauma to identify cardiac, pulmonary, or mediastinal injuries. Critical for determining need for thoracotomy or other intervention.
