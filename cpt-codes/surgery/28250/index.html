28250 Revision of foot fascia

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: foot fascia revision, plantar fascia revision, fascia revision surgery

Revision of foot fascia procedures. This procedure revises or corrects complications of prior fascia surgery (such as plantar fascia release).

Clinical Context

Performed for recurrent plantar fasciitis after release, inadequate relief, or other complications. Often addresses adhesions and residual contracture.
