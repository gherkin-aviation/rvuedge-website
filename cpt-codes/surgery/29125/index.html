29125 application short arm splint static

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: short arm splint, volar splint, static forearm splint

Application of a static short arm splint from the elbow to the fingers, providing semi-rigid immobilization without circumferential wrapping for conservative management.

Clinical Context

Indicated for mild to moderate wrist and forearm injuries, carpal tunnel syndrome, and temporary stabilization of hand fractures.
