65235 Remove foreign body from eye

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: foreign body removal, corneal FB removal

Removal of metallic foreign body from the anterior segment of the eye without general anesthesia; corneal wound closure not required.

In Plain Language

removing metal from eye surface; extracting foreign object from cornea

Clinical Context

Used for patients with metallic foreign bodies embedded in the cornea or conjunctiva after trauma from grinding, welding, or similar occupational injuries. Common in emergency departments and ophthalmology clinics.
