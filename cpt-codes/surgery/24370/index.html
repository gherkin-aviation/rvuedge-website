24370 Revise reconst elbow joint

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: elbow revision reconstruction, revision elbow surgery

Revision of reconstructed elbow joint. Surgical revision of failed or complicated elbow reconstruction.

Clinical Context

Used to address complications, failures, or suboptimal outcomes of previous elbow reconstruction procedures.
