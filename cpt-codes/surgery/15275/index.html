15275 Skin sub graft face/nk/hf/g

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: facial skin substitute, specialized bioengineered graft

Skin substitute graft applied to face, neck, hands, feet, genitals, or other specialized anatomical areas. Bioengineered or acellular substitute for cosmetically critical regions.

Clinical Context

Used for facial burns, hand injuries, and genital reconstruction where skin substitutes provide optimal cosmetic and functional recovery.
