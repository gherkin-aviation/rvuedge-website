69661 Revise middle ear bone

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: stapes revision with ossiculoplasty, revision stapes and ossicular reconstruction

Revision of stapes surgery with ossicular chain reconstruction to restore continuity when revision stapes prosthesis alone insufficient.

In Plain Language

redo stapes surgery with ear bone repair

Clinical Context

Used for failed stapes revision when ossicular discontinuity or displacement also present requiring reconstruction.
