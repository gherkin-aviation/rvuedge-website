59410 Obstetrical care

Surgery

Also known as: OB care with cesarean, global care with cesarean delivery, vaginal delivery with postpartum

Obstetrical care including antepartum and postpartum care, but with cesarean delivery as the final delivery method.

Clinical Context

Bundled payment for obstetrical care ending in primary cesarean delivery or cesarean for failed trial of labor.
