33017 Prcrd drainage 6yr+ without cgen car

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: pericardial drain placement, pericardial catheter, percutaneous pericardial drainage

Percutaneous pericardial drainage in patients 6 years of age or older without congenital heart disease. Needle or catheter placement in pericardial space.

In Plain Language

tube placed around heart to drain fluid

Clinical Context

Used to treat pericardial effusion and tamponade in children and adults without congenital cardiac lesions. Allows continuous drainage and monitoring.
