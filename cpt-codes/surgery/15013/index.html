15013 Prepj skn cll ssp agrft first

Surgery

Also known as: cultured skin cell prep, CEA preparation

Preparation of cultured autologous skin cells for transplantation, including patient skin biopsy processing and culture expansion, first increment. Laboratory processing of patient's own skin cells.

In Plain Language

growing skin cells in lab; culturing patient's own skin cells

Clinical Context

Used when harvested skin cells must be cultured and expanded in laboratory before application. Critical for burns and large wound coverage where large areas need treatment.

RVU Information
