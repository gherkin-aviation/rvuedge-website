43882 revision/removal gstr neurostimulator eltrd

Surgery

Also known as: gastric pacemaker revision, enterra revision/removal, neurostimulation device explantation

Revision or removal of previously implanted gastric neurostimulation electrode(s) for device malfunction or complication.

In Plain Language

stomach stimulation device repair or removal; neurostimulator removal

Clinical Context

Revision, repositioning, or complete removal of gastric neurostimulation system for malfunction, infection, loss of efficacy, or patient preference.
