26496 Revise thumb tendon

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Thumb tendon revision, Thumb re-repair, Thumb functional restoration

Surgical revision or re-repair of thumb tendons with potential transfer or repositioning to optimize thumb function, opposition, or pinch in patients with prior failed surgery or suboptimal result.

In Plain Language

Fixing a thumb tendon that was previously repaired; Revision to improve thumb opposition or pinch

Clinical Context

Performed for failed thumb tendon repairs, inadequate opposition restoration, or when revision with potential transfer provides better functional outcome.
