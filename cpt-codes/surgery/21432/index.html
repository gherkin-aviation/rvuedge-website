21432 Optx cranfcl sep with wiring

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Le Fort open reduction, craniofacial separation ORIF with wiring

Open treatment of craniofacial separation with interdental wiring and internal fixation. Surgical exposure and fixation of maxillomalar complex separation from cranial base using direct visualization and stabilization techniques.

In Plain Language

upper face fracture surgery with fixation; facial separation surgical repair

Clinical Context

Indicated for displaced craniofacial separations requiring direct visualization for anatomic reduction. Uses combination of internal fixation and interdental wiring to restore facial height, projection, and occlusion.
