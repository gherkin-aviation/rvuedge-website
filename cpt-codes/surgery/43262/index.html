43262 Endoscopic cholangiopancreatograph

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: ERCP therapeutic, therapeutic cholangiography, pancreatic duct therapy

Endoscopic retrograde cholangiopancreatography with additional diagnostic imaging or therapeutic intervention on the pancreatic or biliary ductal system. This therapeutic ERCP procedure.

Clinical Context

Includes therapeutic components such as sphincterotomy, stone extraction, stent placement, or stricture dilation; most ERCP procedures are therapeutic.
