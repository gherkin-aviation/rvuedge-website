33211 Insert card electrodes dual

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: dual transvenous leads, dual catheter approach, two-lead insertion

Insertion of dual transvenous cardiac pacemaker electrodes/leads via dual-lumen or separate catheter approach. Dual lead placement technique.

Clinical Context

Used for dual-chamber pacemaker implantation with transvenous approach. Each lead is placed through separate access for independent positioning.
