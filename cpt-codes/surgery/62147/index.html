62147 Crnop with autograft>5 centimeters diam

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: autograft cranioplasty large, large bone graft skull repair

Cranioplasty with autologous bone graft for skull defect greater than 5 centimeters in diameter. Uses patient's own bone.

Clinical Context

Used for cranioplasty using the patient's own bone graft for large defects exceeding 5 centimeters.
