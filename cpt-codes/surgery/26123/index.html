26123 Release palm contracture

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: extensive Dupuytren's release, Dupuytren's with skin graft

Release (surgical release) of palm contracture (Dupuytren's contracture) with extensive excision and possible skin grafting.

Clinical Context

Used for extensive Dupuytren's contracture requiring significant fascial excision and possible skin grafting for coverage.
