66680 Repair iris & ciliary body

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: iris repair traumatic, ciliary body repair simple

Repair of iris, ciliary body, choroid; traumatic injury, primary, simple.

Clinical Context

Simple primary repair of traumatic iris or ciliary body injury. Often performed at time of primary repair of penetrating injury.
