58555 Hysteroscopy diagnosis separate procedure

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: diagnostic hysteroscopy, hysterosc DX

Hysteroscopy with diagnostic evaluation as separate procedure is visualization of uterine cavity under direct endoscopic view for diagnostic purposes only.

Clinical Context

Office or operative procedure to evaluate abnormal uterine bleeding, polyps, fibroids, septum, synechiae, or recurrent pregnancy loss. No operative intervention performed.
