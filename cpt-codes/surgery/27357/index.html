27357 Remove femur lesion/graft

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: femoral lesion resection with fixation, complex femoral reconstruction

Removal of femoral lesion with bone graft and possible internal fixation for complex reconstruction.

Clinical Context

Applied to larger or more complex femoral lesions requiring graft and fixation for stability.
