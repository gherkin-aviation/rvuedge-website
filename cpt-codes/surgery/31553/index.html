31553 Laryngoplasty laryngeal sten

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: laryngoplasty alternative approach, laryngeal reconstruction variant, stenosis repair approach

Laryngoplasty for treatment of laryngeal stenosis using another surgical approach or technique distinct from codes 31551 and 31552, for restoration of normal laryngeal anatomy and airway.

Clinical Context

Surgical reconstruction for laryngeal stenosis using a different technical approach from 31551 and 31552. Allows coding of various reconstruction methods based on surgical technique.
