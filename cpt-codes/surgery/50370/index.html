50370 removal transplanted rnl algrft

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: graft nephrectomy, transplant nephrectomy, allograft removal

Surgical removal of a previously transplanted renal allograft (kidney) that is rejected or otherwise nonfunctional.

Clinical Context

Used for rejected kidneys (acute or chronic), infected grafts, or grafts with complications such as thrombosis. May be followed by retransplantation.
