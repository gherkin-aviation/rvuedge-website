64742 Incision of facial nerve

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: facial nerve surgery, facial nerve repair, Bell palsy surgery

Surgical incision and repair of the facial nerve (Cranial Nerve VII). May involve decompression, repair, or grafting to restore facial motor function.

In Plain Language

facial nerve surgery; face paralysis treatment

Clinical Context

Indicated for facial nerve injury or paralysis from trauma, Bell's palsy, or surgical injury. Surgery aims to restore facial motor function and expression.
