49425 Insert abdomen-venous drainage

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: peritoneovenous shunt, abdominal-venous shunt, LeVeen shunt

Insertion of venous shunt catheter from abdominal cavity to venous system for drainage of ascites (peritoneovenous shunt).

In Plain Language

tube to drain fluid from abdomen to vein; shunt for ascites drainage

Clinical Context

Surgical insertion of catheter or shunt between peritoneal cavity and venous system for refractory ascites. Used in cirrhosis or malignant ascites unresponsive to medical therapy.
