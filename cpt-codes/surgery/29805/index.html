29805 Sho arthrs diagnosis +- synovial biopsy

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: shoulder arthroscopy, shoulder scope, glenohumeral arthroscopy

Arthroscopy of the shoulder joint with diagnostic evaluation, with or without synovial biopsy for assessment of intra-articular pathology.

Clinical Context

Indicated for evaluation of shoulder pain, loose bodies, adhesions, rotator cuff assessment, and diagnostic confirmation of pathology.
