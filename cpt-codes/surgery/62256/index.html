62256 Remove brain cavity shunt

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: shunt removal, shunt explantation

Removal of cerebrospinal fluid shunt system. Includes removal of catheter and valve component.

In Plain Language

brain shunt removal; hydrocephalus shunt removal

Clinical Context

Used for removal of cerebrospinal fluid shunt when no longer needed or in cases of infection.
