33982 Replace vad intra without bp

Surgery

Also known as: LVAD pump exchange, intracorporeal pump replacement, device exchange

Replacement of ventricular assist device intracorporeal component without additional bypass; replacement of the implanted ventricular assist device pump without requiring cardiopulmonary bypass support.

Clinical Context

Replacement of failed intracorporeal LVAD pump when off-pump technique is feasible, typically using inflow/outflow graft disconnection approach.
