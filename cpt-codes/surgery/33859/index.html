33859 As-aort grf f/ds oth/thn dsj

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Ascending aortic graft, aortic aneurysm repair, ascending aorta replacement

Ascending aortic graft for aortic disease other than dissection; surgical replacement of the ascending aorta with a prosthetic graft to address aneurysm, rupture, or other non-dissection pathology.

Clinical Context

Used for ascending aortic aneurysm, aortic root disease, atherosclerotic disease, or other pathology not related to dissection. Allows reconstruction of the ascending aorta and restoration of hemodynamic function.
