58750 Repair oviduct

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: tubal repair, salpingoplasty

Repair of oviduct (fallopian tube) involves surgical reconstruction or restoration of fallopian tube function.

Clinical Context

Surgical repair of damaged or occluded fallopian tube. Includes fimbrioplasty, anastomosis, or salpingostomy. For fertility preservation.
