26587 Reconstruct extra finger

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Polydactyly reconstruction, Extra finger removal, Digital reconstruction

Surgical reconstruction of an extra or duplicated finger (polydactyly), performed to remove the supernumerary digit while optimizing remaining digit function and appearance.

Clinical Context

Indicated for polydactyly (congenital extra digit) to improve hand function and appearance, typically performed in childhood.
