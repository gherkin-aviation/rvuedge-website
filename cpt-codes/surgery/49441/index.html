49441 Place duod/jej tube percutaneous

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: percutaneous feeding tube, PEJ tube, duodenojejunal feeding tube

Placement of duodenal or jejunal feeding tube via percutaneous approach under imaging guidance.

Clinical Context

Percutaneous placement of duodenal or jejunal feeding tube for enteral nutrition distal to stomach. Used when gastrostomy is contraindicated or gastric access fails.
