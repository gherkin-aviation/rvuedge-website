43260 Ercp with specimen collection

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: ERCP with specimen, pancreatic duct brushing, biliary duct biopsy

Endoscopic retrograde cholangiopancreatography with specimen collection for cytology or histology from pancreatic or biliary duct. This diagnostic procedure obtains tissue samples.

Clinical Context

Diagnostic ERCP for suspected ductal malignancy or stricture evaluation; tissue sampling improves diagnostic accuracy for pancreatic and biliary cancers.
