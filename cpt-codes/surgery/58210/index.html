58210 Extensive hysterectomy

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: radical hysterectomy, oncologic hysterectomy, Wertheim hysterectomy

Extensive abdominal hysterectomy with pelvic lymphadenectomy removes uterus, cervix, ovaries, fallopian tubes, and pelvic lymph nodes for oncologic staging and treatment.

Clinical Context

Primary surgical treatment for early-stage gynecologic malignancy. Includes parametrial dissection and pelvic lymph node dissection. Significant operative time with higher morbidity risk.
