23700 Mnpj anesthesia sho joint fixation aprats

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: shoulder manipulation under anesthesia, MUA shoulder, therapeutic shoulder mobilization

Manipulation of shoulder joint under anesthesia (for stiffness, limitation of motion, or adhesions). Therapeutic manipulation of glenohumeral joint for capsular release or adhesion lysis.

In Plain Language

stretching of stiff shoulder while asleep; shoulder joint mobilization

Clinical Context

Performed for frozen shoulder, post-operative stiffness, or adhesive capsulitis to improve range of motion via controlled capsular rupture and tissue mobilization.
