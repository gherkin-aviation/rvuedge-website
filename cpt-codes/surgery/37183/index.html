37183 Revision tips

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: TIPS revision, TIPS restenting, TIPS revision procedure

Revision, replacement, or maintenance of a previously placed transjugular intrahepatic portosystemic shunt (TIPS) due to shunt stenosis or dysfunction.

Clinical Context

TIPS dysfunction occurs in 30-50% of cases; revision required for recurrent variceal bleeding or worsening ascites. Maintain portal decompression.
