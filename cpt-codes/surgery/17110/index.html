17110 Destruction benign lesion up to 14

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: destroy benign lesions, benign moles destruction, destruction benign lesions up to 14, wart destruction, cryotherapy warts

Destruction of benign skin lesions (other than warts) totaling up to 14 lesions using various destructive modalities.

Clinical Context

Performed for benign growths such as seborrheic keratosis, nevi, or other non-malignant lesions. Includes cosmetic removal.
