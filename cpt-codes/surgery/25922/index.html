25922 Amputate hand at wrist

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: hand amputation revision, wrist re-amputation

Amputation (removal) of the hand with revision of stump, secondary amputation or re-amputation at wrist level.

In Plain Language

revision of previously amputated hand; adjustment of hand amputation site

Clinical Context

Used for revision of previous hand amputation at wrist for complications or stump concerns.
