50750 Fusion of ureter & kidney

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ureterocalycostomy open, ureteral to calyx anastomosis open

Ureterocalycostomy with more extensive dissection or open approach, creating a direct anastomosis between ureter and renal calyx.

Clinical Context

Used for complex ureteropelvic junction obstruction, revision cases, or when minimal access is contraindicated. Higher RVU reflects increased operative complexity.
