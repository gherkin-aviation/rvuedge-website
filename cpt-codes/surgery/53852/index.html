53852 Prostatic rf thermotx

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: transurethral radiofrequency thermotherapy, radiofrequency thermotherapy

Transurethral destruction of prostate tissue using radiofrequency energy to reduce obstructive symptoms.

Clinical Context

Used for benign prostatic hyperplasia causing lower urinary tract symptoms. Radiofrequency provides controlled thermal destruction.
