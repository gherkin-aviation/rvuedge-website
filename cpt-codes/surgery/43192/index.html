43192 Esophagoscp rig trnso inject

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: rigid esophagoscopy with injection, esophageal injection

Rigid esophagoscopy (examination of esophagus with rigid scope) via transoral approach with submucosal injection of medication or agent.

Clinical Context

Therapeutic procedure for esophageal varices, bleeding lesions, or sclerosis. Requires precise injection technique and hemostasis monitoring.
