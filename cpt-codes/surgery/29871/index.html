29871 Arthrs knee surgical for infctj

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: knee washout, septic knee irrigation, infected joint debridement

Arthroscopy of the knee with surgical treatment of intra-articular infection or septic arthritis.

Clinical Context

Indicated for septic arthritis requiring urgent debridement, lavage, and removal of infected material.
