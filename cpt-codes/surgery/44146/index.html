44146 Partial removal of colon

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: total colectomy with proctectomy, total proctocolectomy, TAC with proctectomy

Open total abdominal colectomy with proctectomy (removal of entire colon and rectum).

Clinical Context

Removal of entire colon and rectum via open approach. Used for familial polyposis, ulcerative colitis, or extensive colorectal malignancy.
