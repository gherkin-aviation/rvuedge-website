42145 Repair palate pharynx/uvula

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: palate repair, pharyngeal repair, palatoplasty

Surgical repair and reconstruction of defects or damage to the soft palate, pharynx, and uvula using primary closure or tissue rearrangement techniques.

Clinical Context

Performed for palatal insufficiency, pharyngeal fistula, traumatic defects, or post-operative reconstruction after tumor resection.
