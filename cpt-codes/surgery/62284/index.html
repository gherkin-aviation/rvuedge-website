62284 Injection for myelogram

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: myelography injection, contrast myelography, intrathecal contrast

Injection of contrast material into spinal subarachnoid space for myelography imaging. Used to visualize spinal cord and nerve roots.

Clinical Context

Used for diagnostic imaging of spinal cord and nerve roots in evaluation of spinal pathology.
