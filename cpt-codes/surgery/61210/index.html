61210 Burr hole implant ventr catheter

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: burr hole ventricular catheter, external ventricular drain, ventriculostomy, burr hole subdural

Burr hole creation with placement of ventricular catheter for cerebrospinal fluid drainage or intracranial pressure monitoring.

In Plain Language

small skull opening with drainage tube placement; temporary brain fluid drainage

Clinical Context

Used for acute hydrocephalus, intracranial pressure monitoring, or CSF drainage in traumatic brain injury, subarachnoid hemorrhage, or post-operative settings. Allows monitoring and management while definitive treatment occurs.
