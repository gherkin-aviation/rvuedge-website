49429 Removal of shunt

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: shunt removal, shunt explantation, catheter removal

Complete removal of peritoneovenous shunt or abdominal-to-systemic venous catheter.

Clinical Context

Surgical removal of peritoneovenous shunt. Indications include infection, shunt thrombosis, malignancy, or patient transition off shunt therapy.
