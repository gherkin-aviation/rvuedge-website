54015 incision and drainage penis deep

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: penis incision and drainage, penile abscess drainage

Incision and drainage of deep infection or abscess of the penis, including drainage of infected tissue planes.

Clinical Context

Used for deep infections, cavernous abscesses, or necrotizing fasciitis of penis. Requires adequate drainage to prevent sepsis.
