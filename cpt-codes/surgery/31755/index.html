31755 Trachplsty trchphryngl fstlj

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: tracheoplasty with fistula repair, combined tracheal reconstruction, tracheopharyngeal fistula closure

Tracheoplasty for repair of tracheal stenosis combined with tracheopharyngeal fistula closure, addressing both the stenotic segment and abnormal fistula connection.

Clinical Context

Performed for complex tracheal pathology with concurrent stenosis and abnormal fistula between trachea and pharynx, often acquired after surgery or trauma.
