51741 Electro-uroflowmetry first

Surgery

Also known as: electro-uroflowmetry, electronic flow study

Electro-uroflowmetry (first measurement), initial electronic recording and analysis of urine flow characteristics.

Clinical Context

First urinary flow measurement using electronic uroflowmeter. Used for baseline assessment before detailed urodynamic studies.
