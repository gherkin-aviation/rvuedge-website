20650 Insert and remove bone pin

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: skeletal pin insertion, traction pin

Insertion and removal of skeletal bone pin for traction or immobilization.

In Plain Language

pin placed through bone for traction; temporary bone pin for alignment

Clinical Context

Used for external skeletal traction in fracture treatment, particularly for long bone fractures requiring alignment and weight-bearing reduction.
