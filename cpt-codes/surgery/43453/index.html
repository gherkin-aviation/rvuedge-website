43453 Dilate esophagus

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: fluoroscopic dilation, radiographic dilation, dilator dilation

Dilation of esophageal stricture with contrast radiographic guidance and dilator manipulation under fluoroscopic visualization. This stretches the stricture under imaging.

Clinical Context

Safer technique for esophageal stricture dilation; fluoroscopy improves visualization and reduces perforation risk compared to blind dilation.
