53600 Dilate urethra stricture

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: urethral dilation, stricture dilation, endoscopic dilation

Endoscopic dilation of a urethral stricture using mechanical dilation technique.

Clinical Context

First-line treatment for urethral strictures. Involves passage of progressively larger dilators to open obstructed urethra.
