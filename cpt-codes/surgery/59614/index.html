59614 Vbac care after delivery

Surgery

Also known as: VBAC with postpartum, trial of labor postpartum care, VBAC delivery with postpartum care

Vaginal birth after previous cesarean section with postpartum care but without antepartum management.

In Plain Language

vaginal birth after c-section with follow-up care

Clinical Context

Used when provider performs postpartum care following another provider's antepartum trial of labor after cesarean management.
