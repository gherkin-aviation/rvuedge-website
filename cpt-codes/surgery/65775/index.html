65775 Correction of astigmatism

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: repeat astigmatic keratotomy, re-treatment LRI

Corneal incision for correction of astigmatism (including use of topography-guided and wavefront-guided technology); subsequent treatment.

In Plain Language

additional corneal cuts to further correct astigmatism

Clinical Context

Repeat procedure for residual astigmatism after initial incisional surgery. May be performed months to years after initial treatment.

Billing & Documentation

As a surgical CPT code, proper documentation must include the operative report detailing the procedure performed, patient positioning, approach, findings, and any complications. This code has a 90-day global period, which includes the day of the procedure, 1 day preoperative, and 90 days of postoperative care.
