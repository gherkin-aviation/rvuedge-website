50590 Fragmenting of kidney stone

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: percutaneous lithotripsy, stone fragmentation, intracorporeal lithotripsy, lithotripsy ESWL, shock wave kidney stone treatment

Percutaneous fragmentation (crushing) of a kidney stone using mechanical, ultrasonic, or other device technologies for stone disintegration.

Clinical Context

Used during percutaneous nephrolithotomy for stone fragmentation, often combined with other procedures.
