11443 excision face-mm b9+marg 2.1-3 centimeters

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: facial lesion excision, face excision

Excision of benign skin lesion on face with margins of 2.1 to 3 centimeters. Includes full-thickness excision and closure on facial area.

Clinical Context

Used for removal of benign lesions on face with wider surgical margins.
