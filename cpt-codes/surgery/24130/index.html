24130 Excision radial head

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: radial head excision, radial head removal

Excision of the radial head without arthroplasty. Complete surgical removal of the head of the radius, typically used for post-traumatic arthritis or unrestorable fractures.

Clinical Context

Used for irreparable radial head fractures, post-traumatic arthritis, or other conditions where the radial head cannot be preserved. Common sequela of Mason fractures or chronic instability.
