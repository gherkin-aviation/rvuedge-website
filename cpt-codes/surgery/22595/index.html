22595 Arthrd pst tq atlas-axis

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: C1-C2 posterior fusion, Atlas-axis fusion

Arthrodesis, posterior technique, atlas-axis (C1-C2); single segment.

Clinical Context

Used for odontoid fractures, atlantoaxial instability, or C1-C2 subluxation; posterior approach is standard for trauma and instability.
