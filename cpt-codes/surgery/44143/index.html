44143 Partial removal of colon

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: partial colectomy with ostomy, colectomy with stoma, Hartmann procedure

Partial colectomy with colostomy or ileostomy creation via open approach.

Clinical Context

Resection of colon with creation of proximal stoma (colostomy or ileostomy). Often used in emergency settings (Hartmann procedure) or when distal anastomosis unsafe.
