37247 Trluml balo angiop additional artery

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: arterial angioplasty additional, additional PTA

Percutaneous or open transluminal balloon angioplasty of each additional arterial vessel beyond the first to restore luminal patency.

In Plain Language

balloon widening of additional arteries

Clinical Context

Each additional artery treated in multi-vessel peripheral arterial disease. Increases total ischemia time and procedural risk.
