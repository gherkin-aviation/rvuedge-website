14302 Tissue trnfr additional 30 square centimeters

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: Large tissue transfer add-on, Additional adjacent tissue transfer

Tissue transfer or graft from adjacent area for each additional thirty square centimeters beyond the primary measurement.

Clinical Context

Add-on code used when tissue transfer extends beyond sixty square centimeters.
