36481 Insertion of catheter vein

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: portal vein catheterization, transhepatic catheterization, PVT

Percutaneous portal vein catheterization via transcollateral venipuncture.

In Plain Language

placing a tube into the liver vein; accessing blood vessel inside the liver

Clinical Context

Used for accessing portal circulation for pressure measurement, intervention, or transjugular intrahepatic portosystemic shunt (TIPS) procedures.
