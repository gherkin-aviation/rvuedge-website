12035 Intmd repair s/a/t/external 12.6-20

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: Intermediate laceration repair, Layered wound closure

Repair of intermediate complexity laceration on the scalp, axilla, trunk, or extremities measuring twelve point six to twenty centimeters.

Clinical Context

Used for larger lacerations requiring intermediate complexity closure with layering of tissue planes.
