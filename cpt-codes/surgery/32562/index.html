32562 Lyse chest fibrin subcutaneous day

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: repeat fibrin lysis, subsequent fibrinolytic

Percutaneous lysis of fibrin in the pleural cavity on a subsequent treatment day following the initial lysis, typically performed 24-72 hours later for ongoing loculation management.

Clinical Context

Used for complicated parapneumonic effusion or empyema when initial fibrinolysis requires repeat treatment to improve drainage and prevent fibrothorax.
