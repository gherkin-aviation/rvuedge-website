50825 Construct bowel bladder

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ileal neobladder, continent urinary reservoir, catheterizable pouch

Construction of ileal bladder with more complex reconstruction or additional techniques such as creation of catheterizable outlet or augmentation.

Clinical Context

Used when complex reconstruction required including catheterizable mechanism, ureteral complications, or revision surgery. Significantly higher RVU reflects complexity.
