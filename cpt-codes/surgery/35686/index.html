35686 Bypass graft/av fist patency

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: bypass-arteriovenous fistula revision, AV fistula repair in graft, graft-fistula revision

Revision of bypass graft anastomosis or arteries to restore patency in thrombosed graft or to correct arteriovenous fistula complications. Restores vascular function.

Clinical Context

Used for graft-related complications including arteriovenous fistula at anastomosis or adjacent sites.
