50250 Opn ablation 1/> rnl mass crysrg

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: open renal ablation, thermal ablation, cryotherapy

Open surgical ablation of one or more renal masses using cryotherapy, radiofrequency, or crystalline agent injection for percutaneous or open approaches.

In Plain Language

surgical freezing or heat treatment of kidney tumors; kidney tumor destruction surgery

Clinical Context

Used for small renal masses (typically less than 4 centimeters) in patients who are poor surgical candidates or prefer nephron-sparing approach. Can be combined with open pyelotomy.
