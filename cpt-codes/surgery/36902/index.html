36902 Intro catheter dialysis circuit

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: implantable port insertion, central venous access, Port-a-Cath insertion

Insertion of implantable venous access device; central venous catheter (eg, Port-a-Cath, Mediport, Hickman, Groshong).

Clinical Context

Used for dialysis circuit access via implanted port. Requires percutaneous needle access for dialysis.
