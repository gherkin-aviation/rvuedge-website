33852 repair hypopl a-arch wo byp

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Hypoplastic arch repair, aortic arch plasty, arch augmentation

Repair of hypoplastic aortic arch without bypass; surgical reconstruction of an underdeveloped aortic arch to restore adequate luminal diameter and prevent obstruction, performed without cardiopulmonary bypass.

In Plain Language

Surgery to enlarge underdeveloped aorta arch; Aorta arch straightening without heart-lung machine

Clinical Context

Performed in patients with significant aortic arch hypoplasia (underdevelopment) presenting with systemic hypertension or hemodynamic compromise. Off-pump approach suitable for less complex cases.
