26476 Tendon lengthening

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Tendon lengthening, Tendon elongation, Contracture lengthening

Surgical lengthening of tendons in the hand or finger to improve range of motion and functional positioning when tendons have become shortened due to contracture or scarring.

Clinical Context

Indicated for tendon contracture limiting joint motion, typically performed when lengthening is preferable to release. May involve Z-plasty or other lengthening techniques.
