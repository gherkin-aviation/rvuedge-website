22802 Arthrd pst dfrm 7-12 vrt segment

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Posterior spinal fusion 7-12 levels, Deformity correction multi-level

Arthrodesis, posterior technique, spinal deformity correction, with bone graft or nonvascularized bone graft; 7 to 12 vertebral segments.

Clinical Context

Used for extensive scoliosis or kyphosis correction spanning 7-12 vertebral segments; more complex than shorter constructs.
