40814 Excise/repair mouth lesion

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: complex lesion excision, oral lesion excision with flap

Excision and repair of mouth lesion with more complex reconstruction. May involve flap or graft procedures.

Clinical Context

Used for larger lesions or those requiring tissue reconstruction to maintain oral function and appearance.
