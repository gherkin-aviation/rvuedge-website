36415 Coll venous bld venipuncture

Surgery

Also known as: venipuncture, blood draw, venous stick

Venipuncture for collection of venous blood specimen; each additional venipuncture and venous blood collection, performed at a separate site in a patient with or without an established IV.

Clinical Context

Used for routine blood specimen collection in patients undergoing laboratory testing. Common in all clinical settings for diagnostic purposes.
