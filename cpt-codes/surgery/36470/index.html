36470 Njx sclrsnt 1 incmptnt vein

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: sclerotherapy, incompetent vein treatment, venous insufficiency sclerosis

Injection of sclerosing solution, non-compound, into a single incompetent vein.

In Plain Language

treatment of a faulty vein that leaks blood backward; fixing a vein that doesn't work properly

Clinical Context

Used for symptomatic venous insufficiency where valve dysfunction allows retrograde flow. Targets specific incompetent saphenous or other veins.
