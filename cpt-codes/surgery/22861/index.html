22861 Revision rplcm arthrp 1ntrspc crv

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Cervical ADIS revision, Disc replacement revision

Revision or replacement of total disc arthroplasty (including endplate preparation, discectomy, and interbody device insertion), single interspace; cervical.

In Plain Language

Fixing or replacing artificial neck disc; Artificial disc revision surgery

Clinical Context

Used when cervical artificial disc requires revision or replacement due to failure, migration, subsidence, or inadequate decompression.
