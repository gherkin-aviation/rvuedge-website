25675 Cltx dstl radiation/uln dislc mnpj

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: closed reduction DRUJ dislocation, conservative radioulnar dislocation

Closed treatment (reduction) of distal radioulnar joint dislocation using manipulation and immobilization.

Clinical Context

Used for distal radioulnar joint dislocations managed by closed reduction and immobilization. Appropriate for acute dislocations with adequate reduction.
