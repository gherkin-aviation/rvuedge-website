52353 Cystouretero with lithotripsy

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: cysto with ureteral stone lithotripsy, ureteroscopic lithotripsy, cystoscopy with laser lithotripsy, laser stone treatment, ureteroscopy with lithotripsy

Cystoureteroscopy with insertion of ureteroscope and use of lithotripsy device (laser, ultrasonic, or pneumatic) to fragment upper urinary tract stones.

In Plain Language

breaking up kidney stone with scope

Clinical Context

Used for large or hard stones exceeding passive extraction capability. Laser lithotripsy preferred for stone-free rates.
