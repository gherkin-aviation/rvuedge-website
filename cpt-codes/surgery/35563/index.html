35563 Artery byp graft ilioiliac

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ilioiliac bypass, iliofemoral bypass

Arterial bypass graft from iliac to iliac artery with restoration of contralateral lower extremity blood flow.

Clinical Context

Revascularization for unilateral iliac artery occlusion with patent ipsilateral iliac system. Alternative to aortoiliac reconstruction for select patients.
