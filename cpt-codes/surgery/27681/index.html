27681 Release of lower leg tendons

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: multiple tendon release, posterior calf release

Surgical release or lengthening of multiple lower leg tendons for contracture or spasticity management.

Clinical Context

Used for severe plantarflexor contracture, spastic equinus, or multiple tendon tightness affecting ankle/foot function.
