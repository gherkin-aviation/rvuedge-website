57460 Biopsy of cervix with scope leep

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: LEEP biopsy, Loop biopsy, Cervical LEEP

Biopsy of cervix with Loop Electrosurgical Excision Procedure (LEEP). Tissue removal using electrosurgical loop under colposcopy.

Clinical Context

Both diagnostic and therapeutic. Used for cervical dysplasia evaluation and treatment.
