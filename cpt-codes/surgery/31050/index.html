31050 Exploration sphenoid sinus

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: sphenoid exploration, transnasal sphenoid access, sphenoid sinus surgery

Surgical exploration of the sphenoid sinus via transnasal approach with limited visualization and inspection of sphenoid sinus cavity.

Clinical Context

Open surgical exploration of sphenoid sinus for chronic disease or inflammatory conditions. Transnasal approach allows direct inspection of posterior sinus.
