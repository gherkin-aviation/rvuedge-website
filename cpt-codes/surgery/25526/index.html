25526 Optx rdl shft fracture & dstl radiation/ul

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ORIF radial shaft and distal fracture, extensive radial fracture repair

Open treatment (surgical repair) of radial shaft fracture combined with open treatment (surgical repair) of associated distal radial and/or ulnar fracture.

In Plain Language

major forearm fracture surgery at multiple levels; surgical repair of complex forearm breaks

Clinical Context

Used for complex polytrauma or high-energy injuries affecting multiple levels of the forearm requiring operative fixation at both radial shaft and distal radius or ulna sites.
