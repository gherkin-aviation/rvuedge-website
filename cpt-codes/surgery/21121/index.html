21121 Geniop sldg osteot 1

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: sliding genioplasty, sliding osteotomy, chin osteotomy

Genioplasty with sliding osteotomy of the chin in a single piece, involving surgical fracture and repositioning of the anterior mandible.

Clinical Context

Used for microgenia, retrognathia, or major chin deformities. Provides greater correction than simple augmentation.
