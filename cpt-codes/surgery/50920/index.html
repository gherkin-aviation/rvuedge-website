50920 Closure ureter/skin fistula

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ureteral fistula repair, ureterocutaneous fistula closure, ureter-skin fistula closure

Closure of ureter to skin fistula, surgical elimination of abnormal communication between ureter and skin by repair and tissue approximation.

Clinical Context

Performed for ureterocutaneous fistula from prior surgery, trauma, or stone erosion. Requires identification and repair of fistula tract.
