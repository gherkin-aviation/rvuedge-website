28322 Repair of metatarsals

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: metatarsal fracture repair, ORIF metatarsal, metatarsal ORIF

Surgical repair of metatarsal bone fracture using open reduction and internal fixation with hardware placement to achieve and maintain anatomic alignment.

In Plain Language

Metatarsal fracture surgery; Foot bone fracture repair; Metatarsal fixation

Clinical Context

Used for displaced metatarsal fractures (typically base or midshaft fractures) requiring operative fixation to maintain alignment and promote union.
