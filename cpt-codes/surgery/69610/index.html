69610 Tympanic membrane repair

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: tympanic membrane repair, TM repair, traumatic drum repair

Simple repair of traumatic or surgical defects of the tympanic membrane to restore anatomical integrity and prevent infection.

Clinical Context

Used for traumatic perforations, post-surgical defects, or perforations causing conductive hearing loss or recurrent infection.
