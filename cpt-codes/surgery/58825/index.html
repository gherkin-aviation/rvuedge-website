58825 Transposition ovary(s)

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: oophoropexy, ovarian transposition, ovarian mobilization

Ovarian transposition (oophoropexy) involves surgical repositioning of one or both ovaries away from the pelvis, typically to protect them from radiation or chemotherapy effects.

In Plain Language

moving the ovary to protect it from cancer treatment

Clinical Context

Performed in premenopausal women who will receive pelvic radiation or chemotherapy for malignancy to preserve ovarian function and fertility.
