65750 Corneal transplant

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: repeat corneal transplant, re-graft, second PKP

Corneal transplant; penetrating keratoplasty, repeat graft.

Clinical Context

Repeat full-thickness corneal transplant after rejection or failure of previous graft. Higher complexity and somewhat higher rejection risk than primary graft.
