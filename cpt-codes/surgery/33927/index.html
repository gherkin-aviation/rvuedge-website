33927 Impltj tot replacement hrt sys

Surgery

Also known as: Total artificial heart, TAH implantation, artificial heart

Implantation of total replacement heart system (artificial heart); surgical placement of a completely implantable artificial heart to replace native cardiac function.

Clinical Context

Destination therapy or bridge-to-transplantation procedure for end-stage biventricular heart failure. Provides complete hemodynamic support replacing native heart.
