61140 Burr hole/treph biopsy brain/lesion

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: burr hole biopsy, brain biopsy via burr hole, lesion biopsy

Burr hole(s) and/or trephination of the skull with biopsy of brain tissue or lesion and/or removal of lesion from brain parenchyma.

Clinical Context

Performed for diagnosis of suspected brain tumor, abscess, demyelinating disease, or other parenchymal lesions when location precludes larger craniotomy. Stereotactic guidance improves diagnostic yield and safety.
