31551 Laryngoplasty laryngeal sten

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: laryngoplasty for stenosis, laryngeal reconstruction, stenosis repair

Laryngoplasty (surgical reconstruction of the larynx) for treatment of laryngeal stenosis (narrowing) using various techniques such as scar lysis, material injection, or structural reconstruction to restore airway patency.

Clinical Context

Performed for acquired laryngeal stenosis from trauma, intubation, caustic ingestion, or radiation, or for congenital stenosis. Goal is to restore airway while preserving voice function.
