51570 Removal of bladder

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: total cystectomy, bladder removal, cystectomy

Removal of bladder (total cystectomy), complete surgical excision of bladder for cancer or end-stage dysfunction.

Clinical Context

Indicated for invasive bladder cancer, severely dysfunctional bladder, or intractable pain. Requires urinary diversion (ileal conduit or neobladder).
