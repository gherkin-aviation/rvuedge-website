20526 Therapy injection carp tunnel

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: carpal tunnel injection, wrist tunnel injection

Therapeutic injection of medication into the carpal tunnel space to reduce inflammation and relieve compression neuropathy symptoms. This procedure provides steroid therapy directly to affected area.

Clinical Context

Used for symptomatic relief of carpal tunnel syndrome, reducing median nerve compression symptoms. Often first-line treatment before surgical decompression.
