36558 Insert tunneled cv catheter

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: tunneled central line, Hickman catheter, permanent central line, long-term central catheter

Insertion of tunneled central venous catheter, without subcutaneous port or pump; 5 years of age or older.

Clinical Context

Used for extended intravenous access in pediatric and adult patients requiring chemotherapy, prolonged antibiotics, parenteral nutrition, or frequent labs.
