36005 Injection external venography

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: venography injection, peripheral venogram, contrast venography

Percutaneous injection for external venography (contrast study) of peripheral veins without catheter. Diagnostic venographic imaging.

Clinical Context

Used for venous disease evaluation, thrombosis diagnosis, or anatomic assessment in select cases.
