69420 Incision of eardrum

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: myringotomy, tympano-centesis

Incision of tympanum (eardrum) including simple myringotomy for ventilation tube insertion or fluid drainage.

In Plain Language

cutting eardrum hole; making drainage hole in eardrum

Clinical Context

Used for otitis media with effusion or acute otitis media. Common indications include middle ear effusion, ear drainage, or ventilation tube placement.
