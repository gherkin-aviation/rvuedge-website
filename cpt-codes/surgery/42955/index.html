42955 Surgical opening of throat

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: pharyngotomy, laryngofissure, throat opening

Laryngofissure, pharyngotomy, or creation of surgical opening into the pharynx (throat) for therapeutic access or reconstruction.

In Plain Language

surgical opening into the throat; throat opening surgery for treatment

Clinical Context

Performed to access deep pharyngeal pathology, remove obstructing lesions, or establish access for reconstruction. Used for laryngeal stenosis, vocal cord paralysis, or pharyngeal lesions.
