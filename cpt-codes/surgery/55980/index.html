55980 Sex transformation f to m

Surgery

Also known as: Female-to-male transition surgery, Metoidioplasty, Phalloplasty

Surgical procedure for sex transformation from female to male. Involves construction of male external and internal genitalia.

Clinical Context

Performed in patients with gender dysphoria who have completed psychological evaluation and hormone therapy. May include metoidioplasty or phalloplasty with urethral reconstruction.
