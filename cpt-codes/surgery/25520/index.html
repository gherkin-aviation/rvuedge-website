25520 Cltx rdl shft fracture & dislc

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: closed reduction radial fracture with DRUJ injury, radial shaft fracture-dislocation

Closed treatment (reduction) of radial shaft fracture combined with distal radioulnar joint (DRUJ) dislocation or injury, managed without open surgery.

Clinical Context

Used for combined injuries involving both radial shaft fracture and disruption of the distal radioulnar articulation. Closed treatment is suitable when adequate reduction can be maintained by immobilization.
