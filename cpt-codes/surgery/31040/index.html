31040 Exploration behind upper jaw

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: pterygoid exploration, pterygopalatine fossa exploration, maxillary exploration

Surgical exploration of the pterygopalatine fossa and superior maxillary region, typically via Caldwell-Luc or infraorbital approach.

Clinical Context

Exploration of deep maxillary and pterygoid regions for tumor, infection, or vascular lesion. Complex procedure requiring special expertise due to proximity to orbital and intracranial structures.
