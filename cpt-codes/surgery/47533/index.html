47533 Plmt biliary drainage catheter

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: Percutaneous biliary drainage, PTBD catheter placement

Percutaneous placement of a biliary drainage catheter through the skin and into the bile ducts to relieve obstruction and allow external drainage of bile.

Clinical Context

Performed for malignant or benign biliary obstruction, cholangitis, or biliary leak. Provides temporary or definitive drainage depending on underlying pathology.
