31623 Diagnosis bronchoscope/brush

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: diagnostic bronchoscopy with brush, brush cytology, bronchial brush biopsy

Diagnostic bronchoscopy with brush cytology, using a specialized brush to sample cells from bronchial mucosa for microscopic examination and diagnosis of mucosal pathology.

Clinical Context

Performed to obtain cell samples from airway mucosa for cytologic examination when malignancy or infection is suspected. Less invasive than tissue biopsy.
