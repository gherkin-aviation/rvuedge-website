53448 Remov/replc ur sphinctr complicated

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: complicated AUS replacement, complex sphincter replacement

Surgical removal and replacement of a previously implanted artificial urinary sphincter complicated by tissue changes, erosion, or other complex pathology.

Clinical Context

Indicated when removal and replacement requires extensive tissue dissection due to fibrosis, adhesions, erosion, or infection. Higher complexity than simple replacement.
