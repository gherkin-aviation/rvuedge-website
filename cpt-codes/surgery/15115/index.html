15115 Epdrm agrft f/s/n/h/f/g/m 1

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: epidermal graft face, facial epidermal graft

Epidermal autograft applied to face, scalp, neck, hands, feet, genitals, or mucosal surfaces, first increment. Specialized epidermal graft for cosmetically sensitive areas.

In Plain Language

thin skin layer graft on face or special area; epidermal transplant to sensitive areas

Clinical Context

Used for facial burns, scalp injuries, hand injuries, and genital reconstruction requiring epidermal grafts for optimal cosmetic and functional outcomes.
