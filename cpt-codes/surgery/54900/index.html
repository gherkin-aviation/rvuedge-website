54900 Fusion of spermatic ducts

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: vasovasostomy, epididymovasostomy

Fusion or anastomosis of spermatic ducts (vasal deferens) or epididymal reconstruction.

In Plain Language

sperm duct reconnection

Clinical Context

Surgical reconstruction of vas deferens for reversal of vasectomy or repair of obstruction to restore sperm flow.
