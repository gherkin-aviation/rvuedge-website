27097 Revision of hip tendon

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: hip tendon revision, hip tendon lengthening, revision hip tendon

Surgical revision and lengthening of hip tendon to address contracture, imbalance, or failed prior procedure.

In Plain Language

revising hip tendon surgery; fixing previous hip tendon problem

Clinical Context

Used for revision of hip tendon procedures addressing residual contracture, muscle imbalance, or inadequate prior treatment.

Billing & Documentation

As a surgical CPT code, proper documentation must include the operative report detailing the procedure performed, patient positioning, approach, findings, and any complications. This code has a 90-day global period, which includes the day of the procedure, 1 day preoperative, and 90 days of postoperative care.
