43227 Esophagoscopy control bleed

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: esophageal bleeding control, hemostasis endoscopy

Esophagoscopy (flexible) with control of bleeding from esophageal source using hemostatic technique (cautery, sclerosis, or suturing).

In Plain Language

camera examination to stop esophageal bleeding

Clinical Context

Emergency/urgent procedure for upper gastrointestinal bleeding from esophageal source. Critical for hemostasis in acute bleeding.
