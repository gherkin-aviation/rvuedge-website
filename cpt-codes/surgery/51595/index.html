51595 Remove bladder/revise tract

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: cystectomy with neobladder, ileal bladder creation

Removal of bladder with creation of urinary pouch (continent reservoir), total cystectomy with ileal neobladder or continent pouch construction.

Clinical Context

Provides continence alternative to conduit. Allows normal voiding pattern and improved quality of life in appropriate candidates.
