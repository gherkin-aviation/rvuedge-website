43193 Esophagoscp rig trnso biopsy

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: rigid esophagoscopy with biopsy, esophageal biopsy

Rigid esophagoscopy (examination of esophagus with rigid scope) via transoral approach with tissue biopsy.

Clinical Context

Diagnostic procedure for evaluation of esophageal lesions with tissue diagnosis. Allows histologic analysis of abnormal esophageal tissue.
