47381 Open ablate liver tumor cryo

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: open cryoablation, open cryosurgery, hepatic cryosurgery

Open (non-laparoscopic) ablation of liver tumor using cryosurgery. Direct surgical access with cryogenic ablation.

Clinical Context

Performed for hepatic tumors requiring open cryogenic destruction. Allows intraoperative ultrasound guidance.
