62192 Establish brain cavity shunt

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ventriculovenous shunt, supraperitoneal shunt, alternative shunt placement

Establishment of ventriculovenous cerebrospinal fluid shunt or other supraperitoneal drainage procedure. Alternative drainage pathway for cerebrospinal fluid.

Clinical Context

Used for cerebrospinal fluid diversion in patients requiring alternative shunt placement due to peritoneal or cardiac contraindications.
