27286 Arthrd hip joint sbtrchc osteot

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: hip fusion with osteotomy, subtrochanteric osteotomy hip

Arthrodesis of hip joint with subtrochanteric osteotomy, combining fusion with femoral bone cut for positioning and biomechanics.

Clinical Context

Used for hip arthrodesis cases requiring subtrochanteric deformity correction through simultaneous osteotomy for optimal positioning.
