38230 Bone marrow harvest allogen

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: Allogeneic bone marrow harvest, Donor marrow harvest

Harvesting of bone marrow from an allogeneic donor for hematopoietic cell transplantation to a recipient.

Clinical Context

Initial step in allogeneic bone marrow transplantation where marrow is harvested from matched or related donor.
