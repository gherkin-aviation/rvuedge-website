49040 Drainage open abdominal abscess

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Abdominal abscess drainage, open drainage

Surgical drainage of an abdominal abscess through an open incision with placement of drainage catheter for external drainage of infection.

Clinical Context

Performed for abdominal abscess from perforated viscus, diverticulitis, or other source. Open approach when percutaneous approach is not feasible.
