26498 Finger tendon transfer

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Complex finger tendon transfer, Multi-component transfer, Extensive transfer

Surgical transfer of a finger tendon to a new location with complex reconstruction, potentially involving multiple transfers or extensive routing to restore comprehensive hand or finger function.

Clinical Context

Performed for complex hand dysfunction requiring multiple finger reconstructions or extensive routing, often in severe paralysis or significant muscle loss.
