45340 Sig with tndsc balloon dilation

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: sigmoidoscopy with stricture dilation, flexible sig with dilation

Sigmoidoscopy with dilation of sigmoid or rectal stricture via balloon, bougie, or mechanical technique.

In Plain Language

widening of narrow area in lower colon; balloon dilation of sigmoid stricture

Clinical Context

Used for symptomatic benign strictures of sigmoid colon or rectum from inflammatory bowel disease, anastomotic scarring, or radiation changes.
