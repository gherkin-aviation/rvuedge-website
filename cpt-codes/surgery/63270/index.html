63270 Excise intrspinl lesion crvl

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: cervical intraspinal tumor excision with dural repair

Excision of intraspinal lesion in the cervical region with dura mater repair. This more extensive procedure involves complete removal of the lesion and reconstruction or repair of the dural membrane.

Clinical Context

Used for intraspinal lesions requiring dural opening and reconstruction, typically for intramedullary tumors, larger extramedullary lesions, or lesions involving the dura.
