41018 Drainage of mouth lesion

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: salivary gland duct repair, sialodochoplasty, gland exploration

Surgical drainage with exploration and repair of salivary gland duct or complex glandular lesion.

Clinical Context

Used for salivary gland duct obstruction or stricture requiring sialodochoplasty or gland exploration. Common with ductal stones, strictures, or duct injuries.
