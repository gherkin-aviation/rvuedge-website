24073 Ex arm/elbow tumor deep 5 centimeters/>

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: deep tumor excision >5cm, deep lesion removal, deep tumor removal

Excision of arm/elbow tumor (deep, greater than 5 centimeters). Removal of deep soft tissue tumor from arm or elbow, size greater than 5 cm.

In Plain Language

removal of deep growth from arm; deep tumor removal

Clinical Context

Used for benign or low-grade malignant deep soft tissue tumors of arm/elbow exceeding 5 cm requiring en bloc resection.
