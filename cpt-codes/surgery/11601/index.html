11601 excision tr-ext mal+marg 0.6-1 centimeters

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: malignant excision, skin cancer removal, excision malignant lesion 0.6-1cm, skin cancer excision small

Excision of malignant skin lesion on trunk or extremity with margins of 0.6 to 1 centimeter. Includes closure of the excision site.

Clinical Context

Used for removal of melanoma or malignant skin cancers on trunk and extremities with moderate margins.
