43310 Repair of esophagus

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: open esophagectomy, esophageal resection, transhiatal esophagectomy

Open esophageal resection with esophagogastric or esophagocolonic anastomosis via abdominal approach. This major surgical procedure removes diseased esophagus.

In Plain Language

removing esophagus and reconnecting stomach; open esophageal surgery

Clinical Context

Standard surgical approach for esophageal cancer or severe benign disease; requires major hospitalization and has significant morbidity.

Billing & Documentation
