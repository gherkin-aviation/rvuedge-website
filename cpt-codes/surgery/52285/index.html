52285 Cystoscopy and treatment

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: cysto with ureteral balloon dilation

Cystourethroscopy with dilation of the ureter or ureteral stricture using endoscopic balloon catheter technique to restore urine flow from kidney to bladder.

Clinical Context

Used for ureteral strictures from prior surgery, radiation, or trauma. Less invasive than open surgical correction.
