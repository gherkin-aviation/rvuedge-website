47145 Prep donor liver lobe split

Surgery

Also known as: split-lobe preparation, lobe-split procurement, bilobe split graft

Preparation of donor liver for split-lobe procurement. Graft preparation dividing liver into left and right lobes.

In Plain Language

preparing a donor liver split into left and right lobes; split-lobe graft preparation

Clinical Context

Performed to divide deceased donor liver for living donor (right lobe) and adult recipient (left lobe).

RVU Information

CPT 47145 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
