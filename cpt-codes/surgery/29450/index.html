29450 Application clubfoot cast

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: clubfoot cast, talipes cast, serial cast

Application of a specialized plaster cast for correction and immobilization of clubfoot deformity, typically applied in serial casts for progressive correction.

Clinical Context

Used in pediatric clubfoot management following Ponseti method or post-operative immobilization of surgical clubfoot correction.
