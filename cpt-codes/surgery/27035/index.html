27035 Denervation of hip joint

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: hip denervation, hip capsular denervation, nerve ablation hip

Surgical denervation of the hip joint through division or ablation of nerve branches to reduce pain.

Clinical Context

Indicated for severe hip arthritis pain unresponsive to other treatments where pain relief through nerve denervation is desired.
