61590 Infratemporal approach/skull

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: infratemporal approach, temporal bone approach

Infratemporal approach to skull base lesion with temporal bone drilling and intracranial dissection for tumor removal.

In Plain Language

Surgery through temporal bone to skull base; Ear region skull base surgery

Clinical Context

Provides lateral skull base access for temporal bone tumors, jugular foramen lesions, and lateral brainstem pathology.
