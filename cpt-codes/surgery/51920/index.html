51920 Close bladder-uterus fistula

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: vesicotuterine fistula repair, bladder-uterus fistula closure

Surgical closure of a fistula connecting the bladder and uterus through an abdominal approach, typically combined with appropriate tissue repair to restore anatomic integrity.

Clinical Context

Rare complication of hysterectomy, cesarean section, or other pelvic surgery. Presents with pneumaturia and fecal material in urine if bladder-bowel fistula also present.
