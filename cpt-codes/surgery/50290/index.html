50290 Excision perinephric cyst

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: perinephric cyst excision, perirenal cyst removal, cyst resection

Surgical excision of a perinephric cyst (cyst outside the kidney capsule but within the perirenal space).

In Plain Language

surgical removal of cyst around kidney; kidney surrounding cyst removal

Clinical Context

Used for symptomatic perinephric cysts causing flank pain, obstruction, or infection. Usually requires careful dissection to avoid renal vessel injury.
