33364 Replace aortic valve open

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: tissue aortic valve replacement, bioprosthetic AVR

Replacement of aortic valve with biological/tissue prosthesis via open surgical approach.

In Plain Language

surgically replacing aortic valve with tissue valve

Clinical Context

Used for aortic stenosis/regurgitation when tissue valve is preferred (age, anticoagulation concerns).
