25100 Biopsy of wrist joint

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: wrist joint biopsy, synovial biopsy

Biopsy of the wrist joint by needle or open technique to obtain synovial tissue or fluid for diagnostic evaluation of joint pathology.

Clinical Context

Indicated for suspected joint infection (septic arthritis), rheumatoid arthritis evaluation, or other inflammatory joint conditions requiring tissue diagnosis.
