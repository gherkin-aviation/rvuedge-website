41153 Tongue mouth neck surgery

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: composite resection with neck, tongue floor mouth neck dissection, extensive head and neck resection

Surgical resection of tongue and floor of mouth with removal of the neck soft tissues and lymph nodes.

Clinical Context

Used for extensive tongue and floor of mouth cancers requiring en bloc resection with neck dissection. Involves removal of primary tumor and nodal disease.
