63688 revision/removal imp sp npg/r dtch cn

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: spinal pulse generator removal, neurostimulator explant, IPG revision

Revision or removal of implanted spinal neurostimulation device receiver/control unit after lead disconnection. This procedure involves revision or extraction of the pulse generator following lead removal or disconnection.

In Plain Language

removal or adjustment of spinal pain relief device control unit

Clinical Context

Used for revision, relocation, or removal of implanted pulse generators in spinal cord stimulation systems.
