64893 Nerve grf 1strnd arm/leg >4 centimeters

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: single strand nerve graft arm/leg long, extended extremity nerve graft

Surgical nerve graft using autologous nerve tissue in a single strand 4 centimeters or greater for repair of arm or leg nerve injuries.

Clinical Context

Used for arm or leg nerve gaps of 4 cm or longer. May require multiple graft segments to cover the gap while minimizing donor site morbidity.
