58552 Laparo-vag hyst incl t/o

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: LAVH with BSO, laparoscopic-assisted vaginal hysterectomy with ovary removal

Laparoscopic-assisted vaginal hysterectomy with salpingo-oophorectomy combines laparoscopic-assisted hysterectomy with removal of fallopian tubes and ovaries.

In Plain Language

Laparoscopic-assisted uterus and ovary removal; Hybrid ovary and uterus removal

Clinical Context

Hybrid approach allowing assessment of ovaries and removal via laparoscopy before completing hysterectomy vaginally. Combines advantages of minimally invasive assessment with vaginal completion.
