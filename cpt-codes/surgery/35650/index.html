35650 Bpg axillary-axillary

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: axillary-axillary bypass, axillary crossover, bilateral axillary bypass

Arterial bypass graft from axillary artery to contralateral axillary artery for bilateral subclavian or innominate disease with arm ischemia. Restores bilateral upper extremity circulation.

In Plain Language

crossover arm artery bypass; connecting both arm arteries

Clinical Context

Used for bilateral subclavian/innominate disease when endovascular therapy or bilateral reconstruction is not feasible. Alternative to bilateral transpositions.
