15773 Grfg autol fat lipo 25 cc/<

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: fat grafting small volume, minimal lipofilling

Autologous fat injection or transplantation harvested by liposuction technique, first 25 cubic centimeters or less. Smaller volume fat grafting procedure.

Clinical Context

Used for smaller defects, fine contour adjustments, and areas requiring precise volume restoration.
