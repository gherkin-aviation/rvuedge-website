44366 Small bowel endoscopy

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: small bowel endoscopy with ultrasound, enteroscopy with EUS, intraoperative ultrasound

Endoscopy of small bowel performed via enterotomy with endoscopic ultrasound examination for enhanced imaging evaluation of bowel wall and adjacent structures.

In Plain Language

camera and ultrasound examination of small intestine; surgical ultrasound evaluation of bowel

Clinical Context

Used for detailed imaging of small bowel pathology including evaluation of wall thickness, lymph nodes, or adjacent structures. Helps determine depth of invasion in malignancy.
