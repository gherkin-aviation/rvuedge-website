Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: penile injection test, diagnostic/therapeutic injection

Injection of medication into the corpora cavernosa for vascular evaluation or therapeutic purposes.

Clinical Context

Used for diagnostic assessment of erectile function or therapeutic treatment. Allows evaluation of vascular response.
