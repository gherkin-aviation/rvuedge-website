63075 Neck spine disk surgery

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: cervical discectomy, cervical disc surgery, neck disc operation

Cervical spine disc surgery. This procedure involves accessing the intervertebral disc space in the neck, typically for discectomy, fusion preparation, or decompression of neural structures.

Clinical Context

Frequently used code for anterior cervical discectomy and fusion (ACDF) or cervical arthroplasty in patients with cervical radiculopathy or myelopathy from disc herniation.
