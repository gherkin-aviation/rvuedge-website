65865 Incise inner eye adhesions

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: anterior synechiae laser incision, adhesiolysis laser

Incision of anterior synechiae (with or without injection of aqueous); laser surgery.

Clinical Context

Laser incision of anterior synechiae for angle-closure glaucoma or uveitic disease. Restores angle access.
