61510 Craniectomy treph excision brn tumor sttl

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: supratentorial brain tumor resection, craniotomy for tumor

Craniotomy or craniectomy with excision of brain tumor from supratentorial (above tentorium) location and exploration of surgical bed.

Clinical Context

Performed for supratentorial brain tumors (gliomas, meningiomas, metastases, pituitary adenomas, etc.). Standard approach for most accessible anterior and lateral brain tumors.
