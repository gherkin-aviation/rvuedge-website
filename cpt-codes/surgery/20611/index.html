20611 drainage/injection joint/bursa with ultrasound

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: ultrasound-guided joint aspiration, US-guided bursa injection

Drainage or injection into a joint or bursa with ultrasound guidance.

In Plain Language

guided fluid removal from joint; ultrasound-assisted injection

Clinical Context

Performed with real-time ultrasound visualization to increase accuracy and safety when accessing small or difficult-to-palpate joints or bursae.
