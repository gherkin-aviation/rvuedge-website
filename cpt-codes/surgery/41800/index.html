41800 Drainage of gum lesion

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: gum drainage, gingivitis abscess drainage, gingival abscess drainage

Surgical drainage of an abscess or lesion of the gingiva (gums) with intraoral approach.

Clinical Context

Used for infected lesions or abscesses of the gingiva requiring drainage. Common with periodontal infections or gingival abscesses.
