62190 Establish brain cavity shunt

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: VA shunt placement, ventriculoatrial shunt, cardiac shunt surgery

Establishment of ventriculoatrial cerebrospinal fluid shunt. Creates drainage system from ventricles to right atrium of heart.

Clinical Context

Used for cerebrospinal fluid diversion in patients with contraindications to peritoneal shunting or in revision cases.
