67904 Repair eyelid defect

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: eyelid repair, lower eyelid reconstruction

Repair of lower eyelid defect involving the margin or full-thickness reconstruction of eyelid tissue with basic closure techniques.

Clinical Context

Used for traumatic lower eyelid defects, surgical defects from lesion removal, or congenital eyelid anomalies. Common indications include lower eyelid margin trauma, eyelid lacerations, or small full-thickness eyelid defects.
