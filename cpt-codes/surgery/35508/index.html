35508 Artery byp graft carotid-vertbrl

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: carotid-vertebral bypass, CV bypass

Arterial bypass graft from carotid to vertebral artery with restoration of posterior circulation perfusion.

Clinical Context

Revascularization for symptomatic vertebral artery stenosis or occlusion causing posterior circulation ischemic symptoms. Less commonly performed than carotid-carotid bypass.
