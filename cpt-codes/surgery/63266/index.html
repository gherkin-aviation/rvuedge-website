63266 Excise intrspinl lesion thrc

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: thoracic intraspinal tumor removal, thoracic spinal lesion excision

Excision of intraspinal lesion in the thoracic region. This procedure involves removal of tumors, cysts, or other pathological lesions located within the thoracic spinal canal.

In Plain Language

removal of growth or abnormal tissue in mid-back spinal canal

Clinical Context

Used for thoracic intraspinal tumors including meningiomas, schwannomas, ependymomas, or hemangioblastomas causing myelopathy.
