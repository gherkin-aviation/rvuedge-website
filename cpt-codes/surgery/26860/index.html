26860 Fusion of finger joint

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: DIP fusion, PIP fusion, finger arthrodesis

Surgical fusion of the interphalangeal joint of a finger without bone graft using direct bone-to-bone apposition and internal fixation.

Clinical Context

Used for interphalangeal joint arthritis, post-traumatic arthritis, or chronic pain where fusion provides functional stability and pain relief.
