46715 Rep perf anoper fistu

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: rectoperineal fistula repair, perineal fistula closure, fistula reconstruction

Repair of a perineal anorectal fistula. Reconstructs communication between rectum and perineal skin.

In Plain Language

fixing a tunnel between the rectum and perineal skin; closure of perineal anal fistula

Clinical Context

Performed for perineal anorectal fistulas, often associated with cloacal anomalies or previous surgery.
