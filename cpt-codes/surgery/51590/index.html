51590 Remove bladder/revise tract

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: cystectomy with extensive reconstruction

Removal of bladder with revision of urinary tract including extensive pelvic reconstruction.

Clinical Context

Used for complex cases requiring cystectomy with additional pelvic reconstruction for neighboring organ repair or involvement.
