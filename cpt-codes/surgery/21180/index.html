21180 Rcnstj forehead with autograft

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: forehead reconstruction autograft, forehead autograft

Reconstruction of the forehead region using autogenous bone graft material harvested from the patient for structural support and contouring.

Clinical Context

Used for significant forehead defects or deformities. Autograft provides superior incorporation and reduced infection risk.
