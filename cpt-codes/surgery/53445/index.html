53445 Insert uro/ves nck sphincter

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: artificial urinary sphincter, AUS implant, sphincter implant

Surgical insertion of an artificial urinary sphincter or vesical neck sphincter to restore continence in males with intrinsic sphincter deficiency.

In Plain Language

implanted continence device; bladder control implant

Clinical Context

Indicated for intrinsic sphincter deficiency from trauma, radiation, prostatectomy, or neurogenic causes. Provides mechanical support for continence.
