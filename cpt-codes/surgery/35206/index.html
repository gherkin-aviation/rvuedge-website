35206 Repair blood vessel dir uxtr

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: upper extremity vessel repair, arm/shoulder vessel repair

Repair of blood vessel injury in the upper extremity using direct suture technique without graft placement.

Clinical Context

Surgical management of upper extremity vascular injuries from penetrating trauma or iatrogenic causes. Direct repair appropriate for clean wounds with minimal contamination and good wound edges amenable to primary anastomosis.
