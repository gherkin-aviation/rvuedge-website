43278 Ercp lesion ablate with dilate

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: ERCP lesion ablation with dilation, ductal lesion ablation, ampullary lesion ablation

Endoscopic retrograde cholangiopancreatography with ablation of a pancreatic or biliary ductal lesion and ductal dilation. This therapeutic procedure removes lesions.

Clinical Context

Used for ampullary adenomas, ductal papillomas, or strictures with suspicious lesions; combines ablation with dilation for improved patency.
