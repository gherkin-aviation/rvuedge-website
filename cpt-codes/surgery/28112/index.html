28112 Part removal of metatarsal

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: distal metatarsal excision, metatarsal head and neck removal, metatarsal distal amputation

Partial excision of metatarsal with distal removal (head and neck). This procedure removes the distal metatarsal including head and neck portions.

Clinical Context

Used for metatarsal head necrosis, symptomatic bony prominences, or failed forefoot surgery. More extensive than simple head removal.
