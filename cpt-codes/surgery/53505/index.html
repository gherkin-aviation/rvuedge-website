53505 Repair of urethra injury

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: urethral injury repair, moderate complexity urethral repair

Surgical repair of an acute or chronic urethral injury with moderate complexity, requiring tissue debridement or minor mobilization.

Clinical Context

Indicated for urethral injuries with tissue damage requiring debridement. Includes single-stage repairs of traumatic injuries with tissue loss.
