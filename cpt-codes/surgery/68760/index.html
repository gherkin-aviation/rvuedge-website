68760 Close tear duct opening

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: punctal closure, punctal plug, canalicular closure

Closure of lacrimal punctum or canaliculus opening using suture, adhesive, or surgical closure technique.

Clinical Context

Used to reduce tear drainage in conditions with excessive tear loss. Common indications include Stevens-Johnson syndrome, graft-versus-host disease, or treatment of dry eye.
