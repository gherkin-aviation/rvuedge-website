21138 Rdctj forehead cntrg & prostc

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: forehead reduction with implant, forehead contouring with prosthesis

Reduction-contouring of the forehead with placement of a prosthetic implant to enhance or reshape the contour.

Clinical Context

Used when reduction alone is insufficient and augmentation is needed for optimal contour or projection.
