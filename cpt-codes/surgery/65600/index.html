65600 Revision of cornea

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: therapeutic contact lens fit, bandage lens placement

Implantation of contact lens for therapeutic purposes.

Clinical Context

Placement of bandage contact lens for severe dry eye, corneal erosion, or post-surgical protection. Provides mechanical protection and pain relief.
