11450 excision skn hdrdnt ax simple/ntrm

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: axillary excision, hidradenitis excision

Excision of skin lesion involving hidden skin fold area such as axilla with simple or intermediate closure. Includes assessment and removal of excessive skin or lymphoid tissue.

Clinical Context

Used for removal of skin abnormalities in axilla, often in context of hidradenitis suppurativa or other axillary pathology with simple wound management.
