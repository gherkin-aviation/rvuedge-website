42725 incision and drainage absc phrngl external appr

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: pharyngeal abscess drainage, external approach drainage

Surgical incision and drainage of a pharyngeal abscess using an external cervical approach through an incision in the neck.

In Plain Language

drainage of deep throat abscess through neck

Clinical Context

External approach drainage of deep pharyngeal or retropharyngeal abscess for large collections or when intraoral approach insufficient.
