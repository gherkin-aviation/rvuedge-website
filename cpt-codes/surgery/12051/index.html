12051 Intmd repair face/millimeters 2.5 centimeters/<

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: Intermediate facial laceration repair, Layered facial wound closure, intermediate face lac repair 2.5cm or less, layered face lac repair

Repair of intermediate complexity laceration on the face or mucous membrane measuring two point five centimeters or less.

Clinical Context

Used for small facial lacerations requiring layered closure technique with attention to cosmetic outcome.
