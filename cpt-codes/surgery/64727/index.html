64727 Internal neurolysis

Surgery

Also known as: internal neurolysis, fascicular dissection, intraneural neurolysis

Surgical procedure performing internal neurolysis, which involves dissection and freeing of individual nerve fascicles from scar tissue or constricting tissue to improve nerve conduction.

Clinical Context

Used for chronic nerve compression or injury where scarring restricts nerve movement and function. Particularly useful for post-traumatic nerve dysfunction or longstanding entrapment.
