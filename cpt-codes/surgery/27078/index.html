27078 Rsect hip tumor incl femur

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: hip tumor resection with femur, hip and femur tumor removal, extended hip tumor resection

Surgical resection of hip tumor including femur (thighbone) with potential reconstruction.

In Plain Language

hip tumor surgery including thighbone; hip and thighbone tumor removal

Clinical Context

Indicated for hip tumors extending into the femoral shaft requiring en bloc resection of hip and femoral components.
