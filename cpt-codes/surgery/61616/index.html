61616 resection/excise lesion skull

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: complex reconstruction, lesion resection with flap

Complex resection of intracranial lesion with extensive bone reconstruction and potential flap reconstruction for defect coverage.

Clinical Context

Performed for extensive skull base tumors requiring both osseous and soft tissue reconstruction for adequate coverage and protection.
