28264 Release of midfoot joint

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: extensive midfoot release, multiple midfoot joint release, complex midfoot decompression

Release of the midfoot joint with extensive dissection or multiple joint involvement. This comprehensive procedure releases complex midfoot pathology.

Clinical Context

Used for complex midfoot arthropathy involving multiple articulations or requiring extensive dissection for adequate release and reconstruction.
