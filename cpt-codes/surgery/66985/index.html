66985 Insert lens prosthesis

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: IOL implantation, lens prosthesis insertion

Insertion of intraocular lens prosthesis following cataract removal, including any necessary manipulation to achieve optimal positioning within the capsular bag or ciliary sulcus.

Clinical Context

Performed after cataract extraction to restore vision; most common approach in modern cataract surgery; can be done primarily (during initial surgery) or secondarily (after previous cataract removal).
