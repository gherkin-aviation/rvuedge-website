69535 Remove part of temporal bone

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: subtotal petrosectomy with neuroplasty, petrous apex surgery, temporal bone resection

Subtotal petrosectomy with neuroplasty, extirpation or removal of temporal bone lesions including petrous apex disease, residual or recurrent cholesteatoma with extended dissection.

Clinical Context

Used for petrous apex cholesteatoma, meningioma, cholesterol granuloma, or residual disease involving the petrous bone requiring neurological decompression.
