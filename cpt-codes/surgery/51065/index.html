51065 Remove ureter calculus

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ureterolithotomy, ureteral calculus removal, open stone extraction

Removal of ureteral calculus (stone) via cystotomy with ureterotomy, alternative coding for open ureteral stone extraction.

Clinical Context

Used for ureteral calculi requiring open surgical approach. Provides direct access to extract stone from ureteral lumen.
