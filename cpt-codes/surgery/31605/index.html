31605 Emer tracheostomy cthyr mem

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: cricothyrotomy, emergency airway, emergency cricothyrotomy

Emergency tracheostomy performed through the cricothyroid membrane (space between cricoid and thyroid cartilage) for rapid airway access in life-threatening airway emergency.

In Plain Language

emergency breathing hole through neck cartilage

Clinical Context

Performed emergently when standard tracheostomy cannot be rapidly performed; provides temporary airway access during resuscitation but typically converted to standard tracheostomy.
