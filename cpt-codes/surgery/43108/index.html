43108 Removal of esophagus

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: esophagectomy with reconstruction, total esophagectomy with graft

Esophagectomy (total removal of the esophagus) via thoracic approach with reconstruction using gastric pull-up, colonic interposition, or jejunal graft.

In Plain Language

removal of esophagus and reconstruction with stomach or bowel

Clinical Context

Performed for esophageal malignancy or severe disease requiring total esophageal replacement. Reconstruction maintains continuity of gastrointestinal tract.
