64451 Njx aa and/or strd nerve nrvtg si joint

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: sacroiliac joint nerve block, sacroiliac joint block, superior cluneal nerve block

Nerve injection for anesthetic and/or steroid administration to a nerve branch supplying the sacroiliac joint with imaging guidance.

In Plain Language

sacroiliac joint nerve numbing injection; SI joint pain injection with imaging

Clinical Context

Used for sacroiliac joint pain, dysfunction, and arthritis. Imaging improves accuracy in targeting small nerve branches supplying the joint.
