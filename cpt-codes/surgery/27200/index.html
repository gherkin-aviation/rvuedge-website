27200 Treat tail bone fracture

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: closed tailbone fracture, closed coccyx treatment

Closed treatment of coccygeal fracture through conservative management without operative intervention.

Clinical Context

Standard approach for isolated coccygeal fractures managed conservatively with analgesics, cushioning, and activity modification.
