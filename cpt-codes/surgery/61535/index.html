61535 Remove brain electrodes

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: electrode removal, brain electrode explant

Craniotomy or craniectomy with removal of chronically implanted brain electrodes used for prior recording or stimulation.

In Plain Language

surgical removal of implanted brain recording device

Clinical Context

Performed after completion of preoperative seizure monitoring or when deep brain stimulation therapy is discontinued. Requires opening brain to remove intracranial electrode grids or leads safely.
