21155 Rcnstj mdfc lefort iii with i

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Le Fort III with maxilla, bipartition with Le Fort I, combined midface advancement

Reconstruction of the midface using Le Fort III osteotomy with concurrent maxillary advancement or Le Fort I, combining multiple facial levels.

In Plain Language

Advancing upper face and jaw together; Complex multi-level facial reconstruction

Clinical Context

Used for severe craniofacial deformities affecting both upper face and maxilla, requiring coordinated advancement.
