53605 Dilate urethra stricture

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: internal urethrotomy, endoscopic urethrotomy, cold knife urethrotomy

Endoscopic dilation of a urethral stricture using an internal urethrotome or endoscopic cutting instrument.

Clinical Context

Used for strictures amenable to incision. More aggressive than mechanical dilation but less morbid than formal urethroplasty.
