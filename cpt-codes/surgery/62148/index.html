62148 Retr bone flap to fix skull

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: bone flap reattachment, skull flap fixation

Reattachment or replacement of previously removed bone flap to restore skull integrity. Add-on procedure for complex cases.

Clinical Context

Used for reattachment of temporarily removed bone flaps during staged neurosurgical procedures or correction of malposition.
