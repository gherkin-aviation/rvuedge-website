20969 Bone/skin graft microvasc

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: bone-skin free flap, osteocutaneous flap

Microvascular free composite graft of bone and skin with vascular anastomosis.

Clinical Context

Used for large defects requiring both bone reconstruction and skin coverage with reliable vascularization.
