33822 Repair pda div<18 years

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: PDA division, pediatric PDA repair, ductus division

Repair of patent ductus arteriosus by division in patients less than 18 years of age; surgical closure of the PDA through complete division and ligation in pediatric patients.

Clinical Context

Performed in symptomatic pediatric patients requiring definitive PDA closure when ligation alone is insufficient or when recurrent shunting occurs in patients under 18 years.
