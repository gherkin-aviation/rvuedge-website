26426 Repair finger/hand tendon

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Mid-palmar repair, Palmar approach repair, Mid-palmar tendon repair

Surgical repair of a finger or hand tendon using a palmar or mid-palmar approach, typically referring to repair of flexor or extensor systems through specialized exposure.

In Plain Language

Repairing a tendon through the palm of the hand; Finger tendon repair using palm approach

Clinical Context

Performed for tendon injuries requiring access through the palm, particularly useful for lacerations involving multiple zones or complex anatomic patterns.
