40840 Reconstruction of mouth

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: mouth reconstruction, oral cavity reconstruction, lip reconstruction

Surgical reconstruction of the oral cavity including the lips and mouth structures using tissue grafts or flaps.

In Plain Language

rebuilding the mouth; oral tissue repair; mouth surgery

Clinical Context

Used for significant defects of the mouth or lips resulting from trauma, cancer resection, congenital defects, or burn injuries. Involves use of grafts or local/regional flaps.
