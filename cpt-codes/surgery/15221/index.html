15221 Fth/gft fr s/a/l each additional

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: full-thickness graft limb additional, extremity pedicle add-on

Full-thickness or pedicle skin graft or flap, from scalp, arm, or leg, each additional 20 square centimeters. Add-on code for extended full-thickness coverage from extremities.

Clinical Context

Used for extended full-thickness coverage when extremity donor sites provide adequate graft material for larger defects.
