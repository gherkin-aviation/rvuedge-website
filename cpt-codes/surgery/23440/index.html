23440 Remove/transplant tendon

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: shoulder tendon transfer, tendon rerouting, tendon removal shoulder

Removal or transfer of shoulder tendon. Rerouting or transfer of shoulder tendon (excluding rotator cuff) for functional restoration or decompression.

In Plain Language

moving a shoulder tendon to another location; shoulder tendon rearrangement

Clinical Context

Applied in subscapularis transfer for posterior instability, biceps transfer, or other tendon transfers to improve shoulder mechanics and function.
