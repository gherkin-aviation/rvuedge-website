37761 Ligate leg veins open

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Leg vein ligation, Open vein ligation

Open surgical approach for ligation of leg veins, typically for venous disease or perforator vein management.

Clinical Context

Used for perforator vein incompetence, venous obstruction, or revision of previous venous surgery in the lower extremity.
