64681 Injection treatment of nerve

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: therapeutic nerve injection multiple sites, multi-site nerve pain injection

Injection of medication to additional nerves or branches for pain relief beyond the primary site.

Clinical Context

Used when multiple nerve sites require medication injection for comprehensive pain relief.
