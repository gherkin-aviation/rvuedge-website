25391 Lengthen radius or ulna

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: radial or ulnar lengthening

Lengthening of the radius or ulna bone through distraction osteogenesis or graft augmentation.

In Plain Language

making forearm bone longer

Clinical Context

Used for shortening from tumor resection, growth disturbances, or length-related instability.
