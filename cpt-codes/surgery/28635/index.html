28635 Treat toe dislocation

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: toe dislocation reduction, lesser toe manipulation, closed reduction

Closed treatment of toe dislocation with manipulation to achieve anatomic reduction, typically involving lesser digit interphalangeal or metatarsophalangeal joints.

In Plain Language

Toe reduction; Toe dislocation reduction; Joint realignment

Clinical Context

Used for displaced lesser toe dislocations; closed reduction restores joint anatomy and functional digit position.
