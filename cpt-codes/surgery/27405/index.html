27405 Repair of knee ligament

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ACL repair, MCL repair, ligament repair, ACL reconstruction

Surgical repair or reconstruction of a single knee ligament including cruciate ligaments (anterior or posterior), collateral ligaments, or posterolateral corner structures.

Clinical Context

Used for acute ligament tears, chronic ligament insufficiency, and knee instability. Very common in athletes and after traumatic knee injuries.
