60545 Explore adrenal gland

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: adrenalectomy, partial adrenalectomy, open adrenalectomy

Surgical exploration of the adrenal gland with partial or complete removal of the gland, typically performed through an open abdominal or flank approach.

Clinical Context

Performed for adrenal adenomas, pheochromocytoma, adrenocortical carcinoma, or metastatic disease. Open approach provides excellent visualization and hemostasis for potentially vascular or malignant lesions.
