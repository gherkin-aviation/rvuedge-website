26554 Double transfer toe-hand

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Double toe transfer, Two-toe reconstruction, Extensive toe transfer

Surgical transfer of two toes to the hand to reconstruct multiple digits, performed using microsurgical techniques for patients with loss of multiple hand digits.

Clinical Context

Indicated for loss of multiple hand digits (typically thumb and one or more fingers) where microsurgical transfer of two toes provides comprehensive reconstruction.
