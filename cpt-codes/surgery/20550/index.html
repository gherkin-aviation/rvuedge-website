20550 Njx 1 tendon sheath/ligament

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: tendon sheath injection, ligament injection

Injection of therapeutic agent into single tendon sheath or ligament for treatment of inflammation or pain. This procedure delivers medication to synovial sheath around tendon.

Clinical Context

Used for treatment of tenosynovitis, tendon sheath inflammation, or ligamentous pain conditions. Provides symptomatic relief and reduces inflammation.
