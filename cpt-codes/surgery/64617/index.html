64617 Chemodener muscle larynx emg

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: spasmodic dysphonia injection, laryngeal dystonia treatment, voice dystonia injection

Chemodenervation of laryngeal muscles for treatment of laryngeal dystonia (spasmodic dysphonia) with electromyography guidance.

Clinical Context

Used for spasmodic dysphonia causing voice dysfunction. Electromyography guidance improves accuracy.
