46705 Repair of anal stricture

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: stricture repair, anoplasty with flap, stricture reconstruction

Repair of anal stricture using alternative reconstruction technique. May involve skin graft or more complex flap reconstruction.

In Plain Language

fixing a narrowed anus with tissue reconstruction; repairing an anal stricture with flap

Clinical Context

Used for severe anal strictures requiring flap anoplasty or skin grafting. May be staged procedure.
