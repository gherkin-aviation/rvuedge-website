62362 Implant spine infusion pump

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: complex spinal pump implant, advanced infusion pump placement

Implantation of spinal cord infusion pump with implantable catheter and complex surgical techniques.

Clinical Context

Used for implantation of more complex programmable infusion pump systems with tunneled catheters.
