64570 Remove vagus n eltrd

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: vagus nerve stimulator removal, VNS device explant, vagus nerve electrode removal

Removal of vagus nerve electrode and associated components of the neuroelectrode stimulation system.

Clinical Context

Used for complications, device malfunction, or patient choice to discontinue vagus nerve stimulation therapy.
