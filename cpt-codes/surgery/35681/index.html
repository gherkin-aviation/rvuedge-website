35681 Composite byp graft pros & vein

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: composite bypass graft, prosthetic-vein composite, hybrid graft

Composite arterial bypass graft using combination of prosthetic material and autogenous vein segments. Used when saphenous vein alone is inadequate.

Clinical Context

Performed when autogenous vein is insufficient in length or quality. Add-on code for composite graft construction.
