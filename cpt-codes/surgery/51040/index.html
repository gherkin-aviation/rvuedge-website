51040 Incise & drainage bladder

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: bladder incision and drainage, cystotomy for abscess

Incision and drainage of bladder abscess, surgical opening and drainage of infected fluid collection within bladder wall.

Clinical Context

Indicated for bladder wall abscess from infection, trauma, or inflammatory process. Open surgical drainage when percutaneous approach not feasible.
