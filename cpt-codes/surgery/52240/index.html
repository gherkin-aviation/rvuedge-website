52240 Cystoscopy and treatment

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: cysto with laser ablation of tumor, TURBT large

Cystourethroscopy with removal of bladder tumor and use of laser energy for ablation and hemostasis, providing precise tissue destruction with minimal tissue damage.

In Plain Language

bladder scope with laser removal of tumor

Clinical Context

Used for non-muscle invasive tumors with advantages of minimal bleeding, reduced fluid absorption, and faster operative time compared to electrocautery.
