63283 biopsy/excision idrl spine lesion scrl

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: sacral intradural extramedullary lesion, sacral schwannoma or meningioma excision

Biopsy and/or excision of intradural extramedullary spinal lesion in the sacral region. This procedure involves sampling or removal of lesions within the dura but outside the spinal cord at sacral levels.

Clinical Context

Used for sacral intradural extramedullary tumors including schwannomas and meningiomas.
