43647 Laparoscopic implant electrode antrum

Surgery

Also known as: lap gastric pacemaker placement, enterra implant, gastric neurostimulation

Laparoscopic implantation of gastric stimulation electrode array in the gastric antrum for gastroparesis.

In Plain Language

stomach pacemaker placement; electrical stomach stimulation device

Clinical Context

Treatment of refractory gastroparesis (delayed gastric emptying) when medical management fails. Enterra device provides electrical stimulation to improve gastric motility.

RVU Information

CPT 43647 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
