35081 Repair defect of artery

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: abdominal aortic repair, AAA defect repair, aortic repair

Repair of a defect of the abdominal aorta (suture or patch angioplasty). Surgical correction of infrarenal aortic defect.

Clinical Context

Used for infrarenal abdominal aortic aneurysm repair. May be elective (aneurysm) or emergency (rupture).
