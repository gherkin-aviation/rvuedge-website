50010 Renal exploration

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: renal exploration, kidney exploration, nephrectomy exploration

Surgical exploration of kidney and renal hilum via open incision for diagnostic purposes.

Clinical Context

Open surgical exploration of kidney and renal hilum to evaluate for stone disease, mass, infection, or vascular abnormality.
