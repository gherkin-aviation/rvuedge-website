46320 Removal of hemorrhoid clot

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: thrombectomy, clot removal, hemorrhoid drainage

Surgical removal of a thrombosed hemorrhoid clot. This procedure evacuates the blood clot from a thrombosed hemorrhoid for acute pain relief.

In Plain Language

removing a blood clot from a hemorrhoid; draining painful hemorrhoid clot

Clinical Context

Performed acutely for thrombosed internal or external hemorrhoids causing severe pain. Most effective within 72 hours of symptom onset.
