64505 N block spenopalatine gangl

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: sphenopalatine ganglion block, pterygopalatine ganglion block, SPG block

Nerve block of the sphenopalatine ganglion through injection of anesthetic and/or steroid medication.

Clinical Context

Used for cluster headaches, facial pain, and migraines. Blocks autonomic and sensory innervation to head and face.
