61690 Intracranial vessel surgery

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: dural AV fistula repair, dural fistula surgery

Open surgical approach to intracranial blood vessel for repair or reconstruction of dural arteriovenous fistula.

Clinical Context

Surgical treatment for high-risk dural fistulas. May follow or complement endovascular therapy.
