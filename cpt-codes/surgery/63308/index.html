63308 Remove vertebral body add-on

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: additional vertebrectomy, vertebral body removal extension

Removal of vertebral body, add-on code for each additional vertebra. Used when vertebrectomy via any approach extends to multiple vertebral bodies.

Clinical Context

Applied when multi-level vertebrectomy is performed regardless of approach used.
