33611 Repair double ventricle

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: DORV repair, double outlet right ventricle repair, intraventricular tunnel

Surgical repair of double-outlet right ventricle (DORV) through intraventricular tunneling or rerouting of blood flow.

Clinical Context

Indicated for double-outlet right ventricle suitable for biventricular repair; achieves separation of systemic and pulmonary circulations through intraventricular tunnel creation.
