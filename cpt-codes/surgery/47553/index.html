47553 Biliary endoscopy thru skin

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: Percutaneous therapeutic cholangioscopy, POCS therapeutic

Percutaneous (through-the-skin) endoscopy of the biliary tree for therapeutic intervention such as stricture dilation, stone removal, or stent manipulation.

Clinical Context

Performed for management of strictures, intrahepatic stones, or other therapeutic interventions. Allows direct visualization and treatment.
