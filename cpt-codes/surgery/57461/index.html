57461 Conz of cervix with scope leep

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: LEEP conization, Loop conization

Conization of cervix with Loop Electrosurgical Excision Procedure (LEEP). Cone-shaped excision of cervix using electrosurgical loop.

Clinical Context

Both diagnostic and therapeutic for cervical dysplasia and carcinoma. Allows for pathologic assessment.
