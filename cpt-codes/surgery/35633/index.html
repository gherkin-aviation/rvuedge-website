35633 Bpg ilio-mesenteric

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: iliac-mesenteric bypass, iliomesenteric bypass, mesenteric revascularization

Arterial bypass graft from iliac artery to superior mesenteric artery for mesenteric ischemia. Restores blood flow to the small intestine and proximal colon.

In Plain Language

lower artery to bowel artery bypass; restoring blood flow to the intestines

Clinical Context

Performed for symptomatic superior mesenteric artery stenosis or occlusion causing chronic abdominal pain and weight loss. Often combined with celiac repair for panvisceral disease.
