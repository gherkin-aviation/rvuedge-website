64449 Njx aa and/or strd lumbar plex nfs

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: lumbar plexus block, psoas compartment block, lumbar plexus block with ultrasound

Nerve injection for anesthetic and/or steroid administration to the lumbar plexus with imaging guidance. Ultrasound or fluoroscopy used to target the nerve plexus.

In Plain Language

lower back nerve bundle numbing injection with imaging; lumbar nerve plexus pain injection

Clinical Context

Used for lower abdominal, hip, and lower extremity pain or anesthesia. Provides broad coverage for major lower extremity surgery with single block.
