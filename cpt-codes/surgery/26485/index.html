26485 Transplant palm tendon

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Palmar tendon transfer, Palm tendon transplant, Intrinsic transfer

Surgical transplantation of a palm tendon to a new location to restore function or improve hand positioning for patients with paralysis or muscle loss.

In Plain Language

Moving a palm tendon to improve hand function; Palmar tendon transplant for restoration

Clinical Context

Indicated for intrinsic muscle paralysis or loss, particularly in ulnar nerve palsy or when intrinsic function requires restoration through transfer of available musculature.
