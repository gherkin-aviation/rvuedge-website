29873 Arthrs knee surgical with lateral rls

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: lateral release, LR procedure, patellar release

Arthroscopy of the knee with lateral retinacular release to correct patellofemoral tracking abnormalities.

Clinical Context

Used for patellofemoral pain syndrome, patellar maltracking, and lateral compression syndrome.
