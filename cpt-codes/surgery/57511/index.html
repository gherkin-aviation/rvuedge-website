57511 Cryocautery of cervix

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: cryotherapy, cervical cryosurgery, cryoablation

Cryocautery of cervix uses extreme cold (liquid nitrogen or cryoprobe) to destroy abnormal cervical tissue through freezing.

Clinical Context

Used for treatment of cervical dysplasia, human papillomavirus-related lesions, and benign cervical polyps. Common modality for cervical intraepithelial neoplasia grade 1 treatment.
