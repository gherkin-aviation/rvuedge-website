29058 application cast plaster velpeau

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: Velpeau cast, immobilizer cast

Application of a Velpeau plaster cast, a restrictive immobilization device that encompasses the shoulder, arm, and torso to maintain reduced position of shoulder fractures or dislocations.

Clinical Context

Used for initial management of proximal humerus fractures, anterior shoulder dislocations, and rotator cuff repairs to prevent arm motion during early healing.
