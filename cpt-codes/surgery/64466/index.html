64466 Thrc fascial pln blk uni njx

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: thoracic fascial plane block, serratus anterior plane block, pectoralis plane block

Thoracic fascial plane block performed with single injection on one side of the chest. Medication is deposited in the fascial plane between muscles.

In Plain Language

chest wall nerve block injection; chest pain numbing shot

Clinical Context

Used for rib fractures, thoracic surgery anesthesia, and post-operative chest wall pain. Fascial plane blocks have fewer complications than traditional nerve blocks.
