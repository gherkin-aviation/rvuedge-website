57545 Remove cervix/repair pelvis

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: cervicectomy with pelvic floor repair, trachelectomy with pelvic repair

Removal of cervix with repair of pelvic floor involves cervicectomy combined with pelvic floor reconstruction to address concurrent pelvic relaxation or defects.

In Plain Language

Cervix removal and pelvic repair; Cervix and pelvic tissue repair

Clinical Context

Addresses both cervical pathology and pelvic floor dysfunction in a single procedure. Used when cervical removal is needed and pelvic floor defect repair is indicated.
