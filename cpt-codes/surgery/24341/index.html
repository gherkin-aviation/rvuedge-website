24341 repair tdn/muscle upper a/e each

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: tendon repair, muscle repair, upper arm repair

Repair of tendon or muscle of the upper arm and elbow region, each unit. Surgical repair of torn or damaged tendon or muscle.

Clinical Context

Used for repair of acute or chronic tendon or muscle tears of the upper arm and elbow, addressing traumatic injuries or degenerative conditions.
