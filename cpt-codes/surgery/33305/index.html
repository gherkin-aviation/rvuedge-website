33305 Repair of heart wound

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: complex cardiac wound repair, heart wound repair with bypass

Repair of traumatic heart or pericardial wound with cardiopulmonary bypass, providing circulatory support for complex repairs.

Clinical Context

Used for extensive cardiac injuries or when bypass is needed for hemodynamic stability.
