39402 Mediastinoscpy with lmph nod biopsy

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: mediastinoscopy with lymph node biopsy, mediastinal lymph node sampling

Mediastinoscopy with biopsy of lymph nodes within the mediastinum. This minimally invasive endoscopic procedure allows visualization and lymph node tissue sampling.

Clinical Context

Primary staging procedure for lung cancer to assess mediastinal lymph node involvement. Critical for determining operability and treatment planning in lung malignancies.
