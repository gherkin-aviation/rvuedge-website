49412 Ins device for right guide open

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: device insertion for guidance, navigation marker placement, intraoperative guidance device

Insertion of imaging or navigation device for percutaneous radiotherapy guidance during open surgical procedure.

Clinical Context

Insertion of imaging or navigation devices to guide percutaneous biopsy or ablation during open procedure. Used for real-time intraoperative localization.
