27027 Dcmprn fasct pel compartment

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: pelvic compartment release, compartment decompression, pelvic fasciotomy

Surgical decompression and fasciotomy of the pelvic and hip compartments to treat compartment syndrome.

In Plain Language

relieving pelvic compartment pressure; hip compartment decompression

Clinical Context

Used for pelvic compartment syndrome with life-threatening complications or significant soft tissue damage requiring emergency decompression.
