35091 Repair defect of artery

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: abdominal aorta valve repair, aortic valve defect repair

Repair of a defect of the abdominal aorta with aortic valve involvement (suture or patch angioplasty). Surgical correction with valve repair/replacement.

Clinical Context

Used for aortic dissection involving visceral aorta with aortic regurgitation. Requires complex reconstructive surgery.
