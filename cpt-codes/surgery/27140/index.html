27140 Transplant femur ridge

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: femoral ridge transfer, greater trochanter transfer, trochanteric transfer

Surgical transfer and transplant of femoral ridge (greater trochanter) to improve hip abduction or address muscle imbalance.

In Plain Language

moving hip bone ridge; trochanteric transfer surgery

Clinical Context

Indicated for hip abductor deficiency or weakness requiring surgical transfer of the greater trochanter for functional improvement.
