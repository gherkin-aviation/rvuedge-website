21932 excision back tumor deep < 5 centimeters

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: deep back lesion small excision, small deep back tumor removal

Excision of deep lesion or tumor of back, less than 5 centimeters. Surgical removal of deep back lesion extending into muscle or deeper structures, with lesion under 5 centimeters.

Clinical Context

Used for deep back pathology of moderate size under 5 centimeters requiring removal through deeper tissue planes. Less extensive than larger deep lesions but still requiring careful dissection.
