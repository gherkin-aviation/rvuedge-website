26518 Fusion of knuckle joints

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Complex knuckle fusion, Extensive multi-joint fusion, Advanced arthrodesis

Surgical fusion of multiple knuckle joints with more extensive arthrodesis or complex technique, performed for severe polyarticular disease or specific functional requirements.

In Plain Language

Complex surgery to fuse multiple finger joints; Extensive joint fusion for severe arthritis

Clinical Context

Indicated for severe polyarticular hand arthropathy, multiple joint involvement, or when specialized fusion techniques are required for optimal outcomes.
