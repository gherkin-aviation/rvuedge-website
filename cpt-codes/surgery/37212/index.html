37212 Thrombolytic venous therapy

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: venous thrombolysis, intra-venous thrombolysis, venous CDT

Catheter-directed thrombolytic therapy delivered intra-venously to dissolve acute venous thrombosis and restore venous patency.

Clinical Context

Used for acute deep vein thrombosis, pulmonary embolism, and venous graft thrombosis. Preserves venous valve function and reduces post-thrombotic syndrome.
