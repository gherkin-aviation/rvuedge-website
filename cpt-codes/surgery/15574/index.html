15574 Pedcle fh/ch/ch/m/n/ax/g/h/f

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: facial pedicle flap, specialized flap

Pedicle flap transfer to face, chin, cheek, mouth, neck, axilla, genitals, hands, or feet. Specialized pedicle flap for cosmetically critical areas.

Clinical Context

Used for facial reconstruction, hand injuries, and genital repair where pedicle flaps maintain excellent cosmetic and functional outcomes.
