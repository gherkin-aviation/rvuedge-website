52276 Cystoscopy and treatment

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: cysto with urethral incision and steroid injection

Cystourethroscopy with initial endoscopic incision of strictured urethra followed by steroid injection to reduce inflammation and improve healing, reducing stricture recurrence.

Clinical Context

Newer technique showing improved outcomes in urethral stricture treatment. Steroid reduces fibroblast proliferation and inflammation.
