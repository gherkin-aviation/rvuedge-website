60661 Ablation 1/+thyr ndul additional prq

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: additional thyroid nodule ablation, ablation of contralateral nodule, multiple nodule ablation

Ablation of one or more additional thyroid nodules in different lobe(s) or ipsilateral additional nodules using percutaneous energy-based techniques, reported as an add-on code.

In Plain Language

treatment of additional thyroid nodules; treatment of thyroid nodules in other lobe

Clinical Context

Used when ablating nodules in contralateral lobe or multiple ipsilateral nodules beyond the initial procedure. Allows comprehensive treatment of multifocal nodular disease in single session.
