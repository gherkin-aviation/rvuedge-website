49442 Place cecostomy tube percutaneous

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: percutaneous cecostomy, PEC tube, cecostomy tube placement

Placement of cecostomy tube via percutaneous approach under imaging guidance for colonic drainage or venting.

In Plain Language

placement of drainage tube in large intestine; colon venting or irrigation tube

Clinical Context

Percutaneous placement of cecostomy tube for colonic drainage, decompression, or administration of medications. Used in obstruction or fecal incontinence.
