58662 Laparoscopy excise lesions

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: laparoscopic ovarian cystectomy, laparoscopic endometrioma removal

Laparoscopy with excision of lesions of ovary and fallopian tube involves endoscopic removal of pathologic lesions including endometrioma, cysts, or tumor.

Clinical Context

Treats endometrioma, benign ovarian cysts, or other ovarian pathology. Preserves ovarian tissue when possible. Important for fertility preservation.
