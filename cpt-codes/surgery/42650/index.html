42650 Dilation of salivary duct

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: duct dilation, salivary duct dilation

Surgical dilation of a salivary gland duct obstruction using instruments or catheterization to restore salivary flow.

Clinical Context

Non-surgical or minimally invasive treatment of salivary duct obstruction or stenosis by dilation to restore salivary gland function.
