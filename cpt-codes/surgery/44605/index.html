44605 Repair of bowel lesion

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: bowel lesion repair, bowel injury repair, traumatic bowel repair

Repair of bowel lesion including small intestinal perforation, laceration, or traumatic injury via open approach, often requiring resection when primary repair is not feasible.

Clinical Context

Used for complex bowel injuries requiring more extensive repair including possible limited resection with anastomosis, or repair of contaminated or devitalized segments.
