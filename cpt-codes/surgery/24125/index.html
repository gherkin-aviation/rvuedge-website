24125 excision/curettage bone cyst/tumor rds agrf

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: radius cyst excision with graft, radial bone tumor removal with autograft

Excision or curettage of a benign cyst or tumor of the radius including autograft. Addresses pathologic lesions in the radius requiring removal and bone graft reconstruction.

Clinical Context

Used for benign bone lesions of the radius requiring excision with autograft bone reconstruction to restore bone continuity and strength.
