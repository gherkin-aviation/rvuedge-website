17284 destruction mal ls f/e/e/n/l/m3.1-4

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: destroy malignant 3.1-4.0 face

Destruction of malignant skin lesion (face, ears, eyelids, nose, lips, mucous membrane) with largest diameter between 3.1 and 4.0 centimeters.

Clinical Context

Used for larger facial malignancies measuring 3.1-4.0 cm. Often requires reconstruction with local or regional flaps.
