32036 Thoracostomy with flap drainage

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: thoracostomy with flap drainage, flap dependent drainage

Surgical opening of the chest cavity (thoracostomy) with creation of a flap-based drainage approach, providing dependent drainage for chronic empyema or pleural space infection.

Clinical Context

Used for chronic empyema, bronchopleural fistula, or extensive pleural space infection requiring flap-based open drainage for prolonged healing.
