65114 Remove eye/revise socket

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: evisceration with extensive socket revision

Surgical removal of the eye and surrounding orbital contents with extensive socket revision including potential bone grafting or implantation for optimal reconstruction.

Clinical Context

Most complex orbit removal procedure with significant socket reconstruction. Higher relative value reflects extensive revision and potential bone grafting required.
