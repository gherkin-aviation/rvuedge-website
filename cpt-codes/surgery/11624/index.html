11624 excision s/n/h/f/g mal+mrg 3.1-4

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: head malignant excision, facial cancer removal

Excision of malignant skin lesion on scalp, neck, head, face, or genitalia with margins of 3.1 to 4 centimeters. Includes closure of the excision site.

Clinical Context

Used for removal of melanoma or malignant skin cancers on head and neck with extensive surgical margins.
