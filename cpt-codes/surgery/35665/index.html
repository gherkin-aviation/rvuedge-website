35665 Bpg iliofemoral

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: iliofemoral bypass, iliac-femoral bypass, iliac-fem

Arterial bypass graft from iliac artery to femoral artery for iliac or proximal femoral occlusion. Restores blood flow to the lower extremity.

Clinical Context

Used for symptomatic iliac or proximal femoral artery disease. May be stage 1 of more extensive repair.
