35011 Repair defect of artery

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: extremity artery defect repair, limb artery repair

Repair of a defect of an extremity artery (suture or patch angioplasty). Surgical correction of arterial defect in arm or leg.

Clinical Context

Used for peripheral arterial defects including aneurysms, pseudoaneurysms, or traumatic injury to extremity arteries.
