64643 Chemodenerv 1 extremity 1-4 each

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: extremity chemodenervation additional muscle groups

Chemodenervation of one extremity for each additional set of one to four muscles beyond the first group.

Clinical Context

Used when additional muscle groups in one extremity require treatment.
