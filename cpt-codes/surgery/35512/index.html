35512 Artery byp graft subclav-brchial

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: subclavian-brachial bypass, SB bypass

Arterial bypass graft from subclavian to brachial artery with restoration of forearm and hand perfusion.

Clinical Context

Revascularization for brachial artery occlusion or stenosis causing hand ischemia when proximal correction not possible. Direct approach bypasses diseased segments.
