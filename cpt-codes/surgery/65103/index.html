65103 Remove eye/insert implant

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: enucleation with implant, eye removal with implant

Surgical removal of the eye (enucleation) with simultaneous insertion of an orbital implant to maintain orbital volume and support prosthetics.

Clinical Context

Performed when enucleation required and orbital implant insertion desired for improved esthetics and function with prosthetic eye.
