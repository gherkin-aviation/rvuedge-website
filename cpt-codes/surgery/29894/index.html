29894 Ankle arthroscopy/surgery

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ankle scope, diagnostic ankle arthroscopy, ankle exploration

Arthroscopic diagnosis and evaluation of ankle joint pathology, allowing visualization of intra-articular structures without therapeutic intervention.

In Plain Language

ankle joint camera procedure; ankle joint inspection

Clinical Context

Performed when imaging is inconclusive or when internal derangement is suspected; useful for evaluating unexplained ankle pain, instability, or mechanical symptoms.
