42950 Reconstruction of throat

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: pharyngeal reconstruction, throat reconstruction with flap

Reconstruction of the pharynx (throat) including replacement of pharyngeal tissue with flap or graft following resection or trauma.

Clinical Context

Performed following pharyngeal resection for cancer, trauma, or extensive disease. Restores continuity and function using local, regional, or free flap tissue.
