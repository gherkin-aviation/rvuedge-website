23921 Disarticulation sho sec closure

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: shoulder disarticulation with closure, SD with flap closure, staged shoulder disarticulation

Disarticulation of shoulder with secondary closure (closure of residual wound). Shoulder disarticulation with subsequent soft tissue reconstruction or closure.

Clinical Context

Secondary procedure following initial shoulder disarticulation when delayed closure is required for infection control, flap design, or tissue healing optimization.
