47370 Laparoscopic ablate liver tumor rf

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: laparoscopic radiofrequency ablation, laparoscopic RFA, minimally invasive liver ablation

Laparoscopic ablation of liver tumor using radiofrequency energy. Minimally invasive thermal destruction of hepatic lesion.

In Plain Language

burning a liver tumor using a scope and radiofrequency probe; laparoscopic tumor ablation

Clinical Context

Performed for hepatocellular carcinoma or metastases less than 5 centimeters. Lower morbidity than resection.
