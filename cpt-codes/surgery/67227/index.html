67227 destruction extensive retinopathy

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: extensive laser treatment, pan-retinal destruction, severe retinopathy treatment

Destructive laser treatment of extensive areas of retinopathy affecting large retinal surface area, used for severe proliferative or advanced ischemic disease.

In Plain Language

extensive laser treatment of retinopathy; widespread burn treatment for eye disease

Clinical Context

Applied to severe proliferative diabetic retinopathy, advanced retinal vein occlusion, or extensive retinal ischemia; typically multiple sessions required.
