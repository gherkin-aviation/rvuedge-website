62326 Njx interlaminar lumbar/sacral

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: CT-guided interlaminar injection lumbar, fluoroscopic interlaminar lumbar injection

Injection of therapeutic or diagnostic agent via interlaminar approach to lumbar or sacral spinal cord region with computed tomography or fluoroscopic guidance.

In Plain Language

CT or x-ray guided spine injection lower spine; advanced imaging guided lumbar injection

Clinical Context

Used for precise image-guided delivery of therapeutic agents to lumbar or sacral spinal cord region.
