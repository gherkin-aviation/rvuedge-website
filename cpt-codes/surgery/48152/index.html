48152 Pancreatectomy

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Total pancreaticoduodenectomy, extended whipple

Total pancreaticoduodenectomy with en bloc resection of pancreatic head, entire pancreas, duodenum, common bile duct, and regional lymph nodes.

In Plain Language

Removing entire pancreas, duodenum, and bile duct

Clinical Context

Performed for extensive pancreatic cancer or when body/tail involvement requires total pancreatic resection.

This code has a 90-day global period, which includes the day of the procedure, 1 day preoperative, and 90 days of postoperative care.
