63281 biopsy/excision idrl spine lesion thrc

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: thoracic intradural extramedullary lesion, thoracic meningioma or schwannoma excision

Biopsy and/or excision of intradural extramedullary spinal lesion in the thoracic region. This procedure involves sampling or removal of lesions within the dura but outside the spinal cord at thoracic levels.

In Plain Language

removal of growth inside spinal sac but outside spinal cord in mid-back

Clinical Context

Used for thoracic meningiomas, schwannomas, or other intradural extramedullary tumors.
