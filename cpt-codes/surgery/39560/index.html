39560 Resection diaphragm simple

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: diaphragm resection, partial diaphragmectomy

Surgical resection of a portion of the diaphragm for tumor removal or treatment. This is a simpler diaphragm resection without extensive reconstruction.

Clinical Context

Used for primary diaphragmatic tumors or invasion from adjacent structures. Less common than hernia repair. Often requires reconstruction depending on extent of resection.
