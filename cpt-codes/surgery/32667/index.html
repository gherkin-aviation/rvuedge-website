32667 Thoracoscopy with w resection additional

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: additional VATS wedge, VATS wedge add-on

Add-on code for additional thoracoscopic wedge resection(s) performed in conjunction with another primary thoracoscopic procedure.

Clinical Context

Used when multiple wedge resections are needed during same operation, such as resection of satellite nodules during thoracoscopic lobectomy.
