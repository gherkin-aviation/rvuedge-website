69955 Release facial nerve

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: extended facial nerve decompression, facial nerve release petrous apex

Surgical decompression or release of the facial nerve within the temporal bone or petrous apex with extended dissection.

Clinical Context

Used for traumatic injury, temporal bone fracture, or petrous apex lesion requiring decompression.
