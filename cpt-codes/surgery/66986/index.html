66986 Exchange lens prosthesis

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: IOL exchange, lens exchange, IOL repositioning

Exchange of an existing intraocular lens prosthesis for a new intraocular lens prosthesis, including manipulation and repositioning of lens elements.

Clinical Context

Used when patient's vision goals change, incorrect lens power implanted, or original lens becomes decentered or damaged; occurs weeks to years after initial implantation.
