33207 Insert heart pm ventricular

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ventricular lead insertion, right ventricular lead, V lead pacemaker, pacemaker insertion ventricular

Insertion of single ventricular electrode/lead for pacemaker or sensing function. Transvenous placement of ventricular lead.

In Plain Language

placing pacemaker wire in lower heart chamber

Clinical Context

Used for ventricular pacing in heart block or bradycardia. Essential for single-chamber ventricular pacemakers or as part of dual-chamber systems.
