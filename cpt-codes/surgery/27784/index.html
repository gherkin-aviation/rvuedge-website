27784 Treatment of fibula fracture

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: open reduction fibular fracture, fibular ORIF

Initial open treatment of fibular shaft fracture with direct visualization and internal fixation.

Clinical Context

Performed for open fibular fractures or when operative fixation is indicated. Allows anatomic reduction.
