49406 Image catheter fluid peri-procedural/retro

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: image-guided catheter placement, percutaneous drain, peritoneal drainage

Image-guided percutaneous catheter placement in peritoneal or retroperitoneal fluid collection for drainage.

Clinical Context

Percutaneous catheter placement under imaging guidance for drainage of peritoneal or retroperitoneal fluid collections. Common indications are ascites, infected fluid, or postoperative collections.
