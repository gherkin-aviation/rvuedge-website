21137 Rdctj forehead cntrg only

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: forehead reduction, forehead contouring, bossing reduction

Reduction-contouring of the forehead using burring or bone removal alone, without implant or bone graft placement.

Clinical Context

Used for excessive frontal bossing, aesthetic improvement, or to correct deformity from trauma or disease.
