67229 Tr retinal lesion preterm inferior

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ROP treatment, retinopathy of prematurity surgery, posterior ROP management

Treatment of retinal lesion in premature infant retinopathy, including vitrectomy and advanced membrane dissection for stage 4 or 5 retinopathy of prematurity.

Clinical Context

Performed in infants with advanced retinopathy of prematurity; vitrectomy and membrane dissection aim to restore retinal anatomy and improve visual potential.
