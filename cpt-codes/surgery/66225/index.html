66225 Repair/graft eye lesion

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: corneal scleral wound repair, trauma repair

Repair or graft of wounds of cornea and/or sclera; not otherwise specified.

Clinical Context

Repair or grafting of corneal-scleral wounds not fitting other specific codes. Includes complex trauma with combined anterior segment injury.
