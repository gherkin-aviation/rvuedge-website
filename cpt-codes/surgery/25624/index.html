25624 Cltx carpl scphd fracture with mnpj

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: closed reduction with manipulation scaphoid, percutaneous reduction scaphoid

Closed treatment (reduction) of carpal scaphoid bone fracture with manipulation to improve alignment.

In Plain Language

realigning broken wrist bone without surgery; manipulated scaphoid fracture treatment

Clinical Context

Used for displaced scaphoid fractures requiring manual reduction to restore alignment, then maintained in immobilization. Appropriate when closed reduction achieves acceptable alignment.
