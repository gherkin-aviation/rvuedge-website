50974 Ureter endoscopy & biopsy

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: ureteroscopy with biopsy, transurethral ureteral biopsy

Endoscopy of ureter with biopsy via transurethral approach to obtain tissue samples for diagnostic evaluation.

Clinical Context

Indicated for ureteral lesion or mass evaluation via retrograde approach. Provides histologic diagnosis for malignancy or stricture.
