64868 Fusion of facial/other nerve

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: facial nerve to CN fusion, cranial nerve transfer

Surgical fusion or transfer of the facial nerve to another cranial nerve for restoration of facial function. May connect to hypoglossal, accessory, or other cranial nerve.

Clinical Context

Advanced technique for extensive facial nerve injuries where primary repair impossible. Typically hypoglossal-facial anastomosis performed for long-standing paralysis.
