35111 Repair defect of artery

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: aortic visceral repair, aortic branch repair, juxtarenal aortic repair

Repair of a defect of the aorta and its visceral branches (suture or patch angioplasty). Surgical correction of aortic defect with branch vessel involvement.

In Plain Language

surgical repair of aorta and its side branches

Clinical Context

Used for juxtarenal aortic aneurysm requiring celiac, SMA, and/or renal artery reconstruction. Complex vascular surgery.
