22865 removal tot arthrp 1ntrspc lumbar

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Lumbar ADIS removal, Lumbar disc arthroplasty removal

Removal of total disc arthroplasty (including endplate preparation), single interspace; lumbar.

Clinical Context

Used for failed lumbar total disc arthroplasty; removal may be followed by fusion or conservative management.
