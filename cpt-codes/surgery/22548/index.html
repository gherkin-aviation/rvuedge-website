22548 Arthrd anterior toral/xoral c1-c2

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Anterior cervical fusion, C1-C2 fusion, ACDF

Arthrodesis, anterior/anterolateral approach, including discectomy and/or corpectomy to decompress spinal cord and/or nerve roots, cervical; including C1-C2.

In Plain Language

Neck spinal fusion through front approach; Bone grafting for neck instability

Clinical Context

Used for C1-C2 instability from trauma, odontoid fractures, or ligamentous injuries; anterior approach provides direct access to upper cervical spine.
