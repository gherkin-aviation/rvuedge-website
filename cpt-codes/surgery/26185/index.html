26185 Remove finger bone

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: phalangeal excision, finger bone removal

Removal (excision) of finger bone (including base or entire bone).

In Plain Language

removal of finger bone; partial finger amputation

Clinical Context

Used for excision of phalangeal bone in finger (proximal, middle, or distal phalanx), such as for arthritis, tumor, or severe injury.
