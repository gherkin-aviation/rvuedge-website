17264 destruction mal lesion t/a/l 3.1-4.0

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: destroy malignant 3.1-4.0, skin cancer large

Destruction of malignant skin lesion (trunk, arms, legs) with largest diameter between 3.1 and 4.0 centimeters.

Clinical Context

Used for larger malignant lesions on body measuring 3.1-4.0 cm. May require margin assessment and deeper excision.
