21920 Biopsy soft tissue of back

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: back soft tissue biopsy, back lesion biopsy

Biopsy of soft tissue of back. Tissue sampling from back lesion via needle aspiration, core needle biopsy, or open biopsy for histologic diagnosis.

In Plain Language

tissue sample from back mass; tissue test from back lesion

Clinical Context

Used for diagnostic evaluation of back lesions of uncertain etiology. Method varies from needle aspiration for cystic lesions to open biopsy for solid masses. Essential for differentiating malignancy, infection, or benign pathology.
