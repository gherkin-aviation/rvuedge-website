19325 Breast augmentation with implant

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: breast augmentation, augmentation mammoplasty, breast implant

Surgical augmentation of the breast using prosthetic implants to increase breast volume and improve contour. Implants may be placed above or below the pectoral muscle.

In Plain Language

breast enlargement with implants

Clinical Context

Used to increase breast volume for cosmetic enhancement, reconstruct breasts after mastectomy, or correct asymmetry. Patient consultation regarding implant type and placement is essential.
