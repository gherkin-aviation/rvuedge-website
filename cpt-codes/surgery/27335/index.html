27335 Remove knee joint lining

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: arthroscopic synovectomy, arthroscopic synovial removal

Removal of knee joint synovial lining through arthroscopic approach to treat synovitis or inflammatory disease.

Clinical Context

Indicated for inflammatory knee conditions managed arthroscopically with reduced morbidity compared to open synovectomy.
