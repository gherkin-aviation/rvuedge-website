Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: robotic water-jet prostate resection, Aquabeam TURP

Transurethral robotic water-jet resection of prostate tissue for benign prostatic hyperplasia, using advanced robotic technology with water-jet energy for tissue ablation.

Clinical Context

Newer technology offering high precision resection with minimal thermal injury. Potentially better functional outcomes than standard TURP.
