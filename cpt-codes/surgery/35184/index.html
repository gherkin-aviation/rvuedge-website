35184 repair cgen av fistula xtr

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: congenital AV fistula extremity, limb AVF repair

Repair of congenital arteriovenous fistula in the extremity with vessel reconstruction or ligation.

Clinical Context

Surgical treatment of congenital arteriovenous fistulas in arms or legs presenting with limb swelling, steal phenomenon, or functional impairment. Often requires venous reconstruction to restore appropriate hemodynamics.
