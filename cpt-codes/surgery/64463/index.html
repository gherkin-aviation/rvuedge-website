64463 Pvb thoracic cont infusion

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: thoracic paravertebral catheter, continuous PVB, thoracic paravertebral continuous infusion

Paravertebral nerve block for thoracic region with continuous infusion of anesthetic and/or steroid medication through a catheter.

Clinical Context

Used for post-operative pain control in thoracic surgery patients requiring prolonged anesthesia. Catheter remains in place for hours to days.
