37192 Redo endovas vena cava filtr

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: IVC filter repositioning, IVC filter revision, IVC filter redo

Repositioning, replacement, or additional component placement for a previously inserted inferior vena cava filter.

Clinical Context

Performed when initial filter placement is suboptimal, malpositioned, or has failed. Maintains protection against pulmonary embolism.
