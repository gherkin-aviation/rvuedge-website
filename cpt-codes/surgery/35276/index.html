35276 repair blvs gr ot/t vn ntrth wo

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: thoracic prosthetic repair, intrathoracic synthetic reconstruction

Repair of blood vessel injury in the intrathoracic region using prosthetic or arterial graft without bypass.

Clinical Context

Thoracic vascular reconstruction with prosthetic interposition graft in injuries not requiring bypass. PTFE or Dacron provides durable conduit for medium-term patency.
