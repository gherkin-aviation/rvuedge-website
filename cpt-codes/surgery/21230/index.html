21230 Rib cartilage graft

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: rib cartilage graft, costal cartilage graft

Graft of rib cartilage to the facial skeleton for structural support or augmentation of facial contours.

Clinical Context

Used for nasal reconstruction, chin augmentation, or malar augmentation. Provides natural contour and durability.
