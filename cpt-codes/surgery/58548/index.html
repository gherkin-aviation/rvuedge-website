58548 Laparoscopic radical hyst

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: laparoscopic radical hysterectomy, laparoscopic Wertheim procedure

Laparoscopic radical hysterectomy is an oncologic procedure removing uterus, cervix, parametrial tissue, ovaries and fallopian tubes endoscopically for cervical cancer.

In Plain Language

Minimally invasive cancer hysterectomy; Laparoscopic extensive cancer removal

Clinical Context

Oncologic alternative to open radical hysterectomy for early-stage cervical cancer. May include pelvic lymphadenectomy. Significant operative time with higher morbidity risk than simple hysterectomy.
