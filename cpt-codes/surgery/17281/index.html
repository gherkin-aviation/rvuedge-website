17281 destruction mal ls f/e/e/n/l/m .6-1

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: destroy malignant 0.6-1.0 face

Destruction of malignant skin lesion (face, ears, eyelids, nose, lips, mucous membrane) with largest diameter between 0.6 and 1.0 centimeter.

Clinical Context

Used for small malignant lesions on facial structures where cosmetic preservation is critical. Common location for basal cell carcinoma.
