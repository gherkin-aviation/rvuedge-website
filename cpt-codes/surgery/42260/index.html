42260 Repair nose to lip fistula

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: fistula repair, oro-nasal fistula closure

Surgical repair of an oronasal fistula, an abnormal communication between the mouth and nasal cavity, by primary closure or tissue reconstruction.

In Plain Language

repair of hole between mouth and nose

Clinical Context

Surgical correction of fistula from cleft palate, trauma, infection, or post-operative complications affecting palate and nasal structures.
