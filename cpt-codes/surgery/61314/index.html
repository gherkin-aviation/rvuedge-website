61314 craniectomy/craniotomy ittl xdrl/sdrl

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: posterior fossa extradural decompression, infratentorial decompression

Craniotomy or craniectomy with exploration and/or decompression of extradural infratentorial (outside dura in posterior fossa) space.

Clinical Context

Performed for posterior fossa epidural hematoma, extradural mass effect, or decompression of infratentorial pathology. Less common than supratentorial extradural decompression.
