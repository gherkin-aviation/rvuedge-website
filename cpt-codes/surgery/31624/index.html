31624 Diagnosis bronchoscope/lavage

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: diagnostic bronchoscopy with lavage, BAL, bronchoalveolar wash

Diagnostic bronchoscopy with bronchoalveolar lavage, collecting fluid and cells from lower airways for diagnostic analysis in evaluation of suspected pulmonary pathology.

Clinical Context

Performed for evaluation of pulmonary infections, aspiration, inflammation, or malignancy where alveolar cell and fluid analysis is diagnostically valuable.
