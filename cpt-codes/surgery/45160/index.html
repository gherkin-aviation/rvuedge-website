45160 Excision of rectal lesion

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: rectal lesion excision, rectal tumor excision, full-thickness rectal excision

Excision of benign or malignant rectal lesion, performed through full-thickness rectal wall excision for localized disease.

Clinical Context

Applied to early rectal cancers (T1-T2) amenable to local excision, benign lesions (polyps, adenomas), or strictures requiring definitive excision.
