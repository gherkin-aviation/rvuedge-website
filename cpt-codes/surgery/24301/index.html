24301 muscle/tdn transfer upper a/e 1

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: muscle transfer, tendon transfer, muscle-tendon transfer

Muscle or tendon transfer of the upper arm and elbow region, single transfer. Surgical relocation of muscle or tendon to restore function.

In Plain Language

Moving a muscle or tendon from one place to another in the arm; Transfer of arm muscle to restore movement

Clinical Context

Used to restore function after nerve injuries, paralysis, or other conditions causing loss of muscle function in the upper arm or elbow.
