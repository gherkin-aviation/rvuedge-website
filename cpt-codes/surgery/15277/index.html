15277 Skn sub graft f/n/hf/g child

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: pediatric facial skin substitute, child specialized graft

Skin substitute graft applied to face, neck, hands, feet, genitals, or other specialized anatomical areas in pediatric patients. Bioengineered or acellular substitute for children.

In Plain Language

artificial skin for child's face or sensitive area; pediatric specialized substitute

Clinical Context

Used for pediatric facial burns and hand injuries where skin substitutes minimize complications in children.
