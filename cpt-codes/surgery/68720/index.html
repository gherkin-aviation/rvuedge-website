68720 Create tear sacral drainage

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: dacryocystorhinostomy, DCR bypass, lacrimal bypass

Creation of nasolacrimal duct drainage by bypass procedure (dacryocystorhinostomy) for chronic obstruction.

Clinical Context

Used for chronic nasolacrimal duct obstruction. Common indications include chronic dacryocystitis, nasolacrimal duct obstruction, or failed probing.
