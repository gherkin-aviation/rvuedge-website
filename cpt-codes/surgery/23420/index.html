23420 Repair of shoulder

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: shoulder repair non-RC, soft tissue repair shoulder, deltoid repair

Repair of shoulder (non-rotator cuff). Repair of shoulder structures excluding rotator cuff, such as coracoacromial ligament, deltoid, or other soft tissue repair.

In Plain Language

repair of shoulder tissues (not rotator cuff); shoulder muscle or ligament repair

Clinical Context

Used for deltoid injuries, coracoacromial ligament repair, superior capsular reconstruction without cuff involvement, or other non-cuff shoulder soft tissue injuries.
