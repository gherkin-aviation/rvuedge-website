36260 Insertion of infusion pump

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: infusion pump insertion, intraarterial pump placement, hepatic artery pump

Surgical placement of indwelling intra-arterial infusion pump and catheter system for chemotherapy, pain medication, or other infused drugs. Implantable vascular access device.

Clinical Context

Used for regional chemotherapy (hepatic artery infusion for liver cancer) or chronic pain management.
