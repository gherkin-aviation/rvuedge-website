51596 Remove bladder/create pouch

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: cystectomy with neobladder, continent pouch creation

Removal of bladder with creation of pouch or continent mechanism, alternative coding for cystectomy with neobladder.

Clinical Context

Similar to 51595, provides continent urinary diversion. Used interchangeably for cystectomy with continent pouch.
