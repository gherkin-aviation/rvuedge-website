15774 Gfrg autol fat lipo each additional

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: fat grafting small additional, minimal lipofilling add-on

Autologous fat injection or transplantation harvested by liposuction, each additional 25 cubic centimeters. Add-on code for extended small volume fat grafting.

Clinical Context

Used for extended small volume fat grafting when initial 25 cubic centimeter increment is insufficient.
