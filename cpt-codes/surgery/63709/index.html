63709 Repair spinal fluid leakage

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: CSF leak repair with dural graft, pseudomeningocele repair with graft

Repair of spinal cord cerebrospinal fluid leak or pseudomeningocele with dural graft placement. This more extensive procedure involves closure of cerebrospinal fluid leak with additional dural grafting.

Clinical Context

Used for more complex cerebrospinal fluid leaks or pseudomeningoceles requiring dural grafting for secure closure.
