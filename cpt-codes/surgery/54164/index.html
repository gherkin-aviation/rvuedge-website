54164 Frenulotomy of penis

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: frenulotomy, frenulum division

Surgical division (cutting) of the penile frenulum to increase range of motion or treat frenulum breve.

Clinical Context

Used for frenulum breve causing pain during erection or intercourse. Frenulotomy improves mobility and reduces discomfort.
