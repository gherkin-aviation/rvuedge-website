61340 Subtemporal cranial dcmprn

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: subtemporal decompression, subtemporal approach

Subtemporal cranial approach with decompression of intracranial contents and/or space, typically for skull base lesions or expanded access.

In Plain Language

surgical opening behind temple to access brain

Clinical Context

Used for skull base tumors, large aneurysms, cavernous sinus lesions, or decompression of structures in sella turcica region. Provides access to ventral and medial intracranial structures.
