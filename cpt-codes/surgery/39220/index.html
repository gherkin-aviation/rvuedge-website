39220 Resection mediastinal tumor

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: mediastinal tumor resection, tumor excision

Surgical resection of a mediastinal tumor, which is a solid neoplasm in the central chest cavity. The tumor is removed along with surrounding tissue as indicated.

Clinical Context

Used for primary mediastinal malignancies (lymphoma, thymoma, germ cell tumors) or metastatic disease. May be combined with chemotherapy or radiation. Often requires extensive dissection.
