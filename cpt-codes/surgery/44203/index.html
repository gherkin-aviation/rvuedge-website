44203 Laparoscopic resection s/intestine additional

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: lap additional SB resection, second lap segment resection, additional laparoscopic resection

Add-on code for each additional segment of small intestine resected laparoscopically during the same operative session.

In Plain Language

additional laparoscopic bowel removal; second segment resection

Clinical Context

Used when multiple non-contiguous segments of small bowel are resected laparoscopically, or when additional segments resected at same time.
