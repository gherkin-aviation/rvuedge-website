43283 Laparoscopic esophageal lengthening

Surgery

Also known as: lap Collis gastroplasty, esophageal lengthening, short esophagus repair

Laparoscopic esophageal lengthening procedure such as Collis gastroplasty for short esophagus. This surgical procedure creates functional esophageal length.

Clinical Context

Used when primary repair of large hiatal hernias would create excessive tension; allows safe repair of complex hernias.
