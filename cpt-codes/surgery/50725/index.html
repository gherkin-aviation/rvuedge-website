50725 Release/revise ureter

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ureterolysis with revision, ureteral release and repair, adhesiolysis with revision

Ureterolysis with revision of the ureter, surgically freeing the ureter and reconstructing it to restore normal anatomy and function.

Clinical Context

Indicated when ureterolysis alone is insufficient and the ureter requires reconstruction due to stricture, kinking, or structural abnormality.
