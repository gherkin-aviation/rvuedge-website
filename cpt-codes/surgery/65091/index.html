65091 Revise eye

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ocular implant revision, prosthesis revision

Surgical revision of ocular prosthesis or orbital implant following enucleation or evisceration. Addresses complications or patient dissatisfaction with initial implant.

Clinical Context

Indicated for revision of orbital implant due to infection, extrusion, asymmetry, or poor orbital contour. Performed at secondary surgical sitting after enucleation.
