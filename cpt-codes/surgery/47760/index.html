47760 Fuse bile ducts and bowel

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Hepaticoduodenostomy, intrahepatic bile duct bypass

Surgical creation of an internal anastomosis between the intrahepatic bile ducts and duodenum (hepaticoduodenostomy) for proximal bile duct obstruction.

Clinical Context

Performed for hilar cholangiocarcinoma, hepatic strictures, or cholangitis refractory to other interventions. Allows bilateral drainage.
