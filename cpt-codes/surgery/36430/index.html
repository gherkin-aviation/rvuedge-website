36430 Transfusion bld/bld compnt

Surgery

Also known as: blood transfusion, RBC transfusion, packed RBCs

Transfusion, blood or blood component; each single unit transfusion.

In Plain Language

getting blood from a donor; transfusion of blood product

Clinical Context

Used for anemia, hemorrhage, or specific component deficiencies. Administered intravenously with careful monitoring for transfusion reactions.

RVU Information

CPT 36430 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
