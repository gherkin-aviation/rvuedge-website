33922 Transect pulmonary artery

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: PA transection, pulmonary artery transection, PA division

Transection of pulmonary artery with cardiopulmonary bypass support; surgical division of the main pulmonary artery for definitive separation and reconstruction, typically for anomalous origin or complex repair.

Clinical Context

Used for anomalous origin of pulmonary artery, complex pulmonary atresia repair, or other conditions requiring complete separation and reconstruction of the pulmonary trunk.
