33608 Repair anomaly with conduit

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: conduit repair, interposition graft, conduit placement

Surgical repair of a congenital cardiac anomaly by interposition of a conduit to bridge a gap or establish continuity.

In Plain Language

fixing heart defect by placing a tube

Clinical Context

Used for complex congenital lesions requiring reconstruction with a prosthetic or homograft conduit to establish pulmonary blood flow or systemic-pulmonary connection.
