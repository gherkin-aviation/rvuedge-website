26685 Treat hand dislocation

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Hand dislocation open treatment, Open reduction, Surgical fixation

Open treatment of a hand joint dislocation with operative reduction and fixation, using surgical intervention to reduce the dislocation and maintain alignment.

In Plain Language

Surgery to fix a dislocated hand joint; Operative treatment of hand dislocation

Clinical Context

Indicated for hand joint dislocations that cannot be reduced by closed means, have associated fractures, or require operative reduction for stable reduction.
