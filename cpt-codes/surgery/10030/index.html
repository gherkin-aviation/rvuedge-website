10030 Img gid flu coll drainage soft tissue

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: Ultrasound-guided drainage, Fluid collection drainage

Image-guided fluid collection drainage with needle or catheter placement in soft tissue. Percutaneous aspiration or drainage of fluid collections visualized on imaging under real-time guidance.

Clinical Context

Used for symptomatic seroma, abscess, cyst, or other fluid collections causing pain or infection risk. Provides both therapeutic relief and diagnostic fluid for analysis.
