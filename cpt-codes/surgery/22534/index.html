Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: Lateral extracavitary fusion add-on, Additional lateral fusion

Arthrodesis, lateral extracavitary technique, each additional vertebral segment or level beyond the first.

Clinical Context

Add-on code for lateral extracavitary fusion at multiple vertebral levels in same session; extends fusion construct when treating multi-level pathology.
