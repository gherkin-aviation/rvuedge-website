67040 Laser treatment of retina

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: pan-retinal photocoagulation, PRP, extensive laser treatment, scatter laser

Extensive laser photocoagulation of multiple areas of the retina or substantial retinal surface area using thermal laser energy for pan-retinal photocoagulation.

In Plain Language

extensive laser treatment of back of eye; widespread laser burn treatment

Clinical Context

Standard treatment for proliferative diabetic retinopathy, severe retinal ischemia, and neovascular glaucoma; typically requires multiple sessions.
