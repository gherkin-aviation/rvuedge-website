69511 Extensive mastoid surgery

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ossiculoplasty with mastoidectomy, ossicular reconstruction, ossicular chain repair

Extensive mastoid surgery with ossicular reconstruction or major ossicular replacement to restore middle ear mechanics and improve hearing after disease removal.

Clinical Context

Combines mastoid disease eradication with restoration of ossicular chain continuity using autograft (bone, cartilage) or prosthetic ossicular replacement prostheses (PORP/TORP).
