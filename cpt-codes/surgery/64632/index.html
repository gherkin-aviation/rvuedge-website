64632 N block injection common digit

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: digital nerve block, finger/toe nerve block, common digital nerve block

Nerve block of the common digital nerves to the fingers or toes with anesthetic injection.

Clinical Context

Used for finger or toe pain, injuries, and minor surgical procedures on digits.
