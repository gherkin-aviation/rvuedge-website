28675 Repair of toe dislocation

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: toe dislocation ORIF, open reduction IP joint, open fixation

Open surgical treatment of toe dislocation with reduction and internal fixation, including open reduction and hardware placement for irreducible interphalangeal joint dislocations.

In Plain Language

Toe dislocation surgery; Open toe repair; Surgical toe reduction

Clinical Context

Used for irreducible interphalangeal joint dislocations requiring open reduction; internal fixation restores joint alignment.
