25680 Cltx trns-scphprlnr fracture mnpj

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: closed reduction perilunate injury, conservative trans-scaphoid dislocation

Closed treatment (reduction) of trans-scaphoid and perilunate fracture-dislocation using manipulation and immobilization.

Clinical Context

Used for trans-scaphoid perilunate fracture-dislocations managed by closed reduction when adequate alignment can be achieved and maintained.
