46615 Anoscopy

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: anoscopic ablation, anoscopic destruction, anoscopic cautery

Anoscopy with ablation procedure. The anoscope is used to visualize the anal canal and perform ablation (destruction) of tissue using thermal or chemical methods.

In Plain Language

burning or destroying tissue inside the anus; ablation of anal canal lesions

Clinical Context

Performed for dysplasia, HPV-related lesions, or bleeding lesions in the anal canal. Uses radiofrequency, laser, or other ablative techniques.
