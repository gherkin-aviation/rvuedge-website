61450 Craniectomy stpl sctj cmprn/dcmprn

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: superficial temporal artery compression, vascular compression procedure

Craniotomy or craniectomy with compression of the superficial temporal artery (or other compression/decompression procedure) in skull base region.

Clinical Context

Performed for hemifacial spasm (STA compression), TIC douloureux, or other neurovascular compression syndromes. Provides access for microvascular decompression or vessel compression.
