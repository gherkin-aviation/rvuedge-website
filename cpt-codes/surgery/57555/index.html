57555 Remove cervix/repair vagina

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: cervicectomy with vaginoplasty, trachelectomy with vaginal repair

Removal of cervix with repair of vaginal wall involves cervicectomy combined with repair or reconstruction of the vaginal canal and perineal body.

In Plain Language

Cervix removal with vagina repair; Cervix and vaginal tissue repair

Clinical Context

Addresses cervical pathology while simultaneously repairing vaginal defects or addressing vaginal atrophy. Used when both cervical and vaginal pathology present.
