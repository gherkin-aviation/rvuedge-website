12041 Intmd repair n-hf/genit 2.5cm/<

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: Intermediate laceration repair, Layered wound closure

Repair of intermediate complexity laceration on the neck, non-head/non-foot genitalia measuring two point five centimeters or less.

Clinical Context

Used for small lacerations on the neck or genitalia requiring layered closure due to tissue complexity.
