57455 Biopsy of cervix with scope

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: Cervical colposcopy with biopsy, Cervical biopsy

Biopsy of cervix with endoscope. Direct visualization with magnification and tissue sampling.

Clinical Context

Most common cervical biopsy technique. Used to evaluate abnormal cytology or lesions.
