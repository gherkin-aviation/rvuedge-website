64901 Nerve graft add-on

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: additional nerve graft

Add-on code for nerve graft to additional nerve branch or for extension of primary nerve graft in head, neck, or other region.

Clinical Context

Used when grafting additional nerve branches beyond the primary nerve graft in the same surgical session.
