37261 Revsc evasc ivt st complex each

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: infrapop stent complex additional, additional complex tibial stent

Percutaneous or open revascularization of the infrapopliteal artery (tibial or peroneal) using stent placement with complex technique in additional vessels.

Clinical Context

Each additional infrapopliteal stent with complex pathology. Significantly increases procedural complexity.
