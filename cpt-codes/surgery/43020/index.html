43020 Incision of esophagus

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: esophagotomy, esophageal incision

Esophagotomy (surgical opening and incision into the esophagus) to access esophageal contents or lesions.

In Plain Language

surgical opening of the esophagus

Clinical Context

Performed for removal of foreign bodies, esophageal lesions, or reconstruction of esophageal strictures. Now largely replaced by endoscopic approaches but still used for specific pathology.
