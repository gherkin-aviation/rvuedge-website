23570 Cltx scapular fracture without mnpj

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: closed treatment scapular fracture, conservative scapular treatment, non-operative scapula fracture

Closed treatment of scapular fracture without manipulation. Conservative management of scapular fracture via supportive care and sling immobilization.

Clinical Context

Appropriate management for most scapular fractures; vast majority heal reliably with conservative care, sling support, and early mobilization.
