65110 Removal of eye

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: evisceration, orbital exenteration

Surgical removal of the eye and surrounding orbital contents (evisceration). More extensive than enucleation, used primarily for malignancy or severe orbital disease.

Clinical Context

Indicated for intraocular malignancy with orbital involvement, severe orbital infection, or orbital trauma. More extensive than enucleation but preserves eye muscles when possible.
