27424 Revision/removal of kneecap

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: patellectomy, patellar removal, patellar ablation

Surgical revision or removal of the patella (kneecap) including partial or complete patellar ablation, patellectomy, or reconstruction for severe degeneration or chronic instability.

Clinical Context

Used for severe patellofemoral arthritis, failed prior patellar surgery, or extensive patellar damage. Relative contraindication in active patients due to functional loss.
