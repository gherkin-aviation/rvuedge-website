42409 Drainage of salivary cyst

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: cyst drainage, salivary cyst drainage

Surgical incision and drainage of a salivary gland cyst without complete excision, allowing continued drainage and decompression.

In Plain Language

drainage of salivary cyst without full removal

Clinical Context

Conservative drainage of mucoceles or retention cysts in minor salivary glands to decompress lesion and allow healing.
