44210 Laparoscopic total proctocolectomy

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: lap total proctocolectomy, lap ileoanal pouch, IPAA

Laparoscopic total proctocolectomy with creation of internal pouch (ileal pouch anal anastomosis) and temporary ileostomy for complete removal of colon and rectum with restoration of bowel function.

Clinical Context

Performed for familial adenomatous polyposis, ulcerative colitis, or other conditions requiring total proctocolectomy. IPAA restores intestinal continuity and allows potential future ileostomy reversal.
