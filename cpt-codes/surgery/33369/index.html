33369 Replace aortic valve with byp

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: add-on aortic valve replacement multivalve

Add-on code for aortic valve replacement with cardiopulmonary bypass for extensive or multiple valve disease.

Clinical Context

Used when aortic valve replacement with bypass is performed with other valve repairs/replacements.
