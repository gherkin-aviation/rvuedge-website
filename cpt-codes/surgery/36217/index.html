36217 Place catheter in artery

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: third-order catheterization, additional selective catheter, distal branch catheterization

Percutaneous placement of arterial catheter into third-order or beyond branch artery (selective additional) for angiography or intervention.

Clinical Context

Used for selective embolization, distal angiography, or complex vascular interventions.
