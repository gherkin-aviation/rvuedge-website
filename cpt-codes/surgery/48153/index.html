48153 Pancreatectomy

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Distal pancreatectomy with gastrectomy, en bloc resection, Whipple total pancreatectomy

Distal pancreatectomy with en bloc distal gastrectomy and splenectomy for advanced gastric or distal pancreatic cancer with contiguous involvement.

In Plain Language

Removing distal pancreas, part of stomach, and spleen

Clinical Context

Performed for gastric cancer invading distal pancreas or distal pancreatic cancer with gastric invasion.
