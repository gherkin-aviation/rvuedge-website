63273 Excise intrspinl lesion scrl

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: sacral intraspinal tumor excision with dural repair

Excision of intraspinal lesion in the sacral region with dura mater repair. This procedure involves removal of sacral intraspinal lesions with dural reconstruction.

Clinical Context

Used for sacral intraspinal lesions requiring dural opening and repair.
