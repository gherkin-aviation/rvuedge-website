65815 Drainage of eye

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: hyphema evacuation, anterior chamber washout

Paracentesis of anterior chamber of eye; with removal of blood.

In Plain Language

draining blood from front of eye; removing blood from anterior chamber

Clinical Context

Evacuation of significant hyphema (blood in anterior chamber) to prevent secondary glaucoma and restore visual axis. Often done post-traumatic or post-operative.
