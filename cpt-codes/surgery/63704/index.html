63704 Repair of spinal herniation

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: myelomeningocele repair, spinal neural tube defect closure

Repair of spinal cord myelomeningocele with closure of associated skin defect. This comprehensive procedure involves closure of a defect containing both neural tissue and cerebrospinal fluid.

Clinical Context

Used for neonatal or pediatric repair of spinal dysraphism including myelomeningocele, the most common neural tube defect.
