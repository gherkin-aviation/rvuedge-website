32561 Lyse chest fibrin initial day

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: fibrin lysis, fibrinolytic therapy

Percutaneous lysis of fibrin in the pleural cavity on the initial treatment day, performed to break up loculations and improve drainage of infected or hemorrhagic fluid.

In Plain Language

dissolving chest fluid clots

Clinical Context

Used for loculated empyema or complex pleural effusion on initial intervention day to improve drainage and prevent progression to fibrothorax.
