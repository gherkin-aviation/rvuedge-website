54322 Reconstruction of urethra

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: transection urethroplasty, anastomotic urethral repair

Reconstruction of the urethra within the penis using transection and anastomosis without graft.

In Plain Language

fixing urethra by cutting and reconnecting

Clinical Context

Used for short urethral strictures amenable to transection. Allows excision of scar with primary repair.
