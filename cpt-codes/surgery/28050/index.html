28050 Biopsy of foot joint lining

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: synovial biopsy ankle, joint lining biopsy, synovitis biopsy

Biopsy of the synovial membrane (lining) of an ankle joint or ankle region joint. This procedure obtains tissue for histologic analysis to diagnose joint pathology.

Clinical Context

Performed for diagnosis of inflammatory arthropathies, infection, or other joint pathology. Particularly useful in rheumatoid arthritis, tuberculosis, or fungal infections.
