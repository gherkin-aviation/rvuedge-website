29882 Arthrs knee srg mnisc repair m/l

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: meniscal repair, meniscus repair, single meniscus fix, knee scope meniscus repair and meniscectomy

Arthroscopy of the knee with repair of a single meniscal tear (medial or lateral meniscus).

In Plain Language

stitching torn knee cartilage; repairing meniscus

Clinical Context

Used for repairable meniscal tears in peripheral zones to preserve meniscal tissue.
