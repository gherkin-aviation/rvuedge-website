30124 Removal of nose lesion

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: septal lesion removal, septum polyp removal, septal mass removal

Removal of a benign lesion of the nasal septum or septum wall, including polyps, cysts, or other pathological growths affecting the septal partition.

Clinical Context

Indicated for symptomatic septal lesions causing obstruction, epistaxis, or other symptoms; provides tissue for pathological examination.
