63081 Remove vertebral body dcmprn crvl

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: cervical vertebrectomy, cervical corpectomy, vertebral body excision

Removal of vertebral body for decompression in the cervical region. This extensive procedure involves excision of the vertebral body itself to decompress the spinal cord and nerve roots, often requiring bone graft and instrumentation.

Clinical Context

Used for severe cervical stenosis, particularly in cases of ossified posterior longitudinal ligament (OPLL), central cord syndrome, or extensive cervical myelopathy requiring substantial decompression.
