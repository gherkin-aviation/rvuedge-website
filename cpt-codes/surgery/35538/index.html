35538 Artery byp graft aortobi-iliac

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: aorto-bifemoral bypass, bilateral iliac bypass

Arterial bypass graft from aorta to bilateral iliac arteries with restoration of bilateral lower extremity blood flow.

Clinical Context

Revascularization for bilateral iliac stenosis or occlusion causing claudication or critical limb ischemia. Excellent long-term patency with direct aortic inflow.
