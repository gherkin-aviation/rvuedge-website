23410 Repair rotator cuff acute

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: rotator cuff repair, acute RC repair, primary cuff repair

Repair of rotator cuff tear (acute). Primary repair of fresh rotator cuff (supraspinatus, infraspinatus, teres minor, subscapularis) tear without retraction or atrophy.

In Plain Language

repair of shoulder muscle tear; rotator cuff stitching

Clinical Context

Most favorable rotator cuff repairs, usually from acute traumatic tear in relatively good tissue quality, with preserved cuff tissue allowing primary healing without significant mobilization.
