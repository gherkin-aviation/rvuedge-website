50225 Removal kidney open complex

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: complex open nephrectomy, difficult nephrectomy, extended nephrectomy

Open surgical removal of the kidney (nephrectomy) through an incision for complex cases requiring extended dissection.

Clinical Context

Used for complicated renal pathology including large tumors, significant inflammation, prior surgery, or extensive adjacent organ involvement.
