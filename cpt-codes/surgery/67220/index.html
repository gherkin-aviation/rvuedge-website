67220 Treatment of choroid lesion

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: CNV treatment, choroidal neovascular membrane ablation, laser treatment of wet macular degeneration

Treatment of choroidal neovascular membrane or other choroidal lesions using laser photocoagulation to destroy abnormal vascular tissue and prevent further vision loss.

Clinical Context

Used for choroidal neovascularization in wet age-related macular degeneration; now often supplemented or replaced by anti-VEGF injections.
