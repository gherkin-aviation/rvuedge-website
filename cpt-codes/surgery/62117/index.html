62117 Reduction of skull defect

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: large skull defect reduction, major cranial defect repair

Reduction of cranial or skull defect greater than 5 centimeters in diameter using bone graft or bone flap repositioning.

In Plain Language

large skull hole repair; major cranial bone defect treatment

Clinical Context

Used for correction of large congenital or acquired skull defects exceeding 5 centimeters, often requiring bone grafting.
