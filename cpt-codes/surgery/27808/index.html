27808 Treatment of ankle fracture

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: closed reduction bimalleolar ankle, conservative bimalleolar fracture

Initial closed treatment of ankle fracture (bimalleolar) with manipulation and immobilization.

Clinical Context

Used for non-displaced or minimally displaced bimalleolar ankle fractures amenable to conservative management.
