17314 Mohs additional stage t/a/l

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: Mohs additional stage trunk, Mohs add-on body

Mohs micrographic surgery additional stage on trunk, arms, and legs when more than one tissue layer examination is required.

Clinical Context

Add-on code for additional Mohs stages on body. Used when deeper layers require examination for complete tumor clearance.
