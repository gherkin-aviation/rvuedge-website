58267 Vag hyst with urinary repair

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: VH with urinary repair, vaginal hysterectomy with urinary reconstruction

Vaginal hysterectomy with removal of tubes and ovaries plus urinary tract repair involves hysterectomy combined with bladder repair for incontinence or fistula.

In Plain Language

Uterus and ovary removal with bladder repair; Vaginal hysterectomy with bladder fix

Clinical Context

Addresses gynecologic pathology while concurrently treating stress incontinence or repairing urinary fistula. Requires expertise in urogynecology.
