54300 Revision of penis

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: penile revision, penile straightening

Surgical revision of the penis for correction of curvature, deformity, or other structural abnormality.

Clinical Context

Used for congenital or acquired penile curvature, Peyronie's disease sequelae, or post-traumatic deformity.
