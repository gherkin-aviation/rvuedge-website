36475 Endovenous rf first vein

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: radiofrequency ablation, RF ablation, RFA, VNUS closure

Endovenous ablation with radiofrequency energy, first vein treated.

Clinical Context

Used for varicose veins via radiofrequency-induced thermal ablation. Minimally invasive with excellent occlusion rates and minimal complications.
