27486 Revise/replace knee joint

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: knee revision arthroplasty, TKA revision

Surgical revision of a total knee arthroplasty with prosthetic component replacement or adjustment, typically a primary revision of a failed total knee replacement.

Clinical Context

Used for failure of primary total knee arthroplasty including loosening, wear, infection (chronic), or instability.
