44055 Correct malrotation of bowel

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: malrotation correction, Ladd procedure, bowel rotation correction

Correction of bowel malrotation (normal rotation of small intestine around superior mesenteric artery) via open approach.

In Plain Language

intestine position correction; bowel rotation surgery

Clinical Context

Surgical correction of intestinal malrotation, typically using Ladd procedure. Involves division of Ladd bands, widening of mesentery, and appendectomy.
