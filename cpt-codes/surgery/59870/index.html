59870 Evacuate mole of uterus

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: molar pregnancy evacuation, hydatidiform mole removal, mole evacuation

Evacuation of hydatidiform mole from the uterus via dilation and curettage or suction evacuation.

In Plain Language

removal of abnormal pregnancy tissue

Clinical Context

Treatment of hydatidiform mole (gestational trophoblastic disease); requires careful evacuation and follow-up for malignant transformation risk.
