14041 Tissue trnfr f/c/c/m/n/a/g/h/f

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Adjacent tissue transfer, Larger local flap, Tissue rearrangement

Tissue transfer or graft from adjacent area to cover defect on the face, cheek, chin, mouth, nose, axilla, groin, hands, or feet measuring ten point one to thirty square centimeters.

Clinical Context

Used for coverage of medium-sized defects on the face or hands using adjacent tissue.
