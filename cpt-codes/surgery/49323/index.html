49323 Laparoscopic drainage lymphocele

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: laparoscopic lymphocele drainage, lap lymphocele drain, minimally invasive lymph drainage

Laparoscopic drainage of lymphocele (fluid collection from lymphatic system) with catheter placement.

In Plain Language

camera-guided drainage of lymph fluid; minimally invasive treatment of lymph collection

Clinical Context

Laparoscopic drainage of lymphoceles commonly occurring after lymph node dissection or renal transplantation. Provides therapeutic decompression and allows healing.
