20102 exploration pentrg wnd abdominal/flnk/bk

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: abdominal wound exploration, belly wound exam

Surgical exploration of a penetrating wound to the abdomen, flank, or back to identify and evaluate organ or vascular injury. This procedure examines internal structures for damage.

Clinical Context

Used for initial assessment of penetrating abdominal trauma to identify solid organ, hollow viscus, or vascular injuries requiring operative intervention.
