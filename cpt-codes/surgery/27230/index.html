27230 Treat thigh fracture

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: closed femur fracture, conservative thighbone care

Closed treatment of femoral shaft fracture without reduction or with minimal manipulation.

Clinical Context

Used for non-displaced or minimally displaced femoral shaft fractures that may heal with conservative immobilization alone.
