28730 Fusion of foot bones

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: midfoot fusion, naviculocuneiform fusion, midfoot arthrodesis

Surgical arthrodesis (fusion) of midfoot bones (naviculocuneiform, intercuneiform, or other midfoot joints), permanently fusing these bones to treat arthritis or deformity.

Clinical Context

Used for midfoot arthritis, Lisfranc injury, or midfoot deformity; fusion provides pain relief and arch stability.
