32400 Needle biopsy chest lining

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: pleural needle biopsy, pleural bx

Diagnostic needle biopsy of the pleura (chest lining) using a percutaneous needle technique to obtain tissue samples for histological diagnosis.

Clinical Context

Used for diagnosis of pleural disease including malignancy, tuberculosis, or other pleural infections when imaging suggests pleural pathology.
