27227 Treat hip fracture(s)

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: acetabular ORIF, complex acetabular repair

Treatment of acetabular fracture through operative open reduction and internal fixation, addressing more complex fracture patterns or both-column involvement.

Clinical Context

Used for complex acetabular fractures including both-column or T-type patterns requiring surgical reduction for anatomic restoration.
