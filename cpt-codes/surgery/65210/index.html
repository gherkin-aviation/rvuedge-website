65210 Remove foreign body from eye

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: anterior segment foreign body removal

Removal of a foreign body from the anterior segment or anterior chamber of the eye with slightly increased complexity, possibly requiring localization techniques but without operating microscope.

Clinical Context

Indicated for foreign bodies in anterior segment requiring more involved removal technique than simple 65205. May include imaging or specialized instruments.
