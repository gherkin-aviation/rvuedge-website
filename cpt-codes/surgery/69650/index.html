69650 Stapes mobilization

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: stapes mobilization, stapes freeing, stapes mobilization surgery

Stapes mobilization is the surgical freeing of a fixed stapes footplate to restore ossicular mobility and improve conductive hearing.

Clinical Context

Used historically for otosclerosis or stapes fixation. Now largely replaced by stapes replacement (stapedectomy/stapedotomy) for superior outcomes.
