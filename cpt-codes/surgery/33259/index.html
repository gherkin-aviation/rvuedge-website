33259 Ablate atria with bypass add-on

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: add-on atrial ablation with bypass, add-on ablation with CPB

Add-on code for atrial ablation with cardiopulmonary bypass performed in conjunction with a primary surgical procedure, representing additional ablation with bypass support.

Clinical Context

Used when comprehensive atrial ablation requiring bypass support is performed as an add-on to a primary cardiac operation.
