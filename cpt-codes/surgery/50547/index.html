50547 Laparoscopic removal donor kidney

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: lap donor nephrectomy, laparoscopic living donor, minimally invasive donor surgery

Laparoscopic removal and procurement of a kidney from a living donor for renal transplantation.

In Plain Language

minimally invasive kidney removal from living donor; laparoscopic donor surgery

Clinical Context

Preferred approach for living donor nephrectomy with faster recovery and less morbidity than open approach.
