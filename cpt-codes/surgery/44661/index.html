44661 Repair bowel-bladder fistula

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: colovesical fistula repair complex, complex bowel-bladder fistula

Repair of bowel-to-bladder fistula with more extensive reconstruction or resection of involved bowel segment via open surgical approach.

Clinical Context

Used for more complex bowel-bladder fistulas requiring bowel resection and anastomosis in addition to bladder closure, or when single layer repair is not feasible.
