49327 Laparoscopic ins device for right

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: laparoscopic brachytherapy device, lap radiation device, internal radiation placement

Laparoscopic insertion of device for radiotherapy (brachytherapy) as add-on procedure.

Clinical Context

Laparoscopic placement of radiotherapy sources or applicators for brachytherapy. Used in cancer treatment to deliver targeted internal radiation.
