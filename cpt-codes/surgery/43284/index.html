43284 Laps esophgl sphnctr agmntj

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: lap magnetic sphincter device, LINX device, esophageal sphincter augmentation

Laparoscopic esophageal sphincter augmentation with insertion of magnetic implant device. This minimally invasive procedure increases lower esophageal sphincter pressure.

In Plain Language

placing magnetic rings to strengthen sphincter; sphincter augmentation device

Clinical Context

Newer surgical option for GERD; maintains sphincter competence while allowing normal swallowing; outcomes longer-term data still emerging.
