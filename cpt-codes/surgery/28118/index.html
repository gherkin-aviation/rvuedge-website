28118 Removal of heel bone

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: calcaneal excision, heel bone removal, calcaneus resection

Excision of the calcaneus (heel bone) or a substantial portion thereof. This procedure removes part or all of the calcaneal bone for severe pathology.

In Plain Language

removing part of the heel bone; heel bone surgery

Clinical Context

Performed for calcaneal osteomyelitis, necrotic calcaneus, severe Paget's disease, or malignancy. Major surgery with significant functional consequences.
