44141 Partial removal of colon

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ileocolic resection, right colectomy with ileal involvement, extended partial colectomy

Partial colectomy with extension into small intestine (ileocolic resection).

Clinical Context

Resection involving transition zone between terminal ileum and colon. Commonly performed for ileocecal Crohn disease or right-sided malignancy.
