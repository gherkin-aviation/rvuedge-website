35266 repair blvsl grf oth/th vn uxtr

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: arm prosthetic graft repair, upper extremity synthetic bypass

Repair of blood vessel injury in the upper extremity using prosthetic or arterial graft reconstruction.

Clinical Context

Upper extremity vascular reconstruction using prosthetic graft when venous conduit unavailable. Polytetrafluoroethylene (PTFE) commonly used but carries infection risk in contaminated wounds.
