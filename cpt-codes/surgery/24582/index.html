24582 Prq skel fix humeral cndylr fracture

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: percutaneous pinning, percutaneous skeletal fixation

Percutaneous skeletal fixation of humeral condylar fracture. Minimally invasive surgical stabilization using percutaneous pins.

In Plain Language

Minimally invasive surgery with pins for condylar fracture; Small incisions with metal pins for arm bone knuckle fracture

Clinical Context

Used for displaced humeral condylar fractures requiring percutaneous pin fixation.
