35602 Bpg crtd-clat crtd

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: crossover carotid bypass, carotid-to-carotid crossover, contralateral carotid bypass

Arterial bypass graft from carotid artery to contralateral carotid artery, typically using composite prosthetic and vein material. Restores cerebral circulation when bilateral carotid disease exists.

Clinical Context

Indicated for bilateral carotid occlusion or severe stenosis, pseudoaneurysm, or aneurysm where endarterectomy is not suitable. Requires staged procedures in some cases.
