58544 Lsh with t/o uterus above 250 g

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: TLH with BSO large uterus, laparoscopic hysterectomy with ovary removal

Laparoscopic hysterectomy with salpingo-oophorectomy and uterus greater than 250 grams combines BSO with laparoscopic removal of enlarged uterus.

Clinical Context

Combines oophorectomy with laparoscopic removal of large uterus. Increased complexity due to uterine size and need for specimen extraction.
