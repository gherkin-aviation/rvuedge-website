44125 Removal of small intestine

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: SB resection for Crohn, ileocolic resection, small bowel Crohn resection

Removal of segment of small intestine with intestinal reconstruction via open approach for Crohn disease.

Clinical Context

Resection of small bowel involved by Crohn disease with primary anastomosis. Often ileocolic resection. May require multiple resections in recurrent disease.
