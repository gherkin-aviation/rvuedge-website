23520 Cltx strnclav dislc without mnpj

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: closed treatment SC dislocation, conservative SC treatment, non-operative SC dislocation

Closed treatment (manipulation) of sternoclavicular dislocation without manipulation. Conservative treatment with immobilization via strapping or figure-of-eight bandage.

Clinical Context

Initial management for most anterior sternoclavicular dislocations; directed toward stabilization and pain control through immobilization.
