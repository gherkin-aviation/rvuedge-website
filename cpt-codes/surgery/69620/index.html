69620 Myringoplasty

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: myringoplasty, tympanoplasty, eardrum grafting, perforation repair

Myringoplasty is the surgical repair of the tympanic membrane using a graft material (cartilage, bone, or fascia) to restore its integrity.

Clinical Context

Used for chronic perforations, conductive hearing loss, or preventing recurrent infection. Commonly performed with canal wall up cholesteatoma surgery or as standalone procedure.
