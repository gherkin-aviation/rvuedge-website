67400 Explore/biopsy eye socket

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: orbital biopsy, orbit exploration, tissue diagnosis

Exploration and biopsy of orbital contents including soft tissue sampling to evaluate orbital mass, inflammation, or suspected pathology.

In Plain Language

diagnostic sampling of eye socket tissue

Clinical Context

Performed for orbital tumors, inflammatory conditions, infectious processes, or undiagnosed orbital masses; establishes histologic diagnosis.
