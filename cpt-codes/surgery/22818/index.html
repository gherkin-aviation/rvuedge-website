22818 Kyphectomy 1-2 segments

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Kyphectomy, Kyphosis correction

Kyphectomy, congenital kyphosis; single or double segment.

In Plain Language

Surgery to straighten severe humped back; Kyphosis correction

Clinical Context

Rare procedure for fixed congenital kyphosis; requires removal of vertebral bodies and fusion to achieve correction.
