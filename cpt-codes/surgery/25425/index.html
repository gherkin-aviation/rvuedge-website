25425 Repair/graft radius or ulna

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: open reduction internal fixation, ORIF

Repair of a radius or ulna fracture with internal fixation and soft tissue management.

Clinical Context

Indicated for displaced fractures requiring surgical intervention. ORIF provides superior alignment and early mobilization.
