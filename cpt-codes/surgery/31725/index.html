31725 Clearance of airways

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: complex airway clearance, advanced airway management, obstinate obstruction clearance

Clearance of airways using more complex techniques such as bronchoscopy-assisted clearance, mechanical ventilation adjustments, or advanced suctioning for obstinate airway obstruction.

Clinical Context

Performed for complicated airway obstruction not responding to simple suctioning, requiring advanced clearance techniques in critical care settings.
