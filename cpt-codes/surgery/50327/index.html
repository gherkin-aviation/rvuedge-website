50327 Prep renal graft/venous

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: graft venous prep, renal vein preparation, venous anastomosis prep

Preparation of a renal allograft for transplantation with specific attention to venous anastomosis preparation and evaluation.

Clinical Context

Used when specific technical preparation of the renal vein is required, such as extending the vein graft or managing vein-to-vein anastomosis.
