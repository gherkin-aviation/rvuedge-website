49020 Drainage abdominal abscess open

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Abscess drainage, open drainage, incision and drainage

Surgical drainage of an intra-abdominal abscess through an open incision by providing external drainage or marsupializing cavity.

Clinical Context

Performed for localized intra-abdominal abscess when percutaneous drainage fails or anatomy precludes it.
