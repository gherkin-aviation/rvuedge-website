35180 repair cgen av fistula head & nck

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: congenital AV fistula repair, arteriovenous fistula head/neck repair

Repair of congenital arteriovenous fistula in the head and neck region with vessel ligation or reconstruction.

Clinical Context

Surgical treatment of congenital arteriovenous fistulas presenting with cosmetic concerns, steal phenomenon, or hemodynamic compromise. Requires careful dissection to preserve neurovascular structures in complex neck anatomy.
