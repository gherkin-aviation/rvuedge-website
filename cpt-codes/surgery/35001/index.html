35001 Repair defect of artery

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: aortic defect repair, aortic suture repair, aortic patch angioplasty

Repair of a defect of the aorta (suture or patch angioplasty). Surgical correction of aortic defect by direct suture or patch closure.

Clinical Context

Used for aortic aneurysm, dissection, or traumatic aortic injury repair. May involve simple suture or patch grafting.
