46080 Sphnctrotmy anal div sphnctr

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: lateral sphincterotomy, internal anal sphincterotomy, anal myotomy

Anal sphincterotomy with division of the anal sphincter muscle. This procedure involves cutting the internal or external anal sphincter to reduce pressure and pain, primarily used for anal fissures.

In Plain Language

cutting the tight muscle around the anus; loosening the sphincter to reduce pain

Clinical Context

Standard surgical treatment for chronic anal fissures refractory to medical therapy. Relieves pain by reducing anal pressure and improving blood flow to the fissure.
