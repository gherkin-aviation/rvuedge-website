52351 Cystouretero & or pyeloscope

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: cysto with pyeloscopy, cystoureteroscopy

Cystoureteroscopy with insertion of ureteroscope (pyeloscope) for direct visualization and treatment of upper urinary tract lesions including stones and tumors.

Clinical Context

Allows comprehensive upper urinary tract evaluation and treatment. Can be combined with various therapeutic maneuvers.
