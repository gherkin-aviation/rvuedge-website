33949 Ecmo/ecls daily management artery

Surgery

Also known as: ECMO daily management arterial, ECLS daily care arterial, arterial ECMO maintenance

Extracorporeal membrane oxygenation/extracorporeal life support daily management with arterial circulation support; ongoing management of arterial ECMO including oxygenation, anticoagulation, and hemodynamic monitoring.

Clinical Context

Daily management of arterial ECMO including circuit monitoring, pump flow, oxygenation, anticoagulation, hemodynamics, perfusion assessment, and cannula surveillance.
