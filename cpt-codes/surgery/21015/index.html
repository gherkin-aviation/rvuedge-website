21015 Resection face/scalp tumor < 2 centimeters

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: facial tumor resection, scalp tumor removal

Resection of facial or scalp tumor less than two centimeters with reconstruction.

Clinical Context

Resection with reconstruction of soft tissue and bone if involved for tumors of face or scalp.
