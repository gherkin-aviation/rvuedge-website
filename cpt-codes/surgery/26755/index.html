26755 Treat finger fracture each

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: DIP ORIF, PIP ORIF, finger joint fixation

Surgical treatment of interphalangeal joint dislocation with open reduction and internal fixation using pins, wires, or screws.

Clinical Context

Indicated for interphalangeal dislocations that cannot be maintained in reduction or are associated with fractures requiring operative stabilization.
