48146 Pancreatectomy

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Total pancreatectomy, pancreatectomy

Surgical removal of the entire pancreas (total pancreatectomy) for extensive disease affecting the entire organ, causing severe malabsorption and diabetes.

Clinical Context

Rarely performed due to severe endocrine and exocrine insufficiency. May be considered for extensive pancreatic cancer or severe pancreatitis.
