43198 Esophagosc flex trnsn biopsy

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: flexible esophagoscopy with biopsy, esophageal biopsy

Flexible esophagoscopy (examination of esophagus with flexible scope) via transoral approach with transmucosal tissue biopsy.

Clinical Context

Diagnostic procedure for esophageal lesion evaluation with tissue diagnosis. Most common approach for histologic analysis.
