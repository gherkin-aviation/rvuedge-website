28270 Release of foot contracture

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: foot contracture release, foot deformity release, plantar contracture release

Release of foot contracture (including dupuytren-like fascia contracture). This procedure releases fixed deformity from scarring or contracture.

In Plain Language

loosening a tight, contracted foot; foot deformity correction

Clinical Context

Performed for Dupuytren-like disease of foot, post-burn contracture, or other fixed deformities. Improves foot position and function.
