26861 Fusion of finger jnt add-on

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: additional DIP fusion, additional PIP fusion, add-on finger fusion

Surgical fusion of each additional interphalangeal joint of a finger as an add-on procedure to a primary fusion code.

In Plain Language

fusing additional finger joints; multiple finger joint fusion

Clinical Context

Performed when multiple interphalangeal joints require fusion during the same operative session, used in conjunction with primary fusion codes.
