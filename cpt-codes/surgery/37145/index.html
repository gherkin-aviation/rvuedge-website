37145 Revision of circulation

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: fem-pop graft revision, femoropopliteal revision

Revision of a femoropopliteal vascular graft or bypass with thrombectomy to restore blood flow in the lower extremity circulation.

Clinical Context

Addresses failed femoropopliteal grafts, typically due to thrombosis or intimal hyperplasia. Used to restore lower extremity perfusion and prevent limb loss.
