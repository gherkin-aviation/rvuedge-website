61534 Removal of brain lesion

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: posterior fossa lesion resection, infratentorial mass removal

Craniotomy or craniectomy with removal of intracranial lesion from infratentorial location following prior electrode mapping or localization.

In Plain Language

surgical opening to remove lower brain lesion after localization

Clinical Context

Performed for infratentorial lesions after localization studies (electrode grids, imaging). Includes removal of seizure focus, tumor, or other pathology identified on mapping.
