32853 Lung transplant double

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: double lung transplant, bilateral lung transplant, DLTx

Double lung transplantation without cardiopulmonary bypass support. Surgical placement of two donor lungs into a recipient with end-stage pulmonary disease without extracorporeal circulation.

In Plain Language

transplant of two new lungs

Clinical Context

Preferred approach for most double lung recipients who can tolerate the procedure without extracorporeal support. Reduces bypass-related complications while providing better long-term survival.
