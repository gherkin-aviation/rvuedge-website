26025 Drainage of palm bursa

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: I&D palm bursa, palm space drainage

Drainage (incision and drainage) of palm bursa (space) of the hand.

In Plain Language

draining infected palm pocket; palm bursa drainage

Clinical Context

Used for palm bursa infections requiring drainage, often related to traumatic contamination or spread of infection from other hand spaces.
