27365 Resection femur/knee tumor

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: femoral tumor resection, wide excision femur

Resection of femoral or knee tumor with wide margins and possible reconstruction for malignancy.

Clinical Context

Indicated for malignant tumors of femur or knee requiring wide surgical resection with potential limb reconstruction.
