35207 repair bld vsl dir hand finger

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: hand vessel repair, digital artery repair

Repair of blood vessel injury in the hand or fingers using direct suture technique with microsurgical reconstruction.

Clinical Context

Microsurgical repair of hand and digital vessel injuries from lacerations, crush injuries, or amputations. Requires specialized magnification and delicate technique to restore perfusion and preserve hand function.
