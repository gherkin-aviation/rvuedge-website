64610 Injection treatment of nerve

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: chemodenervation dystonia, botulinum toxin dystonia

Chemodenervation with administration of chemical agent for treatment of severe muscle spasticity or dystonia in major muscle groups.

Clinical Context

Used for dystonia, severe spasticity, and movement disorders affecting large muscle groups.
