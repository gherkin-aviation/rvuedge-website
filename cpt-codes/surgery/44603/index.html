44603 Suture small intestine

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: small bowel repair multiple sites, extensive small bowel repair

Primary repair of small intestine via open approach for multiple sites or more extensive repair involving significant tissue trauma or complex laceration.

In Plain Language

surgical repair of multiple small intestine tears; extensive small bowel stitching

Clinical Context

Used when small bowel sustains multiple injuries or requires more complex repair due to extensive trauma, contamination, or tissue devitalization.
