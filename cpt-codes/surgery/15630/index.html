15630 Delay flap eye/not otherwise specified/ear/lip

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: facial feature flap delay, eyelid/nasal flap delay

Delay procedure for skin pedicle flap on eye, nose, ear, or lips. Blood supply enhancement for delicate facial feature flap transfer.

Clinical Context

Used in reconstructive surgery for eyelid, nasal, ear, and lip defects where delay procedure improves flap viability.
