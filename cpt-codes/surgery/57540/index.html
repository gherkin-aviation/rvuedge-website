57540 Removal of residual cervix

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: completion cervicectomy, cervical stump removal, trachelectomy for stump

Removal of residual cervix following previous hysterectomy involves excision of remnant cervical tissue left in place after supracervical or total hysterectomy.

In Plain Language

Leftover cervix removal; Cervix stump removal

Clinical Context

Performed when a cervical stump remains after prior subtotal hysterectomy and requires removal due to dysplasia, malignancy, or chronic infection. Important for cancer surveillance.
