64600 Injection treatment of nerve

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: botulinum toxin injection, chemodenervation, botox injection for spasticity

Chemodenervation with administration of chemical agent (typically botulinum toxin) for treatment of muscle spasticity or overactive muscle.

In Plain Language

muscle relaxing injection; spasticity treatment shot

Clinical Context

Used for muscle spasticity from stroke, cerebral palsy, and other neurological conditions causing muscle hypertonicity.
