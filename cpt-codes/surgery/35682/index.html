35682 Composite byp graft 2 veins

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: two-vein composite graft, bilateral vein composite, composite bifemoral

Composite arterial bypass graft using two segments of autogenous vein combined with prosthetic material. Used for longer bypass distances.

In Plain Language

bypass using vein from two different places; combining vein segments with artificial material

Clinical Context

Add-on code when two vein segments are used in bypass construction, such as contralateral leg for composite graft.
