61791 Treat trigeminal tract

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: trigeminal tractotomy, mesencephalic tractotomy

Surgical treatment of trigeminal nerve tract or nucleus in brainstem via tractotomy for pain management.

In Plain Language

Cutting trigeminal nerve pathway in brainstem

Clinical Context

Performed for refractory trigeminal pain when peripheral approaches fail. Brainstem lesioning technique.
