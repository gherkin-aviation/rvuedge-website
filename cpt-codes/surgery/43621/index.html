43621 Removal of stomach

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: total gastrectomy with esophagectomy, En bloc resection, esophagogastric resection

Total gastrectomy with distal esophagus removal when esophageal involvement present and creation of esophagojejunal anastomosis. This resects stomach and lower esophagus.

In Plain Language

removing stomach and lower esophagus; combined gastric and esophageal resection

Clinical Context

Performed for gastric cancer with gastroesophageal junction involvement or distal esophageal cancer; extensive resection with significant morbidity.
