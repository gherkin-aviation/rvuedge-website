15758 Free fascial flap microvasc

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: free fascial flap, fascia free flap

Free fascial flap with microvascular anastomosis. Microsurgical transfer of fascia tissue with reconnected blood vessels for specialized reconstruction.

Clinical Context

Used for specialized defects requiring thin tissue coverage, nerve wrapping, or cushioning where free fascial flaps provide optimal outcomes.
