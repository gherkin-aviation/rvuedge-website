63088 Remove vertebral body add-on

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: additional thoracolumbar vertebrectomy, TL extension

Removal of vertebral body for decompression, add-on code for each additional vertebra in the thoracolumbar region. Used when thoracolumbar vertebrectomy extends to additional levels.

In Plain Language

additional bone removal at thoracolumbar junction

Clinical Context

Applied when comprehensive thoracolumbar decompression requires multiple vertebral body removals.
