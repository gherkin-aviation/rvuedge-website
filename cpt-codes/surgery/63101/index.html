63101 Remove vertebral body dcmprn thrc

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: thoracic anterior corpectomy with reconstruction, thoracic vertebrectomy with fusion

Removal of vertebral body with anterior decompression and reconstruction in the thoracic region. This comprehensive procedure includes vertebrectomy with instrumented reconstruction for thoracic pathology.

In Plain Language

front approach spinal reconstruction in mid-back

Clinical Context

Used for thoracic vertebral body pathology requiring anterior decompression and stabilization, including trauma, tumors, or severe stenosis.
