57800 Dilation of cervical canal

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: cervical dilation, cervix dilation

Dilation of cervical canal is a procedure to enlarge the cervical opening for diagnosis, treatment of stenosis, or to facilitate other procedures.

In Plain Language

Opening the cervix; Cervix widening

Clinical Context

Performed for cervical stenosis, to facilitate endometrial sampling or hysteroscopy, or as initial step in dilation and curettage procedures.
