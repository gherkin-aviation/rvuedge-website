59840 Induced abortion dilation and curettage

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: induced abortion D&C, first trimester abortion

Induced abortion via dilation and curettage approach (first trimester procedure) for pregnancy termination.

Clinical Context

Operative approach for induced abortion typically performed in first 12-14 weeks; alternative to medical abortion or dilation and evacuation.
