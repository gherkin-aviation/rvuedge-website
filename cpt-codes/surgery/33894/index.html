33894 Evasc st repair thrc/aa acrs br

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: TEVAR with branch reconstruction, branched TEVAR, fenestrated arch repair

Endovascular stent repair of thoracic aorta with branch vessel reconstruction across branches; percutaneous repair with endovascular techniques and reconstruction of branch vessels during the same procedure.

Clinical Context

Advanced endovascular technique for aortic arch and thoracic aortic pathology requiring preservation of branch vessels (innominate, left carotid, left subclavian).
