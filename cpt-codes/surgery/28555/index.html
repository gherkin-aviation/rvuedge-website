28555 Repair foot dislocation

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: foot dislocation ORIF, open reduction foot dislocation, open fixation

Open surgical treatment of foot dislocation with reduction and internal fixation, including open reduction and hardware placement for complex or irreducible foot dislocations.

Clinical Context

Used for irreducible or complex foot dislocations requiring open reduction; internal fixation provides stable reduction for healing.
