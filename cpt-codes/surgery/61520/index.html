61520 Removal of brain lesion

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: intracranial mass resection, brain tumor removal

Craniotomy or craniectomy with removal of an intracranial mass or lesion from supratentorial location, often representing more extensive resection.

In Plain Language

surgical opening for extensive brain tumor removal

Clinical Context

Performed for large or complex supratentorial brain tumors, infiltrative gliomas, or other extensive intracranial masses. May require multiple staging procedures.
