57135 Excision vaginal cyst/tumor

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: Vaginal cyst/tumor excision

Excision of vaginal cyst or tumor. Removal of benign or malignant vaginal neoplasm or cyst.

In Plain Language

Removal of cyst or growth from vagina

Clinical Context

Used for vaginal cysts (Gartner duct, mucous), fibromas, or other vaginal masses. Provides tissue diagnosis.
