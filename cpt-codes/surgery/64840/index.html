64840 Repair of leg nerve

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: leg nerve repair, lower extremity nerve repair

Surgical repair of a nerve injury in the leg, including primary repair or grafting to restore sensory or motor function.

In Plain Language

leg nerve surgery; lower leg nerve repair

Clinical Context

Indicated for significant nerve injuries in the leg causing motor or sensory deficits. Common sites include sciatic nerve branches, tibial nerve, or peroneal nerve.
