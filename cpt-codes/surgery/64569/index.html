64569 Revise/repl vagus n eltrd

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: vagus nerve stimulator revision, VNS device replacement, vagus nerve electrode revision

Revision or replacement of vagus nerve electrode including removal and re-implantation of the neuroelectrode system.

Clinical Context

Used when vagus nerve electrode requires replacement due to malfunction, battery depletion, or positioning problems.
