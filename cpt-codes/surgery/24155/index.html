24155 Resection of elbow joint

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: elbow joint resection, elbow resection arthroplasty

Resection of the elbow joint with partial or complete removal of articular surfaces. Salvage procedure for severe joint destruction or failed arthroplasty.

Clinical Context

Used for severe post-traumatic arthritis, failed prosthetic joints, or joint infections where arthrodesis or arthroplasty is not feasible.
