Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: hip/perianal excision complex, perineal excision

Excision of skin lesion involving hidden skin fold area such as hip, perianal, or perineal with complex closure. Includes advanced wound reconstruction.

In Plain Language

hip or bottom area lesion removal with advanced closure; complex perineal surgery

Clinical Context
