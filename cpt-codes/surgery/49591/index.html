Surgery Global 0d

Initial repair of abdominal wall hernia (primary) less than 3 centimeters with simple closure.

In Plain Language

surgical repair of small belly button or abdominal hernia; closure of abdominal muscle separation

Clinical Context

Surgical repair of primary ventral or abdominal wall hernia less than 3 centimeters. Simple closure may be used for small defects with good tissue quality.
