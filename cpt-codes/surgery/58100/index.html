58100 Biopsy of uterus lining

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: endometrial biopsy, uterine biopsy

Biopsy of uterus lining (endometrial biopsy) is a procedure to obtain tissue from the endometrium for histopathologic evaluation of abnormal uterine bleeding or malignancy.

In Plain Language

Uterus tissue sampling; Uterus lining biopsy

Clinical Context

Used to evaluate abnormal uterine bleeding, assess endometrial cancer risk, evaluate infertility, or diagnose endometritis. Can be office-based with pipette biopsy or operative under anesthesia.
