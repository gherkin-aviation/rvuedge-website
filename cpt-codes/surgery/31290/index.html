31290 Nasal/sinus endoscopy surgical

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: endoscopic orbital decompression, endoscopic medial decompression, orbital ESS

Nasal and sinus endoscopic surgery with extensive management, including medial orbital decompression, addressing orbital compartment syndrome or optic neuropathy.

Clinical Context

Endoscopic medial orbital wall removal for Graves ophthalmopathy or orbital compartment syndrome. Allows expansion of orbital volume.
