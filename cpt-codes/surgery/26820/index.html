26820 Thumb fusion with graft

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: thumb fusion with graft, thumb joint fusion, thumb arthrodesis

Surgical fusion of the thumb interphalangeal or metacarpophalangeal joint with autogenous bone graft to create bony union.

Clinical Context

Performed for thumb joint arthritis, instability, or post-traumatic changes where joint fusion with grafting is necessary to provide stability and pain relief.
