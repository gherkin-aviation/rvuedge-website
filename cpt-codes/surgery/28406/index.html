28406 Prq skel fix clcnl fracture with mnpj

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: percutaneous fixation heel fracture, percutaneous pinning, minimally invasive fixation

Percutaneous skeletal fixation of calcaneal fracture with manipulation, using percutaneous pins or screws to maintain reduction achieved through manipulation, combining closed reduction with minimal incision fixation.

Clinical Context

Used for calcaneal fractures requiring fixation to maintain reduction; percutaneous approach avoids extensive soft tissue damage while providing fracture stability.
