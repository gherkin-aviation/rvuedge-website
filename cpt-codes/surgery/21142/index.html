21142 Rcnstj midface lefort i 2pcs

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Le Fort I 2-piece, segmental Le Fort I, two-piece midface

Reconstruction of the midface using Le Fort I osteotomy as a two-piece separation of the maxilla for greater flexibility in positioning.

Clinical Context

Used when two-piece separation allows superior positioning control in severe deformities or asymmetry.
