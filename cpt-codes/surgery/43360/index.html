43360 Gastrointestinal repair

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: gastrojejunostomy, bypassing gastric outlet obstruction, duodenoduodenostomy

Open gastrointestinal repair for gastric outlet obstruction or duodenal stenosis with creation of gastrojejunal or gastroduodenal anastomosis. This bypasses the obstruction.

Clinical Context

Treatment for gastric outlet obstruction from malignancy, peptic ulcer disease, or pyloric stenosis; restores gastric emptying and nutritional intake.
