29895 Ankle arthroscopy/surgery

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ankle scope with treatment, therapeutic ankle arthroscopy, ankle debridement

Arthroscopic treatment of ankle joint with therapeutic intervention for cartilage damage, loose bodies, or synovitis, performed endoscopically.

In Plain Language

ankle joint repair during scope; ankle cleaning and treatment

Clinical Context

Indicated for osteoarthritis, loose bodies, synovitis, or chondral damage requiring direct visualization and therapeutic manipulation or removal.
