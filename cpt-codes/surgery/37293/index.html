37293 Rvsc evsc tpvt st athr sf each

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: tibioperoneal stent after atherectomy additional, additional TPT atherectomy plus stent

Percutaneous or open revascularization of the tibioperoneal trunk or artery using stent placement following atherectomy in additional vessels.

Clinical Context

Each additional tibioperoneal vessel with atherectomy and stent. Extends revascularization in complex disease.
