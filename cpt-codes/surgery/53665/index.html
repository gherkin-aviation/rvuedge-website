53665 Dilation of urethra

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: graduated dilation, high-pressure dilation

Dilation of the urethra using graduated bougie dilators with enhanced technique or increased dilation force.

In Plain Language

thorough opening of narrow urethra

Clinical Context

Used for more resistant strictures requiring greater dilation pressure. May involve use of heavier or graduated set of dilators.
