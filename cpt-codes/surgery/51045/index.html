51045 Incise bladder/drainage ureter

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: cystotomy for ureteral drainage, bladder incision for ureteral obstruction

Incision of bladder with drainage of ureteral obstruction, surgical opening of bladder to relieve ureteral obstruction or drain ureteral calculus.

Clinical Context

Used for acute ureteral obstruction with need for urgent decompression. Provides direct access to relieve obstruction.
