46947 Hemorrhoidopexy by stapling

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: stapled hemorrhoidopexy, stapling hemorrhoids, hemorrhoidal stapling

Hemorrhoidopexy performed using stapling technique. Staples and suspends hemorrhoid tissue proximally to treat internal hemorrhoids.

Clinical Context

Minimally invasive alternative to excisional hemorrhoidectomy for internal hemorrhoids. Less post-operative pain than traditional surgery.
