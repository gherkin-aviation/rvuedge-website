67801 Remove eyelid lesions

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: multiple eyelid lesion removal, multiple eyelid excision

Excision of multiple eyelid lesions including benign tumors, cysts, or growths requiring separate excisions with primary closure.

Clinical Context

Removal of 2-3 separate eyelid lesions; requires individual excision and closure for each lesion.
