30905 Control of nosebleed

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: severe epistaxis control, complex bilateral packing, difficult epistaxis management

Surgical control of epistaxis with extensive hemostasis including bilateral nasal packing, specialized pressure dressings, or detailed cauterization of multiple bleeding sites.

Clinical Context

Used for severe, refractory, or posterior epistaxis requiring extensive intervention. Often requires operating room with general anesthesia and multiple hemostasis techniques.
