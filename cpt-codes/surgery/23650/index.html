23650 Cltx sho dslc with mnpj wo anesthesia

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: closed reduction shoulder dislocation, shoulder reduction without anesthesia, reduction shoulder dislocation

Closed treatment of shoulder dislocation (anterior, posterior, or inferior) with manipulation without anesthesia. Reduction of shoulder dislocation via manual manipulation without anesthesia.

Clinical Context

Appropriate for acute shoulder dislocations reducible by physical reduction techniques in alert, cooperative patients; low complexity reduction.
