41005 Drainage of mouth lesion

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: lingual gland drainage, sublingual gland drainage, salivary gland drainage

Surgical drainage of a lesion or abscess of the lingual gland (salivary gland under the tongue).

In Plain Language

draining a salivary gland infection; opening infected gland under tongue; salivary gland abscess drainage

Clinical Context

Used for infections or abscesses involving the sublingual salivary gland. Common with obstructed salivary ducts, sialoadenitis, or ductal stones.
