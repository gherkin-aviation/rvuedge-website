34151 Removal of artery clot

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: tibial thrombectomy, infrapopliteal thrombectomy, below-knee clot removal

Removal of an arterial thrombus or embolus from the tibioperoneal or infrapopliteal arteries. Surgical extraction of blood clot from below-knee vessels.

In Plain Language

removing blood clot from calf artery; clearing blockage below knee

Clinical Context

Performed for acute tibial/peroneal artery thrombosis causing acute limb ischemia. Often combined with angiography and may require lysis.
