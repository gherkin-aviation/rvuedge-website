67225 Eye photodynamic therapy add-on

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: PDT add-on, photodynamic therapy supplement

Add-on code for photodynamic therapy used in conjunction with a primary treatment code to indicate supplemental use during retinal or choroidal lesion management.

Clinical Context

Used when photodynamic therapy is performed as adjunct to primary code; typically combined with laser or injection procedures.
