34842 Endovasc visc aorta 2 graft

Surgery

Also known as: fenestrated EVAR dual graft, visceral aorta EVAR 2 vessels

Endovascular repair of visceral aorta using 2 fenestrated graft components. Placement of dual fenestrated grafts for visceral aortic repair.

In Plain Language

custom stent with 2 branch openings for aorta repair

Clinical Context

Used for juxtarenal/suprarenal AAA with two visceral vessel involvement (celiac and SMA, or other combinations).

RVU Information

CPT 34842 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
