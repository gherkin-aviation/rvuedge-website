27443 Revision of knee joint

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: bicompartmental revision, femoral and tibial revision

Surgical revision of the knee joint with replacement or adjustment of both femoral and tibial prosthetic components to address multiple component failures or severe complications.

In Plain Language

replacing both parts of a knee implant

Clinical Context

Used when both femoral and tibial components require revision, indicating complex failure. Higher morbidity than single-component revision.
