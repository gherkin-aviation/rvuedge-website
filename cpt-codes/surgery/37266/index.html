37266 Rvsc evsc fpvt angio complex each

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: fem-pop angioplasty complex additional, additional complex femoropopliteal

Percutaneous or open revascularization of the femoropopliteal artery using complex angioplasty technique in each additional vessel.

Clinical Context

Each additional femoropopliteal vessel with complex pathology. Increases procedural difficulty substantially.
