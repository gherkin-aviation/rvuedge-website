40820 Treatment of mouth lesion

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: oral lesion treatment, cautery

Treatment of mouth lesion by cauterization, chemical destruction, or other destructive modality without excision.

Clinical Context

Used for non-excisional treatment of small lesions, verrucae, or benign growths using chemical agents, cautery, or other destructive methods.
