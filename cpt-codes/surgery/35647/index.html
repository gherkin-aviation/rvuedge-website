35647 Bpg aortofemoral

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: aortofemoral bypass, aortounifemoral bypass, aortoFAG

Arterial bypass graft from abdominal aorta to single femoral artery for aortoiliac and superficial femoral artery occlusion. Unilateral lower extremity revascularization.

Clinical Context

Performed for unilateral aortoiliac occlusion with contralateral patent vessels. Less common than bifemoral repair.
