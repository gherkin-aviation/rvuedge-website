11771 excision pilonidal cyst xtnsv

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Extensive pilonidal cyst excision, Wide pilonidal excision

Excision of pilonidal cyst performed with extensive dissection and removal technique, involving more comprehensive removal of diseased tissue at the sacrococcygeal region.

Clinical Context

Used for more complicated pilonidal cyst disease with extensive inflammation, multiple tracts, or recurrent infections requiring broader tissue removal.
