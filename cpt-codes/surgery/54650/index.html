54650 Orchiopexy (fowler-stephens)

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Fowler-Stephens orchiopexy, staged orchiopexy

Staged surgical fixation of high undescended testis using Fowler-Stephens technique with vascular ligation.

In Plain Language

staged testicle fixation

Clinical Context

Advanced technique for intra-abdominal or high undescended testis allowing collateral blood supply development before mobilization and fixation.
