32320 Free/remove chest lining

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: complete pleurectomy, total pleural stripping

Surgical removal of the pleura with complete stripping of both parietal and visceral components, performed for extensive pleural disease including mesothelioma or recurrent malignancy.

Clinical Context

Used for pleural malignancy, mesothelioma, or extensive recurrent pleural disease requiring aggressive pleural resection for disease control.
