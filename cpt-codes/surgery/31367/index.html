31367 Partial removal of larynx

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: partial laryngectomy, partial laryngeal resection, voice-sparing laryngectomy

Partial removal of the larynx (partial laryngectomy) via anterior approach, preserving voice and swallowing function.

Clinical Context

Anterior partial laryngectomy for early-stage laryngeal cancer limited to one side. Preserves voice quality and swallowing function.
