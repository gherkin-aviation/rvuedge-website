43243 Egd injection varices

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: EGD injection varices, variceal injection, sclerotherapy

Endoscopic retrograde cholangiopancreatography with injection of esophageal or gastric varices for hemostasis. This therapeutic endoscopic procedure is used to control bleeding from variceal sources.

Clinical Context

Used to control active bleeding from esophageal or gastric varices in patients with portal hypertension, typically from cirrhosis or other chronic liver disease.
