33745 Tissue cgen car anomal first shnt

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: surgical shunt creation, systemic-pulmonary shunt, palliative shunt

Creation of a surgically constructed shunt (systemic-to-pulmonary artery connection) for congenital cardiac anomaly via transvenous or transarterial approach.

Clinical Context

Palliative procedure for single ventricle or severe cyanotic heart disease; increases pulmonary blood flow pending definitive correction.
