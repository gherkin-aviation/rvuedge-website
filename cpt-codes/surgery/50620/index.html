50620 Removal of ureter stone

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: open ureterolithotomy with repair, ureteral stone removal with reconstruction, open stone extraction

Surgical removal of a calculus (stone) from the ureter through an open incision with reconstruction of the ureter.

Clinical Context

Used when ureteral damage occurs during stone removal or when the ureter requires repair.
