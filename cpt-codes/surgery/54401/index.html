54401 Insert self-contd prosthesis

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: self-contained prosthesis, malleable prosthesis

Insertion of a self-contained or malleable penile prosthesis for treatment of erectile dysfunction.

Clinical Context

Used for erectile dysfunction. Self-contained prosthesis is bendable, allowing more concealment than semi-rigid.
