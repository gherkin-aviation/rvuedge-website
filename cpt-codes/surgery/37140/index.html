37140 Revision of circulation

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: graft revision, bypass revision, vascular revision

Revision or replacement of a previously placed vascular graft or bypass to restore circulation when the original conduit has failed or become stenotic.

Clinical Context

Performed when a prior arterial bypass or graft has failed due to thrombosis, stenosis, or other complications. Common in peripheral vascular disease management.
