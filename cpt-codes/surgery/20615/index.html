20615 Treatment of bone cyst

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: bone cyst treatment, cyst aspiration and injection

Treatment of a bone cyst, typically involving aspiration and/or injection of sclerosing agent.

Clinical Context

Indicated for symptomatic bone cysts (simple or aneurysmal) causing pain, pathologic fracture risk, or functional impairment.
