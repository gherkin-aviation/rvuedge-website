16000 Initial treatment of burn(s)

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: burn treatment, burn initial care

Initial treatment of acute burn injury including evaluation, treatment planning, pain management, and wound assessment.

Clinical Context

Performed in emergency department or acute care setting for newly sustained thermal injuries. Includes fluid resuscitation assessment, airway evaluation, and wound documentation.
