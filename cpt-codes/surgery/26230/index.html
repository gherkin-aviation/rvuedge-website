26230 Partial removal of hand bone

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: hand bone partial resection, hand bone debulking

Partial removal (partial resection) of hand bone.

Clinical Context

Used for partial excision or debulking of hand bone when complete excision is not needed or desired.
