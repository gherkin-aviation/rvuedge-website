61556 Incise skull/sutures

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: craniotomy, incision of skull, dural opening

Incision and opening of cranial vault with separation of skull sutures and bone flap elevation for intracranial access.

Clinical Context

Fundamental approach for intracranial pathology. Provides access for tumor resection, aneurysm repair, hemorrhage evacuation, and diagnostic procedures.
