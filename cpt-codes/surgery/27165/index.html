27165 Incision/fixation of femur

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: open reduction internal fixation femur, ORIF femur, femur fixation

Incision and open reduction with internal fixation of the femur, typically for fracture repair with fixation devices such as plates, screws, or intramedullary nails.

In Plain Language

Hip fracture repair with metal; Internal plate for thighbone

Clinical Context

Applied to intertrochanteric, subtrochanteric, or femoral shaft fractures requiring surgical reduction and permanent internal fixation.
