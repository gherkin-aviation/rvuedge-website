27702 Reconstruct ankle joint

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ankle arthroplasty, ankle fusion

Surgical reconstruction of ankle joint with arthroplasty or arthrodesis for degenerative disease or instability.

In Plain Language

Clinical Context

Indicated for severe ankle arthritis or chronic instability unresponsive to conservative treatment. May involve joint replacement or fusion.
