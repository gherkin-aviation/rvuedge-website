32505 Wedge resection of lung initial

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: wedge resection, lung wedge

Surgical wedge resection of the lung as the initial/primary procedure performed via thoracotomy, removing a small peripheral portion of lung tissue.

In Plain Language

small piece of lung removal

Clinical Context

Used for small peripheral nodules suspicious for malignancy, diagnostic biopsies, or benign lesions suitable for parenchyma-sparing approach.

Billing & Documentation

As a surgical CPT code, proper documentation must include the operative report detailing the procedure performed, patient positioning, approach, findings, and any complications. This code has a 90-day global period, which includes the day of the procedure, 1 day preoperative, and 90 days of postoperative care.
