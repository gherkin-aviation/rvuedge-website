32505 Wedge resection of lung initial

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: wedge resection, lung wedge

Surgical wedge resection of the lung as the initial/primary procedure performed via thoracotomy, removing a small peripheral portion of lung tissue.

Clinical Context

Used for small peripheral nodules suspicious for malignancy, diagnostic biopsies, or benign lesions suitable for parenchyma-sparing approach.
