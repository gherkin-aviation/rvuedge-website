27454 Realignment of thigh bone

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: femoral osteotomy, valgus osteotomy, varus osteotomy

Surgical osteotomy of the femur with realignment to correct angulation deformity, improve lower extremity alignment, or redistribute knee joint loading forces.

Clinical Context

Used for genu varum or valgum, malalignment contributing to osteoarthritis, or to correct biomechanical abnormalities and preserve knee joint.
