36578 Replace tunneled cv catheter

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: catheter replacement, line replacement, central venous replacement

Replacement of tunneled or non-tunneled central venous catheter; at catheter tip, in jugular, innominate or subclavian vein.

Clinical Context

Used when tunneled or non-tunneled catheter is infected, thrombosed, or otherwise non-functional. Complete replacement may be preferable to repair.
