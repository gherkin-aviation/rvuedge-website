27132 Total hip arthroplasty

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: complex total hip replacement, revision total hip replacement, advanced hip arthroplasty

Surgical total hip arthroplasty using complex reconstruction techniques including specialized approaches or component positioning.

Clinical Context

Used for complex hip anatomy, acetabular deficiency, or revision cases requiring specialized techniques for component placement.
