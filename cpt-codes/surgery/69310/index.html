69310 Rebuild outer ear canal

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: canal reconstruction, meatoplasty

Reconstruction of external auditory canal including canal enlargement or canal resurfacing for congenital or acquired defect.

Clinical Context

Used for external auditory canal defects. Common indications include canal atresia, canal stenosis, or canal reconstruction after surgery.
