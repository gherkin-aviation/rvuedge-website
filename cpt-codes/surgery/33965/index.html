33965 Ecmo/ecls removal perph cannula

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: ECMO peripheral removal, peripheral cannula removal, ECLS removal

Extracorporeal membrane oxygenation/extracorporeal life support removal of peripheral venous/arterial cannula; percutaneous or open removal of peripheral ECMO cannulae.

Clinical Context

Removal of peripheral ECMO cannulae at time of ECMO weaning or when conversion to alternative access is needed.
