68135 Destruction lesion conjunctiva

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: conjunctival lesion destruction, laser ablation conjunctiva

Destruction of conjunctival lesion using laser, cryotherapy, electrocautery, or other ablative technique.

In Plain Language

burning or freezing growth on eye membrane; destroying conjunctival growth

Clinical Context

Used for treatment of conjunctival lesions without formal excision. Common indications include molluscum contagiosum, conjunctival papillomas, xanthelasma, or vascular lesions.
