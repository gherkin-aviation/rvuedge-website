48150 Partial removal of pancreas

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Whipple procedure, pancreaticoduodenectomy, PD

Pancreaticoduodenectomy (Whipple procedure) involving en bloc removal of the pancreatic head, duodenum, and common bile duct with reconstruction of biliary and gastric continuity.

In Plain Language

Removing pancreas head, duodenum, and bile duct with reconstruction

Clinical Context

Standard resection for pancreatic head cancer, ampullary cancer, bile duct cancer. Complex procedure with significant morbidity.

Billing & Documentation

As a surgical CPT code, proper documentation must include the operative report detailing the procedure performed, patient positioning, approach, findings, and any complications. This code has a 90-day global period, which includes the day of the procedure, 1 day preoperative, and 90 days of postoperative care.
