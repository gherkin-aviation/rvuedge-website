61105 Tdh sdrl/ventr puncture

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: burr hole with dural puncture, ventricular puncture via burr hole, subdural tap

Burr hole creation with puncture of the dura mater and penetration into the subdural space or lateral ventricle for diagnostic or therapeutic purposes.

Clinical Context

Performed for diagnosis or treatment of subdural hematoma, ventriculitis, or acute hydrocephalus. Provides access for fluid evacuation, CSF sampling, or intracranial pressure monitoring in emergency situations.
