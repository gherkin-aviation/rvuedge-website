44314 Revision of ileostomy

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ileostomy revision, ileostomy reconstruction, complex ileostomy repair

Revision of ileostomy with more extensive reconstruction including restoration of bowel continuity through anastomosis or significant modification of stoma site.

Clinical Context

Used for complex ileostomy revisions requiring anastomosis or significant site modification. Indicated for severe complications or failed simple revision.
