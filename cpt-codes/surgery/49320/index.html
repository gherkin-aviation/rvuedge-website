49320 Diagnostic laparoscopic separate procedure

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: diagnostic laparoscopy, diag lap, exploratory laparoscopy

Diagnostic laparoscopy (separate procedure) to visualize and assess abdominal and pelvic organs.

In Plain Language

camera inspection of abdomen; laparoscopic exploration

Clinical Context

Minimally invasive visualization of abdominal and pelvic structures via camera. Used to evaluate acute abdomen, assess for metastatic disease, or guide therapeutic interventions.

Billing & Documentation

As a surgical CPT code, proper documentation must include the operative report detailing the procedure performed, patient positioning, approach, findings, and any complications. This code has a 10-day global period — follow-up visits within 10 days of the procedure are included in the reimbursement.
