45111 Partial removal of rectum

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: partial proctectomy, low anterior resection, LAR

Partial proctectomy via open approach removing distal rectum but preserving proximal rectum with anastomosis to restore continuity.

Clinical Context

Used for distal rectal cancer when adequate distal margin can be achieved and sphincter preservation is feasible. Allows restoration of continuity.
