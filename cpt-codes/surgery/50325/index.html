50325 Prep donor renal graft

Surgery

Also known as: graft preparation, renal allograft prep, donor kidney workup

Preparation and evaluation of a donor renal allograft (living or cadaveric) for transplantation with standard vascular and ureteral preparation.

In Plain Language

preparation of donor kidney for transplant; donor kidney preparation

Clinical Context

Performed in the operating room to prepare the renal allograft with standard techniques including vascular assessment and ureteral evaluation.

RVU Information

CPT 50325 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

As a surgical CPT code, proper documentation must include the operative report detailing the procedure performed, patient positioning, approach, findings, and any complications. Ensure the diagnosis code (ICD-10) supports medical necessity for the procedure.
