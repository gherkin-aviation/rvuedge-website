64776 Remove digit nerve lesion

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: digit nerve lesion removal, digital neuroma excision, finger nerve tumor removal

Surgical removal of a lesion affecting a nerve of the digit (finger or toe). May include neuroma, neurofibroma, or other benign nerve pathology.

Clinical Context

Indicated for painful neuromas or nerve tumors in fingers or toes causing pain with palpation or movement. Common in patients with prior nerve injury or amputation.
