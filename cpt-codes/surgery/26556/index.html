26556 Toe joint transfer

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Toe joint transfer, Joint transplant, Microsurgical joint transfer

Surgical transfer of a toe joint to the hand for finger joint reconstruction or replacement, performed using microsurgical techniques when significant joint reconstruction is needed.

Clinical Context

Indicated for severe hand joint loss or destruction where transfer of a toe joint provides anatomically appropriate reconstruction with preserved joint function.
