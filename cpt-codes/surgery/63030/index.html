63030 Lamot dcmprn nerve right 1 lumbar

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: lumbar nerve root decompression, lumbar radiculopathy surgery, lumbar laminotomy with discectomy, lumbar discectomy, lumbar disc surgery, microdiscectomy

Laminotomy with decompression of nerve root in lumbar spine for single segment. Includes root exposure and decompression.

In Plain Language

lumbar nerve relief surgery; lower back nerve decompression

Clinical Context

Used for decompression of compressed lumbar nerve roots in radiculopathy or sciatica.
