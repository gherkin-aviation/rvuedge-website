63620 Srs spinal lesion

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: spinal radiosurgery, gamma knife spinal, cyberknife spinal

Stereotactic radiosurgery of spinal lesion, single session. This procedure delivers highly focused radiation to a spinal lesion in a single treatment.

In Plain Language

precise radiation treatment of spinal growth

Clinical Context

Used for benign and malignant spinal tumors, metastases, or vascular malformations unsuitable for surgery or refractory to other treatments.
