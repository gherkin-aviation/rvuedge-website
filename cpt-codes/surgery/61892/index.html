61892 removal sk-mnt crnl nstm pulse generator/rcvr

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: corneal implant removal, peg and receiver extraction

Removal of skin-mounted corneal implant system including peg and receiver. This is a complete extraction procedure.

Clinical Context

Used for complete removal of a corneal implant system when the device is no longer needed, infected, or causing complications.
