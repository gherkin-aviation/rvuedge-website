21813 Treatment of rib fracture

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: multiple rib fracture ORIF, complex rib fracture fixation

Open treatment of rib fracture with fixation including multiple complex fractures. Surgical open reduction and fixation of multiple rib fractures or complex fracture patterns with extensive reconstruction.

Clinical Context

Used for severe rib fractures with multiple sites or complex patterns causing significant flail chest. Extensive fixation of multiple fractures restores chest wall mechanics and improves pulmonary function.
