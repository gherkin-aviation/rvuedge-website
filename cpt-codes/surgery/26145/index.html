26145 Tendon excision palm/finger

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: tendon excision hand, tenosynovectomy

Tendon excision (removal) of palm or finger tendon.

Clinical Context

Used for partial or complete excision of diseased or damaged tendons in palm or fingers, such as in rheumatoid arthritis or after trauma.
