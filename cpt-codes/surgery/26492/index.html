26492 Tendon transfer with graft

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Thumb tendon transfer with graft, Thumb opposition transfer, Opposition restoration

Surgical transfer of a tendon with concurrent graft augmentation to restore thumb function or improve thumb positioning in patients with paralysis or muscle loss affecting thumb opposition.

Clinical Context

Performed to restore thumb opposition in patients with motor nerve injury, paralysis, or intrinsic muscle loss preventing thumb-to-finger opposition.
