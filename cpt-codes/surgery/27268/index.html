27268 Cltx thigh fracture with mnpj

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: femur closed with pin fixation, percutaneous femur fixation

Closed treatment of femoral shaft fracture with manipulation and percutaneous skeletal fixation including Steinmann pins or wires.

Clinical Context

Applied to femoral shaft fractures managed through closed reduction and temporary percutaneous pin fixation.
