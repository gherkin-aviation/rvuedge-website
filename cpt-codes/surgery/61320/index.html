61320 craniectomy/craniotomy drainage intracranial abs sttl

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: acute subdural hematoma evacuation, craniotomy for SDH

Craniotomy or craniectomy with drainage and/or evacuation of acute subdural hematoma and exploration of intracranial space.

Clinical Context

Emergent procedure for acute traumatic subdural hematoma with mass effect and neurologic symptoms. Provides definitive treatment with excellent visualization and hemostasis.
