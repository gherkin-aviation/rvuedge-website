51585 Removal of bladder & nodes

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: radical cystectomy with PLND and diversion, cystoprostatectomy with diversion

Removal of bladder with pelvic lymph node dissection and urinary diversion, radical cystectomy with lymphadenectomy and conduit creation.

Clinical Context

Comprehensive cancer surgery combining cystectomy, lymphadenectomy, and urinary diversion. Highest RVU of non-neobladder procedures.
