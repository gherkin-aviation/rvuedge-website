20553 Njx 1/mlt trigger points 3/>

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: trigger point injection multiple, TPI 3 or more, trigger point injection multiple muscles, multiple trigger points

Injection of therapeutic agent into multiple trigger points (three or more) for treatment of myofascial pain syndrome. This treatment addresses multiple pain generator sites.

Clinical Context

Used for treatment of diffuse myofascial pain syndrome involving multiple trigger points. Provides comprehensive pain relief by addressing all pain generators.
