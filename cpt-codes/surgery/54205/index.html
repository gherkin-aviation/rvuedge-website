54205 Njx procedures peyronie ds exps plaq

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: injection with plaque excision, Peyronie's disease surgery

Injection of medication into the corpora cavernosa with excision of Peyronie's plaque for treatment of Peyronie's disease.

Clinical Context

Used for Peyronie's disease with significant curvature or plaque. Excision combined with injection optimizes straightening.
