53860 Transurethral rf treatment

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: transurethral radiofrequency treatment, radiofrequency therapy

Transurethral treatment of prostate using radiofrequency energy for reduction of obstructive symptoms in benign prostatic hyperplasia.

Clinical Context

Used for symptomatic benign prostatic hyperplasia. Provides minimally invasive alternative to standard transurethral resection.
