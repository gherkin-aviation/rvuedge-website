42970 Control nose/throat bleeding

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: nasopharyngeal hemorrhage control, nose and throat bleeding control

Control of hemorrhage (bleeding) from the nose and pharynx (throat) via combined transoral and transnasal approach with moderate complexity.

Clinical Context

Performed for hemorrhage originating from nasopharyngeal area, posterior epistaxis with pharyngeal involvement, or bleeding extending from nose to throat.
