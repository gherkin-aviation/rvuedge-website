61570 Remove foreign body brain

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: foreign body removal, bullet removal

Craniotomy with removal of penetrating foreign body or metallic object embedded in brain parenchyma or ventricles.

Clinical Context

Performed for penetrating head trauma with retained projectile, glass, or metallic fragments. Timing and approach depend on depth and location of foreign body.
