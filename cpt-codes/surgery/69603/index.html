69603 Revision mstdc rsltg radiation mstdc

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: radical mastoidectomy revision, revision radical mastoid

Revision of mastoidectomy resulting from complications of previous radical mastoidectomy, managing residual disease, drainage problems, or recurrent infection.

Clinical Context

Used for recurrent drainage, granulation, infection, or other complications from previous radical mastoidectomy approach.
