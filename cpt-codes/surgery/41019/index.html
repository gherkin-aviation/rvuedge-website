41019 Place needles h & n for right

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: head and neck brachytherapy, radioactive implant placement, radiation needle placement

Placement of radioactive needles or sources in the head and neck region for brachytherapy or radiation therapy.

Clinical Context

Used for brachytherapy of head and neck cancers including tongue, mouth, pharynx, or other oral/oropharyngeal malignancies. Requires fluoroscopic or ultrasound guidance.
