25375 Revise radius & ulna

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: complex radius-ulna revision

Revision of both radius and ulna with complex procedures including derotation, lengthening/shortening, or significant reconstruction.

Clinical Context

Indicated for severe bilateral deformity, combined malunion with derotation needs, or complex trauma sequelae.
