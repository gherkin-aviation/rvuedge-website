27685 Revision of lower leg tendon

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: tendon revision, tendon reconstruction

Surgical revision or reconstruction of lower leg tendon repair for failed primary repair or functional deficit.

In Plain Language

fixing failed tendon repair

Clinical Context

Indicated for re-rupture, inadequate healing, or loss of function from previous tendon repair. Addresses complications from primary repair.
