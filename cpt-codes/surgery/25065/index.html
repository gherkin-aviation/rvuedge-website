25065 Biopsy forearm soft tissues

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: needle biopsy forearm, percutaneous soft tissue biopsy

Needle biopsy of soft tissues in the forearm to obtain a tissue sample for diagnostic evaluation without open surgical exploration.

Clinical Context

Indicated for evaluation of soft tissue masses, infections, or inflammatory conditions of the forearm. Minimally invasive approach suitable for small lesions or initial diagnostic assessment.
