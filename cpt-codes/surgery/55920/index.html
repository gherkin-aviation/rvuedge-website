55920 Place needles pelvic for right

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: Brachytherapy needle placement, Pelvic seed implant

Placement of needles or catheters into the pelvic area for radiation therapy. Used to guide radiation treatment delivery to pelvic malignancies.

Clinical Context

Used in prostate cancer and other pelvic malignancies to facilitate brachytherapy (internal radiation therapy). Performed under imaging guidance to ensure proper needle placement.
