43206 Esophageal optical endomicroscopy

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: confocal laser endomicroscopy, optical endomicroscopy

Flexible esophagoscopy (examination of esophagus with flexible scope) via transoral approach with optical endomicroscopy or high-resolution imaging of esophageal tissue.

Clinical Context

Diagnostic procedure providing real-time tissue microscopy during endoscopy. Allows evaluation of dysplasia in Barrett's esophagus or esophageal lesions.
