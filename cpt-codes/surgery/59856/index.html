59856 Induced ab 1+vag supp dilation and curettage

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: vaginal suppository abortion with D&C

Induced abortion with vaginal suppository abortifacient followed by dilation and curettage for evacuation of retained products.

In Plain Language

medication abortion with surgical evacuation

Clinical Context

Combined medical-surgical approach when vaginal suppository alone insufficient for complete abortion.
