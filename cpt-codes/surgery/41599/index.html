41599 Unlisted procedures tongue flr mouth

Surgery

Also known as: unlisted tongue surgery, unlisted floor of mouth procedure, tongue unlisted procedure

Unlisted procedure code for any surgical procedure on the tongue, floor of mouth, or alveolar ridge not described by another CPT code.

Clinical Context

Used when a specific surgical procedure on the tongue or floor of mouth is performed but no specific CPT code exists for that procedure. Requires documentation and justification.
