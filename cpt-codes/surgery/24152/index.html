24152 Radiation resection tumor radial h/n

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: radial head radical resection, radial tumor wide excision

Radical resection of tumor of the radial head or neck with en bloc removal of affected bone and surrounding structures. Wide excision for malignant bone tumors.

Clinical Context

Used for primary malignant bone tumors of the radial head or neck requiring wide surgical resection with adequate margins.
