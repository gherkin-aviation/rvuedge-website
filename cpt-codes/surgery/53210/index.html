53210 Removal of urethra

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: urethrectomy, female urethrectomy

Complete surgical removal (urethrectomy) of female urethra for malignant disease, typically combined with cystectomy and pelvic lymphadenectomy.

In Plain Language

removal of entire urethra

Clinical Context

Radical surgery for primary urethral carcinoma. Often combined with anterior exenteration for advanced disease.
