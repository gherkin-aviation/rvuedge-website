31291 Nasal/sinus endoscopy surgical

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: endoscopic medial/inferior decompression, extensive orbital ESS, bilateral orbital decomp

Nasal and sinus endoscopic surgery with extensive management including medial and inferior orbital decompression for orbital decompression.

Clinical Context

Comprehensive endoscopic decompression of orbital medial and inferior walls. For severe Graves or orbital compartment syndrome.
