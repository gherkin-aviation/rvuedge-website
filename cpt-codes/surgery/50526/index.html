50526 Close nephrovisceral fistula

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: complex fistula closure, extensive fistula repair, complicated fistula closure

Surgical closure of a complex nephrovisceral fistula requiring extensive dissection and reconstruction.

Clinical Context

Used for large, infected, or adherent fistulas with significant inflammatory reaction or involvement of multiple structures.
