22556 Arthrd anterior ntrbd minutes dsc thc

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Anterior thoracic fusion with bone graft, Thoracic ACDF variant

Arthrodesis, anterior/anterolateral approach, including discectomy and/or corpectomy to decompress spinal cord and/or nerve roots, thoracic; with mineralized bone void filler/bone matrix, single interspaces.

Clinical Context

Used for thoracic spondylosis, tumor, or trauma requiring anterior decompression and fusion with bone graft material for enhanced fusion.
