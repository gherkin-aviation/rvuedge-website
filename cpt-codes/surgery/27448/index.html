27448 Incision of thigh

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: thigh incision, thigh exploration, fasciotomy

Surgical incision and exploration of the thigh compartment to relieve pressure, drain fluid, or address acute compartment syndrome, muscle necrosis, or soft tissue infection.

In Plain Language

opening the thigh to relieve pressure or drain infection

Clinical Context

Used for acute compartment syndrome, necrotizing soft tissue infection, hematoma, or rhabdomyolysis. Urgent surgical procedure.
