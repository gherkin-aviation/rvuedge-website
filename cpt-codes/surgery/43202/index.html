43202 Esophagoscopy flex biopsy

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: flexible esophagoscopy biopsy, EGD with biopsy

Flexible esophagoscopy (examination of esophagus with flexible scope) via transoral approach with tissue biopsy using forceps.

Clinical Context

Diagnostic procedure for esophageal pathology with tissue diagnosis. Standard approach for Barrett's esophagus, suspected malignancy, or chronic esophagitis.
