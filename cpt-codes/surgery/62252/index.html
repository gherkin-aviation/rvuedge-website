62252 Csf shunt reprogram

Surgery

Also known as: shunt reprogramming, programmable shunt adjustment

Reprogramming of cerebrospinal fluid shunt pump or programmable shunt system. Does not include valve replacement.

In Plain Language

shunt adjustment; programmable shunt reset

Clinical Context

Used for adjustment of programmable shunt systems to optimize cerebrospinal fluid flow without surgical intervention.
