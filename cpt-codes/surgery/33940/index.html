33940 Removal of donor heart

Surgery

Also known as: Donor heart harvest, heart procurement, organ removal

Removal of donor heart; surgical procurement of donor heart for transplantation from deceased donor.

In Plain Language

Organ removal for heart transplant; Donor heart recovery

Clinical Context

Operative procedure performed at organ procurement operation for removal of deceased donor heart for subsequent heart transplantation.

RVU Information

CPT 33940 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

As a surgical CPT code, proper documentation must include the operative report detailing the procedure performed, patient positioning, approach, findings, and any complications. Ensure the diagnosis code (ICD-10) supports medical necessity for the procedure.
