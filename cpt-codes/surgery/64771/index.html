64771 Sever cranial nerve

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: cranial nerve section, trigeminal rhizotomy

Surgical sectioning or transection of a cranial nerve to interrupt pain pathways or modify nerve function for therapeutic purposes.

Clinical Context

Historically used for intractable facial pain or trigeminal neuralgia unresponsive to medical management. Modern applications limited due to effective pharmacotherapy and alternative procedures.
