61619 Repair dura

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: dural graft, duraplasty

Repair of dural defect with graft interposition or patch placement for reconstruction of dural integrity.

Clinical Context

Used for extensive dural defects or when primary closure not possible. Grafts can be autologous fascia or synthetic dural substitutes.
