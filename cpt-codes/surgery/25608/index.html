25608 Optx dst rd xart fracture/epi sep2

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ORIF distal radius two fragments, open reduction internal fixation wrist, distal radius fracture ORIF complex

Open treatment (surgical repair) of distal radial fracture with internal fixation of two fracture fragments or surfaces.

In Plain Language

surgical repair of wrist fracture with two main pieces; metal plate fixation of wrist

Clinical Context

Used for intra-articular distal radial fractures with two main fracture components requiring surgical reduction and fixation to restore joint surface.
