62143 Rpl bone flp/prostc plate skl

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: bone flap replacement, prosthetic plate replacement

Replacement of bone flap or prosthetic plate of skull. Usually performed to correct malposition or replace infected hardware.

Clinical Context

Used for replacement or revision of skull hardware following infection, malposition, or inadequate fit.
