31627 Navigational bronchoscopy

Surgery

Also known as: navigational bronchoscopy, electromagnetic navigation, guided bronchoscopy

Navigational bronchoscopy using electromagnetic navigation technology or other guidance systems to access small or peripheral lung lesions with improved diagnostic yield.

Clinical Context

Performed to improve diagnostic sampling of small peripheral pulmonary lesions that are difficult to access with standard bronchoscopy, increasing diagnostic sensitivity.
