33647 Repair heart septum defects

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: multiple VSD closure, large VSD repair, VSD patch closure

Surgical closure of multiple ventricular septal defects or a single large muscular VSD with tissue patch.

In Plain Language

closing one or more holes in heart chamber wall

Clinical Context

Indicated for multiple muscular VSDs or large restrictive defects requiring tissue augmentation; achieves complete hemodynamic correction of left-to-right shunt.
