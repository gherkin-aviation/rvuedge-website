31231 Nasal endoscopy diagnosis

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: diagnostic nasal endoscopy, office nasal endoscopy, sinus visualization, nasal scope, nasal endoscopy diagnostic

Diagnostic nasal endoscopy performed via transnasal approach to visualize and evaluate nasal and sinus anatomy.

In Plain Language

nasal camera examination

Clinical Context

Office-based diagnostic procedure using rigid or flexible endoscope to evaluate nasal cavity, turbinates, and sinus ostia. Foundation procedure for sinus disease evaluation.
