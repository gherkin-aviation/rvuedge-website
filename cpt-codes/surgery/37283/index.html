Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: tibioperoneal angioplasty complex additional, additional TPT complex angioplasty

Percutaneous or open revascularization of the tibioperoneal trunk or artery using complex angioplasty technique in each additional vessel.

Clinical Context

Each additional tibioperoneal vessel with complex pathology. Increases procedural complexity significantly.
