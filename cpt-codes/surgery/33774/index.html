Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: TGA revision from baffle, arterial switch revision from baffle, baffle removal with arterial switch

Surgical repair of transposition of the great arteries with removal of a previously placed atrial baffle (revision from atrial switch).

In Plain Language

removing old heart repair and replacing with new surgery

Clinical Context

Reoperative procedure converting previous atrial baffle (Senning/Mustard) to anatomic correction via arterial switch.
