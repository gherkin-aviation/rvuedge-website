49427 Injection abdominal shunt

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: shunt injection, shunt patency test, contrast shunt study

Injection of contrast or sclerosing agent into peritoneovenous shunt for testing patency and function.

In Plain Language

dye injection into shunt tube; testing of shunt function

Clinical Context

Percutaneous injection into peritoneovenous shunt to assess patency, function, and identify malfunction or obstruction.
