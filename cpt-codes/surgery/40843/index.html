40843 Reconstruction of mouth

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: extensive mouth reconstruction, complex oral reconstruction, mouth flap reconstruction

Surgical reconstruction of the oral cavity involving more extensive tissue transfer and repair including musculocutaneous flaps.

In Plain Language

complex mouth surgery with tissue transfer; extensive oral repair; mouth rebuilding with flaps

Clinical Context

Used for large oral cavity defects requiring muscle and skin flap coverage. Common in head and neck cancer reconstruction, major trauma, and severe burn injuries.
