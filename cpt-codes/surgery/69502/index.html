69502 Mastoidectomy

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: modified radical mastoidectomy, canal wall down mastoidectomy, MRM

Modified radical mastoidectomy involves surgical removal of mastoid air cells and disease with canal wall down (external auditory canal wall removal) to provide disease control.

Clinical Context

Used for recurrent or aggressive cholesteatoma, revision cases, or when canal wall up approach has failed. Creates a permanent mastoid cavity for drainage.
