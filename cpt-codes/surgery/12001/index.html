12001 repair s/n/ax/gen/trnk 2.5cm/<

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: Simple laceration repair, Wound closure, simple laceration repair 2.5cm or less, simple lac repair

Repair of simple laceration or wound on the scalp, neck, axilla, genitalia, trunk, or extremities measuring two point five centimeters or less.

Clinical Context

Used for simple closure of uncomplicated lacerations with minimal tissue involvement. Common in emergency department and urgent care settings.
