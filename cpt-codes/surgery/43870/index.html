43870 Closure gastrostomy surgical

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: gastrostomy closure, G-tube closure, fistula closure

Surgical closure of gastrostomy tract created from prior gastrostomy (G-tube) removal or fistula closure.

Clinical Context

Closure of gastrostomy fistula tract after removal of long-term gastrostomy tube. Includes closure of mucosa and skin.
