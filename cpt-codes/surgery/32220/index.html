32220 Release of lung

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: decortication, lung decortication, pleural stripping

Surgical release or mobilization of the entire visceral pleura and lung surface (decortication), performed to restore lung expansion in restrictive lung disease.

Clinical Context

Used for restrictive pleurisy, chronic empyema with thick pleural peel, hemothorax with fibrothorax, or pulmonary artery stenosis causing restriction.
