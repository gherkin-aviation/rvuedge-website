24310 Tnot opn elbow to sho each tdn

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: open tenotomy, tendon division

Tenotomy, open procedure from elbow to shoulder on each tendon. Surgical division of tendon(s) to release contracture or reduce muscle tension.

Clinical Context

Used for treatment of contractures, spasticity, or chronic muscle tightness affecting upper arm and elbow motion.
