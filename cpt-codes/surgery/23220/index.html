23220 Radical rescj tumor proximal humerus

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: radical proximal humerus resection, en bloc humeral excision, wide local excision proximal humerus

Radical resection of tumor (soft tissue and bone), proximal humerus. En bloc removal of malignant tumor from proximal humerus with surrounding soft tissues maintaining adequate oncologic margins.

In Plain Language

complete removal of cancer from upper arm; wide tumor removal upper arm

Clinical Context

Applied in primary humeral bone sarcomas or metastatic disease, requiring wide margin resection of bone and soft tissues for adequate oncologic control with consideration of reconstruction.
