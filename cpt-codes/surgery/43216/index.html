43216 Esophagoscopy lesion removal

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: EGD with lesion removal, endoscopic lesion ablation

Esophagogastroduodenoscopy (EGD) with excision or removal of esophageal lesion using electrocautery, laser, or hot biopsy forceps.

Clinical Context

Therapeutic procedure for esophageal lesions including polyps, sessile lesions, or cauterization of bleeding lesions.
