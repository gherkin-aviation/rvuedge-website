63051 C-laminoplasty with graft/plate

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: cervical laminoplasty with instrumentation, laminoplasty with graft and plate

Cervical laminoplasty with graft and plate augmentation. This is a modified technique using bone graft and instrumentation to support the expanded lamina and enhance fusion potential.

In Plain Language

spinal canal enlargement with bone graft and support plate

Clinical Context

Indicated when cervical laminoplasty requires additional structural support through bone grafting and posterior plating, particularly in revision cases or when there is poor bone quality.
