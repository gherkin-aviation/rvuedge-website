47144 Prep donor liver 3-segment

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: 3-segment split preparation, split liver preparation, trisegmental graft

Preparation of donor liver for split procurement (3-segment split). Graft preparation splitting liver into multiple segments.

In Plain Language

preparing a donor liver split into 3 portions; split liver graft preparation

Clinical Context

Performed to maximize organ utility by splitting deceased donor liver for multiple recipients.

RVU Information

CPT 47144 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation
