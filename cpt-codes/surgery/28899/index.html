28899 Unlisted procedures foot/toes

Surgery

Also known as: unlisted foot surgery, unlisted procedure, misc foot surgery

Unlisted procedure code for foot and toe surgeries not otherwise classified, used when no other CPT code appropriately describes the procedure performed.

Clinical Context

Used for novel, innovative, or rare foot and toe procedures not captured by standard CPT codes; requires documentation and may require prior authorization.
