24516 Treatment humeral shaft fracture imed implant

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ORIF humerus intramedullary nail, intramedullary fixation

Open treatment of humeral shaft fracture with intramedullary implant. Surgical stabilization using an intramedullary nail or rod.

In Plain Language

Surgical repair of arm bone fracture with internal metal rod; Surgery inserting rod through the middle of arm bone

Clinical Context

Used for displaced or unstable humeral shaft fractures treated with intramedullary nail fixation.
