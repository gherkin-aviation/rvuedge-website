47534 Plmt biliary drainage catheter

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: Complex percutaneous biliary drainage, PTBD with intervention

Percutaneous placement of a biliary drainage catheter for external drainage with associated interventions such as stone removal, stricture dilation, or multiple levels of drainage.

Clinical Context

Performed when initial drainage is combined with therapeutic interventions like stricture dilation, stone extraction, or management of complex anatomy.
