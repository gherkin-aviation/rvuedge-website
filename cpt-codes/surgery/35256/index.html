Surgery Global 90d

Repair of blood vessel injury in the lower extremity using venous graft reconstruction.

In Plain Language

Fixing leg blood vessel with vein graft; Repairing leg artery with transplanted vein

Clinical Context

Vascular reconstruction of lower extremity injuries requiring venous graft due to vessel loss or damage extent. Saphenous vein harvest provides optimal conduit for infrainguinal reconstruction.
