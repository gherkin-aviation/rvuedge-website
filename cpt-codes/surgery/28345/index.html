28345 Repair webbed toe(s)

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: syndactyly repair, webbed toe separation, toe separation

Surgical repair of webbed toes (syndactyly) in the foot, including separation of fused digits and soft tissue reconstruction with skin grafting or flap transfer as needed.

Clinical Context

Used for congenital syndactyly causing functional impairment or cosmetic concern; surgical separation restores independent digit function and appearance.
