11772 excision pilonidal cyst complicated

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Complex pilonidal cyst excision, Complicated pilonidal cyst removal

Excision of pilonidal cyst performed with complex closure technique involving complicated closure methods, primary closure with special techniques, or management of extensive tissue defects.

Clinical Context

For recurrent, extensive, or complicated pilonidal disease requiring advanced closure techniques such as flap procedures or complex wound management.
