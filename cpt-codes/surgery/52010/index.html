52010 Cystoscopy & duct catheter

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: cysto with duct catheterization, retrograde pyelography

Cystourethroscopy with insertion of a ureteral catheter, typically for diagnostic retrograde pyelography or contrast injection to visualize the upper urinary tract anatomy.

Clinical Context

Used for diagnostic imaging when intravenous pyelography is contraindicated or inconclusive. Allows visualization of upper ureteral and renal pelvic anatomy.
