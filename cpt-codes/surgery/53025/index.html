53025 Incision of urethra

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: penile urethrotomy

Surgical incision of the penile urethra to relieve stricture or obstruction in the anterior/penile portion.

Clinical Context

Used for anterior urethral strictures refractory to endoscopic treatment.
