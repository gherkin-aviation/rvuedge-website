35701 exploration n/flwd surgical neck artery

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: neck vessel exploration, cervical artery exploration, vascular exploration neck

Exploration of neck vessels with or without flowmetry for vascular insufficiency diagnosis, surveillance, or intervention planning. Assesses cervical vascular disease.

In Plain Language

examining neck blood vessels; checking the arteries and veins in the neck

Clinical Context

Performed for carotid stenosis, vertebral disease, or trauma assessment. Intraoperative flowmetry may guide intervention.
