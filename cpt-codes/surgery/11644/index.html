11644 excision f/e/e/n/l mal+mrg 3.1-4

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: facial cancer excision, lip cancer removal

Excision of malignant skin lesion on face, ear, eyelid, nose, or lip with margins of 3.1 to 4 centimeters. Includes closure of the excision site.

In Plain Language

facial cancer removal; lip cancer surgery

Clinical Context

Used for removal of skin cancers on facial structures with extensive surgical margins.
