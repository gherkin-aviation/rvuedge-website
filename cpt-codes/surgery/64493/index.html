64493 Injection paravert f jnt l/s 1 lev

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: lumbar facet joint injection, sacral facet injection, lumbar zygapophysial joint injection, facet joint injection lumbar, lumbar facet block

Injection of anesthetic and/or steroid medication into the facet joint area in the lumbosacral region at a single spinal level.

Clinical Context

Used for lumbar facet syndrome, facet osteoarthritis, and mechanical low back pain. Common treatment for chronic back pain.
