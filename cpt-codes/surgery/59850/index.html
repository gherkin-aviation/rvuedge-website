59850 Induced abortion 1+ njx

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: intra-amniotic abortion, injection abortion

Induced abortion using one or more intra-amniotic injections for instillation of abortifacient agents (saline, urea, prostaglandins).

In Plain Language

chemical abortion with injections

Clinical Context

Used primarily in second or early third trimester for pregnancy termination; instillation induces uterine contractions and fetal death.
