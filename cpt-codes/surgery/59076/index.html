59076 Fetal shunt placement with ultrasound

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: fetal shunt, thoracoamniotic shunt, vesicoamniotic shunt

Placement of a shunt into a fetal cavity (thoracic-amniotic, vesicoamniotic, or other) under ultrasound guidance for continuous drainage.

Clinical Context

Indicated for pleural effusion, cystic adenomatoid malformation, or lower urinary tract obstruction causing fetal hydrops and threatening fetal viability.
