69805 Explore inner ear

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: labyrinth exploration, inner ear exploration, endolymphatic sac surgery

Surgical exploration of the inner ear (labyrinth) with endoscopic visualization to evaluate or treat inner ear pathology.

Clinical Context

Used for endolymphatic sac decompression, perilymphatic fistula evaluation, or labyrinthitis exploration.
