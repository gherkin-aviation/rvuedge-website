44361 Small bowel endoscopy/biopsy

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: small bowel endoscopy with biopsy, enteroscopy biopsy

Endoscopy of small bowel performed via enterotomy with tissue sampling via biopsy for histopathologic examination to establish diagnosis.

Clinical Context

Used when small bowel pathology requires histologic confirmation including inflammatory bowel disease, lymphoma, Celiac disease, or evaluation of strictures or masses.
