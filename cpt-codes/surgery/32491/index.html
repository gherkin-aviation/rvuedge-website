32491 Lung volume reduction

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: lung volume reduction surgery, LVRS

Surgical lung volume reduction (LVRS) procedure performed to remove emphysematous lung tissue, improving respiratory mechanics and function in severe emphysema.

In Plain Language

emphysema surgery

Clinical Context

Used for severe emphysema with upper-lobe predominance and low baseline exercise capacity when optimal medical management has failed.
