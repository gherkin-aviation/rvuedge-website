27093 Injection for hip x-ray

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: hip arthrography, hip joint injection, hip fluoroscopy injection

Injection of contrast material into the hip joint with fluoroscopic guidance for hip arthrography imaging.

Clinical Context

Performed for diagnostic imaging of hip joint structures to evaluate for pathology, fractures, or ligamentous injuries.
