19316 Mastopexy

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: breast lift, mastopexy

Surgical procedure to elevate and reshape drooping breast tissue and skin to improve breast contour and position. This cosmetic procedure restores youthful breast appearance.

In Plain Language

lifting and reshaping sagging breasts; breast lift surgery

Clinical Context

Used to address breast ptosis (drooping) caused by aging, gravity, pregnancy, or weight loss. Can be combined with implant placement or fat transfer for volume restoration.

Billing & Documentation
