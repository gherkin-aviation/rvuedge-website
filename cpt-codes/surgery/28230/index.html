28230 Incision of foot tendon(s)

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: foot tendon tenotomy, foot tendon incision, foot tenotomy

Incision of foot tendon(s) without transfer or repair. This procedure divides a tendon for deformity correction or decompression.

Clinical Context

Performed for spasticity, contracture, or deformity where simple division without transfer achieves goal. Often combined with other procedures.
