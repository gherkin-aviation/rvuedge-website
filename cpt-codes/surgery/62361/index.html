62361 Implant spine infusion pump

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: spinal pump with reservoir, refillable spinal pump placement

Implantation of spinal cord infusion pump with refilling reservoir. Includes placement of programmable pump and reservoir.

Clinical Context

Used for implantation of programmable infusion pumps with refillable reservoirs for chronic intrathecal therapy.
