13160 Sec closure surgical wnd/dehsn xtn

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Secondary wound closure, Delayed closure, Wound reconstruction

Secondary closure or reconstruction of surgical wound with significant tissue loss, extensive healing difficulty, or complex wound management.

In Plain Language

Closure of complicated wound that did not heal normally; Advanced wound reconstruction

Clinical Context

Used for secondary closure of complex wounds, management of wound dehiscence or complication, or wounds requiring reconstruction beyond initial closure.
