50540 Revision of horseshoe kidney

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: horseshoe kidney repair, renal fusion correction, horseshoe kidney division

Surgical revision or correction of a horseshoe kidney (congenital fusion of the two kidneys at the lower poles).

Clinical Context

Used when horseshoe kidney causes symptoms such as obstruction, recurrent infection, or stone disease. May involve division at the isthmus.
