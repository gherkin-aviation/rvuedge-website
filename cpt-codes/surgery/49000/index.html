49000 Exploration of abdomen

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Abdominal exploration, exploratory laparotomy, open exploration

Surgical exploration of the abdominal cavity through an incision to evaluate intra-abdominal pathology, including organ inspection and tissue biopsy.

Clinical Context

Performed for acute abdomen, trauma evaluation, or when imaging cannot establish diagnosis. Allows direct visualization of intra-abdominal contents.
