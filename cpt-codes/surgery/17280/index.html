17280 destruction mal ls f/e/e/n/l/m .5/<

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: destroy malignant small face, facial cancer small

Destruction of malignant skin lesion (face, ears, eyelids, nose, lips, mucous membrane) with largest diameter 0.5 centimeter or less.

Clinical Context

Used for small malignant lesions on the face, particularly those near eyes, lips, or other high-visibility areas requiring specialized technique.
