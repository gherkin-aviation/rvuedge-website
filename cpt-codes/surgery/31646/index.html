31646 Brnchsc with therapy aspiration sbsq

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: additional therapeutic aspiration, repeat suctioning

Subsequent or additional therapeutic bronchoscopy aspiration of secretions, mucus, or material from the bronchial tree during the same or separate procedure.

Clinical Context

Performed when repeat airway clearance is needed for continued secretion management in mechanically ventilated or high-risk patients.
