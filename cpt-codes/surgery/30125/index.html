30125 Removal of nose lesion

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: extensive septal lesion removal, complex septal mass removal, septal reconstruction

Removal of an extensive lesion of the nasal septum using endoscopic approach with significant debridement and possible reconstruction.

Clinical Context

Indicated for large, infiltrative, or complex septal lesions requiring extensive debridement or affecting nasal airway; may involve functional reconstruction.
