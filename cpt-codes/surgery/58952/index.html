58952 Resection ovarian malignancy

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: TAH-BSO staging with lymph nodes, comprehensive ovarian cancer staging

Resection of ovarian malignancy with comprehensive surgical staging including Total Abdominal Hysterectomy with Bilateral Salpingo-Oophorectomy, omentectomy, lymph node sampling, and peritoneal biopsies.

In Plain Language

removing ovarian cancer with complete staging and lymph node assessment

Clinical Context

Most comprehensive staging approach for ovarian cancer, evaluates peritoneal surfaces, lymph nodes, and omental involvement for treatment planning.
