33235 Removal pacemaker electrode

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: lead extraction, pacemaker lead removal, transvenous lead extraction

Removal of single pacemaker lead (electrode) from transvenous position. Extraction of single lead without thoracotomy.

In Plain Language

removing pacemaker wire

Clinical Context

Used for infected, fractured, or malfunctioning leads. Requires careful technique to avoid vascular injury during extraction.
