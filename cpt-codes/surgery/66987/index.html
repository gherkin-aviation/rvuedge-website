66987 Extracapsular cataract removal complex with endoscopic cyclophotocoagulation

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: complex ECCE with ECP, complicated cataract extraction

Extracapsular cataract removal with complex presentation, including endothelial keratoplasty component for corneal endothelial cell replacement or protection in severely compromised corneas.

In Plain Language

removal of cloudy lens with corneal repair; complex cataract removal with cornea protection

Clinical Context

Reserved for complex cataracts with significant corneal involvement, Fuchs dystrophy, bullous keratopathy, or previous corneal trauma; requires combined expertise.

RVU Information

CPT 66987 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
