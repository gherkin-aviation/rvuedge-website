28153 Partial removal of toe

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: partial toe removal, phalanx amputation, toe partial amputation

Partial removal of a toe (phalanges only, metatarsal head preservation). This procedure removes part of the toe while preserving the metatarsal head.

In Plain Language

removing part of a toe; partial toe amputation

Clinical Context

Used for distal toe infection or trauma with proximal metatarsal head preservation. Maintains better weight-bearing mechanics than complete ray resection.
