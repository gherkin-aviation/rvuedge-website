37296 Revsc evasc imvt angio sf 1

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: iliac angioplasty simple first, aortoiliac angioplasty first

Percutaneous or open revascularization of the iliac artery using simple angioplasty technique in the first treated vessel.

Clinical Context

Indicated for aortoiliac disease presenting with claudication or critical limb ischemia. Simple angioplasty, first vessel.
