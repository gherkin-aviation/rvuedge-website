47125 Partial removal of liver

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: hepatectomy with thrombectomy, vascular hepatectomy, complex liver resection

Partial hepatectomy with subtotal vein thrombectomy or other major vascular intervention.

Clinical Context

Performed for hepatic tumors involving hepatic vein or portal vein, requiring concurrent thrombectomy or reconstruction.
