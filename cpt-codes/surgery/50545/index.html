50545 Laparoscopic radical nephrectomy

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: lap radical nephrectomy, laparoscopic cancer nephrectomy, minimally invasive radical nephrectomy

Laparoscopic radical nephrectomy (removal of entire kidney with surrounding tissues) for renal malignancy using minimally invasive approach.

Clinical Context

Used for renal cell carcinoma with excellent oncologic outcomes and reduced morbidity compared to open approach.
