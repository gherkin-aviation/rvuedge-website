33746 Tissue cgen car anomal each additional

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: additional shunt, shunt revision, shunt enlargement

Surgical revision, enlargement, or additional shunt creation for congenital cardiac anomaly (add-on code for each additional shunt).

Clinical Context

Used when an additional shunt is needed for adequate pulmonary blood flow or revision of existing shunt for improved hemodynamics.
