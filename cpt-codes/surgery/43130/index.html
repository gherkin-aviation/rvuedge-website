43130 Removal of esophagus pouch

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: esophageal diverticulectomy, Zenker's diverticulum removal

Esophageal diverticulotomy or removal of esophageal diverticulum (outpouching of esophageal wall) via external approach.

In Plain Language

surgical removal of esophageal pouch

Clinical Context

Performed for Zenker's diverticulum, traction diverticulum, or other esophageal outpouching causing dysphagia or aspiration. External approach with diverticulopexy.
