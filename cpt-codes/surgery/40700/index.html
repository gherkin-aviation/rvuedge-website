40700 Repair cleft lip/nasal

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: cleft lip repair, cheiloplasty

Surgical repair of unilateral complete cleft lip with nasal correction. Restores lip continuity and corrects associated nasal deformity.

Clinical Context

Used for complete unilateral cleft lip/palate. Primary surgery typically performed around 3 months of age. Requires precise surgical technique to optimize outcome.
