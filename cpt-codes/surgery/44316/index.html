44316 Devise bowel pouch

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: J-pouch creation, ileal pouch, ileal pouch-anal anastomosis, IPAA

Creation of ileal pouch from small bowel, often in conjunction with total proctocolectomy, to establish internal reservoir and restore intestinal continuity after bowel resection.

Clinical Context

Primarily used during total proctocolectomy for familial adenomatous polyposis or ulcerative colitis. Creates continent reservoir from terminal ileum anastomosed to anus, avoiding permanent external ostomy.
