15040 Harvest cultured skin graft

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: cultured skin harvest, CEA harvest

Harvest of cultured epidermal autografts including donor tissue collection, culture expansion, and preparation of cells for transplantation. Includes all processing and preparation work.

Clinical Context

Used for severe and extensive burns requiring rapid coverage with patient's own cultured skin cells. Reduces morbidity compared to traditional grafts.
