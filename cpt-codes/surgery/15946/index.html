15946 excision isch pr ulc prep mus flp

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ischial muscle flap, ischial myocutaneous flap

Excision of ischial pressure ulcer with preparation and coverage using muscle flap for maximal vascularization and wound healing.

Clinical Context

Used for large ischial pressure ulcers where muscle flap provides superior blood supply. Highest success rate and lowest recurrence for extensive ischial wounds.
