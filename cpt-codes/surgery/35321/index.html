35321 Rechanneling of artery

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: axillary endarterectomy, axillary artery rechanneling

Rechanneling (thrombectomy with endarterectomy) of axillary artery with restoration of upper extremity perfusion.

Clinical Context

Surgical management of axillary artery stenosis/occlusion causing arm claudication or critical limb ischemia. Often combined with other procedures addressing subclavian disease.
