24164 Removal prosthesis radial head

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: radial head prosthesis removal, radial implant extraction

Removal of prosthetic implant of the radial head. Extraction of failed radial head replacement.

Clinical Context

Used for removal of failed radial head prosthesis due to loosening, breakage, infection, or other complications.
