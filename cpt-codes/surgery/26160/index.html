26160 Remove tendon sheath lesion

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: tendon sheath ganglion removal, synovial cyst excision, De Quervain release

Removal (excision) of tendon sheath lesion (ganglion, cyst, or mass) of hand.

In Plain Language

removal of cyst from tendon sheath; ganglion removal from hand

Clinical Context

Used for excision of ganglion cysts, synovial cysts, or other benign lesions of tendon sheaths.
