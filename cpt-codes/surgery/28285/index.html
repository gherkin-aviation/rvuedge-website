28285 Repair of hammertoe

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: hammertoe repair, hammertoe correction, hammertoe surgery

Repair of hammertoe deformity (includes osseous and soft tissue procedures). This procedure corrects hammertoe with bone and soft tissue modifications.

Clinical Context

Performed for symptomatic hammertoes causing pain and dysfunction. Combines tendon release, joint fusion or arthroplasty, and skin closure.
