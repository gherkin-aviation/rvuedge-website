43610 Excision of stomach lesion

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: gastric ulcer excision, duodenal ulcer excision, ulcer removal

Excision of gastric or duodenal ulcer with closure of ulcer base via open approach. This removes ulcerated tissue.

Clinical Context

Surgical management of refractory gastric or duodenal ulcer disease; rare indication with modern medical therapy.
