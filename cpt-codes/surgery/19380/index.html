19380 Revision reconstructed breast

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: reconstruction revision, secondary reconstruction

Surgical revision of previously reconstructed breast tissue to improve contour, symmetry, or address complications. This secondary procedure optimizes reconstruction results.

Clinical Context

Used to address volume loss, contour irregularities, asymmetry, or scarring following initial breast reconstruction. Can involve lipoinjection, flap adjustment, or implant exchange.
