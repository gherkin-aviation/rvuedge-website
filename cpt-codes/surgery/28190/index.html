28190 Removal of foot foreign body

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: foreign body removal foot simple, splinter removal foot, superficial foreign object removal

Removal of a foreign body from the foot without significant skin incision. This procedure removes a superficial foreign object with minimal tissue trauma.

In Plain Language

removing something stuck in the foot; splinter removal

Clinical Context

Used for superficial foreign bodies (splinters, glass, nails) that can be removed without formal surgery. Short global period reflects simple nature.
