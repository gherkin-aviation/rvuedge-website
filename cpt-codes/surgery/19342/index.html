19342 Insj/replacement brst implant sep d

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: reconstruction with implant delayed, secondary implant placement

Surgical insertion or replacement of a breast implant in a separate surgical stage after previous mastectomy, or during a secondary reconstruction procedure. This represents staged reconstruction.

In Plain Language

placing implant in separate surgery after breast removal

Clinical Context

Used for staged breast reconstruction weeks to months after mastectomy, or replacement of failed reconstruction. Allows adequate tissue healing before implant insertion.
