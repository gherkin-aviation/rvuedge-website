63091 Remove vertebral body add-on

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: additional lumbar vertebrectomy, lumbar extension

Removal of vertebral body for decompression, add-on code for each additional lumbar vertebra. Used when lumbar vertebrectomy requires excising multiple vertebral bodies.

Clinical Context

Applied when multi-level lumbar vertebrectomy is performed for extensive compressive pathology affecting multiple levels.
