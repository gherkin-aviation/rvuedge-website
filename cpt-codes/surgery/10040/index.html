10040 Extraction

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: Foreign body removal, FB extraction, Foreign object removal

Extraction of embedded foreign body from subcutaneous tissue or deeper structures. Surgical removal of radiopaque or palpable retained objects.

Clinical Context

Performed for retained metal fragments, glass, wood splinters, or other objects causing infection, inflammation, or functional impairment.
