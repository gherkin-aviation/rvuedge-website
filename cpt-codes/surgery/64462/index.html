64462 Pvb thoracic second+ injection site

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: thoracic paravertebral block multi-level, PVB thoracic multiple levels, bilateral paravertebral block

Paravertebral nerve block for thoracic region with medication injection at two or more injection sites. Each additional level requires separate injection.

Clinical Context

Used for extensive thoracic pain or when multiple levels require anesthesia. Covers broader area than single injection but requires multiple needle passes.
