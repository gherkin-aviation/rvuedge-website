20816 Replantation digit complete

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: digit replantation, finger reattachment

Replantation of a completely amputated finger or thumb with vascular and nerve repair.

Clinical Context

Emergency procedure for complete digit amputation; success rates are high with appropriate vascular and nerve repair.
