33877 Thoracoabdominal graft

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Thoracoabdominal aorta repair, TAA graft, thoracoabdominal graft

Thoracoabdominal aortic graft; surgical replacement of the aorta extending from the thorax through the abdomen with a prosthetic graft to address extensive aortic disease spanning multiple visceral segments.

Clinical Context

Most extensive aortic reconstruction, used for chronic thoracoabdominal aortic aneurysm. Requires segmental aortic clamping or left heart bypass, careful management of spinal cord perfusion, and visceral artery reimplantation.
