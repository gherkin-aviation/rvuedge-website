43351 Surgical opening esophagus

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: esophagostomy, esophageal feeding tube, cervical esophagostomy

Esophagostomy with creation of surgical opening into the esophagus for access or diversion with placement of external drainage or feeding tube. This creates esophageal access.

In Plain Language

creating opening in esophagus for tube; surgical esophageal feeding

Clinical Context

Used for long-term nutritional support when gastric access not feasible; cervical approach provides easier management than PEG.
