59150 Treat ectopic pregnancy

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: lap ectopic salpingostomy, laparoscopic tube salvage

Laparoscopic treatment of ectopic pregnancy with evacuation and salpingostomy (tube-preserving approach) or salpingectomy.

In Plain Language

camera surgery to save the fallopian tube with ectopic pregnancy

Clinical Context

Minimally invasive tube-sparing approach for hemodynamically stable patients with unruptured ectopic, improving future fertility outcomes.
