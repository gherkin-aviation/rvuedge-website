42420 Excise parotid gland/lesion

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: complete parotidectomy, parotid removal

Surgical excision of the parotid gland with complex dissection, including removal of tumors with extension into deep lobe or requiring facial nerve reconstruction.

Clinical Context

Complete parotidectomy for malignant tumors, extensive disease, or deep lobe involvement requiring extensive dissection and facial nerve management.
