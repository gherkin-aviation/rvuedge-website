36011 Place catheter in vein

Surgery

Also known as: IJ central line, internal jugular catheter, jugular venous catheter

Percutaneous placement of central venous catheter via internal jugular vein approach. Central venous access for infusion or monitoring.

Clinical Context

Used for critical care, total parenteral nutrition, or hemodynamic monitoring. Common ICU access.
