32673 Thoracoscopy with thymus resection

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: VATS thymectomy, thoracoscopic thymus removal

Thoracoscopic resection of the thymus gland, performed for myasthenia gravis, thymoma, or other thymic pathology via minimally invasive approach.

Clinical Context

Used for myasthenia gravis, thymoma, or thymic hyperplasia when VATS approach provides adequate visualization for safe thymectomy.
