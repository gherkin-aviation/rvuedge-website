55040 Removal of hydrocele

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: hydrocele excision, hydrocele repair

Surgical removal of hydrocele (fluid and sac) through open approach with scrotal or inguinal incision.

Clinical Context

Definitive surgical treatment of symptomatic hydrocele with opening or removal of tunica vaginalis.
