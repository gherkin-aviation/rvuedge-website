54150 Circumcision with regionl block

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: circumcision with regional block, adult circumcision

Circumcision of the prepuce under regional anesthesia, used for phimosis or religious/cultural reasons.

Clinical Context

Performed in older children and adults with phimosis or for cultural/religious reasons. Regional block provides adequate anesthesia.
