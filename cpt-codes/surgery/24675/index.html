24675 Cltx ulnar fracture proximal with mnpj

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: closed treatment with reduction, ulnar fracture reduction

Closed treatment of proximal ulnar fracture with manipulation. Conservative management with reduction for displaced fracture.

Clinical Context

Used for displaced proximal ulnar fractures treated with closed reduction and immobilization.
