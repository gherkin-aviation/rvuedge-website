63001 Remove spine lamina 1/2 crvl

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: cervical laminotomy, cervical decompression

Laminotomy with removal of lamina and exploration in cervical spine for one or two levels. Includes inspection of neural elements.

In Plain Language

cervical bone removal for nerve relief; neck spine decompression

Clinical Context

Used for cervical spinal decompression by removal of lamina in cases of myelopathy or radiculopathy.
