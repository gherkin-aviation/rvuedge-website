61120 Burr hole for ventr puncture

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: burr hole ventricular puncture, ventricular tap via burr hole

Burr hole creation in the skull followed by ventricular puncture for sampling, drainage, or therapeutic intervention in the lateral ventricles.

Clinical Context

Performed for diagnostic sampling in suspected ventriculitis, measurement of intracranial pressure, or therapeutic drainage in hydrocephalus when percutaneous access unavailable or unsuccessful.
