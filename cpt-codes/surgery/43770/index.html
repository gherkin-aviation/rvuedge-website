43770 Laparoscopic place gastric adj device

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: lap gastric band placement, laparoscopic lap-band, adjustable gastric band

Laparoscopic placement of an adjustable gastric restriction device (gastric band) for morbid obesity.

Clinical Context

Restrictive bariatric procedure for morbid obesity. Silicone band placed around proximal stomach, adjustable via subcutaneous port. Mechanism creates sensation of early satiety.
