44345 Revision of colostomy

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: colostomy revision, colostomy reconstruction, moderate colostomy revision

Revision of colostomy with moderate complexity including reconstruction and possible relocation of stoma, or restoration of colonic continuity.

In Plain Language

surgery to repair and reshape colostomy; more complex ostomy repair

Clinical Context

Used for more complex colostomy revisions requiring stoma relocation, reconstruction, or partial restoration of continuity. Indicated for failed simple revisions or significant anatomic changes.
