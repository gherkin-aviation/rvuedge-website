34844 Endovasc visc aorta 4 graft

Surgery

Also known as: fenestrated EVAR quadruple graft, visceral aorta EVAR 4 vessels

Endovascular repair of visceral aorta using 4 fenestrated graft components. Placement of quadruple fenestrated graft for complex visceral aortic repair.

Clinical Context

Used for most complex suprarenal AAA involving all major visceral vessels and renal arteries.
