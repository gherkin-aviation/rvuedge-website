63664 Revise spine eltrd plate

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: spinal electrode revision with plate, surgical electrode revision, implanted electrode revision

Revision of spinal neuroelectrode array from plate or surgical implant. This procedure involves repositioning, replacement, or repair of surgically placed electrode arrays.

Clinical Context

Used for revision of surgically implanted spinal cord stimulation electrodes.
