44372 Small bowel endoscopy

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: small bowel endoscopy with laser, enteroscopy laser therapy, surgical laser treatment

Endoscopy of small bowel performed via enterotomy with laser therapy for ablation or coagulation of bleeding lesions or mucosal pathology.

Clinical Context

Used for control of small bowel bleeding from angiodysplasia or Meckel diverticulum, and for ablation of vascular malformations refractory to other endoscopic interventions.
