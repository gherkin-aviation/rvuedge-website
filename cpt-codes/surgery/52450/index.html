52450 Transurethral incision prostate

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: TUIP, transurethral prostate incision

Transurethral incision and resection of prostate tissue to relieve bladder outlet obstruction, typically in patients with benign prostatic hyperplasia unsuitable for TURP.

Clinical Context

Useful for small prostate glands or high-risk patients. Maintains continence better than TURP in some populations.
