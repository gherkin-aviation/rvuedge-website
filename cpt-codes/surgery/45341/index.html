45341 Sigmoidoscopy with ultrasound

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: sigmoidoscopy with ultrasound, flexible sig with EUS

Sigmoidoscopy with endoscopic ultrasound (EUS) probe for assessment of lesions or submucosal structures.

Clinical Context

Used to evaluate depth of invasion of rectal lesions, assess submucosal pathology, or guide fine-needle aspiration biopsy.
