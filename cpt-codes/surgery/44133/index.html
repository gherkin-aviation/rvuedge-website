44133 Enterectomy live donor

Surgery

Also known as: living donor enterectomy, bowel procurement living donor, living related intestine donation

Enterectomy from living donor for intestinal transplantation procurement.

In Plain Language

donor intestine removal from living person; organ donation surgery

Clinical Context

Surgical removal of donor small intestine from living donor for transplantation. Requires careful technique to preserve remaining donor bowel function.

RVU Information

CPT 44133 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
