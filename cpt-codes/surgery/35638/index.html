35638 Bpg aortobi-iliac

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: aortobiiliac bypass, aortobi-iliac graft, bilateral iliac bypass

Arterial bypass graft from abdominal aorta to bilateral iliac arteries for extensive aortoiliac occlusive disease. Restores bilateral lower extremity perfusion.

In Plain Language

main artery to both pelvic arteries bypass; restoring blood flow to both legs

Clinical Context

Indicated for symptomatic bilateral aortoiliac occlusion with extensive disease. Larger graft with higher morbidity than unilateral repair.
