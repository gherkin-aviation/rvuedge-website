27339 excision thigh/knee tumor dep 5cm/>

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: deep soft tissue excision large, tumor excision ≥5cm

Excision of soft tissue tumor of thigh or knee in deep tissues measuring 5 centimeters or greater.

Clinical Context

Applied to subfascial or intramuscular tumors 5 centimeters or larger requiring operative excision.
