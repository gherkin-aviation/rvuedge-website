34451 Removal of vein clot

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: vena cava thrombectomy, IVC thrombectomy, SVC thrombectomy

Removal of a venous thrombus or embolus from the vena cava (superior or inferior). Surgical extraction of blood clot from the vena cava.

In Plain Language

removing blood clot from main vein to heart

Clinical Context

Rare procedure for acute thrombosis of the vena cava. Often used in life-threatening presentations with impending hemodynamic collapse.
