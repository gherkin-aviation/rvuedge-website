43881 implant/replacement gstr neurostimulator eltrd

Surgery

Also known as: gastric pacemaker implant, enterra system implantation, neurostimulation electrode placement

Implantation or replacement of gastric neurostimulation electrode(s) for neuromodulation of gastric function.

In Plain Language

stomach electrical stimulation device placement; gastroparesis treatment device

Clinical Context

Placement of electrostimulation electrodes for treatment of refractory gastroparesis. Enterra system provides regular electrical pulses to improve gastric contractions.

RVU Information

CPT 43881 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
