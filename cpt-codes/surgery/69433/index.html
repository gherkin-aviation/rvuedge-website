69433 Create eardrum opening

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: grommet insertion, tympanostomy

Creation of opening in tympanum for drainage of middle ear effusion using grommet or ventilation tube.

Clinical Context

Used for otitis media with effusion. Common indications include middle ear effusion causing hearing loss or recurrent infections.
