38228 Car-t admn autologous

Surgery

Also known as: CAR-T infusion, CAR-T administration

Infusion of autologous chimeric antigen receptor T-cells into the patient for therapeutic treatment of hematologic malignancy.

Clinical Context

Therapeutic infusion of patient's own engineered CAR-T cells for treatment of leukemias and lymphomas.
