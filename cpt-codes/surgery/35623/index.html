35623 Bpg axillary-pop/-tibial

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: axillopopliteal bypass, axillotibial bypass, axillary-lower leg bypass

Arterial bypass graft from axillary artery to popliteal or tibial artery for extensive lower extremity ischemia. Restores distal circulation below the knee.

Clinical Context

Performed for critical limb ischemia with multilevel disease in poor surgical candidates. Usually requires saphenous vein as conduit. May be staged procedure.
