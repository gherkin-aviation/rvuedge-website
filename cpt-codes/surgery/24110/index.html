24110 excision/curettage bone cyst/benign tumor humerus

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: humerus cyst excision, bone lesion curettage, benign bone tumor removal

Excision or curettage of bone cyst or benign bone tumor of humerus. Removal or curettage of benign bone lesion from humerus with cortical preservation when possible.

Clinical Context

Performed for simple bone cyst, aneurysmal bone cyst, or benign bone tumors (osteochondroma, etc.) of humerus causing pain or fracture risk.
