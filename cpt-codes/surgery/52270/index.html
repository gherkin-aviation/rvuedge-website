52270 Cystoscopy & revise urethra

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: cysto with urethral incision, cysto with urethrotomy

Cystourethroscopy with endoscopic revision or incision of the urethra, typically to relieve stricture or correct anatomic abnormality obstructing normal urine flow.

Clinical Context

Used for urethral strictures in both male and female patients. Endoscopic approach has high recurrence rates; repeat procedures often necessary.
