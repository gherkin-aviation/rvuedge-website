43121 Partial removal of esophagus

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: esophageal replacement with colon, colon interposition

Total esophageal replacement with colon interposition graft via abdominal and thoracic approaches.

Clinical Context

Performed for extensive esophageal pathology unsuitable for gastric conduit. Used when stomach is unavailable or unsuitable for reconstruction.
