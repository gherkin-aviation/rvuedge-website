19357 Tissue xpndr plmt brst rcnstj

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: tissue expander placement, tissue expansion, breast reconstruction flap

Surgical placement of a tissue expander beneath the mastectomy skin flaps to create an implant pocket and expansion chamber. This prepares the breast pocket for subsequent implant insertion.

In Plain Language

placing a balloon-like device to stretch skin for reconstruction

Clinical Context

First stage of tissue expansion-based breast reconstruction. The expander is gradually filled over weeks to months to stretch tissue before implant placement.
