11462 excision skn hdrdnt ing simple/ntrm

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: inguinal excision, groin lesion excision

Excision of skin lesion involving hidden skin fold area such as inguinal fold with simple or intermediate closure. Includes removal and wound management.

Clinical Context

Used for removal of skin lesions in inguinal area with simple closure, often related to hidradenitis suppurativa or pilonidal disease.
