45171 excision rectal tumor transanal part

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: transanal endoscopic microsurgery, TEM partial, endoscopic rectal excision

Excision of rectal tumor via transanal endoscopic microsurgery (partial resection), removing selected lesions through the anus using operating microscope.

Clinical Context

Indicated for early rectal cancers (T1), benign polyps, or lesions in the mid-to-distal rectum unsuitable for endoscopic polypectomy but not requiring full proctectomy.
