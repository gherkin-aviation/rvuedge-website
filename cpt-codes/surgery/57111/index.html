57111 Vagnc complicated removal paravag tissue

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Complete vaginectomy with paravaginal dissection

Vaginectomy with complete removal of vaginal wall and paravaginal tissue and structures. Most extensive vaginectomy procedure.

Clinical Context

Used for advanced vaginal carcinoma extending into paravaginal structures. Provides most comprehensive oncologic resection.
