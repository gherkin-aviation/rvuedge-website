11441 excision face-mm b9+marg 0.6-1 centimeters

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: facial lesion excision, face excision

Excision of benign skin lesion on face with margins of 0.6 to 1 centimeter. Includes full-thickness excision and closure on facial area.

Clinical Context

Used for removal of benign lesions on face with moderate surgical margins.
