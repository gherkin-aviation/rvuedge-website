26535 Revise finger joint

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Finger joint arthroplasty, PIP arthroplasty, Finger joint reconstruction

Surgical arthroplasty or revision of a finger joint, typically the proximal interphalangeal or distal interphalangeal joint, to restore or improve function.

Clinical Context

Indicated for proximal interphalangeal or distal interphalangeal joint arthropathy, trauma sequelae, or dysfunction where reconstruction is preferable to fusion.
