44158 Colectomy with neo-rectum pouch

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: J-pouch creation, neorectum creation, pouch construction

Total colectomy with creation of neorectum or ileal pouch (J-pouch) for bowel continuity restoration.

In Plain Language

new pouch creation from intestine; bowel function restoration surgery

Clinical Context

Surgical creation of ileal reservoir (J-pouch, S-pouch, or W-pouch) to serve as neorectum after total colectomy for restoration of intestinal continuity.
