62351 Implant spinal canal catheter

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: tunneled spinal catheter, spinal catheter with pump

Implantation of spinal canal catheter with tunneling of catheter and more extensive surgical procedure including pump placement.

Clinical Context

Used for placement of intrathecal catheters with tunneling and/or pump device for chronic drug infusion.
