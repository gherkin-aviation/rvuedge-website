29899 Ankle arthroscopy/surgery

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: extensive ankle arthroscopy, ankle reconstruction scope, comprehensive ankle repair

Arthroscopic treatment of ankle with major reconstruction or extensive soft tissue repair, addressing severe intra-articular pathology or significant joint surface damage.

Clinical Context

Reserved for severe or complex ankle pathology requiring extensive reconstruction, such as anterior ankle impingement with scar tissue excision and bony spur removal, or significant articular surface damage.
