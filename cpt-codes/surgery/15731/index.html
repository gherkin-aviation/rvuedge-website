15731 Forehead flap with vascular pedicle

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: forehead flap nasal repair, pedicled forehead flap

Forehead flap with preserved vascular pedicle, including tissue elevation and transfer. Advanced nasal reconstruction with excellent color match.

In Plain Language

forehead skin moved to repair nose; specialized nose reconstruction

Clinical Context

Used for complex nasal defects and reconstructions where forehead tissue provides optimal cosmetic match and maintains robust blood supply.
