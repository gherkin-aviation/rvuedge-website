58140 Myomectomy abdominal method

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: abdominal myomectomy, open myomectomy, abdominal fibroid removal

Myomectomy via abdominal approach is removal of uterine fibroids (leiomyomas) through an open incision in the abdominal wall.

Clinical Context

Used for symptomatic fibroids causing bleeding, pain, or infertility. Preferred for large fibroids (>5-6 cm), multiple fibroids, or when cervical fibroids present. Preserves uterus for fertility.
