21261 Revise eye sockets

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: orbital revision with implant, eye socket reconstruction with implant

Revision of the eye socket (orbital walls) with insertion of implant material to improve contour or fill defects.

Clinical Context

Used when orbital wall defects require implant placement for structural support and aesthetic improvement.
