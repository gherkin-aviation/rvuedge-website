33910 Remove lung artery emboli

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Pulmonary embolectomy, PE removal, thromboembolectomy

Removal of pulmonary artery thrombus by pulmonary embolectomy with cardiopulmonary bypass; surgical extraction of massive pulmonary embolism with extracorporeal support.

Clinical Context

Emergency procedure for massive pulmonary embolism causing hemodynamic collapse refractory to thrombolytics. Cardiopulmonary bypass provides hemodynamic support during clot extraction.
