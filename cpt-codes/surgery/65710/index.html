65710 Corneal transplant

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: lamellar keratoplasty, partial thickness graft

Corneal transplant; lamellar.

Clinical Context

Partial thickness corneal transplantation for anterior or posterior corneal pathology. Lamellar approaches (DLEK, DMEK) have better outcomes than penetrating keratoplasty for endothelial disease.
