59514 Cesarean delivery only

Surgery

Also known as: cesarean delivery only, cesarean surgical only, c-section delivery only

Cesarean delivery only (without antepartum or postpartum care), when another provider managed prenatal and postpartum care.

In Plain Language

c-section surgery without prenatal care

Clinical Context

Used when cesarean performed by surgeon while obstetrical care managed by another provider (e.g., mid-pregnancy referral).
