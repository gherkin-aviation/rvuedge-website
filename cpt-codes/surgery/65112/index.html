65112 Remove eye/revise socket

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: evisceration with socket revision, socket reconstruction

Surgical removal of the eye and surrounding orbital contents with revision of the orbital socket to improve orbital contour for prosthetic fitting.

Clinical Context

Performed when evisceration combined with socket reconstruction to improve esthetic outcome and prosthetic eye fit. Involves bone contouring or grafting.
