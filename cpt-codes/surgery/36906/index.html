36906 Thrmbc/nfs dialysis circuit

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: repeat thrombectomy, second thrombectomy, declotting repeat

Thrombectomy, open; of mature arteriovenous fistula or graft (subsequent attempt); same day as original attempt.

Clinical Context

Used when initial thrombectomy unsuccessful and second attempt needed same day. Indicates technical difficulty.
