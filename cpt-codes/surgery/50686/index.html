50686 Measure ureter pressure

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: ureteral pressure study, pressure flow study, ureteral manometry

Measurement and recording of ureteral pressure (Whitaker test for ureter) through a percutaneous catheter to assess obstructive level.

Clinical Context

Used to differentiate obstructive from non-obstructive ureteral dilation when imaging is equivocal.
