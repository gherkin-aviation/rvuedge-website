29530 Strapping of knee

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: knee strapping, knee taping, knee support

Application of strapping or taping to the knee joint for support and restriction of pathological motion.

In Plain Language

knee support tape; knee wrap

Clinical Context

Used for knee ligament sprains, patellofemoral pain syndrome, and post-injury knee stabilization.
