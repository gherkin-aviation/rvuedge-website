27893 Decompression of leg

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: posterior compartment fasciotomy, deep posterior release, leg decompression deep

Decompression of the lower leg including the deep posterior compartment. This fasciotomy releases the posterior compartment in addition to other compartments to address compartment syndrome.

Clinical Context

Performed for compartment syndrome involving the deep posterior compartment, often combined with other compartment releases. Essential in extensive crush injuries or major fractures.
