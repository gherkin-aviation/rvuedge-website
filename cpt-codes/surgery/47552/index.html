47552 Biliary endoscopic percutaneous diagnosis with speci

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: Percutaneous cholangioscopy, POCS diagnostic, therapeutic cholangioscopy

Percutaneous (through-the-skin) endoscopy of the biliary tree for diagnostic evaluation (visualization) with specimen collection to diagnose obstruction, strictures, or malignancy.

Clinical Context

Performed to visualize and assess biliary strictures, masses, or infection. Allows tissue diagnosis and helps guide further intervention.
