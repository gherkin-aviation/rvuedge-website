43118 Partial removal of esophagus

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: thoracoabdominal esophagectomy, complex partial esophagectomy

Partial esophagectomy (removal of a portion of the esophagus) via combined thoracic and abdominal approach with reconstruction.

Clinical Context

Performed for esophageal pathology requiring optimal exposure through combined approach. Allows for thoracic assessment and abdominal reconstruction.
