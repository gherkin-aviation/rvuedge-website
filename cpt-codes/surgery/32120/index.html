32120 Re-exploration of chest

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: re-exploration of chest, post-op thoracotomy

Surgical re-exploration of the chest performed during the acute post-operative period, typically to evaluate bleeding, infection, or other complications necessitating return to surgery.

Clinical Context

Used for acute post-operative complications such as post-operative bleeding, infection, cardiac tamponade, or respiratory compromise within 24-48 hours of initial surgery.
