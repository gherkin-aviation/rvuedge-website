28047 Resection foot/toe tumor 3 centimeters/>

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: foot tumor resection large malignant, foot cancer removal large, extensive foot tumor resection

Radical resection (comprehensive removal) of a malignant tumor of the foot or toe with a diameter of 3 centimeters or greater. This procedure removes larger cancers with wide margins and appropriate depth.

Clinical Context

Indicated for larger malignant lesions requiring more extensive soft tissue removal, possible amputation, or reconstruction. Often requires multidisciplinary approach including plastic surgery.
