65779 Cover eye with membrane suture

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: amniotic membrane transplant with fixation, sutured AMT

Placement of amniotic membrane on the ocular surface; with sutures.

In Plain Language

placing and stitching special healing membrane on eye

Clinical Context

Amniotic membrane placement with sutures for additional fixation in severe ocular surface disease or chemical burns. Provides longer-term tissue coverage and reconstruction.
