24430 repair non/mal humerus wo graft

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: humerus nonunion repair, malunion repair

Repair of nonunion or malunion of the humerus without graft. Surgical treatment of failed fracture healing without bone graft.

Clinical Context

Used to address nonunion or malunion of humerus fractures using fixation alone without the need for bone grafting.
