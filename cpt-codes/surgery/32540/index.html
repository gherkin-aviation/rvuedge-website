32540 Removal of lung lesion

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: lung lesion removal, localized lung resection

Surgical removal of a localized lung lesion via thoracotomy, combining wedge or segmental resection with direct lesion removal for benign or malignant pathology.

Clinical Context

Used for solitary lung nodules, benign lesions, or small malignancies suitable for limited resection preserving maximum lung parenchyma.
