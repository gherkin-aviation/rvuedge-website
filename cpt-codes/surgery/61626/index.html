61626 Transcatheter perm occls/embol noncns

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: arterial embolization, endovascular occlusion

Percutaneous transluminal catheter-based permanent occlusion or embolization of non-intracranial arterial lesion.

In Plain Language

Sealing blood vessel outside brain

Clinical Context

Embolization of head and neck vascular lesions, tumors, or fistulas outside the cranium. Used for hemostasis and tumor devascularization.
