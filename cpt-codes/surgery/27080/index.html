27080 Removal of tail bone

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: coccyx removal, coccygectomy, tailbone removal

Surgical removal of the coccyx (tailbone) with appropriate soft tissue dissection and reconstruction.

Clinical Context

Performed for coccyx fracture with nonunion, infected tailbone, or tumor requiring complete removal for symptom resolution.
