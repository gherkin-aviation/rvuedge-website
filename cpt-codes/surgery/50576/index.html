50576 Kidney endoscopy & treatment

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: retrograde endoscopy with therapy, ureteroscopic intervention, transurethral renal intervention

Transurethral endoscopy of the kidney (retrograde endoscopy) with additional therapeutic intervention.

Clinical Context

Used for retrograde treatment of upper urinary tract pathology with endoscopic therapeutic procedures.
