64778 Digit nerve surgery add-on

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: additional digit neuroma removal

Surgical removal of additional nerve lesion(s) in the digit(s) as an add-on procedure to the primary digit nerve lesion removal.

Clinical Context

Used when multiple neuromas or lesions are removed from different digits in the same operative session. Each additional digit requires this add-on code.
