29355 application long leg cast walker

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: long leg walking cast, cast with walker, weight-bearing long leg cast

Application of a long leg cast with an integrated walker or foot extension to provide weight-bearing capability and ambulation support.

In Plain Language

walking cast for leg; full leg cast with foot pad

Clinical Context

Used for stable lower extremity fractures where early weight-bearing is permitted, allowing patient ambulation during healing.
