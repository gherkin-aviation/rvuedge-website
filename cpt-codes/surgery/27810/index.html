27810 Treatment of ankle fracture

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: percutaneous fixation bimalleolar, bimalleolar fracture fixation

Initial closed treatment of ankle fracture (bimalleolar) with manipulation and percutaneous fixation.

In Plain Language

setting broken ankle (2 sides) with pins

Clinical Context

Performed for displaced bimalleolar ankle fractures requiring fixation through minimal incisions or closed technique.
