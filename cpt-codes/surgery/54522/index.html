54522 Orchiectomy partial

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: partial orchiectomy, wedge excision

Partial removal of testicular tissue (partial orchiectomy) while preserving portion of viable testis.

Clinical Context

Conservative surgery for testicular lesions, benign tumors, or partial necrosis while preserving hormone-producing tissue and testicular function.
