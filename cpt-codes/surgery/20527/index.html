20527 Njx nzm palmar fascial cord

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: dupuytren injection, enzymatic fascial injection

Injection of enzyme (hyaluronidase/collagenase) into palmar fascia or fascia of forearm to treat Dupuytren contracture. This procedure chemically breaks down abnormal fibrous tissue.

Clinical Context

Used for nonsurgical treatment of Dupuytren contracture, enzymatically breaking down pathologic palmar fascia to improve hand function.
