62263 Epidural lysis mult sessions

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: epidural lysis multiple sessions, adhesion lysis, epidural adhesiolysis

Epidural lysis of adhesions in spinal canal performed over multiple treatment sessions. Includes administration of lytic agents.

Clinical Context

Used for treatment of epidural scar tissue causing spinal cord compression in multiple sessions for improved blood flow and symptom relief.
