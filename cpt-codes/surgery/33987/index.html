33987 Artery expos/graft artery

Surgery

Also known as: artery exposure, artery graft

Arterial exposure and graft placement for a native artery. Provides surgical access and revascularization pathway for arterial reconstruction.

Clinical Context

Used when establishing access to the artery or placing a graft to restore blood flow. Common in peripheral arterial disease and revascularization procedures.
