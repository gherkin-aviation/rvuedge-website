44136 Intestine transplant live

Surgery

Also known as: intestine transplant living donor, living related bowel transplant, LRSBX

Transplantation of living donor small intestine allograft into recipient for short bowel syndrome.

Clinical Context

Surgical placement of living donor small intestine allograft into recipient with short bowel syndrome. Provides potential for better long-term graft survival than cadaver source.
