36455 Bld exchange truj oth thn nb

Surgery

Also known as: exchange transfusion, partial exchange, adult exchange transfusion

Blood exchange transfusion, other than newborn; total volume transfusion (one or more repeated exchanges).

In Plain Language

replacing blood to treat blood cell disorders; exchange of blood in older children and adults

Clinical Context

Used in pediatric and adult patients for sickle cell disease, severe anemia, or polycythemia requiring plasma exchange and cell replacement.
