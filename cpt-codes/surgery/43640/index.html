43640 Vagotomy & pylorus repair

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: vagotomy and pyloroplasty, open vagotomy, V&P

Vagotomy and pyloroplasty via open approach for peptic ulcer disease or gastric outlet obstruction.

Clinical Context

Historically used for treatment of peptic ulcer disease and gastric acid hypersecretion, now rarely performed due to proton pump inhibitors. Used for severe gastric outlet obstruction.
