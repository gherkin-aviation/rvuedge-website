11104 Punch biopsy skin single lesion

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: punch biopsy, skin punch, punch biopsy skin

Punch biopsy of skin from a single lesion using a circular cutting tool. This procedure removes a cylindrical full-thickness sample of skin for histopathological evaluation.

Clinical Context

Used for evaluation of pigmented lesions, inflammatory conditions, or when deeper tissue assessment is needed. Provides full-thickness specimen including subcutaneous tissue.
