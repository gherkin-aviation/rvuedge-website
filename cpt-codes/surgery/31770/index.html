31770 Repair/graft of bronchus

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: bronchus repair, bronchial graft

Surgical repair or graft of a damaged bronchus, typically performed to restore continuity and function of the bronchial tube following injury, stenosis, or defect.

Clinical Context

Used for traumatic bronchial injuries, post-pneumonectomy complications, or congenital bronchial defects requiring restoration of normal anatomy and function.
