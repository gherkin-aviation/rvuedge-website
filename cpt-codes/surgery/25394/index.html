25394 Repair carpal bone shorten

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: carpal bone shortening, carpal osteotomy

Repair of a carpal (wrist) bone by shortening or realignment through osteotomy to improve alignment.

Clinical Context

Performed for carpal bone length discrepancies or malalignment affecting wrist mechanics.
