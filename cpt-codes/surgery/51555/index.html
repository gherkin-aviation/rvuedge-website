51555 Partial removal of bladder

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: partial cystectomy with reconstruction, segmental cystectomy with repair

Partial removal of bladder with reconstruction, segmental cystectomy with bladder reconstruction or augmentation.

Clinical Context

Used for bladder cancer or dysfunction where partial removal feasible with reconstruction to maximize continence and capacity.
