33391 Valvuloplasty aortic valve

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: complex aortic valve repair, extensive valve reconstruction

Complex reconstruction of aortic valve with extensive repairs or techniques, preserving valve function.

Clinical Context

Used for complex aortic regurgitation when extensive valve repair is needed.
