58323 Sperm washing

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: sperm preparation, semen preparation, sperm processing

Sperm washing is processing and preparation of semen specimen to separate motile sperm from seminal fluid and cells for use in fertility treatments.

Clinical Context

Laboratory procedure to concentrate and purify sperm for intrauterine insemination or other assisted reproductive techniques. Important for male factor infertility treatment.
