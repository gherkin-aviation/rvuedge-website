21436 Optx crnfcl sep complicated mlt int

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: complex Le Fort multi-fixation, craniofacial separation complex multi-site fixation

Open treatment of craniofacial separation with complex internal fixation of multiple sites. Surgical reconstruction addressing craniofacial separation with sophisticated multi-point internal fixation addressing maxilla, nasal complex, and orbital elements.

Clinical Context

Indicated for most complex craniofacial separations with involvement of orbital rims, nasal bones, and pterygoid plates requiring reconstruction at multiple anatomic levels. Highest morbidity case representing severe facial trauma.
