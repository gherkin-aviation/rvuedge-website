23210 Radical rescj tumor scapula

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: radical scapular resection, en bloc scapula excision, wide local excision scapula

Radical resection of tumor (soft tissue and bone), scapula. En bloc removal of malignant tumor involving scapula and surrounding tissues with adequate margins.

Clinical Context

Utilized for primary scapular bone malignancies (chondrosarcoma, osteosarcoma) or metastatic tumors, requiring comprehensive soft tissue and skeletal resection for oncologic control.
