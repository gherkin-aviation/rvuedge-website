27612 Exploration of ankle joint

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ankle exploration, ankle joint exploration

Diagnostic exploration of the ankle joint performed to identify, assess, or treat pathology within the joint capsule and surrounding structures.

Clinical Context

Performed for suspected intra-articular loose bodies, synovitis, cartilage damage, or recurrent ankle instability. Used both diagnostically and therapeutically.
