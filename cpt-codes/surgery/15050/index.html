15050 Pinch graft up to 2 centimeters diam

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: pinch graft, punch graft

Pinch graft applied to wounds or burns, diameter up to 2 centimeters. Simple skin graft technique using small punch or pinch of skin.

In Plain Language

small piece of skin graft; pinching skin onto wound

Clinical Context

Used for small to medium wounds, burns, or defects. Pinch grafts heal faster than split-thickness grafts and are useful for facial reconstruction.
