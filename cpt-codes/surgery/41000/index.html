41000 Drainage of mouth lesion

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: floor of mouth drainage, mouth floor abscess drainage, oral floor drainage

Surgical drainage of an abscess or lesion of the mouth floor using intraoral approach.

Clinical Context

Used for infected lesions or abscesses of the floor of the mouth requiring drainage. Common with Ludwig's angina, submandibular gland infections, and oral infections.
