15770 Derma-fat-fascia graft

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: derma-fat-fascia graft, composite tissue graft

Derma-fat-fascia autologous graft including skin, fat, and fascia layers. Thick composite autograft for volume restoration and reconstruction.

Clinical Context

Used for facial contour restoration, depressed scars, and defects requiring volume restoration with autologous tissue.
