22586 Arthrd pre-sac ntrbdy l5-s1

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Presacral ALIF, Anterior lumbar interbody fusion

Arthrodesis, presacral interbody technique, including discectomy and/or corpectomy and interbody device placement, lumbar; single vertebral segment (L5-S1).

Clinical Context

Used for lumbosacral instability or degeneration; presacral approach provides excellent visualization and fusion rates for L5-S1 pathology.
