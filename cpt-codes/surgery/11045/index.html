11045 Debridement subcutaneous tissue each additional

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: Additional subcutaneous debridement, Extra fat tissue removal

Debridement of subcutaneous tissue, each additional twenty square centimeters or less. Add-on code for additional areas of subcutaneous debridement.

In Plain Language

Additional fat tissue cleanup; Extra area subcutaneous removal

Clinical Context

Applied for extensive subcutaneous infections or trauma requiring debridement of multiple regions.
