45330 Diagnostic sigmoidoscopy

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: sigmoidoscopy, rigid proctosigmoidoscopy, lower bowel scope

Diagnostic sigmoidoscopy (visual examination of sigmoid colon) via rigid or flexible endoscope without therapeutic intervention.

Clinical Context

Performed for evaluation of lower gastrointestinal symptoms, screening for colorectal cancer, or surveillance of known colonic pathology.
