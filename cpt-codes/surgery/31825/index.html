31825 Repair of windpipe defect

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: tracheal defect repair, tracheal patch repair

Surgical repair of a tracheal defect using more advanced techniques including patch grafting, flap advancement, or other reconstructive procedures to address the defect.

In Plain Language

windpipe patch repair; tracheal opening reconstruction

Clinical Context

Used for larger tracheal defects, post-pneumonectomy complications, or defects requiring material augmentation with grafts or flaps for adequate closure.
