50081 Percutaneous nl/pl lithotrp cplx>2cm

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: PCNL complex, extended PCNL, staghorn PCNL

Percutaneous nephrolithotomy for complex stone greater than two centimeters in largest diameter, requiring multiple fragments or advanced techniques.

Clinical Context

Used for large, complex renal calculi (greater than 2 centimeters) including staghorn stones, requiring multiple tracts or multiple procedures. Indicated for stones unsuitable for ESWL or flexible ureteroscopy.
