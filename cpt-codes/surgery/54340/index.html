54340 repair hypspad complicated simple

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: simple hypospadias repair, basic hypospadias correction

Repair of hypospadias with simple technique, typically one-stage repair without tissue transfer.

Clinical Context

Used for glandular or coronal hypospadias without chordee. Single-stage repair with adequate success rate.
