61526 Removal of brain lesion

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: complex brain lesion resection, deep brain tumor removal

Craniotomy or craniectomy with removal of complex or deep intracranial brain lesion from supratentorial location, requiring advanced techniques.

Clinical Context

Performed for intra-axial brain tumors in eloquent or deep locations (thalamus, basal ganglia, brainstem), requiring advanced intraoperative monitoring and surgical techniques.
