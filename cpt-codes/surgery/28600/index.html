28600 Treat foot dislocation

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: foot dislocation closed, midfoot joint dislocation non-operative, closed treatment

Closed treatment of foot dislocation without manipulation, typically involving metatarsocuneiform or other midfoot joint dislocations managed with immobilization.

Clinical Context

Used for minimally displaced foot dislocations suitable for conservative management with immobilization and protected weight-bearing.
