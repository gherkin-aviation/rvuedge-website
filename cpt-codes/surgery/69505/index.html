69505 Remove mastoid structures

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: subtotal petrosectomy, extended mastoidectomy, petrous apex disease removal

Subtotal petrosectomy or removal of mastoid structures including the entire air cell system and portions of the petrous bone for extensive disease control.

In Plain Language

removal of larger mastoid area; extensive ear bone surgery

Clinical Context

Used for extensive cholesteatoma, petrous bone disease, recurrent cholesteatoma with canal wall reconstruction failure, or severe chronic otitis media with extensive bone destruction.
