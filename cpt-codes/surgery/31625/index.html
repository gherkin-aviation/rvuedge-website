31625 Bronchoscopy with biopsy(s)

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: bronchoscopic biopsy, bronchial biopsy, lung biopsy via scope

Bronchoscopy with one or more tissue biopsies taken from bronchial mucosa or pulmonary lesions for histopathologic examination and tissue diagnosis.

Clinical Context

Performed for suspected pulmonary malignancy, infections (tuberculosis, fungal), or other parenchymal pathology requiring histologic diagnosis.
