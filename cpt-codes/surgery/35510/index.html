35510 Artery byp graft carotid-brchial

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: carotid-brachial bypass, CB bypass

Arterial bypass graft from carotid to brachial artery with restoration of upper extremity perfusion.

Clinical Context

Extrathoracic revascularization for upper extremity ischemia when subclavian approach not feasible. Less commonly used; requires careful monitoring for steal syndrome.
