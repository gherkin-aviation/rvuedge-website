57291 Construction of vagina

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Vaginal reconstruction, Vaginoplasty

Construction of vagina using intestinal flap or skin graft. Creation of neovagina for vaginal agenesis or loss.

In Plain Language

Creating new vagina

Clinical Context

Used for Mayer-Rokitansky-Küster-Hauser syndrome (MRKH), vaginal agenesis, or complete vaginal loss. Restores sexual function.
