50760 Ureteroureterostomy

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ureteroureterostomy, ureteral anastomosis, end-to-end ureteral repair

Ureteroureterostomy, surgical anastomosis between two segments of ureter or between ureter and contralateral ureter to restore urine drainage.

Clinical Context

Indicated for ureteral stricture, injury, or obstruction when primary repair is feasible. Common in trauma and post-operative stricture management.
