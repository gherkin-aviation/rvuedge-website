26412 Repair/graft hand tendon

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Hand tendon repair with graft, Tendon graft, Autograft repair

Surgical repair and grafting of a hand tendon with graft material placed to bridge defects, augment repair, or restore adequate length when primary repair is not feasible.

Clinical Context

Indicated when primary repair is impossible due to tissue loss, infection, or retraction. May be performed at initial injury or during delayed repair after infection resolution.
