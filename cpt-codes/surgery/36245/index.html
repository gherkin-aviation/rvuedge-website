36245 Ins catheter abdominal/l-ext artery first

Surgery

Also known as: abdominal-extremity catheterization, visceral artery catheter, lower extremity arteriography

Percutaneous placement of catheter into abdominal or lower extremity artery as first selective catheterization for angiography or intervention.

Clinical Context

Used for aortic, renal, mesenteric, or lower extremity angiography and intervention.
