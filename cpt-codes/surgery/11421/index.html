11421 excision h-f-nk-sp b9+marg 0.6-1

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: head lesion excision, facial excision

Excision of benign skin lesion on head, face, neck, or scalp with margins of 0.6 to 1 centimeter. Includes closure of the excision site.

In Plain Language

removal of facial growth; head growth removal

Clinical Context

Used for removal of benign lesions on head and neck areas with moderate surgical margins.
