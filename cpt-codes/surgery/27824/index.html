27824 Treat lower leg fracture

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: closed reduction lower leg, conservative leg fracture

Initial closed treatment of lower leg fracture (fibula-tibia) with manipulation and immobilization without operative fixation.

Clinical Context

Used for uncomplicated closed lower leg fractures of tibia and fibula amenable to non-operative management.
