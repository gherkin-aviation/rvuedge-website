69540 Excision aural polyp

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: polyp removal, aural polyp excision, external auditory canal polyp removal

Excision of aural or external auditory canal polyps, which are benign growths arising from the external auditory canal or middle ear.

In Plain Language

removal of ear polyp; taking out a growth in the ear canal

Clinical Context

Used for symptomatic aural polyps causing drainage, otalgia, hearing loss, or mass sensation. Benign lesions but may obstruct canal or middle ear.
