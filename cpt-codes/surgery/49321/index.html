49321 Laparoscopy biopsy

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: laparoscopic biopsy, lap biopsy, minimally invasive biopsy

Laparoscopic biopsy of abdominal or pelvic organs with tissue collection for pathological examination.

In Plain Language

tissue sample via camera-guided procedure; minimally invasive tissue collection

Clinical Context

Laparoscopic tissue sampling of suspicious abdominal or pelvic lesions. Used when diagnosis cannot be established by imaging alone or to confirm malignancy.
