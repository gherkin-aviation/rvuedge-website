66179 Aqueous shunt eye without graft

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: aqueous shunt without graft, tube shunt, glaucoma implant

Aqueous shunt to subconjunctival space without provision for filtration bleb; does not include implantation of graft material.

Clinical Context

Aqueous shunt implant for glaucoma without tissue graft. Primary drainage to subconjunctival space.
