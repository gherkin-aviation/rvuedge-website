65270 Repair of eye wound

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: corneal abrasion repair, superficial corneal wound repair

Repair of partial thickness corneal wound; corneal abrasion.

Clinical Context

Minor corneal injuries from contact lens trauma, chemical exposure, or mechanical injury. Often self-limited but may require protective patching, antibiotics, and pain management.
