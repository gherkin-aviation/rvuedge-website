25575 Optx rdl & uln shft fracture rds & uln

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ORIF both bones forearm, open reduction internal fixation radius and ulna

Open treatment (surgical repair) of both radial and ulnar shaft fractures with internal fixation of both bones.

Clinical Context

Used for complex or unstable fractures involving both radial and ulnar shafts requiring operative intervention and fixation at both sites. Standard treatment for most displaced both-bone fractures in adults.
