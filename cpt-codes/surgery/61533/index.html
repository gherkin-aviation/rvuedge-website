61533 Implant brain electrodes

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: additional electrode placement, electrode repositioning

Craniotomy or craniectomy with placement of additional or repositioned electrodes for intracranial recording or stimulation.

Clinical Context

Used when initial electrode grid or strip placement requires expansion, repositioning, or addition of electrodes for better seizure localization or functional mapping.
