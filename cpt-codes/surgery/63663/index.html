63663 Revise spine eltrd percutaneous aray

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: spinal electrode revision, percutaneous electrode repositioning, SCS array revision

Revision of spinal neuroelectrode array from percutaneous placement. This procedure involves repositioning, replacement, or repair of percutaneously placed electrode arrays.

Clinical Context

Used for revision of percutaneously placed spinal cord stimulation electrodes due to malposition, malfunction, or pain relief optimization.
