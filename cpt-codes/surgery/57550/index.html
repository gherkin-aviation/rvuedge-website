57550 Removal of residual cervix

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: simple cervical stump removal, completion cervicectomy

Removal of residual cervix (simplified procedure) involves excision of cervical stump following previous hysterectomy with minimal additional dissection.

Clinical Context

Less extensive procedure than 57540, used for removal of asymptomatic cervical stump or low-grade dysplasia. Vaginal approach commonly used.
