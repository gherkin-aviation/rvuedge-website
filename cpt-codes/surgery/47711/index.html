47711 Excision of bile duct tumor

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Bile duct tumor resection, cholangiocarcinoma resection

Surgical excision (complete removal) of a bile duct tumor with restoration of biliary continuity through choledochoduodenostomy or choledochojejunostomy.

Clinical Context

Performed for distal bile duct cancers, ampullary carcinomas, or benign bile duct tumors. Requires reconstruction of biliary anatomy.
