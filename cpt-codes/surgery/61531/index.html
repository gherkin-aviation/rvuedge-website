61531 Implant brain electrodes

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: brain electrode placement, intracranial electrode implantation, epilepsy surgery prep

Craniotomy or craniectomy with implantation of electrodes for recording or stimulation of brain activity, used for seizure monitoring or functional mapping.

Clinical Context

Used for preoperative seizure localization in temporal lobe epilepsy, functional brain mapping, or deep brain stimulation for movement disorders or other conditions.
