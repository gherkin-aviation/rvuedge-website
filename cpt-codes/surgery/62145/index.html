62145 Repair of skull & brain

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: skull and dura repair, complex skull repair

Repair of skull and dura mater for large defects. Includes any necessary reconstruction of brain covering and skull structure.

Clinical Context

Used for comprehensive repair of skull and dural defects involving significant structural loss or defects.
