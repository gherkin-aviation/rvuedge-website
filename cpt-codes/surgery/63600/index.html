63600 Remove spinal cord lesion

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: intramedullary lesion removal, spinal cord tumor excision

Removal of spinal cord lesion. This procedure involves excision of lesions that arise from within the spinal cord parenchyma itself, distinct from extradural or intradural extramedullary lesions.

Clinical Context

Used for intramedullary lesions including syrinx, tumor, cyst, or vascular malformation removal.
