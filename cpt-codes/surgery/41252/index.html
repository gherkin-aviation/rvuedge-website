41252 Repair tongue laceration

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: extensive tongue laceration repair, tongue laceration with reconstruction, complex tongue wound repair

Surgical repair of a laceration of the tongue with extensive tissue involvement and possible reconstruction.

In Plain Language

repairing extensive tongue cut; major tongue wound repair; tongue laceration with tissue loss

Clinical Context

Used for very extensive tongue lacerations with significant tissue loss or involvement of multiple layers. May require tissue grafting.
