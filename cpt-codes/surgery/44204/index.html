44204 Laparoscopic partial colectomy

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: lap partial colectomy, laparoscopic segmental colectomy, lap left colectomy

Laparoscopic partial resection of colon with restoration of intestinal continuity via primary anastomosis.

Clinical Context

Laparoscopic resection of colonic segment for malignancy, inflammatory bowel disease, or other pathology. Provides less morbidity than open approach.
