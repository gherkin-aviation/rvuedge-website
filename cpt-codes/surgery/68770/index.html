68770 Close tear system fistula

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: lacrimal fistula closure, lacrimal fistula repair

Closure of fistula of lacrimal system using surgical repair and closure of abnormal communication.

In Plain Language

closing abnormal tear duct opening; fixing tear duct leak

Clinical Context

Used for lacrimal system fistula closure. Common indications include lacrimal fistula from trauma, surgery, or congenital abnormality.
