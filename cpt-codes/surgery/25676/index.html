25676 Optx radiation/uln dislc aqt/chrnc

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ORIF DRUJ dislocation, open reduction radioulnar dislocation

Open treatment (surgical repair) of distal radioulnar joint dislocation, acute or chronic.

Clinical Context

Used for complex or chronic distal radioulnar joint dislocations requiring operative intervention. Appropriate for cases with associated fracture, ligamentous injury, or failed closed treatment.
