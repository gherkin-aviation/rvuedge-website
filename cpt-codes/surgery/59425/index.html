59425 Antepartum care only

Surgery

Also known as: antepartum care only, prenatal care only, antepartum care 4-6 visits, prenatal care partial

Antepartum care only package, including office visits for routine prenatal evaluation without intrapartum or postpartum care.

Clinical Context

Used when another provider will manage delivery or when patient presents late for prenatal care; includes routine visits only.
