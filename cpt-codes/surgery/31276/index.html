31276 Nsl/sinus ndsc frnt tissue removal

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: endoscopic frontal sinus surgery, frontal sinus tissue removal, frontal endoscopy, endoscopic sinus surgery frontal, FESS frontal

Nasal and sinus endoscopic surgery with removal of frontal sinus tissue, treating frontal sinusitis or frontal sinus disease.

Clinical Context

Endoscopic approach to frontal sinus disease, often combined with ethmoidectomy. Allows disease management without external incision.
