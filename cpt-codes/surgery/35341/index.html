35341 Rechanneling of artery

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ulnar/radial endarterectomy, forearm artery rechanneling

Rechanneling (thrombectomy with endarterectomy) of ulnar or radial artery with restoration of forearm blood flow.

Clinical Context

Surgical management of radial or ulnar artery stenosis/occlusion causing hand claudication or digital ischemia. Less commonly performed; may require duplex assessment of palmar arch adequacy.
