47555 Biliary endoscopy thru skin

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: Percutaneous cholangioscopic stricture dilation, POCS dilation

Percutaneous endoscopy of the biliary tree with stricture dilation using balloon or other technique to open narrowed segments of the bile ducts.

In Plain Language

Stretching narrowed bile ducts using scope through skin

Clinical Context

Performed for benign and malignant biliary strictures under direct visualization. Allows accurate identification and treatment of stenosis.
