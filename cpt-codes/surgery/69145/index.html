69145 Remove ear canal lesion(s)

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: simple canal lesion removal, benign lesion excision

Removal of benign lesion of external auditory canal using simple excision or limited technique.

Clinical Context

Used for simple benign ear canal lesions. Common indications include exostosis, cysts, polyps, or small benign tumors.
