47143 Prep donor liver whole

Surgery

Also known as: donor liver preparation, whole liver procurement, liver graft preparation

Preparation of donor liver (whole liver). In situ or ex vivo perfusion and preparation of whole liver graft.

Clinical Context

Performed during organ recovery to prepare whole liver graft with preservation solutions and assessment.
