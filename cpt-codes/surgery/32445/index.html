32445 Removal of lung extrapleural

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: extrapleural pneumonectomy, EPP

Extrapleural pneumonectomy removing the entire lung along with the parietal pleura and chest wall (ribs/sternum) en bloc, performed primarily for mesothelioma.

In Plain Language

complete lung and chest wall removal

Clinical Context

Used for pleural mesothelioma to remove all pleural disease and allow adequate margins, often combined with chemotherapy and radiation therapy.
