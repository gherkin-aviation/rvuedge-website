35518 Artery byp graft axillary-axilry

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: axillary-axillary bypass, AA bypass

Arterial bypass graft from axillary to axillary artery with restoration of contralateral upper extremity blood flow.

Clinical Context

Revascularization for bilateral axillary/brachial artery disease when subclavian approach not feasible. Maintains hemodynamic continuity across disease.
