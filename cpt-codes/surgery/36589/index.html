36589 Removal tunneled cv catheter

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: catheter exchange, line exchange, CVC exchange

Removal of tunneled or non-tunneled central venous catheter, with insertion of replacement tunneled central venous catheter, at same venous access site.

Clinical Context

Used when catheter removal and replacement needed at same site. Reduces morbidity compared to separate removal and insertion.
