27036 Capsulectomy/capsulotomy hip

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: hip capsulectomy, hip capsulotomy, hip capsule release

Surgical capsulectomy (removal of joint capsule) or capsulotomy (incision of joint capsule) of the hip to improve motion or address contracture.

Clinical Context

Used for hip contracture, stiffness, or limited motion due to capsular tightness requiring surgical capsule release or removal.
