11047 Debridement bone each additional

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: Additional bone debridement, Extra bone removal

Debridement of bone, each additional twenty square centimeters or less. Add-on code for additional areas of bone debridement.

In Plain Language

Additional dead bone removal; Extra bone cleanup

Clinical Context

Applied for extensive osteomyelitis or multi-site bone necrosis requiring sequential debridement.
