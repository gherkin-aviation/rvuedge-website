63052 Lam facetc/frmt arthrd lum 1

Surgery

Also known as: lumbar laminectomy, L1 laminectomy with fusion, facetectomy with arthrodesis

Laminectomy with facetectomy or foraminotomy for arthrodesis at the first lumbar level. This is an initial add-on code used when the first lumbar segment requires fusion with decompression elements.

Clinical Context

Used when laminectomy at L1 level involves joint fusion for instability or degeneration with nerve compression.
