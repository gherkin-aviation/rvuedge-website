28360 Reconstruct cleft foot

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: cleft foot reconstruction, cleft foot repair, cleft foot surgery

Surgical reconstruction of cleft foot deformity, including multiple procedures to restore anatomic alignment, separate any webbed or fused structures, and establish normal foot function.

Clinical Context

Used for congenital cleft foot deformity causing functional impairment; comprehensive reconstruction restores digital anatomy and gait function.
