41016 Drainage of mouth lesion

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: salivary gland marsupialization, gland cyst drainage, sialocele drainage

Surgical drainage of an abscess with partial removal or marsupialization of a salivary gland cyst or lesion.

In Plain Language

draining salivary gland cyst; opening salivary gland pocket; gland lesion drainage

Clinical Context

Used for salivary gland cysts or ranulas requiring marsupialization or drainage. Common with mucoceles and sialoceles that do not resolve with conservative management.
