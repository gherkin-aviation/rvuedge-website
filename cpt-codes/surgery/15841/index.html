15841 Nerve palsy muscle graft

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: muscle nerve graft, muscle transfer graft

Nerve graft using muscle tissue for repair of facial nerve paralysis or significant nerve injuries, providing better blood supply and innervation potential.

Clinical Context

Preferred for nerve injuries requiring vascularized tissue and functional reinnervation potential. More complex than fascial grafts with higher success rates for motor recovery.
