32820 Reconstruct injured chest

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: chest wall reconstruction, rib fracture repair, flail chest stabilization

Surgical reconstruction of the chest wall following trauma, including repair of fractured ribs, sternal fractures, and soft tissue injuries. Restoration of chest wall stability and function.

Clinical Context

Used in trauma patients with multiple rib fractures, sternal fractures, or chest wall instability causing pain and respiratory compromise. Improves pulmonary mechanics and healing.
