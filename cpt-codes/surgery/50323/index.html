50323 Prep cadaver renal allograft

Surgery

Also known as: cadaver graft preparation, donor kidney prep, allograft workup

Preparation and evaluation of a cadaveric renal allograft (deceased donor kidney) for transplantation including vascular and ureteral preparation.

In Plain Language

preparation of deceased donor kidney for transplant; donor kidney preparation

Clinical Context

Performed in the operating room to prepare the cadaveric kidney for implantation including evaluation of vascular structures, ureteral patency, and tissue viability.

RVU Information

CPT 50323 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation
