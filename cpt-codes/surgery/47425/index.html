47425 Incision of bile duct

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Supraduodenal choledocholithotomy, supraduodenal duct incision

Surgical incision of the common bile duct with supraduodenal approach, typically performed to remove stones or address obstruction above the duodenum.

Clinical Context

Performed for choledocholithiasis using an approach above the duodenum, allowing direct access to the common bile duct without duodenal mobilization.
