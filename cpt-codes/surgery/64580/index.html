64580 Opn impltj neuroelectrode array neuromuscular

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: neuromuscular stimulator implant, neuromuscular electrode implantation

Open surgical implantation of a neuroelectrode near the neuromuscular junction for long-term electrical stimulation.

Clinical Context

Used for muscle paralysis, dystonia, and neuromuscular disorders.
