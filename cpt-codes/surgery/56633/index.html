56633 Vulvectomy radical complete

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Radical complete vulvectomy

Radical vulvectomy with complete tissue removal. Removes entire vulva with en bloc dissection of underlying tissues without lymph node dissection.

Clinical Context

Used for complete removal of vulvar carcinoma without nodal dissection, typically when nodes are clinically negative.
