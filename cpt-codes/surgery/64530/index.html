64530 N block injection celiac pelus

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: celiac plexus block, celiac nerve block, splanchnic nerve block

Nerve block of the celiac plexus through injection of anesthetic and/or steroid medication. Treats upper abdominal and visceral pain.

Clinical Context

Used for chronic pancreatitis, cancer pain, and post-operative abdominal pain. Blocks visceral sensory innervation to upper abdomen.
