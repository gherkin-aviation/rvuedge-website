35572 Harvest femoropopliteal vein

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: saphenous vein harvest, vein procurement

Harvest of femoropopliteal vein (greater saphenous vein from thigh and knee region) for use as bypass graft conduit.

Clinical Context

Autogenous vein procurement for lower extremity bypass grafting. Saphenous vein harvesting enables conduit selection with optimal size matching to recipient vessel.
