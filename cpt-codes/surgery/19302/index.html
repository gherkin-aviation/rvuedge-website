19302 P-mastectomy with ln removal

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: partial mastectomy with lymph node dissection, lumpectomy with sentinel biopsy or ALND, partial mastectomy with ALND

Surgical removal of a portion of the breast tissue with ipsilateral axillary lymph node removal. This procedure combines breast conservation with lymph node staging.

In Plain Language

removal of breast lump with underarm lymph nodes

Clinical Context

Used for breast cancer treatment when lymph node staging is necessary. Allows assessment of nodal involvement while preserving most breast tissue.
