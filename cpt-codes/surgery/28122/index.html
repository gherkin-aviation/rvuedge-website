28122 Partial removal of foot bone

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: distal tarsal/metatarsal removal, tarsal distal excision, bone end removal

Partial excision of the tarsal or metatarsal bone (distal removal). This procedure removes the distal (end) portion of a tarsal or metatarsal bone.

In Plain Language

removing the end of a foot bone; distal bone removal

Clinical Context

Used for distal bone necrosis, stress fracture sequelae, or malunion. Preserves proximal bone for structural support.
