26561 Repair of web finger

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Complex web space repair, Extensive syndactyly release, Web reconstruction

Surgical repair of webbing between fingers with more extensive reconstruction, potentially involving graft placement or multiple web spaces.

In Plain Language

Extensive surgery to separate connected fingers; Complex web space reconstruction

Clinical Context

Performed for extensive syndactyly, multiple web involvement, or when graft or flap reconstruction is needed for optimal separation.
