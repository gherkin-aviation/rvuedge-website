31573 Largsc with therapy injection

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: laryngeal therapeutic injection, medicament injection, laryngeal drug injection

Laryngoscopic therapeutic injection of a medicament or therapeutic agent (such as antibiotic, steroid, or sclerotic agent) into or adjacent to a laryngeal lesion or area of pathology.

Clinical Context

Performed for therapeutic effect of injected medications such as botulinum toxin, steroids, or sclerosing agents in treatment of laryngeal spasticity, webs, or other pathology.
