43226 Esophageal endoscopy dilation

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: esophageal dilation, esophagoscopy with dilation

Esophagoscopy (flexible) with dilation of esophageal stricture using sequential dilators or balloon technique.

In Plain Language

camera examination with stretching of esophagus narrowing

Clinical Context

Therapeutic procedure for esophageal stricture. Sequential dilation allows controlled enlargement without perforation.
