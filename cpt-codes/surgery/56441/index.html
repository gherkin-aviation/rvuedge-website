56441 Lysis of labial adhesions

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: Labial adhesion lysis, Labial fusion release

Lysis of labial adhesions in female patients. Labial adhesions are abnormal fusion of the labia minora.

In Plain Language

Separating fused vaginal lips; Releasing stuck together labia

Clinical Context

Used in girls with congenital or acquired labial adhesions causing symptoms such as impaired urinary stream, dysuria, or discomfort. Often seen after trauma or infection.
