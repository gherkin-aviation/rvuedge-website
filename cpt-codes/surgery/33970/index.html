33970 Aortic circulation assist

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: LVAD insertion, ventricular assist device, circulation support

Aortic circulation assist device insertion or replacement; initial or replacement insertion of left ventricular assist device or other aortic circulation support system.

Clinical Context

Percutaneous or catheter-based insertion of LVAD or aortic circulation assist devices for acute hemodynamic support without formal surgical incisions.
