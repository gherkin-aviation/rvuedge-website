28309 Incision of metatarsals

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: multiple metatarsal incision, pan-metatarsal access, metatarsal exposure

Surgical incision into multiple metatarsal bones for comprehensive decompression, biopsy, or treatment of pathology affecting multiple metatarsals such as widespread stress fractures or osteomyelitis.

Clinical Context

Used for pathology affecting multiple metatarsals such as widespread stress fractures, infections, or osteochondritis requiring comprehensive surgical access.
