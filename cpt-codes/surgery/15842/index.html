15842 Nerve palsy microsurg graft

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: microsurgical graft, microsurg nerve graft

Microsurgical nerve graft using advanced operating microscopy for precise repair of facial nerve paralysis or complex nerve injuries with anastomosis under magnification.

Clinical Context

Highest complexity nerve repair using operating microscope, appropriate for small nerve fibers and complex facial nerve repairs. Highest RVU and best outcomes for neural recovery.
