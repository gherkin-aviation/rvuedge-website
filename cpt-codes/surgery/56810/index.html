56810 Perineoplasty repair per nonob

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: Perineoplasty, Perineal repair

Perineoplasty with repair of perineum in non-obstetric patient. Reconstructive surgery to restore normal perineal anatomy.

Clinical Context

Used for perineal trauma, chronic pain, or anatomic abnormality from non-obstetric causes. Improves anatomy and function.
