35241 repair blvsl vn grf ntrthrc with b

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: thoracic vein graft repair, intrathoracic vascular bypass

Repair of blood vessel injury in the intrathoracic region using venous graft with bypass technique.

Clinical Context

Reconstruction of major thoracic vessel injuries requiring bypass due to extent of trauma or underlying vessel pathology. Vein graft provides expanded conduit maintaining hemodynamic flow to vital structures.
