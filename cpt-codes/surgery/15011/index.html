15011 Hrv skn cll ssp agrft first 25

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: cell suspension graft, spray graft

Harvesting of skin cells via suspension technique for autologous graft, first 25 square centimeters or less. Used to obtain cultured epidermal cells for transplantation.

In Plain Language

collecting skin cells for transplant; cell spraying technique

Clinical Context

Used for severe burns, chronic wounds, and extensive skin loss where traditional grafts are insufficient. Allows coverage of large areas with minimal donor site.

RVU Information
