35102 Repair defect of artery

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: aortic visceral repair valve, aortic branches valve repair

Repair of a defect of the aorta and its visceral branches with aortic valve involvement (suture or patch angioplasty). Surgical correction of complex aortic defect with branch involvement.

Clinical Context

Complex procedure for juxtarenal aortic aneurysm with aortic regurgitation or aortic dissection with branch involvement.
