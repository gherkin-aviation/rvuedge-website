21160 Reconstruction mdfc lfrtiii advm with i

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: advanced Le Fort III with maxilla, facial monobloc with maxilla

Reconstruction of the midface using advanced Le Fort III osteotomy with monobloc advancement and concurrent maxillary adjustment or Le Fort I.

Clinical Context

Used for severe craniofacial syndromes requiring maximal advancement of all facial levels for optimal respiratory and functional outcomes.
