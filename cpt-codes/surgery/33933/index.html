33933 Prepare donor heart/lung

Surgery

Also known as: Organ preparation, heart-lung bench work, donor preparation

Preparation of donor heart and lung; ex vivo assessment, preparation, and conditioning of procured donor heart and lungs for implantation.

Clinical Context

Bench preparation of procured donor heart-lung block including inspection, assessment of viability, organ system perfusion, and technique modifications for implantation.
