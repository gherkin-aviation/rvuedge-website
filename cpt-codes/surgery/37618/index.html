37618 Ligation major artery xtr

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Extremity artery ligation, Limb artery tie

Surgical ligation of a major artery in the extremity region for hemorrhage control or vascular management.

Clinical Context

Used for traumatic vascular injury in limbs, uncontrolled hemorrhage, or management of arterial pseudoaneurysms in the extremities.
