26415 Excision hand/finger tendon

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Tendon excision, Scar excision, Fibrosed tendon removal

Surgical excision of a diseased, fibrosed, or damaged tendon from the hand or finger, typically performed to remove scarred tissue, infection, or facilitate revision with graft or transfer.

Clinical Context

Performed when a tendon is irreparably damaged, infected, or fibrosed beyond functional restoration. Often precedes tendon grafting or transfer to restore function.
