26650 Treat thumb fracture

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Thumb fracture reduction, Fracture manipulation, Closed reduction

Closed treatment of a thumb bone fracture with manipulation, performed when the fracture requires reduction under manual guidance without operative fixation.

Clinical Context

Indicated for thumb fractures requiring reduction where alignment can be maintained with external immobilization after manipulation.
