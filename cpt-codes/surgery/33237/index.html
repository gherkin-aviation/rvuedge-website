33237 Remove electrode/thoracotomy

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: dual lead removal, thoracotomy dual extraction, open dual lead removal

Removal of dual pacemaker leads via thoracotomy approach with direct epicardial extraction. Open surgical removal of two leads.

Clinical Context

Used for infected or malfunctioning epicardial dual-chamber systems. Requires open thoracotomy for access.
