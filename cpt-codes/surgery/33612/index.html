33612 Repair double ventricle

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Rastelli procedure, DORV arterial switch, complex DORV repair

Surgical repair of double-outlet right ventricle with arterial switch (Rastelli procedure) or additional major reconstruction.

Clinical Context

Employed for double-outlet right ventricle when arterial switch is indicated; combines intraventricular tunnel with conduit placement from left ventricle to pulmonary artery.
