67414 Explr/decompress eye socket

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: orbital decompression, pressure relief, compartment syndrome release

Exploration and decompression of orbit with release of orbital pressure to relieve vision loss or orbital compartment syndrome from trauma, infection, or inflammation.

In Plain Language

surgery to relieve pressure in eye socket

Clinical Context

Emergency procedure for orbital compartment syndrome; lateral wall decompression or medial wall removal to restore perfusion and vision.
