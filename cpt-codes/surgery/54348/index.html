54348 repair hypspad complicated dsj & urtp

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: complex hypospadias repair, extensive hypospadias correction

Repair of hypospadias with high complexity requiring significant tissue rearrangement, graft, and chordee correction.

Clinical Context

Used for proximal hypospadias with severe chordee and tissue deficiency. Requires tissue transfer and complex reconstruction.
