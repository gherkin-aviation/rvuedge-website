23920 Disarticulation shoulder

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: shoulder disarticulation, glenohumeral disarticulation, shoulder amputation

Disarticulation of shoulder (glenohumeral disarticulation). Amputation of upper extremity via disarticulation at shoulder joint.

In Plain Language

removal of arm from shoulder joint; shoulder joint separation

Clinical Context

Performed for severe shoulder pathology including massive tumors, severe infection, or extensive tissue destruction not amenable to limb salvage.
