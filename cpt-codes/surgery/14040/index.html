14040 Tissue trnfr f/c/c/m/n/a/g/h/f

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Adjacent tissue transfer, Local flap, Tissue rearrangement, local tissue transfer face

Tissue transfer or graft from adjacent area to cover defect on the face, cheek, chin, mouth, nose, axilla, groin, hands, or feet measuring ten square centimeters or less.

In Plain Language

Skin coverage using nearby skin on face or hands; Tissue rearrangement for facial reconstruction

Clinical Context

Used for coverage of small defects on the face or hands using adjacent tissue, important for functional and cosmetic restoration.
