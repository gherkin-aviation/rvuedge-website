48540 Fuse pancreas cyst and bowel

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Cystojejunostomy, jejunal cyst drainage

Surgical creation of an internal anastomosis between a pancreatic cyst and the small intestine (cyst-enterostomy) using jejunum for long-term drainage.

In Plain Language

Connecting pancreatic cyst to small intestine

Clinical Context

Performed for symptomatic pancreatic pseudocysts. Jejunal approach preferred over gastric for larger cysts and lower risk of reflux.
