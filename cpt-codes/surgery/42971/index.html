42971 Control nose/throat bleeding

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: complex nasopharyngeal hemorrhage control, difficult nose and throat bleeding control

Control of hemorrhage (bleeding) from the nose and pharynx (throat) via combined transoral and transnasal approach with increased complexity.

Clinical Context

Indicated for significant nasopharyngeal hemorrhage requiring more extensive technique than simple control. May include endoscopic assistance or multiple hemostasis methods.
