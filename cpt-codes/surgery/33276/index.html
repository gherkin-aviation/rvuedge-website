33276 Insj phrnc nerve stim sys

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: phrenic nerve stimulation, diaphragmatic pacemaker, phrenic pacing system

Insertion of a phrenic nerve stimulator system, placing electrodes on the phrenic nerve to provide respiratory assistance.

Clinical Context

Used for patients with respiratory insufficiency or central sleep apnea who require diaphragmatic stimulation.
