45391 Colonoscopy with endoscope ultrasound

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: colonoscopy with ultrasound, colon EUS

Colonoscopy with endoscopic ultrasound (EUS) evaluation for assessment of lesions or layer invasion.

Clinical Context

Used to evaluate depth of invasion of colonic lesions, assess wall layers, guide fine-needle aspiration, or evaluate submucosal pathology.
