57287 Revise/remove sling repair

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Sling revision, Sling removal

Revision or removal of synthetic sling for stress incontinence. Adjustment or removal of previously placed urethral sling.

Clinical Context

Used for sling complications including erosion, infection, or pelvic pain. May involve partial or complete removal.
