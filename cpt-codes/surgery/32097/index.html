32097 Open wedge/biopsy lung nodule

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: open nodule biopsy, open wedge nodule resection

Open wedge resection or biopsy of the lung performed to evaluate a solitary pulmonary nodule, including nodules suspicious for malignancy or other significant pathology.

In Plain Language

open lung nodule removal; lung spot biopsy

Clinical Context

Used for solitary pulmonary nodules when imaging characteristics are indeterminate or suspicious, or when diagnostic imaging cannot definitively establish benignity.
