38520 Biopsy/removal lymph nodes

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Inguinofemoral lymph node dissection

Surgical removal of inguinal and femoral lymph nodes, typically performed for staging or therapeutic purposes.

Clinical Context

Performed in staging of melanoma, squamous cell carcinoma of lower extremity or genitals, or therapeutic lymph node dissection.
