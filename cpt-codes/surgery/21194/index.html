21194 Reconst lower jaw with graft

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: mandibular reconstruction with graft, jawbone graft

Reconstruction of the lower jaw with the addition of bone graft material to restore mandibular continuity and structure.

Clinical Context

Used for significant mandibular defects requiring bony reconstruction. Essential for restoring jaw continuity after ablative surgery.
