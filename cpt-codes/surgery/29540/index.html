29540 Strapping ankle and/or foot

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: ankle strapping, ankle taping, foot strapping

Application of strapping or taping to the ankle and foot for support and motion limitation.

Clinical Context

Indicated for ankle sprains, foot ligament injuries, and proprioceptive support during rehabilitation.
