22830 Exploration of spinal fusion

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Fusion exploration, Revision exploration

Exploration of spinal fusion site; single segment.

In Plain Language

Surgery to examine previous fusion; Check on spinal fusion surgery

Clinical Context

Used when prior fusion site requires evaluation for infection, non-union, or hardware issues; includes inspection and debridement as needed.
