45505 Repair of rectum

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: transanal rectal repair, rectal perforation repair transanal

Repair of laceration or perforation of rectum via transanal approach with primary repair.

In Plain Language

surgical repair of rectal tear through anus

Clinical Context

Emergency procedure for transanal traumatic injury or iatrogenic perforation (e.g., from TEM, colonoscopy) requiring primary closure.
