35301 Rechanneling of artery

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: carotid endarterectomy, carotid artery rechanneling

Rechanneling (thrombectomy with endarterectomy) of artery in the carotid region with restoration of antegrade flow.

Clinical Context

Surgical management of carotid artery stenosis (>50-70%) causing cerebrovascular symptoms or found incidentally. Gold standard for symptomatic disease with lower perioperative risk compared to stenting.
