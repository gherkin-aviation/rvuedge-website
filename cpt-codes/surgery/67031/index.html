67031 Laser surgery eye strands

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: laser membrane dissection, laser hyaloidotomy, nd:yag hyaloidotomy, laser capsulotomy

Laser ablation or photoablation of vitreous strands, membranes, or posterior hyaloid membrane to relieve traction and improve visualization.

In Plain Language

laser cutting of scar tissue in eye; laser treatment of vitreous membranes

Clinical Context

Non-incisional alternative to surgical membrane dissection; useful for posterior capsular opacification, anterior vitreous opacities, and accessible vitreous strands.
