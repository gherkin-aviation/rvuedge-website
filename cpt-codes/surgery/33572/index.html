33572 Open coronary endarterectomy

Surgery

Also known as: coronary endarterectomy, coronary artery plaque removal, open endarterectomy

Surgical removal of atherosclerotic plaque from a coronary artery using an endarterectomy technique under direct visualization.

Clinical Context

Indicated for diffuse coronary artery disease not suitable for bypass alone; provides revascularization of otherwise un-graftable vessels with acceptable long-term outcomes.
