25107 Remove wrist joint cartilage

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: wrist chondrectomy, cartilage removal

Excision of articular cartilage from the wrist joint (chondrectomy) to address cartilage damage, defects, or degenerative joint disease.

Clinical Context

Performed for osteoarthritis, chondral defects, or osteochondritis dissecans of the wrist. May be palliative or preparatory for future arthrodesis.
