59622 Attempted vbac after care

Surgery

Also known as: TOLAC with cesarean and postpartum

Trial of labor after cesarean section with cesarean delivery and postpartum care (without antepartum management).

Clinical Context

Used when provider manages delivery attempt and postpartum care for trial of labor after cesarean that results in cesarean delivery.
