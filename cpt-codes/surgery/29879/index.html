29879 Arthrs knee srg abrasj arthrp

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: abrasion arthroplasty, arthroscopic abrasion, microfracture alternative

Arthroscopy of the knee with abrasion arthroplasty using motorized instruments to stimulate fibrocartilage formation in chondral defects.

In Plain Language

roughening cartilage to promote healing; stimulating cartilage growth

Clinical Context

Used for symptomatic chondral lesions and osteoarthritis to promote fibrocartilage healing.
