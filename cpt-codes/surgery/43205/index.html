43205 Esophagus endoscopy/ligation

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: esophageal variceal ligation, endoscopic band ligation

Flexible esophagoscopy (examination of esophagus with flexible scope) via transoral approach with endoscopic band ligation of esophageal varices.

Clinical Context

Therapeutic procedure for esophageal varices. Band ligation causes variceal thrombosis and hemostasis. Preferred over sclerotherapy in many centers.
