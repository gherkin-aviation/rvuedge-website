55200 Incision of sperm duct

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: vasal incision, vas deferens opening

Incision of vas deferens (vasal opening) for sperm extraction or preparation for repair.

Clinical Context

Surgical opening of vas deferens for sperm retrieval in azoospermia or in preparation for vasal reconstruction.
