15015 application skn cl ssp agrft t/a/l 1

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: cell spray application trunk, spray graft application

Application of autologous cultured skin cells via spray technique to trunk, arms, or legs, first increment. Spray application of patient's own cultured epidermal cells.

Clinical Context

Used for large surface area burns and chronic wounds on trunk and extremities. Cultured cells promote rapid epithelialization and reduce infection risk.
