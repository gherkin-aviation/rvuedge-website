28107 Remove/graft foot lesion

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: moderate foot lesion excision with graft, bone cyst removal moderate, foot lesion moderate reconstruction

Excision of lesion or cyst of the foot including bone with skin graft or flap of moderate complexity. This procedure removes bone lesions with adequate reconstruction.

Clinical Context

Used for moderately complex bone lesions requiring reconstruction but not as extensive as major flap procedures. Common in ganglion cysts or bony lesions of the midfoot.
