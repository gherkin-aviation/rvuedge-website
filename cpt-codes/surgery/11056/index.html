11056 paring/cutting benign hyperkeratotic lesion 2-4

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: Multiple callus removal, Multiple corn removal

Paring and cutting of benign hyperkeratotic lesions, two to four lesions. Non-invasive removal of calluses or thickened skin on multiple lesions.

In Plain Language

Multiple callus trimming; Multiple corn removal

Clinical Context

Used for removal of multiple plantar calluses, corns, or hyperkeratotic lesions in a single session.
