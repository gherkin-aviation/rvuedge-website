47600 Cholecystectomy

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Open cholecystectomy, standard cholecystectomy

Open surgical removal of the gallbladder (cholecystectomy) performed through a standard abdominal incision for symptomatic cholelithiasis or chronic cholecystitis.

Clinical Context

Performed when laparoscopic approach is contraindicated, conversion from laparoscopy is necessary, or anatomy is hostile. Gold standard for complex cases.
