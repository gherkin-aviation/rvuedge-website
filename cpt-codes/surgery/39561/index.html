39561 Resection diaphragm complex

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: complex diaphragm resection, diaphragm resection with reconstruction

Surgical resection of the diaphragm with reconstruction, typically for extensive tumors or large defects. This more complex procedure may require flap reconstruction or prosthetic replacement.

Clinical Context

Used for extensive diaphragmatic malignancies, large traumatic defects, or resection requiring significant reconstruction. Often combined with adjacent organ resection.
