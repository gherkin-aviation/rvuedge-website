65400 Removal of eye lesion

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: conjunctival biopsy, episcleral biopsy

Biopsy of conjunctiva or episcleral tissue.

In Plain Language

tissue sample from eye surface; taking sample from covering of white of eye

Clinical Context

Diagnostic procedure for suspected conjunctival malignancy, chronic inflammation, or rare conditions like amyloidosis. Pathology evaluation guides further management.
