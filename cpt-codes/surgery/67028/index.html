67028 Injection eye drug

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: intravitreal injection, IVTA, IVBI, anti-VEGF injection, steroid injection

Intravitreal injection of therapeutic drug or agent directly into the vitreous cavity for treatment of retinal or macular conditions.

In Plain Language

injection of medicine into back of eye; medication injection into eye cavity

Clinical Context

Most common treatment for diabetic retinopathy, macular edema, wet age-related macular degeneration, retinal vein occlusion, and other retinal diseases; administered in office.
