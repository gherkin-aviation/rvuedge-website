44640 Repair bowel-skin fistula

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: enterocutaneous fistula repair, bowel-skin fistula repair

Repair of bowel-to-skin fistula (enterocutaneous fistula) via open surgical approach, typically involving resection of fistula tract and primary anastomosis.

Clinical Context

Used for enterocutaneous fistula, usually from inflammatory bowel disease, prior surgery, or trauma. Often requires bowel resection and anastomosis for definitive repair.
