42106 Excision lesion mouth roof

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: palatal lesion excision, palate lesion removal

Surgical excision and removal of a moderate-sized lesion from the palate with primary closure. Represents intermediate lesion size requiring more extensive removal.

Clinical Context

Surgical procedure for benign or malignant palatal lesions of intermediate size requiring excision with tissue flap reconstruction and closure.
