69641 Revise middle ear & mastoid

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: middle ear revision surgery, mastoid revision, revision cholesteatoma surgery

Revision of middle ear and mastoid surgery to address recurrent disease, residual cholesteatoma, or complications from previous canal wall up procedures.

Clinical Context

Used for recurrent cholesteatoma after canal wall up surgery, residual disease, or inadequate initial disease eradication.
