31380 Partial removal of larynx

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: laryngeal framework resection, cartilage laryngectomy, framework partial laryngectomy

Partial laryngeal resection with removal of laryngeal framework cartilage structures (cricoid or thyroid cartilage resection).

In Plain Language

voice box cartilage removal

Clinical Context

Partial laryngectomy involving laryngeal framework cartilage. For cancer involving cartilage invasion or subglottic extension.
