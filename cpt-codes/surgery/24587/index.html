24587 Optx partclr fracture and/or dslc elbow w

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ORIF complex fracture with extensive repair, open reduction with extensive reconstruction

Open treatment of intra-articular fracture with or without distal ulna/humerus involvement and dislocation of elbow, with more extensive reconstruction. Complex surgical management with extensive soft tissue repair.

Clinical Context

Used for extensive intra-articular elbow fractures with dislocation requiring complex reconstruction and soft tissue repair.
