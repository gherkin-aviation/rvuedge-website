46083 incision thrombosed hroid external

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: thrombosed hemorrhoid drainage, external hemorrhoid thrombectomy, clot evacuation

Incision of a thrombosed external hemorrhoid. This is an acute care procedure to drain the blood clot and provide immediate pain relief in patients with acutely thrombosed external hemorrhoids.

Clinical Context

Performed for acute thrombosed external hemorrhoids causing severe pain. Most effective within 72 hours of symptom onset when pain is maximal.
