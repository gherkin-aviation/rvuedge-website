52204 Cystoscopy with biopsy(s)

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: cysto with biopsy, bladder biopsy

Cystourethroscopy with tissue biopsy (one or more samples) of bladder lesions or abnormalities, allowing histologic diagnosis of inflammation, infection, or malignancy.

Clinical Context

Essential for diagnosis of bladder cancer, tuberculosis, chronic cystitis, and other bladder pathology. Multiple biopsies from different sites improve diagnostic yield.
