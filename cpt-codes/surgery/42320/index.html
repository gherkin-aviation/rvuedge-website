42320 Drainage of salivary gland

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: salivary gland drainage

Surgical incision and drainage of a salivary gland obstruction involving deeper structures or requiring more extensive surgical access.

Clinical Context

Complex drainage of salivary gland infection or obstruction involving deeper parotid or submandibular structures.
