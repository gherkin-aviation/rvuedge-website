43246 Egd place gastrostomy tube

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: EGD G-tube placement, endoscopic gastrostomy, percutaneous endoscopic gastrostomy, EGD with PEG placement, PEG tube

Esophagogastroduodenoscopy with endoscopic placement of a gastrostomy tube for nutritional support or gastric decompression. This percutaneous endoscopic gastrostomy alternative provides tube placement under direct visualization.

In Plain Language

feeding tube placement via camera; endoscopic feeding tube insertion

Clinical Context

Used for long-term nutritional support in patients unable to eat orally due to dysphagia, neurological impairment, or malnutrition; alternative to fluoroscopic-guided placement.
