59015 Chorion biopsy

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: chorionic villus sampling, CVS, chorionic biopsy

Chorionic villus sampling (biopsy) for prenatal genetic diagnosis, performed via transabdominal or transcervical approach under ultrasound guidance.

Clinical Context

Performed at 10-13 weeks gestation for genetic counseling, fetal karyotyping, and early diagnosis of chromosomal abnormalities.
