31611 Constj trachesophgl fstl

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: tracheoesophageal fistula creation, TEF creation, speech rehabilitation

Construction of a tracheoesophageal fistula (surgical connection between trachea and esophagus) to allow speech in laryngectomy patients using esophageal pulmonary air flow.

Clinical Context

Performed in laryngectomy patients to restore speech capability by creating a one-way valve between trachea and esophagus allowing air from lungs to be shaped into speech.
