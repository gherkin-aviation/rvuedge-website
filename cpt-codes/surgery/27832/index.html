27832 Treat lower leg dislocation

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: open reduction ankle dislocation, ankle dislocation ORIF

Initial open treatment of ankle joint dislocation with direct visualization and reduction with fixation.

Clinical Context

Indicated for complex ankle dislocations with associated fractures or soft tissue damage requiring open reduction.
