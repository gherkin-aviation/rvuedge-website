43405 Ligate/staple esophagus

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: esophageal transection, esophageal stapling, emergency variceal transection

Transection and ligation or stapling of esophageal varices via open approach with creation of esophagogastric resection line. This controls variceal hemorrhage.

Clinical Context

Emergency procedure for massive variceal hemorrhage uncontrolled by endoscopy; provides hemostasis but high morbidity and limited long-term benefit.
