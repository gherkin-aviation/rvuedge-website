38232 Bone marrow harvest autolog

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: Autologous bone marrow harvest, ABMT harvest

Harvesting of bone marrow from the patient for autologous hematopoietic cell transplantation after chemotherapy.

In Plain Language

Collecting patient's bone marrow

Clinical Context

Used in autologous stem cell transplant procedures to harvest patient's own marrow for reinfusion after high-dose therapy.
