47010 Hepatot opn drainage absc/cyst 1/2

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: liver abscess drainage, hepatic cyst drainage, open liver drainage

Hepatotomy with opening and drainage of hepatic abscess or cyst. Surgical incision of liver for drainage of infected or fluid-filled cavity.

Clinical Context

Performed for large liver abscesses or cysts not amenable to percutaneous drainage. Often for multiple loculations.
