22845 Insert spine fixation device

Surgery

Also known as: Pedicle screw fixation 5-7 levels, Extended segmental instrumentation

Posterior segmental instrumentation (eg, pedicle screw, hook); 5-7 segments.

Clinical Context

Posterior segmental instrumentation for extensive thoracolumbar instability, deformity, or trauma; spans 5-7 vertebral segments.
