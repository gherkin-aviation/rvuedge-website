56515 Destroy vulva lesion/s complicated

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: Extensive vulvar lesion destruction, Complex condyloma removal

Destruction of vulvar lesions using extensive surgical techniques. Complex destruction involves extensive area coverage or multiple complex lesions.

Clinical Context

Used for extensive condyloma acuminata, large molluscum contagiosum fields, or recurrent/resistant lesions. May require multiple treatment sessions.
