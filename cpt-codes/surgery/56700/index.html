56700 Prtl hymnctmy/revision hymnl rng

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: Partial hymenotomy, Hymenal revision

Partial hymenotomy or revision of the hymenal ring. Involves incision or reformation of partial hymenal tissue.

In Plain Language

Partial surgical opening or repair of hymen

Clinical Context

Used for partial hymenal obstruction or to revise previous hymenotomy. Less extensive than complete hymenotomy.
