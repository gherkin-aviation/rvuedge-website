28291 Corrj halux rigdus with implant

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: hallux rigidus with implant, first MTP joint arthroplasty, joint replacement hallux

Correction of hallux rigidus with implant placement, including preparation of the first metatarsophalangeal joint and insertion of a prosthetic implant to restore motion and reduce pain.

In Plain Language

Big toe joint replacement; Stiff big toe with artificial joint; Hallux replacement surgery

Clinical Context

Used for advanced hallux rigidus cases where bone-preserving procedures are insufficient and functional restoration is needed; implant provides pain relief and improved joint motion.
