20103 exploration pentrg wound extremity

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: extremity wound exploration, limb wound exam

Surgical exploration of a penetrating wound to an extremity (arm or leg) to identify and evaluate soft tissue, bone, or vascular damage. This procedure assesses injury severity.

Clinical Context

Used for assessment of penetrating extremity trauma to identify vascular, nerve, tendon, or bony injury requiring operative management or repair.
