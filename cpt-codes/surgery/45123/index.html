45123 Partial proctectomy

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: low anterior resection, partial rectal resection, LAR

Partial proctectomy (removal of portion of rectum) with sphincter preservation, typically through low anterior resection technique.

Clinical Context

Used for mid-to-distal rectal cancer, benign rectal pathology, or inflammatory conditions requiring partial rectal resection while maintaining bowel continuity.
