27703 Reconstruction ankle joint

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ankle replacement, complex ankle reconstruction

Surgical reconstruction of ankle joint with complex arthroplasty, total joint replacement, or multiple structure reconstruction.

Clinical Context

Used for advanced ankle arthrosis or when arthrodesis is contraindicated. Higher work RVU reflects implant placement and complexity.
