47570 Laparoscopic cholecystoenterostomy

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Lap cholecystoenterostomy, bypass procedure

Laparoscopic creation of an internal fistula between the gallbladder and small intestine (cholecystoenterostomy), bypassing obstruction to allow internal drainage.

Clinical Context

Performed for gallbladder outlet obstruction, benign stricture, or when cholecystectomy is not feasible. Palliates symptoms while preserving gallbladder.
