44187 Laparoscopic ileo/jejuno-stomy

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: lap ileostomy, laparoscopic stoma creation, lap ileojejunostomy

Laparoscopic creation of ileostomy or jejunostomy for bowel diversion and stoma creation.

In Plain Language

laparoscopic bowel opening creation; minimally invasive stoma placement

Clinical Context

Laparoscopic creation of small bowel stoma (ileostomy or jejunostomy) for bowel diversion in patients unable to undergo colectomy with anastomosis.
