14301 Tissue trnfr any 30.1-60 square centimeters

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Large tissue transfer, Extensive adjacent tissue transfer, Large local flap

Tissue transfer or graft from adjacent area to cover defect on any body area measuring thirty point one to sixty square centimeters.

In Plain Language

Large skin coverage using nearby tissue; Major tissue rearrangement

Clinical Context

Used for coverage of larger defects spanning thirty to sixty square centimeters using tissue adjacent to the defect.
