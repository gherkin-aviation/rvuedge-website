47535 Conversion external bil drainage catheter

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: Catheter conversion, internal-external conversion

Conversion of an external percutaneous biliary drainage catheter to an internal/external system that allows internal drainage into the duodenum while maintaining external access.

Clinical Context

Performed after initial external drainage has controlled infection/sepsis, allowing patient comfort and potential stent placement for internal drainage.
