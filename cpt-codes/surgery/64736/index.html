64736 Incision of chin nerve

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: mental nerve incision, chin nerve decompression

Surgical incision and decompression of the mental nerve in the chin region. Treats compression or entrapment causing chin and lower lip numbness or pain.

Clinical Context

Indicated for mental neuralgia or neuropathy causing lower lip, chin, and anterior gingival pain or dysesthesia. Often related to dental work or mandibular trauma.
