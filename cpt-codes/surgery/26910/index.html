26910 Amputate metacarpal bone

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: metacarpal amputation, ray amputation, finger ray removal

Surgical amputation of a metacarpal bone including removal through the midcarpal level with appropriate soft tissue handling.

In Plain Language

removing metacarpal bone; finger ray amputation

Clinical Context

Performed for severe trauma, osteomyelitis, nonunion, avascular necrosis of metacarpal bones, or malignancy requiring complete removal.
