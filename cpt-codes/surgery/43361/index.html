43361 Gastrointestinal repair

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: revision gastrojejunostomy, anastomotic revision, revisional gastric surgery

Open gastrointestinal repair with revision or revision of prior gastric bypass anastomosis. This addresses complications of previous bypass surgery.

In Plain Language

revising prior stomach surgery; fixing failed gastric bypass

Clinical Context

Performed for stricture, afferent/efferent loop obstruction, or poor outcomes after initial bypass; high morbidity and lower success rates.
