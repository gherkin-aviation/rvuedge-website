65420 Removal of eye lesion

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: conjunctival lesion excision, conjunctival mass removal

Excision of conjunctival lesion; over 1 quadrant.

Clinical Context

Surgical removal of conjunctival nevi, papillomas, or suspicious lesions. Important to include adequate margins for malignant lesions.
