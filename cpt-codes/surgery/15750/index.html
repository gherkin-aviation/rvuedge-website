15750 Neurovascular pedicle flap

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: neurovascular flap, sensate flap

Neurovascular pedicle flap transfer. Flap that includes nerve and vascular structures for functional reconstruction with preserved sensation.

Clinical Context

Used for specialized hand reconstruction and facial injuries where sensation preservation enhances functional outcomes.
