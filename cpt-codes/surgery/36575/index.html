36575 Repair tunneled cv catheter

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: catheter repair, line repair, CVC repair

Repair of tunneled or non-tunneled central venous catheter, without imaging guidance.

Clinical Context

Used for repair of damaged or malfunctioning tunneled or non-tunneled catheters, often to salvage device rather than replacement.
