28320 Repair of foot bones

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: foot fracture repair, ORIF foot, foot bone fixation

Surgical repair of foot bone fracture or other structural defect using open reduction and internal fixation techniques with plates, screws, or other implants as needed.

Clinical Context

Used for displaced or unstable foot bone fractures requiring operative reduction and fixation to achieve and maintain anatomic alignment for healing.
