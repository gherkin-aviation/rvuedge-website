67027 Implant eye drug system

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: intravitreal implant, drug delivery implant, Ozurdex, dexamethasone implant, sustained release implant

Implantation of drug delivery system into or near the eye for sustained release of therapeutic medication (intraocular implant for steroid, antibiotic, or anti-inflammatory agents).

In Plain Language

implanted medication in eye; slow-release drug device placement

Clinical Context

Used for chronic eye conditions requiring long-term treatment: diabetic macular edema, retinal vein occlusion, uveitis, macular degeneration; avoids repeated injections.
