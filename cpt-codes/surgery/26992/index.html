26992 Drainage of bone lesion

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: bone abscess drainage, osteomyelitis debridement, hip bone drainage

Surgical drainage and debridement of bone abscess or osteomyelitis in the hip or pelvic region with removal of infected bone tissue.

Clinical Context

Used for hip or pelvic bone infection with abscess formation requiring incision, drainage, and debridement to remove infected material.
