33507 Repair artery intramural

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: intramural coronary repair, intramural artery unroofing, myocardial tunnel repair

Surgical repair of an intramural coronary artery (artery coursing within the myocardial wall), typically using techniques such as unroofing to restore normal external course.

In Plain Language

fixing heart artery trapped in heart muscle; releasing trapped heart artery

Clinical Context

Performed for symptomatic intramural coronary artery segments causing exercise-induced ischemia or arrhythmias; unroofing or enlargement of the tunnel provides symptom relief and reduces sudden death risk.
