23077 Radiation rescj tumor shoulder<5 centimeters

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Radical shoulder tumor resection, Wide local excision

Radical resection of tumor (eg, malignant neoplasm), soft tissue of shoulder area; less than 5 centimeters.

Clinical Context

Used for malignant shoulder soft tissue tumors less than 5 cm; wide margins removed for oncologic control.
