17276 destruction mal lesion s/n/h/f/g >4.0

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: destroy malignant 4.0+ sensitive

Destruction of malignant skin lesion (scalp, neck, hands, feet, genitals) with largest diameter greater than 4.0 centimeters.

Clinical Context

Used for extensive malignant lesions on cosmetically sensitive areas greater than 4 cm. May require reconstruction with grafts or flaps.
