33883 Delayed plmt proximal xtn prosthesis

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Proximal extension graft, staged TEVAR extension, delayed proximal graft

Delayed placement of proximal stent graft extension prosthesis; percutaneous placement of additional stent graft extension at the proximal end to extend coverage after initial endovascular repair.

Clinical Context

Performed in staged fashion after initial endovascular repair when proximal extension is needed to address additional pathology or to achieve adequate seal zones, commonly in multi-segment aortic disease.
