26756 Pin finger fracture each

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: DIP pinning, PIP pinning, percutaneous interphalangeal fixation

Surgical treatment of interphalangeal joint dislocation with percutaneous pinning for stabilization and healing.

In Plain Language

fixing dislocated finger joint with pins; finger joint stabilization

Clinical Context

Used for interphalangeal dislocations requiring percutaneous pin fixation to maintain reduction during healing, minimally invasive approach.
