33981 Replace vad pump external

Surgery

Also known as: VAD pump replacement, external pump exchange, external controller replacement

Replacement of ventricular assist device external pump; replacement of the external pump component of a ventricular assist device without replacing the intracorporeal portion.

In Plain Language

Heart pump external part replacement; VAD pump component exchange

Clinical Context

Replacement of external pump unit in VAD systems with external components when pump malfunction or failure occurs.
