27818 Treatment of ankle fracture

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: percutaneous fixation trimalleolar, trimalleolar fracture fixation

Initial closed treatment of ankle fracture (trimalleolar) with manipulation and internal fixation.

Clinical Context

Performed for trimalleolar fractures amenable to closed reduction and percutaneous or limited fixation.
