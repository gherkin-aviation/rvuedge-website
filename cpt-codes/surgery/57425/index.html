57425 Laparoscopy surgical colpopexy

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Laparoscopic colpopexy, Lap sacrocolpopexy

Laparoscopic sacrocolpopexy. Minimally invasive fixation of vaginal apex to sacrum for prolapse.

Clinical Context

Used for post-hysterectomy vault prolapse via laparoscopic approach. Minimally invasive alternative to open colpopexy.
