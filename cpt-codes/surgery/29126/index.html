29126 application short arm splint dyn

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: dynamic splint, active short arm splint, functional forearm splint

Application of a dynamic short arm splint with movable or articulated components allowing controlled finger movement during healing and rehabilitation.

In Plain Language

movable arm splint; interactive wrist splint

Clinical Context

Used for tendon repairs, nerve injuries, and conditions requiring early mobilization to prevent stiffness while protecting healing structures.
