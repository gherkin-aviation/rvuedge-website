35521 Artery byp graft axill-femoral

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: axillofemoral bypass, AxFem bypass

Arterial bypass graft from axillary to femoral artery with restoration of lower extremity blood flow.

Clinical Context

Revascularization for aortoiliac occlusive disease when aortofemoral bypass not feasible (hostile abdomen, reoperation). Excellent extraanatomic alternative with lower operative risk.
