28740 Fusion of foot bones

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: tarsometatarsal fusion, Lisfranc fusion, metatarsocuneiform arthrodesis

Surgical arthrodesis (fusion) of metatarsocuneiform joint or other tarsal-metatarsal joints, permanently fusing the forefoot to the midfoot to treat arthritis or Lisfranc injury.

In Plain Language

Lisfranc joint fusion; Forefoot fusion; Midfoot arch fusion

Clinical Context

Used for Lisfranc injury, tarsometatarsal joint arthritis, or forefoot-midfoot instability; fusion provides pain relief and foot stability.
