56632 Vlvctmy radiation prtl bi lymphad

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Radical vulvectomy with bilateral node dissection

Radical vulvectomy with partial tissue removal and bilateral lymphadenectomy. Removes vulva and lymph nodes on both sides.

Clinical Context

Used for central or extensive vulvar carcinoma with bilateral nodal staging. Standard approach for most primary vulvar cancer cases.
