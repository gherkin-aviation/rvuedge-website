21198 Reconstruction lower jaw segment

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: mandibular segment reconstruction, partial jaw reconstruction

Reconstruction of a single segment of the lower jaw, typically involving a portion of the mandible without full continuity restoration.

Clinical Context

Used for limited segmental defects affecting a portion of the mandible.
