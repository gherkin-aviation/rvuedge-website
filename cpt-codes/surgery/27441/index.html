27441 Revision of knee joint

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: femoral component revision, distal femoral revision

Surgical revision of the knee joint with femoral prosthetic component replacement or adjustment to address femoral component-related complications or wear.

Clinical Context

Used for femoral component loosening, wear, malposition, or failure. Common cause of knee revision surgery.
