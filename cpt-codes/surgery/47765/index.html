47765 Fuse liver ducts & bowel

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Hepaticojejunostomy, intrahepatic bile duct bypass

Surgical creation of an internal anastomosis between the intrahepatic bile ducts and jejunum (hepaticojejunostomy) to bypass proximal bile duct obstruction.

Clinical Context

Performed for hilar cholangiocarcinoma or hepatic strictures. Jejunal approach preferred over duodenal for hepatic anastomosis.
