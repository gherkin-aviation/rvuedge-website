33983 Replace vad intra with bp

Surgery

Also known as: LVAD pump exchange with CPB, intracorporeal pump replacement on pump, VAD pump change

Replacement of ventricular assist device intracorporeal component with cardiopulmonary bypass support; replacement of the implanted ventricular assist device pump requiring cardiopulmonary bypass for pump exchange.

In Plain Language

Heart pump replacement with heart-lung machine; Implantable VAD pump exchange with bypass

Clinical Context

Replacement of failed intracorporeal LVAD pump requiring cardiopulmonary bypass support due to extensive adhesions or other technical factors.
