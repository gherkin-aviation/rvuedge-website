58562 Hysteroscopy remove foreign body

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: hysteroscopic foreign body removal, retained product removal

Hysteroscopy with removal of foreign body involves endoscopic extraction of intrauterine foreign material such as retained products of conception or intrauterine device.

Clinical Context

Emergency or urgent procedure for retained products of conception after abortion or delivery, lost IUD threads, or other intrauterine foreign material.
