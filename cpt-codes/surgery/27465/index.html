27465 Shortening of thigh bone

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: femoral shortening, femoral osteotomy, bone shortening

Surgical shortening of the femur through osteotomy and bone removal or compression to correct limb length discrepancy and improve lower extremity alignment.

In Plain Language

shortening the thigh bone

Clinical Context

Used for limb length discrepancy when shortening is preferred over lengthening. May be combined with realignment or as standalone procedure.
