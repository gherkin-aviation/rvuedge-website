49326 Laparoscopic with omentopexy add-on

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: laparoscopic omentopexy, lap omentopexy, omental fixation

Laparoscopic omentopexy (fixation of omentum to abdominal wall or other structure) as add-on procedure.

In Plain Language

camera-guided fixation of abdominal fatty tissue; stitch to secure omental flap

Clinical Context

Laparoscopic omentopexy performed as adjunctive procedure during other abdominal operations. Used to secure omental flap in place for reconstruction or reinforcement.
