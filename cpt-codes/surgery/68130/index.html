68130 excision lesion conjunctiva adj scl

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: conjunctival-scleral lesion excision, scleral involved lesion removal

Excision of conjunctival lesion with adjacent scleral tissue when lesion involves or requires scleral removal.

Clinical Context

Used for lesions that extend to or require removal of scleral tissue. Common indications include limbal tumors, conjunctival lesions extending to sclera, or scleral involvement.
