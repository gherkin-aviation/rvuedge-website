44391 Colonoscopy for bleeding

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: colonoscopy stoma hemorrhage control, transcolostomy bleeding treatment

Colonoscopy performed through colostomy stoma for control of hemorrhage via hemostatic intervention such as cauterization or injection therapy.

In Plain Language

camera surgery to stop colon bleeding through colostomy; bleeding control via stoma

Clinical Context

Used for treatment of lower gastrointestinal bleeding in patients with colostomy when bleeding source is identified in remaining colon or rectum.
