24160 removal prosthhumrl & ulnar cmpnt

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: elbow prosthesis removal, elbow implant extraction, prosthesis takeout

Removal of prosthetic implant (humeral and ulnar component) from elbow. Extraction of failed elbow arthroplasty components.

In Plain Language

Removal of artificial elbow joint; Surgical removal of failed elbow implant

Clinical Context

Used for removal of failed total elbow arthroplasty due to loosening, infection, mechanical failure, or other complications requiring component extraction.
