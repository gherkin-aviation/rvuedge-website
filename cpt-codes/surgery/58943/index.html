58943 Removal of ovary(s)

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: laparoscopic oophorectomy, lap oophorectomy, laparoscopic ovary removal

Total removal of ovary or ovaries via laparoscopic approach, which is minimally invasive compared to open oophorectomy.

In Plain Language

removing the ovary through a small camera incision

Clinical Context

Minimally invasive approach for benign pathology, endometriosis, or elective removal, allowing faster recovery than open oophorectomy.
