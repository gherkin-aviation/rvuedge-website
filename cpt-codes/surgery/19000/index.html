19000 Puncture aspiration cyst breast

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: breast cyst aspiration, breast FNA

Percutaneous needle aspiration and cytology of breast cyst to evaluate for malignancy and provide therapeutic drainage.

In Plain Language

Needle biopsy of breast lump; Breast cyst fluid removal

Clinical Context

Performed for evaluation of palpable breast cyst and symptom relief. May be done under ultrasound guidance. Used diagnostically and therapeutically.
