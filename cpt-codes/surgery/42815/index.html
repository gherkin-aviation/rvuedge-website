42815 Excision of neck cyst

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: complex neck cyst removal, difficult cyst excision

Excision (surgical removal) of a cyst of the neck with complex technique involving extensive dissection or difficult anatomy.

Clinical Context

Performed for neck cysts with complicated anatomy, recurrent lesions, or intimate relationship with important structures. Includes cases requiring extensive dissection or reconstruction.
