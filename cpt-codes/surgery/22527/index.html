22527 Idet 1 or more levels

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: IDET add-on, Additional IDET level

Percutaneous intradiscal electrothermal treatment (IDET) of intervertebral disc, each additional vertebral segment or level beyond the first.

Clinical Context

Add-on code for treating multiple intervertebral discs with IDET in same session; used when pain generators identified at multiple levels.
