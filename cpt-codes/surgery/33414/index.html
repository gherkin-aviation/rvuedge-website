33414 Repair of aortic valve

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: aortic valve and subvalvular repair

Repair of aortic valve and subvalvular tissue, correcting both valve and subvalvular pathology.

Clinical Context

Used for aortic regurgitation with subvalvular involvement such as ventricular septal defect.
