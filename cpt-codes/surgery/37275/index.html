37275 Rvsc evsc fpvt st athrc sf 1

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: fem-pop stent after atherectomy first, atherectomy plus stent

Percutaneous or open revascularization of the femoropopliteal artery using stent placement following atherectomy in the first treated vessel.

Clinical Context

Atherectomy to debulk followed by stent for scaffolding. First femoropopliteal vessel in calcified disease.
