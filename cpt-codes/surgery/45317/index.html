45317 Proctosigmoidoscopy bleed

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: proctoscopy with bleeding control, sigmoidoscopy with hemorrhage management

Proctosigmoidoscopy with hemostasis of bleeding lesion via injection, cautery, or clip application.

In Plain Language

stopping rectal bleeding with scope; cauterization of bleeding site

Clinical Context

Emergency intervention for acute lower gastrointestinal bleeding, diverticular bleeding, or bleeding from polyp sites or ulcerations in the rectosigmoid.
