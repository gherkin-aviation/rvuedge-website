54056 Cryosurgery penis lesion(s)

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: cryotherapy of penis, liquid nitrogen destruction

Destruction of benign or malignant lesions of the penis using cryosurgery or extreme cold temperature technique.

Clinical Context

Used for multiple or larger penile lesions. Cryosurgery provides effective destruction with preservation of underlying tissue.
