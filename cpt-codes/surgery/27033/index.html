27033 Arthrotomy hip exploration/removal loose/foreign body

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: hip arthrotomy exploration, loose body removal hip, hip joint debridement

Surgical arthrotomy of the hip joint with exploration and removal of loose bodies or fibrous tissue.

In Plain Language

opening hip joint to remove loose pieces; hip joint debris removal

Clinical Context

Used for intra-articular loose bodies, osteochondral fragments, or debris causing mechanical symptoms requiring arthrotomy and removal.
