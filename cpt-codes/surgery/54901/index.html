54901 Fusion of spermatic ducts

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: vasovasostomy extensive, epididymovasostomy

Fusion or anastomosis of spermatic ducts with more extensive dissection and reconstruction.

Clinical Context

Extended vasovasostomy or epididymovasostomy for complex obstruction, bypass, or prior failed reconstruction.
