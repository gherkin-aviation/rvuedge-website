42808 Excise pharynx lesion

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: pharyngeal lesion excision, throat lesion removal

Excision (removal) of a lesion of the pharynx (throat) tissue using transoral approach.

Clinical Context

Performed for benign or malignant pharyngeal lesions including polyps, cysts, or tumors. Allows definitive treatment while obtaining tissue for histopathology.
