31546 Remove vc lesion scope/graft

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: vocal cord lesion removal with graft, vocal cord grafting, laryngeal grafting

Laryngoscopic removal of a vocal cord lesion with operating microscope visualization combined with graft placement (such as free tissue graft, fascial graft, or mucosal graft) to restore vocal cord contour and function.

Clinical Context

Performed for vocal cord lesions requiring resurfacing with graft material to restore normal anatomy, vocal cord pliability, and voice quality. Commonly used for scars or significant defects.
