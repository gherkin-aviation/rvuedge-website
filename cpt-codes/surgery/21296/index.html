21296 Revision of jaw muscle/bone

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: jaw muscle/bone revision, jaw reconstruction revision

Revision of jaw muscles and/or jaw bone for correction of deformity or dysfunction, more extensive than simple revision.

In Plain Language

Correcting jaw muscle or bone problems; Jaw revision surgery

Clinical Context

Used for more significant muscular imbalance or skeletal malposition requiring surgical correction.
