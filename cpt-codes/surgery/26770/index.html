26770 Treat finger dislocation

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: finger dislocation reduction, closed finger dislocation repair, finger joint reduction

Surgical reduction and treatment of finger dislocation at the metacarpophalangeal or interphalangeal joint with closed manipulation.

Clinical Context

Employed for acute traumatic finger dislocations managed with closed reduction technique, commonly from sports injuries or mechanical trauma.
