61150 Bur hol/trph drainage brn abs/cyst

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: burr hole abscess drainage, brain abscess drainage

Burr hole and/or trephination with evacuation and drainage of brain abscess or other intracranial fluid collection (cyst, hematoma).

In Plain Language

small skull opening to drain brain infection; brain abscess drainage surgery

Clinical Context

Used for symptomatic brain abscesses, particularly those not amenable to percutaneous drainage. Can be diagnostic (culture and drainage) or therapeutic (decompression and source control).
