26525 Release finger contracture

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Finger contracture release, PIP contracture release, Proximal joint release

Surgical release of contracture affecting the finger joints or proximal interphalangeal joint, performed to restore extension or mobility when contracture limits function.

Clinical Context

Indicated for proximal interphalangeal or distal interphalangeal contracture from burn injury, spasticity, arthritis, or post-traumatic scarring.
