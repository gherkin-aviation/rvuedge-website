51800 Revision of bladder/urethra

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: bladder neck revision, bladder outlet revision

Revision of bladder and urethra, reconstruction of bladder and urethral junction for dysfunction or obstruction.

Clinical Context

Performed for bladder outlet obstruction, incontinence, or anatomic abnormality at bladder-urethral junction.
