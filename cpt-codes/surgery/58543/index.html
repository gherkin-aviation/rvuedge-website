58543 Lsh uterus above 250 g

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: TLH large uterus, laparoscopic hysterectomy large

Laparoscopic hysterectomy with uterus greater than 250 grams involves removal of enlarged uterus endoscopically with increased operative complexity.

In Plain Language

Laparoscopic removal of large uterus; Minimally invasive large uterus removal

Clinical Context

Large fibroids or adenomyosis increase difficulty of laparoscopic approach. Requires morcellation for specimen removal or vaginal extraction component.
