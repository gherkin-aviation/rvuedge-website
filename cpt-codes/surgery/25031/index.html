25031 incision and drainage forearm and/or wrist bursa

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: bursa drainage, bursitis I&D

Incision and drainage of a bursa (fluid-filled sac) in the forearm and/or wrist area, removing accumulated fluid to relieve pressure and inflammation.

Clinical Context

Indicated for symptomatic bursitis that is refractory to conservative treatment or shows signs of infection. Bursae commonly affected include the olecrananon and wrist bursae.
