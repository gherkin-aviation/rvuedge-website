62365 Remove spine infusion device

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: spinal pump removal, intrathecal pump explantation

Removal of spinal cord infusion pump or programmable delivery device previously implanted.

Clinical Context

Used for removal of intrathecal infusion pumps when treatment is discontinued or due to complications.
