66988 Extracapsular cataract removal with endoscopic cyclophotocoagulation

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ECCE with ECP, cataract extraction with corneal replacement

Extracapsular cataract removal with endothelial keratoplasty, combining manual or expression-based cataract extraction with corneal endothelial replacement in cases with compromised corneal health.

Clinical Context

Performed when patient requires both cataract removal and corneal endothelial cell replacement due to primary or secondary corneal dysfunction.
