65770 Keratoprosthesis

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: artificial cornea, Boston Keratoprosthesis

Keratoprosthesis.

In Plain Language

implanting artificial cornea; replacing cornea with synthetic device

Clinical Context

Implantation of artificial corneal device for severe corneal disease unsuitable for conventional transplant. Used in failed grafts or severe ocular surface disease.
