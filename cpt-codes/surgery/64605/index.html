64605 Injection treatment of nerve

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: chemodenervation large muscle groups, botulinum toxin multiple muscles

Chemodenervation with administration of chemical agent for treatment of muscle spasticity in larger muscle groups or multiple muscles.

Clinical Context

Used for more extensive spasticity affecting multiple or larger muscles in post-stroke or cerebral palsy patients.
