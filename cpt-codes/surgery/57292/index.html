57292 Construct vagina with graft

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Vaginal construction with graft, Skin graft vaginoplasty

Construction of vagina with graft. Creation of neovagina using split-thickness skin graft or other tissue.

Clinical Context

Used for vaginal reconstruction with tissue graft to improve healing and functional outcome.
