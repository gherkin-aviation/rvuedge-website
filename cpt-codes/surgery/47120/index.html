47120 Partial removal of liver

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: partial hepatectomy, liver resection, hepatic segmentectomy, hepatectomy partial

Partial resection (hepatectomy) of liver. Removal of portion of liver parenchyma.

In Plain Language

removing part of the liver; surgical removal of liver tissue

Clinical Context

Performed for hepatic tumors, cysts, abscesses, or donor preparation for transplantation. Common in hepatobiliary surgery.
