21461 Optx mndblr fracture wo ntrdntl

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: mandible ORIF without dental wiring, open reduction mandible no arch bars

Open treatment of mandibular fracture without interdental fixation. Surgical reduction and internal fixation of mandibular fractures without relying on dental occlusal guidance for stability.

Clinical Context

Used for mandibular fractures requiring open fixation when dental occlusion cannot reliably guide healing or when teeth are absent. Rigid fixation devices maintain fracture reduction independent of occlusal relationship.
