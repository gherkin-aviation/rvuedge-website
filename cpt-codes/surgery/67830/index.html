67830 Revise eyelashes

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: trichiasis surgery, lash margin surgery, anterior lamellar repositioning

Revision of eyelashes using surgical methods including removal of abnormal lash follicles, lash margin splitting, or targeted anterior lamellar surgery.

Clinical Context

Surgical approach for extensive trichiasis or recurrent cases; removes misdirected follicles or repositions anterior lamellar structures.
