36216 Place catheter in artery

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: second-order catheterization, selective branch catheter, selective second-order catheterization

Percutaneous placement of arterial catheter into second-order or higher branch artery (selective additional) for angiography or intervention.

Clinical Context

Used for selective renal segmental angiography, splenic branch imaging, or selective visceral intervention.
