51860 Repair of bladder wound

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: bladder repair, cystorrhaphy, bladder closure

Repair of bladder wound or rupture, primary closure of bladder defect from trauma or surgical injury.

Clinical Context

Emergency or urgent repair of bladder trauma or intraoperative injury. Requires prompt recognition and repair.
