45342 Sigmoidoscopy with ultrasound guide biopsy

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: sigmoidoscopy with ultrasound-guided biopsy, flexible sig with EUS-FNA

Sigmoidoscopy with endoscopic ultrasound-guided fine-needle aspiration (EUS-FNA) for tissue sampling.

In Plain Language

needle biopsy using ultrasound guidance; ultrasound-guided tissue sampling

Clinical Context

Performed for tissue diagnosis of submucosal lesions, lymph nodes, or lesions not amenable to standard endoscopic biopsy.
