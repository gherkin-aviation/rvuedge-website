46742 Repair of imperforated anus

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: imperforate anus repair, high imperforate anus repair, abdominoperineal repair

Repair of imperforate anus. Surgical correction creating functional anal canal for complete imperforate anus anomaly.

In Plain Language

fixing a completely closed anus opening; surgery for birth defect with no anus

Clinical Context

Performed for high imperforate anus requiring abdominoperineal sacroperineal approach. Often requires colostomy initially with later closure.
