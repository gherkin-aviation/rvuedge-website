62200 Establish brain cavity shunt

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: lumbar CSF drainage, thecal sac drainage, spinal fluid drainage

Establishment of cerebrospinal fluid drainage system via lumbar puncture or thecal sac needle placement. Includes placement of drain or catheter.

In Plain Language

spinal fluid drainage surgery; lumbar drain placement

Clinical Context

Used for temporary or permanent drainage of cerebrospinal fluid from the thecal sac in cases of hydrocephalus or increased intracranial pressure.
