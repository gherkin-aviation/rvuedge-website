50940 Release of ureter

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ureterolysis, ureteral release, external obstruction release

Release of ureter from external obstruction or adhesion, freeing the ureter from compression caused by fibrosis, inflammation, or surrounding tissue.

In Plain Language

freeing the ureter from obstruction

Clinical Context

Performed for ureteral obstruction from retroperitoneal fibrosis, adhesions, or external compression. Often associated with broader abdominal or pelvic surgery.
