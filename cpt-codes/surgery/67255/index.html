67255 Reinforce/graft eye wall

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: scleral graft, graft reinforcement, scleral myectomy graft

Reinforcement and graft of scleral wall using full-thickness or partial-thickness autograft, allograft, or synthetic material combined with myectomy or structural repair.

Clinical Context

Complex repair combining scleral grafting with myectomy for severe thinning, staphyloma, or prior surgery complications.
