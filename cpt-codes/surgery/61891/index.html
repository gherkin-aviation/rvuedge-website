61891 revision/replacement sk-mnt crnl nstm

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: corneal implant revision, skin-mounted implant replacement

Revision or replacement of skin-mounted corneal implant system. Includes removal of existing implant and placement of new system.

In Plain Language

corneal implant replacement; eye implant replacement surgery

Clinical Context

Used when a previously implanted corneal prosthetic device requires revision or replacement due to malfunction, displacement, or patient dissatisfaction.
