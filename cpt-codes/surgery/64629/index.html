64629 Trml destruction ios bvn each additional

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: trigeminal nerve thermal destruction additional levels

Thermal destruction of branches of the inferior alveolar and other branches of the trigeminal nerve for each additional spinal level beyond the second.

Clinical Context

Used when multiple trigeminal nerve branches require destruction for comprehensive pain relief.
