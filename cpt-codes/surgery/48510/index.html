48510 Drainage pancreatic pseudocyst

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Pancreatic pseudocyst drainage, cystojejunostomy

Surgical drainage of a pancreatic pseudocyst by creating an internal fistula between the cyst and small intestine (cyst-enterostomy) for long-term decompression.

Clinical Context

Performed for symptomatic pancreatic pseudocysts causing pain or obstruction. Provides long-term internal drainage.
