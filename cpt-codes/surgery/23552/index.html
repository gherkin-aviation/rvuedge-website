23552 Optx acrclv dslc aq/chrn grf

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ORIF AC with graft, AC reconstruction with ligament, AC repair with ligamentous reconstruction

Open treatment of acromioclavicular dislocation (acute or chronic) with osseous graft (ligamentous reconstruction). Open AC repair with ligament reconstruction and graft.

Clinical Context

Applied in chronic AC dislocations, horizontal instability, or when direct repair proves inadequate; augments with ligamentous reconstruction for superior stability.
