32650 Thoracoscopy with pleurodesis

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: VATS pleurodesis, thoracoscopic pleural sclerosis

Thoracoscopic pleurodesis procedure using chemical or mechanical sclerosing agent to create pleuritis and symphysis, preventing recurrent pleural effusion.

Clinical Context

Used for recurrent malignant pleural effusion when thoracoscopic approach allows direct visualization and pleurodesis agent application.
