40510 Partial excision of lip

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: lip wedge excision, lip lesion removal

Partial excision of the lip for removal of lesion, similar to 40500 but with different extent or approach. Another variation of partial lip excision.

Clinical Context

Similar to 40500, used for partial lip defects requiring closure. Extent varies between codes distinguishing different amounts of tissue removed.
