24300 Mnpj elbow under anesthesia

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: elbow manipulation under anesthesia, MUA elbow

Manipulation of the elbow joint under anesthesia. Forceful mobilization of a stiff or contracted elbow joint performed under general or regional anesthesia.

Clinical Context

Used for post-traumatic or post-operative stiffness of the elbow joint to restore motion and functional range, typically when conservative measures fail.
