27412 Autochondrocyte implant knee

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ACI, autologous chondrocyte implantation, cartilage cell transplant

Autologous chondrocyte implantation (ACI) procedure for repair of focal cartilage defects in the knee using cultured patient-derived cartilage cells implanted with periosteal or collagen membrane.

In Plain Language

knee cartilage regrowth using your own cells; cartilage cell implant

Clinical Context

Used for symptomatic focal cartilage lesions, primarily in younger patients to prevent osteoarthritis. Requires initial cartilage harvest and culture period.
