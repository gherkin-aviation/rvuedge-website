11765 Wedge excision skn nail fold

Surgery Global 10d

Wedge excision of the nail fold involving removal of a triangular wedge of tissue from the skin fold adjacent to the nail, typically performed to correct nail deformities or remove diseased tissue.

Clinical Context

Used to treat nail fold disorders, paronychia complications, or nail bed abnormalities. This procedure removes damaged tissue to improve nail health and function.
