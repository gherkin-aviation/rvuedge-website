61563 Excision of skull tumor

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: skull base tumor resection, skull tumor excision

Excision of skull base or skull vault tumor with removal of intraosseous and extracranial disease components.

In Plain Language

Removal of tumor from the skull; Skull tumor surgery

Clinical Context

Performed for benign and malignant skull tumors including meningioma, osteosarcoma, hemangiopericytoma, and chordoma.
