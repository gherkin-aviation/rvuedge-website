23415 Release of shoulder ligament

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: shoulder capsular release, adhesiolysis, shoulder mobilization

Release of shoulder ligament(s) for capsular restriction. Surgical release or lengthening of shoulder capsule and ligaments for improved motion in frozen shoulder or capsular contracture.

Clinical Context

Performed in frozen shoulder (adhesive capsulitis), post-operative stiffness, or significant capsular contracture limiting glenohumeral motion after conservative management failure.
