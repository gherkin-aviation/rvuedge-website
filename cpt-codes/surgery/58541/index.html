58541 Lsh uterus 250 g or less

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: total laparoscopic hysterectomy, TLH

Laparoscopic hysterectomy via total laparoscopic technique with uterus 250 grams or less involves removal of the entire uterus endoscopically without vaginal removal.

Clinical Context

Minimally invasive approach preferred for uncomplicated cases. Lower blood loss and faster recovery than abdominal hysterectomy with similar efficacy.
