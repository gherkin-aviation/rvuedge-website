27179 Revise head/neck of femur

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: femoral head revision, revision femur repair, re-operation femoral neck

Revision of femoral head and neck procedures, addressing complications or inadequate prior treatment of femoral head or neck pathology.

In Plain Language

Re-do hip repair; Hip revision surgery

Clinical Context

Used for salvage of failed prior femoral head or neck procedures, complications such as malunion, nonunion, or persistent avascular necrosis.

Billing & Documentation

As a surgical CPT code, proper documentation must include the operative report detailing the procedure performed, patient positioning, approach, findings, and any complications.
