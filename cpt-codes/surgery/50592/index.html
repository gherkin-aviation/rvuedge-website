50592 Percutaneous rf ablate renal tumor

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: percutaneous RFA, RF ablation, percutaneous thermal ablation

Percutaneous radiofrequency ablation of a renal tumor using thermal energy for tumor destruction.

Clinical Context

Used for small renal masses (typically less than 4-5 centimeters) in patients with medical comorbidities or solitary kidneys.
