Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: multilevel interlaminar injection cervical, expanded interlaminar injection

Injection of therapeutic or diagnostic agent via interlaminar approach to cervical or thoracic spinal cord region with multiple level guidance.

Clinical Context

Used for delivery of therapeutic agents to multiple cervical or thoracic spinal levels via interlaminar approach.
