68510 Biopsy of tear gland

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: lacrimal gland biopsy, tear gland biopsy

Biopsy of lacrimal gland using needle or open technique to obtain tissue for diagnosis.

Clinical Context

Used for diagnosis of lacrimal gland pathology. Common indications include suspected malignancy, lymphoma, chronic inflammation, or infiltrative disease.
