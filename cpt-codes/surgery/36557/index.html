36557 Insert tunneled cv catheter

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: tunneled central line, Hickman catheter, Groshong catheter, long-term central line

Insertion of tunneled central venous catheter, without subcutaneous port or pump (eg, Hickman, Groshong); younger than 5 years of age.

In Plain Language

placing a permanent central line that goes under the skin; long-term central catheter in young children

Clinical Context

Used for long-term access in pediatric patients requiring chemotherapy, antibiotics, nutrition, or frequent blood draws.
