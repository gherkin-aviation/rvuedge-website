21627 Sternal debridement

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: sternal debridement, infected sternal tissue removal

Sternal debridement. Surgical removal of necrotic, infected, or devitalized sternal tissue, typically for management of sternal wound infection or osteomyelitis.

Clinical Context

Used for post-operative sternal infections, osteomyelitis, or mediastinitis requiring debridement of devitalized tissue. Goal is removal of infected material while preserving remaining viable sternal structure.
