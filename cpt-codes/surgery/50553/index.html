50553 Kidney endoscopy

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: percutaneous nephroscopy with treatment, renal endoscopy with therapy, therapeutic nephroscopy

Percutaneous endoscopy of the kidney (renal endoscopy) with additional management, such as minor therapeutic intervention beyond visualization.

In Plain Language

camera examination of kidney with treatment; kidney endoscopy with intervention

Clinical Context

Used when diagnostic and simple therapeutic procedures (such as stone basket retrieval or biopsy) are performed simultaneously.
