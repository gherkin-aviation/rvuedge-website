38531 Open biopsy/excision inguinofem nodes

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Open inguinofemoral biopsy/excision

Open surgical biopsy or excision of inguinofemoral lymph nodes, performed for diagnostic evaluation or limited therapeutic removal.

Clinical Context

Diagnostic or limited therapeutic procedure for inguinal lymphadenopathy of uncertain etiology.
