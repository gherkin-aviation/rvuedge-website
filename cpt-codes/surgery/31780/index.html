31780 Reconstruct windpipe

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: tracheal reconstruction, trachea repair

Surgical reconstruction of the trachea (windpipe), performed to restore normal anatomy and function after injury, stenosis, malacia, or other pathological conditions affecting the tracheal structure.

Clinical Context

Used for post-intubation stenosis, traumatic tracheal injury, tracheomalacia, or congenital abnormalities requiring surgical restoration of the tracheal airway.
