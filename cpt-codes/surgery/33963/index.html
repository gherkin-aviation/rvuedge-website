Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: ECMO peripheral multiple adjustments, serial repositioning, repeated adjustments

Extracorporeal membrane oxygenation/extracorporeal life support repositioning of peripheral venous/arterial cannula (second or greater repositioning); multiple re-adjustments of peripherally placed cannulae.

In Plain Language

Multiple adjustments of peripheral ECMO tubing; Serial peripheral catheter movements

Clinical Context

Multiple repositioning procedures of peripheral ECMO cannulae due to recurrent technical issues or complications.
