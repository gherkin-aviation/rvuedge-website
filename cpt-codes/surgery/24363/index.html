24363 Replace elbow joint

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: total elbow arthroplasty, total elbow replacement, TEA

Total elbow arthroplasty (replacement). Surgical insertion of prosthetic implant to replace the entire elbow joint.

Clinical Context

Used for severe elbow arthritis, rheumatoid arthritis, or failed reconstruction where joint preservation is not possible.
