37238 Open/percutaneous place stent same

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: same-segment stent placement, restenting same vessel

Open surgical or percutaneous placement of stent(s) within the same arterial segment or graft previously treated by endovascular intervention.

Clinical Context

Addresses restenosis or primary failure within a previously treated segment. Distinct from separate segment stenting.
