23532 Optx strclv dslc aq/chrn grf

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ORIF SC with graft, SC reconstruction with ligament, SC repair with ligamentous reconstruction

Open treatment of sternoclavicular dislocation (acute or chronic) with osseous graft (ligamentous reconstruction). Open SC repair with ligament reconstruction using graft material.

Clinical Context

Used for SC reconstruction when ligamentous repair alone is insufficient or in chronic cases with significant ligamentous attenuation requiring augmentation.
