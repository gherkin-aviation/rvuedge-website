64487 Tap block uni by infusion

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: adductor canal block continuous infusion, tap block catheter, saphenous nerve continuous infusion

Tap block (adductor canal block) performed unilaterally with continuous infusion of anesthetic through a catheter.

Clinical Context

Used for extended post-operative anesthesia in knee surgery. Catheter provides 24-48 hours of pain relief.
