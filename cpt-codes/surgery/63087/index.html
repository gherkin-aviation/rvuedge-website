63087 Remov vertbr dcmprn thrclmbr

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: thoracolumbar vertebrectomy, thoracolumbar corpectomy

Removal of vertebral body for decompression in the thoracolumbar region. This extensive procedure involves vertebrectomy spanning the thoracic and lumbar junction for comprehensive decompression.

In Plain Language

removal of bone at the junction of mid-back and lower back

Clinical Context

Used for pathology at the thoracolumbar junction including trauma, tumors, OPLL, or severe stenosis affecting multiple levels.
