50715 Release of ureter

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ureterolysis, ureteral release, adhesiolysis of ureter

Ureterolysis with or without repositioning, involving surgical release of the ureter from adhesions, strictures, or obstruction by surrounding tissues.

In Plain Language

freeing the ureter from scar tissue or obstruction

Clinical Context

Performed for ureteral obstruction due to adhesions from prior surgery, retroperitoneal fibrosis, or crossing vessels. High work RVU indicates significant surgical complexity.
