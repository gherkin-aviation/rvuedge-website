34701 Evasc repair a-ao ndgft

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: endovascular aortic repair, EVAR, aortic stent

Endovascular repair of the aorta and aortoiliac vessels without graft. Percutaneous or minimally invasive treatment of aortic aneurysm or occlusion.

Clinical Context

Used for infrarenal abdominal aortic aneurysm (AAA) repair without graft material. Less invasive alternative to open surgery.
