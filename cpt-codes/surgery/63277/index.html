63277 biopsy/excision xdrl spine lesion lumbar

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: lumbar extradural lesion removal, lumbar epidural tumor excision

Biopsy and/or excision of extradural spinal lesion in the lumbar region. This procedure involves sampling or removal of pathological lesions outside the dura at the lumbar level.

Clinical Context

Used for lumbar epidural lesions including metastases, disc herniations with tumor, or other epidural masses causing cauda equina compression.
