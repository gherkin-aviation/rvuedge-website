21936 Resection back tumor 5 centimeters/>

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: radical back tumor resection large, extensive back tumor removal

Radical resection of back tumor, greater than 5 centimeters. Extensive surgical removal of significant back malignancy with wide margins, for lesion exceeding 5 centimeters.

In Plain Language

major cancer surgery of back; large tumor removal with wide margins

Clinical Context

Used for advanced malignant back lesions exceeding 5 centimeters requiring extensive resection with wide margins. Higher morbidity reflecting larger lesion and more extensive reconstruction.
