33980 Remove intracorporeal device

Surgery

Also known as: Implantable LVAD removal, intracorporeal VAD removal, internal pump explantation

Removal of intracorporeal ventricular assist device; removal or explantation of a previously implanted intracorporeal left ventricular assist device.

Clinical Context

Removal of intracorporeal LVAD at time of transplantation or when mechanical support is no longer required.
