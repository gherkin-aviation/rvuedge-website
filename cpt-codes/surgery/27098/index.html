27098 Transfer tendon to pelvis

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: hip tendon transfer, pelvic tendon transfer, muscle transfer hip

Surgical transfer of tendon to the pelvis to improve hip function, stability, or address muscular imbalance.

In Plain Language

moving tendon to improve hip function; hip muscle transfer surgery

Clinical Context

Indicated for hip muscle imbalance, paralysis, or functional deficit where tendon transfer provides improved function and stability.
