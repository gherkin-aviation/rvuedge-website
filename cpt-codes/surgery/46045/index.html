46045 incision and drainage absc tranal under anesthesia

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: transsphincteric I&D, deep anal abscess drainage

Incision and drainage of a transsphincteric anal abscess under anesthesia. This is a deep abscess involving the anal sphincter complex and requires surgical intervention under general or regional anesthesia.

Clinical Context

Used for transsphincteric abscesses (those crossing the anal sphincter) requiring surgical drainage. Common in patients with Crohn's disease or idiopathic abscesses with severe pain and risk of sepsis.
