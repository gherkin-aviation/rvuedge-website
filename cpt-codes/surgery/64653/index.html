64653 Chemodenerv eccrine glands

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: hyperhidrosis injection extended, eccrine gland chemodenervation additional

Chemodenervation of eccrine sweat glands with administration of botulinum toxin for extended areas or additional sites of hyperhidrosis.

Clinical Context

Used for widespread hyperhidrosis requiring treatment of multiple body areas.
