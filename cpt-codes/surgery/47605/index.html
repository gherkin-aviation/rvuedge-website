47605 Cholecystectomy with cholang

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Open chole with CBD exploration, open choledochotomy

Open cholecystectomy with simultaneous cholangiography or exploration of the common bile duct to identify and manage bile duct stones or obstruction.

Clinical Context

Performed when choledocholithiasis or bile duct pathology is present. Allows combined management of gallbladder and bile duct disease.
