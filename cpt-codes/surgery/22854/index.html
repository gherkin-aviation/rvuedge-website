22854 Insj biomechanical device

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: Interbody device add-on, Additional disc replacement

Insertion of interbody biomechanical device(s) without interbody arthrodesis, including endplate preparation, each additional interspace.

Clinical Context

Add-on code for multi-level artificial disc placement; extends motion-preserving surgery to multiple intervertebral spaces.
