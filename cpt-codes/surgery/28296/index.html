28296 Cor hlx vlgs dstl mtar osteo

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: distal metatarsal osteotomy, bunion surgery, Austin osteotomy

Correction of hallux valgus with distal metatarsal osteotomy, realigning the first metatarsal head through bone cutting near its distal end to correct the metatarsophalangeal angle deformity.

Clinical Context

Used for mild to moderate hallux valgus with normal or minimally elevated intermetatarsal angle; distal osteotomy addresses the metatarsophalangeal angle component of deformity.
