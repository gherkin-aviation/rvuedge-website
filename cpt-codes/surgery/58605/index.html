58605 Division of fallopian tube

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: tubal ligation, simple tubal ligation

Division of fallopian tube via simplified technique involves less extensive tubal interruption for permanent sterilization.

Clinical Context

Simplified approach to tubal sterilization with minimal dissection. May be used at time of other procedures. Lower morbidity than extensive tubal procedures.
