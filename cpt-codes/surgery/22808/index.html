22808 Arthrd anterior dfrm 2-3 vrt segment

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Anterior spinal fusion 2-3 levels, Anterior deformity correction

Arthrodesis, anterior technique, spinal deformity correction, with bone graft or nonvascularized bone graft; 2-3 vertebral segments.

Clinical Context

Used for anterior column support in deformity correction; limited to 2-3 segments for anterior approach.
