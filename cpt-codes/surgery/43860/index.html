43860 Revision gstr/jj anast without vgtmy

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: open GJ revision, revision gastrojejunostomy, reanastomosis

Revision of gastroenteric anastomosis (stomach to jejunum) via open approach without vagotomy for complications or inadequate function.

Clinical Context

Revision of gastrojejunal or gastroduodenal anastomosis for stenosis, ulcer, afferent loop syndrome, or other complications. Non-vagotomy approach.
