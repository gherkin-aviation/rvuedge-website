60660 Ablation 1/+thyr ndul 1lobe prq

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: thyroid nodule ablation, radiofrequency ablation of thyroid, percutaneous thyroid ablation

Ablation of one or more thyroid nodules in one lobe of the thyroid gland using percutaneous radiofrequency, ultrasound, or other energy-based techniques.

Clinical Context

Performed for benign thyroid nodules causing compressive symptoms or cosmetic concerns, and increasingly for small papillary cancers. Less invasive alternative to surgery with potential for preservation of gland function.
