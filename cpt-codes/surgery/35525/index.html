35525 Artery byp graft brachial-brchl

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: brachial-brachial bypass, BB bypass

Arterial bypass graft from brachial to brachial artery with restoration of contralateral arm blood flow.

Clinical Context

Revascularization for bilateral brachial artery disease or unilateral occlusion with contralateral involvement. Provides direct flow restoration to affected arm.
