57130 Excision vaginal septum

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: Vaginal septum excision, Septate vagina resection

Excision of vaginal septum. Removal of congenital or acquired vaginal septation.

Clinical Context

Used for congenital transverse or longitudinal vaginal septum causing obstruction, hematometra, or dyspareunia.
