48154 Pancreatectomy

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Extended pancreaticoduodenectomy, en bloc resection

Pancreaticoduodenectomy with en bloc resection of additional organs (duodenum, spleen, or distal stomach) required due to tumor invasion.

Clinical Context

Performed for locally advanced pancreatic head cancer with vascular or organ invasion requiring multi-organ resection.
