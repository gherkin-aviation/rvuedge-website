27745 Reinforce tibia

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: tibial reinforcement, strut graft tibia

Surgical reinforcement or strut grafting of tibia for structural integrity restoration in deficiency or pathologic conditions.

Clinical Context

Performed for pathologic bone, stress fracture, or structural deficiency. Graft provides mechanical support during healing.
