48020 Removal of pancreatic stone

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Pancreatic stone removal, pancreatic ductotomy, stone extraction

Surgical removal of stones from the pancreatic duct through an open pancreatic duct opening (pancreatic ductotomy) to relieve ductal obstruction.

In Plain Language

Removing stones from pancreas ducts

Clinical Context

Performed for chronic pancreatitis with ductal obstruction and stone disease causing pain, malabsorption, and ductal hypertension.
