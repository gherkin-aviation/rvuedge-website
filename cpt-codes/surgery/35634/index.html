35634 Bpg iliorenal

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: iliorenal bypass, iliac-renal bypass, renal artery bypass

Arterial bypass graft from iliac artery to renal artery for renal artery stenosis or occlusion with hypertension or renal insufficiency. Restores renal perfusion.

Clinical Context

Indicated for renovascular hypertension or ischemic nephropathy due to renal artery stenosis. Alternative to aortic bypass when aorta is diseased.
