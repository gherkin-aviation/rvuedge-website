33944 Prepare donor heart

Surgery

Also known as: Donor heart preparation, heart bench work, organ prep

Preparation of donor heart; ex vivo assessment, preparation, and conditioning of procured donor heart for implantation.

In Plain Language

Preparation of heart for transplant; Donor heart conditioning

Clinical Context

Bench preparation of procured donor heart including inspection, assessment of viability, flushing and preservation, and technique modifications for implantation.

RVU Information

CPT 33944 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
