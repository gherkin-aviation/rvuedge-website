63275 biopsy/excision xdrl spine lesion crvl

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: cervical extradural lesion removal, cervical epidural tumor excision

Biopsy and/or excision of extradural spinal lesion in the cervical region. This procedure involves sampling or complete removal of pathological lesions located outside the dura mater at the cervical level.

In Plain Language

removal of growth outside spinal sac in neck

Clinical Context

Used for cervical epidural lesions including metastases, lymphomas, or other extradural tumors causing myelopathy or radiculopathy.
