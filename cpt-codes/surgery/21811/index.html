21811 Optx of rib fracture with fixation scope

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: rib fracture ORIF with scope, endoscopic rib fixation

Open treatment of rib fracture with fixation and endoscopic visualization. Surgical fixation of rib fractures using plates or screws with endoscopic assistance for reduction and plate placement.

Clinical Context

Used for rib fractures causing significant pain, instability, or flail chest that benefit from surgical stabilization. Endoscopic assistance aids in reduction and minimizes soft tissue trauma compared to open fixation alone.
