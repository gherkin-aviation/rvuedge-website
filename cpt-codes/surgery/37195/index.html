37195 Thrombolytic therapy stroke

Surgery

Also known as: thrombolytic therapy, fibrinolytic therapy, catheter-directed thrombolysis

Thrombolytic (fibrinolytic) therapy administered via catheter to dissolve acute arterial or venous thrombosis, particularly for acute ischemic stroke.

In Plain Language

clot-busting medicine injection; dissolving a clot with medicine

Clinical Context

Used in acute ischemic stroke within therapeutic window, acute limb ischemia, and acute mesenteric ischemia. Catheter-directed approach maximizes local drug concentration.

RVU Information

CPT 37195 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
