57335 Repair vagina

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Vaginal flap repair, Vaginal reconstruction

Repair of vaginal defect with flap. Reconstruction of significant vaginal defect using tissue flap.

Clinical Context

Used for extensive vaginal defects from trauma, cancer resection, or fistula repair.
