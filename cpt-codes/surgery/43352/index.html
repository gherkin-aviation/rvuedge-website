43352 Surgical opening esophagus

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: diverting esophagostomy, protective esophagostomy, esophageal diversion

Esophagostomy with intramural diversion of esophageal content for bypass or protective purposes such as in severe caustic injury. This diverts esophageal secretions.

Clinical Context

Performed for severe caustic esophageal injury or esophageal cancer; diverts saliva to prevent aspiration and allows esophageal healing.
