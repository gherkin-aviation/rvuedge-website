43248 Egd guide wire insertion

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: EGD guidewire insertion, guide wire placement, Savary guidewire

Esophagogastroduodenoscopy with guidewire insertion for difficult or malignant stricture dilation. This procedural step facilitates subsequent dilation of challenging strictures.

Clinical Context

Used in complex or malignant esophageal strictures where direct dilation is hazardous; enables safe passage for dilation equipment over a flexible guidewire.
