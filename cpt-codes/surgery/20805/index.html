20805 Replant forearm complete

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: forearm replantation, distal arm reattachment

Replantation of a completely amputated forearm with vascular and nerve anastomosis.

Clinical Context

Emergency procedure for complete forearm amputation; requires comprehensive reconstruction of vessels, nerves, muscles, and tendons.
