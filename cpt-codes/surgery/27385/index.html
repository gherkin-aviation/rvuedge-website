27385 Repair of thigh muscle

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: thigh muscle repair, muscle laceration repair

Repair of thigh muscle through operative approach for tear, laceration, or avulsion.

Clinical Context

Indicated for significant muscle injuries causing loss of function or open lacerations requiring surgical repair.
