26117 Radiation resection hand tumor < 3 centimeters

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: radical hand tumor resection, wide excision hand malignancy

Radical resection (wide excision) of hand tumor, less than 3 centimeters.

Clinical Context

Used for radical resection of malignant or high-grade hand tumors less than 3 cm with adequate margins.
