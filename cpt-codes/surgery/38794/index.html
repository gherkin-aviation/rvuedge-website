38794 Access thoracic lymph duct

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: thoracic duct access, duct ligation

Surgical access to the thoracic lymph duct for visualization, biopsy, or therapeutic intervention. This procedure involves opening the chest or mediastinum to directly access the main lymphatic vessel of the thorax.

Clinical Context

Used for lymph duct ligation in cases of chylothorax (lymph fluid in pleural space), chylous ascites, or diagnostic evaluation of thoracic lymphatic obstruction. Common in post-surgical complications or traumatic injuries.
