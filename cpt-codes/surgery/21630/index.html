21630 Radical resection sternum

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: sternal resection radical, extensive sternal removal

Radical resection of sternum. Extensive surgical removal of large sternal segment for malignancy or extensive infection, often requiring reconstruction.

Clinical Context

Used for sternal malignancy or extensive osteomyelitis requiring wide resection. Often followed by reconstruction with soft tissue or prosthetic material. High morbidity reflecting extensive thoracic surgery.
