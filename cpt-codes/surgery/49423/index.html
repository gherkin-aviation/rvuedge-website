49423 Exchange drainage catheter

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: catheter exchange, drain replacement, percutaneous exchange

Percutaneous exchange or replacement of drainage catheter in established abdominal tract.

In Plain Language

replacement of abdominal drain; catheter swap procedure

Clinical Context

Percutaneous exchange of catheter in established tract for continued drainage of intra-abdominal collections. Used to prevent infection and maintain drainage continuity.
