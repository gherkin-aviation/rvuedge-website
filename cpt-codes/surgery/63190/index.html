63190 Incise spine nerve >2 segmnts

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: multi-segment rhizotomy, selective dorsal rhizotomy, dorsal root sectioning

Incision of spinal nerve root for two or more segments. This procedure involves selective division of spinal nerve rootlets or tracts across multiple vertebral segments for pain management or functional restoration.

Clinical Context

Commonly used for selective dorsal rhizotomy in spasticity management in children with cerebral palsy or for multi-level pain ablation in intractable conditions.
