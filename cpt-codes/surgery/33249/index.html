33249 Insj/replacement defib with lead(s)

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ICD placement, defibrillator implantation, ICD with lead, device and lead insertion

Insertion or replacement of a defibrillator device with one or more lead(s), providing therapy for life-threatening arrhythmias.

Clinical Context

Performed for secondary prevention after sustained ventricular tachycardia/fibrillation or primary prevention in high-risk patients with reduced ejection fraction.
