50551 Kidney endoscopy

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: percutaneous nephroscopy, renal endoscopy, percutaneous renal scope

Percutaneous endoscopy of the kidney (renal endoscopy) for diagnostic visualization and minor therapeutic procedures.

Clinical Context

Used for diagnostic evaluation via percutaneous access, often precursor to therapeutic stone removal or ablation procedures.
