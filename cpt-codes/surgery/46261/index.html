46261 Remove in/ex hem grps & fiss

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: extensive hem removal with fissure, multiple group hemorrhoidectomy with fissurectomy

Surgical removal of internal and external hemorrhoid groups (2 or more) with concurrent anal fissure treatment. Comprehensive hemorrhoidectomy with fissure management.

Clinical Context

Performed for extensive hemorrhoid disease with concurrent fissures. Addresses multiple hemorrhoidal groups and anal fissure pathology simultaneously.
