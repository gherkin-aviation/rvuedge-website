42305 Drainage of salivary gland

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: salivary gland drainage, gland marsupilization

Surgical incision, drainage, and more extensive treatment of a salivary gland including marsupilization or complex drainage procedures.

Clinical Context

More complex salivary gland drainage involving infection, cyst formation, or requiring intraoral flap procedures for access and healing.
