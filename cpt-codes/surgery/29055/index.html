29055 application cast shoulder spica

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: shoulder spica cast, shoulder immobilization, arm spica

Application of shoulder spica cast providing immobilization of the shoulder joint and arm in flexion and abduction, used for proximal humerus and shoulder stability.

In Plain Language

Shoulder and arm cast; Shoulder immobilization cast; Arm cast

Clinical Context

Used for proximal humerus fractures, shoulder instability, or rotator cuff repairs; spica maintains the arm in functional position during healing.
