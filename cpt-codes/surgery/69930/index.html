69930 Implant cochlear device

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: cochlear implant, cochlear implantation, CI implant

Implantation of a cochlear prosthesis (cochlear implant) to provide electric stimulation of the cochlear nerve for hearing restoration.

Clinical Context

Used for severe-to-profound bilateral sensorineural hearing loss unresponsive to amplification, post-lingual or prelingual deafness.
