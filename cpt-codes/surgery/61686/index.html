61686 Intracranial vessel surgery

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: complex AVM repair, AVM resection with bypass

Open surgical repair of intracranial arteriovenous malformation with extensive dissection, bypass grafting, or vascular reconstruction.

In Plain Language

Complex surgery for tangled brain vessels with vascular repair

Clinical Context

Complex AVM cases requiring vascular reconstruction or bypass for flow restoration after malformation resection.
