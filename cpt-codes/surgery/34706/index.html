34706 Evasc repair a-biiliac report

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: repeat aortobi-iliac EVAR, EVAR redo bilateral

Endovascular repair of abdominal aorta with bilateral iliac involvement without graft (repeat procedure). Percutaneous redo aortobi-iliac repair.

Clinical Context

Performed for endoleak or other complications after initial aortobi-iliac EVAR. Often involves component exchange.
