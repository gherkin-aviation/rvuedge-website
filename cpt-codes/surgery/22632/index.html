22632 Arthrd pst tq 1ntrspc lm each

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: Posterior lumbar fusion add-on, Additional PLIF level

Arthrodesis, posterior or posterolateral technique, lumbar; each additional interspaces with single or double intertransverse process bone graft.

Clinical Context

Add-on code for multi-level posterior lumbar fusion with intertransverse bone grafts; extends fusion for multi-level degenerative disease.
