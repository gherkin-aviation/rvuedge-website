Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: spinal lesion excision, spinal tumor removal, spinal biopsy

Biopsy and/or excision of extramedullary or intramedullary spinal lesion at any spinal level. This is a comprehensive code covering lesions that may span multiple levels or regions.

In Plain Language

removal of spinal growth at any level

Clinical Context

Used as a comprehensive code for spinal lesion biopsies or excisions when the specific anatomical code does not apply or for complex multi-level pathology.
