26550 Pollicization digit

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Pollicization, Index pollicization, Digit-to-thumb transfer

Surgical transfer of a digit, typically an index finger, to the thumb position to restore thumb function, performed when thumb is absent or non-functional.

Clinical Context

Indicated for thumb absence, severe thumb dysfunction, or loss where functional reconstruction as pollicized digit improves hand function.
