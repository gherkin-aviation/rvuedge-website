27442 Revision of knee joint

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: tibial component revision, proximal tibial revision

Surgical revision of the knee joint with tibial prosthetic component replacement or adjustment to address tibial component-related problems such as loosening or wear.

Clinical Context

Used for tibial component loosening, wear, malposition, or polyethylene insert degradation. Common knee revision indication.
