35702 exploration n/flwd surgical uxtr artery

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: upper extremity vessel exploration, arm artery exploration, proximal extremity exploration

Exploration of upper extremity vessels with or without flowmetry for ischemia evaluation, trauma assessment, or intervention planning. Assesses arm/shoulder vascular status.

In Plain Language

examining arm and shoulder blood vessels; checking the arteries in the arm

Clinical Context

Used for subclavian, axillary, or brachial disease assessment or trauma. May include intraoperative doppler flowmetry.
