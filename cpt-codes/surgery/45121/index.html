45121 Removal of rectum and colon

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: total colectomy with proctectomy, total abdominal colectomy and proctectomy, TACE

Proctocolectomy (removal of both rectum and colon) with or without ileostomy, used primarily for colorectal disease requiring total colonic and rectal resection.

In Plain Language

removal of entire colon and rectum; complete large bowel removal; colon and rectum removal

Clinical Context

Indicated for familial adenomatous polyposis, ulcerative colitis with pan-colonic involvement, or colorectal cancer with extensive colon involvement.
