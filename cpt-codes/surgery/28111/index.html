28111 Part removal of metatarsal

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: metatarsal head excision, metatarsal head removal, distal metatarsal amputation

Partial excision of metatarsal bone with removal of metatarsal head. This procedure removes the metatarsal head while preserving the base for structural support.

Clinical Context

Performed for painful metatarsal head pathology, transfer metatarsalgia, or rheumatoid forefoot reconstruction. Provides pain relief in weight-bearing areas.
