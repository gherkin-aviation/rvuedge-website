64447 Njx aa and/or strd femoral nerve img

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: femoral nerve block, femoral block with ultrasound, inguinal paravascular block

Nerve injection for anesthetic and/or steroid administration to the femoral nerve with imaging guidance. Ultrasound or fluoroscopy confirms needle placement.

Clinical Context

Used for anterior and medial thigh pain, quadriceps anesthesia, and lower extremity surgery. Imaging reduces vascular complications and improves block quality.
