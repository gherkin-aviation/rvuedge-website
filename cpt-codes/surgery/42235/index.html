42235 Repair palate

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: palate repair, soft palate repair

Surgical repair of the soft palate to restore function, typically addressing palatal insufficiency, fistula, or post-operative complications.

Clinical Context

Revision or definitive repair of soft palate defects including insufficiency causing velopharyngeal dysfunction, fistulas, or speech problems.
