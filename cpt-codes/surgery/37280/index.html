37280 Revsc evsc tpvt angio sf first

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: tibioperoneal angioplasty simple first, TPT angioplasty first

Percutaneous or open revascularization of the tibioperoneal trunk or artery using simple angioplasty in the first treated vessel.

Clinical Context

Addresses tibioperoneal disease in critical limb ischemia. Simple angioplasty, first vessel treated.
