29830 Elbow arthroscopy

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: elbow arthroscopy, elbow scope, arthroscopic elbow exam

Arthroscopy of the elbow joint with diagnostic evaluation and assessment of intra-articular structures.

Clinical Context

Indicated for elbow pain, loose bodies, arthritis, and diagnostic confirmation of osteochondral pathology.
