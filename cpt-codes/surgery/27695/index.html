27695 Repair of ankle ligament

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ankle ligament repair, ankle ligament reconstruction

Surgical repair of ankle ligament complex including primary repair and augmentation of lateral or medial ligaments.

Clinical Context

Indicated for ankle sprains with ligament disruption causing chronic instability. Restores ankle proprioception and stability.
