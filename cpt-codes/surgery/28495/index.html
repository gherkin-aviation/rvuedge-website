28495 Treat big toe fracture

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: hallux fracture reduction, big toe fracture manipulation, closed reduction

Closed treatment of hallux fracture with manipulation to achieve anatomic alignment, used for displaced great toe fractures amenable to closed reduction.

Clinical Context

Used for displaced great toe fractures; closed reduction restores alignment and maintains functional toe position for weight-bearing.
