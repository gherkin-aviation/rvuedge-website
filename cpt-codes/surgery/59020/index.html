59020 Fetal contract stress test

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: contraction stress test, stress test, CST, non-stress test

Fetal contraction stress test involves observation of fetal heart rate response to spontaneous or induced uterine contractions for assessment of fetal well-being.

In Plain Language

checking baby's heart rate response to contractions

Clinical Context

Used to assess fetal reserve and placental function in high-risk pregnancies; reactive response indicates fetal well-being.
