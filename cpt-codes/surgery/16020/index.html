16020 Dress/debrid p-thick burn s

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: partial thickness burn dressing small, burn dress small

Dressing and debridement of partial-thickness (second-degree) burn injury for small treatment area less than defined square centimeters.

Clinical Context

Performed in acute or outpatient settings for small partial-thickness burns. Includes removal of devitalized tissue and application of topical agents and dressings.
