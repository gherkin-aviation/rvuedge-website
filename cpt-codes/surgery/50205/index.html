50205 Renal biopsy surgical exposure kdn

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: open renal biopsy, surgical kidney biopsy, incisional biopsy

Renal biopsy with surgical exposure of the kidney through an open incision, followed by tissue sampling for diagnosis.

Clinical Context

Used when percutaneous biopsy is contraindicated (coagulopathy, solitary kidney, failed percutaneous attempts) or when macroscopic inspection is needed. May be combined with partial nephrectomy.
