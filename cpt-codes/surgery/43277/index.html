43277 Ercp each duct/ampulla dilate

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: ERCP ampullary dilation, sphincterotomy with dilation, ampulla dilation

Endoscopic retrograde cholangiopancreatography with dilation of the pancreatic or biliary duct and ampulla of Vater. This therapeutic procedure improves ductal drainage.

In Plain Language

opening the pancreas and bile duct opening; ampullary dilation

Clinical Context

Therapeutic ERCP for ductal obstruction, strictures, or sphincter of Oddi dysfunction; improves flow and reduces pressure gradients.
