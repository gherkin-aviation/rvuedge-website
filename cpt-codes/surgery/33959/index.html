33959 Ecmo/ecls reposition perph cnula

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: ECMO peripheral multiple repositioning, serial cannula adjustment, repeated reposition

Extracorporeal membrane oxygenation/extracorporeal life support repositioning of peripheral venous/arterial cannula (second or greater repositioning); serial repositioning of peripherally placed cannulae.

Clinical Context

Multiple repositioning procedures of peripheral ECMO cannulae due to recurrent malfunction or complications requiring serial adjustments.
