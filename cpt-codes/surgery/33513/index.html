33513 Cabg vein four

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: four vessel CABG, quadruple vein bypass, four vein CABG

Surgical placement of four coronary artery bypass grafts using saphenous vein conduits, providing revascularization of four coronary territories.

Clinical Context

Used for extensive multivessel disease with four territories requiring revascularization; provides comprehensive ischemic relief in patients with critical stenosis and reduced ejection fraction.
