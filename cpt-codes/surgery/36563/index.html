36563 Insert tunneled cv catheter

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: tunneled pump catheter, implanted pump, programmable pump

Insertion of tunneled central venous catheter, with subcutaneous pump; younger than 5 years of age.

Clinical Context

Used for programmable medication delivery, particularly for pain management or chemotherapy in pediatric patients.
