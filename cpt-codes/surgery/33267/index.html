33267 Excl laa open any method

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: left atrial appendage exclusion, LAA closure surgery, open LAA exclusion

Exclusion of the left atrial appendage via open surgical approach using any exclusion method, preventing thrombus formation in atrial fibrillation.

Clinical Context

Performed in atrial fibrillation patients to reduce stroke risk by preventing thrombus formation in the left atrial appendage.
