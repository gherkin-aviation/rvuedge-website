27187 Reinforce hip bones

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: hip reinforcement, acetabular augmentation, femoral head support

Reinforcement of hip bones through surgical augmentation or support of the femoral head or acetabulum to improve load-bearing capacity and stability.

Clinical Context

Used for dysplastic hips, developmental dysplasia of the hip, or acetabular deficiency requiring surgical improvement of coverage and stability.
