63741 Install spinal shunt

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: spinal shunt placement, simple syrinx shunt

Implantation of spinal shunt for cerebrospinal fluid diversion with simple construction. This is a simplified spinal shunt placement for fluid management.

Clinical Context

Used for straightforward spinal shunt placement when complex reconstruction is not required.
