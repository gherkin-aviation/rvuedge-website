43305 Repair esophagus and fistula

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: esophageal perforation with fistula repair, esophageal fistula repair

Repair of esophageal laceration with concurrent repair of associated esophageal or tracheal fistula. This procedure addresses both the defect and any fistulous connection.

Clinical Context

Complex repair for complicated perforations with fistulization; requires careful dissection and multi-layer closure techniques.
