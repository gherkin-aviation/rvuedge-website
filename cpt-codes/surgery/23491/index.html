23491 Reinforce shoulder bones

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: shoulder girdle reinforcement, multi-site shoulder fixation, shoulder osseous reinforcement

Reinforcement of shoulder bones (multiple osseous structures). Reinforcement of shoulder girdle bones through plating, fixation, or structural support of multiple sites.

Clinical Context

Applied when multiple shoulder girdle bones (clavicle, scapula, humerus) require simultaneous reinforcement or fixation in complex trauma or reconstruction.
