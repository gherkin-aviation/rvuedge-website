47141 Partial removal donor liver

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: left lobe hepatectomy, left lobe donor hepatectomy, split liver procedure

Partial hepatectomy with left lobe procurement from living or deceased donor for transplant.

In Plain Language

removing the left portion of a donor liver; left lobe liver procurement

Clinical Context

Performed for living donor liver transplantation (less common) or split liver procedures from deceased donors.
