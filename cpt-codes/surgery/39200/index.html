39200 Resection mediastinal cyst

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: mediastinal cyst resection, cyst excision

Surgical resection of a mediastinal cyst, which is an abnormal fluid-filled cavity within the mediastinum (central chest cavity). The entire cyst is removed.

Clinical Context

Indicated for symptomatic mediastinal cysts causing compression of vital structures, recurrent infection, or suspected malignancy. Often discovered incidentally on imaging.
