62201 Brain cavity shunt with scope

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: endoscopic CSF drainage, endoscopic lumbar drain

Establishment of cerebrospinal fluid drainage system via lumbar puncture with endoscopic guidance. Uses neuroendoscopy for precision placement.

Clinical Context

Used for precise placement of lumbar cerebrospinal fluid drainage using endoscopic visualization.
