49014 Reexploration pelvic wound

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: Pelvic reexploration, pelvic second look

Reexploration of the pelvis and pelvic wound following pelvic surgery or trauma to assess for complications, perform second-look evaluation, or revise prior procedures.

In Plain Language

Opening pelvis again to check for problems

Clinical Context

Performed in damage control setting for pack removal, or to evaluate pelvic complications post-op.
