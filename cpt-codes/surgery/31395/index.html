31395 Reconstruct larynx & pharynx

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: laryngopharyngeal reconstruction, post-laryngectomy reconstruction, flap reconstruction larynx

Reconstruction of the larynx and pharynx following total laryngopharyngectomy using flap tissue transfer and reconstruction.

Clinical Context

Reconstructive surgery following laryngopharyngectomy using free or pedicled flaps to restore esophageal continuity and allow swallowing and voice.
