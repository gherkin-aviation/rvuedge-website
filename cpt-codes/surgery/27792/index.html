27792 Treatment of ankle fracture

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: open reduction lateral ankle, lateral malleolus ORIF

Initial open treatment of ankle fracture (lateral malleolus) with direct visualization and fixation.

Clinical Context

Indicated for displaced lateral malleolar fractures requiring open reduction and plate or screw fixation.
