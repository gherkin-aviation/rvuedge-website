25270 Repair forearm tendon/muscle

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: flexor tendon repair, FDP/FDS repair

Repair or reconstruction of flexor tendon(s) in the forearm with restoration of flexor function.

Clinical Context

Performed for flexor digitorum superficialis and profundus injuries. Zone-specific repair protocols guide surgical approach.
