35331 Rechanneling of artery

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: brachial endarterectomy, brachial artery rechanneling

Rechanneling (thrombectomy with endarterectomy) of brachial artery with restoration of forearm and hand perfusion.

Clinical Context

Surgical treatment of brachial artery stenosis/occlusion causing hand claudication or critical limb ischemia. May be combined with subclavian or axillary interventions for more proximal disease.
