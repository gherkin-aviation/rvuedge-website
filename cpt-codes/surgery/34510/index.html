34510 Transposition of vein valve

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: venous valve transposition, valve transfer procedure

Transposition of a venous valve. Surgical relocation of an incompetent valve to a more proximal venous location to restore one-way flow.

Clinical Context

Performed for primary chronic venous insufficiency with valve incompetence. Preserves native valve function by repositioning.
