69905 Remove inner ear

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: labyrinthectomy, inner ear removal, translabyrinthine approach

Surgical removal of the inner ear (labyrinth) through a translabyrinthine or transtympanic approach to treat inner ear disease.

Clinical Context

Used for intractable vertigo from Meniere's disease, vestibular schwannoma access, or suppurative labyrinthitis.
