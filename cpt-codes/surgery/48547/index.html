48547 Duodenal exclusion

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Duodenal exclusion, duodenojejunostomy

Surgical exclusion of the duodenum by bypassing the duodenal-gastric junction while maintaining biliary and pancreatic drainage for treatment of complicated duodenal ulcer disease.

Clinical Context

Rarely performed in current era due to proton pump inhibitors. Historical procedure for complicated duodenal ulcers with gastric outlet obstruction.
