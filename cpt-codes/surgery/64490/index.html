64490 Injection paravert f jnt c/t 1 lev

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: cervical facet joint injection, thoracic facet injection, zygapophysial joint injection, facet joint injection cervical, cervical facet block

Injection of anesthetic and/or steroid medication into the facet joint area in the cervical/thoracic region at a single spinal level.

In Plain Language

neck/upper back joint pain injection; facet joint numbing shot

Clinical Context

Used for facet joint osteoarthritis, facet syndrome, and mechanical neck pain. Provides both diagnostic and therapeutic benefit.
