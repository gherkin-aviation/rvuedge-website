34830 Open aortic tube prosthesis repair

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: aortic tube graft repair, aortic tube replacement, open aortic repair

Open repair of aortic tube graft prosthesis (replacement). Surgical replacement or repair of existing aortic tube graft.

Clinical Context

Used for infected, stenosed, or failed aortic tube grafts. Requires complete surgical exposure and graft replacement.
