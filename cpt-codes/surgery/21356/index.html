21356 Opn treatment dprsd zygomatic arch

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: zygomatic arch elevation, arch reduction

Open treatment of depressed zygomatic arch fracture involving surgical elevation and reduction of the depressed fracture.

Clinical Context

Used for depressed zygomatic arch fractures causing facial deformity or masticatory dysfunction.
