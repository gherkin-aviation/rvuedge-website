15851 Removal suture/staple req anesthesia

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: suture removal, staple removal

Removal of sutures or staples requiring anesthesia due to depth, difficulty, or patient pain tolerance, performed as a separate procedure.

Clinical Context

Performed when simple office removal is not feasible due to location complexity, depth, or patient anxiety. Used for deep fascial closures or complex wound repairs.
