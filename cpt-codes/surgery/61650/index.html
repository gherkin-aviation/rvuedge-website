61650 Evasc prlng admn prescription agnt first

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: intraarterial thrombolysis, intra-arterial tPA

Percutaneous transluminal infusion of thrombolytic or fibrinolytic agent for initial treatment of intracranial arterial occlusion.

Clinical Context

Treatment for acute ischemic stroke. Often used adjunctively with mechanical thrombectomy in endovascular therapy.
