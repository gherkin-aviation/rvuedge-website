24076 Ex arm/elbow tumor deep < 5 centimeters

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: deep tumor excision <5cm, small deep tumor removal, deep lesion removal small

Excision of arm/elbow tumor (deep, less than 5 centimeters). Removal of deep soft tissue tumor from arm or elbow, size less than 5 cm.

Clinical Context

Used for benign or low-grade malignant deep soft tissue tumors of arm/elbow less than 5 cm requiring excision.
