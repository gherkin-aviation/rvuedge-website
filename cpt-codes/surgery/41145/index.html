41145 Tongue removal neck surgery

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: glossectomy with neck dissection, tongue removal with RND, radical glossectomy with neck

Surgical removal of the tongue with comprehensive neck dissection for lymph node metastases.

In Plain Language

tongue and neck lymph node surgery; tongue removal with lymph node removal; comprehensive tongue cancer surgery

Clinical Context

Used for tongue cancer with lymph node involvement requiring both glossectomy and neck dissection. Common with advanced T3-T4 cancers with N1-N3 nodal disease.
