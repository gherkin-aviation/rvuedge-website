15780 Dermabrasion total face

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: full face dermabrasion, complete facial abrasion

Dermabrasion of entire face including forehead, cheeks, nose, lips, and chin. Mechanical abrasion removing superficial skin layers for scar and blemish correction.

Clinical Context

Used for extensive facial scars, severe acne scarring, and facial resurfacing requiring complete facial treatment.
