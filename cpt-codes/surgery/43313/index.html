43313 Esophagoplasty congenital

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: esophageal atresia repair, esophageal stenosis repair, esophagoplasty

Esophagoplasty for repair of congenital esophageal atresia or stenosis with creation of esophageal continuity. This pediatric surgical procedure restores esophageal patency.

Clinical Context

Surgical treatment of congenital esophageal atresia and tracheoesophageal fistula; typically performed in immediate neonatal period.
