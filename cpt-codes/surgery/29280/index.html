29280 Strapping of hand or finger

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: hand strapping, finger taping, digit strapping

Application of strapping or taping to the hand or individual fingers for support and motion limitation.

Clinical Context

Used for finger sprains, thumb injuries, ligament strains, and post-injury stabilization of hand structures.
