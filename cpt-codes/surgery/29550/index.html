29550 Strapping of toes

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: toe strapping, toe taping, digit support

Application of strapping or taping specifically to the toes for support and immobilization.

In Plain Language

toe support tape; toe wrap

Clinical Context

Used for toe fractures, hallux limitus, turf toe, and ligament injuries of the forefoot.
