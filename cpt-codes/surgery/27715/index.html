27715 Ostpl tibfib lngth/shrt

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: tibia-fibula lengthening/shortening, osteoplasty

Osteoplasty of tibia and fibula for lengthening or shortening with reconstruction for limb length or deformity management.

Clinical Context

Used for limb length discrepancy or deformity requiring both bones be cut and adjusted. Complex procedure with highest work RVU in group.
