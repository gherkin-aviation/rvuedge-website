25251 Removal of wrist prosthesis

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: complex prosthesis removal

Removal of a wrist joint prosthesis with more complex dissection or additional reconstruction required.

In Plain Language

removing artificial wrist and fixing damage

Clinical Context

Used when prosthetic removal requires extensive soft tissue or bone work, such as significant bone loss or incorporation.

Billing & Documentation

This code has a 90-day global period, which includes the day of the procedure, 1 day preoperative, and 90 days of postoperative care.
