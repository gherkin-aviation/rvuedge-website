35509 Artery byp graft contral carotid

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: carotid-carotid bypass, CC bypass

Arterial bypass graft from contralateral carotid to ipsilateral carotid artery (carotid-carotid bypass).

In Plain Language

Bypass from opposite side neck artery; Connecting neck arteries on opposite sides

Clinical Context

Extrathoracic revascularization for bilateral carotid artery disease or unilateral occlusion with contralateral stenosis. Offers excellent outcomes with minimal morbidity.
