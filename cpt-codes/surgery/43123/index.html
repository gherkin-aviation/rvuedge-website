43123 Partial removal of esophagus

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: complex esophageal replacement, esophageal replacement with complex reconstruction

Total esophageal replacement via combination of thoracic resection and abdominal reconstruction with complex graft or flap procedure.

Clinical Context

Performed for complex esophageal pathology requiring maximal surgical expertise. May involve free tissue transfer or multiple-stage reconstruction.
