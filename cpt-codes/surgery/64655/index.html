64655 revision/replacement bat mod sys lead

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: neurostimulation lead revision, battery-powered modulator lead replacement

Revision or replacement of the lead components of an implanted battery-powered modulator system for neurostimulation.

Clinical Context

Used when leads malfunction, migrate, or require repositioning in an implanted system.
