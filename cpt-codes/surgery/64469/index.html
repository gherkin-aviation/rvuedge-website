64469 Thrc fascial pln blk bi nfs

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: bilateral thoracic fascial plane continuous infusion, thoracic fascial plane bilateral catheter

Thoracic fascial plane block performed with continuous infusion of anesthetic and/or steroid through catheters on both sides of the chest (bilateral).

In Plain Language

both sides chest continuous pain relief; bilateral prolonged chest numbing

Clinical Context

Used for bilateral thoracic conditions requiring extended anesthetic coverage. Bilateral catheters for 24-72 hours of post-operative anesthesia.
