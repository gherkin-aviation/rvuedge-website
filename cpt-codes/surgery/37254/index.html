37254 Revsc evasc ivt angio sf first

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: infrapopliteal angioplasty simple first, tibial angioplasty, crural angioplasty first

Percutaneous or open revascularization of the infrapopliteal artery (tibial or peroneal) using simple angioplasty in the first treated vessel.

Clinical Context

Indicated for critical limb ischemia with tibial/peroneal stenosis. Simple angioplasty without stent or other adjunctive therapy.
