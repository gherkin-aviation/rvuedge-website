58940 Removal of ovary(s)

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: oophorectomy, ovary removal, bilateral oophorectomy

Total removal of one or both ovaries (oophorectomy or bilateral oophorectomy) via open abdominal approach.

Clinical Context

Indicated for ovarian malignancy, endometriosis with severe symptoms, recurrent benign pathology, or elective removal in premenopausal women undergoing hysterectomy.
