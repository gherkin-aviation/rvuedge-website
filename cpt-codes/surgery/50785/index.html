50785 Reimplant ureter in bladder

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ureteral reimplantation, ureteroneocystostomy, bladder flap ureteral implant

Ureteroneocystostomy (ureteral reimplantation) with full bladder mobilization or flap creation, using advanced techniques for complex anatomic reconstruction.

In Plain Language

surgically reconnecting ureter to bladder with bladder flap

Clinical Context

Indicated for cases requiring flap creation due to distance or anatomy, including psoas hitch or boari flap procedures. Highest RVU of reimplantation codes.
