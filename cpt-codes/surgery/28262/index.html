28262 Revision of foot and ankle

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: foot and ankle revision, extensive foot reconstruction revision, comprehensive foot re-operation

Revision of multiple foot and ankle structures (tendon, bone, joint). This extensive procedure addresses multiple foot and ankle pathology requiring comprehensive reconstruction.

Clinical Context

Performed for Charcot foot, severe clubfoot residuals, or complex post-traumatic deformity requiring multiple simultaneous corrections. Major surgery with highest complexity.
