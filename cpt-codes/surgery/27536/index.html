27536 Treat knee fracture

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: complex tibial plateau ORIF, tibial plateau with graft

Open reduction and internal fixation of a complex or comminuted tibial plateau fracture with plate and screw fixation and bone grafting for support.

Clinical Context

Used for severe comminuted tibial plateau fractures with bone loss requiring plate fixation and bone graft.
