36580 Replace cvad catheter

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: IVC catheter replacement, inferior vena cava replacement, catheter replacement

Replacement of tunneled or non-tunneled central venous catheter; in inferior vena cava.

Clinical Context

Used for replacement of non-functioning IVC catheters in patients with superior vena cava obstruction or thrombosis.
