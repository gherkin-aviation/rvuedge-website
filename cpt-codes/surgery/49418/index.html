49418 Insert tun inpatient catheter percutaneous

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: peritoneal dialysis catheter, percutaneous IP catheter, tunneled peritoneal cath

Insertion of tunneled intraperitoneal catheter via percutaneous approach without subcutaneous port component.

Clinical Context

Percutaneous placement of tunneled intraperitoneal catheter for peritoneal dialysis. Used in chronic kidney disease patients requiring dialysis therapy.
