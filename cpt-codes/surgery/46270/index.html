46270 Remove anal fist subcutaneous

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: subcutaneous fistula excision, low anal fistula removal, fistulotomy

Surgical removal of a subcutaneous anal fistula. This is a fistula tract located in the subcutaneous tissue superficial to the sphincter complex.

In Plain Language

removing a tunnel between the anal canal and skin; surgery to close an anal tunnel

Clinical Context

Used for simple subcutaneous anal fistulas (low fistulas) with primary tract only. Has low recurrence and continence preservation rates.
