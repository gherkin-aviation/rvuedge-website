54408 Repair multi-comp penis pros

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: prosthesis repair, IPP repair

Surgical repair or adjustment of a previously implanted multi-component penile prosthesis to restore function.

Clinical Context

Performed when penile prosthesis develops mechanical failure, leak, or pump malfunction amenable to repair.
