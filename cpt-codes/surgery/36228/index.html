36228 Place catheter intracranial artery

Surgery

Also known as: intracranial catheter, cerebral vessel catheterization, neuro catheterization

Percutaneous placement of catheter into intracranial artery for selective angiography or neurointerventional procedure. Intracranial vascular access.

Clinical Context

Used for intracranial aneurysm evaluation/treatment, arteriovenous malformation intervention, or cerebral angiography.
