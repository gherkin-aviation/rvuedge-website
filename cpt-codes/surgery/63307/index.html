63307 Remov vertebral idrl bdy lumbar/sacral

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: lumbar posterolateral vertebrectomy, sacral posterolateral vertebrectomy

Removal of vertebral body with posterolateral approach in the lumbar or sacral region. This procedure uses a posterolateral access for lumbar or sacral vertebrectomy.

In Plain Language

side and back approach removal of lower back or sacral vertebral bone
