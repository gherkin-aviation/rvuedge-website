51700 Irrigation of bladder

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: bladder irrigation, bladder lavage, CBI

Irrigation of bladder, mechanical cleansing or therapeutic irrigation of bladder to remove debris, blood clots, or sediment.

Clinical Context

Therapeutic procedure for hematuria, blood clot evacuation, or post-operative bladder cleaning. Typically minor procedure.
