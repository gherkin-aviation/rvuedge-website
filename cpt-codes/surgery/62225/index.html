62225 Replace/irrigate catheter

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: subarachnoid catheter replacement, spinal drain replacement, drain irrigation

Replacement or irrigation of ventricular drain or catheter via lumbar, cisternal, or cervical access. Includes removal and new placement.

Clinical Context

Used for replacement of malfunctioning, infected, or occluded subarachnoid drains or catheters.
