35189 repair acq av fistula thrx & abdominal

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: acquired AV fistula thorax/abdomen, post-traumatic visceral AVF

Repair of acquired arteriovenous fistula in the thorax and abdomen with restoration of vascular integrity.

Clinical Context

Surgical intervention for acquired arteriovenous fistulas from blunt/penetrating trauma, vascular surgery complications, or inflammatory conditions. Management depends on involved vessels and presence of intervening organs; may require staged intervention.
