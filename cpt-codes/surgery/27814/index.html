27814 Treatment of ankle fracture

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: open reduction bimalleolar ankle, bimalleolar ankle ORIF

Initial open treatment of ankle fracture (bimalleolar) with direct visualization and internal fixation.

Clinical Context

Indicated for displaced bimalleolar ankle fractures requiring open reduction and stable fixation with plates and screws.
