31652 Bronchial ebus samplng 1/2 node

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: EBUS-TBNA, endobronchial ultrasound, guided needle aspiration

Bronchoscopy with endobronchial ultrasound-guided transbronchial needle aspiration (EBUS-TBNA) of one to two lymph nodes or lesions for tissue diagnosis.

Clinical Context

Performed for mediastinal and hilar lymph node evaluation in lung cancer staging, sarcoidosis, or infection workup with high sensitivity and specificity.
