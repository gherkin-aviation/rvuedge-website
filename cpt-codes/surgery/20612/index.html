20612 Aspirate/injection ganglion cyst

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: ganglion cyst aspiration, ganglion injection

Aspiration or injection of a ganglion cyst.

Clinical Context

Used to aspirate fluid from or inject therapeutic agents into ganglion cysts, which are benign fluid-filled tumors typically found on wrists or hands.
