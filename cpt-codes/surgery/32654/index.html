32654 Thoracoscopy contrl bleeding

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: VATS hemostasis, thoracoscopic bleeding control

Thoracoscopic control of hemorrhage from pleural surface or intrathoracic structures via hemostatic techniques including cautery or hemostatic agents.

Clinical Context

Used for post-operative bleeding, bleeding from visceral pleura, or hemothorax when thoracoscopic approach allows visualization and hemorrhage control.
