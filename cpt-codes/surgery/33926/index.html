33926 Repair pul artery unifocal with cpb

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Unifocal PA repair with CPB, single PA branch repair on pump, PA plasty with bypass

Repair of pulmonary artery unifocal branch stenosis with cardiopulmonary bypass; surgical reconstruction of a single pulmonary artery branch stenosis with extracorporeal circulation support.

In Plain Language

Surgery to widen lung artery with heart-lung machine; Single pulmonary artery narrowing repair with bypass

Clinical Context

Repair of significant unifocal pulmonary artery branch stenosis requiring cardiopulmonary bypass for adequate visualization, hemodynamic stability, and complete repair.
