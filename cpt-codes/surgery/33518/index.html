33518 Cabg artery-vein two

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: dual arterial-vein CABG, two arterial one vein bypass, mixed conduit CABG

Surgical placement of two arterial and one venous coronary artery bypass graft, combining multiple arterial conduits with venous backup.

In Plain Language

two artery grafts and one vein graft

Clinical Context

Employed when multiple arterial grafts are strategically used with venous supplementation; provides excellent long-term patency with versatile revascularization options.
