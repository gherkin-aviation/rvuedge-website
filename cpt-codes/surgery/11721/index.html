11721 Debride nail 6 or more

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: nail debridement multiple, nail cleaning

Debridement of nail(s) (6 or more nails). Removal of thickened, dystrophic, or abnormal nail tissue without complete nail removal for multiple nails.

Clinical Context

Used for removal of fungal-infected or thickened nails on multiple digits when conservative treatment is appropriate.
