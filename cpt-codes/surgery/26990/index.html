26990 Drainage of pelvis lesion

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: hip abscess drainage, pelvic drainage, incision and drainage hip

Surgical incision and drainage of deep abscess or fluid collection in the pelvis or hip region with appropriate tissue handling.

In Plain Language

draining infection from hip area; treating hip infection

Clinical Context

Performed for hip joint infection, psoas abscess, or deep pelvic infections requiring drainage to eliminate purulent material and control infection.
