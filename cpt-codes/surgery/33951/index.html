33951 Ecmo/ecls insj prph cannula

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: ECMO peripheral cannulation, ECLS peripheral insertion, peripheral ECMO access

Extracorporeal membrane oxygenation/extracorporeal life support insertion of peripheral venous and/or arterial cannula; percutaneous or open insertion of peripheral cannulae for ECMO access.

In Plain Language

Placement of life support tubing in arm or leg vein; Peripheral heart-lung machine connection

Clinical Context

Percutaneous or open insertion of peripheral (femoral or other) venous and/or arterial cannulae for ECMO support establishment in patients requiring extracorporeal circulation.
