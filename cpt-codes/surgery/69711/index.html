69711 Remove/repair hearing aid

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: OI implant removal, hearing implant removal, implant repair

Removal or repair of an osseointegrated bone conduction hearing aid device or percutaneous component.

Clinical Context

Used for implant malfunction, patient dissatisfaction, infection, implant failure, or device replacement.
