20692 application mltpln uni external fixation sys

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: multiplanar fixator, delta-frame external fixation

Application of a multiplanar unilateral external fixation system.

Clinical Context

Applied for complex fractures with multi-planar deformity or instability; the multiplanar design provides enhanced stability.
