63300 Remove vertebral xdrl body crvcl

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: cervical anterior vertebrectomy, anterior cervical corpectomy

Removal of vertebral body with anterior approach in the cervical region. This procedure involves anterior vertebrectomy for decompression of the cervical spinal cord.

Clinical Context

Used for cervical pathology requiring anterior decompression including OPLL, cervical spondylotic myelopathy, or vertebral body pathology.
