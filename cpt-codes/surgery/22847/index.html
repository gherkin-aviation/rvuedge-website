22847 Insert spine fixation device

Surgery

Also known as: Anterior plate fixation, Anterior instrumentation

Anterior segmental instrumentation (eg, plate, rod, screw); 1-2 segments.

Clinical Context

Anterior segmental fixation for cervical or lumbar instability; provides direct load sharing and rigid stabilization.
