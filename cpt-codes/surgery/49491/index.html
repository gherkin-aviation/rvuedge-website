49491 repair hern preemie reduc

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: premature infant hernia repair, preemie hernia repair, infant inguinal repair

Repair of inguinal hernia in premature infant with primary closure or small mesh patch.

Clinical Context

Surgical repair of inguinal hernia in premature infants. Common finding in prematurity requiring operative intervention to prevent incarceration.
