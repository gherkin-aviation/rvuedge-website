57000 Colpotomy with exploration

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: Vaginal exploration, Colpotomy

Colpotomy with exploration of vaginal cavity. Surgical incision into the vagina for exploration and evaluation.

Clinical Context

Used to explore vaginal pathology, remove foreign bodies, or evaluate for abnormalities. Provides direct access to pelvic structures.
