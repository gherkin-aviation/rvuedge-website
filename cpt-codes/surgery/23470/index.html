23470 Reconstruct shoulder joint

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: shoulder arthrodesis, shoulder joint reconstruction, shoulder fusion

Reconstruction of shoulder joint (arthrodesis or total joint replacement). Surgical fusion of glenohumeral joint or prosthetic replacement for severe arthropathy.

Clinical Context

Performed for advanced glenohumeral arthritis, failed arthroplasty, or severe joint destruction; choice between arthrodesis and arthroplasty based on patient factors and pathology.
