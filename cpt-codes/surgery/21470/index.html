21470 Optx complicated mndblr fracture

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: complex mandible fracture ORIF, complicated jaw fracture surgery

Open treatment of complicated mandibular fracture. Surgical reduction and fixation of complex mandibular fractures involving multiple fracture lines, severe comminution, or associated injuries requiring advanced reconstruction.

In Plain Language

major lower jaw fracture surgery; complex jaw reconstruction

Clinical Context

Used for highly complex mandibular injuries including those with multiple fracture sites, severe bone loss, severe soft tissue trauma, or associated injuries. Represents highest complexity mandibular fracture requiring advanced technique.
