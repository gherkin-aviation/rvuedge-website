43756 Diagnosis duod intub with asp specimen

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: diagnostic duodenal intubation, duodenal aspiration, duodenal sampling

Diagnostic duodenal intubation with aspiration and specimen collection for assessment of duodenal function or pathology.

Clinical Context

Used for diagnostic evaluation of duodenal secretions, culture, or assessment of duodenal transit. May assess for bacterial overgrowth or evaluate duodenal dysfunction.
