27071 Part removal hip bone deep

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: deep hip bone resection, deep pelvic bone removal, extensive hip bone resection

Surgical partial removal of hip bone with deep extent requiring extensive soft tissue dissection.

Clinical Context

Indicated for deep hip bone pathology requiring partial resection with careful preservation of surrounding neurovascular structures.
