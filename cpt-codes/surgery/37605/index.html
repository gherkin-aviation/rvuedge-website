37605 Ligation int/com carotid artery

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ICA ligation, CCA ligation, Internal carotid tie

Surgical ligation of the internal or common carotid artery without occlusion, used for major hemorrhage control or vascular lesion management.

In Plain Language

Tying main neck artery; Carotid vessel surgery

Clinical Context

Used in severe head and neck trauma with uncontrollable hemorrhage, large arteriovenous fistulas, or as treatment for giant intracranial aneurysms.
