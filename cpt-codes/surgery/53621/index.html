53621 Dilate urethra stricture

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: electrocautery urethrotomy, transurethral urethrotomy

Endoscopic dilation of a urethral stricture using urethrotomy with electrocautery to control bleeding and tissue vaporization.

Clinical Context

Used for strictures with significant bleeding risk. Electrocautery provides hemostasis while cutting tissue.
