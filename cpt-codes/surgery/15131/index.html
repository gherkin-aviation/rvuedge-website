15131 Drm agrft t/a/l each additional

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: dermal graft additional, dermal add-on

Dermal autograft applied to trunk, arms, or legs, each additional 100 square centimeters beyond the first. Add-on code for extended dermal coverage.

Clinical Context

Used for large surface area burns or trauma requiring extended dermal replacement beyond initial 100 square centimeter coverage.
