23530 Optx strnclav dislc aqt/chrn

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ORIF SC dislocation, open SC dislocation repair, surgical SC reduction

Open treatment of sternoclavicular dislocation (acute or chronic). Open reduction internal fixation of acute or chronic SC dislocation via direct visualization.

Clinical Context

Performed for acute irreducible SC dislocations, chronic recurrent dislocations, or posterior dislocations with compression symptoms requiring surgical stabilization.
