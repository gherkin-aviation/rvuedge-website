68325 Revise/graft eyelid lining

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: conjunctival reconstruction, advanced conjunctival graft

Revision and grafting of conjunctival tissue with advancement or reconstruction for complex ocular surface disease.

Clinical Context

Used for complex conjunctival reconstruction. Common indications include severe ocular surface disease, extensive conjunctival scarring, symblepharon, or severe chemical injuries.
