11960 Insert tissue expander(s)

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Tissue expander placement

Insertion of one or more tissue expanders, devices placed beneath the skin to gradually stretch tissue for later reconstruction purposes.

Clinical Context

Used in reconstructive surgery to create extra skin and soft tissue for coverage in cases of large burns, traumatic defects, or post-mastectomy reconstruction.
