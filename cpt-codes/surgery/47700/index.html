47700 Exploration of bile ducts

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Bile duct exploration, choledochotomy, intraoperative exploration

Surgical exploration of the common bile duct (choledochotomy) to visualize anatomy, remove stones, or assess for pathology during abdominal surgery.

Clinical Context

Performed to confirm bile duct patency, remove stones missed preoperatively, or assess for strictures. Allows direct visualization and intervention.
