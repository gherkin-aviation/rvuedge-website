67917 Repair eyelid defect

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: complex eyelid repair, eyelid reconstruction

Complex repair of eyelid defect not involving the margin using staged reconstruction or advanced flap technique.

Clinical Context

Used for large or complex eyelid defects away from the margin requiring advanced reconstruction. Common indications include extensive post-operative defects, large burns, complex traumatic injuries, or tumors of the eyelid body.
