51960 Revision of bladder & bowel

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: bladder-bowel revision, ileal conduit revision, urinary diversion revision

Surgical revision of both bladder and bowel, typically including creation of a new urinary diversion or reconstruction of a previously created ileal conduit or continent reservoir.

In Plain Language

fixing a prior bladder-bowel surgery; revising a urine bypass

Clinical Context

Used for revision of failed urinary diversions, strictures of ureteral anastomoses, or complications from prior continent pouch surgery. Requires advanced reconstructive skills.
