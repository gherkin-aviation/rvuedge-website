26030 Drainage of palm bursas

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: I&D multiple palm bursas, multi-space palm drainage

Drainage (incision and drainage) of multiple palm bursas of the hand.

In Plain Language

draining multiple infected palm pockets; extensive palm drainage

Clinical Context

Used for complex hand infections involving multiple palm spaces requiring drainage of multiple compartments.
