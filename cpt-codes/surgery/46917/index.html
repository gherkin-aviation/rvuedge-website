46917 Laser surgery anal lesions

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: laser destruction, laser ablation, CO2 laser surgery

Destruction of anal lesions using laser energy. Laser beam ablates anal canal pathology.

Clinical Context

Used for warts, condylomas, dysplastic lesions, and small benign tumors. Provides good hemostasis and precision.
