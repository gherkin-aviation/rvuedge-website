47612 Removal of gallbladder

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Open chole with choledochojejunostomy, jejunal anastomosis

Open cholecystectomy with choledochojejunostomy, creating a permanent internal anastomosis between the bile duct and jejunum for complex biliary obstruction.

Clinical Context

Performed for choledochal cysts, hilar cholangiocarcinoma, or strictures requiring jejunal limb for better drainage and prevention of reflux.
