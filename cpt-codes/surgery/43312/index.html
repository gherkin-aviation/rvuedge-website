43312 Repair esophagus and fistula

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: complex esophageal repair, esophageal repair with anastomosis, fistula repair with reconstruction

Repair of esophageal laceration with esophagogastric or esophagocolonic anastomosis and fistula repair. This major surgical procedure addresses complex esophageal defects.

Clinical Context

Complex reconstruction for severe perforations with fistulization; often requires additional flap procedures and intensive perioperative care.
