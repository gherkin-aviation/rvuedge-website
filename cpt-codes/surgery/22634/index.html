Surgery +Add-on

Arthrodesis, posterior or posterolateral combined with anterior/anterolateral approach, lumbar; each additional interspaces.

In Plain Language

Extra spine fusion from both sides; Additional circumferential fusion

Clinical Context

Add-on code for multi-level combined anterior-posterior lumbar fusion; used when extensive fusion needed for complex deformity or instability.
