19294 Prepj tumor cav iort prtl mast

Surgery

Also known as: IORT closure, cavity closure

Preparation and closure of the tumor cavity following intraoperative radiation therapy or accelerated partial breast irradiation after partial mastectomy. This involves visualization and closure of the surgical defect.

Clinical Context

Used following breast-conserving surgery with intraoperative radiation to ensure proper hemostasis and cosmetic result. Performed after radiation therapy has been delivered to reduce recurrence risk.
