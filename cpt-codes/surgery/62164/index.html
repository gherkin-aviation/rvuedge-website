62164 Remove brain tumor with scope

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: endoscopic tumor removal, neuroendoscopic resection

Neuroendoscopic removal of brain tumor. Includes visualization, dissection, and removal of neoplastic tissue via endoscopic approach.

Clinical Context

Used for endoscopic resection of accessible brain tumors providing visualization with reduced morbidity compared to open craniotomy.
