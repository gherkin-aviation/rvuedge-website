28340 Resection enlarged toe tissue

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: exostosis removal, bunion exostosis resection, bone spur removal

Surgical resection of enlarged soft tissue and bone around the great toe joint, typically addressing bunion exostosis and associated soft tissue prominence for cosmetic and functional improvement.

Clinical Context

Used for painful bunion exostosis or prominent bone creating pressure, pain, or skin irritation; resection improves footwear fit and pain.
