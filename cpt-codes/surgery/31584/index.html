31584 Laryngoplasty fracture rdctj fixation

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: laryngoplasty with fixation, laryngeal reduction and fixation, laryngeal reduction

Laryngoplasty for treatment of laryngeal stenosis or airway obstruction with reduction, fixation, or manipulation of laryngeal structures to restore airway patency.

Clinical Context

Surgical reconstruction for laryngeal stenosis involving repositioning and fixation of laryngeal cartilage and soft tissues to enlarge airway while preserving voice function.
