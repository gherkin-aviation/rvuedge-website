27488 Removal of knee prosthesis

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: prosthesis removal, TKA removal, component removal

Surgical removal of a knee prosthesis or prosthetic components without immediate replacement, typically for infection, loosening, or spacer placement.

In Plain Language

removing a knee implant

Clinical Context

Used for infected prostheses (often staged removal before reimplantation), severe loosening, or temporary spacer placement.
