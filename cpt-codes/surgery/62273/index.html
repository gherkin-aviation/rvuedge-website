62273 Inject epidural patch

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: epidural blood patch, autologous blood patch, blood patch

Epidural injection of blood or autologous blood-derived material for sealing cerebrospinal fluid leak (epidural blood patch).

In Plain Language

spinal fluid leak sealing treatment; epidural patch procedure

Clinical Context

Used for treatment of post-dural puncture headache or cerebrospinal fluid leak via epidural blood patch.
