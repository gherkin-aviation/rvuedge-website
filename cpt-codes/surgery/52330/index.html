52330 Cystoscopy and treatment

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: cysto with lesion fulguration

Cystourethroscopy with fulguration or cauterization of bladder wall lesions including angiomas, cysts, or other benign mucosal pathology.

Clinical Context

Used for bleeding bladder hemangiomas, small cysts, or benign lesions causing symptoms. Reduces need for open surgical intervention.
