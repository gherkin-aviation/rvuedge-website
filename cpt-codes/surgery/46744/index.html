46744 Repair of cloacal anomaly

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: cloacal repair, cloacal reconstruction, primary cloacal repair

Repair of cloacal anomaly as primary procedure. Reconstructs anatomy in rare developmental abnormality with common channel.

Clinical Context

Performed for cloacal anomalies (common cloaca with single perineal opening). High-risk surgery with risk to urinary/fecal/reproductive function.
