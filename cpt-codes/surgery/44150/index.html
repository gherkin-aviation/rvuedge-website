44150 Removal of colon

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: total colectomy with permanent ileostomy, procto-colectomy with ileostomy, permanent ostomy, total abdominal colectomy

Total removal of colon and rectum via open approach with creation of permanent ileostomy.

In Plain Language

complete colon removal with external pouch; permanent bowel opening

Clinical Context

Complete colectomy and proctectomy with permanent ileostomy creation. Used for extensive disease, familial polyposis, or when sphincter preservation not possible.
