Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: trabeculectomy with iridectomy, combined filtering surgery

Trabeculectomy ab externo; with iridectomy.

Clinical Context

Trabeculectomy combined with iridectomy for angle-closure glaucoma or to prevent pupillary block.
