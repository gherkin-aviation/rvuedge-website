22585 Arthrd anterior ntrbd minutes dsc each

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: Anterior fusion with bone graft add-on, Additional mineralized bone fusion

Arthrodesis, anterior/anterolateral approach, including discectomy and/or corpectomy, with mineralized bone void filler/bone matrix; each additional interspace.

Clinical Context

Add-on code for multi-level anterior fusion procedures with bone graft material; extends fusion construct across multiple vertebral segments.
