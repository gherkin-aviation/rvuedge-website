31540 Laryngoscopy with excision of tumor

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: laryngeal tumor removal, laryngeal cancer resection, laryngeal tumor excision

Laryngoscopic removal of a tumor or malignant lesion from the larynx using direct visualization with a laryngoscope for complete resection and pathologic assessment.

In Plain Language

removing a tumor from voice box; cancer removal from larynx

Clinical Context

Performed for laryngeal malignancies, including squamous cell carcinoma, preserving voice quality when feasible. May be curative for early-stage disease or palliative for advanced disease.
