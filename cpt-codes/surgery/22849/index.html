22849 Reinsert spinal fixation

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Reinsert hardware, Hardware replacement

Reinsertion of spinal fixation device.

In Plain Language

Replacement of spinal fixation; Putting back spinal hardware

Clinical Context

Used when previously implanted spinal hardware is removed and replaced; can be related to infection, failure, or repositioning.
