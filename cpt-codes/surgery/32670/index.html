32670 Thoracoscopy bilobectomy

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: VATS bilobectomy, thoracoscopic two-lobe removal

Thoracoscopic removal of two lung lobes (bilobectomy) performed via minimally invasive approach for disease involving multiple lobes.

Clinical Context

Used for lung cancer involving two adjacent lobes or extensive disease when VATS approach allows adequate exposure for safe bilobectomy.
