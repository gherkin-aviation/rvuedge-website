36590 Removal tunneled cv catheter

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: catheter removal, line removal, central venous catheter removal

Removal of tunneled central venous catheter, without insertion of replacement.

Clinical Context

Used for removal of tunneled central catheters when no replacement needed. May be due to infection, thrombosis, or end of therapy.
