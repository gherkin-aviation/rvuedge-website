48001 Placement of drainage pancreas

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Pancreatic pseudocyst drainage, pancreatic cyst drainage

Surgical drainage of a pancreatic pseudocyst or fluid collection through an abdominal incision with placement of a drainage catheter for external drainage.

Clinical Context

Performed for symptomatic pancreatic pseudocysts causing pain, obstruction, or infection. Provides rapid decompression in acute setting.
