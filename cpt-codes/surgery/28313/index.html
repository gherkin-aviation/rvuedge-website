28313 Repair deformity of toe

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: hammertoe repair, claw toe correction, toe deformity repair

Surgical repair of toe deformity such as hammertoe, claw toe, mallet toe, or other digital contracture, including soft tissue and bony realignment to restore functional toe position.

Clinical Context

Used for symptomatic hammertoe, claw toe, or mallet toe causing pain, callus formation, or cosmetic concern; surgery restores functional digital position.
