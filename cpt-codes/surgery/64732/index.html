64732 Incision of brow nerve

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: supraorbital nerve incision, brow nerve decompression

Surgical incision and exploration of the supraorbital nerve in the brow region. Used to treat compression or entrapment of this sensory branch of the trigeminal nerve.

In Plain Language

forehead nerve surgery; brow nerve release

Clinical Context

Indicated for supraorbital neuralgia or entrapment causing forehead and brow pain and headaches. Surgical decompression provides pain relief.
