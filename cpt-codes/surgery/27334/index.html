27334 Remove knee joint lining

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: synovectomy knee, synovial removal

Removal of knee joint synovial lining through arthrotomy to treat chronic synovitis or inflammatory conditions.

Clinical Context

Applied to chronic inflammatory conditions such as rheumatoid arthritis or pigmented villonodular synovitis.
