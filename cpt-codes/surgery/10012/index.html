10012 Fna biopsy with mr gdn each additional

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: FNA with MRI guidance additional, MRI-guided FNA add-on

Fine needle aspiration biopsy with magnetic resonance imaging guidance of each additional lesion. Multiple-target sampling during a single MRI session.

Clinical Context

Applied for multiple lesions visualized on MRI requiring tissue diagnosis, common in evaluation of multifocal soft tissue or osseous abnormalities.
