20664 application halo cranial 6+pins

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: multi-pin halo, reinforced halo

Application of a cranial halo device with six or more pins for secure cervical spine fixation.

In Plain Language

extra-strong halo brace with many skull pins; reinforced cervical immobilization

Clinical Context

Used for high-impact cervical injuries requiring maximum pin security and immobilization; additional pins increase stability compared to standard four-pin halo.
