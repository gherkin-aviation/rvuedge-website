64416 Njx aa and/or strd brch pl nfs img

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: brachial plexus block with branches, upper arm nerve block

Injection of anesthetic and/or steroid into brachial plexus and/or nerves of upper arm with imaging guidance. This procedure involves injecting medication to the brachial plexus or its branches with imaging.

Clinical Context

Used for more targeted analgesia when specific peripheral branches need medication delivery in addition to main plexus.
