43259 Egd ultrasound examination duodenum/jejunum

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: EGD EUS, endoscopic ultrasound, EUS duodenum, EGD with ultrasound, endoscopic ultrasound upper GI

Esophagogastroduodenoscopy with endoscopic ultrasound examination of the duodenum and proximal jejunum. This diagnostic technique provides high-resolution sonographic imaging.

Clinical Context

Diagnostic modality for staging pancreatic and biliary cancers, evaluating submucosal lesions, and assessing chronic pancreatitis; superior to conventional ultrasound.
