50783 Reimplant ureter in bladder

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ureteral reimplantation, ureteroneocystostomy, ureteral implant with flap

Ureteroneocystostomy (ureteral reimplantation) with extensive dissection or tissue flap creation, providing more secure anastomosis and reflux prevention.

Clinical Context

Performed for complex cases including vesicoureteral reflux, stricture recurrence, or anatomy requiring flap creation (psoas hitch or boari flap).
