11303 Shave skin lesion >2.0 centimeters

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: shave removal large, shave excision

Shave excision of skin lesion greater than 2.0 centimeters in diameter. The lesion is removed by horizontal shaving technique.

Clinical Context

Used for removal of larger benign skin lesions when shaving approach is appropriate.
