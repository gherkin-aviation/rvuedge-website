Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: eyelid lesion removal, eyelid mass excision, eyelid biopsy

Excision or removal of a single lesion from eyelid including nevus, cyst, lipoma, or other benign tumor with complete closure by primary intention.

In Plain Language

Clinical Context

Treatment of benign eyelid lesions; excision biopsy useful for diagnostic confirmation and complete removal.
