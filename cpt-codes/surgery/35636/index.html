35636 Bpg splenorenal

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: splenorenal bypass, splenic-renal bypass, renal salvage

Arterial bypass graft from splenic artery to renal artery for renal artery stenosis with renal insufficiency. Restores renal perfusion using splenic artery as inflow source.

Clinical Context

Performed for renal artery stenosis when aorta and iliac arteries are diseased or unavailable. Preserves renal function and manages hypertension.
