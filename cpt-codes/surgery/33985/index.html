33985 Ecmo/ecls removal ctr cannula

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: ECMO central removal, central ECLS removal, central cannula removal

Extracorporeal membrane oxygenation/extracorporeal life support removal of central venous/arterial cannula; removal of centrally placed ECMO cannulae via sternotomy or thoracotomy.

Clinical Context

Removal of central ECMO cannulae at time of ECMO weaning and discontinuation of extracorporeal support.
