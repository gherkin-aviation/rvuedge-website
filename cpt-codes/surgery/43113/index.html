43113 Removal of esophagus

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: esophagectomy with thoracoabdominal approach, thoracoabdominal esophagectomy

Esophagectomy (total removal of the esophagus) with abdominal and thoracic approaches for maximum access and lymphadenectomy.

In Plain Language

removal of esophagus through chest and abdomen

Clinical Context

Performed for esophageal cancer requiring optimal exposure, lymph node dissection, and reconstruction. Highest morbidity approach but best for advanced disease.
