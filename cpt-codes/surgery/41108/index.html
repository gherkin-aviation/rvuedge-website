41108 Biopsy of floor of mouth

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: floor of mouth biopsy, sublingual biopsy, oral floor biopsy

Surgical biopsy of the floor of the mouth including tissue sampling for histopathologic evaluation.

In Plain Language

tissue sample from under tongue; testing mouth floor lesion; biopsy of mouth bottom

Clinical Context

Used for evaluation of floor of mouth lesions, masses, or suspicious areas. Important for cancer screening in high-risk sites and evaluation of swelling.
