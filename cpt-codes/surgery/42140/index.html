42140 Excision of uvula

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: uvula removal, uvulectomy

Surgical removal of the uvula, the small tissue mass that hangs from the soft palate. May be performed for obstructive sleep apnea, chronic infection, or other pathology.

In Plain Language

removal of tissue hanging from soft palate; uvula surgery

Clinical Context

Performed for obstructive sleep apnea, chronic tonsilitis, recurrent infection, or obstructive lesions. Often combined with other pharyngeal procedures.
