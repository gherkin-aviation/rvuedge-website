26140 Revise finger joint each

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: finger synovectomy with arthrotomy, joint excision synovectomy

Synovectomy (removal of synovial lining) of finger joint with arthrotomy, each joint.

Clinical Context

Used for synovectomy of finger joints performed with arthrotomy approach, often in inflammatory arthropathies.
