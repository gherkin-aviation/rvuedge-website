31637 Bronchoscopy stent add-on

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: additional stent placement, supplemental bronchial stent

Add-on code for each additional bronchial stent placed beyond the initial stent during the same bronchoscopy procedure for multi-level airway obstruction.

In Plain Language

placing extra tubes in airways

Clinical Context

Used when multiple stents are needed in different airway segments to address multi-level obstruction.
