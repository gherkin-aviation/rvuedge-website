34832 Open aortofemor prosthesis repair

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: aortofemoral graft repair, aortofemoral tube replacement

Open repair of aortofemoral tube graft prosthesis. Surgical replacement or repair of aortofemoral tube graft.

Clinical Context

Used for infected, stenosed, or failed aortofemoral tube grafts. Extensive surgical procedure with multiple incisions.
