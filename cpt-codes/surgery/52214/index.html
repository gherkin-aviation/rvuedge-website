52214 Cystoscopy and treatment

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: cysto with tumor fulguration, cysto with bladder tumor ablation

Cystourethroscopy with fulguration (cauterization) of bladder tumors, typically small low-grade transitional cell carcinomas, using electrocautery through the scope.

Clinical Context

Treatment for non-muscle invasive bladder cancer and small benign bladder lesions. Lowest risk approach for low-grade Ta/T1 tumors.
