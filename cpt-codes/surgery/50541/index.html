50541 Laparoscopic ablate renal cyst

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: lap cyst ablation, laparoscopic marsupialization, minimally invasive cyst treatment

Laparoscopic ablation of a renal cyst using thermal, chemical, or mechanical techniques for symptomatic cysts.

Clinical Context

Used for symptomatic simple renal cysts causing pain or obstruction. Laparoscopic approach reduces morbidity compared to open approach.
