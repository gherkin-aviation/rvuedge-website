52346 Cystouretero with renal strict

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: cysto with comprehensive ureteral stricture treatment

Cystoureteroendoscopy with treatment of renal pelvic stricture combined with other ureteral stricture management to relieve high-grade upper urinary tract obstruction.

Clinical Context

Complex reconstruction requiring combined antegrade and retrograde approaches. Success dependent on stricture location and extent.
