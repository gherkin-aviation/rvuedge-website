37252 Intrvasc ultrasound noncoronary first

Surgery

Also known as: intravascular ultrasound first, IVUS non-coronary first

Intravascular ultrasound imaging of non-coronary vasculature (peripheral arteries, veins, or grafts) performed in the first vessel studied.

Clinical Context

Provides high-resolution imaging of vessel wall, plaque morphology, and true lumen in peripheral interventions. First vessel examined.
