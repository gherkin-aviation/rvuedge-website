43197 Esophagoscopy flex diagnosis brush

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: flexible esophagoscopy, esophagoscopy with brush

Flexible esophagoscopy (examination of esophagus with flexible scope) via transoral approach with diagnostic brush and cell collection.

Clinical Context

Diagnostic procedure for esophageal evaluation with tissue sampling. Allows brush biopsy for cytology without full tissue biopsy.
