43632 Removal of stomach partial

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: proximal gastrectomy, total or subtotal gastrectomy proximal, proximal gastric resection

Partial gastrectomy with proximal gastric resection when antrum preserved and creation of appropriate anastomosis. This removes proximal stomach.

In Plain Language

removing upper part of stomach; proximal gastric resection

Clinical Context

Treatment for proximal gastric cancer; preserves antrum for improved gastric emptying compared to total gastrectomy.
