64726 Dcmprn plantar digital nerve

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: plantar digital nerve decompression, Morton neuroma release

Surgical decompression of the plantar digital nerve in the foot. Relieves compression or entrapment to restore sensory function and eliminate pain.

Clinical Context

Indicated for plantar digital nerve entrapment or Morton neuroma causing metatarsalgia or pain between toes. Surgical decompression provides relief when conservative management fails.
