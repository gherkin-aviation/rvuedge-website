37291 Revsc evsc tpvt athr complex each

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: tibioperoneal atherectomy complex additional, additional TPT complex debulking

Percutaneous or open revascularization of the tibioperoneal trunk or artery using atherectomy with complex technique in each additional vessel.

Clinical Context

Each additional tibioperoneal vessel with complex atherectomy. Substantially increases procedural complexity.
