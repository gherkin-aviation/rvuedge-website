66920 Extraction of lens

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: intracapsular cataract extraction, ICCE, mature cataract extraction

Removal of crystalline lens (intracapsular cataract extraction with or without IOL implantation).

Clinical Context

Intracapsular cataract extraction with or without IOL. Rarely performed; modern phacoemulsification preferred.
