Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: Complex laceration add-on, Additional complex wound closure

Complex repair of laceration on the eyelid, nose, ear, or lip for each additional five centimeters or less.

Clinical Context

Add-on code for complex repair of eyelid, nose, ear, or lip extending beyond initial measurement.
