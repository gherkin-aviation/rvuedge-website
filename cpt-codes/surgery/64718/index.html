64718 Revise ulnar nerve at elbow

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ulnar nerve decompression elbow, cubital tunnel surgery, ulnar nerve transposition

Surgical revision of the ulnar nerve at the elbow region, typically involving decompression or repositioning to relieve compression or restore function. Commonly performed for cubital tunnel syndrome.

In Plain Language

releasing the nerve at the funny bone; elbow nerve decompression

Clinical Context

Indicated for cubital tunnel syndrome with nerve compression at the elbow causing pain, numbness, or weakness in the ring and little fingers. Surgery relieves pressure on the nerve.
