19306 Mast radiation urban type

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: urban mastectomy, radical mastectomy urban type

Surgical removal of the breast, ipsilateral axillary lymph nodes, and chest wall muscles using an urban (anterolateral) approach and technique. This represents a radical mastectomy variant.

Clinical Context

Rarely used variant of radical mastectomy with specific surgical approach. Historical technique largely replaced by more conservative approaches.
