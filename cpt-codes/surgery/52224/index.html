52224 Cystoscopy and treatment

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: cysto with tumor resection, transurethral resection of bladder tumor, TURBT

Cystourethroscopy with resection of bladder tumor(s) using the resecting loop, removing tissue down to the lamina propria for intermediate-grade non-muscle invasive tumors.

In Plain Language

bladder scope with removal of tumor

Clinical Context

Standard treatment for non-muscle invasive bladder cancer including Ta, T1, and carcinoma in situ. Allows staging and complete tumor removal.
