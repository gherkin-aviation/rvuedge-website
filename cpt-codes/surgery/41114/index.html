41114 Excision of tongue lesion

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: radical glossectomy, tongue cancer surgery, glossectomy with lymph node dissection

Surgical excision of a tumor of the tongue requiring radical dissection and neck lymph node removal.

In Plain Language

major tongue cancer surgery; tongue removal with lymph node surgery; extensive cancer tongue surgery

Clinical Context

Used for advanced tongue cancer requiring radical glossectomy with neck dissection. Common with T3-T4 squamous cell carcinomas with lymph node involvement.
