31561 Larynscop remve cart + scop

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: microscopic arytenoid removal, operating scope cartilage removal, micro cartilage resection

Laryngoscopic removal of laryngeal cartilage (such as arytenoid or thyroid cartilage) using an operating microscope for magnified visualization and precise surgical technique.

In Plain Language

Clinical Context
