45327 Proctosigmoidoscopy with stent

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: proctoscopy with stent placement, sigmoidoscopy with stent insertion

Proctosigmoidoscopy with placement of expandable metal stent in rectum or sigmoid colon for stricture relief or obstruction palliation.

Clinical Context

Performed for malignant or benign rectal strictures causing obstruction, often as bridge to surgery or for palliative management of inoperable disease.
