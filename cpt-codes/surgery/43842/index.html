43842 Gstr rstcv procedures v-banded gstp

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: open gastric banding with gastroplasty, open VBG, banded gastroplasty

Gastric restrictive procedure with Verband-style banding and gastroplasty for morbid obesity.

Clinical Context

Restrictive bariatric procedure combining gastric banding with vertically oriented gastroplasty pouch. Creates small stomach volume with restricted outlet.
