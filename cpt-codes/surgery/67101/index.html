67101 Repair detached retina crtx

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: scleral buckle, buckle surgery, pneumatic retinopexy, retinal detachment repair

Repair of rhegmatogenous retinal detachment by means of scleral buckling or pneumatic retinopexy through corneal or limbal approach.

Clinical Context

Treatment for rhegmatogenous retinal detachment with breaks; buckle indents sclera to relieve vitreous traction; pneumatic approach uses gas bubble.
