20670 Removal implant superficial

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: implant removal, superficial implant extraction

Removal of a superficial implant.

In Plain Language

removal of implant near skin surface; taking out shallow implant

Clinical Context

Used for removal of pins, plates, wires, or other hardware implanted in subcutaneous or superficial soft tissue layers.
