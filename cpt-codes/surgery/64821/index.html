64821 Sympathectomy radial artery

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: radial artery sympathectomy, radial artery stripping

Surgical sympathectomy of the radial artery and its sympathetic innervation. Performs stripping of nerve plexus around the radial artery to improve distal hand blood flow.

In Plain Language

radial artery nerve surgery; thumb/radial side hand surgery

Clinical Context

Used for severe Raynaud's phenomenon or vascular insufficiency affecting the radial aspect of the hand. May be combined with ulnar artery sympathectomy for complete hand coverage.
