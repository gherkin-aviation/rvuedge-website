56620 Vulvectomy simple partial

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Simple vulvectomy, Partial vulvectomy

Vulvectomy with removal of partial vulvar tissue. Simple vulvectomy removes epidermis and dermis without deep pelvic structures.

In Plain Language

Partial removal of vulva; Removing outer vulvar tissue

Clinical Context

Used for vulvar intraepithelial neoplasia (VIN), benign tumors, or early vulvar carcinoma. Preserves function while achieving adequate margins.
