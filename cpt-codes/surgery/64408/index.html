64408 Njx aa and/or strd vagus nerve

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: vagus nerve block, vagus nerve injection

Injection of anesthetic and/or steroid into vagus nerve. This procedure involves injection of medication to the vagus nerve.

Clinical Context

Less commonly used for vagal pain syndromes or in preparation for vagal nerve stimulation procedures.
