21210 Face bone graft

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: facial bone graft, facial graft

Bone graft to the facial skeleton for reconstruction of defects or augmentation, using autogenous, allograft, or xenograft material.

Clinical Context

Used for facial defects, post-tumor reconstruction, or augmentation requiring bony restoration.
