50100 Trnsxj/reposition abrrnt rnl vsls

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: renal artery transposition, renal vessel repositioning, vascular correction

Transposition and repositioning of aberrant renal blood vessels that may be causing obstruction or other complications to the kidney or ureter.

In Plain Language

kidney blood vessel repositioning; correction of misplaced kidney blood vessels

Clinical Context

Used when aberrant renal vasculature causes ureteral obstruction (ureteropelvic junction obstruction) or other complications. More common in revision cases when UPJO repair is complicated by anomalous vessels.
