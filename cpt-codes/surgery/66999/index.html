66999 Unlisted procedures anterior segment eye

Surgery

Also known as: unlisted anterior segment procedure

Unlisted procedure code for unspecified anterior segment of the eye services that do not have a specific coded procedure available; requires special documentation and operative report.

Clinical Context

Use when an anterior segment procedure performed does not have a specific CPT code; must include detailed operative report; often triggers manual review by payers.
