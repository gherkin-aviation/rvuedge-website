24330 Flexor-plasty elbow

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: flexor reconstruction, elbow flexorplasty

Flexorplasty of the elbow. Surgical reconstruction of flexor muscles and tendons to restore elbow flexion.

In Plain Language

Surgical repair of muscles that bend the elbow; Restoration of elbow bending ability

Clinical Context

Used to restore elbow flexion function after paralysis, injury, or muscle loss due to conditions like brachial plexus injury or anterior interosseous nerve palsy.
