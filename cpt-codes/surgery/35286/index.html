35286 repair blvsl grf oth/th vn lxtr

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: leg prosthetic graft, lower extremity synthetic bypass

Repair of blood vessel injury in the lower extremity using prosthetic or arterial graft reconstruction.

In Plain Language

Fixing leg blood vessel with synthetic material; Repairing leg artery with man-made graft

Clinical Context

Lower extremity vascular reconstruction with prosthetic graft when venous conduit unavailable. PTFE grafts used for infrainguinal positions though vein graft preferred due to superior long-term patency.
