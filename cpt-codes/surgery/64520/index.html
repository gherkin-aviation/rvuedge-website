64520 N block lumbar/thoracic

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: lumbar sympathetic block, thoracic sympathetic block, sympathetic chain block

Nerve block of the lumbar or thoracic sympathetic chain through injection of anesthetic and/or steroid medication.

In Plain Language

sympathetic nerve pain injection; leg/chest sympathetic relief

Clinical Context

Used for complex regional pain syndrome, phantom limb pain, and lower extremity neuropathic pain. Blocks sympathetic innervation.
