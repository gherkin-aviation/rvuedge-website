28140 Removal of metatarsal

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: metatarsal removal complete, metatarsal amputation, complete metatarsal excision

Complete removal of a metatarsal bone. This procedure removes an entire metatarsal from the base to the head.

Clinical Context

Performed for metatarsal osteomyelitis, severe trauma, or malignancy. Results in functional limitation of forefoot but essential when pathology is severe.
