50706 Balloon dilate urtrl strix

Surgery

Also known as: ureteral balloon dilation, stricture dilation, balloon angioplasty of ureter

Balloon dilation of ureteral stricture, a narrowing of the ureter, using percutaneous or endoscopic approach to restore normal urine flow.

In Plain Language

stretching a narrowed ureter with a balloon

Clinical Context

Indicated for ureteral strictures caused by inflammation, prior surgery, radiation, or stone passage. Provides minimally invasive alternative to surgical repair.
