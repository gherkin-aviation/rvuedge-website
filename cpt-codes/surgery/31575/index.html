31575 Diagnostic laryngoscopy

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: rigid laryngoscopy, telescope laryngoscopy, diagnostic rigid laryngoscopy

Diagnostic laryngoscopic examination of the larynx using a rigid laryngoscope with telescopic magnification for visualization of laryngeal structures and pathology assessment.

In Plain Language

examining voice box with rigid scope

Clinical Context

Diagnostic procedure used to evaluate laryngeal pathology, vocal cord mobility, and airway status. Rigid scopes provide excellent visualization compared to flexible scopes.
