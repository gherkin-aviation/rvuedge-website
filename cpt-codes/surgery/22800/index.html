22800 Arthrd pst dfrm<6 vrt segment

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Posterior spinal fusion, Deformity correction

Arthrodesis, posterior technique, spinal deformity correction, with bone graft or nonvascularized bone graft; fewer than 6 vertebral segments.

Clinical Context

Used for scoliosis, kyphosis, or lordosis correction; posterior approach with bone graft provides fusion and deformity correction.
