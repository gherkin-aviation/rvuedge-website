35645 Bpg subclavian-vertebral

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: subclavian-vertebral bypass, subclavian-to-vertebral bypass, vertebral revascularization

Arterial bypass graft from subclavian artery to vertebral artery for vertebral artery stenosis or occlusion with posterior circulation insufficiency. Alternative to carotid-vertebral bypass.

In Plain Language

shoulder artery to back-of-brain artery bypass; rerouting blood to the vertebral artery

Clinical Context

Performed for vertebral artery insufficiency when carotid-vertebral bypass is not feasible. Often combined with subclavian transposition.
