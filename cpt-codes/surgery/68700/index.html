68700 Repair tear ducts

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: nasolacrimal duct repair, canalicular repair

Repair of nasolacrimal duct including canaliculus reconstruction using graft or direct repair technique.

Clinical Context

Used for nasolacrimal duct injury or obstruction requiring surgical repair. Common indications include traumatic laceration, avulsion, stricture, or post-surgical obstruction.
