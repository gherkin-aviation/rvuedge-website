22511 Percutaneous lumbosacral injection

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: Percutaneous lumbosacral injection, Lumbar discography

Percutaneous injection of contrast and/or therapeutic substance into lumbosacral vertebral body or intervertebral disc, under imaging guidance, performed on a single vertebral segment.

Clinical Context

Performed for diagnostic discography to assess disc integrity and pain generators, or therapeutic injection of steroids and local anesthetics in the lumbosacral region.
