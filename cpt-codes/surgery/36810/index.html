36810 Insertion of cannula

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: arteriovenous fistula, AVF, native fistula, AV fistula

Insertion of cannula for hemodialysis, arteriovenous; internal, autogenous.

Clinical Context

Preferred permanent dialysis access. Created surgically by anastomosis of artery to vein, allowing blood flow to increase vein diameter.
