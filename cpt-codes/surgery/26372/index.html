26372 Repair/graft hand tendon

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Flexor-extensor repair with graft, Dual system graft repair, Primary F-E graft

Surgical repair and grafting of flexor and extensor tendons of the finger, combining tissue graft augmentation with primary repair of both the flexor and extensor systems in one digit.

Clinical Context

Performed when both flexor and extensor tendons require repair and grafting, such as in delayed presentations, infection-complicated repairs, or when tissue quality mandates graft placement.
