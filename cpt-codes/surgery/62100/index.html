62100 Repair brain fluid leakage

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: cerebrospinal fluid leak repair, CSF fistula repair, dural leak repair

Repair of cerebrospinal fluid leak including identification and sealing of cerebrospinal fluid fistula. May include grafting or sealant application.

Clinical Context

Used for repair of dural or skull base defects causing cerebrospinal fluid leakage, which can lead to meningitis if untreated.
