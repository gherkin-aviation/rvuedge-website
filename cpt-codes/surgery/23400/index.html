23400 Fixation of shoulder blade

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: scapular fixation, scapulodesis, serratus anterior plication

Fixation of scapula (serratus anterior plication or scapulodesis). Surgical fixation of scapula to chest wall to stabilize winged scapula or improve function.

In Plain Language

stabilization of shoulder blade; shoulder blade fixation

Clinical Context

Used for winged scapula secondary to serratus anterior palsy, spinal accessory nerve injury, or scapular dyskinesis causing pain and functional impairment.
