63042 Laminotomy single lumbar

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: single lumbar laminotomy, lumbar level decompression

Laminotomy with or without discectomy in lumbar spine for single segment. Includes nerve root decompression.

Clinical Context

Used for lumbar spinal decompression with laminotomy and/or discectomy at single segment.
