50972 Ureter endoscopy & catheter

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: transurethral ureteroscopy with catheter, retrograde stent placement

Endoscopy of ureter with ureteral catheter insertion via transurethral approach for drainage or diagnostic access.

Clinical Context

Used for ureteral stent placement, catheter drainage, or diagnostic access in cases of obstruction or stricture.
