21195 Reconst lower jaw without fixation

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: non-fixed jaw reconstruction, soft tissue stabilized reconstruction

Reconstruction of the lower jaw without internal fixation devices, relying on tissue support and healing for stability.

In Plain Language

Jaw reconstruction without plates or screws

Clinical Context

Used for limited defects where internal fixation is not necessary or cannot be employed.
