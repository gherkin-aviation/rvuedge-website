28890 Hi enrgy eswt plantar fascia

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: extracorporeal shock wave, ESWT, high-energy shockwave

Extracorporeal shock wave therapy (ESWT) for plantar fasciitis, using focused sound waves directed at the plantar fascia attachment to reduce inflammation and pain in chronic heel pain.

Clinical Context

Used for chronic plantar fasciitis refractory to conservative treatment; ESWT promotes healing and reduces pain in heel pain syndrome.
