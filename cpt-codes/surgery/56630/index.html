56630 Vulvectomy radical partial

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Radical vulvectomy partial, Hemivulvectomy

Radical vulvectomy with partial tissue removal. Removes vulva with en bloc dissection of underlying tissues and lymph nodes.

In Plain Language

Deep removal of half or part of vulva; Radical vulvar tissue removal

Clinical Context

Used for vulvar carcinoma with potential nodal involvement. Radical dissection provides better oncologic control than simple vulvectomy.
