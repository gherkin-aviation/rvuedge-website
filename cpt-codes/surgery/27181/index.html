27181 Treat slipped epiphysis

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: SCFE valgus repair, slipped epiphysis realignment

Treatment of slipped capital femoral epiphysis with open reduction and internal fixation, including cases requiring valgus realignment for improved biomechanics.

In Plain Language

Hip angle correction; Growth plate angle repair

Clinical Context

Applied to slipped capital femoral epiphysis with significant varus deformity requiring correction to improve hip biomechanics and reduce arthritic progression.
