27760 Cltx medial ankle fracture

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: closed reduction medial ankle, conservative ankle fracture treatment

Closed treatment of medial ankle malleolus fracture with manipulation and casting without operative intervention.

Clinical Context

Appropriate for non-displaced or minimally displaced medial malleolar fractures. Non-operative approach with immobilization.
