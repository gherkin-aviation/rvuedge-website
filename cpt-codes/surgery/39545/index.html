39545 Revision of diaphragm

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: diaphragm revision, recurrent hernia repair

Surgical revision of a previously repaired diaphragm or revision repair for diaphragmatic defect. This procedure addresses complications or inadequacy of prior repair.

In Plain Language

fixing or re-doing a prior diaphragm repair

Clinical Context

Used for recurrent diaphragmatic hernias after prior repair, inadequate initial closure, or prosthetic material complications. Often more challenging due to scar tissue.

Billing & Documentation

This code has a 90-day global period, which includes the day of the procedure, 1 day preoperative, and 90 days of postoperative care.
