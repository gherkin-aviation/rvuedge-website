55970 Sex transformation m to f

Surgery

Also known as: Male-to-female transition surgery, Vaginoplasty

Surgical procedure for sex transformation from male to female. Involves construction of female external and internal genitalia.

In Plain Language

Sex reassignment surgery; Transition surgery from male to female

Clinical Context

Performed in patients with gender dysphoria who have completed psychological evaluation and hormone therapy. Typically includes vaginoplasty, clitoroplasty, and vulvaplasty.

RVU Information

CPT 55970 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

As a surgical CPT code, proper documentation must include the operative report detailing the procedure performed, patient positioning, approach, findings, and any complications. Ensure the diagnosis code (ICD-10) supports medical necessity for the procedure.
