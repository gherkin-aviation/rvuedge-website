67415 Aspiration orbital contents

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: orbital aspiration, needle aspiration, orbital fluid drainage

Aspiration of orbital contents to obtain diagnostic material or relieve pressure from fluid collection, mass, or inflammatory process.

Clinical Context

Minimally invasive diagnostic procedure for orbital fluid, hemorrhage, or cyst; provides material for culture or cytology.
