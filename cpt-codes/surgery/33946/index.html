33946 Ecmo/ecls initiation venous

Surgery

Also known as: ECMO initiation venous, ECLS venous setup, extracorporeal support initiation

Extracorporeal membrane oxygenation/extracorporeal life support initiation of venoarterial, venovenous, or arteriovenous circulation support; percutaneous or surgical placement of venous cannula for extracorporeal oxygenation.

In Plain Language

Placement of life support breathing machine tubing; Heart-lung machine support start

Clinical Context

Initial placement of venous cannula(e) for establishment of ECMO support in acute cardiogenic shock, respiratory failure, or other conditions requiring extracorporeal circulatory support.
