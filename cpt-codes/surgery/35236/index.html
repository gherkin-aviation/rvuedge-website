35236 Repair blvsl vn grf uxtr

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: arm vein graft repair, upper extremity vein bypass

Repair of blood vessel injury in the upper extremity using venous graft reconstruction.

In Plain Language

Fixing arm blood vessel with vein graft; Repairing shoulder/arm vessel with transplanted vein

Clinical Context

Vascular reconstruction of upper extremity injuries requiring grafting due to vessel loss or inadequate length for primary anastomosis. Vein graft provides viable conduit with lower infection and restenosis rates compared to prosthetic grafts.
