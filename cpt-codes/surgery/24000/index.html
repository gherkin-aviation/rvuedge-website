Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: elbow arthrotomy, elbow joint drainage, elbow exploration

Arthrotomy of elbow (exploration, drainage, or removal of foreign body). Opening of elbow joint for exploration, drainage, or foreign body removal.

In Plain Language

opening of elbow joint; elbow joint surgery

Clinical Context

Performed for septic arthritis, traumatic hemarthrosis, foreign body in joint space, or diagnostic exploration of elbow pathology.
