54860 Removal of epididymis

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: epididymectomy, epididymal removal

Removal of epididymis (epididymectomy) with ligation of vas deferens.

Clinical Context

Complete removal of epididymis for chronic infection, inflammatory disease, or malignancy while preserving testis.
