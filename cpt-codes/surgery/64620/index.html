64620 Injection treatment of nerve

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: neurolytic injection, nerve destruction injection, neuralgia treatment

Injection of chemical agent or neurolytic solution for destruction of a nerve or treatment of neuralgia.

Clinical Context

Used for trigeminal neuralgia, post-herpetic neuralgia, and intractable nerve pain.
