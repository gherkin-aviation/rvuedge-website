30110 Removal of nose polyp(s)

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: nasal polyp removal, polyp excision simple, polypectomy simple

Endoscopic or direct visualization and removal of nasal polyp or polyps via a simple excision technique using minimal instrumentation.

In Plain Language

nose bump removal; nasal growth removal

Clinical Context

Indicated for symptomatic nasal polyps causing obstruction, rhinosinusitis, or sleep dysfunction; simple polyps without significant attachment or inflammation.
