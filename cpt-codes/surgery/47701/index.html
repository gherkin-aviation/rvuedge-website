47701 Bile duct revision

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Bile duct revision, stricture repair, redo biliary surgery

Revision of the bile ducts including stricture repair, dilation, or reconstruction using choledochoduodenostomy or choledochojejunostomy for stricture disease.

In Plain Language

Surgically fixing a narrowed or damaged bile duct

Clinical Context

Performed for post-surgical strictures, recurrent cholangitis, or primary sclerosing cholangitis. Often a redo operation with significant morbidity.
