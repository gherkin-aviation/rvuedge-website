22206 Incis spine 3 column thoracic

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: thoracic spine arthroscopic incision drainage, minimally invasive thoracic I and D

Arthroscopic incision and drainage of thoracic vertebral column. Surgical drainage of infection or fluid collection of thoracic vertebral region using arthroscopic minimally invasive technique with full vertebral column visualization.

Clinical Context

Used for thoracic spine infections or fluid collections amenable to arthroscopic approach. Minimally invasive technique reduces soft tissue trauma while providing adequate visualization for drainage.
