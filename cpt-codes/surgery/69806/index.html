69806 Explore inner ear

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: endolymphatic sac decompression, ELS decompression, endolymphatic sac exploration

Surgical exploration and decompression of the endolymphatic sac to treat endolymphatic hydrops or inner ear pressure disorders.

In Plain Language

relieving pressure in the inner ear

Clinical Context

Used for Meniere's disease, endolymphatic hydrops, or recurrent vertigo with inner ear pressure elevation.
