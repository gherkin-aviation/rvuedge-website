44620 Repair bowel opening

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: bowel opening repair, bowel defect repair, bowel wall reconstruction

Repair of bowel opening or defect, including perforation or fistula tract, via open approach with reconstruction of bowel wall integrity.

Clinical Context

Used for repair of enterotomy sites, inadvertent bowel openings, or defects from disease (Crohn's fistula, diverticular perforation) when simple closure suffices.
