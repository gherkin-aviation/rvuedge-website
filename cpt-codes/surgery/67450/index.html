Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: extensive orbital biopsy, orbital sampling, deep orbital biopsy

Exploration and biopsy of orbit through extended surgical approach with detailed sampling of orbital tissue for comprehensive pathologic evaluation.

Clinical Context

Diagnosis of infiltrative conditions, lymphoma, inflammatory myositis, or orbital tumors requiring multiple tissue samples.
