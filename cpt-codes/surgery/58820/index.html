58820 Drainage ovary abscess open

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ovarian abscess drainage open, open drainage, I&D ovarian abscess

Drainage of an ovarian abscess via open surgical approach (laparotomy) with incision and drainage of the infected collection.

In Plain Language

surgically draining infected ovarian pocket

Clinical Context

Indicated for symptomatic ovarian abscess with fever, leukocytosis, and pelvic pain; open approach used when laparoscopic access is contraindicated.
