20205 Deep muscle biopsy

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: deep muscle biopsy, open muscle biopsy

Open or direct visualization biopsy of deep muscle tissue requiring significant dissection to access. This procedure obtains tissue from deeper muscle compartments.

In Plain Language

taking a sample of muscle from deep inside leg or arm

Clinical Context

Used for diagnosis of deep muscle disorders when superficial sampling is inadequate or when specific deep muscle involvement is suspected.
