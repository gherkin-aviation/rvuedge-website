33238 Remove electrode/thoracotomy

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: multiple lead removal, thoracotomy multi-lead extraction, open multi-lead removal

Removal of three or more pacemaker leads via thoracotomy approach with direct epicardial extraction. Open surgical removal of multiple leads.

Clinical Context

Used for infected or malfunctioning multi-chamber epicardial systems. Requires thoracotomy for complete lead visualization and extraction.
