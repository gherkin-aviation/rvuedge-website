27596 Amputation follow-up surgery

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: stump revision, amputation revision, prosthetic preparation

Surgical revision of an amputation stump including tissue reconstruction, scar revision, or implant removal to improve prosthetic fitting and function.

Clinical Context

Used for prosthetic fitting problems, painful neuromas, skin grafting needs, or inadequate residual limb shape.
