22214 Incis 1 vertebral seg lumbar

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: lumbar vertebral body incision drainage, lumbar spine abscess drainage

Incision and drainage of lumbar vertebral body and epidural space. Surgical drainage of infection or abscess in lumbar vertebral body with extension into epidural space.

Clinical Context

Used for lumbar vertebral osteomyelitis or discitis with epidural abscess requiring surgical drainage. Anterior or posterolateral approach may be selected based on anatomy.
