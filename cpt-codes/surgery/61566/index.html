61566 Removal of brain tissue

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: brain tissue removal, parenchymal resection

Stereotactic or open craniotomy with removal of parenchymal brain tissue for focal pathology, aspiration, or biopsy.

Clinical Context

Used for arteriovenous malformations, cavernomas, gliomas, and other focal lesions requiring tissue resection. May be combined with electrophysiologic mapping.
