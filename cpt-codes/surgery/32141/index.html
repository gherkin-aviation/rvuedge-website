32141 Remove/treat lung lesions

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: lung lesion removal with treatment, complex lung lesion resection

Surgical removal and treatment of lung lesions with more extensive approach, potentially including cystic lesions, vascular malformations, or conditions requiring parenchymal reconstruction.

Clinical Context

Used for complex lung lesions including congenital anomalies, arteriovenous malformations, cystic lung disease, or benign lesions requiring reconstructive techniques.
