11400 excision tr-ext b9+marg 0.5 cm<

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: excision with margins, lesion excision, excision benign lesion 0.5cm or less trunk/extremity, mole removal

Excision of benign skin lesion on trunk or extremity with margins of 0.5 centimeters or less. Includes closure of the excision site.

Clinical Context

Used for removal of benign skin lesions with narrow surgical margins on non-facial areas. Common for seborrheic keratosis, nevi, or lipomas.
