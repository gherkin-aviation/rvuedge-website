22514 Percutaneous vertebral augmentation

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: Thoracolumbar vertebroplasty, Thoracolumbar kyphoplasty

Percutaneous vertebral augmentation (vertebroplasty or kyphoplasty) of thoracolumbar vertebra, unilateral or bilateral, single vertebral segment.

In Plain Language

Bone cement injection into mid-lower back vertebra; Spine fracture stabilization

Clinical Context

Used for vertebral compression fractures in the thoracolumbar region; provides stabilization and pain relief for osteoporotic or metastatic fractures.
