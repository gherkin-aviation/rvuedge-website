27228 Treat hip fracture(s)

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: extensive acetabular ORIF, dual-incision acetabular repair

Treatment of acetabular fracture through extensive operative open reduction and internal fixation, often involving dual incisions or combined approaches.

Clinical Context

Applied to highly complex acetabular fractures requiring extensive surgical exposure, often via kocher-langenbeck and ilioinguinal approaches.
