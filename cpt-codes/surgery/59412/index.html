59412 Antepartum manipulation

Surgery

Also known as: external cephalic version, ECV, external version

Antepartum manipulation of fetus (such as external cephalic version) performed to convert fetus from abnormal presentation to vertex (head-down) for vaginal delivery.

In Plain Language

manually turning the baby from breech to head-down

Clinical Context

Performed at 36+ weeks in breech or transverse lie presentations to attempt vaginal delivery; success rate 60-70% in multiparous women.
