33688 Closure 1vsd with wo ptch removal bnd

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: VSD closure with band removal, completion VSD repair, single VSD band removal

Surgical closure of a single ventricular septal defect with removal of a previously placed pulmonary artery band.

In Plain Language

closing hole and removing band from lung artery

Clinical Context

Performed as definitive completion procedure following earlier PA banding palliation; achieves hemodynamic correction through combined VSD closure and PA debanding.
