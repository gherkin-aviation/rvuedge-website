64744 Incise nerve back of head

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: greater occipital nerve incision, occipital nerve decompression

Surgical incision and decompression of the greater occipital nerve in the back of the head. Treats compression or entrapment of this sensory nerve.

In Plain Language

back of head nerve surgery; occipital headache treatment

Clinical Context

Used for occipital neuralgia causing posterior scalp pain and headaches. Surgical decompression at the point of compression (usually at the semispinalis capitis) provides relief.
