43196 Esophagoscp guide wire dilation

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: rigid esophagoscopy with guide wire dilation, esophageal guide wire dilation

Rigid esophagoscopy (examination of esophagus with rigid scope) via transoral approach with guide wire insertion and dilation of esophagus.

Clinical Context

Therapeutic procedure for esophageal stricture using guide wire technique. Allows controlled dilation of strictures under direct visualization.
