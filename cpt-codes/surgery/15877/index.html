15877 Suction lipectomy trunk

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: trunk lipo, abdominal liposuction, flank lipo

Suction-assisted lipectomy of the trunk including chest, abdomen, flanks, and lower back to reduce excess subcutaneous fat deposits.

In Plain Language

Fat removal from torso; Trunk contouring surgery

Clinical Context

Common procedure for body contouring and cosmetic improvement of trunk contours. Used in post-bariatric patients and cosmetic enhancement.

RVU Information

CPT 15877 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
