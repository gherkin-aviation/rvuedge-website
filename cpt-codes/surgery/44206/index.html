44206 Laparoscopic part colectomy with stoma

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: lap partial colectomy with ostomy, laparoscopic Hartmann procedure, lap colectomy with stoma

Laparoscopic partial colectomy with creation of colostomy or ileostomy.

In Plain Language

laparoscopic colon removal with external pouch; minimally invasive colectomy with opening

Clinical Context

Laparoscopic resection of colon with creation of proximal stoma when distal anastomosis unsafe. Minimally invasive alternative to open Hartmann.
