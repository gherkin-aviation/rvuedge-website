36905 Thrmbc/nfs dialysis circuit

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: fistula thrombectomy, graft thrombectomy, declotting

Thrombectomy, open; of mature arteriovenous fistula or graft (first attempt).

Clinical Context

Used for restoration of thrombosed but otherwise functional arteriovenous fistula or graft.
