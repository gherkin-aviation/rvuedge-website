58760 Fimbrioplasty

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: fimbriae repair, fimbrial reconstruction, fimbrioplasty

Fimbrioplasty is a surgical procedure to repair or reconstruct the fimbriae (finger-like projections) at the end of the fallopian tube to restore their function in capturing released eggs.

Clinical Context

Used to treat damaged or scarred fimbriae that prevent normal ovum capture, often indicated in infertility workup.
