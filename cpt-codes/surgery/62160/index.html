62160 Neuroendoscopy add-on

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: endoscopic neuroendoscopy, neuroendoscopy add-on

Neuroendoscopy (endoscopic intracranial procedures) performed as add-on to another intracranial procedure. Does not include standalone neuroendoscopy.

Clinical Context

Used as an adjunct to other intracranial procedures to provide improved visualization and minimally invasive options.
