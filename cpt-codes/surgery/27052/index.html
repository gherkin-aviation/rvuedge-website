27052 Biopsy of hip joint

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: hip joint biopsy, hip biopsy, synovial biopsy hip

Surgical biopsy of the hip joint through arthroscopic or open approach to obtain tissue diagnosis.

Clinical Context

Indicated for diagnosis of hip joint pathology including infection, inflammatory arthritis, or synovial disorders requiring tissue analysis.
