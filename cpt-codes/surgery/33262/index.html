33262 removal& replc pulse gen 1 lead

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: PM generator replacement 1 lead, pacemaker box change single lead, pulse gen removal replacement

Removal and replacement of pacemaker pulse generator with a single lead system, removing the old device and implanting a new one while managing the existing lead.

In Plain Language

replacing pacemaker battery with one wire; changing pacemaker box

Clinical Context

Performed for pacemaker battery depletion, malfunction, or infection; single lead devices are less common but used in select cases.
