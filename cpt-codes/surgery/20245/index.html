20245 Bone biopsy open deep

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: open deep bone biopsy, deep bone biopsy surgical

Open surgical biopsy of deep bone to obtain diagnostic tissue sample from cancellous or deep cortical bone through direct visualization. This provides access to deep bone pathology.

Clinical Context

Used for diagnosis of deep bone pathology not accessible with percutaneous technique, including infections, malignancies, or metabolic disorders.
