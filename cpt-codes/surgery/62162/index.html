62162 Remove colloid cyst with scope

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: endoscopic cyst removal, colloid cyst neuroendoscopy

Neuroendoscopic removal of colloid cyst of the brain. Usually located in the third ventricle near the foramen of Monro.

In Plain Language

camera-assisted brain cyst removal; minimally invasive cyst removal

Clinical Context

Used for removal of colloid cysts causing obstructive hydrocephalus or neurological symptoms via endoscopic approach.
