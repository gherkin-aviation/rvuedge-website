67208 Treatment of retinal lesion

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: laser treatment of retinal lesion, focal laser, retinal lesion ablation

Treatment of intraocular lesion such as retinal tear, retinal hole, or choroidal lesion using laser photocoagulation with detailed visualization and precise thermal ablation.

Clinical Context

Used for isolated retinal tears, retinal holes, small hemorrhages, or choroidal lesions causing visual symptoms; prevents progression to detachment.
