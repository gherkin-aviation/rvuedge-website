17263 destruction mal lesion t/a/l 2.1-3.0

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: destroy malignant 2.1-3.0, skin cancer medium-large

Destruction of malignant skin lesion (trunk, arms, legs) with largest diameter between 2.1 and 3.0 centimeters.

Clinical Context

Used for malignant lesions on body that are larger, 2.1-3.0 cm range. Often requires deeper tissue involvement assessment.
