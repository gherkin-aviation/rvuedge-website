67340 Revise eye muscle add-on

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: muscle transection, muscle rerouting, complex muscle manipulation

Add-on code for transection, reattachment, or relocation of extraocular muscle origin/insertion during complex strabismus surgery requiring advanced surgical technique.

Clinical Context

Used for restrictive strabismus, paralytic strabismus, or cases requiring major muscle position changes beyond standard advancement/recession.
