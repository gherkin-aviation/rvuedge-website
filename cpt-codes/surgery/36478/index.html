36478 Endovenous laser first vein

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: endovenous laser ablation, EVLA, laser vein closure, 980-nm laser

Endovenous ablation with laser energy, first vein treated.

Clinical Context

Used for varicose vein ablation via laser-induced thermal damage to vein wall. Common for saphenous vein insufficiency.
