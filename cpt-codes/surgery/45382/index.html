45382 Colonoscopy with control bleed

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: colonoscopy with bleeding control, colon hemostasis

Colonoscopy with hemostasis of actively bleeding lesion via injection, cautery, clip placement, or banding.

In Plain Language

stopping colon bleeding; cauterization of bleeding site

Clinical Context

Emergency intervention for acute colonic bleeding from diverticula, polyps, angiodysplasia, ulcers, or anastomoses.
