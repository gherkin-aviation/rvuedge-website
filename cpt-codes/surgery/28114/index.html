28114 Removal of metatarsal heads

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: multiple metatarsal head removal, metatarsal head excision multiple, forefoot head resection

Excision of metatarsal head(s) of the great toe and two or more lesser metatarsals. This comprehensive procedure removes multiple metatarsal heads for severe forefoot pathology.

In Plain Language

removing the ball of the foot area; extensive forefoot surgery

Clinical Context

Performed for severe rheumatoid arthritis, diabetic forefoot ulceration, or post-traumatic deformity. Extensive surgery with significant functional impact.
