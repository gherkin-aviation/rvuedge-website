15783 Dermabrasion suprfl any site

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: superficial dermabrasion, lesion abrasion

Dermabrasion of superficial skin lesion at any location. Minimal mechanical abrasion for superficial skin conditions.

Clinical Context

Used for superficial scars, tattoo removal, and minor skin lesions requiring minimal dermabrasion depth.
