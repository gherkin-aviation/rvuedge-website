26516 Fusion of knuckle joint

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Knuckle fusion, Joint arthrodesis, Finger joint fusion

Surgical arthrodesis or fusion of a single metacarpophalangeal or interphalangeal knuckle joint to eliminate motion and reduce pain while maintaining overall hand function.

Clinical Context

Indicated for painful joint arthropathy, instability, or post-traumatic arthritis affecting a single knuckle joint where fusion is preferable to arthroplasty.
