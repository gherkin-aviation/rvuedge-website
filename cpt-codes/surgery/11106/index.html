11106 Incal biopsy skn single lesion

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: incisional biopsy, elliptical biopsy, incisional biopsy skin, incisional skin biopsy

Incisional biopsy of skin from a single lesion using a knife incision technique. This procedure removes a wedge-shaped or elliptical sample that includes epidermis and dermis.

In Plain Language

wedge-shaped skin sample; surgical skin biopsy

Clinical Context

Used for evaluation of larger lesions, multiple areas within a lesion, or when orientation is important for pathological interpretation. Provides representative sample of lesion.
