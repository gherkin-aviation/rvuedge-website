52005 Cysto with urtrl cathj

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: cysto with ureteral catheterization, retrograde pyelography

Cystourethroscopy with insertion of a ureteral catheter into one or both ureters to obtain urine samples or relieve upper urinary tract obstruction.

Clinical Context

Used for diagnostic imaging of upper urinary tract, urine culture, urodynamics, and relief of acute ureteral obstruction from stones or strictures.
