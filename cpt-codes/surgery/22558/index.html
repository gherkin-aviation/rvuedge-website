22558 Arthrd anterior ntrbd minutes dsc lum

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Anterior lumbar fusion with bone graft, ALIF with bone graft

Arthrodesis, anterior/anterolateral approach, including discectomy and/or corpectomy to decompress spinal cord and/or nerve roots, lumbar; with mineralized bone void filler/bone matrix, single interspaces.

Clinical Context

Used for lumbar spondylosis, spondylolisthesis, or disc herniation via anterior approach; mineralized bone material enhances fusion rate and stability.
