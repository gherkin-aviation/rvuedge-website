59050 Fetal monitor with report

Surgery

Also known as: fetal monitoring, cardiotocography, electronic fetal monitoring

Fetal monitoring with clinical interpretation and report, including continuous electronic fetal heart rate monitoring and generation of clinical assessment.

Clinical Context

Standard of care during labor; assessment of baseline rate, variability, and presence/absence of decelerations guides clinical management.
