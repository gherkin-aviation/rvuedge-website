27358 Remove femur lesion/fixation

Surgery

Also known as: femoral lesion excision with fixation, femur lesion ORIF

Removal of femoral lesion with internal fixation without bone graft.

Clinical Context

Applied to femoral lesions where internal fixation provides structural support without requiring bone graft.
