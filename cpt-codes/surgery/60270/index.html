60270 Removal of thyroid

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: total thyroidectomy, thyroid lobectomy with isthmusectomy, complete thyroid removal

Total or near-total thyroidectomy, typically performed for malignancy or diffuse thyroid disease, removing the entire thyroid gland with preservation of parathyroid glands when possible.

In Plain Language

removal of entire thyroid; full thyroid gland removal

Clinical Context

Used for differentiated thyroid cancer, Graves' disease refractory to medical therapy, and extensive goiter. Requires radioactive iodine therapy and lifelong thyroid hormone replacement in cancer cases.
