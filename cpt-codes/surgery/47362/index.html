47362 Repair liver wound

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: liver debridement, hepatic resection for injury, devitalized tissue removal

Repair of liver wound with resection of devitalized or necrotic tissue. Hepatic debridement and wound closure.

Clinical Context

Performed for liver wounds with devitalized tissue requiring resection for hemostasis and healing.
