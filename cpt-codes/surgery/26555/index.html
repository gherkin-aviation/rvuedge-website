26555 Positional change of finger

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Finger repositioning, Digit transfer, Positional correction

Surgical repositioning, transfer, or realignment of a finger or digit to improve hand position, function, or appearance, typically performed for positional deformity.

In Plain Language

Moving or repositioning a finger to improve hand function; Surgery to change finger position

Clinical Context

Indicated for finger positional deformity, poor positioning for hand function, or when surgical repositioning improves functional outcome.
