20560 Needle insj without njx 1 or 2 muscle

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: muscle needle insertion, needle placement no injection

Needle insertion into one or two muscles without injection of therapeutic medication. This procedure establishes needle access for potential future intervention.

In Plain Language

needle placed in muscle without medication

Clinical Context

Used as add-on component of procedures requiring multiple muscle needle placements without therapeutic injection at each site.
