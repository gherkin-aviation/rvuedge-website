28116 Revision of foot

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: forefoot reconstruction, forefoot revision, foot reconstruction

Reconstruction or revision of the forefoot (midfoot and metatarsal region). This procedure performs extensive reshaping of forefoot anatomy for deformity or reconstruction.

Clinical Context

Performed for Charcot foot, severe flatfoot deformity, or post-traumatic reconstruction. Often combined with multiple other procedures.
