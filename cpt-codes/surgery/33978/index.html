33978 Remove ventricular device

Surgery

Also known as: Second VAD removal, BVAD explantation, biventricular device removal

Removal of ventricular assist device (second removal); removal of a second or additional ventricular assist device, typically in biventricular systems.

In Plain Language

Second heart pump removal; Biventricular support device removal

Clinical Context

Removal of second (right) ventricular assist device when biventricular support is no longer needed or at transplantation.
