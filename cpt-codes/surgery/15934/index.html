15934 excision sacral pr ulc skn flap

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: sacral ulcer flap, sacral wound flap

Excision of sacral pressure ulcer with coverage using skin flap reconstruction to achieve durable wound closure.

Clinical Context

Performed for moderately sized sacral pressure ulcers requiring flap coverage. Better functional outcome and lower recurrence than primary closure alone.
