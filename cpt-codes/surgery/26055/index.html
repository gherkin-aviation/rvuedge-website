26055 Incise finger tendon sheath

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: tenotomy sheath, tendon sheath incision

Incision (surgical incision) of finger tendon sheath for drainage or relief.

Clinical Context

Used for incision of finger flexor or extensor tendon sheath to address compartment syndrome, infection, or to provide access for other procedures.
