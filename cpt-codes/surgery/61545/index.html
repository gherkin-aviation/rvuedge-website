61545 Excision of brain tumor

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: brain tumor resection, tumor excision, craniotomy tumor removal

Excision of brain tumor or intracranial neoplasm with complete removal of lesion tissue and surrounding margins.

In Plain Language

Surgical removal of a brain tumor; Cutting out a tumor in the brain

Clinical Context

Performed for symptomatic brain tumors causing mass effect, neurologic deficit, or requiring diagnostic tissue. Primary approach for glioblastoma, meningioma, and other intracranial neoplasms.
