35631 Bpg aor-celiac-msn-renal

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: aortoceliac-mesenteric-renal bypass, visceral-renal bypass, aortic visceral graft

Arterial bypass graft from abdominal aorta to celiac, mesenteric, and renal arteries for mesenteric and renal artery disease combined. Restores visceral and renal perfusion.

In Plain Language

bowel and kidney artery bypass from the main aorta; restoring blood flow to the intestines and kidneys

Clinical Context

Performed for symptomatic atherosclerotic disease of multiple visceral vessels with ischemia, stenosis >70%, or thrombosis. High morbidity; endovascular therapy preferred when feasible.
