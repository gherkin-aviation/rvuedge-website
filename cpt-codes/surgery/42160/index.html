42160 Treatment mouth roof lesion

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: palatal lesion treatment

Surgical treatment of a lesion on the roof of the mouth or palate, including removal, cauterization, or other therapeutic intervention.

Clinical Context

Used for benign lesions, ulcers, or small growths on hard or soft palate requiring therapeutic intervention without full excision.
