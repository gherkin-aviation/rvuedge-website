37215 Transcath stent cca with eps

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: carotid stent placement with EPS, carotid artery stent, carotid stenting

Transcatheter placement of a stent in the carotid artery (cervical or intracranial) with embolic protection system during the same procedure.

Clinical Context

Indicated for carotid artery stenosis in high-risk surgical patients or symptomatic disease. Embolic protection prevents distal embolization during intervention.
