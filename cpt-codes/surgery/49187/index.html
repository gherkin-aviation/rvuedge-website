49187 Opn excision/destruction ntra-abd 5.1-10

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: abdominal tumor excision, tumor removal 5-10cm, open mass excision

Open excision and destruction of intra-abdominal neoplasm measuring 5.1 to 10 centimeters.

Clinical Context

Open surgical removal of intermediate-sized intra-abdominal neoplasms. Used for primary tumor resection or metastatic disease when malignancy is suspected or confirmed.
