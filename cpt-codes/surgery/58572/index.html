58572 Tlh uterus over 250 g

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: TLH large uterus, laparoscopic hysterectomy enlarged uterus, lap total hysterectomy with tubes/ovaries

Total laparoscopic hysterectomy with uterus greater than 250 grams involves endoscopic removal of enlarged uterus with increased operative complexity.

In Plain Language

Laparoscopic removal of large uterus; Minimally invasive large uterus removal

Clinical Context

Large uterus increases difficulty of laparoscopic approach. Requires morcellation or vaginal extraction. Fibroids or adenomyosis commonly responsible.
