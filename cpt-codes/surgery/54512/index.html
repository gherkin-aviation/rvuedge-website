54512 Excise lesion testis

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: testicular lesion excision, testis mass removal

Excision of lesion or mass from testicular tissue with histopathologic examination.

Clinical Context

Surgical removal and biopsy of testicular lesion, nodule, or mass for pathologic diagnosis. Used for suspected malignancy or benign lesions.
