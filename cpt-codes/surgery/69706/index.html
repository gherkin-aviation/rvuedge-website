69706 Nps surgical dilation eust tube bi

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: Eustachian tube dilation bilateral, ET dilation bilateral, balloon dilation bilateral

Endoscopic or non-powered surgical dilation of the Eustachian tube bilaterally to improve middle ear ventilation on both sides.

In Plain Language

widening both ear-throat tubes

Clinical Context

Used for bilateral Eustachian tube dysfunction, chronic bilateral otitis media with effusion, or bilateral ventilation problems.
