46230 Removal of anal tags

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: anal tag removal, skin tag excision, hemorrhoid tag removal

Surgical removal of anal tags (external hemorrhoidal skin tabs). These are benign skin excrescences at the anal margin removed when symptomatic.

In Plain Language

removing hanging skin from the anus; removal of anal skin growths

Clinical Context

Indicated for symptomatic anal tags causing itching, irritation, or hygiene difficulties. Can be performed alone or in combination with other anorectal procedures.
