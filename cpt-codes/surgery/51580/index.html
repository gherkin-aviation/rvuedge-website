51580 Remove bladder/revise tract

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: cystectomy with diversion, bladder removal with conduit

Removal of bladder with revision of urinary tract, total cystectomy with creation of urinary diversion.

In Plain Language

bladder removal with new urinary drainage system

Clinical Context

Cystectomy combined with creation of ileal conduit or other non-continent diversion. More extensive than simple cystectomy.
