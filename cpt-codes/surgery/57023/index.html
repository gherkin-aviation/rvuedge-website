57023 incision and drainage vaginal hematoma non-ob

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: Vaginal hematoma drainage non-obstetric

Incision and drainage of vaginal hematoma in non-obstetric patient. Evacuation of blood collection from non-delivery related trauma.

Clinical Context

Used for vaginal hematomas from trauma, sexual assault, or surgical procedures. Provides symptom relief.
