57200 Repair of vagina

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Vaginal repair, Vaginoplasty

Repair of vaginal tissue. General vaginal reconstruction or tissue approximation for anatomic defects.

Clinical Context

Used for vaginal lacerations, strictures, or other defects. Restores normal anatomy.
