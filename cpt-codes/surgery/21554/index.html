21554 excision neck tumor deep 5 centimeters/>

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: deep neck tumor excision, deep neck lesion removal

Excision of deep lesion or tumor of neck, greater than 5 centimeters. Surgical removal of deep neck lesion extending into muscle or deeper structures, with lesion exceeding 5 centimeters in maximum dimension.

In Plain Language

surgery to remove deep neck tumor; removal of large deep neck growth

Clinical Context

Used for significant deep neck pathology including lymphadenopathy, deep cysts, or tumors requiring removal of deeper structures. Greater than 5 centimeters denotes significant lesion requiring careful dissection to avoid neurovascular structures.
