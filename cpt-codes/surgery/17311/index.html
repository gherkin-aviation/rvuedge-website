17311 Mohs 1 stage h/n/hf/g

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: Mohs first stage, Mohs surgery first

Mohs micrographic surgery for skin cancer with first stage histological analysis and mapping on head, neck, hands, feet, or genitals.

Clinical Context

Performed for basal cell or squamous cell carcinoma in cosmetically sensitive areas. First stage includes defect mapping and tissue processing.
