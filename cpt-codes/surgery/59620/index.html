59620 Attempted vbac delivery only

Surgery

Also known as: TOLAC with cesarean surgical only, repeat cesarean delivery, repeat c-section

Attempted vaginal birth after previous cesarean section with subsequent cesarean delivery (surgical portion only).

Clinical Context

Cesarean delivery during trial of labor after cesarean when managed by delivery surgeon only.
