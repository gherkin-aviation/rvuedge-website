62322 Njx interlaminar lumbar/sacral

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: interlaminar injection lumbar, interlaminar sacral injection, interlaminar epidural injection lumbar, lumbar epidural

Injection of therapeutic or diagnostic agent via interlaminar approach to lumbar or sacral spinal cord region.

Clinical Context

Used for delivery of therapeutic agents to lumbar or sacral spinal cord region via interlaminar needle approach.
