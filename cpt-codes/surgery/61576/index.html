61576 Skull base/brainstem surgery

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: complex skull base surgery, brainstem approach

Craniotomy with complex skull base approach combined with brainstem or posterior fossa resection for advanced pathology.

In Plain Language

Complex surgery at skull base and brainstem; Posterior fossa tumor surgery

Clinical Context

Performed for advanced or recurrent skull base tumors, vascular malformations, or lesions requiring posterior fossa extension and brainstem exposure.
