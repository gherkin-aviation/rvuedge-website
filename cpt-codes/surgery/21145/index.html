21145 Rcnstj mdfc lefort i 1 with grf

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Le Fort I with graft, midface reconstruction with augmentation

Reconstruction of the midface using Le Fort I osteotomy as a single piece with the addition of bone graft material.

In Plain Language

Upper face surgery with bone added; Midface reconstruction and augmentation; Upper jaw surgery with bone graft

Clinical Context

Used when Le Fort I advancement requires additional volume or augmentation, particularly in severe skeletal deficiency.
