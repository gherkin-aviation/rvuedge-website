54304 Revision of penis

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: penile revision with reconstruction, complex straightening

Surgical revision of the penis with extensive reconstruction for complex curvature or deformity.

Clinical Context

Used for severe penile curvature or complex deformities requiring extensive tissue rearrangement or grafting.
