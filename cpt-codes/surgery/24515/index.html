24515 Optx humeral shft fracture plate/scr

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ORIF humerus plate and screw, plate fixation, open reduction

Open treatment of humeral shaft fracture with plate and screw fixation. Surgical stabilization using plate and screw hardware.

Clinical Context

Used for displaced or unstable humeral shaft fractures requiring surgical fixation with plate and screw for alignment and stability.
