25393 Lengthen radius & ulna

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: bilateral lengthening

Lengthening of both the radius and ulna bones through osteotomy and bone grafting or distraction.

Clinical Context

Used for bilateral shortening or growth disturbances affecting both bones.
