49505 Prp i/hern initial reduc >5 yr

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: inguinal hernia repair adult, hernia repair older child, groin hernia repair, inguinal hernia repair initial, open inguinal hernia repair

Repair of inguinal hernia in patient age greater than five years with primary closure.

Clinical Context

Surgical repair of inguinal hernia in patients over five years old. Standard treatment for symptomatic hernias or prevention of incarceration.
