31087 Removal of frontal sinus

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: frontal obliteration orbital/intracranial, comprehensive frontal surgery, complex osteoplastic

Removal of frontal sinus mucosa with obliteration and management of combined orbital and intracranial complications.

Clinical Context

Most complex frontal sinus obliteration addressing simultaneous orbital and intracranial complications requiring both orbital decompression and dural repair.
