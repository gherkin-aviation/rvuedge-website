63251 Revise spinal cord vsls thrc

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: thoracic spinal AVM repair, thoracic vascular repair

Revision or repair of spinal cord vascular lesions in the thoracic region. This procedure addresses arteriovenous malformations, fistulas, or other vascular abnormalities affecting the thoracic spinal cord.

Clinical Context

Used for symptomatic spinal dural arteriovenous fistulas or malformations in the thoracic region causing myelopathy, often associated with progressive weakness or pain.
