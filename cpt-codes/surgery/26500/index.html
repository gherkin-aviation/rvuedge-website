26500 Hand tendon reconstruction

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Hand tendon reconstruction, Hand reconstruction, Tendon restoration

Surgical reconstruction of hand tendons, muscle-tendon units, or soft tissue structures to restore functional capacity and improve hand positioning after injury, paralysis, or tissue loss.

Clinical Context

Performed for complex hand injuries with multiple tendon deficiencies, extensive tissue loss, or chronic dysfunction requiring comprehensive reconstruction.
