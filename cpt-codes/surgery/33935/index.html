33935 Transplantation heart/lung

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Heart-lung transplant, cardiopulmonary transplant, HLT

Transplantation of heart and lung; surgical implantation of donor heart and lungs into recipient for treatment of end-stage cardiopulmonary disease.

Clinical Context

Performed for end-stage disease of both heart and lungs (usually primary pulmonary hypertension, Eisenmenger syndrome, or cystic fibrosis). Requires orthotopic implantation with careful attention to pulmonary and bronchial anastomoses.
