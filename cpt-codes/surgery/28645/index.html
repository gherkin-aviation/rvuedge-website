28645 Repair toe dislocation

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: toe dislocation ORIF, open reduction toe, open fixation

Open surgical treatment of toe dislocation with reduction and internal fixation, including open reduction and hardware placement for complex or irreducible digit dislocations.

Clinical Context

Used for irreducible or complex toe dislocations requiring open reduction; internal fixation restores stable joint anatomy.
