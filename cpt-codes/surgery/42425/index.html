42425 Excise parotid gland/lesion

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: parotidectomy with neck dissection

Surgical excision of the parotid gland with additional resection of affected lymph nodes or surrounding structures involved in malignancy.

In Plain Language

removal of parotid gland and lymph nodes

Clinical Context

Parotid surgery combined with neck dissection for parotid malignancy with nodal involvement requiring en bloc resection.
