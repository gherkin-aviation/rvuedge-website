17107 destruction cutaneous vascular proliferative les10-50sq

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: destroy vascular lesion medium, hemangioma 10-50

Destruction of cutaneous vascular lesion (hemangioma or port-wine stain) between 10 and 50 square centimeters.

Clinical Context

Used for moderate-sized vascular lesions requiring more extensive treatment. May require multiple sessions for complete clearance.
