23485 Revision of collar bone

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ORIF clavicle revision, clavicle revision with fixation, complex clavicle revision

Revision of clavicle with increased complexity (open reduction, internal fixation, or extensive reconstruction). Advanced clavicular revision with plate or screw fixation.

In Plain Language

re-setting and pinning of collar bone; complex collar bone fix

Clinical Context

Applied in clavicular malunion with significant displacement, nonunion, or comminution requiring open reduction and internal fixation with plate and screws.
