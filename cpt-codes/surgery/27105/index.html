27105 Transfer of spinal muscle

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: spinal muscle transfer, paraspinal transfer hip, back muscle transfer

Surgical transfer of spinal or paraspinal muscle to the hip for reconstruction and functional improvement.

In Plain Language

moving back muscle to improve hip; spinal muscle transfer surgery

Clinical Context

Indicated for hip function restoration through transfer of spinal muscles in cases of paralysis or severe muscle deficiency.
