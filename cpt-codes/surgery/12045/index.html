12045 Intmd repair n-hf/genit12.6-20

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: Intermediate laceration repair, Layered wound closure

Repair of intermediate complexity laceration on the neck, non-head/non-foot genitalia measuring twelve point six to twenty centimeters.

Clinical Context

Used for larger lacerations on the neck or genitalia requiring layered closure technique.
