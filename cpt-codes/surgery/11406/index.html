11406 excision tr-ext b9+marg >4.0 centimeters

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: excision with wide margins, lesion excision

Excision of benign skin lesion on trunk or extremity with margins greater than 4 centimeters. Includes closure of the excision site.

Clinical Context

Used for removal of larger benign skin lesions or lesions requiring very wide margins on trunk and extremities.
