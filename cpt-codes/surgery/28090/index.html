28090 Removal of foot lesion

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: plantar cyst excision, foot plantar lesion removal, plantar nodule excision

Excision of plantar lesion or cyst from the foot. This procedure removes cysts, nodules, or other benign lesions from the plantar fascia.

Clinical Context

Performed for symptomatic plantar cysts (such as ganglion cysts) or nodules causing pain. Careful dissection prevents disruption of plantar nerves.
