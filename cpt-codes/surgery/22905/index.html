Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Radical tumor resection large, Extensive wide local excision

Radical resection of tumor (eg, fibrosarcoma), abdominal wall, 5 centimeters or greater.

Clinical Context

Used for larger abdominal wall malignancies 5 cm or greater; requires extensive resection with wide safety margins.
