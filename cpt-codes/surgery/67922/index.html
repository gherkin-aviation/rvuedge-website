67922 Repair eyelid defect

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ectropion correction, ectropion suture

Correction of eyelid ectropion (outward-turning eyelid) using suture technique with rotation or strengthening of structures.

Clinical Context

Used for ectropion causing tearing, conjunctival exposure, or cosmetic concerns. Common indications include age-related involutional ectropion, paralytic ectropion from facial nerve palsy, or cicatricial ectropion.
