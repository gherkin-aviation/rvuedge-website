42900 Repair throat wound

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: pharyngeal wound repair, throat laceration repair

Repair of an acute or traumatic wound of the pharynx (throat) with primary closure.

Clinical Context

Performed for acute pharyngeal trauma, iatrogenic perforation, or penetrating injury. Requires careful repair with attention to airway management and risk of infection.
