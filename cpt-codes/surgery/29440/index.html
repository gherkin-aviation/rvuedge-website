29440 Adding walker to preventive cast

Surgery +Add-on Global 0d

Also known as: walker addition, add walker, cast walker

Addition of a walker mechanism to a previously applied cast, converting a non-weight-bearing cast to a weight-bearing cast for mobilization.

Clinical Context

Applied to existing casts when fracture stability permits weight-bearing, allowing patient to ambulate without crutches.
