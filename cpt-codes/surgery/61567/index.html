61567 Incision of brain tissue

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: corticotomy, functional brain surgery

Craniotomy with incision into brain parenchyma (corticotomy or lobotomy) for treatment of seizures or functional disorder.

Clinical Context

Performed for intractable seizures with well-defined focus amenable to surgical disconnection. Also historical use for psychiatric disease (limited modern indication).
