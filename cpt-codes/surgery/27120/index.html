27120 Reconstruction of hip socket

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: acetabular reconstruction, hip socket reconstruction, acetabular augmentation

Surgical reconstruction of the hip socket (acetabulum) through reshaping, grafting, or repositioning of bone.

Clinical Context

Performed for hip dysplasia, arthritis, acetabular deficiency, or reconstruction after tumor resection requiring socket restoration.
