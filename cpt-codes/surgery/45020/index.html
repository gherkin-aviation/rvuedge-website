45020 Drainage of rectal abscess

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: rectal abscess drainage complex, extensive perirectal drainage

Drainage of rectal abscess via open surgical approach with more extensive dissection or drainage of multiple loculations.

Clinical Context

Used for complex rectal abscesses with multiple loculations or extensive spread requiring more extensive dissection and drainage.
