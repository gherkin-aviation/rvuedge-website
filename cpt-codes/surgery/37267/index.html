37267 Revsc evsc fpvt stent sf first

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: fem-pop stent simple first, femoropopliteal stent first

Percutaneous or open revascularization of the femoropopliteal artery using stent placement with simple technique in the first treated vessel.

Clinical Context

Stenting of superficial femoral or popliteal artery. Simple technique, first vessel in multilevel disease.
