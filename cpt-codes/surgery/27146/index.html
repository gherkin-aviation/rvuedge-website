27146 Incision of hip bone

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: hip osteotomy, pelvic osteotomy, hip bone cutting

Surgical opening (osteotomy) of the hip bone with cutting and repositioning to change hip biomechanics.

In Plain Language

cutting and moving hip bone; hip bone realignment surgery

Clinical Context

Performed for hip dysplasia, coxa valga, or abnormal hip angle to improve alignment and reduce arthritic changes.
