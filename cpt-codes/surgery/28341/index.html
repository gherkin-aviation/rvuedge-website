28341 Resection enlarged toe

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: bunion exostosis resection, medial eminence resection, bunionectomy

Surgical resection of enlarged bone and surrounding soft tissue of the great toe region (bunion deformity), including removal of the medial exostosis and joint debridement.

In Plain Language

Bunion surgery; Bone bump removal; Bunion bone shaving

Clinical Context

Used for painful bunion exostosis causing pressure, shoe irritation, or pain; resection provides symptomatic relief and improved cosmesis.
