46200 Removal of anal fissure

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: fissurectomy, anal fissure excision, fissure removal

Surgical excision (removal) of an anal fissure. This procedure involves excising the fissure tissue and is often combined with lateral sphincterotomy to prevent recurrence.

Clinical Context

Used for chronic anal fissures, often in combination with sphincterotomy. Indicated when medical management fails or for anterior/posterior fissures.
