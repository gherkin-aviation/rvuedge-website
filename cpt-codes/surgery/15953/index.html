15953 excision trchntr pr ulc flp ostc

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: trochanteric ulcer flap osteo, trochanteric flap bone removal

Excision of trochanteric pressure ulcer with skin flap closure and removal of underlying bone for complete defect management.

Clinical Context

Used for larger trochanteric pressure ulcers with bone involvement. Combines flap reconstruction with osteectomy for comprehensive treatment.
