34717 Evasc repair a-iliac ndgft

Surgery

Also known as: aortoiliac EVAR no graft, percutaneous aortoiliac repair

Endovascular repair of aortiliac artery without graft. Percutaneous treatment of aortoiliac pathology without prosthetic graft material.

Clinical Context

Used for aortoiliac stenosis or occlusion. Bare-metal stent or other non-graft endovascular intervention.
