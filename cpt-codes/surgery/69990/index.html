69990 Microsurgery add-on

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: microsurgery add-on, operative microscopy

Add-on code for microsurgical techniques using operative microscope during otologic or neurotologic procedures for enhanced visualization.

Clinical Context

Used in conjunction with other temporal bone codes when operative microscopy enhances visualization during complex ossicular, nerve, or inner ear surgery.
