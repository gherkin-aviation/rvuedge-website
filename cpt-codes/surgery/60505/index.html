60505 Explore parathyroid glands

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: bilateral parathyroid exploration, four-gland parathyroid surgery, bilateral parathyroidectomy

Bilateral exploration of parathyroid glands with assessment of all four glands and removal of abnormal or hyperplastic tissue for primary hyperparathyroidism management.

In Plain Language

surgery to examine all parathyroid glands; complete parathyroid gland evaluation and treatment

Clinical Context

Performed when preoperative imaging is inconclusive or when multiglandular disease is present or suspected. Allows intraoperative assessment of all four glands with selective removal of enlarged/abnormal glands.
