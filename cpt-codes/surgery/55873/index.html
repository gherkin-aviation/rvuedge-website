55873 Cryoablate prostate

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: cryotherapy, prostate cryoablation

Cryoablation (freezing) of prostate tissue for cancer or benign disease using cryoprobes.

Clinical Context

Minimally invasive ablation of prostate tissue using extreme cold for cancer or symptomatic benign hyperplasia.
