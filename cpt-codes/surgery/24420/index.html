24420 Osteoplasty humerus

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: humerus osteoplasty, bone reshaping

Osteoplasty of the humerus. Surgical reshaping or contouring of the humerus bone.

Clinical Context

Used to modify bone shape or contour of the humerus for correction of deformity or improvement of mechanics.
