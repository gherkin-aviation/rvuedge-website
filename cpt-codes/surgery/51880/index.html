51880 Repair of bladder opening

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: bladder fistula repair, vesical defect closure

Surgical repair of a bladder fistula, an abnormal opening in the bladder wall that allows urine to leak into surrounding tissues. This procedure involves identifying and closing the defect with sutures.

Clinical Context

Performed for bladder fistulas from trauma, prior surgery, or urinary obstruction complications. Allows restoration of normal urinary function and prevents chronic infection or skin damage.
