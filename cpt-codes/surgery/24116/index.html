24116 excision/curettage bone cyst/tumor humerus algr

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: humerus cyst excision with graft, humerus tumor removal with reconstruction

Excision or curettage of a benign cyst or tumor of the humerus including all grafts, with complex closure or reconstruction. More extensive than 24115 with additional grafting complexity.

Clinical Context

Used for larger or more complex benign bone lesions of the humerus requiring excision with autograft or allograft reconstruction, addressing cases with significant bone loss.
