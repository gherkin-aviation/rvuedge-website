37257 Revsc evsc ivt angio complex each

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: infrapopliteal angioplasty complex additional, additional complex tibial angioplasty

Percutaneous or open revascularization of the infrapopliteal artery (tibial or peroneal) using complex techniques in each additional vessel.

Clinical Context

Each additional infrapopliteal vessel with complex pathology. Increases procedural time and complexity significantly.
