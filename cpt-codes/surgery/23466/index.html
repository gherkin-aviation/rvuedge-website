23466 Repair shoulder capsule

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: capsular repair with RC repair, combined repair, capsulorrhaphy with RC repair

Repair of shoulder capsule (with rotator cuff involvement). Combined capsular and rotator cuff repair in same surgical session.

In Plain Language

repair of both shoulder tightness and muscle tear; combined shoulder repair

Clinical Context

Applied when both capsular pathology (instability or contracture) and rotator cuff pathology coexist requiring concurrent repair in same operative session.
