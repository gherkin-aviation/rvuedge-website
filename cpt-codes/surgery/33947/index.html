33947 Ecmo/ecls initiation artery

Surgery

Also known as: ECMO initiation arterial, ECLS arterial setup, arterial cannulation ECMO

Extracorporeal membrane oxygenation/extracorporeal life support initiation with arterial cannulation; percutaneous or surgical placement of arterial cannula for extracorporeal oxygenation and perfusion.

In Plain Language

Placement of life support machine artery tube; Heart-lung machine artery connection

Clinical Context

Initial placement of arterial cannula for establishment of ECMO support providing both oxygenation and circulatory support in acute cardiopulmonary failure.
