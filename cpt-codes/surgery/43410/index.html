43410 Repair esophagus wound

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: cervical esophageal repair, esophageal laceration repair cervical, esophageal wound repair

Repair of esophageal laceration or wound via cervical approach with primary tissue closure. This addresses injuries in the cervical esophagus.

In Plain Language

stitching esophageal tear in neck; cervical esophageal repair

Clinical Context

Management of iatrogenic or traumatic cervical esophageal injuries; cervical approach allows safe repair with lower morbidity.
