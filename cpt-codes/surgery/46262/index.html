46262 Remove in/ex hem grps with fist

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: extensive hem removal with fistula, multiple group hemorrhoidectomy with fistula repair

Surgical removal of internal and external hemorrhoid groups (2 or more) with concurrent anal fistula treatment. Complex procedure addressing extensive hemorrhoid disease and fistula.

In Plain Language

removing multiple hemorrhoids and closing an anal fistula; comprehensive treatment of hemorrhoids and fistula

Clinical Context

Performed when extensive hemorrhoids coexist with anal fistulas. Addresses all pathology simultaneously, though complex and carries higher morbidity.
