20225 Bone biopsy trocar/needle deep

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: deep bone biopsy, bone marrow biopsy

Bone biopsy using needle or trocar technique to obtain deep bone marrow or trabecular bone sample. This procedure accesses deeper bone structures for diagnostic evaluation.

Clinical Context

Used to diagnose deep bone pathology, hematologic disorders, infections, or malignancy involving bone marrow or cancellous bone.
