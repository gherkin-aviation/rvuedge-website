65450 Treatment of corneal lesion

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: corneal lesion destruction, corneal cautery

Destruction of lesion of cornea by cryotherapy, photocoagulation, or thermocautery.

Clinical Context

Destruction of corneal dystrophy, scarring, or benign lesions using cryotherapy, laser, or cautery. Selective approach depends on lesion characteristics and location.
