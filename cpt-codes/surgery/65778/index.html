65778 Cover eye with membrane

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: amniotic membrane transplant, AMT, biological dressing

Placement of amniotic membrane on the ocular surface; without sutures.

In Plain Language

placing special membrane on eye surface; applying fetal tissue dressing to eye

Clinical Context

Placement of amniotic membrane for severe dry eye, ocular surface disease, or protective covering after surgery. Promotes healing and reduces inflammation.
