50387 Change nephroureteral catheter

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: nephrostomy tube exchange, catheter exchange, percutaneous catheter change

Percutaneous change (exchange) of a nephroureteral catheter (combined kidney and ureter drainage) under fluoroscopic guidance.

Clinical Context

Used for maintenance of percutaneous access when internal ureteral stenting is insufficient or complex obstruction requires dual drainage.
