33976 Implant ventricular device

Surgery

Also known as: Biventricular VAD, second VAD implant, BVAD implantation

Implantation of ventricular assist device (second system); surgical implantation of additional or biventricular ventricular assist device.

Clinical Context

Implantation of second (right) ventricular assist device when biventricular support is needed for severe biventricular dysfunction.
