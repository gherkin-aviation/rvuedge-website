23802 Arthrd glenohumeral joint with grf

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: shoulder arthrodesis with graft, shoulder fusion with bone graft, arthrodesis with bone graft

Arthrodesis of glenohumeral joint (shoulder fusion) with bone graft. Surgical fusion of shoulder joint with osseous graft for enhanced stability and union.

Clinical Context

Performed when glenohumeral arthrodesis requires graft augmentation for bone deficiency, poor vascularity, or when optimal fusion position requires graft-assisted fixation.
