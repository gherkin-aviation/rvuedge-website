28080 Removal of foot lesion

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: plantar mass excision, foot plantar lesion removal, plantar wart removal

Excision of plantar lesion or mass of the foot. This procedure removes benign masses or lesions from the plantar (bottom) surface of the foot.

Clinical Context

Performed for plantar warts, fibromas, or other plantar lesions causing pain with weight bearing. Often combined with other foot procedures.
