51940 Correction of bladder defect

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: bladder augmentation, bladder reconstruction, bladder contracture repair

Surgical correction of a significant bladder defect or contracture, often involving partial cystectomy with reconstruction or bladder augmentation using bowel interposition to restore bladder capacity and function.

In Plain Language

enlarging a damaged bladder; reconstructing the bladder; bladder capacity restoration

Clinical Context

Performed for severe bladder contracture from chronic irritation, radiation, or chemical cystitis. Improves incontinence, reduces urinary tract infections, and protects renal function.
