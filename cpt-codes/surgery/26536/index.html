26536 Revise/implant finger joint

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Finger joint implant arthroplasty, Prosthetic finger joint, PIP implant replacement

Surgical arthroplasty of a finger joint with placement of an implant, such as a prosthetic joint replacement, to restore joint function.

In Plain Language

Replacing a damaged finger joint with an artificial joint; Finger joint implant surgery

Clinical Context

Performed for severe finger joint arthropathy where joint replacement provides better functional outcome than fusion or simple arthroplasty.
