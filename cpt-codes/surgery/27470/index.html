27470 Repair of thigh

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: femur fracture repair, femoral fracture fixation, ORIF

Surgical repair of a thigh fracture through reduction and fixation using plates, screws, intramedullary devices, or other internal fixation methods.

Clinical Context

Used for femoral shaft or subtrochanteric fractures requiring operative fixation. Very common orthopedic procedure.
