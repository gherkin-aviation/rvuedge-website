36261 Revision of infusion pump

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: infusion pump revision, pump replacement, catheter revision

Revision (repair, replacement, or repositioning) of previously implanted intra-arterial infusion pump and catheter system. Device maintenance.

Clinical Context

Performed for pump malfunction, catheter thrombosis, infection, or device failure.
