50437 Dilation xst trc new access rcs

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: new tract dilation, percutaneous access creation, tract establishment

Dilation of a new percutaneous tract for improved access during percutaneous nephrolithotomy or other therapeutic procedures.

Clinical Context

Used to create and dilate a percutaneous tract when new percutaneous access is required for complex stone removal.
