58553 Laparo-vag hyst complex

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: complex LAVH, difficult laparoscopic-assisted hysterectomy

Complex laparoscopic-assisted vaginal hysterectomy involves challenging pathology requiring increased operative time and skill via hybrid approach.

In Plain Language

Complex hybrid uterus removal; Difficult hybrid hysterectomy

Clinical Context

Used for complex pathology (dense adhesions, severe endometriosis, large fibroids) when hybrid approach chosen. Requires advanced laparoscopic and vaginal surgical skills.
