63278 biopsy/excision xdrl spine lesion scrl

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: sacral extradural lesion removal, sacral epidural tumor excision

Biopsy and/or excision of extradural spinal lesion in the sacral region. This procedure involves sampling or removal of pathological lesions outside the dura at the sacral level.

In Plain Language

removal of growth outside spinal sac in sacral area

Clinical Context

Used for sacral epidural lesions including metastases or other extradural pathology.
