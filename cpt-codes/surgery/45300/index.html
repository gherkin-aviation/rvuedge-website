45300 Proctosigmoidoscopy diagnosis

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: proctoscopy, sigmoidoscopy, rectal exam with scope

Diagnostic proctosigmoidoscopy (visual examination of rectum and sigmoid colon) without therapeutic intervention.

In Plain Language

camera test of lower intestines; viewing the rectum and sigmoid colon; lower bowel visualization

Clinical Context

Performed for evaluation of rectal bleeding, constipation, diarrhea, abdominal pain, or screening in patients with lower gastrointestinal symptoms.
