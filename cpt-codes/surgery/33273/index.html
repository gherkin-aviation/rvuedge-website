33273 Reposition preventive impltbl subcutaneous defibrillator

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: S-ICD repositioning, subcutaneous defibrillator relocation

Repositioning of a previously implanted subcutaneous defibrillator system, moving or adjusting the device position without complete removal.

Clinical Context

Performed for patient comfort, device-related complications, or optimization of device placement.
