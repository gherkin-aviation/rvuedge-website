44020 Explore small intestine

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: exploratory laparotomy small bowel, small intestine exploration, SBE

Exploration of small intestine via open approach for diagnosis, assessment, or management of intra-abdominal pathology.

In Plain Language

small intestine examination; bowel investigation surgery

Clinical Context

Systematic exploration of small bowel in open surgery for evaluation of obstruction, bleeding, perforation, or other pathology. Does not include resection.
