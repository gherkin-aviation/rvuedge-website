42800 Biopsy of throat

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: throat biopsy, pharyngeal biopsy

Biopsy of the pharynx (throat) tissue obtained via transoral approach to establish tissue diagnosis.

In Plain Language

tissue sample from throat; throat tissue biopsy

Clinical Context

Used when suspected pathology in the pharynx requires tissue diagnosis. Common indications include suspected malignancy, persistent ulcers, or inflammatory conditions.
