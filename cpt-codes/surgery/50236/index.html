50236 Removal of kidney & ureter

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: radical nephroureterectomy, nephroureterectomy with cuff, en bloc resection

Open surgical removal of the kidney and ureter (nephroureterectomy) for malignancy including en bloc resection of bladder cuff for complete tumor removal.

In Plain Language

surgical removal of kidney, ureter, and bladder tissue; complete upper urinary tract removal

Clinical Context

Used for high-grade or invasive upper tract urothelial carcinoma to reduce intravesical recurrence by removing the distal ureter and adjacent bladder tissue.
