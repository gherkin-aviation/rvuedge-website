35522 Artery byp graft axill-brachial

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: axillary-brachial bypass, AB bypass

Arterial bypass graft from axillary to brachial artery with restoration of forearm and hand blood flow.

Clinical Context

Revascularization for brachial artery stenosis/occlusion causing hand ischemia. Provides direct perfusion bypass for diseased upper extremity vessels.
