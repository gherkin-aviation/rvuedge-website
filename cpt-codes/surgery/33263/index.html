33263 removal & replacement defibrillator gen 2 lead

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ICD generator replacement 2 leads, defibrillator box change dual lead

Removal and replacement of implantable cardioverter-defibrillator (defibrillator) generator with two leads, removing the old device and implanting a new one.

In Plain Language

replacing defibrillator battery with two wires

Clinical Context

Common procedure for ICD battery depletion or device malfunction; dual-lead ICDs typical for most patients.
