28705 Arthrodesis pantalar

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: pantalar fusion, pan-talar arthrodesis, triple fusion ankle

Surgical arthrodesis (fusion) of the pantalar joint (pan-talar fusion), permanently fusing the talus to the calcaneus, navicular, and tibia for severe ankle arthritis or instability.

Clinical Context

Used for severe ankle arthritis, chronic instability, or post-traumatic arthritis unresponsive to conservative treatment; provides pain relief at cost of ankle motion.
