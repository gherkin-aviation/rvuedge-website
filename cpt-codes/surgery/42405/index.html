42405 Biopsy of salivary gland

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: salivary gland biopsy, open biopsy

Surgical open biopsy or partial excision of salivary gland tissue for diagnosis and potential therapeutic removal of lesions.

Clinical Context

Open surgical biopsy of salivary gland masses, tumors, or lesions requiring tissue diagnosis and potential specimen removal.
