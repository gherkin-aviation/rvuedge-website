27707 Osteotomy fibula

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: fibular osteotomy, fibula realignment

Surgical cutting and realignment of fibular bone (osteotomy) for deformity correction or proximal tibio-fibular joint management.

In Plain Language

realignment of outer leg bone

Clinical Context

Used for fibular deformity or occasionally to correct proximal tibiofibular joint pathology. Less commonly performed than tibial osteotomy.
