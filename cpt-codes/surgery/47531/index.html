47531 Injection for cholangiogram

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: Percutaneous cholangiogram, transhepatic cholangiogram

Injection of contrast material into the biliary tree through a percutaneous catheter to visualize the bile ducts and hepatic ducts on cholangiogram (X-ray imaging).

Clinical Context

Diagnostic procedure for evaluating biliary obstruction, strictures, or bile leak. Often performed as first step in percutaneous biliary drainage procedures.
