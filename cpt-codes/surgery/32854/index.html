32854 Lung transplant with bypass

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: double lung transplant with bypass, bilateral lung transplant with CPB, DLTx variant

Double lung transplantation with cardiopulmonary bypass support, alternative code for specific donor-recipient scenarios. Surgical placement of bilateral lungs using extracorporeal circulation.

In Plain Language

transplant of two new lungs with machine support

Clinical Context

Used for double lung recipients requiring hemodynamic support due to pulmonary hypertension, right heart failure, or hemodynamic instability during transplantation.
