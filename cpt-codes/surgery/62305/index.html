62305 Myelography lumbar injection

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: fluoroscopic myelography, image-guided myelography lumbar

Myelography with lumbar injection and spinal puncture with image-guided or fluoroscopic technique. Includes real-time imaging guidance.

Clinical Context

Used for diagnostic imaging of lumbar spine with fluoroscopic guidance for precise needle placement and imaging.
