27590 Amputate leg at thigh

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: above-knee amputation, AKA, disarticulation

Surgical amputation of the lower extremity through the femur (above-knee amputation) for severe trauma, infection, malignancy, or vascular insufficiency.

Clinical Context

Used for severe limb-threatening conditions including trauma, infection, malignancy, or vascular disease.
