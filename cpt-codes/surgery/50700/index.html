50700 Revision of ureter

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: ureteral revision, ureter reoperation, revision ureteroplasty

Surgical revision or reoperation of the ureter for correction of previously failed procedures or new pathology.

Clinical Context

Used for ureteral stricture, failed previous repair, or complications of prior ureteral surgery.
