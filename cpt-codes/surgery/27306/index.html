27306 Incision of thigh tendon

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: tenotomy thigh, tendon division, tendon release

Incision of thigh tendon for access, release, or division to address contracture or improve range of motion.

Clinical Context

Used for tendon contractures, spasticity, or fixed deformities requiring surgical division or lengthening.
