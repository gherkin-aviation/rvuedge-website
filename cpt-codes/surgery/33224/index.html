33224 Insert pacing lead & connect

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: add pacing lead to ICD, ICD pacing lead addition, single-lead addition

Insertion of single transvenous pacing lead and connection to existing defibrillator pulse generator. Addition of pacing lead to existing ICD.

Clinical Context

Used to convert ICD to ICD-pacemaker (CRT-D or DDD) for patients developing bradycardia or requiring synchronized pacing.
