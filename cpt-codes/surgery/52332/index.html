52332 Cystoscopy and treatment

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: cysto with laser ablation

Cystourethroscopy with ablation or resection of bladder lesions using laser energy for precision tissue removal with controlled hemostasis.

In Plain Language

laser treatment of bladder lesion

Clinical Context

Useful for vascular lesions, cysts, or polyps with bleeding potential. Laser provides precise control of tissue depth.

Billing & Documentation
