14000 Tissue trnfr trunk 10 square centimeters/<

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Adjacent tissue transfer, Tissue rearrangement, Local flap

Tissue transfer or graft from adjacent area to cover trunk defect measuring ten square centimeters or less.

Clinical Context

Used for coverage of small trunk defects using tissue adjacent to the defect. Common for closure after tumor removal or traumatic loss.
