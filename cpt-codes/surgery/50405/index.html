50405 Revision of kidney/ureter

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: complex revision nephroplasty, extensive revision, redo pyeloplasty

Surgical revision or reoperation of the kidney and/or ureter with more extensive dissection or reconstruction for complex revision cases.

Clinical Context

Used for failed pyeloplasty with significant scarring, multiple prior repairs, or concurrent vascular or parenchymal issues.
