27822 Treatment of ankle fracture

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: open reduction trimalleolar, trimalleolar ankle ORIF

Initial open treatment of ankle fracture (trimalleolar) with direct visualization and internal fixation.

In Plain Language

surgical repair of broken ankle (3 sides)

Clinical Context

Indicated for displaced trimalleolar ankle fractures requiring open reduction and anatomic fixation of all three malleoli.
