57310 Repair urethrovaginal lesion

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Urethrovaginal fistula repair

Repair of urethrovaginal fistula. Surgical closure of abnormal communication between urethra and vagina.

In Plain Language

Closing hole between urethra and vagina

Clinical Context

Used for urethrovaginal fistulas from trauma or erosion. Restores urinary continence.
