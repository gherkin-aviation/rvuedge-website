31288 Nasal/sinus endoscopy surgical

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: endoscopic ESS complex, endoscopic sinus surgery advanced, reconstructive endoscopy

Nasal and sinus endoscopic surgery with additional management or reconstruction procedure, often including orbital or intracranial considerations.

Clinical Context

Advanced endoscopic sinus surgery involving reconstruction or management of complicated anatomy. For revision cases or complications.
