26477 Tendon shortening

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Tendon shortening, Tendon plication, Tendon tightening

Surgical shortening of tendons in the hand to address laxity, improve functional positioning, or prevent overstretching of weakened tendon units.

In Plain Language

Tightening a loose tendon in the hand; Shortening a tendon for better hand function

Clinical Context

Performed for lax or overstretched tendons that result in loss of functional positioning or grip strength, particularly in neurologic or paralytic conditions.
