24140 Partial excision bone humerus

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: humerus diaphysectomy, humerus segmental excision

Partial excision (diaphysectomy) of the humerus shaft. Surgical removal of a segment of the humerus shaft, typically for infection, tumor, or other pathology.

Clinical Context

Used for chronic osteomyelitis, segmental defects, or pathologic lesions requiring removal of a portion of the humerus shaft.
