11641 excision f/e/e/n/l mal+mrg 0.6-1

Surgery Global 10d

Also known as: facial cancer excision, lip cancer removal

Excision of malignant skin lesion on face, ear, eyelid, nose, or lip with margins of 0.6 to 1 centimeter. Includes closure of the excision site.

Clinical Context

Used for removal of skin cancers on facial structures with moderate surgical margins.
