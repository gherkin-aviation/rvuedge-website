64448 Njx aa and/or strd fem nerve nfs img

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: femoral nerve block with branches, saphenous nerve block, medial/lateral femoral cutaneous block

Nerve injection for anesthetic and/or steroid administration to the femoral nerve with imaging guidance, including administration to associated nerve branches. Ultrasound localization of multiple branches.

Clinical Context

Used for comprehensive lower extremity anesthesia including cutaneous branches. Provides broader coverage than femoral nerve alone for knee surgery and thigh pain.
