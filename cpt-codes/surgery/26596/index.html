26596 Excision constricting tissue

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Constricting tissue excision, Scar excision, Tissue release

Surgical excision of constricting tissue in the hand, including fascia, scar, or other structures limiting motion or causing functional impairment.

Clinical Context

Indicated for scar contracture, adhesions, or constricting tissue affecting hand function, particularly post-traumatic or post-burn scenarios.
