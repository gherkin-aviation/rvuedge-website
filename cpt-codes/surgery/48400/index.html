48400 Injection intraop add-on

Surgery +Add-on

Also known as: Intraoperative injection, dye injection, contrast injection

Intraoperative injection of contrast or dye to facilitate visualization and identification of pancreatic anatomy, ducts, or islet cells during surgical procedure.

Clinical Context

Used to identify pancreatic ducts, locate islet cells for partial pancreatectomy, or confirm anatomic integrity.
