45402 Laparoscopic proctopexy with sig resection

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: laparoscopic rectopexy with sigmoidectomy, lap prolapse repair with resection

Laparoscopic proctopexy with sigmoid colon resection for rectal prolapse or redundant sigmoid repair.

Clinical Context

Used for full-thickness rectal prolapse with redundant sigmoid requiring both fixation and sigmoid resection via laparoscopic approach.
