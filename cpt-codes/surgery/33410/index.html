33410 Replacement aortic valve opn

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: aortic valve replacement with other procedure

Replacement of aortic valve via open surgical approach with mitral valve involvement or concomitant cardiac procedure.

Clinical Context

Used when aortic valve replacement is performed with another cardiac operation.
