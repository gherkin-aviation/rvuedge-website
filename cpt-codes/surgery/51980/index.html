51980 Construct bladder opening

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: vesicostomy, bladder stoma creation, bladder opening creation

Surgical creation of a new opening (stoma) between the bladder and the abdominal wall skin, typically a vesicostomy, allowing direct bladder drainage externally via an appliance.

In Plain Language

creating an opening from the bladder to the skin; making a bladder opening

Clinical Context

Used as an alternative urinary diversion in patients with severe neurogenic bladder, spinal cord injury, or when other options are not feasible. Allows non-catheterizable bladder.
