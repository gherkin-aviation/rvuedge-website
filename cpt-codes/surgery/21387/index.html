21387 Opn treatment orbit fracture combined

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: orbital fracture combined approach, combined orbital access

Open treatment of orbital fracture using combined approaches (transantral and periorbital) for comprehensive access and reduction.

Clinical Context

Used for complex orbital fractures requiring exposure from multiple approaches for optimal reduction.
