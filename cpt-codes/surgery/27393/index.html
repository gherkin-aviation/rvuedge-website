27393 Lengthening of thigh tendon

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: thigh tendon lengthening, plasty procedure

Lengthening of thigh tendon through operative approach using lengthening procedure.

Clinical Context

Applied to hamstring or quadriceps contractures requiring surgical lengthening to improve range of motion.
