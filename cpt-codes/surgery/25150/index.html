25150 Partial removal of ulna

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: partial ulnectomy, ulnar resection repair

Partial removal (segmental resection) of the ulna bone with restoration of forearm length or radioulnar relationships.

Clinical Context

Used for ulnar pathology including nonunion, malunion, or tumor. Often combined with radius procedures to restore radioulnar mechanics.
