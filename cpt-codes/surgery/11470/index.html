11470 excision skn h/p/p/u simple/ntrm

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: hip/perianal excision, perineal excision

Excision of skin lesion involving hidden skin fold area such as hip, perianal, or perineal with simple or intermediate closure. Includes wound management of complex anatomical area.

Clinical Context

Used for removal of lesions in perianal, perineal, or hip areas with simple closure, often for hidradenitis suppurativa.
