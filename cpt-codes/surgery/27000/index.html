27000 Tenotomy adductor hip percutaneous

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: adductor tenotomy, percutaneous adductor release, limited incision adductor release

Surgical release of the adductor longus tendon at the hip through a percutaneous or limited approach without open dissection.

In Plain Language

releasing adductor muscle tendon; hip muscle tendon release

Clinical Context

Performed for hip adductor muscle contracture, spasticity in cerebral palsy, or hip pain from adductor muscle tightness via limited incision.
