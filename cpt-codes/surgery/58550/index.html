58550 Laparo-asst vag hysterectomy

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: LAVH, laparoscopic-assisted vaginal hysterectomy

Laparoscopic-assisted vaginal hysterectomy involves laparoscopic mobilization of uterus with vaginal removal to combine benefits of minimally invasive and vaginal approaches.

In Plain Language

Laparoscopic-assisted uterus removal; Hybrid uterus removal surgery

Clinical Context

Bridges minimally invasive and vaginal approaches. Allows assessment of upper abdomen and pelvis while completing hysterectomy vaginally. Less commonly used with increased TLH utilization.
