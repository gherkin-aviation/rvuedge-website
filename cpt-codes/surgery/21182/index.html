21182 Reconstruct cranial bone

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: cranial reconstruction, skull reconstruction

Reconstruction of cranial bone following tumor removal or trauma, involving replacement of bone and restoration of structural integrity.

Clinical Context

Used after cranial vault defects from tumor, trauma, or infection. Restores protection and cosmetic contour.
