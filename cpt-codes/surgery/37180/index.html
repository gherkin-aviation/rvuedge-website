37180 Revision of circulation

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: lower extremity graft revision, fem-tib revision

Revision of a vascular graft in the lower extremity circulation, typically involving femoral, popliteal, or tibial arteries with thrombectomy.

Clinical Context

Performed when lower extremity vascular grafts fail, common in patients with critical limb ischemia or prior amputation risk. Preserves limb viability.
