27328 excision thigh/knee tumor deep <5cm

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: deep soft tissue excision, tumor excision <5cm

Excision of soft tissue tumor of thigh or knee in deep tissues measuring less than 5 centimeters.

In Plain Language

Deep bump removal from leg; Deep muscle tumor removal

Clinical Context

Applied to subfascial or intramuscular tumors less than 5 centimeters requiring operative excision.
