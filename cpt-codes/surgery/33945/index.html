33945 Transplantation of heart

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: Heart transplant, orthotopic heart transplant, cardiac transplantation

Transplantation of heart; surgical implantation of donor heart into recipient for treatment of end-stage heart disease.

Clinical Context

Definitive surgical treatment for end-stage heart disease refractory to medical management and mechanical support. Requires careful management of cardiopulmonary bypass, allograft preservation, and anastomotic technique.
