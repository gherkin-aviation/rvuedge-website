50557 Kidney endoscopy & treatment

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: percutaneous stone removal, nephroscopic treatment, percutaneous renal intervention

Percutaneous endoscopy of the kidney (renal endoscopy) with therapeutic treatment including stone removal, coagulation, or ablation.

Clinical Context

Used for therapeutic stone removal or lesion treatment via percutaneous nephroscopic approach.
