27786 Treatment of ankle fracture

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: closed reduction lateral ankle, conservative ankle fracture

Initial closed treatment of ankle fracture (lateral malleolus) with manipulation and immobilization.

Clinical Context

Used for uncomplicated, non-displaced lateral malleolar fractures. Non-operative approach with casting.
