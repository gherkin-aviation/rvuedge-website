11044 Debridement bone first 20 square centimeters/<

Surgery Global 0d

Also known as: Bone debridement, Bone removal, debridement to bone, wound debridement bone

Debridement of bone for first twenty square centimeters or less. Removal of necrotic or contaminated bone tissue.

In Plain Language

Bone tissue removal; Dead bone cleanup

Clinical Context

Used for osteomyelitis, bone necrosis, or removal of damaged bone fragments in trauma.
