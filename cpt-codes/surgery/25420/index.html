25420 Repair/graft radius & ulna

Surgery Global 90d

Also known as: both-bone repair with graft

Repair of both the radius and ulna with autogenous bone graft for nonunion or significant bone loss.

In Plain Language

fixing both forearm bones with grafting

Clinical Context

Used for infected nonunion of both bones, segmental defects, or comminuted fractures with bone loss.
