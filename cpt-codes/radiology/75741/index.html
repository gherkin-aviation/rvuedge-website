75741 Artery x-rays lung

Radiology

Also known as: pulmonary angiography, pulmonary artery imaging, lung vessel study

Angiographic radiographic imaging of pulmonary arteries to assess lung vasculature and screen for pulmonary arterial hypertension or pulmonary embolism.

Clinical Context

Evaluates pulmonary embolism, pulmonary hypertension, pulmonary vasculitis, and lung masses; provides definitive assessment of pulmonary vascular disease.
