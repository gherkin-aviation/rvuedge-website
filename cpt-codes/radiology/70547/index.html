70547 Mr angiography neck without dye

Radiology

Also known as: MRA neck, carotid MRA, vertebral artery MRA, noncontrast neck angiography

Magnetic resonance angiography of the cervical arteries including the neck vasculature without intravenous gadolinium contrast medium using inherent blood flow signal.

Clinical Context

Used for carotid stenosis evaluation, vertebral artery pathology assessment, and stroke risk stratification. Noncontrast technique provides reasonable vessel visualization with no gadolinium administration.
