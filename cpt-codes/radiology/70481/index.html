70481 CT orbit/ear/fossa with dye

Radiology

Also known as: CT orbit with contrast, enhanced orbital imaging, temporal bone CTA

Computed tomography scan of the orbits, ears, and temporal fossae with intravenous contrast medium enhancement. Provides detailed visualization of orbital soft tissues, vascular structures, and inflammatory changes.

Clinical Context

Indicated for orbital cellulitis, cavernous sinus thrombosis, orbital tumors with vascular assessment, and inflammatory orbital diseases. Contrast enhancement improves visualization of soft tissue involvement and vasculature.
