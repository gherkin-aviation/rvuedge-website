78300 Bone imaging limited area

Radiology

Also known as: bone scan limited, skeletal imaging limited

Bone imaging of limited anatomic area performed using nuclear medicine to assess skeletal uptake and detect pathology.

Clinical Context

Single-site skeletal imaging for suspected fracture, infection, or metastasis. Used when focal pathology is suspected.
