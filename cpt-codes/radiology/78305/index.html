78305 Bone imaging multiple areas

Radiology

Also known as: bone scan multiple areas

Bone imaging of multiple anatomic areas performed to assess multiple skeletal sites for pathology.

Clinical Context

Multi-site skeletal imaging for metastatic disease, multiple fractures, or systemic bone disease evaluation.
