75989 Abscess drainage under x-ray

Radiology

Also known as: abscess drainage, percutaneous drainage, image-guided drainage

Radiographically-guided percutaneous drainage of an infected collection using needle/catheter placement under continuous fluoroscopic visualization.

In Plain Language

X-ray guided abscess drainage; guided infection drainage

Clinical Context

Percutaneous drainage of intra-abdominal, pelvic, or thoracic abscesses under fluoroscopic guidance for treatment of infection.
