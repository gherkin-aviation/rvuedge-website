74328 X-ray bile duct endoscopy

Radiology

Also known as: endoscopic cholangiography, ERCP X-ray, retrograde cholangiography

Radiographic imaging of the bile ducts using contrast medium delivered via endoscopic retrograde technique during endoscopic retrograde cholangiopancreatography procedure.

Clinical Context

Diagnostic and therapeutic endoscopic procedure for evaluating and treating biliary obstruction, sphincter of Oddi dysfunction, and choledocholithiasis.
