71111 X-ray examination ribs/chest4/> vws

Radiology

Also known as: bilateral chest and rib X-ray, complete thoracic series, extensive rib examination

Bilateral rib and chest radiography including four or more views providing comprehensive evaluation of the entire thorax, both ribs, and chest cavity.

In Plain Language

complete chest and rib imaging; full thorax X-ray

Clinical Context

Most comprehensive radiographic assessment when detailed bilateral rib and pulmonary evaluation is required. Useful for complex polytrauma, extensive rib disease, or detailed thoracic assessment.
