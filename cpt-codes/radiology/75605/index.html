75605 Contrast examination thoracic aorta

Radiology

Also known as: thoracic aortography with branches, arch aortography, comprehensive aortic imaging

Radiographic imaging of the thoracic aorta using contrast medium with additional imaging including arch branches and descending aorta for comprehensive evaluation.

Clinical Context

Comprehensive evaluation of aortic arch, ascending, and descending thoracic aorta; assesses branch vessels and complex aortic pathology.
