72052 X-ray examination neck spine 6/>vws

Radiology

Also known as: extended cervical spine series, comprehensive C-spine radiography

Radiography of the cervical spine including six or more views providing maximum radiographic detail of cervical vertebrae from multiple projections and angles.

Clinical Context

Most comprehensive radiographic cervical assessment, particularly useful for detailed fracture detection, instability assessment, and when multiple oblique or specialized views are needed.
