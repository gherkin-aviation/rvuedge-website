77600 Hyperthermia external gen superficial

Radiology

Also known as: Superficial hyperthermia, External hyperthermia

Hyperthermia therapy externally applied using external heating source for superficial regions.

Clinical Context

External heating applied to superficial tumors and tissue. Used as adjunctive therapy with radiation and chemotherapy.
