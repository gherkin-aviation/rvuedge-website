76936 Echo guide for artery repair

Radiology

Also known as: Echo guided arterial repair, Ultrasound guided vascular intervention

Echocardiography guidance for arterial repair or intervention including ultrasound visualization and real-time guidance.

Clinical Context

Ultrasound guidance for arterial repair, shunt assessment, or other vascular interventions requiring real-time visualization.
