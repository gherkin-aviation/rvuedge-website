75902 Remove cva lumen obstruct

Radiology

Also known as: lumen declotting, graft declotting, access declotting

Radiographic imaging during catheter-based intervention to remove an obstruction from a vascular access device lumen to restore device patency.

Clinical Context

Removes intraluminal thrombus from dialysis grafts or vascular access devices using catheter-based technique.
