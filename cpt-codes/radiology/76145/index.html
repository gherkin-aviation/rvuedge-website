76145 Medial physic dos evaluation radiation exps

Radiology

Also known as: Physics evaluation, Dosimetry evaluation, Radiation safety check

Medical physicist's evaluation for radiation exposure dosimetry and safety assessment in radiologic procedures.

In Plain Language

Radiation safety review; Exposure calculation by physicist

Clinical Context

Medical physicist reviews radiation dose, technique, and safety considerations for radiologic examinations, particularly important for pediatric or high-dose procedures.
