73120 X-ray examination of hand

Radiology

Also known as: Hand X-ray 1-2 views, Basic hand X-ray

Plain radiographic examination of the hand in two or fewer views to assess carpal, metacarpal, and phalangeal bone anatomy and alignment.

Clinical Context

Evaluates hand fractures, arthritis, congenital abnormalities, and soft tissue masses. Standard posteroanterior and oblique views typical.
