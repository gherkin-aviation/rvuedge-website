74181 MRI abdomen without contrast

Radiology

Also known as: Abdominal MRI without contrast, Non-contrast abdomen MRI

Magnetic resonance imaging of the abdomen without intravenous gadolinium contrast to assess abdominal organs and detect pathology.

In Plain Language

MRI scan of the abdomen without dye

Clinical Context
