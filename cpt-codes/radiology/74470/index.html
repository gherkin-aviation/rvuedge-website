74470 X-ray examination of kidney lesion

Radiology

Also known as: renal angiography, renal arteriography, kidney imaging

Radiographic imaging of the kidney and renal lesion using contrast medium to characterize renal mass and assess for perfusion abnormalities.

Clinical Context

Evaluates renal artery stenosis, vasculitis, and renal mass vascularity; assesses for hypertension etiology and characterizes cystic versus solid lesions.
