76821 Middle cerebral artery echo

Radiology

Also known as: Middle cerebral artery Doppler, MCA Doppler

Obstetric Doppler ultrasound examination of middle cerebral artery to assess fetal intracranial blood flow.

Clinical Context

Assessment of middle cerebral artery peak systolic velocity to evaluate for fetal anemia, hypoxia, and intrauterine growth restriction.
