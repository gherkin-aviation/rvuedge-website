75801 Lymph vessel x-ray arm/leg

Radiology

Also known as: lymphangiography, limb lymphoscintigraphy, lymphatic imaging

Radiographic imaging of lymphatic vessels in an arm or leg using contrast medium injected subcutaneously to visualize lymphatic drainage.

Clinical Context

Evaluates lymphatic obstruction, lymphedema, lymph node anatomy, and lymphatic malformations in extremities.
