70355 Panoramic x-ray of jaws

Radiology

Also known as: panoramic X-ray, panoral, OPG, panorex

Panoramic radiographic imaging that captures a complete view of both the upper and lower jaws in a single radiograph. This extraoral technique shows the entire dentition and surrounding bone.

Clinical Context

Obtained during comprehensive dental exams or prior to major dental procedures such as extractions, implant planning, or orthodontic treatment. Allows assessment of all teeth, alveolar bone, and temporomandibular joints.
