72156 MRI neck spine without & with dye

Radiology

Also known as: cervical spine MRI with and without contrast, dual-phase C-spine MRI

Magnetic resonance imaging of the cervical spine performed with noncontrast and intravenous gadolinium contrast acquisitions. Comprehensive imaging combining baseline and enhanced sequences.

Clinical Context

Comprehensive cervical protocol when both baseline anatomy and enhanced pathology characterization are needed for complex cervical spine pathology.
