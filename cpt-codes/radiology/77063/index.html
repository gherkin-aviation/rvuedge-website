77063 Breast tomosynthesis bi

Radiology

Also known as: Tomosynthesis with mammography, Hybrid breast imaging

Breast tomosynthesis imaging of both breasts with supplemental single-view conventional mammography.

Clinical Context

Combines tomosynthesis with conventional mammography for comprehensive breast evaluation. Provides both three-dimensional and two-dimensional imaging perspectives.
