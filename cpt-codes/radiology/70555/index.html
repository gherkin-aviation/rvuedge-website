70555 Fmri brain by phys/psychiatric

Radiology

Also known as: fMRI brain physician interpretation, functional MRI professional analysis

Functional magnetic resonance imaging of the brain performed and interpreted by a physician or other qualified health care professional. Includes detailed analysis and clinical correlation of brain activation maps.

Clinical Context

Clinical application of functional imaging with full radiologist or neurologist interpretation for pre-operative planning, characterization of eloquent cortex, and detailed clinical correlation.
