78761 Testicular imaging with flow

Radiology

Also known as: testicular imaging, scrotal scan

Nuclear medicine imaging of testis with blood flow assessment to evaluate scrotal contents.

Clinical Context

Evaluates testicular viability and perfusion; used for suspected testicular torsion (emergency), trauma, or epididymitis to assess testicular blood supply.
