72125 CT neck spine without dye

Radiology

Also known as: cervical spine CT, C-spine CT noncontrast, neck spine imaging

Computed tomography of the cervical spine without intravenous contrast medium. High-resolution imaging evaluates cervical vertebrae, spinal canal, and neural foramina.

In Plain Language

neck spine scan without dye; cervical CT imaging

Clinical Context

Used for cervical trauma, myelopathy evaluation, and assessment of cervical pathology. Excellent for detecting fractures and evaluating spinal canal narrowing.
