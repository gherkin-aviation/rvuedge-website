76830 Transvaginal ultrasound non-ob

Radiology

Also known as: Transvaginal ultrasound, Vaginal ultrasound, TVUS

Transvaginal ultrasound examination for non-obstetric evaluation of pelvic structures and gynecologic pathology.

Clinical Context

High-resolution transvaginal assessment of uterus, ovaries, and pelvic pathology for evaluation of pain, infertility, or suspected abnormalities.
