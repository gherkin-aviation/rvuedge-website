70540 MRI orbit/face/neck without dye

Radiology

Also known as: MRI orbit/face/neck, facial MRI noncontrast, neck MRI

Magnetic resonance imaging of the orbits, face, and neck without intravenous contrast medium. Provides excellent soft tissue contrast resolution for evaluation of orbital contents, facial structures, and neck tissues.

Clinical Context

Used for orbital masses, thyroid pathology, facial soft tissue evaluation, and neck masses. Excellent for soft tissue characterization without radiation. Noncontrast often sufficient for tissue characterization.
