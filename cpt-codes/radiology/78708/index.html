78708 K flow/funct image with drug

Radiology

Also known as: captopril renal scan, renal flow-function with drug

Nuclear medicine imaging of kidney to assess blood flow and function with pharmacologic agent (captopril or similar) administration to enhance diagnostic capability.

In Plain Language

kidney function test with medication; kidney scan with blood pressure medicine

Clinical Context

Evaluates for renovascular hypertension using pharmacologic stimulation; most commonly captopril renography to assess split renal function and detect renal artery stenosis.
