76776 Ultrasound examination k transpl with doppler

Radiology

Also known as: Renal transplant ultrasound, Transplant kidney ultrasound

Ultrasound examination of kidney transplant including Doppler assessment of vascular flow and perfusion.

Clinical Context

Assessment of transplant kidney for evaluation of size, echogenicity, collections, and vascular perfusion using Doppler imaging.
