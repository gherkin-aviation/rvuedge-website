76937 Ultrasound guide vascular access

Radiology

Also known as: Ultrasound guided vascular access, US guided line placement

Ultrasound guidance for vascular access including visualization of target vessel and needle placement.

In Plain Language

Ultrasound guided IV line; Sound wave guided needle placement in vein

Clinical Context

Ultrasound visualization to guide placement of central venous catheters, peripheral IV lines, or other vascular access devices.
