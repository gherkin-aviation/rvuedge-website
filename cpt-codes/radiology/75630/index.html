75630 X-ray aorta leg arteries

Radiology

Also known as: aorto-iliac-femoral angiography, full aortic imaging, aorta to leg angiography

Radiographic imaging of the aorta including both thoracic and abdominal segments with imaging of lower extremity arteries for complete arterial assessment.

In Plain Language

full body artery scan; complete aorta to leg imaging

Clinical Context

Comprehensive evaluation of aortic pathology with assessment of iliac and lower extremity arteries; evaluates peripheral arterial disease.
