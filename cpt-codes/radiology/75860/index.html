75860 Vein x-ray neck

Radiology

Also known as: neck venography, jugular vein imaging, cervical venography

Radiographic imaging of veins in the neck using contrast medium to assess neck venous anatomy and venous obstruction.

Clinical Context

Evaluates jugular vein thrombosis, neck venous obstruction, and superior vena cava syndrome.
