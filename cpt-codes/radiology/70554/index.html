70554 Fmri brain by tech

Radiology

Also known as: fMRI brain, functional MRI, blood oxygen level-dependent imaging, task-based fMRI

Functional magnetic resonance imaging of the brain performed and interpreted by a technologist or technician. Maps brain activation during specific tasks or at rest using blood oxygen level-dependent imaging.

In Plain Language

brain activity map; brain function scan

Clinical Context

Used for pre-operative brain mapping in patients with lesions near eloquent cortex, evaluation of brain function after stroke or injury, and research applications. Helps identify critical motor, language, and sensory areas.
