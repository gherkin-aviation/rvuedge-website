73502 X-ray examination hip uni 2-3 views

Radiology

Also known as: Unilateral hip X-ray 2-3 views, Unilateral hip imaging

Plain radiographic examination of one hip joint in two to three views to assess femoral head, acetabulum, and hip joint space.

In Plain Language

X-ray of one hip from multiple angles

Clinical Context

Standard evaluation of hip pain, fractures, dislocation, and osteoarthritis. Multiple views improve fracture detection and alignment assessment.
