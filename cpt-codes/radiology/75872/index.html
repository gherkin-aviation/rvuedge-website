75872 Vein x-ray skull epidural

Radiology

Also known as: epidural venography, intracranial epidural imaging, dural venography

Radiographic imaging of intracranial venous structures specifically including epidural venous plexuses for assessment of venous anatomy.

Clinical Context

Evaluates epidural hematoma, venous drainage patterns, and dural venous sinus anatomy.
