75889 Vein x-ray liver with hemodynam

Radiology

Also known as: transjugular hepatic venography, TJHV, transjugular portal pressure

Radiographic imaging of liver veins with hemodynamic measurements via transjugular approach to assess portal hypertension and liver dysfunction.

Clinical Context

Evaluates portal hypertension severity via transjugular venography with hemodynamic pressure measurement for risk stratification.
