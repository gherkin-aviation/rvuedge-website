76125 Cine/video x-rays add-on

Radiology +Add-on

Also known as: Cine X-ray add-on, Additional fluoroscopy video

Cine or video x-ray fluoroscopic recording add-on code for additional sites or extended recording during radiographic examination.

Clinical Context

Supplement to 76120 when evaluating multiple sites or when extended recording is necessary for complete evaluation of dynamic motion.
