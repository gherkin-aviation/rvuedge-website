78700 Kidney imaging morphol

Radiology

Also known as: renal imaging, kidney scan

Nuclear medicine imaging of kidney to evaluate renal morphology and structural anatomy.

Clinical Context

Used to assess kidney size, shape, and structural abnormalities. Common indication for suspected pyelonephritis, renal infarction, or anatomic assessment.
