76816 Ob ultrasound follow-up per fetus

Radiology

Also known as: Follow-up obstetric ultrasound, Interval fetal ultrasound

Obstetric ultrasound examination for follow-up assessment of one or more fetus(es) during pregnancy.

Clinical Context

Interval ultrasound for assessment of fetal growth, interval development, and management of known abnormalities or complications.
