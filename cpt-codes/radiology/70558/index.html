70558 MRI brain with dye

Radiology

Also known as: brain MRI with contrast, enhanced cerebral MRI, gadolinium-enhanced brain MRI

Magnetic resonance imaging of the entire brain with intravenous gadolinium contrast medium enhancement. Provides improved visualization of brain pathology with blood-brain barrier disruption.

Clinical Context

Used for brain tumors, infections, inflammatory lesions, and vascular pathology. Gadolinium enhancement identifies lesions disrupting the blood-brain barrier.
