70210 X-ray examination of sinuses

Radiology

Also known as: sinus x-ray limited, paranasal sinus imaging

Radiographic examination of the paranasal sinuses (frontal, maxillary, ethmoid, sphenoid) with limited views.

Clinical Context

Used for sinusitis, sinus fracture, or mass evaluation with limited projections.
