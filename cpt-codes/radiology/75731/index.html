75731 Artery x-rays adrenal gland

Radiology

Also known as: adrenal angiography, adrenal artery imaging, adrenal vascular imaging

Angiographic radiographic imaging of the adrenal gland vasculature to assess adrenal gland blood supply and visualize adrenal lesions.

Clinical Context

Evaluates adrenal masses, pheochromocytoma, and adrenal insufficiency; assesses adrenal vasculature and collateral flow.
