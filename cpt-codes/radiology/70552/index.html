70552 MRI brain stem with dye

Radiology

Also known as: brainstem MRI with contrast, enhanced brainstem imaging, gadolinium-enhanced brainstem MRI

Magnetic resonance imaging of the brainstem with intravenous gadolinium contrast medium enhancement. Provides enhanced visualization of brainstem pathology with improved lesion characterization.

In Plain Language

brainstem scan with dye; enhanced lower brain imaging

Clinical Context

Indicated for brainstem tumors, inflammatory demyelinating lesions, and infection evaluation. Gadolinium enhancement improves visualization of lesions disrupting blood-brain barrier.
