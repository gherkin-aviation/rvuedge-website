74410 Urography nfs drip and/or bolus

Radiology

Also known as: IV pyelography, intravenous urography, IVP

Radiographic imaging of the urinary system via intravenous contrast administration using drip or bolus injection without fluoroscopy for functional assessment.

In Plain Language

kidney X-ray with dye; urinary system imaging

Clinical Context

Evaluates renal function, obstruction, stones, hydronephrosis, and tumors; drip or bolus technique allows assessment of differential renal function.
