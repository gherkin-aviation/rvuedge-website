77012 CT scan for needle biopsy

Radiology

Also known as: CT guided biopsy, CT guided needle biopsy

Computed tomography (CT) scan for guidance of needle biopsy procedure including real-time visualization.

Clinical Context

CT guidance for percutaneous needle biopsy of chest, abdomen, pelvis, or other lesions with cross-sectional imaging.
