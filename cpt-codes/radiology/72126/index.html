72126 CT neck spine with dye

Radiology

Also known as: cervical spine CT with contrast, myelogram CT cervical, enhanced C-spine imaging

Computed tomography of the cervical spine with intravenous contrast medium enhancement. Provides enhanced visualization of cervical cord and nerve roots.

Clinical Context

Used for myelopathic symptoms, nerve root compression assessment, and detailed evaluation of spinal cord pathology. Contrast enhancement improves nerve root visualization.
