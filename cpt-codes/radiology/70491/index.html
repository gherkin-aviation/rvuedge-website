70491 CT soft tissue neck with dye

Radiology

Also known as: soft tissue neck CT with contrast, enhanced cervical imaging, neck CTA

Computed tomography scan of the cervical soft tissues with intravenous contrast medium enhancement. Provides detailed visualization of neck vasculature, lymph node enhancement patterns, and pathologic soft tissue changes.

In Plain Language

neck scan with dye; throat imaging with contrast

Clinical Context

Standard for neck masses, suspected malignancy, thyroid imaging with vascular assessment, carotid pathology, and infection. Contrast enhancement improves lesion characterization and vascular definition.
