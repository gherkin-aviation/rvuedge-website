74713 MRI fetal each additional gestation

Radiology +Add-on

Also known as: additional fetal MRI, twin MRI, multiple gestation imaging

Magnetic resonance imaging of each additional fetus in a multiple gestation pregnancy beyond the first fetus already imaged.

Clinical Context

Evaluates additional fetuses in twin/multiple gestations for anomalies, growth discordance, and complications; performed in same session as first fetus imaging.
