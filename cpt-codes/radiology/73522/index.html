73522 X-ray examination hips bi 3-4 views

Radiology

Also known as: Bilateral hip X-ray 3-4 views, Bilateral hip imaging

Plain radiographic examination of both hip joints (bilateral) in three to four views to provide comprehensive bilateral hip assessment.

Clinical Context

Detailed bilateral hip evaluation for developmental dysplasia, femoroacetabular impingement, osteoarthritis, and comparative assessment.
