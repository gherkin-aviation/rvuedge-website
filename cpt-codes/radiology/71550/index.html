71550 MRI chest without dye

Radiology

Also known as: chest MRI, thoracic MRI noncontrast, cardiac MRI

Magnetic resonance imaging of the thorax (chest) without intravenous gadolinium contrast medium. Provides excellent soft tissue contrast of the mediastinum and chest wall.

Clinical Context

Used for cardiac and pericardial evaluation, mediastinal mass assessment, and chest wall soft tissue pathology. Excellent for viability assessment and tissue characterization without contrast.
