74300 X-ray bile ducts/pancreas

Radiology

Also known as: cholangiography, pancreaticography, ERCP imaging

Radiographic examination of the bile ducts and pancreatic duct system using contrast medium to visualize ductal anatomy and identify obstruction or pathology.

Clinical Context

Diagnostic evaluation of biliary obstruction, cholangitis, pancreatitis, and ductal dilation; performed endoscopically or percutaneously with fluoroscopic guidance.
