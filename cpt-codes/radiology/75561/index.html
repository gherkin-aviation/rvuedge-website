75561 Cardiac MRI for morph with dye

Radiology

Also known as: contrast-enhanced cardiac MRI, cardiac MRI with gadolinium, late gadolinium enhancement

Magnetic resonance imaging of the heart for morphologic assessment with intravenous contrast enhancement to visualize myocardial perfusion and fibrosis.

In Plain Language

heart scan with dye; detailed heart imaging with contrast

Clinical Context

Evaluates myocardial infarction, scar tissue, fibrosis, and myocarditis using late gadolinium enhancement; assesses viability and perfusion defects.
