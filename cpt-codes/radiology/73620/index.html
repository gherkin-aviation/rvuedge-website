73620 X-ray examination of foot

Radiology

Also known as: Foot X-ray 1-2 views, Basic foot X-ray

Plain radiographic examination of the foot including tarsal, metatarsal, and phalangeal bones in two or fewer views.

Clinical Context

Evaluates foot pain, fractures, arthritis, and alignment. Standard posteroanterior and lateral views typical.
