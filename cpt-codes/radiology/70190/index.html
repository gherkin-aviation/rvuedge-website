70190 X-ray examination of eye sockets

Radiology

Also known as: orbit x-ray limited, eye socket imaging

Radiographic examination of the orbit (eye socket) with limited views to evaluate orbital contents and structure.

Clinical Context

Used for orbital fracture, mass, or proptosis evaluation.
