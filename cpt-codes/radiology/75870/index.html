75870 Vein x-ray skull

Radiology

Also known as: cerebral venography, intracranial venography, cerebral vein imaging

Radiographic imaging of veins in the skull and intracranial cavity using contrast medium to assess cerebral venous anatomy and sinus thrombosis.

Clinical Context

Evaluates cerebral venous sinus thrombosis, cortical vein thrombosis, and intracranial venous anatomy.
