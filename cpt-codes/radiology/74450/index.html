74450 X-ray urethra/bladder

Radiology

Also known as: urethrography, voiding cystourethrography, retrograde urethrography

Radiographic imaging of the urethra and bladder using contrast medium to evaluate urethral stricture, diverticula, masses, and bladder anomalies.

In Plain Language

urine tube and bladder X-ray; urethra imaging

Clinical Context

Diagnoses urethral strictures, false passages, diverticula, and anterior urethral lesions; retrograde technique avoids bladder filling.
