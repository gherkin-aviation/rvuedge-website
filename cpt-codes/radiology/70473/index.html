70473 CT cere prfu aly c+wo CT/cta

Radiology

Also known as: CT perfusion, CTP, perfusion-only CT, brain perfusion imaging

Computed tomographic perfusion analysis of the cerebrum with intravenous contrast without concurrent computed tomographic angiography. Evaluates cerebral hemodynamics including blood flow, blood volume, and transit times.

In Plain Language

blood flow measurement in brain; tissue perfusion study

Clinical Context

Used when perfusion assessment is needed without angiographic detail, such as in selected stroke patients or brain tumor characterization. Provides hemodynamic data without additional radiation from separate angiographic acquisition.
