78598 Lung perf & ventilat diferentl

Radiology

Also known as: differential V/Q study

Lung perfusion and ventilation imaging with differential measurement performed to assess asymmetric lung function.

Clinical Context

Quantifies relative ventilation and perfusion contribution of each lung in chronic lung disease and post-surgical assessment.
