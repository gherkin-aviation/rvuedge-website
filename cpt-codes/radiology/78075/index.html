78075 Adrenal cortex & medulla img

Radiology

Also known as: adrenal scan, adrenal imaging, adrenal scintigraphy

Adrenal gland imaging of both cortex and medulla performed using nuclear medicine to assess adrenal function and anatomy.

In Plain Language

nuclear medicine adrenal scan; adrenal gland imaging

Clinical Context

Evaluates adrenal cortical and medullary function in patients with suspected pheochromocytoma, adrenal insufficiency, or Cushing syndrome.
