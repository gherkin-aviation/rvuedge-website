75893 Venous sampling by catheter

Radiology

Also known as: venous sampling, selective venous sampling, adrenal vein sampling

Radiographic imaging with catheter-based venous blood sampling from multiple sites to assess hormonal gradients or oxygen saturation.

In Plain Language

blood sample collection via catheter with X-ray; diagnostic blood collection during imaging

Clinical Context

Evaluates primary aldosteronism lateralization, pheochromocytoma location, and other endocrine disorders via selective venous sampling.
