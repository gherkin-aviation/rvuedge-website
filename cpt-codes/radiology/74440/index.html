74440 X-ray male genital tract

Radiology

Also known as: male genital imaging, seminal vesiculography, vasography

Radiographic imaging of the male genital tract including seminal vesicles, vas deferens, and prostate using contrast medium for assessment of obstruction or anomalies.

Clinical Context

Evaluates male infertility, obstruction of seminal ducts, vasitis, and anomalies; performed via injection into the vas deferens or seminal vesicle.
