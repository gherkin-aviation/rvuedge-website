72132 CT lumbar spine with dye

Radiology

Also known as: lumbar spine CT with contrast, myelogram CT lumbar, enhanced L-spine imaging

Computed tomography of the lumbar spine with intravenous contrast medium enhancement. Provides enhanced visualization of lumbar nerve roots and spinal cord.

Clinical Context

Used for evaluation of sciatica, nerve root compression, and detailed assessment of spinal cord pathology. Contrast enhancement improves nerve root and soft tissue visualization.
