76932 Echo guide for heart biopsy

Radiology

Also known as: Echo guided cardiac biopsy, Ultrasound guided heart biopsy

Echocardiography guidance for cardiac biopsy procedure including ultrasound visualization and needle guidance.

Clinical Context

Ultrasound guidance for transvenous endomyocardial biopsy to obtain tissue samples for diagnosis of cardiomyopathy or myocarditis.
