74400 Urography intravenous +-kub tomog

Radiology

Also known as: IV pyelography, intravenous pyelogram, IVP

Intravenous urography with optional tomography and kidney-ureter-bladder radiography to visualize renal and urinary collecting system structure and function.

Clinical Context

Evaluates renal function, hydronephrosis, obstruction, stones, tumors, and urinary tract anomalies; contrast excretion provides assessment of renal perfusion and collecting system.
