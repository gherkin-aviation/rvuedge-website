72195 MRI pelvis without dye

Radiology

Also known as: Pelvis MRI without contrast, Non-contrast pelvis MRI

Magnetic resonance imaging of the pelvis performed without intravenous gadolinium contrast agent to visualize soft tissue structures and assess pathology.

Clinical Context

Preferred for evaluating pelvic soft tissue abnormalities including gynecologic tumors, prostate lesions, and musculoskeletal pathology without radiation exposure.
