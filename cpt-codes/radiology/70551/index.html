70551 MRI brain stem without dye

Radiology

Also known as: brainstem MRI, MRI brainstem noncontrast, pons MRI, MRI brain without contrast, brain MRI

Magnetic resonance imaging of the brainstem without intravenous gadolinium contrast medium. High-resolution imaging evaluates brainstem structures including midbrain, pons, and medulla.

Clinical Context

Used for evaluation of brainstem lesions, demyelinating disease, vascular accidents, and tumors. Excellent soft tissue contrast for identifying pathology without need for contrast enhancement.
