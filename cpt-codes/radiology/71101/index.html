71101 X-ray examination unilateral ribs/chest

Radiology

Also known as: unilateral rib and chest X-ray, single side chest and rib series

Unilateral chest radiography including the ribs and chest cavity on one side with multiple views providing comprehensive ipsilateral thoracic assessment.

Clinical Context

Ordered when both rib trauma and concurrent pulmonary or chest wall pathology requires detailed ipsilateral evaluation. Provides comprehensive single-side assessment.
