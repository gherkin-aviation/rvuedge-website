75831 Vein x-ray kidney

Radiology

Also known as: renal venography, renal vein imaging, single kidney venography

Radiographic imaging of renal veins in a single kidney using contrast medium to assess renal venous drainage and patency.

In Plain Language

kidney vein X-ray; kidney vein imaging

Clinical Context

Evaluates renal vein thrombosis, Budd-Chiari syndrome, renal artery stenosis, and renal vein stenosis.
