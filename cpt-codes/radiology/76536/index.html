Radiology

Also known as: Head and neck ultrasound, Neck ultrasound

Ultrasound examination of the head and neck including superficial structures and deep cervical tissues.

In Plain Language

Ultrasound of head and neck area; Sound wave imaging of neck structures

Clinical Context

Assessment of thyroid, lymph nodes, salivary glands, and soft tissues of head and neck for evaluation of masses, inflammation, or vascular abnormalities.
