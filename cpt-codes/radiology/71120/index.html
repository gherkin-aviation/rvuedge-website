71120 X-ray examination breastbone 2/>vws

Radiology

Also known as: sternal X-ray, sternum radiograph, breastbone films

Radiography of the sternum (breastbone) including two or more views. Evaluates sternal fractures, degenerative changes, and post-surgical healing.

Clinical Context

Obtained when sternal injury is suspected, particularly after chest trauma or cardiac surgery. Multiple views allow visualization of sternal fractures and assess integrity of surgical fixation.
