77293 Respirator motion management simul

Radiology

Also known as: Respiratory gating simulation, Motion management

Respiratory motion management simulation for therapeutic radiation treatment.

Clinical Context

Simulation technique addressing tumor and organ motion due to respiration. Critical for thoracic and upper abdominal tumors to improve targeting accuracy.
