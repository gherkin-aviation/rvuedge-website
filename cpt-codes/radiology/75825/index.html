75825 Vein x-ray trunk

Radiology

Also known as: trunk venography, thoracic venography, abdominal venography

Radiographic imaging of veins in the trunk (thorax or abdomen) using contrast medium to assess thoracoabdominal venous anatomy.

In Plain Language

chest/belly vein X-ray; central vein imaging

Clinical Context

Evaluates superior vena cava obstruction, hepatic vein patency, portal vein thrombosis, and superior vena cava syndrome.
