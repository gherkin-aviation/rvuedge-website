73090 X-ray examination of forearm

Radiology

Also known as: Forearm X-ray, Radius and ulna X-ray

Plain radiographic examination of the forearm including the radius and ulna bones to assess for fractures, displacement, and structural abnormalities.

In Plain Language

X-ray of the forearm bones

Clinical Context

Evaluates forearm fractures, angulation, rotation, and post-operative fixation. Anteroposterior and lateral views standard for adequate fracture assessment.
