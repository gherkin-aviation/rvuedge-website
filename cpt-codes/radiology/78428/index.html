78428 Cardiac shunt imaging

Radiology

Also known as: cardiac shunt study, shunt imaging

Cardiac shunt imaging performed to detect and quantify intracardiac or vascular shunts using nuclear medicine.

Clinical Context

Detects left-to-right and right-to-left cardiac shunts and quantifies shunt magnitude. Useful in congenital heart disease.
