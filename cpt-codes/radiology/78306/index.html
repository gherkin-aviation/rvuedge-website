78306 Bone imaging whole body

Radiology

Also known as: bone scan whole body, skeletal scintigraphy

Bone imaging of entire body performed for comprehensive skeletal assessment and detection of bone pathology.

Clinical Context

Complete skeletal imaging to detect metastatic disease, systemic infection, and metabolic bone disease.
