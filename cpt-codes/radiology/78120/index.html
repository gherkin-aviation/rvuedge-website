78120 Red cell mass single

Radiology

Also known as: red cell mass single, RBC mass study

Red blood cell mass measurement using single injection technique to quantify total circulating erythrocyte volume.

In Plain Language

red blood cell measurement; RBC volume test

Clinical Context

Quantifies total red cell mass to diagnose true versus relative polycythemia and assess for myeloproliferative disease.
