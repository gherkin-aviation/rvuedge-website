71551 MRI chest with dye

Radiology

Also known as: chest MRI with contrast, enhanced cardiac MRI, gadolinium-enhanced thoracic MRI

Magnetic resonance imaging of the thorax (chest) with intravenous gadolinium contrast medium enhancement. Provides enhanced visualization of cardiac, mediastinal, and pericardial pathology.

In Plain Language

chest scan with dye; enhanced heart imaging

Clinical Context

Used for myocardial characterization, cardiac viability assessment, pericardial disease evaluation, and mediastinal mass enhancement pattern analysis. Essential for cardiac ischemia and infarction assessment.
