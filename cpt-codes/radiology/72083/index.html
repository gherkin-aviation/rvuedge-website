72083 X-ray examination entire spinal 4/5 vw

Radiology

Also known as: full spine X-ray four to five views, extended complete spine series

Radiography of the entire spine from cervical to sacral region including four to five views from multiple projections and angles.

In Plain Language

detailed whole spine X-rays; extended backbone imaging

Clinical Context

More comprehensive spinal assessment than standard series, particularly useful for detailed scoliosis evaluation, complex deformity assessment, or when multiple oblique views are beneficial.
