78013 Thyroid imaging with blood flow

Radiology

Also known as: Thyroid scan with flow, Thyroid imaging with perfusion

Radioisotope imaging of thyroid with assessment of blood flow and clearance.

Clinical Context

Thyroid imaging with dynamic assessment of blood flow and clearance patterns. Evaluates functional thyroid tissue and vascular characteristics.
