70559 MRI brain without & with dye

Radiology

Also known as: brain MRI with and without contrast, dual-phase brain imaging, complete brain MRI

Magnetic resonance imaging of the entire brain performed with noncontrast and intravenous gadolinium contrast acquisitions. Comprehensive imaging combining baseline and enhanced sequences.

Clinical Context

Comprehensive protocol for complex brain pathology when both baseline characteristics and enhancement patterns are important for diagnosis and characterization.
