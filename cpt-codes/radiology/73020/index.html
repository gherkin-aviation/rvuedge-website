73020 X-ray examination of shoulder

Radiology

Also known as: Shoulder X-ray 1-2 views, Basic shoulder X-ray

Plain radiographic examination of the shoulder joint in two or fewer views to assess glenohumeral joint alignment, rotator cuff pathology, and shoulder pathology.

Clinical Context

Initial screening for shoulder pain, suspected fractures, dislocation, and osteoarthritis. Standard views are anteroposterior and lateral.
