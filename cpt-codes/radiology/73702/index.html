73702 CT lower extremity without & with dye

Radiology

Also known as: Lower extremity CT with and without contrast

Computed tomography imaging of the lower extremity performed as a comprehensive study with both non-contrast and contrast-enhanced sequences.

Clinical Context

Comprehensive evaluation of complex lower extremity pathology where both baseline anatomy and tissue enhancement are needed.
