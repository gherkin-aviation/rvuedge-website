72159 Mr angio spine without & with dye

Radiology

Also known as: spinal angiography, spinal MRA, vascular cord imaging

Magnetic resonance angiography of the entire spinal column including cervical, thoracic, and lumbar vasculature performed with noncontrast and/or intravenous gadolinium contrast medium.

In Plain Language

spinal blood vessel scan; cord blood flow imaging

Clinical Context

Used for evaluation of spinal vascular malformations, arteriovenous fistulas, and spinal cord ischemia. Provides non-invasive assessment of spinal vasculature.
