76882 Ultrasound lmtd joint/facial evl nvasc xtr

Radiology

Also known as: Limited joint ultrasound, Focal area ultrasound

Ultrasound examination of a joint or focal area including limited evaluation with non-vascular assessment.

Clinical Context

Limited ultrasound for evaluation of specific joint abnormality, effusion, or tendon pathology without comprehensive assessment.
