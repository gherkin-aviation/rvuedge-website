76946 Echo guide for amniocentesis

Radiology

Also known as: Echo guided amniocentesis, Ultrasound guided amniocentesis

Echocardiography guidance for amniocentesis procedure including ultrasound visualization of amniotic sac and needle placement.

Clinical Context

Ultrasound guidance for amniocentesis to obtain amniotic fluid for prenatal testing including karyotype and genetic analysis.
