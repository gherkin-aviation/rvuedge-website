77073 Bone length studies

Radiology

Also known as: Bone length study, Limb length measurement

Radiographic measurement of bone length and assessment of skeletal growth.

Clinical Context

Used to evaluate limb length discrepancy, assess growth in children with suspected dysplasia, or monitor progression of skeletal abnormalities.
