76948 Echo guide ova aspiration

Radiology

Also known as: Echo guided oocyte retrieval, Ultrasound guided egg retrieval

Echocardiography guidance for ovum aspiration during assisted reproductive procedures including oocyte retrieval.

Clinical Context

Transvaginal ultrasound guidance for oocyte aspiration during in vitro fertilization (IVF) procedures.
