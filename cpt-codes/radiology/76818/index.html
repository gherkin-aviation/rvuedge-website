76818 Fetal biophys profile with nst

Radiology

Also known as: Biophysical profile with NST, BPP with NST

Fetal biophysical profile ultrasound examination with non-stress testing (NST) to assess fetal well-being.

Clinical Context

Assessment of fetal movement, tone, breathing, amniotic fluid, and reactivity via non-stress testing for evaluation of fetal well-being in high-risk pregnancies.
