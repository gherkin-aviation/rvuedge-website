71555 MRI angio chest w or without dye

Radiology

Also known as: thoracic MRA, aortic MRA, pulmonary artery MRA

Magnetic resonance angiography of the thorax with or without intravenous gadolinium contrast medium. Evaluates aorta, pulmonary arteries, and systemic venous structures.

Clinical Context

Used for aortic dissection evaluation, pulmonary embolism assessment, and aortic disease characterization. Provides non-invasive vascular imaging without catheterization.
