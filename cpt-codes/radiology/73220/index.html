73220 MRI uppr extremity without & with dye

Radiology

Also known as: Upper extremity MRI with and without contrast

Magnetic resonance imaging of the upper extremity performed as a comprehensive study with both non-contrast and contrast-enhanced sequences.

Clinical Context

Comprehensive evaluation of complex upper extremity pathology where complete assessment requires both baseline anatomy and contrast enhancement patterns.
