73650 X-ray examination of heel

Radiology

Also known as: Heel X-ray, Calcaneus X-ray

Plain radiographic examination of the heel and calcaneus bone to assess for fractures, talocrural alignment, and heel pathology.

In Plain Language

X-ray of the heel

Clinical Context

Evaluates calcaneal fractures, stress fractures, and heel spur syndrome. Axial and lateral views are standard.
