77047 MRI breast c- bilateral

Radiology

Also known as: Breast MRI bilateral, Bilateral breast MRI

Magnetic resonance imaging of both breasts with contrast material administration.

In Plain Language

Breast scan using magnetic resonance imaging on both sides

Clinical Context

Used for comprehensive breast evaluation in high-risk patients, assessment of suspected malignancy, or surveillance in cancer survivors. Provides excellent visualization of both breast tissues.
