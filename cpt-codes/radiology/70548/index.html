70548 Mr angiography neck with dye

Radiology

Also known as: contrast-enhanced neck MRA, CE-MRA neck, dynamic carotid imaging

Magnetic resonance angiography of the cervical arteries with intravenous gadolinium contrast medium administration during arterial phase imaging.

In Plain Language

neck blood vessel scan with dye; enhanced carotid imaging

Clinical Context

Provides excellent carotid and vertebral artery visualization for stenosis quantification, dissection assessment, and vascular anomaly evaluation. Gadolinium enhancement produces superior arterial detail.
