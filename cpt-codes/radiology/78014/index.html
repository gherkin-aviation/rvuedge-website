78014 Thyroid imaging with blood flow

Radiology

Also known as: thyroid scan with flow, thyroid perfusion scan

Thyroid imaging with blood flow assessment performed using nuclear medicine techniques to evaluate thyroid perfusion and function.

Clinical Context

Used to assess thyroid gland perfusion, detect nodules, and evaluate hyperthyroidism or thyroiditis. Common in evaluating unexplained thyroid enlargement or thyroid hormone dysregulation.
