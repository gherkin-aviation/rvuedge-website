71047 X-ray examination chest 3 views

Radiology

Also known as: three-view chest X-ray, extended chest series, comprehensive chest radiography

Chest radiography including three views of the thoracic cavity obtained from multiple projections typically including posteroanterior, lateral, and either apical lordotic or decubitus views.

Clinical Context

More comprehensive than standard two-view study, particularly useful for evaluation of apical pathology, suspected pneumothorax (especially with decubitus view), or detailed parenchymal assessment.
