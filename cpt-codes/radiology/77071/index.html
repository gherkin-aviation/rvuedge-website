77071 Mnl application strs joint radiography

Radiology

Also known as: Stress radiography, Stress testing radiography

Manual application of stress to a joint followed by radiographic imaging.

Clinical Context

Used to assess joint stability and ligamentous integrity. Common in evaluation of ankle, knee, and other joint injuries. Reveals functional instability not visible on routine radiographs.
