75574 CT angio hrt with 3d image

Radiology

Also known as: coronary CTA with 3D, cardiac CT angiography, coronary artery CTA

Computed tomography angiography of the heart with three-dimensional image reconstruction and contrast to visualize coronary arteries and cardiac structures.

Clinical Context

Comprehensive evaluation of coronary artery anatomy, stenosis, and anomalies with superior 3D visualization; alternative to invasive angiography.
