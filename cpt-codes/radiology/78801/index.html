Radiology

Also known as: multiarea tumor scan, tumor localization multiple areas

Radiopharmaceutical tumor localization imaging of two or more body areas with single-day imaging to detect primary or metastatic disease.

Clinical Context

Evaluates multiple anatomic regions simultaneously for malignancy; useful for comprehensive staging and metastatic workup in cancer patients.
