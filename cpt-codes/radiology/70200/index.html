70200 X-ray examination of eye sockets

Radiology

Also known as: orbit x-ray complete, orbital imaging comprehensive

Radiographic examination of the orbit (eye socket) with comprehensive views for detailed orbital assessment.

Clinical Context

Used for comprehensive orbital assessment including fracture, mass, enophthalmos, or imaging staging.
