72074 X-ray examination thoracic spine4/>vw

Radiology

Also known as: thoracic spine X-ray four or more views, comprehensive thoracic series

Radiography of the thoracic spine including four or more views providing maximum radiographic detail from multiple projections and angles.

Clinical Context

Most comprehensive radiographic thoracic spine assessment, particularly useful when detailed fracture patterns, subtle pathology, or complex anatomy requires maximum imaging detail.
