71100 X-ray examination ribs uni 2 views

Radiology

Also known as: rib series one side, unilateral rib X-ray, single side rib films

Unilateral rib radiography including two views of the affected side. Evaluates rib fractures, cortical disruption, and osseous pathology.

Clinical Context

Used for suspected rib fracture evaluation, particularly after trauma. Two-view approach provides assessment from orthogonal planes to identify non-displaced or subtle fractures.
