72295 X-ray of lower spine disk

Radiology

Also known as: Lumbar disc X-ray, Lower spine disc imaging

Plain radiographic examination of the intervertebral discs in the lower lumbar spine to assess disc space height, alignment, and degenerative changes.

In Plain Language

X-ray of spinal discs in lower back; Low back disc imaging

Clinical Context

Screening for disc space narrowing, spondylosis, spondylolisthesis, and degenerative disc disease. Often part of standard lumbar spine radiography.
