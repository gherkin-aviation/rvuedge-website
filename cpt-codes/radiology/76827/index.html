76827 Echo examination of fetal heart

Radiology

Also known as: Enhanced fetal echo, Saline enhanced fetal cardiac

Echocardiography examination of fetal heart including saline or contrast enhancement for improved visualization.

Clinical Context

Fetal cardiac imaging with contrast enhancement for improved visualization of septal defects or complex anatomy.
