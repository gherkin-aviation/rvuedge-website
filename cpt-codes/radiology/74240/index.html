74240 X-ray xm upper gi trc 1cntrst

Radiology

Also known as: Upper GI X-ray single series, Barium meal

Plain radiographic examination of the upper gastrointestinal tract using barium contrast in a single series to assess gastric and duodenal anatomy.

Clinical Context

Evaluates upper GI pathology including ulcers, gastritis, pyloric stenosis, and malignancy. Single series provides basic assessment.
