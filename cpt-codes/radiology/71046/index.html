71046 X-ray examination chest 2 views

Radiology

Also known as: chest X-ray two views, PA and lateral chest, standard chest series, chest x-ray 2 views, CXR PA and lateral

Chest radiography including two views typically consisting of posteroanterior and lateral projections of the thoracic cavity.

In Plain Language

two-view lung X-ray; front and side chest pictures

Clinical Context

Standard comprehensive chest radiography allowing assessment from two orthogonal planes. Lateral view provides depth assessment and retrocardiac opacity evaluation that PA view cannot provide.
