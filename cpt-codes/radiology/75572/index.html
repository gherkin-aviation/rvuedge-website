75572 CT hrt with 3d image

Radiology

Also known as: coronary CTA, cardiac CT, CT angiography

Computed tomography angiography of the heart with three-dimensional image reconstruction to visualize coronary artery anatomy and assess for stenosis.

Clinical Context

Evaluates coronary artery disease, anomalous origin of coronary arteries, and assesses coronary anatomy prior to cardiac surgery.
