76805 Ob ultrasound >/= 14 wks sngl fetus

Radiology

Also known as: Second/third trimester ultrasound, Standard obstetric ultrasound

Obstetric ultrasound examination of a single fetus at 14 weeks gestation or greater for standard anatomic survey.

Clinical Context

Routine anatomic survey to assess fetal development, growth, anatomy, placental position, amniotic fluid volume, and maternal structures.
