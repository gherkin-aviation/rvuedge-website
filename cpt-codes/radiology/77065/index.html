Radiology

Diagnostic mammography of a single breast including computer-aided detection analysis.

In Plain Language

X-ray breast exam with computer analysis on one side to find problems

Clinical Context

Used to evaluate symptomatic patients or abnormal findings on screening mammography. Computer-aided detection assists radiologist in identifying potential lesions.
