70496 CT angiography head

Radiology

Also known as: CTA head, cerebral angiography, intracranial angiography, head CTA

Computed tomographic angiography specifically of the intracranial and extracranial arteries of the head with intravenous contrast medium during arterial phase imaging.

In Plain Language

head blood vessel imaging; stroke prevention test

Clinical Context

Essential for acute stroke evaluation, intracranial aneurysm detection and characterization, arterial dissection assessment, and evaluation of vascular malformations. Rapid acquisition provides excellent vessel visualization.
