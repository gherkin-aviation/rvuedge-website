74019 Radex abdomen 2 views

Radiology

Also known as: Abdominal X-ray 2 views, Two-view abdominal radiograph

Plain radiographic examination of the abdomen in two views to improve detection of free air, obstruction, and intra-abdominal pathology.

In Plain Language

X-ray of the abdomen from two angles

Clinical Context

Standard acute abdomen series evaluating free air, obstruction, and perforation. Typically supine and upright views.
