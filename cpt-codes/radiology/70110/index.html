Radiology

Also known as: jaw x-ray complete, full jaw imaging, comprehensive jaw radiography

Radiographic examination of the jaw (mandible/maxilla) with 4 or more radiographic views for comprehensive evaluation.

Clinical Context

Used for comprehensive jaw assessment including fracture evaluation, tumor staging, or pre-surgical planning.
