73010 X-ray examination of shoulder blade

Radiology

Also known as: Scapula X-ray, Shoulder blade X-ray

Plain radiographic examination of the scapula (shoulder blade) to assess for fractures, displacement, and shoulder girdle pathology.

Clinical Context

Evaluates scapular fractures, winging, dyskinesis, and post-traumatic changes. Often combined with clavicle and shoulder radiographs.
