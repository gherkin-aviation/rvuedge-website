74355 X-ray guide intestinal tube

Radiology

Also known as: fluoroscopic tube placement, jejunal tube guidance, small bowel tube placement

Fluoroscopic radiographic guidance during placement, repositioning, or manipulation of a gastrointestinal feeding tube positioned beyond the stomach into the small bowel.

In Plain Language

X-ray guided feeding tube into small bowel; guided small bowel tube positioning

Clinical Context

Real-time fluoroscopic imaging guides endoscopic or percutaneous placement of jejunal/post-pyloric feeding tubes for enteral nutrition support.
