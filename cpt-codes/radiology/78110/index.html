78110 Plasma volume single

Radiology

Also known as: plasma volume study single, blood volume measurement

Plasma volume measurement using single injection technique and nuclear medicine methods to quantify circulating plasma volume.

Clinical Context

Measures absolute plasma volume to differentiate true polycythemia from relative increases in red cell mass. Used in evaluation of suspected myeloproliferative disorders.
