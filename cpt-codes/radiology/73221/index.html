73221 MRI joint upper extremity without dye

Radiology

Also known as: Joint MRI without contrast, Single joint upper extremity MRI, MRI shoulder without contrast, shoulder MRI

Magnetic resonance imaging of a single upper extremity joint without gadolinium contrast to assess articular cartilage, ligaments, and intra-articular structures.

Clinical Context

Focused evaluation of shoulder, elbow, wrist, or hand joint pathology including rotator cuff, labral tears, meniscal lesions, and ligament injuries.
