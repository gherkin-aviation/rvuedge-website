73218 MRI upper extremity without dye

Radiology

Also known as: Upper extremity MRI without contrast, Arm MRI non-contrast

Magnetic resonance imaging of the upper extremity without intravenous gadolinium contrast to assess soft tissue, bone, and neurovascular structures.

Clinical Context

Evaluates soft tissue injuries, bone marrow lesions, complex fractures, and neurovascular pathology. Preferred imaging for rotator cuff assessment.
