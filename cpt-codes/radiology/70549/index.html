70549 Mr angiograph neck without & with dye

Radiology

Also known as: MRA neck with and without contrast, dual-phase neck angiography

Magnetic resonance angiography of the cervical arteries performed with noncontrast and intravenous gadolinium contrast acquisitions. Combines multiple angiographic techniques for comprehensive cervical vascular assessment.

Clinical Context

Comprehensive angiographic protocol for complex carotid and vertebral artery pathology assessment, particularly useful when flow dynamics and anatomic detail are both important.
