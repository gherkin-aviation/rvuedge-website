76977 Ultrasound bone density measure

Radiology

Also known as: Quantitative ultrasound, QUS, Bone density ultrasound

Ultrasound bone density measurement using quantitative ultrasound (QUS) for assessment of bone mineral status.

Clinical Context

Quantitative ultrasound assessment of bone density typically at heel for osteoporosis screening.
