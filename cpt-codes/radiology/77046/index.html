77046 MRI breast c- unilateral

Radiology

Also known as: Breast MRI unilateral, Unilateral breast MRI

Magnetic resonance imaging of a single breast with contrast material administration.

Clinical Context

Used to evaluate breast lesions, assess silicone implant integrity, or detect occult malignancy in high-risk patients. Provides superior soft tissue contrast compared to mammography.
