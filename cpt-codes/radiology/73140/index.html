73140 X-ray examination of finger(s)

Radiology

Also known as: Finger X-ray, Digit X-ray

Plain radiographic examination of individual fingers or digits to assess for fractures, dislocations, and phalangeal pathology.

Clinical Context

Evaluates finger fractures, mallet finger, joint dislocations, and arthritis. Often performed as isolated digit imaging for targeted evaluation.
