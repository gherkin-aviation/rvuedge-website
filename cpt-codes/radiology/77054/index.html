77054 X-ray of mammary ducts

Radiology

Also known as: Multiple ductogram, Duct imaging bilateral

X-ray imaging of multiple mammary ducts.

Clinical Context

Used to evaluate bilateral pathologic nipple discharge or assess multiple ductal abnormalities. Provides comprehensive evaluation of the ductal system.
