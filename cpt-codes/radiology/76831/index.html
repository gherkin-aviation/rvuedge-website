76831 Echo examination uterus

Radiology

Also known as: Uterine echo, Uterine ultrasound

Echocardiography examination of the uterus using ultrasound imaging for assessment of endometrial pathology.

Clinical Context

Ultrasound evaluation of uterine size, contours, endometrial thickness, and intrauterine pathology.
