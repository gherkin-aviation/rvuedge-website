75810 Vein x-ray spleen/liver

Radiology

Also known as: splenic-portal venography, hepatic venography, splenic venography

Radiographic imaging of veins draining the spleen and liver using contrast medium to assess hepatic and splenic venous drainage.

Clinical Context

Evaluates portal hypertension, Budd-Chiari syndrome, splenic vein thrombosis, and hepatic vein patency.
