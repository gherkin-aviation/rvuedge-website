78473 Gated heart multiple

Radiology

Also known as: gated cardiac imaging multiple, MUGA scan multiple

Gated heart imaging performed with multiple imaging sessions to comprehensively assess cardiac wall motion and function.

Clinical Context

Multi-session gated imaging for detailed assessment of left ventricular function, wall motion, and diastolic properties.
