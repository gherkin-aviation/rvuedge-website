72081 X-ray examination entire spinal 1 vw

Radiology

Also known as: full spine X-ray, complete spine radiograph, entire spine one view

Radiography of the entire spine from cervical to sacral region in a single view. Provides overview of complete spinal alignment and gross pathology.

Clinical Context

Used for screening of spinal alignment, scoliosis assessment, and overview of spinal pathology. Limited by anatomic overlap but useful for rapid comprehensive assessment.
