73130 X-ray examination of hand

Radiology

Also known as: Hand X-ray 3+ views, Complete hand X-ray

Plain radiographic examination of the hand in three or more views to provide comprehensive assessment of hand bones and joints.

In Plain Language

Detailed X-ray of the hand from multiple angles

Clinical Context

Detailed evaluation of complex hand fractures, rheumatoid arthritis, osteoarthritis, and post-operative assessment. Additional views improve fracture characterization.
