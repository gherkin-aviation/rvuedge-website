78457 Venous thrombosis imaging

Radiology

Also known as: DVT imaging, venous thrombosis scan

Venous thrombosis imaging performed to detect blood clots in venous system using nuclear medicine techniques.

Clinical Context

Detects deep vein thrombosis with functional assessment of venous obstruction and collateral formation.
