70553 MRI brain stem without & with dye

Radiology

Also known as: brainstem MRI with and without contrast, dual-contrast brainstem imaging, MRI brain with and without contrast, brain MRI with contrast

Magnetic resonance imaging of the brainstem performed with noncontrast and intravenous gadolinium contrast acquisitions. Comprehensive imaging combining baseline and enhanced sequences.

Clinical Context

Comprehensive protocol for complex brainstem pathology assessment when both baseline characteristics and enhancement pattern are diagnostically important.
