77081 Dxa bone density appendiculr

Radiology

Also known as: DEXA appendicular, Peripheral bone density scan

Dual-energy X-ray absorptiometry measurement of bone mineral density of appendicular skeleton.

Clinical Context

Measures bone mineral density in peripheral skeleton including forearm, wrist, and leg. Used when axial skeleton assessment is not feasible or for research purposes.
