75803 Lymph vessel x-ray arms/legs

Radiology

Also known as: bilateral lymphangiography, bilateral lymphatic imaging

Radiographic imaging of lymphatic vessels in bilateral arms and/or legs using contrast medium to visualize bilateral lymphatic drainage patterns.

Clinical Context

Comprehensive evaluation of bilateral lymphatic drainage, lymphedema, and lymph node involvement.
