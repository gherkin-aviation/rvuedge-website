78262 Gastroesophageal reflux std

Radiology

Also known as: GERD study, reflux imaging

Gastroesophageal reflux study performed using nuclear medicine to detect and quantify gastric reflux into esophagus.

Clinical Context

Detects and quantifies gastroesophageal reflux to evaluate symptoms and guide treatment. Useful in refractory GERD and aspiration risk assessment.
