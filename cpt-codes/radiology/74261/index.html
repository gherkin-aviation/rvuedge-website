74261 CT colonography diagnosis

Radiology

Also known as: virtual colonoscopy, CTC, CT colonography, VC

Computed tomography colonography (virtual colonoscopy) without therapeutic intervention, used for diagnostic evaluation of the colon and rectum.

Clinical Context

Used for colorectal cancer screening and diagnosis, particularly in patients who cannot tolerate conventional colonoscopy or have incomplete optical colonoscopy. Evaluates colonic polyps and masses.
