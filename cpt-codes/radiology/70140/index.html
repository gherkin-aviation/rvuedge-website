70140 X-ray examination of facial bones

Radiology

Also known as: facial bone x-ray limited, face x-ray

Radiographic examination of the facial bones (maxilla, zygomatic arches, nasal bones) with limited views.

Clinical Context

Used for suspected facial fracture, sinusitis, or facial trauma assessment.
