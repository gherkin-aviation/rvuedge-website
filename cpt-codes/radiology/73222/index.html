73222 MRI joint upper extremity with dye

Radiology

Also known as: Joint MRI with contrast, Contrast-enhanced joint MRI

Magnetic resonance imaging of a single upper extremity joint with intravenous gadolinium contrast to enhance visualization of synovium and detect inflammation.

Clinical Context

Evaluates inflammatory arthropathies, infection, and synovitis. Contrast uptake helps differentiate active inflammation from chronic changes.
