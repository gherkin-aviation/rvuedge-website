72200 X-ray examination si joints

Radiology

Also known as: Sacroiliac joint X-ray, SI joint X-ray, Sacroiliac imaging

Plain radiographic examination of the sacroiliac joints in two or fewer views to assess alignment, degenerative changes, and inflammatory joint disease.

In Plain Language

X-ray of the joints between spine and pelvis; Sacroiliac joint imaging

Clinical Context

Used in evaluation of sacroiliac joint pain, ankylosing spondylitis, spondyloarthropathy, and post-fusion assessment. Limited views provide quick screening.
