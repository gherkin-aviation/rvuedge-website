76098 X-ray examination surgical specimen

Radiology

Also known as: Specimen radiography, Intraop specimen X-ray

X-ray examination of surgical specimen to provide intraoperative or postoperative confirmation of pathology and anatomic relationships.

In Plain Language

Imaging of removed tissue sample; X-ray of surgical specimen

Clinical Context

Performed on excised tissue to confirm complete removal of lesions, assess margins, or evaluate anatomic relationships during or immediately after surgery.
