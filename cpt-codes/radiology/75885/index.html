75885 Vein x-ray liver with hemodynam

Radiology

Also known as: hepatic venography, liver vein imaging, portal pressure study

Radiographic imaging of liver veins and hepatic vasculature with hemodynamic measurements for assessment of portal hypertension and liver disease.

In Plain Language

liver vein X-ray with pressure measurement; liver vessel imaging with measurements

Clinical Context

Evaluates portal hypertension, cirrhosis, and Budd-Chiari syndrome with measurement of wedged and free hepatic venous pressures.
