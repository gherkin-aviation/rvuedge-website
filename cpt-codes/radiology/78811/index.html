78811 Pet image ltd area

Radiology

Also known as: PET scan limited, PET limited area

Positron emission tomography imaging limited to single body area for tumor detection and metabolic assessment.

Clinical Context

Single-region PET imaging for focused evaluation of suspected malignancy or metabolic abnormality; lower cost than whole-body imaging.
