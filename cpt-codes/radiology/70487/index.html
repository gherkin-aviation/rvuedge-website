70487 CT maxillofacial with dye

Radiology

Also known as: maxillofacial CT with contrast, enhanced facial imaging, facial soft tissue CT

Computed tomography scan of the maxillofacial region with intravenous contrast medium enhancement. Provides detailed visualization of maxillofacial soft tissues, vasculature, and pathologic enhancement patterns.

Clinical Context

Indicated for oral and maxillofacial cancers, infection assessment, vascular malformations, and soft tissue tumors. Contrast enhancement improves characterization of pathology and vascular involvement.
