72050 X-ray examination neck spine 4/5vws

Radiology

Also known as: comprehensive cervical spine series, extended C-spine radiography

Radiography of the cervical spine including four to five views providing comprehensive assessment of cervical vertebrae and intervertebral spaces from multiple projections.

Clinical Context

More comprehensive than standard 2-3 view series, useful for detailed cervical pathology assessment, particularly when subtle fractures or instability is suspected.
