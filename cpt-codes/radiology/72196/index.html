72196 MRI pelvis with dye

Radiology

Also known as: Pelvis MRI with contrast, Contrast-enhanced pelvis MRI

Magnetic resonance imaging of the pelvis performed with intravenous gadolinium contrast agent to enhance visualization of tissue perfusion and characterize lesions.

Clinical Context

Used for detailed assessment of pelvic malignancies, endometriosis, adenomyosis, and inflammatory conditions where contrast enhancement aids in tissue characterization.
