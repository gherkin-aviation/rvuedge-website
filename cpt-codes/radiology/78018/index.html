78018 Thyroid met imaging body

Radiology

Also known as: thyroid whole body scan, total body thyroid imaging

Thyroid metabolic imaging including whole body imaging assessment performed with nuclear medicine techniques.

In Plain Language

full body thyroid scan; whole body thyroid imaging

Clinical Context

Extended imaging to detect thyroid cancer metastases or ectopic thyroid tissue throughout the body. Essential in thyroid cancer follow-up and staging.
