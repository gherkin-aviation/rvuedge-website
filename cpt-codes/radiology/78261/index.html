78261 Gastric mucosa imaging

Radiology

Also known as: Meckel scan, gastric mucosa imaging

Gastric mucosa imaging performed to detect ectopic gastric mucosa in the gastrointestinal tract.

In Plain Language

stomach lining scan; Meckel diverticulum test

Clinical Context

Detects Meckel diverticulum and ectopic gastric mucosa causing gastrointestinal bleeding. Common in pediatric patients with unexplained gastrointestinal hemorrhage.
