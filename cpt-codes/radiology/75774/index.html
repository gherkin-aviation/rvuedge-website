75774 Artery x-ray each vessel

Radiology +Add-on

Also known as: selective arteriography, individual vessel study, targeted angiography

Angiographic radiographic imaging of individual vascular beds for assessment of each specific arterial segment or organ vasculature.

Clinical Context

Evaluates individual arterial segments, organ-specific ischemia, or localized vascular pathology requiring detailed assessment.
