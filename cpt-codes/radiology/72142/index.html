72142 MRI neck spine with dye

Radiology

Also known as: cervical spine MRI with contrast, enhanced C-spine imaging, gadolinium-enhanced myelography

Magnetic resonance imaging of the cervical spine with intravenous gadolinium contrast medium enhancement. Enhanced visualization of nerve roots and spinal cord pathology.

In Plain Language

neck spine scan with dye; enhanced cervical imaging

Clinical Context

Used for evaluation of spinal cord syrinx, arachnoiditis, infection, and enhanced lesion characterization. Gadolinium administration improves visualization of inflammatory and pathologic conditions.
