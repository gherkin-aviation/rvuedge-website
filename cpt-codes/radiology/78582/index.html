78582 Lung ventilat & perfus imaging

Radiology

Also known as: V/Q scan, ventilation perfusion study

Lung ventilation and perfusion imaging performed simultaneously to assess both air movement and blood flow.

Clinical Context

Comprehensive assessment of lung ventilation and perfusion for pulmonary embolism diagnosis and chronic lung disease evaluation.
