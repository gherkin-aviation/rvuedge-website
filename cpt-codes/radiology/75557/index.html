75557 Cardiac MRI for morph

Radiology

Also known as: cardiac MRI, heart MRI, structural cardiac MRI

Magnetic resonance imaging of the heart for morphologic assessment of cardiac structure including chambers, valves, and myocardium without contrast.

In Plain Language

heart scan; detailed heart imaging

Clinical Context

Evaluates cardiac anatomy, ventricular function, wall thickness, and structural abnormalities; provides superior soft tissue contrast for cardiomyopathy assessment.
