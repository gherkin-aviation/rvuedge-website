78227 Hepatobil syst image with drug

Radiology

Also known as: HIDA with CCK, hepatobiliary imaging with drug

Hepatobiliary system imaging with pharmacologic intervention performed to assess gallbladder function and biliary dynamics.

Clinical Context

Evaluates gallbladder ejection fraction and contractility response to cholecystokinin stimulation. Diagnoses biliary dyskinesia and functional cholecystitis.
