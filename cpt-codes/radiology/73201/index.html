73201 CT upper extremity with dye

Radiology

Also known as: Upper extremity CT with contrast, Arm CT with dye

Computed tomography imaging of the upper extremity with intravenous contrast to enhance visualization of vasculature, soft tissue, and organs.

Clinical Context

Evaluates upper extremity trauma with vascular injury, soft tissue infections, masses, and inflammatory conditions where contrast enhancement aids diagnosis.
