76941 Echo guide for transfusion

Radiology

Also known as: Echo guided transfusion, Ultrasound guided transfusion

Echocardiography guidance for transfusion procedure including ultrasound visualization during blood product administration.

Clinical Context

Ultrasound guidance during intrauterine fetal transfusion or other transfusion procedures requiring visualization.
