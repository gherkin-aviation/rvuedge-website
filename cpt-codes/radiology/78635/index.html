78635 Csf ventriculography

Radiology

Also known as: CSF ventriculography, ventricular imaging

Cerebrospinal fluid ventriculography performed to assess cerebrospinal fluid flow through ventricular system.

Clinical Context

Evaluates ventricular cerebrospinal fluid flow dynamics and detects ventricular obstruction.
