72197 MRI pelvis without & with dye

Radiology

Also known as: Pelvis MRI with and without contrast, Complete pelvis MRI, Dual-phase pelvis MRI

Magnetic resonance imaging of the pelvis performed as a comprehensive study with both non-contrast and contrast-enhanced sequences to fully characterize pelvic pathology.

Clinical Context

Comprehensive imaging for complex pelvic diagnoses including staging malignancies, evaluating chronic pain, and assessing post-treatment changes where both enhancement patterns and baseline anatomy are needed.
