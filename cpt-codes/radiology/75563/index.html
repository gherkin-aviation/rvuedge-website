75563 Card MRI with stress img & dye

Radiology

Also known as: stress cardiac MRI with contrast, comprehensive cardiac MRI, dobutamine MRI with gadolinium

Magnetic resonance imaging of the heart for morphologic and functional assessment with stress imaging and intravenous contrast enhancement for comprehensive cardiac evaluation.

Clinical Context

Combined assessment of resting morphology, stress-induced ischemia, and myocardial viability with contrast enhancement; comprehensive evaluation of coronary artery disease.
