74174 Cta abdominal & plvs with contrast

Radiology

Also known as: Abdominopelvic CTA, Abdomen and pelvis angiography

Computed tomographic angiography of the abdomen and pelvis with intravenous contrast to visualize abdominal vasculature and detect vascular pathology.

Clinical Context

Evaluates aortic aneurysm, dissection, vascular injury, and mesenteric ischemia. Comprehensive vascular assessment.
