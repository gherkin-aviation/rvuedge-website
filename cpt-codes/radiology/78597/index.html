78597 Lung perfusion differential

Radiology

Also known as: differential perfusion study

Lung perfusion imaging with differential measurement performed to assess relative blood flow between lungs.

Clinical Context

Quantifies relative perfusion contribution of each lung to detect asymmetric pulmonary blood flow disease.
