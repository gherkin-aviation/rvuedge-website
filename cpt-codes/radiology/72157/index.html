72157 MRI chest spine without & with dye

Radiology

Also known as: thoracic spine MRI with and without contrast, dual-phase T-spine MRI

Magnetic resonance imaging of the thoracic spine performed with noncontrast and intravenous gadolinium contrast acquisitions. Comprehensive imaging combining baseline and enhanced sequences.

Clinical Context

Comprehensive thoracic protocol when both baseline anatomy and enhanced pathology characterization are needed for complex thoracic spine pathology.
