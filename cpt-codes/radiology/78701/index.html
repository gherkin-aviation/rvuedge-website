78701 Kidney imaging with flow

Radiology

Also known as: renal flow imaging, kidney blood flow scan

Nuclear medicine imaging of kidney with renal blood flow assessment to evaluate both morphology and renal perfusion.

Clinical Context

Evaluates renal perfusion in addition to structure; used for suspected renal artery stenosis, trauma, or renal transplant complications.
