79403 Hematopoietic nuclear treatment

Radiology

Also known as: bone marrow nuclear therapy, hematopoietic radiotherapy

Nuclear medicine therapeutic treatment for hematopoietic stem cell disorders using radiopharmaceutical therapy to modulate bone marrow function.

Clinical Context

Radiopharmaceutical therapy targeting hematopoietic system for treatment of certain leukemias, myeloproliferative disorders, or immunologic conditions.
