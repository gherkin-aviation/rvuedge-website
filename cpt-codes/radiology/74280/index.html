74280 X-ray xm colon 2cntrst std

Radiology

Also known as: double contrast barium enema, DCBE, air-contrast enema

Radiographic examination of the colon with two contrast agents (barium and air) using standard technique to provide optimal visualization of the colonic mucosa.

Clinical Context

Superior technique for detecting polyps and subtle mucosal lesions; used for colorectal cancer screening, diagnosis of inflammatory bowel disease, and detection of diverticular disease.
