70542 MRI orbit/face/neck with dye

Radiology

Also known as: MRI orbit/face/neck with contrast, enhanced MRI, gadolinium-enhanced MRI

Magnetic resonance imaging of the orbits, face, and neck with intravenous gadolinium contrast medium enhancement. Provides superior soft tissue characterization with enhanced visualization of vascular structures and pathologic enhancement.

Clinical Context

Indicated for orbital tumors, optic neuritis evaluation, cavernous sinus pathology, thyroid and neck masses, and inflammatory conditions. Gadolinium enhancement improves lesion characterization.
