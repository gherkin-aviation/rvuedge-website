78215 Lvr & spleen img static only

Radiology

Also known as: liver spleen scan static, hepatosplenic imaging

Liver and spleen imaging using static technique to visualize both organs without vascular perfusion assessment.

Clinical Context

Simultaneous assessment of liver and spleen size, shape, and function. Detects cirrhosis patterns, focal lesions, and organ displacement.
