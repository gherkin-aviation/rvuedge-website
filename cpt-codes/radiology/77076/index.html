77076 Radex osseous survey infant

Radiology

Also known as: Infant skeletal survey, Pediatric skeletal survey

Radiographic skeletal survey of an infant.

Clinical Context

Used in infants to evaluate for abuse, metabolic bone disease, or congenital skeletal dysplasias. Special considerations for radiation protection in neonates.
