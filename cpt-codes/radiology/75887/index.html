75887 Vein x-ray liver without hemodyn

Radiology

Also known as: hepatic venography, liver vein imaging, portal vein imaging

Radiographic imaging of liver veins and hepatic vasculature without hemodynamic pressure measurements for assessment of venous anatomy.

Clinical Context

Evaluates portal vein patency, hepatic vein obstruction, and Budd-Chiari syndrome without pressure measurement.
