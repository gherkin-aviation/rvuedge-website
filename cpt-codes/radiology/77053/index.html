77053 X-ray of mammary duct

Radiology

Also known as: Mammary ductogram, Duct imaging

X-ray imaging of a single mammary duct.

Clinical Context

Used to evaluate pathologic nipple discharge, assess ductal dilation, or identify intraductal masses. Involves injection of contrast material into the duct opening.
