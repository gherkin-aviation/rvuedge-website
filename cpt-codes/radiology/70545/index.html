70545 Mr angiography head with dye

Radiology

Also known as: contrast-enhanced MRA, gadolinium-enhanced angiography, dynamic MRA

Magnetic resonance angiography of the intracranial arteries and head vasculature with intravenous gadolinium contrast medium administration during arterial phase imaging.

In Plain Language

head blood vessel scan with dye; enhanced vessel imaging

Clinical Context

Provides excellent arterial detail for acute stroke evaluation, aneurysm assessment, and vascular pathology. Gadolinium bolus technique produces superior arterial phase images compared to noncontrast MRA.
