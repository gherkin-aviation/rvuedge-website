76800 Ultrasound examination spinal canal

Radiology

Also known as: Spinal ultrasound, Spinal canal ultrasound

Ultrasound examination of the spinal canal including assessment of spinal cord, cerebrospinal fluid, and surrounding structures.

Clinical Context

Assessment of spinal cord, syrinx, tethered cord, and surrounding structures, particularly useful in pediatric patients and those with contraindications to MRI.
