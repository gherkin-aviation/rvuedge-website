77427 Radiation treatment management x5

Radiology

Also known as: RT management 5 fractions, Management with 5 treatments

Radiation therapy management and treatment supervision during a course of therapy with five fractions.

Clinical Context

Physician management and supervision for short course radiation therapy. Includes treatment planning review, dose verification, and patient assessment.
