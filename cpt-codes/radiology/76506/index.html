76506 Echo examination of head

Radiology

Also known as: Head echo, Transcranial ultrasound

Echocardiography examination of the head using ultrasound imaging to assess intracranial structures and blood flow.

Clinical Context

Ultrasound evaluation of intracranial structures, particularly for assessment of cerebral blood flow and stroke evaluation in patients with inadequate acoustic windows.
