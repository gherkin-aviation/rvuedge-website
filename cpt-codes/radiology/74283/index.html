74283 Therapy nma rdctj intus/obstrcj

Radiology

Also known as: therapeutic enema, intussusception reduction, obstruction reduction

Therapeutic fluoroscopic-guided radiographic procedure for reduction of intussusception or relief of colonic obstruction using radiopaque enema technique.

Clinical Context

Primarily used in pediatric intussusception reduction or to manage partial colonic obstruction; fluoroscopic guidance allows real-time reduction of telescoped bowel loops.
