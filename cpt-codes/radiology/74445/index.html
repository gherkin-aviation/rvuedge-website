74445 X-ray examination of penis

Radiology

Also known as: penile arteriography, cavernography, penile imaging

Radiographic imaging of the penis using contrast medium for assessment of penile vasculature, cavernous bodies, and urethral anatomy.

Clinical Context

Evaluates erectile dysfunction from vascular insufficiency, penile trauma, priapism, and penile curvature; assesses arterial and cavernous filling.
