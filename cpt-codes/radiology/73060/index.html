73060 X-ray examination of humerus

Radiology

Also known as: Humerus X-ray, Upper arm bone X-ray

Plain radiographic examination of the humerus (upper arm bone) to assess for fractures, displacement, arthritis, and structural abnormalities.

In Plain Language

X-ray of the upper arm bone

Clinical Context

Evaluates humeral fractures, non-union, malunion, and post-operative changes. Standard for acute arm trauma and shoulder injuries.
