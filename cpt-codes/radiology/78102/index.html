78102 Bone marrow imaging ltd

Radiology

Also known as: bone marrow scan limited

Bone marrow imaging of limited area performed using nuclear medicine to assess marrow function and hematopoiesis.

Clinical Context

Evaluates specific bone marrow regions for myelodysplasia, infection, or infiltrative disease. Used when evaluation is limited to one or two skeletal sites.
