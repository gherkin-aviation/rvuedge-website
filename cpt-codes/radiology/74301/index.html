74301 X-rays at surgery add-on

Radiology +Add-on

Also known as: intraoperative cholangiography, surgical X-ray, on-table imaging

Intraoperative radiographic imaging performed at the time of surgery for real-time visualization and guidance during biliary or gastrointestinal surgical procedures.

In Plain Language

X-ray during surgery; operating room imaging

Clinical Context

Performed intraoperatively to confirm bile duct clearance, identify retained stones, assess surgical anatomy, and guide therapeutic maneuvers during hepatobiliary surgery.
