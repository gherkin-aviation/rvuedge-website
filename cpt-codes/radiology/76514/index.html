76514 Echo examination of eye thickness

Radiology

Also known as: Eye thickness measurement, Corneal thickness

Echocardiography examination to measure eye thickness or corneal thickness using ultrasound imaging.

In Plain Language

Measurement of eye wall thickness; Cornea thickness check

Clinical Context

Measurement of corneal thickness for evaluation of glaucoma risk, refractive surgery candidacy, and corneal pathology assessment.
