70488 CT maxillofacial without & with dye

Radiology

Also known as: maxillofacial CT with and without contrast, dual-phase facial imaging

Computed tomography scan of the maxillofacial region performed with noncontrast and intravenous contrast acquisitions. Comprehensive imaging combines bony detail with soft tissue characterization for complete maxillofacial evaluation.

Clinical Context

Utilized for complex maxillofacial trauma, extensive surgical planning, malignancy with osseous invasion assessment, and when both fine bony anatomy and soft tissue detail are required.
