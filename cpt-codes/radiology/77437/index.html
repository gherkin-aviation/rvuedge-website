77437 Surf radj therapy supfc<=150kv

Radiology

Also known as: Superficial therapy ≤150kV, Low energy superficial

Superficial radiation therapy delivery with orthovoltage photons at energy 150 kilovolt or less.

Clinical Context

Treatment delivery using orthovoltage X-rays for superficial lesions. Minimal penetration, excellent for skin tumors and superficial structures.
