78103 Bone marrow imaging mult

Radiology

Also known as: bone marrow scan multiple areas

Bone marrow imaging of multiple areas performed to assess hematopoietic function across several skeletal regions.

Clinical Context

Comprehensive marrow assessment involving multiple skeletal sites for evaluation of hematologic disorders, fever of unknown origin, or systemic disease.
