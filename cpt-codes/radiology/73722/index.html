73722 MRI joint of lower extr with dye

Radiology

Also known as: Lower extremity joint MRI with contrast

Magnetic resonance imaging of a single lower extremity joint with intravenous gadolinium contrast to enhance visualization of synovium and detect inflammation.

Clinical Context

Evaluates inflammatory arthropathies, infection, synovitis, and synovial disease. Contrast uptake distinguishes active inflammation.
