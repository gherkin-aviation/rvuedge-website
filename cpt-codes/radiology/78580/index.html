78580 Lung perfusion imaging

Radiology

Also known as: lung perfusion scan, V/Q perfusion

In Plain Language

lung blood flow test; blood circulation in lungs

Clinical Context

Evaluates pulmonary blood flow distribution to diagnose pulmonary embolism and other perfusion defects.
