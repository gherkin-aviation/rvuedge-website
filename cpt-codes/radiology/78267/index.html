78267 Urea breath tst c-14 acquisj

Radiology

Also known as: C-14 urea breath test acquisition, UBT acquisition

Urea breath test with Carbon-14 acquisition phase performed to detect Helicobacter pylori infection via breath sample collection.

In Plain Language

breath test for H. pylori bacteria part 1

Clinical Context

Initial breath collection phase of urea breath test for Helicobacter pylori detection. Part of two-phase testing for H. pylori infection.
