74455 X-ray urethra/bladder

Radiology

Also known as: voiding cystourethrography, VCUG, micturating cystourethrography

Radiographic imaging of the urethra and bladder using contrast medium during voiding to assess for reflux, obstruction, and urethral patency.

Clinical Context

Evaluates vesicoureteral reflux, bladder outlet obstruction, urethral strictures, and post-void residual; functional imaging during micturition.
