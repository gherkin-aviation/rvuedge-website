71110 X-ray examination ribs bil 3 views

Radiology

Also known as: bilateral rib X-ray, two-sided rib series, complete rib examination

Bilateral rib radiography including three views encompassing both sides of the thorax for comprehensive evaluation of both rib cages.

In Plain Language

both-sided rib X-ray; complete rib imaging

Clinical Context

Used when bilateral rib evaluation is necessary, such as multiple trauma or when comparison between sides is diagnostically important. Three views provide orthogonal assessment of both ribs.
