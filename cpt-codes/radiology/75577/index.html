75577 Quan & char c athrosclrtc plaq

Radiology

Also known as: plaque analysis, atherosclerotic plaque evaluation, coronary plaque characterization

Quantitative and qualitative characterization of coronary artery atherosclerotic plaque composition and degree of stenosis using advanced imaging analysis.

Clinical Context

Identifies high-risk plaque features including low attenuation plaque, positive remodeling, and napkin-ring sign; predicts plaque rupture risk.
