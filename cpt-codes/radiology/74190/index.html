74190 Peritoneogram rs & i

Radiology

Also known as: Peritoneogram

Peritoneography with radiographic spot imaging to visualize the peritoneal cavity and assess peritoneal pathology.

Clinical Context

Evaluates peritoneal disease, ascites characterization, and abdominal masses. Rarely performed with modern imaging availability.
