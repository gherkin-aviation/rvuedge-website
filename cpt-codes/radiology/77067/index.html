77067 Scr mammo bi incl cad

Radiology

Also known as: Screening mammo CAD bilateral, Screening mammography with CAD, screening mammogram bilateral, screening mammo

Screening mammography of both breasts including computer-aided detection analysis.

Clinical Context

Standard screening mammography for asymptomatic women. Computer-aided detection helps identify early signs of breast cancer in both breasts simultaneously.
