77080 Dxa bone density axial

Radiology

Also known as: DEXA scan, Bone density scan, DXA

Dual-energy X-ray absorptiometry measurement of bone mineral density of axial skeleton.

Clinical Context

Standard screening test for osteoporosis in axial skeleton including spine and hip. Determines fracture risk and guides treatment decisions.
