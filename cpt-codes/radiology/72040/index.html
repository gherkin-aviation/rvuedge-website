72040 X-ray examination neck spine 2-3 vw

Radiology

Also known as: cervical spine X-ray, C-spine radiograph, neck spine series

Radiography of the cervical spine including two to three views providing assessment of cervical vertebral bodies, intervertebral spaces, and cervical alignment.

In Plain Language

neck bone X-ray; cervical vertebra imaging

Clinical Context

Essential for evaluation of neck trauma, cervical spondylosis, and alignment assessment. Two to three view approach allows orthogonal assessment of cervical pathology.
