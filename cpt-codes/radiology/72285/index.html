72285 Discography cervical/thoracic spine

Radiology

Also known as: Cervical discography, Thoracic discography

Discographic examination with contrast injection into intervertebral discs of the cervical and/or thoracic spine combined with radiographic imaging to assess disc integrity and morphology.

In Plain Language

X-ray of spinal discs with dye injection; Disc imaging of neck and upper back

Clinical Context

Evaluates internal disc disruption, discogenic pain, and pre-operative surgical planning. Increasingly complemented by disc anesthesia testing.
