76998 Ultrasound guide intraop

Radiology

Also known as: Intraoperative ultrasound guidance, Intraop US guidance

Ultrasound guidance for intraoperative procedure including real-time visualization and needle or instrument positioning.

Clinical Context

Ultrasound guidance during surgery for visualization of structures, confirmation of lesion location, or guidance of surgical instruments.
