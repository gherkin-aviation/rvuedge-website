73200 CT upper extremity without dye

Radiology

Also known as: Upper extremity CT without contrast, Arm CT non-contrast

Computed tomography imaging of the upper extremity without intravenous contrast to visualize bone, soft tissue, and vascular structures.

Clinical Context

Evaluates complex upper extremity fractures, soft tissue masses, bony lesions, and pre-operative planning for reconstruction. No radiation from contrast.
