75743 Artery x-rays lungs

Radiology

Also known as: bilateral pulmonary angiography, bilateral lung vessel imaging

Angiographic radiographic imaging of pulmonary arteries bilaterally to assess bilateral lung vasculature and pulmonary vascular disease.

In Plain Language

both lung blood vessel X-ray; bilateral lung vessel imaging

Clinical Context

Evaluates pulmonary embolism bilaterally, pulmonary hypertension, vasculitis, and chronic thromboembolic disease.
