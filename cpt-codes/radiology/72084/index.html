72084 X-ray examination entire spinal 6/> vw

Radiology

Also known as: full spine X-ray six or more views, comprehensive complete spine series

Radiography of the entire spine from cervical to sacral region including six or more views providing maximum radiographic detail from multiple projections.

In Plain Language

extensive whole spine X-rays; maximum backbone detail

Clinical Context

Most comprehensive spinal radiography when maximum anatomic detail is required, such as complex scoliosis assessment with multiple projections and specialized views.
