72020 X-ray examination of spine 1 view

Radiology

Also known as: spine X-ray one view, full spine radiograph, complete spine series

Radiography of the entire spinal column including cervical, thoracic, lumbar, and sacral spine in a single view. Provides overview of spinal alignment and gross pathology.

In Plain Language

backbone one-view X-ray; complete spine picture

Clinical Context

Used for screening of spinal alignment, detection of major pathology, and as initial survey in trauma. Limited by anatomic overlap but useful for rapid assessment.
