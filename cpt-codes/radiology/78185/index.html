78185 Spleen imaging

Radiology

Also known as: spleen scan, splenic imaging

Splenic imaging performed using nuclear medicine to assess spleen size, function, and blood pooling.

Clinical Context

Evaluates splenic function, detects functional asplenia, assesses for splenic infarction, and characterizes blood cell sequestration patterns.
