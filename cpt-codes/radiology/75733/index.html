75733 Artery x-rays adrenals

Radiology

Also known as: bilateral adrenal angiography, bilateral adrenal imaging, adrenal artery imaging

Angiographic radiographic imaging of bilateral adrenal glands and their vasculature to assess adrenal pathology and compare blood supply.

In Plain Language

both adrenal glands blood vessel X-ray; bilateral adrenal vessel imaging

Clinical Context

Comprehensive evaluation of bilateral adrenal masses, pheochromocytoma, and assessment of symmetric vascular anatomy.
