74270 X-ray xm colon 1cntrst std

Radiology

Also known as: barium enema, single contrast enema, lower GI series

Radiographic examination of the colon with single contrast (barium) using standard technique to visualize colonic mucosa and assess for abnormalities.

Clinical Context

Used to diagnose colorectal polyps, carcinoma, diverticular disease, inflammatory bowel disease, and colonic obstruction; single contrast provides clear mucosal outline.
