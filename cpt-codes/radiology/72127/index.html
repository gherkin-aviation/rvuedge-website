72127 CT neck spine without & with dye

Radiology

Also known as: cervical spine CT with and without contrast, dual-phase C-spine CT

Computed tomography of the cervical spine performed with noncontrast and intravenous contrast acquisitions. Comprehensive imaging combining baseline and enhanced assessment.

Clinical Context

Comprehensive protocol when both baseline anatomy and enhanced soft tissue characterization are needed for complex cervical pathology assessment.
