74175 Cta abdomen with contrast

Radiology

Also known as: Abdominal CTA, Abdominal angiography

Computed tomographic angiography of the abdomen with intravenous contrast to visualize abdominal vasculature and detect vascular abnormalities.

Clinical Context

Evaluates aortic pathology, renal artery stenosis, mesenteric ischemia, and solid organ vascularity.
