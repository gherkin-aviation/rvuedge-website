71045 X-ray examination chest 1 view

Radiology

Also known as: chest X-ray, posteroanterior (PA) chest X-ray, CXR, lung film, chest x-ray single view, CXR 1 view

Frontal chest radiograph obtained in one view showing the thoracic cavity including lungs, heart, mediastinum, and adjacent structures.

Clinical Context

Most common radiographic study for evaluation of dyspnea, cough, fever, chest pain, and screening. Useful for pneumonia, pleural effusion, pneumothorax, and cardiac silhouette assessment.
