76080 X-ray examination of fistula

Radiology

Also known as: Fistula X-ray, Fistulography

X-ray examination of fistula using radiographic imaging to visualize abnormal tract formation.

Clinical Context

Used to visualize and evaluate abnormal tracts or channels (fistulas) between organs or body cavities, often used to assess recurrent infections or complications.
