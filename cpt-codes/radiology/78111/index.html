78111 Plasma volume multiple

Radiology

Also known as: plasma volume study multiple

Plasma volume measurement using multiple injection technique to assess total plasma volume with enhanced accuracy.

Clinical Context

Multiple time-point plasma volume assessment for more accurate quantification, particularly in patients with abnormal fluid distribution.
