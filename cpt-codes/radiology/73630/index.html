73630 X-ray examination of foot

Radiology

Also known as: Foot X-ray 3+ views, Complete foot X-ray

Plain radiographic examination of the foot in three or more views to provide comprehensive assessment of tarsal, metatarsal, and phalangeal anatomy.

Clinical Context

Comprehensive foot evaluation including weight-bearing and axial views for complex deformities, arthritis, and fracture characterization.
