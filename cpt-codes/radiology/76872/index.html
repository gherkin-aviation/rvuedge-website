76872 Ultrasound transrectal

Radiology

Also known as: Transrectal ultrasound, Endorectal ultrasound

Ultrasound examination using transrectal approach for assessment of prostate and surrounding pelvic structures.

Clinical Context

High-resolution transrectal ultrasound for prostate evaluation, biopsy guidance, and assessment of local extent of cancer.
