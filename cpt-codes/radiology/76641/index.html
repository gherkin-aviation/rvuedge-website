76641 Ultrasound breast complete

Radiology

Also known as: Breast ultrasound, Breast sonogram

Ultrasound examination of breast tissue including complete bilateral or unilateral assessment of glandular and ductal structures.

Clinical Context

Comprehensive evaluation of breast tissue for assessment of masses, cysts, microcalcifications, and determination of lesion characteristics.
