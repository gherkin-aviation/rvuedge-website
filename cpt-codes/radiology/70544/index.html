70544 Mr angiography head without dye

Radiology

Also known as: MRA head, time-of-flight MRA, noncontrast angiography, intracranial MRA

Magnetic resonance angiography of the intracranial arteries and head vasculature without intravenous gadolinium contrast medium using inherent blood flow signal.

Clinical Context

Used for intracranial aneurysm screening and characterization, arterial dissection evaluation, and vascular malformation assessment. Noncontrast technique utilizing blood flow to create angiographic images.
