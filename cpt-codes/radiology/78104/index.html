78104 Bone marrow imaging body

Radiology

Also known as: bone marrow whole body scan

Bone marrow imaging of the entire body performed to comprehensively assess total bone marrow function.

Clinical Context

Complete skeletal marrow evaluation for diffuse marrow disease, systemic infection, or metastatic disease involving bone marrow.
