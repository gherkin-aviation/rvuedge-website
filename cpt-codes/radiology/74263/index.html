74263 CT colonography screening

Radiology

Also known as: screening colonography, screening virtual colonoscopy, CTC screening

Computed tomography colonography (virtual colonoscopy) specifically performed for colorectal cancer screening in asymptomatic individuals at standard risk.

Clinical Context

Used for colorectal cancer screening in asymptomatic individuals who are candidates for screening; detects polyps greater than 6-10 millimeters with high sensitivity.
