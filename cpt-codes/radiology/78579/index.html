78579 Lung ventilation imaging

Radiology

Also known as: lung ventilation scan, V/Q ventilation

Lung ventilation imaging performed using nuclear medicine to assess air movement and ventilation distribution.

In Plain Language

breathing ability test; air distribution imaging

Clinical Context

Evaluates lung ventilation distribution to diagnose ventilation defects in chronic obstructive pulmonary disease and asthma.
