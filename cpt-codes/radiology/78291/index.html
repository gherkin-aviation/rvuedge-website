78291 Pertl-ven shunt patency test

Radiology

Also known as: shunt patency study, VP shunt study

Peritoneal-venous shunt patency test performed to evaluate ventriculoperitoneal (VP) or peritoneovenous shunt function.

Clinical Context

Evaluates shunt patency and function in patients with VP shunts or peritoneovenous shunts. Diagnoses shunt malfunction.
