70543 MRI orbt/fac/nck without &with dye

Radiology

Also known as: MRI orbit/face/neck with and without contrast, dual-contrast MRI

Magnetic resonance imaging of the orbits, face, and neck performed both without and with intravenous gadolinium contrast medium enhancement. Comprehensive imaging combining nonenhanced and enhanced sequences.

In Plain Language

complete orbital and facial scan; comprehensive neck imaging

Clinical Context

Comprehensive protocol used for complex orbital and facial pathology, extensive neck disease evaluation, and when both baseline and enhanced characteristics are needed for diagnosis.
