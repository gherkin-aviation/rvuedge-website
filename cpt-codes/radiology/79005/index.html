79005 Nuclear prescription oral administration

Radiology

Also known as: oral nuclear therapy, radioactive therapy by mouth

Nuclear medicine therapeutic treatment with radiopharmaceutical administered orally for systemic therapeutic effect.

In Plain Language

radioactive medicine pill; nuclear therapy swallowed

Clinical Context

Oral administration of therapeutic radiopharmaceuticals, most commonly radioactive iodine (I-131) for hyperthyroidism or thyroid cancer treatment.
