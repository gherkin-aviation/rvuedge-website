72270 Myelogphy 2/> spine regions

Radiology

Also known as: Multi-level myelography, Myelography 2+ regions

Myelographic examination involving two or more spinal regions using contrast injection and radiography to provide comprehensive assessment of neural structures across multiple spine levels.

Clinical Context

Used when pathology spans multiple spinal regions, requiring comprehensive imaging of cervical, thoracic, and/or lumbar areas in a single study.
