70490 CT soft tissue neck without dye

Radiology

Also known as: soft tissue neck CT, cervical soft tissue imaging, neck CT noncontrast

Computed tomography scan of the cervical soft tissues without intravenous contrast medium. Evaluates neck soft tissues including muscles, lymph nodes, thyroid, and surrounding structures.

Clinical Context

Used to evaluate neck masses, lymphadenopathy, thyroid pathology, neck trauma, and post-operative changes. Noncontrast imaging provides baseline for inflammatory or infectious processes.
