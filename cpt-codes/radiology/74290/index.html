74290 Contrast x-ray gallbladder

Radiology

Also known as: oral cholecystography, gallbladder series, oral cholangiogram

Radiographic examination of the gallbladder using contrast medium (oral or intravenous) to assess gallbladder function and visualize biliary structures.

Clinical Context

Evaluates gallbladder contractility, demonstrates cholelithiasis, and assesses for biliary dyskinesia; less commonly used since ultrasound and hepatobiliary scintigraphy are preferred.
