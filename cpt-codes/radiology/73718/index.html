73718 MRI lower extremity without dye

Radiology

Also known as: Lower extremity MRI without contrast, Leg MRI non-contrast

Magnetic resonance imaging of the lower extremity without intravenous gadolinium contrast to assess soft tissue, bone, and neurovascular structures.

Clinical Context

Evaluates soft tissue injuries, bone marrow lesions, complex fractures, and neurovascular pathology. Excellent for muscle and ligament assessment.
