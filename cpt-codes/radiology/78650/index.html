78650 Csf leakage imaging

Radiology

Also known as: CSF leak imaging, rhinorrhea detection

Cerebrospinal fluid leakage imaging performed to detect and localize cerebrospinal fluid leak.

Clinical Context

Detects and localizes cerebrospinal fluid leaks from ears or nose (rhinorrhea/otorrhea).
