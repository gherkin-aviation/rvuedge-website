71250 CT thorax diagnosis c-

Radiology

Also known as: chest CT, thoracic CT noncontrast, lung CT, chest CT without contrast, CT chest

Computed tomography of the thorax (chest) without intravenous contrast medium. Evaluates mediastinal structures, lungs, pleura, and adjacent chest wall.

In Plain Language

chest scan without dye; lung imaging

Clinical Context

Used for detection of lung nodules, pulmonary embolism screening (when combined with perfusion), mediastinal masses, pleural disease, and interstitial lung disease characterization.
