77002 Needle localization by xray

Radiology

Also known as: Needle localization, Needle wire localization

Needle localization by X-ray imaging for surgical lesion identification and biopsy guidance.

Clinical Context

Radiographic needle or wire placement to localize non-palpable lesions for surgical excision or biopsy.
