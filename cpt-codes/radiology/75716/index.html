75716 Artery x-rays arms/legs

Radiology

Also known as: bilateral limb arteriography, aortoiliac/bilateral extremity angiography, multi-limb angiography

Angiographic radiographic imaging of arteries supplying bilateral arms and/or legs to assess for stenosis, occlusion, and lower extremity runoff.

In Plain Language

both arms/legs blood vessel X-ray; bilateral limb vessel imaging

Clinical Context

Comprehensive evaluation of bilateral peripheral arterial disease and comparison; assesses aortoiliac and femoral-popliteal-tibial segments.
