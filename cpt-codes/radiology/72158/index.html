72158 MRI lumbar spine without & with dye

Radiology

Also known as: lumbar spine MRI with and without contrast, dual-phase L-spine MRI

Magnetic resonance imaging of the lumbar spine performed with noncontrast and intravenous gadolinium contrast acquisitions. Comprehensive imaging combining baseline and enhanced sequences.

Clinical Context

Comprehensive lumbar protocol when both baseline anatomy and enhanced pathology characterization are needed for complex low back pathology.
