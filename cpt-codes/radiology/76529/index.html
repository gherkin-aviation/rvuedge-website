76529 Echo examination of eye

Radiology

Also known as: Extended eye echo, Orbital ultrasound with assessment

Echocardiography examination of the eye with additional imaging assessment or extended evaluation of orbital pathology.

Clinical Context

Extended ophthalmic ultrasound examination for comprehensive evaluation of complex orbital pathology or multiple abnormalities.
