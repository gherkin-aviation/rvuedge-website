72170 X-ray examination of pelvis

Radiology

Also known as: pelvic X-ray, pelvis radiograph, hip film

Radiography of the pelvis showing hip joint articulations, pelvic bones, and sacroiliac joints. Simple frontal view.

Clinical Context

Used for evaluation of hip osteoarthritis, pelvic trauma, sacroiliac joint disease, and general pelvic pathology. Simple frontal view provides basic assessment.
