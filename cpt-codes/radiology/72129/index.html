72129 CT chest spine with dye

Radiology

Also known as: thoracic spine CT with contrast, myelogram CT thoracic, enhanced T-spine imaging

Computed tomography of the thoracic spine with intravenous contrast medium enhancement. Provides enhanced visualization of thoracic cord and nerve roots.

Clinical Context

Used for thoracic myelopathy evaluation, nerve root compression assessment, and detailed spinal cord pathology visualization. Contrast enhancement improves tissue characterization.
