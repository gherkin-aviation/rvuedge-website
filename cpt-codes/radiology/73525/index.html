73525 Contrast x-ray of hip

Radiology

Also known as: Hip arthrogram, Hip contrast X-ray

Arthrographic examination of the hip joint with contrast injection followed by radiographic imaging to visualize articular cartilage, labrum, and joint spaces.

Clinical Context

Evaluates hip labral tears, femoroacetabular impingement, loose bodies, and hip instability. Increasingly combined with fluoroscopic guidance.
