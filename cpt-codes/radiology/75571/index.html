75571 CT hrt without dye with ca test

Radiology

Also known as: coronary calcium scoring, CAC score, non-contrast cardiac CT

Computed tomography imaging of the heart without contrast, performed with coronary artery calcium scoring to quantify atherosclerotic burden.

Clinical Context

Quantifies coronary artery calcification for cardiovascular risk stratification in asymptomatic individuals; calcium score predicts future cardiac events.
