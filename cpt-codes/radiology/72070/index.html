72070 X-ray examination thoracic spine 2vws

Radiology

Also known as: thoracic spine X-ray, dorsal spine radiograph, mid-back series

Radiography of the thoracic spine including two views from anteroposterior and lateral projections. Evaluates thoracic vertebral bodies and intervertebral spaces.

Clinical Context

Used for evaluation of thoracic trauma, kyphosis assessment, infection, and malignancy. Two-view approach provides orthogonal assessment of thoracic spine.
