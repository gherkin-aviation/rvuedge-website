70150 X-ray examination of facial bones

Radiology

Also known as: facial bone x-ray complete, facial imaging comprehensive

Radiographic examination of the facial bones (maxilla, zygomatic arches, nasal bones) with comprehensive views.

Clinical Context

Used for comprehensive facial fracture evaluation, sinusitis assessment, or pre-surgical planning.
