73000 X-ray examination of collar bone

Radiology

Also known as: Collarbone X-ray, Clavicular X-ray

Plain radiographic examination of the clavicle (collarbone) to assess for fractures, dislocations, arthritis, and congenital variations.

Clinical Context

Commonly performed for shoulder injuries, acromioclavicular joint pathology, and post-traumatic assessment. Anterior-posterior and angled views standard.
