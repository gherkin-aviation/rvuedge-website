70350 X-ray head for orthodontia

Radiology

Also known as: ortho X-ray, intraoral head film, orthodontic radiograph

Intraoral X-ray imaging of the head and jaw specifically for orthodontic assessment and treatment planning. Used to evaluate dental occlusion, tooth positioning, and skeletal anatomy.

Clinical Context

Orthodontists use this code when initiating braces treatment or assessing progress. Essential for evaluating crowding, malocclusion, and bite relationships before and during orthodontic intervention.
