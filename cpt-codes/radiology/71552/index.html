71552 MRI chest without & with dye

Radiology

Also known as: chest MRI with and without contrast, dual-phase cardiac MRI, complete thoracic MRI

Magnetic resonance imaging of the thorax (chest) performed with noncontrast and intravenous gadolinium contrast acquisitions. Comprehensive imaging combining baseline and enhanced cardiac assessment.

Clinical Context

Comprehensive cardiac protocol when both baseline tissue characterization and enhancement patterns are needed for diagnosis, particularly for complex cardiac pathology.
