72100 X-ray examination l-s spine 2/3 vws

Radiology

Also known as: lumbar spine X-ray, L-S spine radiograph, lumbosacral series

Radiography of the lumbar-sacral spine including two to three views from anteroposterior and lateral projections. Evaluates lumbar and sacral vertebrae.

Clinical Context

Most common spinal radiography study used for low back pain evaluation, disc disease assessment, and vertebral stability. Two to three view approach is standard.
