77295 3-d radiotherapy plan

Radiology

Also known as: 3D conformal therapy, 3D-CRT planning

Three-dimensional conformal radiation therapy treatment planning.

Clinical Context

Sophisticated planning using three-dimensional dose calculations and beam shaping to conform dose to tumor geometry while minimizing normal tissue exposure.
