73070 X-ray examination of elbow

Radiology

Also known as: Elbow X-ray 1-2 views, Basic elbow X-ray

Plain radiographic examination of the elbow joint in two or fewer views to assess joint alignment, fractures, and elbow pathology.

Clinical Context

Initial screening for elbow pain, fractures, dislocation, epicondylitis, and osteoarthritis. Standard views are anteroposterior, lateral, and axial.
