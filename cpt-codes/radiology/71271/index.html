71271 CT thorax lung cancer scr c-

Radiology

Also known as: lung cancer screening CT, low-dose chest CT, LDCT

Computed tomography of the thorax without intravenous contrast medium specifically for lung cancer screening in high-risk patients. Low-dose acquisition emphasizes pulmonary parenchyma.

In Plain Language

lung cancer screening scan; preventive lung imaging

Clinical Context

Used for screening of high-risk patients meeting criteria (age 55-77, significant smoking history). Low-dose technique reduces radiation exposure while maintaining adequate lung nodule detection.
