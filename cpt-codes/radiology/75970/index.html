75970 Vascular biopsy

Radiology

Also known as: vascular biopsy, angioscopically-guided biopsy, vascular tissue sampling

Percutaneous radiographically-guided biopsy of vascular tissue or lesion for histologic diagnosis during angiographic procedure.

Clinical Context

Obtains tissue diagnosis of vascular lesions, vasculitis, or intravascular masses under radiographic visualization.
