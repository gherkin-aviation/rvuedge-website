78740 Ureteral reflux study

Radiology

Also known as: reflux study, voiding cystography

Nuclear medicine study to detect and quantify ureteral reflux (backflow of urine from bladder to ureters).

Clinical Context

Evaluates for vesicoureteral reflux, particularly in children with recurrent urinary tract infections or abnormal bladder-ureteral anatomy; assesses severity and laterality of reflux.
