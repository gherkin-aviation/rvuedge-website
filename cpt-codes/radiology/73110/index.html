73110 X-ray examination of wrist

Radiology

Also known as: Wrist X-ray 3+ views, Complete wrist X-ray

Plain radiographic examination of the wrist joint in three or more views to provide comprehensive assessment of carpal anatomy and wrist pathology.

Clinical Context

Detailed evaluation of complex wrist fractures, carpal instability, scaphoid pathology, and osteoarthritis. Additional views including clenched fist views improve assessment.
