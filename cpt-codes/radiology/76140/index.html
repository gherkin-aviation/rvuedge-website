76140 X-ray consultation

Radiology

Also known as: X-ray consultation, Radiologist consultation, Film review

Radiologist's consultation interpretation of X-ray films or images, providing diagnostic opinion without performing the actual examination.

Clinical Context

Represents a radiologist's interpretive consultation on films obtained elsewhere, common for second opinions or distant consultations.
