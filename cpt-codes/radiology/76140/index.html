76140 X-ray consultation

Radiology

Also known as: X-ray consultation, Radiologist consultation, Film review

Radiologist's consultation interpretation of X-ray films or images, providing diagnostic opinion without performing the actual examination.

In Plain Language

Doctor reviews imaging; Second opinion on X-rays

Clinical Context

Represents a radiologist's interpretive consultation on films obtained elsewhere, common for second opinions or distant consultations.

RVU Information

CPT 76140 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Radiology codes require a written order, clinical indication, and a formal interpretation report. The professional component (modifier -26) and technical component (modifier -TC) may be billed separately.
