76817 Transvaginal ultrasound obstetric

Radiology

Also known as: Transvaginal obstetric ultrasound, Vaginal ultrasound

Transvaginal ultrasound examination during obstetric evaluation for enhanced assessment of early pregnancy or pelvic structures.

Clinical Context

Higher resolution transvaginal approach for evaluation of early pregnancy viability, multiple gestations, and assessment of cervical length.
