77074 Radex osseous survey lmtd

Radiology

Also known as: Limited skeletal survey, Partial skeletal survey

Limited radiographic survey of the skeletal system.

Clinical Context

Used to evaluate specific skeletal regions for fractures, metastatic disease, or metabolic bone disease. Limited scope compared to complete skeletal survey.
