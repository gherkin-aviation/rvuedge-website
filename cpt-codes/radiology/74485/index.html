74485 Dilation urtr/urt rs & i

Radiology

Also known as: ureteral dilation, urethral dilation, fluoroscopic dilation

Fluoroscopic radiographic guidance and imaging during dilation of a narrowed ureteral or urethral segment to restore normal urinary flow.

Clinical Context

Real-time fluoroscopic guidance during percutaneous or endoscopic dilation of ureteral and urethral strictures to relieve obstruction and restore normal flow.
