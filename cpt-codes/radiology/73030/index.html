73030 X-ray examination of shoulder

Radiology

Also known as: Shoulder X-ray 3+ views, Complete shoulder X-ray

Plain radiographic examination of the shoulder joint in three or more views to provide comprehensive assessment of glenohumeral joint and surrounding structures.

Clinical Context

Detailed evaluation of shoulder pathology including complex fractures, instability, rotator cuff calcification, and osteoarthritis. Additional views improve diagnostic accuracy.
