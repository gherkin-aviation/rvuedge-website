70160 X-ray examination of nasal bones

Radiology

Also known as: nasal bone x-ray, nasal imaging

Radiographic examination of the nasal bones to evaluate for fracture or structural deformity.

Clinical Context

Used for nasal bone fracture assessment following trauma.
