76000 Fluoroscopy <1 hour phys/qhp

Radiology

Also known as: fluoroscopy supervision, real-time fluoroscopy, fluoroscopic guidance

Fluoroscopic real-time radiographic imaging lasting less than one hour, supervised and interpreted by a physician or qualified health professional.

Clinical Context

Provides real-time radiographic visualization during various diagnostic and therapeutic procedures for guidance and documentation.
