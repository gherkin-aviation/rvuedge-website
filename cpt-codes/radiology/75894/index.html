75894 X-rays transcath therapy

Radiology

Also known as: interventional angiography, fluoroscopic guidance, transcatheter therapy imaging

Radiographic imaging during transcatheter therapeutic intervention to visualize vascular anatomy and guide placement of therapeutic devices.

Clinical Context

Provides real-time fluoroscopic guidance during vascular interventions including angioplasty, stent placement, embolization, and thrombolysis.
