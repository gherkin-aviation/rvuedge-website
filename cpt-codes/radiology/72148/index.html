72148 MRI lumbar spine without dye

Radiology

Also known as: lumbar spine MRI, L-spine MRI noncontrast, lumbosacral imaging, MRI lumbar spine without contrast, MRI L-spine

Magnetic resonance imaging of the lumbar spine without intravenous gadolinium contrast medium. Excellent soft tissue contrast for evaluation of lumbar discs, spinal cord, and nerve roots.

In Plain Language

lower back spine scan without dye; lumbar MRI imaging

Clinical Context

Most commonly used MRI study for low back pain, disc herniation, spinal stenosis, and radiculopathy. Standard of care for detailed lumbar pathology evaluation.
