73085 Contrast x-ray of elbow

Radiology

Also known as: Elbow arthrogram, Elbow contrast X-ray

Arthrographic examination of the elbow joint with contrast injection followed by radiographic imaging to visualize articular surfaces, synovial structures, and loose bodies.

Clinical Context

Evaluates elbow instability, loose bodies, osteochondral lesions, and synovial pathology. Less commonly used now with advanced ultrasound and MRI availability.
