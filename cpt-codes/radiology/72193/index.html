72193 CT pelvis with dye

Radiology

Also known as: pelvic CT with contrast, enhanced pelvic imaging, contrast-enhanced pelvis CT

Computed tomography of the pelvis with intravenous contrast medium enhancement. Provides improved visualization of pelvic soft tissues, organs, and vascular structures.

In Plain Language

pelvic scan with dye; enhanced pelvic imaging

Clinical Context

Used for pelvic tumors, infection assessment, inflammatory bowel disease, and detailed organ evaluation. Contrast enhancement improves soft tissue characterization.
