75710 Artery x-rays arm/leg

Radiology

Also known as: limb arteriography, extremity angiography, arm/leg vessel imaging

Angiographic radiographic imaging of an artery supplying an arm or leg to assess for stenosis, occlusion, or anomalies.

Clinical Context

Evaluates peripheral arterial disease, claudication, critical limb ischemia, and arterial trauma; assesses runoff vessels.
