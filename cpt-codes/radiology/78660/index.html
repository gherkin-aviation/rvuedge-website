78660 Nuclear examination of tear flow

Radiology

Also known as: tear flow study, lacrimal imaging

Tear flow imaging performed to assess lacrimal gland function and tear drainage using nuclear medicine.

In Plain Language

tear duct function test; eye tear imaging

Clinical Context

Evaluates lacrimal gland function and tear drainage to diagnose dry eye syndrome and lacrimal obstruction.
