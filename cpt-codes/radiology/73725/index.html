73725 Mr ang lower external w or without dye

Radiology

Also known as: Lower extremity MRA, Leg vascular MRI

Magnetic resonance angiography of the lower extremity with and without intravenous contrast to visualize arterial and venous vasculature.

Clinical Context

Evaluates lower extremity vascular anatomy, arterial stenosis, venous insufficiency, and malformations without ionizing radiation.
