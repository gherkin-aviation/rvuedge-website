74360 X-ray guide gi dilation

Radiology

Also known as: fluoroscopic dilation, esophageal dilation, stricture dilation

Fluoroscopic radiographic guidance during endoscopic dilation of a strictured or narrowed gastric or upper gastrointestinal segment for restoration of normal lumen diameter.

In Plain Language

X-ray guided throat/stomach widening; guided stricture treatment

Clinical Context

Real-time fluoroscopic guidance during endoscopic dilation of esophageal, pyloric, or gastric strictures caused by caustic ingestion, radiation, or surgery.
