77438 Surf radiation therapy orthvlt>150-500

Radiology

Also known as: Superficial therapy 150-500kV, Intermediate energy superficial

Superficial radiation therapy delivery with orthovoltage photons at energy greater than 150 kilovolt to 500 kilovolt.

Clinical Context

Treatment delivery using orthovoltage X-rays with moderate energy for superficial and some intermediate-depth lesions.
