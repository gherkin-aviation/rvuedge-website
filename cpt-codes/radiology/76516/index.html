76516 Echo examination of eye

Radiology

Also known as: Eye echo, Orbital ultrasound

Echocardiography examination of the eye including orbit and surrounding structures using ultrasound imaging.

Clinical Context

Comprehensive ultrasound evaluation of eye structures and orbit for assessment of lesions, masses, retinal detachment, and ocular pathology.
