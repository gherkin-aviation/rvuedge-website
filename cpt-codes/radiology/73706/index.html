73706 CT angio lower extr without & with dye

Radiology

Also known as: Lower extremity CTA, Leg CTA, Lower extremity vascular CT

Computed tomographic angiography of the lower extremity with non-contrast and contrast-enhanced sequences to visualize arterial and venous vasculature.

Clinical Context

Evaluates lower extremity vascular injury, arterial stenosis, venous thrombosis, deep vein thrombosis, and peripheral arterial disease.
