75880 Vein x-ray eye socket

Radiology

Also known as: orbital venography, eye socket vein imaging, ophthalmic venography

Radiographic imaging of veins in the orbit (eye socket) using contrast medium to assess orbital venous drainage and venous abnormalities.

In Plain Language

eye socket vein X-ray; orbital vein imaging

Clinical Context

Evaluates orbital venous obstruction, cavernous sinus thrombosis, and orbital venous malformations.
