78600 Brain image < 4 views

Radiology

Also known as: brain scan limited views, cerebral imaging limited

Brain imaging with fewer than 4 views performed using nuclear medicine to assess cerebral blood flow and perfusion.

Clinical Context

Limited brain imaging for acute evaluation of stroke, focal lesions, or dementia screening.
