76801 Ob ultrasound < 14 wks single fetus

Radiology

Also known as: First trimester ultrasound, Dating ultrasound

Obstetric ultrasound examination of a single fetus at less than 14 weeks gestation for dating and initial viability assessment.

Clinical Context

Initial ultrasound for pregnancy confirmation, gestational age dating, viability assessment, and evaluation for multiple gestations.
