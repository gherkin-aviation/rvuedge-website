78730 Urinary bladder retention

Radiology

Also known as: bladder retention study, post-void residual imaging

Nuclear medicine imaging to evaluate urinary bladder retention and postvoid residual urine volume.

Clinical Context

Used to assess incomplete bladder emptying in patients with urinary retention, neurogenic bladder, or obstructive symptoms; documents volume of urine remaining after voiding.
