71260 CT thorax diagnosis c+

Radiology

Also known as: chest CT with contrast, enhanced thoracic CT, contrast-enhanced lung CT, CT chest with contrast

Computed tomography of the thorax (chest) with intravenous contrast medium enhancement. Provides enhanced visualization of mediastinal vasculature and soft tissue pathology.

Clinical Context

Used for mediastinal mass characterization, vascular pathology evaluation, and improved soft tissue contrast. Particularly useful for cardiac, aortic, and large vessel assessment.
