75559 Cardiac MRI with stress img

Radiology

Also known as: stress cardiac MRI, cardiac stress MRI, dobutamine MRI

Magnetic resonance imaging of the heart for morphologic and functional assessment with stress imaging to evaluate myocardial ischemia and inducible wall motion abnormalities.

In Plain Language

heart stress scan; heart imaging under stress

Clinical Context

Evaluates myocardial ischemia, viability, and functional reserve using pharmacologic stress (dobutamine); assesses regional wall motion and perfusion.
