78016 Thyroid met imaging/studies

Radiology

Also known as: thyroid scan with additional studies, comprehensive thyroid imaging

Thyroid metabolic imaging with additional studies performed to evaluate thyroid function using nuclear medicine techniques.

In Plain Language

extended thyroid scan; detailed thyroid nuclear medicine study

Clinical Context

Provides more comprehensive thyroid assessment including uptake and imaging studies for complex thyroid conditions or when initial studies are inconclusive.
