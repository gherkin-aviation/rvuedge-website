79445 Nuclear prescription intra-arterial

Radiology

Also known as: intra-arterial nuclear therapy, regional radiotherapy

Nuclear medicine therapeutic treatment with radiopharmaceutical administered intra-arterially for regional therapy of vascular territory disease.

Clinical Context

Direct intra-arterial injection of therapeutic radiopharmaceuticals for regional treatment of malignancy or vascular disease in specific organ territories.
