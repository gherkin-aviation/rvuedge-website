70130 X-ray examination of mastoids

Radiology

Also known as: mastoid x-ray complete, mastoid imaging comprehensive

Radiographic examination of the mastoid bone(s) with complete views (Towne's, Schuller, or other comprehensive projections).

Clinical Context

Used for comprehensive mastoid assessment, cholesteatoma evaluation, or temporal bone fracture assessment.
