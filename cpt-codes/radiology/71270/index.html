71270 CT thorax diagnosis c-/c+

Radiology

Also known as: chest CT with and without contrast, dual-phase thoracic CT, split-dose chest CT

Computed tomography of the thorax (chest) performed with noncontrast and intravenous contrast acquisitions. Combines imaging techniques for comprehensive thoracic evaluation.

Clinical Context

Comprehensive protocol when both baseline lung detail and enhanced vascular/soft tissue characterization are needed, such as complex mediastinal masses or combined pulmonary and cardiac pathology.
