78230 Salivary gland imaging

Radiology

Also known as: salivary gland scan, sialocintigraphy

Salivary gland imaging performed using nuclear medicine to assess salivary gland function and anatomy.

In Plain Language

salivary gland test; spit gland imaging

Clinical Context

Evaluates salivary gland function, detects Sjögren syndrome, assesses obstruction, and identifies salivary gland tumors.
