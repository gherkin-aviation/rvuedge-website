75833 Vein x-ray kidneys

Radiology

Also known as: bilateral renal venography, bilateral kidney vein imaging

Radiographic imaging of bilateral renal veins using contrast medium to assess bilateral renal venous drainage and thrombosis.

Clinical Context

Comprehensive evaluation of bilateral renal vein thrombosis, renal vein stenosis, and comparison of bilateral renal venous anatomy.
