70100 X-ray examination of jaw <4views

Radiology

Also known as: jaw x-ray limited, mandibular x-ray, jaw imaging

Radiographic examination of the jaw (mandible/maxilla) with fewer than 4 radiographic views.

Clinical Context

Used for suspected fracture, tumor evaluation, or dental pathology with limited imaging.
