73721 MRI jnt of lower extre without dye

Radiology

Also known as: Lower extremity joint MRI without contrast, MRI knee without contrast, knee MRI

Magnetic resonance imaging of a single lower extremity joint without gadolinium contrast to assess articular cartilage, ligaments, and intra-articular structures.

In Plain Language

MRI of one leg joint without dye

Clinical Context

Focused evaluation of ankle, knee, or hip joint pathology including ligament tears, meniscal lesions, and cartilage damage.
