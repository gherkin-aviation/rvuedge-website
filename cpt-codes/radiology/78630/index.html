78630 Cerebrospinal fluid scan

Radiology

Also known as: CSF scan, cerebrospinal fluid imaging

Cerebrospinal fluid imaging performed using nuclear medicine to assess cerebrospinal fluid flow and clearance.

In Plain Language

spinal fluid imaging; brain fluid flow test

Clinical Context

Evaluates cerebrospinal fluid flow dynamics, detects obstruction, and assesses normal pressure hydrocephalus.
