76813 Ob ultrasound nuchal meas 1 gest

Radiology

Also known as: Nuchal translucency scan, NT scan, First trimester screening

Obstetric ultrasound examination for nuchal translucency measurement during first gestation for aneuploidy screening.

Clinical Context

First trimester (11-14 weeks) measurement of fetal nuchal translucency for aneuploidy risk assessment combined with biochemical markers.
