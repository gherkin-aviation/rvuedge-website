73223 MRI joint upper extr without & with dye

Radiology

Also known as: Joint MRI with and without contrast, Complete joint MRI

Magnetic resonance imaging of a single upper extremity joint performed as a comprehensive study with both non-contrast and contrast-enhanced sequences.

Clinical Context

Comprehensive joint evaluation when both baseline anatomy and inflammatory assessment are needed for complex pathology.
