72130 CT chest spine without & with dye

Radiology

Also known as: thoracic spine CT with and without contrast, dual-phase T-spine CT

Computed tomography of the thoracic spine performed with noncontrast and intravenous contrast acquisitions. Comprehensive imaging combining baseline and enhanced assessment.

Clinical Context

Comprehensive protocol when both baseline thoracic anatomy and enhanced pathology characterization are needed for complex thoracic spine evaluation.
