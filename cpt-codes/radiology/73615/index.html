73615 Contrast x-ray of ankle

Radiology

Also known as: Ankle arthrogram, Ankle contrast X-ray

Arthrographic examination of the ankle joint with contrast injection followed by radiographic imaging to visualize articular cartilage and intra-articular pathology.

Clinical Context

Evaluates osteochondral lesions, impingement, and intra-articular loose bodies. Less commonly used with advanced ultrasound and MRI availability.
