Radiology

Three-dimensional (3D) surface rendering with interpretation and post-processing of imaging data to create volumetric reconstructions.

In Plain Language

3D picture made from imaging; Computer processing to create 3D images

Clinical Context

Advanced visualization technique to create 3D volumetric reconstructions from computed tomography or magnetic resonance data for surgical planning or complex anatomic assessment.
