72141 MRI neck spine without dye

Radiology

Also known as: cervical spine MRI, C-spine MRI noncontrast, neck spine imaging, MRI cervical spine without contrast, MRI C-spine

Magnetic resonance imaging of the cervical spine without intravenous gadolinium contrast medium. Excellent soft tissue contrast for evaluation of cervical spinal cord and neural structures.

Clinical Context

Standard study for cervical myelopathy, radiculopathy, disc herniation, and cervical cord pathology evaluation. Superior soft tissue contrast to CT without radiation.
