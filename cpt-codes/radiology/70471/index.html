70471 Cta h & n c+ with noncontrast img

Radiology

Also known as: CTA head and neck, CT angiogram, cerebral angiography, carotid CTA

Computed tomographic angiography of the head and neck with intravenous contrast medium combined with noncontrast imaging. High-quality arterial phase imaging evaluates cerebral and carotid vasculature, including intracranial and extracranial vessels.

In Plain Language

blood vessel imaging; stroke prevention scan

Clinical Context

Standard study for acute ischemic stroke workup, evaluation of carotid stenosis, intracranial aneurysm screening, and assessment of vascular dissection. Provides vessel-specific visualization superior to conventional CT for vascular pathology.
