73100 X-ray examination of wrist

Radiology

Also known as: Wrist X-ray 1-2 views, Basic wrist X-ray

Plain radiographic examination of the wrist joint in two or fewer views to assess alignment, carpal bone relationships, and wrist pathology.

Clinical Context

Initial screening for wrist pain, fractures, sprains, and osteoarthritis. Standard views are posteroanterior, lateral, and oblique.
