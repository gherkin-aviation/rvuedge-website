73050 X-ray examination of shoulders

Radiology

Also known as: Bilateral shoulder X-ray, Both shoulders X-ray

Plain radiographic examination of both shoulders (bilateral) in a single view to compare anatomy and assess symmetry.

In Plain Language

X-ray of both shoulders at once

Clinical Context

Used to compare shoulder alignment, assess bilateral pathology, or for postural evaluation. Single view limits detail compared to individual shoulder radiography.
