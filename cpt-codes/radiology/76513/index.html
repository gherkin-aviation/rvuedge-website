76513 Ophthalmic ultrasound diagnosis anterior segment ultrasound uni/bi

Radiology

Also known as: Anterior segment ultrasound, OCT

Ophthalmic ultrasound diagnostic imaging of anterior segment structures unilaterally or bilaterally using high-frequency ultrasound.

In Plain Language

Ultrasound of front of eye; Imaging of eye's front part

Clinical Context

High-resolution assessment of cornea, anterior chamber, lens, and iris for measurement and evaluation of anterior chamber depth, corneal thickness, and anterior pathology.
