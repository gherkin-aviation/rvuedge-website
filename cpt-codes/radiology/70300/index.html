70300 X-ray examination of teeth

Radiology

Also known as: dental x-ray single, periapical x-ray, tooth imaging

Radiographic examination of teeth with single or limited periapical view for dental assessment.

Clinical Context

Used for dental caries, periapical pathology, or endodontic treatment planning.
