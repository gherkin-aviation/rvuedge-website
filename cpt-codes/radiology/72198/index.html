72198 Mr angio pelvis without & with dye

Radiology

Also known as: Pelvis MR angiography, Pelvic vascular MRI

Magnetic resonance angiography of the pelvis with both non-contrast and contrast-enhanced imaging to visualize blood vessels and assess vascular patency.

Clinical Context

Evaluates pelvic vascular anatomy and detects arterial or venous abnormalities including iliac artery stenosis, pelvic varices, and vascular malformations without ionizing radiation.
