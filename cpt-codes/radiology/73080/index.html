73080 X-ray examination of elbow

Radiology

Also known as: Elbow X-ray 3+ views, Complete elbow X-ray

Plain radiographic examination of the elbow joint in three or more views to provide comprehensive assessment of elbow anatomy and pathology.

Clinical Context

Detailed evaluation of complex elbow fractures, instability, contractures, and osteoarthritis. Additional views including radial head views improve diagnosis.
