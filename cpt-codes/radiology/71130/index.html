71130 X-ray strenoclavic joint 3/>vws

Radiology

Also known as: sternoclavicular joint X-ray, SC joint radiograph, clavicle X-ray

Radiography of the sternoclavicular joints including three or more views. Evaluates joint space narrowing, degenerative disease, subluxation, and acromioclavicular pathology.

Clinical Context

Ordered for shoulder/chest junction pain, acromioclavicular joint pathology, post-traumatic changes, and degenerative joint disease of the sternoclavicular and acromioclavicular joints.
