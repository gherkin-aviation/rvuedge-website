96547 Intraop hipec procedures first 60 minutes

Medicine

Also known as: HIPEC, Intraoperative chemotherapy, Heated peritoneal chemo

Intraoperative hyperthermic intraperitoneal chemotherapy procedure during cytoreductive surgery, including first 60 minutes of heated chemotherapy perfusion.

Clinical Context

Used intraoperatively after cytoreductive surgery for peritoneal carcinomatosis, appendiceal cancer, and ovarian cancer. Heated chemotherapy has enhanced penetration into tissues.
