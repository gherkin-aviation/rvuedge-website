95939 C motor evoked upper & lower limbs

Medicine

Also known as: CMEP bilateral, MEP four limb, motor evoked potential

Cranial motor evoked potential testing of both upper and lower limbs to comprehensively assess bilateral motor pathway integrity.

Clinical Context

Evaluates comprehensive motor pathway function during complex spinal or intracranial surgery; essential for extensive procedures requiring bilateral lower and upper limb motor monitoring.
