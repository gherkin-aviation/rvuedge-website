92950 Heart/lung resuscitation cpr

Medicine Global 0d

Also known as: CPR, closed chest compression, resuscitation

Cardiopulmonary resuscitation (CPR) including closed chest massage and artificial ventilation performed by a physician.

Clinical Context

Emergency procedure for cardiac arrest to restore blood circulation and oxygenation, performed when patient loses pulse and consciousness, critical intervention to prevent death.
