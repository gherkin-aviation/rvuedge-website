92555 Speech threshold audiometry

Medicine

Also known as: speech recognition threshold, SRT

Speech threshold audiometry; assessment of quietest speech intensity at which patient can understand 50 percent of words.

In Plain Language

speech hearing test

Clinical Context

Used to determine speech discrimination ability and to verify consistency with pure tone thresholds.
