90935 Hemodialysis one evaluation

Medicine Global 0d

Also known as: Dialysis access study, Vascular access study, Hemodialysis access evaluation

Hemodialysis access study, evaluation of vascular access function and patency for dialysis treatment planning.

Clinical Context

Initial evaluation of arteriovenous fistula or graft to assess adequacy for hemodialysis before initiating treatment.
