90587 Dengue vacc quad 3 dose subcutaneous

Medicine

Also known as: dengue vaccine 3-dose, dengvaxia 3-dose

Dengue virus vaccine quadrivalent administered by subcutaneous injection in 3-dose series for immunization.

In Plain Language

dengue fever vaccine series; tropical disease prevention

Clinical Context

Live attenuated quadrivalent dengue vaccine 3-dose series for dengue prevention in endemic regions, particularly for seronegative or partially immune individuals.
