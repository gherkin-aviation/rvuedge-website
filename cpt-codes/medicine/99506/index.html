99506 Home visit intramuscular injection

Medicine

Also known as: home injection visit, IM injection at home

Home visit service for intramuscular injection administration, including medication preparation, injection technique, and post-injection monitoring.

Clinical Context

Used for home-based administration of intramuscular medications (vitamin B12, antibiotics, biologics) when office or clinic visits are impractical.
