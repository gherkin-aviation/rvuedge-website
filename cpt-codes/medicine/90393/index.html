90393 Vaccina ig intramuscular

Medicine

Also known as: vaccinia IG, smallpox IG, vaccinia treatment

Vaccinia immunoglobulin administered intramuscularly for treatment of vaccinia complications in vaccinated individuals.

In Plain Language

smallpox vaccine complication treatment; vaccinia infection prevention

Clinical Context

Immunoglobulin directed against vaccinia virus complications such as progressive vaccinia, eczema vaccinatum, and vaccinia keratitis in vaccinated patients.
