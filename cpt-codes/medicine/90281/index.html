90281 Human ig intramuscular

Medicine

Also known as: human IG IM, immunoglobulin injection

Human immunoglobulin injection administered by intramuscular route for passive immunity and disease prevention.

In Plain Language

antibody injection in muscle; immune protein shot

Clinical Context

Used for passive immunization against infectious diseases and for treatment of immunodeficiency disorders via intramuscular administration.
