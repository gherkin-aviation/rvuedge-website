94004 Vent management nf per day

Medicine

Also known as: nursing facility ventilator care, skilled nursing vent management, facility ventilation

Management of patients on mechanical ventilation in a nursing facility or care setting, per day of ventilator support.

Clinical Context

Used for chronic ventilator management in post-acute care settings including nursing facilities and long-term acute care hospitals.
