90847 Family psytx with pt 50 minutes

Medicine

Also known as: Family therapy with patient, Family systems therapy, Conjoint family therapy, family psychotherapy with patient, family therapy

Family psychotherapy with patient present, 50 minutes of therapeutic interaction including the identified patient and family members.

Clinical Context

Provided to patient and family members together to improve family communication, relationships, and environmental support for psychiatric treatment.
