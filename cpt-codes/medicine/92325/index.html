92325 Modification of contact lens

Medicine

Also known as: contact lens modification, lens adjustment, contact lens alteration

Modification of existing contact lens to adjust parameters for optimal fit and comfort.

In Plain Language

fixing or adjusting contact lens; lens adjustment service

Clinical Context
