96567 Pdt destruction prmlg lesion skn

Medicine

Also known as: PDT, Photodynamic therapy, Light therapy

Photodynamic therapy distending lesions on skin. Includes administration of photosensitizing agent and light activation for non-malignant dermatologic lesions.

In Plain Language

Light therapy for skin lesions; Special light and medicine skin treatment

Clinical Context

Used for actinic keratosis, superficial basal cell carcinoma, and other non-melanoma skin lesions. Topical photosensitizers activated by light destroy abnormal tissue.
