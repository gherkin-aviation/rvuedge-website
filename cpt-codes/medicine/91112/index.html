91112 Gi wireless capsule measure

Medicine

Also known as: capsule endoscopy with measurement, capsule transit time, capsule imaging assessment

Gastrointestinal tract imaging using wireless capsule technology with measurement of transit time and identification of lesions.

Clinical Context

Wireless capsule imaging with quantitative assessment of intestinal transit time. Evaluates for bleeding sources, polyps, and motility disorders.
