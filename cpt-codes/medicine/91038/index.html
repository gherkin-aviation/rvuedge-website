91038 Esophageal imped funct test > 1hr

Medicine +Add-on Global 0d

Also known as: extended impedance test, prolonged clearance study, extended bolus transit

Esophageal impedance function test performed for more than one hour to provide extended assessment of bolus clearance.

In Plain Language

extended test of swallowing ability; long-term esophagus clearance monitoring

Clinical Context

Extended diagnostic study over more than one hour to comprehensively assess esophageal clearance of multiple boluses. Identifies subtle clearing defects.
