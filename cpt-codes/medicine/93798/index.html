93798 Phys/qhp outpatient car rhab with ecg

Medicine Global 0d

Also known as: cardiac rehab with ECG, monitored cardiac rehab, ECG-monitored cardiac rehabilitation

Cardiac rehabilitation services provided by a physician or qualified health professional for outpatient cardiac rehabilitation with electrocardiogram monitoring.

In Plain Language

monitored heart recovery program; supervised cardiac exercise with heart monitoring; post-heart heart rehabilitation with ECG

Clinical Context

Used for higher-risk cardiac patients requiring continuous electrocardiographic surveillance during rehabilitation, including those with prior arrhythmias or complex cardiac history.
