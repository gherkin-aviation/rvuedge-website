93224 External ecg rec up to 48 hours

Medicine

Also known as: Holter monitor, 24-48 hour ECG, ambulatory ECG

External electrocardiogram (ECG) recording up to 48 hours in duration with analysis and interpretation.

Clinical Context

Continuous ECG recording in ambulatory patients to detect episodic arrhythmias, ischemia, or bradycardia not captured on standard ECG, standard for syncope and palpitation evaluation.
