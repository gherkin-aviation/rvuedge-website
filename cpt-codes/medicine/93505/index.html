93505 Endomyocardial biopsy

Medicine +Add-on Global 0d

Also known as: Endomyocardial biopsy, EMB, myocardial biopsy

Endomyocardial biopsy performed during cardiac catheterization using specialized biopsy catheter to obtain myocardial tissue samples.

Clinical Context

Used for diagnosis of myocarditis, cardiomyopathy etiology, cardiac transplant rejection, infiltrative diseases (amyloidosis, sarcoidosis), and infectious causes of myocardial disease. Essential for definitive diagnosis in uncertain cases.
