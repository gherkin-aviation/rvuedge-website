93581 Transcath closure of vsd

Medicine Global 0d

Also known as: Transcatheter VSD closure, catheter VSD closure, percutaneous VSD closure

Transcatheter closure of ventricular septal defect using percutaneous catheter-based technique with deployment of closure device.

In Plain Language

ventricular hole closure with catheter; lower heart chamber hole repair without surgery; catheter-based ventricle hole closure

Clinical Context

Minimally invasive closure for selected ventricular septal defects, particularly muscular VSDs or post-infarction mechanical complications. Emerging technique as alternative to surgical repair in appropriate anatomy.
