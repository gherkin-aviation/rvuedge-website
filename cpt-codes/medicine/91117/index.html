91117 Colon motility 6 hour study

Medicine Global 0d

Also known as: colon manometry, colonic motility, 6-hour transit study

Colon motility study performed over a 6-hour period to evaluate colonic muscle function and peristalsis.

In Plain Language

test of large bowel muscle strength; measurement of colon muscle movement

Clinical Context

Diagnostic study to assess colonic muscle contractions and peristaltic activity. Evaluates constipation, irritable bowel syndrome, and colonic inertia over extended period.
