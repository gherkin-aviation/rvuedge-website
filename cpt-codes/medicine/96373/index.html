96373 therapy/proph/diagnostic injection ia

Medicine

Also known as: joint injection, intra-articular injection, joint steroid

Therapeutic, prophylactic, or diagnostic injection administered intra-articularly (into a joint).

Clinical Context

Administration of medications directly into joint space for arthritis treatment, diagnostics, or therapeutic pain management.
