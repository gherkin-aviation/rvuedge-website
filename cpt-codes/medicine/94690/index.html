94690 O2 uptk rest indirect

Medicine

Also known as: resting metabolic rate test, indirect calorimetry, resting VO2

Indirect calorimetry for measurement of oxygen uptake at rest to assess resting metabolic rate without exercise.

In Plain Language

resting calorie burning measurement; at-rest breathing efficiency test

Clinical Context

Used to determine resting energy expenditure for nutritional assessment and planning in critically ill and metabolically abnormal patients.
