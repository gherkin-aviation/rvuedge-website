92002 Intrm ophthalmic examination new patient

Medicine

Also known as: intermediate eye exam, limited eye exam new patient, office visit eye exam

Intermediate comprehensive eye examination for a new patient, including assessment of visual function and ocular health.

Clinical Context

Intermediate complexity eye examination for new patients including refraction, visual acuity, eye pressure, and anterior segment assessment.
