90837 Psytx w pt 60 minutes

Medicine

Also known as: Psychotherapy 60 minutes, Individual therapy 60 min, Therapy session hour

Psychotherapy with patient for 60 minutes of direct face-to-face therapeutic interaction without medical evaluation and management services.

Clinical Context

Provided by psychiatrists or other qualified mental health professionals for intensive psychotherapeutic treatment allowing extended therapeutic engagement.
