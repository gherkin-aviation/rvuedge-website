98005 Synch audio-video established low 20

Medicine

Also known as: telehealth follow-up, virtual office visit, video follow-up

Synchronous (real-time) audio-video interactive telehealth service between physician/qualified healthcare provider and established patient for low complexity care, up to 20 minutes.

Clinical Context

Used for real-time video consultations with established patients for routine follow-ups and low-complexity clinical issues.
