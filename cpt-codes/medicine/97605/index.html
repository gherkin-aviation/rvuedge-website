97605 Neg prs wnd therapy dme<=50sqcm

Medicine

Also known as: Negative pressure therapy, Wound vac therapy, Vacuum-assisted closure

Negative pressure wound therapy including device dressing and monitoring for wounds up to 50 square centimeters using durable medical equipment.

Clinical Context

Used for chronic wounds, diabetic ulcers, and pressure ulcers. Promotes healing through negative pressure.
