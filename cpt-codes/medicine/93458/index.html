93458 L hrt artery/ventricle angio

Medicine Global 0d

Also known as: Left coronary angiography, LCA angio, left selective angio, left heart catheterization, left heart cath

Left heart catheterization with selective angiography of left coronary artery and left ventricular angiography for assessment of left system anatomy and function.

In Plain Language

left heart artery and pump pictures; left side artery imaging; left coronary vessel study

Clinical Context

Evaluates left coronary system anatomy and disease burden, assesses left ventricular function, and detects wall motion abnormalities. Often performed when left dominant system or left main disease is suspected.
