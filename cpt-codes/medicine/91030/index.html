91030 Acid perfusion of esophagus

Medicine Global 0d

Also known as: Bernstein test, acid perfusion test, acid challenge test

Acid perfusion test of the esophagus (Bernstein test) to evaluate whether acid in the esophagus reproduces symptoms like chest pain.

Clinical Context

Diagnostic study used to correlate esophageal acid exposure with symptoms in patients with suspected gastroesophageal reflux disease. Helps confirm acid-related symptoms.
