96001 Motion test with ft press meas

Medicine

Also known as: gait analysis with force plate, pressure analysis, dynamic motion analysis

Motion analysis with dynamic force plate measurement for assessment of weight distribution and pressure dynamics.

Clinical Context

Motion analysis incorporating force plate technology to measure ground reaction forces, weight distribution, and pressure dynamics during movement; used for gait abnormality assessment.
