98961 Edu & trn pt slf-mgmt nqhp 2-4

Medicine

Also known as: patient education series, self-management training, disease education

Education and training for patient self-management provided by a non-physician healthcare provider for patients with one or more chronic conditions, two to four sessions.

Clinical Context

Used for a series of two to four patient education sessions on disease management by non-physician providers.
