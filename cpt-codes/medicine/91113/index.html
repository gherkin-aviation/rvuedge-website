91113 Gi trc img intral colon i & r

Medicine

Also known as: colon capsule, colonic capsule, colon imaging capsule

Gastrointestinal tract imaging using intraluminal capsule technology of the colon with image interpretation and recording.

Clinical Context

Wireless capsule visualization of the colon for surveillance of polyps, colorectal cancer screening in poor surgical candidates, and evaluation of incomplete colonoscopies.
