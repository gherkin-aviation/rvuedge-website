99082 Unusual physician travel

Medicine

Also known as: unusual travel, physician travel, distant visit

Unusual or extraordinary physician travel to see a patient at a location significantly distant from the usual practice location.

In Plain Language

doctor traveling long distance to visit you

Clinical Context

Used when a physician travels significantly beyond the usual practice area to provide care, such as house calls to distant locations.

RVU Information

CPT 99082 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
