92512 Nasal function studies

Medicine

Also known as: nasal function test, rhinomanometry, nasal endoscopy

Nasal function studies; objective assessment of nasal patency, airflow, and nasal physiology.

Clinical Context

Used for evaluation of nasal obstruction, deviated septum, or to objectively quantify nasal airway resistance before and after treatment.
