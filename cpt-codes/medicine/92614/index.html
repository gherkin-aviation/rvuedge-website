92614 Laryngoscopic sensory vid

Medicine

Also known as: laryngeal sensory test, laryngeal sensation assessment

Laryngoscopic sensory testing with video documentation; assessment of laryngeal sensation using endoscopy.

Clinical Context

Used for assessment of laryngeal protective sensation in patients at risk for aspiration.
