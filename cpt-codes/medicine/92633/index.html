92633 Aud rehabilitation postling hear loss

Medicine

Also known as: auditory rehab post-lingual

Auditory rehabilitation for post-lingual hearing loss; rehabilitation services for hearing loss acquired after language development.

In Plain Language

hearing rehabilitation for late-onset deafness

Clinical Context

Used for rehabilitation and counseling services for patients who lost hearing after developing speech and language.
