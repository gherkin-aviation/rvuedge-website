92651 Aep hearing status deter i & r

Medicine

Also known as: AEP hearing test, auditory evoked potential

Auditory evoked potential (AEP) assessment to determine current hearing status including interpretation and reporting of results.

In Plain Language

hearing nerve test; electrical hearing assessment

Clinical Context

Used to objectively measure hearing function in patients who cannot provide reliable behavioral responses, including infants, young children, and individuals with developmental delays or communication disorders.
