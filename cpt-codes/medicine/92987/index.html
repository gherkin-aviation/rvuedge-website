92987 Revision of mitral valve

Medicine Global 90d

Also known as: TMVR mitral valve, percutaneous mitral valve replacement, transcatheter mitral repair

Percutaneous revision or replacement of mitral valve using catheter-based approach with or without transthoracic guidance.

In Plain Language

catheter mitral valve repair or replacement; heart valve replacement through catheter

Clinical Context

Minimally invasive percutaneous approach to treat mitral regurgitation or stenosis in high-risk surgical candidates using edge-to-edge repair or valve replacement.
