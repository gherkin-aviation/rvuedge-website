93888 Intracranial limited study

Medicine

Also known as: limited TCD, focused transcranial Doppler, limited intracranial study

Limited transcranial Doppler ultrasound study of intracranial arteries performed on selected vessels or specific segments.

In Plain Language

focused brain blood flow scan; partial brain vessel ultrasound

Clinical Context

Used for targeted evaluation of specific intracranial vessels when hemodynamic concerns are localized, or for serial monitoring of known vasospasm.
