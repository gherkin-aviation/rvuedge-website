90619 Menacwy-tt vaccine intramuscular

Medicine

Also known as: MenACWY-TT vaccine, menactra tetanus, meningococcal conjugate tetanus

Meningococcal conjugate vaccine tetravalent with tetanus toxoid administered intramuscularly.

In Plain Language

meningitis vaccine; bacterial meningitis protection

Clinical Context

Conjugate meningococcal vaccine against serogroups A, C, W, and Y with enhanced immunogenicity through tetanus toxoid carrier protein.
