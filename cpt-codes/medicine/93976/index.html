93976 Vascular study

Medicine

Also known as: complete visceral duplex, bilateral visceral study, full abdominal vascular scan

Duplex ultrasound scan of visceral blood vessels (aorta, mesenteric, celiac, and renal arteries) performed as a complete bilateral evaluation.

In Plain Language

full abdominal vessel ultrasound; bilateral artery assessment

Clinical Context

Used for comprehensive assessment of visceral arteries to evaluate for bilateral renal stenosis, aortic disease, or mesenteric insufficiency.
