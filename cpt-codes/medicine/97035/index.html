97035 application mdlty 1+ultrasound each 15

Medicine

Also known as: Ultrasound therapy, Therapeutic ultrasound, Ultrasound modality

Application of therapeutic modality including ultrasound therapy for each 15-minute period. Uses sound waves for pain relief and tissue healing.

Clinical Context

Used for soft tissue injuries, inflammation, and pain management. Ultrasound waves promote tissue healing and reduce inflammation.
