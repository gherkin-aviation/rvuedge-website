99002 Device handling phys/qhp

Medicine

Also known as: device handling, courier service, equipment transport

Device handling and courier service provided by a physician or qualified healthcare provider for collection, delivery, and transport of clinical specimens or medical equipment.

Clinical Context

Used when a physician or qualified healthcare provider directly handles and transports medical devices or specimens.
