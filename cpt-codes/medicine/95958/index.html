95958 Eeg monitoring/function test

Medicine

Also known as: EEG with activation, routine EEG with hyperventilation, photic EEG

Electroencephalography monitoring and functional testing (such as photic stimulation or hyperventilation) with interpretation.

Clinical Context

Standard diagnostic electroencephalography with activation procedures to detect epileptiform abnormalities, sleep abnormalities, and cerebral dysfunctions.
