94453 Hast with suppl oxygen titrj

Medicine

Also known as: HAST with oxygen titration, altitude test with oxygen, hypoxia testing with O2

Hypoxia altitude simulation test with supplemental oxygen titration to determine required oxygen supplementation for altitude exposure.

Clinical Context

Used to determine appropriate oxygen prescription for patients with underlying lung disease who plan air travel or high-altitude activities.
