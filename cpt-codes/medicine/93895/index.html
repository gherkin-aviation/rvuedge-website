93895 Carotid intima atheroma evaluation

Medicine

Also known as: carotid IMT, intima-media thickness measurement, atheroma assessment

Evaluation of carotid intima-media thickness and atheroma morphology to assess cardiovascular disease risk and atherosclerotic burden.

In Plain Language

carotid artery thickness measurement; atherosclerosis assessment ultrasound

Clinical Context

Used as a screening and prognostic tool in patients with cardiovascular risk factors to quantify subclinical atherosclerosis and stratify stroke risk.

RVU Information
