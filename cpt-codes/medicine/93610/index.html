93610 Intra-atrial pacing

Medicine +Add-on Global 0d

Also known as: Intra-atrial pacing, atrial pacing, atrial pacing study

Electrophysiology study with intra-atrial pacing to assess atrial conduction, atrioventricular properties, and inducibility.

In Plain Language

upper heart chamber electrical stimulation; atrial pacing test; arrhythmia provocation

Clinical Context

Evaluates atrial conduction and atrioventricular relationship through programmed pacing, assesses arrhythmia inducibility, and determines refractory periods.
