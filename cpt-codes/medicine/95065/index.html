95065 Dir nsl mucous membrane test

Medicine

Also known as: nasal allergy test, nasal mucous membrane test, intranasal allergy testing

Direct nasal mucous membrane testing for allergens using application to nasal mucosa to evaluate nasal allergy.

In Plain Language

nose allergy test; nasal allergen test

Clinical Context

Used to diagnose nasal allergies and allergen sensitivities in patients with allergic rhinitis.
