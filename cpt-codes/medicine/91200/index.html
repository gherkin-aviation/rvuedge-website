91200 Liver elastography

Medicine

Also known as: liver stiffness, transient elastography, liver fibrosis assessment

Liver elastography using ultrasound or magnetic resonance to assess liver fibrosis and elasticity non-invasively.

In Plain Language

liver stiffness test; non-invasive liver scarring test

Clinical Context

Non-invasive imaging technique to assess degree of liver fibrosis in patients with chronic liver disease, hepatitis, or cirrhosis. Predicts portal hypertension risk.
