94762 N-invas ear/pls oximtry cont

Medicine

Also known as: continuous pulse oximetry, continuous SpO2 monitoring, continuous oximetry

Noninvasive continuous ear lobe or pulse oximetry monitoring throughout a procedure or extended time period.

In Plain Language

continuous oxygen monitoring; constant oxygen level tracking

Clinical Context

Used for continuous real-time oxygen saturation monitoring during procedures, sleep studies, and critical care management.
