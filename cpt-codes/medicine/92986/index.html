92986 Revision of aortic valve

Medicine Global 90d

Also known as: TAVR aortic valve revision, transcatheter aortic valve, percutaneous aortic valve replacement

Percutaneous revision or replacement of aortic valve using catheter-based approach with or without transthoracic guidance.

Clinical Context

Minimally invasive alternative to surgical aortic valve replacement for patients with aortic stenosis who are at high surgical risk or are not surgical candidates.
