Medicine

Also known as: extremity manipulation, peripheral joint adjustment, non-spinal chiropractic

Chiropractic manipulative treatment of extraspatial/non-spinal regions such as extremities or other non-vertebral sites during a single treatment session.

Clinical Context

Used for chiropractic manipulative therapy of peripheral joints and extremities, not including spinal regions.
