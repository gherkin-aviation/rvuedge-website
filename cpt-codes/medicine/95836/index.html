95836 Ecog impltd brn npgt <30 d

Medicine

Also known as: implanted electrode monitoring, intracranial EEG, iEEG

Electrocorticogram, from implanted brain electrodes; postoperative monitoring less than 30 days.

Clinical Context

Monitoring of epileptic activity via surgically implanted electrodes during postoperative period. Guides seizure focus localization.
