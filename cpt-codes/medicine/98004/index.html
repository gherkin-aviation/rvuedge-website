98004 Synch audio-video established sf 10

Medicine

Also known as: telehealth follow-up, virtual check-in, video follow-up

Synchronous (real-time) audio-video interactive telehealth service between physician/qualified healthcare provider and established patient for straightforward complexity care, up to 10 minutes.

Clinical Context

Used for brief real-time video consultations with established patients for medication refills, symptom checks, or quick follow-up on known conditions.
