92341 Fit spectacles bifocal

Medicine

Also known as: bifocal glasses, bifocal spectacles, bifocal fitting

Fitting of bifocal spectacle lenses with two distinct focal powers for distance and near vision.

Clinical Context

Prescription fitting for bifocal lenses to correct presbyopia and distance refractive error simultaneously.
