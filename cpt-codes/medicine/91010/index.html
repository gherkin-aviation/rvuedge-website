91010 Esophagus motility study

Medicine Global 0d

Also known as: esophageal manometry, motility study, esophagus function test

Esophageal motility study to evaluate the muscle function and peristaltic activity of the esophagus.

In Plain Language

test of swallowing muscle strength; measurement of esophagus muscle movement

Clinical Context

Diagnostic study used to evaluate dysphagia, chest pain, or suspected motility disorders. Assesses pressure gradients and muscle contractions along the esophagus.
