93990 Doppler flow testing

Medicine

Also known as: Doppler flow study, spectral Doppler, flow analysis

Doppler ultrasound velocity and flow pattern analysis of blood vessels to assess vascular hemodynamics and detect flow abnormalities.

In Plain Language

blood flow speed measurement; vessel flow assessment

Clinical Context

Used as an adjunctive assessment for vascular pathology including stenosis, thrombosis, steal syndrome, and assessment of venous reflux.
