93612 Intraventricular pacing

Medicine +Add-on Global 0d

Also known as: Intraventricular pacing, ventricular pacing, ventricular pacing study

Electrophysiology study with intraventricular pacing to assess ventricular conduction and inducibility.

Clinical Context

Evaluates ventricular conduction properties through programmed pacing, assesses ventricular arrhythmia inducibility, and determines ventricular refractory periods.
