96360 Hydration intravenous infusion initial

Medicine

Also known as: IV hydration, fluid replacement, intravenous fluids

Intravenous hydration infusion therapy with initial establishment of access and administration of fluid therapy.

Clinical Context

Initial intravenous hydration therapy for dehydration management, often used in patients with vomiting, diarrhea, or inability to maintain oral intake.
