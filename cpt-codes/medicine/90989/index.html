90989 Dialysis training complete

Medicine

Also known as: dialysis training complete, ESRD patient training, renal training completion

Dialysis training provided to end-stage renal disease patients for self-management, when training is completed within the specified period.

In Plain Language

training on how to do dialysis at home; kidney disease self-care instruction

Clinical Context

Educational services for dialysis patients to learn self-dialysis techniques, vascular access care, diet management, and medication compliance when training is successfully completed.
