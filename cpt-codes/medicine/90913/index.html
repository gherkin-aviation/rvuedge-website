90913 Bfb training each additional 15 minutes

Medicine +Add-on

Also known as: Biofeedback additional 15 minutes, Biofeedback continuation, Extended biofeedback training

Biofeedback training for each additional 15 minutes beyond the initial session for ongoing feedback-based behavioral treatment.

Clinical Context

Continuation of biofeedback training in 15-minute increments beyond initial session for reinforcement and advancement of self-regulation skills.
