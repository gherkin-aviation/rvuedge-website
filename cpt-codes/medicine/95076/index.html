95076 Ingest challenge ini 120 minutes

Medicine

Also known as: food challenge test, oral food challenge, oral provocation test

Oral ingestion challenge test for food allergens performed over initial 120 minutes to diagnose food allergy.

Clinical Context

Used to confirm food allergy diagnosis and assess tolerance in patients with suspected food allergy when history and testing are inconclusive.
