96900 Actinotherapy uv light

Medicine

Also known as: UV therapy, Light therapy, Actinotherapy

Ultraviolet light therapy (actinotherapy) treatment of skin conditions. Includes exposure to ultraviolet A and/or B wavelengths for therapeutic benefit.

Clinical Context

Used for psoriasis, vitiligo, atopic dermatitis, and other photosensitive skin conditions. Standard treatment for various dermatologic inflammatory conditions.
