93568 Njx car cth nslc p-art angrp

Medicine +Add-on

Also known as: Non-selective PA angiography, pulmonary angiography, PA angiography

Injection during congenital heart catheterization with non-selective pulmonary artery angiography to visualize pulmonary vascular anatomy and branch distribution.

Clinical Context

Evaluates pulmonary artery anatomy, demonstrates branch pulmonary artery stenosis, assesses pulmonary blood flow distribution, and characterizes abnormal pulmonary connections in congenital disease.
