97022 Whirlpool therapy

Medicine

Also known as: Whirlpool, Hubbard tank, Aquatic whirlpool

Whirlpool therapy providing therapeutic heat and water agitation for pain relief and joint mobility. Used for extremity treatment and wound care.

Clinical Context

Used for burn care, post-surgical wounds, and pain management. Water jet agitation promotes circulation and facilitates movement.
