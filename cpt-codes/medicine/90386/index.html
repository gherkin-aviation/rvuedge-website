90386 Rh ig intravenous

Medicine

Also known as: IV RhIG, Rh IG IV, intravenous RhoGAM

Rho immunoglobulin administered by intravenous route for prevention of Rh sensitization in Rh negative patients.

Clinical Context

Intravenous route for Rh immunoglobulin administration when intramuscular route is contraindicated or ineffective in preventing Rh sensitization.
