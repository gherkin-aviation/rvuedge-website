93319 3d echo img cgen car anomal

Medicine

Also known as: 3D echo, 3D cardiac imaging, volumetric echo

Three-dimensional echocardiographic imaging with congenital cardiac anomaly evaluation, providing volumetric reconstruction of abnormal cardiac structures and their spatial relationships.

Clinical Context

Specifically designed for congenital heart disease assessment, allowing improved visualization of complex anatomical relationships, septal defects, and abnormal connections. Essential for surgical planning in complex cases.
