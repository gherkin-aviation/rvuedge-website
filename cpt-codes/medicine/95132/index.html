95132 Immntx 3 sting insects

Medicine

Also known as: venom immunotherapy, multi-venom therapy, combined sting therapy

Allergen immunotherapy for insect sting venom; three venom doses.

Clinical Context

Treats sensitivity to three different Hymenoptera venoms. Used for patients with documented reactions to multiple insect species.
