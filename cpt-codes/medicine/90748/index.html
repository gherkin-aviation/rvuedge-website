90748 Hib-hepb vaccine intramuscular

Medicine

Also known as: Hib-HepB vaccine, Comvax, Combination Hib-HepB

Haemophilus influenzae type b conjugate vaccine and hepatitis B vaccine (Hib-HepB) combination vaccine administered intramuscularly.

In Plain Language

Combination vaccine; Hib and hepatitis B vaccine

Clinical Context

Administered to infants for protection against Haemophilus influenzae type b meningitis and hepatitis B infection in a single vaccine.
