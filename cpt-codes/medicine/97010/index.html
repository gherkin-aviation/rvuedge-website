97010 Hot or cold packs therapy

Medicine

Also known as: Heat therapy, Ice pack therapy, Hot/cold packs

Application of therapeutic heat (hot packs) or cold (cold packs) therapy for pain management and inflammation reduction. Each 15-minute application.

Clinical Context

Basic thermal therapy for acute and chronic pain, muscle spasms, and joint stiffness. Used in physical therapy and rehabilitation settings.
