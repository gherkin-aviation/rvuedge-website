93574 Njx catheter slct pulmonary vn angrph

Medicine +Add-on

Also known as: Selective pulmonary vein angio, pulmonary vein imaging, PV angiography

Injection during congenital heart catheterization with selective pulmonary vein angiography to visualize pulmonary venous anatomy and connections.

Clinical Context

Essential for evaluating anomalous pulmonary venous return, assessing pulmonary vein anatomy in transposition or complex disease, and demonstrating pulmonary vein course before intervention.
