93595 L hrt catheter chd nm/abnormal nt cnj

Medicine Global 0d

Also known as: LHC CHD, left heart cath congenital, LHC measurements only

Left heart catheterization in congenital heart disease with normal or abnormal anatomy, non-injected hemodynamic measurement.

Clinical Context

Hemodynamic assessment of left heart without angiography in congenital disease. Determines left ventricular pressures, estimates diastolic function, and evaluates mitral valve disease.
