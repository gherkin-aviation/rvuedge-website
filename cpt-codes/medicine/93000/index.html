93000 Electrocardiogram complete

Medicine

Also known as: 12-lead ECG, EKG, electrocardiogram, 12-lead ECG with interpretation, EKG complete

Complete electrocardiogram (ECG) with at least 12 leads including interpretation and report.

Clinical Context

Fundamental diagnostic test for evaluating cardiac rhythm, conduction abnormalities, ischemia, and structural changes in patients with chest pain, arrhythmia, or routine screening.
