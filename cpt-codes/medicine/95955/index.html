95955 Eeg during surgery

Medicine

Also known as: intraop EEG, operating room EEG, surgical EEG

Electroencephalography monitoring performed during operative procedures (including anesthesia) with interpretation.

Clinical Context

Continuous brain activity monitoring during neurosurgical procedures, particularly for epilepsy surgery, tumor resection, or carotid endarterectomy to guide surgical decisions.
