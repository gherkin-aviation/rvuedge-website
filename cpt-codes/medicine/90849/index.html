90849 Multiple family group psytx

Medicine

Also known as: Multiple family group therapy, Multi-family group therapy

Multiple family group psychotherapy, therapeutic session involving several families together to facilitate shared learning and support.

Clinical Context

Group therapy format where multiple families participate together for mutual support, shared problem-solving, and learning about psychiatric disorders affecting multiple families.
