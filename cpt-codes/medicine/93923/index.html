93923 upper/lxtr artery stdy 3+ lvls

Medicine

Also known as: multilevel extremity duplex, 3+ level arterial scan, comprehensive limb study

Duplex ultrasound scan of upper or lower extremity arteries including 3 or more arterial levels for comprehensive evaluation.

Clinical Context

Used for complete arterial mapping of extremities to define extent of peripheral arterial disease, determine revascularization candidacy, or assess graft patency.
