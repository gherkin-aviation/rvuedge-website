90713 Poliovirus ipv sternoclavicular/intramuscular

Medicine

Also known as: Polio vaccine, IPV, Polio shot

Inactivated poliovirus vaccine (IPV) containing all three serotypes (1, 2, and 3) administered subcutaneously or intramuscularly for prevention of poliomyelitis.

Clinical Context

Administered to infants and children as part of the primary immunization series for prevention of poliomyelitis caused by all three poliovirus serotypes.
