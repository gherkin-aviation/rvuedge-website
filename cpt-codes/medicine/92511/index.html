92511 Nasopharyngoscopy

Medicine Global 0d

Also known as: nasopharyngoscopy, flexible laryngoscopy, endoscopy

Nasopharyngoscopy; flexible fiberoptic visualization of the nasopharynx, pharynx, and larynx.

Clinical Context

Used for evaluation of chronic sinusitis, nasopharyngeal masses, post-nasal drip, vocal cord assessment, and swallowing disorders.
