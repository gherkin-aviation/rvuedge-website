93615 Esophageal recording

Medicine Global 0d

Also known as: Esophageal recording, esophageal electrogram, esophageal EP

Electrophysiology study using esophageal electrode recording to assess cardiac electrical activity and conduction from esophageal approach.

In Plain Language

throat electrical heart recording; esophageal electrical measurement; non-invasive heart rhythm test

Clinical Context

Non-invasive technique recording atrial electrogram through esophageal electrode, useful for assessing arrhythmias and atrial activity without intracardiac catheterization.
