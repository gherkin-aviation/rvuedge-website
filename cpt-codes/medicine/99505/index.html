99505 Home visit stoma care

Medicine

Also known as: ostomy care visit, stoma management

Home visit service for management of ostomy care (colostomy, ileostomy, urostomy), including site assessment, skin care, and equipment management.

Clinical Context

Used for home-based ostomy care and patient education in patients with surgical diversions requiring ongoing specialized care.
