94016 Review patient spirometry

Medicine

Also known as: spirometry review, remote PFT interpretation, home spirometry review

Physician or qualified healthcare provider review and interpretation of patient-recorded spirometry data transmitted from home or remote location.

Clinical Context

Used for clinical assessment of patient-generated spirometry data in remote monitoring programs for chronic respiratory disease management.
