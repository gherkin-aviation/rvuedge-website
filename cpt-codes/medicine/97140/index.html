97140 Manual therapy 1/> regions

Medicine

Also known as: Manual therapy, Joint mobilization, Soft tissue mobilization

Manual therapy techniques including massage, mobilization, and manipulation of one or more body regions. Performed to improve mobility and reduce pain.

Clinical Context

Evidence-based manual techniques for musculoskeletal dysfunction. Used to improve joint mobility and tissue extensibility.
