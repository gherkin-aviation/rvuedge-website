99155 Mod sed oth phys/qhp <5 yrs

Medicine

Also known as: conscious sedation separate, procedural sedation separate, anesthesia provider

Moderate sedation or anesthesia administered by a different physician/qualified healthcare provider (not the procedure provider) on a patient for less than five years or first encounter.

In Plain Language

sedation given by a separate anesthesia doctor

Clinical Context

Used when a dedicated anesthesia provider administers moderate sedation while another physician performs the procedure, for patients under five years.
