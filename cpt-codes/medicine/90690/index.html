90690 Typhoid vaccine oral

Medicine

Also known as: Typhoid vaccine oral, Vivotif, Oral typhoid vaccine

Typhoid vaccine, oral live attenuated vaccine for prevention of typhoid fever infection.

In Plain Language

Typhoid shot; Typhoid protection

Clinical Context

Administered orally to travelers to endemic areas and individuals at high risk of typhoid exposure to prevent infection with Salmonella typhi.
