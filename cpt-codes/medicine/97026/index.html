97026 Infrared therapy

Medicine

Also known as: Infrared therapy, Infrared light, Heat lamp therapy

Infrared light therapy for pain relief and circulation improvement. Uses infrared wavelengths to deliver heat to tissue.

Clinical Context

Used for localized pain, muscle tension, and circulation enhancement. Infrared light penetrates skin and increases local blood flow.
