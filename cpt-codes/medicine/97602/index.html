97602 Wound(s) care non-selective

Medicine

Also known as: Non-selective wound care, Wet dressing therapy, Enzymatic debridement

Wound treatment and management using non-selective debridement techniques without use of sharp instruments. Includes dressing application.

Clinical Context

Conservative wound care approach for chronic wounds and pressure ulcers. Allows autolytic and enzymatic debridement.
