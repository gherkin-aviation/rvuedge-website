94621 Cardiopulm exercise testing

Medicine

Also known as: cardiopulmonary exercise test, CPET, VO2 max testing

Cardiopulmonary exercise testing with comprehensive assessment of cardiovascular and pulmonary response to maximal exercise including electrocardiogram and gas exchange.

In Plain Language

complete exercise test; heart and lung exercise test

Clinical Context

Used to evaluate unexplained dyspnea, assess cardiac functional capacity, diagnose exercise limitations, and determine prognosis in cardiopulmonary disease.
