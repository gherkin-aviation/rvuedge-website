95875 Limb exercise test

Medicine

Also known as: ischemic exercise test, forearm ischemic test

Ischemic limb exercise test with serial specimen collection for lactic acid and ammonia measurement.

Clinical Context

Exercise test under ischemic conditions to assess metabolic abnormalities in myopathy or mitochondrial disorders. Detects abnormal lactate or ammonia response.
