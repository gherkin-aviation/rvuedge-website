90632 Hepa vaccine adult intramuscular

Medicine

Also known as: Hep A vaccine adult, hepatitis A shot

Hepatitis A vaccine administered intramuscularly to adults for immunization against hepatitis A virus.

Clinical Context

Inactivated hepatitis A vaccine for adults susceptible to hepatitis A virus infection, including travelers and healthcare workers.
