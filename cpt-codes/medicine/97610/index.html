97610 Low frequency non-thermal ultrasound

Medicine

Also known as: Therapeutic ultrasound, Low-frequency ultrasound, Ultrasound therapy

Low-frequency non-thermal ultrasound therapy for wound healing and pain management without thermal heating effect.

Clinical Context

Used for chronic wounds and soft tissue healing. Non-thermal ultrasound promotes cellular activity without heat generation.
