98013 Synch audio-only established low 20

Medicine

Also known as: phone follow-up, telephone visit, audio telehealth

Synchronous (real-time) audio-only interactive telehealth service between physician/qualified healthcare provider and established patient for low complexity care, up to 20 minutes.

Clinical Context

Used for real-time telephone consultations with established patients for routine follow-ups.
