90744 Hepb vacc 3 dose pediatric/adol intramuscular

Medicine

Also known as: Hepatitis B vaccine pediatric, HepB vaccine child, Engerix-B pediatric

Hepatitis B vaccine, recombinant, 3-dose series for pediatric and adolescent patients administered intramuscularly.

Clinical Context

Administered to infants, children, and adolescents for prevention of hepatitis B infection in the routine immunization schedule.
