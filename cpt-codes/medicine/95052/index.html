95052 Photo patch tests

Medicine

Also known as: photopatch test, photo contact dermatitis test, photosensitivity testing

Photo patch testing to evaluate photosensitizing allergens by combining patch testing with ultraviolet light exposure.

In Plain Language

light and patch allergy test; photosensitivity allergy test

Clinical Context

Used to diagnose photoallergic contact dermatitis from substances that require ultraviolet light exposure to cause allergic reaction.
