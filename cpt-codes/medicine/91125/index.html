91125 Anrct mano rct snsatn & balo

Medicine +Add-on

Also known as: anorectal manometry complete, defecography study, complete anorectal testing

Anorectal manometry with rectal sensation testing and balloon expulsion to evaluate complete anorectal function.

Clinical Context

Comprehensive assessment combining pressure measurements, sensory testing, and balloon expulsion. Gold standard for evaluating fecal incontinence and defecatory dysfunction.
