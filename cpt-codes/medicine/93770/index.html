93770 Determination venous press

Medicine +Add-on

Also known as: Venous pressure measurement, central venous pressure, CVP measurement

Venous pressure determination through catheterization and measurement of central venous pressure.

Clinical Context

Establishes baseline central venous pressure for hemodynamic assessment, guides fluid management, and monitors response to therapy in acute and chronic conditions.
