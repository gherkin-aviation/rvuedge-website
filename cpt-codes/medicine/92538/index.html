92538 Caloric vestibular test with rec

Medicine

Also known as: partial caloric test

Caloric vestibular test with partial bithermal and interpretation; limited thermal stimulation assessment.

Clinical Context

Used for abbreviated caloric testing when complete bithermal testing is not necessary or feasible.
