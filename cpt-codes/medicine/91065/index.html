91065 Breath hydrogen/methane test

Medicine Global 0d

Also known as: hydrogen breath test, lactose tolerance test, SIBO test

Breath hydrogen and methane test to evaluate intestinal carbohydrate malabsorption and bacterial overgrowth.

Clinical Context

Non-invasive test measuring hydrogen and methane in breath to diagnose lactose intolerance, fructose malabsorption, and small intestinal bacterial overgrowth syndrome.
