95056 Photo tests

Medicine

Also known as: phototesting, photo provocation test, UV sensitivity test

Photo testing to assess photosensitivity and phototoxic reactions using controlled ultraviolet light exposure.

In Plain Language

light sensitivity test; UV reaction test

Clinical Context

Used to diagnose phototoxic and photoallergic dermatitis and assess sensitivity to ultraviolet radiation.
