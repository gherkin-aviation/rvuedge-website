97802 Medical nutrition indiv in

Medicine

Also known as: nutrition therapy, dietitian consult, MNT initial

Initial individual medical nutrition therapy consultation and intervention provided by a registered dietitian to assess nutritional status and develop a nutrition care plan.

Clinical Context

Used for the first nutrition therapy appointment for patients with diseases requiring dietary modification, such as diabetes, heart disease, kidney disease, or cancer.
