92605 Ex for nonspeech device prescription

Medicine

Also known as: AAC evaluation, augmentative device assessment

Evaluation for augmentative and alternative communication device; assessment of communication needs for nonspeech device.

Clinical Context

Used for comprehensive evaluation of patients with severe speech disorders to determine appropriateness for augmentative communication devices.
