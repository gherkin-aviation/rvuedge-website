98003 Synch audio-video new hi 60

Medicine

Also known as: telehealth visit, virtual office visit, video consultation

Synchronous (real-time) audio-video interactive telehealth service between physician/qualified healthcare provider and patient for high complexity care, up to 60 minutes.

Clinical Context

Used for real-time video consultations for new patients with high-complexity conditions requiring comprehensive evaluation, detailed history, and complex decision-making.
