92979 Endoluminl ivus oct c each

Medicine +Add-on

Also known as: IVUS OCT each vessel, additional imaging, coronary imaging per artery

Endoluminal intravascular ultrasound (IVUS) and optical coherence tomography (OCT) imaging of each additional coronary artery during intervention.

Clinical Context

Additional intracoronary imaging of each subsequent vessel beyond the first, providing comprehensive anatomic assessment for multivessel intervention planning.
