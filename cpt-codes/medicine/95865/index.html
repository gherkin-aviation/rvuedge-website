95865 Needle emg larynx

Medicine

Also known as: laryngeal EMG, vocal cord EMG

Needle electromyography; larynx.

Clinical Context

EMG of laryngeal muscles. Evaluates vocal cord paralysis, myasthenia gravis affecting larynx, or laryngeal dystonia.
