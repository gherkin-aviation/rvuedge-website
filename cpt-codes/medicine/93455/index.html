93455 Coronary artery/graft angio supervision and interpretation

Medicine Global 0d

Also known as: Coronary and graft angiography, graft angio, post-bypass angio

Coronary artery and coronary artery bypass graft angiography with catheter manipulation and selective imaging of native coronaries and grafts.

In Plain Language

heart artery and bypass tube pictures; artery and graft imaging; bypass vessel evaluation

Clinical Context

Essential for post-coronary artery bypass grafting evaluation, assessing graft patency, bypass quality, and progression of native disease. Also used in patients with prior bypass presenting with symptoms.
