Medicine

Also known as: patient education, self-management training, disease education

Education and training for patient self-management provided by a non-physician healthcare provider for patients with one or more chronic conditions, per session.

In Plain Language

learning how to manage your condition; patient education class

Clinical Context

Used for non-physician provider patient education on disease management, medication use, lifestyle modifications, or self-care techniques for chronic conditions.
