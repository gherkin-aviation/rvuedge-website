97009 Mchnl sclp cool after chemotherapy

Medicine

Also known as: Post-chemo cooling, Post-treatment scalp cooling, Recovery cooling

Mechanical scalp cooling after chemotherapy administration. Includes continuation of cooling therapy post-treatment.

Clinical Context

Continuation of scalp cooling following chemotherapy completion. Timing and duration important for alopecia prevention efficacy.
