93453 right and left hrt catheter with ventriclgrphy

Medicine Global 0d

Also known as: Bilateral heart cath, R and L heart cath, complete heart cath

Combined right and left heart catheterization with ventriculography, providing comprehensive hemodynamic assessment of both right and left ventricular pressures and function.

Clinical Context

Comprehensive hemodynamic evaluation assessing both right and left ventricular function, pulmonary and systemic hemodynamics, and valvular disease. Used in complex cases requiring complete functional assessment before intervention.
