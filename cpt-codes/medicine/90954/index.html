90954 Esrd serv 4 vsts p mo 2-11

Medicine

Also known as: ESRD services pediatric, Dialysis management child, Renal disease management

End-stage renal disease services, monthly evaluation and management of dialysis patient with 4 or more visits per month for patients ages 2-11 years.

Clinical Context

Comprehensive monthly nephrology services for end-stage renal disease dialysis patients ages 2-11 years including intensive evaluation and medical management.
