96574 Debridement prmlg lesion with pdt

Medicine Global 0d

Also known as: PDT with debridement, Debriding lesion plus PDT, PDT with prep

Debridement of non-malignant distending lesions on skin performed concurrently with photodynamic therapy by qualified healthcare provider.

In Plain Language

Skin removal with light therapy for lesions

Clinical Context

Combined procedure for non-malignant skin lesions requiring tissue removal plus photodynamic therapy. Used when lesions have thick or keratotic components.
