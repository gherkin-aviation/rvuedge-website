97124 Massage therapy

Medicine

Also known as: Therapeutic massage, Clinical massage, Massage therapy

Massage therapy performed therapeutically for pain relief, improved circulation, and muscle relaxation.

Clinical Context

Used for muscle tension, pain management, and circulation improvement. Can be adjunct to other rehabilitation therapies.
