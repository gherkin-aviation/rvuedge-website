93459 L hrt artery/graft angio

Medicine Global 0d

Also known as: Left coronary and graft angiography, left selective with grafts, LCA and graft study, left heart cath with left ventriculography

Left heart catheterization with selective angiography of left coronary artery and coronary artery bypass grafts to left system.

Clinical Context

Post-bypass evaluation assessing patency of left-sided grafts (left anterior descending, circumflex, ramus) and progression of native left coronary disease.
