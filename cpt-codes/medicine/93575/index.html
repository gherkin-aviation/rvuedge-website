93575 Njx catheter slct p angrph mapca

Medicine +Add-on

Also known as: Selective MAPCAs angiography, collateral artery imaging, aortopulmonary collateral study

Injection during congenital heart catheterization with selective pulmonary artery angiography to visualize major aortopulmonary collateral arteries in complex cyanotic heart disease.

In Plain Language

extra heart artery pictures; backup blood vessel imaging; collateral circulation visualization

Clinical Context

Essential imaging in complex cyanotic heart disease (tetralogy of Fallot, pulmonary atresia, hypoplastic left heart) to delineate major aortopulmonary collateral arteries that supply lungs and guide surgical/interventional planning.
