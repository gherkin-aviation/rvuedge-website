95803 Actigraphy testing

Medicine

Also known as: actigraphy, wearable sleep monitor, activity monitor

Actigraphy testing; includes single or multiple 24-hour periods, with sleep analysis.

Clinical Context

Wearable device measuring sleep-wake patterns over extended periods. Evaluates insomnia, circadian rhythm disorders, and total sleep time.
