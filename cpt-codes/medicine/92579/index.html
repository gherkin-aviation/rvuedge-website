92579 Visual audiometry (vra)

Medicine

Also known as: VRA, visual reinforcement audiometry

Visual reinforcement audiometry; hearing assessment in children using visual rewards for behavioral response.

Clinical Context

Used for hearing assessment in young children ages 6 months to 3 years using behavioral conditioning.
