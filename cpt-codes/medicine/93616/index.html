93616 Esophageal recording with pacg

Medicine Global 0d

Also known as: Esophageal pacing, esophageal pacing study, transesophageal pacing

Esophageal recording with esophageal pacing to assess cardiac response and provoke arrhythmias.

In Plain Language

throat electrical heart stimulation test; esophageal pacing study; non-invasive arrhythmia induction

Clinical Context

Non-invasive arrhythmia induction through esophageal pacing combined with recording to assess arrhythmia mechanism and risk without intracardiac catheterization.
