93655 Intracardiac catheter ablation dscrt arrhyt

Medicine

Also known as: Discrete arrhythmia ablation, focused ablation, targeted ablation

Intracardiac catheter ablation targeting discrete arrhythmias with focused ablation of specific substrate.

Clinical Context

Targeted ablation of specific arrhythmia substrate not classified as atrioventricular node, supraventricular tachycardia, or ventricular tachycardia. Includes atypical arrhythmias requiring specialized technique.
