93609 Intra-vntr mapg tchycar site

Medicine +Add-on

Also known as: Intraventricular mapping, ventricular mapping, tachycardia mapping

Intraventricular mapping to localize tachycardia source using multielectrode catheter mapping techniques during electrophysiology study.

Clinical Context

Precisely locates source of ventricular tachycardia or other ventricular arrhythmia origin using activation mapping, enabling targeted ablation therapy.
