93572 Intravenous dop vel and/or press c flo each

Medicine +Add-on

Also known as: Additional IV Doppler measurement, each additional vessel flow, repeated Doppler assessment

Intravenous Doppler velocity and pressure measurement with coronary flow assessment for each additional vessel during congenital heart catheterization.

Clinical Context

Provides comprehensive flow assessment across multiple coronary vessels, quantifies comparative flow reserves, and guides decision-making in complex coronary anomalies.
