97169 Athletic trn evaluation low cmplx

Medicine

Also known as: Athletic training eval low, ATC eval simple, Low-complexity sports eval

Athletic trainer evaluation of low complexity including history, assessment of injuries, and treatment recommendations.

Clinical Context

Used for straightforward musculoskeletal sports injuries. Athletic trainer establishes baseline functional status.
