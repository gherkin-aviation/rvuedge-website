93986 Dup-scan hemo complicated uni std

Medicine

Also known as: unilateral access duplex, single access assessment, focal fistula study

Duplex ultrasound scan for hemodialysis access assessment performed unilaterally or as a complete study of a single access site.

In Plain Language

one dialysis access vein check; single kidney treatment vessel ultrasound

Clinical Context

Used for surveillance of a known hemodialysis access site or investigation of access-related complications such as steal syndrome or access failure.
