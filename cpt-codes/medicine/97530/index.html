97530 Therapeutic activities

Medicine

Also known as: Therapeutic activities, Purposeful activity, Functional training

Therapeutic activities designed to improve function, independence, and quality of life through purposeful occupation and task-specific training.

Clinical Context

Used in occupational therapy to improve activities of daily living and instrumental activities of daily living through meaningful activity.
