Medicine +Add-on

Also known as: conscious sedation add-on, procedural sedation add-on

Moderate sedation or anesthesia administered by a different physician/qualified healthcare provider for each subsequent or additional service period.

Used for each additional service period for moderate sedation administered by a separate anesthesia provider.
