93892 Tcd emboli detection without injection

Medicine

Also known as: TCD emboli detection, microembolus detection, contrast-free emboli study

Transcranial Doppler ultrasound study to detect microemboli without intravenous injection of microbubble contrast agent.

Clinical Context

Used to detect microembolic signals suggestive of arterial source emboli, paradoxical embolism, or thrombosis in patients at stroke risk.
