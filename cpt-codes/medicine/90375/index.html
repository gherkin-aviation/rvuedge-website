90375 Rabies ig intramuscular/sternoclavicular

Medicine

Also known as: RIG, rabies IG, rabies immunoglobulin

Rabies immunoglobulin administered by intramuscular or subcutaneous route for post-exposure prophylaxis.

In Plain Language

rabies antibody injection; rabies prevention shot

Clinical Context

Critical post-exposure prophylaxis for suspected rabies exposure, providing passive immunity while active vaccination series generates active immunity.
