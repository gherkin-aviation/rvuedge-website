96571 Photodynamic treatment additional 15 minutes

Medicine +Add-on

Also known as: PDT extension, Additional PDT light, 15-minute PDT add-on

Photodynamic therapy light application for each additional 15 minutes beyond initial time. Add-on code for extended photodynamic therapy treatment.

Clinical Context

Used to extend photodynamic therapy sessions beyond initial allocation. Can be stacked multiple times for longer treatments.
