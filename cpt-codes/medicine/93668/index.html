93668 Peripheral vascular rehabilitation

Medicine

Also known as: Peripheral vascular rehab, claudication program, exercise rehabilitation

Peripheral vascular rehabilitation program with supervised exercise training and vascular assessment for claudication and peripheral artery disease.

Clinical Context

Supervised exercise program improving walking tolerance and functional capacity in patients with peripheral artery disease and intermittent claudication. Evidence-based conservative therapy.
