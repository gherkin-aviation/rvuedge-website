92546 Sinusoidal rotational test

Medicine

Also known as: rotational chair test, VOR test

Sinusoidal rotational test with interpretation; assessment of vestibular function through rotational chair stimulation.

Clinical Context

Used to assess vestibulo-ocular reflex and overall vestibular system function through automated rotational stimulus.
