93981 Penile vascular study

Medicine

Also known as: limited penile duplex, focused erectile function study, targeted penile vascular scan

Duplex ultrasound scan of penile arteries and cavernous tissue performed as a limited study for erectile function assessment.

Clinical Context

Used for limited vascular assessment when specific penile hemodynamic questions arise or for follow-up surveillance of known penile vascular abnormalities.
