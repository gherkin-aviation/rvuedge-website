94668 Mnpj chest wall sbsq

Medicine

Also known as: percussion therapy follow-up, continued chest wall therapy, subsequent percussion

Mechanical percussion and manipulation of chest wall on subsequent sessions to continue secretion mobilization and airway clearance therapy.

In Plain Language

ongoing chest tapping therapy; continued manual airway clearance

Clinical Context

Used for continued mechanical airway clearance therapy in chronic conditions requiring repeated percussion and vibration for secretion removal.
