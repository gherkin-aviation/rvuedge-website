95885 Muscle tst done with nerve tst lim

Medicine

Also known as: cranial nerve limited EMG, focal EMG

Needle electromyography; muscle(s) innervated by cranial nerves; limited study, single extremity.

Clinical Context

Limited EMG of cranial nerve-innervated muscles in single location. Evaluates focal facial, pharyngeal, or tongue weakness.
