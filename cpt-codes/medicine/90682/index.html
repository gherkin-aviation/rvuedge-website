90682 Riv4 vacc recombinant dna intramuscular

Medicine

Also known as: Rotavirus recombinant vaccine, RIV4, recombinant rotavirus

Rotavirus vaccine, recombinant DNA technology-based, four-valent (RIV4), given intramuscularly for prevention of rotavirus infection.

In Plain Language

Rotavirus shot; Stomach virus vaccine

Clinical Context

Newer recombinant rotavirus vaccine administered intramuscularly as an alternative to live oral vaccines for rotavirus prevention in infants.
