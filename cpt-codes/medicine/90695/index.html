90695 H5n8 vacc drv cll cul adj intramuscular

Medicine

Also known as: H5N8 flu vaccine, Avian influenza vaccine, Cell-derived flu vaccine

Influenza H5N8 vaccine, cell culture derived, adjuvanted formulation, administered intramuscularly for pandemic preparedness and prevention of avian influenza infection.

In Plain Language

Bird flu vaccine; Avian flu shot

Clinical Context

Administered to individuals at occupational risk of avian influenza exposure including poultry workers and laboratory personnel to prevent H5N8 infection.
