95718 Eeg phys/qhp 2-12 hour with veeg

Medicine

Also known as: video EEG review, EEG interpretation, EEG analysis

Electroencephalogram (EEG); physician or other qualified health care professional review and interpretation of recording; 2-12 hours duration with video recording.

Clinical Context

Professional interpretation of video EEG recording by physician. Includes analysis of both EEG patterns and recorded clinical events.
