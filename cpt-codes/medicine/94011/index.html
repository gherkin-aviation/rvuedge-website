94011 Spirometry up to 2 yrs old

Medicine

Also known as: infant spirometry, pediatric PFT, infant lung testing

Spirometry testing performed in children up to 2 years old to measure lung volumes and airflow to assess pediatric pulmonary function.

Clinical Context

Used to assess respiratory function in infants and toddlers with suspected lung disease, assess developmental status, or monitor chronic respiratory conditions.
