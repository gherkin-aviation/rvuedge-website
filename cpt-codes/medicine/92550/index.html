92550 Tympanometry & reflex thresh

Medicine

Also known as: tympanometry, acoustic reflex test

Tympanometry and acoustic reflex threshold testing; assessment of middle ear function and eustachian tube patency.

Clinical Context

Used for objective assessment of eustachian tube function, middle ear pressure, and ossicular chain integrity.
