90840 Psytx crisis each additional 30 minutes

Medicine +Add-on

Also known as: Crisis therapy additional, Crisis intervention continuation, Extended crisis therapy

Psychotherapy for a crisis, each additional 30 minutes of interactive treatment beyond the initial hour for crisis stabilization.

In Plain Language

Extended emergency counseling; Additional crisis support

Clinical Context

Continuation of crisis treatment when initial 60 minutes prove insufficient for stabilization; billed in 30-minute increments for time beyond the first hour.
