Medicine

Duplex ultrasound scan of upper extremity arteries performed as a limited study of specific segments.

In Plain Language

partial arm blood vessel ultrasound; focused upper arm assessment

Clinical Context

Used for targeted evaluation when hemodynamically significant disease is known or for surveillance of previously identified stenosis or fistula complications.
