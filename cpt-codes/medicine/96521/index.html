96521 Refill/maint portable pump

Medicine

Also known as: Pump refill, Portable pump maintenance, Pump charging

Refilling and maintenance service for a portable, non-programmable infusion pump, including medication loading and calibration. Does not include supply replacement.

In Plain Language

Refilling a portable medicine pump; Maintaining a portable pump

Clinical Context

Used for patients on continuous infusions with portable pumps (pain management, chemotherapy). Regular maintenance ensures pump functionality and medication delivery accuracy.
