Also known as: pH monitoring with electrode, wireless pH monitoring, advanced GERD study

In Plain Language

24-hour acid and fluid reflux test; advanced reflux monitoring

Clinical Context

Extended monitoring study using electrode-based technology to detect both acidic and non-acidic reflux events. Superior for correlating reflux with symptoms.
