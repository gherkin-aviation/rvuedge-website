92941 Prq trlml revsc tot occl ami

Medicine Global 0d

Also known as: PTCA total occlusion AMI, thrombectomy intervention acute MI

Percutaneous transluminal coronary angioplasty (PTCA) with revascularization of a totally occluded coronary artery in acute myocardial infarction.

Clinical Context

Emergency intervention in acute ST-segment elevation myocardial infarction (STEMI) with total coronary artery occlusion, performed emergently to restore myocardial perfusion and limit infarction.
