90993 Dialysis training incompl

Medicine

Also known as: dialysis training incomplete, ESRD patient training partial, ongoing renal training

Dialysis training provided to end-stage renal disease patients when training is incomplete or not yet finished.

Clinical Context

Educational services for dialysis patients to learn self-dialysis techniques when the training period has not been completed; used for interim training periods.
