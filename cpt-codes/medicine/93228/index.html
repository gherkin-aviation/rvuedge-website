93228 Remote 30 day ecg revision/report

Medicine

Also known as: 30-day event monitor, extended Holter, prolonged ECG monitoring

Remote 30-day electrocardiogram (ECG) monitoring with manual data review and written interpretation report.

Clinical Context

Extended ambulatory monitoring over 30 days for detection of infrequent arrhythmias or transient ischemia, patient-activated or continuous recording.
