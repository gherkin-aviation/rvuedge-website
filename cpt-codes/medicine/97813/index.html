97813 Acup 1/> with estim first 15 minutes

Medicine

Also known as: electroacupuncture, acupuncture with stim, electrical acupuncture

Acupuncture treatment with one or more needles including electrical stimulation (electroacupuncture) for the initial 15-minute period during a therapeutic session.

Clinical Context

Used for electroacupuncture treatment combining needle insertion with electrical stimulation to enhance therapeutic effect for pain management and other conditions.
