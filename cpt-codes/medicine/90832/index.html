90832 Psytx w pt 30 minutes

Medicine

Also known as: Psychotherapy 30 minutes, Individual therapy 30 min, Therapy session 30 min

Psychotherapy with patient for 30 minutes of direct face-to-face therapeutic interaction without medical evaluation and management services.

Clinical Context

Provided by psychiatrists or other qualified mental health professionals for ongoing psychotherapeutic treatment of psychiatric disorders.
