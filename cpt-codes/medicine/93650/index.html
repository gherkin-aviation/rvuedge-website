93650 Intracardiac catheter ablation av node func

Medicine Global 0d

Also known as: AV node ablation, AV nodal modification, slow pathway ablation

Intracardiac catheter ablation targeting atrioventricular node function modification with assessment of conduction properties.

Clinical Context

Catheter-based radiofrequency ablation targeting atrioventricular node reentry or atrioventricular node dysfunction. Effective treatment for atrioventricular nodal reentry tachycardia and atrioventricular node-dependent arrhythmias.
