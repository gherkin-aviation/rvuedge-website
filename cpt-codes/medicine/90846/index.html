90846 Family psytx without pt 50 minutes

Medicine

Also known as: Family therapy without patient, Family systems therapy, Collateral family therapy

Family psychotherapy without patient present, 50 minutes of therapeutic interaction focused on family systems and dynamics.

Clinical Context

Provided to family members without the identified patient present to address family dynamics, communication patterns, and environmental factors affecting treatment.
