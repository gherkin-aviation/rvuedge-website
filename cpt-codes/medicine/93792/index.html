93792 Pt/caregiver traing home inr

Medicine

Also known as: Home INR training, patient anticoagulation training, home monitoring education

Patient and caregiver education and training for home international normalized ratio monitoring and testing.

Clinical Context

Educates patient and caregivers on home international normalized ratio monitoring, blood draw technique, device operation, and when to seek medical advice. Enables safe home-based anticoagulation management.
