94150 Vital capacity test

Medicine

Also known as: vital capacity test, VC measurement, slow vital capacity

Vital capacity testing to measure the maximum volume of air that can be expelled from the lungs after maximum inhalation.

Clinical Context

Used to assess baseline pulmonary reserve and detect restrictive lung disease; useful in patients unable to perform forced breathing maneuvers.
