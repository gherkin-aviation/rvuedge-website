92522 Evaluate speech production

Medicine

Also known as: articulation evaluation, phonological assessment

Evaluation of speech production; assessment of articulation, phonological patterns, and motor speech skills.

Clinical Context

Used for evaluation of articulation disorders, dysarthria, apraxia, and developmental speech sound disorders.
