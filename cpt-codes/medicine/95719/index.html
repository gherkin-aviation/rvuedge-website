95719 Eeg phys/qhp each incr without vid

Medicine

Also known as: extended EEG review, EEG interpretation, EEG analysis

Electroencephalogram (EEG); physician or other qualified health care professional review and interpretation of recording; each additional 12-26 hour period without video recording.

Clinical Context

Professional interpretation of extended ambulatory EEG (12-26 hours) without video. Used for analysis of prolonged monitoring.
