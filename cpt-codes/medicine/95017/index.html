95017 All tstg percutaneous & iq with venoms

Medicine

Also known as: venom allergy testing, bee venom test, venom immunotherapy testing

Allergy testing including percutaneous and intradermal testing with venoms to identify venom allergies.

Clinical Context

Used to diagnose venom allergies in patients with history of anaphylaxis to hymenoptera stings to guide immunotherapy decisions.
