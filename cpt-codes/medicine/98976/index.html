Medicine

Also known as: remote respiratory monitoring, RTM respiratory, pulmonary monitoring

Remote therapeutic monitoring for respiratory system data collection, interpretation, and management for 16-30 consecutive calendar days.

Used for remote monitoring of respiratory parameters such as oxygen saturation, respiratory rate, or pulmonary function over 16-30 days.
