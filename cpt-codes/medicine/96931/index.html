96931 Rcm img acquisj & i&r first lesion

Medicine

Also known as: RCM, Confocal microscopy, Skin confocal imaging

Reflectance confocal microscopy with image acquisition, interpretation, and report on first lesion. Real-time microscopic visualization of skin layers without biopsy.

Clinical Context

Used for melanoma and non-melanoma skin cancer diagnosis to avoid unnecessary biopsies. Allows cellular-level visualization of suspicious lesions.
