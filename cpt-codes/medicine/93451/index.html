93451 Right heart catheter

Medicine Global 0d

Also known as: Right heart cath, RHC, pulmonary artery catheterization

Right heart catheterization with cannulation of right atrium, right ventricle, and pulmonary artery, measuring right-sided pressures and hemodynamics.

In Plain Language

right side heart tube; lung blood vessel pressure measurement; right heart function test

Clinical Context

Essential for diagnosis of pulmonary hypertension, evaluation of cardiogenic shock, assessment of right ventricular function, and determination of cardiac output and pulmonary vascular resistance. Critical in acute decompensated heart failure management.
