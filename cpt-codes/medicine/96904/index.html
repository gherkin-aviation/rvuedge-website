96904 Whole body photography

Medicine

Also known as: Total body photography, Skin photography, Body mapping

Whole body photography for skin assessment and documentation. Creates photographic record of skin surface for comparison and surveillance.

In Plain Language

Taking pictures of entire skin for records; Whole body skin photos

Clinical Context

Used for melanoma surveillance and baseline documentation of extensive skin lesions. Enables comparison of lesions over time for monitoring progression.
