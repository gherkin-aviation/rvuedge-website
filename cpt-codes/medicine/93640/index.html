Medicine Global 0d

Also known as: Pacemaker evaluation, PM interrogation, pacemaker check

Electrophysiology evaluation of single or dual-chamber pacemaker with pacing cardiovascular feedback testing.

Clinical Context

Periodic assessment of pacemaker function, battery status, lead integrity, and appropriate sensing/pacing characteristics. Standard office-based follow-up procedure.
