94781 Cars/bd tst inft-12mo +30min

Medicine +Add-on

Also known as: infant sleep study additional, extended infant sleep study, infant sleep study continuation

Cardiorespiratory sleep study in infants ages 1 month to 12 months for each additional 30 minutes beyond initial 60 minutes.

Clinical Context

Used as an add-on when infant cardiorespiratory sleep study requires extended monitoring beyond initial 60-minute period.
