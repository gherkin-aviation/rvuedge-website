92547 Supplemental electrical test

Medicine

Also known as: electrical vestibular stimulation

Supplemental electrical stimulation test for vestibular assessment; additional testing modality to supplement caloric or rotational testing.

In Plain Language

electrical balance test

Clinical Context

Used as supplementary vestibular testing when standard caloric or rotational testing is inconclusive.
