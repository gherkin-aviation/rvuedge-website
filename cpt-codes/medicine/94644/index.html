94644 Cont inhlj treatment first hour

Medicine

Also known as: continuous inhalation therapy, prolonged nebulizer treatment, 1-hour continuous inhalation

Continuous inhalation treatment provided for first hour to deliver sustained medication dosing via aerosol or other inhalation method.

Clinical Context

Used in acute respiratory exacerbations and status asthmaticus requiring prolonged continuous inhalation therapy for optimal bronchodilation.
