95957 Eeg digital analysis

Medicine

Also known as: digital EEG, quantitative EEG, QEEG

Electroencephalography with digital analysis and interpretation, including signal processing and pattern recognition.

Clinical Context

Advanced electroencephalography utilizing automated digital analysis for detection of seizures, sleep disorders, and cerebral dysfunction; often used in intensive care settings.
