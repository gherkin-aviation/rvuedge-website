92072 Fitg c-lens keratoconus first

Medicine

Also known as: keratoconus contact lens, KC lens fit, specialty contact lens fitting

Contact lens fitting for keratoconus on initial visit, including evaluation and fabrication of specialty lens.

Clinical Context

Specialized fitting of rigid or hybrid contact lenses for patients with keratoconus to provide clear vision and stabilize cone progression.
