90593 Chikungunya vacc recomb intramuscular

Medicine

Also known as: chikungunya vaccine recombinant, CHIKV recombinant

Chikungunya virus vaccine recombinant administered intramuscularly for immunization against chikungunya virus.

Clinical Context

Recombinant chikungunya vaccine for prevention of chikungunya fever and associated arthritis in travelers and endemic area residents.
