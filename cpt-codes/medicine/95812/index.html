95812 Eeg 41-60 minutes

Medicine

Also known as: routine EEG, standard EEG, office EEG

Electroencephalogram (EEG); 41-60 minutes duration.

Clinical Context

Standard office-based EEG recording for evaluation of seizures, syncope, altered mental status, or brain function assessment.
