96105 Assessment of aphasia

Medicine

Also known as: aphasia test, language assessment, speech-language evaluation

Standardized assessment of aphasia and language disorders with interpretation and detailed report.

Clinical Context

Comprehensive evaluation of language and communication deficits following stroke, trauma, or neurological disease; identifies type and severity of aphasia.
