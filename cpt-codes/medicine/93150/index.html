93150 Therapy activation ipnss

Medicine

Also known as: ICD testing, device testing, defibrillator testing

Therapy activation and testing of implantable pacemaker or defibrillator system.

Clinical Context

Intraoperative or office testing to verify appropriate sensing, pacing, and/or defibrillation therapy in newly implanted or existing devices.
