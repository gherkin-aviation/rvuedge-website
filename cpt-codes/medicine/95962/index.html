95962 Electrode stim brain add-on

Medicine +Add-on

Also known as: brain mapping add-on, additional cortical mapping

Electrode stimulation of the brain (including mapping and localization) performed as an add-on procedure.

Clinical Context

Additional brain mapping beyond initial electrode stimulation; extends comprehensive functional mapping in complex epilepsy or extensive tumor resection cases.
