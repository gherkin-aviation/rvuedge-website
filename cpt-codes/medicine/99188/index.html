99188 application topical fluoride varnish

Medicine

Also known as: fluoride varnish, topical fluoride, dental prevention

Application of topical fluoride varnish to tooth surfaces for prevention of dental caries and tooth decay in pediatric or high-risk patients.

Clinical Context

Used for preventive dental treatment with topical fluoride varnish to reduce cavity risk in children and high-risk patients.
