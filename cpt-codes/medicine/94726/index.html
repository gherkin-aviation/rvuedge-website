94726 Plethysmography lung volumes

Medicine

Also known as: body plethysmography, lung volume measurement, plethysmography

Plethysmography to measure lung volumes including total lung capacity, functional residual capacity, and residual volume.

Clinical Context

Used to assess restrictive lung disease, hyperinflation, and air trapping; essential for detecting and monitoring obstructive and restrictive patterns.
