90702 Dt vaccine under 7 yrs intramuscular

Medicine

Also known as: DT vaccine, Diphtheria-tetanus vaccine, Pediatric DT

Diphtheria toxoid and tetanus toxoid vaccine (DT) for children under 7 years of age administered intramuscularly, used when pertussis vaccine is contraindicated.

In Plain Language

Diphtheria and tetanus vaccine; DT shot

Clinical Context

Administered to infants and young children under 7 years who have contraindications to pertussis vaccine to provide protection against diphtheria and tetanus.

RVU Information
