99607 Mtms by pharmaceutical additional 15 minutes

Medicine +Add-on

Also known as: MTM additional time, extended pharmacist consultation

Medication therapy management service by pharmacist for each additional 15 minutes of consultation time beyond initial visit.

Clinical Context

Used for extended medication therapy management when complex drug regimens require additional pharmacist consultation and therapy optimization.
