93145 Interrog crtd sinus bat inpatient wo

Medicine

Also known as: pacemaker interrogation, pacemaker check, device interrogation

Interrogation and analysis of implantable cardiac pacemaker system with parameters without programming capability (in office/outpatient).

Clinical Context

Routine office-based evaluation of pacemaker function including battery status, lead integrity, and sensing/pacing parameters in devices without wireless telemetry.
