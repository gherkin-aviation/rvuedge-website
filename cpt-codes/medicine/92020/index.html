92020 Gonioscopy

Medicine

Also known as: gonioscopy, angle assessment, anterior chamber angle exam

Gonioscopy to visualize and assess the anterior chamber angle structure using specialized lens and microscope.

Clinical Context

Diagnostic procedure to evaluate the anterior chamber angle for glaucoma assessment, management of angle-closure disease, and anterior chamber pathology.
