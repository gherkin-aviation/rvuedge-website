95830 Insert electrodes for eeg

Medicine

Also known as: sphenoidal electrode placement, sphenoid lead placement

Insertion by physician of sphenoidal electrodes for EEG recording.

Clinical Context

Placement of specialized electrodes into sphenoidal region for enhanced temporal lobe EEG recording. Used for presurgical temporal lobe evaluation.
