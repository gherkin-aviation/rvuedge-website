92534 Optokinetic nystagmus test

Medicine

Also known as: optokinetic test, OKN test

Optokinetic nystagmus test; assessment of eye movement tracking reflexes using visual stimuli.

Clinical Context

Used to evaluate ocular motor control and brainstem function through assessment of eye movements following moving visual patterns.
