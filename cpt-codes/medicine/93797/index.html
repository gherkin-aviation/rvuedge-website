93797 Phys/qhp outpatient car rhab wo ecg

Medicine Global 0d

Also known as: cardiac rehab, outpatient cardiac rehab, cardiac recovery program

Cardiac rehabilitation services provided by a physician or qualified health professional for outpatient cardiac rehabilitation without electrocardiogram monitoring.

Clinical Context

Used following myocardial infarction, coronary artery bypass surgery, or heart transplantation to improve cardiac function and reduce risk of future cardiac events through supervised exercise and education.
