91110 Gi trc img intral esoph-ile

Medicine

Also known as: capsule endoscopy, wireless capsule, small bowel capsule

Gastrointestinal tract imaging using intraluminal capsule technology from esophagus to ileum.

Clinical Context

Non-invasive visualization of the entire small bowel using a wireless capsule camera. Indicated for occult bleeding, Crohn's disease, polyp surveillance.
