93600 Bundle of his recording

Medicine Global 0d

Also known as: Bundle of His recording, HIS recording, electrophysiology study

Electrophysiology study with bundle of His recording to assess atrioventricular conduction and diagnose conduction abnormalities.

In Plain Language

heart electrical pathway measurement; heart conduction recording; electrical delay assessment

Clinical Context

Measures atrioventricular conduction intervals (AH, HV intervals) to assess conduction delay location, evaluate syncope etiology, and determine risk of atrioventricular block.
