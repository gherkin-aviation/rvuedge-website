91020 Gastric motility studies

Medicine Global 0d

Also known as: gastric manometry, gastric motility test, stomach function study

Gastric motility studies to evaluate the muscular function and emptying capacity of the stomach.

In Plain Language

test of stomach muscle strength; measurement of stomach function

Clinical Context

Diagnostic study to evaluate gastroparesis, gastric dysrhythmias, or postoperative gastric dysfunction. Measures pressure waves and coordinated muscle contractions.
