92081 Limited visual field xm

Medicine

Also known as: limited visual field, confrontation fields, screening visual field

Limited visual field examination using confrontation or basic perimetry to screen for gross defects.

Clinical Context

Screening assessment of visual field for gross defects in glaucoma suspects, optic neuropathy, or neurological conditions.
