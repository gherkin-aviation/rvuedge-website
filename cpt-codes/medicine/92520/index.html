92520 Laryngeal function studies

Medicine

Also known as: laryngeal testing, voice assessment

Laryngeal function studies; objective assessment of vocal cord mobility, phonatory function, and laryngeal physiology.

Clinical Context

Used for evaluation of hoarseness, dysphonia, vocal cord paralysis, and assessment of voice disorders in singers and professional voice users.
