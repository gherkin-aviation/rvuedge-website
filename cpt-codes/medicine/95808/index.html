95808 Polysom any age 1-3> param

Medicine

Also known as: limited polysomnography, limited sleep study

Polysomnography; any age, one to three parameters (e.g., cardiac rhythm, oxygen saturation, airflow, or respiratory effort).

Clinical Context

Sleep study with limited parameters. May be used for screening or follow-up in selected populations with known sleep disorders.
