94450 Breathing response hypoxia

Medicine

Also known as: hypoxic ventilatory response, breathing response test, hypoxia challenge

Testing of breathing response to hypoxia (reduced oxygen) to assess ventilatory drive and chemoreceptor function in response to low oxygen levels.

Clinical Context

Used to assess ventilatory control and identify patients with blunted hypoxic response or central sleep apnea-related conditions.
