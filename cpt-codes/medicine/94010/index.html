94010 Breathing capacity test

Medicine

Also known as: spirometry, PFT, pulmonary function test

Spirometry testing to measure breathing capacity including forced vital capacity and forced expiratory volume to assess baseline pulmonary function.

In Plain Language

breathing test; lung function test; air flow measurement

Clinical Context

Used to assess obstructive and restrictive lung disease, monitor disease progression, and evaluate medication response in respiratory conditions.
