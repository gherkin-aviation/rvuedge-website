90384 Rh ig full-dose intramuscular

Medicine

Also known as: RhoGAM, Rh IG, RhIG full dose

Rho immunoglobulin full-dose administered intramuscularly for prevention of Rh sensitization in non-sensitized Rh negative patients.

In Plain Language

Rh prevention shot; blood incompatibility prevention

Clinical Context

Administered to non-sensitized Rh negative women post-partum or after sensitizing events to prevent alloimmunization and hemolytic disease in future pregnancies.

RVU Information
