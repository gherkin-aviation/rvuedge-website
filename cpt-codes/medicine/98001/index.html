98001 Synch audio-video new low 30

Medicine

Also known as: telehealth visit, virtual office visit, video consultation

Synchronous (real-time) audio-video interactive telehealth service between physician/qualified healthcare provider and patient for low complexity care, up to 30 minutes.

Clinical Context

Used for real-time video consultations allowing more thorough assessment for new patients with low-complexity conditions requiring moderate time commitment.
