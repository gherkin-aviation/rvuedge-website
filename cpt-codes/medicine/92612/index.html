92612 Endoscopy swallow (fees) vid

Medicine

Also known as: FEES, endoscopic swallow evaluation

Fiberoptic endoscopic evaluation of swallowing with video documentation; direct endoscopic visualization of pharyngeal swallowing.

Clinical Context

Used for direct visualization of swallowing physiology and assessment of aspiration and laryngeal penetration.
