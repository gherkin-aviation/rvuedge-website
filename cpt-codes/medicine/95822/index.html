95822 Eeg coma or sleep only

Medicine

Also known as: coma EEG, sleep EEG

Electroencephalogram (EEG); coma only or sleep only.

Clinical Context

EEG performed during coma (brain death determination) or sleep only state. Used for prognostication, seizure detection, or consciousness assessment.
