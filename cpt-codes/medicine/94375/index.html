94375 Respiratory flow volume loop

Medicine

Also known as: flow-volume loop, FVL, flow volume testing

Spirometry with measurement of airflow during inspiration and expiration using flow-volume loop graphing to assess breathing patterns.

In Plain Language

detailed breathing pattern test; flow measurement test

Clinical Context

Used to identify upper airway obstruction, variable obstruction patterns, or unusual flow characteristics not apparent on standard spirometry.
