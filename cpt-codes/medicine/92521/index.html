92521 Evaluation of speech fluency

Medicine

Also known as: fluency evaluation, stuttering assessment

Evaluation of speech fluency; assessment of stuttering, cluttering, rhythm, and speech rate abnormalities.

Clinical Context

Used for comprehensive evaluation of fluency disorders in children and adults, informing treatment planning for stuttering intervention.
