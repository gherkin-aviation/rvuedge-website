90385 Rh ig minidose intramuscular

Medicine

Also known as: mini-dose RhIG, Rh mini-dose, 50 mcg RhIG

Rho immunoglobulin mini-dose administered intramuscularly for prevention of Rh sensitization in limited exposure scenarios.

In Plain Language

small dose Rh prevention; minimal Rh protection shot

Clinical Context

Low-dose Rh immunoglobulin used for limited fetal red cell exposure situations such as threatened abortion or ectopic pregnancy in non-sensitized Rh negative women.

RVU Information
