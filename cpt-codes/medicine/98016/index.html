98016 Brief comunicaj tech-bsd service

Medicine

Also known as: e-visit, asynchronous telehealth, digital messaging

Brief communication-based technology service including online care coordination, e-mail communication, or asynchronous digital messaging between qualified healthcare provider and established patient.

Clinical Context

Used for non-real-time communication with established patients for minor clinical issues, test results, prescription refills, or care coordination.
