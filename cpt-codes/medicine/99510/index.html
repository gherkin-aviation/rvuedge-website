99510 Home visit sing/m/fam couns

Medicine

Also known as: home counseling, family counseling visit

Home visit service for individual, marital, or family counseling provided by licensed mental health professional.

In Plain Language

therapist talks with you at home about family or personal issues

Clinical Context

Used for home-based mental health counseling addressing behavioral, emotional, or relational issues in patients who cannot access office-based mental health services.
