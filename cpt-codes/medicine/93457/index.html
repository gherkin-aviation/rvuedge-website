93457 R hrt artery/graft angio

Medicine Global 0d

Also known as: Right coronary and graft angiography, right selective with grafts, RCA and graft study

Right heart catheterization with selective angiography of right coronary artery and coronary artery bypass grafts to right system.

In Plain Language

right heart artery and bypass tube pictures; right coronary and graft imaging; right side vessel evaluation

Clinical Context

Used in post-bypass patients with right coronary disease or right-sided graft assessment needed. Evaluates both native right coronary and grafts anastomosed to right system.
