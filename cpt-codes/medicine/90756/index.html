90756 Cciiv4 vacc abx free intramuscular

Medicine

Also known as: Cell culture RSV vaccine, RSV vaccine, CCIIV4

Respiratory syncytial virus (RSV) vaccine, cell culture inactivated, quadrivalent (CCIIV4), antibiotic-free formulation, administered intramuscularly.

Clinical Context

Administered to older adults for prevention of respiratory syncytial virus infection; inactivated cell culture technology without antibiotic preservatives.
