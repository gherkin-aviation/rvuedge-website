93320 Doppler echo complete

Medicine

Also known as: Complete Doppler echo, Doppler study, color flow Doppler

Complete Doppler echocardiography including spectral and color Doppler analysis for comprehensive assessment of blood flow patterns, valvular function, and hemodynamic parameters.

In Plain Language

heart flow measurements; blood movement through heart imaging; valve function test

Clinical Context

Used to evaluate valvular disease, assess stenosis and regurgitation severity, measure cardiac output, and evaluate diastolic function. Standard in workup of murmurs, dyspnea, and suspected valvular pathology.
