90880 Hypnotherapy

Medicine

Also known as: Hypnotherapy, Therapeutic hypnosis, Clinical hypnosis

Hypnotherapy for treatment of psychiatric conditions or behavioral problems using therapeutic hypnosis as the primary treatment modality.

Clinical Context

Used for anxiety, phobias, habits, pain management, and other psychiatric conditions; therapeutic hypnosis administered by qualified mental health professional.
