92285 External ocular photography

Medicine

Also known as: external eye photo, ocular surface photography, lid photography

External ocular photography to document eyelid, conjunctival, or anterior segment pathology for medical record documentation.

Clinical Context

Photography of external ocular structures for baseline documentation and monitoring of eyelid disease, conjunctival pathology, or post-operative status.
