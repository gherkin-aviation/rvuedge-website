90963 Esrd home pt serv p mo <2yrs

Medicine

Also known as: ESRD home dialysis, Home renal disease care, Pediatric home dialysis

End-stage renal disease services for home dialysis patient, monthly evaluation and management for patients under 2 years old.

Clinical Context

Comprehensive monthly nephrology services for end-stage renal disease patients managed at home using peritoneal dialysis or home hemodialysis.
