90747 Hepb vacc 4 dose immunsup intramuscular

Medicine

Also known as: Hepatitis B vaccine immunosuppressed, HepB vaccine high dose, 4-dose HepB

Hepatitis B vaccine, recombinant, 4-dose series for immunosuppressed patients administered intramuscularly at higher antigen doses.

Clinical Context

Administered to immunosuppressed patients requiring extended immunization schedule with higher antigen doses to achieve adequate antibody response.
