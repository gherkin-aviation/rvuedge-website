99183 Hyperbaric oxygen therapy

Medicine

Also known as: hyperbaric oxygen, HBO therapy, oxygen chamber therapy

Hyperbaric oxygen therapy treatment sessions where the patient breathes pure oxygen in a pressurized chamber to enhance tissue oxygenation and wound healing.

Clinical Context

Used for hyperbaric oxygen therapy for non-healing wounds, diabetic foot ulcers, carbon monoxide poisoning, or other approved indications.
