92584 Electrocochleography

Medicine

Also known as: ECoG, electrocochleogram

Electrocochleography; electrophysiologic measurement of inner ear electrical potential in response to sound.

Clinical Context

Used for objective assessment of cochlear function and to evaluate Meniere's disease and sudden hearing loss.
