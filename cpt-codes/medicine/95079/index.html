95079 Ingest challenge additional 60 minutes

Medicine +Add-on

Also known as: food challenge additional time, extended oral food challenge, prolonged food challenge

Oral ingestion challenge test for food allergens performed for each additional 60 minutes beyond initial test period.

Clinical Context

Used as an add-on when food challenge testing requires extended observation beyond initial 120-minute period.
