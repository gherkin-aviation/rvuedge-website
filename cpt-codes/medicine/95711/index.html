95711 Veeg 2-12 hour unmonitored

Medicine

Also known as: video EEG, ambulatory video EEG, home video EEG

Electroencephalogram (EEG) with video recording; 2-12 hours duration; unmonitored.

In Plain Language

portable brain wave monitor with camera

Clinical Context

Portable video EEG for outpatient seizure monitoring without active technician oversight. Video correlates clinical events with EEG patterns.
