95705 Eeg without vid 2-12 hour unmntr

Medicine

Also known as: ambulatory EEG, portable EEG, home EEG

Electroencephalogram (EEG) without video recording; 2-12 hours duration; unmonitored.

Clinical Context

Portable EEG recording for outpatient monitoring of epilepsy, parasomnias, or other neurological conditions. No active monitoring during recording.
