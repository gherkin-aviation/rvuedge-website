91124 Rct snsatn tone & cmplianc std

Medicine

Also known as: rectal sensation testing, rectal compliance study, anorectal manometry

Rectosigmoid sensation, tone, and compliance study evaluating rectal sensory perception, muscle tone, and elasticity.

Clinical Context

Diagnostic test to evaluate rectal sensory thresholds, baseline muscle tone, and wall compliance. Used in fecal incontinence, constipation, and irritable bowel syndrome.
