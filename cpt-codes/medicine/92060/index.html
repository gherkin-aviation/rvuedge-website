92060 Sensorimotor examination

Medicine

Also known as: motility exam, eye movement test, alignment assessment

Sensorimotor examination to evaluate eye movements, alignment, binocular vision, and neuromuscular function.

Clinical Context

Comprehensive assessment of ocular motility, strabismus, diplopia, convergence, and accommodation for diagnosis of binocular vision disorders.
