94680 O2 uptk rst & xers dir simple

Medicine

Also known as: oxygen consumption test, metabolic rate testing, VO2 measurement

Oxygen uptake and carbon dioxide output testing with simple direct measurement and exercise testing to assess metabolic rate.

Clinical Context

Used to assess metabolic function and energy expenditure at rest and during exercise for nutritional and rehabilitation planning.
