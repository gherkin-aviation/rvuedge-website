Medicine

Also known as: postop visit, follow-up visit, post-surgical check

Postoperative follow-up visit included in the global surgical package during the postoperative period with no additional payment.

Clinical Context

Used for follow-up visits to patients after surgery during the postoperative global period, included in the surgical package without separate reimbursement.
