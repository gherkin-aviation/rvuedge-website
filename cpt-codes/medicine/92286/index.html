92286 Anterior segment img i & r speclr mic

Medicine

Also known as: specular microscopy, endothelial cell count, anterior segment imaging

Anterior segment imaging with internal and/or external view using specular microscopy for corneal endothelial assessment.

Clinical Context

Microscopic examination and photographic documentation of corneal endothelial cells to assess cell count and morphology prior to cataract surgery.
