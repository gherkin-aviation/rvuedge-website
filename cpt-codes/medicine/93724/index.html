93724 Elec alys antitchycar pm sys

Medicine Global 0d

Also known as: Antitachycardia pacemaker analysis, ATP device check, antitachycardia device eval

Electrical analysis of implanted antitachycardia pacemaker system including device interrogation and function assessment.

Clinical Context

Assessment of antitachycardia pacemaker function including ATP capability, sensing parameters, and therapy efficacy. Standard periodic evaluation for appropriate device function.
