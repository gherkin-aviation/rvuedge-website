99135 Anesthesia complicated ctrld hypotension

Medicine +Add-on

Also known as: controlled hypotension add-on, hypotensive anesthesia

Anesthesia complexity add-on for patients requiring controlled hypotension (deliberate blood pressure reduction) during surgical procedures.

In Plain Language

extra anesthesia care when blood pressure is intentionally lowered

Clinical Context

Used to report additional anesthesia complexity for procedures requiring controlled hypotension to reduce blood loss.
