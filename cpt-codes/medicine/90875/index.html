90875 Psychophysiological therapy

Medicine

Also known as: Biofeedback therapy, Psychophysiological training, Biofeedback training

Psychophysiological therapy using biofeedback techniques, initial establishment of biofeedback training 30 minutes for treatment of psychosomatic conditions.

Clinical Context

Initial biofeedback training for conditions such as tension headaches, anxiety, hypertension, or irritable bowel syndrome to develop self-regulation skills.
