96372 therapy/proph/diagnostic injection sternoclavicular/intramuscular

Medicine

Also known as: subcut injection, intramuscular injection, subcutaneous shot, therapeutic injection subcutaneous or intramuscular, IM injection, injection admin

Therapeutic, prophylactic, or diagnostic injection administered subcutaneously or intramuscularly.

Clinical Context

Administration of medications via subcutaneous or intramuscular injection including vaccines, biologics, antibiotics, and other therapeutic agents.
