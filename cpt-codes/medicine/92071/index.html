92071 Contact lens fitting for treatment

Medicine

Also known as: contact lens fit, therapeutic lens fitting, custom lens fitting

Contact lens fitting for therapeutic correction of corneal or refractive abnormalities to improve visual function.

Clinical Context

Fitting and evaluation of contact lenses for therapeutic correction of irregular corneas, keratoconus, post-surgical correction, or refractive error.
