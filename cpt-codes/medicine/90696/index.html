90696 Dtap-ipv vaccine 4-6 yrs intramuscular

Medicine

Also known as: DTaP-IPV, Kinrix, Quadracel

Diphtheria toxoid, tetanus toxoid, acellular pertussis, and inactivated poliovirus vaccine (DTaP-IPV) combination vaccine for children ages 4-6 years administered intramuscularly.

Clinical Context

Administered as a booster vaccination to children ages 4-6 years who have previously completed primary DTaP series to maintain immunity against diphtheria, tetanus, pertussis, and polio.
