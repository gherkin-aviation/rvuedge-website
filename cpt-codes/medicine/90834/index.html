90834 Psytx w pt 45 minutes

Medicine

Also known as: Psychotherapy 45 minutes, Individual therapy 45 min, Therapy session 45 min

Psychotherapy with patient for 45 minutes of direct face-to-face therapeutic interaction without medical evaluation and management services.

Clinical Context

Provided by psychiatrists or other qualified mental health professionals for ongoing psychotherapeutic treatment with adequate time for therapeutic work.
