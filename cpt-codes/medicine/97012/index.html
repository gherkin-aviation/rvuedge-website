97012 Mechanical traction therapy

Medicine

Also known as: Traction therapy, Spinal traction, Cervical traction

Mechanical traction therapy applied to spine or extremity to reduce pain and improve function. Includes manual or mechanical traction application.

In Plain Language

Stretching therapy for spine or joint pain

Clinical Context

Used for spinal stenosis, disc herniations, and nerve compression syndromes. Aims to decompress spine and relieve radiculopathy.
