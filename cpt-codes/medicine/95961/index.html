95961 Electrode stimulation brain

Medicine

Also known as: brain mapping, cortical stimulation, subdural stimulation

Electrode stimulation of the brain (including mapping and localization) with interpretation and report.

Clinical Context

Intraoperative brain mapping during epilepsy or tumor resection surgery to identify eloquent cortex (motor, sensory, speech areas) and guide surgical planning.
