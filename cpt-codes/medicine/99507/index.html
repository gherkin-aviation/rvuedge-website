99507 Home visit catheter maintain

Medicine

Also known as: catheter care home visit, catheter maintenance

Home visit service for catheter maintenance including assessment, sterile dressing changes, and troubleshooting of urinary catheter or other indwelling catheters.

Clinical Context

Used for home-based care and monitoring of patients with indwelling catheters, including dressing changes and catheter-related problem management.
