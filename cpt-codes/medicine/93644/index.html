93644 Ep evaluation subcutaneous implant defibrillator

Medicine Global 0d

Also known as: Subcutaneous ICD evaluation, S-ICD check, S-ICD interrogation

Electrophysiology evaluation of subcutaneously implanted implantable cardiovascular defibrillator.

Clinical Context

Assessment of subcutaneously implanted defibrillator function including sensing, pacing capability, and defibrillation threshold. Growing modality avoiding transvenous lead complications.
