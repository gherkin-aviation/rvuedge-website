93593 R hrt catheter chd normal nt cnj

Medicine Global 0d

Also known as: RHC normal CHD, right heart cath non-injected, RHC measurements only

Right heart catheterization in congenital heart disease with normal anatomy, non-injected hemodynamic measurement.

Clinical Context

Hemodynamic assessment without angiographic intervention in congenital cases with normal expected anatomy. Used for pressure measurement and cardiac output determination.
