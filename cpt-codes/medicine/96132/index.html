96132 Nrpsyc tst evaluation phys/qhp first

Medicine

Also known as: neuropsych evaluation, cognitive testing, brain function testing

Neuropsychological testing and evaluation by physician or qualified health professional for first hour, including case review and interpretation.

Clinical Context

Comprehensive neuropsychological assessment of cognitive functions including memory, attention, language, and executive function following neurological disorders.
