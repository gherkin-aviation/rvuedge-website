99027 Out-of-hosp on call service

Medicine

Also known as: on-call service, out-of-house call, remote on-call

Physician out-of-hospital on-call service responsibility (not in-house) including availability, monitoring, and readiness to treat or respond to patient needs outside the hospital facility.

In Plain Language

doctor available by phone to respond to patient emergencies

Clinical Context

Used to report physician on-call coverage duties outside the hospital facility for emergency consultation or patient response.

RVU Information

CPT 99027 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
