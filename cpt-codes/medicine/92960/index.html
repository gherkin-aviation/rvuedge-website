92960 Cardioversion electric external

Medicine Global 0d

Also known as: external cardioversion, defibrillation, electrical shock therapy

Elective cardioversion of abnormal cardiac arrhythmia via external shock.

Clinical Context

Elective treatment for patients with supraventricular or ventricular arrhythmias such as atrial fibrillation or atrial flutter, performed in controlled setting with anesthesia.
