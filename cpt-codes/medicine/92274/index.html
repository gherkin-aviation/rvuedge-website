92274 Multifocal erg with i & r

Medicine

Also known as: multifocal ERG, mERG, regional retinal assessment

Multifocal electroretinography with internal and external recording to map localized retinal function across the macula.

Clinical Context

Electrophysiologic mapping of macular function to identify focal retinal dysfunction in diseases affecting central vision.
