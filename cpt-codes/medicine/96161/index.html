96161 Caregiver health risk assmt

Medicine

Also known as: caregiver risk assessment, family health assessment

Health risk assessment (behavior, social, psychological) performed with patient's caregiver or family member.

Clinical Context

Assessment of health risks in caregiver or family context to identify factors affecting patient health and guide family-based interventions.
