93321 Doppler echo f-up/lmtd std

Medicine

Also known as: Limited Doppler echo, follow-up Doppler, focused Doppler

Limited Doppler echocardiography for follow-up evaluation or focused assessment of specific cardiac structures with spectral and color Doppler analysis.

In Plain Language

focused heart flow test; limited heart ultrasound; quick heart check

Clinical Context

Performed for follow-up of known valvular disease, serial monitoring of ejection fraction, or evaluation of specific clinical question without comprehensive study. More efficient for stable patients requiring interval assessment.
