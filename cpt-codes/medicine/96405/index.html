96405 Chemotherapy intralesional up to 7

Medicine Global 0d

Also known as: intralesional chemo, lesion injection, skin lesion chemo

Chemotherapy intralesional injection into lesions (up to 7 lesions).

Clinical Context

Direct intralesional injection of chemotherapy agents into skin cancers or other lesions for localized treatment; limited to up to 7 lesions.
