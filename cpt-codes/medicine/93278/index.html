93278 Ecg/signal-averaged

Medicine

Also known as: signal-averaged ECG, SAECG, late potential detection

Electrocardiogram (ECG) with signal averaging for detection of late potentials.

Clinical Context

Advanced ECG technique detecting microvolt-level late potentials indicating substrate for ventricular arrhythmias, used for risk stratification in post-infarction patients.
