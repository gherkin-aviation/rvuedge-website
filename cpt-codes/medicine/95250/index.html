95250 Cont gluc mntr phys/qhp eqp

Medicine

Also known as: continuous glucose monitor, glucose monitoring system, CGM

Continuous glucose monitoring system; physician or other qualified health care professional-provided equipment (sensor), insertion, supply replacement, and removal.

Clinical Context

Used for Type 1 and Type 2 diabetes management. Healthcare provider supplies and manages equipment while monitoring glucose trends.
