92250 Fundus photography with i & r

Medicine

Also known as: fundus photo, retinal photography, eye fundus imaging

Fundus photography with internal and/or external view including acquisition, processing, and documentation of retinal images.

Clinical Context

Standard documentation photography of ocular fundus for baseline assessment and monitoring of retinal disease, optic disc pathology, and treatment response.
