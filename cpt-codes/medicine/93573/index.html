93573 Njx catheter slct p-art angrp bi

Medicine +Add-on

Also known as: Bilateral PA angiography, bilateral pulmonary angiography, bilateral selective PA angio

Injection during congenital heart catheterization with bilateral selective pulmonary artery angiography to visualize both right and left pulmonary circulation.

Clinical Context

Evaluates bilateral branch pulmonary artery anatomy, assesses symmetry of pulmonary blood flow distribution, characterizes bilateral pulmonary artery stenosis, and guides bilateral intervention planning.
