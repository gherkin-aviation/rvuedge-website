99511 Home visit fecal/enema management

Medicine

Also known as: fecal management, bowel care visit

Home visit service for fecal management and bowel care, including assessment, education, and management of fecal incontinence or constipation.

Clinical Context

Used for home-based bowel care management including digital stimulation, bowel retraining, and fecal impaction management in patients with neurogenic bowel.
