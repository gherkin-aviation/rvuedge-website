90952 Esrd serv 2-3 vsts p mo <2yr

Medicine

Also known as: ESRD services, Dialysis management, Renal disease management

End-stage renal disease services, monthly evaluation and management of dialysis patient with 2-3 visits per month for patients under 2 years old.

Clinical Context

Comprehensive monthly nephrology services for end-stage renal disease dialysis patients with reduced visit frequency appropriate for clinical stability.
