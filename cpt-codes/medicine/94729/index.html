94729 Diffusing capacity

Medicine

Also known as: DLCO test, carbon monoxide diffusing capacity, gas transfer test

Diffusing capacity testing to measure carbon monoxide transfer across alveolar-capillary membrane to assess gas exchange ability.

Clinical Context

Used to detect alveolar-capillary disease including interstitial lung disease, emphysema, anemia, and pulmonary hypertension.
