92284 Diagnosis dark adaptation examination i & r

Medicine

Also known as: dark adaptation test, scotopic function test, night vision assessment

Diagnostic dark adaptation examination with measurement of scotopic vision recovery time and light sensitivity.

Clinical Context

Assessment of dark adaptation and scotopic vision function to evaluate night blindness and vitamin A deficiency in retinal disease.
