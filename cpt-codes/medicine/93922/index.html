93922 upper/l xtremity artery 2 levels

Medicine

Also known as: extremity duplex, limb artery ultrasound, 2-level vascular study

Duplex ultrasound scan of upper or lower extremity arteries including 2 arterial levels to evaluate for stenosis or occlusion.

In Plain Language

arm or leg blood vessel ultrasound; extremity artery screening

Clinical Context

Used to assess peripheral arterial disease in patients with claudication, ischemic rest pain, or wound assessment in diabetic and vascular patients.
