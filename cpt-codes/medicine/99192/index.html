99192 Special pump services

Medicine

Also known as: pump service, infusion pump service, device support

Special pump services including setup, maintenance, or emergency response for infusion pumps, feeding pumps, or other specialized medical devices.

In Plain Language

service for medical pump devices

Clinical Context

Used for special services related to pump devices such as chemotherapy pumps, pain pumps, or feeding pumps.
