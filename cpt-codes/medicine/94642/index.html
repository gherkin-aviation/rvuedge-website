94642 Aerosol inhalation treatment

Medicine

Also known as: aerosol inhalation treatment, nebulizer therapy, inhaled medication

Aerosol inhalation treatment to deliver medication in aerosol form directly to respiratory tract for therapeutic effect.

In Plain Language

inhaled mist treatment; aerosol medication therapy

Clinical Context

Used for delivery of bronchodilators, mucolytics, or antibiotics via nebulized aerosol in acute exacerbations and chronic respiratory disease.
