97755 Assistive technology assess

Medicine

Also known as: assistive tech eval, AT assessment, mobility device eval

Comprehensive assessment and evaluation of a patient's need for assistive technology and adaptive devices to improve functional independence and quality of life.

In Plain Language

evaluating aids to help with daily activities; assessing need for adaptive equipment

Clinical Context

Used when evaluating patients who may benefit from assistive technology such as mobility aids, communication devices, or adaptive equipment due to physical impairment or disability.
