96548 Ntraop hipec procedures each add 30min

Medicine +Add-on

Also known as: HIPEC add-on, HIPEC additional time, Extended HIPEC

Intraoperative hyperthermic intraperitoneal chemotherapy procedure - each additional 30 minutes of heated chemotherapy perfusion beyond the initial 60 minutes.

Clinical Context

Used when intraoperative HIPEC treatment extends beyond 60 minutes. Code stacked with 96547 for longer procedures.
