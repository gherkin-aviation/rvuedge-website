93882 Extracranial uni/ltd study

Medicine

Also known as: unilateral carotid duplex, limited extracranial scan, one-sided carotid ultrasound

Duplex scan of extracranial arteries performed unilaterally or as a limited study on one or limited segments of the head and neck.

Clinical Context

Used for targeted assessment of extracranial arteries when hemodynamically significant disease is known or suspected on one side, or for follow-up of previously identified stenosis.
