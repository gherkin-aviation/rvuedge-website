Right and left heart catheterization in congenital heart disease with normal anatomy, non-injected hemodynamic measurement.

Comprehensive hemodynamic assessment of both right and left heart without angiography in congenital disease with normal anatomy. Determines complete hemodynamic profile and cardiac output.
