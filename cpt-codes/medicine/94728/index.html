94728 Airwy resist by oscillometry

Medicine

Also known as: oscillometry, airway resistance measurement, impulse oscillometry

Airway resistance measurement by oscillometry or similar methods to assess lung function without forced breathing maneuvers.

Clinical Context

Used in patients unable to perform forced spirometry (young children, elderly) to assess obstructive airway disease and bronchodilator response.
