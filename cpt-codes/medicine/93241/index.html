93241 External ecg rec>48hr<7d

Medicine

Also known as: extended Holter, 5-day monitor, patch monitor

External electrocardiogram (ECG) recording greater than 48 hours but less than 7 days with analysis and interpretation.

Clinical Context

Intermediate-duration ambulatory monitoring for detection of paroxysmal arrhythmias or ischemia over several days, effective for symptomatic patients.
