95070 Inhlj brncl challenge tstg

Medicine

Also known as: methacholine challenge test, bronchial challenge test, bronchial reactivity test

Inhalation or bronchial challenge testing using methacholine or other agents to assess bronchial hyperresponsiveness.

In Plain Language

asthma breathing challenge test; airway sensitivity test

Clinical Context

Used to diagnose asthma and assess airway hyperresponsiveness in patients with suspected asthma but normal baseline spirometry.
