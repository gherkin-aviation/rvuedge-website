94625 Phy/qhp outpatient pulmonary rhb without mntr

Medicine

Also known as: pulmonary rehab, respiratory rehabilitation, COPD rehabilitation program

Physician or qualified health professional-supervised outpatient pulmonary rehabilitation without electronic monitoring to improve respiratory function and exercise tolerance.

Clinical Context

Used for patients with chronic respiratory disease including chronic obstructive pulmonary disease and interstitial lung disease to improve function and quality of life.
