90707 Mmr vaccine sternoclavicular

Medicine

Also known as: MMR vaccine, MMR, M-M-R Vac

Measles, mumps, and rubella vaccine (MMR) live attenuated vaccine administered subcutaneously for prevention of measles, mumps, and rubella infection.

In Plain Language

Measles vaccine; Mumps and rubella vaccine

Clinical Context

Administered to children at 12-15 months with a booster at 4-6 years for prevention of measles, mumps, and rubella; highly effective live vaccine.
