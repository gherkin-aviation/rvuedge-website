Medicine

Also known as: diaphragm EMG, phrenic nerve EMG

Needle electromyography; hemidiaphragm.

Clinical Context

EMG of diaphragm. Evaluates phrenic nerve function, particularly after thoracic surgery or with respiratory complaints.
