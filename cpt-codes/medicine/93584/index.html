93584 Vngrph chd anom/persist service

Medicine +Add-on

Also known as: Anomalous venous return imaging, persistent SVC imaging, superior vena cava angiography

Ventriculography and angiography during congenital heart catheterization to visualize anomalous venous return and persistent superior vena cava anatomy.

Clinical Context

Characterizes persistent superior vena cava, partial anomalous pulmonary venous return to SVC, and abnormal systemic venous connections in congenital heart disease.
