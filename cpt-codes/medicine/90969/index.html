90969 Esrd service pr day pt 12-19

Medicine

Also known as: ESRD service 12-19, renal disease service, dialysis monthly service

End-stage renal disease service per day for patients aged 12-19 years, including the full range of clinical services for dialysis management.

Clinical Context

Used for comprehensive chronic kidney disease management in pediatric patients undergoing dialysis therapy. Includes monitoring, nutritional counseling, and psychosocial support.
