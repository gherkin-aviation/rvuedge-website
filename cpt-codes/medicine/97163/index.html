97163 Pt evaluation high complex 45 minutes

Medicine

Also known as: PT eval high complex, Comprehensive PT eval, Complex PT eval

Physical therapy evaluation of high complexity requiring 45 minutes. For patients with multiple conditions, significant medical history, or complex functional deficits.

Clinical Context

Used for medically complex patients with multiple comorbidities or complex functional impairments. Comprehensive baseline documentation.
