90791 Psychiatric diagnostic evaluation

Medicine

Also known as: Psychiatric evaluation, Mental health evaluation, Psychiatric assessment, psychiatric diagnostic evaluation, psych eval, mental health assessment

Psychiatric diagnostic evaluation without medical services, comprehensive assessment of mental status, history, and psychological functioning.

Clinical Context

Initial evaluation by psychiatrist or other qualified mental health professional to establish diagnosis and treatment plan for psychiatric conditions.
