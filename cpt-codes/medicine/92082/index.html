92082 Intermediate visual field xm

Medicine

Also known as: intermediate visual field, standard perimetry, visual field test

Intermediate visual field examination using manual or automated perimetry to assess peripheral vision function.

In Plain Language

standard side vision test; detailed eye field exam

Clinical Context

Standard visual field test for glaucoma monitoring, optic nerve assessment, and neurological evaluation. Includes central and peripheral field assessment.
