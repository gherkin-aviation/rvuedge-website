95117 Immunotherapy injections

Medicine

Also known as: allergy shots, immunotherapy injections, multi-dose therapy

Allergen immunotherapy (allergy injection) administered by injection; multiple injections given in single visit.

In Plain Language

multiple allergy shots; allergy injection series

Clinical Context

Administered when patient receives more than one allergen injection in a single session, as part of build-up phase treatment.
