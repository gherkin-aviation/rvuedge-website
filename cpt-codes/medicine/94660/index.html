94660 Cpap initiation & management

Medicine

Also known as: CPAP setup, continuous positive airway pressure, CPAP initiation

Continuous positive airway pressure initiation and management for obstructive sleep apnea or other indications requiring positive airway pressure therapy.

Clinical Context

Used to initiate and manage treatment of obstructive sleep apnea and other conditions requiring positive airway pressure during sleep.
