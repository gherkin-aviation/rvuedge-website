90688 Iiv4 vaccine splt 0.5 milliliters intramuscular

Medicine

Also known as: Flu shot, Influenza vaccine, Split-virus IIV4

Inactivated influenza vaccine, quadrivalent (IIV4), split virion formulation, 0.5 milliliter dose, administered intramuscularly for adults and older children.

Clinical Context

Annual seasonal influenza vaccination for adults and older children using split-virion technology; provides protection against four circulating influenza strains.
