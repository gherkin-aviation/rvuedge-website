90839 Psytx crisis initial 60 minutes

Medicine

Also known as: Crisis therapy 60 min, Psychiatric emergency treatment, Crisis intervention

Psychotherapy for a crisis, initial 60 minutes of complex interactive treatment and stabilization in acute psychiatric emergency.

Clinical Context

Provided by psychiatrists for acute psychiatric crises including suicidality, homicidality, or severe functional impairment requiring immediate stabilization.
