93588 Vngrph chd vnvn cltrl below

Medicine +Add-on

Also known as: Infra-atrial collateral imaging, vein-to-vein angiography below atrium, lower collateral pathway visualization

Ventriculography and angiography during congenital heart catheterization to visualize vein-to-vein collateral connections below atrial level.

Clinical Context

Characterizes venous collateral pathways bypassing normal venous channels below atrial level in complex congenital disease and post-surgical anatomy.
