92961 Cardioversion electric int

Medicine Global 0d

Also known as: internal cardioversion, ICD therapy, implantable defibrillator shock

Cardioversion of abnormal cardiac arrhythmia via internal shock delivery using implanted device.

Clinical Context

Therapeutic intervention delivered by implanted cardioverter-defibrillator in response to detected malignant arrhythmia, standard treatment for sustained ventricular arrhythmias in high-risk patients.
