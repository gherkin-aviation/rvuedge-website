90589 Chikungunya vaccine live intramuscular

Medicine

Also known as: chikungunya vaccine live, CHIKV vaccine

Chikungunya virus vaccine live administered intramuscularly for immunization against chikungunya virus.

In Plain Language

chikungunya fever vaccine; tropical disease prevention

Clinical Context

Live attenuated chikungunya vaccine for prevention of chikungunya fever in travelers and residents of endemic areas.
