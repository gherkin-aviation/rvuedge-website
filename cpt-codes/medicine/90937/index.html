90937 Hemodialysis repeated evaluation

Medicine Global 0d

Also known as: Dialysis re-evaluation, Hemodialysis assessment, Dialysis patient visit

Hemodialysis evaluation, repeated comprehensive assessment of patient for ongoing dialysis treatment monitoring and medical management.

Clinical Context

Ongoing evaluation of hemodialysis patients including assessment of access function, nutritional status, anemia management, and blood pressure control.
