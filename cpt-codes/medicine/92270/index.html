92270 Electro-oculography with i & r

Medicine

Also known as: electrooculography, EOG recording, retinal function test

Electro-oculography with internal and external recording to assess retinal pigment epithelium and photoreceptor function.

Clinical Context

Electrophysiologic assessment of retinal pigment epithelium light-evoked potentials to evaluate inherited retinal dystrophies.
