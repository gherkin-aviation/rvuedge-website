94002 Vent management inpat initial day

Medicine

Also known as: ventilator management initial day, mechanical ventilation setup, vent initiation

Management of patients on mechanical ventilation during the initial day of hospitalization including assessment, setup, and adjustment of ventilator parameters.

Clinical Context

Used for initial management of patients requiring mechanical ventilation due to respiratory failure, post-surgical paralysis, or critical illness.
