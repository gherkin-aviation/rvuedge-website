Medicine

Also known as: ophthalmoscopy with drawing, fundus exam with documentation, retinal mapping

Ophthalmoscopy extended with retinal drawing documentation performed on one or both eyes.

Clinical Context

Detailed fundoscopic examination including direct drawing documentation of retinal findings for retinopathy, optic disc pathology, and peripheral retinal disease.
