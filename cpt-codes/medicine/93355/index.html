93355 Echo transesophageal (tee)

Medicine

Also known as: TEE, transesophageal echo, comprehensive TEE

Transesophageal echocardiography with comprehensive real-time imaging of cardiac structures, providing detailed assessment of anatomy, function, and blood flow patterns.

Clinical Context

Gold standard for evaluating endocarditis, assessing valve pathology, detecting thrombi, evaluating structural abnormalities, and guiding interventional procedures. Superior image quality compared to transthoracic approach, especially in obese or mechanically ventilated patients.
