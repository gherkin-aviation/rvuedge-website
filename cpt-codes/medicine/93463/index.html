93463 Drug administration & hemodynmic meas

Medicine +Add-on

Also known as: Drug challenge, pharmacologic testing, drug-induced hemodynamics

Pharmacologic agent administration during cardiac catheterization with hemodynamic and cardiac output measurements to assess drug response.

Clinical Context

Used to assess hemodynamic response to inotropic agents, vasodilators, or vasopressors; evaluate reserve capacity; and guide therapeutic decisions in cardiogenic shock or advanced heart failure. Essential in research protocols and complex hemodynamic assessment.
