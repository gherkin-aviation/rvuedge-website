99170 Anogenital examination child w imag

Medicine Global 0d

Also known as: abuse exam, forensic exam, child abuse evaluation

Anogenital examination of a child with imaging and documentation for evaluation of suspected child sexual abuse, including photographic or diagnostic imaging records.

Clinical Context

Used for comprehensive anogenital examination of suspected child sexual abuse victims with imaging documentation for legal and medical purposes.
