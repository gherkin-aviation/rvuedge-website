92973 Prq trluml c mchn asp thrmbc

Medicine

Also known as: thrombectomy, clot aspiration, mechanical thrombus removal

Percutaneous transluminal coronary mechanical aspiration thrombectomy to remove acute thrombus from coronary artery.

Clinical Context

Interventional procedure to remove acute coronary thrombus in acute myocardial infarction, reducing thrombus burden and improving myocardial reperfusion.
