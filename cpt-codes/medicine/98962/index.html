98962 Edu & trn pt slf-mgmt nqhp 5-8

Medicine

Also known as: patient education series, self-management training program

Education and training for patient self-management provided by a non-physician healthcare provider for patients with one or more chronic conditions, five to eight sessions.

Clinical Context

Used for an extended series of five to eight patient education sessions addressing chronic disease self-management.
