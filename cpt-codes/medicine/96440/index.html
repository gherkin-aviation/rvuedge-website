96440 Chmotx admn plrl cav thrcnts

Medicine Global 0d

Also known as: Pleural chemotherapy, Intrapleural chemo, Thoracentesis chemo

Administration of chemotherapy agents directly into one or more pleural cavities via thoracentesis or implanted catheter. This includes drainage of accumulated fluid and instillation of chemotherapy.

Clinical Context

Used for malignant pleural effusions and primary peritoneal/pleural cancers. Common in ovarian, lung, and mesothelioma cases to deliver chemotherapy directly to affected body cavity.
