95060 Ophthalmic mucous membrane tests

Medicine

Also known as: ocular allergy test, eye mucous membrane test, ophthalmic allergy testing

Ophthalmic mucous membrane testing for allergens using application to eye surface to evaluate ocular allergy.

Clinical Context

Used to diagnose ocular allergies and allergen sensitivities in patients with allergic conjunctivitis.
