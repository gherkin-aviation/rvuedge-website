93701 Bioimpedance cv analysis

Medicine

Also known as: Bioimpedance analysis, impedance cardiography, ICG

Bioimpedance cardiovascular analysis providing non-invasive hemodynamic assessment including cardiac output, stroke volume, and fluid status.

In Plain Language

electrical heart function measurement; non-invasive cardiac output test; fluid status assessment

Clinical Context

Non-invasive hemodynamic monitoring useful for titrating diuretics, assessing fluid responsiveness, and monitoring acute decompensated heart failure. Increasingly used in clinical settings.
