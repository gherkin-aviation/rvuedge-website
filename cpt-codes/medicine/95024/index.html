95024 Iq tests with allergenic xtrcs

Medicine

Also known as: intradermal allergy test, intradermal skin test, ID allergy testing

Intradermal allergy testing with allergenic extracts to identify specific allergens through injection into dermis.

Clinical Context

Used for confirmation and identification of specific allergens when percutaneous testing is negative or inconclusive.
