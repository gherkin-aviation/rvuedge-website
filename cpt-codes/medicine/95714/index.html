95714 Veeg each 12-26 hour unmntr

Medicine

Also known as: extended video EEG, prolonged video EEG

Electroencephalogram (EEG) with video recording; each additional 12-26 hour period; unmonitored.

In Plain Language

extended portable brain wave monitor with camera

Clinical Context

Extended video EEG for 12-26 hours without active monitoring. Increases likelihood of capturing infrequent seizures while recording clinical behavior.
