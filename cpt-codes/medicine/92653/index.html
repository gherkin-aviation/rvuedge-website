92653 Aep neurodiagnostic i & r

Medicine

Also known as: neurodiagnostic AEP, brainstem AEP

Auditory evoked potential (AEP) testing for neurodiagnostic purposes including interpretation and reporting.

Clinical Context

Assesses electrical activity along the auditory pathway from the cochlea to the brainstem, used to diagnose disorders affecting the auditory nerve and central auditory processing in multiple sclerosis, brainstem pathology, and retrocochlear lesions.
