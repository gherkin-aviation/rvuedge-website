98002 Synch audio-video new mod 45

Medicine

Also known as: telehealth visit, virtual office visit, video consultation

Synchronous (real-time) audio-video interactive telehealth service between physician/qualified healthcare provider and patient for moderate complexity care, up to 45 minutes.

Clinical Context

Used for real-time video consultations for new patients with moderate-complexity conditions requiring more extensive evaluation and discussion.
