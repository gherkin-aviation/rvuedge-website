95928 C motor evoked uppr limbs

Medicine

Also known as: CMEP upper limbs, MEP upper, motor evoked potential

Cranial motor evoked potential testing of the upper limbs to assess motor pathway integrity from the brain to the arms.

Clinical Context

Evaluates motor tract function in cervical myelopathy, spinal cord disorders, or during neurosurgical procedures affecting upper extremity motor control.
