90833 Psytx w pt w e/m 30 minutes

Medicine

Also known as: Psychotherapy with E/M 30 minutes, Therapy with medical services 30 min

Psychotherapy with patient for 30 minutes of direct face-to-face therapeutic interaction with concurrent medical evaluation and management services.

Clinical Context

Provided by psychiatrists for psychotherapy combined with concurrent medical assessment and medication management during the same session.
