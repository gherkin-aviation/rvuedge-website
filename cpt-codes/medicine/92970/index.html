92970 Cardioassist internal

Medicine Global 0d

Also known as: IABP, intra-aortic balloon pump, left ventricular assist

Insertion of percutaneous left or right intra-aortic balloon pump or other cardioassist device for internal circulatory support.

Clinical Context

Mechanical circulatory support for cardiogenic shock, severe heart failure, or hemodynamic instability, providing temporary left or right ventricular assistance.
