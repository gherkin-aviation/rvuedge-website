93598 Car outp meas drainage catheter chd

Medicine +Add-on

Also known as: Cardiac output measurement, hemodynamic calculation, CO measurement

Cardiac output measurement and hemodynamic assessment during congenital heart catheterization using thermodilution or other quantitative methods.

Clinical Context

Quantifies cardiac output, calculates systemic and pulmonary vascular resistance, determines shunt ratios, and guides therapeutic decisions in congenital heart disease.
