92137 Cptrz ophthalmic img pst sg rta oct

Medicine

Also known as: retinal OCT, optic disc OCT, OCT posterior segment

Computerized ophthalmic imaging of posterior segment with retinal thickness assessment using optical coherence tomography technology.

Clinical Context

Advanced optical coherence tomography imaging of posterior structures for quantitative assessment of optic disc cupping, retinal thickness, and macular pathology.
