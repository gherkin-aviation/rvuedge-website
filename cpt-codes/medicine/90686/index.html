90686 Iiv4 vacc no prsv 0.5 milliliters intramuscular

Medicine

Also known as: Flu shot, Influenza vaccine, IIV4

Inactivated influenza vaccine, quadrivalent (IIV4), non-preserved formulation, 0.5 milliliter dose, administered intramuscularly for adults and older children.

In Plain Language

Flu vaccine; Flu shot

Clinical Context
