95965 Meg spontaneous

Medicine

Also known as: MEG spontaneous, spontaneous magnetoencephalography

Magnetoencephalography recording of spontaneous brain activity with analysis and interpretation.

Clinical Context

Non-invasive measurement of magnetic fields generated by brain electrical activity; used to evaluate epilepsy, brain tumors, and functional brain mapping.
