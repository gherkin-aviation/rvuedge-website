Medicine

Also known as: phone visit, telephone consultation, audio telehealth

Synchronous (real-time) audio-only interactive telehealth service between physician/qualified healthcare provider and patient for high complexity care, up to 60 minutes.

In Plain Language

60-minute phone call with doctor; comprehensive telephone appointment

Clinical Context
