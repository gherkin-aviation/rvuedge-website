95919 Quan puplmtry phy/qhp uni/bi

Medicine

Also known as: pupil testing, quantitative pupillometry

Quantitative pupillometry with interpretation and report; unilateral or bilateral.

Clinical Context

Objective measurement of pupil size and reactivity. Evaluates afferent pupillary defects, autonomic dysfunction, or subtle optic nerve disease.
