96446 Chemotx admn pertl cav implant

Medicine

Also known as: Intraperitoneal chemo, IP chemotherapy, Peritoneal port chemo

Administration of chemotherapy into peritoneal cavity via percutaneous implanted catheter or port. Chemotherapy agents are infused directly into the abdominal cavity space.

Clinical Context

Used for ovarian cancer, primary peritoneal cancer, and peritoneal carcinomatosis. Delivers high drug concentrations directly to abdominal organs while minimizing systemic toxicity.
