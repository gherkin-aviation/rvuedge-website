95933 Blink reflex test

Medicine

Also known as: blink reflex, corneal reflex test

Blink reflex test assessing the integrity of the trigeminal and facial nerves and their reflex arc.

In Plain Language

facial nerve test; reflex test

Clinical Context

Evaluates brainstem function and trigeminal-facial nerve pathway integrity in cases of facial nerve palsy, brainstem lesions, or polyneuropathy.
