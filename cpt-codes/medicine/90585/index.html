90585 Bcg vaccine percut

Medicine

Also known as: BCG vaccine, bacille Calmette-Guérin

Bacille Calmette-Guérin vaccine administered by percutaneous route for immunization against tuberculosis.

In Plain Language

TB vaccine; tuberculosis protection shot

Clinical Context

Live attenuated vaccine used in countries with high tuberculosis prevalence and selected high-risk populations in low-incidence areas.
