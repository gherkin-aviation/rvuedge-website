96921 Excimer lsr psriasis 250-500

Medicine Global 0d

Also known as: Excimer laser medium area, Mid-range excimer, Excimer 250-500 sqcm

Excimer laser treatment for psoriasis on body surface area between 250 and 500 square centimeters. Intermediate-sized lesion treatment.

Clinical Context

Used for moderate psoriasis involvement. Excimer laser provides targeted phototherapy for larger plaques without systemic effects.
