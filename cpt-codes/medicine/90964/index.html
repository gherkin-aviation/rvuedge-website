90964 Esrd home pt serv p mo 2-11

Medicine

Also known as: ESRD home dialysis pediatric, Home renal disease care, Pediatric home dialysis

End-stage renal disease services for home dialysis patient, monthly evaluation and management for patients ages 2-11 years.

Clinical Context

Comprehensive monthly nephrology services for end-stage renal disease patients ages 2-11 years managed at home with peritoneal or home hemodialysis.
