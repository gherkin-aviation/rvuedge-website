97032 application modality 1+estim each 15

Medicine

Also known as: Modality application, 15-minute modality, Therapeutic modality

Application of therapeutic modality including one or more electrical stimulation or similar modalities for each 15-minute period of treatment.

Clinical Context

Billable unit for therapeutic modality application. Common in physical therapy clinics for pain management and tissue healing.
