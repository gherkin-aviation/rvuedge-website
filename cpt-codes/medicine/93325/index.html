93325 Doppler echo color flow mapg

Medicine

Also known as: Color flow Doppler, color flow mapping, CFM echo

Doppler echocardiography with color flow mapping to visualize and assess blood flow patterns through cardiac chambers and across valves in two-dimensional anatomy.

In Plain Language

colored heart flow pictures; color blood flow imaging; flow visualization

Clinical Context

Essential component of comprehensive echocardiography, allowing real-time visualization of regurgitant jets, shunt flow, and flow turbulence. Critical for quantifying valve disease severity and detecting abnormal flow patterns.
