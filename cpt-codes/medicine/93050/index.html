93050 Artery pressure waveform analys

Medicine

Also known as: arterial waveform analysis, pulse wave analysis

Arterial pressure waveform analysis and determination of cardiovascular hemodynamic parameters.

Clinical Context

Noninvasive analysis of arterial pressure contours to assess vascular stiffness, augmentation index, and other hemodynamic parameters related to cardiovascular risk.
