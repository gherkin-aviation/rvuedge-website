93985 Dup-scan hemo complicated bi std

Medicine

Also known as: dialysis access duplex, fistula ultrasound, bilateral access assessment

Duplex ultrasound scan for hemodialysis access assessment performed bilaterally and completely to evaluate fistula or graft patency and hemodynamics.

Clinical Context

Used to assess arteriovenous fistula or graft function in dialysis patients, identify stenosis or thrombosis, and guide intervention timing.
