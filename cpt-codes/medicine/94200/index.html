94200 Lung function test (mbc/mvv)

Medicine

Also known as: maximum breathing capacity, maximum voluntary ventilation, MBC/MVV test

Lung function testing including measurement of maximum breathing capacity and maximum voluntary ventilation to assess ventilatory reserve.

Clinical Context

Used to assess ventilatory reserve and distinguish between pulmonary and cardiac causes of dyspnea, particularly in exercise evaluation.
