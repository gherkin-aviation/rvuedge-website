93591 Percutaneous transcath cls aortic

Medicine Global 0d

Also known as: Transcatheter aortic closure, aortic valve closure, catheter aortic intervention

Percutaneous transcatheter closure of aortic valve using catheter-based intervention for aortic stenosis or regurgitation.

In Plain Language

aortic valve repair with catheter; main valve closure procedure; catheter-based valve intervention

Clinical Context

Emerging technique for transcatheter aortic valve closure in specific indications. Transcatheter aortic valve replacement (TAVR) is established but distinct from closure procedures.
