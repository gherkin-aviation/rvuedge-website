99503 Home visit resp therapy

Medicine

Also known as: home respiratory therapy, pulmonary home care

Home visit service for respiratory therapy including monitoring, patient instruction, and equipment maintenance for chronic respiratory conditions.

Clinical Context

Used for home-based respiratory care including oxygen therapy management, nebulizer treatments, and monitoring of patients with chronic obstructive pulmonary disease.
