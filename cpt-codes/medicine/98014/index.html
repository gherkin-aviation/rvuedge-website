98014 Synch audio-only established mod 30

Medicine

Also known as: phone visit, telephone consultation, audio telehealth

Synchronous (real-time) audio-only interactive telehealth service between physician/qualified healthcare provider and established patient for moderate complexity care, up to 30 minutes.

Clinical Context

Used for real-time telephone consultations with established patients requiring moderate complexity.
