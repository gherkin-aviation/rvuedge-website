94645 Cont inhlj treatment each additional hour

Medicine +Add-on

Also known as: continuous inhalation additional hour, prolonged nebulizer continuation, each additional hour inhalation

Continuous inhalation treatment provided for each additional hour beyond the first hour to continue sustained medication delivery.

Clinical Context

Used as an add-on for each additional hour of continuous inhalation therapy beyond the first hour in status asthmaticus.
