92541 Spontaneous nystagmus test

Medicine

Also known as: spontaneous nystagmus test

Spontaneous nystagmus test with interpretation; detection and analysis of involuntary eye movements at rest.

Clinical Context

Used to detect abnormal spontaneous eye movements indicating central or peripheral vestibular pathology.
