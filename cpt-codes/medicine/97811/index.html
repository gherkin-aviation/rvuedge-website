97811 Acup 1/> without estim each add 15

Medicine +Add-on

Also known as: acupuncture add-on, acupuncture continuation, extended acupuncture

Acupuncture treatment with one or more needles without electrical stimulation for each additional 15-minute period beyond the initial treatment time.

Clinical Context

Used to report additional 15-minute increments of acupuncture without electrical stimulation beyond the initial period.
