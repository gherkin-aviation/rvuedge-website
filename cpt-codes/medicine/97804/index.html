97804 Medical nutrition group

Medicine

Also known as: group nutrition therapy, nutrition class, MNT group

Group medical nutrition therapy session where multiple patients receive nutrition education and counseling simultaneously from a registered dietitian.

In Plain Language

nutrition class with multiple patients; group nutrition education

Clinical Context

Used for group nutrition education programs such as diabetes management classes, weight loss groups, or cardiac nutrition education sessions.
