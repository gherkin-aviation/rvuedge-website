97028 Ultraviolet therapy

Medicine

Also known as: UV therapy, Ultraviolet therapy, Light therapy

Ultraviolet light therapy for skin conditions and superficial infections. Includes UV-A and UV-B therapeutic applications.

Clinical Context

Used for psoriasis, vitiligo, and certain infections. UV light modulates immune response and promotes skin healing.
