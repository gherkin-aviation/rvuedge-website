99026 In-hospital on call service

Medicine

Also known as: on-call service, in-house call, hospital coverage

Physician in-hospital on-call service responsibility (in-house) including availability, monitoring, and readiness to treat patients during specified hours.

Clinical Context

Used to report physician on-call coverage duties within the hospital facility for emergency or urgent patient management.
