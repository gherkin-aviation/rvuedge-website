92928 Prq transcatheter plmt ntrac st 1 lesion

Medicine Global 0d

Also known as: PTCA with stent, PCI with stent, coronary stenting single lesion

Percutaneous transluminal coronary angioplasty (PTCA) with coronary artery stent placement for a single intracoronary lesion.

In Plain Language

heart stent placement single vessel; metal tube insertion in artery

Clinical Context

Interventional procedure to open coronary narrowing and deploy an intracoronary stent to maintain patency, standard treatment for acute coronary syndromes and significant coronary stenosis.
