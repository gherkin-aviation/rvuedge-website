93930 Upper extremity study

Medicine

Also known as: upper extremity duplex, arm artery ultrasound, comprehensive upper extremity study

Duplex ultrasound scan of upper extremity arteries performed comprehensively on one arm to evaluate for arterial disease or access evaluation.

In Plain Language

arm blood vessel ultrasound; upper arm artery assessment

Clinical Context

Used to evaluate upper extremity ischemia, steal syndrome from dialysis access, or assess candidacy for arteriovenous fistula creation.
