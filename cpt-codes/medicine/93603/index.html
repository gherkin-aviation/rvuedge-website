93603 Right ventricular recording

Medicine Global 0d

Also known as: Right ventricular recording, ventricular electrogram, RV recording

Electrophysiology study with right ventricular recording to assess ventricular electrophysiology and conduction.

Clinical Context

Records ventricular electrogram to assess ventricular electrophysiology, diagnose ventricular arrhythmias, measure conduction properties, and evaluate ventricular function.
