93702 Bis xtracell fluid analysis

Medicine

Also known as: Bioelectrical impedance analysis, extracellular fluid analysis, BIS analysis

Bioelectrical impedance spectroscopy for extracellular fluid analysis and assessment of body composition and hydration status.

In Plain Language

body water composition measurement; fluid balance assessment; nutrition status evaluation

Clinical Context

Assesses hydration status and body composition in heart failure, dialysis patients, and nutritional assessment. Useful for monitoring fluid retention and guiding diuretic therapy.
