99500 Home visit prenatal

Medicine

Also known as: prenatal home visit, home obstetric visit

Home visit service for prenatal care, including assessment and education provided by physician or qualified healthcare professional.

In Plain Language

doctor visits you at home during pregnancy

Clinical Context

Used for home-based prenatal evaluation and monitoring in high-risk pregnancies or where office-based care is unavailable or unsafe.

RVU Information
