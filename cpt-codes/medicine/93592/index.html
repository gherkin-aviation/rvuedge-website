93592 Percutaneous transcath closure each

Medicine +Add-on

Also known as: Additional valve closure, multi-valve transcatheter closure, each additional valve intervention

Percutaneous transcatheter closure of each additional valve (tricuspid, pulmonary, or other) during comprehensive valvular intervention.

Clinical Context

Addresses multiple valve pathology requiring closure in complex cases. Allows comprehensive multi-valve intervention in single catheterization session.
