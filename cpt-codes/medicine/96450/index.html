96450 Chemotherapy into cns

Medicine Global 0d

Also known as: Intrathecal chemo, Central nervous system chemo, Spinal chemotherapy

Administration of chemotherapy agents by intrathecal, intraventricular, or intra-central nervous system injection directly into cerebrospinal fluid. Used to treat malignancies involving the brain or spinal cord.

Clinical Context

Indicated for acute leukemia prophylaxis, central nervous system lymphoma, and metastatic cancers to brain/meninges. Requires careful technique to deliver chemotherapy across blood-brain barrier.
