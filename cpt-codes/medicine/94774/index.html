94774 Pediatric home apnea mntr evnt rec

Medicine

Also known as: home apnea monitor with recording, apnea event monitor, pediatric apnea recorder

Pediatric home apnea monitor with event recording capability to detect and document apneic episodes during sleep or activity.

Clinical Context

Used in infants at risk for sudden infant death syndrome or with history of apparent life-threatening events to monitor for apneic episodes.
