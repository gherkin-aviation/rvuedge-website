91034 Gastroesophageal reflux test

Medicine Global 0d

Also known as: pH monitoring, acid reflux test, GERD study

Gastroesophageal reflux test to monitor and record acid exposure in the esophagus over an extended period.

Clinical Context

Extended monitoring study (typically 24-48 hours) to document frequency, duration, and severity of acid reflux episodes in patients with suspected gastroesophageal reflux disease.
