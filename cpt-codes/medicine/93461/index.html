93461 right and left hrt artery/ventricle angio

Medicine Global 0d

Also known as: Bilateral coronary and graft angiography, complete bypass evaluation, bilateral selective with grafts

Bilateral heart catheterization with selective coronary angiography of right and left systems including coronary artery bypass graft imaging.

In Plain Language

both heart arteries and bypass tube pictures; complete heart artery and graft imaging; full coronary and bypass study

Clinical Context

Comprehensive post-bypass evaluation assessing both native coronary systems and all coronary artery bypass grafts, determining graft patency and native disease progression. Most thorough evaluation for symptomatic post-bypass patients.
