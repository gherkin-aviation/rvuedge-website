93784 Ambl bp mntr with software

Medicine

Also known as: Ambulatory blood pressure monitoring, ABPM, 24-hour blood pressure

Ambulatory blood pressure monitoring with software analysis including report generation and interpretation.

Clinical Context

Measures blood pressure over 24 hours including nocturnal readings, detects white coat hypertension, masked hypertension, and assesses blood pressure variability. Essential for hypertension diagnosis and treatment monitoring.
