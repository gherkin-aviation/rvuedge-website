99509 Home visit day life activity

Medicine

Also known as: home ADL assistance, activities of daily living

Home visit service for activities of daily living, including assessment and assistance with self-care activities and personal hygiene.

Clinical Context

Used for home health aide visits providing non-skilled assistance with activities of daily living for patients unable to perform self-care independently.
