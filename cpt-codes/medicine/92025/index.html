92025 Cptrized corneal topography

Medicine

Also known as: corneal topography, corneal mapping, keratometry

Computerized corneal topography to map the surface curvature and shape of the cornea for refractive assessment.

In Plain Language

cornea shape map; cornea curve measurement

Clinical Context

Diagnostic imaging of corneal surface to evaluate keratoconus, astigmatism, contact lens fitting, and pre-refractive surgery planning.
