99050 Medical services after hours

Medicine

Also known as: after-hours service, evening service, extended hours

Medical service or evaluation provided outside normal business hours (after office hours) in the office or other outpatient setting.

Clinical Context

Used when medical services are provided outside standard office hours but in the office or outpatient facility.
