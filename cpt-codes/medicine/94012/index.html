94012 Spirmtry with brnchdil inf-2 yr

Medicine

Also known as: infant spirometry with bronchodilator, bronchodilator response test, pediatric bronchodilator response

Spirometry testing in infants and children up to 2 years old with inhaled bronchodilator medication to assess bronchial responsiveness and reversibility.

In Plain Language

baby breathing test with medication; infant bronchodilator response test

Clinical Context

Used to determine if airway obstruction is reversible and responsive to bronchodilator therapy in young children with suspected asthma or other obstructive conditions.
