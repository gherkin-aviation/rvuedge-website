97018 Paraffin bath therapy

Medicine

Also known as: Paraffin bath, Wax bath therapy, Paraffin therapy

Paraffin wax bath therapy for hands or feet for pain relief and joint mobility improvement. Involves immersion in heated paraffin wax.

Clinical Context

Used for arthritis, post-operative stiffness, and pain management in small joints. Heat penetrates tissues and provides sustained warmth.
