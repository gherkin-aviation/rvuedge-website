93571 Intravenous dop vel and/or press c flo first

Medicine +Add-on

Also known as: IV Doppler flow measurement, intravenous flow assessment, Doppler coronary flow

Intravenous Doppler velocity and pressure measurement with coronary flow assessment for initial vessel evaluation during congenital heart catheterization.

Clinical Context

Quantifies coronary flow reserve, evaluates flow limitations in anomalous coronaries, assesses flow patterns in congenital shunts, and guides interventional decisions.
