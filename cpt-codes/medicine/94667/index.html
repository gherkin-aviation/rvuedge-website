94667 Mnpj chest wall first

Medicine

Also known as: manual percussion therapy, chest wall percussion, percussion and vibration

Mechanical percussion and manipulation of chest wall on initial session to mobilize secretions and improve clearance in patients with retained secretions.

Clinical Context

Used in cystic fibrosis, bronchiectasis, and other conditions with excessive secretions to enhance airway clearance via manual techniques.
