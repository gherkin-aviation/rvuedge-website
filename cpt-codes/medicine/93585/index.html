93585 Vngrph chd azygs/hemiazygs

Medicine +Add-on

Also known as: Azygos vein imaging, azygos angiography, hemiazygos imaging

Ventriculography and angiography during congenital heart catheterization to visualize anomalous azygos and hemiazygos venous connections.

Clinical Context

Demonstrates anomalous azygos and hemiazygos venous return in heterotaxy syndrome and other complex congenital cases. Important for understanding systemic venous drainage patterns.
