94664 Demo and/or evaluation pt use inhaler

Medicine

Also known as: inhaler training, metered-dose inhaler demonstration, inhaler technique instruction

Demonstration and evaluation of patient technique for proper use of inhaler device including instruction on medication delivery and technique assessment.

Clinical Context

Used to ensure patients with asthma and chronic obstructive pulmonary disease can properly use inhalers for effective medication delivery.
