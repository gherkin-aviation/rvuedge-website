90999 Unlisted dialysis procedure

Medicine

Also known as: unlisted dialysis, miscellaneous dialysis, other renal procedure

Unlisted dialysis or end-stage renal disease related procedure, used when a specific dialysis service code is not available.

Clinical Context

Used for reporting dialysis-related procedures that do not have a specific designated code. Requires detailed documentation and modifier.
