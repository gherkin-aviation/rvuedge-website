94618 Pulmonary stress testing

Medicine

Also known as: pulmonary exercise test, stress test without ECG, pulmonary stress testing

Pulmonary stress testing to assess pulmonary and cardiovascular response to exercise without electrocardiogram monitoring.

In Plain Language

exercise lung function test; breathing during exercise test

Clinical Context

Used to assess pulmonary reserve and response to exercise in patients with dyspnea or suspected exercise limitation without concurrent cardiac monitoring.
