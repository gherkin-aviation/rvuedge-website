90734 Menacwyd/menacwycrm vacc intramuscular

Medicine

Also known as: Menactra, Menveo, MenACWY conjugate vaccine

Meningococcal vaccine, conjugate, serogroups A, C, W-135, and Y (MenACWY) or meningococcal ACWY conjugate vaccine administered intramuscularly.

Clinical Context

Administered to adolescents and young adults ages 11-21 years, or older at-risk groups for prevention of meningococcal disease caused by serogroups A, C, W-135, and Y.
