93893 Tcd std intracranial artery ven-art shnt

Medicine

Also known as: TCD arteriovenous shunt study, TCD shunt detection, right-to-left shunt TCD

Transcranial Doppler ultrasound standard study to assess intracranial arteries with evaluation of arteriovenous shunting patterns.

Clinical Context

Used to diagnose and characterize right-to-left shunts (patent foramen ovale, atrial septal defect) as potential stroke risk factors.
