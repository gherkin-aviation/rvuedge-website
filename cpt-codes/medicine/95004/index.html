95004 Percutaneous tests with alrgnc xtrcs

Medicine

Also known as: skin allergy test, percutaneous allergy test, scratch test

Percutaneous allergy testing with allergenic extracts to identify specific allergens responsible for patient's allergic reactions.

Clinical Context

Used to identify specific allergens in patients with allergic rhinitis, asthma, food allergies, or atopic dermatitis.
