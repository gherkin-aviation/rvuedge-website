93318 Echo transesophageal intraop

Medicine

Also known as: Intraoperative TEE, intraop echo, surgical TEE

Transesophageal echocardiography performed during surgery, providing intraoperative real-time ultrasound imaging of cardiac structures from within the esophagus for guidance during cardiac procedures.

In Plain Language

heart ultrasound during surgery; live heart imaging in operating room; surgical heart monitoring

Clinical Context

Essential during cardiac surgery to assess cardiac function, guide interventions, verify repair adequacy, and detect complications in real time. Used extensively in valve surgery, congenital heart repair, and coronary artery bypass grafting.
