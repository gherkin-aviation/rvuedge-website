94775 Pediatric home apnea mntr att only

Medicine

Also known as: home apnea monitor attachment, apnea monitor setup, apnea surveillance monitor

Pediatric home apnea monitor attachment only without event recording or analysis for continuous apnea surveillance.

In Plain Language

apnea monitor installation and setup; baby breathing monitor connection

Clinical Context

Used to establish apnea monitoring in at-risk infants where the primary focus is equipment attachment and patient/family initiation.
