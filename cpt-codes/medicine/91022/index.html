91022 Duodenal motility study

Medicine Global 0d

Also known as: duodenal manometry, duodenal motility test, small bowel function study

Duodenal motility study to evaluate the muscular function and coordination of the proximal small intestine.

Clinical Context

Diagnostic study to evaluate suspected duodenal dysmotility, obstruction, or disorders of small bowel coordination. Measures pressure and contractions in the duodenum.
