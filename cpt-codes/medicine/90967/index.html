90967 Esrd service pr day pt <2

Medicine

Also known as: ESRD per diem services, Daily renal disease care, Per diem dialysis

End-stage renal disease services, per diem evaluation and management of patient for each day for patients under 2 years old.

Clinical Context

Nephrology services for end-stage renal disease patients billed on a daily basis for partial month or variable frequency treatment.
