Medicine Global 0d

Percutaneous transluminal coronary angioplasty (PTCA) with coronary atherectomy and stent placement with coronary angiography of a single artery.

In Plain Language

plaque removal and stent placement single vessel

Clinical Context

Combined interventional strategy using atherectomy to debulk lesion followed by stent placement, particularly effective for ostial lesions, bifurcation lesions, and heavily calcified stenosis.
