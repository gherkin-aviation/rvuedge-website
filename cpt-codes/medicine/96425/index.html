96425 Chemotherapy infusion method

Medicine

Also known as: IV chemo infusion, IV chemotherapy push

Administration of intravenous chemotherapy infusion technique lasting 15 minutes or longer by qualified healthcare personnel. This code encompasses the clinical work of establishing and managing chemotherapy delivery.

Clinical Context

Used for patients receiving cancer chemotherapy agents delivered intravenously. This includes setup, monitoring, and documentation of the infusion process.
