93452 Left hrt catheter with ventrclgrphy

Medicine Global 0d

Also known as: Left heart cath, LHC, left ventriculography

Left heart catheterization with ventriculography, measuring left ventricular pressures, assessing ventricular function, and evaluating mitral regurgitation through contrast injection.

In Plain Language

left heart tube with fluid pictures; left heart pressure and pumping test; heart squeeze measurement

Clinical Context

Used to assess left ventricular function, evaluate valvular disease (especially mitral regurgitation), measure cardiac output, and detect wall motion abnormalities. Standard component of diagnostic cardiac catheterization in suspected structural heart disease.
