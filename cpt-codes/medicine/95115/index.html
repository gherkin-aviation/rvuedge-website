95115 Immunotherapy one injection

Medicine

Also known as: allergy shot, immunotherapy injection, antigen injection

Allergen immunotherapy (allergy injection) administered by injection; single dose.

Clinical Context

Used to treat allergic rhinitis, allergic asthma, and insect sting allergies. Reduces patient sensitivity to specific allergens through graduated exposure.
