96902 Mcrscp xm hair pluck/clipped

Medicine

Also known as: Hair pluck, Hair pull test, Hair microscopy

Microscopic examination and removal of hair specimens by plucking or clipping for diagnostic evaluation. Used for hair shaft analysis and follicle assessment.

Clinical Context

Used to diagnose alopecia, telogen effluvium, and other hair disorders. Microscopic evaluation helps determine hair cycle phase and pathology.
