92937 Prq trluml revsc cab grf 1

Medicine Global 0d

Also known as: SVG PCI, bypass graft intervention, graft angioplasty

Percutaneous transluminal coronary angioplasty (PTCA) with revascularization of a saphenous vein coronary artery bypass graft.

Clinical Context

Revascularization of degenerated saphenous vein grafts in patients with prior coronary artery bypass surgery, addressing graft stenosis or occlusion causing recurrent angina or acute coronary syndrome.
