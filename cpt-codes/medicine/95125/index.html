95125 Immunotherapy 2/> injections

Medicine

Also known as: allergy shots, multi-antigen therapy, combination immunotherapy

Allergen immunotherapy (allergy injection) administered by injection; two or more injections given in single visit.

Clinical Context

Typical maintenance phase treatment when patients receive multiple allergen injections simultaneously, treating multiple allergen sensitivities.
