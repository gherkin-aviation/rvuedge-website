99504 Home visit mech ventilator

Medicine

Also known as: ventilator home care, mechanical ventilation visit

Home visit service for patient on mechanical ventilator, including assessment, monitoring, maintenance, and troubleshooting of ventilator equipment.

Clinical Context

Used for home-based management of ventilator-dependent patients, including equipment monitoring, weaning assessments, and troubleshooting.
