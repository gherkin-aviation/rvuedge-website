94015 Patient recorded spirometry

Medicine

Also known as: uninterpreted home spirometry, patient-recorded spirometry only, home spirometry without review

Patient-recorded spirometry performed by the patient without physician or qualified provider interpretation or analysis.

Clinical Context

Used when patients perform home spirometry for self-monitoring purposes without requiring formal interpretation or clinical assessment.
