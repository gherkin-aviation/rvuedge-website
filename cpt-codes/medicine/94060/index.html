94060 Evaluation of wheezing

Medicine

Also known as: spirometry for wheezing, asthma evaluation spirometry, obstruction evaluation

Spirometry testing for evaluation of wheezing to assess for obstructive airway disease and determine severity of airflow obstruction.

Clinical Context

Used to evaluate patients presenting with wheezing to determine if obstruction is present and to quantify severity for diagnosis and treatment planning.
