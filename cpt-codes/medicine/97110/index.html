97110 Therapeutic exercises

Medicine

Also known as: Therapeutic exercise, PT exercises, Rehab exercises

Therapeutic exercises performed in clinical or home setting to improve strength, endurance, flexibility, and functional mobility.

Clinical Context

Core physical therapy intervention for various conditions including post-operative rehabilitation, neurological disorders, and orthopedic injuries.
