99601 Home nfs visit <2 hours

Medicine

Also known as: home nursing brief, home health visit <2 hrs

Home nursing service visit of less than 2 hours duration, including assessment, monitoring, and patient education provided by registered nurse.

In Plain Language

nurse visits your home for short health visit

Clinical Context

Used for home nursing visits providing skilled assessment, education, and monitoring for patients with acute or chronic conditions.
