95120 Immunotherapy one injection

Medicine

Also known as: immunotherapy injection, antigen injection

Allergen immunotherapy (allergy injection) administered by injection; single dose, non-allergenic extract.

In Plain Language

allergy injection; immunotherapy treatment

Clinical Context

Used for pre-seasonal allergy prevention and maintenance dosing. May include single or mixed allergen extracts.
