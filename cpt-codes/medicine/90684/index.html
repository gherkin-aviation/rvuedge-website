90684 Pcv21 vaccine intramuscular

Medicine

Also known as: Pneumococcal conjugate vaccine, PCV21, Pneumococcal 21-valent

Pneumococcal vaccine, 21-valent (PCV21), administered intramuscularly for prevention of pneumococcal infections caused by 21 serotypes.

In Plain Language

Pneumonia vaccine; Pneumococcal shot

Clinical Context

Administered to infants and children for prevention of invasive pneumococcal disease including meningitis, bacteremia, and pneumonia caused by prevalent serotypes.
