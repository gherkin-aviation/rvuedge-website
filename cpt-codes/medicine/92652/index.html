92652 Aep thrshld established mlt freq i & r

Medicine

Also known as: AEP threshold test, multi-frequency AEP

Auditory evoked potential (AEP) testing with threshold estimation across multiple frequencies including interpretation and reporting.

Clinical Context

Determines minimum sound intensities detected across different frequencies, essential for diagnosing hearing loss patterns and severity, particularly in neonates and children with communication disorders.
