99195 Phlebotomy

Medicine

Also known as: phlebotomy, blood draw, venipuncture

Phlebotomy service for collection of blood samples from patients for laboratory testing and analysis.

In Plain Language

blood sample collection

Clinical Context

Used for phlebotomy services when blood collection is performed separately from other visit services.
