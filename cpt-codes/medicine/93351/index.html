93351 Stress tte complete

Medicine

Also known as: Complete stress echo, full stress TTE, comprehensive stress echo

Complete stress echocardiography performed with transthoracic ultrasound, including baseline and stress imaging with comprehensive assessment of systolic and diastolic function.

In Plain Language

complete heart stress test with ultrasound; full exercise heart imaging; comprehensive stress study

Clinical Context

Provides comprehensive ischemia detection with both baseline and stress wall motion assessment, diastolic function evaluation, and hemodynamic response. Preferred for thorough cardiac functional assessment in suspected coronary disease or valvular dysfunction.
