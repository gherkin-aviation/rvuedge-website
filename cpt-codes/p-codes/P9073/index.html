P9073 Platelets pheresis path redu

P Codes

Also known as: Pathogen-reduced apheresis platelets, PR pheresis plt

In Plain Language

Treated single-donor platelets

Clinical Context

Single-donor platelets with pathogen reduction technology for hemostasis with reduced infection transmission risk.
