P9046 Albumin (human), 25%, 20 milliliters

P Codes

Also known as: Albumin 25%, 20 ml, HSA 25%

Human serum albumin, 25 percent concentration in 20 milliliter volume for intravenous infusion.

In Plain Language

Concentrated protein infusion

Clinical Context

Hyper-concentrated albumin for severe hypoalbuminemia and specific volume restoration with minimal fluid.

RVU Information

CPT P9046 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.
