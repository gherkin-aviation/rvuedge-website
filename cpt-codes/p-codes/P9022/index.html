P9022 Washed red blood cells unit

P-Codes

Also known as: Washed RBCs, Saline-washed blood

Washed red blood cells with plasma removed through centrifugation, reducing allergens and reactions.

Clinical Context

Used for patients with severe allergic reactions to transfusions or IgA deficiency. Reduces transfusion reaction risk.
