P9051 Blood, l/r, cmv-neg

P-Codes

Also known as: CMV-negative leukoreduced blood, L/R CMV-neg

Blood unit with leukocytes reduced and tested negative for cytomegalovirus (CMV).

Clinical Context

Used in CMV-seronegative immunocompromised patients to prevent CMV transmission via transfusion.
