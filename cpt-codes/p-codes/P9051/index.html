P9051 Blood, l/r, cmv-neg

P Codes

Also known as: CMV-negative leukoreduced blood, L/R CMV-neg

Blood unit with leukocytes reduced and tested negative for cytomegalovirus (CMV).

In Plain Language

Safe blood for CMV-sensitive patients

Clinical Context

Used in CMV-seronegative immunocompromised patients to prevent CMV transmission via transfusion.

RVU Information

CPT P9051 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

P-codes cover pathology screening and blood product services. Documentation must include the ordering provider, clinical indication, and specimen type. For blood products, include the product type, volume, and transfusion details.
