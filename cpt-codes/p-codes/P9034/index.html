P9034 Platelets, pheresis

P-Codes

Also known as: Apheresis platelets, Pheresis platelets

Platelet unit collected via apheresis from a single donor, providing high platelet concentration.

Clinical Context

Single-donor apheresis collection reduces transfusion reactions and alloimmunization compared to pooled platelets.
