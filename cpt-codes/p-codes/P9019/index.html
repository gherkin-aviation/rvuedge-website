P9019 Platelets, each unit

P-Codes

Also known as: Platelet unit, Plt transfusion

Platelet unit from apheresis collection, containing platelets separated from whole blood.

Clinical Context

Used in thrombocytopenia to reduce bleeding risk. Standard apheresis collection improves hemostasis.
