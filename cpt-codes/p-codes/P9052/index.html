P9052 Platelets, hla-m, l/r, unit

P Codes

Also known as: HLA-matched leukoreduced platelets, HLA-M L/R plt

Platelet unit with human leukocyte antigen (HLA) matching and leukocytes reduced.

In Plain Language

Matched immune platelets

Clinical Context

Used in alloimmunized patients to prevent platelet transfusion refractoriness through HLA-compatible selection.

RVU Information

CPT P9052 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation
