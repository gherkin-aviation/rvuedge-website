P9054 Blood, l/r, froz/degly/wash

P-Codes

Also known as: Processed RBCs, Frozen/degly/wash RBC

Red blood cell unit that has been frozen, deglycerolized, and/or washed to remove additives.

Clinical Context

Multiple processing steps reduce contaminants, plasma proteins, and reactions for highly sensitized patients.
