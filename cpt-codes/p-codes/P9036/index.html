P9036 Platelet pheresis irradiated

P-Codes

Also known as: Irradiated apheresis platelets, Irrad pheresis plt

Apheresis-collected platelet unit treated with ionizing radiation to prevent transfusion-associated graft-versus-host disease.

Clinical Context

Single-donor apheresis platelets with irradiation for immunocompromised patients requiring GVHD prevention.
