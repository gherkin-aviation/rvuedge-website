P9040 Rbc leukoreduced irradiated

P-Codes

Also known as: Leukoreduced irradiated RBCs, L/R irrad red cells

Red blood cell unit with both leukocytes reduced and irradiated for dual safety.

Clinical Context

Combines leukoreduction (fewer reactions) with irradiation (GVHD prevention) for severely immunocompromised patients.
