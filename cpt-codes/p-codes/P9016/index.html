P9016 Rbc leukocytes reduced

P-Codes

Also known as: Leukoreduced RBCs, Filtered red cells

Red blood cells with leukocytes (white blood cells) reduced through filtration or centrifugation.

Clinical Context

Reduces febrile non-hemolytic transfusion reactions and may lower infection transmission risk. Standard in many transfusion protocols.
