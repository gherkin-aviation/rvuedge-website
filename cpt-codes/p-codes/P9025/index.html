P9025 Plasma cryo redu path each

P Codes

Also known as: Cryo-reduced plasma, Cryoprecipitate-poor plasma

Plasma with cryoprecipitate removed but containing other clotting factors and proteins.

In Plain Language

Plasma without fibrinogen

Clinical Context

Used when fibrinogen replacement alone is not needed but other coagulation factors are required.

RVU Information

CPT P9025 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

P-codes cover pathology screening and blood product services. Documentation must include the ordering provider, clinical indication, and specimen type. For blood products, include the product type, volume, and transfusion details.
