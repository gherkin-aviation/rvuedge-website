P9017 Plasma 1 donor frz with in 8 hour

P-Codes

Also known as: Fresh frozen plasma, FFP single donor

Fresh frozen plasma from a single donor, frozen within 8 hours of collection.

Clinical Context

Contains all clotting factors for treating coagulopathy and massive transfusion. Requires thawing before transfusion.
