P9035 Platelet pheres leukoreduced

P Codes

Also known as: Leukoreduced apheresis platelets, L/R pheresis plt

Apheresis-collected platelet unit with leukocytes reduced through filtration.

In Plain Language

Cleaned single-donor platelets

Clinical Context

Combines apheresis (single donor) with leukoreduction (fewer reactions) for optimal hemostasis and safety.

RVU Information

CPT P9035 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.
