P9612 Catheterize for urine specimen

P-Codes

Also known as: Straight catheterization, Cath for specimen

Catheterization of the urinary bladder to obtain a sterile urine specimen for laboratory analysis.

Clinical Context

Sterile urinary catheterization for obtaining uncontaminated urine specimens, especially for culture and sensitivity testing.
