G0402 Initial preventive examination

G-Codes

Also known as: welcome visit, initial preventive exam, IPPE, annual physical

Initial preventive physical examination for Medicare beneficiary including comprehensive history, examination, and preventive screenings.

Clinical Context

Covered preventive service for new Medicare beneficiaries within first 12 months of Part B eligibility. Establishes baseline health status.
