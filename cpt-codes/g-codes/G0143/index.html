G0143 Scr c/v cyto,thinlayer,rescr

G Codes

Also known as: thin-layer with automated rescreen, double-screening cytology

Cervical and vaginal cytology screening using thin-layer preparation with rescreening by an automated system, providing secondary quality assurance.

In Plain Language

Two-step cervical cancer screening; Double-checked Pap test

Clinical Context

Quality assurance approach to cytology using thin-layer preparation with automated rescreening to identify previously missed abnormalities.

RVU Information

CPT G0143 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
