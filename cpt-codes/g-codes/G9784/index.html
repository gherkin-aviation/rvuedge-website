G Codes

Also known as: second pathology opinion

HCPCS code documenting pathologist or dermatologist providing second opinion on biopsy.

In Plain Language

Clinical Context

Documents second opinion consultation by pathologist or dermatologist for challenging or critical biopsy cases in quality measure or case management.
