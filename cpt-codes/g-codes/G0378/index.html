G0378 Hospital observation per hour

G Codes

Also known as: hospital observation per hour, obs hour, observation unit billing

Hospital observation care billing unit representing hourly rate for observation status patients not admitted as inpatient.

In Plain Language

hospital short-term monitoring visit

Clinical Context

Used for patients placed in observation status for diagnostic workup or recovery monitoring without hospital admission. Billed in hourly units.

RVU Information

CPT G0378 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation
