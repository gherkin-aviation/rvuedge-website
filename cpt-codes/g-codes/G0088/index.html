G0088 Adm intravenous drug first home visit

G-Codes

Also known as: first IV drug administration, initial home IV setup

Administration of intravenous drug therapy during the first home health nursing visit, including initial setup, patient/caregiver instruction, and treatment initiation.

Clinical Context

Used for initial IV drug administration in home setting on the first nursing visit. Includes IV access initiation, drug setup, patient education, and safety protocol establishment.
