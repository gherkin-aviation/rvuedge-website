G0041 Pt/pattern decln referral

G Codes

Also known as: declined therapy referral, patient refused referral

Patient or patient's representative declined referral to rehabilitation, therapy, or other recommended services.

In Plain Language

patient turned down referral recommendation

Clinical Context

Quality measure code documenting patient choice to decline a recommended referral.

RVU Information

CPT G0041 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation
