G9987 Bpci advanced in home visit

G-Codes

Also known as: BPCI home visit, home-based care integration

Behavioral and Primary Care Integration plus in-home visit as part of an accountable care model initiative.

Clinical Context

Used for patients enrolled in Behavioral and Primary Care Integration Advanced program receiving coordinated care combining behavioral health and primary care services in home settings.
