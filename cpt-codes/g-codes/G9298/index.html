G9298 Evaluation risk vte card 30d prior

G Codes

Also known as: VTE risk assessment, thromboembolism screening

Evaluation of venous thromboembolism risk in cardiac patient was documented within 30 days prior to procedure.

In Plain Language

blood clot risk check before heart procedure; thrombosis screening

Clinical Context

Quality measure ensuring cardiac patients receive pre-operative assessment for venous thromboembolism risk, critical for preventing postoperative complications.

RVU Information
