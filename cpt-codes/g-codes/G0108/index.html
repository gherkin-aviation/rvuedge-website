G0108 Diab manage trn per indiv

G-Codes

Also known as: diabetes education, diabetes self-management training

Diabetes management training and education provided individually to a patient, including nutritional counseling, self-management skills, and disease education per visit.

Clinical Context

Structured diabetes education for newly diagnosed or poorly controlled diabetic patients. Includes instruction in glucose monitoring, medication use, nutrition, exercise, and complication prevention.
