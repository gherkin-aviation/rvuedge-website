G0559 Unrelat prac follow up visit

G-Codes

Also known as: unrelated follow-up visit, separate visit unrelated, distinct problem visit

Unrelated practice follow-up visit by same provider to patient for management unrelated to recent procedure.

Clinical Context

Office visit during post-operative period or after procedure but addressing separate, unrelated clinical problem.
