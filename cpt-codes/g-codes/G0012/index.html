G0012 Injection of hiv prep drug

G-Codes

Also known as: PrEP injection, HIV prevention injection, cabotegravir injection

Injection of human immunodeficiency virus (HIV) pre-exposure prophylaxis (PrEP) medication such as cabotegravir for HIV prevention in at-risk individuals.

Clinical Context

Used for administration of injectable long-acting antiretroviral pre-exposure prophylaxis medications for HIV prevention in individuals at substantial risk of acquisition.
