G0278 Iliac artery angio,cardiac catheter

G-Codes

Also known as: iliac angiogram, iliofemoral angiography, cardiac cath with iliac angio

Iliac artery angiography performed during cardiac catheterization to visualize blood vessels in the iliac region for diagnostic or interventional purposes.

Clinical Context

Used during cardiac catheterization procedures when access through iliac vessels is required or when iliac artery disease is suspected. Common in peripheral vascular interventions.
