G0069 Adm square infusion drug in home

G-Codes

Also known as: home subcutaneous therapy, subQ home infusion

Administration of subcutaneous infusion drug therapy in the patient's home, including setup, monitoring, and ongoing management of subcutaneous medication delivery.

Clinical Context

Used for patients requiring home-based subcutaneous medication therapy such as biologics, immunoglobulins, or specialty drugs. Includes injection training, administration, and monitoring.
