G8395 Lvef>=40% doc normal or mild

G Codes

Also known as: normal LVEF, preserved ejection fraction, EF >= 40%

Documentation indicator that left ventricular ejection fraction is greater than or equal to 40 percent and is documented as normal or mildly reduced.

In Plain Language

Heart pumping power is normal or mildly reduced; Good heart function documented

Clinical Context

Used in heart failure quality measures to document preserved or mildly reduced left ventricular ejection fraction indicating better systolic function.

RVU Information

CPT G8395 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

G-codes are CMS-specific HCPCS codes for services not covered by standard CPT. Documentation requirements follow the same standards as the equivalent CPT service. Check Medicare LCD/NCD policies for coverage criteria.
