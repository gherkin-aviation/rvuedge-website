G0181 Home health care supervision

G-Codes

Also known as: home health supervision, HHA oversight

Home health agency care supervision including physician oversight and coordination of skilled nursing, therapy, and support services.

Clinical Context

Nursing or physician supervision of home health services ensuring care coordination, quality assurance, and appropriate service utilization for complex patients.
