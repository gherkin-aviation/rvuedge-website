G9395 Ini phq9 >9 no remiss >=5

G Codes

Also known as: persistent depression, PHQ-9 no improvement

Initial Patient Health Questionnaire-9 score greater than 9 with no remission at follow-up assessment, score greater than or equal to 5.

In Plain Language

depression didn't improve; depression screening shows little improvement

Clinical Context

Quality measure tracking depression management when initial PHQ-9 score indicates moderate-to-severe depression but follow-up assessment shows persistent depression (score >= 5), indicating potential need for treatment adjustment.

RVU Information

CPT G9395 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation
