G9312 Surgical site infection

G Codes

Also known as: surgical site infection, postoperative wound infection

Surgical site infection developed within 30 days of surgery.

In Plain Language

incision got infected; wound infection after surgery

Clinical Context

Quality metric tracking adverse outcome of surgical site infection within 30 days postoperatively, indicates need for review of infection prevention practices.
