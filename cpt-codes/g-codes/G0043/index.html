G0043 Pt mech pros ht valv

G Codes

Also known as: mechanical heart valve patient, prosthetic valve quality code

Patient with mechanical prosthetic heart valve, used for quality measure stratification and anticoagulation management reporting.

In Plain Language

patient has a mechanical heart valve replacement

Clinical Context

Quality measure code identifying patients with mechanical valves who require lifelong anticoagulation with warfarin.

RVU Information

CPT G0043 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
