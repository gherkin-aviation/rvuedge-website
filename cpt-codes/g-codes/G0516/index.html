G0516 Insert drug del implant, >=4

G-Codes Global 0d

Also known as: implant insertion, subdermal drug implant, medication implant placement

Insertion of drug delivery implant or device (subdermal, intradermal, or intramuscular) containing 4 or more implants.

Clinical Context

Surgical placement of medication-containing implants for sustained drug delivery, including contraceptive implants, hormone therapy, or pain management.
