G2011 Alcohol/sub misuse assess

G-Codes

Also known as: Substance use screening, Addiction assessment

HCPCS code for alcohol or substance misuse assessment. Evaluates patient substance use patterns and risk factors.

Clinical Context

Used in primary care and mental health settings to assess alcohol and substance use disorders. Important for identifying patients needing addiction treatment.
