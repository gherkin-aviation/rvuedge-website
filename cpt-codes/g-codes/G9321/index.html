G Codes

In Plain Language

previous CT or nuclear scan found in records; prior imaging documented

Clinical Context

Quality measure for imaging appropriateness tracking when prior computed tomography or nuclear medicine studies are identified and documented within 12 months to reduce unnecessary repeat imaging.
