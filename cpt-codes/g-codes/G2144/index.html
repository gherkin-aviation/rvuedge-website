G2144 Fs odi 6-20wk postoperative <= 22

G Codes

Also known as: Oswestry Disability Index, ODI assessment

HCPCS code documenting Functional Status Oswestry Disability Index score at 6-20 weeks post-operatively, with improvement of 22 or less. Spine surgery outcome.

In Plain Language

Disability score minimal improvement early; Limited early recovery

Clinical Context

Used in spine surgery outcome tracking at 6-20 weeks. Documents minimal functional improvement.
