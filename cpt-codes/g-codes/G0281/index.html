G0281 Elec stim unattend for press

G Codes

Also known as: e-stim, electrical stim, unattended ES

Unattended electrical stimulation therapy for pressure ulcer management, where the device operates without direct practitioner oversight during treatment.

In Plain Language

electrical muscle stimulation; electric therapy for bedsores

Clinical Context

Used in pressure ulcer prevention and healing. Electrical stimulation enhances blood flow and promotes tissue repair. Unattended means patient uses device independently after setup.
