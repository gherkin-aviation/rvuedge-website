G0397 Alcohol/subs interv >30 minutes

G-Codes

Also known as: substance abuse intervention >30, alcohol counseling extended

Alcohol and substance abuse intervention and counseling session lasting more than 30 minutes for comprehensive treatment planning.

Clinical Context

Extended intervention for complex substance abuse cases requiring detailed assessment, treatment planning, and patient education.
