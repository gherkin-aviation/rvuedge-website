G0532 Take home supp nasal spray

G Codes

Also known as: naloxone nasal spray, take-home Narcan, opioid overdose kit

Take-home supply of supplemental nasal spray medication for opioid use disorder management.

In Plain Language

Take-home overdose reversal spray; Emergency nasal medication for overdose; Take-home rescue medication

Clinical Context

Supply of intranasal naloxone for opioid use disorder patients and caregivers to reverse opioid overdose in emergency situations.

RVU Information

CPT G0532 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
