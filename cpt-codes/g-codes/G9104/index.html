G9104 Onc diagnosis gastric unknown not otherwise specified

G-Codes

Also known as: unknown gastric cancer status, unstaged gastric cancer

Oncology diagnosis for gastric cancer where the status or extent of disease is unknown.

Clinical Context

Used when adequate staging or follow-up information is unavailable for gastric cancer patients.
