G9424 Site no lung or lung cx

G-Codes

Also known as: non-cancer specimen, benign pathology

Specimen site is not lung or non-lung cancer, representing benign or non-malignant pathology.

Clinical Context

Quality measure exclusion when pathology specimen is benign or non-malignant, making cancer histologic classification requirement inapplicable.
