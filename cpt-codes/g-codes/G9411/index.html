G9411 No admit with in 180d req remov

G Codes

Also known as: stent success, no stent revision needed

No admission to hospital within 180 days requiring removal or revision of percutaneous coronary intervention stent.

In Plain Language

stent stayed in place without problems; no need for stent removal

Clinical Context

Quality measure tracking positive outcome when percutaneous coronary intervention stent remains in place without requiring removal or revision within 180-day follow-up period.

RVU Information

CPT G9411 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
