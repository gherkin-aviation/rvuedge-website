G2173 Uri w comorb 12m oth diagnosis

G Codes

Also known as: URI with comorbidity, Complex respiratory infection

HCPCS code documenting upper respiratory infection with comorbid diagnosis 12 months before episode. URI with complex medical history.

In Plain Language

Cold or flu with other health problems; Complex respiratory infection

Clinical Context

Used in quality measurement to identify URI in patients with multiple chronic conditions. Important for appropriate antibiotic stewardship.
