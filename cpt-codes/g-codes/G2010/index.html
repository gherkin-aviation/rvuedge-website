G2010 Remot image submit by pt

G-Codes

Also known as: Telehealth image, Remote monitoring

HCPCS code for remote image submission by patient using digital technology. Used for remote patient monitoring and telemedicine.

Clinical Context

Used when patients submit images or photos remotely for clinical assessment. Common in dermatology, wound care, and chronic disease monitoring.
