G0459 Telehealth inpt pharmaceutical management

G Codes

Also known as: telehealth pharm consult, remote pharm management, virtual pharmacy consult

Telehealth inpatient pharmacist medication management consultation using real-time audio-visual technology for patients admitted to hospital or other acute care facility.

In Plain Language

Video pharmacist consultation; Remote medication counseling; Virtual pharmacy advice

Clinical Context

Used when a pharmacist provides medication management services to hospitalized patients via telehealth. Common for complex medication regimens, drug interactions, or when onsite pharmacist is unavailable.
