G0406 Inpt/tele follow up 15

G-Codes

Also known as: inpt/telehealth f/u 15, follow-up visit 15 min

Hospital inpatient or telehealth follow-up evaluation and management service lasting approximately 15 minutes.

Clinical Context

Brief follow-up evaluation for hospitalized patient or via telehealth to assess clinical progress and adjust treatment plans.
