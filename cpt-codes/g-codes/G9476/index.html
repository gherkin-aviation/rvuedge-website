G9476 Volun service at hospice

G Codes

Also known as: hospice volunteers, volunteer care services

Volunteer services were provided at hospice.

In Plain Language

volunteers helped care for patients at end-of-life; volunteer support at hospice

Clinical Context

Quality measure tracking volunteer services at hospice, providing companionship, respite care, and practical support enhancing quality of end-of-life care.
