G0081 Care man h v external pt 20 mi

G-Codes

Also known as: care management follow-up visit, established patient home visit

Care management home visit for an established patient lasting approximately 20 minutes, including assessment, care plan review, and ongoing care coordination and management.

Clinical Context

Follow-up home visit for established patients in care management program. Used for ongoing assessment, plan evaluation, medication review, and care coordination.
