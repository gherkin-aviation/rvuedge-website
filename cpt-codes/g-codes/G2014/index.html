G2014 Post-d/c care plan overs 30m

G-Codes

Also known as: Care plan oversight, Post-discharge coordination

HCPCS code for post-discharge care plan oversight by physician or other qualified healthcare professional lasting 30 minutes. Coordination of home health services.

Clinical Context

Used for physician oversight of post-discharge care coordination. Includes review of home health services, medication reconciliation, and care plan updates.
