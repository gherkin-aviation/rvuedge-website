G0307 Cbc without platelet

G Codes

Also known as: CBC no platelets, CBC without diff or platelets

Complete blood count without platelet count and without differential white blood cell count, providing basic cell count assessment.

In Plain Language

basic blood cell count

Clinical Context

Simplified blood count useful for screening or monitoring when differential and platelet count are not indicated.

RVU Information

CPT G0307 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
