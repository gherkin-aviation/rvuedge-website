G9157 Transesoph doppl cardiac mon

G-Codes

Also known as: TEE with Doppler, transesophageal echo Doppler

Transesophageal echocardiography with continuous Doppler monitoring of cardiac structure and function.

Clinical Context

Advanced echocardiographic procedure using endoscopic ultrasound probe positioned in the esophagus to obtain detailed cardiac imaging with Doppler analysis.
