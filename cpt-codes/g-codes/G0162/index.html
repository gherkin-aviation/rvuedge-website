G0162 Hhc rn evaluation and management plan svs, 15 minutes

G-Codes

Also known as: home health RN E&M, nurse care plan visit

Home health care registered nurse evaluation and management for care plan services, provided in 15-minute increments, including assessment and plan supervision.

Clinical Context

Skilled nursing evaluation and management in home setting for care plan development and monitoring. Includes assessment, teaching, and coordination of home services.
