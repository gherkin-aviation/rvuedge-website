G8946 Mibm but no diagnosis of breast ca

G Codes

Also known as: molecular imaging without breast cancer, imaging without diagnosis

Performance measure indicating patient underwent molecular imaging (MIBM) but does not have documented diagnosis of breast cancer.

In Plain Language

special imaging was done but cancer was not found; breast imaging performed without cancer diagnosis

Clinical Context

Quality measure identifying molecular imaging of breast (such as positron emission tomography or molecular imaging) without corresponding breast cancer diagnosis.

RVU Information

CPT G8946 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

G-codes are CMS-specific HCPCS codes for services not covered by standard CPT. Documentation requirements follow the same standards as the equivalent CPT service. Check Medicare LCD/NCD policies for coverage criteria.
