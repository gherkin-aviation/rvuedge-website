G9790 Most rct bp >/= 130/80

G-Codes

Also known as: BP above goal >=130/80

HCPCS code documenting most recent blood pressure measurement greater than or equal to 130/80 millimeters of mercury.

Clinical Context

Documents uncontrolled hypertension (>=130/80 mmHg) for quality measure tracking and treatment intensification indication.
