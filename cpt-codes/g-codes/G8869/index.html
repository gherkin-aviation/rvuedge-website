G8869 Doc immune hep b antitnf

G-Codes

Also known as: hepatitis B immunity documented, hepatitis B immune status verified

Performance measure documenting that patient was immune to hepatitis B virus or had evidence of immunity during anti-tumor necrosis factor (anti-TNF) therapy consideration.

Clinical Context

Critical quality measure for patients being considered for anti-TNF therapy to prevent hepatitis B reactivation, particularly in rheumatologic and inflammatory bowel disease patients.
