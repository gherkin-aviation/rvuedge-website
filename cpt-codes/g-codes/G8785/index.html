G8785 Bp screening no perf at interval

G Codes

Also known as: BP screening overdue, Screening not done at interval, Missed screening

Documentation that blood pressure screening was not performed at the recommended interval.

In Plain Language

Blood pressure check not done on schedule; Preventive screening overdue

Clinical Context

Quality measure identifying missed blood pressure screening within recommended preventive care intervals.

RVU Information

CPT G8785 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation
