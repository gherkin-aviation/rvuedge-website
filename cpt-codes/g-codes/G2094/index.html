Also known as: Patient refusal, Medication non-adherence

HCPCS code documenting patient's reason for not taking ACE inhibitor, Angiotensin II Receptor Blocker, or Angiotensin Receptor Neprilysin Inhibitor.

Clinical Context

Used when patients decline prescribed renin-angiotensin inhibitors. Documents patient preference and allows for continued care coordination.
