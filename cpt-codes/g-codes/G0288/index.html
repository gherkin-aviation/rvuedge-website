G0288 Recon, cta for surgical plan

G-Codes

Also known as: CTA reconstruction, CT reconstruction for surgery, 3D surgical planning

Computed tomography angiography reconstruction for surgical planning, providing 3D visualization to optimize operative approach and technique.

Clinical Context

Used preoperatively for complex vascular, abdominal, or thoracic procedures. Allows surgeons to visualize anatomy and plan optimal surgical access and technique.
