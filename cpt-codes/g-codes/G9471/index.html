G Codes

Also known as: DEXA scan not ordered, bone density screening not done

Dual-energy x-ray absorptiometry scan was not ordered within 2 years.

In Plain Language

bone density test not done in 2 years; osteoporosis screening not ordered

Clinical Context

Quality measure exception when dual-energy x-ray absorptiometry bone density screening is not ordered within recommended 2-year interval for appropriate osteoporosis surveillance.

RVU Information
