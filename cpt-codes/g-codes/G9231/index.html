G9231 Doc esrd dia transverse preg

G-Codes

Also known as: ESRD documented pregnancy, end-stage kidney disease pregnancy

Documentation of end-stage renal disease diagnosed by dialysis or transplantation in patient who is pregnant or of childbearing age.

Clinical Context

Quality measure documenting recognition and appropriate management of end-stage renal disease in pregnant or potentially pregnant patients.
