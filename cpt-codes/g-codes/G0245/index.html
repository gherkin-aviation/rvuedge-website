G0245 Initial foot examination pt lops

G-Codes

Also known as: initial diabetic foot exam, neuropathy foot assessment

Initial foot examination and evaluation in patient with loss of protective sensation (LOPS), including assessment of ulceration risk and neuropathic complications.

Clinical Context

Comprehensive initial foot examination in patients with diabetic neuropathy and loss of protective sensation. Assesses amputation risk and establishes preventive care baseline.
