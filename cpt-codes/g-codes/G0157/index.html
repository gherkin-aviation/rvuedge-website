G0157 Hhc pt assistant each 15

G-Codes

Also known as: home health PT assistant, PTA service

Home health care physical therapy assistant service for a patient, provided in 15-minute increments, including therapeutic exercise under PT supervision and functional training.

Clinical Context

Home-based physical therapy assistant services under supervision of licensed PT. Includes therapeutic exercise, gait training, and functional activities.
