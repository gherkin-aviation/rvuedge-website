G9830 Her-2 pos

G Codes

Also known as: HER2 positive, HER-2 positive breast cancer

Healthcare quality measure documenting Human Epidermal Growth Factor Receptor 2 positive breast cancer status.

In Plain Language

breast cancer protein marker positive; HER2 positive cancer

Clinical Context

Quality measure used in breast cancer oncology to identify patients with HER2-positive tumors, which have prognostic and therapeutic implications, particularly for trastuzumab (Herceptin) eligibility.

RVU Information

CPT G9830 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation
