G0517 Remove drug implant

G-Codes Global 0d

Also known as: implant removal, remove drug device, extraction of implant

Removal of previously implanted drug delivery implant or device from subcutaneous or intramuscular site.

Clinical Context

Surgical removal of previously placed medication implants for completion of therapy, adverse effects, or transition to alternative therapy.
