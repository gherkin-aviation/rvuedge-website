G2183 Doc pt unable comm

G-Codes

Also known as: unable to communicate, communication barrier, patient unable

Documentation code indicating that patient is unable to communicate to participate in care planning discussions, used for quality measure exclusions and care coordination.

Clinical Context

Used to document communication limitations that may prevent patient participation in shared decision-making or care planning activities, affecting quality measure compliance and care delivery.
