G9839 Anti-egfr mon anti therapy

G Codes

Also known as: anti-EGFR monoclonal antibody therapy, EGFR inhibitor therapy

Healthcare quality measure documenting anti-Epidermal Growth Factor Receptor monoclonal antibody therapy administered to patient.

In Plain Language

targeted cancer treatment blocking cell growth; monoclonal antibody for epidermal growth factor

Clinical Context

Quality measure tracking administration of anti-EGFR monoclonal antibodies (such as cetuximab or panitumumab) in patients with EGFR-expressing tumors, particularly colorectal cancer.

RVU Information

CPT G9839 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
