G9297 No doc share dec prior procedure

G Codes

Also known as: no shared decision-making, undocumented discussion

Physician did not document sharing of decision with patient regarding prior procedure.

In Plain Language

decision discussion not documented; no record of patient talk

Clinical Context

Quality metric exception code when shared decision-making conversation regarding prior procedures was not documented in the medical record.

RVU Information
