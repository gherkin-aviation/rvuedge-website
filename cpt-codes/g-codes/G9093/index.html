G9093 Onc diagnosis rectal t4,n,m0 no prg

G-Codes

Also known as: rectal cancer T4, advanced rectal cancer locally, rectal cancer through peritoneum

Oncology diagnosis for rectal cancer stage T4 with any regional lymph node status and no distant metastasis.

Clinical Context

Documentation of locally advanced rectal cancer with invasion into adjacent structures, requiring multimodal treatment planning.
