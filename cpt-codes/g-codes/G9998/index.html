G9998 Doc medial rsn <3 colon

G-Codes

Also known as: colonoscopy exclusion, colorectal screening exclusion

Documentation of medical reason for not screening a patient for colorectal cancer as an exclusion criterion for quality measures.

Clinical Context

Used to document medical contraindications or clinical reasons precluding colorectal cancer screening in patients who would otherwise be included in colorectal cancer screening quality measures.
