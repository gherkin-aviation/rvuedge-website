G0023 Pin service 60m per month

G-Codes

Also known as: behavioral health coordination, care management, prolonged visit

Prolonged in-person individual evaluation and management service lasting 60 minutes per month for patients with complex behavioral or medical health conditions requiring intensive coordination.

Clinical Context

Used for intensive monthly care coordination and evaluation for patients with significant behavioral health, substance use, or complex medical comorbidities requiring extensive time and coordination.
