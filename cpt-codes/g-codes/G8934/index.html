G8934 Lvef <=40% or dep lv sys fcn

G-Codes

Also known as: reduced LVEF, systolic heart failure documented, depressed LV function

Performance measure indicating left ventricular ejection fraction at 40% or less, or depressed left ventricular systolic function confirmed.

Clinical Context

Documents baseline finding of reduced ejection fraction for initiation of heart failure quality measures including ACE inhibitor/ARB therapy protocols.
