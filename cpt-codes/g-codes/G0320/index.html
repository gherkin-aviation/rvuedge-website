G0320 Two-way audio and video hhs

G-Codes

Also known as: telehealth home visit video, virtual home health, two-way video HHS

Home health services using two-way audio and video communication technology for remote patient assessment and care delivery.

Clinical Context

Used for remote monitoring, assessment, and management of home-bound patients using synchronous video technology, reducing unnecessary visits.
