G8561 Pt inelig for ref otological evaluation

G Codes

Also known as: Ineligible for OTO referral, Clinical exclusion, Not a candidate

Documentation that patient is ineligible for otolaryngology referral evaluation based on clinical criteria.

In Plain Language

Doesn't need ear specialist evaluation; Not appropriate for ENT referral

Clinical Context

Quality measure documenting clinical ineligibility for otolaryngology referral, such as resolved conditions or patients already under specialist care.

RVU Information
