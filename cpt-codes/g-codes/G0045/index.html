G0045 Mrs 90 days posterior stk

G-Codes

Also known as: modified Rankin Scale post-stroke, 90-day stroke outcome, MRS assessment

Modified Rankin Scale (MRS) assessment performed within 90 days following acute stroke, measuring degree of disability or dependence.

Clinical Context

Quality measure tracking functional outcomes after stroke; MRS scores range from 0 (no symptoms) to 6 (death).
