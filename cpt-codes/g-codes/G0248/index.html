G0248 Demonstrate use home inr mon

G Codes

Also known as: INR monitor training, home coagulation testing training

Demonstration and instruction on proper use of home international normalized ratio (INR) monitoring device for patient self-testing of anticoagulation status.

In Plain Language

Teaching blood thinner level home test; Coagulation monitoring training

Clinical Context

Patient and caregiver education for self-monitoring of INR in patients on warfarin. Includes device operation, test performance, and result interpretation.

RVU Information

CPT G0248 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
