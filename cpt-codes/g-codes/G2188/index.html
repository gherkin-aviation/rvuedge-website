Also known as: age-based imaging criteria, senior head imaging, age 50+ imaging

Documentation indicator for patient age 50 years or older with clinical indication for head imaging, supporting appropriate imaging decisions in older populations.

Clinical Context

Used to document age-appropriate indications for neuroimaging in patients aged 50 and above, helping justify imaging orders based on age-specific clinical decision rules.
