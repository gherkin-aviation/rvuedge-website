G8849 Doc reas no pos air press

G Codes

Also known as: No PAP; reason documented, PAP contraindicated, Documented exception

Documentation of documented reason for not prescribing positive airway pressure (PAP) therapy.

In Plain Language

Breathing machine not prescribed; reason given; Medical reason for no CPAP prescription

Clinical Context

Quality measure documenting clinical justification for positive airway pressure non-prescribing.
