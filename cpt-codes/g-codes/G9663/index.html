G9663 Fast/dir ldl >= 190 milligrams/dl

G-Codes

Also known as: very high LDL, severe dyslipidemia

Documentation that patient's fasting or direct low-density lipoprotein cholesterol level is greater than or equal to one hundred ninety milligrams per deciliter.

Clinical Context

Cardiology quality measure identifying patients with very high LDL cholesterol requiring intensive statin therapy.
