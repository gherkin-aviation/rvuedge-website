G0239 Oth resp procedure, group

G-Codes

Also known as: group respiratory therapy, pulmonary group program

Other respiratory procedure or therapy service provided in group format for patients with respiratory insufficiency or pulmonary dysfunction.

Clinical Context

Respiratory therapies and procedures in group settings allowing peer support and cost efficiency for multiple patients with similar respiratory conditions.
