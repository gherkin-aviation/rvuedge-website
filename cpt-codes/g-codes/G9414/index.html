G9414 1dose menig vac btwn 10 & 13

G Codes

Also known as: meningococcal vaccination, MCV4 at age 10-13

In Plain Language

meningitis vaccine given to preteen; meningococcal vaccine given at correct age

Clinical Context

Quality measure tracking appropriate preventive care when adolescents receive meningococcal conjugate vaccine between ages 10-13 years as recommended by CDC vaccination schedule.
