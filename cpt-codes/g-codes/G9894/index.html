G9894 Adr dep thrpy prescribed

G Codes

Also known as: antidepressant prescribed, depression medication treatment

Healthcare quality measure documenting antidepressant medication therapy prescribed for eligible patient with depression.

In Plain Language

Clinical Context

Quality measure for depression management in behavioral health settings, documenting appropriate pharmacotherapy for major depressive disorder.
