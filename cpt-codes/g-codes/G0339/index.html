G0339 Robot lin-radsurg com, first

G Codes

Also known as: robotic radiosurgery planning, CyberKnife planning, linear accelerator plan

Robotic-assisted linear accelerator radiosurgery with comprehensive treatment planning and 3D dose computation for initial stereotactic procedure.

In Plain Language

robot-guided radiation therapy planning

Clinical Context

Used for initial planning and setup of stereotactic radiosurgery for intracranial lesions, spinal tumors, or functional conditions using precision robotics.

RVU Information
