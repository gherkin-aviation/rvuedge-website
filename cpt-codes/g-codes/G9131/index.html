G9131 Onc diagnosis brst unknown not otherwise specified

G-Codes

Also known as: unknown breast cancer stage, unstaged breast cancer

Oncology diagnosis for breast cancer where the stage or status of disease is unknown.

Clinical Context

Used when adequate staging information is unavailable for breast cancer patients.
