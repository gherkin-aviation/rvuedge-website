G2194 New hdache pediatric pt dis

G Codes

Also known as: pediatric headache serious, child headache disorder, concerning child headache

Documentation indicator for new onset headache in pediatric patient with documented serious or concerning diagnosis requiring appropriate management.

In Plain Language

New headache in child with serious diagnosis; Concerning head pain in pediatric patient

Clinical Context

Used to document identification of serious underlying pathology in children with new-onset headache, such as intracranial masses, infections, or vascular abnormalities.

RVU Information

CPT G2194 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
