G0086 Care man home care plan 30 m

G-Codes

Also known as: home care plan development, care plan establishment

Care management home care plan development and oversight, approximately 30 minutes, including establishment of comprehensive care plan with patient and family involvement.

Clinical Context

Used for development and initiation of formal home care plans. Includes collaboration with patient, family, and interdisciplinary team to establish goals and care strategies.
