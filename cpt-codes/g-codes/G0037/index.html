G0037 Pt not able to participate

G Codes

Also known as: unable to participate in assessment, patient unable to assess

Patient not able to participate in functional outcome assessment due to cognitive, physical, or other limitations.

In Plain Language

patient couldn't do the assessment

Clinical Context

Quality measure exclusion code when patient is unable to complete required assessments.
