G9766 Cva stroke diagnosis treatment transf fac

G-Codes

Also known as: stroke - transferred for treatment

HCPCS code documenting cerebrovascular accident or stroke diagnosis with transfer to another facility for treatment.

Clinical Context

Documents stroke patients transferred to higher-level facilities for advanced treatment (thrombolytics, thrombectomy) for quality measure reporting.
