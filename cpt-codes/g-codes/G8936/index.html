G8936 Pt not eligible ace/arb

G-Codes

Also known as: patient not eligible for ACE/ARB, ACE inhibitor contraindicated

Performance measure indicating patient was not eligible for ACE inhibitor or ARB therapy based on contraindications or clinical criteria.

Clinical Context

Documents appropriate exclusion from ACE/ARB therapy due to contraindications such as bilateral renal artery stenosis, hyperkalemia, or pregnancy.
