G9914 Pt initiated anti-tnf agent

G Codes

Also known as: TNF inhibitor started, anti-TNF therapy initiated

Healthcare quality measure documenting patient initiated anti-Tumor Necrosis Factor agent therapy.

In Plain Language

started immunosuppressive treatment for inflammation; biologic therapy begun

Clinical Context

Quality measure documenting initiation of TNF-alpha inhibitor therapy in patients with rheumatologic or inflammatory diseases.
