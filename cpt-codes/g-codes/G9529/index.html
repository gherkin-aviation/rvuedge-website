G9529 Minor blunt trauma with head CT

G Codes

Also known as: minor head injury with CT, head trauma imaging

Documentation that patient had minor blunt head trauma and underwent head computed tomography scan.

In Plain Language

bump on head, got a CT scan

Clinical Context

Emergency medicine quality measure documenting appropriate imaging for minor head trauma to assess for intracranial injury.

RVU Information

CPT G9529 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

G-codes are CMS-specific HCPCS codes for services not covered by standard CPT. Documentation requirements follow the same standards as the equivalent CPT service. Check Medicare LCD/NCD policies for coverage criteria.
