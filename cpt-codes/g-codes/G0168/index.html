G0168 Wound closure by adhesive

G Codes Global 0d

Also known as: skin adhesive closure, liquid stitches

Wound closure using skin adhesive (medical-grade adhesive), applied directly to approximated wound edges as alternative to sutures or staples.

In Plain Language

Special glue to close wounds; Adhesive wound closure

Clinical Context

Wound closure technique using medical adhesive for clean, small to moderate wounds as alternative to sutures. Provides watertight seal and may reduce infection risk.
