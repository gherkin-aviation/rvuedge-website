G9091 Onc diagnosis rectal t3 n0 no prog

G Codes

Also known as: rectal cancer T3 N0, locally advanced rectal cancer no nodes

Oncology diagnosis for rectal cancer stage T3 with negative lymph nodes and no disease progression.

In Plain Language

moderate rectal cancer; rectal cancer through muscle layer

Clinical Context

Staging documentation for rectal cancer patients with tumor invasion through the muscularis propria but without lymph node involvement.

RVU Information

CPT G9091 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
