G9344 Sysrsn no dicom srch

G Codes

Also known as: DICOM unavailable, system limitation documented

System reason documented explaining why Digital Imaging and Communications in Medicine (DICOM) search could not be performed.

In Plain Language

imaging system didn't work for search; technical issue prevented finding old images

Clinical Context

Quality measure exception when imaging search cannot be performed due to documented system limitations or DICOM interface unavailability, explaining inability to access prior studies.

RVU Information
