G9315 Amox with wo clav prescription

G Codes

Also known as: amoxicillin-clavulanate therapy, amoxicillin for otitis

Amoxicillin with or without clavulanic acid was prescribed for acute otitis media.

In Plain Language

antibiotic with or without potassium clavulanate; standard ear infection antibiotic

Clinical Context

Quality measure tracking prescription of amoxicillin or amoxicillin-clavulanate as treatment for acute otitis media, documenting antibiotic use patterns.
