G8541 No doc cur funct assess

G-Codes

Also known as: No functional assessment, FOA missing, Assessment not completed

Documentation that current functional status assessment was not documented or the assessment was not completed during the encounter.

Clinical Context

Quality measure documenting absence of functional assessment completion, affecting care planning and coordination for aging or disabled populations.
