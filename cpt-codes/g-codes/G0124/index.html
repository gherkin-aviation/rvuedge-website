G0124 Screening c/v thin layer by md

G-Codes

Also known as: physician-interpreted Pap test, MD-reviewed cytology

Cervical and vaginal cytology screening using thin-layer preparation technique, interpreted by a physician, for cervical cancer and precancerous cell detection.

Clinical Context

Thin-layer cytology screening with physician interpretation of slide for cervical cancer detection. Includes direct review by physician rather than automated systems.
