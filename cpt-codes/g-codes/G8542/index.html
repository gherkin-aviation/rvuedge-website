G8542 Doc funct no deficiencies

G-Codes

Also known as: Functional assessment normal, No functional impairment, Normal functional status

Documentation of current functional status assessment with no functional deficiencies identified during the encounter.

Clinical Context

Quality measure documenting complete functional assessment with normal results, indicating independent activities of daily living (ADL) and instrumental activities of daily living (IADL) capacity.
