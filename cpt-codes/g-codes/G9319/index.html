G9319 Image not std nomenclature

G-Codes

Also known as: non-standard image reporting, non-standard terminology

Medical imaging study report does not use standard nomenclature for body location and anatomy.

Clinical Context

Quality metric exception when imaging reports fail to use standardized anatomic nomenclature, potentially causing communication errors and clinical workflow disruption.
