G9479 Pharmacist at hospice

G Codes

Also known as: hospice pharmacist, pharmacy services

In Plain Language

pharmacist helped with medications at end-of-life; pharmaceutical support at hospice

Clinical Context

Quality measure tracking pharmacist services at hospice, addressing medication management, symptom control, pain management, and drug interactions in end-of-life care.
