G0472 Hep c screening high risk/other

G Codes

Also known as: Hep C screen, HCV testing, hepatitis C antibody test

Hepatitis C virus screening test for high-risk individuals or persons with other specific risk factors.

In Plain Language

Hepatitis C blood test; Liver infection screening; HCV check

Clinical Context

Screening for Hepatitis C in patients with identified risk factors (IVDU history, healthcare workers, incarcerated), as recommended by CDC and USPSTF.

RVU Information

CPT G0472 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation
