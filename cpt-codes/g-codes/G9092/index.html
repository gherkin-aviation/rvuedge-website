G9092 Onc diagnosis rectal t1-3,n1-2noprg

G Codes

Also known as: rectal cancer with lymph nodes, node-positive rectal cancer

Oncology diagnosis for rectal cancer stages T1-T3 with positive regional lymph nodes N1-N2 and no disease progression.

In Plain Language

rectal cancer with lymph node spread; rectal cancer that has affected lymph nodes

Clinical Context

Used for rectal cancer patients with regional lymph node involvement but without distant metastasis, guiding treatment decisions.

RVU Information
