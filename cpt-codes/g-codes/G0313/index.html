G0313 Immunize couns < 21yr 6-30 m

G Codes

Also known as: immunization counseling pediatric extended, vaccine counsel <21 extended

Immunization counseling for patients younger than 21 years of age lasting 16-30 minutes, providing comprehensive vaccine education.

In Plain Language

vaccine talk for young people longer

Clinical Context

Extended vaccine counseling for adolescents addressing complex decisions about catch-up vaccinations or contraindications.

RVU Information

CPT G0313 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
