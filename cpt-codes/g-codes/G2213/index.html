G2213 Initiat medial assist treatment in er

G-Codes

Also known as: MAT in ER, emergency addiction treatment, opioid-assisted therapy ER

Code for initiation of medication-assisted treatment in emergency department setting, including assessment and first medication dose administration.

Clinical Context

Used to document initiation of medication-assisted treatment for opioid use disorder in emergency settings, improving access to substance use disorder treatment.
