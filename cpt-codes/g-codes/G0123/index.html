G0123 Screening cervical/vag thin layer

G Codes

Also known as: thin-layer Pap smear, liquid-based cytology

Cervical and vaginal cytology screening using thin-layer preparation technique to assess for cervical cancer and precancerous changes in women.

In Plain Language

Cervical cancer screening test; Updated Pap smear test

Clinical Context

Cervical cancer screening using thin-layer cytology allowing better cell visualization and reduced inflammation artifact compared to conventional smears.

RVU Information
