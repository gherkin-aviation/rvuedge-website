G0153 Hhcp-svs of s/l path,each 15mn

G Codes

Also known as: home health SLP, speech therapy home visit

Home health care provider speech-language pathology service for a patient, provided in 15-minute increments, including communication and swallowing assessment and training.

In Plain Language

At-home speech therapy session; Speech and swallowing training at home

Clinical Context

Skilled speech-language pathology in home setting for patients with communication or swallowing disorders. Includes articulation training, aphasia rehabilitation, and swallowing exercises.

RVU Information

CPT G0153 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

G-codes are CMS-specific HCPCS codes for services not covered by standard CPT. Documentation requirements follow the same standards as the equivalent CPT service. Check Medicare LCD/NCD policies for coverage criteria.
