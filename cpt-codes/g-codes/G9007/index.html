G Codes

Also known as: CCM with team conference, care coordination conference

Performance measure code for chronic care management services including scheduled team conference to coordinate care.

In Plain Language

chronic disease management including meeting with care team; multidisciplinary care coordination meeting

Clinical Context

Billing code for Medicare CCM services that include formal interdisciplinary team conferences to optimize patient care coordination.
