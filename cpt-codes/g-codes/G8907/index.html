G8907 Pt doc no events on discharg

G Codes

Also known as: no discharge events, safe discharge from surgery center, no complications at discharge

Performance measure indicating patient was documented as experiencing no adverse events during the discharge process from ambulatory surgery facility.

In Plain Language

patient left the surgery center without any problems; no complications when leaving

Clinical Context

Quality and safety measure for ambulatory surgery centers tracking that discharge occurred without adverse events like burns, falls, or wrong site procedures.

RVU Information

CPT G8907 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

G-codes are CMS-specific HCPCS codes for services not covered by standard CPT. Documentation requirements follow the same standards as the equivalent CPT service. Check Medicare LCD/NCD policies for coverage criteria.
