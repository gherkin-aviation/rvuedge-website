G0475 Hiv combination assay

G Codes

Also known as: HIV combo test, HIV Ag/Ab combo, fourth-generation HIV test

Human Immunodeficiency Virus combination assay test including antibody and/or nucleic acid detection for screening or diagnosis.

In Plain Language

HIV screening test; AIDS blood test; HIV combination blood test

Clinical Context

Screening or diagnostic testing for HIV infection using combination antigen/antibody or nucleic acid testing methods with improved sensitivity.

RVU Information
