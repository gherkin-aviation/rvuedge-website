G0295 Electromagnetic therapy onc

G Codes

Also known as: EM therapy cancer, electromagnetic oncology therapy

Electromagnetic therapy for oncology/cancer treatment, using electromagnetic fields to influence tumor biology or pain management.

In Plain Language

electromagnetic cancer treatment

Clinical Context

Investigational therapy for cancer treatment or cancer-related symptoms. May be used in clinical trials examining electromagnetic field effects on tumor metabolism.

RVU Information

CPT G0295 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
