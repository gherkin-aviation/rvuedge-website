G0463 Hospital outpt clinic visit

G-Codes

Also known as: hospital clinic visit, outpatient clinic appt, facility visit

Hospital-based outpatient clinic visit for established or new patient comprehensive evaluation and medical management.

Clinical Context

Used for visits in hospital-affiliated outpatient clinics where hospital resources and infrastructure support the visit.
