G0407 Inpt/tele follow up 25

G-Codes

Also known as: inpt/telehealth f/u 25, follow-up visit 25 min

Hospital inpatient or telehealth follow-up evaluation and management service lasting approximately 25 minutes.

Clinical Context

Moderate length follow-up evaluation for hospitalized patient or via telehealth for more complex clinical assessments.
