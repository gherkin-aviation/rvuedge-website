G8937 No prescription ace/arb therapy

G Codes

Also known as: ACE/ARB not prescribed, missed heart failure therapy

Performance measure indicating ACE inhibitor or ARB therapy was not prescribed without documented contraindication.

In Plain Language

patient should have received this heart medicine but did not; guideline-recommended therapy not given

Clinical Context

Tracks instances where guideline-recommended ACE/ARB therapy for heart failure was not prescribed without appropriate clinical justification.

RVU Information

CPT G8937 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
