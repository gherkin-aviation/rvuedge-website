G8430 Doc medial rsn no medrec

G-Codes

Also known as: med rec not possible, medication list unavailable, med rec contraindicated

Documentation indicator with documented reason that medication reconciliation could not be performed or medication record was unavailable.

Clinical Context

Used to document valid reasons preventing completion of medication reconciliation such as unavailable medication records.
