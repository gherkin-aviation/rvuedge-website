G3002 Chronic pain management 30 minutes

G Codes

Also known as: pain management visit, chronic pain consult, pain clinic visit

Code for chronic pain management service provided in first 30 minutes of clinical contact with patient.

In Plain Language

Doctor appointment for long-term pain; Chronic pain treatment visit

Clinical Context

Used to report initial or established pain management consultation and treatment planning for chronic pain conditions.
