G0493 Rn care each 15 minutes hh/hospice

G Codes

Also known as: home health RN visit, hospice RN care, nursing assessment

Registered nurse care or skilled nursing assessment and management in 15-minute increments in home health or hospice setting.

In Plain Language

Nurse home care visit; Hospice nursing service; Home health nursing

Clinical Context

Skilled nursing care provided in home health or hospice settings billed in 15-minute units for assessment, monitoring, or direct care.
