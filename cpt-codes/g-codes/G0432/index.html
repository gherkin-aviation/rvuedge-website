G0432 Eia hiv-1/hiv-2 screening

G Codes

Also known as: EIA HIV-1/2, fourth-generation HIV test, antigen/antibody test

Enzyme immunoassay for human immunodeficiency virus types 1 and 2 screening in diverse patient populations.

In Plain Language

HIV blood test; AIDS screening test

Clinical Context

Initial screening test for HIV infection detecting both antibodies and antigens. Fourth-generation test with shorter window period.
