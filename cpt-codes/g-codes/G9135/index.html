G Codes

Oncology diagnosis for non-Hodgkin lymphoma stages 3-4 without relapse and no disease progression.

In Plain Language

Clinical Context

Documentation of advanced-stage non-Hodgkin lymphoma patients without relapse, typically managed with rituximab-containing combination chemotherapy.
