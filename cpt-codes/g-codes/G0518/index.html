G0518 Remove w insert drug implant

G-Codes Global 0d

Also known as: implant exchange, replace drug device, implant switch

Removal of previously implanted drug delivery implant or device with simultaneous insertion of new implant.

Clinical Context

Surgical removal and replacement of medication implants in single procedure, for continuous drug delivery with device change.
