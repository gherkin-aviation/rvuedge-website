G0498 Chemotherapy extend intravenous infusion with pump

G-Codes

Also known as: extended chemo infusion, chemo pump service, prolonged IV chemo

Chemotherapy extended infusion service with electronic infusion pump for continuous or prolonged intravenous drug administration.

Clinical Context

Administration of chemotherapy drugs over extended periods using infusion pump technology for malignancy treatment.
