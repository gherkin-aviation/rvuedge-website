G9354 1 or no CT sinus with in 90d diagnosis

G Codes

Also known as: appropriate sinus imaging, single sinus CT appropriate

One or no computed tomography scan of sinuses documented within 90 days of diagnosis, meeting appropriateness criteria.

In Plain Language

right number of sinus scans done; appropriate imaging for sinuses

Clinical Context

Quality measure documenting appropriate imaging frequency for rhinosinusitis diagnosis when one or fewer computed tomography scans performed within 90-day window.

RVU Information

CPT G9354 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
