G9133 Onc diagnosis prostate clinical met

G Codes

Also known as: clinical metastatic prostate cancer, prostate cancer with mets

Oncology diagnosis for prostate cancer with clinical evidence of metastatic disease.

In Plain Language

prostate cancer that has spread

Clinical Context

Documentation of prostate cancer patients with clinical evidence of metastatic disease, typically managed with androgen deprivation therapy.
