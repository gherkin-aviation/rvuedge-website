G0433 Elisa hiv-1/hiv-2 screening

G Codes

Also known as: ELISA HIV-1/2, ELISA HIV test, antibody screen

Enzyme-linked immunosorbent assay for human immunodeficiency virus types 1 and 2 screening for antibody detection.

In Plain Language

HIV antibody blood test

Clinical Context

Traditional HIV screening test detecting HIV-1 and HIV-2 antibodies in serum or plasma with good sensitivity.

RVU Information

CPT G0433 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
