G8856 Ref for otological evaluation

G Codes

Also known as: ENT referral made, otolaryngology referral, ENT evaluation ordered

Performance measure indicating referral for otolaryngologic evaluation was made during the measurement period.

In Plain Language

patient was referred to ear, nose, and throat doctor; sent to ENT specialist

Clinical Context

Documents appropriate referral to Otolaryngology/ENT for conditions such as hearing loss, balance disorders, or throat pathology requiring specialist evaluation.

RVU Information

CPT G8856 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
