G8839 Sleep apnea assess

G-Codes

Also known as: Sleep apnea assessed, OSA screening performed, Sleep evaluation documented

Documentation that patient underwent assessment for obstructive sleep apnea (OSA) using appropriate screening tool or clinical evaluation.

Clinical Context

Quality measure documenting appropriate sleep apnea screening using validated instruments such as STOP-BANG or Epworth Sleepiness Scale.
