G0109 Diab manage trn ind/group

G-Codes

Also known as: diabetes group education, group diabetes training

Diabetes management training and education provided in individual or group format to patients, including self-management skills and disease education per individual.

Clinical Context

Diabetes education in group settings allowing peer support and interaction. Includes diabetes fundamentals, medication management, nutrition, and lifestyle modification.
