G2025 Dis site tele services rhc/fqhc

G-Codes

Also known as: Telehealth delivery, Remote rural health services

HCPCS code for distant site services delivered via telehealth in Rural Health Clinic or Federally Qualified Health Center. Remote healthcare delivery.

Clinical Context

Used when telemedicine services are provided to patients at distant sites through Rural Health Clinics or FQHCs. Expands access to care in underserved areas.
