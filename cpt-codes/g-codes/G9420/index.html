G9420 Specimen site no lung

G-Codes

Also known as: non-lung specimen, extra-pulmonary pathology

Specimen site is not lung or lung cancer, representing non-pulmonary pathology.

Clinical Context

Quality measure exclusion when pathology specimen originates from non-pulmonary site, making lung cancer histologic classification requirement inapplicable.
