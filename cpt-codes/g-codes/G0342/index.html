G0342 Laparoscopy islet cell transverse

G-Codes Global 90d

Also known as: lap islet transplant, laparoscopic islet cell transplant

Laparoscopic islet cell transplantation procedure using minimally invasive endoscopic technique to deliver insulin-producing cells.

Clinical Context

Laparoscopic technique for islet transplantation in Type 1 diabetes management. Less invasive than open surgery with faster recovery.
