G8473 Ace/arb thxpy rx'd

G-Codes

Also known as: ACE/ARB prescribed, Renin-angiotensin inhibitor therapy, Blood pressure medication

Documentation that Angiotensin-Converting Enzyme (ACE) inhibitor or Angiotensin II Receptor Blocker (ARB) therapy was prescribed or ordered.

Clinical Context

Quality measure for hypertension and chronic kidney disease management. Used to document appropriate prescribing of Angiotensin-Converting Enzyme inhibitors or Angiotensin II Receptor Blockers for cardiovascular or renal protection.
