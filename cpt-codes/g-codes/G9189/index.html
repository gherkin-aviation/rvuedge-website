G9189 Beta pres or already taking

G Codes

Also known as: beta blocker prescribed, beta blocker on board

Beta-blocker medication prescribed to acute myocardial infarction patient or patient already taking beta-blocker.

In Plain Language

heart attack patient given beta blocker

Clinical Context

Quality measure documentation indicating appropriate beta-blocker therapy initiated or continued in acute myocardial infarction patients.

RVU Information
