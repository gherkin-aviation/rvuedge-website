G0270 Mnt subs treatment for change diagnosis

G-Codes

Also known as: substance abuse treatment management, medication-assisted treatment

Maintenance of substance use disorder treatment with medication adjustment or change in diagnostic context, including assessment and plan modification.

Clinical Context

Clinical management of patients in medication-assisted treatment programs with medication adjustments based on response and diagnostic changes.
