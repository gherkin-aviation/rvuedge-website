G2082 Visit esketamine 56m or less

G-Codes

Also known as: Esketamine visit, Ketamine treatment

HCPCS code for esketamine treatment visit lasting 56 minutes or less. Rapid-acting antidepressant therapy monitoring.

Clinical Context

Used for office-based esketamine treatment in treatment-resistant depression. Includes patient monitoring, vital signs, and therapy integration.
