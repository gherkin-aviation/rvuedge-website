G Codes

Also known as: intensive outpatient program, day treatment program

Intensive outpatient services with minimum of 9 procedure codes or service units per week for up to 7 days, for patients requiring structured day treatment program.

In Plain Language

Daytime treatment program; Intensive outpatient therapy

Clinical Context

Structured day program for patients with behavioral health or rehabilitation needs requiring frequent service intensity. Includes multiple therapeutic modalities and coordinated care.
