G9075 Onc diagnosis brst metastic/ recur

G Codes

Also known as: metastatic breast cancer, recurrent breast cancer

Performance measure documenting breast cancer with metastatic disease or evidence of recurrence.

In Plain Language

breast cancer has spread to other parts of body; breast cancer has returned

Clinical Context

Quality measure documenting advanced/metastatic breast cancer for palliative and systemic therapy planning.
