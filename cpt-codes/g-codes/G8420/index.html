G8420 Calc bmi norm parameters

G Codes

Also known as: normal BMI, healthy weight documented, BMI normal

Documentation indicator that Body Mass Index was calculated within normal parameters for age and patient population.

In Plain Language

Weight is healthy; Normal body weight confirmed

Clinical Context

Used to document body mass index within appropriate range for patient age and population.
