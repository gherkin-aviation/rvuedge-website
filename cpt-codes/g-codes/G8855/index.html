G8855 Therapy not assessed annually

G-Codes

Also known as: therapy not assessed, treatment evaluation not documented

Performance measure indicating that prescribed therapy was not assessed during the measurement period for documentation purposes.

Clinical Context

Documents instances where therapy assessment could not be completed or was not performed during the measurement year.
