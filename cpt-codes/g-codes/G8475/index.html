G8475 Ace/arb thxpy not rx'd

G-Codes

Also known as: ACE/ARB not prescribed, Therapy not initiated, No ACE/ARB documented

Documentation that Angiotensin-Converting Enzyme (ACE) inhibitor or Angiotensin II Receptor Blocker (ARB) therapy was not prescribed without documentation of medical reason.

Clinical Context

Quality measurement code identifying cases where ACE inhibitors or ARBs were not prescribed and no clinical justification was documented, potentially indicating a care gap.
