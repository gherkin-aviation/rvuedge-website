G9539 Intent pot remv time placemt

G Codes

Also known as: retrievable filter placement, temporary filter intent

Documentation that intent was to perform removal of inferior vena cava filter at time of placement.

In Plain Language

temporary filter put in vein to catch blood clots

Clinical Context

Vascular quality measure documenting appropriate temporary placement of removable IVC filters with documented removal plan.

RVU Information
