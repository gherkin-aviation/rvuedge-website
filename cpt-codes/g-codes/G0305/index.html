G0305 Posterior outpatient service lvrs minutes 6

G Codes

Also known as: post-op LVRS, postoperative LVRS care, post-op recovery

Post-operative evaluation and management services for LVRS patients with minimum 6-day stay, covering recovery and complications.

In Plain Language

surgery recovery care

Clinical Context

Covers postoperative evaluation, management of complications, and follow-up for lung volume reduction surgery patients during at least 6-day hospitalization.

RVU Information

CPT G0305 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation
