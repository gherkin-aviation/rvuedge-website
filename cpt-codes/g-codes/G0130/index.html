G0130 Single energy x-ray study

G-Codes

Also known as: single energy X-ray absorptiometry, forearm DEXA

Single energy X-ray absorptiometry (SXA) study for bone mineral density assessment, typically of the forearm or heel, for osteoporosis screening and monitoring.

Clinical Context

Bone mineral density assessment using single-photon absorptiometry at peripheral sites. Used for osteoporosis screening and monitoring in high-risk individuals.
