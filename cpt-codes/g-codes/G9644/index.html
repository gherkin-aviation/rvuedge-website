G9644 No smok b/4 anesthesia day of surgical

G Codes

Also known as: smoking cessation before surgery, pre-operative abstinence

Documentation that patient did not smoke before anesthesia on the day of surgery.

In Plain Language

patient didn't smoke before surgery

Clinical Context

Perioperative quality measure documenting smoking cessation on operative day for risk reduction.
