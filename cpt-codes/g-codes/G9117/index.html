G9117 Onc diagnosis ovarian unknown not otherwise specified

G-Codes

Also known as: unknown ovarian cancer stage, unstaged ovarian cancer

Oncology diagnosis for ovarian cancer where the stage or status of disease is unknown.

Clinical Context

Used when adequate staging information is unavailable for ovarian cancer patients.
