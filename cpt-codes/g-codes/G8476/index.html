G8476 Bp sys <140 and dias <90

G-Codes

Also known as: Blood pressure controlled, BP at goal, Hypertension controlled

Documentation of systolic blood pressure less than 140 millimeters of mercury (mmHg) AND diastolic blood pressure less than 90 mmHg at time of measurement.

Clinical Context

Quality measure documentation indicating achievement of target blood pressure control, a key goal in managing hypertension, diabetes, chronic kidney disease, and cardiovascular disease.
