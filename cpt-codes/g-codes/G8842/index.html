G8842 Ahi rdi rei doc win 2mo

G-Codes

Also known as: AHI/RDI/REI documented, Sleep study result documented, Apnea index calculated

Documentation that Apnea Hypopnea Index (AHI) or Respiratory Disturbance Index (RDI) or Respiratory Events Index (REI) was documented within 2 months of initial sleep apnea diagnosis.

Clinical Context

Quality measure documenting formal sleep study with measured apnea events index, essential for diagnosis confirmation and severity assessment.
