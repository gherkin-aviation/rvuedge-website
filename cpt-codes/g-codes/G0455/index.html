G0455 Fecal microbiota prep instil

G-Codes Global 0d

Also known as: fecal microbiota transplant, FMT, fecal transplant

Fecal microbiota preparation and instillation for treatment of recurrent Clostridioides difficile infection through microbiota restoration.

Clinical Context

Curative treatment for severe or recurrent C. difficile infection by restoring healthy intestinal bacterial flora.
