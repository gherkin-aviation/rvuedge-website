G9156 Evaluation for wheelchair

G Codes

Also known as: wheelchair evaluation, mobility device assessment

Evaluation and fitting for wheelchair or other mobility device.

In Plain Language

wheelchair fitting; mobility aid evaluation

Clinical Context

Clinical assessment and measurement for proper wheelchair selection and fitting to meet patient mobility and comfort needs.
