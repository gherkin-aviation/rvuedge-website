G9605 Surv score no improv with treatment

G Codes

Also known as: treatment non-response, no improvement

Documentation that patient's survival score did not improve from baseline following treatment.

In Plain Language

patient's health didn't improve much

Clinical Context

Oncology quality measure documenting inadequate treatment response with stable or declining prognostic indicators.
