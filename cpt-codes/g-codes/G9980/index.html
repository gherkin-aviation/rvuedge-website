G9980 Remote e/m new pt 30 minutes

G Codes

Also known as: virtual visit, telehealth eval, remote new patient visit

Remote evaluation and management service for a new patient via telehealth lasting approximately 30 minutes.

In Plain Language

online doctor visit; video appointment with a new doctor

Clinical Context

Used for initial consultations with a new patient conducted via real-time synchronous interaction at a distance. Appropriate for detailed evaluation of new patients with multiple concerns or complex medical histories.
