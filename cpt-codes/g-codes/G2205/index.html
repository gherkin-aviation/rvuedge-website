G2205 Preg drainage adjv trtmt

G Codes

Also known as: pregnancy treatment, pregnant therapy, adjuvant pregnancy

Documentation indicator for pregnant patient receiving adjuvant treatment or therapy during pregnancy for cancer or other conditions.

In Plain Language

Pregnant woman receiving medical treatment; Therapy given during pregnancy

Clinical Context

Used to document administration of therapeutic interventions in pregnant patients, including cancer treatment, with appropriate safety monitoring.

RVU Information
