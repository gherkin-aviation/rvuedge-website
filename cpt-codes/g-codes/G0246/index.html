G0246 Follow-up evaluation of foot pt lop

G-Codes

Also known as: follow-up diabetic foot exam, neuropathy foot follow-up

Follow-up evaluation and assessment of foot in patient with loss of protective sensation (LOPS), including monitoring for ulceration and functional changes.

Clinical Context

Periodic follow-up foot assessment in patients with diabetic neuropathy. Includes monitoring for new ulcerations, progression, and preventive interventions.
