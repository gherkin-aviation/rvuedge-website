G0446 Intens behave therapy cardiovascular diagnosis

G-Codes

Also known as: behavioral therapy cardiovascular, cardiac behavioral health

Intensive behavioral therapy for established cardiovascular disease directed at behavioral and psychosocial modification.

Clinical Context

Structured behavioral health intervention for patients with diagnosed heart disease addressing depression, stress, and lifestyle factors.
