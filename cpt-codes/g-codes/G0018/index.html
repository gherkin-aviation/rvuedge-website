G0018 Crisis psychotherapy add 30m

G Codes +Add-on

Also known as: crisis therapy add-on, extended crisis counseling

Additional crisis psychotherapy session lasting 30 minutes, providing continued mental health support and intervention during acute psychological distress episodes.

In Plain Language

additional 30 minutes of crisis counseling

Clinical Context

Used as an add-on code for prolonged crisis psychotherapy exceeding the initial 60-minute session, extending mental health support during acute psychiatric emergencies.
