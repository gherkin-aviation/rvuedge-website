G8845 Pos airway press prescribed

G-Codes

Also known as: PAP prescribed, CPAP/BiPAP prescribed, Positive pressure therapy initiated

Documentation that positive airway pressure (PAP) therapy was prescribed for obstructive sleep apnea (OSA) management.

Clinical Context

Quality measure documenting appropriate prescription of continuous positive airway pressure (CPAP) or bilevel positive airway pressure (BiPAP) for moderate to severe sleep apnea.
