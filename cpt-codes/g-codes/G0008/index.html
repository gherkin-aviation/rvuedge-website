G0008 Administration influenza virus vac

G Codes

Also known as: flu shot, flu vaccine, influenza vaccination

Administration of influenza virus vaccine, including the vaccine material and professional services for preparation, injection, and documentation.

In Plain Language

flu shot; flu vaccine

Clinical Context

Used for annual administration of seasonal influenza vaccine as part of preventive healthcare and disease control measures, typically offered in medical and dental settings.

RVU Information

CPT G0008 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
