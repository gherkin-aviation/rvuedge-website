G0257 Unsched dialysis esrd pt hos

G-Codes

Also known as: emergency dialysis, urgent hemodialysis

Unscheduled dialysis service in hospital outpatient department for end-stage renal disease (ESRD) patient requiring emergent or urgent hemodialysis treatment.

Clinical Context

Unscheduled dialysis in hospital for patients with ESRD who present with acute fluid or electrolyte complications outside normal dialysis schedule.
