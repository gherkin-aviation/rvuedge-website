G8958 Assess vol management not doc

G Codes

Also known as: volume assessment not documented, fluid status not assessed

Performance measure indicating volume management assessment was not documented for hemodialysis patient during measurement period.

In Plain Language

doctor did not check patient's fluid balance; volume status assessment was not recorded

Clinical Context

Quality measure identifying gaps in dialysis management documentation related to fluid status assessment, important for patient safety.

RVU Information

CPT G8958 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

G-codes are CMS-specific HCPCS codes for services not covered by standard CPT. Documentation requirements follow the same standards as the equivalent CPT service. Check Medicare LCD/NCD policies for coverage criteria.
