G0166 Extrnl counterpulse, per treatment

G-Codes

Also known as: external counterpulsation, EECP therapy

External counterpulsation therapy (EECP), a non-invasive cardiac treatment involving synchronized external pneumatic cuff compression, per treatment session.

Clinical Context

Non-invasive treatment for angina and heart failure using external pneumatic cuff compression synchronized to cardiac cycle. Improves coronary blood flow and cardiac performance.
