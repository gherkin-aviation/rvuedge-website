G8477 Bp sys>=140 and/or dias >=90

G-Codes

Also known as: Elevated blood pressure, Uncontrolled hypertension, BP above goal

Documentation of systolic blood pressure greater than or equal to 140 millimeters of mercury (mmHg) and/or diastolic blood pressure greater than or equal to 90 mmHg at time of measurement.

Clinical Context

Quality measure documentation indicating suboptimal blood pressure control, used to identify patients requiring treatment intensification or medication adjustment.
