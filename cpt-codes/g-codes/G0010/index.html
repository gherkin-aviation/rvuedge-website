G0010 Administration hepatitis b vaccine

G-Codes

Also known as: hepatitis B shot, hepatitis B vaccination, HBV vaccine

Administration of hepatitis B vaccine, including the vaccine material and professional services for preparation, injection, and documentation.

Clinical Context

Used for administration of hepatitis B vaccine series to prevent hepatitis B virus infection, particularly important for healthcare workers, infants, adolescents, and at-risk populations.
