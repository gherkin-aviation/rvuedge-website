G0460 Autolog prp not diab ulcer

G-Codes

Also known as: autologous PRP therapy, platelet-rich plasma application, PRP wound treatment

Autologous platelet-rich plasma application for non-diabetic chronic wounds or ulcers to promote wound healing.

Clinical Context

Applied to chronic non-diabetic wounds (pressure ulcers, venous ulcers, surgical wounds) to stimulate healing through concentrated platelets and growth factors.
