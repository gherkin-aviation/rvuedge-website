G9004 Mccd, risk adj lo, initial

G Codes

Also known as: CCM low-risk initial, risk-adjusted CCM low initial

Performance measure code for chronic care management services at risk-adjusted low rate, initial enrollment for lower-complexity patients.

In Plain Language

doctor's fee for managing patients with less complexity initially; initial CCM for lower-risk patients

Clinical Context

Billing code for Medicare CCM services for patients meeting low-risk criteria at initial rate.
