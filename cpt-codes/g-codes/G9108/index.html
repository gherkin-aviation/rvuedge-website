G9108 Onc diagnosis pancreatic unknwn not otherwise specified

G-Codes

Also known as: unknown pancreatic cancer status, unstaged pancreatic cancer

Oncology diagnosis for pancreatic cancer where the status or extent of disease is unknown.

Clinical Context

Used when adequate staging information is unavailable for pancreatic cancer patients.
