G0104 Ca screen;flexi sigmoidscope

G-Codes Global 0d

Also known as: flex sigmoidoscopy screening, flexible sig

Colorectal cancer screening using flexible sigmoidoscopy, allowing visualization of the rectum and lower colon to detect polyps, masses, or other abnormalities.

Clinical Context

Preventive screening for colorectal cancer in asymptomatic, average-risk individuals. Allows direct visualization and biopsy of lesions; no anesthesia required.
