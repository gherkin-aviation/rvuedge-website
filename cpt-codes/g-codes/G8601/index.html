G8601 No elig tpa initial with in 4.5 hour

G-Codes

Also known as: Not eligible for tPA, tPA contraindicated, Outside thrombolysis window

Documentation that patient is not eligible for tissue plasminogen activator (tPA) thrombolytic therapy within the 4.5-hour window.

Clinical Context

Quality measure documenting clinical ineligibility for thrombolytic therapy due to contraindications or presentation outside therapeutic window.
