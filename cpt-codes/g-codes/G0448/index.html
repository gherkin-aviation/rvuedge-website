G0448 Place perm pacing cardiovert

G-Codes

Also known as: pacemaker placement, ICD placement, device implantation

Placement of permanent pacemaker and cardioverter-defibrillator device for cardiac rhythm management in appropriate candidates.

Clinical Context

Implantation of device for management of bradycardia, tachyarrhythmias, or both in patients with indicated conditions.
