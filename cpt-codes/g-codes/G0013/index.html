G0013 Hiv prep counsel, clin staff

G-Codes

Also known as: clinical staff HIV counseling, staff-provided PrEP counseling, prevention counseling

Human immunodeficiency virus (HIV) prevention counseling provided by clinical staff lasting 15 to 30 minutes, addressing transmission prevention and risk reduction strategies.

Clinical Context

Used for structured counseling on HIV prevention and pre-exposure prophylaxis provided by clinical staff such as nurses, health educators, or counselors.
