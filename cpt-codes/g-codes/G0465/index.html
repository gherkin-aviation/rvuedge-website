G0465 Autolog prp diab wound ulcer

G Codes Global 0d

Also known as: autologous PRP therapy diabetes, platelet-rich plasma diabetic wound, PRP diabetic ulcer treatment

Autologous platelet-rich plasma application for diabetic foot wounds or venous insufficiency ulcers to promote tissue regeneration and healing.

In Plain Language

Platelet therapy for diabetic wounds; Blood cell growth treatment; Concentrated platelet injection

Clinical Context

Applied to diabetic foot ulcers and related wounds to leverage platelet growth factors for enhanced healing in diabetic populations with compromised wound healing.
