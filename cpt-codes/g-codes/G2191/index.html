G2191 Ind img hd pos hd ache

G Codes

Also known as: headache imaging, head pain imaging, headache imaging indication

Documentation indicator for imaging indication in head region for evaluation of positive head-related pain or headache symptoms.

In Plain Language

Brain scan for headache evaluation; Imaging for head pain assessment

Clinical Context

Used to document clinical justification for neuroimaging in patients presenting with head pain or headache symptoms suggesting central nervous system pathology.
