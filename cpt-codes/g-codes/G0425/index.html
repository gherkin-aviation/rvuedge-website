G0425 Inpt/ed teleconsult30

G-Codes

Also known as: telehealth consult 30 min, virtual consult inpt/ED

Hospital inpatient or emergency department teleconsultation service lasting approximately 30 minutes with remote specialist.

Clinical Context

Real-time video consultation between treating team and remote specialist for acute hospitalized or emergency department patients.
