G9396 Ini phq9 >9 not assess

G-Codes

Also known as: depression not reassessed, PHQ-9 follow-up not documented

Initial Patient Health Questionnaire-9 score greater than 9 not assessed at follow-up.

Clinical Context

Quality metric exception when patient with initial PHQ-9 score greater than 9 does not have documented follow-up assessment, missing opportunity to track treatment response.
