G8433 Scr for dep not cpt doc rsn

G Codes

Also known as: depression screening not done, depression screen omitted, no depression screening

Documentation indicator that depression screening was not completed or documented with documented reason for omission.

In Plain Language

Depression test not done; Screening not performed with reason

Clinical Context

Used to document intentional non-performance of depression screening with clinical justification.

RVU Information
