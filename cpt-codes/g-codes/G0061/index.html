G0061 Anesthesiology subtalar sinus

G-Codes

Also known as: anesthesia service, perioperative anesthesia

Specialty supplemental or ancillary services provided by an anesthesiologist for perioperative anesthesia management, pain control, and critical care anesthesia services.

Clinical Context

Represents anesthesia services in operating room and perioperative settings. Includes general anesthesia, regional anesthesia, monitored anesthesia care, and acute pain management.
