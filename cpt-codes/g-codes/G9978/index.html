G9978 Remote e/m new pt 10mins

G-Codes

Also known as: virtual visit, telehealth eval, remote new patient visit

Remote evaluation and management service for a new patient via telehealth lasting approximately 10 minutes.

Clinical Context

Used for initial consultations with a new patient conducted via real-time synchronous interaction at a distance. Appropriate for patients establishing care remotely without prior clinical relationship.
