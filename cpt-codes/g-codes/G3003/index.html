G3003 Chronic pain management additional 15m

G-Codes +Add-on

Also known as: pain management add-on, prolonged pain visit, pain time add-on

Add-on code for each additional 15-minute increment of chronic pain management service beyond the initial 30-minute period.

Clinical Context

Used as an add-on code to report additional time spent in chronic pain management services beyond the initial 30-minute evaluation.
