G2192 >55 yrs temp hd ache

G Codes

Also known as: temporal headache, older age headache, GCA screening

Documentation indicator for patients over 55 years old presenting with temporal headache patterns, often related to giant cell arteritis screening.

In Plain Language

Senior with temple area headache; Age-related temporal headache

Clinical Context

Used to document clinical indication for evaluation in patients over 55 with temporal headache, important for identifying potential giant cell arteritis requiring urgent diagnosis.
