G0044 Pt mitral stenosis

G Codes

Also known as: mitral stenosis patient, mitral valve stenosis quality code

Patient with mitral stenosis, used for quality measure reporting and anticoagulation management in valvular heart disease.

In Plain Language

patient has narrowed mitral heart valve

Clinical Context

Quality measure code identifying mitral stenosis patients who may require anticoagulation, particularly with concurrent atrial fibrillation.

RVU Information

CPT G0044 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation
