G9114 Onc diagnosis ovarian stg1a-b or 2

G-Codes

Also known as: early ovarian cancer stage 2, stage 1-2 ovarian cancer

Oncology diagnosis for ovarian cancer stages 1a-1b or stage 2 without distant metastasis and no disease progression.

Clinical Context

Documentation of ovarian cancer patients with stage 1-2 disease amenable to cytoreductive surgery with or without adjuvant chemotherapy.
