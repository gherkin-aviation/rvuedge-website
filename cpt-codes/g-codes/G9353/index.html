G9353 Medrsn >1 sinus CT w 90d diagnosis

G Codes

Also known as: multiple sinus CT with reason, appropriate repeat sinus imaging

Medical reason documented for more than one computed tomography scan of sinuses within 90 days of diagnosis.

In Plain Language

doctor explained why multiple sinus scans needed; medical justification for repeat imaging

Clinical Context

Quality measure exception when multiple sinus computed tomography scans performed within 90 days but justified by documented clinical reasons such as surgical planning or complicated infection.

RVU Information

CPT G9353 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
