G8857 No elig ref for otological evaluation

G-Codes

Also known as: not eligible for ENT referral, ENT referral not indicated

Performance measure indicating patient was not eligible for referral to otolaryngologic evaluation based on clinical criteria.

Clinical Context

Documents when otolaryngologic referral criteria are not met, such as normal hearing screening or absence of ENT-specific pathology.
