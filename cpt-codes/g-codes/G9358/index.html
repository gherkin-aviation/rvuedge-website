G9358 Pp evaluation/edu not perf

G Codes

Also known as: postpartum screening not done, mood assessment omitted

Postpartum evaluation and education on perinatal depression and/or anxiety was not performed.

In Plain Language

postpartum depression not checked; no screening for mood disorders

Clinical Context

Quality metric exception when postpartum mood screening or patient education regarding depression and anxiety disorders is not documented in postpartum visit.

RVU Information

CPT G9358 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation
