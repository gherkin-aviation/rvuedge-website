G0513 Prolong preventive services, first 30m

G-Codes

Also known as: extended preventive visit, prolonged health exam, comprehensive health assessment

Prolonged preventive medicine service in office setting for comprehensive assessment, lasting first 30 minutes.

Clinical Context

Extended preventive visits requiring comprehensive assessment for multiple chronic conditions, complex medication review, or extensive counseling.
