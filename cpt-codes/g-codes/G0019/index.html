Also known as: social determinants services, community health services, health equity services

Community health integration services addressing social determinants of health lasting 60 minutes, coordinating care for health-related social needs affecting health outcomes.

Used for structured interventions addressing social determinants of health such as housing, food security, transportation, employment, and other factors affecting patient health and treatment success.
