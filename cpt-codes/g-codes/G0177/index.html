G Codes

Outpatient (OPPS), partial hospitalization program (PHP), or intensive outpatient program (IOP) service - training and education activities for skill development.

In Plain Language

Classes and training in day program; Educational activities

Clinical Context

Structured educational and training activities in day programs including psychoeducation, life skills training, and symptom management education.

RVU Information
