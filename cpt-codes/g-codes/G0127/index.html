G0127 Trim nail(s)

G-Codes Global 0d

Also known as: nail trimming, toenail care

Trimming and maintenance of patient's toenails, including assessment of nail and foot health, removal of nails, and foot care education.

Clinical Context

Routine foot and nail care service, particularly important for patients with diabetes, neuropathy, or poor foot access. Includes assessment for infections and ulceration risk.
