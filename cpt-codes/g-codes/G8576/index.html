G8576 No postoperative renal fail

G Codes

Also known as: No postop renal failure, Normal postop kidney function, No AKI

Documentation that acute kidney injury or postoperative renal failure did not develop following surgical procedure.

In Plain Language

Kidney function normal after surgery; No kidney failure after operation

Clinical Context

Quality measure documenting absence of postoperative renal complications, indicating appropriate perioperative management and fluid/hemodynamic optimization.

RVU Information

CPT G8576 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation
