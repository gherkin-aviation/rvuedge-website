G0089 Adm subcutaneous drug first home visit

G Codes

Also known as: first subcutaneous injection, initial home subcutaneous setup

Administration of subcutaneous drug therapy during the first home health nursing visit, including initial setup, patient/caregiver instruction, and treatment initiation.

In Plain Language

Starting under-skin injection therapy on first visit; Initial subcutaneous medication setup at home

Clinical Context

Used for initial subcutaneous drug administration in home setting on the first nursing visit. Includes injection technique teaching, drug setup, patient education, and monitoring protocol.

RVU Information

CPT G0089 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

G-codes are CMS-specific HCPCS codes for services not covered by standard CPT. Documentation requirements follow the same standards as the equivalent CPT service. Check Medicare LCD/NCD policies for coverage criteria.
