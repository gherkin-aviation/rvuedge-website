G9505 Abx pres with in 10 dys of symp

G Codes

Also known as: antibiotic within 10 days, early antibiotic intervention

Documentation that prophylactic antibiotic therapy was prescribed or provided within ten days of symptom onset for eligible acute respiratory infection.

In Plain Language

antibiotics given within 10 days of getting sick

Clinical Context

Respiratory infection quality measure tracking timely antibiotic administration for conditions like acute sinusitis or acute otitis media.

RVU Information
