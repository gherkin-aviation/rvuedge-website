Also known as: body temp >=35.5C documented

HCPCS code documenting one body temperature measurement greater than or equal to 35.5 degrees Celsius.

Clinical Context

Documents core temperature measurement maintaining normothermia (>=35.5C) during intraoperative period for quality measure compliance.
