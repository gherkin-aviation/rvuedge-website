G9823 Endoscopic simple/hyst biopsy res doc

G-Codes

Also known as: endometrial biopsy documented, endo sample results documented

Healthcare quality measure for endometrial sampling or hysterectomy biopsy results with documented pathology findings.

Clinical Context

Quality measure tracking proper documentation of pathology results from endometrial sampling or hysterectomy biopsies in patients with abnormal uterine bleeding or suspected endometrial pathology.
