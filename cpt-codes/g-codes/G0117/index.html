G0117 Glaucoma screening hgh risk direc

G-Codes

Also known as: glaucoma screening, IOP measurement

Glaucoma screening in high-risk individuals with direct visualization and tonometry to measure intraocular pressure and assess for glaucoma risk.

Clinical Context

Preventive screening for glaucoma in high-risk populations (African American, family history, elevated intraocular pressure). Includes eye examination and tonometry measurement.
