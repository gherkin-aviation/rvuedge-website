G0148 Scr c/v cyto, autosys, rescr

G Codes

Also known as: automated cytology MD review, computer-assisted with physician review

Cervical and vaginal cytology screening using automated system with physician review and interpretation for comprehensive cervical cancer detection.

In Plain Language

Computer plus doctor reviewed cervical test; Automated then verified Pap test

Clinical Context

Automated cytology with mandatory physician review ensuring both automated accuracy and pathologist expertise in interpretation of cervical specimens.
