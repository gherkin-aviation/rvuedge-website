G0277 Hbot, full body chamber, 30m

G-Codes

Also known as: HBOT, HBO therapy, hyperbaric oxygen

Hyperbaric oxygen therapy administered in a full body chamber for 30 minutes, used to enhance tissue oxygenation in treating wounds and infections.

Clinical Context

HBOT is used for diabetic foot ulcers, non-healing wounds, carbon monoxide poisoning, and decompression sickness. Administered as a course of multiple sessions over weeks.
