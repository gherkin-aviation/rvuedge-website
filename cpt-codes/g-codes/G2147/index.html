G2147 Leg pain vas 9-15mo > 3

G Codes

Also known as: Leg pain assessment, VAS score long-term

HCPCS code documenting leg pain Visual Analog Scale score between 9-15 months post-operative, with improvement greater than 3. Positive long-term outcome.

In Plain Language

Leg pain improved more than 3 points at 9-15 months; Good long-term improvement

Clinical Context

Used for long-term spine surgery outcome evaluation. Documents meaningful radicular pain relief at 9-15 months.

RVU Information

CPT G2147 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
