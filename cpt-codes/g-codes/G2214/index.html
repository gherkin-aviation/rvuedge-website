G2214 initial/sub psychiatric care m first 30

G Codes

Also known as: psych intake, mental health start, behavioral health visit

Code for initiation or subsequent psychiatric care or mental health service in patient's first 30 minutes of contact.

In Plain Language

First mental health appointment; Psychiatric evaluation begun

Clinical Context

Used to report initial or follow-up psychiatric evaluation and management services, particularly for substance use disorders or mental health crises.
