G0533 Buprenorphone injection weekly

G Codes

Also known as: buprenorphine injection, weekly buprenorphine, Sublocade, Buvidal

Buprenorphine injection (subdermal or intramuscular) administered weekly for opioid use disorder management.

In Plain Language

Weekly opioid addiction injection; Long-acting addiction treatment; Opioid disorder medication injection

Clinical Context

Administration of long-acting buprenorphine injection weekly for maintenance opioid use disorder treatment.

RVU Information

CPT G0533 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
