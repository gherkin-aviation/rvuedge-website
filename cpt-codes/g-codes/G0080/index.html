G0080 Care manag h vst new pt 75 m

G-Codes

Also known as: care management home visit 75 min, extended intensive visit

Care management home visit for a new patient lasting approximately 75 minutes, including exhaustive assessment of complex medical and psychosocial needs with comprehensive care planning.

Clinical Context

Initial home visit for most complex new patients requiring extensive time for thorough evaluation, detailed history, and comprehensive care plan development with multiple stakeholders.
