G8950 Pre-htn or htn doc, follow-up indc

G Codes

Also known as: hypertension diagnosed and managed, pre-hypertension documented

Performance measure indicating documented diagnosis of hypertension or pre-hypertension with indicated follow-up plan during measurement period.

In Plain Language

high blood pressure was diagnosed with follow-up plan made; blood pressure elevated with treatment plan

Clinical Context

Quality measure documenting appropriate hypertension diagnosis and management planning for chronic disease control.

RVU Information
