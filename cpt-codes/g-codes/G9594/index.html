G9594 Pt mbht hd CT ord ec prov

G Codes

Also known as: missed CT ordering, imaging gap in child

Documentation that patient met criteria for head computed tomography but head computed tomography was not ordered by the provider in pediatric patient.

In Plain Language

child needed CT scan but didn't get one

Clinical Context

Pediatric emergency medicine quality measure identifying missed opportunities for appropriate head imaging in pediatric trauma.
