G2088 Off base opioid treatment, add30

G-Codes +Add-on

Also known as: Additional office-based OAT, OBAT add-on

HCPCS code for office-based opioid treatment add-on lasting 30 minutes. Extended service time during office visit.

Clinical Context

Used as add-on when office-based opioid treatment exceeds standard duration. Appropriate for complex medical or psychiatric comorbidities.
