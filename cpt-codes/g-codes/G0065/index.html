G0065 Chiropractic subtalar sinus

G Codes

Also known as: chiropractic service, spinal manipulation

Specialty supplemental or ancillary services provided by a licensed chiropractor for spinal manipulation, adjustment, and musculoskeletal condition management.

In Plain Language

Chiropractor back and spine care; Spinal adjustment treatment

Clinical Context

Represents chiropractic services for management of musculoskeletal conditions, primarily of the spine. Includes spinal manipulation, adjustment, and other chiropractic treatments for pain management.

RVU Information

CPT G0065 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

G-codes are CMS-specific HCPCS codes for services not covered by standard CPT. Documentation requirements follow the same standards as the equivalent CPT service. Check Medicare LCD/NCD policies for coverage criteria.
