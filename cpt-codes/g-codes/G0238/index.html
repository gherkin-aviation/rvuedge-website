G0238 Oth resp procedure, indiv

G Codes

Also known as: respiratory therapy, pulmonary procedure

Other respiratory procedure or therapy service provided in individual format for patients with respiratory insufficiency or pulmonary dysfunction.

In Plain Language

Breathing therapy treatment; Lung rehabilitation service

Clinical Context

Specialized respiratory procedures and therapies not captured by other codes for patients with COPD, interstitial lung disease, or respiratory failure.

RVU Information
