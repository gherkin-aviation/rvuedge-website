G0085 Care man h v external pt 75 m

G-Codes

Also known as: care management follow-up 75 min, intensive complex reassessment

Care management home visit for an established patient lasting approximately 75 minutes, including exhaustive reassessment, intensive care plan development, and comprehensive coordination activities.

Clinical Context

Follow-up home visit for established patients with severe complexity, multiple acute issues, or major care plan changes. Requires substantial time for reassessment and care coordination.
