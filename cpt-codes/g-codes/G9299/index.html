G9299 No evaluation risk vte card prior

G Codes

Also known as: VTE risk not assessed, thromboembolism risk undocumented

Evaluation of venous thromboembolism risk in cardiac patient was not documented.

In Plain Language

blood clot risk not checked; no thrombosis screening

Clinical Context

Quality metric exception when pre-operative venous thromboembolism risk assessment was not performed or documented for cardiac patients.

RVU Information

CPT G9299 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
