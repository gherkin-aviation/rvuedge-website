G9136 Onc diagnosis nhl transverse to lg bcell

G Codes

Also known as: NHL transformation to DLBCL, transformed lymphoma

Oncology diagnosis for non-Hodgkin lymphoma that has transformed to large B-cell lymphoma.

In Plain Language

lymphoma transformation; lymphoma changed to aggressive form

Clinical Context

Documentation of indolent non-Hodgkin lymphoma patients experiencing histologic transformation to large B-cell lymphoma, requiring more aggressive therapy.

RVU Information
