G8511 Scr dep pos, no plan doc rng

G-Codes

Also known as: Pos SUD screen; no plan, Positive substance screening, Treatment plan not documented

Documentation that substance use disorder screening result was positive without documentation of treatment plan during the specified reporting range.

Clinical Context

Quality measure identifying positive substance use disorder screens lacking documented management plans, indicating potential care gaps in addiction medicine or behavioral health integration.
