G0121 Colon ca screening not hi rsk ind

G-Codes Global 0d

Also known as: colonoscopy average risk, screening colonoscopy

Colorectal cancer screening using colonoscopy in individuals not at high risk for colorectal cancer, allowing visualization and polypectomy in average-risk patients.

Clinical Context

Colorectal cancer screening in average-risk, asymptomatic individuals. Includes complete colonic visualization, polyp removal, and biopsy capability for lesions.
