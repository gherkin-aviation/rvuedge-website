G0343 Laparotomy islet cell transp

G-Codes Global 90d

Also known as: open islet transplant, islet cell transplant open

Open surgical islet cell transplantation procedure using formal laparotomy to deliver insulin-producing cells to the pancreas.

Clinical Context

Open surgical approach for islet transplantation, typically reserved for complex cases or revision procedures. Includes full dissection and visualization.
