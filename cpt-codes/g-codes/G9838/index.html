G9838 Pt met dis at diagnosis

G Codes

Also known as: metastatic cancer at diagnosis, stage IV cancer at presentation

Healthcare quality measure documenting patient with metastatic cancer identified at diagnosis.

In Plain Language

cancer spread to other parts of body; advanced cancer at initial diagnosis

Clinical Context

Quality measure identifying cancer patients presenting with distant metastases at the time of initial diagnosis, indicating advanced disease requiring systemic therapy.

RVU Information

CPT G9838 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
