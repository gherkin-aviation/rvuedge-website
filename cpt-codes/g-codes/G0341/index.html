G0341 Percutaneous islet celltrans

G-Codes Global 0d

Also known as: percutaneous islet transplant, needle islet injection

Percutaneous islet cell transplantation procedure using minimally invasive needle approach to deliver insulin-producing cells to the pancreas.

Clinical Context

Performed for Type 1 diabetes, particularly after total pancreatectomy or in patients with brittle diabetes. Minimally invasive compared to surgical approaches.
