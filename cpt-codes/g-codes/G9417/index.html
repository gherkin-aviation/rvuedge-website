G9417 Pt not 1 tdap betw 10-13 yrs

G Codes

Also known as: Tdap vaccine not given, missed Tdap vaccination

Patient did not receive one dose of Tdap vaccine between ages 10 and 13 years.

In Plain Language

tetanus/pertussis vaccine not given at correct age; Tdap booster not completed

Clinical Context

Quality metric exception when adolescent does not receive Tdap vaccine booster during recommended age window of 10-13 years.

RVU Information

CPT G9417 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
