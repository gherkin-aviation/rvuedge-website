G0268 Removal of impacted wax md

G-Codes Global 0d

Also known as: cerumen impaction removal, earwax removal

Removal of impacted cerumen (earwax) from external auditory canal performed by a physician using appropriate instrumentation and techniques.

Clinical Context

Physician removal of cerumen impaction causing hearing loss, ear discomfort, or tinnitus. Includes examination, instrumentation, and patient education on prevention.
