G8602 No tpa initial with in 4.5 hour

G Codes

Also known as: tPA not given in time, Thrombolysis not performed, Missed thrombolysis window

Documentation that tissue plasminogen activator (tPA) thrombolytic therapy was not initiated within the 4.5-hour treatment window.

In Plain Language

Clot-busting drug not given in time; Thrombolytic therapy missed

Clinical Context

Quality measure identifying missed opportunities for acute stroke thrombolysis, important for quality improvement in stroke center protocols.

RVU Information

CPT G8602 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

G-codes are CMS-specific HCPCS codes for services not covered by standard CPT. Documentation requirements follow the same standards as the equivalent CPT service. Check Medicare LCD/NCD policies for coverage criteria.
