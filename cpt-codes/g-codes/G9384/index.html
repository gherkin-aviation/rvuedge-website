G9384 Doc medial rsn no hcv screening

G-Codes

Also known as: HCV screening not done with reason, contraindication to HCV test

Physician documented medical reason for not screening patient for hepatitis C virus infection.

Clinical Context

Quality measure exception when hepatitis C screening is not performed but physician documents legitimate medical contraindications or clinical reasons for omission.
