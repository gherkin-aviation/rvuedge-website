G8875 Breast cancer diagnosis minutes invsive

G Codes

Also known as: breast cancer diagnosed by biopsy, minimally invasive breast biopsy

Performance measure indicating breast cancer diagnosis was established using minimally invasive diagnostic technique.

In Plain Language

breast cancer was diagnosed with a small needle procedure; cancer confirmed with a breast biopsy

Clinical Context

Documents quality measure compliance for breast cancer diagnosis using core needle biopsy or excisional biopsy rather than non-invasive imaging alone.

RVU Information

CPT G8875 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
