G0235 Pet not otherwise specified

G Codes

Also known as: PET imaging, metabolic imaging

Positron emission tomography (PET) imaging not otherwise specified, for oncologic and non-oncologic indications requiring specialized metabolic imaging.

In Plain Language

Special scanning test for disease detection; Metabolic disease imaging

Clinical Context

PET imaging for various oncologic and non-oncologic conditions including dementia, cardiac viability, and complex diagnostic questions.

RVU Information
