G9908 No pt tbco cess interv rng

G Codes

Also known as: no tobacco cessation given, smoker not counseled

Healthcare quality measure documenting no tobacco cessation intervention provided to patient identified as tobacco user.

In Plain Language

tobacco user not offered help to quit; no cessation intervention

Clinical Context

Quality measure identifying care gaps where tobacco users were not offered cessation interventions.

RVU Information

CPT G9908 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
