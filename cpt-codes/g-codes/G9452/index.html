G9452 Doc medial reas no hcv test

G Codes

Also known as: HCV testing not done with reason, contraindication to HCV test

Physician documented medical reason for not performing hepatitis C virus antibody test in patient.

In Plain Language

medical reason why hepatitis C test not needed; valid reason for skipping HCV screening

Clinical Context

Quality measure exception when hepatitis C virus testing is not performed but physician documents legitimate medical contraindications or clinical reasons for omission.

RVU Information
