G9412 Admit with in 180d req surgical revision

G-Codes

Also known as: stent requiring surgery, surgical stent revision

Admission to hospital within 180 days requiring surgical revision of percutaneous coronary intervention stent.

Clinical Context

Quality measure tracking adverse outcome when percutaneous coronary intervention stent requires surgical revision via bypass or other surgical intervention within 180 days, indicating major stent-related complication.
