G0183 Software meas of cardiac vol

G Codes

Also known as: cardiac volume analysis, ejection fraction measurement

Software-based measurement and analysis of cardiac function including ventricular volumes, ejection fraction, and wall motion assessment.

In Plain Language

Computer measurement of heart function; Heart pumping strength assessment

Clinical Context

Quantitative analysis of cardiac function using echocardiography, cardiac CT, or cardiac MRI software. Used for diagnosis and monitoring of heart failure and cardiomyopathy.

RVU Information

CPT G0183 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
