G0379 Direct refer hospital observ

G Codes

Also known as: direct refer to observation, ED refer to obs

Physician referral and direct admission of patient to hospital observation care with initial evaluation and management.

In Plain Language

direct hospital monitoring admission

Clinical Context

Covers direct placement to observation status with physician evaluation and care coordination at time of referral.

RVU Information
