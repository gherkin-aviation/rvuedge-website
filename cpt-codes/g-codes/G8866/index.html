G8866 Doc pt reas no pneumococcal

G Codes

Also known as: patient refused pneumococcal vaccine, patient declined vaccination

Performance measure documenting patient-related reasons why pneumococcal vaccine was not administered.

In Plain Language

patient refused pneumonia vaccine; patient chose not to get the shot

Clinical Context

Documents patient refusal or decline of pneumococcal vaccination even when appropriate, important for compliance tracking and shared decision-making documentation.
