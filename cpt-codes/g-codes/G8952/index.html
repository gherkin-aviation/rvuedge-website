G8952 Pre-htn/htn, no follow-up, not gvn

G Codes

Also known as: hypertension not managed, no blood pressure follow-up

Performance measure indicating documented pre-hypertension or hypertension but no follow-up plan was documented and no antihypertensive therapy was given.

In Plain Language

high blood pressure was found but no treatment plan was made; blood pressure problem not addressed

Clinical Context

Documents instances where hypertension was identified but management plan or treatment was not initiated, indicating quality gap.

RVU Information

CPT G8952 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

G-codes are CMS-specific HCPCS codes for services not covered by standard CPT. Documentation requirements follow the same standards as the equivalent CPT service. Check Medicare LCD/NCD policies for coverage criteria.
