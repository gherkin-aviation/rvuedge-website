G2252 Brief chkin by md/qhp, 11-20

G-Codes

Also known as: medium visit, 11-20 minute check-in, brief E/M alternative

Code for brief clinical check-in or contact lasting 11-20 minutes by physician, advanced practice provider, or qualified healthcare personnel, not billed as evaluation and management.

Clinical Context

Used to report brief clinical contacts lasting 11-20 minutes in remote monitoring or urgent care settings that do not meet standard evaluation and management criteria.
