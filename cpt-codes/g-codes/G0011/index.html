G0011 Hiv prep counsel, md 15-30m

G-Codes

Also known as: HIV prevention counseling, pre-exposure prophylaxis counseling, PrEP counseling

Human immunodeficiency virus (HIV) prevention counseling provided by a physician lasting 15 to 30 minutes, including discussion of transmission risk and prevention strategies.

Clinical Context

Used for structured physician-provided counseling on HIV prevention, risk assessment, and pre-exposure prophylaxis for individuals at risk of HIV acquisition.
