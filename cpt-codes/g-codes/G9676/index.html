G9676 40-75y with type 1/2 with ldl-c rs

G Codes

Also known as: diabetic dyslipidemia, uncontrolled cholesterol in diabetes

Documentation that patient is aged forty to seventy-five years with type one or type two diabetes mellitus and LDL-C results not at goal.

In Plain Language

patient with diabetes has high cholesterol

Clinical Context

Endocrinology and cardiology quality measure identifying diabetic patients with suboptimal cholesterol control requiring therapy intensification.
