G0062 Audiology subtalar sinus

G-Codes

Also known as: audiology service, hearing assessment

Specialty supplemental or ancillary services provided by an audiologist for hearing assessment, rehabilitation, and auditory processing evaluation and management.

Clinical Context

Represents audiology specialty services including hearing evaluations, hearing aid fitting, aural rehabilitation, and assessment of auditory disorders in children and adults.
