G0337 Hospice evaluation preelecti

G-Codes

Also known as: hospice evaluation, hospice pre-election assessment, terminal prognosis eval

Physician-performed comprehensive hospice evaluation and assessment to determine medical eligibility and prognosis before hospice election.

Clinical Context

Required before patient formally elects hospice benefit. Includes physician documentation of terminal condition and prognosis of 6 months or less.
