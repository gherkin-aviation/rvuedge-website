G0546 Phone/internet ehr assess

G-Codes

Also known as: phone/internet assessment, virtual assessment, electronic communication eval

Phone or internet-based assessment and evaluation via electronic health record communication for medical decision-making.

Clinical Context

Assessment via phone or secure electronic messaging without face-to-face visit for initial evaluation or follow-up.
