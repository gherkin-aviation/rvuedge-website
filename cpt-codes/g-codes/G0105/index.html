G0105 Colorectal screening; hi risk ind

G Codes Global 0d

Also known as: colonoscopy high risk, screening colonoscopy

Colorectal cancer screening using colonoscopy in high-risk individuals, including complete visualization of the entire colon and rectum with biopsy and polyp removal capability.

In Plain Language

Complete colon exam with camera; Full bowel cancer screening

Clinical Context

Comprehensive colorectal cancer screening in high-risk patients (family history of polyps/cancer, inflammatory bowel disease). Allows complete colonic visualization and therapeutic interventions.
