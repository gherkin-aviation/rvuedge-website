G9762 Pt had >= 2-3 hpv vaccines

G Codes

Also known as: HPV vaccine series completed

HCPCS code identifying patients who received two to three doses of human papillomavirus vaccine.

In Plain Language

cervical cancer vaccine given

Clinical Context

Documents completion of HPV vaccination series (2-3 doses) for quality measure reporting in cervical cancer prevention programs.
