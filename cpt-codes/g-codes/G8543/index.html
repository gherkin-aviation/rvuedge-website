G8543 Cur funct asses; no care pln

G-Codes

Also known as: Functional assessment; no plan, Limitations not addressed, No intervention plan

Documentation that current functional status assessment was completed with functional limitations identified but no care plan was documented addressing the limitations.

Clinical Context

Quality measure identifying functional assessment findings without corresponding intervention planning, indicating potential care coordination gaps.
