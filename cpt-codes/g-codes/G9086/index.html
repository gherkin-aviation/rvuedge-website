G9086 Onc diagnosis colon t1-4 no diagnosis prog

G Codes

Also known as: colon cancer staging, colorectal cancer T1-T4, colon cancer no progression

Oncology diagnosis for colon cancer stages T1-T4 without distant metastasis and no evidence of disease progression.

In Plain Language

colon cancer diagnosis; bowel cancer staging without spread; early to locally advanced colon cancer

Clinical Context

Used in cancer surveillance and quality reporting for patients with colon cancer diagnosed at various local tumor stages without evidence of metastatic disease or recurrence.

RVU Information

CPT G9086 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation
