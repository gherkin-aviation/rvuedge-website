G0398 Home sleep test/type 2 porta

G Codes

Also known as: home sleep test Type 2, portable sleep apnea test, home sleep monitor

Home sleep apnea test using Type 2 portable sleep monitor for detection of obstructive sleep apnea in home environment.

In Plain Language

sleep apnea test at home; portable sleep study

Clinical Context

Less resource-intensive sleep testing for patients with high pretest probability of moderate-severe obstructive sleep apnea. Performed at home.

RVU Information

CPT G0398 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation
