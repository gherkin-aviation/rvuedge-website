G9547 Cys renal lesion or adren

G Codes

Also known as: renal findings, adrenal incidentaloma

Documentation that imaging study identified cysts of the kidney, renal lesion, or adrenal mass.

In Plain Language

bump found on kidney or adrenal gland

Clinical Context

Oncology and nephrology quality measure documenting detection of renal cysts and adrenal masses requiring appropriate follow-up.

RVU Information
