G9352 Not >1 sinus CT w 90d diagnosis

G-Codes

Also known as: appropriate sinus imaging, single sinus CT

Only one or no computed tomography scan of sinuses documented within 90 days of rhinosinusitis diagnosis.

Clinical Context

Quality measure tracking appropriate imaging utilization when one or fewer computed tomography scans of sinuses documented within 90 days of rhinosinusitis diagnosis.
