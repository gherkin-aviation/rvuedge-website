G0082 Care man h v external pt 30 m

G-Codes

Also known as: care management follow-up 30 min, established patient reassessment

Care management home visit for an established patient lasting approximately 30 minutes, including assessment update, care plan revision, and ongoing care coordination activities.

Clinical Context

Follow-up home visit for established patients with moderate care management needs. Includes periodic reassessment, plan updates, symptom monitoring, and care adjustments.
