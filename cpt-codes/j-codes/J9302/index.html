J9302 Ofatumumab injection

J-Codes

Also known as: ofatumumab, Arzerra

Injection of ofatumumab, an anti-CD20 monoclonal antibody. Human monoclonal antibody for chronic lymphocytic leukemia and follicular lymphoma.

Clinical Context

Used for chronic lymphocytic leukemia refractory to fludarabine and alemtuzumab. Provides alternative anti-CD20 targeting with direct cell lysis.
