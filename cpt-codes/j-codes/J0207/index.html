J0207 Amifostine

J-Codes

Also known as: amifostine, Ethyol

Amifostine injection, a cytoprotective agent for chemotherapy and radiation therapy side effects.

Clinical Context

Intravenous cytoprotective agent administered before chemotherapy or radiation to reduce xerostomia (dry mouth), mucositis, and other toxicities. Selective protection of normal tissues.
