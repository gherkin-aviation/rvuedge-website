J1453 Fosaprepitant injection

J-Codes

Also known as: fosaprepitant, Emend IV

Fosaprepitant injection. A neurokinin-1 receptor antagonist for chemotherapy-induced nausea and vomiting prevention.

Clinical Context

Used for highly emetogenic chemotherapy-induced nausea and vomiting (CINV) prevention. Intravenous formulation of aprepitant for patients unable to take oral medication.
