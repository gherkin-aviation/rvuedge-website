J3244 Injection. tigecycline (accord)

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: tigecycline, tigecycline Accord, broad-spectrum antibiotic

Tigecycline (Accord) injection is a broad-spectrum tetracycline antibiotic formulation by Accord Pharmaceuticals with same clinical indication as other tigecycline preparations.

In Plain Language

strong infection medication; resistant bacteria treatment

Clinical Context

Indicated for complicated skin/soft tissue infections, complicated intra-abdominal infections, and community-acquired pneumonia caused by resistant organisms. Same indications and administration as other tigecycline products.

RVU Information

CPT J3244 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
