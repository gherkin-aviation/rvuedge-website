J1808 Injection, folic acid, 0.1 milligrams

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: folic acid, vitamin B9

Injection of folic acid, 0.1 milligram. Used to treat or prevent folic acid deficiency and megaloblastic anemia.

In Plain Language

B vitamin injection; anemia treatment

Clinical Context

Used for folic acid deficiency, megaloblastic anemia, nutritional deficiency, and in chemotherapy regimens. Often given with other B vitamins in conjunction with vitamin B12.

RVU Information

CPT J1808 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
