J0520 Bethanechol chloride inject

J-Codes

Also known as: bethanechol injection, Urecholine injection

Bethanechol chloride injection for subcutaneous or intramuscular administration, a parasympathomimetic agent that promotes urinary and gastric motility.

Clinical Context

Used to treat urinary retention and gastroesophageal reflux by enhancing bladder and gastric smooth muscle contractions. Indicated post-operatively or in neurogenic bladder conditions.
