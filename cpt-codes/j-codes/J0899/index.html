J0899 Argatroban dialysis, auromed

J-Codes

Also known as: Argatroban Auromed dialysis, argatroban ESRD

Argatroban injection (Auromed pharmaceutical formulation) for patients on dialysis with end-stage renal disease, a direct thrombin inhibitor.

Clinical Context

Used for anticoagulation in dialysis patients with heparin-induced thrombocytopenia with Auromed formulation.
