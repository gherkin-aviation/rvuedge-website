J0577 Injection, brixadi, 7 days or less

J-Codes

Also known as: brixadi, buprenorphine monthly, bup injection

Brixadi injection, 7 days or less duration, a long-acting buprenorphine formulation for subcutaneous administration in opioid use disorder treatment.

Clinical Context

Extended-release subcutaneous buprenorphine injection providing therapeutic levels for up to 7 days, improving adherence in opioid use disorder treatment. Administered weekly for initial induction phase.
