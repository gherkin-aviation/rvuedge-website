J0136 Injection, acetaminophen (b braun)

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: acetaminophen injection, IV Tylenol B. Braun

Acetaminophen injection manufactured by B. Braun, a nonopioid analgesic and antipyretic.

In Plain Language

pain reliever injection; fever reducer injection

Clinical Context

Intravenous acetaminophen (B. Braun brand) for acute pain management and fever control. Used perioperatively or when oral route unavailable.

RVU Information

CPT J0136 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
