Injection of tislelizumab-jsgr, a programmed cell death ligand 1 (PD-L1) monoclonal antibody. Immune checkpoint inhibitor.

Clinical Context

Used for non-small cell lung cancer and other malignancies expressing PD-L1. Checkpoint inhibitor with reduced immunotoxicity profile.
