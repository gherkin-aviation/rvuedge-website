J0752 Hiv prep, oral lenacapavir

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: Lenacapavir PrEP, oral lenacapavir

Lenacapavir oral capsule, a capsid inhibitor for Human Immunodeficiency Virus pre-exposure prophylaxis with long-acting pharmacokinetics allowing infrequent dosing.

In Plain Language

Long-acting HIV prevention pill; Extended-interval HIV prevention

Clinical Context

Used for pre-exposure prophylaxis in individuals with high Human Immunodeficiency Virus acquisition risk, offering improved adherence due to extended dosing intervals with novel mechanism of action.

RVU Information

CPT J0752 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
