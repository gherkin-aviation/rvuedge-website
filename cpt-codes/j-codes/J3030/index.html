J3030 Sumatriptan succinate / 6 milligrams

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: sumatriptan injection, migraine injection

Sumatriptan succinate 6 milligrams injection is a selective serotonin receptor agonist administered by injection for acute migraine and cluster headache treatment.

In Plain Language

migraine headache shot; fast-acting headache medication

Clinical Context

Used for acute migraine and cluster headache treatment when oral formulations are unavailable or ineffective. Commonly administered in emergency departments and urgent care settings for severe migraine attacks.

RVU Information

CPT J3030 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
