J9360 Vinblastine sulfate injection

J-Codes

Also known as: vinblastine, Velban

Injection of vinblastine sulfate, a vinca alkaloid antitublin agent. Used for Hodgkin lymphoma and other malignancies.

Clinical Context

Component of ABVD regimen for Hodgkin lymphoma. Also used for testicular cancer and other solid tumors.
