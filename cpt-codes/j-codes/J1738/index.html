J1738 Injection. meloxicam 1 milligrams

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: meloxicam, NSAID injection

Meloxicam injection, 1 milligram, not otherwise specified. A nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory for pain and inflammation.

In Plain Language

pain and inflammation treatment; arthritis relief

Clinical Context

Used for management of moderate pain and inflammation from various musculoskeletal and inflammatory conditions. Intravenous administration.

RVU Information

CPT J1738 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
