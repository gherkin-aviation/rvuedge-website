J1438 Etanercept injection

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: etanercept, Enbrel, TNF inhibitor

Etanercept injection. A TNF-alpha inhibitor monoclonal antibody fusion protein for inflammatory disease treatment.

In Plain Language

immune system blocker; inflammation reducer injection

Clinical Context

Used for rheumatoid arthritis, psoriatic arthritis, ankylosing spondylitis, and other TNF-mediated inflammatory conditions. Subcutaneous or intravenous administration.

RVU Information

CPT J1438 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
