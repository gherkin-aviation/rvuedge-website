J0281 Injection aminocaproic acid 1 gram

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: aminocaproic acid, Amicar

Aminocaproic acid injection 1 gram, a fibrinolysis inhibitor for bleeding disorders.

In Plain Language

blood clot stabilizer; bleeding prevention medication

Clinical Context

Intravenous antifibrinolytic agent that inhibits plasmin activity. Used for postoperative bleeding, hereditary angioedema, and hemorrhage from thrombolytic therapy.

RVU Information

CPT J0281 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
