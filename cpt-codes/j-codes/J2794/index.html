J2794 Injection risperdal consta, 0.5 milligrams

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: risperdal consta, risperidone microspheres

Injection of risperidone long-acting (Risperdal Consta), 0.5 milligrams, a long-acting antipsychotic administered intramuscularly every 2 weeks.

In Plain Language

long-acting antipsychotic injection

Clinical Context

Used for maintenance treatment of schizophrenia and bipolar I disorder in stable patients requiring long-acting antipsychotic therapy.

RVU Information

CPT J2794 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
