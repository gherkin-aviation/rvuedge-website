J3415 Pyridoxine hcl 100 milligrams

J-Codes

Also known as: pyridoxine, Vitamin B6, pyridoxine HCl

Pyridoxine hydrochloride (Vitamin B6) 100 milligram injection is used for vitamin B6 deficiency and medication-induced neuropathy prevention.

Clinical Context

Used for vitamin B6 deficiency, isoniazid-induced neuropathy prevention in tuberculosis treatment, and certain metabolic disorders. Administered intramuscularly or intravenously.
