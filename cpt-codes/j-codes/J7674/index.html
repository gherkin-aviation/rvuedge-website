J7674 Methacholine chloride, neb

J-Codes

Also known as: Provocal, Methacholine challenge test

Methacholine chloride delivered by nebulizer; a cholinergic agent used for bronchial hyperresponsiveness testing in asthma diagnosis.

Clinical Context

Used in pulmonary function testing to diagnose asthma by measuring airway hyperresponsiveness. Causes bronchospasm in asthmatic patients.
