J7674 Methacholine chloride, neb

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: Provocal, Methacholine challenge test

Methacholine chloride delivered by nebulizer; a cholinergic agent used for bronchial hyperresponsiveness testing in asthma diagnosis.

In Plain Language

Asthma diagnostic test; Breathing challenge test medication

Clinical Context

Used in pulmonary function testing to diagnose asthma by measuring airway hyperresponsiveness. Causes bronchospasm in asthmatic patients.

RVU Information

CPT J7674 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
