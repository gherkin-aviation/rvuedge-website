J7178 Injection human fibrinogen con not otherwise specified

J-Codes

Also known as: fibrinogen concentrate, fibrin glue precursor, clotting factor

Injection of human fibrinogen concentrate, a purified plasma-derived fibrinogen product, not otherwise specified, supplied per dose.

Clinical Context

Used for prophylaxis or treatment of bleeding in patients with congenital or acquired fibrinogen deficiencies.
