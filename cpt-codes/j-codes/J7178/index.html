J7178 Injection human fibrinogen con not otherwise specified

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: fibrinogen concentrate, fibrin glue precursor, clotting factor

Injection of human fibrinogen concentrate, a purified plasma-derived fibrinogen product, not otherwise specified, supplied per dose.

In Plain Language

fibrinogen replacement; bleeding prevention

Clinical Context

Used for prophylaxis or treatment of bleeding in patients with congenital or acquired fibrinogen deficiencies.

RVU Information

CPT J7178 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
