J1110 Injection dihydroergotamine mesylt

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: DHE 45, dihydroergotamine, ergot derivative

Dihydroergotamine mesylate injection, an ergot alkaloid for treatment of migraine and other vascular headaches.

In Plain Language

Migraine headache injection

Clinical Context

Used for acute migraine and status migrainosus in emergency and acute care settings, particularly effective for severe migraine with aura.

RVU Information

CPT J1110 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
