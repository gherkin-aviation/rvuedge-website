J7325 Synvisc or synvisc-one

J-Codes

Also known as: Synvisc, Synvisc-One, hylan G-F 20

Injection of Synvisc or Synvisc-One (hylan G-F 20), a high-molecular-weight hyaluronate derivative viscosupplement.

Clinical Context

Used for intra-articular viscosupplementation in osteoarthritis, cross-linked hyaluronate provides extended duration of action.
