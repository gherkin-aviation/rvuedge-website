J3095 Telavancin injection

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: telavancin, Vibativ, lipoglycopeptide antibiotic

Telavancin injection is a lipoglycopeptide antibiotic effective against gram-positive bacteria including vancomycin-resistant organisms, used for complicated skin and respiratory infections.

In Plain Language

advanced antibiotic injection; resistant infection treatment

Clinical Context

Indicated for complicated skin and soft tissue infections and hospital-acquired/ventilator-associated bacterial pneumonia. Used when standard antibiotics are ineffective. Requires renal function monitoring.

RVU Information

CPT J3095 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
