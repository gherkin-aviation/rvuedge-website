J8498 Antiemetic rectal/supp not otherwise specified

J-Codes

Also known as: Rectal antiemetic, Suppository anti-nausea medication

Antiemetic drugs delivered rectally or as suppositories, not otherwise specified; used to treat nausea and vomiting.

Clinical Context

Used in patients with nausea and vomiting who cannot take oral medications. Common in cancer chemotherapy and postoperative settings.
