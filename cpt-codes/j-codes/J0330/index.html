J0330 Succinycholine chloride injection

J-Codes

Also known as: succinylcholine, Anectine

Succinylcholine chloride injection, a depolarizing neuromuscular blocking agent for anesthesia.

Clinical Context

Rapid-onset depolarizing neuromuscular blocker for rapid sequence intubation and short procedures. Rapidly metabolized by pseudocholinesterase.
