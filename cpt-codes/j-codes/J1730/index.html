J1730 Diazoxide injection

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: diazoxide, Hyperstat

Diazoxide injection. A non-diuretic thiazide for severe hypoglycemia and hypertensive crisis management.

In Plain Language

blood sugar emergency medication; blood pressure emergency treatment

Clinical Context

Used for management of severe hypoglycemia from hypersecretion of insulin and hypertensive crises. Rapid intravenous injection for acute management.

RVU Information

CPT J1730 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
