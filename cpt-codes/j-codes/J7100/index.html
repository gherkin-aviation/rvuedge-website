J7100 Dextran 40 infusion

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: Dextran 40, LMD, low molecular weight dextran

Dextran 40 infusion is a plasma volume expander used for fluid resuscitation and microvascular circulation improvement.

In Plain Language

blood volume expander; circulation support fluid

Clinical Context

Plasma volume expander for trauma resuscitation and vascular surgery. Low molecular weight dextran with shorter duration than Dextran 75. Improves microvascular circulation and reduces platelet aggregation.

RVU Information

CPT J7100 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
