J7100 Dextran 40 infusion

J-Codes

Also known as: Dextran 40, LMD, low molecular weight dextran

Dextran 40 infusion is a plasma volume expander used for fluid resuscitation and microvascular circulation improvement.

Clinical Context

Plasma volume expander for trauma resuscitation and vascular surgery. Low molecular weight dextran with shorter duration than Dextran 75. Improves microvascular circulation and reduces platelet aggregation.
