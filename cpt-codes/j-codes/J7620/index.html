J7620 Albuterol ipratrop non-comp

J-Codes

Also known as: Combivent, Albuterol-ipratropium

Albuterol and ipratropium bromide combination delivered by inhalation in a non-compact form; used for maintenance and acute relief in chronic obstructive pulmonary disease.

Clinical Context

Used in COPD patients requiring both short-acting beta-2 agonist and anticholinergic effects for bronchodilation and symptom relief.
