J7355 Injection travoprost intra implant

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: travoprost implant, glaucoma implant, IOP-lowering implant

Injection of travoprost intracameral implant, a prostaglandin F-alpha analog implant for intraocular pressure reduction.

In Plain Language

glaucoma implant; eye pressure reduction

Clinical Context

Indicated for reduction of intraocular pressure in glaucoma and ocular hypertension through sustained prostaglandin analog delivery.

RVU Information

CPT J7355 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
