J1412 Injection roctavian milliliters 2x10^13vc g

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: roctavian, fitusiran gene therapy

Roctavian (fitusiran) injection, 2x10^13 vector copies per dose. A gene therapy for hemophilia A treatment via intravenous infusion.

In Plain Language

hemophilia A gene therapy; clotting factor gene treatment

Clinical Context

Used for severe hemophilia A. Gene therapy designed to enhance Factor VIII production through genetic modification. Single administration can provide sustained therapeutic benefit.

RVU Information

CPT J1412 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
