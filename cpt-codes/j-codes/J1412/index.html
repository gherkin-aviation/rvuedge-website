J1412 Injection roctavian milliliters 2x10^13vc g

J-Codes

Also known as: roctavian, fitusiran gene therapy

Roctavian (fitusiran) injection, 2x10^13 vector copies per dose. A gene therapy for hemophilia A treatment via intravenous infusion.

Clinical Context

Used for severe hemophilia A. Gene therapy designed to enhance Factor VIII production through genetic modification. Single administration can provide sustained therapeutic benefit.
