J3375 Injection vancomycin (xellia) 10mg

J-Codes

Also known as: vancomycin, vancomycin Xellia, glycopeptide antibiotic

Vancomycin (Xellia) 10 milligram injection is a vancomycin formulation by Xellia Pharmaceuticals for methicillin-resistant Staphylococcus aureus and gram-positive infections.

Clinical Context

Used for serious gram-positive infections including methicillin-resistant Staphylococcus aureus sepsis. Xellia Pharmaceuticals formulation with equivalent clinical efficacy to other vancomycin products.
