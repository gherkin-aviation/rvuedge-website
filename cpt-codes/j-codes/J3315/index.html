J3315 Triptorelin pamoate

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: triptorelin, Trelstar, GnRH agonist

Triptorelin pamoate injection is a gonadotropin-releasing hormone agonist used for prostate cancer and endometriosis treatment.

In Plain Language

hormone suppression injection; prostate cancer medication

Clinical Context

Indicated for advanced prostate cancer requiring hormone suppression. Also used for endometriosis management. Gonadotropin-releasing hormone agonist that suppresses luteinizing hormone and follicle-stimulating hormone.

RVU Information

CPT J3315 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
