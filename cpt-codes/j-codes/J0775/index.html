J0775 Collagenase, clost hist injection

J-Codes

Also known as: Enzymatic debridement, collagenase injection

Collagenase clostridium histolyticum injection, an enzymatic debriding agent that breaks down collagen to remove necrotic tissue in wounds.

Clinical Context

Used for enzymatic debridement of necrotic tissue in chronic wounds, pressure ulcers, and burn wounds as adjunctive treatment to surgical debridement.
