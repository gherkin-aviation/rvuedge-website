J2472 Injection, pantoprazole sodium chl

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: pantoprazole sodium chloride, pantoprazole

Injection of pantoprazole sodium chloride formulation, used for intravenous acid suppression and prevention of acid-related gastrointestinal complications.

In Plain Language

stomach acid medication

Clinical Context

Administered intravenously for acute gastric acid suppression in perioperative and critical care patients when oral proton pump inhibitor therapy is unavailable.

RVU Information

CPT J2472 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
