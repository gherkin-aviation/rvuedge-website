J2249 Injection, remimazolam, 1 milligrams

J-Codes

Also known as: remimazolam, Byfavo

Injection of remimazolam, 1 milligram. An ultra-short-acting benzodiazepine for procedural sedation.

Clinical Context

Used for procedural sedation in anesthesia with rapid onset and offset. Remimazolam is a newer benzodiazepine with improved pharmacokinetics.
