J2781 Injection, pegcetacoplan, 1mg

J-Codes

Also known as: pegcetacoplan, empaveli

Injection of pegcetacoplan (Empaveli), 1 milligram, a complement C3 inhibitor used for treatment of paroxysmal nocturnal hemoglobinuria (PNH).

Clinical Context

Used for treatment of paroxysmal nocturnal hemoglobinuria (PNH) to prevent complement-mediated hemolysis and thrombosis.
