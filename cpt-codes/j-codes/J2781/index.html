J2781 Injection, pegcetacoplan, 1mg

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: pegcetacoplan, empaveli

Injection of pegcetacoplan (Empaveli), 1 milligram, a complement C3 inhibitor used for treatment of paroxysmal nocturnal hemoglobinuria (PNH).

In Plain Language

blood cell protection medication

Clinical Context

Used for treatment of paroxysmal nocturnal hemoglobinuria (PNH) to prevent complement-mediated hemolysis and thrombosis.

RVU Information

CPT J2781 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
