J2545 Pentamidine non-comp unit

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: pentamidine, nebulized pentamidine

Injection of pentamidine isethionate, per unit dose, for prophylaxis and treatment of Pneumocystis pneumonia in non-compliant or intravenous patient populations.

In Plain Language

PCP prevention medication

Clinical Context

Administered intravenously or by inhalation for PCP prophylaxis in HIV-infected patients with CD4+ T-cell counts below 200 cells/mm3.

RVU Information

CPT J2545 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
