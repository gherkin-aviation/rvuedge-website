J1642 Injection heparin sodium per 10 u

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: heparin, unfractionated heparin, UFH

Heparin sodium injection, per 10 units. An anticoagulant for thromboembolism prevention and treatment.

In Plain Language

blood thinner injection; clot prevention medication

Clinical Context

Used for acute thromboembolism treatment, thromboprophylaxis, and anticoagulation. Intravenous or subcutaneous administration with activated partial thromboplastin time monitoring.

RVU Information

CPT J1642 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
