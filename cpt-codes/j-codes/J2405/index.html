J2405 Ondansetron hcl injection

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: ondansetron, zofran

Injection of ondansetron hydrochloride (HCl), a selective 5-hydroxytryptamine 3 (5-HT3) receptor antagonist used for postoperative nausea and vomiting (PONV) prevention.

In Plain Language

nausea and vomiting prevention; anti-nausea medication

Clinical Context

Administered prophylactically or therapeutically in operating rooms, recovery areas, and cancer chemotherapy settings to prevent or treat nausea and vomiting.

RVU Information

CPT J2405 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
