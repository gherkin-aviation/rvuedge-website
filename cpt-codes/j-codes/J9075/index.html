J9075 Injection, cyclophosphamide, not otherwise specified

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: cyclophosphamide, Cytoxan

Intravenous injection of cyclophosphamide, an alkylating agent chemotherapy, not otherwise specified.

In Plain Language

DNA-damaging alkylating cancer drug; immune-suppressing chemotherapy

Clinical Context

Used for hematologic and solid malignancies and autoimmune diseases. Forms DNA cross-links and is immunosuppressive.

RVU Information

CPT J9075 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
