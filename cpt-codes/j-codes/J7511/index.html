J7511 Antithymocyte globuln rabbit

Also known as: ATG rabbit, antithymocyte globulin, Thymoglobulin

Injection of antithymocyte globulin rabbit (ATG rabbit), a polyclonal immunoglobulin for induction immunosuppression in transplant.

Clinical Context

Used for induction immunosuppression in organ transplant recipients and treatment of acute cellular rejection.
