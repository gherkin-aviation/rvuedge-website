J0602 Sevelamer carbonate pdr 20mg

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: sevelamer carbonate, Renvela powder, phosphate binder

Sevelamer carbonate powder, 20 milligrams, a phosphate-binding polymer for treatment of hyperphosphatemia with flexible dosing in dialysis patients.

In Plain Language

phosphate control powder for kidney disease; dissolvable phosphate medication

Clinical Context

Powdered form of sevelamer carbonate allowing flexible dosing and easier administration in patients with swallowing difficulties. Same phosphate-binding mechanism as tablet form.

RVU Information

CPT J0602 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
