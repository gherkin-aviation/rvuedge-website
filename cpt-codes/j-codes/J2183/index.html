J2183 Injection meropenem (wg critical care)

J-Codes

Also known as: meropenem

Injection of meropenem (weight-based critical care dosing). A carbapenem antibiotic for serious infections.

Clinical Context

Used for serious infections including sepsis, meningitis, and hospital-acquired infections. Meropenem has broad gram-negative and gram-positive coverage.
