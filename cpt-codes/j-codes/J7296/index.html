J7296 Kyleena, 19.5 milligrams

J-Codes

Also known as: Kyleena, hormonal IUD, levonorgestrel IUS

Insertion of Kyleena (levonorgestrel-releasing intrauterine system), 19.5 milligrams, a 3-year reversible contraceptive device.

Clinical Context

Indicated for contraception and treatment of heavy menstrual bleeding in women, provides 3-year protection with local hormonal release.
