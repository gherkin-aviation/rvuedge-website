J7313 Injection., iluvien, 0.01 milligrams

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: Iluvien, fluocinolone implant, steroid implant

Injection of Iluvien (fluocinolone acetonide intravitreal implant), 0.01 milligram, a long-acting ophthalmic steroid implant.

In Plain Language

eye steroid implant; inflammation treatment

Clinical Context

Indicated for treatment of chronic uveitis affecting the posterior segment, provides up to 3 years of corticosteroid delivery.

RVU Information

CPT J7313 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
