J7322 Hyaluronan, hymo or hymo one

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: Hymo, hyaluronic acid, sodium hyaluronate

Injection of hyaluronic acid (Hymo or Hymo One), a viscous sodium hyaluronate preparation for intra-articular viscosupplementation.

In Plain Language

joint lubrication injection; arthritis treatment

Clinical Context

Used for intra-articular injection to relieve osteoarthritis symptoms through joint fluid restoration and lubrication.

RVU Information

CPT J7322 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
