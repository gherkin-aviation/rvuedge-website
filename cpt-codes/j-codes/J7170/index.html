J7170 Injection., emicizumab-kxwh 0.5 milligrams

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: emicizumab, Hemlibra, bridging antibody

Injection of emicizumab-kxwh, a bispecific monoclonal antibody that bridges Factor Xa and Factor IXa in the coagulation cascade, supplied as 0.5 milligrams per dose.

In Plain Language

clotting factor replacement therapy; hemophilia A treatment

Clinical Context

Indicated for prophylaxis of breakthrough bleeding in hemophilia A patients with Factor VIII inhibitors (inhibitor patients), administered as prophylactic therapy to prevent bleeding episodes.

RVU Information

CPT J7170 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
