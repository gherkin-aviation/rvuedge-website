J0132 Acetylcysteine injection

J-Codes

Also known as: acetylcysteine, N-acetylcysteine, NAC

Acetylcysteine injection, an antioxidant and mucolytic agent used primarily as an antidote for acetaminophen overdose.

Clinical Context

Intravenous acetylcysteine used as life-saving antidote for acetaminophen (Tylenol) overdose. Also used in critical illness for antioxidant support. Most important use is paracetamol/acetaminophen toxicity.
