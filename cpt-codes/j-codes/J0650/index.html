J0650 Injection, levothyroxine not otherwise specified 10mcg

J-Codes

Also known as: levothyroxine injection, T4 injection, synthetic thyroid hormone

Levothyroxine sodium injection, not otherwise specified, 10 micrograms, a synthetic thyroid hormone for acute hypothyroidism and myxedema coma.

Clinical Context

Intravenous levothyroxine for emergency treatment of hypothyroidism complications including myxedema coma. Provides immediate thyroid hormone replacement when oral administration is not feasible.
