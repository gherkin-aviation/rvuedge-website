J0650 Injection, levothyroxine not otherwise specified 10mcg

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: levothyroxine injection, T4 injection, synthetic thyroid hormone

Levothyroxine sodium injection, not otherwise specified, 10 micrograms, a synthetic thyroid hormone for acute hypothyroidism and myxedema coma.

In Plain Language

thyroid hormone emergency injection; hypothyroidism crisis treatment

Clinical Context

Intravenous levothyroxine for emergency treatment of hypothyroidism complications including myxedema coma. Provides immediate thyroid hormone replacement when oral administration is not feasible.

RVU Information

CPT J0650 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
