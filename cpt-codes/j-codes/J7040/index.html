J7040 Normal saline solution infusion

J-Codes

Also known as: normal saline, NS, 0.9% saline

Normal saline solution infusion is intravenous 0.9 percent sodium chloride solution for fluid replacement and medication dilution.

Clinical Context

Used for intravenous hydration, electrolyte maintenance, and medication dilution. Isotonic solution for systemic administration. Primary crystalloid solution for volume replacement.
