J3032 Injection. eptinezumab-jjmr 1 milligrams

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: eptinezumab, Vyepti, CGRP receptor antagonist

Eptinezumab-jjmr (Vyepti) 1 milligram injection is a monoclonal antibody targeting calcitonin gene-related peptide receptor for intravenous chronic migraine prevention.

In Plain Language

migraine prevention IV infusion; quarterly migraine treatment

Clinical Context

FDA-approved for chronic migraine prevention, administered intravenously every 12 weeks. Blocks calcitonin gene-related peptide receptor to reduce neuronal inflammation and migraine frequency in chronic migraine patients.

RVU Information

CPT J3032 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
