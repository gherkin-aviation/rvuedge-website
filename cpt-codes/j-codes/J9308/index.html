J9308 Injection, ramucirumab

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: ramucirumab, Cyramza

Injection of ramucirumab, a vascular endothelial growth factor receptor 2 monoclonal antibody. Angiogenesis inhibitor for solid tumors.

In Plain Language

cancer blood vessel injection; angiogenesis inhibitor

Clinical Context

Used for gastric cancer, non-small cell lung cancer, and colorectal cancer. Inhibits tumor angiogenesis and vascular endothelial growth factor signaling.

RVU Information

CPT J9308 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
