J1746 Injection., ibalizumab-uiyk, 10 milligrams

J-Codes

Also known as: ibalizumab-uiyk, Trogarzo, HIV entry inhibitor

Ibalizumab-uiyk injection, 10 milligrams. A post-attachment HIV inhibitor for HIV-1 infection treatment.

Clinical Context

Used for HIV-1 infection in treatment-experienced patients with multidrug-resistant virus. Intravenous administration in combination with other antiretroviral agents.
