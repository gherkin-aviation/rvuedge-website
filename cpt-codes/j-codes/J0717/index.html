J0717 Certolizumab pegol injection 1mg

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: certolizumab pegol, Cimzia, TNF-alpha antagonist

Certolizumab pegol injection, 1 milligram, a TNF-alpha antagonist monoclonal antibody fragment for treatment of inflammatory diseases.

In Plain Language

inflammation blocker injection; rheumatoid arthritis and inflammatory disease treatment

Clinical Context

Pegylated TNF-alpha antagonist monoclonal antibody fragment for treatment of rheumatoid arthritis, Crohn disease, and other inflammatory conditions. Can be used during pregnancy.

RVU Information

CPT J0717 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
