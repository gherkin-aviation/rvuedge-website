J3485 Zidovudine

J-Codes

Also known as: zidovudine, Retrovir, AZT

Zidovudine (Retrovir) injection is a nucleoside reverse transcriptase inhibitor used for human immunodeficiency virus treatment.

Clinical Context

Used for human immunodeficiency virus infection management. Nucleoside reverse transcriptase inhibitor administered intravenously. Now typically used in combination antiretroviral therapy for human immunodeficiency virus.
