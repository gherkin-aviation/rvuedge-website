J7667 Metaproterenol complicated con

J-Codes

Also known as: Alupent compact, Metaproterenol compact

Metaproterenol delivered by inhalation in a compact container form; a beta-2 adrenergic agonist for acute relief of bronchospasm.

Clinical Context

Used for acute bronchospasm relief in patients with asthma or COPD. Beta-2 selective with moderate duration of action.
