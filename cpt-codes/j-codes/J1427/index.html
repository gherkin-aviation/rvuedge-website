J1427 Injection. viltolarsen

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: viltolarsen, DMD exon 53 therapy

Viltolarsen injection. An antisense oligonucleotide for Duchenne muscular dystrophy exon 53 mutation treatment.

In Plain Language

muscular dystrophy genetic treatment; muscle-wasting disease injection

Clinical Context

Used for Duchenne muscular dystrophy in patients with exon 53 deletions. Antisense oligonucleotide that facilitates exon 53 skipping to restore dystrophin production and slow disease progression.

RVU Information

CPT J1427 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
