J9289 Injection nivolumab 2 milligrams hyaluron

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: nivolumab hyaluronidase, Opdivo subcutaneous

Injection of nivolumab combined with hyaluronidase, 2 milligram. A PD-1 checkpoint inhibitor with enhanced subcutaneous delivery formulation.

In Plain Language

immunotherapy injection; cancer immune checkpoint drug

Clinical Context

Subcutaneous formulation of nivolumab used for melanoma, non-small cell lung cancer, renal cell carcinoma, and other malignancies.

RVU Information

CPT J9289 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
