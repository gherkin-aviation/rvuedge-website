J3402 Injection. remestemcel-l-rknd/ td

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: remestemcel-l, Alofisel/Stemirac, mesenchymal stem cells

Remestemcel-l-rknd/td injection is allogeneic mesenchymal stem cells for graft-versus-host disease treatment.

In Plain Language

stem cell therapy; immune rejection treatment

Clinical Context

Allogeneic bone marrow-derived mesenchymal stem cells for steroid-refractory graft-versus-host disease. Intravenous or local administration to reduce inflammation and promote tissue healing in hematopoietic stem cell transplant patients.

RVU Information

CPT J3402 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
