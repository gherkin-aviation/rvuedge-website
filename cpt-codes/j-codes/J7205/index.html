J7205 Factor viii fc fusion recomb

Also known as: Factor VIII Fc-fusion, extended Factor VIII, Eloctate

Injection of Factor VIII Fc-fusion recombinant, an extended half-life recombinant Factor VIII with Fc immunoglobulin fusion.

Clinical Context

Used for routine prophylaxis and on-demand treatment in hemophilia A patients, prolonged half-life allows less frequent dosing.
