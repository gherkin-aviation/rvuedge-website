J7665 Mannitol for inhaler

J-Codes

Also known as: Asmaneex, Inhaled mannitol

Mannitol delivered by inhalation for use with portable inhalers; an osmotic agent used as a bronchial challenge test agent or to treat bronchospasm.

Clinical Context

Used for bronchial hyperresponsiveness testing in asthma diagnosis or as a therapeutic agent in patients with asthma. Osmotic mechanism.
