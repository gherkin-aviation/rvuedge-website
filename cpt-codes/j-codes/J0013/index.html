J0013 Esketamine, nasal spray

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: esketamine nasal spray, Spravato

Esketamine nasal spray injection, a rapid-acting dissociative anesthetic used for depression and anesthesia adjuncts.

In Plain Language

nasal spray antidepressant medication; fast-acting depression treatment

Clinical Context

FDA-approved rapid-acting antidepressant for treatment-resistant depression and major depressive disorder. Also used as anesthetic adjunct. Administered under medical supervision in healthcare setting.

RVU Information

CPT J0013 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
