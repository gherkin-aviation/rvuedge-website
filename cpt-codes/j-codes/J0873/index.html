J0873 Injection daptomycin (xellia)

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: Daptomycin Xellia, daptomycin generic

Daptomycin injection (Xellia pharmaceutical formulation), a cyclic lipopeptide antibiotic supplied for treatment of serious gram-positive bacterial infections.

In Plain Language

Antibiotic for serious resistant infections

Clinical Context

Used for Methicillin-resistant Staphylococcus aureus infections, endocarditis, and complicated skin and soft tissue infections.

RVU Information

CPT J0873 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
