J9600 Porfimer sodium injection

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: porfimer sodium, Photofrin

Injection of porfimer sodium, a photosensitizing porphyrin derivative. Used for photodynamic therapy in advanced cancer.

In Plain Language

light-activated cancer drug; photodynamic therapy injection

Clinical Context

Used for obstructing esophageal and endobronchial cancers via photodynamic therapy. Activated by red light to produce singlet oxygen.

RVU Information

CPT J9600 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
