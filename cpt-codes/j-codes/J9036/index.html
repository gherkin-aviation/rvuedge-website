J9036 Injection. belrapzo/bendamustine

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: bendamustine, Belrapzo

Intravenous injection of bendamustine/belrapzodone combination therapy or bendamustine (Belrapzo brand) alkylating agent chemotherapy.

In Plain Language

dual-action chemotherapy drug; alkylating cancer agent

Clinical Context

Brand-specific formulation of bendamustine for hematologic malignancies including chronic lymphocytic leukemia and indolent lymphomas.

RVU Information

CPT J9036 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
