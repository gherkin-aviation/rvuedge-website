J0896 Injection luspatercept-aamt 0.25mg

J-Codes

Also known as: Reblozyl, luspatercept, maturation agent

Luspatercept-aamt injection supplied in 0.25 milligram units, an erythroid maturation agent for treatment of anemia in myelodysplastic syndrome.

Clinical Context

Used for treatment of anemia in patients with lower-risk myelodysplastic syndrome requiring transfusion support.
