J0600 Edetate calcium disodium injection

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: edetate calcium disodium, calcium EDTA, CaNa2EDTA

Edetate calcium disodium injection for intravenous or intramuscular administration, a chelating agent for treatment of lead poisoning and heavy metal toxicity.

In Plain Language

lead poisoning treatment; heavy metal removal injection

Clinical Context

Chelating agent that binds and removes lead and other heavy metals from blood and soft tissues. Used for symptomatic and asymptomatic lead poisoning with elevated blood lead levels.

RVU Information

CPT J0600 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
