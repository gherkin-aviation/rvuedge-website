J9359 Injection lon tesirin-lpyl 0.075mg

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: loncastuximab tesirin, Zynlonta

Injection of loncastuximab tesirin-lpyl, 0.075 milligram. An antibody-drug conjugate targeting CD19 for B-cell lymphoma.

In Plain Language

lymphoma injection; antibody-drug combination

Clinical Context

Used for relapsed/refractory diffuse large B-cell lymphoma. CD19-targeting antibody-drug conjugate with topoisomerase I inhibitor.

RVU Information

CPT J9359 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
