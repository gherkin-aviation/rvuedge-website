J1700 Hydrocortisone acetate injection

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: hydrocortisone acetate, intra-articular steroid

Hydrocortisone acetate injection. A corticosteroid for inflammation and adrenal insufficiency management.

In Plain Language

inflammation-reducing steroid injection; cortisone shot

Clinical Context

Used for intra-articular injection for joint inflammation, acute adrenal insufficiency, and severe inflammatory conditions. Intramuscular, intravenous, or intra-articular administration.

RVU Information

CPT J1700 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
