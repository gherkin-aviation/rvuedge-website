J7649 Isoetharine non-comp unit

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: Isoetharine unit dose, Isoetharine single dose

Isoetharine delivered by inhalation in a non-compact unit dose form; a beta-2 adrenergic agonist for acute relief of bronchospasm.

In Plain Language

Quick-relief asthma/COPD inhaler; Breathing medication

Clinical Context

Used for acute bronchospasm relief in patients with asthma or COPD. Single dose unit form for convenient delivery.

RVU Information

CPT J7649 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
