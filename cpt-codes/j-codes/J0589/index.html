J0589 Injection daxibotulinumtoxina-lanm

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: daxibotulinumtoxin A-lanm, Daxxify, botulinum toxin A

Daxibotulinumtoxin A-lanm injection, a botulinum toxin with nanoparticle formulation for enhanced tissue penetration in muscle disorders and spasticity.

In Plain Language

longer-lasting muscle relaxer; extended spasticity treatment

Clinical Context

Newest botulinum toxin formulation with extended duration of 6 months or longer (versus standard 3-4 months), used for cervical dystonia, blepharospasm, and cosmetic applications.

RVU Information

CPT J0589 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
