J0220 Alglucosidase alfa injection

J-Codes

Also known as: alglucosidase alfa, Myozyme

Alglucosidase alfa injection, a recombinant enzyme replacement therapy for Pompe disease.

Clinical Context

Intravenous enzyme replacement for Pompe disease (acid alpha-glucosidase deficiency). Replaces deficient enzyme, reducing glycogen accumulation in muscles and tissues.
