J0283 Injection, amiodarone (nexterone)

J-Codes

Also known as: amiodarone Nexterone, Nexterone

Amiodarone hydrochloride (Nexterone) injection, an antiarrhythmic agent for cardiac arrhythmias.

Clinical Context

Intravenous Class III antiarrhythmic (Nexterone brand) for ventricular fibrillation and sustained ventricular tachycardia. Used in acute settings.
