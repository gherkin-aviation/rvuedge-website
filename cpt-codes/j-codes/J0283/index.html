J0283 Injection, amiodarone (nexterone)

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: amiodarone Nexterone, Nexterone

Amiodarone hydrochloride (Nexterone) injection, an antiarrhythmic agent for cardiac arrhythmias.

In Plain Language

heart rhythm medication; irregular heartbeat treatment

Clinical Context

Intravenous Class III antiarrhythmic (Nexterone brand) for ventricular fibrillation and sustained ventricular tachycardia. Used in acute settings.

RVU Information

CPT J0283 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
