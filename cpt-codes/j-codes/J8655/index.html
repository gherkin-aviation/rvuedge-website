J8655 Oral netupitant, palonosetro

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: Akynzeo, Netupitant-palonosetron

Netupitant and palonosetron combination administered orally; a neurokinin-1 receptor antagonist combined with 5-hydroxytryptamine-3 antagonist for antiemetic effects.

In Plain Language

Nausea and vomiting prevention pill; Combination antiemetic

Clinical Context

Used to prevent acute and delayed chemotherapy-induced nausea and vomiting. Fixed combination providing dual antiemetic mechanism.

RVU Information

CPT J8655 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
