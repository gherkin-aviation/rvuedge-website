J0712 Ceftaroline fosamil injection

J-Codes

Also known as: ceftaroline fosamil, Teflaro, 5th-generation cephalosporin

Ceftaroline fosamil injection, a fifth-generation cephalosporin with methicillin-resistant Staphylococcus aureus (MRSA) coverage.

Clinical Context

Fifth-generation cephalosporin with unique MRSA coverage, used for community-acquired bacterial pneumonia and skin and soft tissue infections.
