J1442 Injection filgrastim excl biosimil

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: filgrastim, G-CSF, Neupogen

Filgrastim injection, non-biosimilar product. A granulocyte colony-stimulating factor used for chemotherapy-induced neutropenia management.

In Plain Language

white blood cell booster; infection prevention injection

Clinical Context

Used for chemotherapy-induced neutropenia, severe chronic neutropenia, and mobilization of hematopoietic progenitor cells. Stimulates neutrophil production and function.

RVU Information

CPT J1442 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
