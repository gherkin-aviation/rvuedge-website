J9355 Injection trastuzumab excl biosimi

J-Codes

Also known as: trastuzumab, Herceptin

Injection of trastuzumab excluding biosimilars, an anti-HER2 monoclonal antibody. First-line therapy for HER2-positive breast cancer.

Clinical Context

Standard therapy for HER2-positive breast cancer in early and metastatic settings. Often combined with chemotherapy and other targeted agents.
