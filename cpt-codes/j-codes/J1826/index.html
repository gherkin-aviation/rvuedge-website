J1826 Interferon beta-1a injection

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: interferon beta-1a, INF beta-1a, Avonex

Injection of interferon beta-1a. A cytokine produced through recombinant DNA technology for immune modulation.

In Plain Language

immune-boosting protein injection; MS treatment

Clinical Context

Used for relapsing-remitting multiple sclerosis to slow disease progression and reduce exacerbations. Enhances immune suppression and reduces inflammatory cytokine production.

RVU Information

CPT J1826 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
