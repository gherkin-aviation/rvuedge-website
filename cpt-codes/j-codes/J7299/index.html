J7299 Intraut copp cont (miudella)

J-Codes

Also known as: Miudella, copper IUD, intrauterine copper device

Insertion of intrauterine copper contraceptive device (Miudella), a non-hormonal copper-based reversible contraceptive implant.

Clinical Context

Used for contraception in women, copper mechanism prevents fertilization, reversible with immediate return to fertility.
