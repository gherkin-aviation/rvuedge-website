J9042 Brentuximab vedotin injection

J-Codes

Also known as: brentuximab vedotin, Adcetris

Intravenous injection of brentuximab vedotin, an anti-CD30 monoclonal antibody linked to a microtubule-disrupting agent.

Clinical Context

Used to treat Hodgkin lymphoma and systemic anaplastic large cell lymphomas expressing CD30 on cell surface.
