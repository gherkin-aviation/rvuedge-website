J9293 Mitoxantrone hydrochl / 5 milligrams

J-Codes

Also known as: mitoxantrone, Novantrone

Injection of mitoxantrone hydrochloride, 5 milligram. A topoisomerase II inhibitor anthracenedione used for leukemia and multiple sclerosis.

Clinical Context

Used for secondary progressive multiple sclerosis and acute myeloid leukemia. Provides both antimicrobial and antileukemic activities.
