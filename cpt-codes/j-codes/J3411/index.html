J3411 Thiamine hcl 100 milligrams

J-Codes

Also known as: thiamine, Vitamin B1, thiamine HCl

Thiamine hydrochloride (Vitamin B1) 100 milligram injection is used for thiamine deficiency and Wernicke encephalopathy treatment.

Clinical Context

Used for thiamine deficiency in alcoholism, malnutrition, and dialysis patients. Treatment and prevention of Wernicke encephalopathy. Administered intramuscularly or intravenously for rapid correction.
