J3489 Zoledronic acid 1mg

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: zoledronic acid, Zometa, bisphosphonate

Zoledronic acid 1 milligram injection is a bisphosphonate used for bone metastasis pain, hypercalcemia, and Paget disease treatment.

In Plain Language

bone treatment injection; cancer bone disease medication

Clinical Context

Used for bone metastasis pain management, hypercalcemia of malignancy, and Paget disease. Bisphosphonate that inhibits osteoclast function. Intravenous administration every 3-4 weeks for malignancy-related hypercalcemia.

RVU Information

CPT J3489 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
