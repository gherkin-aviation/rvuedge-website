J9325 Injection talimogene laherparepvec

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: talimogene laherparepvec, Imlygic

Injection of talimogene laherparepvec, an oncolytic herpes simplex virus for melanoma immunotherapy. Genetically engineered virus for intratumoral injection.

In Plain Language

melanoma immune injection; oncolytic virus therapy

Clinical Context

Used for advanced melanoma via intratumoral injection. Oncolytic virus stimulates local and systemic anti-tumor immunity.

RVU Information

CPT J9325 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
