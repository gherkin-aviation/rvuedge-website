J2950 Promazine hcl injection

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: promazine, sparine

Injection of promazine hydrochloride (HCl), a first-generation phenothiazine antipsychotic used for psychotic agitation and acute behavioral disturbance.

In Plain Language

antipsychotic medication

Clinical Context

Used for acute management of psychotic symptoms and severe behavioral disturbance in schizophrenia and other psychotic disorders.

RVU Information

CPT J2950 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
