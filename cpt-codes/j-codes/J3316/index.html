J3316 Injection., triptorelin x-ray 3.75 milligrams

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: triptorelin XR, extended-release triptorelin, long-acting GnRH agonist

Triptorelin extended-release 3.75 milligram injection is a long-acting gonadotropin-releasing hormone agonist for prostate cancer and endometriosis.

In Plain Language

long-lasting hormone suppression injection; prostate cancer medication

Clinical Context

Indicated for advanced prostate cancer requiring sustained hormone suppression. Extended-release formulation allows quarterly dosing versus more frequent standard triptorelin. Used in endometriosis and precocious puberty.

RVU Information

CPT J3316 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
