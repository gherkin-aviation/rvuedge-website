J0184 Injection, amisulpride, 1 milligrams

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: amisulpride, Solian

Amisulpride injection 1 milligram, an atypical antipsychotic for psychotic disorders.

In Plain Language

antipsychotic medication injection; schizophrenia treatment

Clinical Context

Selective dopamine antagonist used for schizophrenia and other psychotic disorders. Used when oral medication unavailable or for depot (long-acting) formulation.

RVU Information

CPT J0184 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
