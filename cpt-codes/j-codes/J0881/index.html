J0881 Darbepoetin alfa, non-esrd

J-Codes

Also known as: Aranesp, darbepoetin alfa, ESA

Darbepoetin alfa injection for patients with anemia not requiring end-stage renal disease management, an erythropoiesis-stimulating agent.

Clinical Context

Used for treatment of anemia in patients with chronic kidney disease not yet requiring dialysis, cancer patients undergoing chemotherapy, or other chronic anemia conditions.
