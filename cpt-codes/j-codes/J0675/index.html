J0675 Injection, carboprost, 0.1 milligrams

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: carboprost, Hemabate, prostaglandin analog

Carboprost tromethamine injection, 0.1 milligram, a prostaglandin F2-alpha analog for treatment of postpartum hemorrhage and incomplete abortion.

In Plain Language

postpartum bleeding medication; uterine contraction injection

Clinical Context

Uterotonic prostaglandin used for treatment of postpartum uterine atony and hemorrhage when oxytocin is ineffective or contraindicated. Administered intramuscularly.

RVU Information

CPT J0675 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
