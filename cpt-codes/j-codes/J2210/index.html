J2210 Methylergonovin maleate injection

J-Codes

Also known as: methylergonovine, Methergine

Injection of methylergonovine maleate. An ergot alkaloid for uterine contraction in obstetrics.

Clinical Context

Used to treat or prevent postpartum hemorrhage by causing uterine contraction. Administered after delivery of baby and usually after delivery of placenta.
