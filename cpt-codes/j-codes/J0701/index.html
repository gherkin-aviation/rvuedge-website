J0701 Injection. cefepime hcl (baxter)

J-Codes

Also known as: cefepime baxter, branded cefepime, 4th-generation cephalosporin

Cefepime hydrochloride injection, Baxter brand, a fourth-generation cephalosporin for broad-spectrum bacterial infection treatment.

Clinical Context

Branded formulation of cefepime for serious infections with gram-positive and gram-negative coverage including Pseudomonas. Used in sepsis and immunocompromised patients.
