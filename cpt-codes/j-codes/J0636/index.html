J0636 Injection calcitriol per 0.1 micrograms

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: calcitriol, 1,25-dihydroxyvitamin D3, active vitamin D

Calcitriol injection per 0.1 microgram, the active form of vitamin D for intravenous administration in chronic kidney disease and hypocalcemia treatment.

In Plain Language

vitamin D hormone injection; kidney disease calcium regulator

Clinical Context

Active form of vitamin D administered intravenously, primarily used in hemodialysis patients to treat secondary hyperparathyroidism and prevent bone disease.

RVU Information

CPT J0636 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
