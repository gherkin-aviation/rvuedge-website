J2860 Injection, siltuximab

J-Codes

Also known as: siltuximab, sylvant

Injection of siltuximab (Sylvant), a monoclonal antibody against interleukin-6 (IL-6) used for treatment of multicentric Castleman disease (MCD).

Clinical Context

Used for multicentric Castleman disease (MCD) in human immunodeficiency virus (HIV)-negative and hepatitis C virus (HCV)-negative patients.
