J9306 Injection, pertuzumab, 1 milligrams

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: pertuzumab, Perjeta

Injection of pertuzumab, 1 milligram. A human epidermal growth factor receptor 2 dimerization inhibitor monoclonal antibody.

In Plain Language

breast cancer injection; HER2 targeted therapy

Clinical Context

Used for HER2-positive breast cancer with trastuzumab and chemotherapy. Approved for both neoadjuvant and metastatic disease.

RVU Information

CPT J9306 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
