Injection of zanidatamab-hrii, 2 milligram. A bispecific antibody targeting HER2 with dual-engaging mechanism for HER2-expressing cancers.

Clinical Context

Used for HER2-expressing gastric and gastroesophageal junction cancers. Provides activity in patients with metastatic disease.
