J0884 Argatroban esrd dialysis 1mg

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: Argatroban ESRD, argatroban dialysis

Argatroban injection supplied in 1 milligram units for patients with end-stage renal disease undergoing dialysis, a direct thrombin inhibitor.

In Plain Language

Blood thinner for dialysis patients

Clinical Context

Used for anticoagulation in dialysis patients with heparin-induced thrombocytopenia with adjusted dosing for renal failure.

RVU Information

CPT J0884 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
