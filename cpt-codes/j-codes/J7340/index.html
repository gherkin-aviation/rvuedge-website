J7340 Carbidopa levodopa ent 100ml

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: Duopa, carbidopa/levodopa suspension, enteral levodopa

Enteral infusion of carbidopa and levodopa suspension, 100 milliliters, for Parkinson disease management via gastric or jejunal tube.

In Plain Language

Parkinson's disease treatment; stomach pump medication

Clinical Context

Used for continuous infusion via gastrostomy or jejunostomy tube in advanced Parkinson disease for motor symptom control.

RVU Information

CPT J7340 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
