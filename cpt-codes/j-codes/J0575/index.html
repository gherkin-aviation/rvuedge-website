J0575 Bupren/nal over 10mg bupreno

J-Codes

Also known as: buprenorphine/naloxone, Suboxone, bup/nal maximum

Buprenorphine/naloxone combination oral, over 10 milligrams buprenorphine, for sublingual administration in opioid use disorder treatment.

Clinical Context

Maximum maintenance dosing for opioid use disorder, exceeding 10 mg buprenorphine. Reserved for patients with severe opioid dependence unresponsive to lower doses or those with prior high-dose opioid exposure.
