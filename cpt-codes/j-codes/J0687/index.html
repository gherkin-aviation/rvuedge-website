J0687 Injection cefazolin (wg critical care)

J-Codes

Also known as: cefazolin critical care, Ancef critical care, first-generation cephalosporin

Cefazolin sodium injection for critical care use, a first-generation cephalosporin for serious gram-positive and some gram-negative infections.

Clinical Context

Critical care formulation of cefazolin for use in intensive care settings requiring rapid infection treatment in seriously ill patients.
