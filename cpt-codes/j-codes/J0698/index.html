J0698 Cefotaxime sodium injection

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: cefotaxime, Claforan, 3rd-generation cephalosporin

Cefotaxime sodium injection, a third-generation cephalosporin with similar coverage to ceftriaxone for serious systemic infections.

In Plain Language

powerful antibiotic injection; serious infection treatment

Clinical Context

Third-generation cephalosporin for serious infections including meningitis, with excellent central nervous system penetration. Used for hospital-acquired and community-acquired serious infections.

RVU Information

CPT J0698 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
