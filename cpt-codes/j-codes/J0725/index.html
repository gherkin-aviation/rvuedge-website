J0725 Chorionic gonadotropin/1000u

J-Codes

Also known as: chorionic gonadotropin, human chorionic gonadotropin, hCG

Chorionic gonadotropin injection per 1000 international units, a hormone for treatment of infertility in males and females.

Clinical Context

Hormone used for ovulation induction in women and testosterone stimulation in men with infertility. Also used for cryptorchidism in children.
