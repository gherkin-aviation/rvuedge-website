J0725 Chorionic gonadotropin/1000u

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: chorionic gonadotropin, human chorionic gonadotropin, hCG

Chorionic gonadotropin injection per 1000 international units, a hormone for treatment of infertility in males and females.

In Plain Language

fertility hormone injection; pregnancy hormone treatment

Clinical Context

Hormone used for ovulation induction in women and testosterone stimulation in men with infertility. Also used for cryptorchidism in children.

RVU Information

CPT J0725 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
