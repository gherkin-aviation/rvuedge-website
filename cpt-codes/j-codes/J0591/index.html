J0591 Injection deoxycholic acid, 1 milligrams

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: deoxycholic acid, Kybella

Deoxycholic acid injection, 1 milligram, a naturally occurring bile acid that destroys adipocytes for non-surgical fat reduction, particularly under the chin.

In Plain Language

double chin reduction injection; fat dissolving shot

Clinical Context

Only FDA-approved non-surgical treatment for submental fullness (double chin). Administered via multiple intradermal injections under the chin with permanent destruction of fat cells.

RVU Information

CPT J0591 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
