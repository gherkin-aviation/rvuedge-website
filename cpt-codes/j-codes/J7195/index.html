J-Codes

Also known as: recombinant Factor IX, rFIX, Factor IX concentrate

Injection of Factor IX recombinant, not otherwise specified, a genetically engineered coagulation factor for hemophilia B treatment.

Clinical Context

Used for routine prophylaxis and on-demand treatment of bleeding in hemophilia B patients.
