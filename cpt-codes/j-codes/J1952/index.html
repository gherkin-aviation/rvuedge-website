J1952 Leuprolide injection, camcevi, 1mg

J-Codes

Also known as: Camcevi, leuprolide

Injection of leuprolide (Camcevi brand), 1 milligram. A 1-month GnRH agonist formulation.

Clinical Context

Used for prostate cancer and other GnRH-responsive conditions with monthly dosing. Camcevi is an alternative formulation providing standard monthly suppression.
