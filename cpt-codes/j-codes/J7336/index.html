J7336 Capsaicin 8% patch

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: Qutenza, capsaicin patch, 8% capsaicin

Topical application of capsaicin 8% patch, a high-concentration capsaicin preparation for transdermal pain management.

In Plain Language

pain relief patch; burning sensation treatment

Clinical Context

Used for treatment of postherpetic neuralgia and peripheral neuropathic pain, capsaicin depletizes substance P.

RVU Information

CPT J7336 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
