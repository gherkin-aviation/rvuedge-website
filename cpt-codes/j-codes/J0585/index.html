J0585 Injection,onabotulinumtoxina

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: onabotulinumtoxin A, Botox, botulinum toxin A

Onabotulinumtoxin A injection, a neurotoxin that blocks acetylcholine release for muscular and cosmetic applications including dystonia and spasticity.

In Plain Language

wrinkle and muscle tension relaxer; spasticity treatment injection

Clinical Context

Used for cervical dystonia, blepharospasm, hemifacial spasm, overactive bladder, migraine prophylaxis, and spasticity. Also approved for aesthetic facial rejuvenation. Injected directly into target muscles to prevent acetylcholine release.

RVU Information

CPT J0585 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
