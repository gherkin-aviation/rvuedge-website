J7308 Aminolevulinic acid hcl top

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: Levulan, ALA, photodynamic agent

Topical application of aminolevulinic acid hydrochloride, a photosensitizing agent used in photodynamic therapy for dermatologic conditions.

In Plain Language

skin cancer treatment cream; light therapy preparation

Clinical Context

Used for photodynamic therapy of non-hyperkeratotic actinic keratosis on face and scalp, applied topically before light activation.

RVU Information

CPT J7308 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
