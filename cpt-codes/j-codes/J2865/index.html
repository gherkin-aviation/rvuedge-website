J2865 Injection sulfameth/trim 5 milligrams/1 milligrams

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: sulfamethoxazole/trimethoprim, smx-tmp, bactrim

Injection of sulfamethoxazole/trimethoprim (SMX-TMP, Bactrim, Septra), 5 milligrams/1 milligram ratio, a combination antibiotic for broad-spectrum bacterial coverage.

In Plain Language

combination antibiotic

Clinical Context

Used for Pneumocystis pneumonia (PCP) prophylaxis and treatment in immunocompromised patients and for urinary tract and other infections.

RVU Information

CPT J2865 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
