J9196 Injection gemcitabine hcl (accord)

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: gemcitabine, Accord

Intravenous injection of gemcitabine hydrochloride (Accord brand), a nucleoside analog chemotherapy.

In Plain Language

DNA-inhibiting cancer drug; pancreatic cancer chemotherapy

Clinical Context

Brand-specific formulation of gemcitabine for pancreatic, bladder, and other solid tumors.

RVU Information

CPT J9196 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
