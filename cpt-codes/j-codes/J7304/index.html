J7304 Contraceptive hormone patch

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: Ortho Evra, contraceptive patch, transdermal contraceptive

Transdermal contraceptive patch delivery system with hormone formulation, providing weekly contraceptive protection for 3 weeks per cycle.

In Plain Language

birth control patch; weekly contraception

Clinical Context

Used for contraception in women, applied weekly for 3 weeks with 1 week off, delivers consistent hormone levels.

RVU Information

CPT J7304 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
