J0736 Injection, clindamycin phosp 300mg

J-Codes

Also known as: clindamycin phosphate, Cleocin, lincosamide antibiotic

Clindamycin phosphate injection, 300 milligrams, a lincosamide antibiotic for anaerobic and some gram-positive infections.

Clinical Context

Effective against anaerobic bacteria and gram-positive cocci. Used for respiratory infections, skin and soft tissue infections, and surgical prophylaxis.
