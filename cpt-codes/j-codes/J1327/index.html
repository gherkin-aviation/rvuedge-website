J1327 Eptifibatide injection

J-Codes

Also known as: Integrilin, eptifibatide

Eptifibatide injection, a glycoprotein IIb/IIIa inhibitor for acute coronary syndrome and percutaneous coronary intervention.

Clinical Context

Used for acute coronary syndrome and as adjunctive therapy during percutaneous coronary intervention to prevent thrombotic complications.
