J9263 Oxaliplatin

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: oxaliplatin, Eloxatin

Injection of oxaliplatin, a platinum-based chemotherapy agent. An alkylating agent used for colorectal cancer and other malignancies.

In Plain Language

platinum cancer injection; colorectal cancer drug

Clinical Context

First-line therapy for metastatic colorectal cancer, often used in FOLFOX regimen combinations. Also used for gastric and ovarian cancers in select patients.

RVU Information

CPT J9263 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
