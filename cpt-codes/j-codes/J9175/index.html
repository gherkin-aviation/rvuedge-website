J9175 Elliotts b solution per milliliters

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: Elliott's B solution, CSF replacement

Intrathecal injection of Elliott's B solution, a synthetic cerebrospinal fluid substitute per milliliter.

In Plain Language

brain fluid replacement; intrathecal support solution

Clinical Context

Used as a vehicle for intrathecal chemotherapy administration and as cerebrospinal fluid replacement.

RVU Information

CPT J9175 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
