J0801 Injection. acthar gel to 40 units

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: ACTH gel, Acthar, corticotropin

Adrenocorticotropic hormone (Acthar) injection supplied up to 40 units, a corticotropin for treating adrenal insufficiency and inflammatory conditions.

In Plain Language

Hormone that stimulates natural steroid production

Clinical Context

Used for replacement therapy in adrenal insufficiency and as immunosuppressive therapy for inflammatory conditions such as infantile spasms and nephrotic syndrome.

RVU Information

CPT J0801 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
