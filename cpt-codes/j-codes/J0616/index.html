J0616 Injection metoprolol tartrate 1 milligrams

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: metoprolol tartrate, Lopressor IV, beta blocker

Metoprolol tartrate injection, 1 milligram, a selective beta-1 adrenergic blocker for acute hypertension and acute myocardial infarction management.

In Plain Language

heart rate and blood pressure reducer; heart attack emergency medication

Clinical Context

Rapid-acting intravenous beta-blocker for acute management of hypertension, tachycardia, and acute coronary syndrome. Provides rapid cardioprotection and heart rate control.

RVU Information

CPT J0616 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
