J0457 Injection, aztreonam, 100 milligrams

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: aztreonam, Azactam

Aztreonam injection 100 milligrams, a monobactam antibiotic for gram-negative infections.

In Plain Language

antibiotic for gram-negative bacteria; resistant infection treatment

Clinical Context

Intravenous monobactam antibiotic with excellent gram-negative coverage and minimal cross-reactivity with beta-lactam allergies. Used for resistant organisms.

RVU Information

CPT J0457 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
