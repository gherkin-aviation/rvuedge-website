J3470 Hyaluronidase injection

J Codes (Drugs) +Add-on

Also known as: hyaluronidase, hyaluronate lyase, spreading agent

Hyaluronidase injection is an enzyme used as a spreading agent to increase diffusion and absorption of injected drugs and fluids.

In Plain Language

enzyme spreader for other medications

Clinical Context

Used as a spreading agent to enhance diffusion and absorption of concurrently injected medications and intravenous infusions. Applied to subcutaneous, intramuscular, or intravenous administration sites. Enhances drug distribution.

RVU Information

CPT J3470 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
