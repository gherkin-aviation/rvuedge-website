J1744 Icatibant injection

J-Codes

Also known as: icatibant, Firazyr

Icatibant injection. A bradykinin B2 receptor antagonist for acute hereditary angioedema attacks.

Clinical Context

Used for acute attacks of hereditary angioedema (C1 esterase inhibitor deficiency or C1 esterase inhibitor dysfunction). Subcutaneous injection for rapid symptom relief.
