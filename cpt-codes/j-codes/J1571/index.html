J1571 Hepagam b intramuscular injection

J-Codes

Also known as: Hepagam B IM, hepatitis B immune globulin

Hepagam B (hepatitis B immune globulin) injection, intramuscular. Passive hepatitis B immunity for post-exposure prophylaxis.

Clinical Context

Used for post-exposure prophylaxis after hepatitis B exposure, passive immunization of non-immune individuals, and hepatitis B re-infection prevention in transplant recipients.
