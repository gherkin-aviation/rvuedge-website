J1748 Injection, zymfentra, 10 milligrams

J-Codes

Also known as: Zymfentra, infliximab biosimilar, TNF inhibitor

Zymfentra (infliximab-dyyb biosimilar) injection, 10 milligrams. A TNF-alpha inhibitor biosimilar for inflammatory disease treatment.

Clinical Context

Used for rheumatoid arthritis, Crohn's disease, ulcerative colitis, and other TNF-mediated inflammatory conditions. Biosimilar with equivalent efficacy to reference infliximab.
