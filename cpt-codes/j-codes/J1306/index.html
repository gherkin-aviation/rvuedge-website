J1306 Injection, inclisiran, 1 milligrams

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: Leqvio, inclisiran

Inclisiran injection supplied in 1 milligram units, a small interfering ribonucleic acid for reduction of low-density lipoprotein cholesterol.

In Plain Language

Gene-based cholesterol medicine

Clinical Context

Used for reduction of low-density lipoprotein cholesterol in patients with familial or severe hypercholesterolemia via proprotein convertase subtilisin/kexin type 9 inhibition.

RVU Information

CPT J1306 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
