J1306 Injection, inclisiran, 1 milligrams

J-Codes

Also known as: Leqvio, inclisiran

Inclisiran injection supplied in 1 milligram units, a small interfering ribonucleic acid for reduction of low-density lipoprotein cholesterol.

Clinical Context

Used for reduction of low-density lipoprotein cholesterol in patients with familial or severe hypercholesterolemia via proprotein convertase subtilisin/kexin type 9 inhibition.
