J0458 Aztreonam/avibactam 10 milligrams

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: aztreonam-avibactam, Zavicefta

Aztreonam combined with avibactam 10 milligrams, a monobactam with beta-lactamase inhibitor.

In Plain Language

antibiotic for resistant bacteria; serious infection treatment

Clinical Context

Intravenous combination of monobactam and beta-lactamase inhibitor for carbapenem-resistant gram-negative infections, especially pseudomonas.

RVU Information

CPT J0458 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
