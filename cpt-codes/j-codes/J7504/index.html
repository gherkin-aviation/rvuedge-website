J7504 Lymphocyte immune globulin

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: ATG rabbit, antithymocyte globulin, lymphocyte immune globulin

Injection of lymphocyte immune globulin (antithymocyte globulin rabbit), parenteral immunosuppressive therapy for organ transplant.

In Plain Language

transplant rejection prevention; immune-suppressing antibody

Clinical Context

Used for induction immunosuppression in organ transplant recipients and treatment of acute allograft rejection.

RVU Information

CPT J7504 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
