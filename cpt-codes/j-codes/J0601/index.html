J0601 Sevelamer carbonate 20 milligrams

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: sevelamer carbonate, Renvela, phosphate binder

Sevelamer carbonate oral, 20 milligrams, a phosphate-binding polymer for treatment of hyperphosphatemia in chronic kidney disease patients.

In Plain Language

phosphate control medication for kidney disease; dialysis patient supplement

Clinical Context

Binds dietary phosphate in the gastrointestinal tract to reduce serum phosphate levels in end-stage renal disease (ESRD) patients. Does not contain calcium, avoiding hypercalcemia risks.

RVU Information

CPT J0601 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
