J9267 Paclitaxel injection

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: paclitaxel, Taxol

Injection of paclitaxel, a naturally occurring diterpene alkaloid. A microtubule-stabilizing chemotherapy agent.

In Plain Language

breast cancer injection; microtubule cancer drug

Clinical Context

Used for breast cancer (metastatic and adjuvant), ovarian cancer, lung cancer, and Kaposi sarcoma. First-line therapy for many solid tumors in combination regimens.

RVU Information

CPT J9267 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
