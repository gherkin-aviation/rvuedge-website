Ravulizumab-cwvz injection supplied in 10 milligram units, a proximal complement C5 inhibitor for paroxysmal nocturnal hemoglobinuria.

Clinical Context

Used for treatment of paroxysmal nocturnal hemoglobinuria with more potent and sustained C5 inhibition than earlier agents.
