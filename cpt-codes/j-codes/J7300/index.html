J7300 Intra copp contra (paragard)

J-Codes

Also known as: Paragard, copper IUD, intrauterine copper device

Insertion of intrauterine copper contraceptive device (Paragard), a non-hormonal copper-releasing reversible contraceptive.

Clinical Context

Used for contraception in women, copper releases ions inhibiting sperm motility and fertilization, provides 10+ years of protection.
