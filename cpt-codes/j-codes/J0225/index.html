J0225 Injection, vutrisiran, 1 milligrams

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: vutrisiran, Revozlyr

Vutrisiran injection 1 milligram, a small interfering ribonucleic acid (siRNA) for transthyretin amyloidosis.

In Plain Language

genetic disease gene therapy; hereditary amyloidosis treatment

Clinical Context

Intravenous siRNA targeting transthyretin messenger ribonucleic acid for hereditary transthyretin amyloidosis. Reduces transthyretin levels, slowing amyloid accumulation and disease progression.

RVU Information

CPT J0225 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
