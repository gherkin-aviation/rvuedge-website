J0666 Injection, bupivacaine liposome

J-Codes

Also known as: bupivacaine liposome, Exparel, liposomal formulation

Bupivacaine liposome injection, a proprietary formulation with enhanced tissue penetration for local and regional anesthesia with prolonged duration.

Clinical Context

Liposomal encapsulation of bupivacaine providing extended anesthesia duration up to 72 hours, used in surgical infiltration, nerve blocks, and regional anesthesia.
