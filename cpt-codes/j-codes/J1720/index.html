J1720 Hydrocortisone sodium succ i

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: hydrocortisone sodium succinate, Solu-Cortef

Hydrocortisone sodium succinate injection. A soluble corticosteroid for acute inflammation and adrenal crisis management.

In Plain Language

emergency steroid injection; inflammation reducer

Clinical Context

Used for acute adrenal insufficiency (adrenal crisis), severe inflammatory reactions, and anaphylaxis. Rapid-acting intravenous or intramuscular administration.

RVU Information

CPT J1720 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
