J1720 Hydrocortisone sodium succ i

J-Codes

Also known as: hydrocortisone sodium succinate, Solu-Cortef

Hydrocortisone sodium succinate injection. A soluble corticosteroid for acute inflammation and adrenal crisis management.

Clinical Context

Used for acute adrenal insufficiency (adrenal crisis), severe inflammatory reactions, and anaphylaxis. Rapid-acting intravenous or intramuscular administration.
