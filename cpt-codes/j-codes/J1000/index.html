J1000 Depo-estradiol cypionate injection

J-Codes

Also known as: Depo-Estradiol, estradiol cypionate

Estradiol cypionate injection, a long-acting estrogen for hormone replacement therapy and management of menopause symptoms.

Clinical Context

Used for hormone replacement therapy in postmenopausal women to manage vasomotor symptoms and for estrogen-dependent conditions.
