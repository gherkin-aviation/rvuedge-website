J7180 Factor xiii anti-hem factor

J-Codes

Also known as: Factor XIII, fibrin-stabilizing factor, antihemophilic factor

Injection of Factor XIII (fibrin-stabilizing factor), an antihemophilic factor, used to prevent bleeding in Factor XIII deficiency.

Clinical Context

Indicated for treatment and prophylaxis of bleeding in congenital or acquired Factor XIII deficiency.
