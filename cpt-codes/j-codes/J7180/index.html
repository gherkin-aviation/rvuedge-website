J7180 Factor xiii anti-hem factor

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: Factor XIII, fibrin-stabilizing factor, antihemophilic factor

Injection of Factor XIII (fibrin-stabilizing factor), an antihemophilic factor, used to prevent bleeding in Factor XIII deficiency.

In Plain Language

clotting factor replacement; fibrin stabilization

Clinical Context

Indicated for treatment and prophylaxis of bleeding in congenital or acquired Factor XIII deficiency.

RVU Information

CPT J7180 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
