J7515 Cyclosporine oral 25 milligrams

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: cyclosporine, Neoral, calcineurin inhibitor

Oral cyclosporine (cyclosporin A), 25 milligrams per dose, a calcineurin inhibitor immunosuppressant for transplant maintenance.

In Plain Language

organ transplant medication; immune suppressant

Clinical Context

Used for immunosuppression in organ transplant recipients and management of severe autoimmune conditions.

RVU Information

CPT J7515 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
