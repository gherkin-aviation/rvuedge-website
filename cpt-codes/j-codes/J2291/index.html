J Codes (Drugs)

In Plain Language

staphylococcal infection treatment

Clinical Context

Identical clinical use to standard nafcillin for Staphylococcus aureus infections. Baxter is a specific manufacturer of injectable nafcillin.

RVU Information

CPT J2291 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
