J3380 Injection vedolizumab intravenous 1 milligrams

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: vedolizumab, Entyvio, integrin inhibitor

Vedolizumab intravenous 1 milligram injection is a monoclonal antibody targeting alpha-4-beta-7 integrin for inflammatory bowel disease treatment.

In Plain Language

inflammatory bowel disease injection; Crohn's/colitis treatment

Clinical Context

Indicated for moderate to severe Crohn's disease and ulcerative colitis. Blocks alpha-4-beta-7 integrin adhesion molecule to reduce intestinal inflammation. Administered intravenously with induction and maintenance dosing.

RVU Information

CPT J3380 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
