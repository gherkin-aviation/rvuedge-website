J3380 Injection vedolizumab intravenous 1 milligrams

J-Codes

Also known as: vedolizumab, Entyvio, integrin inhibitor

Vedolizumab intravenous 1 milligram injection is a monoclonal antibody targeting alpha-4-beta-7 integrin for inflammatory bowel disease treatment.

Clinical Context

Indicated for moderate to severe Crohn's disease and ulcerative colitis. Blocks alpha-4-beta-7 integrin adhesion molecule to reduce intestinal inflammation. Administered intravenously with induction and maintenance dosing.
