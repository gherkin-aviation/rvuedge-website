J7328 Gelsyn-3 injection 0.1 milligrams

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: Gelsyn-3, hyaluronic acid, sodium hyaluronate

Injection of Gelsyn-3 (sodium hyaluronate), 0.1 milligram per injection, a three-injection viscosupplement course for osteoarthritis.

In Plain Language

joint lubrication injection series; arthritis relief

Clinical Context

Used for intra-articular viscosupplementation administered as 3-injection series for knee osteoarthritis pain management.

RVU Information

CPT J7328 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
