J2788 Rho d immune globulin 50 micrograms

J-Codes

Also known as: rho(d) immune globulin, rhogam, rh prophylaxis

Injection of Rho(D) immune globulin, 50 micrograms, used for immunosuppression following delivery in Rho(D)-negative, non-sensitized mothers.

Clinical Context

Administered to Rho(D)-negative pregnant women within 72 hours of delivery to prevent Rho(D) alloimmunization.
