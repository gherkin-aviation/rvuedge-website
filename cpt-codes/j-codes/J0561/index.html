J0561 Penicillin g benzathine injection

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: penicillin G benzathine, benzathine penicillin, Bicillin

Penicillin G benzathine injection for intramuscular administration, a long-acting penicillin formulation providing sustained antibiotic levels for systemic infections.

In Plain Language

long-acting penicillin shot; strep throat treatment

Clinical Context

Primary indication is treatment of group A streptococcal pharyngitis and prophylaxis of rheumatic fever. Also used for syphilis and other susceptible bacterial infections requiring extended antibiotic exposure.

RVU Information

CPT J0561 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
