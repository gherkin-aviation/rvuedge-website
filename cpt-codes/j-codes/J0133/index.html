J0133 Acyclovir injection

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: acyclovir, Zovirax

Acyclovir injection, an antiviral agent used for herpes simplex and varicella-zoster virus infections.

In Plain Language

herpes treatment injection; shingles and cold sore medicine

Clinical Context

Intravenous antiviral for severe herpes simplex, varicella-zoster, and Epstein-Barr virus infections. Used for disseminated disease, immunocompromised patients, and meningitis.

RVU Information

CPT J0133 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
