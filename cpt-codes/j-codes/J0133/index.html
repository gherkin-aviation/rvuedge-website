J0133 Acyclovir injection

J-Codes

Also known as: acyclovir, Zovirax

Acyclovir injection, an antiviral agent used for herpes simplex and varicella-zoster virus infections.

Clinical Context

Intravenous antiviral for severe herpes simplex, varicella-zoster, and Epstein-Barr virus infections. Used for disseminated disease, immunocompromised patients, and meningitis.
