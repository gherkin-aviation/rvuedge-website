J0275 Alprostadil urethral suppos

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: alprostadil suppository, MUSE

Alprostadil urethral suppository, a prostaglandin for intraurethral treatment of erectile dysfunction.

In Plain Language

erectile dysfunction suppository; urethral treatment for impotence

Clinical Context

Intraurethral prostaglandin E1 suppository for erectile dysfunction. User-friendly alternative to intracavernous injection in some patients.

RVU Information

CPT J0275 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
