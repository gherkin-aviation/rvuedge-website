J0476 Baclofen intrathecal trial

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: baclofen trial, intrathecal baclofen test

Baclofen intrathecal trial dose injection for spasticity evaluation.

In Plain Language

spasticity treatment trial; intrathecal injection test

Clinical Context

Intrathecal test dose of baclofen to assess efficacy before implanting intrathecal pump for chronic spasticity management.

RVU Information

CPT J0476 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
