J1230 Methadone injection

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: Methadone IV/IM, opioid

Methadone injection, an opioid agonist for pain management and opioid use disorder maintenance therapy.

In Plain Language

Pain medicine for addiction treatment

Clinical Context

Used for acute pain management and as part of medication-assisted treatment programs for opioid use disorder.

RVU Information

CPT J1230 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
