J0596 Injection, ruconest

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: ruconest, C1-INH recombinant, recombinant esterase inhibitor

Ruconest (C1 esterase inhibitor recombinant) injection for intravenous administration, used for acute treatment and prophylaxis of hereditary angioedema attacks.

In Plain Language

hereditary swelling emergency treatment; angioedema relief injection

Clinical Context

Recombinant C1 esterase inhibitor for acute management of hereditary angioedema (HAE) attacks affecting any site including potentially life-threatening facial/airway involvement.

RVU Information

CPT J0596 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
