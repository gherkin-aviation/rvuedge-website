J9370 Vincristine sulfate 1 milligrams injection

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: vincristine, Oncovin

Injection of vincristine sulfate, 1 milligram. A vinca alkaloid microtubule disruptor. Used for leukemia, lymphoma, and other malignancies.

In Plain Language

leukemia chemotherapy injection; vinca alkaloid cancer drug

Clinical Context

Component of combination chemotherapy for acute lymphoblastic leukemia, Hodgkin lymphoma, and other hematologic malignancies.

RVU Information

CPT J9370 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
