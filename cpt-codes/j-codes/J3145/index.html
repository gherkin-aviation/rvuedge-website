J3145 Testosterone undecanoate 1mg

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: testosterone undecanoate, Aveed, long-acting testosterone

Testosterone undecanoate (Aveed) 1 milligram injection is a long-acting testosterone ester used for testosterone replacement in hypogonadal males.

In Plain Language

testosterone injection; hormone replacement therapy

Clinical Context

Indicated for primary and secondary male hypogonadism. Ultra-long-acting ester with intravenous administration only. Requires 10-minute post-injection observation period for anaphylaxis risk.

RVU Information

CPT J3145 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
