J1437 Injection. fe derisomaltose 10 milligrams

J-Codes

Also known as: iron isomaltoside, iron complex injection

Iron isomaltoside injection, 10 milligrams. An iron carbohydrate complex for iron deficiency anemia treatment.

Clinical Context

Used for iron deficiency anemia in patients unable to tolerate or absorb oral iron supplementation. Provides bioavailable iron for erythropoiesis.
