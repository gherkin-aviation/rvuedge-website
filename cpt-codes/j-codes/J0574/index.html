J0574 Bupren/nal 6.1 to 10mg bupre

J-Codes

Also known as: buprenorphine/naloxone, Suboxone, bup/nal high-dose

Buprenorphine/naloxone combination oral, 6.1 to 10 milligrams buprenorphine, for sublingual administration in opioid use disorder treatment.

Clinical Context

High-dose combination formulation for patients with opioid use disorder requiring 6.1-10 mg buprenorphine for adequate maintenance therapy. Used in more severe addiction cases or those previously treated with high-dose opioids.
