J8560 Etoposide oral 50 milligrams

J-Codes

Also known as: VePesid, Oral etoposide

Etoposide administered orally at 50 milligrams per dose; a topoisomerase II inhibitor chemotherapy drug used for various malignancies.

Clinical Context

Used for small cell lung cancer, germ cell tumors, and lymphomas. Inhibits topoisomerase II enzyme leading to DNA strand breaks.
