J3486 Ziprasidone mesylate

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: ziprasidone, Geodon, atypical antipsychotic

Ziprasidone mesylate injection is an atypical antipsychotic used for acute psychosis and schizophrenia management.

In Plain Language

psychiatric medication; psychosis treatment

Clinical Context

Used for acute agitation in schizophrenia and bipolar disorder. Intramuscular administration for rapid onset. Atypical antipsychotic with lower risk of extrapyramidal side effects than typical antipsychotics.

RVU Information

CPT J3486 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
