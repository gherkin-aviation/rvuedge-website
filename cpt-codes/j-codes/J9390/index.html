J9390 Vinorelbine tartrate injection

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: vinorelbine, Navelbine

Injection of vinorelbine tartrate, a semi-synthetic vinca alkaloid. Used for non-small cell lung cancer and breast cancer.

In Plain Language

lung cancer chemotherapy injection; vinca alkaloid cancer drug

Clinical Context

Used for non-small cell lung cancer as single agent or combined therapy. Also used for metastatic breast cancer.

RVU Information

CPT J9390 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
