J7501 Azathioprine parenteral

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: azathioprine IV, parenteral immunosuppressant

Parenteral injection of azathioprine, an intravenous immunosuppressive antimetabolite for autoimmune disease management.

In Plain Language

IV immune suppressant; rheumatoid arthritis injection

Clinical Context

Used for intravenous immunosuppression in severe autoimmune conditions, transplant rejection prevention, and inflammatory disease.

RVU Information

CPT J7501 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
