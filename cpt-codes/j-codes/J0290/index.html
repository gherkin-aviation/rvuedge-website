J0290 Ampicillin 500 milligrams injection

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: ampicillin, Omnipen

Ampicillin 500 milligrams injection, a beta-lactam antibiotic for bacterial infections.

In Plain Language

antibiotic injection; bacterial infection treatment

Clinical Context

Intravenous penicillin-based antibiotic for respiratory, urinary, skin, and other gram-positive and some gram-negative infections. First-line for many community-acquired infections.

RVU Information

CPT J0290 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
