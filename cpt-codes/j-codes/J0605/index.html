J0605 Sucroferric oxyhydroxide 5mg

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: sucroferric oxyhydroxide, Velphoro, iron phosphate binder

Sucroferric oxyhydroxide oral, 5 milligrams, an iron-based phosphate binder for hyperphosphatemia treatment in chronic kidney disease.

In Plain Language

iron and phosphate control medication; dialysis mineral balance supplement

Clinical Context

Novel iron-based phosphate binder that does not contain calcium, aluminum, or lanthanum. Reduces phosphate absorption while providing supplemental iron, beneficial in anemic ESRD patients.

RVU Information

CPT J0605 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
