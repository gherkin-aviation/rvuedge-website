J0558 Peng benzathine/procaine injection

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: penicillin G benzathine/procaine combo, mixed penicillin G

Penicillin G benzathine/procaine combination injection for intramuscular administration, a long-acting penicillin formulation for systemic infection treatment.

In Plain Language

long-acting antibiotic shot; mixed penicillin injection

Clinical Context

Used for treatment of syphilis, streptococcal pharyngitis, and other susceptible infections requiring prolonged penicillin levels. The benzathine component provides extended duration.

RVU Information

CPT J0558 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
