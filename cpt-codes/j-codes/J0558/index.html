J0558 Peng benzathine/procaine injection

J-Codes

Also known as: penicillin G benzathine/procaine combo, mixed penicillin G

Penicillin G benzathine/procaine combination injection for intramuscular administration, a long-acting penicillin formulation for systemic infection treatment.

Clinical Context

Used for treatment of syphilis, streptococcal pharyngitis, and other susceptible infections requiring prolonged penicillin levels. The benzathine component provides extended duration.
