J3300 Triamcinolone a injection prs-free

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: triamcinolone, Kenalog, preservative-free corticosteroid

Triamcinolone acetonide injection is a preservative-free corticosteroid formulation used for articular and periarticular injection in joint inflammation.

In Plain Language

joint inflammation injection; cortisone shot

Clinical Context

Used for intra-articular injection in rheumatoid arthritis, osteoarthritis, and other joint inflammatory conditions. Preservative-free formulation reduces tissue irritation. Provides anti-inflammatory effect for 2-4 weeks.

RVU Information

CPT J3300 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
