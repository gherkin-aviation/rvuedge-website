J1201 Injection. cetirizine hcl 0.5mg

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: Zyrtec injection, cetirizine

Cetirizine hydrochloride injection supplied in 0.5 milligram units, a second-generation antihistamine for allergic reactions.

In Plain Language

Non-drowsy allergy medicine injection

Clinical Context

Used for acute allergic reactions and urticaria in acute care settings with reduced sedation compared to first-generation antihistamines.

RVU Information

CPT J1201 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
