J7070 D5w infusion

J-Codes

Also known as: D5W, 5% dextrose/water

D5W infusion is 5 percent dextrose in water administered intravenously for hydration and caloric supplementation.

Clinical Context

Maintenance intravenous fluid providing hydration and dextrose calories. Hypotonic solution requiring careful use in neurologic conditions. Used for glucose supplementation.
