J7402 Mometasone sinus sinuva

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: Sinuva, mometasone implant, sinus steroid

Sustained-release mometasone furoate sinus implant (Sinuva), a biodegradable corticosteroid implant for post-sinus surgery inflammation.

In Plain Language

sinus surgery inflammation prevention; steroid implant

Clinical Context

Indicated for prevention of nasal polyposis and reduction of inflammation following sinus surgery in chronic rhinosinusitis patients.

RVU Information

CPT J7402 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
