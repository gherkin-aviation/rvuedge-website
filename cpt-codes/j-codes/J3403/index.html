J3403 Revakinagene, per implant

J-Codes

Also known as: revakinagene autotemcel, TIL therapy, tumor-infiltrating lymphocyte therapy

Revakinagene autotemcel per implant injection is autologous T cell immunotherapy for melanoma treatment.

Clinical Context

Autologous tumor-infiltrating lymphocytes isolated from resected melanoma, expanded, and reinfused. Intravenous administration to treat metastatic melanoma. Enhances anti-tumor immune response.
