J7171 Injection, adzynma, 10 iu

J-Codes

Also known as: Adzynma, long-acting Factor VIII, pegylated Factor VIII

Injection of Adzynma (antihemophilic factor [recombinant], porcine sequence, pegylated), a long-acting recombinant Factor VIII, supplied as 10 International Units per dose.

Clinical Context

Used for routine prophylaxis to prevent or reduce the frequency of bleeding episodes in hemophilia A patients, benefits from extended half-life reducing injection frequency.
