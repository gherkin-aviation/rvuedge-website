J1980 Hyoscyamine sulfate injection

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: hyoscyamine, Levsin

Injection of hyoscyamine sulfate. An anticholinergic medication for gastrointestinal spasm and irritable bowel syndrome.

In Plain Language

stomach cramp relief; digestive tract antispasm medication

Clinical Context

Used for functional bowel disorders, irritable bowel syndrome, and gastrointestinal spasm. Hyoscyamine blocks acetylcholine to reduce smooth muscle contractions.

RVU Information

CPT J1980 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
