J9351 Topotecan injection

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: topotecan, Hycamtin

Injection of topotecan, a topoisomerase I inhibitor. Used for ovarian cancer, small cell lung cancer, and other malignancies.

In Plain Language

cancer chemotherapy injection; topoisomerase inhibitor

Clinical Context

Used for metastatic ovarian cancer after platinum-taxane failure and small cell lung cancer. Topoisomerase I inhibitor causing DNA strand breaks.

RVU Information

CPT J9351 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
