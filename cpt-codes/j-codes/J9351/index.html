J9351 Topotecan injection

J-Codes

Also known as: topotecan, Hycamtin

Injection of topotecan, a topoisomerase I inhibitor. Used for ovarian cancer, small cell lung cancer, and other malignancies.

Clinical Context

Used for metastatic ovarian cancer after platinum-taxane failure and small cell lung cancer. Topoisomerase I inhibitor causing DNA strand breaks.
