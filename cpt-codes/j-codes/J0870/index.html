J0870 Injection, imetelstat, 1 milligrams

J-Codes

Also known as: GRN163L, imetelstat, Gamifant analog

Imetelstat injection supplied in 1 milligram units, a telomerase inhibitor for treatment of myelodysplastic syndrome and chronic lymphocytic leukemia.

Clinical Context

Used in patients with myelodysplastic syndrome or chronic lymphocytic leukemia to inhibit telomerase activity and reduce abnormal cell proliferation.
