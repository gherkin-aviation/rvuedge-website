J7301 Skyla, 13.5 milligrams

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: Skyla, hormonal IUD, levonorgestrel IUS

Insertion of Skyla (levonorgestrel-releasing intrauterine system), 13.5 milligrams, a 3-year reversible contraceptive device.

In Plain Language

intrauterine birth control; hormone-releasing coil

Clinical Context

Indicated for contraception in women, smaller size designed for nulliparous women, provides 3-year efficacy with minimal bleeding.

RVU Information

CPT J7301 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
