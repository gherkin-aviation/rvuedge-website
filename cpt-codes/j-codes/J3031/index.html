J3031 Injection., fremanezumab-vfrm 1 milligrams

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: fremanezumab, Ajovy, CGRP antagonist

Fremanezumab-vfrm (Ajovy) 1 milligram injection is a monoclonal antibody that blocks calcitonin gene-related peptide ligand, administered for chronic migraine prevention.

In Plain Language

migraine prevention injection; monthly migraine shot

Clinical Context

Indicated for chronic migraine prevention in patients with four or more migraine days per month. Administered monthly or quarterly depending on formulation. Reduces migraine frequency by targeting neuroinflammatory pathways.

RVU Information

CPT J3031 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
