J0280 Aminophyllin 250 milligrams injection

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: aminophylline, IV bronchodilator

Aminophylline 250 milligrams injection, a bronchodilator and theophylline derivative for bronchospasm.

In Plain Language

asthma breathing medication; airway opening injection

Clinical Context

Intravenous xanthine bronchodilator for acute asthma exacerbations and apnea of prematurity. Relaxes smooth muscle and has mild diuretic properties.

RVU Information

CPT J0280 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
