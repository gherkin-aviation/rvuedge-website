J1305 Injection, evinacumab-dgnb, 5mg

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: Evkeeza, evinacumab

Evinacumab-dgnb injection supplied in 5 milligram units, a monoclonal antibody against angiopoietin-3 for severe hypercholesterolemia.

In Plain Language

Medicine for extremely high cholesterol

Clinical Context

Used for treatment of severe hypercholesterolemia and familial hypercholesterolemia resistant to standard therapies with novel mechanism.

RVU Information

CPT J1305 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
