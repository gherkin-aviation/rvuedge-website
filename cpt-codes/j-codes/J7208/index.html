J7208 Injection. jivi 1 iu

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: Jivi, Fc-fusion Factor VIII, extended Factor VIII

Injection of Jivi (antihemophilic factor [recombinant], Fc fusion protein), 1 International Unit of Factor VIII per dose.

In Plain Language

hemophilia A treatment; long-acting clotting factor

Clinical Context

Used for prophylaxis and on-demand treatment of bleeding in hemophilia A patients.

RVU Information

CPT J7208 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
