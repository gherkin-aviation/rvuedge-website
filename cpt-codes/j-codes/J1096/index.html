J1096 Dexametha opth insert 0.1 milligrams

J-Codes

Also known as: Dexamethasone eye insert, ocular implant

Dexamethasone ophthalmic insert supplied in 0.1 milligram units for ocular anti-inflammatory effects in the eye.

Clinical Context

Used for treatment of ocular inflammation, dry eye disease, and post-operative ocular inflammation with sustained release from subconjunctival implant.
