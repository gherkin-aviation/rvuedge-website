J0665 Injection, bupivacaine, not otherwise specified, 0.5mg

J-Codes

Also known as: bupivacaine HCl, Marcaine, local anesthetic

Bupivacaine hydrochloride injection, not otherwise specified, 0.5 milligrams, a local anesthetic for infiltration, nerve block, and epidural/spinal anesthesia.

Clinical Context

Long-acting amide local anesthetic used for infiltration anesthesia, peripheral nerve blocks, epidural and spinal anesthesia. Provides prolonged anesthesia lasting several hours.
