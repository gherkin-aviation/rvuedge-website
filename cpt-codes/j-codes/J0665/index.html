J0665 Injection, bupivacaine, not otherwise specified, 0.5mg

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: bupivacaine HCl, Marcaine, local anesthetic

Bupivacaine hydrochloride injection, not otherwise specified, 0.5 milligrams, a local anesthetic for infiltration, nerve block, and epidural/spinal anesthesia.

In Plain Language

numbing medication injection; local pain relief anesthetic

Clinical Context

Long-acting amide local anesthetic used for infiltration anesthesia, peripheral nerve blocks, epidural and spinal anesthesia. Provides prolonged anesthesia lasting several hours.

RVU Information

CPT J0665 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
