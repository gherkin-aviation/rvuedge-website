J2468 Injection, palonosetron (posfrea)

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: palonosetron, posfrea, aloxi

Injection of palonosetron hydrochloride (Posfrea brand), a highly selective 5-HT3 receptor antagonist with longer duration than ondansetron, used for chemotherapy-induced nausea and vomiting (CINV).

In Plain Language

nausea prevention for chemotherapy; long-acting anti-nausea medication

Clinical Context

Administered prophylactically before highly emetogenic chemotherapy and in postoperative nausea and vomiting (PONV) prevention due to its prolonged receptor occupancy.

RVU Information

CPT J2468 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
