J0883 Argatroban nonesrd use 1mg

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: Argatroban, direct thrombin inhibitor

Argatroban injection supplied in 1 milligram units for non-end-stage renal disease patients, a direct thrombin inhibitor for anticoagulation.

In Plain Language

Blood thinner for people without kidney disease

Clinical Context

Used for anticoagulation in patients with heparin-induced thrombocytopenia requiring non-kidney dialysis adapted dosing.

RVU Information

CPT J0883 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
