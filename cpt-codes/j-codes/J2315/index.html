J2315 Naltrexone, depot form

J-Codes

Also known as: naltrexone depot, Vivitrol

Injection of naltrexone, depot form. A long-acting opioid antagonist for alcohol and opioid dependence.

Clinical Context

Used for maintenance treatment of opioid and alcohol dependence with monthly intramuscular or extended dosing. Provides sustained antagonism with improved compliance.
