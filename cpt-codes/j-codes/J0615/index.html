J0615 Calcium acetate, oral, 23 milligrams

J-Codes

Also known as: calcium acetate, PhosLo, phosphate binder

Calcium acetate oral, 23 milligrams, a phosphate binder and calcium supplement for treatment of hyperphosphatemia in chronic kidney disease and dialysis.

Clinical Context

Calcium salt that binds dietary phosphate in the GI tract while providing supplemental calcium. First-line phosphate binder in ESRD patients without severe hypercalcemia.
