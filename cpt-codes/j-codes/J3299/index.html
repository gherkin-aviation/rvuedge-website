J3299 Injection xipere 1 milligrams

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: xipere, triamcinolone acetonide, intravitreal steroid

Xipere 1 milligram injection is a novel therapeutic agent administered by intraocular injection for macular edema treatment.

In Plain Language

eye inflammation treatment; macular swelling injection

Clinical Context

Indicated for macular edema treatment via intravitreal injection. Long-acting corticosteroid formulation for retinal inflammation. Used in diabetic macular edema, retinal vein occlusion, and uveitic macular edema.

RVU Information

CPT J3299 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
