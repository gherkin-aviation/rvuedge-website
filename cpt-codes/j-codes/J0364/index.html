J0364 Apomorphine hydrochloride

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: apomorphine, Apokyn

Apomorphine hydrochloride injection, a dopamine agonist for Parkinson disease motor fluctuations.

In Plain Language

Parkinson disease rescue medication; movement disorder treatment

Clinical Context

Subcutaneous dopamine agonist for acute off episodes (motor fluctuations) in Parkinson disease. Rapid onset (10-15 minutes). Requires antiemetic pretreatment.

RVU Information

CPT J0364 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
