J0131 Injection, acetaminophen (not otherwise specified)

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: acetaminophen injection, Tylenol IV, paracetamol

Acetaminophen injection not otherwise specified, a nonopioid analgesic and antipyretic for pain and fever.

In Plain Language

pain reliever injection; fever reducer injection

Clinical Context

Intravenous acetaminophen for moderate to severe acute pain and fever. Used when oral route unavailable. Administered as adjunct to other analgesics or standalone for mild-moderate pain.

RVU Information

CPT J0131 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
