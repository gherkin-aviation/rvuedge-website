J3396 Verteporfin injection

J-Codes

Also known as: verteporfin, Visudyne, photosensitizing agent

Verteporfin injection is a photosensitizing dye used with light activation for photodynamic therapy in age-related macular degeneration.

Clinical Context

Used in age-related macular degeneration with subfoveal choroidal neovascularization. Intravenous injection followed by laser light activation to treat abnormal blood vessels. Minimizes damage to healthy retinal tissue.
