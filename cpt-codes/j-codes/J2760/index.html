J2760 Phentolaine mesylate injection

J-Codes

Also known as: phentolamine, regitine

Injection of phentolamine mesylate (Regitine), an alpha-adrenergic antagonist used for management of hypertension in pheochromocytoma and treatment of extravasation.

Clinical Context

Used in emergency management of pheochromocytoma crisis and to prevent tissue necrosis from extravasation of vasoconstrictive agents.
