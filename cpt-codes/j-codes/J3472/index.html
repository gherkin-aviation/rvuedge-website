J3472 Ovine, 1000 usp units

J-Codes +Add-on

Also known as: hyaluronidase, ovine hyaluronidase, spreading agent

Hyaluronidase (ovine) 1000 USP units injection is an enzyme spreading agent for enhanced drug diffusion.

Clinical Context

Used as spreading agent for co-administered medications. Ovine-derived enzyme at 1000 USP units per injection. Enhances subcutaneous and intramuscular drug distribution.
