J2427 Injection, invega hafyera/trinza

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: invega hafyera, invega trinza, paliperidone extended-release

Injection of paliperidone palmitate extended-release formulations (Invega Hafyera or Invega Trinza), used for maintenance antipsychotic therapy in schizophrenia.

In Plain Language

very long-acting antipsychotic; 3-month or 6-month psychiatric injection

Clinical Context

Used for long-term maintenance treatment of schizophrenia in stable patients, with Hafyera given every 3 months and Trinza every 6 months.

RVU Information

CPT J2427 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
