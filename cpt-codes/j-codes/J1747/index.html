J1747 Injection, spesolimab-sbzo, 1 milligrams

J-Codes

Also known as: spesolimab-sbzo, Spevigo, IL-36 antagonist

Spesolimab-sbzo injection, 1 milligram. An interleukin-36 receptor antagonist for generalized pustular psoriasis.

Clinical Context

Used for generalized pustular psoriasis, an acute life-threatening inflammatory skin condition. Intravenous administration with rapid improvement of pustules and systemic symptoms.
