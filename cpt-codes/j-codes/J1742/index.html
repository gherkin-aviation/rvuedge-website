J1742 Ibutilide fumarate injection

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: ibutilide, Corvert

Ibutilide fumarate injection. An antiarrhythmic agent for atrial fibrillation and atrial flutter conversion.

In Plain Language

heart rhythm medication; irregular heartbeat treatment

Clinical Context

Used for acute conversion of atrial fibrillation or atrial flutter to sinus rhythm. Intravenous administration with continuous cardiac monitoring required.

RVU Information

CPT J1742 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
