J0640 Leucovorin calcium injection

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: leucovorin calcium, folinic acid, citrovorum factor

Leucovorin calcium (folinic acid) injection for intravenous or intramuscular administration, a reduced form of folic acid used as chemotherapy rescue agent.

In Plain Language

chemotherapy rescue medication; folate supplement injection

Clinical Context

Reduced folate cofactor administered after high-dose methotrexate to prevent myelosuppression and gastrointestinal toxicity. Also used to enhance fluorouracil efficacy in colorectal cancer.

RVU Information

CPT J0640 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
