J0278 Amikacin sulfate injection

J-Codes

Also known as: amikacin, Amikin

Amikacin sulfate injection, an aminoglycoside antibiotic for gram-negative bacterial infections.

Clinical Context

Intravenous aminoglycoside antibiotic used for serious gram-negative infections. Used in combination therapy for pseudomonas and other resistant organisms.
