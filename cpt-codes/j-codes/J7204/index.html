Also known as: esperoct, Fc-fusion Factor VIII, extended Factor VIII

Injection of recombinant esperoct (coagulation Factor VIII recombinant, Fc-fusion protein, per International Unit).

Clinical Context

Used for routine prophylaxis and on-demand treatment of bleeding in hemophilia A patients, extended half-life reduces injection frequency.
