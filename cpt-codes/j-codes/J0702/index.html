J0702 Betamethasone acet & sod phosp

J-Codes

Also known as: betamethasone acetate/phosphate, Celestone, corticosteroid combination

Betamethasone acetate and sodium phosphate injection, a corticosteroid combination for anti-inflammatory and immunosuppressive effects.

Clinical Context

Combination betamethasone formulation providing rapid onset (phosphate) and sustained action (acetate) for various inflammatory and autoimmune conditions.
