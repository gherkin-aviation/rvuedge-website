J0525 Injection cefotetan disodium 10 milligrams

J-Codes

Also known as: cefotetan, Cefotan

Cefotetan disodium injection, 10 milligrams, a second-generation cephalosporin antibiotic for systemic bacterial infections.

Clinical Context

Used for serious infections including lower respiratory tract, urinary tract, skin, and intra-abdominal infections, particularly against anaerobic bacteria and gram-negative organisms.
