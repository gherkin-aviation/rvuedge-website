J1561 Gamunex-c/gammaked

J-Codes

Also known as: Gamunex-C, Gammaked, IVIG

Gamunex-C and Gammaked (human immunoglobulin intravenous) injection. Intravenous immunoglobulin formulations for immunodeficiency and immune disorders.

Clinical Context

Used for primary and secondary immunodeficiency, chronic inflammatory demyelinating polyneuropathy, immune thrombocytopenia, and immune-mediated conditions.
