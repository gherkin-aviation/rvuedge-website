J7050 Normal saline solution infusion

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: normal saline, NS, saline

Normal saline solution infusion is intravenous 0.9 percent sodium chloride for hydration and medication administration.

In Plain Language

salt water IV; hydration fluid

Clinical Context

Standard intravenous fluid for hydration, medication dilution, and electrolyte maintenance. Isotonic crystalloid solution. Used universally in acute care and perioperative settings.

RVU Information

CPT J7050 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
