J8597 Antiemetic drug oral not otherwise specified

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: Unlisted antiemetic, Other anti-nausea pill

Antiemetic drugs administered orally, not otherwise specified; catch-all for oral anti-nausea medications without specific codes.

In Plain Language

Nausea and vomiting prevention pill not listed elsewhere

Clinical Context

Used for nausea and vomiting when specific antiemetic codes are not available or applicable.

RVU Information

CPT J8597 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
