J0137 Injection, acetaminophen (hikma)

J-Codes

Also known as: acetaminophen injection, IV Tylenol Hikma

Acetaminophen injection manufactured by Hikma, a nonopioid analgesic and antipyretic.

Clinical Context

Intravenous acetaminophen (Hikma brand) for perioperative analgesia and fever management. Used when oral pain relievers cannot be taken.
