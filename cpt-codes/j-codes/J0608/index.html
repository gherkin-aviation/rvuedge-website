J0608 Lanthanum carbonate pwdr 5mg

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: lanthanum carbonate, Fosrenol powder, lanthanide binder

Lanthanum carbonate powder, 5 milligrams, a lanthanide-based phosphate binder offered in powder form for flexible dosing in dialysis patients.

In Plain Language

powdered lanthanide phosphate control; dissolvable dialysis mineral medication

Clinical Context

Powder formulation of lanthanum carbonate allowing flexible dosing adjustment and easier administration in patients with dysphagia. Same phosphate-binding efficacy as tablet form.

RVU Information

CPT J0608 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
