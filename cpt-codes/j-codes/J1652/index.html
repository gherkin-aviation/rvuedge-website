J1652 Fondaparinux sodium

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: fondaparinux, Arixtra, Factor Xa inhibitor

Fondaparinux sodium injection. A selective Factor Xa inhibitor for venous thromboembolism treatment and prevention.

In Plain Language

blood thinner injection; clot prevention medication

Clinical Context

Used for acute venous thromboembolism treatment and thromboprophylaxis in high-risk surgical patients. Subcutaneous administration with renal function monitoring.

RVU Information

CPT J1652 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
