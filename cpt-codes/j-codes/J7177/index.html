J7177 Injection., fibryga, 1 milligrams

J-Codes

Also known as: Fibryga, fibrinogen concentrate, clotting factor

Injection of Fibryga (fibrinogen concentrate [human]), supplied as 1 milligram per dose for treating fibrinogen deficiency and coagulation disorders.

Clinical Context

Indicated for treatment of afibrinogenemia or hypofibrinogenemia in patients experiencing or at risk for bleeding complications.
