J1811 Fiasp for insulin pump use

J-Codes

Also known as: fiasp, fast-acting insulin, insulin pump insulin

Fiasp (faster-acting insulin aspart) formulation specifically prepared for use in insulin pump devices.

Clinical Context

Used exclusively for continuous subcutaneous insulin infusion via pump. Fiasp has faster onset and offset compared to standard insulin aspart, allowing better glycemic control in pump users.
