J0652 Injection, levothyroxine, hikma

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: levothyroxine hikma, Hikma thyroid hormone, branded T4 injection

Levothyroxine sodium injection, Hikma brand, for intravenous administration as thyroid hormone replacement in acute hypothyroidism.

In Plain Language

brand name thyroid hormone injection; emergency hypothyroidism treatment

Clinical Context

Branded formulation of intravenous levothyroxine for acute hypothyroidism management, myxedema coma, and emergency thyroid hormone replacement when oral administration is unavailable.

RVU Information

CPT J0652 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
