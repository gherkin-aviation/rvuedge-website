J0696 Ceftriaxone sodium injection

J-Codes

Also known as: ceftriaxone, Rocephin, 3rd-generation cephalosporin

Ceftriaxone sodium injection, a third-generation cephalosporin with broad spectrum for serious infections including meningitis.

Clinical Context

Third-generation cephalosporin with excellent cerebrospinal fluid penetration, making it preferred for meningitis. Also used for pneumonia, endocarditis, gonorrhea, and other serious infections.
