J9173 Injection., durvalumab, 10 milligrams

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: durvalumab, Imfinzi

Intravenous injection of durvalumab, a human monoclonal antibody targeting programmed death ligand-1 (PD-L1), dosed per 10 milligrams.

In Plain Language

immune checkpoint inhibitor; lung cancer immunotherapy

Clinical Context

Used for unresectable stage III non-small cell lung cancer and advanced bladder cancer, blocking PD-L1 immune checkpoint.

RVU Information

CPT J9173 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
