J9155 Degarelix injection

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: degarelix, Firmagon

Intravenous injection of degarelix, a gonadotropin-releasing hormone (GnRH) antagonist.

In Plain Language

testosterone-lowering prostate cancer treatment; hormone therapy

Clinical Context

Used for advanced prostate cancer, achieving rapid testosterone suppression without initial testosterone surge.

RVU Information

CPT J9155 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
