J9318 Injection romidepsin non-lyo 0.1mg

J-Codes

Also known as: romidepsin, Istodax

Injection of romidepsin non-lyophilized formulation, 0.1 milligram. A histone deacetylase inhibitor for cutaneous T-cell lymphoma.

Clinical Context

Used for cutaneous T-cell lymphoma (mycosis fungoides). Inhibits histone deacetylase, leading to gene expression alterations in malignant cells.
