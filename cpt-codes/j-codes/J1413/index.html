J1413 Injection delandistrogene mox rokl

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: delandistrogene moxeparvovec, SMA gene therapy

Delandistrogene moxeparvovec injection. A gene therapy for spinal muscular atrophy type 1 administered intravenously.

In Plain Language

spinal muscular atrophy treatment; genetic muscle disease therapy

Clinical Context

Used for spinal muscular atrophy type 1 (SMA1), a severe genetic neuromuscular disease in infants. Gene therapy designed to restore SMN protein production. Single-dose therapy with potential for sustained benefit.

RVU Information

CPT J1413 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
