J0743 Cilastatin sodium injection

J-Codes

Also known as: cilastatin sodium, enzyme inhibitor, beta-lactam protector

Cilastatin sodium injection, a renal dehydropeptidase inhibitor that prevents inactivation of imipenem and other beta-lactams.

Clinical Context

Renal enzyme inhibitor that prevents inactivation of imipenem and other beta-lactams by renal dehydropeptidase, preserving antibiotic levels and reducing nephrotoxicity.
