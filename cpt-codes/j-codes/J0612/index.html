J0612 Injection, calcium gluconate, not otherwise specified

J-Codes

Also known as: calcium gluconate, calcium salt injection, electrolyte replacement

Calcium gluconate injection, not otherwise specified, for intravenous or intramuscular administration in treatment of hypocalcemia and electrolyte emergencies.

Clinical Context

Rapidly restores serum calcium in symptomatic hypocalcemia from hypoparathyroidism, vitamin D deficiency, or acute electrolyte abnormalities. Administered intravenously to avoid tissue necrosis.
