J0289 Amphotericin b liposome injection

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: amphotericin B liposome, AmBisome

Amphotericin B liposome injection, a liposomal-formulated antifungal for fungal infections.

In Plain Language

fungal infection treatment; gentler antifungal formulation

Clinical Context

Intravenous liposomal amphotericin B with significantly reduced nephrotoxicity and infusion reactions. Preferred formulation for immunocompromised patients and serious fungal infections.

RVU Information

CPT J0289 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
