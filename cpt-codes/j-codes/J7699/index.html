J7699 Inhalation solution for dme

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: Unlisted inhalation solution, Other inhalation medication

Inhalation solutions for durable medical equipment use; catch-all code for inhalation medications not specifically listed elsewhere.

In Plain Language

Breathing medication not listed elsewhere

Clinical Context

Used for inhalation drugs administered via durable medical equipment when specific CPT codes are not available.

RVU Information

CPT J7699 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
