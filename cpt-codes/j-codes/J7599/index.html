J7599 Immunosuppressive drug noc

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: Unlisted immunosuppressant, Other immunosuppressive drug

Immunosuppressive drugs not otherwise classified; used when specific immunosuppressive agents are not separately coded.

In Plain Language

Transplant rejection prevention medication not listed elsewhere

Clinical Context

Used for immunosuppressive agents without specific CPT codes. Appropriate for novel or rarely-used immunosuppressive medications.

RVU Information

CPT J7599 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
