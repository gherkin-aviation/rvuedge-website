J7295 Eth estr and eton monthly

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: NuvaRing, contraceptive ring, monthly hormonal contraceptive

Ethinyl estradiol and etonogestrel monthly subdermal contraceptive implant or vaginal ring, providing 28-30 days of contraceptive protection.

In Plain Language

birth control ring; monthly contraception

Clinical Context

Used for contraception in women, monthly formulation inserted vaginally or subdermally, reversible with quick return to fertility.

RVU Information

CPT J7295 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
