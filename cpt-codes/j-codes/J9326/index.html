J9326 Telisotuzumab vedotin-tllv

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: telisotuzumab vedotin, Subtam

Injection of telisotuzumab vedotin-tllv, an antibody-drug conjugate targeting c-Met receptor. Used for solid tumors with c-Met expression.

In Plain Language

lung cancer injection; targeted therapy drug

Clinical Context

Used for metastatic non-small cell lung cancer with c-Met expression. Antibody-drug conjugate targeting hepatocyte growth factor receptor.

RVU Information

CPT J9326 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
