J1611 Injection glucagon hcl, fresenius

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: glucagon Fresenius, emergency glucose

Glucagon hydrochloride injection (Fresenius formulation). A generic glucagon for severe hypoglycemia emergency treatment.

In Plain Language

blood sugar emergency treatment; hypoglycemia rescue

Clinical Context

Used for severe hypoglycemia emergency treatment in diabetic patients. Fresenius formulation with same therapeutic indication as reference product.

RVU Information

CPT J1611 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
