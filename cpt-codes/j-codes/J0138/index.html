J0138 Injection acetaminoph 10mg/ibu 3mg

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: acetaminophen/ibuprofen combination, APAP/ibuprofen

Acetaminophen 10 milligrams with ibuprofen 3 milligrams injection, a combination nonopioid analgesic.

In Plain Language

pain reliever combination injection; two pain medications together

Clinical Context

Combination of acetaminophen and ibuprofen (nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drug) for enhanced analgesia in acute moderate pain. Synergistic effect allows lower individual doses.

RVU Information

CPT J0138 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
