J7632 Cromolyn sodium complicated unit

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: Intal compact unit, Cromolyn compact

Cromolyn sodium delivered by inhalation in a compact unit dose form; a mast cell stabilizer used for asthma prevention and allergic airway disease.

In Plain Language

Asthma prevention inhaler; Allergy prevention medication

Clinical Context

Used for prophylaxis of asthma exacerbations and prevention of allergic airway responses. Compact unit dose for convenient, portable use.

RVU Information

CPT J7632 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
