J0791 Injection crizanlizumab-tmca 5mg

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: Adakveo, crizanlizumab

Crizanlizumab-tmca monoclonal antibody injection supplied in 5 milligram units, a selective adhesion molecule inhibitor for vaso-occlusive crisis prevention.

In Plain Language

Medicine to prevent sickle cell pain crises

Clinical Context

Used in adults with sickle cell disease to reduce the frequency of vaso-occlusive crises by inhibiting vascular adhesion of red blood cells.

RVU Information

CPT J0791 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
