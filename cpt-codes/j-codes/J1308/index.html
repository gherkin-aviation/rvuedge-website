J1308 Injection, famotidine, 0.25 milligrams

J-Codes

Also known as: Pepcid IV, famotidine

Famotidine injection supplied in 0.25 milligram units, a histamine 2 receptor antagonist for treatment of peptic ulcer disease and gastroesophageal reflux.

Clinical Context

Used for acute management of peptic ulcer disease, gastroesophageal reflux, and prevention of stress ulcers in critically ill patients.
