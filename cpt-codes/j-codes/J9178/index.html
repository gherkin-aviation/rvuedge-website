J9178 Injection, epirubicin hcl, 2 milligrams

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: epirubicin, Ellence

Intravenous injection of epirubicin hydrochloride, an anthracycline topoisomerase II inhibitor, dosed per 2 milligrams.

In Plain Language

red chemotherapy drug; DNA-damaging cancer drug

Clinical Context

Used for breast cancer and other cancers as component of combination chemotherapy regimens.

RVU Information

CPT J9178 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
