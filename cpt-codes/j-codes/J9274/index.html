J9274 Injection, tebentafusp-tebn, 1 micrograms

J-Codes

Also known as: tebentafusp, Imlygic

Injection of tebentafusp-tebn, 1 microgram. A gp100 peptide-major histocompatibility complex bispecific molecule for melanoma immunotherapy.

Clinical Context

Used for HLA-A*02:01-positive melanoma with metastatic disease or unresectable primary tumors. Activates T-cells against gp100-expressing melanoma cells.
