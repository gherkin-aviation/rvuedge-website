J0216 Injection, alfentanil hcl, 500mcg

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: alfentanil, Alfenta

Alfentanil hydrochloride injection 500 micrograms, a rapid-onset opioid for anesthesia.

In Plain Language

strong pain medication injection; anesthesia medication

Clinical Context

Intravenous rapid-onset, short-duration opioid used during anesthesia induction and maintenance, and for breakthrough pain. Used in critical care settings.

RVU Information

CPT J0216 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
