J7686 Treprostinil, non-comp unit

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: Tyvaso unit dose, Treprostinil unit dose

Treprostinil delivered by inhalation in a non-compact unit dose form; a prostacyclin analog used for maintenance therapy in pulmonary arterial hypertension.

In Plain Language

Pulmonary high blood pressure inhaler; Pulmonary hypertension medication

Clinical Context

Used for treatment of pulmonary arterial hypertension to improve exercise capacity and reduce symptoms. Inhaled prostacyclin mimic.

RVU Information

CPT J7686 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
