J7686 Treprostinil, non-comp unit

J-Codes

Also known as: Tyvaso unit dose, Treprostinil unit dose

Treprostinil delivered by inhalation in a non-compact unit dose form; a prostacyclin analog used for maintenance therapy in pulmonary arterial hypertension.

Clinical Context

Used for treatment of pulmonary arterial hypertension to improve exercise capacity and reduce symptoms. Inhaled prostacyclin mimic.
