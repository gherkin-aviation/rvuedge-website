J1675 Histrelin acetate

J-Codes

Also known as: histrelin, Vantas, LHRH agonist

Histrelin acetate injection. An LHRH agonist for prostate cancer and central precocious puberty treatment.

Clinical Context

Used for advanced prostate cancer to suppress testosterone production and for central precocious puberty in children. Subcutaneous implant with sustained hormone delivery.
