J2151 Injection, mannitol, 250 milligrams

J-Codes

Also known as: mannitol

Injection of mannitol, 250 milligrams. An osmotic diuretic for cerebral edema and increased intracranial pressure.

Clinical Context

Used for increased intracranial pressure, cerebral edema, glaucoma, and acute kidney injury prevention. Acts as an osmotic agent to remove fluid from tissues.
