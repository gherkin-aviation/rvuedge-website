J0583 Bivalirudin

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: bivalirudin, Angiomax, direct thrombin inhibitor

Bivalirudin injection, a direct thrombin inhibitor anticoagulant for systemic use in preventing thrombotic complications during cardiac procedures.

In Plain Language

blood thinner injection; anticoagulant medication

Clinical Context

Systemic anticoagulation for acute coronary syndromes and cardiac procedures. Direct thrombin inhibitor offering more predictable anticoagulation than heparin, particularly beneficial in HIT-positive patients.

RVU Information

CPT J0583 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
