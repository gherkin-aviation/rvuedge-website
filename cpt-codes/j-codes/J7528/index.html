J7528 Mycophen mofetil for susp

J-Codes

Also known as: Cellcept suspension, Mycophenolate suspension

Mycophenolate mofetil formulated for oral suspension; a prodrug metabolized to mycophenolic acid, used as an immunosuppressive agent for transplant rejection prevention.

Clinical Context

Used in transplant recipients, particularly pediatric patients or those with difficulty swallowing tablets. Dosed 1-3 grams daily in divided doses.
