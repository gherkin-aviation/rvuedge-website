J0400 Aripiprazole injection

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: aripiprazole, Abilify

Aripiprazole injection, an atypical antipsychotic for schizophrenia and bipolar disorder.

In Plain Language

antipsychotic medication injection; schizophrenia treatment

Clinical Context

Intravenous or intramuscular partial dopamine agonist antipsychotic for acute agitation and psychosis in schizophrenia and bipolar disorder.

RVU Information

CPT J0400 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
