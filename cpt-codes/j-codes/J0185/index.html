J0185 Injection., aprepitant, 1 milligrams

J-Codes

Also known as: aprepitant, Emend IV

Aprepitant injection 1 milligram, a neurokinin-1 receptor antagonist for chemotherapy-induced nausea and vomiting.

Clinical Context

Intravenous aprepitant (neurokinin-1 antagonist) for highly emetogenic chemotherapy. Part of multimodal antiemetic regimen. Prevents acute and delayed nausea/vomiting.
