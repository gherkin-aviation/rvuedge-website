J0210 Methyldopate hcl injection

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: methyldopa IV, methyldopate

Methyldopate hydrochloride injection, an alpha-2 adrenergic agonist for hypertensive crisis.

In Plain Language

blood pressure lowering injection; hypertension emergency treatment

Clinical Context

Intravenous alpha-2 agonist used for hypertensive emergencies, particularly in pregnancy (safe in eclampsia). Decreases peripheral vascular resistance and heart rate.

RVU Information

CPT J0210 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
