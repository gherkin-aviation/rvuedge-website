J7323 Euflexxa injection per dose

J-Codes

Also known as: Euflexxa, hyaluronic acid, sodium hyaluronate

Injection of Euflexxa (sodium hyaluronate), a three-injection course of hyaluronic acid viscosupplement per dose.

Clinical Context

Used for viscosupplementation in osteoarthritis, administered as series of 3 intra-articular injections weekly.
