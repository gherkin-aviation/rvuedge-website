J0893 Injection, decitabine (sun pharma)

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: Dacogen Sun Pharma, decitabine generic

Decitabine injection (Sun Pharma pharmaceutical formulation), a hypomethylating agent for treatment of myelodysplastic syndrome.

In Plain Language

Cancer chemotherapy medicine

Clinical Context

Used for treatment of myelodysplastic syndrome patients, particularly those ineligible for intensive chemotherapy.

RVU Information

CPT J0893 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
