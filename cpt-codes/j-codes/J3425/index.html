J3425 Hydroxocobalamin intramuscular 10mcg

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: hydroxocobalamin, B12 injection, hydroxocobalamin IM

Hydroxocobalamin intramuscular 10 microgram injection is used for vitamin B12 deficiency maintenance therapy.

In Plain Language

B12 injection; vitamin B12 treatment

Clinical Context

Used for vitamin B12 deficiency maintenance therapy via intramuscular injection. Hydroxocobalamin formulation preferred over cyanocobalamin in some patients. Regular maintenance dosing for pernicious anemia.

RVU Information

CPT J3425 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
