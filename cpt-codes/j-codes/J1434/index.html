J1434 Injection, focinvez, 1mg

J-Codes

Also known as: focinvez, GVHD monoclonal antibody

Focinvez injection, 1 milligram. A bispecific monoclonal antibody for T-cell mediated acute graft-versus-host disease treatment.

Clinical Context

Used for acute graft-versus-host disease (aGVHD) in patients post-hematopoietic stem cell transplantation. Bispecific antibody redirects T-cell cytotoxicity away from host tissues.
