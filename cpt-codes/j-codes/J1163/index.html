J1163 Injection, diltiazem hcl, 0.5 milligrams

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: Cardizem injection, diltiazem

Diltiazem hydrochloride injection supplied in 0.5 milligram units, a nondihydropyridine calcium channel blocker for rate control and hypertension.

In Plain Language

Heart rate slowing medicine injection

Clinical Context

Used for acute rate control in atrial fibrillation with rapid ventricular response and for acute hypertension management in perioperative settings.

RVU Information

CPT J1163 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
