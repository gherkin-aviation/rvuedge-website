J7294 Seg acet and eth estr yearly

J-Codes

Also known as: Annovera, segesterone/ethinyl estradiol, yearly contraceptive ring

Segesterone acetate and ethinyl estradiol yearly subdermal implant, a long-acting reversible contraceptive implant providing twelve months of contraception.

Clinical Context

Used for contraception in women of reproductive age, provides 12-month protection with single annual insertion, reversible.
