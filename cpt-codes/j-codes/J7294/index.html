J7294 Seg acet and eth estr yearly

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: Annovera, segesterone/ethinyl estradiol, yearly contraceptive ring

Segesterone acetate and ethinyl estradiol yearly subdermal implant, a long-acting reversible contraceptive implant providing twelve months of contraception.

In Plain Language

birth control implant; yearly contraception

Clinical Context

Used for contraception in women of reproductive age, provides 12-month protection with single annual insertion, reversible.

RVU Information

CPT J7294 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
