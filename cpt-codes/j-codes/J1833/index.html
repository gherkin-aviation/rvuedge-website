J1833 Injection, isavuconazonium

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: isavuconazonium, Cresemba

Injection of isavuconazonium sulfate. A next-generation azole antifungal agent with improved pharmacokinetics.

In Plain Language

antifungal injection; fungal infection treatment

Clinical Context

Used for invasive aspergillosis and invasive mucormycosis infections. Isavuconazonium has good tissue penetration and can be used as bridging therapy from IV to oral formulation.

RVU Information

CPT J1833 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
