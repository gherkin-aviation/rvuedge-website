J7198 Anti-inhibitor

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: AICC, bypassing agent, Factor inhibitor bypass

Injection of anti-inhibitor coagulant complex, a prothrombin complex concentrate bypassing Factor inhibitors for hemostasis.

In Plain Language

clotting bypass medication; bleeding control

Clinical Context

Used for treatment of bleeding episodes in hemophilia A or B patients with Factor inhibitors, bypasses neutralizing antibodies.

RVU Information

CPT J7198 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
