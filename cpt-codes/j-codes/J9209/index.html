J9209 Mesna injection

J-Codes

Also known as: mesna, Mesnex

Intravenous injection of mesna, a uroprotective agent that prevents hemorrhagic cystitis.

Clinical Context

Used as protective agent with ifosfamide or high-dose cyclophosphamide to prevent hemorrhagic cystitis.
