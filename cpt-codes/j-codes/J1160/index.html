J1160 Digoxin injection

J-Codes

Also known as: Digoxin IV, cardiac glycoside

Digoxin injection, a cardiac glycoside for treatment of heart failure and atrial fibrillation arrhythmias.

Clinical Context

Used for acute management of heart failure with reduced ejection fraction and rate control in atrial fibrillation, particularly in acute decompensated heart failure.
