J9264 Paclitaxel protein bound

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: abraxane, nab-paclitaxel, protein-bound paclitaxel

Injection of paclitaxel protein-bound formulation (Abraxane). A microtubule-stabilizing agent nanoparticle albumin-bound to paclitaxel.

In Plain Language

breast cancer chemotherapy; albumin-bound cancer drug

Clinical Context

Used for metastatic breast cancer, non-small cell lung cancer, and pancreatic cancer. Preferred over standard paclitaxel in patients with neuropathy risk or hypersensitivity concerns.

RVU Information

CPT J9264 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
