J9017 Arsenic trioxide injection

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: Trisenox, Arsenic trioxide

Arsenic trioxide administered by injection; used for acute promyelocytic leukemia treatment.

In Plain Language

Acute promyelocytic leukemia treatment injection; Arsenic chemotherapy

Clinical Context

Used for acute promyelocytic leukemia with APL fusion gene. Induces apoptosis and differentiation of leukemic cells.

RVU Information

CPT J9017 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
