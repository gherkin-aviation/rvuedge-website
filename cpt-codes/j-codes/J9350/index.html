J9350 Injection mosunetuzumab-axgb, 1 milligrams

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: mosunetuzumab, Lunsumio

Injection of mosunetuzumab-axgb, 1 milligram. A bispecific antibody engaging CD3-positive T-cells to CD20-positive B-cell lymphomas.

In Plain Language

lymphoma immune injection; T-cell engaging therapy

Clinical Context

Used for relapsed/refractory B-cell non-Hodgkin lymphoma. Bispecific antibody engaging T-cells to CD20-expressing B-lymphoma cells.

RVU Information

CPT J9350 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
