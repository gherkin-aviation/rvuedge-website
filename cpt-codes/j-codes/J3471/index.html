J3471 Ovine, up to 999 usp units

J-Codes +Add-on

Also known as: hyaluronidase, ovine hyaluronidase, spreading agent

Hyaluronidase (ovine) up to 999 USP (United States Pharmacopeia) units injection is an enzyme spreading agent with limited dosing.

Clinical Context

Used as spreading agent for co-administered medications. Ovine-derived enzyme up to 999 USP units per injection. Enhances drug diffusion and absorption at injection site.
