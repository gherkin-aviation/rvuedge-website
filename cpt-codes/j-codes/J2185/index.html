J2185 Meropenem

J-Codes

Also known as: meropenem, Merrem

Injection of meropenem. A carbapenem antibiotic for serious gram-negative and gram-positive infections.

Clinical Context

Used for intra-abdominal infections, bacterial meningitis, endocarditis, and other serious infections. First carbapenem approved for meningitis.
