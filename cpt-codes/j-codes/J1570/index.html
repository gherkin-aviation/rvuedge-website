J1570 Ganciclovir sodium injection

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: ganciclovir, Cytovene

Ganciclovir sodium injection. An antiviral nucleoside analog for cytomegalovirus infection treatment.

In Plain Language

CMV treatment; viral infection medication

Clinical Context

Used for cytomegalovirus retinitis in immunocompromised patients and CMV prevention in transplant recipients. Intravenous administration required.

RVU Information

CPT J1570 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
