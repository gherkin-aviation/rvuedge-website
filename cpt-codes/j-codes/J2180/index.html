J2180 Meperidine/promethazine injection

J-Codes

Also known as: meperidine/promethazine, Demerol with Phenergan

Injection of meperidine with promethazine. Opioid pain relief combined with antihistamine and sedation.

Clinical Context

Used for pain management with added antiemetic and sedative benefits. Combination provides analgesia plus nausea control and anxiolysis.
