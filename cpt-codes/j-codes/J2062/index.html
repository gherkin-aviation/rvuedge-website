J2062 Loxapine for inhalation 1 milligrams

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: loxapine inhalation, Adasuve

Injection of loxapine for inhalation, 1 milligram. An inhaled antipsychotic for acute agitation.

In Plain Language

rapid antipsychotic medication; acute agitation treatment

Clinical Context

Used for acute agitation in schizophrenia and bipolar disorder with rapid onset. Inhaled formulation provides faster antipsychotic effect than oral or standard injections.

RVU Information

CPT J2062 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
