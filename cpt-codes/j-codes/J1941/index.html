J1941 Injection, furoscix, 20 milligrams

J-Codes

Also known as: Furoscix, subcutaneous furosemide

Injection of Furoscix (subcutaneous furosemide), 20 milligrams. A subcutaneously-administered loop diuretic.

Clinical Context

Used for chronic heart failure management, can be administered in home or outpatient settings. Subcutaneous administration avoids IV access and enables chronic diuretic therapy.
