J1941 Injection, furoscix, 20 milligrams

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: Furoscix, subcutaneous furosemide

Injection of Furoscix (subcutaneous furosemide), 20 milligrams. A subcutaneously-administered loop diuretic.

In Plain Language

under-the-skin water pill; home diuretic therapy

Clinical Context

Used for chronic heart failure management, can be administered in home or outpatient settings. Subcutaneous administration avoids IV access and enables chronic diuretic therapy.

RVU Information

CPT J1941 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
