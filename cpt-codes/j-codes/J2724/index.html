J2724 Protein c concentrate

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: protein c concentrate, ceprotin

Injection of protein C concentrate (Ceprotin), used for treatment of protein C deficiency and prevention of thrombotic complications.

In Plain Language

blood clot prevention; protein replacement

Clinical Context

Used for acute treatment of severe protein C deficiency and thrombophilia in patients at risk for disseminated intravascular coagulation (DIC).

RVU Information

CPT J2724 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
