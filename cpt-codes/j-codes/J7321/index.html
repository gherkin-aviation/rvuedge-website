J7321 Hyalgan supartz visco-3 dose

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: Hyalgan, Supartz, hyaluronic acid

Injection of Hyalgan or Supartz Visco-3 dose (sodium hyaluronate), a hyaluronic acid viscosupplement for intra-articular use.

In Plain Language

joint lubrication injection; arthritis pain relief

Clinical Context

Used for intra-articular injection in osteoarthritis management, provides viscosupplementation reducing joint friction and pain.

RVU Information

CPT J7321 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
