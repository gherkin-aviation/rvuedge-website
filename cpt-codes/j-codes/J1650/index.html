J1650 Injection enoxaparin sodium

J-Codes

Also known as: enoxaparin, Lovenox, LMWH

Enoxaparin sodium injection. A low-molecular-weight heparin for thromboembolism treatment and prevention.

Clinical Context

Used for acute venous thromboembolism treatment, thromboprophylaxis in surgery and hospitalization, and acute coronary syndrome management. Subcutaneous administration.
