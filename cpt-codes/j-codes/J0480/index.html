J0480 Basiliximab

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: basiliximab, Simulect

Basiliximab injection, a monoclonal antibody against interleukin-2 receptor for transplant rejection prevention.

In Plain Language

transplant rejection prevention; immune system medication

Clinical Context

Intravenous chimeric monoclonal antibody for interleukin-2 receptor blockade. Used with cyclosporine and corticosteroids for immunosuppression in organ transplantation.

RVU Information

CPT J0480 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
