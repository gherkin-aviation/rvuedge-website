J1573 Hepagam b intravenous, injection

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: Hepagam B IV, hepatitis B immune globulin

Hepagam B (hepatitis B immune globulin) injection, intravenous. Passive hepatitis B immunity for immunodeficient patients and re-infection prevention.

In Plain Language

hepatitis B protection infusion; viral infection prevention

Clinical Context

Used for hepatitis B re-infection prevention in transplant recipients, acute hepatitis B exposure management, and passive immunization in immunocompromised patients.

RVU Information

CPT J1573 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
