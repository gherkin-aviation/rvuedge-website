J1573 Hepagam b intravenous, injection

J-Codes

Also known as: Hepagam B IV, hepatitis B immune globulin

Hepagam B (hepatitis B immune globulin) injection, intravenous. Passive hepatitis B immunity for immunodeficient patients and re-infection prevention.

Clinical Context

Used for hepatitis B re-infection prevention in transplant recipients, acute hepatitis B exposure management, and passive immunization in immunocompromised patients.
