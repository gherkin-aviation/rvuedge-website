J9262 Injection, omacetaxine mep, 0.01mg

J-Codes

Also known as: omacetaxine, Synribo

Injection of omacetaxine mepesuccinate, 0.01 milligram. A topoisomerase I inhibitor used primarily for chronic myeloid leukemia treatment.

Clinical Context

Used for patients with chronic myeloid leukemia who are resistant to tyrosine kinase inhibitors. Often used for Philadelphia chromosome-positive disease refractory to standard therapy.
