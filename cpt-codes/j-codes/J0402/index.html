J0402 Injection, abilify asimtufii, 1 milligrams

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: aripiprazole Asimtufii, Abilify Asimtufii

Aripiprazole (Abilify Asimtufii) injection 1 milligram, an extended-release antipsychotic.

In Plain Language

extended-release antipsychotic injection; long-acting schizophrenia treatment

Clinical Context

Intramuscular extended-release formulation of aripiprazole for 6-week dosing intervals. Used for maintenance treatment of schizophrenia and bipolar disorder.

RVU Information

CPT J0402 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
