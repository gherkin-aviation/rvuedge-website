J1726 Makena, 10 milligrams

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: Makena, hydroxyprogesterone caproate

Makena (hydroxyprogesterone caproate) injection, 10 milligrams. A progestin for preterm birth prevention in high-risk pregnancies.

In Plain Language

preterm birth prevention; pregnancy hormone injection

Clinical Context

Used for prevention of preterm birth in women with singleton pregnancy and history of prior preterm birth. Weekly intramuscular injections from 16-36 weeks gestation.

RVU Information

CPT J1726 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
