J3410 Hydroxyzine hcl injection

J-Codes

Also known as: hydroxyzine, Atarax, antihistamine anxiolytic

Hydroxyzine hydrochloride injection is an antihistamine with anxiolytic and sedative properties used for anxiety and pre-medication.

Clinical Context

Used for anxiety disorders, pre-operative sedation, and anti-nausea. Antihistamine with central nervous system depressant effects. Intramuscular or intravenous administration for rapid onset.
