J0163 Epinephrine in nacl (endo)

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: epinephrine in saline, epinephrine/NaCl Endo

Epinephrine in normal saline solution manufactured by Endo, a catecholamine formulation for resuscitation.

In Plain Language

emergency heart medication; preloaded allergy emergency medication

Clinical Context

Epinephrine in normal saline (Endo brand) for cardiac resuscitation and anaphylaxis management. Saline formulation allows easier administration.

RVU Information

CPT J0163 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
