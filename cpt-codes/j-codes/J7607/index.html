J7607 Levalbuterol complicated con

J-Codes

Also known as: Xopenex, Levalbuterol compact

Levalbuterol delivered by inhalation in a compact container; a short-acting beta-2 adrenergic agonist used for acute bronchospasm relief.

Clinical Context

Used for acute bronchospasm relief in patients with asthma or COPD. Levalbuterol is the active R-enantiomer with fewer side effects than racemic albuterol.
