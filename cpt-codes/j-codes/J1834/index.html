J1834 Injection, isoniazid, 1 milligrams

J-Codes

Also known as: isoniazid, INH

Injection of isoniazid, 1 milligram. A first-line agent for tuberculosis treatment.

Clinical Context

Used as a component of multi-drug tuberculosis regimens. First-line agent for both latent and active tuberculosis infections. Often given with pyridoxine to prevent neuropathy.
