J1630 Haloperidol injection

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: haloperidol, Haldol

Haloperidol injection. A typical antipsychotic for acute agitation and psychotic symptom management.

In Plain Language

antipsychotic injection; agitation control medication

Clinical Context

Used for acute psychotic episodes, severe agitation, and behavioral control. Intramuscular or intravenous administration with careful monitoring for extrapyramidal effects.

RVU Information

CPT J1630 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
