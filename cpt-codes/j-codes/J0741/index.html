In Plain Language

monthly HIV treatment injection; long-acting antiretroviral medication

Clinical Context

Long-acting combination of integrase inhibitor and non-nucleoside reverse transcriptase inhibitor administered monthly for treatment-experienced HIV patients with controlled viral load.

RVU Information

CPT J0741 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
