J7635 Atropine complicated con

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: Atropine inhaler compact, Inhalation atropine

Atropine delivered by inhalation in a compact container form; an anticholinergic agent used to prevent bronchospasm and reduce secretions.

In Plain Language

Anticholinergic breathing medication; Drying agent for lungs

Clinical Context

Used in chronic obstructive pulmonary disease and asthma to reduce airway secretions and prevent bronchospasm. Anticholinergic effects reduce mucus production.

RVU Information

CPT J7635 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
