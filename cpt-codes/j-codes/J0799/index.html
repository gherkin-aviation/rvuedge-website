J0799 Hiv prep, fda approved, noc

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: HIV PrEP NOC, other approved PrEP

Pre-exposure prophylaxis for Human Immunodeficiency Virus, Food and Drug Administration approved formulation, not otherwise classified for all other qualifying agents.

In Plain Language

Other approved HIV prevention medicine

Clinical Context

Used as a placeholder code for emerging or newly approved Human Immunodeficiency Virus pre-exposure prophylaxis agents not yet assigned specific codes.

RVU Information

CPT J0799 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
