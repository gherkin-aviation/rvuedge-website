J2562 Plerixafor injection

J-Codes

Also known as: plerixafor, mozobil

Injection of plerixafor (Mozobil), a chemokine receptor antagonist used to mobilize hematopoietic stem cells from bone marrow for collection in autologous stem cell transplantation.

Clinical Context

Used in combination with granulocyte colony-stimulating factor (G-CSF) for mobilization of peripheral blood stem cells in patients undergoing autologous hematopoietic stem cell transplantation.
