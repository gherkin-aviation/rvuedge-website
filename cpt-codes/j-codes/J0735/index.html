J0735 Clonidine hydrochloride

J-Codes

Also known as: clonidine HCl, epidural clonidine, alpha-2 agonist

Clonidine hydrochloride injection for epidural administration, an alpha-2 adrenergic agonist for management of cancer pain and other chronic pain conditions.

Clinical Context

Epidural alpha-2 agonist for severe refractory cancer pain and other chronic pain conditions. Reduces pain perception and spasticity via spinal cord mechanisms.
