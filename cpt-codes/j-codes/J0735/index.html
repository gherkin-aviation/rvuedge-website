J0735 Clonidine hydrochloride

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: clonidine HCl, epidural clonidine, alpha-2 agonist

Clonidine hydrochloride injection for epidural administration, an alpha-2 adrenergic agonist for management of cancer pain and other chronic pain conditions.

In Plain Language

pain blocker injection for spine; chronic pain relief epidural

Clinical Context

Epidural alpha-2 agonist for severe refractory cancer pain and other chronic pain conditions. Reduces pain perception and spasticity via spinal cord mechanisms.

RVU Information

CPT J0735 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
