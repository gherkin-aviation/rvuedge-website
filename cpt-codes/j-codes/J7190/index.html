J7190 Factor viii

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: Factor VIII, cryoprecipitate, FVIII concentrate

Injection of Factor VIII (antihemophilic factor), a coagulation factor used for replacement therapy in hemophilia A deficiency.

In Plain Language

clotting factor replacement; hemophilia A treatment

Clinical Context

Used for treatment and prophylaxis of bleeding in patients with hemophilia A or Factor VIII deficiency from any source.

RVU Information

CPT J7190 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
