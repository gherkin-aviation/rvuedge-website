J7191 Factor viii (porcine)

J-Codes

Also known as: porcine Factor VIII, Factor VIII porcine, FVIII porcine

Injection of Factor VIII (antihemophilic factor) derived from porcine source, used for replacement in Factor VIII deficiency.

Clinical Context

Used for treatment of bleeding in hemophilia A patients with Factor VIII inhibitors, porcine source provides alternative epitope.
