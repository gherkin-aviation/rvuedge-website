J3570 Laetrile amygdalin vit b17

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: laetrile, amygdalin, Vitamin B17

Laetrile (amygdalin, Vitamin B17) injection is a controversial substance previously used for cancer treatment.

In Plain Language

alternative cancer agent

Clinical Context

Laetrile (amygdalin) injection previously claimed for cancer treatment. Not approved by FDA for cancer therapy. Carries toxicity risks. Code retained for historical/administrative purposes.

RVU Information

CPT J3570 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
