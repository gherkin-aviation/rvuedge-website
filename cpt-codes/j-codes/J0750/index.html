J0750 Hiv prep, ftc/tdf 200/300mg

J-Codes

Also known as: Truvada PrEP, FTC/TDF, emtricitabine/tenofovir

Emtricitabine/tenofovir disoproxil fumarate oral pre-exposure prophylaxis formulation, supplied as 200 milligram emtricitabine and 300 milligram tenofovir disoproxil fumarate per dose for Human Immunodeficiency Virus prevention.

Clinical Context

Used for pre-exposure prophylaxis in high-risk individuals to prevent Human Immunodeficiency Virus transmission, typically in those with high sexual exposure risk or intravenous drug use risk.
