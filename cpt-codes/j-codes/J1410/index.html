J1410 Injection estrogen conjugate 25 milligrams

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: estrogen conjugate injection

Conjugated estrogen injection, 25 milligrams. A combination of naturally occurring and synthetic estrogens used for hormone deficiency management.

In Plain Language

estrogen replacement shot; hormone therapy injection

Clinical Context

Used for managing vasomotor symptoms of menopause, primary ovarian failure, and estrogen deficiency conditions. Given by intramuscular injection when oral therapy is not feasible.

RVU Information

CPT J1410 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
