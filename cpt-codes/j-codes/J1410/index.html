J1410 Injection estrogen conjugate 25 milligrams

J-Codes

Also known as: estrogen conjugate injection

Conjugated estrogen injection, 25 milligrams. A combination of naturally occurring and synthetic estrogens used for hormone deficiency management.

Clinical Context

Used for managing vasomotor symptoms of menopause, primary ovarian failure, and estrogen deficiency conditions. Given by intramuscular injection when oral therapy is not feasible.
