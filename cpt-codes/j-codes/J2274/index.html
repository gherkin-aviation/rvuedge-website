J2274 Injection morphine pf epid ithc

J-Codes

Also known as: morphine PF, epidural morphine, intrathecal morphine

Injection of morphine preservative-free for epidural/intrathecal administration. Opioid for spinal anesthesia and pain relief.

Clinical Context

Used for epidural and intrathecal opioid analgesia providing prolonged pain relief. Preservative-free formulation required for neuraxial administration.
