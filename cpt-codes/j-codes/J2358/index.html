J2358 Olanzapine long-acting injection

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: olanzapine long-acting, Zyprexa Relprevv

Injection of olanzapine, long-acting formulation. An antipsychotic for schizophrenia and bipolar disorder.

In Plain Language

monthly psychiatric medication; long-acting antipsychotic

Clinical Context

Used for maintenance treatment of schizophrenia and bipolar disorder with monthly intramuscular administration. Improves medication adherence.

RVU Information

CPT J2358 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
