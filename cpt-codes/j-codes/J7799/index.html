J7799 Non-inhalation drug for dme

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: Unlisted non-inhalation drug, Other non-inhalation medication

Non-inhalation drugs for durable medical equipment use; catch-all code for non-inhalation medications delivered via durable medical equipment.

In Plain Language

Medication not listed for durable medical equipment

Clinical Context

Used for non-inhalation drugs administered via durable medical equipment when specific CPT codes are not available.

RVU Information

CPT J7799 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
