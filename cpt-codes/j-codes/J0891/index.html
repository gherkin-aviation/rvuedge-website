J0891 Argatroban nonesrd (accord)

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: Argatroban Accord, argatroban generic

Argatroban injection (Accord pharmaceutical formulation) for non-end-stage renal disease patients, a direct thrombin inhibitor for anticoagulation.

In Plain Language

Blood thinner

Clinical Context

Used for anticoagulation in patients with heparin-induced thrombocytopenia in non-dialysis population.

RVU Information

CPT J0891 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
