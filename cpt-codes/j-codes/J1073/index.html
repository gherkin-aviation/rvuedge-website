J1073 Testosterone pellet 75 milligrams

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: Testosterone pellet, subcutaneous implant

Testosterone pellet implant supplied as 75 milligram units, a subcutaneous implant for prolonged androgen replacement therapy.

In Plain Language

Long-lasting hormone implant

Clinical Context

Used for testosterone replacement in hypogonadal men with extended duration of action lasting several months per implant.

RVU Information

CPT J1073 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
