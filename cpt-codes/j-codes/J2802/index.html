J2802 Injection, romiplostim 1 microgram

J-Codes

Also known as: romiplostim, nplate

Injection of romiplostim (Nplate), 1 microgram, a thrombopoietin-receptor agonist used for treatment of thrombocytopenia in immune thrombocytopenia (ITP).

Clinical Context

Used for chronic immune thrombocytopenia (ITP) to increase platelet counts and reduce bleeding risk in patients non-responsive to other therapies.
