J0586 Abobotulinumtoxina

J Codes (Drugs)

Also known as: abobotulinumtoxin A, Dysport, botulinum toxin A

Abobotulinumtoxin A injection, a botulinum neurotoxin formulation that blocks acetylcholine release for treatment of muscle spasticity and hyperkinetic disorders.

In Plain Language

muscle spasm and wrinkle relaxer; spasticity treatment

Clinical Context

Used for cervical dystonia, blepharospasm, spasticity, and post-stroke upper limb spasticity. Different formulation from Botox with different units and potency, requiring dose adjustment based on clinical indication.

RVU Information

CPT J0586 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

J-codes represent drugs administered by a healthcare provider (not self-administered). Documentation must include the drug name, dosage, route of administration, and medical necessity. Most payers require the National Drug Code (NDC) on the claim. Bill the appropriate administration code (96365-96379) in addition to the drug code.
