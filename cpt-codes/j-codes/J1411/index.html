J1411 Injection, hemgenix, per treatment dose

J-Codes

Also known as: hemgenix, eteplirsen gene therapy

Hemgenix (eteplirsen) injection, per treatment dose. A gene therapy administered by intravenous infusion for hemophilia B treatment.

Clinical Context

Used for hemophilia B (Factor IX deficiency) in patients with severe disease. Gene therapy designed to provide sustained Factor IX production. Single infusion can provide long-term correction of Factor IX deficiency.
