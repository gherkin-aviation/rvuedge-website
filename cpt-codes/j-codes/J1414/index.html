J1414 Injection, beqvez, per treatment dose

J-Codes

Also known as: beqvez, valoctocogene roxaparvovec

Beqvez (valoctocogene roxaparvovec) injection, per treatment dose. A gene therapy for severe hemophilia A administered intravenously.

Clinical Context

Used for severe hemophilia A in patients with Factor VIII deficiency. Gene therapy engineered to deliver functional Factor VIII gene. Single-dose therapy providing long-term Factor VIII expression.
