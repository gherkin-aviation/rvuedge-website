V2307 Lens sphcy trifocal 4.25-7/.

V-Codes

Also known as: trifocal lens, spherocyl trifocal, astigmatic trifocal

Spherocylindrical trifocal lens with base power range 4.25 to 7.00 diopters, cylindrical power under 0.25 diopter. Provides three focal distances with minimal astigmatism.

Clinical Context

Used for presbyopic patients with moderate refractive error (4.25-7.00D) and minimal astigmatism requiring trifocal correction.
