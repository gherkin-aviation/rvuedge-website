V5150 Glasses binaural hearing aid

V Codes (Vision)

Also known as: binaural eyeglass aid, eyeglass binaural hearing aid

Eyeglass binaural hearing aid system with amplification integrated into both eyeglass lenses or frames.

In Plain Language

hearing aids built into both glasses; combined dual-lens hearing aid

Clinical Context

Used for bilateral hearing loss with integrated eyeglass-hearing amplification providing convenient single-device solution.

RVU Information

CPT V5150 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

V-codes cover vision services, supplies, and equipment including lenses, frames, and hearing aids. A valid prescription from an authorized provider is required. Document the specific product dispensed, measurements, and clinical indication.
