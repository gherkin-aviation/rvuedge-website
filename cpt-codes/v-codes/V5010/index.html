V5010 Assessment for hearing aid

V-Codes

Also known as: hearing aid evaluation, fitting assessment, audiological evaluation

Assessment for hearing aid candidacy including comprehensive audiological evaluation and determination of hearing aid need.

Clinical Context

Performed when hearing loss is identified and hearing aid consideration is warranted.
