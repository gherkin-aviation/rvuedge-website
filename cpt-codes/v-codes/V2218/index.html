V2218 Lens aniseikonic bifocal

V-Codes

Also known as: aniseikonic bifocal, size-corrected bifocal

Aniseikonic bifocal lens with segment design to correct for size differential of retinal images between the two eyes. Used when significant anisometropia requires magnification compensation.

Clinical Context

Prescribed for patients with anisometropia (unequal refractive error) where retinal image magnification differences are significant. Reduces diplopia and improves binocular vision.
