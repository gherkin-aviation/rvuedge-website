V2782 Lens, 1.54-1.65 p/1.60-1.79g

V-Codes

Also known as: high-index lens, thin lens, light lens

High-index lens with refractive index between 1.54-1.65 (plastic) or 1.60-1.79 (glass). Thinner and lighter lens for improved aesthetics.

Clinical Context

Used for moderate to high refractive errors to reduce lens thickness and weight.
