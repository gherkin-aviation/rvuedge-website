V2310 Lens sphc trifocal 4.25-7/>6

V Codes (Vision)

Also known as: trifocal lens, high power trifocal, spherocyl trifocal

Spherocylindrical trifocal lens with base power range 4.25 to 7.00 diopters, cylindrical power exceeding 6.00 diopters. Designed for presbyopic patients with very high astigmatism.

In Plain Language

three-power glasses with very strong astigmatism correction

Clinical Context

Indicated for presbyopic patients with base power 4.25-7.00D and very high astigmatism exceeding 6.00 diopters.

RVU Information

CPT V2310 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation
