V5272 Tdd

V Codes (Vision)

Also known as: TDD, text telephone, TTY

Telecommunications device for the deaf (TDD) or text telephone for phone communication.

In Plain Language

text-based phone device; TTY phone

Clinical Context

Device that enables deaf and hard-of-hearing individuals to communicate via telephone using text-based input and output.

RVU Information

CPT V5272 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.
