V5259 Hearing aid, digit, bin, itc

V-Codes

Also known as: digital binaural ITC, dual digital ITC aids

Binaural digital in-the-canal hearing aid (ITC style) with devices in both ears.

Clinical Context

Used for bilateral mild to moderate hearing loss with digital ITC technology providing advanced noise reduction and binaural coordination.
