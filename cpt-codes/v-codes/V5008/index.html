V5008 Hearing screening

V Codes (Vision)

Also known as: hearing test, hearing screening, audiology test

Hearing screening service including audiometric testing to detect presence of hearing loss. Initial hearing assessment.

In Plain Language

hearing test to check if you can hear well

Clinical Context

Prescribed as preventive screening or when hearing loss is suspected.

RVU Information

CPT V5008 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

V-codes cover vision services, supplies, and equipment including lenses, frames, and hearing aids. A valid prescription from an authorized provider is required. Document the specific product dispensed, measurements, and clinical indication.
