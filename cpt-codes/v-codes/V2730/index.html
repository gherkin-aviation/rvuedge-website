V2730 Special base curve

V-Codes

Also known as: special base curve, custom base curve, specialty contact lens

Contact lens with special base curve design different from standard curvature. Customized lens fitting for corneal topography variations.

Clinical Context

Used for patients with irregular corneal surfaces or special fitting needs requiring non-standard base curve.
