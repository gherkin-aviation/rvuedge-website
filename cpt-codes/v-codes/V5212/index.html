V5212 Hearing aid binaural ite/itc

V Codes (Vision)

Also known as: mixed binaural aids, ITE/ITC binaural aid

Binaural hearing aid system with in-the-ear (ITE) device in one ear and in-the-canal (ITC) device in the other.

In Plain Language

hearing aids with different fits in each ear; mixed-style bilateral device

Clinical Context

Used for bilateral hearing loss when patients have different ear canal anatomies or preferences requiring asymmetric fitting.

RVU Information

CPT V5212 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

V-codes cover vision services, supplies, and equipment including lenses, frames, and hearing aids. A valid prescription from an authorized provider is required. Document the specific product dispensed, measurements, and clinical indication.
