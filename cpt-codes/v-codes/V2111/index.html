V2111 Spherocylindr 7.25d/.25-2.25

Also known as: Spherocylindrical lens, Astigmatism lens

Lens spherocylindrical prescription, 7.25 diopter sphere with 0.25 to 2.25 diopter cylinder. Corrective lens for high sphere with mild astigmatism.

Clinical Context

Medicare/Medicaid coverage for spherocylindrical corrective lenses with high sphere and mild astigmatism.
