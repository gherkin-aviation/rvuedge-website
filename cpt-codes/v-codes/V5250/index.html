V5250 Hearing aid, prog, bin, cic

V Codes (Vision)

Also known as: programmable binaural CIC, dual programmable CIC aids

Binaural programmable completely-in-the-canal hearing aid (CIC style) with multiple adjustment settings.

In Plain Language

programmable tiny hearing aids in both ears; adjustable invisible bilateral device

Clinical Context

Used for bilateral mild to moderate hearing loss with programmable CIC devices allowing adjustment for different listening environments.

RVU Information

CPT V5250 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

V-codes cover vision services, supplies, and equipment including lenses, frames, and hearing aids. A valid prescription from an authorized provider is required. Document the specific product dispensed, measurements, and clinical indication.
