V5214 Hearing aid binaural itc/itc

V-Codes

Also known as: binaural ITC aids, dual in-the-canal aids

Binaural hearing aid system with in-the-canal (ITC) devices in both ears for bilateral amplification.

Clinical Context

Used for bilateral mild to moderate hearing loss with matching ITC devices providing good cosmetics and ear canal fitting.
