V5140 Behind ear binaur hearing ai

V-Codes

Also known as: binaural BTE, behind-the-ear binaural aids

Behind-the-ear binaural hearing aid system with devices worn on the back of both ears.

Clinical Context

Used for bilateral mild to severe hearing loss with behind-the-ear placement allowing greater amplification power and easier manipulation.
