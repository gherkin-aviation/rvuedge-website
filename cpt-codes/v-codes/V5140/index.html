V5140 Behind ear binaur hearing ai

V Codes (Vision)

Also known as: binaural BTE, behind-the-ear binaural aids

Behind-the-ear binaural hearing aid system with devices worn on the back of both ears.

In Plain Language

hearing aids worn behind both ears; bilateral external aids

Clinical Context

Used for bilateral mild to severe hearing loss with behind-the-ear placement allowing greater amplification power and easier manipulation.

RVU Information

CPT V5140 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.
