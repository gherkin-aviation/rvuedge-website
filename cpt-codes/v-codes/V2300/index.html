V2300 Lens sphere trifocal 4.00d

V-Codes

Also known as: trifocal lens, spherical trifocal, three-power lens

Spherical trifocal lens with power up to 4.00 diopters. Provides three distinct focal areas: distance, intermediate, and near vision in a single lens.

Clinical Context

Prescribed for presbyopic patients requiring three focal distances, particularly beneficial for progressive presbyopia or those with significant intermediate vision demands.
