V5181 Hearing aid monaural bte

V-Codes

Also known as: monaural BTE, behind-the-ear aid

Monaural behind-the-ear hearing aid (BTE style) for unilateral hearing loss with device worn on the back of the ear.

Clinical Context

Used for unilateral mild to severe hearing loss with behind-the-ear placement allowing maximum power and flexibility.
