V5242 Hearing aid, monaural, cic

V-Codes

Also known as: monaural CIC, completely-in-the-canal aid

Monaural completely-in-the-canal hearing aid (CIC style) for unilateral hearing loss with device fully inserted in ear canal.

Clinical Context

Used for unilateral mild to moderate hearing loss with completely-in-the-canal placement providing maximum cosmetic concealment.
