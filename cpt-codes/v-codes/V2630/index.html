V2630 Anter chamber intraocul lens

V-Codes

Also known as: anterior chamber IOL, AC lens implant, front chamber lens

Anterior chamber intraocular lens (IOL) designed to be positioned in the anterior chamber of the eye. Provides optical correction following cataract removal.

Clinical Context

Used in cataract surgery for patients requiring anterior chamber IOL positioning.
