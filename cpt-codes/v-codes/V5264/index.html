V5264 Ear mold/insert

V-Codes

Also known as: ear mold, earmold, ear insert

Ear mold or earmold insert custom-fabricated to fit the patient's ear canal for hearing aid coupling.

Clinical Context

Custom-made earmold or insert used to properly couple hearing aids to the ear canal, ensuring acoustic seal and comfort.
