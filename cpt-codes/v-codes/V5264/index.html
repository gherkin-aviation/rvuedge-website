V5264 Ear mold/insert

V Codes (Vision)

Also known as: ear mold, earmold, ear insert

Ear mold or earmold insert custom-fabricated to fit the patient's ear canal for hearing aid coupling.

In Plain Language

custom ear insert for hearing aid; hearing aid ear piece

Clinical Context

Custom-made earmold or insert used to properly couple hearing aids to the ear canal, ensuring acoustic seal and comfort.

RVU Information

CPT V5264 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.
