V5100 Body-worn bilateral hearing aid

V Codes (Vision)

Also known as: body-worn binaural aid, bilateral body-worn aid

Body-worn bilateral hearing aid system, with one device worn on the body providing amplification to both ears.

In Plain Language

hearing aid worn on body for both ears; dual-ear body-worn device

Clinical Context

Used for bilateral hearing loss with amplification delivered from a single body-worn unit to both ears via ear-level receivers or wired connections.

RVU Information

CPT V5100 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

V-codes cover vision services, supplies, and equipment including lenses, frames, and hearing aids. A valid prescription from an authorized provider is required. Document the specific product dispensed, measurements, and clinical indication.
