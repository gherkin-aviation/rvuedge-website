V5240 Disp fee contralateral binau

V-Codes

Also known as: contralateral binaural dispensing fee, second-ear binaural fitting fee

Professional dispensing fee for fitting and adjustment of contralateral binaural hearing aid as a second bilateral unit.

Clinical Context

Service fee for professional fitting and programming of a binaural unit to complement existing hearing aid in the opposite ear.
