V2744 Tint photochromatic lens/es

V-Codes

Also known as: photochromic lens, transition lens, light-adaptive lens

Photochromic tint applied to spectacle lens that darkens in ultraviolet light and lightens in darkness. Light-reactive lens tinting.

Clinical Context

Prescribed for patients desiring automatic UV-protection tinting changing with light levels.
