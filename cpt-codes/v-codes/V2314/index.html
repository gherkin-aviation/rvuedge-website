V2314 Lens sphcyl trifocal over 12

V-Codes

Also known as: high power trifocal, strong trifocal, specialty trifocal

Spherocylindrical trifocal lens with base power exceeding 12.00 diopters. Designed for presbyopic patients with very high refractive error requiring trifocal correction.

Clinical Context

Indicated for highly ametropic presbyopic patients with base power exceeding 12.00 diopters requiring three focal distances.
