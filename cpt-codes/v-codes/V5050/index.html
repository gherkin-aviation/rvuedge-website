V5050 Hearing aid monaural in ear

V-Codes

Also known as: in-the-ear aid, ITE hearing aid, monaural ITE

Monaural in-the-ear hearing aid, a single device worn inside the ear canal for unilateral hearing loss.

Clinical Context

Used for mild to moderate unilateral (one-sided) hearing loss. Patient wears one device in the affected ear for amplification and sound localization.
