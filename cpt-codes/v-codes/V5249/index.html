V5249 Hearing aid, binaural, itc

V-Codes

Also known as: binaural ITC, dual ITC aids

Binaural in-the-canal hearing aid (ITC style) with devices partially inserted in both ear canals.

Clinical Context

Used for bilateral mild to moderate hearing loss with in-the-canal placement providing good cosmetics and ear canal fit.
