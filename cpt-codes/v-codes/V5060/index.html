V5060 Behind ear hearing aid

V-Codes

Also known as: behind-the-ear aid, BTE, monaural BTE

Behind-the-ear hearing aid, worn externally on the back of the ear with a tube delivering sound to the ear canal.

Clinical Context

Used for mild to severe unilateral hearing loss. Behind-the-ear aids allow for larger batteries, more power, and easier handling compared to in-the-ear models.
