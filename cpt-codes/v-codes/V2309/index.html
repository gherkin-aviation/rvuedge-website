V2309 Lens sphc trifocal 4.25-7/4.

V-Codes

Also known as: trifocal lens, toric trifocal, high cyl trifocal

Spherocylindrical trifocal lens with base power range 4.25 to 7.00 diopters, cylindrical power 4.25 diopters. Provides three focal areas with significant astigmatic correction.

Clinical Context

Used for presbyopic patients with base power 4.25-7.00D and high astigmatism (4.25D) requiring trifocal lens.
