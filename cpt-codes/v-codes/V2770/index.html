V2770 Occluder lens/es

V-Codes

Also known as: occluder lens, blocking lens, occlusion lens

Occluder lens used to block vision in one eye, typically for amblyopia therapy or ocular dominance testing. Opaque or light-blocking lens.

Clinical Context

Used in amblyopia treatment or diagnostic testing requiring monocular vision elimination.
