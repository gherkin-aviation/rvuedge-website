V5256 Hearing aid, digit, mon, ite

V Codes (Vision)

Also known as: digital monaural ITE, monaural digital ITE aid

Monaural digital in-the-ear hearing aid (ITE style) for unilateral hearing loss.

In Plain Language

digital hearing aid inside ear; digital custom ear device

Clinical Context

Used for unilateral mild to moderate hearing loss with digital ITE technology providing superior sound processing and personalization.

RVU Information

CPT V5256 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.
