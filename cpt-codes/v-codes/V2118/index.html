V2118 Lens aniseikonic single

V-Codes

Also known as: Aniseikonic lens, Image size adjustment lens

Lens aniseikonic single vision. Single focus corrective lens compensating for significant difference in image size between eyes (aniseikonia).

Clinical Context

Medicare/Medicaid coverage for specialized aniseikonic corrective lenses addressing image size discrepancies from refractive differences between eyes.
