V2512 Cntct lens gas permbl bifocl

V Codes (Vision)

Also known as: gas permeable bifocal lens, GP bifocal lens, rigid bifocal

Contact lens made of gas permeable material with bifocal optical design. Provides two focal powers in a gas permeable lens for presbyopic vision correction.

In Plain Language

oxygen-permeable hard bifocal contact lens

Clinical Context

Prescribed for presbyopic patients requiring gas permeable lens with bifocal correction.

RVU Information
