V2211 Lens sphcy bifo 7.25-12/.25-

V-Codes

Also known as: bifocal lens, spherocyl bifocal, astigmatic bifocal

Spherocylindrical bifocal lens with base power range 7.25 to 12.00 diopters, cylindrical power under 0.25 diopter, with lower power addition. Corrects astigmatism and presbyopia.

Clinical Context

Prescribed for presbyopic patients with mild astigmatism requiring bifocal correction. Used when patient needs combined distance vision, reading correction, and astigmatic correction.
