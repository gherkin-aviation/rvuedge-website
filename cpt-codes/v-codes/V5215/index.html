V5215 Hearing aid binaural itc/bte

V-Codes

Also known as: mixed binaural aids, ITC/BTE binaural aid

Binaural hearing aid system with in-the-canal (ITC) device in one ear and behind-the-ear (BTE) device in the other.

Clinical Context

Used for asymmetric bilateral hearing loss requiring different device styles, such as greater power needed in one ear.
