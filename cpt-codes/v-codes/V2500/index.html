V2500 Contact lens pmma spherical

V Codes (Vision)

Also known as: PMMA contact lens, hard contact lens, rigid lens

Contact lens made of polymethyl methacrylate (PMMA) material with spherical optical design. Rigid material providing excellent optical clarity and durability.

In Plain Language

hard contact lens for clear vision

Clinical Context

Prescribed for patients who can tolerate rigid contact lenses, offering excellent optical performance and longevity.
