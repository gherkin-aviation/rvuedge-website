V2624 Polishing artifical eye

V Codes (Vision)

Also known as: eye prosthesis polishing, prosthesis finishing, artificial eye service

Polishing and finishing of artificial eye prosthesis to achieve smooth surface and cosmetic enhancement. Post-fabrication finishing service.

In Plain Language

Clinical Context
