V2520 Contact lens hydrophilic

V-Codes

Also known as: soft contact lens, hydrophilic lens, aqueous lens

Contact lens made of hydrophilic (water-loving) material with spherical optical design. Soft contact lens providing comfort and oxygen permeability.

Clinical Context

Prescribed for patients preferring soft contact lenses, offering comfort and ease of adaptation.
