V5290 Ald transmitter microphone

V-Codes

Also known as: FM microphone, DM microphone, transmitter microphone

Assistive listening device (ALD) transmitter microphone for FM/DM systems.

Clinical Context

Wireless microphone component of FM/DM systems used by speakers to transmit audio to hearing aid wearers.
