V2700 Balance lens

V-Codes

Also known as: balance lens, dummy lens, plano lens

Balance lens used to equalize visual fields and minimize induced prism effects between two spectacle lenses. Non-corrective lens for visual balance.

Clinical Context

Used in spectacles when one eye requires correction and the other does not, to maintain optical balance.
