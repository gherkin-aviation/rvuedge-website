V2710 Glass/plastic slab off prism

V Codes (Vision)

Also known as: slab-off prism, ground prism lens, decentered lens

Prism lens fabricated from glass or plastic material using slab-off grinding technique. Reduces induced vertical prism in high power lenses.

In Plain Language

special lens to reduce dizziness from strong glasses

Clinical Context

Used in high-power lenses to reduce induced vertical prism causing visual discomfort.

RVU Information

CPT V2710 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.
