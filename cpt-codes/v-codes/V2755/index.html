V2755 Uv lens/es

V-Codes

Also known as: UV protection, UV blocking, UV lens

Ultraviolet light protection (UV) filtering applied to lens to block harmful UV radiation from sun exposure. Protects ocular tissues from UV damage.

Clinical Context

Recommended for all outdoor lens prescriptions to prevent ocular diseases from UV exposure.
