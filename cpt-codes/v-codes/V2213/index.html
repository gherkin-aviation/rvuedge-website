V2213 Lens sphcyl bifo 7.25-12/4.2

V-Codes

Also known as: bifocal lens, toric bifocal, high cyl bifocal

Spherocylindrical bifocal lens with base power range 7.25 to 12.00 diopters, cylindrical power 4.25 diopters. Designed for presbyopic patients with significant astigmatism.

Clinical Context

Used for presbyopic patients with significant astigmatism requiring bifocal correction. Combines presbyopic reading segment with substantial cylindrical power.
