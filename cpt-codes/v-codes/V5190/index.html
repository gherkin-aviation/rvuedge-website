V5190 Hearing aid monaural glasses

V-Codes

Also known as: monaural eyeglass aid, single-side eyeglass aid

Monaural eyeglass hearing aid worn on one side of eyeglass frames for unilateral hearing loss.

Clinical Context

Used for unilateral hearing loss with hearing amplification integrated into one eyeglass lens or side.
