V5281 Ald fm/dm system, monaural

V Codes (Vision)

Also known as: FM system monaural, DM system monaural, wireless hearing system

Assistive listening device (ALD) frequency modulation (FM) or digital modulation (DM) monaural system for unilateral hearing assistance.

In Plain Language

wireless hearing aid system for one ear; FM hearing system

Clinical Context

Wireless ALD systems that transmit audio via frequency modulation or digital modulation to monaural receivers, used in classrooms and groups.

RVU Information
