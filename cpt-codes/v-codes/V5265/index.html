V5265 Ear mold/insert, disp

V Codes (Vision)

Also known as: ear mold dispensing fee, earmold fitting fee

Professional dispensing fee for custom ear mold or insert fitting and adjustment.

In Plain Language

ear mold fitting cost; custom insert fee

Clinical Context

Service fee charged for professional fitting and adjustment of custom ear molds to ensure proper hearing aid coupling.

RVU Information

CPT V5265 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.
