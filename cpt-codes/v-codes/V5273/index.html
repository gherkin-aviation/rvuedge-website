V5273 Ald for cochlear implant

V Codes (Vision)

Also known as: cochlear implant ALD, implant assistive device

In Plain Language

assistive device for cochlear implant; implant accessory

Clinical Context

Assistive devices designed specifically to work with cochlear implant systems, including remote microphones and wireless receivers.
