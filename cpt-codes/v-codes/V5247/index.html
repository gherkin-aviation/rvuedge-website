V5247 Hearing aid, prog, mon, bte

V Codes (Vision)

Also known as: programmable monaural BTE, multi-program BTE aid

Monaural programmable behind-the-ear hearing aid (BTE style) with multiple adjustment settings for unilateral hearing loss.

In Plain Language

programmable hearing aid behind ear; adjustable external device

Clinical Context

Used for unilateral mild to severe hearing loss with programmable BTE devices allowing multiple listening programs and flexibility.

RVU Information

CPT V5247 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.
