V2599 Contact lens/es other type

V Codes (Vision)

Also known as: specialty contact lens, custom contact lens, other contact lens

Contact lens of miscellaneous or specialized type not otherwise classified. Includes any contact lens design not captured by other codes.

In Plain Language

special custom contact lens

Clinical Context

Used for specialized contact lens prescriptions not captured by standard codes.

RVU Information

CPT V2599 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

V-codes cover vision services, supplies, and equipment including lenses, frames, and hearing aids. A valid prescription from an authorized provider is required. Document the specific product dispensed, measurements, and clinical indication.
