V2627 Scleral cover shell

V Codes (Vision)

Also known as: scleral shell, cover shell, ocular conformer

Scleral cover shell or conformer designed to protect anophthalmic socket and maintain shape. Transparent device allowing light perception.

In Plain Language

protective shell for eye socket

Clinical Context

Used for anophthalmic patients or those unable to wear full prosthesis, providing socket protection and maintenance.

RVU Information

CPT V2627 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.
