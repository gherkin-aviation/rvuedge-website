V5270 Ald, tv amplifier, any type

V Codes (Vision)

Also known as: TV amplifier, television amplifier, TV caption decoder

Assistive listening device (ALD) television amplifier or caption decoding system for improved television access.

In Plain Language

television volume booster; TV sound amplifier

Clinical Context

Device that amplifies television signals for hearing aid users or provides caption decoding for television access.
