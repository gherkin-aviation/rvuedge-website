V2600 Hand held low vision aids

V-Codes

Also known as: low vision aid, hand magnifier, optical magnifier

Hand-held low vision optical aids designed to magnify images for patients with significant visual impairment. Portable devices used for near and distance magnification.

Clinical Context

Prescribed for low vision patients requiring portable magnification assistance for reading and detail work.
