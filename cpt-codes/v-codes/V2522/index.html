V2522 Cntct lens hydrophil bifocl

V Codes (Vision)

Also known as: soft bifocal lens, hydrophilic bifocal lens, progressive contact lens

Contact lens made of hydrophilic material with bifocal optical design. Soft bifocal lens providing near and distance vision correction.

In Plain Language

soft bifocal contact lens for distance and reading

Clinical Context

Prescribed for presbyopic patients seeking soft contact lens bifocal correction.

RVU Information
