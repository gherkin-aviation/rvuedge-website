V2430 Lens variable asphericity bi

V-Codes

Also known as: aspherical bifocal, aspheric bifocal, variable asph bifocal

Bifocal lens with variable asphericity design. Aspheric surface reduces spherical aberration in both distance and reading segments.

Clinical Context

Used for presbyopic patients requiring bifocal correction with improved optical quality through aspheric design.
