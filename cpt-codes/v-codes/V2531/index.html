V2531 Contact lens gas permeable

V Codes (Vision)

Also known as: gas permeable lens, GP lens, rigid gas permeable

Contact lens made of gas permeable material allowing oxygen transmission to the cornea. Rigid lens offering excellent optics with improved comfort.

In Plain Language

hard contact lens that allows oxygen through

Clinical Context

Prescribed for patients requiring rigid contact lens with enhanced oxygen permeability.
