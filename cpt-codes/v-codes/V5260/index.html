V5260 Hearing aid, digit, bin, ite

V Codes (Vision)

Also known as: digital binaural ITE, dual digital ITE aids

Binaural digital in-the-ear hearing aid (ITE style) with devices in both ears.

In Plain Language

digital hearing aids inside both ears; digital bilateral custom devices

Clinical Context

Used for bilateral mild to moderate hearing loss with digital ITE technology providing superior sound processing and personalization.

RVU Information

CPT V5260 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

V-codes cover vision services, supplies, and equipment including lenses, frames, and hearing aids. A valid prescription from an authorized provider is required. Document the specific product dispensed, measurements, and clinical indication.
