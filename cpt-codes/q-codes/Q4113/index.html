Q4113 Graftjacket xpress

Q Codes

Also known as: GraftJacket Express, pre-sized allograft, dermal matrix sheet

GraftJacket Express, an acellular dermal allograft matrix in pre-sized, ready-to-use sheet form for simplified surgical application.

In Plain Language

pre-cut human tissue sheet for wounds; ready-to-use tissue scaffold

Clinical Context

Used for surgical ease in wound closure and soft tissue reconstruction. Designed for streamlined operating room use.

RVU Information

CPT Q4113 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
