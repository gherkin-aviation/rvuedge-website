Q4312 Acesso acute, per square centimeters

Q Codes

Also known as: Accesso AC

Accesso AC per square centimeter of accessible allograft with amniotic core layer.

In Plain Language

accessible amniotic core graft

Clinical Context

Used for complex wounds where accessible format with amniotic protection is indicated.

RVU Information

CPT Q4312 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
