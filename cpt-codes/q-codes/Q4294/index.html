Q4294 Amnio quad-core, per square centimeters

Q Codes

Also known as: Amnio Quad-Core

Amnio quad-core per square centimeter of quadruple-core amniotic allograft with reinforced architecture.

In Plain Language

four-core amniotic graft

Clinical Context

Applied to complex wounds, burns, and surgical defects requiring maximum structural reinforcement and moisture control.

RVU Information

CPT Q4294 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
