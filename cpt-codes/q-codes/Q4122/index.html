Q4122 Dermacell, awm, porous square centimeters

Q Codes

Also known as: DermaCell, acellular wound matrix, porous dermal scaffold

DermaCell or other acellular wound matrix per square centimeter, a porous acellular dermal scaffold for wound repair and reconstruction.

In Plain Language

porous tissue scaffold for wound filling; cellular-free matrix for healing

Clinical Context

Used for acute and chronic wounds, burns, and surgical defects. Porous structure promotes angiogenesis.

RVU Information

CPT Q4122 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
