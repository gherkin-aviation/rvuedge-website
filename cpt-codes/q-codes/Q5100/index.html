Q5100 Injection ustekinumab-kfce, 1 milligrams

Q Codes

Also known as: Sotyktu injection, ustekinumab-kfce

Injection of ustekinumab-kfce (Sotyktu) biosimilar per 1 milligram, for immunosuppressive therapy.

In Plain Language

biologic immunosuppressive drug; inflammatory disease treatment

Clinical Context

Used for various inflammatory and autoimmune conditions requiring selective immune modulation.

RVU Information

CPT Q5100 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation
