Q4124 Oasis tri-layer wound matrix

Q Codes

Also known as: Oasis tri-layer, tri-layered wound matrix, three-layer scaffold

Oasis Tri-Layer wound matrix, a tri-layered acellular porcine small intestinal submucosa providing cellular environment for wound healing.

In Plain Language

three-layer tissue dressing for wounds; multi-layer biological scaffold

Clinical Context

Used for chronic wounds, burns, and traumatic injuries. Multi-layered design optimizes cellular incorporation.

RVU Information

CPT Q4124 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
