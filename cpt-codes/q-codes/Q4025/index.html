Q4025 Cast superior hip spica plaster

Q-Codes

Also known as: adult hip spica plaster cast, body cast

Prefabricated hip spica cast for adults made of plaster material. Provides immobilization spanning trunk and both lower extremities.

Clinical Context

Indicated for pelvic fractures, hip fractures, femoral fractures, or post-operative hip reconstruction requiring extensive immobilization. Restricts major motion but highly stable.
