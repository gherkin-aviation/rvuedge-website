Q4074 Iloprost non-comp unit dose

Q-Codes

Also known as: Iloprost inhaler, Ventavis, prostacyclin

Iloprost inhalation solution supplied as non-compounded unit dose vial. Prostacyclin analog used for pulmonary hypertension treatment.

Clinical Context

Indicated for pulmonary arterial hypertension requiring vasodilation and anti-platelet effects. Administered via inhalation 6-9 times daily at 2.5-5 micrograms per dose.
