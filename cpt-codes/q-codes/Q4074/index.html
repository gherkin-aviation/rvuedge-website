Q4074 Iloprost non-comp unit dose

Q Codes

Also known as: Iloprost inhaler, Ventavis, prostacyclin

Iloprost inhalation solution supplied as non-compounded unit dose vial. Prostacyclin analog used for pulmonary hypertension treatment.

In Plain Language

inhaled medication for high blood pressure in lungs; breathing treatment for pulmonary hypertension

Clinical Context

Indicated for pulmonary arterial hypertension requiring vasodilation and anti-platelet effects. Administered via inhalation 6-9 times daily at 2.5-5 micrograms per dose.

RVU Information

CPT Q4074 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
