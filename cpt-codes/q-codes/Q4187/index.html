Q4187 Epicord 1 square centimeters

Q-Codes

Also known as: Epicord, umbilical cord tissue, cord tissue graft

Epicord is a decellularized umbilical cord tissue allograft used for wound coverage and tissue repair, supplied per square centimeter.

Clinical Context

Applied for chronic wounds, surgical wounds, and tissue repair. Provides growth factors and extracellular matrix for healing.
