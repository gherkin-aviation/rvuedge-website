Q4372 Amchoplast excl per square centimeters

Q Codes

Also known as: Amchoplast exclusive, amniotic tissue graft

Amchoplast exclusive applied per square centimeter, an amniotic-derived tissue product used for wound coverage and regenerative applications.

In Plain Language

placental tissue covering; regenerative wound dressing

Clinical Context

Used in treatment of chronic wounds, burns, and surgical sites where anti-inflammatory and regenerative properties are beneficial.

RVU Information

CPT Q4372 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
