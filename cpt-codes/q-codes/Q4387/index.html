Q4387 Neothelium ft per square centimeters

Q Codes

Also known as: Neothelium full-thickness, amniotic tissue dressing

Neothelium full-thickness applied per square centimeter, a processed amniotic-derived tissue for wound coverage and biological healing.

In Plain Language

placental tissue covering; regenerative graft

Clinical Context

Indicated for chronic wounds, burns, and surgical sites where anti-inflammatory effects and growth factor signaling enhance outcomes.

RVU Information

CPT Q4387 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
