Q2028 Injection, sculptra, 0.5mg

Q Codes

Also known as: Sculptra, poly-L-lactic acid, biostimulator

Sculptra injection 0.5 milligram, poly-L-lactic acid biostimulatory filler for facial volume loss and collagen restoration.

In Plain Language

collagen booster; volume filler

Clinical Context

Used for facial lipoatrophy, age-related volume loss, and HIV lipodystrophy. Stimulates new collagen formation over weeks with serial injections for gradual improvement.

RVU Information

CPT Q2028 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
