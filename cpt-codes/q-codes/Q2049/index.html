Q2049 Imported lipodox injection

Q Codes

Also known as: Doxil, liposomal doxorubicin, pegylated liposomal doxorubicin

Imported liposomal doxorubicin injection, a chemotherapy drug delivered in liposomal formulation for improved distribution and reduced cardiotoxicity.

In Plain Language

cancer chemotherapy; IV chemotherapy

Clinical Context

Used for Kaposi sarcoma, ovarian cancer, and breast cancer. Liposomal formulation reduces cardiotoxicity compared to conventional doxorubicin.

RVU Information

CPT Q2049 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
