Q4010 Cast superior sht arm adult fbrgl

Q Codes

Also known as: adult short arm fiberglass cast, forearm fiberglass cast

Prefabricated short arm cast for adult patients made of fiberglass material. Covers forearm and wrist for immobilization below elbow.

In Plain Language

fiberglass cast for forearm and wrist; lightweight plastic-like forearm covering

Clinical Context

Used for adult patients with distal radius/ulna fractures or wrist injuries requiring lightweight immobilization. Fiberglass material allows faster return to ADLs and water resistance.

RVU Information

CPT Q4010 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
