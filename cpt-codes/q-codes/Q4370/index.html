Q4370 Aeroguard per square centimeters

Q Codes

Also known as: Aeroguard dressing, allograft sheet

Aeroguard applied per square centimeter, a biological wound dressing derived from allograft tissue used to cover acute and chronic wounds.

In Plain Language

donated tissue wound covering; protective skin substitute

Clinical Context

Used for moderate to large wound coverage including diabetic ulcers, pressure injuries, and surgical wounds where moisture control and nutrient delivery are needed.

RVU Information

CPT Q4370 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
