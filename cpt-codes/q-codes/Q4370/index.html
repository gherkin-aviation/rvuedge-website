Q4370 Aeroguard per square centimeters

Q-Codes

Also known as: Aeroguard dressing, allograft sheet

Aeroguard applied per square centimeter, a biological wound dressing derived from allograft tissue used to cover acute and chronic wounds.

Clinical Context

Used for moderate to large wound coverage including diabetic ulcers, pressure injuries, and surgical wounds where moisture control and nutrient delivery are needed.
