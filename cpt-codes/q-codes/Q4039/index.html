Q4039 Cast superior shrt leg pediatric plster

Q-Codes

Also known as: pediatric short leg plaster cast, child below-knee plaster cast

Prefabricated short leg cast for pediatric patients made of plaster material. Provides immobilization from knee to ankle for children.

Clinical Context

Applied to children with ankle or lower leg fractures. Plaster provides excellent moldability for pediatric foot anatomy.
