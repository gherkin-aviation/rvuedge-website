Q4039 Cast superior shrt leg pediatric plster

Q Codes

Also known as: pediatric short leg plaster cast, child below-knee plaster cast

Prefabricated short leg cast for pediatric patients made of plaster material. Provides immobilization from knee to ankle for children.

In Plain Language

plaster cast from child's knee to ankle; hard plaster covering kid's lower leg

Clinical Context

Applied to children with ankle or lower leg fractures. Plaster provides excellent moldability for pediatric foot anatomy.

RVU Information

CPT Q4039 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
