Q4020 Cast superior lng arm splnt pediatric f

Q Codes

Also known as: pediatric long arm fiberglass splint, child fiberglass arm splint

Prefabricated long arm splint for pediatric patients made of fiberglass material. Provides immobilization from shoulder to wrist for children.

In Plain Language

fiberglass splint for child's upper arm; lightweight splint from shoulder to wrist for kids

Clinical Context

Used for pediatric upper arm injuries with need for lightweight, waterproof partial immobilization. Fiberglass improves compliance due to lower weight.

RVU Information

CPT Q4020 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
