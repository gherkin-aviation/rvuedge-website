Q4041 Cast superior lng leg splnt plstr

Q-Codes

Also known as: adult long leg plaster splint, lower extremity plaster splint

Prefabricated long leg splint for adults made of plaster material. Provides partial immobilization from hip to ankle with splint-type fixation.

Clinical Context

Indicated for femoral or lower extremity fractures requiring less rigid immobilization than full cast. Allows some movement while supporting healing.
