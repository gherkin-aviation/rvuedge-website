Q4194 Novachor 1 square centimeters

Q Codes

Also known as: Novachor, collagen dressing, collagen matrix

Novachor is a collagen-based wound dressing used for wound healing and tissue repair, supplied per square centimeter for topical application.

In Plain Language

collagen wound patch; protein-based tissue support

Clinical Context

Applied for chronic and acute wounds, surgical wounds, and trauma. The collagen matrix promotes angiogenesis and tissue formation.

RVU Information

CPT Q4194 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
