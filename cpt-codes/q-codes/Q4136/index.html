Q4136 Ezderm

Q Codes

Also known as: EzDerm, porcine dermal matrix, acellular xenograft

EzDerm acellular porcine dermal matrix per square centimeter for wound coverage.

In Plain Language

pig tissue scaffold per square cm; porcine matrix for wounds

Clinical Context

Indicated for burn wounds, surgical defects, and traumatic injuries. Billed per square centimeter.

RVU Information

CPT Q4136 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
