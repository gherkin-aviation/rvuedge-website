Q4202 Keroxx (2.5g/cc), 1cc

Q Codes

Also known as: Keroxx, keratin injection, keratin filler

Keroxx is a keratin-derived injectable product used for soft tissue augmentation and wound healing, supplied per cubic centimeter at 2.5 grams per cubic centimeter concentration.

In Plain Language

injectable keratin protein; tissue volume filler

Clinical Context

Injected for soft tissue augmentation, scar revision, and tissue defect filling. Keratin provides volume and structural support.

RVU Information

CPT Q4202 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
