Q4205 Membrane graft or wrap square centimeters

Q Codes

Also known as: Membrane graft, tissue membrane, membrane wrap

Membrane Graft or Membrane Wrap is a decellularized tissue membrane allograft used for tissue coverage and wound healing, supplied per square centimeter.

In Plain Language

biological membrane patch; protective tissue covering

Clinical Context

Applied for wound protection, surgical coverage, and tissue repair. Provides barrier function and promotes healing.

RVU Information

CPT Q4205 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
