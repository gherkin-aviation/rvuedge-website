Q4415 Alexiguard st-l per square centimeters

Q Codes

Also known as: Alexiguard STL, short-term membrane

Alexiguard short-term low thickness applied per square centimeter, a thin protective membrane for temporary wound coverage.

In Plain Language

thin temporary covering; short-term dressing

Clinical Context

Indicated for temporary protection of wounds during initial healing phases.

RVU Information

CPT Q4415 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
