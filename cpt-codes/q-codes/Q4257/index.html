Q4257 Relese, per square centimeters

Q Codes

Also known as: Relese, tissue graft, biological graft

Relese is a tissue-derived biological graft used for wound coverage and tissue repair, supplied per square centimeter.

In Plain Language

tissue wound patch; biological repair material

Clinical Context

Applied for chronic wounds, surgical wounds, and tissue repair. Provides biological coverage and promotes healing.

RVU Information

CPT Q4257 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
