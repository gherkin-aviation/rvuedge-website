Q5005 Hospice, inpatient hospital

Q Codes

Also known as: Hospital hospice, inpatient hospice

Hospice services provided in an inpatient hospital setting.

In Plain Language

hospital-based end-of-life care; acute care hospice

Clinical Context

Used for hospice patients requiring acute hospital-level care and monitoring.

RVU Information

CPT Q5005 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
