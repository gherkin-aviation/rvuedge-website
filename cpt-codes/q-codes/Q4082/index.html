Q4082 Drug/bio noc part b drug cap

Q Codes

Also known as: not otherwise classified drug, non-covered drug, Part B drug

Nonprescription drug or biologic agent not otherwise classified that is subject to Part B drug payment limitations. Used for Part B carrier billing.

In Plain Language

medication that doesn't fit other categories; drug not covered by standard Medicare codes

Clinical Context

Used as a miscellaneous code when a specific biologic or drug code is unavailable and payment is subject to Part B limitations.

RVU Information

CPT Q4082 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
