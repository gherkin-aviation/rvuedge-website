Q2042 Tisagenlecleucel car-pos t

Q Codes

Also known as: Kymriah, tisagenlecleucel, CAR-T

Tisagenlecleucel CAR-positive T-cell therapy, chimeric antigen receptor T-cell immunotherapy for CD19-positive hematologic malignancies.

In Plain Language

cancer cell fighter; engineered cell therapy

Clinical Context

Used for relapsed/refractory B-cell acute lymphoblastic leukemia in children and adults, and certain lymphomas. Engineered cells target CD19+ malignancies.

RVU Information

CPT Q2042 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
