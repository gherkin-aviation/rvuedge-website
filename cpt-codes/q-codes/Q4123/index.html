Q4123 Alloskin

Q-Codes

Also known as: AlloDerm per square cm, dermal allograft matrix, human skin matrix

AlloDerm acellular dermal matrix per square centimeter, a preserved human cadaver skin allograft for wound coverage and reconstruction.

Clinical Context

Indicated for soft tissue reconstruction, burns, ulcers, and surgical wounds. Billed per square centimeter of coverage.
