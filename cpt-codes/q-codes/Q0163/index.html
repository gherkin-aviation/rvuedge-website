Q0163 Diphenhydramine hcl 50mg

Q Codes

Also known as: Benadryl, diphenhydramine, DPH

Diphenhydramine hydrochloride 50 milligram formulation, a first-generation antihistamine used for allergic reactions and as an antiemetic.

In Plain Language

allergy medicine; anti-itch medication

Clinical Context

Used for allergic reactions, urticaria, motion sickness, insomnia, and as an adjunctive antiemetic. 50 mg dose commonly used for allergic reactions and antiemetic purposes.

RVU Information

CPT Q0163 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
