Q4143 Repriza, 1cm

Q Codes

Also known as: Repriza, dermal matrix, acellular scaffold

Repriza acellular dermal matrix per square centimeter for wound coverage and reconstruction.

In Plain Language

tissue scaffold per square cm for wounds; biological matrix for repair

Clinical Context

Indicated for chronic wounds, ulcers, and surgical defects. Provides structural support per square centimeter.

RVU Information

CPT Q4143 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
