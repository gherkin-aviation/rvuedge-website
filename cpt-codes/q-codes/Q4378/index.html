Q4378 Renew ft matrix per square centimeters

Q-Codes

Also known as: Renew full-thickness, tissue regeneration matrix

Renew full-thickness tissue matrix applied per square centimeter, a regenerative tissue matrix derived from allograft for wound healing and reconstruction.

Clinical Context

Indicated for chronic wounds, ulcers, and surgical sites where tissue regeneration and neovascularization are therapeutic goals.
