Q2043 Sipuleucel-t auto cd54+

Q Codes

Also known as: Provenge, sipuleucel-T, autologous cell therapy

Sipuleucel-T autologous CD54+ T-cell antigen presenting cell therapy for castration-resistant prostate cancer.

In Plain Language

prostate cancer vaccine; personalized immune therapy

Clinical Context

Personalized vaccine therapy for asymptomatic or minimally symptomatic castration-resistant prostate cancer. Requires apheresis collection and ex vivo cell processing.

RVU Information

CPT Q2043 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation
