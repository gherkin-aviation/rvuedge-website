Q4261 Tag, per square centimeter

Q-Codes

Also known as: TAG, tissue graft, biological graft

TAG is a tissue-derived biological graft used for wound coverage and tissue repair, supplied per square centimeter.

Clinical Context

Applied for chronic wounds, surgical wounds, and tissue repair. Provides biological coverage and promotes healing.
