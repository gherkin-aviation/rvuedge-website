Q4142 Xcm biologic tissue matrix 1cm

Q Codes

Also known as: XCUR matrix, biological tissue matrix, wound scaffold

XCUR Therapeutics biological tissue matrix per square centimeter for wound healing and reconstruction.

In Plain Language

biological tissue framework per square cm; matrix for wound repair

Clinical Context

Used for chronic wounds, surgical defects, and tissue reconstruction. Billed per square centimeter.

RVU Information

CPT Q4142 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
