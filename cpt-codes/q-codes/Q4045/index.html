Q4045 Cast superior sht leg splnt plstr

Q Codes

Also known as: adult short leg plaster splint, below-knee splint

Prefabricated short leg splint for adults made of plaster material. Provides partial immobilization from knee to ankle with splint-type fixation.

In Plain Language

plaster splint from below knee to ankle; partial plaster support for lower leg

Clinical Context

Indicated for ankle or lower leg injuries requiring partial immobilization. Splint configuration allows more functional activity than cast.

RVU Information

CPT Q4045 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
