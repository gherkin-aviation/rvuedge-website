Q4275 Esano aca, per square centimeters

Q Codes

Also known as: Esano ACA

In Plain Language

specialized amniotic graft

Clinical Context

Applied to ocular surface disease, limbal stem cell deficiency, and specialized dermatologic wounds requiring conformability.

RVU Information

CPT Q4275 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation
