Q4114 Integra flowable wound matri

Q Codes

Also known as: Integra flowable, flowable wound matrix, pourable dermal template

Integra flowable wound matrix, a pourable collagen and glycosaminoglycan matrix providing regeneration template for complex wound anatomy.

In Plain Language

flowable tissue scaffold for hard-to-reach wounds; pourable collagen matrix for wound fill

Clinical Context

Indicated for complex wound geometries, tunneling wounds, and irregular defects where sheet forms are difficult to apply.

RVU Information

CPT Q4114 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
