Q4029 Cast superior long leg plaster

Q Codes

Also known as: adult long leg plaster cast, full leg plaster cast

Prefabricated long leg cast for adults made of plaster material. Provides immobilization from hip to ankle for the entire lower extremity.

In Plain Language

plaster cast covering entire leg from hip to ankle; heavy plaster leg cast from groin to foot

Clinical Context

Indicated for femoral fractures, knee injuries, tibial fractures, or post-operative lower extremity reconstruction. Provides maximum stability for major lower extremity injuries.

RVU Information

CPT Q4029 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
