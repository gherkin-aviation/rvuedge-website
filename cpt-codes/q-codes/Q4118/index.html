Q4118 Matristem micromatrix

Q Codes

Also known as: MatriStem Micromatrix, fetal tissue matrix, ECM scaffold

MatriStem Micromatrix, an acellular extracellular matrix derived from fetal tissues providing growth factor and cytokine-rich scaffold.

In Plain Language

tissue scaffold with growth factors from embryonic tissue; matrix that stimulates healing

Clinical Context

Indicated for chronic wounds, ulcers, and tissue defects. Fetal tissue origin provides high growth factor content.

RVU Information

CPT Q4118 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
