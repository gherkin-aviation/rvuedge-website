Q4149 Excellagen, 0.1 cc

Q Codes

Also known as: Excellagen, collagen injectable, collagen filler

Excellagen injectable type I collagen per 0.1 cubic centimeter for soft tissue augmentation.

In Plain Language

injectable collagen protein; liquid collagen for wrinkles and defects

Clinical Context

Used for soft tissue augmentation, depressed scars, and facial contours. Injected directly into tissue defects.

RVU Information

CPT Q4149 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
