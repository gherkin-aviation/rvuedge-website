Q4009 Cast superior sht arm adult plstr

Q Codes

Also known as: adult short arm plaster cast, forearm plaster cast

Prefabricated short arm cast for adult patients made of plaster material. Covers forearm and wrist for immobilization below elbow.

In Plain Language

plaster cast for forearm and wrist; hard plaster covering from elbow to hand

Clinical Context

Indicated for adult patients with distal radius/ulna fractures, wrist sprains, or post-operative hand immobilization. Plaster provides excellent moldability but heavier and slower to dry than fiberglass.

RVU Information

CPT Q4009 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
