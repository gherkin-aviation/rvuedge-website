Q4305 Amer am acute tri-lay per square centimeters

Q Codes

Also known as: American Amnion AC Tri-Lay

American amnion AC tri-lay per square centimeter of triple-layer American allograft with amniotic core.

In Plain Language

three-layer amniotic graft

Clinical Context

Used for complex wounds, burns, and reconstructive procedures requiring superior moisture retention.

RVU Information

CPT Q4305 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
