Q4013 Cast superior gauntlet plaster

Q-Codes

Also known as: adult gauntlet plaster cast, hand and wrist plaster cast

Prefabricated gauntlet cast for adults made of plaster material. Covers hand and wrist extending to mid-forearm for complete hand immobilization.

Clinical Context

Indicated for metacarpal fractures, hand injuries, post-operative hand reconstruction, or complex wrist injuries. Immobilizes all hand structures while preserving some elbow motion.
