Q4013 Cast superior gauntlet plaster

Q Codes

Also known as: adult gauntlet plaster cast, hand and wrist plaster cast

Prefabricated gauntlet cast for adults made of plaster material. Covers hand and wrist extending to mid-forearm for complete hand immobilization.

In Plain Language

plaster cast covering hand and forearm; hard plaster wrapping around hand and wrist

Clinical Context

Indicated for metacarpal fractures, hand injuries, post-operative hand reconstruction, or complex wrist injuries. Immobilizes all hand structures while preserving some elbow motion.

RVU Information

CPT Q4013 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
