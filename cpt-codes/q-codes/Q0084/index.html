Q0084 Chemotherapy by infusion

Q Codes

Also known as: IV chemotherapy, Chemo infusion

Chemotherapy administration via intravenous infusion.

In Plain Language

Cancer drug IV infusion

Clinical Context

Intravenous administration of chemotherapy agents for malignancy treatment, often requiring specialized infusion pumps.

RVU Information

CPT Q0084 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
