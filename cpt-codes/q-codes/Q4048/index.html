Q4048 Cast superior sht leg splnt pediatric f

Q Codes

Also known as: pediatric short leg fiberglass splint, child lightweight below-knee splint

Prefabricated short leg splint for pediatric patients made of fiberglass material. Provides partial immobilization from knee to ankle for children.

In Plain Language

fiberglass splint from child's knee to ankle; lightweight partial support for kid's lower leg

Clinical Context

Used for pediatric ankle or lower leg injuries with lightweight partial immobilization. Fiberglass improves compliance in children.

RVU Information

CPT Q4048 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
