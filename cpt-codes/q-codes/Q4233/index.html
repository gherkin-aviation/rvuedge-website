Q4233 Surfactor /nudyn per 0.5 cc

Q-Codes

Also known as: Surfactor, Nudyn, tissue augmentation injection

Surfactor or Nudyn is a decellularized tissue-derived injectable product used for soft tissue augmentation, supplied per 0.5 milliliter dose.

Clinical Context

Injected for soft tissue augmentation, scar revision, and tissue defect filling. Provides volume and structural support.
