Q4297 Emerge matrix, per square centimeters

Q Codes

Also known as: Emerge Matrix

Emerge matrix per square centimeter of emerging-growth oriented dermal allograft tissue matrix.

In Plain Language

growth-supportive tissue matrix

Clinical Context

Used for chronic wounds and slow-healing defects to promote tissue regeneration and wound closure.

RVU Information

CPT Q4297 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
