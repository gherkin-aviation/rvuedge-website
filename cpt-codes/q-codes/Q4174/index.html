Q4174 Palingen or promatrx

Q Codes

Also known as: Palingen, ProMatrix, placental allograft, amniotic-placental graft

Palingen and ProMatrix are processed placental membrane allografts used for tissue repair, wound healing, and regenerative applications.

In Plain Language

placental healing material; biological tissue framework

Clinical Context

Used in chronic and surgical wound management, orthopedic procedures, and tissue regeneration. Provides structural support and biological signals for healing.

RVU Information

CPT Q4174 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
