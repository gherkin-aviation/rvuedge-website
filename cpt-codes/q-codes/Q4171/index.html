Q4171 Interfyl, 1 milligrams

Q Codes

Also known as: Interfyl, hyaluronic acid filler, injectable tissue augmentation

Interfyl is a hyaluronic acid-based injectable tissue filler derived from amniotic products, supplied per 1 milligram dose for soft tissue augmentation.

In Plain Language

injectable wrinkle filler; tissue volume restoration

Clinical Context

Used for soft tissue augmentation, scar revision, and dermal filling. Can be injected into tissue defects or applied topically.

RVU Information

CPT Q4171 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
