Q4139 Amnio or biodmatrix, injection 1cc

Q Codes

Also known as: Amnio injectable, Biodmatrix injectable, injectable amniotic matrix

Amnio or Biodmatrix injectable form per cubic centimeter for soft tissue augmentation and space maintenance.

In Plain Language

injectable placental tissue; liquid tissue filler

Clinical Context

Indicated for soft tissue defects, depressed areas, and tissue augmentation. Injected directly into tissue.

RVU Information

CPT Q4139 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
