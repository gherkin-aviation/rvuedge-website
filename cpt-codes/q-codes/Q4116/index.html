Q4116 Alloderm

Q Codes

Also known as: AlloDerm, dermal allograft, acellular matrix

AlloDerm or AlloDerm RTI acellular dermal matrix, a preserved human cadaver skin allograft providing structural framework for reconstruction.

In Plain Language

treated human skin tissue for reconstruction; preserved dermal scaffold

Clinical Context

Indicated for tissue reconstruction, augmentation, and wound coverage. Commonly used in plastic and reconstructive surgery.

RVU Information

CPT Q4116 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
