Q4022 Cast superior sht arm splint fbrg

Q-Codes

Also known as: adult short arm fiberglass splint, forearm fiberglass splint

Prefabricated short arm splint for adults made of fiberglass material. Provides partial immobilization from elbow to hand.

Clinical Context

Used for forearm or wrist injuries with need for lightweight, waterproof partial immobilization. Fiberglass allows better functional activity and water tolerance.
