Q4280 Xcell amnio matrix per square centimeters

Q Codes

Also known as: Xcell Amnio, Xcell

Xcell amnio matrix per square centimeter of human amniotic membrane-derived acellular matrix for wound coverage.

In Plain Language

amniotic matrix wrap

Clinical Context

Indicated for chronic wounds, venous ulcers, and diabetic foot ulcers providing protective barrier and anti-inflammatory effects.

RVU Information

CPT Q4280 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
