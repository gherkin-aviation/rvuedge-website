Q2004 Bladder calculi irrig sol

Q Codes

Also known as: bladder stone solution, irrigation fluid

Bladder calculi irrigation solution, sterile liquid used to flush and dissolve bladder stones during urological procedures.

In Plain Language

stone dissolving solution; bladder flush

Clinical Context

Specialized irrigation solution used during cystolithotripsy or percutaneous lithotripsy for bladder calculi. May contain dissolution agents or basic irrigation.

RVU Information

CPT Q2004 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
