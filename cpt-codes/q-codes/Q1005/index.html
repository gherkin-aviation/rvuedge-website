Q1005 Ntiol category 5

Q Codes

Also known as: IOL category 5, premium IOL

Intraocular lens category 5, premium intraocular lens with advanced optical properties including multifocal or toric corrections.

In Plain Language

advanced eye lens; multi-function cataract lens

Clinical Context

Used in cataract surgery for patients desiring premium vision correction including multifocal or toric functions. Corrects astigmatism and presbyopia.

RVU Information

CPT Q1005 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
