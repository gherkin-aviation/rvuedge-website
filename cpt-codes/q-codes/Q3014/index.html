Q3014 Telehealth facility fee

Q Codes

Also known as: telehealth fee, virtual visit fee, remote care fee

Telehealth facility fee, professional fee charged by healthcare facility for virtual visit infrastructure and support.

In Plain Language

video doctor visit fee; virtual appointment charge

Clinical Context

Facility fee associated with providing telehealth/telemedicine services. Covers platform, security, and infrastructure costs.

RVU Information

CPT Q3014 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
