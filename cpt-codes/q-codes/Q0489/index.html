Q0489 Pwr pck base combo vad, rep

Q Codes

Also known as: power pack, combo battery

Power pack base combination ventricular assist device replacement component for hybrid systems requiring unified external power management.

In Plain Language

dual pump battery; combination power unit

Clinical Context

External power unit for combination VAD systems (LVAD plus RVAD). Manages power delivery to multiple pumps simultaneously while maintaining safety and reliability.

RVU Information

CPT Q0489 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
