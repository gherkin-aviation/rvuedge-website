Q4255 Reguard, topical use per square

Q Codes

Also known as: Reguard, protective dressing, topical biological covering

Reguard is a protective biological dressing for topical use only, derived from decellularized tissue, supplied per square centimeter.

In Plain Language

protective wound dressing; barrier biological covering

Clinical Context

Applied topically for wound protection and coverage. Provides barrier function and reduces infection risk.

RVU Information

CPT Q4255 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
