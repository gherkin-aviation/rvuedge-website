Q0500 Filters elec/combo vad, rep

Q-Codes

Also known as: VAD filters, air filters, protective filters

Filters electronic or combination ventricular assist device replacement components, the air and fluid filtration systems protecting VAD internal mechanisms.

Clinical Context

Critical maintenance components preventing contamination and wear in VAD systems. Regular filter changes required per manufacturer specifications for optimal pump function.
