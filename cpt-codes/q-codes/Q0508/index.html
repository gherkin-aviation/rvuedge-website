Q0508 Mis superior/acc imp vad

Q Codes

Also known as: implant VAD supplies, VAD accessories

Miscellaneous supplies and accessories implanted ventricular assist device, unspecified items needed for implanted VAD management.

In Plain Language

pump supplies; implant care items

Clinical Context

Catch-all code for various implanted VAD supplies including driveline dressings, protective covers, antibiotic-impregnated materials, and infection prevention supplies.

RVU Information

CPT Q0508 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
