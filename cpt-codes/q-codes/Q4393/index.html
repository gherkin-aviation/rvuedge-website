Q4393 Surgraft acute per square centimeters

Q Codes

Also known as: Surgraft acellular, decellularized allograft

Surgraft acellular applied per square centimeter, a decellularized allograft tissue for wound coverage and reconstruction.

In Plain Language

processed donor tissue; scaffold material

Clinical Context

Indicated for burns, chronic wounds, and surgical defects where acellular matrix provides structural support for tissue regeneration.

RVU Information

CPT Q4393 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
