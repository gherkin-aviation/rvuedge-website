Q0513 Disp fee inhal drugs/30 days

Q Codes

Also known as: inhalation dispensing fee, monthly inhaler fee

Dispensing fee for inhaled drugs for 30 days, pharmacy fee for supplying and monitoring month-long inhaled medication therapy.

In Plain Language

inhaler supply charge; breathing medication fee

Clinical Context

Professional fee for dispensing, monitoring, and patient education for 30-day supply of inhaled medications used in respiratory disease management.

RVU Information

CPT Q0513 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
