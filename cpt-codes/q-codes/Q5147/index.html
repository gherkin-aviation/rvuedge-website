Q5147 Injection, aflibercept-ayyh, 1 milligrams

Q-Codes

Also known as: aflibercept biosimilar, Eylea, VEGF inhibitor, anti-VEGF

Injection of aflibercept-ayyh (Eylea), a biosimilar to aflibercept, administered at 1 milligram per dose.

Clinical Context

Used for wet age-related macular degeneration, diabetic macular edema, and retinal vein occlusion. Administered by intravitreal injection monthly or every 8 weeks. Inhibits vascular endothelial growth factor.
