Q4028 Cast superior hip spica pediatric fbrgl

Q-Codes

Also known as: pediatric hip spica fiberglass cast, child lightweight body cast

Prefabricated hip spica cast for pediatric patients made of fiberglass material. Provides immobilization spanning trunk and both lower extremities for children.

Clinical Context

Used for pediatric hip or pelvic fractures with need for lightweight immobilization. Fiberglass reduces burden on small patients while maintaining required stability.
