Q4253 Zenith amniotic membrane psc

Q-Codes

Also known as: Zenith, Zenith amniotic membrane, amnion patch

Zenith is an amniotic membrane allograft product used for wound coverage and tissue repair, supplied per square centimeter.

Clinical Context

Used for chronic wounds, surgical wounds, and acute trauma. Provides biological coverage and contains growth factors for healing.
