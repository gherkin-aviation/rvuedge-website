Q Codes

Also known as: Caregraft

In Plain Language

care-optimized tissue graft

Clinical Context

Used for wounds requiring easy handling and application with minimal care requirements.

RVU Information

CPT Q4322 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
