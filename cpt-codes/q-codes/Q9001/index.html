Q9001 Chaplain assessment

Q Codes

Also known as: chaplain visit, spiritual assessment, pastoral care evaluation

Chaplaincy assessment including spiritual evaluation, history, and initial treatment planning.

In Plain Language

spiritual counselor visit; faith-based support; pastoral evaluation

Clinical Context

Utilized for inpatient and outpatient spiritual care services in hospital and healthcare settings. Provides assessment of spiritual distress and coping mechanisms.

RVU Information

CPT Q9001 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
