Q0169 Promethazine hcl 12.5mg oral

Q-Codes

Also known as: Phenergan, promethazine, PMZ

Promethazine hydrochloride 12.5 milligram oral formulation, a first-generation antihistamine and phenothiazine with potent antiemetic and sedative properties.

Clinical Context

Used for nausea/vomiting, allergic reactions, sedation preoperatively, and motion sickness. 12.5 mg dose commonly used for moderate antiemetic effect with acceptable sedation.
