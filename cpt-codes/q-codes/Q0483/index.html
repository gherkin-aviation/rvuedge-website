Q0483 Monitor elec vad, rep

Q-Codes

Also known as: VAD monitor, display unit, system monitor

Monitor electronic ventricular assist device replacement component, the display unit and monitoring system for electronic VAD control and status.

Clinical Context

Bedside or portable monitor for electronic VAD systems displaying pump parameters, alarms, and status information. Essential for patient management and early detection of device issues.
