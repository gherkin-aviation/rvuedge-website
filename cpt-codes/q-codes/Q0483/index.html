Q0483 Monitor elec vad, rep

Q Codes

Also known as: VAD monitor, display unit, system monitor

Monitor electronic ventricular assist device replacement component, the display unit and monitoring system for electronic VAD control and status.

In Plain Language

pump monitor; VAD display

Clinical Context

Bedside or portable monitor for electronic VAD systems displaying pump parameters, alarms, and status information. Essential for patient management and early detection of device issues.

RVU Information

CPT Q0483 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
