Q4246 Coretext or protext, per cc

Q Codes

Also known as: Coretext, Protext, tissue product, tissue concentrate

Coretext or Protext is a processed tissue-derived allograft containing tissue components and growth factors, supplied per cubic centimeter for injection.

In Plain Language

injectable tissue product; tissue concentrate injection

Clinical Context

Injected for tissue repair, joint treatment, and tissue augmentation. Contains bioactive tissue components for healing and regeneration.

RVU Information

CPT Q4246 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
