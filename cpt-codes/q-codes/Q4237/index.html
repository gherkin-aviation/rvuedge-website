Q4237 Cryo-cord, per square centimeters

Q Codes

Also known as: Cryo-Cord, cryopreserved umbilical cord, frozen cord tissue

Cryo-Cord is a cryopreserved umbilical cord tissue allograft used for wound coverage and tissue repair, supplied per square centimeter.

In Plain Language

frozen umbilical cord patch; preserved cord tissue

Clinical Context

Applied for chronic wounds, surgical wounds, and tissue repair. Cryopreservation maintains cell viability and growth factor activity.

RVU Information

CPT Q4237 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
