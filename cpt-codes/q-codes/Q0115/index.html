Q0115 Post-coital mucous examination

Q Codes

Also known as: post-coital test, Sims-Huhner test

Post-coital mucous examination performed on cervical mucus obtained after sexual intercourse to assess sperm motility and cervical mucus compatibility.

In Plain Language

sperm-mucus test; fertility test

Clinical Context

Historically used in infertility workup to evaluate sperm-cervical mucus interaction, though largely replaced by semen analysis. Still occasionally used in fertility clinics for specific indications.

RVU Information

CPT Q0115 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
