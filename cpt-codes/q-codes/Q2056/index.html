Q2056 Ciltacabtagene car-pos t

Q Codes

Also known as: Symplicity, ciltacabtagene autoleucel, CAR-T

Ciltacabtagene autoleucel CAR-T cell immunotherapy for CD19-positive relapsed or refractory B-cell lymphomas.

In Plain Language

cancer cell fighter; engineered cell therapy

Clinical Context

Used for relapsed/refractory large B-cell lymphoma (primary refractory and early relapsed). Second-generation CAR-T with enhanced efficacy.

RVU Information

CPT Q2056 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation
