Q4164 Helicoll, per square centimeters

Q-Codes

Also known as: Helicoll, collagen membrane, collagen graft

Helicoll is a lyophilized Type I collagen membrane derived from bovine collagen, used for tissue regeneration and wound repair, supplied per square centimeter.

Clinical Context

Applied in periodontal, orthopedic, and wound care applications. Acts as a scaffold for tissue regeneration and serves as a barrier membrane in guided tissue regeneration procedures.
