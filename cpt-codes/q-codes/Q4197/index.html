Q4197 Puraply xt 1 square centimeters

Q Codes

Also known as: Puraply XT, extended-release collagen dressing, advanced antimicrobial dressing

Puraply XT is an advanced collagen and silver-impregnated wound dressing with extended antimicrobial activity, used for wound healing, supplied per square centimeter.

In Plain Language

long-lasting collagen dressing; extended protection wound patch

Clinical Context

Applied for chronic and infected wounds where extended antimicrobial coverage is needed. Provides sustained silver release and collagen support.

RVU Information

CPT Q4197 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
