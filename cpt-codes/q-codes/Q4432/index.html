Q4432 510(k) skin subs, not otherwise specified

Q Codes

Also known as: 510(k) skin substitute, cleared product

510(k) cleared skin substitute not otherwise specified applied per square centimeter, for 510(k) cleared products.

In Plain Language

FDA-cleared tissue substitute; approved wound covering

Clinical Context

Used for wounds where 510(k) cleared skin substitute products provide appropriate biological coverage.

RVU Information

CPT Q4432 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
