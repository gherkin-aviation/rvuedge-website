Q4193 Coll-e-derm 1 square centimeters

Q-Codes

Also known as: Coll-E-Derm, collagen dressing, collagen wound matrix

Coll-E-Derm is a collagen-based wound dressing derived from bovine collagen, used for wound healing and tissue repair, supplied per square centimeter.

Clinical Context

Applied for chronic wounds, burns, surgical wounds, and trauma. Provides collagen matrix for tissue regeneration and hemostasis.
