Q4036 Cast superior lngleg cylndr pediatric f

Q Codes

Also known as: pediatric long leg cylinder fiberglass cast, child lightweight knee cylinder cast

Prefabricated long leg cylinder cast for pediatric patients made of fiberglass material. Provides knee and lower leg immobilization in cylindrical configuration for children.

In Plain Language

fiberglass tube cast for child's lower leg; lightweight cylindrical cast for kid's knee and shin

Clinical Context

Used for pediatric tibial fractures or knee injuries with lightweight immobilization. Fiberglass reduces burden while maintaining knee stability.

RVU Information

CPT Q4036 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
