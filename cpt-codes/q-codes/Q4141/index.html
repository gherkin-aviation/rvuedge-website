Q4141 Alloskin acute, 1 centimeters

Q Codes

Also known as: AlloDerm AC, acellular dermal allograft, dermal matrix

AlloDerm AC acellular dermal allograft per centimeter, acellular human dermal matrix for reconstruction.

In Plain Language

human tissue scaffold per measurement; preserved skin tissue for reconstruction

Clinical Context

Indicated for soft tissue reconstruction, burn coverage, and surgical defects. Measured by length or area.

RVU Information

CPT Q4141 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
