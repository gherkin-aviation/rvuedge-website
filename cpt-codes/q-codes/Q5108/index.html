Q5108 Injection, fulphila

Q Codes

Also known as: Fulphila injection, pegfilgrastim-jmdb, peg-filgrastim

Injection of Fulphila, pegfilgrastim-jmdb biosimilar, a long-acting granulocyte-colony stimulating factor.

In Plain Language

long-acting white blood cell booster; extended chemotherapy support

Clinical Context

Used to prevent chemotherapy-induced neutropenia in cancer patients requiring extended hematopoietic support.

RVU Information

CPT Q5108 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
