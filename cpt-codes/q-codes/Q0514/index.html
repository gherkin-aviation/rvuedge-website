Q0514 Disp fee inhal drugs/90 days

Q Codes

Also known as: quarterly inhalation fee, 3-month inhaler fee

Dispensing fee for inhaled drugs for 90 days, pharmacy fee for supplying and monitoring quarterly inhaled medication therapy.

In Plain Language

inhaler supply charge; breathing medication fee

Clinical Context

Professional fee for dispensing and monitoring 90-day supply of inhaled medications. Encourages use of longer-acting agents and improves medication adherence.

RVU Information

CPT Q0514 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
