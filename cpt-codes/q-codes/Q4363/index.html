Q4363 Am bur mem hydro per square centimeters

Q Codes

Also known as: Amnio Bursa Mem Hydro

Amniotic bursa membrane hydrated per square centimeter of hydrated amniotic bursal allograft.

In Plain Language

hydrated amniotic bursa membrane

Clinical Context

Indicated for bursal-space injuries and specialized wound protection requiring amniotic bursal matrix.

RVU Information

CPT Q4363 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
