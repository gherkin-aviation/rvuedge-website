Q5160 Injection, jobevne, 10 milligrams

Q Codes

Also known as: G-CSF, filgrastim, colony-stimulating factor

Injection of jobevne (filgrastim), administered at 10 milligrams per dose.

In Plain Language

white blood cell booster; bone marrow stimulator; infection fighter

Clinical Context

Used to reduce incidence and duration of neutropenia in patients receiving chemotherapy or undergoing bone marrow transplantation. Stimulates neutrophil production.

RVU Information

CPT Q5160 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
