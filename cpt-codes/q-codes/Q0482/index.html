Q0482 Microprcsr cu combo vad, rep

Q Codes

Also known as: combo VAD microprocessor, control unit

Microprocessor central unit combination ventricular assist device replacement component for systems supporting both left and right ventricles.

In Plain Language

double pump computer; bi-VAD control unit

Clinical Context

Control unit for combination VAD systems managing both left and right ventricular support. Coordinates dual pump operation and monitors hemodynamics for both pumps.

RVU Information

CPT Q0482 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
