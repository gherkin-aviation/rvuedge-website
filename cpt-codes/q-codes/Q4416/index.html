Q4416 Alexiguard tl-t per square centimeters

Q Codes

Also known as: Alexiguard TLT, thickness-transitioned membrane

Alexiguard thickness-transitioned to thick applied per square centimeter, a membrane with graduated thickness for progressive wound management.

In Plain Language

graduated thickness covering; transitional dressing

Clinical Context

Used for wounds requiring progressive thickness adjustment during healing phases.

RVU Information

CPT Q4416 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
