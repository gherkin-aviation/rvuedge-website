Q0488 Pwr pack base elec vad, rep

Q Codes

Also known as: power pack, battery pack, external power unit

Power pack base electronic ventricular assist device replacement component, the external power connection and management unit for electronic systems.

In Plain Language

heart pump battery; portable power

Clinical Context

External power supply unit for electronic VAD systems, allowing patient mobility. Portable or stationary configurations support patient activities and quality of life.

RVU Information

CPT Q0488 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
