Q4289 Revoshield+ amnio, per square centimeters

Q Codes

In Plain Language

Clinical Context

Used for chronic wounds, ulcers, and surgical sites requiring extended protection with antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties.

Billing & Documentation
