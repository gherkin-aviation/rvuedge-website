Q9002 Chaplain counsel individu

Q Codes

Also known as: chaplain counseling, pastoral counseling, one-on-one spiritual care

Chaplain individual counseling including pastoral/spiritual support session for one patient.

In Plain Language

spiritual counseling session; faith-based counseling; one-on-one pastoral care

Clinical Context

Provided for spiritual guidance and support during medical crises, end-of-life discussions, and grief counseling. Documented time-based billing.

RVU Information

CPT Q9002 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
