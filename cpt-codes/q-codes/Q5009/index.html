Q5009 Hospice/home hlth, place not otherwise specified

Q Codes

Also known as: Hospice place NOS, other setting care

Hospice or home health services provided in unspecified or other place of service.

In Plain Language

care in other location; hospice in varied setting

Clinical Context

Used for hospice services where place of service does not fit standard categories.

RVU Information

CPT Q5009 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
