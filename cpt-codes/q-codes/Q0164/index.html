Q0164 Prochlorperazine maleate 5mg

Q Codes

Also known as: Compazine, prochlorperazine, PCZ

Prochlorperazine maleate 5 milligram oral formulation, a phenothiazine antipsychotic with potent antiemetic properties used for nausea and vomiting.

In Plain Language

nausea medication; anti-vomiting pill

Clinical Context

Used for nausea/vomiting associated with gastroenteritis, migraines, postoperative recovery, and anxiety. Standard 5 mg dose effective for multiple etiologies of emesis.

RVU Information

CPT Q0164 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
