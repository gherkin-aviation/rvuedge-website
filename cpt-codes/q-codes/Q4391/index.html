Q4391 Amnioplast double per square centimeters

Q Codes

Also known as: Amnioplast double, double-layer amniotic

Amnioplast double-layer applied per square centimeter, a dual-layer amniotic tissue product for comprehensive wound management.

In Plain Language

two-layer tissue dressing; layered amniotic product

Clinical Context

Indicated for chronic wounds, ulcers, and surgical defects where dual-layer coverage provides enhanced healing support.

RVU Information

CPT Q4391 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
