Q4201 Matrion 1 square centimeters

Q-Codes

Also known as: Matrion, collagen tissue graft, collagen matrix

Matrion is a collagen-based tissue graft used for wound healing and tissue repair, supplied per square centimeter.

Clinical Context

Applied for chronic and surgical wounds. Provides collagen matrix that supports tissue regeneration and hemostasis.
