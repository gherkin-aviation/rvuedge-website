Q4227 Amniocore per square centimeters

Q-Codes

Also known as: Amniocore, amniotic core membrane, amnion core

Amniocore is a decellularized amniotic membrane core product used for wound coverage and tissue repair, supplied per square centimeter.

Clinical Context

Used for chronic wounds, surgical wounds, and tissue repair. Provides biological coverage and growth factors for healing.
