Q4040 Cast superior shrt leg pediatric fbrgls

Q Codes

Also known as: pediatric short leg fiberglass cast, child below-knee fiberglass cast

Prefabricated short leg cast for pediatric patients made of fiberglass material. Provides immobilization from knee to ankle for children.

In Plain Language

fiberglass cast from child's knee to ankle; lightweight plastic-like lower leg cast for kids

Clinical Context

Used for pediatric ankle or lower leg fractures with lightweight, waterproof immobilization. Fiberglass improves compliance due to lower weight.

RVU Information

CPT Q4040 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
