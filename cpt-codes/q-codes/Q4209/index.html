Q4209 Surgraft per square centimeters

Q Codes

Also known as: Surgraft, decellularized skin, human skin graft

Surgraft is a decellularized human skin allograft used for wound coverage and tissue repair, supplied per square centimeter.

In Plain Language

human skin patch; biological skin covering

Clinical Context

Applied for chronic wounds, surgical wounds, burns, and trauma. Provides biological coverage and contains growth factors for healing.

RVU Information

CPT Q4209 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
