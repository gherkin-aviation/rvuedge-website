Q5153 Injection, aflibercept-yszy, 1 milligrams

Q-Codes

Also known as: aflibercept biosimilar, Vabysmo, anti-VEGF, VEGF inhibitor

Injection of aflibercept-yszy (Vabysmo), a biosimilar to aflibercept, administered at 1 milligram per dose.

Clinical Context

Used for diabetic macular edema and wet age-related macular degeneration. Administered as intravitreal injection for treatment of retinal vascular diseases.
