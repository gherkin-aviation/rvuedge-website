Q5153 Injection, aflibercept-yszy, 1 milligrams

Q Codes

Also known as: aflibercept biosimilar, Vabysmo, anti-VEGF, VEGF inhibitor

Injection of aflibercept-yszy (Vabysmo), a biosimilar to aflibercept, administered at 1 milligram per dose.

In Plain Language

eye injection; diabetic eye disease treatment; retinal medication

Clinical Context

Used for diabetic macular edema and wet age-related macular degeneration. Administered as intravitreal injection for treatment of retinal vascular diseases.

RVU Information

CPT Q5153 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
