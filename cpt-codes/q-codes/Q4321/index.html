Q4321 Renograft, per square centimeters

Q Codes

Also known as: Renograft

Renograft per square centimeter of regenerative allograft tissue for enhanced healing.

In Plain Language

regenerative healing graft

Clinical Context

Indicated for chronic wounds and slow-healing defects requiring growth-stimulating allograft.

RVU Information

CPT Q4321 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
