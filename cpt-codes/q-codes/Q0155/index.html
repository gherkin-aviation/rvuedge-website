Q0155 Dronabinol (syndros) 0.1 milligrams

Q-Codes

Also known as: Syndros, dronabinol, THC synthetic

Dronabinol (Syndros) 0.1 milligram oral liquid formulation, a synthetic cannabinoid used for nausea, vomiting, and appetite stimulation.

Clinical Context

Used in cancer chemotherapy-induced nausea/vomiting and appetite stimulation in HIV/AIDS-related cachexia. Requires careful monitoring for psychiatric side effects and drug interactions.
