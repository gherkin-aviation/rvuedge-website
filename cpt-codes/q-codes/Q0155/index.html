Q0155 Dronabinol (syndros) 0.1 milligrams

Q Codes

Also known as: Syndros, dronabinol, THC synthetic

Dronabinol (Syndros) 0.1 milligram oral liquid formulation, a synthetic cannabinoid used for nausea, vomiting, and appetite stimulation.

In Plain Language

appetite stimulant; nausea medication

Clinical Context

Used in cancer chemotherapy-induced nausea/vomiting and appetite stimulation in HIV/AIDS-related cachexia. Requires careful monitoring for psychiatric side effects and drug interactions.

RVU Information

CPT Q0155 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
