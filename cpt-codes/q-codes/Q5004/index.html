Q5004 Hospice in snf

Q Codes

Also known as: Skilled nursing hospice, SNF hospice care

Hospice services provided in a skilled nursing facility.

In Plain Language

end-of-life nursing care; hospice in nursing home

Clinical Context

Used for hospice patients requiring skilled nursing services in a certified nursing facility.

RVU Information

CPT Q5004 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
