Q4044 Cast superior lng leg splnt pediatric f

Q Codes

Also known as: pediatric long leg fiberglass splint, child lightweight lower extremity splint

Prefabricated long leg splint for pediatric patients made of fiberglass material. Provides partial immobilization from hip to ankle for children.

In Plain Language

fiberglass splint covering child's entire leg; lightweight partial support for kid's full leg

Clinical Context

Used for pediatric lower extremity fractures with lightweight partial immobilization. Fiberglass reduces burden on young patients.

RVU Information

CPT Q4044 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
