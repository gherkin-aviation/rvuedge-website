Q9004 Va whole health partner serv

Q Codes

Also known as: whole health services, integrative health, preventive health care

Veterans Affairs whole health partner services providing integrative and preventive health support.

In Plain Language

holistic health support; prevention and wellness; integrative medicine

Clinical Context

Available to Veterans Affairs patients for comprehensive whole-person health promotion including physical, mental, social, and spiritual dimensions of wellness.

RVU Information

CPT Q9004 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
