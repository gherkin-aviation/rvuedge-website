Q4272 Esano a, per square centimeters

Q Codes

Also known as: Esano A

Esano A per square centimeter of acellular amniotic membrane-derived allograft biologic wound dressing.

In Plain Language

amniotic membrane covering

Clinical Context

Applied to chronic wounds, diabetic ulcers, and surgical sites for anti-inflammatory protection and moisture management.

RVU Information

CPT Q4272 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
