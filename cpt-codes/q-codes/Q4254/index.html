Q4254 Novafix dl per square centimeters

Q Codes

Also known as: Novafix DL, dual-layer collagen dressing, dermal-layered dressing

Novafix DL is a dermal-layered collagen dressing used for wound healing and tissue repair, supplied per square centimeter.

In Plain Language

two-layer collagen dressing; dermal-structure wound patch

Clinical Context

Applied for chronic wounds, surgical wounds, and tissue repair. The dual-layer structure mimics skin anatomy for enhanced healing.

RVU Information

CPT Q4254 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
