Q4268 Surgraft ft per square centimeters

Q Codes

Also known as: Surgraft FT, full-thickness skin graft, thick skin allograft

Surgraft FT is a full-thickness decellularized human skin allograft used for wound coverage and tissue repair, supplied per square centimeter.

In Plain Language

thick human skin patch; full-thickness biological skin

Clinical Context

Applied for chronic wounds, surgical wounds, burns, and deep tissue repair. Full-thickness structure provides complete skin anatomy replacement.

RVU Information

CPT Q4268 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
