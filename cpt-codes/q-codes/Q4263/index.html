Q4263 Surgraft tl, per square centimeters

Q-Codes

Also known as: Surgraft TL, tissue-lined skin graft, layered skin allograft

Surgraft TL is a tissue-lined decellularized human skin allograft used for wound coverage and tissue repair, supplied per square centimeter.

Clinical Context

Applied for chronic wounds, surgical wounds, burns, and deep tissue repair. The tissue-lined structure provides enhanced support.
