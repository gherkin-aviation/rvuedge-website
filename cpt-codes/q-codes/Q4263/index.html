Q4263 Surgraft tl, per square centimeters

Q Codes

Also known as: Surgraft TL, tissue-lined skin graft, layered skin allograft

Surgraft TL is a tissue-lined decellularized human skin allograft used for wound coverage and tissue repair, supplied per square centimeter.

In Plain Language

layered human skin patch; tissue-lined biological skin

Clinical Context

Applied for chronic wounds, surgical wounds, burns, and deep tissue repair. The tissue-lined structure provides enhanced support.

RVU Information

CPT Q4263 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
