Q4162 Wndex flw, bioskn flw, 0.5cc

Q Codes

Also known as: Woundex Flow, Bioskin Flow, amniotic fluid injection

Woundex Flow and Bioskin Flow are decellularized amniotic fluid products used for tissue repair and wound healing, supplied in 0.5 milliliter format for injection or topical application.

In Plain Language

liquid biological product for wound healing; injected tissue repair material

Clinical Context

Applied for tissue repair in orthopedic and wound care procedures. The liquid format allows injection into tissue defects and can be applied topically to wounds to promote healing.

RVU Information

CPT Q4162 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
