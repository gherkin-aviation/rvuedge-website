Q4148 Neox neox right or clarix cord

Q Codes

Also known as: Neox, Neox RT, Clarix Cord, amniotic allograft

Neox, Neox RT, or Clarix Cord amniotic tissue allograft per square centimeter for wound coverage and healing.

In Plain Language

umbilical cord or amniotic tissue per square cm; placental tissue for wounds

Clinical Context

Indicated for chronic wounds, burns, and surgical defects. Natural anti-inflammatory properties promote healing.
