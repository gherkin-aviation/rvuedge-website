Q2037 Fluvirin vacc, 3 yrs & >, intramuscular

Q Codes

Also known as: Fluvirin, seasonal flu vaccine

Fluvirin vaccine, inactivated influenza vaccine administered intramuscularly for adults age 3 years and older.

In Plain Language

flu shot; influenza vaccine

Clinical Context

Seasonal inactivated influenza vaccine administered by intramuscular route. Provides protection against current season flu strains.

RVU Information

CPT Q2037 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
