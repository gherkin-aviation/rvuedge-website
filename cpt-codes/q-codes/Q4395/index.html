Q4395 Acelagraft per square centimeters

Q Codes

Also known as: Acelagraft, allograft tissue

Acelagraft applied per square centimeter, an allograft tissue product for wound coverage and healing.

In Plain Language

donor tissue covering; wound dressing

Clinical Context

Indicated for acute and chronic wounds, burns, and surgical sites where biological wound coverage supports tissue repair.

RVU Information

CPT Q4395 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
