Q0506 Lith-ion batt elec/pneum vad

Q Codes

Also known as: lithium-ion battery, Li-ion battery, lithium battery

Lithium-ion battery electronic or pneumatic ventricular assist device replacement component, the modern rechargeable battery technology for VAD systems.

In Plain Language

modern pump battery; rechargeable battery

Clinical Context

Advanced battery technology providing improved performance, longevity, and safety compared to older battery types. Standard in modern VAD systems for better patient outcomes.

RVU Information

CPT Q0506 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
