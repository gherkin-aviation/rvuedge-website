Q5002 Hospice/home hlth in asst lv

Q Codes

Also known as: Assisted living hospice, group home care

Hospice or home health services provided in an assisted living facility or group home setting.

In Plain Language

care in assisted facility; group living support

Clinical Context

Used for patients receiving hospice or home health services in assisted living environments.

RVU Information

CPT Q5002 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
