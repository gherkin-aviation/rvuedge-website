Q4408 Xwrap hydro per square centimeters

Q Codes

Also known as: Xwrap hydro, hydrophilic membrane

Xwrap hydro applied per square centimeter, a hydrophilic membrane wrap for wound coverage and moisture management.

In Plain Language

water-absorbing membrane; moisture-managing wrap

Clinical Context

Used for exuding wounds requiring hydrophilic properties to manage excess moisture.

RVU Information

CPT Q4408 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
