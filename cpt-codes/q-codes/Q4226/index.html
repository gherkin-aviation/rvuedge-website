Q4226 Myown harv prep procedure square centimeters

Q-Codes

Also known as: MyOwn Harvest, autologous cell therapy, patient-derived graft

MyOwn Harvest and Preparation Procedure is a custom tissue graft procedure using patient-derived cells harvested and processed for autologous grafting, supplied per square centimeter.

Clinical Context

Used for chronic wounds and tissue repair using the patient's own cells. Reduces immune rejection and promotes healing.
