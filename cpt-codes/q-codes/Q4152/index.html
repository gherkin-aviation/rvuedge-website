Q4152 Dermapure 1 square centimeters

Q Codes

Also known as: DermaPure, dermal matrix, acellular allograft

DermaPure acellular dermal matrix per square centimeter for wound repair and reconstruction.

In Plain Language

tissue scaffold per square cm for wounds; dermal matrix for healing

Clinical Context

Indicated for chronic wounds, surgical defects, and wound coverage. Billed per square centimeter.

RVU Information

CPT Q4152 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation
