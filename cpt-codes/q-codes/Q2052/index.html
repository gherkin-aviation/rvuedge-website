Q2052 Home ivig, services/supplies

Q Codes

Also known as: home IVIG, IVIG at home

Home intravenous immunoglobulin therapy services and supplies, home-based treatment with intravenous immunoglobulin.

In Plain Language

immune system therapy at home; antibody treatment

Clinical Context

Used for primary immunodeficiency, secondary immune disorders, and autoimmune conditions. Home administration improves quality of life and reduces hospitalization.

RVU Information

CPT Q2052 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
