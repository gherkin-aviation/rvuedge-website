Q4206 Fluid flow or fluid gf 1 cc

Q-Codes

Also known as: Fluid Flow, Fluid Graft Flow, tissue fluid product

Fluid Flow or Fluid Graft Flow is a decellularized tissue-derived liquid product used for tissue repair and wound healing, supplied per cubic centimeter for injection or topical application.

Clinical Context

Injected or applied topically for tissue repair, wound healing, and tissue augmentation. The fluid contains growth factors and matrix components.
