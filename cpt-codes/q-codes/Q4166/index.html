Q4166 Cytal, per square centimeter

Q Codes

Also known as: Cytal, elastin-hyaluronic acid matrix, regeneration membrane

Cytal is a biopolymeric elastin-hyaluronic acid matrix used for guided tissue regeneration and wound healing, supplied per square centimeter.

In Plain Language

biological regeneration material; tissue support scaffold

Clinical Context

Applied in periodontal, orthopedic, and dental applications for guided tissue regeneration. Acts as a space-maintaining barrier and scaffold for new tissue formation.

RVU Information

CPT Q4166 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
