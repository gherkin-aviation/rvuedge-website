Q4166 Cytal, per square centimeter

Q-Codes

Also known as: Cytal, elastin-hyaluronic acid matrix, regeneration membrane

Cytal is a biopolymeric elastin-hyaluronic acid matrix used for guided tissue regeneration and wound healing, supplied per square centimeter.

Clinical Context

Applied in periodontal, orthopedic, and dental applications for guided tissue regeneration. Acts as a space-maintaining barrier and scaffold for new tissue formation.
