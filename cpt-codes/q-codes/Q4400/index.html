Q4400 Polygon3 per square centimeters

Q Codes

Also known as: Polygon 3D, 3D tissue matrix

Polygon three-dimensional tissue product applied per square centimeter, a three-dimensional engineered tissue for wound healing.

In Plain Language

three-dimensional tissue product; engineered graft

Clinical Context

Used for complex wounds and defects where three-dimensional tissue architecture supports optimal healing and integration.

RVU Information

CPT Q4400 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
