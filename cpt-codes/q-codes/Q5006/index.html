Q5006 Hospice in hospice facility

Q Codes

Also known as: Hospice facility care, inpatient hospice unit

Hospice services provided in a dedicated hospice facility.

In Plain Language

dedicated hospice center; specialized end-of-life facility

Clinical Context

Used for hospice patients in specialized hospice care facilities providing comprehensive end-of-life services.

RVU Information

CPT Q5006 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
