Q0144 Azithromycin dihydrate, oral

Q Codes

Also known as: Zithromax, azithromycin, Z-pack

Azithromycin dihydrate oral antibiotic formulation used for treatment of bacterial infections including respiratory tract infections and sexually transmitted infections.

In Plain Language

antibiotic tablet; Z-pack antibiotic

Clinical Context

Broad-spectrum macrolide antibiotic used for community-acquired pneumonia, pertussis, sexually transmitted infections, and respiratory tract infections. Often dosed as a 5-day course.

RVU Information

CPT Q0144 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
