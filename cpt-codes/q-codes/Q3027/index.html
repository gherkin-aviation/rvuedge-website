Q3027 Injection beta interferon intramuscular 1 micrograms

Q-Codes

Also known as: Avonex, interferon beta-1a IM

Interferon beta-1a injection 1 microgram intramuscular formulation, immunomodulatory therapy for multiple sclerosis.

Clinical Context

Used for relapsing-remitting multiple sclerosis to reduce relapses and slow disability progression. Weekly intramuscular administration.
