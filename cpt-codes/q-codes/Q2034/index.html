Q2034 Agriflu vaccine

Also known as: Agriflu, seasonal flu vaccine

Agriflu vaccine, inactivated influenza vaccine for prevention of seasonal influenza in adults.

Clinical Context

Seasonal influenza vaccine administered annually. Suitable for adults age 3 years and older. Provides protection against circulating influenza strains.
