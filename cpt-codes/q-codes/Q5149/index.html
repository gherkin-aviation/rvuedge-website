Q5149 Injection, aflibercept-abzv, 1 milligrams

Q Codes

Also known as: aflibercept biosimilar, Yannuzay, anti-VEGF, VEGF inhibitor

Injection of aflibercept-abzv (Yannuzay), a biosimilar to aflibercept, administered at 1 milligram per dose.

In Plain Language

eye injection; retinal treatment; vision medication

Clinical Context

Used for neovascular age-related macular degeneration, diabetic macular edema, and macular edema secondary to retinal vein occlusion. Administered by intravitreal injection as maintenance therapy.

RVU Information

CPT Q5149 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
