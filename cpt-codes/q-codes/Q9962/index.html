Q9962 Hocm 300-349mg/milliliters iodine,1ml

Q Codes

Also known as: HOCM, high osmolar contrast, ionic contrast

High osmolar contrast medium containing 300 to 349 milligrams of iodine per milliliter, per milliliter.

In Plain Language

X-ray dye; angiography dye; contrast solution

Clinical Context

Utilized for angiography and radiographic procedures requiring high concentration iodine for optimal visualization of cardiovascular and cerebrovascular structures.

RVU Information

CPT Q9962 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
