Q4240 Corecyte topical only 0.5 cc

Q-Codes

Also known as: Corecyte, cellular therapy product, topical cell therapy

Corecyte is a cellular therapy product derived from decellularized tissue for topical application only, supplied per 0.5 milliliter dose.

Clinical Context

Applied topically for wound healing and tissue repair. Contains bioactive components that promote healing without injection.
