Q5148 Injection, nyposi 1 micrograms

Q Codes

Also known as: CGRP antagonist, nyposi, migraine preventive

Injection of nyposi (calcitonin gene-related peptide antagonist), administered at 1 microgram per dose.

In Plain Language

migraine injection; headache prevention; nerve pain blocker

Clinical Context

Used for preventive treatment of episodic or chronic migraine in adults. Administered subcutaneously monthly as a disease-modifying therapy targeting calcitonin gene-related peptide pathways.

RVU Information

CPT Q5148 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
