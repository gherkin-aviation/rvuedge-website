Q4104 Integra bmwd

Q Codes

Also known as: Integra, Integra bilayer, dermal regeneration template

Integra Bilayer Wound Dressing, a bilayered dermal regeneration template consisting of collagen and glycosaminoglycan matrix with outer silicone layer.

In Plain Language

two-layer artificial skin with silicone covering; engineered wound dressing that regrows skin layers

Clinical Context

Used for deep dermal wounds, burns, traumatic injuries, and surgical reconstruction. Requires skin graft placement once dermis regenerates.

RVU Information

CPT Q4104 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
