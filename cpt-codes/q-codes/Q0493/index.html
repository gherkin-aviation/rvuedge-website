Q0493 Emr pwr cbl combo vad, rep

Q Codes

Also known as: emergency power cable, combo backup cable

Emergency power cable combination ventricular assist device replacement component, backup power connection for dual pump systems.

In Plain Language

backup cable; safety connection

Clinical Context

Emergency power connection for combination VAD systems. Maintains connection between dual pumps and emergency power source during primary power interruption.

RVU Information

CPT Q0493 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
