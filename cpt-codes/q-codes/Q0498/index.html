Q0498 Holster elec/combo vad, rep

Q Codes

Also known as: VAD holster, controller case, pump case

Holster electronic or combination ventricular assist device replacement component, the protective carry case for external controller or power unit.

In Plain Language

pump carrier; controller holder

Clinical Context

Protective carrying pouch or holster for external VAD controller or power unit worn at waist or shoulder. Enables patient mobility and protection of delicate electronics.

RVU Information

CPT Q0498 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
