Q5107 Injection mvasi 10 milligrams

Q Codes

Also known as: Mvasi injection, bevacizumab-awwb

Injection of Mvasi, bevacizumab-awwb biosimilar per 10 milligrams, a vascular endothelial growth factor inhibitor.

In Plain Language

cancer-fighting biologic; tumor growth blocker

Clinical Context

Used for metastatic colorectal, breast, lung, and ovarian cancers to inhibit tumor angiogenesis.
