Q9954 Oral mr contrast, 100 milliliters

Q Codes

Also known as: oral MRI contrast, GI contrast agent, barium-free contrast

Oral magnetic resonance imaging contrast medium suspension, 100 milliliters.

In Plain Language

MRI swallowing agent; bowel contrast; GI tract imaging dye

Clinical Context

Administered orally for magnetic resonance imaging of gastrointestinal tract. Enhances visualization of stomach, small bowel, and colon pathology.

RVU Information

CPT Q9954 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
