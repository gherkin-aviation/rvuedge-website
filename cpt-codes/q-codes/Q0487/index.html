Q0487 Leads any type vad, rep only

Q Codes

Also known as: VAD leads, pump leads, electrical leads

Leads any type ventricular assist device replacement component, the electrical leads connecting the VAD pump to the control and power systems.

In Plain Language

pump wires; heart pump connections

Clinical Context

Essential electrical connections between implanted pump and external systems. Transmit power and monitor signals. Replacement required for electrical discontinuity or insulation failure.

RVU Information

CPT Q0487 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
