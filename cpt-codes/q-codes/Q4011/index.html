Q4011 Cast superior sht arm pediatric plaster

Q Codes

Also known as: pediatric short arm plaster cast, child forearm plaster cast

Prefabricated short arm cast for pediatric patients made of plaster material. Covers forearm and wrist below elbow for children.

In Plain Language

plaster cast for child's forearm and wrist; hard plaster covering for small arm below elbow

Clinical Context

Applied to children with distal radius/ulna fractures or wrist injuries. Plaster provides excellent moldability for small anatomies but requires care to prevent water exposure.

RVU Information

CPT Q4011 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
