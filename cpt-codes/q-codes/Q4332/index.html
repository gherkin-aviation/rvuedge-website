Q4332 Axolotl dualgraft, per square centimeters

Q Codes

Also known as: Axolotl Dualgraft

Axolotl dualgraft per square centimeter of dual-format axolotl-derived allograft.

In Plain Language

dual-format regenerative graft

Clinical Context

Indicated for complex wounds where dual-format regenerative allograft is beneficial.

RVU Information

CPT Q4332 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation
