Q5151 Injection, eculizumab-aagh, 2 milligrams

Q Codes

Also known as: eculizumab biosimilar, C5 inhibitor, complement inhibitor

Injection of eculizumab-aagh (Empeglifercept), a biosimilar to eculizumab, administered at 2 milligrams per dose.

In Plain Language

immune disease treatment; rare disease medication; complement blocker

Clinical Context

Used for paroxysmal nocturnal hemoglobinuria and atypical hemolytic uremic syndrome. Administered intravenously as complement-mediated disease therapy. Inhibits terminal complement pathway.

RVU Information

CPT Q5151 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
