Q4292 Lamellas, per square centimeters

Q Codes

Also known as: Lamellas

Lamellas per square centimeter of acellular lamellar allograft tissue for dermal reconstruction.

In Plain Language

laminar tissue graft

Clinical Context

Applied to wound closure, skin graft reconstruction, and surgical repair providing organized dermal structure.

RVU Information

CPT Q4292 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
