Q4047 Cast superior sht leg splnt pediatric p

Q-Codes

Also known as: pediatric short leg plaster splint, child below-knee splint

Prefabricated short leg splint for pediatric patients made of plaster material. Provides partial immobilization from knee to ankle for children.

Clinical Context

Applied to children with ankle or lower leg injuries requiring partial immobilization. Plaster provides good moldability for pediatric foot.
