Q4003 Cast superior shoulder cast plstr

Q-Codes

Also known as: shoulder cast plaster, plaster shoulder cast

Cast supplies for shoulder cast in plaster material, materials and supplies for constructing shoulder immobilization plaster cast.

Clinical Context

Used for shoulder fractures, separation, and soft tissue injuries. Immobilizes shoulder and upper arm in anatomic position.
