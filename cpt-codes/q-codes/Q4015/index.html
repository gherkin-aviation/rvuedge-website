Q4015 Cast superior gauntlet pediatric plster

Q-Codes

Also known as: pediatric gauntlet plaster cast, child hand plaster cast

Prefabricated gauntlet cast for pediatric patients made of plaster material. Covers hand and wrist extending to mid-forearm for children.

Clinical Context

Applied to children with hand or metacarpal fractures requiring immobilization. Plaster provides excellent fit for small anatomies though requires moisture protection.
