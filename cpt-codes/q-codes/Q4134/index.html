Q4134 Hmatrix

Q Codes

Also known as: HMatrix, human ECM matrix, tissue scaffold

HMatrix, an acellular extracellular matrix scaffold derived from human tissue for wound healing and reconstruction.

In Plain Language

human tissue framework for wound repair; biological scaffold for healing

Clinical Context

Indicated for chronic wounds, ulcers, and soft tissue defects. Human-derived matrix promotes natural healing.

RVU Information

CPT Q4134 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
