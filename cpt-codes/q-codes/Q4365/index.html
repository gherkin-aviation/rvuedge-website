Q4365 Amnio bur dl mem per square centimeters

Q Codes

Also known as: Amnio Bursa DL Mem

Amnio bursal dual layer membrane per square centimeter of dual-layer amniotic bursal allograft.

In Plain Language

dual-layer amniotic bursa membrane

Clinical Context

Indicated for complex bursal injuries requiring dual-layer amniotic bursal protection.

RVU Information

CPT Q4365 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation
