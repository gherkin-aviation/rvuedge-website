Q4234 Xcellerate, per square centimeters

Q Codes

Also known as: Xcellerate, tissue allograft, accelerated healing graft

Xcellerate is a tissue-derived allograft product used for wound healing and tissue repair, supplied per square centimeter.

In Plain Language

tissue patch for faster healing; biological repair material

Clinical Context

Applied for chronic wounds, surgical wounds, and tissue repair. Designed to accelerate healing through optimized growth factor delivery.

RVU Information

CPT Q4234 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
