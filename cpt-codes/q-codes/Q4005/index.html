Q4005 Cast superior long arm adult plst

Q Codes

Also known as: long arm cast plaster, adult long arm cast

Cast supplies for long arm cast in adult in plaster material, materials and supplies for adult long arm plaster cast.

In Plain Language

long arm cast; elbow to hand cast

Clinical Context

Immobilizes arm from shoulder to hand for fractures of forearm, wrist, or elbow. Maintains elbow position and prevents pronation/supination.

RVU Information

CPT Q4005 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
