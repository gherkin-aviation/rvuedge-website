Q4342 Theramend, per square centimeters

Q Codes

Also known as: Theramend

In Plain Language

therapeutic healing graft

Clinical Context

Indicated for wounds requiring therapeutic modification of healing pathway.

RVU Information

CPT Q4342 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
