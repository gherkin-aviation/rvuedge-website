Q4386 Acesso trifaca per square centimeters

Q Codes

Also known as: Acesso tri-facial, tri-layer allograft

Acesso tri-facial allograft applied per square centimeter, a three-component tissue product for comprehensive wound treatment.

In Plain Language

multi-component tissue product; layered graft

Clinical Context

Used for complex wounds and reconstruction requiring multi-layered biological support and diverse tissue characteristics.

RVU Information

CPT Q4386 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
