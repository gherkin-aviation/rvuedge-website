Q5135 Injection, tyenne, 1 milligrams

Q Codes

Also known as: Tyenne injection, tyrosine kinase inhibitor

Injection of Tyenne, tyrosine kinase inhibitor biosimilar per 1 milligram, a Janus kinase modulator.

In Plain Language

inflammatory pathway blocker; immune modulating drug

Clinical Context

Indicated for rheumatoid arthritis, ulcerative colitis, and other immune-mediated inflammatory disorders.
