Q4103 Oasis burn matrix

Q Codes

Also known as: Oasis burn matrix, burn dressing, burn wound scaffold

Oasis burn matrix, an acellular porcine small intestinal submucosa matrix specifically processed for burn wound applications.

In Plain Language

tissue dressing made from intestines for burn wounds; biological covering for burned skin areas

Clinical Context

Indicated for acute and chronic burn wounds, full-thickness burns, and burn reconstruction. Provides matrix for neovascularization.

RVU Information

CPT Q4103 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
