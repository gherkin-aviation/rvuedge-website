Q4218 Surgicord per square centimeters

Q Codes

Also known as: Surgicord, umbilical cord tissue, cord allograft

Surgicord is a decellularized umbilical cord tissue allograft used for wound coverage and tissue repair, supplied per square centimeter.

In Plain Language

umbilical cord tissue patch; biological cord material

Clinical Context

Applied for chronic wounds, surgical wounds, and tissue repair. Provides growth factors and extracellular matrix for healing.

RVU Information

CPT Q4218 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
