Q4137 Amnioexcel biodexcel 1sq centimeters

Q Codes

Also known as: Amnioexcel, BioDexcel, amniotic membrane

Amnioexcel or BioDexcel amniotic membrane allograft per square centimeter for wound protection and healing.

In Plain Language

placental membrane tissue per square cm; biological dressing from amniotic fluid sac

Clinical Context

Indicated for burns, chronic wounds, ulcers, and surgical wounds. Natural anti-inflammatory properties.

RVU Information

CPT Q4137 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
