Q9955 Injection perflexane lip micros,ml

Q Codes

Also known as: ultrasound contrast, microbubbles, perflexxane, US enhancer

Injection of perflexxane lipid microspheres suspension for ultrasound visualization, per milliliter.

In Plain Language

ultrasound booster; echo contrast; sound wave enhancer

Clinical Context

Used as ultrasound contrast agent for cardiac imaging and perfusion assessment. Microbubbles enhance echo signal in cardiac wall motion and viability studies.

RVU Information

CPT Q9955 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
