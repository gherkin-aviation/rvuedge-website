Q4017 Cast superior lng arm splint plst

Q-Codes

Also known as: adult long arm plaster splint, shoulder to wrist splint

Prefabricated long arm splint for adults made of plaster material. Provides immobilization from shoulder to wrist with splint-type fixation.

Clinical Context

Used for upper arm fractures, post-operative immobilization, or conditions requiring less rigid immobilization than full casts. Allows some movement while protecting healing structures.
