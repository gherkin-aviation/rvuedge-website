Q4017 Cast superior lng arm splint plst

Q Codes

Also known as: adult long arm plaster splint, shoulder to wrist splint

Prefabricated long arm splint for adults made of plaster material. Provides immobilization from shoulder to wrist with splint-type fixation.

In Plain Language

plaster splint for arm from shoulder to wrist; partial plaster covering for upper arm support

Clinical Context

Used for upper arm fractures, post-operative immobilization, or conditions requiring less rigid immobilization than full casts. Allows some movement while protecting healing structures.

RVU Information

CPT Q4017 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
