Q4405 Xwrap fenestra plus square centimeters

Q Codes

Also known as: Xwrap fenestra plus, fenestrated wrap plus

Xwrap fenestra plus applied per square centimeter, a fenestrated membrane wrap with enhanced properties.

In Plain Language

perforated membrane covering; enhanced fenestrated product

Clinical Context

Indicated for wounds requiring fenestration for drainage while maintaining protective barrier.

RVU Information

CPT Q4405 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
