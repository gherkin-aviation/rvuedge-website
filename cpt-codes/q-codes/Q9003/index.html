Q9003 Chaplain counsel group

Q Codes

Also known as: chaplain group counseling, pastoral group therapy, spiritual support group

Chaplain group counseling providing spiritual and pastoral support for multiple patients or family members.

In Plain Language

spiritual support group; faith-based group meeting; pastoral group session

Clinical Context

Utilized for grief support groups, faith-based wellness sessions, and communal spiritual care in institutional settings.

RVU Information

CPT Q9003 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
