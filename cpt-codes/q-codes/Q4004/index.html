Q4004 Cast superior shoulder cast fbrgl

Q Codes

Also known as: shoulder cast fiberglass, fiberglass shoulder cast

Cast supplies for shoulder cast in fiberglass material, materials and supplies for constructing shoulder fiberglass cast.

In Plain Language

shoulder cast; arm sling cast

Clinical Context

Fiberglass shoulder immobilization cast, lighter and more durable than plaster. Used for shoulder and upper arm fractures and injuries.

RVU Information

CPT Q4004 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
