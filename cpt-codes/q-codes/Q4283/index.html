Q4283 Biovance tri or 3l, square centimeters

Q Codes

Also known as: Biovance Tri, Biovance 3L

Biovance tri or triple layer per square centimeter of tri-laminate allograft cellular skin substitute.

In Plain Language

three-layer skin graft

Clinical Context

Used for full-thickness burns, extensive traumatic wounds, and surgical defects requiring multi-layered dermal reconstruction.

RVU Information

CPT Q4283 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
