Q4008 Cast superior long arm pediatric fbrgls

Q-Codes

Also known as: pediatric long arm fiberglass cast, child fiberglass arm cast

Prefabricated long arm cast for pediatric patients made of fiberglass material. Used for upper extremity immobilization from shoulder to wrist in children.

Clinical Context

Applied to pediatric patients with upper arm fractures, post-operative immobilization, or structural deformities requiring 6-12 weeks of immobilization. Fiberglass offers lighter weight and faster drying than plaster.
