Q5001 Hospice or home hlth in home

Q Codes

Also known as: Home hospice, home health in home

Hospice or home health services provided in the patient's home, applicable to various care settings.

In Plain Language

end-of-life care at home; home-based health services

Clinical Context

Used for patients receiving comfort care or skilled nursing at home in hospice or home health programs.

RVU Information

CPT Q5001 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
