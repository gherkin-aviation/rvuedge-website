Q0235 Injection, monoclon antibody, 1 milligrams

Q Codes

Also known as: monoclonal antibody, mAb injection

Monoclonal antibody injection 1 milligram formulation used for various therapeutic indications including immunosuppression and inflammatory disease management.

In Plain Language

immune therapy injection; targeted antibody treatment

Clinical Context

Broad category for monoclonal antibody therapies used in oncology, immunology, and rheumatology. Specific use depends on the particular monoclonal antibody administered.

RVU Information

CPT Q0235 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.
