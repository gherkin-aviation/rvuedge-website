Q0035 Cardiokymography

Q Codes

Also known as: Cardiokymography, Cardiac kymography

Cardiokymography, a radiographic imaging study recording heart wall motion and cardiac function.

In Plain Language

Heart motion X-ray study

Clinical Context

Fluoroscopic technique measuring cardiac wall motion, now largely replaced by echocardiography and other modern imaging modalities.
