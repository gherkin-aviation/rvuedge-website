Q4392 Grafix duo per square centimeters

Q Codes

Also known as: Grafix Duo, dual-component matrix

Grafix Duo applied per square centimeter, a dual-component tissue matrix product combining cellular and acellular components.

In Plain Language

two-component tissue product; cellular matrix

Clinical Context

Used for diabetic foot ulcers, venous wounds, and surgical sites where cellular viability and matrix structure enhance healing.

RVU Information

CPT Q4392 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
