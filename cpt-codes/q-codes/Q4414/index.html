Q4414 Simplichor per square centimeters

Q Codes

Also known as: Simplichor, chorioamniotic membrane

Simplichor applied per square centimeter, a simplified chorioamniotic membrane product for wound healing.

In Plain Language

simplified membrane covering; basic tissue product

Clinical Context

Used for standard wound management where basic membrane properties are sufficient.

RVU Information

CPT Q4414 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
