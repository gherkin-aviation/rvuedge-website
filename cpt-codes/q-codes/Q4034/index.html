Q4034 Cast superior lng leg cylinder foreign body

Q Codes

Also known as: adult long leg cylinder fiberglass cast, lightweight knee cylinder cast

Prefabricated long leg cylinder cast for adults made of fiberglass material. Provides immobilization of knee and lower leg in cylindrical configuration.

In Plain Language

fiberglass tube cast covering lower leg and knee; lightweight cylindrical cast for knee and shin

Clinical Context

Used for tibial fractures or knee injuries requiring lightweight, waterproof cylindrical immobilization. Fiberglass allows better functional activity.

RVU Information

CPT Q4034 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
