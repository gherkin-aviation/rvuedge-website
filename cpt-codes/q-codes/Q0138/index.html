Q0138 Ferumoxytol, non-esrd

Q Codes

Also known as: Feraheme, ferumoxytol injection

Ferumoxytol, a superparamagnetic iron oxide nanoparticle injection used as an intravenous iron replacement therapy for patients with non-end-stage renal disease.

In Plain Language

IV iron treatment; iron infusion

Clinical Context

Used in patients with iron deficiency anemia who are non-dialysis dependent with chronic kidney disease. Provides rapid iron repletion with fewer doses than traditional IV iron formulations.

RVU Information

CPT Q0138 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
