Q4217 Woundfix biowound plus xplus

Q-Codes

Also known as: Woundfix Biowound, Biowound Plus, engineered wound dressing

Woundfix Biowound Plus or Biowound XPlus is a composite tissue-engineered wound dressing used for wound healing, supplied as a unit.

Clinical Context

Applied for chronic and acute wounds, surgical wounds, and tissue repair. Combines multiple biological components for enhanced healing.
