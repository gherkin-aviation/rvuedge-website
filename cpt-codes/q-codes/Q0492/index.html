Q0492 Emr pwr cbl elec vad, rep

Q Codes

Also known as: emergency power cable, backup cable

Emergency power cable electronic ventricular assist device replacement component, the electrical connection between emergency power system and VAD controller.

In Plain Language

emergency cable; backup connection

Clinical Context

Critical safety connection enabling emergency power delivery to VAD during primary power failure. Must be functional at all times for patient safety.

RVU Information

CPT Q0492 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
