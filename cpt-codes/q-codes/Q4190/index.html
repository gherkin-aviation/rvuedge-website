Q4190 Artacent acute 1 square centimeters

Q Codes

Also known as: Artacent AC, amniotic-chorionic patch, tissue matrix

Artacent AC is an amniotic tissue-derived allograft used for wound coverage and tissue repair, supplied per square centimeter for topical application.

In Plain Language

dual-layer tissue patch; growth factor-rich dressing

Clinical Context

Applied for chronic and surgical wounds, tissue repair, and trauma. Contains growth factors from amnion and chorion for enhanced healing.

RVU Information

CPT Q4190 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
