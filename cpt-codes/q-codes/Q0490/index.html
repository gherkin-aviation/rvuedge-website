Q0490 Emr pwr source elec vad, rep

Q-Codes

Also known as: emergency power, backup battery, emergency battery

Emergency power source electronic ventricular assist device replacement component, backup power system for continued pump operation during power interruptions.

Clinical Context

Critical safety component providing emergency power to VAD during primary power failure. Allows time for power restoration or emergency interventions. Regular testing and replacement required.
