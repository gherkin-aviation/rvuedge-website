Q4245 Amniotext, per cc

Q Codes

Also known as: Amniotext, placental tissue product, tissue concentrate

Amniotext is a placental tissue-derived allograft containing amniotic tissue components and growth factors, supplied per cubic centimeter for injection.

In Plain Language

injectable placental product; tissue concentrate injection

Clinical Context

Injected for tissue repair, joint treatment, and wound healing. The tissue components and growth factors promote regeneration.

RVU Information

CPT Q4245 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
