Q4165 Keramatrix, kerasorb square centimeters

Q Codes

Also known as: Keramatrix, Kerasorb, collagen wound matrix

Keramatrix and Kerasorb are advanced collagen-based wound matrices used for skin repair and tissue regeneration, supplied per square centimeter for topical application.

In Plain Language

advanced collagen dressing; regenerative tissue matrix

Clinical Context

Used in chronic and acute wound management, particularly for difficult-to-heal ulcers. The matrix provides structural support and releases growth factors to promote healing.

RVU Information

CPT Q4165 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. Reimbursement for this code is determined by payer-specific fee schedules.

Billing & Documentation

Q-codes are temporary HCPCS codes used when no permanent code exists. Coverage and reimbursement vary by payer and region. Document the clinical indication and medical necessity. These codes may be replaced by permanent codes in future HCPCS updates.
