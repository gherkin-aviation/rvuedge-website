D9613 Infiltration thera drug

D-Codes

Also known as: infiltration therapy, topical drug infiltration, tissue infiltration

Infiltration of therapeutic medication into the oral tissues, such as infiltration anesthesia combined with anti-inflammatory or antibiotic agents into surgical or inflammatory sites.

Clinical Context

Used for delivery of therapeutic drugs through infiltration technique, particularly for post-operative pain management, infection control, or inflammation reduction in surgical or periodontal sites.
