D5915 Orbital prosthesis

D-Codes

Also known as: orbital prosth, eye socket prosthesis, orbital replacement

Orbital prosthesis. A custom removable device that replaces or augments a missing or defective eye and surrounding orbital tissues.

Clinical Context

Indicated for orbital exenteration, traumatic loss, or enucleation. Provides orbital volume and anterior support for surrounding tissues.
