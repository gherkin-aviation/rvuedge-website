D3425 Root surgery molar

D Codes (Dental)

Also known as: apicoectomy molar, root surgery molar, surgical molar endodontics

Surgical root removal from molar tooth including apical surgery and retrograde filling.

In Plain Language

surgical molar root removal; molar tooth root surgery

Clinical Context

Surgical endodontic treatment of molar teeth with multiple roots and complex anatomy. Challenging procedure but essential for molar preservation when retreatment unsuccessful.

RVU Information

CPT D3425 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

Dental codes (CDT codes) require documentation of the tooth number(s) or area treated, clinical findings, and the procedure performed. Pre-authorization may be required for major restorative, prosthodontic, and oral surgery procedures. Include radiographs when supporting medical necessity.
