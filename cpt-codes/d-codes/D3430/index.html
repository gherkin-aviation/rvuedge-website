D3430 Retrograde filling

D-Codes +Add-on

Also known as: retrograde filling, apical filling, reverse filling

Retrograde filling placement from apical direction to seal apex during apical surgery.

Clinical Context

Placement of filling material from apical direction during surgical endodontic treatment. Seals apex and prevents leakage. Essential component of successful apicoectomy.
