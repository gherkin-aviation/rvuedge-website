D4266 Guided tissue regen resorble

D Codes (Dental)

Also known as: GTR resorbable, guided tissue regeneration, resorbable membrane

Guided tissue regeneration using a resorbable barrier membrane to selectively exclude epithelial and connective tissue from regenerating periodontal structures.

In Plain Language

regenerative gum membrane; biodegradable barrier treatment

Clinical Context

Indicated in periodontal regeneration to facilitate new attachment formation, bone fill, and cementum deposition in intrabony defects when biological wound healing principles support tissue compartmentalization.

RVU Information

CPT D4266 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

Dental codes (CDT codes) require documentation of the tooth number(s) or area treated, clinical findings, and the procedure performed. Pre-authorization may be required for major restorative, prosthodontic, and oral surgery procedures. Include radiographs when supporting medical necessity.
