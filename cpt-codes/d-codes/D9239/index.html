D9239 Intravenous mod sedation, first 15 minutes

D-Codes

Also known as: Intravenous moderate sedation initial, Conscious sedation initial

Administration of intravenous moderate sedation for the initial 15 minutes of a dental procedure.

Clinical Context

Used for baseline charge for intravenous moderate sedation. Additional time codes apply for procedures exceeding 15 minutes.
