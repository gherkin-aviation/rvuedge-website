D7530 Removal foreign body skin/areolar tissue

D-Codes

Also known as: foreign body removal skin, foreign material removal

Removal of foreign body (e.g., fractured instrument, retained glass, metal fragments) from skin or areolar (connective) tissue.

Clinical Context

Surgical removal of foreign bodies embedded in skin or subcutaneous areolar tissue resulting from trauma, iatrogenic causes, or other incidents.
