D6102 Debridement & contouring

D-Codes

Also known as: implant contouring, bone debridement and recontouring

Surgical debridement and contouring of bone around a dental implant to eliminate infection and restore physiologic bone contours. Includes reshaping of bone defects.

Clinical Context

Applied in moderate to advanced peri-implantitis cases, involving both tissue removal and bone reshaping to improve healing and reduce pocket depth.
