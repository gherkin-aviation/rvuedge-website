D5985 Radiation cone locator

D-Codes

Also known as: cone locator, positioning device, beam locator

Radiation cone locator. An intraoral device used to precisely position radiation cones for extraoral radiography.

Clinical Context

Used in dental radiography to standardize projection geometry and ensure reproducible radiographic positioning.
