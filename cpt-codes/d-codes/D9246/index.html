D9246 Mod sedation noniv-15 minutes

D-Codes

Also known as: Non-IV moderate sedation initial, Enteral sedation initial

Administration of non-intravenous moderate sedation for the initial 15-minute period of a dental procedure.

Clinical Context

Used for baseline charge for non-intravenous moderate sedation (inhalational or enteral). Additional time codes apply.
