D5511 Rep broke complicated dental base man

D-Codes

Also known as: repair broken mandibular denture, mandibular denture repair, broken lower denture fix

Repair of a broken complete mandibular denture base involving reconstruction of fractured acrylic resin.

Clinical Context

Performed when complete mandibular dentures sustain fractures from trauma or material degradation; restores function without complete remake.
