D8090 Compre dental treatment adult

D Codes (Dental)

Also known as: Comprehensive adult treatment, Adult comprehensive care

Comprehensive scope dental treatment for an adult patient including all preventive, diagnostic, restorative, and maintenance care.

In Plain Language

Complete dental care for adult

Clinical Context

Used for thorough dental care in adult patients requiring full range of diagnostic, preventive, restorative, and maintenance procedures.

RVU Information

CPT D8090 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

Dental codes (CDT codes) require documentation of the tooth number(s) or area treated, clinical findings, and the procedure performed. Pre-authorization may be required for major restorative, prosthodontic, and oral surgery procedures. Include radiographs when supporting medical necessity.
