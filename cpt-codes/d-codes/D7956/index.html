D7956 Tissue regen edent resorb

D-Codes

Also known as: Guided bone regeneration, GBR, Tissue regeneration

Guided bone regeneration using resorbable membrane in edentulous sites to enhance osseous repair and ridge reconstruction.

Clinical Context

Used for alveolar ridge reconstruction in edentulous patients to create sufficient bone for implant placement or esthetic restoration.
