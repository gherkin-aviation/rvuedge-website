D9420 Hospital/asc call

D-Codes

Also known as: hospital call, ambulatory surgery center visit, ASC visit

Dental service provided in a hospital or ambulatory surgical center, used for surgical procedures or treatment of patients requiring hospital-based care.

Clinical Context

Used for surgical extraction, implant placement, complex oral surgery, or dental treatment of hospitalized patients in acute care settings.
