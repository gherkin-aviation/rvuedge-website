D3332 Incomplete endodontic treatment

D-Codes

Also known as: incomplete endodontic treatment, partial RCT, incomplete root canal

Incomplete endodontic therapy where treatment cannot be completed in single visit.

Clinical Context

Endodontic treatment left intentionally incomplete, typically requiring calcium hydroxide dressing and future completion. Used for complex cases or when time is limited.
