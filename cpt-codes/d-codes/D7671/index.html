D7671 Alveolus open reduction

D-Codes

Also known as: alveolar fracture open reduction, surgical socket bone repair

Open reduction of alveolar bone fractures (surgical approach for complex or unstable socket bone fractures).

Clinical Context

Surgical approach and fixation of alveolar fractures requiring open reduction, often with plate fixation or screws to ensure proper alignment and stability.
