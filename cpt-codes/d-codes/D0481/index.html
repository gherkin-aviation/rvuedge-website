D0481 Electron microscopy

D-Codes

Also known as: electron microscopy, EM

Electron microscopy examination of tissue or cellular material providing ultra-high magnification for ultrastructural analysis.

Clinical Context

Used for ultrastructural diagnosis of rare conditions, viral infections, and assessment of cellular components at submicroscopic level.
