D5933 Refitting of obturator

D-Codes

Also known as: obturator refitting, obturator adjustment, obturator remake

Refitting of obturator. Adjustment or remake of an existing obturator to accommodate tissue changes and improve fit.

Clinical Context

Indicated as tissues heal and remodel after surgery. Can include minor adjustments or complete remake depending on extent of changes.
