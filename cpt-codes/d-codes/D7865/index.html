D7865 Tmj reshaping components

D-Codes

Also known as: TMJ arthroplasty, joint component reshaping

Temporomandibular joint reshaping of joint components (arthroplasty to reshape condyle, disc, or fossa).

Clinical Context

Surgical reshaping and contouring of temporomandibular joint components (condyle, disc, fossa) to improve mechanics and function in osteoarthritis or post-trauma deformity.
