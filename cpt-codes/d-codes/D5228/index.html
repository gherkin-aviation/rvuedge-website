D5228 Immed mand part denture

D-Codes

Also known as: immediate mand partial, immed partial mand, extraction mand partial

Fabrication of an immediate mandibular partial denture constructed and placed at time of tooth extraction.

Clinical Context

Preoperatively fabricated for placement immediately after mandibular extraction; provides interim replacement during tissue healing.
