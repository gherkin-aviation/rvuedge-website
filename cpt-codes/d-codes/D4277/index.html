D4277 Soft tissue graft firsttooth

D-Codes

Also known as: soft tissue graft first tooth, graft first site, tissue augmentation

Soft tissue graft at the first tooth recipient site to enhance attached gingiva, treat recession, or augment keratinized tissue.

Clinical Context

Indicated for isolated gingival recession, root coverage, esthetic improvement, or areas requiring increased width of attached gingiva in single tooth locations.
