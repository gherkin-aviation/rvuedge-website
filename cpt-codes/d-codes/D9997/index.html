D9997 Dental case management special needs

D-Codes

Also known as: special needs management, special care coordination, special populations care

Dental case management services specifically for patients with special healthcare needs, including coordination, planning, and support addressing unique medical and psychosocial barriers.

Clinical Context

Used for case management specific to patients with developmental disabilities, medical compromises, behavioral challenges, or psychosocial complexities requiring comprehensive coordination and specialized approaches.
