D4270 Pedicle soft tissue graft pr

D-Codes

Also known as: pedicle graft, lateral pedicle graft, free pedicle graft

Pedicle soft tissue graft procedure transferring tissue with intact blood supply from an adjacent donor site to recipient area.

Clinical Context

Indicated for gingival recession, root coverage, esthetic concerns, or areas requiring increased keratinized gingiva where the blood supply preservation enhances graft viability.
