D2332 Resin three surfaces-anterio

D-Codes

Also known as: composite three surface, resin filling, tooth-colored filling

Placement of a resin-based composite dental restoration on three surfaces of an anterior tooth, including cavity preparation and finishing.

Clinical Context

Used for three-surface caries lesions in anterior teeth; challenging restoration requiring careful moisture control.
