D5410 Dentures adjust complete maxil

D-Codes

Also known as: adjust upper complete denture, denture adjustment max, complete denture reline

Adjustment of a complete maxillary denture including occlusal and fit modifications to optimize retention and function.

Clinical Context

Routine maintenance to correct retention loss, stability issues, or occlusal discrepancies following alveolar resorption or extended wear.
