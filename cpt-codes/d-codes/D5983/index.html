D5983 Radiation applicator

D-Codes

Also known as: radiation applicator, brachytherapy applicator, radiation mold

Radiation applicator. An intraoral device used to position and direct radiation therapy for oral or oropharyngeal malignancies.

Clinical Context

Used for intraoral or intracavitary brachytherapy delivery. Provides precise radiation positioning and reproducibility.
