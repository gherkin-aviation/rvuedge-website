D1704 Moderna vacc administration second dose

D-Codes

Also known as: Moderna vaccine second dose, Spikevax second dose

Administration of Moderna messenger RNA vaccine against SARS-CoV-2 as a second dose injection in a dental setting by qualified dental personnel.

Clinical Context

Completes initial two-dose primary series of Moderna vaccination; administered 4 weeks after first dose.
