D6101 Debridement of a periimplant

D Codes (Dental)

Also known as: implant debridement, peri-implant debridement

Surgical debridement of periimplantitis (infected bone and tissue around a dental implant). Removal of infected and necrotic tissue to control peri-implant disease.

In Plain Language

cleaning around implant; removing infected tissue around implant

Clinical Context

Used in the treatment of peri-implantitis, a destructive inflammatory disease affecting tissues around implants, to halt disease progression and preserve remaining bone.

RVU Information

CPT D6101 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.
