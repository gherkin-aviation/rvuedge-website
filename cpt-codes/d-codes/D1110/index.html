D1110 Dental prophylaxis adult

D-Codes

Also known as: prophy, prophylaxis, cleaning

Mechanical removal of plaque, calculus, and stains from exposed tooth surfaces and the coronal portion of the root in adult patients, including polishing with a rotary instrument.

Clinical Context

Fundamental preventive procedure recommended at regular intervals (typically every 6 months) to maintain oral health, prevent caries and periodontitis, and remove accumulated biofilm.
