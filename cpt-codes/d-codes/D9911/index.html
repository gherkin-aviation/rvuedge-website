D9911 application desensitizing resin

D-Codes

Also known as: resin desensitizer, bonded desensitizing resin, barrier application

Application of a desensitizing resin-based coating to exposed tooth surfaces to create a protective barrier against hypersensitivity and reduce sensitivity reactions.

Clinical Context

Used to treat dentinal hypersensitivity by applying adhesive resin-based materials that create a lasting protective layer over exposed dentin, particularly effective for cervical lesions and gingival recession.
