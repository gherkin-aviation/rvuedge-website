D4268 Surgical revision procedure

D-Codes

Also known as: surgical revision, flap revision, tissue modification

Surgical revision procedure to reopen and refine previously treated periodontal surgical sites or modify tissue contours.

Clinical Context

Indicated when initial periodontal surgery requires modification, additional osseous contouring, or tissue repositioning to optimize treatment outcome or address unforeseen tissue changes.
