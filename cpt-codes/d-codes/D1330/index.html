D1330 Oral hygiene instructions

D-Codes

Also known as: hygiene instruction, oral hygiene instruction, OHI

Patient instruction and education in proper oral hygiene techniques including toothbrushing, flossing, and use of other plaque control devices to maintain optimal oral health.

Clinical Context

Essential preventive procedure recommended for all patients, particularly those with inadequate oral hygiene or high caries and periodontal risk.
