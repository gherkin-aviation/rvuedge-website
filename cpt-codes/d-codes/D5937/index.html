D5937 Trismus appliance

D-Codes

Also known as: trismus appliance, mouth opening device, jaw stretch device

Trismus appliance. An intraoral device designed to improve and maintain mouth opening in patients with limited jaw mobility.

Clinical Context

Indicated for trismus post-radiation, post-surgery, or from scleroderma. Used therapeutically to gradually improve jaw opening.
