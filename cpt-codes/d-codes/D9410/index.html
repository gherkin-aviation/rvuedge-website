D9410 Dental house call

D-Codes

Also known as: house call, home dental visit, domiciliary visit

Dental service provided at the patient's residence, typically for homebound or institutionalized patients unable to visit a dental office.

Clinical Context

Applicable for elderly patients, disabled patients, or those in nursing facilities who require dental evaluation and treatment at their residence due to mobility limitations or medical conditions.
