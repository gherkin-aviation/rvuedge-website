D5992 Adjust max prostate appliance

D-Codes

Also known as: adjust max prosth, maxillary adjustment, prosth adjustment

Adjust maxillary prosthetic appliance. Minor adjustment or modification of an existing maxillary prosthetic device.

Clinical Context

Performed when maxillary prosthesis requires minor fit adjustments, tissue adaptation, or minor repairs.
