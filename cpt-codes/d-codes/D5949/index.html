D5949 Mand implant superior fix part

D-Codes

Also known as: implant-supported bridge, implant bridge, fixed implant RPD

Mandibular implant-supported prosthesis, fixed partial. A fixed partial denture supported by dental implants in the mandible.

Clinical Context

Used for mandibular partial edentulism with adequate bone and implants. Provides superior function over removable options.
