D5214 Dentures mandibl part metal

D Codes (Dental)

Also known as: metal partial lower, mandibular metal partial, chrome partial lower

Fabrication of a mandibular partial denture with cast metal framework and resin saddles replacing mandibular teeth.

In Plain Language

lower metal and tooth plate; cast framework denture

Clinical Context

Used for partially edentulous mandible with metal framework; improves structural integrity, retention, and esthetic outcomes versus all-resin alternatives.

RVU Information

CPT D5214 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

Dental codes (CDT codes) require documentation of the tooth number(s) or area treated, clinical findings, and the procedure performed. Pre-authorization may be required for major restorative, prosthodontic, and oral surgery procedures. Include radiographs when supporting medical necessity.
