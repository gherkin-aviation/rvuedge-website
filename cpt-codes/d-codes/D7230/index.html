D7230 Impact tooth remov part bony

D-Codes

Also known as: impacted tooth removal, partial bone, moderately impacted extraction

Removal of impacted tooth requiring mucoperiosteal flap elevation and partial bone removal.

Clinical Context

Used for extraction of teeth with partial bony impaction requiring flap elevation and significant alveolar bone removal. Common indications include deeply impacted wisdom teeth or other fully to partially impacted permanent teeth.
