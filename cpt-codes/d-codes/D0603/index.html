D0603 Caries risk assess high risk

D-Codes

Also known as: caries risk assessment, cavity risk check

Assessment of dental caries risk status in a patient identified as having high risk for future caries development based on clinical examination and risk factors.

Clinical Context

Performed to identify patients who require intensive preventive protocols including fluoride applications, dietary modifications, and frequent recall visits.
