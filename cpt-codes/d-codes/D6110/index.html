D6110 implant/abut remov dental max

D-Codes

Also known as: maxillary implant removal, upper jaw implant extraction

Surgical removal of an implant and abutment from the maxilla (upper jaw) where all teeth in the arch have been lost. Complete maxillary implant removal.

Clinical Context

Performed in fully edentulous maxillary cases when implants fail or require replacement, involving surgical access and removal of all implant components.
