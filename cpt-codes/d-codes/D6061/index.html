D6061 Abutment supported mtl crown

D-Codes

Also known as: titanium crown, titanium implant crown, ti crown

Abutment supported metal crown. A single crown with titanium or titanium alloy substructure on an implant abutment.

Clinical Context

Used for biocompatibility concerns or when implant esthetic demands are minimal. Excellent for posterior teeth.
