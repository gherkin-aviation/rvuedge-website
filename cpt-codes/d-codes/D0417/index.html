D0417 Collect & prep saliva sample

D-Codes

Also known as: saliva collection, spit test

Collection and preparation of saliva sample from patient for laboratory analysis to assess salivary composition and function.

Clinical Context

Used to collect unstimulated or stimulated saliva for assessment of salivary flow, pH, buffering capacity, and composition in caries and xerostomia evaluation.
