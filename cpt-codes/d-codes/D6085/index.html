D6085 Interim implant crown

D-Codes

Also known as: temporary implant crown, interim crown, provisional crown

Interim implant crown. A temporary esthetic crown placed on an implant during healing phase.

Clinical Context

Placed immediately after abutment placement or before definitive restoration to maintain esthetics and guide tissue healing.
