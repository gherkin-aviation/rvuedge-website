D6191 Semi precision abutment

D-Codes

Also known as: semi-precision abutment, semi-rigid abutment

Semi-precision abutment for use with dental implants. Provides semi-rigid attachment for implant restorations with some limited movement.

Clinical Context

Used in implant restorations where some stress relief is desired, particularly beneficial in esthetic areas or combined natural-implant restorations.
