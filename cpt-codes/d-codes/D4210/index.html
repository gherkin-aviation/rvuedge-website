D4210 Gingivectomy/plasty 4 or mor

D-Codes

Also known as: gingivectomy, gingival plasty, gum surgery

Gingivectomy or gingival plasty on four or more teeth per quadrant.

Clinical Context

Surgical removal or reshaping of gingival tissue affecting four or more teeth. Used for esthetic or functional reasons including crown lengthening preparation.
