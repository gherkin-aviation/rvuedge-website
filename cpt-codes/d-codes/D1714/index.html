D1714 Pfizer vacc adm pediatric second dose

D-Codes

Also known as: Pfizer pediatric second dose, Pfizer kids vaccine second dose

Administration of pediatric-formulation Pfizer-BioNTech messenger RNA vaccine against SARS-CoV-2 as a second dose injection in a dental setting by qualified dental personnel.

Clinical Context

Completes initial two-dose primary series of pediatric Pfizer vaccination in children.
