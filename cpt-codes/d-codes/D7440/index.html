D7440 Malignant tumor excision to 1.25 centimeters

D-Codes

Also known as: malignant tumor excision small, cancer removal limited

Malignant tumor excision up to 1.25 centimeters with adequate oncologic margins and potential reconstruction.

Clinical Context

Excision of oral malignant tumors up to 1.25 cm with wide margins. Includes potential bone removal and reconstruction as needed.
