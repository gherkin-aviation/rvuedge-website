D7990 Emergency tracheotomy

D-Codes

Also known as: Emergency tracheotomy, Emergency airway

Emergency surgical creation of an artificial airway (tracheostomy) to bypass upper airway obstruction in dental patients.

Clinical Context

Used for life-threatening upper airway obstruction in dental settings due to anaphylaxis, edema, or aspiration. Requires immediate intervention.
