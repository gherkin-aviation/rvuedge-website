D2394 Posterior >=4srfc resinbase cmpst

D-Codes

Also known as: posterior composite crown, resin crown, multi-surface crown

Fabrication and placement of a resin-based composite crown restoration on a posterior tooth with four or more facial surfaces exposed, providing full coronal coverage.

Clinical Context

Used for posterior tooth with extensive esthetic demands; four or more surfaces are exposed or visible.
