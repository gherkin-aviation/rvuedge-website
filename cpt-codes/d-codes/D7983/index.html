D7983 Closure of salivary fistula

D-Codes

Also known as: Salivary fistula closure, Fistula repair

Surgical closure of a patent or symptomatic salivary gland fistula or abnormal opening.

Clinical Context

Used for salivary fistulas resulting from trauma, surgery, or infection. Closure prevents continuous drainage and allows healing.
