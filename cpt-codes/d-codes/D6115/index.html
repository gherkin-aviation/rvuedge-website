D6115 implant/abut fixed dental mand

D-Codes

Also known as: mandibular implant bridge, lower fixed implant prosthesis

Implant-supported fixed denture (bridge) with removable components for the mandible (lower jaw) in patients with complete loss of all mandibular teeth.

Clinical Context

Selected for fully edentulous mandibular patients requiring fixed restoration supported by multiple implants for improved function and stability.
