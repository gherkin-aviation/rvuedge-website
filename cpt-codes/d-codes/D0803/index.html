D0803 3d facial scan direct

D-Codes

Also known as: facial scan, 3D facial scan, face scan

Three-dimensional digital scan of facial structures and soft tissues captured directly from the patient's face using extraoral facial scanning technology.

Clinical Context

Utilized in esthetic treatment planning, orthognathic surgery planning, and prosthodontic rehabilitation to assess facial proportions and soft tissue relationships.
