D5725 Rebase hybrid prosthesis

D Codes (Dental)

Also known as: rebase hybrid prosthesis, implant denture rebase, implant hybrid rebase

Rebase of a hybrid prosthesis combining implant support with tooth support elements.

In Plain Language

refitting of implant-supported denture; hybrid denture adjustment

Clinical Context

Performed on hybrid prostheses with both implant and tooth support to restore fit and function following implant osseointegration or ridge resorption.

RVU Information

CPT D5725 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

Dental codes (CDT codes) require documentation of the tooth number(s) or area treated, clinical findings, and the procedure performed. Pre-authorization may be required for major restorative, prosthodontic, and oral surgery procedures. Include radiographs when supporting medical necessity.
