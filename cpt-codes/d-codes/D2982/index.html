D2982 Onlay repair

D-Codes

Also known as: onlay repair, onlay fix, restore onlay

Repair of a defective or damaged onlay restoration.

Clinical Context

Repair of damaged onlay restoration including fracture, chipping, or loss of retention. May involve resin repair, adjustments, or recementing.
