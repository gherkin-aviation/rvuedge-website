D7911 Dental suture wound to 5 centimeters

D-Codes

Also known as: Dental laceration repair, Oral laceration suturing

Suturing of an oral laceration of recent onset or complicating factor measuring up to 5 centimeters in length.

Clinical Context

Used for traumatic lacerations to oral tissues up to 5 cm, may include some complicating factors such as deeper involvement or location.
