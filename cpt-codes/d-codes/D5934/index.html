D5934 Mandibular flange prosthesis

D-Codes

Also known as: mandibular prosth, lower jaw prosthesis, flange prosthesis

Mandibular flange prosthesis. A removable lower jaw device with an extended tissue-bearing border for supporting tissues.

Clinical Context

Used for mandibular bone loss requiring soft tissue support. Common after tumor resection or when dental implants are contraindicated.
