D2949 Restorative foundation

D-Codes

Also known as: foundation, base restoration, build-up base

Construction of a restorative foundation or base to rebuild tooth structure.

Clinical Context

Procedural code for constructing restorative foundation on endodontically treated teeth or large restorations. Provides supportive base for crown or other restorations.
