D7490 Maxilla or mandible resectio

D-Codes

Also known as: jaw resection, mandibular/maxillary resection, bone resection

Surgical resection of maxilla or mandible (partial or complete removal of jaw bone) in cases of disease, tumor, or trauma.

Clinical Context

Surgical removal (partial or complete) of maxilla or mandible due to malignancy, trauma, severe infection, or other pathology. Major procedure requiring extensive reconstruction planning.
