D6013 Surgical place mini implant

D-Codes

Also known as: mini implant, mini implant placement, narrow implant

Surgical placement of a miniature or mini dental implant. Placement of a small-diameter implant for minimal invasive applications.

Clinical Context

Used for narrow ridge sites or temporary stabilization of dentures. Less invasive than standard implants.
