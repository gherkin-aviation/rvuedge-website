D7310 Alveoplasty with extraction

D-Codes

Also known as: alveoloplasty with extraction, ridge contouring, ridge reshaping

Alveoloplasty (surgical reshaping of alveolar ridge) performed in conjunction with tooth extraction.

Clinical Context

Surgical contouring and reshaping of alveolar ridge performed at the time of tooth extraction to improve esthetics, function, or prosthetic fit. Common in preparation for prosthodontic treatment.
