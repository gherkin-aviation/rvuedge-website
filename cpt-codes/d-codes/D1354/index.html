D1354 Int caries medial application per tooth

D-Codes

Also known as: caries medicament, interim caries treatment, silver diamine fluoride

Application of therapeutic medicinal agents (such as silver diamine fluoride) to an individual tooth surface to arrest incipient caries lesions and prevent progression.

Clinical Context

Increasingly used for early childhood caries management, primary dentition, and as an interim treatment prior to definitive restoration.
