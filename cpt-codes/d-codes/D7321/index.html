D7321 Alveoloplasty not with extracts

D-Codes

Also known as: limited alveoloplasty, minor ridge contouring

Limited alveoloplasty without tooth extraction, used for minor ridge recontouring and irregularities.

Clinical Context

Minimal surgical reshaping of alveolar ridge to correct minor irregularities without concurrent extraction. Often used for final prosthetic preparation.
