D5925 Facial augmentation implant

D-Codes

Also known as: facial implant, augmentation implant, facial contour implant

Facial augmentation implant. A prosthetic material surgically placed to increase facial volume or contour, typically in soft tissues.

Clinical Context

Used to restore facial volume loss from aging, trauma, or congenital defects. Can be alloplastic or autogenous materials.
