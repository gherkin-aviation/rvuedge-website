D6791 Crown full base metal cast

D-Codes

Also known as: full crown, base metal crown, complete crown

Full crown restoration (complete coverage on all surfaces) constructed from base metal alloy. Economical full-coverage crown.

Clinical Context

Used when full tooth coverage and cost-effectiveness are desired with adequate strength.
