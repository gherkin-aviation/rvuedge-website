D8670 Periodic orthodontc treatment visit

D-Codes

Also known as: Orthodontic visit, Periodic orthodontic treatment

Periodic orthodontic treatment visit during active fixed or removable appliance therapy to monitor progress and adjust treatment.

Clinical Context

Used for routine monthly or periodic visits during active orthodontic treatment to adjust appliances, monitor progress, and ensure compliance.
