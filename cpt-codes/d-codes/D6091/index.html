D6091 Repl semi/precision attach

D-Codes

Also known as: precision attachment replacement, abutment attachment swap

Replacement of semi-precision or precision attachment for dental implant support. This procedure involves removing and replacing worn or damaged attachment components.

Clinical Context

Performed when existing precision attachments become worn, loose, or damaged, requiring replacement to maintain proper retention and function of implant-supported restorations.
