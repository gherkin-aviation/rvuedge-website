D9945 Occ guard, soft, full arch

D-Codes

Also known as: soft guard, flexible guard, full-arch soft guard

Soft acrylic or flexible occlusal guard custom fabricated to cover the entire maxillary or mandibular arch for bruxism or sensitive tooth protection.

Clinical Context

Used for fabrication of flexible acrylic guards for patients preferring softer materials or those with sensitive teeth, providing comfort for bruxism and clenching management.
