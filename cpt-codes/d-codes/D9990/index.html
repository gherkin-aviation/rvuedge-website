D9990 Transverse or sign language services

D Codes (Dental)

Also known as: interpreter services, translation services, sign language interpreter

Translation or sign language interpretation services provided during dental appointments to facilitate communication between dental providers and patients with language barriers.

In Plain Language

translator or sign language services; interpreter at dental visit

Clinical Context

Used when professional translation or sign language interpretation services are provided to enable clear communication and informed consent for non-English speaking or deaf patients.

RVU Information

CPT D9990 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

Dental codes (CDT codes) require documentation of the tooth number(s) or area treated, clinical findings, and the procedure performed. Pre-authorization may be required for major restorative, prosthodontic, and oral surgery procedures. Include radiographs when supporting medical necessity.
