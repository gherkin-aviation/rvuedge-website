D5711 Dentures rebase complete mand

D-Codes

Also known as: rebase lower complete denture, complete denture rebase mandibular, denture refitting

Rebase of a complete mandibular denture replacing the base tissue surface while retaining denture teeth and framework.

Clinical Context

Indicated to optimize fit and retention in mandibular complete dentures experiencing retention loss from ridge resorption.
