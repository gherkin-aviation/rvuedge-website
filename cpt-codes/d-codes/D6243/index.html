D6243 Pontic porcelain to titanium

D-Codes

Also known as: porcelain on titanium pontic, titanium pontic

Pontic with porcelain veneer constructed on a titanium framework for bridge. Combines biocompatible titanium with natural-looking porcelain.

Clinical Context

Used in bridge pontics where excellent biocompatibility and esthetics are both important, particularly beneficial near implants.
