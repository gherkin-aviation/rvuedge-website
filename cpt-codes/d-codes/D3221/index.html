D3221 Gross pulpal debridement

D-Codes

Also known as: pulpal debridement, emergency pulp removal, relief debridement

Gross pulpal debridement for pain relief of acutely infected or exposed pulp.

Clinical Context

Emergency procedure for acute pulpitis or irreversible pulp inflammation. Removes source of inflammation/pain. May be temporary prior to definitive endodontic therapy.
