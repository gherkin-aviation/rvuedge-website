D7840 Removal of tmj condyle

D-Codes

Also known as: condylectomy, TMJ condyle removal

Removal of temporomandibular joint condyle (surgical excision of the mandibular condylar head).

Clinical Context

Surgical removal of the mandibular condyle for treatment of severe pathology such as ankylosis, malignancy, or irreparable trauma.
