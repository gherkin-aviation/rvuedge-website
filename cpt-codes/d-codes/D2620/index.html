D2620 Inlay porcelain/ceramic 2 su

D-Codes

Also known as: ceramic inlay, porcelain inlay, two surface ceramic

Fabrication and placement of a porcelain or ceramic inlay restoration on two surfaces of a tooth, including preparation, fabrication, and cementation.

Clinical Context

Used for two-surface caries with esthetic requirements; combines ceramic strength with natural appearance.
