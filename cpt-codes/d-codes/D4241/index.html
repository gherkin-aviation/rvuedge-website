D4241 Gngvl flap w rootplan 1-3 th

D-Codes

Also known as: limited gingival flap, single-tooth flap therapy, flap debridement 1-3 teeth

Gingival flap procedure with root planing addressing 1-3 teeth, tailored for localized periodontal disease treatment.

Clinical Context

Indicated for localized periodontal pockets or defects affecting a small number of teeth with moderate bone loss or inflammation.
