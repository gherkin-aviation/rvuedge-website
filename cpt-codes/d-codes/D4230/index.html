D4230 Ana crown exploration 4 or> per quad

D-Codes

Also known as: crown lengthening, gum contouring, anatomic exposure

Anatomic crown exposure via gingival surgery - four or more teeth per quadrant.

Clinical Context

Surgical gingival alteration to expose more anatomic crown on four or more teeth. Used for esthetic concerns or to gain retention space for restorations.
