D1208 Topical application fluorid ex vrnsh

D-Codes

Also known as: fluoride gel, fluoride rinse, fluoride application

Topical application of therapeutic fluoride in forms other than varnish (such as gel or solution) directly to exposed tooth surfaces to prevent caries and remineralize early enamel lesions.

Clinical Context

Used as an alternative to varnish for caries prevention in high-risk patients, particularly when varnish application is not feasible or tolerated.
