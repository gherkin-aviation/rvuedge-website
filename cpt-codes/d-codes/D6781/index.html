D6781 Crown 3/4 cast based metal

D-Codes

Also known as: three-quarter crown, 3/4 crown, base metal 3/4

Three-quarter crown restoration (partial coverage crown on three surfaces) constructed from base metal alloy. Economical tooth-preserving design.

Clinical Context

Used when tooth preservation and cost-effectiveness are desired with adequate strength.
