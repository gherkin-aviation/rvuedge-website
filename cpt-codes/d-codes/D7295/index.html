D7295 Bone harvest,auto graft procedure

D-Codes

Also known as: bone harvest and graft, autogenous bone graft, intraoral bone harvest

Harvest of autogenous bone graft from intraoral site and placement for alveolar or other bone augmentation procedures.

Clinical Context

Used for intraoral bone harvesting and placement as autogenous graft material for alveolar bone augmentation, ridge reconstruction, or implant site development. Common in implant and periodontal surgery.
