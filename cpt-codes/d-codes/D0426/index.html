D0426 Coll, analys of saliva

D-Codes

Also known as: saliva caries test

Collection and analysis of saliva sample to assess caries risk factors including salivary pH, buffering capacity, and antimicrobial properties.

Clinical Context

Used to evaluate salivary protective mechanisms and identify patients at increased caries risk requiring enhanced preventive care.
