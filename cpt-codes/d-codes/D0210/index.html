D0210 Intraor comprehensive series

D-Codes

Also known as: full mouth x-rays, complete series, FMX

Intraoral comprehensive series of radiographic images, documenting full mouth dentition including all tooth-bearing areas with multiple radiographs taken intraorally.

Clinical Context

Used for comprehensive radiographic documentation of all teeth and supporting structures to assess caries, bone level, root morphology, and pathology during initial evaluation or periodic screening.
