D5864 Overdenture partial max

D-Codes

Also known as: partial maxillary overdenture, overdenture partial upper, implant retained maxillary partial

Partial maxillary overdenture fabricated over retained root(s) or implant(s) in a partially edentulous arch.

Clinical Context

Constructed in partially edentulous maxilla over retained teeth or implants for improved retention and function.
