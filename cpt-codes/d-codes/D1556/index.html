D1556 Rem fixed unilateral space maint

D-Codes

Also known as: space maintainer removal and replacement, unilateral replacement

Removal of a unilateral fixed space maintainer appliance that has been cemented to tooth structure and replacement with a new unilateral space maintainer.

Clinical Context

Performed when a previously placed space maintainer requires replacement due to damage, wear, or failure to achieve its intended function.
