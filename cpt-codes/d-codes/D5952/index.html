D5952 Pediatric speech aid

D-Codes

Also known as: pediatric speech aid, speech therapy device, speech prosthesis

Pediatric speech aid. An intraoral device designed to improve speech and articulation in children with speech disorders.

Clinical Context

Used for children with cleft palate, velopharyngeal insufficiency, or other speech impediments. Facilitates normal speech development.
