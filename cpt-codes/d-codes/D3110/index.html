D3110 Pulp cap direct

D-Codes

Also known as: direct pulp cap, pulp capping, direct cap

Direct pulp capping procedure where capping agent applied directly to exposed pulp tissue.

Clinical Context

Emergency treatment for mechanical or traumatic pulp exposure. Capping agent promotes healing and may maintain pulp vitality. Success depends on contamination control.
