D0230 Intraoral periapical each add

D-Codes +Add-on

Also known as: additional PA x-ray, additional periapical film

Intraoral periapical radiographic image, each additional tooth, capturing apical region of additional teeth during comprehensive radiographic series.

Clinical Context

Used as supplemental periapical radiographs to evaluate multiple teeth for caries, periapical pathology, and bone levels in addition to initial tooth assessed.
