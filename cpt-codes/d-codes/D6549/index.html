D6549 Resin retainer

D-Codes

Also known as: resin retainer, composite retainer

Dental retainer component constructed from resin-based materials. Provides support and retention for removable prosthesis with esthetic properties.

Clinical Context

Used in precision attachment dentures for esthetic retention with tooth-colored components.
