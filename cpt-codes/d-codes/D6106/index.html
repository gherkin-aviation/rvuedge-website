D6106 Tissue regen resorbable

D-Codes

Also known as: tissue regeneration membrane, resorbable guided tissue regen

Guided tissue regeneration using resorbable membrane barriers around a dental implant to promote bone and soft tissue healing. Material is absorbed and does not require removal.

Clinical Context

Applied in combination with surgical procedures to promote selective cell growth and tissue regeneration around failing or compromised implants.
