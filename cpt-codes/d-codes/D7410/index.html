D7410 Radiation excision lesion up to 1.25 centimeters

D-Codes

Also known as: radical excision small lesion, lesion excision limited

Radical excision of an oral lesion up to 1.25 centimeters in size, including surrounding tissue and bone removal as necessary.

Clinical Context

Surgical removal of oral lesion with adequate margins and potential bone removal. Used for benign and malignant lesions up to 1.25 cm. Excised tissue typically sent for histopathology.
