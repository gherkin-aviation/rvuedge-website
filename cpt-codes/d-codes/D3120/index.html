D3120 Pulp cap indirect

D-Codes

Also known as: indirect pulp cap, indirect capping, dentin capping

Indirect pulp capping procedure where capping agent applied to dentin near pulp.

Clinical Context

Conservative treatment for deep caries near pulp without actual pulp exposure. Capping agent promotes secondary dentin formation and arrests caries. May preserve pulp vitality.
