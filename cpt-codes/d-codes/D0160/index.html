D0160 Extensv oral evaluation prob focus

D-Codes

Also known as: extensive problem-focused exam, detailed limited exam

Extensive oral evaluation focused on a problem area, involving detailed assessment of specific site(s) of concern with comprehensive documentation and treatment planning.

Clinical Context

Used when a patient presents with complex or extensive problems in specific oral regions requiring detailed evaluation beyond a simple problem-focused assessment.
