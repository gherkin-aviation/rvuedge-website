D6980 Fixed partial repair

D-Codes

Also known as: fixed bridge repair, crown bridge repair

Repair or adjustment of an existing fixed partial denture (bridge), including minor modifications to maintain function and retention.

Clinical Context

Used when a previously placed fixed partial denture requires repair, adjustment, or restoration to return it to functional status. Common indications include fractured pontics, failed cement, or component loosening.
