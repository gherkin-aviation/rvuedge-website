D9955 Oral application thxpy titration vis

D-Codes

Also known as: titration visit, adjustment visit, therapy titration

Titration visit for oral appliance therapy involving assessment and adjustment of mandibular positioning to optimize therapeutic effectiveness and patient tolerance.

Clinical Context

Used for chairside titration visits to progressively adjust mandibular advancement devices, evaluate therapeutic response, and ensure patient compliance with oral appliance therapy for sleep apnea.
