D4260 Osseous surgery 4 or more

D-Codes

Also known as: osseous surgery, bone contouring, multiple tooth osseous surgery

Osseous surgery involving bone contouring and/or recontouring affecting four or more teeth to treat periodontal defects.

Clinical Context

Performed in conjunction with flap therapy to eliminate intrabony defects, reduce anatomic compromises, and facilitate long-term maintenance in moderate to advanced periodontitis affecting multiple teeth.
