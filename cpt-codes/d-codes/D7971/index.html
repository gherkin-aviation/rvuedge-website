D7971 Excision pericoronal gingiva

D-Codes

Also known as: Pericoronectomy, Soft tissue excision

Surgical excision of pericoronal gingiva or soft tissue associated with impacted or erupting teeth.

Clinical Context

Used during extraction of impacted teeth or during surgical tooth exposure when excess pericoronal tissue requires removal.
