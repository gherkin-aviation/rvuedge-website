D6010 Odontics endosteal implant

D-Codes

Also known as: endosteal implant, implant placement, dental implant

Odontics endosteal implant. Placement of an endosseous implant body within the jawbone for future tooth replacement.

Clinical Context

First stage surgery for implant-supported prostheses. Placed in adequate bone after site preparation.
