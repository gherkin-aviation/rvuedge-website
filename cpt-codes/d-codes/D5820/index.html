D5820 Denture interm part maxill

D-Codes

Also known as: interim upper partial denture, temporary maxillary partial, provisional partial

Interim maxillary partial denture constructed as temporary prosthesis pending definitive treatment or healing.

Clinical Context

Constructed as transitional prosthesis in partially edentulous patients awaiting definitive treatment or tissue maturation.
