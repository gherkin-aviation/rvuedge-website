D1321 Couns for high risk sub use

D-Codes

Also known as: substance abuse counseling, substance use counseling

Patient counseling and education regarding substance use disorder, abuse of recreational drugs, and oral health consequences of substance abuse including bruxism and xerostomia.

Clinical Context

Part of comprehensive patient education for individuals at risk for or affected by substance use disorder; addresses oral manifestations and systemic health implications.
