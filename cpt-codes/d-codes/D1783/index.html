D1783 Vac administration human pap dose 3

D-Codes

Also known as: HPV vaccine third dose, cervical cancer vaccine

Administration of human papillomavirus vaccine as a third dose injection in a dental setting by qualified dental personnel for prevention of human papillomavirus-related cancers.

Clinical Context

Final dose in HPV vaccination series; completes protection against HPV-related oral and pharyngeal cancers.
