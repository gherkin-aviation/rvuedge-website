D3352 Apexification/recalc interim

D-Codes

Also known as: apexification interim, apex closure interim, root development interim

Interim appointment for apexification or root development continuation treatment.

Clinical Context

Follow-up appointment during apexification protocol for replacement of calcium hydroxide dressing. Multiple interim appointments may be required over months.
