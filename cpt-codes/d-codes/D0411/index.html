D0411 Hba1c in office testing

D-Codes

Also known as: HbA1c test, A1c screening

Hemoglobin A1c (HbA1c) in-office blood test providing rapid assessment of patient's average blood glucose levels over 2-3 months.

Clinical Context

Used for diabetes screening and monitoring in dental office settings to identify undiagnosed or poorly controlled diabetes in patients.
