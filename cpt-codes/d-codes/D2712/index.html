D2712 Crown 3/4 resin-based compos

D-Codes

Also known as: three-quarter composite crown, resin three-quarter crown

Fabrication and placement of a three-quarter (¾) resin-based composite crown restoration covering three-quarters of the tooth surface with veneer effect.

Clinical Context

Used when facial tooth display is limited and lingual surface can remain intact; less invasive preparation than full crown.
