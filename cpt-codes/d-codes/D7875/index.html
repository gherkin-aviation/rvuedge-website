D7875 Tmj arthroscopy synovectomy

D Codes (Dental)

Also known as: TMJ synovectomy, Arthroscopic synovectomy

Arthroscopic synovectomy of the temporomandibular joint to remove inflamed synovial tissue and improve joint function.

In Plain Language

Removing inflamed joint lining; Cleaning the jaw joint lining

Clinical Context

Indicated for chronic temporomandibular joint synovitis causing pain, swelling, and functional limitation. Removes inflamed tissue to reduce symptoms.

RVU Information

CPT D7875 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.
