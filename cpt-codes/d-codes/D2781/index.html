D2781 Crown 3/4 cast base metal

D-Codes

Also known as: 3/4 crown, partial crown, base metal 3/4

Three-quarter cast crown restoration using base metal alloy.

Clinical Context

Conservative restoration covering three surfaces with base metal substructure. Cost-effective option for posterior teeth with lower esthetic demands.
