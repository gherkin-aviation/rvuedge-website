D9245 Administration mod sedation-enteral

D Codes (Dental)

Also known as: Oral moderate sedation, Enteral moderate sedation

Administration of moderate sedation using enteral route (oral) sedative medications with patient monitoring.

In Plain Language

Light to medium sedation by mouth

Clinical Context

Used for moderate sedation via oral/enteral route with appropriate monitoring for anxious patients requiring deeper sedation.

RVU Information

CPT D9245 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

Dental codes (CDT codes) require documentation of the tooth number(s) or area treated, clinical findings, and the procedure performed. Pre-authorization may be required for major restorative, prosthodontic, and oral surgery procedures. Include radiographs when supporting medical necessity.
