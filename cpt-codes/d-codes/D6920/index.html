D6920 Dental connector bar

D-Codes

Also known as: connector bar, framework, rest

Dental connector bar or framework component joining two or more teeth or implants in prosthetic design. Provides rigid connection between units.

Clinical Context

Used in fixed and removable prosthetics to provide structural connection and support between multiple dental units.
