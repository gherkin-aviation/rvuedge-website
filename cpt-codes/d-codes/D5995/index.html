D5995 Peri-procedural medicament with seal, max

D-Codes

Also known as: medicament base maxillary, medicated base, sealed medicament

Periprosthetic medicament with seal, maxillary. Application of medicated base or medicament beneath a maxillary prosthesis with sealing.

Clinical Context

Used for denture stomatitis, sore tissue, or to deliver therapeutic agents under maxillary dentures.
