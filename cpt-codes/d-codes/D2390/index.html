D2390 Anterior resin-based cmpst crown

D-Codes

Also known as: composite crown, resin crown, bonded crown

Fabrication and placement of a resin-based composite crown restoration on an anterior tooth, providing full coronal coverage.

Clinical Context

Used for anterior tooth restoration when remaining tooth structure is insufficient for direct restoration; esthetic alternative to porcelain.
