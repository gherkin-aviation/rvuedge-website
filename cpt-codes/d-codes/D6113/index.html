D6113 Imp/abut rem dental part mand

D-Codes

Also known as: partial mandibular implant removal, lower arch implant removal

Surgical removal of an implant and abutment from the mandible (lower jaw) in a partially edentulous arch. Removal of implant(s) in an arch with remaining natural teeth.

Clinical Context

Used when implants in partially edentulous mandibular patients require removal while preserving remaining natural teeth.
