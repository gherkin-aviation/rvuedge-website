D1520 Remove unilateral space maintain

D-Codes

Also known as: space maintainer removal, unilateral removal

Removal of a unilateral space maintainer appliance from the dental arch after completion of its space maintenance function or when permanent teeth have erupted.

Clinical Context

Performed when the primary purpose of the appliance has been achieved and permanent teeth have erupted, or if complications require removal.
