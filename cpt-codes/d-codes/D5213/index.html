D5213 Dentures maxill part metal

D-Codes

Also known as: metal partial upper, maxillary metal partial, chrome partial upper

Fabrication of a maxillary partial denture with cast metal framework and resin saddles replacing maxillary teeth.

Clinical Context

Indicated for partially edentulous maxilla; metal framework provides superior strength, retention, and esthetics compared to all-resin construction.
