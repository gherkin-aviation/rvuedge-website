D0702 2d cephal radio image

D-Codes

Also known as: cephal, lateral ceph, cephalogram

Two-dimensional cephalometric radiographic image captured in lateral or anteroposterior projection for assessment of skeletal and dental relationships and facial proportions.

Clinical Context

Essential in orthodontic treatment planning and maxillofacial surgery for analyzing skeletal relationships, bite relationships, and planning corrective procedures.
