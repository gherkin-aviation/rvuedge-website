D1558 Remove fixed bilateral man

D-Codes

Also known as: mandibular space maintainer replacement, bilateral mandibular replacement

Removal of a bilateral fixed space maintainer appliance from the mandibular arch that has been cemented to tooth structure and replacement with a new bilateral mandibular maintainer.

Clinical Context

Performed when a previously placed mandibular space maintainer requires replacement due to damage, wear, or failure.
