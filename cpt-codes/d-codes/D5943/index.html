D5943 Proth mandib implant superior remv

D-Codes

Also known as: implant-supported denture, implant overdenture, implant mand prosth

Mandibular implant-supported prosthesis, removable complete. A removable complete denture supported by dental implants in the mandible.

Clinical Context

Used for complete mandibular edentulism. Implants provide superior retention and stability compared to conventional dentures.
