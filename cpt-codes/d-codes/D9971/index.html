D9971 Odontoplasty per tooth

D-Codes

Also known as: odontoplasty, tooth reshaping, enamel reshaping

Odontoplasty or reshaping of a single tooth surface to improve esthetics, correct morphology, or remove minor defects through selective grinding or shaping.

Clinical Context

Used for minor esthetic improvements such as correcting sharp edges, small chipping, asymmetry, or morphologic defects through selective enamel removal without restoration.
