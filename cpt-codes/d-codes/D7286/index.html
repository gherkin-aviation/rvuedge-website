D7286 Biopsy of oral tissue soft

D-Codes

Also known as: soft tissue biopsy, mucosal biopsy, gingival biopsy

Biopsy of soft oral tissue (mucosa, gingiva, etc.), including removal of tissue for histopathological examination.

Clinical Context

Used for biopsy of soft oral tissues to diagnose lesions, ulcerations, or pathological conditions. Common indications include suspected malignancy, chronic ulcers, or undiagnosed oral lesions.
