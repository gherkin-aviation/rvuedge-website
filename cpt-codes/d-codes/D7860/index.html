D7860 Tmj cutting into joint

D Codes (Dental)

Also known as: TMJ arthrotomy, joint incision/opening

In Plain Language

Clinical Context

Surgical opening into the temporomandibular joint space for diagnostic evaluation, irrigation, removal of debris, or access for other therapeutic procedures.

RVU Information
