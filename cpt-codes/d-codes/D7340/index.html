D7340 Vestibuloplasty ridge extens

D-Codes

Also known as: vestibuloplasty, vestibule deepening, ridge extension

Vestibuloplasty to deepen the vestibule (the space between teeth/ridge and cheeks/lips) through ridge extension procedures.

Clinical Context

Surgical procedure to deepen the oral vestibule for esthetic and functional improvement, often following alveolar bone resorption or to improve denture retention.
