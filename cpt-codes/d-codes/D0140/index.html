D0140 Limit oral evaluation problm focus

D-Codes

Also known as: problem-focused exam, limited exam, complaint-based evaluation

Limited oral evaluation focused on a specific problem area or chief complaint, performed when patient presents with a localized dental concern requiring focused examination.

Clinical Context

Used when a patient presents with a specific dental complaint or problem requiring targeted evaluation of affected area(s) without comprehensive oral assessment.
