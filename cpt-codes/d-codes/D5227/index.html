D5227 Immed max part denture

D-Codes

Also known as: immediate max partial, immed partial max, extraction max partial

Fabrication of an immediate maxillary partial denture constructed and placed at time of tooth extraction.

Clinical Context

Constructed preoperatively for insertion immediately following extraction; restores esthetics and function during healing phase in partial edentulism.
