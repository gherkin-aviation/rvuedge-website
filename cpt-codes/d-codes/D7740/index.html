D7740 Clsd reduct compd mandble fracture

D Codes (Dental)

Also known as: compound mandible fracture management, mandibular fracture conservative treatment

Closed reduction of a compound mandibular fracture (non-surgical management of complex lower jaw fracture).

In Plain Language

non-surgical treatment of complex broken lower jaw

Clinical Context

Non-surgical management of compound mandibular fractures using intermaxillary fixation and splinting when surgical fixation is not indicated.

RVU Information

CPT D7740 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

Dental codes (CDT codes) require documentation of the tooth number(s) or area treated, clinical findings, and the procedure performed. Pre-authorization may be required for major restorative, prosthodontic, and oral surgery procedures. Include radiographs when supporting medical necessity.
