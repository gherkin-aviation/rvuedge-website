D7311 Alveoloplasty with extract 1-3

D Codes (Dental)

Also known as: alveoloplasty 1-3 teeth, limited ridge contouring

Alveoloplasty performed with extraction of one to three teeth, including ridge contouring for bone regularization.

In Plain Language

bone reshaping when removing 1-3 teeth

Clinical Context

Limited alveoloplasty procedure concurrent with extraction of one to three teeth. Used for minor ridge irregularities and bone recontouring.

RVU Information

CPT D7311 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

Dental codes (CDT codes) require documentation of the tooth number(s) or area treated, clinical findings, and the procedure performed. Pre-authorization may be required for major restorative, prosthodontic, and oral surgery procedures. Include radiographs when supporting medical necessity.
