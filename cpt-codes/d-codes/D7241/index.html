D7241 Impact tooth rem bony with complicated

D-Codes

Also known as: complex impacted tooth removal with complications, complicated impaction extraction

Removal of impacted tooth with complete bone removal and additional complex complications requiring extended surgical time.

Clinical Context

Used for extraction of completely bony impacted teeth with significant surgical complexity, including complications such as proximity to inferior alveolar nerve, extensive root curvature, or other anatomical challenges.
