D7872 Tmj diagnostic arthroscopy

D Codes (Dental)

Also known as: TMJ arthroscopy, Diagnostic TMJ scope

Diagnostic arthroscopy of the temporomandibular joint to visualize internal structures and identify pathology without therapeutic intervention.

In Plain Language

Camera inspection of jaw joint; Looking inside the jaw joint with a scope

Clinical Context

Used for diagnostic evaluation of internal temporomandibular joint pathology including disc position, cartilage damage, and synovitis. Allows direct visualization before determining further treatment.

RVU Information

CPT D7872 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.
