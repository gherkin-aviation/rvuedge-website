D7881 Occ orthotic device adjust

D Codes (Dental)

Also known as: Splint adjustment, Orthotic modification

Adjustment and modification of an existing occlusal orthotic device to optimize fit, comfort, and therapeutic effect.

In Plain Language

Adjusting the bite guard; Fine-tuning the jaw joint device

Clinical Context

Routine follow-up care for occlusal orthotic appliances. Adjustments address pressure points, occlusal interferences, and optimize therapeutic benefit.

RVU Information

CPT D7881 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

Dental codes (CDT codes) require documentation of the tooth number(s) or area treated, clinical findings, and the procedure performed. Pre-authorization may be required for major restorative, prosthodontic, and oral surgery procedures. Include radiographs when supporting medical necessity.
