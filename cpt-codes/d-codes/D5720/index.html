D5720 Dentures rebase part maxill

D-Codes

Also known as: rebase upper partial denture, partial denture rebase maxillary, partial refitting

Rebase of a maxillary partial denture replacing the base tissue surface of resin or metal framework.

Clinical Context

Performed on maxillary partial dentures to restore fit and retention following alveolar ridge changes.
