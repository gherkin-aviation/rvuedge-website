D5959 Intraoral con def mod palat

D-Codes

Also known as: congenital palatal prosth, palatal modification device, congenital palatal aid

Intraoral congenital defect modification palatal prosthesis. A palatal device for modifying congenital oral defects.

Clinical Context

Used for cleft palate and related defects requiring palatal support or closure modification. Facilitates feeding and speech.
