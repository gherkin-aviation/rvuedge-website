D7880 Occlusal orthotic appliance

D-Codes

Also known as: Occlusal splint, Night guard, TMJ appliance

Fabrication and placement of an intraoral occlusal orthotic appliance to treat temporomandibular joint dysfunction and bruxism.

Clinical Context

Used for temporomandibular joint dysfunction, myofascial pain, muscle hyperactivity, and bruxism. Orthotic appliances stabilize the jaw position and reduce muscle tension.
