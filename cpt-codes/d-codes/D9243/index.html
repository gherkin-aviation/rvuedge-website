D-Codes +Add-on

Also known as: IV sedation additional time, Moderate sedation add-on

Administration of intravenous moderate sedation for each additional 15-minute increment beyond the initial period.

Clinical Context

Used for each additional 15-minute increment of intravenous moderate sedation beyond the initial code.
