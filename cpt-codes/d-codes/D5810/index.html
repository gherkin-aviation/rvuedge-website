D5810 Denture interm complete maxill

D-Codes

Also known as: interim upper complete denture, temporary maxillary denture, provisional denture

Interim complete maxillary denture constructed as temporary prosthesis pending definitive treatment or tissue healing.

Clinical Context

Fabricated to provide temporary function and esthetics while tissues remodel following extractions or awaiting definitive treatment.
