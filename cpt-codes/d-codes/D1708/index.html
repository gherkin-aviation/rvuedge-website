D1708 Pfizer vacc administration third dose

D-Codes

Also known as: Pfizer vaccine third dose, Pfizer booster

Administration of Pfizer-BioNTech messenger RNA vaccine against SARS-CoV-2 as a third dose injection in a dental setting by qualified dental personnel.

Clinical Context

Administered as third dose in primary series or as first booster dose depending on patient's vaccination status and timeline.
