D9210 Dental anesthesia without surgery

D-Codes

Also known as: Local anesthesia, Anesthesia for non-surgical procedure

Administration of local anesthesia or regional block anesthesia for dental procedures not involving surgical intervention.

Clinical Context

Used for local anesthesia administration for non-surgical dental procedures such as restorations or cleanings.
