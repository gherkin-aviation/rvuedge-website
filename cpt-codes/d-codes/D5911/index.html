D5911 Facial moulage sectional

D Codes (Dental)

Also known as: sectional facial moulage, partial face prosth, moulage prosthesis

Facial moulage prosthesis, sectional. A removable facial replacement device covering a portion of the face, typically for nasal, orbital, or partial facial defects.

In Plain Language

partial replacement of facial features; nose, ear, or eye replacement piece

Clinical Context

Indicated for patients with partial facial defects from congenital anomalies, trauma, or cancer resection. Moulage technique uses tissue molds for anatomic accuracy.

RVU Information

CPT D5911 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation
