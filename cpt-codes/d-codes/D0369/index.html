D0369 Max MRI capture & interprete

D-Codes

Also known as: maxillary MRI

Maxillary magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) capture and interpretation providing soft tissue and bone visualization of upper jaw structures.

Clinical Context

Used for soft tissue assessment of maxillary tumors, TMJ pathology, bone marrow changes, and evaluation of lesions not well-visualized on radiography.
