D4265 Bio mtrls to aid soft/os reg

D-Codes

Also known as: biologic factors, growth factors, regenerative biologics

Biologic materials applied to surgical sites to aid soft tissue and osseous regeneration in periodontal defect treatment.

Clinical Context

Employed in regenerative periodontal procedures to enhance tissue remodeling, promote cellular differentiation, and improve clinical attachment gain in intrabony defects.
