D1310 Nutri counsel-control caries

D-Codes

Also known as: nutrition counseling, dietary counseling, nutri counsel

Patient counseling and education regarding nutrition, diet modification, and oral health relationships to prevent caries and improve overall oral and systemic health outcomes.

Clinical Context

Indicated for all patients, especially those with high caries risk or active caries disease; focuses on reducing dietary sugars and establishing healthy eating patterns.
