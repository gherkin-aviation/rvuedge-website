D3240 Pulpal therapy posterior pri

D-Codes

Also known as: pulpal therapy posterior, primary posterior pulp, primary molar therapy

Pulpal therapy treatment of posterior primary tooth with irreversible pulpitis.

Clinical Context

Endodontic treatment of posterior primary tooth with irreversible pulpitis. Often involves pulpotomy technique. Maintains tooth function until natural shedding.
