D7920 Dental skin graft

D-Codes

Also known as: Skin graft, Mucosal graft

Harvest and placement of an autogenous skin graft to reconstruct oral and perioral soft tissue defects.

Clinical Context

Used for large oral or perioral soft tissue defects that cannot be closed primarily. Includes procurement of graft material and recipient site preparation.
