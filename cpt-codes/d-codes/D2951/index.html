D2951 Tooth pin retention

D-Codes +Add-on

Also known as: pin retention, retention pins, tooth pins

Placement of one or more tooth pins for retention in core building procedures.

Clinical Context

Technique to improve retention in core buildup procedures. Pins mechanically engage tooth structure. May be thread-forming or cemented. Used with extensive coronal loss.
