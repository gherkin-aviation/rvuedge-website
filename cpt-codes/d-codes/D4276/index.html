D4276 Con tissue w pedicle graft

D-Codes

Also known as: connective tissue pedicle graft, tunnel graft, laterally positioned graft

Connective tissue graft with pedicle technique combining elements of both pedicle and free graft procedures for tissue reconstruction.

Clinical Context

Used for recession coverage, root exposure treatment, or ridge augmentation in areas where preserved vascularization and graft stability are essential for optimal outcomes.
