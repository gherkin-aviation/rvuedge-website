D9612 Thera par drugs 2 or > administration

D-Codes

Also known as: multiple drug administration, multiple injections, combination drug therapy

Therapeutic medicinal drugs or medications administered through two or more separate administrations or injections in the oral cavity during a single visit.

Clinical Context

Used when patients receive multiple injections of therapeutic drugs such as antibiotics, anti-inflammatory agents, or other medicinal substances requiring separate administrations.
