D9912 Pre-visit patient screening

D-Codes

Also known as: pre-visit screening, telemedicine screening, pre-appointment assessment

Pre-visit patient screening, including virtual assessment or questionnaire completion prior to appointment to identify medical concerns, contraindications, or special needs.

Clinical Context

Used for pre-appointment virtual or written screening to identify medical history, medications, allergies, or conditions requiring special consideration before in-person dental treatment.
