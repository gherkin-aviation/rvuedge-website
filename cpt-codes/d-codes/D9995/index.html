D9995 Teledentistry real-time

D-Codes

Also known as: teledentistry, virtual consultation, video consultation

Teledentistry consultation or evaluation conducted in real-time using video conferencing technology to provide clinical assessment and treatment planning remotely.

Clinical Context

Used for real-time teledentistry consultations enabling remote clinical evaluation, diagnosis, treatment planning, and follow-up, particularly valuable for geographic barriers or initial assessment.
