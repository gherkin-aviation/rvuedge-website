D5710 Dentures rebase complete maxil

D-Codes

Also known as: rebase upper complete denture, complete denture rebase maxillary, denture refitting

Rebase of a complete maxillary denture replacing the base tissue surface while retaining denture teeth and framework.

Clinical Context

Performed to reestablish fit and retention following significant alveolar ridge resorption; preserves tooth esthetics and framework.
