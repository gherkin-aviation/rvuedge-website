D2955 Posterior removal

D-Codes

Also known as: post removal, dowel removal, post extraction

Removal of an existing post from a tooth, including post channel preparation.

Clinical Context

Removal of previously placed post for re-treatment, post failure, or tooth restoration revision. Requires care to avoid root damage during removal. May involve ultrasonic or mechanical removal.
