D5941 Resct proth mand part remov

D-Codes

Also known as: partial mandibular resect, mand partial resection, partial mand prosth

Resected mandibular prosthesis, partial removable. A removable denture for partial mandibular reconstruction after surgical resection.

Clinical Context

Placed after partial mandibular resection preserving residual anatomy. Provides support and restores functional anatomy.
