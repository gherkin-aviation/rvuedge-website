D6609 Onlay porc/crmc >=3 surfaces

D-Codes

Also known as: ceramic onlay, porcelain multi-surface onlay

Onlay restoration constructed from porcelain or ceramic materials on three or more tooth surfaces. Indirect restoration covering multiple surfaces.

Clinical Context

Used for large defects covering three or more surfaces where comprehensive restorations are needed.
