D7940 Reshaping bone orthognathic

D-Codes

Also known as: Orthognathic surgery, Jaw contouring, Bone reshaping

Surgical bone reshaping and contouring of the maxilla or mandible for orthognathic correction of severe dentofacial deformities.

Clinical Context

Used for severe skeletal malocclusion, facial asymmetry, and dentofacial deformities affecting function and esthetics. Major orthognathic procedure.
