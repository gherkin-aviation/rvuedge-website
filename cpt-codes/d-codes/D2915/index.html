D2915 Recement cast or prefab posterior

D-Codes

Also known as: recement post core, rebond dowel core, reattach post

Recement or re-bond a cast post and core or prefabricated post and core.

Clinical Context

Applied when post and core restoration has become loose. Critical for restoring endodontically treated teeth before crown placement.
