D9933 Clean & inspect rem dental man

D-Codes

Also known as: denture cleaning and inspection, mandibular denture check

Cleaning and inspection of a removable complete denture in the mandible (lower arch) to assess fit, function, and identify any damage requiring adjustment or repair.

Clinical Context

Used during denture maintenance appointments to professionally clean lower dentures, assess for wear or damage, verify proper fit, and identify any needed adjustments or relines.
