D7750 Open red complicated malar/zygma fracture

D-Codes

Also known as: zygomatic fracture open reduction, compound malar fracture surgery

Open reduction of a compound malar or zygomatic fracture (surgical repair of complex cheekbone fracture).

Clinical Context

Surgical treatment of complex/compound zygomatic or malar fractures requiring open reduction and fixation with plates or wires.
