D7943 Cutting ramus open with graft

D-Codes

Also known as: Open ramus osteotomy with graft, Ramus surgery with bone graft

Surgical osteotomy of the ramus via open approach with bone graft placement for mandibular repositioning.

Clinical Context

Used for mandibular deformities requiring bone graft augmentation. Open approach allows better visualization and graft placement.
