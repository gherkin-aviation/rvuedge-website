D0364 Cone beam CT capt & interpretation

D Codes (Dental)

Also known as: CBCT, cone beam CT, 3D x-ray

Cone beam computed tomography (CBCT) image capture and interpretation of volumetric data providing three-dimensional assessment of dental and maxillofacial structures.

In Plain Language

3D dental imaging

Clinical Context

Used for implant planning, orthognathic surgery assessment, complex fracture evaluation, and detailed bone and anatomic visualization.

RVU Information

CPT D0364 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

Dental codes (CDT codes) require documentation of the tooth number(s) or area treated, clinical findings, and the procedure performed. Pre-authorization may be required for major restorative, prosthodontic, and oral surgery procedures. Include radiographs when supporting medical necessity.
