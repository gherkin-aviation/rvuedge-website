D7472 Removal of torus palatinus

D Codes (Dental)

Also known as: torus palatinus removal, palatal torus removal

Removal of torus palatinus (benign bone growth on the hard palate midline).

In Plain Language

removal of palate bone bump

Clinical Context

Surgical removal of torus palatinus, a common benign bony exostosis on the palatal midline. Removal indicated for esthetic concerns, speech/swallowing difficulties, or denture construction.

RVU Information

CPT D7472 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.
