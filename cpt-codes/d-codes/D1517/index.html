D1517 Fixed bilateral space maint, man

D-Codes

Also known as: space maintainer, fixed bilateral maintainer, mandibular maintainer

Construction and cementation of a fixed bilateral space maintainer appliance extending across the mandibular arch to preserve space following premature loss of mandibular primary teeth.

Clinical Context

Indicated when bilateral mandibular space preservation is needed to maintain arch integrity and guide permanent tooth eruption.
