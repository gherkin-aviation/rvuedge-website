D5909 Max guid prothes with flange

D-Codes

Also known as: maxillary guided prosth, upper guided prosthesis

Unspecified maxillary guided prosthesis with flange. A removable upper jaw device that is supported by soft tissue and includes an extended border.

Clinical Context

Used for patients with maxillary bone loss requiring soft tissue support. Common after tumor resection or when dental implants are not viable.
