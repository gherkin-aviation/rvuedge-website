D8703 Replace broken retainer max

D-Codes

Also known as: Maxillary retainer replacement, Upper fixed retainer replacement

Replacement of an entire broken or non-functional maxillary bonded fixed retainer with new appliance.

Clinical Context

Used when maxillary fixed retainer cannot be repaired and requires complete replacement with new bonded retainer.
