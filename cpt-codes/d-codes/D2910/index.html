D2910 Recement inlay onlay or part

D Codes (Dental)

Also known as: recement inlay, rebond onlay, re-seat restoration

Recement or re-bond an existing inlay, onlay, or partial indirect restoration.

In Plain Language

glue indirect restoration back on tooth; replace lost crown cement

Clinical Context

Used when indirect restoration has become loose or lost retention. Involves cleaning tooth surface, applying new adhesive/cement, and repositioning restoration.

RVU Information

CPT D2910 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

Dental codes (CDT codes) require documentation of the tooth number(s) or area treated, clinical findings, and the procedure performed. Pre-authorization may be required for major restorative, prosthodontic, and oral surgery procedures. Include radiographs when supporting medical necessity.
