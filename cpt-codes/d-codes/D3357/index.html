D3357 Pulpal regeneration complete

D Codes (Dental)

Also known as: pulp regeneration complete, regenerative endodontics complete, regrow pulp completion

Completion of pulpal regeneration therapy with final obturation or sealing.

In Plain Language

finish nerve regeneration; seal regenerated pulp

Clinical Context

Final appointment when regenerative protocol is complete. Tooth is sealed with biocompatible material to prevent reinfection and maintain newly formed pulp-like tissue.

RVU Information

CPT D3357 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

Dental codes (CDT codes) require documentation of the tooth number(s) or area treated, clinical findings, and the procedure performed. Pre-authorization may be required for major restorative, prosthodontic, and oral surgery procedures. Include radiographs when supporting medical necessity.
