D4263 Bone replce graft first site

D-Codes

Also known as: bone graft, bone replacement graft, first site graft

Bone replacement graft utilizing allograft, xenograft, or synthetic bone material at the first surgical site to augment bone volume.

Clinical Context

Indicated for reconstruction of osseous defects from periodontal disease, implant site development, or alveolar ridge augmentation where bone regeneration and volumetric gain are treatment goals.
