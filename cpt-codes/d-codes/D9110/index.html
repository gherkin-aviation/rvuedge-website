D9110 Palliative treatment dental pain

D-Codes

Also known as: Emergency pain relief, Temporary pain management, Palliative care

Palliative or temporary treatment to relieve acute dental pain, providing immediate symptomatic relief.

Clinical Context

Used for temporary emergency relief of acute dental pain when definitive treatment cannot be performed immediately.
