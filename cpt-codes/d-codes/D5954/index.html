D5954 Superimposed prosthesis

D-Codes

Also known as: superimposed prosth, overlay prosthesis, surface appliance

Superimposed prosthesis. An intraoral device placed over teeth or abutments to modify contour, color, or function.

Clinical Context

Used to modify tooth anatomy, improve esthetics, or protect compromised teeth. Conserves tooth structure.
