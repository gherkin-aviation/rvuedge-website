D9311 Consultation with medial hlth care prof

D-Codes

Also known as: medical consultation, physician consult

Consultation with a medical health care professional outside of the dental practice to address a patient's medical condition affecting dental treatment.

Clinical Context

Used when a dentist needs to coordinate care with a patient's physician for medically complex cases, including those with systemic conditions affecting oral health or treatment considerations.
