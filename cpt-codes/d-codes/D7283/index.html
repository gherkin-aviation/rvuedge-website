D7283 Place device impacted tooth

D Codes (Dental)

Also known as: impacted tooth bracket placement, orthodontic appliance for impacted tooth

Placement of an orthodontic device or traction apparatus on an impacted tooth to facilitate guided eruption or movement.

In Plain Language

placing braces on buried tooth; bracket placement on impacted tooth

Clinical Context

Used for placement of orthodontic appliances, brackets, or traction devices on impacted teeth to guide eruption or movement into proper position. Common indications include impacted canines or molars requiring orthodontic traction.

RVU Information

CPT D7283 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

Dental codes (CDT codes) require documentation of the tooth number(s) or area treated, clinical findings, and the procedure performed. Pre-authorization may be required for major restorative, prosthodontic, and oral surgery procedures. Include radiographs when supporting medical necessity.
