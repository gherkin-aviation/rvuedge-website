D3470 Intentional replantation

D-Codes

Also known as: intentional replant, replantation, tooth reimplant

Intentional replantation of previously extracted tooth with root canal treatment.

Clinical Context

Surgical extraction and immediate reimplantation of tooth for root canal treatment or lesion removal. Preserves natural tooth when access difficult or impossible.
