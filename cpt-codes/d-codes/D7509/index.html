D7509 Marsupialization odon cyst

D Codes (Dental)

Also known as: cyst marsupialization, cyst opening

Marsupialization of an odontogenic cyst (surgical creation of opening to allow cyst drainage and reduce size).

In Plain Language

creating opening in cyst to allow drainage

Clinical Context

Surgical creation of opening into odontogenic cyst to allow internal drainage and decompression, reducing cyst size over time. Used for large cysts when immediate complete removal is not feasible.

RVU Information

CPT D7509 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation
