D1320 Tobacco counseling

D-Codes

Also known as: tobacco counseling, smoking cessation counseling

Patient counseling and education regarding tobacco use, smoking cessation, and oral health consequences of tobacco products including cancer risk and periodontal disease.

Clinical Context

Recommended for all tobacco users as part of preventive care; supports cessation efforts and educates patients on increased risk for oral cancer and periodontal disease.
