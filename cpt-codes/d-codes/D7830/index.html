D7830 Tmj manipulation under anest

D-Codes

Also known as: TMJ manipulation under anesthesia, anesthetized jaw reduction

Temporomandibular joint manipulation under anesthesia (reduction of joint dislocation under sedation/anesthesia).

Clinical Context

Manipulation and reduction of TMJ dislocation performed under conscious sedation or general anesthesia when difficult or when patient cooperation is limited.
