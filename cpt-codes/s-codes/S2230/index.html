S2230 Implant semi-imp hear

S-Codes

Also known as: semi-implantable hearing aid, bone-conduction implant, implantable hearing device

Surgical implantation of a semi-implantable or partially implantable hearing device that amplifies sound and transmits vibrations to the inner ear.

Clinical Context

Used in otolaryngology for patients with conductive or mixed hearing loss who are candidates for implantable hearing device therapy.
