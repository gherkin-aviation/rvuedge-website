S9088 Services provided in urgent

S-Codes

Also known as: urgent care services, urgent center treatment

Services provided in urgent care settings encompasses diagnostic, therapeutic, and procedural services performed at an urgent care facility for acute patient care needs.

Clinical Context

Includes wound care, splinting, x-ray interpretation, laceration repair, and basic procedures in urgent care settings for patients with time-sensitive but non-emergent conditions.
