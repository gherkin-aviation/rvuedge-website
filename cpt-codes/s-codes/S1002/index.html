S1002 Custom item

S-Codes

Also known as: custom item, custom-made

Custom item or supply fabrication. Patient-specific custom-made device or supply.

Clinical Context

Used for fabrication of patient-specific orthoses, prostheses, or supplies not available as standard products. Examples: custom AFOS, custom hearing aids.
