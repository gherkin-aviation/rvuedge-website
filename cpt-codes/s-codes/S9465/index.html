S9465 Diabetic management program,

S-Codes

Also known as: diabetes management program, diabetes education, diabetes clinic service

Diabetes management program or specialized diabetes education service provided in an ambulatory or community health setting.

Clinical Context

Provides comprehensive diabetes care education including glucose monitoring, medication management, nutrition, exercise, and prevention of acute and chronic complications.
