S9538 Hit blood products diem

S-Codes

Also known as: home blood product infusion, home transfusion, home blood component therapy

Home infusion therapy administration of blood products or blood components on a per-diem basis.

Clinical Context

Provides home administration of packed red blood cells, fresh frozen plasma, platelets, albumin, or other blood products for anemia, coagulopathy, or other blood disorders.
