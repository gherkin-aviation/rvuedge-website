S5100 Adult daycare services 15min

S-Codes

Also known as: adult daycare, day center services

Adult day care center services provided in 15-minute increments, including supervision, meals, and activities for adults requiring daytime supervision and social engagement.

Clinical Context

Billed for seniors and disabled adults attending community day centers for social, recreational, and therapeutic programming during daytime hours.
