S9214 Hm gest dm per diem

S Codes

Also known as: gestational diabetes home care, GDM management

Home gestational diabetes management per diem includes blood glucose monitoring, dietary management, medication adjustment, and fetal surveillance for pregnant patients with gestational diabetes mellitus.

In Plain Language

home diabetes monitoring during pregnancy

Clinical Context

Used for pregnant women diagnosed with gestational diabetes. Home glucose monitoring with dietary and medication adjustment optimizes maternal and fetal outcomes while avoiding complications.

RVU Information

CPT S9214 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

S-codes are used by private payers (not Medicare) for services without a standard CPT or HCPCS code. Coverage varies significantly between insurers. Verify payer acceptance before billing and document medical necessity thoroughly.
