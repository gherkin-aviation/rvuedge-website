S9126 Hospice care, in the home, p

S Codes

Also known as: hospice care, end-of-life care, palliative care

Hospice care in the home per diem provides palliative and end-of-life care including pain management, symptom control, and psychological support for terminally ill patients at home.

In Plain Language

comfort care at home for dying patients

Clinical Context

Used for patients with terminal illness and less than six months to live. Focuses on comfort, dignity, symptom management, and family support throughout end-of-life process.

RVU Information

CPT S9126 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

S-codes are used by private payers (not Medicare) for services without a standard CPT or HCPCS code. Coverage varies significantly between insurers. Verify payer acceptance before billing and document medical necessity thoroughly.
