S4031 Sperm procure subs visit

S Codes

Also known as: sperm procurement follow-up, fertility male follow-up

Subsequent clinical evaluation and follow-up visit for sperm collection, processing, and monitoring of fertility parameters.

In Plain Language

follow-up visits for sperm collection; repeat male fertility visits

Clinical Context

Used in reproductive urology and fertility clinics for follow-up evaluation and sperm collection in male patients.

RVU Information

CPT S4031 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

S-codes are used by private payers (not Medicare) for services without a standard CPT or HCPCS code. Coverage varies significantly between insurers. Verify payer acceptance before billing and document medical necessity thoroughly.
