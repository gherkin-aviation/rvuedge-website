S5110 Family homecare training 15m

S Codes

Also known as: family training, caregiver education

Family or caregiver training services provided in home setting in 15-minute increments, including instruction on patient care, medication administration, and equipment use.

In Plain Language

Family teaching; Helper instruction

Clinical Context

Used for educating family members or non-professional caregivers on care techniques, medical equipment, medication administration, or emergency procedures.

RVU Information

CPT S5110 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

S-codes are used by private payers (not Medicare) for services without a standard CPT or HCPCS code. Coverage varies significantly between insurers. Verify payer acceptance before billing and document medical necessity thoroughly.
