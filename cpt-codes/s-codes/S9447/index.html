S9447 Infant safety class

S-Codes

Also known as: infant safety education, baby safety class

Infant safety class provides education on accident prevention, safe sleep environments, car safety, choking prevention, and emergency response for parents of infants.

Clinical Context

Used for parents of newborns and infants. Covers sudden infant death syndrome (SIDS) prevention, safe sleep, water safety, and choking prevention.
