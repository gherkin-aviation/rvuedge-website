S1034 Artery pancreas system

S Codes

Also known as: artificial pancreas, closed loop, automated insulin pump

Artificial pancreas system. Integrated continuous glucose monitoring and insulin pump automated glucose control device.

In Plain Language

automatic insulin system; smart insulin pump

Clinical Context

Advanced diabetes technology combining continuous glucose sensor, algorithm, and insulin pump for semi-closed loop glucose control. Significantly improves glycemic control and reduces hypoglycemia.

RVU Information

CPT S1034 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

S-codes are used by private payers (not Medicare) for services without a standard CPT or HCPCS code. Coverage varies significantly between insurers. Verify payer acceptance before billing and document medical necessity thoroughly.
