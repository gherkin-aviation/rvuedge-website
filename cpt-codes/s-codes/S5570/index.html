S5570 Insulin dispos pen 1.5 milliliters

S-Codes

Also known as: disposable insulin pen, prefilled pen

Disposable insulin pen pre-filled with insulin in 1.5 milliliter capacity, single-use for insulin delivery without refilling.

Clinical Context

Supplied for convenient single-use insulin delivery to Home Infusion Therapy or home diabetes patients requiring premeasured dosing.
