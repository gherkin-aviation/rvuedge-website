S0255 Hospice refer visit nonmd

S Codes

Also known as: hospice referral, hospice intake

Hospice referral visit by non-physician. Initial assessment and discussion of hospice care options conducted by nurse or social worker.

In Plain Language

hospice introduction; end-of-life care discussion

Clinical Context

Non-physician evaluation of potential hospice candidates, discussion of palliative care benefits, advance directives, and family education. Determines appropriateness for hospice enrollment.

RVU Information

CPT S0255 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

S-codes are used by private payers (not Medicare) for services without a standard CPT or HCPCS code. Coverage varies significantly between insurers. Verify payer acceptance before billing and document medical necessity thoroughly.
