S8130 Interferential stim 2 chan

S Codes

Also known as: interferential stimulation, IF therapy

Interferential electrical stimulation device with 2-channel capacity for pain management and muscle stimulation in physical therapy.

In Plain Language

Electrical pain relief; Muscle stimulation

Clinical Context

Used in rehabilitation settings for pain relief, muscle strengthening, and tissue healing in musculoskeletal conditions.

RVU Information

CPT S8130 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

S-codes are used by private payers (not Medicare) for services without a standard CPT or HCPCS code. Coverage varies significantly between insurers. Verify payer acceptance before billing and document medical necessity thoroughly.
