S0108 Mercaptopurine 50 milligrams

S-Codes

Also known as: 6-MP, mercaptopurine, Purinethol

Mercaptopurine oral medication at 50 milligrams, a purine analogue antimetabolite.

Clinical Context

Used for acute lymphoblastic leukemia maintenance therapy and certain autoimmune conditions. Inhibits purine nucleotide synthesis.
