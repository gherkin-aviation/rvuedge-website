S9454 Stress management class

S Codes

Also known as: stress management class, stress reduction program, relaxation training

Stress management education or behavioral instruction provided by a licensed mental health professional or health educator in an ambulatory or community setting.

In Plain Language

stress relief class; relaxation program; stress reduction training

Clinical Context

Used for anxiety disorders, hypertension management, and chronic pain conditions. Promotes mental health and overall wellbeing through behavioral coping strategies.

RVU Information

CPT S9454 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

S-codes are used by private payers (not Medicare) for services without a standard CPT or HCPCS code. Coverage varies significantly between insurers. Verify payer acceptance before billing and document medical necessity thoroughly.
