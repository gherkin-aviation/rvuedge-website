S0270 Home std case rate 30 days

S-Codes

Also known as: home health case rate, HH case management

Home health services case rate, 30 days. Per diem payment for full range of home care including nursing, therapy, and aide services.

Clinical Context

Used in home-based care programs where comprehensive nursing, therapy, and personal care bundled at fixed 30-day rate for patients recovering from illness or managing chronic conditions.
