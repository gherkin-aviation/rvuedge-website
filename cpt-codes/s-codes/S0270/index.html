S0270 Home std case rate 30 days

S Codes

Also known as: home health case rate, HH case management

Home health services case rate, 30 days. Per diem payment for full range of home care including nursing, therapy, and aide services.

In Plain Language

home nursing care; home health services

Clinical Context

Used in home-based care programs where comprehensive nursing, therapy, and personal care bundled at fixed 30-day rate for patients recovering from illness or managing chronic conditions.

RVU Information

CPT S0270 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

S-codes are used by private payers (not Medicare) for services without a standard CPT or HCPCS code. Coverage varies significantly between insurers. Verify payer acceptance before billing and document medical necessity thoroughly.
