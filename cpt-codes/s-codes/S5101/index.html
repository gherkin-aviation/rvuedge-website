S5101 Adult day care per half day

S-Codes

Also known as: half-day adult daycare, adult day center half-day

Adult day care center services provided on a per half-day basis, including supervision, therapeutic activities, and meals for a 4-hour period.

Clinical Context

Used when adult day center services are provided for a half-day (typically 4 hours) rather than full-day attendance.
