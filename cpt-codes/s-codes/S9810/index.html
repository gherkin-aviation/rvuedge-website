S9810 Ht pharmaceutical per hour

S Codes

Also known as: home infusion pharmacist, home IV pharmacy services, infusion pharmacist consultation

Home infusion therapy pharmaceutical services or pharmacist consultation services on an hourly basis.

In Plain Language

home medication management; home pharmacy consultation

Clinical Context

Provides pharmaceutical oversight, medication management, drug interaction monitoring, and patient education for home infusion therapy patients.

RVU Information

CPT S9810 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

S-codes are used by private payers (not Medicare) for services without a standard CPT or HCPCS code. Coverage varies significantly between insurers. Verify payer acceptance before billing and document medical necessity thoroughly.
