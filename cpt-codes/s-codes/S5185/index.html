S5185 Medial reminder serv per month

S-Codes

Also known as: medication reminder, med adherence service

Medication reminder service providing telephonic or in-person reminders and adherence support for medication management per month.

Clinical Context

Used for patients at risk for medication non-adherence needing regular reminders and monitoring to ensure appropriate medication-taking behavior.
