S0354 Cancer treatment plan change

S Codes

Also known as: treatment plan change, plan revision

Cancer treatment plan revision. Modification of established treatment plan due to response assessment, toxicity, or clinical change.

In Plain Language

change in cancer treatment; treatment adjustment

Clinical Context

Documented change in oncologic treatment strategy based on response imaging, clinical progression, patient tolerance, or new diagnostic information. Common between treatment phases.

RVU Information

CPT S0354 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

S-codes are used by private payers (not Medicare) for services without a standard CPT or HCPCS code. Coverage varies significantly between insurers. Verify payer acceptance before billing and document medical necessity thoroughly.
