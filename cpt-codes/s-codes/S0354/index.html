S0354 Cancer treatment plan change

S-Codes

Also known as: treatment plan change, plan revision

Cancer treatment plan revision. Modification of established treatment plan due to response assessment, toxicity, or clinical change.

Clinical Context

Documented change in oncologic treatment strategy based on response imaging, clinical progression, patient tolerance, or new diagnostic information. Common between treatment phases.
