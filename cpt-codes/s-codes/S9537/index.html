S9537 Ht hem horm injection diem

S Codes

Also known as: home hormone infusion, home growth hormone, home injectable hormone

Home infusion therapy administration of hematopoietic or hormonal injection on a per-diem basis with nursing and pharmaceutical support.

In Plain Language

home hormone injection therapy; home growth hormone treatment; home injectable therapy

Clinical Context

Provides home administration of growth hormone, gonadotropins, erythropoiesis-stimulating agents, and other hematopoietic or hormonal agents.

RVU Information

CPT S9537 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

S-codes are used by private payers (not Medicare) for services without a standard CPT or HCPCS code. Coverage varies significantly between insurers. Verify payer acceptance before billing and document medical necessity thoroughly.
