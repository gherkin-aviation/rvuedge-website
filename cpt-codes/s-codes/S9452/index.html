S9452 Nutrition class

S-Codes

Also known as: nutrition class, diet education, dietary counseling

Nutrition education or dietary counseling group class or individual instruction provided by a dietitian or licensed nutritionist in an ambulatory setting.

Clinical Context

Used for management of metabolic disorders, diabetes, cardiovascular disease, and obesity. Supports therapeutic dietary modifications and lifestyle changes.
