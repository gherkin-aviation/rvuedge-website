S2061 Donor lobectomy (lung)

S-Codes

Also known as: donor lobectomy, living donor lung

Donor lobectomy of lung. Surgical removal of lung lobe from living donor for transplantation.

Clinical Context

Living donor procedure removing one or more lung lobes under video-assisted or open approach with minimal morbidity. Lobes transplanted to recipient.
