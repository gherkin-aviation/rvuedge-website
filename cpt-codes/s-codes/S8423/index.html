S8423 Custom grad sleeve heavy

S Codes

Also known as: heavy compression sleeve, custom strong sleeve

Custom gradient compression sleeve with heavy pressure (high compression) for severe lymphedema management.

In Plain Language

Strong pressure sleeve; Custom high compression

Clinical Context

Prescribed for severe lymphedema requiring high-pressure compression providing individually fitted solution.

RVU Information

CPT S8423 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

S-codes are used by private payers (not Medicare) for services without a standard CPT or HCPCS code. Coverage varies significantly between insurers. Verify payer acceptance before billing and document medical necessity thoroughly.
