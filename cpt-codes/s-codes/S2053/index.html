S2053 Transplantation of small int

S-Codes

Also known as: intestinal transplant, small bowel transplant

Transplantation of small intestine. Surgical grafting of donor small intestine for intestinal failure or short bowel syndrome.

Clinical Context

Complex transplantation procedure for patients with short bowel syndrome or intestinal failure dependent on total parenteral nutrition. Includes procurement and implantation.
