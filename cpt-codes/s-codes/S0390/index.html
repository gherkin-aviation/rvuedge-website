S0390 Rout foot care per visit

S-Codes

Also known as: foot care, podiatry care

Routine foot care per visit. Preventive or therapeutic foot care including nail care, callus removal, and skin assessment.

Clinical Context

Performed by podiatrist or nurse for diabetic patients with neuropathy, circulatory disease, or mobility limitations unable to perform self-care. Prevents ulceration and infection.
