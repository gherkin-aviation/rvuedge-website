S0315 Disease management program

S-Codes

Also known as: disease management program, DMP

Disease management program enrollment and initial assessment. Entry into structured chronic disease management program with baseline evaluation.

Clinical Context

Structured program for specific chronic conditions like congestive heart failure, diabetes, asthma, chronic obstructive pulmonary disease. Initial assessment establishes baseline and treatment goals.
