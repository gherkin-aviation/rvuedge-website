S9449 Weight management class

S-Codes

Also known as: weight loss class, obesity management class

Weight management group class or individual instruction provided by a licensed health professional in an ambulatory or community setting.

Clinical Context

Used for preventive health and chronic disease management in patients with obesity or weight-related conditions. Typically part of comprehensive wellness and lifestyle modification programs.
