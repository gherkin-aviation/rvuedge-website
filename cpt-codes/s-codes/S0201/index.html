S0201 Partial hospitalization serv

S-Codes

Also known as: PHP, day treatment, partial hosp

Partial hospitalization service. Structured daytime mental health treatment program for patients with psychiatric conditions, excluding inpatient stay.

Clinical Context

Used for patients with depression, anxiety, bipolar disorder, or other psychiatric conditions requiring intensive treatment but not requiring 24-hour care. Bridge between outpatient and inpatient.
