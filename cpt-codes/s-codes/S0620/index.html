S0620 Routine ophthalmological exa

Also known as: eye exam, eye doctor visit

Routine ophthalmological examination. Standard eye examination including visual acuity and ocular health assessment.

Clinical Context

Comprehensive ocular evaluation including visual acuity, refraction, intraocular pressure, dilated exam, and assessment of anterior and posterior segments.
