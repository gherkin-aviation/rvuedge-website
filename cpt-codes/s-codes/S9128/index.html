S9128 Speech therapy, in the home,

S-Codes

Also known as: speech therapy, speech-language pathology, SLP services

Speech therapy in the home per diem provides speech-language pathology services including evaluation and treatment of communication, swallowing, voice, and cognitive-linguistic disorders.

Clinical Context

Used for stroke, brain injury, Parkinson's disease, voice disorders, swallowing difficulties, and cognitive communication impairment. Improves communication function and quality of life.
