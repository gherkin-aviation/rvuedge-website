S0182 Procarbazine, oral

S Codes

Also known as: Matulane, procarbazine, alkylating agent

Procarbazine oral medication, an alkylating agent for Hodgkin's lymphoma and other malignancies.

In Plain Language

cancer medicine; lymphoma treatment; chemotherapy

Clinical Context

Used as part of MOPP chemotherapy for Hodgkin's lymphoma and other malignancies. Monoamine oxidase inhibitor with anticancer properties.

RVU Information

CPT S0182 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

S-codes are used by private payers (not Medicare) for services without a standard CPT or HCPCS code. Coverage varies significantly between insurers. Verify payer acceptance before billing and document medical necessity thoroughly.
