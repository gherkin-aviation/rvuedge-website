S0353 Cancer treatmentplan initial

S-Codes

Also known as: cancer treatment plan, oncology plan

Cancer treatment plan, initial development. Creation of comprehensive oncologic treatment strategy including modality selection and sequencing.

Clinical Context

Multidisciplinary planning for newly diagnosed cancer including staging, prognostic assessment, treatment option discussion, and documented treatment plan selecting surgery, radiation, chemotherapy, or combination.
