S2340 Chemodenervation of abductor

S-Codes

Also known as: hip adductor chemodenervation, botulinum toxin hip injection, chemo-denerv hip

Chemical denervation of the abductor muscles of the hip using botulinum toxin injection to reduce muscle overactivity and improve hip function.

Clinical Context

Used in neurology and physical medicine for patients with hip adductor spasticity from cerebral palsy, stroke, or spinal cord injury to improve gait and function.
