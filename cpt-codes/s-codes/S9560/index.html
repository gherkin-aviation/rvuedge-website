S9560 Ht injection hormone diem

S-Codes

Also known as: home hormone infusion, home endocrine therapy, home hormone injection

Home infusion therapy administration of hormone injection on a per-diem basis with nursing and pharmaceutical support.

Clinical Context

Provides home administration of various hormonal agents including estrogen, testosterone, thyroid hormone, corticosteroids, and other endocrine medications.
