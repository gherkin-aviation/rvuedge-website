S8451 Splint wrist or ankle

S-Codes

Also known as: wrist splint, ankle splint

Wrist or ankle immobilization splint providing support and limitation of motion for fracture, sprain, or ligament injury.

Clinical Context

Provided for acute wrist or ankle injuries requiring immobilization and support during healing phase.
