S9977 Meals per diem

S-Codes

Also known as: patient meals, medical meal service, per diem meals

Meal services or dietary provisions provided on a per-diem basis for patients receiving medical treatment away from home.

Clinical Context

Provides meal support for patients receiving treatment or undergoing procedures at facilities distant from their home.
