S5571 Insulin dispos pen 3 milliliters

S Codes

Also known as: disposable insulin pen, prefilled pen

Disposable insulin pen pre-filled with insulin in 3 milliliter capacity, single-use for insulin delivery without refilling.

In Plain Language

Ready-to-use insulin pen; One-time pen

Clinical Context

Supplied for convenient single-use insulin delivery to Home Infusion Therapy or home diabetes patients with larger prefilled capacity.

RVU Information

CPT S5571 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

S-codes are used by private payers (not Medicare) for services without a standard CPT or HCPCS code. Coverage varies significantly between insurers. Verify payer acceptance before billing and document medical necessity thoroughly.
