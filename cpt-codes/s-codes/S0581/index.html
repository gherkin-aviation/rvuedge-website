S0581 Nonstnd lens

S Codes

Also known as: custom lens, specialty lens

Non-standard lens. Specialty lens not meeting standard optical specifications or materials.

In Plain Language

special eye lens; custom prescription

Clinical Context

High-index materials, prism corrections, or specialty coatings for unusual refractive errors, ocular pathology, or complex visual requirements.

RVU Information

CPT S0581 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

S-codes are used by private payers (not Medicare) for services without a standard CPT or HCPCS code. Coverage varies significantly between insurers. Verify payer acceptance before billing and document medical necessity thoroughly.
