S3800 Genetic testing als

S-Codes

Also known as: ALS genetic test, amyotrophic lateral sclerosis testing

Comprehensive genetic testing for mutations associated with Amyotrophic Lateral Sclerosis including SOD1, FUS, and TARDBP genes.

Clinical Context

Used in neurology for patients with suspected or confirmed Amyotrophic Lateral Sclerosis to identify genetic mutations for prognosis and family counseling.
