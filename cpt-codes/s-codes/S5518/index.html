S5518 Hit catheter repair kit

S Codes

Also known as: catheter repair kit, line repair supplies

Home Infusion Therapy catheter repair kit containing supplies for repairing damaged or cracked infusion catheters to extend usability.

In Plain Language

Line repair kit; Catheter fixing supplies

Clinical Context

Provided to Home Infusion Therapy patients to repair damaged catheters extending device life and avoiding premature replacement.

RVU Information

CPT S5518 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

S-codes are used by private payers (not Medicare) for services without a standard CPT or HCPCS code. Coverage varies significantly between insurers. Verify payer acceptance before billing and document medical necessity thoroughly.
