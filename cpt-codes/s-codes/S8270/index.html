S8270 Enuresis alarm

S-Codes

Also known as: bedwetting alarm, enuresis alarm

Enuresis alarm device emitting audible signal when wetness is detected, conditioning bedwetting response in children with nocturnal enuresis.

Clinical Context

Provided to children with primary nocturnal enuresis for behavioral conditioning and alarm awakening to decrease bed-wetting episodes.
