S0341 Lifestyle mod 2 or 3 stage

S Codes

Also known as: lifestyle modification, phase 2-3

Lifestyle modification program, second or third stage. Continuation of structured lifestyle intervention after initial phase.

In Plain Language

continuing health program; sustained behavior change

Clinical Context

Maintenance phase of behavioral intervention with less frequent contact but continued education and monitoring. Reinforces healthy behaviors and prevents relapse.

RVU Information

CPT S0341 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

S-codes are used by private payers (not Medicare) for services without a standard CPT or HCPCS code. Coverage varies significantly between insurers. Verify payer acceptance before billing and document medical necessity thoroughly.
