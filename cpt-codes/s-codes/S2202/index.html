S2202 Echosclerotherapy

S Codes

Also known as: endoscopic ultrasound-guided sclerotherapy, EUS sclerotherapy

Endoscopically-guided sclerotherapy for esophageal or gastric varices using ultrasound or other visualization techniques to ensure accurate injection.

In Plain Language

treating bleeding veins in esophagus with ultrasound guidance; injection therapy for varices

Clinical Context

Used in gastroenterology for patients with portal hypertension and esophageal/gastric varices to achieve hemostasis and prevent variceal bleeding.

RVU Information

CPT S2202 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

S-codes are used by private payers (not Medicare) for services without a standard CPT or HCPCS code. Coverage varies significantly between insurers. Verify payer acceptance before billing and document medical necessity thoroughly.
