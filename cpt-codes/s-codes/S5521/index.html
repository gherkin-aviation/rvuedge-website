S5521 Hit midline catheter insert kit

S Codes

Also known as: midline insertion kit, midline catheter kit

Home Infusion Therapy midline catheter insertion kit containing supplies and materials for placement of midline venous access devices.

In Plain Language

Midline catheter kit; Intermediate line insertion

Clinical Context

Supplied for placement of midline catheters in Home Infusion Therapy for intermediate-term access (longer than peripheral but less invasive than central).

RVU Information

CPT S5521 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

S-codes are used by private payers (not Medicare) for services without a standard CPT or HCPCS code. Coverage varies significantly between insurers. Verify payer acceptance before billing and document medical necessity thoroughly.
