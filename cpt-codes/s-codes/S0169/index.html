S0169 Calcitrol

S Codes

Also known as: Rocaltrol, calcitriol, active vitamin D

Calcitriol (active vitamin D3) oral medication for calcium metabolism disorders.

In Plain Language

calcium regulation medication; bone health supplement; kidney disease treatment

Clinical Context

Used for hypocalcemia, secondary hyperparathyroidism in renal disease, and hypoparathyroidism. Active form of vitamin D.

RVU Information

CPT S0169 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

S-codes are used by private payers (not Medicare) for services without a standard CPT or HCPCS code. Coverage varies significantly between insurers. Verify payer acceptance before billing and document medical necessity thoroughly.
