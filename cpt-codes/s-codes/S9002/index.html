S9002 Intra-vag motion sens biofk

S Codes

Also known as: pelvic floor biofeedback, vaginal biofeedback, Kegel biofeedback

Intravaginal motion sensing biofeedback training uses sensors placed in the vagina to provide feedback for pelvic floor muscle strengthening and urinary incontinence management.

In Plain Language

pelvic muscle training with feedback device

Clinical Context

Used in treatment of stress urinary incontinence, overactive bladder, and pelvic floor dysfunction. Biofeedback helps patients identify and strengthen pelvic floor muscles.

RVU Information

CPT S9002 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

S-codes are used by private payers (not Medicare) for services without a standard CPT or HCPCS code. Coverage varies significantly between insurers. Verify payer acceptance before billing and document medical necessity thoroughly.
