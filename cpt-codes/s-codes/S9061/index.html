S9061 Medical supplies and equipme

S-Codes

Also known as: DME, medical supplies, assistive equipment

Medical supplies and equipment encompasses durable medical equipment, disposable supplies, and assistive devices required for patient care and functional independence at home or facility.

Clinical Context

Covers wheelchairs, walkers, bed frames, oxygen equipment, catheters, ostomy supplies, and other equipment necessary for patient function and safety.
