S5565 Insulin cartridge 150 u

S Codes

Also known as: insulin cartridge, insulin refill

Insulin cartridge containing 150 units for use in reusable insulin pens or pumps for diabetes management.

In Plain Language

Insulin refill; Pen cartridge

Clinical Context

Supplied as refill cartridge for reusable insulin delivery devices in Home Infusion Therapy or home diabetes management.

RVU Information

CPT S5565 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

S-codes are used by private payers (not Medicare) for services without a standard CPT or HCPCS code. Coverage varies significantly between insurers. Verify payer acceptance before billing and document medical necessity thoroughly.
