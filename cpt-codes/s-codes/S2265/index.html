S2265 Induced abortion 25-28 wks

S Codes

Also known as: therapeutic abortion, abortion 25-28 weeks

Surgical termination of pregnancy between 25 and 28 weeks gestation using appropriate surgical technique to achieve complete fetal and placental evacuation.

In Plain Language

ending pregnancy between 25-28 weeks; late-term pregnancy termination

Clinical Context

Used in obstetrics and maternal-fetal medicine for patients with indicated pregnancy termination during the late second trimester due to severe fetal anomalies or maternal health complications.

RVU Information

CPT S2265 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

S-codes are used by private payers (not Medicare) for services without a standard CPT or HCPCS code. Coverage varies significantly between insurers. Verify payer acceptance before billing and document medical necessity thoroughly.
