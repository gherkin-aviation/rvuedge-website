S8425 Custom grad glove medial

S Codes

Also known as: compression glove, custom glove

Custom gradient compression glove with medium pressure for hand lymphedema management following surgery or disease.

In Plain Language

Pressure glove; Custom hand sleeve

Clinical Context

Prescribed for hand lymphedema or swelling requiring medium-pressure compression with individual fitting.

RVU Information

CPT S8425 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

S-codes are used by private payers (not Medicare) for services without a standard CPT or HCPCS code. Coverage varies significantly between insurers. Verify payer acceptance before billing and document medical necessity thoroughly.
