S0504 Singl prscrp lens

S-Codes

Also known as: single vision lens, monofocal

Single prescription lens. Monofocal contact lens for correction of single refractive error.

Clinical Context

Corrects myopia, hyperopia, or astigmatism at single focal length. Most common lens type for younger patients without presbyopia.
