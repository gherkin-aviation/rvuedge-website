S0194 Vitamin suppl 100 caps

S-Codes

Also known as: vitamin suppl

Vitamin supplement, 100 capsules. Nutrient replacement for dietary deficiencies or disease-related malabsorption.

Clinical Context

Used in patients with documented nutritional deficiencies, malabsorption disorders, or as prophylaxis in at-risk populations. Common in elderly or debilitated patients.
