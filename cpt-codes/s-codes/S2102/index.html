S2102 Islet cell tissue transplant

S Codes

Also known as: islet transplant, islet cell graft

Islet cell tissue transplantation. Surgical grafting of pancreatic islet cells for type 1 diabetes treatment.

In Plain Language

pancreas cell transplant; insulin-producing cell graft

Clinical Context

Transplantation of isolated pancreatic islet cells intrahepatically or into portal system for insulin production. Offers insulin independence without whole organ transplant.

RVU Information

CPT S2102 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

S-codes are used by private payers (not Medicare) for services without a standard CPT or HCPCS code. Coverage varies significantly between insurers. Verify payer acceptance before billing and document medical necessity thoroughly.
