S9122 Home health aide or certifie

S-Codes

Also known as: home health aide, certified nursing assistant, personal care assistant

Home health aide or certified nursing assistant services provide in-home personal care assistance including bathing, grooming, toileting, ambulation assistance, and activities of daily living support.

Clinical Context

Used for elderly, disabled, and post-surgical patients requiring assistance with activities of daily living. Allows patients to remain home safely while receiving needed support.
