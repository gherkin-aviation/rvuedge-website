S4011 Ivf package

S Codes

Also known as: IVF package, in vitro fertilization cycle

Comprehensive in vitro fertilization procedure package including ovarian stimulation, oocyte retrieval, laboratory processing, and embryo transfer.

In Plain Language

test tube baby procedure; fertility treatment package

Clinical Context

Used in reproductive endocrinology and fertility clinics for complete IVF treatment cycles.

RVU Information

CPT S4011 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

S-codes are used by private payers (not Medicare) for services without a standard CPT or HCPCS code. Coverage varies significantly between insurers. Verify payer acceptance before billing and document medical necessity thoroughly.
