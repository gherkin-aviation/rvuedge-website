S0508 Trifoc prscrp lens

S-Codes

Also known as: trifocal lens, trifocal contact

Trifocal prescription lens. Contact lens with three focal areas for distance, intermediate, and near vision.

Clinical Context

Advanced multifocal design for presbyopic patients requiring correction at multiple distances. Provides distance, computer, and reading vision in single lens.
