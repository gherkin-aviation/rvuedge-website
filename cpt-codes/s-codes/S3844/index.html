S3844 Dna analysis deafness

S-Codes

Also known as: genetic hearing loss test, deafness gene testing

Deoxyribonucleic acid sequence analysis for mutations associated with hereditary deafness and sensorineural hearing loss.

Clinical Context

Used in audiology and otolaryngology for genetic evaluation of congenital or early-onset sensorineural hearing loss.
