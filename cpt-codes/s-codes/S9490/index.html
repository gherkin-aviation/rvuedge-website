S9490 Hit corticosteroid/diem

S Codes

Also known as: home IV steroid infusion, home corticosteroid therapy, home IV therapy

Home infusion therapy using intravenous corticosteroids provided on a per-diem basis with nursing and pharmaceutical services.

In Plain Language

home steroid IV treatment; home infusion therapy; home medication infusion

Clinical Context

Used for immunosuppression, severe inflammation, auto-immune diseases, and acute exacerbations of chronic conditions requiring high-dose intravenous corticosteroids.

RVU Information

CPT S9490 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

S-codes are used by private payers (not Medicare) for services without a standard CPT or HCPCS code. Coverage varies significantly between insurers. Verify payer acceptance before billing and document medical necessity thoroughly.
