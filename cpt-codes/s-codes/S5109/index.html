S5109 Homecare train pt session

S-Codes

Also known as: home training session, patient education session

Home care patient training services provided per session (typically 1 hour) by skilled personnel covering disease management, self-care techniques, or equipment operation.

Clinical Context

Billed when comprehensive patient education and training is provided in home setting as a complete session rather than shorter intervals.
