S9480 Intensive outpatient psychia

S Codes

Also known as: intensive outpatient program, partial hospitalization program, PHP

Intensive outpatient psychiatric program or partial hospitalization program providing structured psychiatric treatment in an ambulatory setting.

In Plain Language

intensive mental health program; psychiatric day program; mental health intensive treatment

Clinical Context

Used for acute psychiatric symptoms including depression, anxiety, bipolar disorder, and psychosis requiring more intensive monitoring than standard outpatient care but without overnight hospitalization.

RVU Information

CPT S9480 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

S-codes are used by private payers (not Medicare) for services without a standard CPT or HCPCS code. Coverage varies significantly between insurers. Verify payer acceptance before billing and document medical necessity thoroughly.
