S9124 Nursing care, in the home; b

S-Codes

Also known as: LPN home care, LVN home care, in-home practical nursing

Nursing care in the home by licensed practical nurse (LPN/LVN) includes assessment, medication administration, basic wound care, and clinical support provided by licensed practical nurse.

Clinical Context

Used for ongoing medication management, basic wound care, and patient monitoring in home settings. LPN provides care under RN supervision for stable patients.
