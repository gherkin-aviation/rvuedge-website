S5517 Hit declotting kit

S-Codes

Also known as: declotting kit, catheter clearing kit

Home Infusion Therapy catheter declotting kit containing supplies and materials for clearing thrombi or fibrin buildup from infusion catheters.

Clinical Context

Supplied for Home Infusion Therapy patients to address partial or complete catheter occlusion due to blood clot or fibrin formation.
