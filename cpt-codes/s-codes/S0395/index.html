S0395 Impression casting ft

S-Codes

Also known as: foot casting, mold impression

Impression casting of foot. Taking three-dimensional mold of foot for custom orthotic device fabrication.

Clinical Context

Used in fabrication of custom orthotics, braces, or prosthetics. Precise casting ensures proper fit and biomechanical correction for various foot conditions.
