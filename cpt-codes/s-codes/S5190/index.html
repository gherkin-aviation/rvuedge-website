S5190 Wellness assessment by nonph

S-Codes

Also known as: wellness screening, health assessment

Wellness assessment and screening services provided by non-physician healthcare worker including health status evaluation and health promotion counseling.

Clinical Context

Used for preventive health screening and wellness counseling by nurses or other non-physician clinicians identifying health risks and promotion opportunities.
