S9485 Crisis intervention mental h

S-Codes

Also known as: crisis mental health service, 24-hour crisis support, crisis stabilization service

Crisis intervention or mental health crisis service provided on a per-diem basis covering 24-hour crisis support.

Clinical Context

Comprehensive crisis services for acute psychiatric emergencies available on a 24-hour basis to provide stabilization and prevent hospitalization.
