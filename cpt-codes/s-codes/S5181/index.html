S5181 Hh respiratory thrpy not otherwise specified/day

S Codes

Also known as: home respiratory therapy, daily respiratory care

Home health respiratory therapy services not otherwise specified, including treatment, monitoring, and disease management provided per day.

In Plain Language

Daily breathing therapy; Home lung treatment

Clinical Context

Billed for ongoing respiratory therapy services in home setting including equipment monitoring, breathing treatments, and patient management.

RVU Information

CPT S5181 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

S-codes are used by private payers (not Medicare) for services without a standard CPT or HCPCS code. Coverage varies significantly between insurers. Verify payer acceptance before billing and document medical necessity thoroughly.
