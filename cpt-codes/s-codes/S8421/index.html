S8421 Ready gradient sleev/glov

S Codes

Also known as: compression sleeve, ready-made garment

Ready-made (pre-sized) gradient compression sleeve or glove providing standard pressure gradient for lymphedema management.

In Plain Language

Off-the-shelf sleeve; Standard compression sleeve

Clinical Context

Provided as ready-made option for lymphedema or venous insufficiency when custom fitting is not needed.

RVU Information

CPT S8421 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

S-codes are used by private payers (not Medicare) for services without a standard CPT or HCPCS code. Coverage varies significantly between insurers. Verify payer acceptance before billing and document medical necessity thoroughly.
