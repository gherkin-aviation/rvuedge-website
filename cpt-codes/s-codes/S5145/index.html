S5145 Child fostercare th per diem

S-Codes

Also known as: therapeutic foster care, therapeutic group home

Therapeutic foster care placement services for children including therapeutic support, behavioral management, and specialized care provided per diem.

Clinical Context

Used for children with emotional/behavioral disorders placed in therapeutic foster homes providing specialized treatment, supervision, and family-based care.
