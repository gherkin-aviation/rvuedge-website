S9141 Diabetic management program,

S Codes

Also known as: ongoing diabetes management, diabetes support program

Diabetic management program encompasses ongoing diabetes education and support services including monitoring, counseling, and community resources for insulin-dependent and non-insulin patients.

In Plain Language

continuing diabetes education program

Clinical Context

Used for established diabetes patients requiring ongoing education, medication adjustment support, and psychological support. Continuation of management program with periodic reassessment.

RVU Information

CPT S9141 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

S-codes are used by private payers (not Medicare) for services without a standard CPT or HCPCS code. Coverage varies significantly between insurers. Verify payer acceptance before billing and document medical necessity thoroughly.
