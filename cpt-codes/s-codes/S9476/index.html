S9476 Vestibular rehabilitation per diem

S Codes

Also known as: vestibular rehab, balance training program, dizziness rehabilitation

Vestibular rehabilitation program or service on a per diem basis provided by a licensed physical therapist or specialist trained in vestibular disorders.

In Plain Language

balance rehabilitation program; dizziness treatment; inner ear therapy

Clinical Context

Used for benign paroxysmal positional vertigo, vestibular neuritis, post-concussive dizziness, and other balance disorders. Improves proprioception and reduces fall risk.

RVU Information

CPT S9476 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

S-codes are used by private payers (not Medicare) for services without a standard CPT or HCPCS code. Coverage varies significantly between insurers. Verify payer acceptance before billing and document medical necessity thoroughly.
