S0500 Dispos cont lens

S Codes

Also known as: disposable contact lens, daily disposable

Disposable contact lens. Single-use contact lens discarded after brief wear period.

In Plain Language

throw-away contact; daily contact lens

Clinical Context

Prescribed for refractive error correction in patients with astigmatism, myopia, hyperopia. Disposables eliminate need for solution and storage, reducing infection risk.

RVU Information

CPT S0500 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

S-codes are used by private payers (not Medicare) for services without a standard CPT or HCPCS code. Coverage varies significantly between insurers. Verify payer acceptance before billing and document medical necessity thoroughly.
