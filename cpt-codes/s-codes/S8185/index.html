S8185 Flutter device

S Codes

Also known as: flutter valve, airway oscillator

Flutter device for airway clearance using oscillatory positive expiratory pressure to mobilize airway secretions in respiratory disease.

In Plain Language

Cough helper; Mucus clearing device

Clinical Context

Used for patients with cystic fibrosis, chronic bronchitis, and other conditions with excessive airway secretions requiring mobilization.

RVU Information

CPT S8185 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

S-codes are used by private payers (not Medicare) for services without a standard CPT or HCPCS code. Coverage varies significantly between insurers. Verify payer acceptance before billing and document medical necessity thoroughly.
