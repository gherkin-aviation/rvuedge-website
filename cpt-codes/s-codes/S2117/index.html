S2117 Arthroereisis, subtalar

S-Codes

Also known as: subtalar arthroereisis, subtalar implant

Arthroereisis of subtalar joint. Surgical limitation of subtalar motion using implant or arthrodesis.

Clinical Context

Surgical stabilization of subtalar joint using implant to control excessive pronation in pediatric flatfoot or adult dysfunction.
