S9056 Coma stimulation per diem

S-Codes

Also known as: coma stimulation therapy, sensory stimulation program

Coma stimulation is a therapeutic program involving sensory and cognitive stimulation provided daily to patients in minimally conscious or vegetative states to promote neurological recovery.

Clinical Context

Used in traumatic brain injury, hypoxic-ischemic encephalopathy, and prolonged unconsciousness. Organized sensory stimulation may promote arousal and neurological improvement.
