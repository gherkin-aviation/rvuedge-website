S0160 Dextroamphetamine

S Codes

Also known as: Dexedrine, dextroamphetamine, stimulant

Dextroamphetamine oral medication, a sympathomimetic amine for ADHD and narcolepsy.

In Plain Language

ADHD medication; attention booster; focus medication

Clinical Context

Used for attention-deficit/hyperactivity disorder and narcolepsy. CNS stimulant that increases dopamine and norepinephrine.

RVU Information

CPT S0160 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

S-codes are used by private payers (not Medicare) for services without a standard CPT or HCPCS code. Coverage varies significantly between insurers. Verify payer acceptance before billing and document medical necessity thoroughly.
