S3904 Masters two step

S-Codes

Also known as: Master's two-step test, exercise tolerance test

Submaximal exercise tolerance test using a two-step platform to evaluate cardiac response and exercise capacity without achieving maximal exertion.

Clinical Context

Used in cardiology for exercise stress testing in patients unable or unwilling to undergo treadmill testing.
