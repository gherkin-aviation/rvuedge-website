S0250 Complicated geriatr assmt team

S-Codes

Also known as: geriatric assessment, CGA, geriatric eval

Comprehensive geriatric assessment by team. Multidisciplinary evaluation of elderly patient including medical, functional, cognitive, and psychosocial domains.

Clinical Context

Structured evaluation of older adults to identify medical and functional impairments, optimize medications, and develop comprehensive care plan. Often in geriatric clinics or home-based settings.
