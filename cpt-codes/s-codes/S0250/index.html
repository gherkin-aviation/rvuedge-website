S0250 Complicated geriatr assmt team

S Codes

Also known as: geriatric assessment, CGA, geriatric eval

Comprehensive geriatric assessment by team. Multidisciplinary evaluation of elderly patient including medical, functional, cognitive, and psychosocial domains.

In Plain Language

senior health checkup; elder evaluation

Clinical Context

Structured evaluation of older adults to identify medical and functional impairments, optimize medications, and develop comprehensive care plan. Often in geriatric clinics or home-based settings.

RVU Information

CPT S0250 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

S-codes are used by private payers (not Medicare) for services without a standard CPT or HCPCS code. Coverage varies significantly between insurers. Verify payer acceptance before billing and document medical necessity thoroughly.
