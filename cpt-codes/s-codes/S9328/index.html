S9328 Hit pain imp pump diem

S Codes

Also known as: implanted pump pain therapy, intrathecal pain pump

Home infusion therapy pain management with implanted pump per diem manages continuous pain medication delivery through implanted infusion pump with periodic refilling and clinical assessment.

In Plain Language

medication pump implanted for pain relief

Clinical Context

Used for intractable chronic pain managed with implanted intrathecal pump. Home management includes pump refilling, programming adjustment, and monitoring for pump function.

RVU Information

CPT S9328 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

S-codes are used by private payers (not Medicare) for services without a standard CPT or HCPCS code. Coverage varies significantly between insurers. Verify payer acceptance before billing and document medical necessity thoroughly.
