S5115 Nonfamily homecare train/15m

S Codes

Also known as: aide training, non-family caregiver training

Non-family member caregiver training services provided in home setting in 15-minute intervals, including instruction on patient care, safety, and equipment operation.

In Plain Language

Helper instruction; Professional caregiver training

Clinical Context

Used for training paid caregivers or aides not related to patient on specific care procedures, safety protocols, and medical equipment in home environment.

RVU Information

CPT S5115 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

S-codes are used by private payers (not Medicare) for services without a standard CPT or HCPCS code. Coverage varies significantly between insurers. Verify payer acceptance before billing and document medical necessity thoroughly.
