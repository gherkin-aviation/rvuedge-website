S2403 Fetal surgical pulmonary sequest

S Codes

Also known as: fetal pulmonary sequestration repair, fetal lung mass

Fetal surgical treatment of pulmonary sequestration, a non-functioning mass of lung tissue with anomalous blood supply.

In Plain Language

fetal surgery to remove extra lung tissue; prenatal lung lesion removal

Clinical Context

Used in maternal-fetal surgery for fetuses with symptomatic pulmonary sequestration causing mass effect, shunting, or hydrops.

RVU Information

CPT S2403 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

S-codes are used by private payers (not Medicare) for services without a standard CPT or HCPCS code. Coverage varies significantly between insurers. Verify payer acceptance before billing and document medical necessity thoroughly.
