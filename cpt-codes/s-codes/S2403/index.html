S2403 Fetal surgical pulmonary sequest

S-Codes

Also known as: fetal pulmonary sequestration repair, fetal lung mass

Fetal surgical treatment of pulmonary sequestration, a non-functioning mass of lung tissue with anomalous blood supply.

Clinical Context

Used in maternal-fetal surgery for fetuses with symptomatic pulmonary sequestration causing mass effect, shunting, or hydrops.
