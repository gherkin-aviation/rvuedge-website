S9098 Home phototherapy visit

S-Codes

Also known as: home phototherapy, in-home light therapy

Home phototherapy visit involves professional nurse or therapist administration of light therapy (typically for newborn jaundice or skin conditions) in the patient's home setting.

Clinical Context

Primarily used for neonatal hyperbilirubinemia management, allowing infants to remain at home while receiving therapeutic light exposure. Also used for psoriasis and seasonal affective disorder.
