S2118 Total hip resurfacing

S Codes

Also known as: hip resurfacing, resurfacing arthroplasty

Total hip resurfacing. Arthroplasty preserving femoral bone with metal-on-metal articulation.

In Plain Language

hip resurfacing surgery; metal hip replacement

Clinical Context

Joint-sparing procedure preserving proximal femur, creating metal-on-metal bearing surface. Allows higher activity levels and easier revision compared to total hip replacement.

RVU Information

CPT S2118 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

S-codes are used by private payers (not Medicare) for services without a standard CPT or HCPCS code. Coverage varies significantly between insurers. Verify payer acceptance before billing and document medical necessity thoroughly.
