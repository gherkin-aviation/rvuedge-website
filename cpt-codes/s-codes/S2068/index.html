S2068 Breast diep or siea flap

S Codes

Also known as: DIEP flap, SIEA flap, abdominal flap

Breast reconstruction using free DIEP or SIEA flap. Tissue transfer from lower abdomen or lower trunk.

In Plain Language

belly tissue breast; abdominal tissue reconstruction

Clinical Context

Free perforator flaps from abdomen without sacrificing rectus muscle, or superficial epigastric flap from lower trunk. Provides natural breast volume with minimal donor morbidity.

RVU Information

CPT S2068 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

S-codes are used by private payers (not Medicare) for services without a standard CPT or HCPCS code. Coverage varies significantly between insurers. Verify payer acceptance before billing and document medical necessity thoroughly.
