S2068 Breast diep or siea flap

S-Codes

Also known as: DIEP flap, SIEA flap, abdominal flap

Breast reconstruction using free DIEP or SIEA flap. Tissue transfer from lower abdomen or lower trunk.

Clinical Context

Free perforator flaps from abdomen without sacrificing rectus muscle, or superficial epigastric flap from lower trunk. Provides natural breast volume with minimal donor morbidity.
