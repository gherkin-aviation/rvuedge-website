S4042 Ovulation management per cycle

S Codes

Also known as: ovulation management, ovulation induction monitoring

Ovulation induction and monitoring including ultrasound imaging, hormone assessment, and timing coordination per menstrual cycle.

In Plain Language

helping ovulation with medications and monitoring; ovulation timing management

Clinical Context

Used in reproductive endocrinology for patients undergoing ovulation induction with monitoring for timed intercourse or intrauterine insemination.

RVU Information

CPT S4042 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

S-codes are used by private payers (not Medicare) for services without a standard CPT or HCPCS code. Coverage varies significantly between insurers. Verify payer acceptance before billing and document medical necessity thoroughly.
