S0272 Home episodic case 30 days

S Codes

Also known as: episodic home health, short-term HH

Home health episodic case rate, 30 days. Per diem payment for episodic or short-term home health services not requiring continuous care.

In Plain Language

temporary home care; short-term nursing at home

Clinical Context

Used for patients with acute conditions requiring temporary home support, post-discharge recuperation, or periodic skilled nursing needs. Less intensive than continuous home health.

RVU Information

CPT S0272 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

S-codes are used by private payers (not Medicare) for services without a standard CPT or HCPCS code. Coverage varies significantly between insurers. Verify payer acceptance before billing and document medical necessity thoroughly.
