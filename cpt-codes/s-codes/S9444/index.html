S9444 Parenting class

S Codes

Also known as: parenting education, parent training

Parenting class provides education on infant/child development, behavior management, safety, nutrition, and emotional support for new and established parents.

In Plain Language

parenting skills class

Clinical Context

Used for new parents or those seeking parenting skill development. Covers child development, discipline strategies, and parent self-care.

RVU Information

CPT S9444 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

S-codes are used by private payers (not Medicare) for services without a standard CPT or HCPCS code. Coverage varies significantly between insurers. Verify payer acceptance before billing and document medical necessity thoroughly.
