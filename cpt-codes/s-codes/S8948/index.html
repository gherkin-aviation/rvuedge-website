S8948 Low-level laser trmt 15 minutes

S Codes

Also known as: LLLT, photobiomodulation, cold laser therapy

Low-level laser therapy (photobiomodulation) uses non-thermal laser light at low power to stimulate cellular activity, reduce inflammation, and promote tissue healing over a 15-minute treatment session.

In Plain Language

laser light therapy; healing light treatment

Clinical Context

Applied to musculoskeletal pain, wound healing, inflammation reduction, and tissue repair. Commonly used in physical therapy for tendonitis, arthritis, and soft tissue injuries.

RVU Information

CPT S8948 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

S-codes are used by private payers (not Medicare) for services without a standard CPT or HCPCS code. Coverage varies significantly between insurers. Verify payer acceptance before billing and document medical necessity thoroughly.
