S9353 Hit cont insulin diem

S-Codes

Also known as: continuous IV insulin, insulin infusion

Home infusion therapy continuous insulin per diem provides continuous intravenous insulin infusion via infusion pump for severe hyperglycemia or diabetic ketoacidosis management.

Clinical Context

Used for severe hyperglycemia requiring IV insulin, diabetic ketoacidosis management, perioperative diabetes control. Home IV insulin represents selected complex cases.
