S2225 Myringotomy laser-assist

S Codes

Also known as: laser myringotomy, laser-assisted ear tube, tympanostomy

Surgical puncture of the tympanic membrane (myringotomy) assisted by laser technology to establish ventilation drainage for middle ear effusion.

In Plain Language

making a small hole in eardrum with laser light; ear tube procedure

Clinical Context

Used in otolaryngology for patients with chronic otitis media with effusion or recurrent acute otitis media who require myringotomy and ventilation tube placement.

RVU Information

CPT S2225 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

S-codes are used by private payers (not Medicare) for services without a standard CPT or HCPCS code. Coverage varies significantly between insurers. Verify payer acceptance before billing and document medical necessity thoroughly.
