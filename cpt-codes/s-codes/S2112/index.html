S2112 Knee arthroscp harv

S Codes

Also known as: knee cartilage harvest, arthroscopic graft

Knee arthroscopic harvesting of cartilage. Arthroscopic collection of autologous cartilage for transplantation.

In Plain Language

knee cartilage collection; donor cartilage removal

Clinical Context

Arthroscopic collection of healthy cartilage from knee for autologous chondrocyte implantation or osteochondral graft to repair focal defects.

RVU Information

CPT S2112 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

S-codes are used by private payers (not Medicare) for services without a standard CPT or HCPCS code. Coverage varies significantly between insurers. Verify payer acceptance before billing and document medical necessity thoroughly.
