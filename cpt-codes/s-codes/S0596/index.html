S0596 Phakic iol refractive error

S Codes

Also known as: phakic IOL, ICL

Phakic intraocular lens (IOL) for refractive error. Implantable lens placed in front of natural lens for correction without removal.

In Plain Language

implant lens for vision; contact lens implant

Clinical Context

Surgical correction of high myopia, hyperopia, or astigmatism in patients with thin corneas unsuitable for LASIK. Implanted between iris and cornea preserving accommodation.

RVU Information

CPT S0596 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

S-codes are used by private payers (not Medicare) for services without a standard CPT or HCPCS code. Coverage varies significantly between insurers. Verify payer acceptance before billing and document medical necessity thoroughly.
