S5170 Homedelivered prepared meal

S Codes

Also known as: meals on wheels, home-delivered meals

Home-delivered prepared meal services providing nutritionally balanced meals delivered to homebound individuals, typically one meal per delivery.

In Plain Language

Delivered meals; Food delivery service

Clinical Context

Provides nutritional support for homebound individuals unable to prepare meals themselves due to functional limitations or medical conditions.

RVU Information

CPT S5170 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

S-codes are used by private payers (not Medicare) for services without a standard CPT or HCPCS code. Coverage varies significantly between insurers. Verify payer acceptance before billing and document medical necessity thoroughly.
