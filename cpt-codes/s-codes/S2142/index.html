S2142 Cord blood-derived stem-cell

S Codes

Also known as: stem cell expansion, cord blood processing

Preparation and expansion of hematopoietic stem cells derived from umbilical cord blood for research or therapeutic applications.

In Plain Language

processing cord blood for stem cells; growing stem cells from cord blood

Clinical Context

Used in stem cell research and regenerative medicine centers to expand and prepare cord blood-derived hematopoietic stem cells for clinical trials or therapeutic use.

RVU Information

CPT S2142 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

S-codes are used by private payers (not Medicare) for services without a standard CPT or HCPCS code. Coverage varies significantly between insurers. Verify payer acceptance before billing and document medical necessity thoroughly.
