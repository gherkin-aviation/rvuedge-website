S0311 Complicated management care coord adv ill

S Codes

Also known as: care coordination, complex care management

Comprehensive management care coordination for advanced illness. Physician-led care coordination for complex, chronically ill patients including communication with interdisciplinary team.

In Plain Language

health management assistance; doctor coordination

Clinical Context

Intensive care coordination service for patients with multiple comorbidities and complex psychosocial needs. Physician ensures seamless care across settings and between providers.

RVU Information

CPT S0311 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

S-codes are used by private payers (not Medicare) for services without a standard CPT or HCPCS code. Coverage varies significantly between insurers. Verify payer acceptance before billing and document medical necessity thoroughly.
