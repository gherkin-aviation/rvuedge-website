S9435 Medical foods for inborn err

S-Codes

Also known as: metabolic formula, PKU formula, amino acid formula

Medical foods for inborn errors of metabolism are specialized enteral formulations with modified amino acid profiles or specific nutrient composition for genetic metabolic disorders.

Clinical Context

Used for phenylketonuria (PKU), maple syrup urine disease (MSUD), tyrosinemia, and other inborn errors of metabolism. Specialized amino acid profile prevents metabolite accumulation.
