S0109 Methadone oral 5mg

S Codes

Also known as: methadone, opioid maintenance, addiction treatment

Methadone oral medication at 5 milligrams for opioid maintenance therapy.

In Plain Language

addiction treatment medication; opioid replacement therapy; heroin addiction medicine

Clinical Context

Used for medication-assisted treatment of opioid use disorder and chronic pain. Long-acting synthetic opioid with daily monitoring requirements.

RVU Information

CPT S0109 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

S-codes are used by private payers (not Medicare) for services without a standard CPT or HCPCS code. Coverage varies significantly between insurers. Verify payer acceptance before billing and document medical necessity thoroughly.
