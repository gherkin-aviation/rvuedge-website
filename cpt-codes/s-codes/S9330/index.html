S9330 Hit cont chem diem

S-Codes

Also known as: continuous home chemo, continuous IV chemotherapy

Home infusion therapy continuous chemotherapy per diem administers continuous intravenous chemotherapy infusion typically via central catheter with daily clinical monitoring and support.

Clinical Context

Used for cancer regimens requiring continuous chemotherapy infusion such as 5-fluorouracil. Home infusion enables continuous therapy while maintaining patient quality of life.
