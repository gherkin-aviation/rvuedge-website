S9211 Home management gest hypertension

S-Codes

Also known as: gestational hypertension home care, PHIPs management

Home management of gestational hypertension includes blood pressure monitoring, medication management, and fetal surveillance for pregnant patients with pregnancy-induced hypertension.

Clinical Context

Used for pregnant patients with elevated blood pressure without proteinuria. Home monitoring allows safe outpatient management while detecting progression to preeclampsia.
