S9211 Home management gest hypertension

S Codes

Also known as: gestational hypertension home care, PHIPs management

Home management of gestational hypertension includes blood pressure monitoring, medication management, and fetal surveillance for pregnant patients with pregnancy-induced hypertension.

In Plain Language

home monitoring for high blood pressure in pregnancy

Clinical Context

Used for pregnant patients with elevated blood pressure without proteinuria. Home monitoring allows safe outpatient management while detecting progression to preeclampsia.

RVU Information

CPT S9211 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

S-codes are used by private payers (not Medicare) for services without a standard CPT or HCPCS code. Coverage varies significantly between insurers. Verify payer acceptance before billing and document medical necessity thoroughly.
