S9497 Hit antibiotic q3h diem

S Codes

Also known as: home IV antibiotic q3h, home antibiotic infusion q3h, HIT q3h antibiotics

Home infusion therapy using intravenous antibiotics administered every 3 hours on a per-diem basis.

In Plain Language

home antibiotic IV treatment every 3 hours; home IV antibiotic therapy q3h

Clinical Context

Provides frequent intravenous antibiotic administration for serious infections requiring q3 hour dosing intervals to maintain therapeutic levels.

RVU Information

CPT S9497 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

S-codes are used by private payers (not Medicare) for services without a standard CPT or HCPCS code. Coverage varies significantly between insurers. Verify payer acceptance before billing and document medical necessity thoroughly.
