S4030 Sperm procure initial visit

S Codes

Also known as: sperm procurement initial visit, fertility male evaluation

Initial clinical evaluation and consultation for sperm collection and processing including assessment of fertility potential.

In Plain Language

first appointment for sperm collection; male fertility evaluation

Clinical Context

Used in reproductive urology and fertility clinics for initial evaluation of male patients for sperm collection.

RVU Information

CPT S4030 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

S-codes are used by private payers (not Medicare) for services without a standard CPT or HCPCS code. Coverage varies significantly between insurers. Verify payer acceptance before billing and document medical necessity thoroughly.
