S8100 Spacer without mask

S-Codes

Also known as: spacer, aerosol holding chamber

Spacer device for inhaled medication delivery without mask attachment, improving medication deposition in lower airways.

Clinical Context

Used with inhaled bronchodilators and corticosteroids to improve medication delivery efficiency, especially in adults and older children.
