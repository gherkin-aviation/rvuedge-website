S8092 Electron beam computed tomog

S-Codes

Also known as: EBCT, electron beam CT

Electron beam computed tomography providing rapid high-resolution imaging with minimal radiation exposure for cardiac and other diagnostic purposes.

Clinical Context

Used for cardiac calcium scoring, coronary artery imaging, and rapid imaging in critical care situations requiring minimal motion artifact.
