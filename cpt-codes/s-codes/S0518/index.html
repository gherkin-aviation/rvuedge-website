S0518 Sunglass frames

S-Codes

Also known as: sunglass frames, sun frames

Sunglass frames. Eyeglass frames designed to hold tinted or polarized lenses.

Clinical Context

Prescribed frames for UV protection and glare reduction in photophobic patients, post-operative eyes, or macular degeneration. May include prescription.
