S8101 Spacer with mask

S-Codes

Also known as: spacer with mask, holding chamber with mask

Spacer device with attached face mask for inhaled medication delivery, enabling medication administration in young children and infants.

Clinical Context

Used for inhaled medication delivery in young children, infants, and patients unable to form oral seal with spacer alone.
