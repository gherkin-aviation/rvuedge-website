S0400 Global eswl kidney

S Codes

Also known as: ESWL, shock wave lithotripsy

Extracorporeal shock wave lithotripsy (ESWL) of kidney stone, global fee. Non-invasive treatment using shock waves to fragment kidney stones.

In Plain Language

shock wave kidney treatment; stone breaking procedure

Clinical Context

Non-operative treatment of nephrolithiasis (kidney stones). Fluoroscopy-guided positioning of shock wave generator, fragmentation of stones, and post-procedure monitoring included.

RVU Information

CPT S0400 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

S-codes are used by private payers (not Medicare) for services without a standard CPT or HCPCS code. Coverage varies significantly between insurers. Verify payer acceptance before billing and document medical necessity thoroughly.
