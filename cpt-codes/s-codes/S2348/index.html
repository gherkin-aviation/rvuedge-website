S2348 Decompress disc rf lumbar

S Codes

Also known as: radiofrequency disc decompression, RF disc decompression, RFDD

Percutaneous decompression of an intervertebral disc in the lumbar spine using radiofrequency technology to reduce intradiscal pressure and nerve compression.

In Plain Language

heat treatment for disc bulge in lower back; reducing pressure on nerves

Clinical Context

Used in interventional pain management and spine care for patients with lumbar radiculopathy or myelopathy secondary to contained disc herniation.

RVU Information

CPT S2348 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

S-codes are used by private payers (not Medicare) for services without a standard CPT or HCPCS code. Coverage varies significantly between insurers. Verify payer acceptance before billing and document medical necessity thoroughly.
