S1016 Non-pvc intravenous administ

S-Codes

Also known as: non-PVC IV set, DEHP-free IV

Non-polyvinyl chloride (non-PVC) intravenous administration set. IV tubing made from alternative plastic to minimize chemical leaching.

Clinical Context

Used for chemotherapy, parenteral nutrition, or sensitive patients to avoid phthalate exposure. Alternative tubing material prevents chemical leaching from standard PVC.
