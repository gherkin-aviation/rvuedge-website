S9348 Hit sympathomim diem

S-Codes

Also known as: inotropic therapy, sympathomimetic infusion

Home infusion therapy sympathomimetic agent per diem provides intravenous sympathomimetic (adrenergic) agents such as dobutamine or milrinone for inotropic support and hemodynamic management.

Clinical Context

Used for advanced heart failure, cardiogenic shock, low cardiac output states. Home inotropic infusion selected patients may avoid hospitalization while maintaining cardiac support.
