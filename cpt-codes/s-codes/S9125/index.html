S9125 Respite care, in the home, p

S Codes

Also known as: respite care, caregiver relief, respite services

Respite care in the home per diem provides temporary short-term care in the patient's home to relieve primary caregivers from ongoing care responsibilities and provide rest.

In Plain Language

care to give family caregiver a break

Clinical Context

Used for patients with chronic conditions, dementia, disability who have family caregivers needing temporary relief. Maintains patient at home while supporting caregiver wellbeing.

RVU Information

CPT S9125 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

S-codes are used by private payers (not Medicare) for services without a standard CPT or HCPCS code. Coverage varies significantly between insurers. Verify payer acceptance before billing and document medical necessity thoroughly.
