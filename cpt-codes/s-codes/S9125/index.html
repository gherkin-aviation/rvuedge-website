S9125 Respite care, in the home, p

S-Codes

Also known as: respite care, caregiver relief, respite services

Respite care in the home per diem provides temporary short-term care in the patient's home to relieve primary caregivers from ongoing care responsibilities and provide rest.

Clinical Context

Used for patients with chronic conditions, dementia, disability who have family caregivers needing temporary relief. Maintains patient at home while supporting caregiver wellbeing.
