S4991 Nicotine patch nonlegend

S Codes

Also known as: nicotine patch over-the-counter, nonprescription nicotine patch

Transdermal nicotine replacement patch available over-the-counter without prescription for smoking cessation therapy.

In Plain Language

over-the-counter nicotine patch for quitting smoking; over-the-counter smoking patch

Clinical Context

Used by patients and healthcare providers for over-the-counter nicotine replacement smoking cessation.

RVU Information

CPT S4991 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

S-codes are used by private payers (not Medicare) for services without a standard CPT or HCPCS code. Coverage varies significantly between insurers. Verify payer acceptance before billing and document medical necessity thoroughly.
