S0320 Rn telephone calls to dmp

S Codes

Also known as: nurse phone calls, RN telephone support

Registered nurse telephone calls to disease management program participants. Phone-based care coordination and symptom monitoring.

In Plain Language

nurse phone check-in; health hotline calls

Clinical Context

Registered nurse outreach to enrolled patients by phone for symptom assessment, medication reconciliation, education, and care plan adjustment. Documents duration and content.

RVU Information

CPT S0320 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

S-codes are used by private payers (not Medicare) for services without a standard CPT or HCPCS code. Coverage varies significantly between insurers. Verify payer acceptance before billing and document medical necessity thoroughly.
