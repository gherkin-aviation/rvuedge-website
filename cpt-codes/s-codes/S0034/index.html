S0034 Injection, ofloxacin, 400 milligrams

S Codes

Also known as: ofloxacin, Floxin, fluoroquinolone

Injection of ofloxacin, a fluoroquinolone antibiotic, at 400 milligrams.

In Plain Language

antibiotic injection; bacterial infection treatment; broad-spectrum antibiotic

Clinical Context

Used for serious bacterial infections including respiratory, urinary tract, and intra-abdominal infections. Administered intravenously.

RVU Information

CPT S0034 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

S-codes are used by private payers (not Medicare) for services without a standard CPT or HCPCS code. Coverage varies significantly between insurers. Verify payer acceptance before billing and document medical necessity thoroughly.
