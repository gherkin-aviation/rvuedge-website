S4021 Ivf canc aftr aspr case rate

S Codes

Also known as: IVF cancellation post-aspiration, cancelled IVF after retrieval

Global case rate for in vitro fertilization cycle cancelled after oocyte aspiration but before embryo transfer.

In Plain Language

fertility treatment stopped after egg retrieval; cancelled IVF after aspiration

Clinical Context

Used in reproductive endocrinology when in vitro fertilization is cancelled after oocyte retrieval due to fertilization failure or other complications.

RVU Information

CPT S4021 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

S-codes are used by private payers (not Medicare) for services without a standard CPT or HCPCS code. Coverage varies significantly between insurers. Verify payer acceptance before billing and document medical necessity thoroughly.
