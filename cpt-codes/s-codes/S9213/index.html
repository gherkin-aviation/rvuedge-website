S9213 Hm preeclamp per diem

S-Codes

Also known as: preeclampsia home care, PE management

Home preeclampsia management per diem includes intensive monitoring for preeclampsia/eclampsia signs, blood pressure management, and fetal surveillance in pregnant or postpartum patients.

Clinical Context

Used for pregnant women with preeclampsia (hypertension plus proteinuria) or postpartum women with eclampsia risk. Intensive home monitoring enables safe management while detecting emergencies.
