S9542 Ht injection noc per diem

S-Codes

Also known as: home IV infusion not otherwise classified, home IV medication therapy, home intravenous medications

Home infusion therapy administration of intravenous medications other than those specified elsewhere on a per-diem basis.

Clinical Context

Provides home infusion of various intravenous medications not captured by more specific codes, including immunoglobulins, chemotherapy, and other specialized agents.
