S0273 Md home visit outside cap

S-Codes

Also known as: home visit, house call

Physician home visit outside fee schedule cap. House call by licensed physician outside bundled payment limitations.

Clinical Context

Physician provides direct patient care at home for established patients unable to attend office. Often for complex, homebound, or palliative care patients requiring house calls beyond capitated rates.
