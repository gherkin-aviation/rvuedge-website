S9432 Medial food non inborn err meta

S-Codes

Also known as: medical food, nutritional supplement

Medical food for non-inborn errors of metabolism includes specialized enteral nutrition formulations designed for patients with various metabolic and gastrointestinal disorders.

Clinical Context

Used for patients with malabsorption syndromes, inflammatory bowel disease, and other metabolic disorders requiring specialized nutrition.
