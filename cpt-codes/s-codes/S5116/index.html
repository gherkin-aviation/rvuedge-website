S5116 Nonfamily hc train/session

S-Codes

Also known as: aide training session, paid caregiver training

Non-family member caregiver training services provided in home setting per session, including comprehensive instruction on patient care, equipment, and emergency protocols.

Clinical Context

Billed when non-family caregivers (paid staff, aides) receive comprehensive training session on patient-specific care requirements and equipment operation.
