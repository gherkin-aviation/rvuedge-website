S4995 Smoking cessation gum

S Codes

Also known as: nicotine gum, smoking cessation gum

Over-the-counter medicated chewing gum containing nicotine for smoking cessation therapy.

In Plain Language

nicotine gum for quitting smoking; medicated chewing gum for smoking

Clinical Context

Used in smoking cessation programs and primary care for nicotine replacement therapy.

RVU Information

CPT S4995 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

S-codes are used by private payers (not Medicare) for services without a standard CPT or HCPCS code. Coverage varies significantly between insurers. Verify payer acceptance before billing and document medical necessity thoroughly.
