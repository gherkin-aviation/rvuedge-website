S9346 Hit alpha-1-proteinas diem

S-Codes

Also known as: alpha-1 PI therapy, AAT replacement, proteinase inhibitor

Home infusion therapy alpha-1-proteinase inhibitor per diem provides intravenous replacement therapy for alpha-1 antitrypsin deficiency with clinical monitoring and nursing support.

Clinical Context

Used for alpha-1 antitrypsin deficiency with emphysema or liver disease. Intravenous replacement slows disease progression and improves outcomes.
