S9208 Home management preterm labor

S-Codes

Also known as: preterm labor management, PTL home care

Home management of preterm labor includes remote monitoring, patient education, medication management, and professional support for pregnant patients at risk for premature delivery.

Clinical Context

Used for pregnant patients with history of preterm labor, symptoms of preterm labor, or high-risk factors. Home management with monitoring reduces unnecessary hospitalization while detecting danger signs.
