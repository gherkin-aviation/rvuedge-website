S0271 Home hospice case 30 days

S Codes

Also known as: home hospice, hospice case rate

Home hospice case rate, 30 days. Per diem payment for hospice care services including nursing, aides, medications, and medical equipment.

In Plain Language

home hospice care; end-of-life home care

Clinical Context

Daily payment for all inclusive hospice services in home setting including palliative nursing, symptom management, family support, and spiritual care for terminally ill patients.

RVU Information

CPT S0271 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

S-codes are used by private payers (not Medicare) for services without a standard CPT or HCPCS code. Coverage varies significantly between insurers. Verify payer acceptance before billing and document medical necessity thoroughly.
