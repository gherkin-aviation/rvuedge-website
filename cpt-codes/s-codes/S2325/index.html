S2325 Hip core decompression

S Codes

Also known as: hip core decompression, femoral head decompression, core decompression

Surgical decompression of the femoral head to relieve pressure and improve blood flow in patients with avascular necrosis or osteonecrosis of the hip.

In Plain Language

pressure relief surgery on hip bone; treatment for hip bone death

Clinical Context

Used in orthopedic surgery for patients with early to mid-stage avascular necrosis of the femoral head to prevent or delay progressive collapse.

RVU Information

CPT S2325 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

S-codes are used by private payers (not Medicare) for services without a standard CPT or HCPCS code. Coverage varies significantly between insurers. Verify payer acceptance before billing and document medical necessity thoroughly.
