S0622 Phys examination for college

S Codes

Also known as: school physical, college exam

Physical examination for college or school entry. Comprehensive health assessment required for enrollment.

In Plain Language

school physical exam; college entrance exam

Clinical Context

Preventive health examination required by educational institutions including history, physical exam, immunization review, and clearance for sports participation.

RVU Information

CPT S0622 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

S-codes are used by private payers (not Medicare) for services without a standard CPT or HCPCS code. Coverage varies significantly between insurers. Verify payer acceptance before billing and document medical necessity thoroughly.
