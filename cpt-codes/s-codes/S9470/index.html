S9470 Nutritional counseling, diet

S Codes

Also known as: nutrition counseling, diet counseling, medical nutrition therapy, MNT

Nutritional counseling, diet prescription, or dietary consultation including medical nutrition therapy provided by a registered dietitian or licensed nutritionist.

In Plain Language

diet advice; nutrition consultation; meal planning help

Clinical Context

Used for therapeutic diet management in diabetes, cardiovascular disease, renal failure, cancer, gastrointestinal disorders, and metabolic conditions. Provides individualized nutrition assessment and intervention.

RVU Information

CPT S9470 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

S-codes are used by private payers (not Medicare) for services without a standard CPT or HCPCS code. Coverage varies significantly between insurers. Verify payer acceptance before billing and document medical necessity thoroughly.
