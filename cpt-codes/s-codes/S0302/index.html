S0302 Completed epsdt

S-Codes

Also known as: EPSDT completion, preventive screen

Early and Periodic Screening, Diagnosis and Treatment (EPSDT) service completion. Documentation of comprehensive preventive care visit for Medicaid child.

Clinical Context

Comprehensive health screening for children under 21 on Medicaid including assessment of physical, developmental, and behavioral health. Identifies conditions and arranges treatment.
