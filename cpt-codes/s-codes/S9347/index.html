S9347 Hit longterm infusion diem

S Codes

Also known as: chronic IV therapy, long-term infusion

Home infusion therapy long-term infusion per diem provides chronic intravenous infusion therapy for medications requiring sustained IV administration with ongoing professional management.

In Plain Language

long-term medicine through IV at home

Clinical Context

Used for various long-term intravenous therapies including vasodilators, inotropes, antibiotics. Home infusion enables chronic disease management in outpatient setting.

RVU Information

CPT S9347 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

S-codes are used by private payers (not Medicare) for services without a standard CPT or HCPCS code. Coverage varies significantly between insurers. Verify payer acceptance before billing and document medical necessity thoroughly.
