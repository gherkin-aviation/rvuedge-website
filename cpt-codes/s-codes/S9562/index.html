S9562 Ht injection palivizumab/ab diem

S Codes

Also known as: home palivizumab, home monoclonal antibody, home mAb infusion

Home infusion therapy administration of palivizumab or other monoclonal antibody injection on a per-diem basis.

In Plain Language

home antibody injection; home immune protein treatment

Clinical Context

Provides home administration of palivizumab for respiratory syncytial virus prophylaxis in high-risk infants or other monoclonal antibody therapies.

RVU Information

CPT S9562 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

S-codes are used by private payers (not Medicare) for services without a standard CPT or HCPCS code. Coverage varies significantly between insurers. Verify payer acceptance before billing and document medical necessity thoroughly.
