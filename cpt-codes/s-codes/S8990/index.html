S8990 Pt or manip for maint

S-Codes

Also known as: maintenance therapy, PT maintenance, follow-up therapy

Patient physical therapy or manipulation for maintenance provides ongoing therapeutic treatment to maintain functional gains and prevent deterioration in patients with chronic conditions.

Clinical Context

Used for patients achieving therapeutic goals who require ongoing care to maintain function. Common in chronic pain, arthritis, post-surgical recovery, and neurological conditions.
