A9555 Rb82 rubidium

A Codes

Also known as: Rb-82, rubidium PET

Rubidium Rb-82 injection used as a positron emission tomography tracer for myocardial perfusion imaging.

In Plain Language

heart perfusion PET tracer; cardiac imaging dye

Clinical Context

Used in PET cardiology for myocardial perfusion assessment and evaluation of coronary artery disease.

RVU Information

CPT A9555 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
