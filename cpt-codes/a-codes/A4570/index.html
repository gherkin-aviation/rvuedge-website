A4570 Splint

A Codes

Also known as: splint, extremity splint

Splint for immobilization of extremities.

In Plain Language

rigid support device; bone stabilizer

Clinical Context

Used for extremity immobilization in fractures, sprains, and post-operative management. Splints provide stability and pain relief during healing.

RVU Information

CPT A4570 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
