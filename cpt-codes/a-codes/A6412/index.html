A6412 Occlusive eye patch

A Codes

Also known as: occlusive patch, eye patch, occluder

Opaque adhesive patch applied directly to eye to provide protection, occlusion, and pressure dressing.

In Plain Language

covering patch for eyes

Clinical Context

Applied following ophthalmic trauma, corneal abrasion, or as directed therapy for certain eye conditions. Provides occlusion and gentle pressure.

RVU Information

CPT A6412 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
