A6457 Tubular dressing

A-Codes

Also known as: tubular bandage, tube gauze, compression tube

Tubular compression dressing. Provides cylindrical compression coverage for limbs with minimal bulk.

Clinical Context

Used for gentle compression on fingers, toes, and irregular body areas where traditional bandaging is difficult. Common in lymphedema management and post-operative care for extremities.
