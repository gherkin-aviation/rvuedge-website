A7038 Pos airway pressure filter

A-Codes

Also known as: PAP filter, disposable filter, air filter

Positive airway pressure disposable filter for removing contaminants in continuous positive airway pressure therapy systems.

Clinical Context

Used with positive airway pressure devices to filter incoming air. Single-use design prevents cross-contamination and maintains air quality.
