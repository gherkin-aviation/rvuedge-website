A9508 I131 iodobenguate, diagnosis

A-Codes

Also known as: MIBG diagnostic, iodobenguate, I-131 MIBG

Iodine I-131 iodobenguate injection used as a diagnostic radiotracer for adrenal and neuroendocrine imaging.

Clinical Context

Used in diagnostic imaging to detect neuroendocrine tumors, pheochromocytoma, and assess adrenal gland function.
