A6460 Synthetic drsg <= 16 square in

A Codes

Also known as: synthetic dressing, non-adherent pad, wound pad

Synthetic wound dressing, 16 square inches or less. Non-adherent covering for wound management.

In Plain Language

small wound pad; non-stick bandage

Clinical Context

Used for small wounds, abrasions, and minor lacerations. Synthetic dressings provide protection while allowing for easy removal without trauma to healing tissue.

RVU Information

CPT A6460 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
