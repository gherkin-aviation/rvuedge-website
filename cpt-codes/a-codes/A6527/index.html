In Plain Language

custom pressure stocking for sleeping; fitted nighttime full leg and foot compression

Clinical Context

Used for customized night-time compression therapy from thigh to foot. Custom fitting ensures optimal compression for comprehensive lower extremity lymphedema during sleep.

RVU Information

CPT A6527 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
