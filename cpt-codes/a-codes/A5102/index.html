A5102 Bedside drainage btl with wo tube

A-Codes

Also known as: drainage bottle, collection bottle, drain bag

A bedside drainage bottle or bag for collection of fluid drainage from tubes or catheters at bedside. Available with or without integral tubing.

Clinical Context

Used in hospital and home settings for chest tube, abdominal drain, or other wound drain management. Gravity-assisted drainage with volume graduations.
