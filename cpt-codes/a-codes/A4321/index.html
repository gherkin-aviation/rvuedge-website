A4321 Catheter therapeutic irrig agent

A Codes +Add-on

Also known as: irrigation solution, bladder irrigant

Catheter therapeutic irrigation agent, a sterile therapeutic solution used in bladder irrigation for managing hematuria, infections, or post-procedural inflammation.

In Plain Language

medication rinse for bladder; therapeutic washing fluid

Clinical Context

Used during or after urological procedures for bladder irrigation with therapeutic agents such as antibiotics or antiseptics.

RVU Information

CPT A4321 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
