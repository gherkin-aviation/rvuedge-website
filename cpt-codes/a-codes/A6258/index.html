A6258 Transparent film >16<=48 in

A-Codes

Also known as: transparent film, film dressing

Transparent semi-permeable film dressing measuring greater than 16 and up to 48 square inches.

Clinical Context

Indicated for moderate wounds requiring transparent, breathable coverage. Maintains moisture balance while allowing continuous visualization.
