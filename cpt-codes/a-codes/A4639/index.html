A4639 Infrared ht sys replcmnt pad

A-Codes

Also known as: heating pad, infrared pad, heat pad replacement

A replacement heating pad or heating element for an infrared heat therapy system used for pain relief and therapeutic warming. Provides localized therapeutic heat via infrared radiation.

Clinical Context

Used in pain management and rehabilitation for muscle relaxation, improved circulation, and arthritis management. Worn pads reduce heat efficiency and require replacement.
