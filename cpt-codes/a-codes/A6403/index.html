A6403 Sterile gauze>16 <= 48 square in

A Codes

Also known as: sterile gauze pad, sterile gauze dressing

Sterile gauze dressing measuring greater than 16 and up to 48 square inches for moderate wound coverage.

In Plain Language

sterile gauze cloth for medium wounds

Clinical Context

Indicated for moderate-sized surgical or traumatic wounds. Sterile formulation suitable for clean wound environments.

RVU Information

CPT A6403 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
