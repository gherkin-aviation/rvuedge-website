A7014 Compressor nondispos filter

A Codes

Also known as: reusable compressor filter, permanent filter, durable filter

Nondisposable filter for air compressor in nebulizer systems, reusable and cleanable.

In Plain Language

washable filter; cleanable air filter; reusable dust trap

Clinical Context

Used with nebulizer compressors and can be cleaned for extended use. Reduces ongoing supply costs while maintaining equipment performance.

RVU Information

CPT A7014 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
