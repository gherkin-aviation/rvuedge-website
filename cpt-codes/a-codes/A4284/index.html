A4284 Replcmnt breast pump shield

A Codes

Also known as: pump shield, breast shield, flange

Replacement breast pump shield, a component that creates a seal over the areola and breast tissue during milk expression in breast pumping.

In Plain Language

breast cup for pumping; nipple shield

Clinical Context

Critical replacement part for breast pump comfort and effectiveness. Proper shield sizing improves milk expression and prevents nipple trauma.

RVU Information

CPT A4284 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
