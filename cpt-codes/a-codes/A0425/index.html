A0425 Ground mileage

A-Codes

Also known as: ground transport, ambulance mileage, ground ambulance

Ground ambulance transport service for patient movement via non-emergency ground vehicle with basic or advanced life support capability.

Clinical Context

Used for transporting patients requiring emergency or non-emergency medical care via ground ambulance. Typically billed per mile traveled.
