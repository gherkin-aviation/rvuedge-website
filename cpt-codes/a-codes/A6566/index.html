A6566 Grad com garment neck/head

A-Codes

Also known as: compression mask, head compression garment, facial compression

Graduated compression garment for neck and head. Compression covering for facial, neck, and head regions.

Clinical Context

Used for compression therapy on head, face, and neck in lymphedema management, often following head and neck cancer treatment or facial procedures.
