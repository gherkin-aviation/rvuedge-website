A6566 Grad com garment neck/head

A Codes

Also known as: compression mask, head compression garment, facial compression

Graduated compression garment for neck and head. Compression covering for facial, neck, and head regions.

In Plain Language

pressure covering for face and neck; head and neck compression

Clinical Context

Used for compression therapy on head, face, and neck in lymphedema management, often following head and neck cancer treatment or facial procedures.

RVU Information

CPT A6566 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
