A9901 Delivery/set up/dispensing

A-Codes

Also known as: equipment delivery fee, setup service

Durable medical equipment (DME) delivery, setup, or dispensing service fee for providing, assembling, and initializing medical equipment for patient use.

Clinical Context

Used to bill for the professional labor involved in delivering, setting up, and dispensing durable medical equipment to ensure patient safety and proper operation.
