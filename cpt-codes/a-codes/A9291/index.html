A9291 Pres dig cog behav thera fda

A Codes

Also known as: digital CBT app, prescription cognitive therapy app

Prescription digital cognitive behavioral therapy application approved by FDA for treatment of behavioral or mental health conditions.

In Plain Language

app for thinking and behavior training; digital mental health therapy

Clinical Context

Used for cognitive behavioral therapy intervention in depression, anxiety, insomnia, and other mental health conditions as a FDA-approved therapeutic tool.

RVU Information

CPT A9291 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
