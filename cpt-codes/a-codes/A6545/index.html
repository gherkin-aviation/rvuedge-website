A6545 Grad com non-elastic bk surgical

A Codes

Also known as: non-elastic bandage, inelastic compression wrap, surgical wrap

Graduated compression non-elastic bandage for below knee, surgical grade. Inelastic compression wrap for lower leg.

In Plain Language

firm pressure wrap for legs; non-stretch compression bandage

Clinical Context

Used for graduated compression therapy with non-elastic inelastic wrapping on lower legs. Surgical-grade compression for lymphedema and venous insufficiency.

RVU Information

CPT A6545 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
