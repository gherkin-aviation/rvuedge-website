A4709 Acid concentration sol per gallon

A-Codes

Also known as: acid concentrate, acidifying agent, hydrochloric acid

Hydrochloric acid or citric acid concentrate solution supplied in gallon containers used in hemodialysis as an acidifying agent for dialysate preparation and water softening.

Clinical Context

Used to adjust pH and conductivity of dialysate to appropriate ranges. Maintains electrolyte composition essential for hemodialysis efficacy.
