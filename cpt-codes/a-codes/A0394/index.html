A0394 Als intravenous drug therapy supplies

A Codes

Also known as: ALS IV supplies, paramedic drug therapy

Advanced life support ambulance intravenous drug therapy supplies including IV lines, medications, administration sets, and related equipment.

In Plain Language

cost of IV lines and emergency medications

Clinical Context

Used to bill for intravenous medications and supplies administered by paramedics during advanced life support transport.

RVU Information

CPT A0394 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
