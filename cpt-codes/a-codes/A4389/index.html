A4389 Drainable pch w st wear barr

A Codes

Also known as: standard drainable pouch, regular wear drainage bag

Drainable pouch with standard wear skin barrier, a drainable ostomy pouch with integrated standard-wear barrier for regular changeout intervals.

In Plain Language

standard drainable ostomy pouch; regular drainage system

Clinical Context

Combines drainable pouch with standard barrier adhesion. Most commonly used configuration for ostomy patients.

RVU Information

CPT A4389 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
