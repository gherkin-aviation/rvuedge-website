A6591 Urinary catheter suc pump

A Codes

Also known as: urinary catheter, suction catheter, catheter with pump

Urinary catheter with suction pump. Reusable catheter system with suction pump mechanism.

In Plain Language

drainage tube with suction; catheter with pumping device

Clinical Context

Used for urinary drainage with integrated suction pump. Reusable system for patients requiring assisted urine evacuation and drainage.

RVU Information

CPT A6591 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
