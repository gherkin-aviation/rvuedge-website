A2030 Miro3d fibers, per milligrams

A Codes

Also known as: MIRO3D fibers, collagen fibers, fiber particles

MIRO3D FIBERS, Collagen Fiber Particles per milligram, micronized three-dimensional collagen fibers for tissue augmentation and hemostasis.

In Plain Language

collagen fiber powder; hemostatic particles

Clinical Context

Used intra-operatively for tissue augmentation, filling defects, and achieving hemostasis in surgical wounds.

RVU Information

CPT A2030 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
