A6453 Self-adher band w <3"/yd

A Codes

Also known as: self-adherent wrap, cohesive bandage, self-sticking compression

Self-adherent compression bandage, less than 3 inches per yard of length. Provides mild compression without external fasteners.

In Plain Language

sticky compression wrap; self-sticking elastic band

Clinical Context

Used for mild compression support with convenience of self-adhesive application. Common in athletic injury management and post-operative settings where easy application is beneficial.

RVU Information

CPT A6453 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
