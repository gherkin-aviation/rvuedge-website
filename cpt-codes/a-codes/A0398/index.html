A0398 Als routine disposble suppls

A Codes

Also known as: ALS disposable supplies, paramedic supplies

Advanced life support ambulance routine disposable supplies including bandages, dressings, and other consumable medical items.

In Plain Language

cost of bandages and medical supplies used in ambulance

Clinical Context

Used to bill for routine disposable medical supplies used in ALS ambulance transport for patient care.

RVU Information

CPT A0398 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
