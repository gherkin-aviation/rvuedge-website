A9546 Co57/58

A Codes

Also known as: Co-57/58, cobalt isotopes

Cobalt Co-57 and Co-58 labeled compounds used as radiotracers for vitamin B12 absorption studies and various imaging applications.

In Plain Language

vitamin B12 absorption tracer; imaging compound

Clinical Context

Used in gastroenterology for Schilling test assessment of vitamin B12 malabsorption and other diagnostic imaging.

RVU Information

CPT A9546 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
