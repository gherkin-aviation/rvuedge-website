A9546 Co57/58

A-Codes

Also known as: Co-57/58, cobalt isotopes

Cobalt Co-57 and Co-58 labeled compounds used as radiotracers for vitamin B12 absorption studies and various imaging applications.

Clinical Context

Used in gastroenterology for Schilling test assessment of vitamin B12 malabsorption and other diagnostic imaging.
