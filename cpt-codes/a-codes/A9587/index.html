A9587 Gallium ga-68

A Codes

Also known as: Ga-68, gallium PET

Gallium Ga-68 injection used as a positron emission tomography tracer for imaging neuroendocrine tumors and infection.

In Plain Language

neuroendocrine tumor PET tracer; cancer and infection imaging dye

Clinical Context

Used in PET imaging for detection of somatostatin receptor-positive neuroendocrine tumors and select infections.

RVU Information

CPT A9587 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
