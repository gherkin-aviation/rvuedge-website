A7026 Replace chst cmprss sys hose

A Codes

Also known as: chest compression hose, oscillation hose, replacement tubing

Replacement hose for chest compression system used in high-frequency chest wall oscillation therapy.

In Plain Language

vibrating tube; compression system tube; air hose

Clinical Context

Used with high-frequency chest compression systems to deliver pressure waves to the chest wall for secretion clearance in respiratory conditions.

RVU Information

CPT A7026 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
