A6266 Impreg gauze no h20/sal/yard

A Codes

Also known as: impregnated gauze strip, treated gauze

Gauze impregnated with non-saline solution by linear yard for specialty wound packing and dressing.

In Plain Language

special gauze packing

Clinical Context

Used as packing material for tunneling wounds, sinus tracts, or cavitary wounds. Non-saline impregnation provides specific therapeutic properties.

RVU Information

CPT A6266 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
