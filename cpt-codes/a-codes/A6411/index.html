A6411 Non-sterile eye pad

A Codes

Also known as: eye pad, eye dressing

Non-sterile absorbent pad designed for eye protection and moisture absorption.

In Plain Language

patch for eyes

Clinical Context

Applied for eye comfort, protection, and moisture retention in non-infected conditions or during recovery from minor eye procedures.

RVU Information

CPT A6411 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
