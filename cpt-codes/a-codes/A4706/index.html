A4706 Bicarbonate concentration sol per gal

A Codes

Also known as: bicarbonate concentrate, base concentrate, buffer solution

Sodium bicarbonate concentrate solution supplied in gallon containers, used in peritoneal dialysis and hemodialysis to buffer and maintain appropriate pH of dialysate.

In Plain Language

dialysis liquid concentrate; pH balancing solution

Clinical Context

Used to prepare dialysate solutions with appropriate buffer capacity. Maintains acid-base balance during dialysis and prevents metabolic acidosis.

RVU Information

CPT A4706 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
