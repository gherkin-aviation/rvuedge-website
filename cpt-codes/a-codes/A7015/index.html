A7015 Aerosol mask used w nebulize

A-Codes

Also known as: neb mask, aerosol mask, treatment mask

Aerosol mask used with nebulizer systems for delivering aerosolized medications via inhalation.

Clinical Context

Used with nebulizers for targeted aerosol medication delivery. Allows hands-free treatment and is suitable for patients unable to use mouthpieces.
