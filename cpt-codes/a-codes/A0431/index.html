A0431 Rotary wing air transport

A Codes

Also known as: helicopter transport, rotary wing, chopper ambulance, HEMS

Rotary wing aircraft (helicopter) transport service for emergency patient evacuation and inter-facility transfer via helicopter with advanced medical personnel.

In Plain Language

helicopter ambulance; air ambulance service

Clinical Context

Used for critical trauma, stroke, myocardial infarction, or organ donation cases requiring rapid transport where ground transport delays would be life-threatening.

RVU Information

CPT A0431 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
