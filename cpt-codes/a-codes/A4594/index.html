A4594 Neuromod adj rehabilitation mouthpie

A Codes

Also known as: neuromod mouthpiece, rehab mouthpiece

Neuromodulation adjustment rehabilitation mouthpiece component.

In Plain Language

intraoral stimulation device

Clinical Context

Used for intraoral neuromodulation therapy particularly in sleep apnea or other neurological conditions. Mouthpiece allows targeted stimulation.

RVU Information

CPT A4594 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
