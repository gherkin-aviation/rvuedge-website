A9524 I131 serum albumin, diagnosis

A Codes

Also known as: I-131 serum albumin, plasma volume tracer

Iodine I-131 human serum albumin injection used as a diagnostic radiotracer for plasma volume and blood flow studies.

In Plain Language

blood volume imaging dye; plasma tracer

Clinical Context

Used in diagnostic imaging for measurement of plasma volume and assessment of vascular integrity in various clinical conditions.

RVU Information

CPT A9524 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
