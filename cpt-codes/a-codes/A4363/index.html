A4363 Ostomy clamp, replacement

A-Codes +Add-on

Also known as: pouch clamp, ostomy clamp

Ostomy clamp replacement, a reusable or disposable clamp mechanism for closing drainable ostomy pouches to prevent leakage.

Clinical Context

Used to seal open-ended drainable pouches after emptying to prevent leakage between drainage episodes.
