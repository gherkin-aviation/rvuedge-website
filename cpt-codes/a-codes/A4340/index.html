A4340 Indwelling catheter special

A Codes

Also known as: special catheter, coated foley

Indwelling catheter special, a specialized indwelling catheter with specific features such as antimicrobial coating, curved design, or specialty material.

In Plain Language

advanced retention catheter; specialty indwelling tube

Clinical Context

Used for patients with specific catheterization challenges such as strictures, latex allergy, or recurrent infections. Includes antimicrobial-coated options.

RVU Information

CPT A4340 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
