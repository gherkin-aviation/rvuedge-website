A4620 Variable concentration mask

A Codes

Also known as: variable concentration mask, venturi mask

Variable concentration mask for oxygen delivery.

In Plain Language

precision oxygen mask

Clinical Context

Used to deliver specific oxygen concentrations in critical care settings. Variable ports allow precise fraction of inspired oxygen control.

RVU Information

CPT A4620 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
