A4638 Repl batt pulse gen sys

A Codes

Also known as: battery replacement, pulse gen battery, pacemaker battery

A replacement battery for a pulse generator system (implantable device such as pacemaker, implantable cardioverter-defibrillator, or neurostimulator). Essential component for continued device functionality.

In Plain Language

replacement power source for heart device; battery for implanted stimulator

Clinical Context

Used when implanted device batteries deplete. Battery longevity typically 5-10 years depending on device type and settings. Generator replacement is outpatient surgical procedure.

RVU Information

CPT A4638 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
