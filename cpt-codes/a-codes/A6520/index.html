A-Codes

Also known as: night compression glove, sleep compression glove, nighttime hand compression

Graduated compression glove for nighttime use. Compression glove designed for wearing during sleep and rest periods.

Clinical Context

Used for night-time compression therapy on hands. Designed for comfort and compliance during sleep while managing hand lymphedema or edema.
