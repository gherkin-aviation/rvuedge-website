A9700 Echocardiography contrast

A Codes

Also known as: echo contrast, myocardial contrast, bubble agent

Echocardiography contrast agent injection, used to enhance visualization of myocardial perfusion and cardiac chamber boundaries during echocardiographic studies.

In Plain Language

heart ultrasound dye

Clinical Context

Used in contrast-enhanced echocardiography to improve visualization of left ventricular opacification, assess wall motion abnormalities, and evaluate myocardial perfusion in real-time.

RVU Information

CPT A9700 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
