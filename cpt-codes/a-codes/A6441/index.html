A6441 Pad band w>=3" <5"/yd

A-Codes

Also known as: conforming band, elastic wrap

Non-elastic conforming bandage with adhesive backing, width 3 inches or greater and less than 5 inches, per linear yard.

Clinical Context

Applied over dressings or directly to skin for support and compression. Width specified allows various body site applications.
