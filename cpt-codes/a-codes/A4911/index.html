A4911 Drainage bag/bottle

A-Codes

Also known as: drainage bag, urine bag, collection bottle

A disposable drainage bag or collection bottle used for urinary catheter systems or chest/abdominal drain output collection. Allows gravity drainage.

Clinical Context

Essential component of urinary catheter systems. Available with volumetric measurements and sampling ports. Changed when full or per facility protocol.
