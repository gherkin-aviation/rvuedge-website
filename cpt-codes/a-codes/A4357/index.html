A4357 Bedside drainage bag

A-Codes

Also known as: bedside bag, drainage bag

Bedside drainage bag, a large capacity collection container for urinary drainage placed at bedside, designed to prevent backflow.

Clinical Context

Used at night or when patient is bedbound for catheter drainage collection. Large capacity reduces frequent emptying.
