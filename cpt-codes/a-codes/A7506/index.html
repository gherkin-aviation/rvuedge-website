A7506 Hmes/trachvalve adhesivedisk

A Codes

Also known as: HME with adhesive, adhesive HME, stick-on filter

Heat and moisture exchange valve with adhesive disk for attachment to tracheostomy tube.

In Plain Language

stick-on moisture filter; adhesive warming filter; attachment filter

Clinical Context

Used for tracheostomy heat moisture exchange with built-in adhesive backing. Simplifies application and ensures secure attachment to tube.

RVU Information

CPT A7506 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
