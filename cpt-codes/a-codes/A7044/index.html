A7044 Pap oral interface

A Codes

Also known as: PAP oral interface, oral mask, mouth interface

Positive airway pressure oral interface for delivering continuous positive airway pressure via mouth.

In Plain Language

mouth-delivery mask; oral breathing piece; mouth PAP device

Clinical Context

Used with positive airway pressure devices for patients unable to tolerate nasal masks. Delivers pressure directly into mouth during sleep.

RVU Information

CPT A7044 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
