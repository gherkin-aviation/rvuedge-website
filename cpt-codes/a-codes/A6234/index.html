A6234 Hydrocolld drainage <=16 without bdr

A-Codes

Also known as: hydrocolloid dressing, colloidal pad

Hydrocolloid wound dressing without adhesive border measuring 16 square inches or less for moderate exudate management.

Clinical Context

Applied to partial-thickness wounds with light to moderate drainage. Hydrocolloid absorbs fluid while maintaining moist environment and promoting granulation.
