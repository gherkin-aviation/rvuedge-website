A9697 Injection, magtrace per study dose

A-Codes

Also known as: magtrace tracer, magnetic nanoparticle agent

Magtrace radiopharmaceutical injection, supplied per study dose, a magnetized tracer used for sentinel lymph node mapping and perioperative imaging.

Clinical Context

Used in sentinel lymph node detection during breast cancer and melanoma surgery to identify and localize lymph nodes draining the tumor site.
