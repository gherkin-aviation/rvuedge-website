A9999 Dme supply or accessory, not otherwise specified

A Codes

Also known as: DME NOC, equipment NOS

Durable medical equipment (DME) supply or accessory, not otherwise specified, serving as a catch-all code for miscellaneous equipment and supplies.

In Plain Language

medical equipment or supplies not listed elsewhere

Clinical Context

Used for billing miscellaneous medical equipment, supplies, or accessories that do not have a more specific HCPCS code assignment.

RVU Information

CPT A9999 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
