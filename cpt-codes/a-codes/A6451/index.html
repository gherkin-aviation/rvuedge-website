A6451 Mod compres band w>=3"<5"/yd

A-Codes

Also known as: moderate compression wrap, elastic bandage, compression bandage

Moderate compression bandage, 3 to less than 5 inches per yard of length. Provides intermediate-level compression support.

Clinical Context

Used for moderate compression therapy in lymphedema management, chronic venous insufficiency, or edema management. Provides graduated compression at intermediate strength levels.
