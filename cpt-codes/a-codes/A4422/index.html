A4422 Ost pouch absorbent material

A Codes

Also known as: absorbent ostomy pouch, deodorizing pouch

Ostomy pouch with absorbent material for odor and liquid control.

In Plain Language

odor-control pouch; moisture-absorbing bag

Clinical Context

Used for colostomy patients to reduce odor and manage liquid output. Absorbent material in pouch lining reduces environmental odor and improves discretion.

RVU Information

CPT A4422 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
