A7509 Heat & moisture exchange sys

A Codes

Also known as: HME system, moisture exchange system, passive humidifier

Heat and moisture exchange system for tracheostomy tubes providing passive humidification.

In Plain Language

moisture system; humidifying filter system; breathing moisture system

Clinical Context

Complete system for adding humidity and warmth to air breathed through tracheostomy. Used in long-term ventilation and tracheostomy care.

RVU Information

CPT A7509 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
