A4221 Supp non-insulin inferior catheter/week

A Codes

Also known as: non-insulin infusion supplies, drug infusion supplies, weekly supplies

Supply for Non-insulin Infusion Catheter per week, weekly supply components for continuous non-insulin medication infusion including tubing and connectors.

In Plain Language

weekly infusion supplies; medication delivery supplies

Clinical Context

Provided weekly for patients on continuous non-insulin infusions such as antibiotics, anticoagulants, or other parenteral medications.

RVU Information

CPT A4221 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
