A6501 Compres burngarment bodysuit

A-Codes

Also known as: compression bodysuit, burn garment, full-body compression suit

Compression burn garment bodysuit. Full-body compression garment for burn scar management and post-burn wound healing.

Clinical Context

Used in the management of burn scars to prevent hypertrophic scarring and provide graduated compression across the entire body. Essential in post-acute burn recovery programs.
