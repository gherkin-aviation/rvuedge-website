A6261 Wound filler gel/paste /oz

A Codes

Also known as: gel filler, paste filler

Wound filler material in gel or paste form measured by fluid ounce for filling cavitary wounds.

In Plain Language

sticky filling for deep wounds

Clinical Context

Packed into cavitary wounds, sinuses, or tunnels to fill dead space and promote granulation. Gel consistency conforms to irregular shapes.

RVU Information

CPT A6261 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
