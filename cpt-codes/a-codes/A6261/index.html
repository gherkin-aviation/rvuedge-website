A6261 Wound filler gel/paste /oz

A-Codes

Also known as: gel filler, paste filler

Wound filler material in gel or paste form measured by fluid ounce for filling cavitary wounds.

Clinical Context

Packed into cavitary wounds, sinuses, or tunnels to fill dead space and promote granulation. Gel consistency conforms to irregular shapes.
