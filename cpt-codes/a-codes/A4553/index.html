A4553 Nondisp underpads, all sizes

A Codes

Also known as: reusable underpads, washable bed pads

Non-disposable underpads of all sizes for incontinence protection.

In Plain Language

washable protective sheets; mattress protector

Clinical Context

Used for incontinence protection in beds and chairs. Reusable format reduces waste and cost while protecting furniture and linens.

RVU Information

CPT A4553 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
