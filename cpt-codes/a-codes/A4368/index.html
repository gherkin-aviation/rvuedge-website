A4368 Ostomy filter

A-Codes +Add-on

Also known as: charcoal filter, odor filter

Ostomy filter, a charcoal or other filtering device inserted into ostomy pouches to absorb and neutralize odors.

Clinical Context

Inserted into pouches to reduce odor output, improving patient discretion and quality of life.
