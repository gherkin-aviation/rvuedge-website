A4282 Replacement breastpump adpt

A Codes

Also known as: pump adapter, breast pump connector

Replacement breast pump adapter, a connector component that interfaces between the pump motor and collection system.

In Plain Language

breast pump attachment; pump connector piece

Clinical Context

Replacement part for breast pump systems. Necessary when adapter becomes damaged or worn to maintain pump functionality.

RVU Information

CPT A4282 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
