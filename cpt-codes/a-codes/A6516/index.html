A6516 Grad com wrap w strap foo cu

A Codes

Also known as: foot compression wrap, graduated compression strap, adjustable foot wrap

Graduated compression wrap with straps for foot only. Custom-fitted compression device with fastening straps for foot coverage.

In Plain Language

pressure wrap for foot; adjustable foot compression

Clinical Context

Used for graduated compression therapy focused on the foot. Straps provide secure fit and adjustability for optimal compression in foot lymphedema or edema management.

RVU Information

CPT A6516 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
