A6516 Grad com wrap w strap foo cu

A-Codes

Also known as: foot compression wrap, graduated compression strap, adjustable foot wrap

Graduated compression wrap with straps for foot only. Custom-fitted compression device with fastening straps for foot coverage.

Clinical Context

Used for graduated compression therapy focused on the foot. Straps provide secure fit and adjustability for optimal compression in foot lymphedema or edema management.
