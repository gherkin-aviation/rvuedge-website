A6197 Alginate drsg >16 <=48 square in

A Codes

Also known as: alginate dressing, calcium alginate pad

Alginate wound dressing measuring greater than 16 and up to 48 square inches for moderate to heavy exudate management.

In Plain Language

algae-based pad for moderate wounds

Clinical Context

Indicated for moderate to large wounds with significant drainage. Alginate converts to gel upon contact with wound exudate for optimal healing environment.

RVU Information

CPT A6197 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
