A7501 Tracheostoma valve w diaphra

A-Codes

Also known as: trach valve, speaking valve, one-way valve

Tracheostomy valve with diaphragm for speaking and breathing through tracheostomy during mechanical ventilation.

Clinical Context

Used with tracheostomy tubes to allow speech while maintaining mechanical ventilation. Diaphragm closes during exhalation to redirect air through vocal cords.
