A7525 Tracheostomy mask

A Codes

Also known as: trach mask, stoma cover, protective mask

Tracheostomy mask for covering and protecting the stoma opening.

In Plain Language

breathing hole cover; stoma protector; neck cover

Clinical Context

Used to protect tracheostomy stoma from contamination and injury. Improves patient comfort and appearance.

RVU Information

CPT A7525 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
