A6214 Foam drainage > 48 square in with border

A-Codes

Also known as: foam pad with border, large foam dressing with border

Foam wound dressing with adhesive border measuring greater than 48 square inches for large wound protection and fixation.

Clinical Context

Indicated for large wounds requiring self-adhering, integrated-border dressing. Eliminates need for additional tape or secondary securing methods.
