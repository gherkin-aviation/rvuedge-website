A4380 Urinary rubber pouch w fcplt

A Codes

Also known as: rubber urostomy pouch, latex urine pouch

Urinary rubber pouch with face plate, a rubber urostomy drainage pouch for urine collection from urinary diversions with face plate.

In Plain Language

rubber urine bag; latex urinary pouch

Clinical Context

Alternative urostomy pouch in rubber material. Less common due to latex allergy concerns.

RVU Information

CPT A4380 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
