A7049 Epap nasal valve

A-Codes

Also known as: EPAP valve, exhalation valve, nasal valve

Expiratory positive airway pressure nasal valve for delivering pressure during exhalation phase of breathing.

Clinical Context

Used with non-invasive ventilation systems to provide positive pressure during exhalation. Used in bilevel positive airway pressure therapy.
