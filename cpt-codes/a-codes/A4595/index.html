A4595 Tens suppl 2 lead per month

A Codes

Also known as: TENS supplies monthly, two-lead TENS supplies

Transcutaneous electrical nerve stimulation supplies for 2-lead system per month.

In Plain Language

monthly pain relief supplies

Clinical Context

Used for recurring monthly supply of TENS components in 2-lead configurations. Monthly billing ensures continuous therapy availability.

RVU Information

CPT A4595 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
