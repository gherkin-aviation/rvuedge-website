A6603 G com bandge low dn foamchnl

A Codes

Also known as: foam channel bandage, channeled foam wrap, low density compression

Graduated compression bandage with low density foam in channel design for therapeutic compression with lightweight cushioning.

In Plain Language

lightweight padded wrap; channel cushioned bandage; foam pressure bandage

Clinical Context

Used in wound care and lymphedema management where lighter cushioning is preferred. The channel design allows for air circulation and reduces heat buildup during extended wear.

RVU Information

CPT A6603 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation
