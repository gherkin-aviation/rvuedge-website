A4431 Ost pch urine w barrier/tapv

A Codes

Also known as: urine barrier pouch, urostomy barrier tap

Urostomy pouch with skin barrier and faucet valve.

In Plain Language

urinary bag with attached base; barrier urinary collection

Clinical Context

Used for urostomy with integrated skin protection. Barrier reduces need for separate wafer and tap allows controlled drainage.

RVU Information

CPT A4431 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
