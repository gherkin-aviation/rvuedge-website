A0422 Ambulance 02 life sustaining

A-Codes

Also known as: ambulance oxygen, oxygen life support

Ambulance oxygen therapy supplies including oxygen administration, tubing, masks, cannula, and related life-sustaining equipment.

Clinical Context

Used to bill for oxygen therapy and associated life-sustaining supplies administered during ambulance transport.
