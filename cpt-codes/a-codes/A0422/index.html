A0422 Ambulance 02 life sustaining

A Codes

Also known as: ambulance oxygen, oxygen life support

Ambulance oxygen therapy supplies including oxygen administration, tubing, masks, cannula, and related life-sustaining equipment.

In Plain Language

cost of oxygen therapy in ambulance

Clinical Context

Used to bill for oxygen therapy and associated life-sustaining supplies administered during ambulance transport.

RVU Information

CPT A0422 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
