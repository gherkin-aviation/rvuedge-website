A7025 Replace chest compress vest

A-Codes

Also known as: chest compression vest, oscillation vest, replacement vest

Replacement chest compression vest for high-frequency chest wall oscillation therapy systems.

Clinical Context

Used with high-frequency chest wall oscillation devices for airway clearance in cystic fibrosis and other conditions. Replacement component with regular wear cycles.
