A4663 Dialysis blood pressure cuff

A-Codes

Also known as: dialysis BP cuff, dialysis blood pressure cuff

A specialized blood pressure cuff designed for use during hemodialysis procedures to monitor blood pressure in dialysis patients. May have reinforced construction for repeated use.

Clinical Context

Used during hemodialysis sessions to monitor hemodynamic stability and detect intradialytic hypotension or hypertension. Critical for patient safety during treatment.
