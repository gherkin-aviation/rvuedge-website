A4262 Temporary tear duct plug

A Codes

Also known as: temporary plug, tear duct plug, collagen plug

Temporary Tear Duct Plug, a removable occlusive device inserted into lacrimal duct opening for dry eye treatment and tear retention.

In Plain Language

eye duct plug; temporary tear blocker

Clinical Context

Used in dry eye disease treatment to occlude tear ducts temporarily, preserving natural tears and improving ocular comfort.

RVU Information

CPT A4262 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
