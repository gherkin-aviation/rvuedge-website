A6253 Absorpt drainage > 48 square in without b

A Codes

Also known as: absorbent dressing, large super absorber pad

Absorbent wound dressing without border measuring greater than 48 square inches for large exudate management.

In Plain Language

moisture-absorbing pad for large wounds

Clinical Context

Applied to large, heavily draining wounds. High absorption capacity accommodates significant exudate volumes.

RVU Information

CPT A6253 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
