A4349 Disposable male external cat

A-Codes

Also known as: disposable external catheter, condom catheter

Disposable male external catheter, a single-use condom-type urinary catheter for external penile drainage without catheterization.

Clinical Context

Non-invasive urine collection for incontinent males. Single-use improves hygiene and reduces infection risk compared to reusable devices.
