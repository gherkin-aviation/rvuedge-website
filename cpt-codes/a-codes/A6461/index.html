A6461 Synthetic drsg >16<=48 square in

A-Codes

Also known as: synthetic dressing, non-adherent pad, wound pad

Synthetic wound dressing, greater than 16 square inches up to 48 square inches. Non-adherent covering for moderate-sized wounds.

Clinical Context

Used for moderate-sized wounds, post-surgical sites, and lacerations. Synthetic dressings maintain a moist wound environment while providing protection.
