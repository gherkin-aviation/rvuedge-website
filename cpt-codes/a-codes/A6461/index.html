A6461 Synthetic drsg >16<=48 square in

A Codes

Also known as: synthetic dressing, non-adherent pad, wound pad

Synthetic wound dressing, greater than 16 square inches up to 48 square inches. Non-adherent covering for moderate-sized wounds.

In Plain Language

medium wound pad; non-stick bandage

Clinical Context

Used for moderate-sized wounds, post-surgical sites, and lacerations. Synthetic dressings maintain a moist wound environment while providing protection.

RVU Information

CPT A6461 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
