A7521 trachea/laryn tube cuffed

A-Codes

Also known as: cuffed trach tube, inflatable tube, sealed tube

Tracheostomy or laryngeal tube with cuff for sealed airway and mechanical ventilation.

Clinical Context

Used for mechanical ventilation and aspiration prevention. Cuff creates seal preventing air leak and providing pressure support.
