A7521 trachea/laryn tube cuffed

A Codes

Also known as: cuffed trach tube, inflatable tube, sealed tube

Tracheostomy or laryngeal tube with cuff for sealed airway and mechanical ventilation.

In Plain Language

sealing breathing tube; puffed-up tube; sealed airway tube

Clinical Context

Used for mechanical ventilation and aspiration prevention. Cuff creates seal preventing air leak and providing pressure support.

RVU Information

CPT A7521 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
