A7000 Disposable canister for pump

A Codes

Also known as: NPWT canister, suction canister, disposal container

Disposable canister for use with negative pressure wound therapy pump systems.

In Plain Language

suction collection cup; drainage collection container; medical waste container

Clinical Context

Used with negative pressure wound therapy devices to collect wound drainage and exudate. Single-use disposable design prevents cross-contamination and requires regular replacement.

RVU Information

CPT A7000 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
