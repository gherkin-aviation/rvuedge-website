A7000 Disposable canister for pump

A-Codes

Also known as: NPWT canister, suction canister, disposal container

Disposable canister for use with negative pressure wound therapy pump systems.

Clinical Context

Used with negative pressure wound therapy devices to collect wound drainage and exudate. Single-use disposable design prevents cross-contamination and requires regular replacement.
