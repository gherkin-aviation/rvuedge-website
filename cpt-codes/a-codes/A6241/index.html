A6241 Hydrocolloid drainage filler dry

A-Codes

Also known as: hydrocolloid filler, hydrocolloid powder

Hydrocolloid wound filler in dry powder or granule form for absorbing wound drainage and promoting healing.

Clinical Context

Applied to cavitary wounds and sinuses to absorb exudate while maintaining moist healing environment. Conforms to irregular wound shapes.
