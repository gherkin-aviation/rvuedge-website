A0225 Neonatal emergency transport

A-Codes

Also known as: neonatal emergency transport, newborn NICU transport

Neonatal emergency medical transport service providing advanced life support care during interfacility transport of critically ill newborn.

Clinical Context

Used for emergency transport of critically ill neonates requiring advanced life support and specialized neonatal equipment.
