A7008 Disposable nebulizer prefill

A-Codes

Also known as: prefilled SVN, prefilled nebulizer, convenient neb

Disposable small volume nebulizer with prefilled medication reservoir for aerosol drug delivery.

Clinical Context

Used for rapid aerosol medication delivery without manual preparation. Prefilled design increases convenience and reduces medication waste in acute settings.
