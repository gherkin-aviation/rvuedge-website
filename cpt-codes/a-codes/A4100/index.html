A4100 Nosht skin sub fda clrd not otherwise specified

A Codes

Also known as: unlisted skin substitute, noncovered substitute, miscellaneous skin substitute

Noncovered Skin Substitute, FDA-cleared, not otherwise specified, a biologic or synthetic skin replacement not specifically listed in Healthcare Common Procedure Coding System.

In Plain Language

unlisted skin graft; non-standard skin dressing

Clinical Context

Used for new or emerging skin substitute technologies not described by specific codes, requiring supplemental documentation.

RVU Information

CPT A4100 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
