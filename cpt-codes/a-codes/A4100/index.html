A-Codes

Also known as: unlisted skin substitute, noncovered substitute, miscellaneous skin substitute

Noncovered Skin Substitute, FDA-cleared, not otherwise specified, a biologic or synthetic skin replacement not specifically listed in Healthcare Common Procedure Coding System.

Clinical Context

Used for new or emerging skin substitute technologies not described by specific codes, requiring supplemental documentation.
