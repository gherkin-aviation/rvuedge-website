A4430 Ost urine pch w b/bltin conv

A Codes

Also known as: urine pouch with backing plate, urostomy backed convex

Urostomy pouch with backplate and built-in convex feature.

In Plain Language

urinary bag with stiff backing; convex backed pouch

Clinical Context

Used for urostomy patients requiring additional support. Backplate provides structural integrity while built-in convex design manages anatomy.

RVU Information

CPT A4430 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
