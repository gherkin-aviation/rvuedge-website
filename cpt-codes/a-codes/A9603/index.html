A9603 Injection, pafolacianine, 0.1 milligrams

A Codes

Also known as: Lumicell, pafolacianine

Pafolacianine hydrochloride injection at 0.1 milligram concentration used as a fluorescent imaging agent for surgical guidance.

In Plain Language

surgical fluorescence imaging dye; tumor detection fluorophore

Clinical Context

Used as an intraoperative fluorescent imaging agent to help surgeons identify and visualize cancerous tissue during surgical procedures.

RVU Information

CPT A9603 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
