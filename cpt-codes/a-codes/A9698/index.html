A9698 Non-rad contrast materialnoc

A Codes

Also known as: contrast agent, imaging dye

Non-radioactive contrast material, not otherwise classified, used for diagnostic imaging to enhance visualization of anatomical structures and vasculature.

In Plain Language

dye for medical imaging

Clinical Context

Used in radiologic procedures when standard iodinated or gadolinium contrast agents are not appropriate, providing flexibility for specialized imaging protocols.

RVU Information

CPT A9698 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
