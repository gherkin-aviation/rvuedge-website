A9698 Non-rad contrast materialnoc

A-Codes

Also known as: contrast agent, imaging dye

Non-radioactive contrast material, not otherwise classified, used for diagnostic imaging to enhance visualization of anatomical structures and vasculature.

Clinical Context

Used in radiologic procedures when standard iodinated or gadolinium contrast agents are not appropriate, providing flexibility for specialized imaging protocols.
