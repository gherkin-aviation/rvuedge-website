A4465 Non-elastic extremity binder

A Codes

Also known as: extremity binder, compression wrap

Non-elastic extremity binder for compression and support.

In Plain Language

arm or leg wrap; support sleeve

Clinical Context

Used to provide support and compression to extremities following surgery or for edema management. Non-elastic design provides stable, consistent compression.

RVU Information

CPT A4465 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
