A4624 Tracheal suction tube

A Codes

Also known as: suction tube, trach suction

A disposable or reusable tube inserted through a tracheostomy or endotracheal tube for suctioning secretions from the trachea and lower airway. Used to maintain airway patency by removing mucus and fluids.

In Plain Language

tube for clearing mucus from breathing tube; suction device for trach

Clinical Context

Used in patients with tracheostomies or endotracheal intubation to remove secretions and prevent airway obstruction. Essential for respiratory hygiene in ICU and post-operative settings.

RVU Information

CPT A4624 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
