A4649 Surgical supplies

A Codes

Also known as: surgical supplies, misc supplies, surgical materials

Miscellaneous surgical supplies and materials not otherwise specified. General category for small surgical consumables and instruments.

In Plain Language

various surgical tools and materials; operating room supplies

Clinical Context

Catch-all category for surgical items not specifically classified. Used in operating rooms, surgical suites, and procedural areas.

RVU Information

CPT A4649 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
