A4648 Implantable tissue marker

A Codes

Also known as: fiducial marker, tumor marker, implanted marker

A small implantable marker or fiducial used for radiation therapy planning and targeting. Placed within or near tumors to improve radiotherapy accuracy and tracking.

In Plain Language

tiny marker implanted near tumor; tracking device for radiation therapy

Clinical Context

Used in image-guided radiation therapy (IGRT) to improve accuracy of treatment planning and targeting. Inserted percutaneously or endoscopically before radiation therapy.

RVU Information

CPT A4648 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
