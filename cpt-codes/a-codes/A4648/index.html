A4648 Implantable tissue marker

A-Codes

Also known as: fiducial marker, tumor marker, implanted marker

A small implantable marker or fiducial used for radiation therapy planning and targeting. Placed within or near tumors to improve radiotherapy accuracy and tracking.

Clinical Context

Used in image-guided radiation therapy (IGRT) to improve accuracy of treatment planning and targeting. Inserted percutaneously or endoscopically before radiation therapy.
