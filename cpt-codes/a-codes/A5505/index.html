A5505 Diab shoe with metatarsal bar

A Codes

Also known as: diabetic metatarsal bar shoe, met bar diabetic shoe

Diabetic shoe with metatarsal bar support positioned under the metatarsal heads to offload pressure from high-risk areas.

In Plain Language

diabetic shoe with foot bar support; pressure relief shoe for diabetics

Clinical Context

Used for patients with diabetes experiencing metatarsal pain, plantar ulcers, or pressure points. The bar redistributes pressure proximally and provides support during gait.

RVU Information

CPT A5505 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
