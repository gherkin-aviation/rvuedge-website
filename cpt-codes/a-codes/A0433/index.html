A0433 Als 2

A Codes

Also known as: ALS 2, Advanced Life Support Level 2, advanced paramedic

Advanced Life Support Level 2 ambulance response, a ground-based emergency transport service with enhanced paramedic capabilities including advanced procedures.

In Plain Language

advanced ambulance service; high-level paramedic transport

Clinical Context

Used for patients requiring Advanced Life Support Level 2 interventions such as advanced airway management, cardiac pacing, or complex medication administration.

RVU Information

CPT A0433 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
