A9154 Artificial saliva, 1 milliliters

A Codes

Also known as: artificial saliva, saliva substitute, moisturizer

Artificial saliva one milliliter for xerostomia and dry mouth relief.

In Plain Language

fake saliva; mouth moisturizer; oral lubricant

Clinical Context

Used for dry mouth management in cancer patients and those with xerostomia. Provides moisture and comfort to oral tissues.

RVU Information

CPT A9154 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
