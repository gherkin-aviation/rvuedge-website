A9502 Tc99m tetrofosmin

A-Codes

Also known as: Myoview, tetrofosmin, Tc-99m tetrofosmin

Technetium Tc-99m tetrofosmin injection used as a radiotracer for myocardial perfusion imaging and cardiac viability assessment.

Clinical Context

Used in cardiac imaging to assess myocardial perfusion, detect coronary artery disease, and evaluate cardiac viability in ischemic heart disease.
