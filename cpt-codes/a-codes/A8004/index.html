A8004 Repl soft interface, helmet

A Codes +Add-on

Also known as: helmet padding, interface pad, cushion replacement

Replacement soft interface padding for protective helmet.

In Plain Language

helmet cushion; padding replacement; comfort pad

Clinical Context

Replacement padding for soft protective helmets due to normal wear and degradation. Maintains comfort and proper fit.

RVU Information

CPT A8004 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
