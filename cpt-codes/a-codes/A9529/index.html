A9529 I131 iodide sol, diagnosis

A Codes

Also known as: I-131 solution diagnostic, thyroid uptake tracer

Iodine I-131 sodium iodide solution injection used diagnostically for thyroid uptake and imaging studies.

In Plain Language

diagnostic thyroid imaging dye; thyroid function measurement

Clinical Context

Used for diagnostic thyroid uptake measurement and thyroid imaging in evaluation of thyroid disorders.

RVU Information

CPT A9529 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
