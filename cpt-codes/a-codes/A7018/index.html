A7018 Water distilled with nebulizer

A-Codes

Also known as: distilled water, sterile water, neb water

Sterile distilled water for use with nebulizers in preparing aerosol medication solutions.

Clinical Context

Used to prepare nebulizer solutions and as diluent for concentrated medications. Sterile formulation prevents bacterial contamination in respiratory therapy.
