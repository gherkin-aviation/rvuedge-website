A4306 Drug delivery system <=50 milliliters

A-Codes

Also known as: small infusion pump, portable medication reservoir

Drug delivery system with capacity of 50 milliliters or less, a portable infusion pump reservoir or container for smaller volume medication delivery.

Clinical Context

Used for patients requiring portable medication delivery with smaller volumes. Suitable for outpatient or home-based infusion therapy.
