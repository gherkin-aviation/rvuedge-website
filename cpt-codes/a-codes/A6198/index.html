A6198 Alginate dressing > 48 square in

A Codes

Also known as: alginate dressing, large alginate pad

Alginate wound dressing measuring greater than 48 square inches for large wound coverage and heavy drainage absorption.

In Plain Language

algae-based pad for large wounds

Clinical Context

Applied to extensive wounds with high drainage volume. Superior absorption capacity and conformability make it ideal for irregular wound shapes.

RVU Information

CPT A6198 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
