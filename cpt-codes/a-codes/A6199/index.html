A6199 Alginate drsg wound filler

A-Codes

Also known as: alginate filler, alginate packing

Alginate wound filler in powder, paste, or granule form for packing cavitary and tunneling wounds.

Clinical Context

Packed into wound cavities and sinus tracts to fill dead space while absorbing drainage and maintaining moist conditions for optimal healing.
