A6606 G com bandage padded textile

A Codes

Also known as: padded textile bandage, textile padded wrap, cloth compression

Graduated compression bandage with padded textile construction for therapeutic compression combined with protective cushioning.

In Plain Language

padded fabric wrap; cushioned cloth bandage; soft textile wrap

Clinical Context

Used for compression therapy in venous disease and lymphedema with textile-based padding. Provides breathability and comfort with sustained compression for daily wear.

RVU Information

CPT A6606 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
