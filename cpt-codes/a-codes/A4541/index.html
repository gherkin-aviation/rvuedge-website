A4541 Monthly supp use with e0733

A Codes

Also known as: E0733 supplies, monthly stimulator supplies

Monthly supplies for use with E0733 electrical stimulator.

In Plain Language

monthly pain relief kit

Clinical Context

Used as recurring monthly supplies for patients using E0733 devices. Ensures continuous availability of consumable components for uninterrupted therapy.

RVU Information

CPT A4541 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
