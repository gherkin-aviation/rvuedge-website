A7526 Tracheostomy tube collar

A Codes

Also known as: tube collar, tube holder, securing collar

Tracheostomy tube collar for securing and stabilizing tube position.

In Plain Language

tube holder strap; securing neck band; position keeper

Clinical Context

Used to stabilize tracheostomy tube position and prevent accidental dislodgement. Adjustable design fits various patient sizes.

RVU Information

CPT A7526 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
