A9300 Exercise equipment

A Codes

Also known as: therapy equipment, exercise device

Exercise equipment used for physical therapy, rehabilitation, or fitness purposes to improve mobility and strength.

In Plain Language

equipment to help exercise; fitness device

Clinical Context

Used in physical therapy, occupational therapy, and rehabilitation settings to facilitate therapeutic exercise and improve functional mobility.

RVU Information

CPT A9300 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
