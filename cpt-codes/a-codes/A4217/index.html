A4217 Sterile water/saline, 500 milliliters

A-Codes

Also known as: saline solution, sterile saline, irrigation fluid

Sterile Water or Saline 500 milliliters, a medium-volume sterile fluid for wound irrigation, equipment cleaning, and clinical care applications.

Clinical Context

Used for extensive wound irrigation, catheter care, equipment cleansing, and medication reconstitution.
