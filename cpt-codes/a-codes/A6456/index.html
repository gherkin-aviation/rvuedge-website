A6456 Zinc paste band w >=3"<5"/yd

A-Codes

Also known as: zinc paste bandage, calamine bandage, medicated compression wrap

Zinc paste compression bandage, 3 to less than 5 inches per yard of length. Provides compression with therapeutic zinc coating.

Clinical Context

Used for leg ulcer management, venous insufficiency, and skin irritation. The zinc paste provides both compression and anti-inflammatory benefits while treating underlying dermatologic conditions.
