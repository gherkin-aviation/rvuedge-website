A0428 Bls

A Codes

Also known as: BLS, Basic Life Support, EMT transport

Basic Life Support ambulance transport service provided by Emergency Medical Technicians trained in Basic Life Support protocols and procedures.

In Plain Language

basic ambulance service; EMT transport

Clinical Context

Used for patients requiring Basic Life Support intervention such as oxygen administration, splinting, or non-emergency medical transport.

RVU Information

CPT A0428 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
