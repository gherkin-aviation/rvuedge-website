A4246 Betadine/phisohex solution

A Codes

Also known as: betadine solution, iodine solution, antiseptic prep

Betadine or Phisohex Solution, an iodine-based antiseptic solution for comprehensive skin and wound preparation prior to medical procedures.

In Plain Language

iodine skin cleaner; antiseptic solution

Clinical Context

Used for surgical site preparation, wound cleansing, and skin antisepsis in surgical and clinical settings.

RVU Information

CPT A4246 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
