A7048 Vacuum drainage bottle/tube kit

A-Codes

Also known as: drainage kit, vacuum bottle kit, drain collection kit

Vacuum drain bottle and tubing kit for collecting fluid from surgical sites and wound drainage.

Clinical Context

Used post-operatively for collecting fluids from surgical wounds and drains. Self-contained system maintains suction while collecting drainage.
