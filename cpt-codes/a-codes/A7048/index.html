A7048 Vacuum drainage bottle/tube kit

A Codes

Also known as: drainage kit, vacuum bottle kit, drain collection kit

Vacuum drain bottle and tubing kit for collecting fluid from surgical sites and wound drainage.

In Plain Language

fluid collection system; drainage bottle kit; suction collection kit

Clinical Context

Used post-operatively for collecting fluids from surgical wounds and drains. Self-contained system maintains suction while collecting drainage.

RVU Information

CPT A7048 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
