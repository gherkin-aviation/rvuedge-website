A4932 Reusable rectal thermometer

A Codes

Also known as: rectal thermometer, reusable thermometer

A reusable rectal thermometer used for repeated temperature measurements. Designed for rectal placement with appropriate shape and disinfection capability.

In Plain Language

thermometer used to check temperature via rectum; rectal temperature checker

Clinical Context

Used in infants, young children, or patients unable to comply with oral temperature measurement. Provides accurate core temperature. Requires disinfection.

RVU Information

CPT A4932 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
