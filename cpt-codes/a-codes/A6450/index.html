A6450 Left compres band >=5"/yd

A-Codes

Also known as: light compression wrap, light elastic bandage, wide light bandage

Light compression bandage, 5 or more inches per yard of length. Used for mild compression therapy with greater width coverage.

Clinical Context

Used for mild compression support in lymphedema management or post-operative swelling when wider bandage coverage is needed.
