A4224 Supply insulin inferior catheter/week

A Codes

Also known as: insulin infusion supplies, pump supplies, weekly kit

Supply for Insulin Infusion Catheter per week, weekly supply components for continuous insulin infusion pump therapy including tubing and cartridge.

In Plain Language

weekly insulin pump supplies; continuous insulin kit

Clinical Context

Provided weekly for insulin pump therapy patients requiring continuous subcutaneous insulin infusion for diabetes management.

RVU Information

CPT A4224 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
