A4650 Implant radiation dosimeter

A Codes

Also known as: radiation dosimeter, dose monitor, implanted dosimeter

An implantable radiation dosimeter or dose measurement device inserted into or near treatment area to monitor and measure radiation dose delivery during brachytherapy or external beam radiation.

In Plain Language

tiny device measuring radiation exposure; radiation detector implant

Clinical Context

Used in brachytherapy and radiation therapy to verify dose delivery. Helps ensure treatment plans are delivered as prescribed and protects normal tissues.

RVU Information

CPT A4650 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
