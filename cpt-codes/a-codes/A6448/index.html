A6448 Left compres band <3"/yd

A Codes

Also known as: compression wrap, elastic band

Light compression elastic bandage, width less than 3 inches, per linear yard for graduated support.

In Plain Language

stretchy support wrap

Clinical Context

Applied to extremities for mild compression therapy in edema management, injury support, and post-operative care.

RVU Information

CPT A6448 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
