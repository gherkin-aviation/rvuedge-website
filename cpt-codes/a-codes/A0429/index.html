A0429 Bls-emergency

A-Codes

Also known as: BLS emergency, emergency BLS, basic emergency transport

Basic Life Support emergency ambulance response service, a rapid dispatch of Emergency Medical Technicians for urgent patient transport needs.

Clinical Context

Used for emergency-dispatched Basic Life Support transports where rapid response is needed for acute patient conditions.
