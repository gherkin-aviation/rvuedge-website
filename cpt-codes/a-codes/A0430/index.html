A0430 Fixed wing air transport

A Codes

Also known as: air ambulance, fixed wing air transport, airplane transport

Fixed wing aircraft transport service for long-distance patient transfer via airplane, enabling rapid inter-facility or long-distance medical transport.

In Plain Language

airplane ambulance service; long distance air transport

Clinical Context

Used for transferring patients over long distances between facilities when ground transport is impractical or when time-sensitive intervention is required.

RVU Information

CPT A0430 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
