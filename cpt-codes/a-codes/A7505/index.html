A7505 Hmes or trachea valve housing

A-Codes

Also known as: HME housing, valve housing, filter housing

Heat and moisture exchange valve housing for tracheostomy heat moisture exchanger systems.

Clinical Context

Structural component of heat and moisture exchange systems. Houses the filtering element and attaches to tracheostomy tube.
