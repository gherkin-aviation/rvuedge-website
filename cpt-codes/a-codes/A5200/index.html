A5200 Percutaneous catheter anchor

A Codes

Also known as: catheter anchor, catheter securement device, tube holder

Percutaneous (through-the-skin) anchor or fixation device used to secure and stabilize catheters at the insertion site.

In Plain Language

catheter holder; tube attachment patch

Clinical Context

Applied at skin insertion sites for central lines, peripherally inserted central catheters, or other percutaneous devices to prevent migration, tension, and infection. Standard in critical care and home infusion.

RVU Information

CPT A5200 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
