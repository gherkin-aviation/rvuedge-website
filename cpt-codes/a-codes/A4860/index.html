A4860 Disposable catheter tips

A-Codes

Also known as: catheter tip, connector tip, catheter connector

Disposable sterile catheter tips or connectors used in catheterization procedures or to replace damaged catheter ends. Maintains sterility of catheter systems.

Clinical Context

Used when catheter tips are damaged, occluded, or require replacement. Maintains sterile closed system integrity in patients with indwelling catheters.
