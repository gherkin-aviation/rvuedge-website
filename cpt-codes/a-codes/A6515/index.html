A6515 Grad com wrap w str fu le cu

A Codes

Also known as: compression wrap, graduated compression strap, adjustable compression wrap

Graduated compression wrap with straps for lower leg and foot. Custom-fitted compression system with fastening straps.

In Plain Language

pressure wrap for legs; adjustable leg compression

Clinical Context

Used for graduated compression therapy on lower legs and feet. Straps allow adjustment for optimal compression and secure fit during treatment.

RVU Information

CPT A6515 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
