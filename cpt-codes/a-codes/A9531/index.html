A9531 I131 max 100uci

A Codes

Also known as: I-131 low-dose therapy, radioactive iodine treatment

Iodine I-131 sodium iodide therapeutic dose not exceeding 100 microcurie activity for treatment of thyroid disease.

In Plain Language

low-dose radioactive thyroid treatment; thyroid therapy

Clinical Context

Used for therapeutic treatment of hyperthyroidism and thyroid cancer with limited radioactive iodine activity.

RVU Information

CPT A9531 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
