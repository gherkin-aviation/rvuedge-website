A6220 Gauze >16 <=48 square in with bordr

A Codes

Also known as: gauze pad with border, sterile gauze with adhesive border

Sterile gauze dressing with adhesive border measuring greater than 16 and up to 48 square inches for moderate wound coverage.

In Plain Language

sticky-edge sterile gauze for medium wounds

Clinical Context

Indicated for moderate wounds requiring sterile, self-adhering coverage. Border provides edge seal and securement without auxiliary fixation.

RVU Information

CPT A6220 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
