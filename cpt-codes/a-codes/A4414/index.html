A4414 Ost sknbar without conv<=4 square in

A-Codes

Also known as: flat barrier small, standard flat barrier

Ostomy skin barrier without convex surface, 4 square inches or less.

Clinical Context

Used for patients with small to moderate stomas requiring standard flat barrier protection. Appropriate for most colostomies and ileostomies with adequate peristomal contour.
