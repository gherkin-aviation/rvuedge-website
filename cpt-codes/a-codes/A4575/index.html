A4575 Hyperbaric o2 chamber disps

A Codes

Also known as: hyperbaric chamber supplies, HBOT supplies

Hyperbaric oxygen chamber disposable supplies.

In Plain Language

high pressure oxygen therapy supplies

Clinical Context

Used as consumable supplies for hyperbaric oxygen therapy. Disposable components ensure infection control and treatment safety.

RVU Information

CPT A4575 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
