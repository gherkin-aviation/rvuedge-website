A9275 Disp home glucose monitor

A Codes

Also known as: glucometer, glucose meter, blood glucose monitor

Disposable home glucose monitor for blood glucose testing and diabetes management.

In Plain Language

blood sugar checker; glucose tester; diabetes monitor

Clinical Context

Used by diabetic patients for self-monitoring blood glucose levels at home. Essential tool for diabetes control and management.

RVU Information

CPT A9275 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
