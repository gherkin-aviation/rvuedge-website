A4280 Brst prsths adhsv attchmnt

A-Codes

Also known as: breast form adhesive, prosthesis attachment

Breast prosthesis adhesive attachment device, a component used to secure a breast prosthesis to the skin or clothing after mastectomy.

Clinical Context

Used post-mastectomy to secure external breast prosthesis for comfort and appearance. Allows for proper positioning and prevents shifting.
