A7035 Pos airway press headgear

A-Codes +Add-on

Also known as: PAP headgear, mask headgear, head support

Positive airway pressure headgear for securing mask during continuous positive airway pressure and non-invasive ventilation therapy.

Clinical Context

Used to hold and stabilize positive airway pressure masks during therapy. Adjustable design accommodates different head sizes and ensures proper mask position.
