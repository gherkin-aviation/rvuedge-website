A7016 Nebulizer dome & mouthpiece

A Codes

Also known as: neb mouthpiece, dome assembly, aerosol interface

Nebulizer dome and mouthpiece assembly for delivering aerosolized medications during inhalation therapy.

In Plain Language

breathing piece; medicine delivery cup; aerosol mouthpiece

Clinical Context

Used with nebulizers as the primary interface for medication inhalation. The dome shape directs aerosol into airways during breathing.

RVU Information

CPT A7016 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
