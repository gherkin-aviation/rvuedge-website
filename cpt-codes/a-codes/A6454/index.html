A6454 Self-adher band w>=3" <5"/yd

A Codes

Also known as: self-adherent wrap, cohesive bandage, elastic self-sticking wrap

Self-adherent compression bandage, 3 to less than 5 inches per yard of length. Self-sticking with moderate compression.

In Plain Language

sticky compression wrap; self-sticking elastic band

Clinical Context

Used for moderate compression support with self-adhesive convenience. Ideal for athletic injuries, post-operative swelling, and edema management where tape-free application is preferred.

RVU Information

CPT A6454 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
