A6454 Self-adher band w>=3" <5"/yd

A-Codes

Also known as: self-adherent wrap, cohesive bandage, elastic self-sticking wrap

Self-adherent compression bandage, 3 to less than 5 inches per yard of length. Self-sticking with moderate compression.

Clinical Context

Used for moderate compression support with self-adhesive convenience. Ideal for athletic injuries, post-operative swelling, and edema management where tape-free application is preferred.
