A4322 Irrigation syringe

A Codes

Also known as: irrigation syringe, bladder syringe

Irrigation syringe, a sterile syringe used for manual irrigation of catheters or bladders, with capacity typically ranging from 30 to 60 milliliters.

In Plain Language

hand-operated irrigation pump; manual irrigation syringe

Clinical Context

Used for manual bladder or catheter irrigation to maintain patency, remove clots, or deliver therapeutic solutions.

RVU Information

CPT A4322 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
