A6262 Wound filler dry form / gram

A-Codes

Also known as: powder filler, dry filler

Wound filler material in dry powder or granule form measured by gram for absorbing drainage.

Clinical Context

Applied to cavitary wounds to absorb exudate while filling dead space. Dry form facilitates storage and customized application volumes.
