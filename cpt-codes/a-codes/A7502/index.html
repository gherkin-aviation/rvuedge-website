A7502 Replacement diaphragm/fplate

A Codes +Add-on

Also known as: valve diaphragm, face plate, valve replacement

Replacement diaphragm and face plate for tracheostomy valve.

In Plain Language

breathing flap; valve piece; speaking part

Clinical Context

Replacement components for tracheostomy speaking valves. Diaphragm requires regular replacement due to wear and degradation.

RVU Information

CPT A7502 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
