A6224 Gauze > 48 in no with sal without b

A Codes

Also known as: sterile gauze, large dry gauze pad

Non-impregnated sterile gauze dressing measuring greater than 48 square inches for large dry wound coverage.

In Plain Language

sterile gauze cloth for large wounds

Clinical Context

Applied to extensive clean wounds. Non-impregnated formulation provides versatile base for adjunctive wound care therapies.

RVU Information

CPT A6224 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
