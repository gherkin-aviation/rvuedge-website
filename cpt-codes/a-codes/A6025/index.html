A6025 Silicone gel sheet, each

A Codes

Also known as: silicone gel sheet, scar treatment sheet

Silicone gel sheet dressing applied topically to scars to improve appearance and reduce scar tissue formation.

In Plain Language

silicone patch for scars; scar-softening sheet

Clinical Context

Applied over hypertrophic or keloid scars to reduce thickness, soften tissue, and improve cosmetic appearance. Effective for burn scars, surgical scars, and traumatic scars.

RVU Information

CPT A6025 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
