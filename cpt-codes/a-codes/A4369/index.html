A4369 Skin barrier liquid per oz

A-Codes

Also known as: skin barrier liquid, barrier lotion

Skin barrier liquid per ounce, a liquid protective barrier product applied to skin around stoma to prevent irritation and maceration.

Clinical Context

Applied to periostomy skin to prevent contact dermatitis and breakdown from effluent exposure. Billable per fluid ounce.
