A4223 Infusion supplies without pump

A-Codes

Also known as: infusion supplies, non-pump infusion, supply kit

Infusion Supplies without Pump, consumable supply components for non-pump infusion delivery including tubing, connectors, and dressing changes.

Clinical Context

Used for continuous or intermittent non-pump infusions such as gravity-fed medications, antitoxins, or supportive fluids.
