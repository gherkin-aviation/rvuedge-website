A4223 Infusion supplies without pump

A Codes

Also known as: infusion supplies, non-pump infusion, supply kit

Infusion Supplies without Pump, consumable supply components for non-pump infusion delivery including tubing, connectors, and dressing changes.

In Plain Language

infusion supplies without pump; basic infusion kit

Clinical Context

Used for continuous or intermittent non-pump infusions such as gravity-fed medications, antitoxins, or supportive fluids.

RVU Information

CPT A4223 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
