A9572 Indium in-111 pentetreotide

A Codes

Also known as: OctreoScan, pentetreotide, In-111 octreotide

Indium In-111 pentetreotide injection used as a radiotracer for imaging neuroendocrine tumors and somatostatin receptor-positive lesions.

In Plain Language

neuroendocrine tumor imaging dye; somatostatin receptor tracer

Clinical Context

Used in oncology imaging for detection and localization of neuroendocrine tumors including carcinoid and gastroenteropancreatic neuroendocrine tumors.

RVU Information

CPT A9572 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
