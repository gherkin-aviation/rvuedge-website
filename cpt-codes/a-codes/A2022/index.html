A2022 Innovabrn/innovamatx xl sq cm

A Codes

Also known as: INNOVABRANE XL, large collagen matrix, extended matrix

INNOVABRANE/INNOVAMATRIX XL, Acellular Collagen Matrix per square centimeter, an extended surface-area collagen scaffold for large wound coverage.

In Plain Language

large collagen patch; wide-area wound dressing

Clinical Context

Used for extensive wounds and burns where large surface area collagen scaffold provides comprehensive tissue regeneration support.

RVU Information

CPT A2022 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
