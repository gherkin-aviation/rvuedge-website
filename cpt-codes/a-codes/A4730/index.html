A4730 Fistula cannulation set, each

A Codes

Also known as: fistula needle set, AV fistula set, cannulation kit

A sterile, disposable cannulation set used to access arteriovenous fistulas for hemodialysis. Includes needles for arterial and venous puncture with tubing and connecting ports.

In Plain Language

needle set for dialysis access; fistula needles and tubes

Clinical Context

Used at each hemodialysis session for vascular access. Arterial needle removes blood; venous needle returns blood after dialysis. Must be sterile to prevent infection.

RVU Information

CPT A4730 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
