A4390 Drainable pch ex wear convex

A Codes

Also known as: convex extended wear pouch, convex drainable bag

Drainable pouch with extended wear convex skin barrier, a drainable ostomy pouch with convex extended-wear barrier for difficult contours.

In Plain Language

curved durable drainable pouch; shaped drainage system

Clinical Context

Convex barrier shapes to flush stomas or retracts. Extended wear reduces frequent changes needed.

RVU Information

CPT A4390 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
