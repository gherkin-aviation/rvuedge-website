A0424 Extra ambulance attendant

A-Codes

Also known as: additional paramedic, extra EMT charge

Extra ambulance attendant charge for additional paramedic or emergency medical technician required for complex patient care during transport.

Clinical Context

Used when additional ambulance personnel are required for complex patients requiring two paramedics or extensive monitoring during transport.
