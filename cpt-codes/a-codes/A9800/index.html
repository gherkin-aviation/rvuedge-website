A9800 Gallium locametz 1 millicuri

A-Codes

Also known as: locametz imaging agent, Ga-68 gozetotide

Gallium locametz (gallium-68 gozetotide), 1 millicurie radiopharmaceutical diagnostic imaging agent for positron emission tomography and neuroendocrine tumor detection.

Clinical Context

Used in PET imaging to detect and localize neuroendocrine tumors, paragangliomas, and pheochromocytomas with somatostatin receptor specificity.
