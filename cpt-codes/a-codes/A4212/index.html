A4212 Non coring needle or stylet

A-Codes

Also known as: non-coring needle, huber needle, septum-preserving needle

Non-coring Needle or Stylet, a specially designed needle or cannula that does not remove rubber core from injection ports or implanted devices.

Clinical Context

Used for accessing implanted injection ports and venous access devices to prevent coring and prolong device lifespan.
