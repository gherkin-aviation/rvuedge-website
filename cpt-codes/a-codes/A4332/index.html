A4332 Lube sterile packet

A-Codes

Also known as: sterile lubricant, lubricant packet

Lubricant sterile packet, a single-use sterile lubricant packet containing water-soluble or hydrophilic lubricant for catheterization or device insertion.

Clinical Context

Used during catheter insertion to reduce friction and trauma. Essential component of safe catheterization technique.
