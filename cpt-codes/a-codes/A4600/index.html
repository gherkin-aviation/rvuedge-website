A4600 Sleeve, inter limb complicated device

A-Codes

Also known as: compression sleeve, limb sleeve

Sleeve for intermittent limb compression device.

Clinical Context

Used with sequential compression devices for lymphedema and venous insufficiency management. Sleeve design allows periodic compression cycles.
