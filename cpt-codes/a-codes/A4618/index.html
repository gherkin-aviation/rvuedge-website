A4618 Breathing circuits

A Codes

Also known as: breathing circuit, ventilator circuit

Breathing circuits for ventilator and respiratory support systems.

In Plain Language

lung connection tubing

Clinical Context

Used in mechanical ventilation and positive pressure systems. Circuits connect ventilator to patient and include tubing and connections.

RVU Information

CPT A4618 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
