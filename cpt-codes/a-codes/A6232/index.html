A6232 Hydrogel dsg>16<=48 square in

A Codes

Also known as: hydrogel dressing, gel dressing

Hydrogel wound dressing measuring greater than 16 and up to 48 square inches for moderate wound hydration.

In Plain Language

water-based gel wound cover for medium wounds

Clinical Context

Indicated for moderate-sized wounds with minimal to moderate drainage. Hydrogel maintains moist healing environment while rehydrating desiccated tissue.

RVU Information

CPT A6232 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
