A5114 Foam/fabric leg strap

A Codes

Also known as: leg strap, fabric strap, foam strap

A foam or fabric strap used to secure urinary leg bags to the patient's leg. Provides comfort and security without latex sensitivity.

In Plain Language

soft strap to hold leg bag; fabric band for leg bag

Clinical Context

Used with leg bags as latex-free alternative. Provides comfort and reduces skin irritation. Available in various sizes for secure fit.

RVU Information

CPT A5114 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
