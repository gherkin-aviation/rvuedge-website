A0170 Transport parking fees/tolls

A Codes

Also known as: transport parking fees, tolls and parking

Non-emergency transport-related expenses including parking fees, tolls, and bridge/road fees incurred during medical transport.

In Plain Language

parking and toll costs for medical transportation

Clinical Context

Used to reimburse reasonable travel-related expenses (parking, tolls) for non-emergency medical transport.

RVU Information

CPT A0170 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.

Specialties

Medical Transport
