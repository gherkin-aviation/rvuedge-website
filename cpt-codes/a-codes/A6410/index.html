A6410 Sterile eye pad

A-Codes

Also known as: eye pad, sterile eye dressing

Sterile absorbent pad designed for eye protection and moisture absorption at the ocular surface.

Clinical Context

Applied over the eye following ophthalmic surgery, chemical injury, or for corneal protection. Maintains moisture and protects epithelium.
