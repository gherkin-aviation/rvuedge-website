A4412 Ost pouch drainage high output

A-Codes

Also known as: high output pouch, drainable high capacity

Ostomy pouch drainable with high output collection capacity.

Clinical Context

Used for patients with high-volume output (ileostomies, jejunostomies) requiring frequent drainage or bulk collection. Indicated when standard pouches cannot contain fluid discharge.
