A6590 Urinary catheter disp suc pump

A Codes

Also known as: urinary catheter, suction catheter, catheter with pump

Urinary catheter with disposable suction pump. Single-use catheter system with integrated suction mechanism.

In Plain Language

drainage tube with suction; catheter with pumping device

Clinical Context

Used for urinary drainage with integrated suction capability. The disposable pump assists in urine collection and evacuation.

RVU Information

CPT A6590 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
