A6508 Cmprs burngarment foot-thigh

A-Codes

Also known as: compression stocking, burn stocking, leg compression garment

Compression burn garment foot extending to thigh. Full lower extremity compression garment for foot, leg, and thigh burn scar management.

Clinical Context

Used for extensive lower extremity burn scar management from foot to thigh. Provides graduated compression supporting functional recovery and scar management across full lower limb.
