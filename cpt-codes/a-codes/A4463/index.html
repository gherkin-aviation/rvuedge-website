A4463 Surgical dress holder reuse

A-Codes

Also known as: reusable dressing holder, washable dressing cover

Surgical dressing holder for reusable application.

Clinical Context

Used to hold and protect dressings while allowing repeated use. Reusable option reduces cost and waste in long-term wound care.
