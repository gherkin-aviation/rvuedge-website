A4257 Replace lensshield cartridge

A Codes

Also known as: lens cartridge, optical cartridge, replacement cartridge

Replace Lens/Shield Cartridge, replacement protective optical component for glucose meters requiring lens maintenance and optical optimization.

In Plain Language

meter lens; protective cover

Clinical Context

Periodically replaced in glucose meters to maintain optical clarity and measurement accuracy.

RVU Information

CPT A4257 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
