A5073 Urinary pouch on barr with flng

A Codes

Also known as: urinary pouch, urostomy pouch, urinary collection bag

A urinary drainage pouch with integral barrier and flange attachment for two-piece system coupling with compatible skin barriers.

In Plain Language

urine bag with connector ring; urinary pouch with flange

Clinical Context

Two-piece system component allowing independent pouch and barrier changes. Provides flexibility and reduces skin trauma.

RVU Information

CPT A5073 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
