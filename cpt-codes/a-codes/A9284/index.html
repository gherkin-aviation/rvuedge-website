A9284 Non-electronic spirometer

A-Codes

Also known as: spirometer, manual spirometer

Non-electronic spirometer device used to measure lung function and breathing capacity without electronic components.

Clinical Context

Used in pulmonary function testing to assess forced vital capacity and other respiratory measurements in clinical and office settings.
