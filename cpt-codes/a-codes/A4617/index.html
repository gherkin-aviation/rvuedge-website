A4617 Mouth piece

A Codes

Also known as: oxygen mouthpiece, mask alternative

Mouth piece for oxygen or positive pressure delivery.

In Plain Language

mouth oxygen delivery

Clinical Context

Used as alternative to nasal cannula or mask for patients unable to tolerate standard oxygen delivery. Allows oral oxygen administration.

RVU Information

CPT A4617 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
