A6611 Grad com wrap w strap ak cus

A Codes

Also known as: compression wrap with strap, grad comp wrap, adjustable compression

Graduated compression wrap with adjustable straps for therapeutic compression of lower extremity, ankle-knee, custom fit.

In Plain Language

adjustable pressure wrap; velcro compression wrap; strap compression

Clinical Context

Used for edema management and venous disease treatment with the benefit of adjustable compression via straps. Custom fitting allows for individualized pressure and easy application.

RVU Information

CPT A6611 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
