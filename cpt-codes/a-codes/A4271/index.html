A4271 Home lancing/test cartridges

A Codes

Also known as: lancet cartridges, test strips, glucose lancets

Home lancing and test cartridges for blood glucose monitoring, supplies used by patients with diabetes to obtain capillary blood samples for glucose testing.

In Plain Language

finger stick supplies for diabetes testing; blood sugar test equipment

Clinical Context

Essential supplies for home blood glucose monitoring in diabetic patients. Used multiple times daily for point-of-care glucose testing.

RVU Information

CPT A4271 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
