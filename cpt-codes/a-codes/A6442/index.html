A6442 Conform band n/s w<3"/yd

A-Codes

Also known as: conforming band, wrap

Non-elastic conforming bandage without adhesive, non-sterile, width less than 3 inches, per linear yard.

Clinical Context

Applied over dressings for compression and support in narrow areas. Non-sterile formulation suitable for various wound care settings.
