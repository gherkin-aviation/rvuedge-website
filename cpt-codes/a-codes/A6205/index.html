A6205 Composite drsg > 48 square in

A-Codes

Also known as: composite dressing, large multi-layer pad

Composite wound dressing measuring greater than 48 square inches for large wound coverage and multi-layer protection.

Clinical Context

Indicated for extensive wounds requiring comprehensive coverage. Multi-layer construction provides absorbency and bacterial barrier protection.
