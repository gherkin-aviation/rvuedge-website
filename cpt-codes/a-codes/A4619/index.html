A4619 Face tent

A Codes

Also known as: face tent, oxygen tent

Face tent for oxygen or positive pressure gas delivery.

In Plain Language

face covering oxygen delivery

Clinical Context

Used for oxygen delivery to patients unable to tolerate masks or cannulas. Open design reduces sensation of claustrophobia.

RVU Information

CPT A4619 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
