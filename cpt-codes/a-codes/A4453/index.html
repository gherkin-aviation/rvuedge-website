A4453 Rec catheter any transanal, each

A-Codes

Also known as: rectal tube, transanal catheter

Rectal catheter for transanal placement, each unit.

Clinical Context

Used for bowel management, particularly in spinal cord injury or neurogenic bowel patients. Facilitates controlled bowel emptying and prevents accidents.
