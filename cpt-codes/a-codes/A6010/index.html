A6010 Collagen based wound filler

A Codes

Also known as: collagen filler, collagen wound matrix

Wound filler composed of collagen matrix material providing hemostasis and structural support for wound bed preparation.

In Plain Language

collagen padding for wounds; filler for open sores

Clinical Context

Used in acute or chronic wounds to promote hemostasis, reduce bleeding, and provide a moist environment conducive to healing. Often used under gauze or other dressings.

RVU Information

CPT A6010 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
