A6600 G com bandge hgh dn foam sht

A Codes

Also known as: foam compression sheet, foam bandage, cushioned compression

Graduated compression bandage with high density foam sheet for therapeutic compression combined with protective cushioning.

In Plain Language

padded compression wrap; cushioned compression bandage; foam pressure wrap

Clinical Context

Used in wound care and lymphedema management where compression is combined with protective cushioning. The foam layer provides protection to bony prominences and sensitive skin while delivering compression therapy.

RVU Information

CPT A6600 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
