A4674 Chem/antisept solution, 8oz

A Codes

Also known as: antiseptic solution, chemical disinfectant, cleaning solution

A chemical antiseptic or antimicrobial solution supplied in 8-ounce containers used for disinfection, cleaning, and infection prevention in dialysis settings.

In Plain Language

germ-killing liquid for cleaning; antiseptic solution

Clinical Context

Used in dialysis facilities for cleaning dialysis machines, patient connection sites, and equipment. Essential for preventing bloodborne infections in dialysis patients.

RVU Information

CPT A4674 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
