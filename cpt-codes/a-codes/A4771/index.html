A4771 Serum clotting time tube

A Codes

Also known as: clotting tube, bleeding time tube, coagulation tube

A specialized blood collection tube with specific additives that facilitate serum clotting time measurement. Used for coagulation studies and bleeding time tests.

In Plain Language

blood tube for clotting test; bleeding time collection tube

Clinical Context

Used in laboratory medicine for prothrombin time (PT) and activated partial thromboplastin time (aPTT) tests. Contains appropriate clotting activators.

RVU Information

CPT A4771 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
