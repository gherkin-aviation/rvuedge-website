A0382 Basic support routine suppls

A Codes

Also known as: BLS supplies, basic life support supplies

Basic life support ambulance routine medical supplies and equipment charges for BLS-level ambulance transport.

In Plain Language

cost of basic medical supplies in ambulance

Clinical Context

Used to bill for routine supplies and equipment used in BLS ambulance transport.

RVU Information

CPT A0382 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
