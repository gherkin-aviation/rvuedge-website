A9539 Tc99m pentetate

A-Codes

Also known as: Tc-99m DTPA, pentetate

Technetium Tc-99m pentetate (diethylenetriaminepentaacetic acid) injection used as a radiotracer for renal imaging and glomerular filtration assessment.

Clinical Context

Used in renal nuclear medicine for measurement of glomerular filtration rate, effective renal plasma flow, and kidney imaging.
