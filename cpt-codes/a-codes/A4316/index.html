A4316 Catheter with drainage 3-way

A-Codes

Also known as: three-way foley, irrigation foley

Catheter with drainage, three-way indwelling catheter, a triple-lumen Foley catheter for long-term drainage with integrated irrigation capability.

Clinical Context

Used in post-operative urology patients or those requiring bladder irrigation and drainage simultaneously. Three lumens enable separate irrigation and monitoring.
