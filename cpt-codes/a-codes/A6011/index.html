A6011 Collagen gel/paste wound fil

A Codes

Also known as: collagen gel filler, collagen paste

Collagen wound filler in gel or paste form for application to open wounds to provide hemostasis and promote granulation.

In Plain Language

collagen gel for wounds; sticky collagen product

Clinical Context

Applied directly into wound cavities to control bleeding, fill dead space, and stimulate fibroblast activity. Absorbed or resorbed as healing progresses.

RVU Information

CPT A6011 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
