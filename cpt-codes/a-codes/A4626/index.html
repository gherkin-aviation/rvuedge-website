A4626 Tracheostomy cleaning brush

A-Codes

Also known as: trach brush, cleaning brush, tube brush

A specialized brush with soft bristles designed to clean the inside of a tracheostomy tube and removal of dried secretions, mucus, and debris from tube lumen. Facilitates safe hygiene without damaging tube integrity.

Clinical Context

Used during routine tracheostomy care, typically daily, to remove dried secretions and maintain tube patency. Critical for preventing infection and tube obstruction.
