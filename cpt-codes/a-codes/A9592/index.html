A9592 Copper cu 64 dotatate diagnostic

A Codes

Also known as: Cu-64 DOTATATE, copper dotatate

Copper Cu-64 dotatate diagnostic injection used as a positron emission tomography tracer for neuroendocrine tumor imaging.

In Plain Language

neuroendocrine tumor PET tracer; cancer imaging dye

Clinical Context

Used in PET imaging for detection of somatostatin receptor-positive neuroendocrine tumors.

RVU Information

CPT A9592 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
