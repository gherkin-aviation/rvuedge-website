A6524 G com garmnt lower leg/ft nght

A-Codes

Also known as: night compression leg, sleep compression stocking, nighttime leg compression

Graduated compression garment for lower leg and foot, nighttime use. Compression covering from lower leg to foot for sleeping.

Clinical Context

Used for night-time compression therapy on lower legs and feet. Standard sizing for comfortable overnight edema and lymphedema management.
