A6413 Adhesive bandage, first-aid

A-Codes

Also known as: adhesive bandage, band-aid, adhesive strip

Pre-packaged adhesive bandage with absorbent gauze pad for minor wound first aid and protection.

Clinical Context

Applied to minor cuts, abrasions, and scrapes for protection and hemostasis. Self-contained unit suitable for home and workplace first aid.
