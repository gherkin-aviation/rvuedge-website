A2012 Suprathel, per square centimeters

A-Codes

Also known as: SUPRATHEL, polyurethane dressing, synthetic covering

SUPRATHEL, Synthetic Polyurethane Dressing per square centimeter, a lightweight protective biologic covering for wounds and burn sites.

Clinical Context

Used as temporary covering for partial-thickness burns, donor sites, and surgical wounds to prevent fluid loss and bacterial contamination.
