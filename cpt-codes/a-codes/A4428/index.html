A4428 Urine ost pouch w faucet/tap

A-Codes

Also known as: urine pouch with tap, urostomy drainage bag

Urostomy pouch with faucet or tap for controlled drainage.

Clinical Context

Used for urinary diversions (ileal conduit, continent pouch) allowing patient-controlled urine drainage. Tap design prevents accidental spillage and improves convenience.
