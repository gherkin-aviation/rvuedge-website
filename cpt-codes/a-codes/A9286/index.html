A9286 Any hygienic item, device

A Codes

Also known as: hygiene item, hygienic device

Any hygienic item or device not otherwise specified that provides basic hygiene and cleanliness support.

In Plain Language

cleaning or hygiene product; personal care item

Clinical Context

Catch-all code for miscellaneous hygienic supplies and devices that don't fit into more specific categories but support patient hygiene and infection prevention.

RVU Information

CPT A9286 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
