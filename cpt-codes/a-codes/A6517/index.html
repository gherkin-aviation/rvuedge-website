A6517 Grad com wrap w strap bn cus

A Codes

Also known as: compression wrap, graduated compression strap, adjustable compression wrap

Graduated compression wrap with straps for between-limb coverage. Custom-fitted compression device for areas between leg and foot.

In Plain Language

pressure wrap between limbs; adjustable intermediate compression

Clinical Context

Used for graduated compression therapy in between-limb areas where custom fitting is needed. Straps provide security and adjustability.

RVU Information

CPT A6517 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
