A6599 G complicated bandage short stretch

A-Codes

Also known as: short stretch wrap, short stretch compression bandage, inelastic bandage

Graduated compression bandage using short stretch fabric for therapeutic compression therapy in venous and lymphatic conditions.

Clinical Context

Used in lymphedema management and venous disease treatment. Short stretch bandages provide sustained compression and are preferred for intensive phase treatment with excellent pressure maintenance.
