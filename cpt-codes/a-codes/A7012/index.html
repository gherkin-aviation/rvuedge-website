A7012 Nebulizer water collec devic

A Codes

Also known as: water trap, moisture collector, condensation trap

Nebulizer water collection device for separating and collecting moisture in aerosol therapy systems.

In Plain Language

water collector; moisture catcher; drip trap

Clinical Context

Used with nebulizers to collect condensed water and prevent fluid from entering airways. Improves safety and comfort during aerosol therapy.

RVU Information

CPT A7012 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
