A7005 Nondisposable nebulizer set

A Codes

Also known as: reusable SVN, durable neb, permanent nebulizer

Nondisposable small volume nebulizer set for aerosol drug delivery via inhalation therapy, reusable.

In Plain Language

reusable breathing treatment kit; washable nebulizer; sterilizable inhaler

Clinical Context

Used for home-based and chronic respiratory therapy. Reusable design requires proper cleaning and sterilization between uses but provides long-term cost savings.

RVU Information

CPT A7005 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
