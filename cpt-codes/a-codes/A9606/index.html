A9606 Radium ra223 dichloride therapy

A Codes

Also known as: Xofigo, Ra-223, radium therapy

Radium Ra-223 dichloride injection used therapeutically for treatment of metastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer with bone metastases.

In Plain Language

radioactive prostate cancer treatment; bone metastasis therapy

Clinical Context

Used in targeted radiotherapy for treatment of castration-resistant prostate cancer with symptomatic bone metastases.

RVU Information

CPT A9606 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation
