A9900 Supply/accessory/service

A Codes

Also known as: DME supply, medical equipment supply

Durable medical equipment (DME) supply, accessory, or service, not otherwise classified, providing coverage for miscellaneous equipment-related items.

In Plain Language

medical equipment or supplies

Clinical Context

Used for billing of equipment-related items, accessories, or services that do not fit into more specific DME categories and are medically necessary.

RVU Information

CPT A9900 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
