A4296 Coude tip hydrophilic catheter

A-Codes

Also known as: coude catheter, angled catheter

Coude tip hydrophilic coated catheter, an intermittent urinary catheter with an angled insertion tip and water-activated hydrophilic coating for navigating urethral strictures.

Clinical Context

Used in male patients with urethral strictures, benign prostatic hyperplasia, or anatomical abnormalities. Coude tip facilitates easier insertion.
