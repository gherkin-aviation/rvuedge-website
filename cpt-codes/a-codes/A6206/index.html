A6206 Contact layer <= 16 square in

A-Codes

Also known as: contact layer, non-stick layer

Contact layer dressing measuring 16 square inches or less, non-adherent layer applied directly to wound surface.

Clinical Context

Applied directly to wound bed to prevent dressing adherence, allowing secondary dressing placement without trauma upon removal. Facilitates pain-free dressing changes.
