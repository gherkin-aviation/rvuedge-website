A6154 Wound pouch each

A-Codes

Also known as: wound pouch, drainage pouch

Specialized pouch designed to encapsulate and contain wound drainage, protecting surrounding skin and maintaining sanitation.

Clinical Context

Applied around high-output wounds, fistulas, or ostomies to contain and manage excessive drainage, protect periwound skin, and facilitate output measurement.
