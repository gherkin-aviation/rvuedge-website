A6154 Wound pouch each

A Codes

Also known as: wound pouch, drainage pouch

Specialized pouch designed to encapsulate and contain wound drainage, protecting surrounding skin and maintaining sanitation.

In Plain Language

collection bag for wound fluid; drainage container

Clinical Context

Applied around high-output wounds, fistulas, or ostomies to contain and manage excessive drainage, protect periwound skin, and facilitate output measurement.

RVU Information

CPT A6154 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
