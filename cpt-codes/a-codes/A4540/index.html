A4540 Transverse elec nerve peripheral nerve

A-Codes

Also known as: TENS unit supplies, transcutaneous stimulation electrode

Transcutaneous electrical nerve stimulation electrode supplies for peripheral nerve stimulation.

Clinical Context

Used for pain management in chronic pain conditions through electrical nerve stimulation. Electrode supplies are essential consumables for TENS therapy.
