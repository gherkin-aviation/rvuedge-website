A4772 Blood glucose test strips

A-Codes

Also known as: glucose strips, blood sugar strips, test strips

Disposable test strips coated with glucose oxidase enzyme used to measure blood glucose levels in finger stick or capillary samples. For point-of-care glucose testing.

Clinical Context

Used by patients with diabetes for home glucose monitoring and by healthcare providers for point-of-care testing. Essential tool in diabetes management.
