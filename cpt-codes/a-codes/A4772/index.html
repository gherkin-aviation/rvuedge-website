A4772 Blood glucose test strips

A Codes

Also known as: glucose strips, blood sugar strips, test strips

Disposable test strips coated with glucose oxidase enzyme used to measure blood glucose levels in finger stick or capillary samples. For point-of-care glucose testing.

In Plain Language

blood sugar test stick; glucose testing strips

Clinical Context

Used by patients with diabetes for home glucose monitoring and by healthcare providers for point-of-care testing. Essential tool in diabetes management.

RVU Information

CPT A4772 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
