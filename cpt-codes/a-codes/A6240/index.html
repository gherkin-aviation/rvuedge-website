A6240 Hydrocolld drainage filler paste

A-Codes

Also known as: hydrocolloid filler, hydrocolloid paste

Hydrocolloid wound filler in paste or putty form for packing cavitary wounds and filling dead space.

Clinical Context

Packed into wound cavities, sinuses, and tunnels to fill dead space while absorbing exudate and promoting granulation.
