A7001 Nondisposable pump canister

A Codes

Also known as: reusable NPWT canister, pump container, nondisposable container

Nondisposable canister for use with negative pressure wound therapy pump systems, reusable.

In Plain Language

reusable drainage container; washable collection cup; sterilizable canister

Clinical Context

Used with negative pressure wound therapy systems for drainage collection. Nondisposable design allows for cleaning and sterilization between uses, reducing ongoing supply costs.

RVU Information

CPT A7001 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
