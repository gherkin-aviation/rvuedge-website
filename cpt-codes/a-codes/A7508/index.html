A7508 Housing & integrated adhesiv

A Codes

Also known as: adhesive housing, sticky HME housing, integrated adhesive holder

Housing with integrated adhesive for heat and moisture exchange system attachment to tracheostomy.

In Plain Language

sticky device holder; adhesive filter case; attachment housing

Clinical Context

Combined housing and adhesive system for heat and moisture exchange. Streamlines tracheostomy care with single attachment system.

RVU Information

CPT A7508 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
