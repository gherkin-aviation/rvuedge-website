A4405 Nonpectin based ostomy paste

A-Codes +Add-on

Also known as: ostomy paste, stoma sealing paste

Nonpectin based ostomy paste, a paste product without pectin used to fill irregularities around stoma and improve skin barrier seal.

Clinical Context

Applied around stoma to fill gaps between skin and wafer. Improves seal integrity and prevents leakage.
