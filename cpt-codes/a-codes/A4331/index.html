A4331 Extension drainage tubing

A-Codes +Add-on

Also known as: extension tubing, drainage extension, catheter extension

Extension drainage tubing, a flexible tube that extends from catheter or drainage devices to collect containers, allowing increased mobility.

Clinical Context

Used to extend reach from catheter to drainage collection system, enabling patient mobility and comfort during drainage.
