A9609 F18 fdg, 15 millicuries

A Codes

Also known as: FDG PET tracer, 18F-FDG, fluorodeoxyglucose

Fluorodeoxyglucose (FDG) F-18, fluorine-18 radiopharmaceutical injection, 15 millicuries for positron emission tomography (PET) imaging studies to evaluate metabolic activity and detect malignancy.

In Plain Language

radioactive glucose tracer for cancer detection; PET scan tracer

Clinical Context

Used in PET imaging to detect and monitor cancer, evaluate cardiac viability, and assess neurological conditions by visualizing glucose metabolism in tissues.

RVU Information

CPT A9609 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
