A5501 Diabetic custom molded shoe

A-Codes

Also known as: custom-molded diabetic shoe, diabetic orthopedic shoe, bespoke diabetic shoe

Individually custom-molded diabetic shoe designed from plaster or foam casting of the patient's foot.

Clinical Context

Fabricated from individual foot casts for patients with diabetes and severe neuropathy, deformities, or previous ulcers. Provides maximum protection and accommodation.
