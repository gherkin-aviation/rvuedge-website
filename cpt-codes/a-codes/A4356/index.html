A4356 External ureth clmp or compr dvc

A Codes

Also known as: urethral clamp, urine clamp

External urethral clamp or compression device, a non-invasive mechanical device that compresses the urethra externally to prevent urine leakage.

In Plain Language

incontinence compression device; urine flow blocker

Clinical Context

Used in male patients with stress incontinence or post-void dribbling. Provides temporary continence during activities.

RVU Information

CPT A4356 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
