A2025 Miro3d per cubic centimeters

A Codes

Also known as: MIRO3D, 3D collagen, fiber matrix

MIRO3D, Collagen Fiber Matrix per cubic centimeter, a three-dimensional collagen scaffold with macrostructure for tissue regeneration and vascularization.

In Plain Language

three-dimensional collagen matrix; fiber scaffold

Clinical Context

Used for volumetric wound filling and tissue regeneration where three-dimensional collagen structure supports vascular ingrowth.

RVU Information

CPT A2025 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
