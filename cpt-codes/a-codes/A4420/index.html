A4420 Ost pch clsd for bar w lk fl

A Codes

Also known as: closed locking pouch, closed flange system

Ostomy pouch closed for skin barrier with locking flange.

In Plain Language

sealed bag with locking connection; closed locking system

Clinical Context

Used with locking flange skin barriers for secure closure and sealed collection. Ideal for colostomy patients requiring leak-proof odor control.

RVU Information

CPT A4420 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
