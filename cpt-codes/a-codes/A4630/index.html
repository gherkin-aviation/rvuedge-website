A4630 Repl bat t.e.n.s. own by pt

A-Codes

Also known as: TENS battery, battery replacement

A replacement battery for transcutaneous electrical nerve stimulation (TENS) device owned by the patient. The battery provides power for the portable stimulation unit.

Clinical Context

Used to maintain functionality of patient-owned TENS units for chronic pain management. TENS is used for various pain conditions including back pain, neuropathy, and arthritis.
