A5500 Diab shoe for density insert

A Codes

Also known as: diabetic shoe with insert, custom diabetic shoe, density insert shoe

Custom-molded diabetic shoe with interior density insert for foot protection and pressure redistribution in patients with neuropathy.

In Plain Language

special diabetic shoe with padding; protective shoe for diabetic feet

Clinical Context

Prescribed for patients with diabetes mellitus and peripheral neuropathy to prevent ulcer formation, redistribute pressure, and protect at-risk feet. Requires face-to-face evaluation.

RVU Information

CPT A5500 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
