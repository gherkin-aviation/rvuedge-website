A5500 Diab shoe for density insert

A-Codes

Also known as: diabetic shoe with insert, custom diabetic shoe, density insert shoe

Custom-molded diabetic shoe with interior density insert for foot protection and pressure redistribution in patients with neuropathy.

Clinical Context

Prescribed for patients with diabetes mellitus and peripheral neuropathy to prevent ulcer formation, redistribute pressure, and protect at-risk feet. Requires face-to-face evaluation.
