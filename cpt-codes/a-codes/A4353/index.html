A4353 Intermittent urinary catheter

A-Codes

Also known as: intermittent cath, clean-intermittent catheterization, CIC

Intermittent urinary catheter, a generic code for single-use intermittent catheters for self-catheterization or medical staff administration.

Clinical Context

Used multiple times daily for patients with neurogenic bladder, spinal cord injury, or inability to void. Reduces infection risk vs. indwelling catheters.
