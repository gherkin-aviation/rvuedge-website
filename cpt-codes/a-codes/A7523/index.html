A7523 Tracheostomy shower protect

A-Codes

Also known as: trach shower protector, water shield, shower guard

Tracheostomy shower protection device to prevent water entry during bathing.

Clinical Context

Used by tracheostomy patients during bathing to prevent water from entering the tracheostomy. Allows safe hygiene maintenance.
