A6533 Gc stocking thighlngth 18-30

A Codes

Also known as: compression stocking, thigh-high support hose, compression stocking

Graduated compression stocking, thigh length, with pressure between 18 and 30 millimeters of mercury. Extended-length compression hosiery.

In Plain Language

pressure stocking for thigh; support hose to thigh

Clinical Context

Used for graduated compression therapy extending to thigh in lymphedema, venous insufficiency, and edema management. 18-30 millimeters of mercury provides mild to moderate compression.

RVU Information

CPT A6533 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
