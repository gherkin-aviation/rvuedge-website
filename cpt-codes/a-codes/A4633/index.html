A4633 Uvl replacement bulb

A-Codes

Also known as: UV bulb, ultraviolet bulb, light bulb

A replacement bulb or lamp for ultraviolet light (UVL) therapy equipment used in treating skin conditions. Emits ultraviolet radiation for phototherapy purposes.

Clinical Context

Used in phototherapy for psoriasis, vitiligo, eczema, and other photosensitive dermatological conditions. Bulbs lose effectiveness over time and require periodic replacement.
