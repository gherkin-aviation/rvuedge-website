A4305 Drug delivery system >=50 milliliters

A Codes

Also known as: large infusion pump, medication reservoir

Drug delivery system with capacity of 50 milliliters or greater, an infusion pump reservoir or container designed for continuous or intermittent medication delivery.

In Plain Language

large medication holder; drug pump container

Clinical Context

Used for prolonged medication infusions including chemotherapy, antibiotics, pain medications, or insulin. Larger capacity reduces refill frequency.

RVU Information

CPT A4305 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
