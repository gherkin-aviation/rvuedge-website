A4384 Ostomy faceplt/silicone ring

A Codes

Also known as: silicone face plate, silicone flange

Ostomy face plate with silicone ring, an attachment ring incorporating silicone material for enhanced skin compatibility and secure adherence.

In Plain Language

silicone attachment ring; soft stoma connector

Clinical Context

Provides superior adhesion and skin compatibility compared to standard face plates. Particularly beneficial in sensitive skin.

RVU Information

CPT A4384 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
