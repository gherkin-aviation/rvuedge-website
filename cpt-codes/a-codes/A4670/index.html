A4670 Automatic bp monitor, dial

A-Codes

Also known as: automatic BP monitor, digital BP monitor, automated sphygmomanometer

An automated blood pressure monitor with dial or display readout for non-invasive blood pressure measurement. Device inflates cuff and measures pressure without operator intervention.

Clinical Context

Used for home monitoring, office screening, and hospital patient monitoring. Reduces operator error and provides quick, repeatable measurements. Available in wrist, arm, and finger models.
