Also known as: I-131 diagnostic capsule, thyroid uptake tracer

Iodine I-131 sodium iodide capsule used diagnostically for thyroid uptake measurement and thyroid imaging.

Clinical Context

Used for diagnostic thyroid uptake measurement and imaging in patients with suspected thyroid dysfunction.
