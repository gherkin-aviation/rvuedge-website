A6568 G com garment torso/shoulder

A Codes

Also known as: compression vest, torso compression, shoulder compression

Graduated compression garment for torso and shoulder. Compression covering for chest, back, and shoulder regions.

In Plain Language

pressure vest for chest and shoulders; torso and shoulder compression

Clinical Context

Used for compression therapy on torso and shoulders in lymphedema management, particularly post-mastectomy or upper extremity lymphedema.

RVU Information

CPT A6568 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
