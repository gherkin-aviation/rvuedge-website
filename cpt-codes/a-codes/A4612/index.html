A4612 Battery cables

A Codes

Also known as: battery cables, power cables

Battery cables and connectors.

In Plain Language

battery connection wires

Clinical Context

Used to connect batteries to medical equipment. Essential replacement when cables become damaged or corroded.

RVU Information

CPT A4612 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
