A6449 Left compres band >=3" <5"/yd

A Codes

Also known as: light compression wrap, light elastic bandage

Light compression bandage, 3 to less than 5 inches per yard of length. Used for mild compression therapy on extremities.

In Plain Language

mild pressure wrap; light elastic band

Clinical Context

Used for mild compression support in lymphedema management, venous insufficiency, or post-operative swelling. Provides gentle graduated compression to affected limbs.

RVU Information

CPT A6449 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
