A4210 Nonneedle injection device

A-Codes

Also known as: needle-free injector, jet injector, pressure injector

Needle-free Injection Device, a reusable or disposable injection delivery system that administers medications without a hypodermic needle.

Clinical Context

Used in patients with needle phobia, multiple injections, or allergy concerns to provide medication delivery without needle penetration.
