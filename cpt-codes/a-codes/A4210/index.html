A4210 Nonneedle injection device

A Codes

Also known as: needle-free injector, jet injector, pressure injector

Needle-free Injection Device, a reusable or disposable injection delivery system that administers medications without a hypodermic needle.

In Plain Language

needle-free injection; pressure-based injector

Clinical Context

Used in patients with needle phobia, multiple injections, or allergy concerns to provide medication delivery without needle penetration.

RVU Information

CPT A4210 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
