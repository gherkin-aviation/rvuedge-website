A4580 Cast supplies (plaster)

A Codes

Also known as: plaster cast supplies, casting material

Cast supplies comprising plaster materials.

In Plain Language

cast-making material; plaster wrap

Clinical Context

Used for fabrication of protective casts in fracture management. Plaster provides rigidity and support during bone healing.

RVU Information

CPT A4580 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
