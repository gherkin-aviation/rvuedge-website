A4870 Plumb/elec week hm hemo equip

A-Codes

Also known as: equipment installation, home dialysis setup, plumbing and electrical work

Professional plumbing and electrical installation or repair work for home hemodialysis equipment setup. Includes water line, electrical line, and system installation.

Clinical Context

Used for initial setup or repair of home hemodialysis systems. Requires licensed professionals for safety and code compliance. Important for nocturnal home hemodialysis.
