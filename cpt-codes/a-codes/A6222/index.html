A6222 Gauze <=16 in no with sal without b

A-Codes

Also known as: sterile gauze, dry gauze pad

Non-impregnated sterile gauze dressing measuring 16 square inches or less for dry wound care.

Clinical Context

Applied to clean dry wounds or as outer dressing layer. Excellent absorbency without chemical impregnation, allows customization with topical agents.
