A9293 Fertility cycl tracking soft

A Codes

Also known as: fertility tracking app, ovulation tracker

Fertility cycle tracking software or application used to monitor reproductive cycles and predict ovulation for conception planning.

In Plain Language

app to track fertility cycles; pregnancy planning software

Clinical Context

Used by patients attempting conception to track menstrual cycles, predict ovulation windows, and optimize timing for sexual intercourse.

RVU Information

CPT A9293 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
