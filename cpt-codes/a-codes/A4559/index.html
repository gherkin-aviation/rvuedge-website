A4559 Coupling gel or paste

A Codes

Also known as: ultrasound gel, coupling gel

Coupling gel or paste for ultrasound and therapeutic applications.

In Plain Language

medical contact jelly

Clinical Context

Used as coupling medium for ultrasound therapy and diagnostic imaging. Provides acoustic contact and reduces friction between transducer and skin.

RVU Information

CPT A4559 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
