A4559 Coupling gel or paste

A-Codes

Also known as: ultrasound gel, coupling gel

Coupling gel or paste for ultrasound and therapeutic applications.

Clinical Context

Used as coupling medium for ultrasound therapy and diagnostic imaging. Provides acoustic contact and reduces friction between transducer and skin.
