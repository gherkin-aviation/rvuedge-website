A0434 Specialty care transport

A Codes

Also known as: specialty care transport, critical care transport, neonatal transport

Specialty Care Transport providing ground ambulance service with specialized medical personnel for patients with complex medical conditions requiring intensive monitoring.

In Plain Language

specialized ambulance service; intensive care transport

Clinical Context

Used for neonatal, pediatric, burns, or other specialty patient populations requiring specialized equipment and trained personnel during transport.

RVU Information

CPT A0434 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
