A4737 Injection anesthetic per 10 milliliters

A Codes

Also known as: local anesthetic, injectable anesthetic, lidocaine

An injectable local anesthetic agent (such as lidocaine or bupivacaine) supplied in 10-milliliter volumes for infiltration, nerve blocks, or other regional anesthesia.

In Plain Language

injectable numbing medicine; nerve block medication

Clinical Context

Used for local or regional anesthesia in minor procedures, nerve blocks, and wound infiltration. Available in various concentrations (0.5%-2%).

RVU Information

CPT A4737 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
