A0021 Outside state ambulance serv

A-Codes

Also known as: out-of-state ambulance, inter-state transport

Ambulance service provided outside the state where the patient is located; used for inter-state medical transport.

Clinical Context

Used for ambulance services when patient is transported across state lines; typically for regional trauma centers or specialty care facilities.
