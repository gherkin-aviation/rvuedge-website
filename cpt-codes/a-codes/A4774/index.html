A4774 Ammonia test strips

A Codes

Also known as: ammonia strips, ammonia test, NH3 test strips

Disposable test strips that detect and quantify ammonia levels in blood samples. Used to assess hepatic synthetic function and encephalopathy risk.

In Plain Language

test strips for liver function; ammonia level test

Clinical Context

Used in patients with liver disease to monitor ammonia levels and assess risk for hepatic encephalopathy. Helps guide treatment decisions.

RVU Information

CPT A4774 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
