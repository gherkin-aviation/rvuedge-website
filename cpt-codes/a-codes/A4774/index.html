A4774 Ammonia test strips

A-Codes

Also known as: ammonia strips, ammonia test, NH3 test strips

Disposable test strips that detect and quantify ammonia levels in blood samples. Used to assess hepatic synthetic function and encephalopathy risk.

Clinical Context

Used in patients with liver disease to monitor ammonia levels and assess risk for hepatic encephalopathy. Helps guide treatment decisions.
