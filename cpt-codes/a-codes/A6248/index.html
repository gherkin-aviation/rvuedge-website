A6248 Hydrogel drsg gel filler

A-Codes

Also known as: hydrogel filler, hydrogel paste

Hydrogel wound filler in gel or paste form for packing cavitary wounds and filling dead space.

Clinical Context

Packed into wound cavities, sinuses, and tunnels to fill dead space, maintain moisture, and facilitate granulation tissue development.
