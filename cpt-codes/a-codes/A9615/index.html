A9615 Injection, pegulicianine, 1 milligrams

A Codes

Also known as: pegulicianine, NIR fluorescence agent

Pegulicianine injection, 1 milligram per dose, a near-infrared fluorescent agent used for intraoperative optical imaging during surgical procedures.

In Plain Language

surgical fluorescence dye for tumor detection

Clinical Context

Used during intraoperative imaging to identify tumors, assess tissue perfusion, and visualize lymph nodes using near-infrared fluorescence technology in oncologic and vascular surgery.

RVU Information

CPT A9615 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
