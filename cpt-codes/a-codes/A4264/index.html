A4264 Intratubal occlusion device

A-Codes

Also known as: Essure, tubal occlusion, permanent contraception

Intratubal Occlusion Device, a minimally invasive permanent contraceptive device for transcervical fallopian tube occlusion.

Clinical Context

Placed via hysteroscopy for permanent sterilization without surgical incision, providing effective long-term contraception.
