A4264 Intratubal occlusion device

A Codes

Also known as: Essure, tubal occlusion, permanent contraception

Intratubal Occlusion Device, a minimally invasive permanent contraceptive device for transcervical fallopian tube occlusion.

In Plain Language

tube blocker; permanent birth control

Clinical Context

Placed via hysteroscopy for permanent sterilization without surgical incision, providing effective long-term contraception.

RVU Information

CPT A4264 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
