A6242 Hydrogel drainage <=16 in without bdr

A-Codes

Also known as: hydrogel dressing, gel sheet

Hydrogel wound dressing without border measuring 16 square inches or less for dry wound rehydration.

Clinical Context

Applied directly to dry, necrotic, or minimally draining wounds to rehydrate tissue and facilitate autolytic debridement.
