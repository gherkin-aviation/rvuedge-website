A4328 Fem urinary collect pouch

A-Codes

Also known as: female urinary pouch, external urine collection bag

Female urinary collection pouch, a wearable or adhesive-based pouch for external urine collection in female patients with incontinence.

Clinical Context

Non-invasive urine collection device for female patients with incontinence or urinary diversion. Reduces catheter-related infections.
