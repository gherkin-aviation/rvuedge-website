A0130 Noner transport wheelch van

A-Codes

Also known as: wheelchair van transport, wheelchair lift transport

Non-emergency medical transport by wheelchair van equipped with wheelchair lift or ramp for patients with mobility limitations.

Clinical Context

Used for non-emergency transport of wheelchair-dependent or mobility-limited patients requiring specialized vehicle accommodations.
