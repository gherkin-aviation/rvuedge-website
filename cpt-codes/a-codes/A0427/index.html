A0427 Als1-emergency

A Codes

Also known as: ALS 1 emergency, emergency ALS transport, rapid response

Advanced Life Support Level 1 emergency ambulance response, a rapid-response ground transport service with paramedic personnel for critical situations.

In Plain Language

emergency paramedic ambulance; urgent advanced care transport

Clinical Context

Used for emergency-dispatched Advanced Life Support Level 1 transports where rapid response is critical for time-sensitive conditions.

RVU Information

CPT A0427 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
