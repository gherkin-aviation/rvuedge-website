A6215 Foam dressing wound filler

A Codes

Also known as: foam filler, foam packing

Foam wound filler material in granule, particle, or moldable form for packing cavitary and tunneling wounds.

In Plain Language

foam stuffing for deep wounds

Clinical Context

Packed into wound cavities, sinuses, and tunnels to fill dead space while absorbing drainage and supporting granulation tissue formation.

RVU Information

CPT A6215 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
