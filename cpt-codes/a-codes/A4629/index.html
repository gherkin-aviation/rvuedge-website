A4629 Tracheostomy care kit

A-Codes

Also known as: trach kit, tracheostomy maintenance kit, trach supplies

A comprehensive kit containing supplies for ongoing care and maintenance of an established tracheostomy, including dressing materials, cleaning supplies, and protective barriers. Used for regular daily care after initial healing.

Clinical Context

Used for routine daily management of tracheostomy sites in patients requiring long-term airway management. Supports infection prevention and site hygiene.
