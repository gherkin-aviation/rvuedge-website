A6000 Wound warming wound cover

A Codes

Also known as: wound warming cover, thermal dressing, heat therapy wrap

Thermal wound cover designed to apply therapeutic warmth to wounds, promoting circulation and accelerating tissue repair.

In Plain Language

warming pad for wounds; heat treatment for sores

Clinical Context

Applied to chronic wounds, pressure ulcers, or surgical wounds to enhance microcirculation, promote granulation, and accelerate healing through localized heat application.

RVU Information

CPT A6000 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
