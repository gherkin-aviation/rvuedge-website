A6256 Absorpt drainage > 48 square in with bdr

A Codes

Also known as: absorbent pad with border, large super absorber with border

Absorbent wound dressing with adhesive border measuring greater than 48 square inches for large self-contained coverage.

In Plain Language

sticky-edge absorption pad for large wounds

Clinical Context

Applied to large, exudative wounds requiring self-adhering absorbent coverage. Border provides comprehensive seal and fixation.

RVU Information

CPT A6256 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
