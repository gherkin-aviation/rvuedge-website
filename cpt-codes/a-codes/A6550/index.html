A6550 Neg pres wound therapy drsg set

A Codes

Also known as: negative pressure therapy, wound vac, suction dressing

Negative pressure wound therapy dressing set, complete system. Includes all components for negative pressure wound therapy application.

In Plain Language

vacuum pressure wound dressing; suction wound treatment

Clinical Context

Used for negative pressure wound therapy in complex wound management, diabetic ulcers, surgical wounds, and chronic wounds. Creates controlled negative pressure to promote healing.

RVU Information

CPT A6550 does not have a physician work RVU assigned by CMS. This is typical for supply, drug, and equipment codes — reimbursement is based on Average Sales Price (ASP), fee schedules, or payer contracts rather than the RVU system.

Billing & Documentation

HCPCS A-codes cover medical supplies, equipment, and transport services. Documentation must establish medical necessity and include a physician order. For durable medical equipment (DME), a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) or detailed written order may be required.
