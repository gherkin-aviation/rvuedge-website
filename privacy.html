Privacy Policy for RVU Edge

Last Updated: October 15, 2026

Nicholas Len ("we," "our," or "us") operates the RVU Edge mobile application (the "App") and the rvuedge.com website (the "Website"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our App or visit our Website. Section 4.3 covers the Website's analytics and cookies.

The short version

  • What you enter in the App is stored on your device. On iPhone, iPad, and Mac it also syncs to your own private iCloud. On any device you can turn on backups to your own Google Drive. We cannot see your iCloud or Google Drive data.
  • Your case data reaches our server only when you send cases to the RVU Edge Chrome Extension, and it is deleted automatically within about an hour.
  • The App has no analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting tools. The Website uses Google Analytics only if you accept cookies.
  • RVU Edge is not HIPAA-compliant. Never enter patient information.

1. Information the App Stores

1.1 Information You Enter

  • Procedure logs: CPT and ICD-10 codes, modifiers, wRVU values, dates, your role, supervisor names, and optional notes
  • Training records: clinical encounters, rotations, shifts, duty hours, and milestone and EPA assessments
  • Credentials: licenses, DEA registrations, board certifications, hospital privileges, and malpractice coverage, including numbers, dates, and any documents you attach
  • Career records: CV entries and CME credits
  • Profile and settings: name, specialty, career stage, training level, wRVU goals, compensation settings such as your $/wRVU rate, favorites, and preferences

1.2 What the App Does Not Collect

  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking tools, and no crash reporting
  • No location data and no contacts
  • No biometric data. If you turn on the App lock, Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode is checked by your device's operating system; the App only learns whether unlocking succeeded.
  • No patient names, identifiers, or other Protected Health Information (PHI). Do not enter any.

1.3 Camera

The App uses your camera only to scan Peer Compare QR codes. Camera images are not saved or sent anywhere.

2. Where Your Data Is Stored

2.1 On Your Device

The App stores your data in its private storage on your device. That storage is protected by your device's built-in encryption, your passcode, and the operating system's separation between apps. The App does not add its own layer of database encryption.

2.2 Your iCloud (iPhone, iPad, and Mac)

When iCloud is available on your device, the App automatically syncs your data to the App's private database in your own iCloud account, so it appears on your other Apple devices, and saves copies of its automatic backup files to your iCloud. Apple stores this data under Apple's Privacy Policy and encrypts it in transit and on its servers. We cannot access your iCloud data. To stop syncing, turn off iCloud for RVU Edge in your device's Settings → [your name] → iCloud.

2.3 Your Google Drive (Optional)

If you connect Google Drive in the App's Backup settings, the App saves backup files to a hidden folder in your Google Drive that only the App can use. The App cannot see your other Drive files, and we cannot access the backups. You can disconnect in the App's Backup settings or remove RVU Edge's access in your Google Account.

2.4 Files You Export

PDF, Excel, CSV, and backup files you export or share go wherever you send them. They are not sent to us.

3. Information That Reaches Us

3.1 Sending Cases to the Chrome Extension

When you create a transfer code in the App, the cases you selected (CPT codes, dates, roles, supervisor names, and the other case fields needed to fill board portal forms) are uploaded to our server, which runs on Google Firebase in the United States. The cases are kept only until the code is used or expires 10 minutes after it is created, and are then deleted automatically, normally within an hour. To stop people from guessing codes, our server records the IP address of each browser that enters a code on connect.rvuedge.com and keeps it to limit repeated attempts.

3.2 Purchases

Apple and Google process all payments. The App receives your subscription status from the App Store or Google Play so it can unlock features, and the stores send us sales and subscription reports. We never receive your payment card details.

3.3 Support Requests

If you email us, we receive your email address and message and use them only to respond to you.

3.4 Peer Compare

A Peer Compare QR code is created on your device and contains summary figures only: total cases, total wRVUs, cases by category, training level, specialty, and career stage. It is shared only with the person who scans it, directly between devices. Nothing is sent to us.

4. Third-Party Services

4.1 App Store Platforms

Subscription payments are processed through:

  • Apple App Store (governed by Apple's Privacy Policy)
  • Google Play Store (governed by Google's Privacy Policy)

4.2 Apple iCloud, Google Drive, and Google Firebase

iCloud and Google Drive store your data in your own accounts, as described in Section 2. Google Firebase hosts our transfer server and connect.rvuedge.com, as described in Section 3.1.

4.3 Website Analytics and Cookies (rvuedge.com)

Our Website uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are useful. It is off by default. Google Analytics loads only after you click Accept on the cookie banner. If you click Decline, or never answer the banner, no analytics code loads and no analytics cookies are set.

If you accept, Google Analytics receives:

  • The pages you visit, the site that referred you, and when you visited
  • Your browser, operating system, device type, screen size, and language
  • Your approximate location (city and country), which Google works out from your IP address. Google Analytics does not log or store IP addresses.
  • How you use each page: scrolling, video plays, and clicks on App Store and outside links
  • A random visitor ID, stored in the cookies below, that tells a new visit from a repeat visit

Our Website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address and browser information when you load a page and may keep them in server logs for security. Some pages load code libraries from cdnjs.cloudflare.com, cdn.jsdelivr.net, and cdn.tailwindcss.com, which receive the same technical information in order to deliver those files. 7. Your Rights and Choices

7.1 Access and Export

You can export your data at any time in the App: Tools → Export (PDF, Excel, CSV, or a full backup).

7.2 Deletion

  • Procedure logs: Settings → Data Sources → Clear All Data deletes all logged procedures. You can also delete individual records in the App.
  • Everything on a device: uninstalling the App removes its data from that device.
  • iCloud copy: delete RVU Edge's data in your device's iCloud storage settings.
  • Google Drive backups: in Google Drive on the web, open Settings → Manage apps → RVU Edge → Options → Delete hidden app data.
  • Data we hold: transfers delete themselves automatically. For anything else, such as support emails or transfer-code IP records, email us.

7.3 Website Cookies

On the Website, analytics cookies are off unless you accept them. You can change your choice at any time with Cookie settings (see Section 4.3).

11. Chrome Extension ("RVU Edge - Board Portal Auto-Fill")

The RVU Edge Chrome Extension is a companion tool that auto-fills medical board portal case log forms using data exported from the RVU Edge mobile app.

11.1 Data the Extension Collects

  • Case Data: Procedure logs (CPT codes, dates, roles, supervisor names) that you explicitly transfer from the mobile app via a one-time 6-digit code
  • Field Mapping Data: Anonymous DOM selectors (HTML element IDs, names, and types) from board portal form pages, submitted to improve portal compatibility for all users

11.2 Data the Extension Does NOT Collect

  • Patient names, MRNs, or any patient identifiers
  • Portal login credentials or session tokens
  • Browsing history or activity on non-portal pages
  • Personal health information (PHI)

11.3 Data Storage

  • Case data you receive is stored locally in your browser using Chrome's storage API (chrome.storage.local)
  • The extension does not upload case data. Cases pass through our server only during the transfer from your phone (Section 3.1).
  • Transfer codes expire after 10 minutes and are single-use
  • Uninstalling the extension deletes all stored data

11.4 Network Requests

The extension makes the following network requests:

  • connect.rvuedge.com: One-time case transfer from phone to browser (via Firebase Cloud Functions). Transfer data is deleted automatically, normally within an hour of the code being created.
  • Community mapping submission: Anonymous form field structure data (HTML selectors only, no user or patient data) sent to Firebase to improve board portal compatibility.

11.5 Permissions

  • storage: Persist case queue locally across browser sessions
  • activeTab: Access the current tab to fill form fields when the user clicks "Fill Form"
  • scripting: Inject form-filling scripts on portal pages
  • Host permissions: Access specific board portal domains (acgme.org, absurgery.org, abos.org, abog.org, auanet.org, entnet.org) to auto-fill case entry forms

11.6 Form Submission

The extension never automatically submits forms on board portals. It fills fields only — the user must manually review and submit each case on the portal.
