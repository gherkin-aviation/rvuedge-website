Privacy Policy for RVU Edge

Last Updated: October 15, 2026 | Effective Date: January 31, 2026

Nicholas Len ("we," "our," or "us") operates the RVU Edge mobile application (the "App") and the rvuedge.com website (the "Website"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our App or visit our Website. Section 4.3 covers the Website's analytics and cookies.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Procedure Logs: CPT codes, wRVU values, dates, and optional notes you enter
  • User Preferences: Display name, specialty, monthly RVU goals, theme preferences
  • Subscription Information: Purchase history processed through Apple App Store or Google Play Store

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version, app version
  • Usage Data: App feature usage patterns, crash reports (anonymized)

1.3 Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Patient names or identifiers
  • Protected Health Information (PHI)
  • Location data
  • Contact lists or personal contacts
  • Biometric data

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and maintain the App functionality
  • Calculate and display wRVU totals and productivity metrics
  • Store your procedure logs locally on your device
  • Process subscription payments (via App Store/Play Store)
  • Improve App performance and fix bugs
  • Respond to your support requests

3. Data Storage and Security

3.1 Local Storage

All procedure logs, user settings, and personal data are stored locally on your device using encrypted SQLite database storage. We do not operate cloud servers that store your personal data.

3.2 No Cloud Sync

Version 1.0 of RVU Edge does not include cloud synchronization. Your data remains on your device only.

3.3 Data Security

We implement appropriate technical measures to protect your locally stored data, including:

  • SQLite database encryption
  • Secure storage APIs provided by iOS and Android
  • No transmission of personal data over the internet

4. Third-Party Services

4.1 Google Fonts

The App uses Google Fonts to display typography. Font files are downloaded at runtime from Google's servers. Google may collect:

  • IP address
  • Browser/device information

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

4.2 App Store Platforms

Subscription payments are processed through:

  • Apple App Store (governed by Apple's Privacy Policy)
  • Google Play Store (governed by Google's Privacy Policy)

We do not receive or store your payment card information.

4.3 Website Analytics and Cookies (rvuedge.com)

Our Website uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are useful. It is off by default. Google Analytics loads only after you click Accept on the cookie banner. If you click Decline, or never answer the banner, no analytics code loads and no analytics cookies are set.

If you accept, Google Analytics receives:

  • The pages you visit, the site that referred you, and when you visited
  • Your browser, operating system, device type, screen size, and language
  • Your approximate location (city and country), which Google works out from your IP address. Google Analytics does not log or store IP addresses.
  • How you use each page: scrolling, video plays, and clicks on App Store and outside links
  • A random visitor ID, stored in the cookies below, that tells a new visit from a repeat visit
Cookie Set by Purpose Expires

_ga_H9Y3GE1661 Google Analytics Keeps track of the current visit 2 years

Google Analytics' advertising features (Google Signals and ad personalization) are turned off. We do not use Website analytics for advertising, and we do not combine it with data from the App. Google Analytics keeps visit-level data for up to 14 months; after that, only aggregated reports that do not identify anyone remain. Google handles this data under Google's Privacy Policy (see also how Google uses information from sites that use its services).

Changing your choice: use Cookie settings at the bottom of any page. If you withdraw consent, analytics stops and the Google Analytics cookies are deleted from your browser. We honor Global Privacy Control: if your browser sends it, analytics stays off unless you turn it on yourself.

Our Website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address and browser information when you load a page and may keep them in server logs for security. Some pages load code libraries from cdnjs.cloudflare.com, cdn.jsdelivr.net, and cdn.tailwindcss.com, which receive the same technical information in order to deliver those files. 5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties, and we do not share it for targeted (cross-context behavioral) advertising.

We may disclose information only:

  • To comply with legal obligations
  • To protect and defend our rights or property
  • With your explicit consent

6. Your Rights and Choices

6.1 Access and Export

You can export all your procedure data at any time using the Export feature in the App (Settings → Export Data).

6.2 Deletion

You can delete all your data by:

  • Using the "Delete All Data" option in Settings
  • Uninstalling the App (removes all local data)

6.3 Opt-Out

You may opt out of analytics collection in Settings → Privacy.

On the Website, analytics cookies are off unless you accept them. You can change your choice at any time with Cookie settings (see Section 4.3).

10. Chrome Extension ("RVU Edge - Board Portal Auto-Fill")

The RVU Edge Chrome Extension is a companion tool that auto-fills medical board portal case log forms using data exported from the RVU Edge mobile app.

10.1 Data the Extension Collects

  • Case Data: Procedure logs (CPT codes, dates, roles, supervisor names) that you explicitly transfer from the mobile app via a one-time 6-digit code
  • Field Mapping Data: Anonymous DOM selectors (HTML element IDs, names, and types) from board portal form pages, submitted to improve portal compatibility for all users

10.2 Data the Extension Does NOT Collect

  • Patient names, MRNs, or any patient identifiers
  • Portal login credentials or session tokens
  • Browsing history or activity on non-portal pages
  • Personal health information (PHI)

10.3 Data Storage

  • All case data is stored locally in your browser using Chrome's storage API (chrome.storage.local)
  • Case data is never uploaded to our servers
  • Transfer codes expire after 10 minutes and are single-use
  • Uninstalling the extension deletes all stored data

10.4 Network Requests

The extension makes the following network requests:

  • connect.rvuedge.com: One-time case transfer from phone to browser (via Firebase Cloud Functions). Transfer payloads are deleted after retrieval.
  • Community mapping submission: Anonymous form field structure data (HTML selectors only, no user or patient data) sent to Firebase to improve board portal compatibility.

10.5 Permissions

  • storage: Persist case queue locally across browser sessions
  • activeTab: Access the current tab to fill form fields when the user clicks "Fill Form"
  • scripting: Inject form-filling scripts on portal pages
  • Host permissions: Access specific board portal domains (acgme.org, absurgery.org, abos.org, abog.org, auanet.org, entnet.org) to auto-fill case entry forms

10.6 Form Submission

The extension never automatically submits forms on board portals. It fills fields only — the user must manually review and submit each case on the portal.

11. International Data Transfers

Your data is stored locally on your device and is not transferred internationally. Third-party services (Google Fonts, App Stores, and, if you accept cookies, Google Analytics on our Website) may process data in the United States and other countries in accordance with their respective privacy policies.
