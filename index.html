The Only RVU Tracker Built for Your Entire Career

Most wRVU trackers do one thing. RVU Edge does everything — and does it faster.

The Fastest wRVU Tracker Available

Spreadsheets and clunky apps waste your time. RVU Edge's QuickLog lets physicians enter CPT codes at unprecedented speed — type codes continuously and smart parsing handles the rest. No extra taps, no searching through menus. Attendings use it to track wRVU productivity against MGMA benchmarks for 46 specialties. Residents use it to log ACGME cases and meet board minimums before graduation.

ACGME Case Logging Made Simple

Residency case logging shouldn't require a desktop computer and 30 minutes of data entry. RVU Edge supports 14 surgical and medical specialties with board-specific export adapters for ACGME, ABS, ABOS, ABU, ABOG, and more. Log cases from your phone between procedures and export directly to your board portal — or use the Chrome extension to auto-fill portal forms.

MGMA Benchmarks & Practice Analytics

Know where you stand. RVU Edge includes 2025 MGMA benchmark data for 46 specialties, showing your wRVU percentile compared to national physician compensation data. The Practice Analytics spider chart visualizes six dimensions of your clinical practice — Volume, Complexity, Breadth, Consistency, Trend, and Independence — giving you a comprehensive view at a glance.

Credential Vault & CV Builder

Track medical licenses, DEA registrations, CME credits, and board certifications with expiration alerts so nothing lapses. RVU Edge's CV builder auto-generates a professional curriculum vitae from your complete career data — procedures, credentials, education, and publications — all maintained in one place from medical school onward.
