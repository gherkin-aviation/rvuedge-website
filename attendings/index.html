As an attending physician, you are the revenue engine of your practice or health system. Every patient you see, every procedure you perform, and every consult you complete generates wRVUs that directly determine your compensation. Yet most attendings have no independent way to verify that their employer's productivity reports are accurate — or that their compensation truly reflects the work they are doing. You rely on billing departments, administrative dashboards, and quarterly reports that may arrive weeks or months after the fact. By then, missed charges and coding errors have already cost you money.

RVU Edge was built to solve this problem. It gives you a fast, private, independent record of your wRVU production — one that you control. Log your cases in seconds at the point of care, compare your output against national MGMA benchmarks by specialty, and walk into every contract negotiation armed with data instead of guesswork.

Why Attendings Need Independent wRVU Tracking

If your compensation is tied to wRVU production — and for most employed attendings, it is — then you need a reliable, independent way to track your numbers. Relying solely on your employer's reports introduces risk that you may not even realize exists.

  • Verify your employer's productivity reports. Administrative billing systems are complex, and errors are common. Charges get dropped, coding gets downgraded, and procedures get attributed to the wrong provider. An independent log lets you cross-reference your employer's data and catch discrepancies before they affect your paycheck.
  • Ensure all cases are counted. Did that late add-on case get billed? Was the consult you did on the weekend captured? Many attendings discover that 5-15% of their work is never reflected in official reports. Without your own record, you would never know.
  • Catch billing errors and missing charges. Modifier issues, duplicate denials, and delayed charge entry can silently reduce your reported wRVUs. When you track independently, you have a baseline to compare against — and the proof to dispute inaccuracies.
  • Build an independent dataset for contract negotiations. When your contract is up for renewal, your employer controls the data narrative. Having your own verified production history changes that dynamic entirely. You bring facts to the table, not estimates.

Independent tracking is not about distrust — it is about professional diligence. The same way you would never blindly accept a tax return without reviewing it, you should never accept a compensation statement without verifying the underlying productivity data.

Compare Against MGMA Benchmarks

Knowing your wRVU total is only half the equation. The real question is: how does your production compare to your peers nationwide? That is where MGMA benchmarks become essential. The Medical Group Management Association publishes annual compensation and productivity data that serves as the industry standard for physician compensation models.

  • Built-in MGMA data by specialty. RVU Edge includes MGMA benchmark data for every major specialty, so you can see exactly where you stand without looking it up manually or purchasing expensive reports.
  • See your percentile in real-time. As you log cases throughout the month, RVU Edge automatically calculates your annualized pace and shows you which MGMA percentile you are tracking toward — 25th, median, 75th, or 90th.
  • Know where you stand before annual reviews. Do not wait for your employer to tell you how you performed. Walk into your annual review already knowing your percentile ranking, your production trend, and how your compensation compares to market rates for your level of output.
  • Explore detailed benchmark data. For a comprehensive breakdown of MGMA wRVU and compensation benchmarks across specialties, see our MGMA Benchmarks by Specialty guide.

Prepare for Contract Negotiations

Contract negotiations are where your wRVU data becomes your most powerful asset. Whether you are negotiating an initial employment agreement, a renewal, or a transition to a new practice, concrete productivity data changes the conversation from subjective impressions to objective evidence.

  • Document your productivity trend over months and years. A single month of data is interesting. Twelve months of consistent, above-median production is compelling. RVU Edge maintains your full history so you can demonstrate sustained high performance, not just a lucky quarter.
  • Show concrete data, not estimates. When you say "I produce at the 75th percentile," your employer can push back. When you show a detailed log with CPT codes, dates, wRVU values, and MGMA comparisons, the conversation shifts. Data wins negotiations.
  • Export professional reports in PDF and CSV. RVU Edge lets you export your production data in formats suitable for negotiation meetings. Hand your administrator a clean PDF summary or a CSV for their analysts to verify. Professionalism builds credibility.
  • Understand your market value. By combining your wRVU production data with MGMA compensation benchmarks, you can calculate exactly what your work is worth on the open market. If you are producing at the 75th percentile but being compensated at the 50th, you have a clear, data-driven case for an adjustment.
